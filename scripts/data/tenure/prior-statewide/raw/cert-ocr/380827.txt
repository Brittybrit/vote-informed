STAFF REMARKS: The Applicants have requested a sovereignty submerged land lease
modification on Lake Okeechobee for an existing 584,954 square foot public marina. The marina
is currently being operated by the City as a fee-waived facility.

Lease History

On May 7, 1956, the Board of Trustees issued a Use Permit to the City for the use and
control of a parcel of Lake Okeechobee reclaimed lake bottom related to an existing
breakwater, on behalf of the public, to afford protection to marine vessels for navigation,
refuge, wharfage, mooring, docking, and servicing.

On November 18, 1986, the Board of Trustees approved a lease to the City for the adjacent
state-owned uplands for the purpose of developing and managing a public outdoor -
recreation area.

On October 30, 2001, the City requested and obtained approval from the Board of Trustees
to sublease the state-owned uplands to Everglades Ventures Company, L.L.C. (“EVC”)
with reference to the City’s plans to obtain a sovereignty submerged lands lease for the
marina facility and the subsequent sublease of sovereignty submerged lands lease to EVC
for operation of the marina.

On May 13, 2003, the Board of Trustees approved the conversion of the Use Permit to a
five-year sovereignty submerged lands lease, with a five-year renewal option subject to the
Board of Trustees’ approval; and the modification of the fee structure based strictly on six
percent of the annual rental value from the wet slip rental area because the City was
designated as a Rural Area of Critical Economic Concern.

In September of 2004, the marina was severely damaged by Hurricanes Frances and
Jeanne. On May 18, 2005, the Department of Environmental Protection (DEP) issued an
Environmental Resource Permit (No. 50-0129049-008) authorizing the reconstruction and
reconfiguration of the facility.

On June 16, 2005, the Board of Trustees approved a lease modification to increase the
preempted lease area from 401,362 square feet to 584,954 square feet and authorized the
construction of a permeable rock breakwater and fishing pier.

On May 2, 2006, the Board of Trustees approved a 108-slip expansion to the existing 116-
slip docking facility, creating a 224-slip facility within the existing sovereignty submerged
lands lease maintaining the fee structure at six percent of the annual rental value from the
wet slip rental area as approved by the Board of Trustees on May 13, 2003.

On January 27, 2009, the Board of Trustees approved a lease renewal and maintenance of
the existing fee structure of six percent of the annual rental value from the rental of wet
slips.

On January 31, 2012, the City and EVC terminated the sublease, and the facilities’
operation and maintenance responsibilities reverted solely to the City. Since such time,
the marina has operated as a fee-waived facility by the City, pursuant section 18-21.011,
F.A.C.
