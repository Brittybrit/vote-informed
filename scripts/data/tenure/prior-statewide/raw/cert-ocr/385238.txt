Item5 Florida Department of Transportation Modification of Deed Restriction/ Consent

REQUEST: Consideration of (1) a request from Florida Department of Transportation for a
Modification of Deed Restriction for an approximate 18.766-acre parcel of submerged lands
conveyed in Board of Trustees’ Deed No. 19334, now owned by the Florida Department of
Transportation; and (2) provide consent to the Florida Department of Transportation to lease an
approximate .564-acre parcel to Seven Kings Holdings, Inc.

VOTING REQUIREMENT FOR APPROVAL: Three votes

COUNTY: Palm Beach
Deed No. 19334

APPLICANT: Florida Department of Transportation
LOCATION: Section 27, Township 42 South, Range 43 East
STAFF REMARKS:

Background

On June 21, 1947, the Board of Trustees conveyed a portion of submerged lands in Board of
Trustees’ Deed No. 19334 (Deed) to the Florida Department of Transportation (FDOT). The Deed
contained two restriction clauses, to limit the parcel to road purposes and restricting the parcel
from being sold, leased, or encumbered.

Deed No. 19334: “PROVIDED, HOWEVER, this grant is upon the express condition that
if here-after, the land herein above described shall be abandoned for road purposes, then
the fee simple title hereby granted to said State Road Department shall automatically revert
to the Trustees of the Internal Improvement Fund;”

“PROVIDED, HOWEVER, that it is expressly agreed upon by the parties hereto as a
condition to this conveyance that the State Road Department shall have no right or

Current Request

Seven Kings Holdings, Inc. (SKH) has a leasehold interest, for a parcel, owned by Riviera Shores,
LLC, abutting the northwest corner of FDOT’s owned lands. SKH is currently in the development
phases of reconstructing a restaurant, known as Crab Pot, which was destroyed by hurricanes in
2004 and 2005. The reconstruction includes associated parking spaces and docks for vessel
mooring along Lake Worth Lagoon. Six of the proposed parking spaces, and the docking facility,
lie within FDOT lands subject to the aforementioned restrictions. The proposed restaurant will
offer a potential of 80 new employment opportunities, increase the market value of a blighted part
of the City of Riviera Beach (City), and provide an increase in tax revenue for the City.

Due to the nature of the proposed parking, docking, and transient vessel mooring, it is necessary
to modify the purpose of the Deed from road purposes to public purposes. All spaces, whether
vehicular or vessel, will be open to the general public on a first-come first-served basis. FDOT
requests approval to modify the Deed restriction purpose and to approve a lease for an approximate
.564-acre parcel to SKH for a term of five-years with one option to renew for an additional five-
years. All rents due to FDOT will be used to supplement future state-funded transportation
projects. Additionally, the proposed lease to SKH prohibits any use that would interfere with
FDOT’s transportation facilities.

Revised Deed Restriction:

Deed No. 19334: “PROVIDED, HOWEVER, this grant is upon the express condition that
if here-after, the land herein above described shall be abandoned for public purposes, then
the fee simple title hereby granted to said State Road Department shall automatically revert
to the Trustees of the Internal Improvement Fund;”

“PROVIDED, HOWEVER, that it is expressly agreed upon by the parties hereto as a
condition to this conveyance that the State Road Department shall have no right or
authority to sell, lease or encumber the above described property without the written
consent of the said Trustees or their joinder in any sale, lease, or encumbrance.”

Comprehensive Plan

A consideration on the status of the local government comprehensive plan was not made for this
item. The Department of Environmental Protection has determined that this action is not subject
to the local government planning process.
