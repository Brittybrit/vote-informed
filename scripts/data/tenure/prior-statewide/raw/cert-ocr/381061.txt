STAFF REMARKS: In accordance with rules adopted pursuant to Sections 373.427(2) and
253.77(2), F.S., this "Recommended Consolidated Notice” contains a recommendation for
issuance of both the permit required under Part IV of Chapter 373, F.S., and the authorization to
use sovereignty submerged lands under Chapter 253, F.S. The Board of Trustees is requested to
act on those aspects of the activity which require authorization to use sovereignty submerged lands.

The project is required to demonstrate that it is “not contrary to the public interest,” pursuant to
Rule 18-21.004(1)(a), F.A.C. The applicant has provided reasonable assurance that the proposal
will maintain essentially natural conditions; will not significantly impact fish and wildlife and
other natural resources, including public recreation and navigation, and will not interfere with the
riparian rights of adjacent property owners. Therefore, the Department of Environmental
Protection (DEP) is of the opinion that the proposal is “not contrary to the public interest” and
otherwise meets all applicable requirements for a proprietary authorization to use sovereignty
submerged lands.

Background

The site consists of an upland commercial resort including residential communities, retail shops,
restaurants, and a golf course. The applicant currently has a sovereign submerged lands lease for
a 137-slip commercial docking facility with a preempted area of 504,585 square feet of sovereignty
submerged lands.

On October 2, 2018, Permit #015661 1-013-EI/66 was issued for the addition of ten slips along the
western pier and the “squaring off” of the southwestern corner of the facility. The applicant’s
lease modification was inadvertently not processed by the Department. During compliance
discussions, the applicant indicated that they wanted to further expand their commercial docking
facility. On December 2, 2019, a Temporary Use Agreement (TUA) was executed for use of these
ten slips and the associated five finger piers, for an additional preemption of 48,793 square feet.
The applicant is in compliance with the terms of the TUA and has paid all required fees. The TUA
will expire on June 12, 2020.

Project Detail

The applicant is proposing to expand their facility, by removing eight existing slips and
constructing 128 additional slips, thereby creating a 257-slip facility for vessels; ranging from a
standard Personal Water Craft (PWC) to 100-feet in length, with drafts up to 6-feet. The proposed
expansion will preempt an additional 217,145 square feet of sovereignty submerged lands, for a
new total preemption of 721,730 square feet.

Five slips are designated for use by potentially more than one vessel at a time, as they are
temporary mooring to access the fuel/pumpout area and drive-up customers to access the upland
restaurant and resort complex. These slips are located along the eastern face of Dock 4, the
southern faces of Docks 1 through 3, and the western face of the fuel/pumpout platform.
