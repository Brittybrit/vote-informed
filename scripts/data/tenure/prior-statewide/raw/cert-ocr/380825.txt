and Dugan Properties I, LLC to purchase approximately 0.052 acres, 0.004 acres, and 0.019 acres,
respectively, of formerly filled, submerged, sovereignty lands in Collier County.

Background

Pursuant to section 253.12(6), F.S., sovereignty submerged lands filled prior to June 11, 1957,
shail be conveyed to the riparian owners upon application. Following discussions with the
Applicants and review of supporting historic aerial photography, DSL has determined the subject
areas were filled between 1952 and 1954, qualifying for conveyance to the riparian owners. The
area was subsequently dredged sometime before 1958. The consideration for the conveyances
shall be the appraised value of the subject parcels as they existed prior to such filling. Historic
data was used to determine the value of the subject parcels. As part of this agreement, the
Applicants will furnish the Board of Trustees quitclaim deeds for any submerged land waterward
of the area being requested. Approval of this item does not supersede or eliminate any local, state
or federal permitting requirements.

Noticing

The request is not subject to noticing, pursuant to section 253.115(5)(c), F.S.

Comprehensive Plan

A consideration of the status of the local government comprehensive plan was not made for this

item. DEP has determined that the proposed action is not subject to the local government planning
process.
