Item 4 City of Fort Lauderdale and Las Olas SMI, LLC Lease Modification/
Recommended Consolidated Intent/ Private Easement/ Determination

REQUEST: Consideration of an application for (1) a modification of a twenty-five-year
sovereignty submerged lands lease to (a) increase the preempted area from 203,678 square feet to
374,623 square feet, more or less, for a public commercial marina facility and (b) increase
overall slip number from 60 to 66 slips; (2) a new ten-year sovereignty submerged lands private
easement containing 107,867 square feet (88,067 square feet north dredge area, 19,800 square feet
south dredge area) for dredging adjacent to the existing channel and marina; (3) the installation of
approximately 900 linear feet of seawall with a single row of riprap footer; (4) authorization for
dredging of 576,103 square feet (352,986 square feet within the submerged land lease, 107,867
square feet within the proposed private easement, and 115,250 square feet from uplands) adjacent
to the existing channel and marina, and; (5) authorization for the waiver of severance fees for the
dredging of approximately 101,954 cubic yards of sovereignty material (79,192 cubic yards within
the submerged land lease and 22,792 cubic yards within proposed private easement).

VOTING REQUIREMENT FOR APPROVAL: Three votes

STAFF REMARKS: In accordance with rules adopted pursuant to sections 373.427(2) and
253.77(2), F.S., this "Recommended Consolidated Notice" contains a recommendation for
issuance of both the permit required under part IV of chapter 373, F.S., and the authorization to
use sovereignty submerged lands under chapter 253, F.S. The Board of Trustees is requested to
act on those aspects of the activity which require authorization to use sovereignty submerged lands.

The project is required to demonstrate that it is “not contrary to the public interest,” pursuant to
Article X, Section 11 of the Florida Constitution, chapter 253, F.S., and rule 18-21.004(1)(a),
F.A.C. The applicants have provided reasonable assurance that the proposal will maintain
essentially natural conditions and will not significantly impact fish, wildlife and other natural
resources, including public recreation and navigation. Additionally, for the proposed dredge which
interferes with the riparian rights of adjacent property owners, the applicants have received signed
waivers from those affected property owners. Therefore, the Department of Environmental
Protection (DEP) is of the opinion that the proposal is “not contrary to the public interest” and
otherwise meets all applicable requirements for a proprietary authorization to use sovereignty
submerged lands.

DEP’s Bureau of Appraisal hired an independent appraiser to conduct an appraisal to determine
the value of the private easement area and the enhanced value of the easement area. The lessee
paid for the appraisal. DEP’s Bureau of Appraisal accepted the conclusion of $41,650 for the
easement value. The Board of Trustees typically assesses 10 to 25 percent of the enhanced value
of the easement, depending on the degree of preemption by the proposed project. In this case, the
preemption will be minimal because the dredge area may still be used for navigation by the public.
