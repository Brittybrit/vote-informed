STAFF REMARKS:

Lease History

New Hope Sugar Company (New Hope) currently leases 1,234 acres located in the Everglades
Agricultural Area (EAA) in Palm Beach County under Board of Trustees’ Lease No. 3433 (Lease)
for agricultural purposes. The property is surrounded by land owned by the South Florida Water
Management District (SFWMD) which is also leased and farmed by New Hope. New Hope and
the Board of Trustees entered into the original lease for the property in 1995 as part of a land
exchange that allowed SFWMD to acquire other lands owned by New Hope for the construction
of projects associated with the Everglades Forever Act. In 2014, the parties agreed to amend and
extend the lease for a 30-year term as part of another land exchange to implement Everglades
Restoration Strategies. The amended lease contains a special termination provision which provides

Everglades Agricultural Area Reservoir Project

In 2017, the Legislature passed Chapter 2017-10, Laws of Florida (Senate Bill 10), which
established options for providing water storage south of Lake Okeechobee, including the
Everglades Agricultural Area Reservoir Project (EAA Reservoir Project). The reservoir is
anticipated to hold 240,000 acre-feet of water and includes a stormwater treatment area to provide
additional water quality benefits. Section 373.4598(3), Florida Statutes, authorizes the Board of
Trustees and SFWMD to negotiate the amendment or termination of leases on land for the EAA
Reservoir Project. The law also provided $30 million for the purposes of acquiring-land or
negotiating leases to implement the EAA Reservoir Project.

On January 10, 2019, Governor DeSantis issued Executive Order 2019-12 directing SFWMD and
the Department of Environmental Protection (DEP) to expedite the EAA Reservoir Project. Based
on that direction, SFWMD developed an accelerated construction schedule for the stormwater
treatment area associated with the EAA Reservoir Project requiring that SFWMD have access to
the property earlier than would be allowed under the termination clause of this Lease.

Proposed Amendment to Terminate Lease

As authorized by Section 373.4598(3), Florida Statutes, DEP, as staff to the Board of Trustees,
negotiated with New Hope for an early termination of the Lease to facilitate the expedited
construction of the EAA Reservoir. The proposed amendment to the Lease provides for a mutually
agreed upon termination schedule based on the expedited construction schedule established by
SFWMD. The termination schedule provides for a phased termination of certain fields on specific
dates in accordance with SFWMD’s construction schedule. As consideration for the early
termination of the Lease, the Board of Trustees agrees to pay $1,940/acre on the dates of
termination under the schedule. The total consideration for early termination of the Lease is
$2,393,960. This consideration will be paid from the Everglades Trust Fund pursuant to Chapter
2017-10, Laws of Florida.

On September 30, 2019, New Hope provided a Notice of Termination of the lease between
SFWMD and New Hope pursuant to the same schedule as proposed for the termination of Lease
No. 3433. Therefore, if the proposed amendment is executed, SFWMD will have all necessary
access to the property for the expedited construction of the Stormwater Treatment Area associated
with the EAA Reservoir Project.

DEP is recommending the Board of Trustees approve the lease amendment to terminate Lease No.
3433 to facilitate the expedited construction of the Stormwater Treatment Area associated with the
EAA Reservoir Project.
