Item 3 City of Fort Lauderdale Partial Modification of Deed Restriction

REQUEST: Consideration of a request from the City of Fort Lauderdale for a Partial Modification
of Deed Restriction in Board of Trustees Deed No. 21846.

VOTING REQUIREMENT FOR APPROVAL: Three votes

COUNTY: Broward

APPLICANT: City of Fort Lauderdale (“City”)

LOCATION: Section 12, Township 50 South, Range 42 East, New River Sound

STAFF REMARKS:

The City is requesting a partial modification to an existing Board of Trustees’ deed restriction
changing the fee structure to allow for the redevelopment of a waterfront parcel on New River
Sound.

BACKGROUND:

On May 27, 1958, the Board of Trustees conveyed approximately 7.64-acres of sovereignty,
submerged land to the City by Board of Trustees Deed No. 21846. The deed contained a restriction
requiring the property to be used for public municipal purposes. If used for any other purpose,
title to the property would revert to the Board of Trustees. The submerged land was subsequently
filled in the late 1950’s and then developed as a surface parking lot by the City of Fort Lauderdale.

On October 24, 1989, the Board of Trustees approved a Release of Deed Restriction and Reverter
(“Reverter”) modifying the deed restrictions to allow the subject parcel to be leased to a private
developer; a portion of which is now being leased by the City to Las Olas SMI, LLC (“Subject

Parcel”). The City’s plan in 1989 contemplated developing a hospitality-focused resort property
with several high-rise buildings, resort amenities, and conference center spaces. Over
approximately 20 years, the City entertained and subsequently rejected numerous bids and
proposals for large scale developments covering the subject parcel and adjoining property.

In 2009, the City Commission began reviewing preliminary design concepts for key public
improvement projects on the beach, and, over the next eight years, initiated various outreach
procedures to create a long-term plan for implementing these public improvement projects. The
goal of this outreach was to consolidate these improvement projects into an amended Beach
Community Redevelopment Plan (“Plan”). The Plan was amended to include an expansion of the
Las Olas Marina, the development of a public waterfront promenade, 5,000 linear feet of dock
space, a marina operations and marine services building, waterfront restaurants, anda public water
taxi stop as part of the Las Olas Marina expansion.

A Request for Proposal (“RFP”) to develop the Las Olas Marina was then issued in accordance
with the amended Plan and the City received four proposals. On September 19, 2016, the City’s
RFP Evaluation Committee recommended approving the Las Olas SMI, LLC’s (“SMI”) bid, and
on October 4, 2016, the City Commission unanimously voted to award the bid to SMI On April
30, 2018 the City and SMI entered into a Ground Lease Agreement.

The Las Olas Marina expansion project was submitted to the City’s Development Review
Committee in March of 2018 and approved by the City Commission on July 9, 2019, by unanimous
vote.

PROPOSED PROJECT AND ECONOMIC IMPACTS

The approved marina site plan includes development of the subject parcel, pursuant to the
requirements in the City’s Plan. The marina site plan includes the dredging of a portion of the
subject parcel for the expansion of a marina, in addition to waterfront developments, such as retail,
offices, restaurant, and other ancillary facilities to support the marina. Additionally, the Plan
provides substantial infrastructure improvements, including the replacement of over 750 feet of
aging sewer lines, and the construction of a state-of-the-art lift station for use by the marina and
all of the adjacent condominiums. Estimated jobs and revenue from the development of the
