Project Detail

The Board of Trustees holds Lease No. 2628 to the Department of Children and Families, of which
17.78 acres are in Sublease No. 2628-01 to Florida Pathways, Inc. (Pathways) a Florida not for
profit corporation and affiliate of Miami Jewish Health Systems, Inc., (MJHS). Within this
sublease is 3.56-acre Sub-sublease No. 2628-01-01 from Pathways to DGN V, Ltd., to operate an
affordable housing, independent living and health care services as a single-purpose legal entity.
The services include adult day care, nursing and skilled care, medical care and diagnostic centers,
long-term care services and independent housing. Pathways also constructed and operates three
HUD Section 202 buildings on Board of Trustees Lease No. 4329 to DGN; Lease No. 4495 to
DGN II; and Lease No. 4616 to DGN III, with needed apartment units.

There are approximately 14 acres of vacant land within Sublease No. 2628-01, and adjacent to the
DGN leases, that are suitable for additional affordable senior housing. Pathways is now proposing
to construct from one to three affordable senior housing facilities on the vacant 14-acre parcel
utilizing a HUD affordable housing grant, federal Low-Income Housing Tax Credits (LIHTC) or
other similar funding. To provide flexibility when applying for funding to develop future senior
housing on the approximately 14 acres, the Applicants are requesting approval of the following
actions:

e Release Sub-sublease 2628-01-01 to DGN V, Ltd., under authority delegated by the
Board of Trustees, and allow DGN V, Ltd, to enter into a direct 75-year lease with the
Board of Trustees to operate the housing on site;

e Assign Sublease No. 2628-01 to Pathways, under authority delegated by the Board of
Trustees for a direct 75-year lease from the Board of Trustees which meets qualifying
requirements for future funding as a single-purpose legal entity with established site
control.
