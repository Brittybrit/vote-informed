STAFF REMARKS: Florida has a long history as a national leader in conservation, which is
vital to our state’s environmental protection and economic growth. Florida Forever, the state’s
premier land conservation program, helps acquire natural lands that conserve critical habitats,
protect water resources, benefit Everglades restoration and enhance local communities. By
focusing on the conservation and management of Florida’s natural lands, the health of
environmental resources are improved, public access is expanded, and visitor experiences are
enhanced.

The Acquisition and Restoration Council (ARC) is established by section 259.035, F.S., as a ten-
member board composed of four agency heads, four governor appointees, one appointee by the
Commissioner of Agriculture, and one appointee by the Fish and Wildlife Conservation
Commission. In accordance with section 259.105(14), F.S., ARC shall review the most current,
approved project list and present a new list of projects to the Board of Trustees annually for
approval.

In 2018, ARC conducted 8 public hearings and meetings throughout the year and reviewed land
acquisition proposals, evaluated their resource attributes, established or revised project boundaries,
ranked projects in priority order, and adopted an annual Work Plan. ARC approved the following

(1) 2019 Florida Forever Priority List: The 2019 Florida Forever Priority List, adopted by ARC
on December 14, 2018, includes 124 projects. Each project was ranked within one of six categories
as follows:

Critical Natural Lands (CNL) — 33 projects;

Partnerships and Regional Incentives (PRI) — 30 projects;
Less-Than-Fee (LTF) — 33 projects;

Climate Change Lands (CCL) — 14 projects;
Substantially Complete (SC) — 9 projects; and

Critical Historical Resources (CHR) — 5 projects.

In compliance with section 259.04(1)(c), F.S., “...the board shall approve, in whole or in part, the
lists of projects in the order of priority in which such projects are presented.” Additionally,
pursuant to 259.105(14), F.S., “...The board of trustees may remove projects from the list
developed pursuant to this subsection, but may not add projects or rearrange project rankings.”

(2) 2019 Florida Forever Five-Year Plan: The Florida Forever Five-Year Plan, pursuant to
chapter 259, F.S., and rule 18-24, F.A.C, consists of detailed descriptions for each of the 124 land
acquisition projects on the ARC approved 2019 Florida Forever Priority List. The Plan can be
accessed at 2019 Florida Forever Five-Year Plan.
