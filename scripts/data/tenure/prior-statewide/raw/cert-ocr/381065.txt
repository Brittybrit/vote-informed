Project Description

The Strategic Managed Area Lands List (SMALL) Florida Forever project, sponsored by
Department of Environmental Protection’s (DEP) Division of Recreation and Parks (DRP), DEP’s
Office of Greenways and Trails (OGT), Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services’
Florida Forest Service (DACS-FFS), and Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission
(FFWCC), is a collection of parcels statewide that would augment or improve management of
existing state-managed conservation lands, if acquired. The parcels included in this project have
been identified as part of the optimum management boundary within the approved management
plans of ten state parks, seven trail corridors, ten state forests, and seven wildlife
management/environmental areas.

Property Description

The subject parcel is approximately a 17-acre strategic inholding to DRP’s River Rise Preserve
State Park situated on the north bank of the Santa Fe River where first magnitude spring, Columbia
Spring, emerges and flows into the river. Located on the southern Columbia County line, along
the west side of US Highway 41/441, the property contains approximately 10.8 upland acres, 6.4
wetland acres, and benefits from approximately 1,700 feet of frontage on the Santa Fe River.

Columbia Spring, located on the subject parcel, is where a portion of the Santa Fe River emerges
from underground. While technically a spring, the characteristics are that of a river rise, as the
water source is a re-emergence of the Santa Fe River. The spring pool is roughly 75 feet by 150
feet with a total run of 450 feet, including the spring pool.

The subject parcel is improved with a manufactured home that is currently used as a residence as
well as various other smaller storage buildings and shelters. All structures and improvements will
be utilized by DRP as the manager of the property.

Acquisition of this parcel will protect prominent hydrological features of the Santa Fe River
system, Columbia Spring, and its spring-run stream. The parcel’s frontage on the Santa Fe River
could provide excellent resource-based recreational opportunities.

Mortgages and Liens/Encumbrances

All mortgages and liens will be satisfied at the time of closing. On June 22, 1999, the Board of
Trustees approved a staff recommendation to delegate to the Department of Environmental
Protection (DEP) the authority to review and evaluate marketability issues as they arise on all
Chapter 259, F.S., acquisitions and to resolve them appropriately. Therefore, DEP will review,
evaluate, and implement an appropriate resolution for these and any other title issues that arise
prior to closing.
