Item2 City of Pahokee Partial Release of Restriction and Reverter

REQUEST: Consideration of a Partial Release of Restriction and Reverter from Board of
Trustees’ Dedication No. 23006 to an approximately 34.55-acre parcel dedicated to the City of
Pahokee.

VOTING REQUIREMENT FOR APPROVAL: Three votes

COUNTY: Palm Beach
Dedication No. 23006

APPLICANT: City of Pahokee (City)
LOCATION: Section 19, Township 42 South, Range 37 East

CONSIDERATION: $303,500, based on an appraisal, to be deposited into the Internal
Improvement Trust Fund.

STAFF REMARKS:

Background

On February 28, 1962, the Board of Trustees dedicated approximately 54.82 acres to the City for
public municipal purposes subject to the requirement of a dike and other water control measures.
The dedication provides for reversion, at the option of the Board of Trustees, in the event the City
uses the property for other than public municipal purposes or fails to use the property for a period
of three consecutive years.

Dedication No. 23006: “That this dedication shall be and remain in force as long as the lands
herein described and which shall have been made available to the City of Pahokee shall be used
for public municipal purpose only and in the event any area so made available shall be no longer

used for said purposes or shall be abandoned by the City and not be so used for three consecutive
years then, and in such event this dedication shall become, at the option of the Trustees of the
Internal Improvement Trust Fund, subject to revocation as to the area no longer used for such
purposes;”

Subsequently, on November 13, 2012, the City assigned 100 percent of its interest in Dedication
No. 23006, under Dedication No. 23006A, to the Glades Utility Authority subject to the conditions,
covenants, and provisions required to be performed under Dedication 23006. The Glades Utility
Authority had formed to consolidate utility services in Pahokee, Belle Glade, and South Bay and
needed site control to provide the community services.

On June 13, 2013, the Glades Utility Authority assigned to Palm Beach County approximately
20.27 acres, under Dedication No. 23006B, subject to the conditions, covenants, and provisions
required to be performed under Dedication 23006. Palm Beach County assumed the responsibility
for the provision of water and sewer services. Palm Beach County has built those facilities and is
the current service provider.

On February 13, 2015, the Glades Utility Authority assigned approximately 34.55 acres (100
percent of its remaining interest) to the City, under Dedication No. 23006C, subject to the
conditions, covenants, and provisions required to be performed under Dedication No. 23006. The
lands were no longer being used by the Glades Utility Authority and were not needed for the Palm
Beach water and sewer project.

Current Request

The City is requesting the Partial Release of Restriction and Reverter on approximately 34.55 acres
(subject parcel) to allow opportunities for economic development, such as workforce housing,
affordable housing, or Public Private Partnerships.

The City has submitted Resolution 2019-30, approved June 11, 2019, authorizing the request for
a Partial Release of Restriction and Reverter in Board of Trustees’ Dedication No. 23006 for the
subject parcel. If this request for the partial release is approved by the Board of Trustees, the City
will have opportunities to further promote economic development for the City. The City is
designated as an Area of Rural Opportunity and the Department of Environmental Protection
(DEP) is recommending the Board of Trustees approve the City’s request.

This Partial Release of Restriction and Reverter, for the subject parcel, in no way waives any
regulatory requirements including but not limited to those of DEP, Army Corps of Engineers,
South Florida Water Management District (SFWMD), and local government.
