Item 6 Florida Department of Children and Families/ Citrus Health Network, Inc./ Lease
and Sublease Agreements/ Determination

REQUEST: Consideration of (1) a determination that, pursuant to section 18-2.018(3)(a)1.,
F.A.C., extending the term of Board of Trustees Lease No. 2509, and any sublease terms within,
is in the public interest; and (2) a request to extend the current lease term for Board of Trustees

VOTING REQUIREMENT FOR APPROVAL: Three votes
COUNTY: Miami-Dade

APPLICANT: Florida Department of Children and Families (DCF)
LOCATION: Section 31, Township 51 South, Range 41 East

STAFF REMARKS:

Background

DCF currently holds Board of Trustees Lease No. 2509 for health and rehabilitative
purposes. DCF has Sublease No. 2509-10 on a portion of the leased area with Citrus Health
Network, Inc. (CHN), a Florida not for profit corporation, and a service provider for victims of
human trafficking, which is a priority target population. CHN has a long-term business
relationship with DCF and has provided mental health services in Florida since 1979.

The CHN sublease was provided to develop a Statewide Inpatient Psychiatric Program (SIPP) on
the site. A SIPP is an inpatient psychiatric treatment program for individuals under the age of
eighteen (18). CHWN also provides specialized therapeutic foster and group care programs. The
State has identified an increasing need for these types of programs in order to serve the child
welfare adolescent population presenting with serious mental health and behavioral disorders. In
addition, CHN was awarded a contract as the Lead Agency for Community-Based Care Southern
Region — Circuits 11 and 16. This contract is to manage and oversee child welfare services in

Miami-Dade and Monroe Counties. The SIPP, and other potential programs on the site, benefit
adolescents in the child welfare system, but the facility needs to expand for these programs. On
December 3, 2019, DCF and CHN executed a Memorandum of Understanding to address the
extension.

Project Detail

DCF is requesting a thirty-one (31) year extension to their current lease, which expires in June of
2069, to extend the-sublease to assist CHN in securing funding to construct program facilities. In
order to obtain financing for health and rehabilitative projects from funding entities such as Florida
Housing Finance Corporation (FHFC) (a low-income housing tax credit or state apartment
incentive loan [“SAIL”] grant), U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)
Section 202 Supportive Housing (should funding become available), or most bank and municipal
funding, an extended term is needed. Historically, HUD, as well as private and other financing
programs, consider facilities with direct leases, or longer-term leases, more secure and similar to
fee simple ownership. The extended lease term will enable CHN and its Sublessees to seek
financing to construct new facilities for the expansion of therapeutic treatment programs related to
human trafficking and permanent supportive housing. Currently, DCF is in the process of
executing an amendment to Sublease 2509-10 to add a vacant 10-acre parcel that will buffer the
facilities from proposed future development of the adjacent property by the owner, Miami-Dade
County. If this item is approved, then this amendment will have the extended term as well.

Public Interest

Pursuant to section 18-2.018(3)(a)1., F.A.C., the extended lease term is required to be in the public
interest. Staff is recommending the Board of Trustees make the determination that the extended
term is in the public interest, as it would assist CHN in securing funding to construct additional
therapeutic treatment programs facilities for adolescents.

Comprehensive Plan

A local government comprehensive plan has been adopted for this area pursuant to section
163.3167, F.S. DEP has determined that the proposed action is not subject to the local planning
process.
