Item 4 Chapter 18-21, F.A.C., Notice of Change

REQUEST: Consideration of a request to (1) publish a Notice of Change for Rules 18-21.021
and 18-21.022, Florida Administrative Code (F.A.C.); and (2) file the proposed amendment for
final adoption with the Department of State, pursuant to section 120.54(3)(e)(1), Florida Statutes
(F.S.).

VOTING REQUIREMENT FOR APPROVAL: Two members, one of whom is the Governor,
when four members are voting; or any two members, when three members are voting.

COUNTY: Statewide
APPLICANT: Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services (FDACS)

STAFF REMARKS: In accordance with subsection 253.03(7), F.S., FDACS requests the Board
of Trustees approve a Notice of Change to the following rules:

18-21.021 Applications for Aquacultural Activities.

The proposed notice of change is necessary to comply with subparagraph 120.55(1)(a)4., F.S.,
which requires that language incorporating a form into rule include the effective date of the form.
The previous rule text did not include the effective date of the form.

18-21.022 Payments and Fees for Aquacultural Activities.

The proposed notice of change is necessary to comply with subparagraph 120.54(1)(@)1., F.S.,
which requires that the incorporation of materials requires specific incorporating language,
including the effective date of the incorporated materials. The previous rule text did not have the
proper incorporating language.
