Project Description

The Etoniah/Cross Florida Greenway (ECFG) along the Ocklawaha River is important for the
survival of many kinds of wildlife and plants. The ECFG itself is a unique strip of land for
recreation and conservation that makes a cross-section of the peninsula from the Withlacoochee
River to the St. Johns River. The project ensures that wildlife, such as Florida black bear and scrub
jays, and plants, such as the Etoniah rosemary, will have areas in which to live. It also provides
recreation for the public ranging from long-distance hiking trails to fishing, camping, and hunting.
This project may also help complete the Florida National Scenic Trail, a statewide non-motorized
trail that crosses several Florida Forever project sites.

Property Description

The subject property includes a total of 25 non-contiguous individual parcels ranging in size from
1 acre to 200 acres. The subject property is wooded with a blend of pines and hardwoods with
natural underbrush. The majority of the parcels include one-hundred-percent uplands and
approximately half of these parcels include some extent of flood plain. All of the parcels border
ECFG lands along a stretch of the Ocklawaha River in northern Marion County, from Ocklawaha
Lake at the Marion/Putnam County line to just south of Gore’s Landing west of Cedar Creek.

Acquisition of the subject property will enhance the contiguous recreational and wildlife corridor
through the eastern portion of the ECFG, bordering the Ocala National Forest, as well as protect
the hydrological features of the Ocklawaha River system. This acquisition will serve to protect
and enhance natural and cultural resources and water quality for the Ocklawaha River Valley, as
well as expand the regional ecological connectivity function of the ECFG.

Mortgages and Liens/Encumbrances

All mortgages and liens will be satisfied at the time of closing. On June 22, 1999, the Board of
Trustees approved a staff recommendation to delegate to the Department of Environmental
Protection (DEP) the authority to review and evaluate marketability issues as they arise on all
chapter 259, F.S., acquisitions and to resolve them appropriately. Therefore, DEP will review,
evaluate, and implement an appropriate resolution for these and any other title issues that arise
prior to closing.

Closing Information
A title insurance commitment, a survey, and an environmental site assessment will be provided by
the buyer prior to closing.

Management
The subject parcel will be managed by DEP’s Division of Recreation and Parks as part of Marjorie
Harris Carr Cross Florida Greenway State Recreation and Conservation Area.
