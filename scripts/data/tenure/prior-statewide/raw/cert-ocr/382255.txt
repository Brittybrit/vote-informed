Item 5 State Fire Marshal Lease and Sublease Agreements/ Determination
REQUEST: Consideration of (1) a determination that, pursuant to section 18-2.018(3)(a)1.,
F.A.C., extending the lease term for Board of Trustees Lease No. 3920 is in the public interest;
and (2) a request to extend the current term for Board of Trustees Lease No. 3920 and a proposed
fire station sublease within to expire in 2070, with mutual options to extend for two additional 25-
year terms.

VOTING REQUIREMENT FOR APPROVAL: Three votes
COUNTY: Marion

APPLICANT: State Fire Marshal
Florida Department of Financial Services

LOCATION: Section 37, Township 13 South, Range 21 East

STAFF REMARKS:

Background

The Board of Trustees granted Lease No. 3920 to the Department of Financial Services, formerly
the Department of Insurance, on August 6, 1991, for the establishment and operation of the Florida
State Fire College, along with related uses. The lease was amended in 2006 and 2007 adding land,
now totaling 87.75 acres; of which, approximately fifty percent remains undeveloped. The current
lease has twenty-one years remaining on the term, which expires on August 6, 2041.

Project Detail
The Marion County Board of County Commissioners (County) is planning to construct a new
county fire station to replace an existing station that is near the Florida State Fire College. The

County and the Applicant have discussed possibly relocating the proposed fire station to an
approximately five-acre parcel_on the Florida State Fire College campus, fronting N.W.
Gainesville Road. The proposed fire station will be a facility larger than the current fire station to
allow for the housing of Florida Task Force 8 equipment, which includes a boat and trailer as well
as a box-style truck to be used by the state-recognized Urban Search and Rescue Team located in
North Central Florida. The new facility is also being planned to house an advanced life support
transport unit to support the existing paramedic fire engine. Additionally, the County has stated a
willingness to include additional classroom space within the new station to be utilized by the
Florida State Fire College in support of training programs.

The County’s proposed activities qualify for a sublease on the site; however, the remaining term
of the lease limits the sublease to twenty-one years while the investment required to construct and
equip a new, modern fire station is considerable. The Applicant is requesting to extend the term
of Board of Trustees’ Lease No. 3920, to expire in 2070, along with a proposed fire station sublease
within with mutual options to renew or extend for two additional 25-year terms. If approved, the
approximately five-acre parcel can be subleased to the County under the same terms as the master
lease with a corresponding expiration date.

Public Interest

Pursuant to section 18-2.018(3)(a)1., F.A.C., the extended lease term is required to be in the public
interest. Staff is recommending the Board of Trustees make this determination and approve the
request to extend the lease term because of the following:

e The proposed activity would improve public safety activities for county citizens.
e A fire station would benefit the Florida State Fire College by providing classroom space,
fire suppression, and paramedic level services 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Comprehensive Plan
A local government comprehensive plan has been adopted for this area pursuant to section
163.3167, F.S. The proposed action is consistent with the adopted plan.
