STAFF REMARKS: This acquisition was negotiated by FDACS/FFS under its RFLPP. If
approved, this will be the fifty-first perpetual conservation easement proposed for acquisition, with
a total of approximately 55,801 acres preserved under the RFLPP. The S.Y. Hartt project is ranked
in Tier One on the 2018 RFLPP Acquisition List.

Property Description

The Hartt family arrived in Florida in 1919 and purchased this northern Highlands County property
in 1939. For generations, the Hartt family has been involved with ranching and various forms of
agriculture. The S.Y. Hartt Ranch is an 8,95l-acre working cow/calf operation in Highlands
County, Florida, with approximately 2,500 head of cattle. The current easement will encompass
approximately 748 acres.

If approved, 7,370 acres of the ranch will be protected through conservation easements, including
5,311 acres protected under RFLPP easements and 2,059 acres protected under a United States
Department of Agriculture, Natural Resources Conservation Service, Wetlands Reserve Program
(WRP) easement. Approximately 1,580 acres will remain in the S.Y. Hartt Tier One project.

S.Y. Hartt Ranch drains to Arbuckle Creek, which eventually flows into Lake Okeechobee. The
property is located within the Northern Everglades Watershed and also within the Kissimmee
River Basin recharge area. The property contains freshwater marshes, oak hammocks, swamps,
wet and dry prairies, cypress domes, improved pasture, and citrus.

The property is home to a myriad of native plants and animals, some of which are listed as
threatened and endangered species, and include: wild turkey, deer, hogs, indigo snakes, alligator,
quail, coyotes, bald eagles, grasshopper sparrows, scrub jays, gopher tortoises, and black bear.

The ranch borders the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission’s Lake Wales Ridge
Wildlife and Environmental Area and the Lake Wales Ridge National Wildlife Refuge. It is also
adjacent to RFLPP’s Rafter T Ranch conservation easement and lies less than one mile south of
the Avon Park Air Force Range. In addition, the property lies near multiple Florida Forever
projects, including Eastern Scarp Ranchlands, Lake Wales Ridge Ecosystem, and Arbuckle Creek
Watershed.
