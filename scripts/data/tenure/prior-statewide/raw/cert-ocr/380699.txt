Item 1 Palm Beach County Partial Release of Restriction and Reverter

REQUEST: Consideration of a request from the Boys and Girls Clubs of Palm Beach County,
Inc., for a Partial Release of Restriction and Reverter from 1.56 acres, more or less, within Board
of Trustees’ Deed No. 18599.

VOTING REQUIREMENT FOR APPROVAL: Three votes

COUNTY: Palm Beach

APPLICANT: Boys and Girls Clubs of Palm Beach County, Inc.

LOCATION: Section 31, Township 43 South, Range 37 East

CONSIDERATION: $9,500 to be deposited into the Internal Improvement Trust Fund

STAFF REMARKS:

Background

On January 21, 1941, the Board of Trustees conveyed approximately 19.77 acres (“Property”),
located in Belle Glade, to the Board of Public Instruction of Palm Beach County, Florida, under
Board of Trustees’ Deed No. 18599. The deed contained a restriction and reverter clause requiring
the Property to be maintained and used solely for public school purposes or it would revert to the
Board of Trustees. In addition, no mortgages or liens could be placed on the Property. The
Property has subsequently been subdivided and conveyed to other parties with the subject 1.56
acres, more or less, now owned by the Boys and Girls Clubs of Palm Beach County, Inc. (Club).

Portions of the deed restriction and reverter language in Board of Trustees’ Deed No. 18599 have
been modified by Board actions on May 21, 2002, and again on March 17, 2005, to allow for
public recreation, public health, public education, other community purposes including the

development and operation of an assisted living/senior housing facility; however, the mortgage
and lien prohibition remain on the 1.56 acre parcel.

Current Request ;

The Club proposes to construct a new 13,000 square foot Boys & Girls Clubs teen center on the
property adjacent to an existing gymnasium. The new facility will accommodate primarily
technology-based programming and will function as a career incubator, offering exposure to the
most promising careers and a first-rate workforce readiness program serving the surrounding
population.

Title research conducted in association with the financing of the new teen center revealed the
mortgage and lien restriction included in the 1941 conveyance is still in effect. The Club is
requesting that the restrictive language prohibiting mortgages or liens on the parcel be removed to
allow the Club to obtain financing and begin construction on the teen center. The Club will pay
the current appraised value of the partial release of the restriction and reverter.

Staff is recommending the Board of Trustees approve the request for the partial release of
restriction and reverter language related to mortgages and liens encumbering the 1.56-acre parcel
to enable the Club to construct the teen center for the benefit of the Belle Glade community.

This partial release of restrictions in no way waives any regulatory requirements including but not
limited to those of Department of Environmental Protection, SFWMD, and local government.

Comprehensive Plan

A local government comprehensive plan has been adopted for this area pursuant to section
163.3167, F.S. The proposed action is consistent with the adopted plan.
