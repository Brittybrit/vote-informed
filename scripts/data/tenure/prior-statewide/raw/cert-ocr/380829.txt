STAFF REMARKS

This acquisition was negotiated by FDACS/FFS under its RFLPP. If approved, this will be the
forty-eighth perpetual conservation easement proposed for acquisition, with a total of
approximately 57,429 acres preserved under the RFLPP. The Hall’s Tiger Bay Ranch project is
ranked in Tier One on the 2018 RFLPP Acquisition List.

Property Description

Three generations of the Hall family have owned land and participated in the Florida agriculture
industry. In the late 1930’s, Mr. Don Hall’s grandfather purchased approximately 20,000 acres in
Desoto County to establish a cattle ranch and quail hunting habitat. In the following decades, the
Desoto County land was divided between family members. Mr. Don Hall received 5,927 acres of
this land, now known as Hall’s Tiger Bay Ranch.

Hall’s Tiger Bay Ranch is a cow/calf operation, with hay and a 10-acre organic farm as secondary
operations, The property contains three separate cattle herds totaling approximately 500 head of
crossbred beef cattle. Mr. Hall has progressively implemented Best Management Practices to
improve production and land management and the family has worked closely with FDACS and
the local livestock agent. Mr. Hall has installed solar watering stations to keep cattle away from
wetlands areas. Mr. Hall plans to install water control structures to restore more natural hydrology
of the project’s wetlands and reduce erosion along creek beds.

Much of the ecological value of the property lies in the relatively large size of the natural
communities present and their contiguity with those on adjacent public lands. Of particular
significance is the dry prairie, mesic flatwoods, wet prairie, and numerous depression marshes and
hammocks.
