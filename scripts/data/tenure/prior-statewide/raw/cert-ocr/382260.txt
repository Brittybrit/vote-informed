Project Description

The Peace River Refuge project buffers and preserves approximately seven miles of river frontage
while protecting the water quality and habitat of the Peace River and its creeks. It also improves
water quality downstream, which flows into the Charlotte Harbor Estuary. This project protects
aquatic and upland wildlife associated with the floodplains, rivers, creeks and the Estuary.

Property Description

The subject property is owned by Peace River Preserve, LLC, which owns 850 total acres
bordering the Peace River; however, the conservation easement area consists of 761 acres and will
protect approximately 1.5 miles of meandering river frontage. Located within the owners 89-acre
portion, not under the conservation easement, is an existing dirt road that the owner will grant an
ingress/egress easement, to the Board of Trustees of the Internal Improvement Trust Fund at
closing, to provide access to the conservation easement area.

The owner maintains approximately 80 head of cattle on their property, with most of the herd
occupying the fee ownership property not included in the conservation easement. The owner also
maintains existing food plots for game and the ability to hunt on the subject property.

Prohibited Uses

Under the proposed conservation easement, the subject property will be restricted in perpetuity by
the provisions of the easement, a summary of which includes, but is not limited to, the following
prohibited uses:

e Dumping of trash, waste, hazardous materials, and soil will be prohibited;

e Exploration by the grantor for and extraction of oil, gas, minerals, peat, muck, limestone,
etc., by means of surface exploratory and extractors operations will be prohibited, except
as reasonably necessary to combat erosion or flooding, or except as necessary and
lawfully permitted for the conduct of permitted activities;

e Activities detrimental to drainage, flood control, water conservation, erosion control, soil
conservation, or fish and wildlife preservation will be prohibited, unless needed for
maintenance as provided in the easement;

e Acts or uses detrimental to the preservation of any historical, or archaeological area, will
be prohibited;

e The removal, destruction, cutting, trimming, mowing, alteration, or spraying with

biocides of trees, shrubs, or other natural vegetation will be prohibited, unless needed for

conservation purposes, as provided in the easement under Article V;

e There shall be no planting of nuisance exotic or non-native plants;
