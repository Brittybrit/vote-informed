STAFF REMARKS: This acquisition was negotiated by FDACS/FFS under its RFLPP. If
approved, this project will be the fifty-seventh perpetual conservation easement proposed for
acquisition, with a total of approximately 66,421 acres preserved under the RFLPP. The Hendrie
Ranch project is ranked in Tier One on the RFLPP Acquisition List. The Nature Conservancy has
committed $100,000 in funding for establishment of a non-wasting endowment to provide for the
long-term management of the acquired property (e.g., prescribed fire and invasive exotic
vegetation control).

Property Description

Hendrie Ranch is located on U.S. Highway 27 in Highlands County approximately 11 miles south
of the City of Lake Placid and near the Town of Venus. The Hendrie Family has managed the
property for timber and beef for more than 65 years and contributes to the conservation of the
greater Fisheating Creek conservation landscape.

Acquisition of this conservation easement will be funded, in part, through a USFWS RLA grant to
the FWC. The RLA grant’s purpose is to protect the habitats of the Florida panther and listed bird
species, including the Florida Scrub-Jay and Crested Caracara.

The project site is adjacent to Archbold Biological Station, two United States Department of
Agriculture Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) Wetlands Reserve Program
easements, the Department of Environmental Protection’s (DEP) Fisheating Creek/Smoak Groves
Conservation Easement, and the FWC’s Fishing Creek/Lykes Brothers Conservation Easement.
The project is within the Lake Wales Ridge Florida Forever Project and is located within the Lake
Okeechobee Basin Management Action Plan boundary.

Prohibited Uses

e Dumping of non-biodegradable, toxic or hazardous substances, trash garbage, wastes,
abandoned vehicles, appliances, machinery or similar material is prohibited;

e The exploration for and extraction of oil, gas, minerals, dolostone, peat, muck, marl,
limestone, limerock, kaolin, fuller’s earth, phosphate, common clays, gravel, shell, sand
and similar substances either directly or indirectly by Grantor or on Grantor’s behalf, etc.
Limited mining is allowed to combat erosion or flooding, or for limited on-farm
agricultural uses;

e Activities that affect the hydrology of the land or that detrimentally affect water
conservation, erosion control, soil conservation, or fish and wildlife habitat, etc.;
