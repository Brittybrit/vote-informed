Project Description

Seven Runs Creek Final Phase covers 7,398 acres in Walton and Washington Counties. The
parcels border the Choctawhatchee River and Pine Log Creek for about 1.4 miles and .3 miles,
respectively. It is a series of disjunct parcels that fill in gaps in the existing 53,505-acre Nokuse
Plantation conservation area, all of them contiguous to conservation easements or existing state-
owned lands.

Property Description

The subject property is owned by Stella Davis 2015 Plantation Trust, established by Ms. Davis
after her husband, M.C. Davis, passed in 2015. Through the preservation of a forever longleaf
pine forest, and by providing habitat for ecological research and species relocation, the
landowner’s goal is to teach younger generations the value of conservation and land stewardship
in a fun, educational manner with hands-on opportunities to learn.

The proposed conservation easement will protect groundwater resources, significant Strategic
Habitat Conservation areas for rare species, and provide ecological restoration of lands that were
changed by tree farming and other activities. It will also offer an important expansion to the
existing connection between Eglin Air Force Base and the Choctawhatchee Wildlife Management
Area. Restoration of sandhill habitats within the subject property will protect and benefit several
listed species.

The conservation easement will permanently limit development while allowing the landowner to
harvest timber. It is the intent of the owner that all silviculture be conducted solely for the purpose
of regaining a natural forest of longleaf pine over the entire property and to maintain and protect
the same in perpetuity.
