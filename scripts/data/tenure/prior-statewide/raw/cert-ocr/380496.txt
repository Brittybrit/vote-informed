Project Description

Seven Runs Creek Final Phase covers 6,769 acres in Walton and Washington County. It is a series
of disjunct parcels that fill in gaps in the existing 53,505-acre Nokuse Plantation conservation area,
all of them contiguous to conservation easements or existing state lands.

Property Information

The proposed conservation easement on the subject property will protect groundwater resources,
significant Strategic Habitat Conservation areas for rare species and provide ecological restoration
of these conservation lands. Additionally, the conservation easement will provide an important
addition to the existing connection between Eglin Air Force Base and the Choctawhatchee Wildlife
Management Area. Restoration of historic and current sandhill habitats within the subject property
will protect and benefit several listed species.

The conservation easement will permanently limit development, while allowing the landowner to
harvest timber. It is the intent of the owner that all silviculture be conducted solely for the purpose
of regaining a natural forest over the entire subject property and to maintain and protect the same
in perpetuity.

The subject property is owned by Nokuse Education, Inc., a not-for-profit corporation with a vision
to create a long-leaf pine forest “as far as the eye could see” and teach younger generations the
importance of conservation, land stewardship and protection of natural resources.

Prohibited Uses

Under the proposed conservation easement, the subject property will be restricted in perpetuity by
the provisions of the easement, a summary of which includes, but is not limited to, the following
prohibited uses:

e Dumping of trash, waste, hazardous materials and soil will be prohibited;

e Exploration by the grantor for and extraction of oil, gas, minerals, peat, muck, limestone, etc.,
by means of surface exploratory and extractors operations will be prohibited, except as
reasonably necessary to combat erosion or flooding or except as necessary and lawfully
permitted for the conduct of permitted activities;

e Activities detrimental to drainage, flood control, water conservation, erosion control, soil
conservation or fish and wildlife preservation will be prohibited, unless needed for
maintenance as provided im the easement under Article V. A., E., and H.;
