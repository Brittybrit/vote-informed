Item 7 Florida Department of Children and Families/ City of Pembroke Pines/ Lease,
Sublease and Sub-sublease Agreements/ Determination

REQUEST: Consideration of (1) a determination that, pursuant to section 18-2.018(3)(a)1.,
F.A.C., extending the term for Board of Trustees Lease No. 2628, and any subleases and sub-
subleases within, are in the public interest; and (2) a request to extend Board of Trustees Lease
No. 2628, and any subleases and sub-subleases within, to January 3, 2090.

VOTING REQUIREMENT FOR APPROVAL: Three votes
COUNTY: Broward

APPLICANT: Florida Department of Children and Families (DCF)
LOCATION: Sections 16 and 21, Township 51 South, Range 41 East

STAFF REMARKS:
Background
Florida Department of Children and Families (DCF) currently holds Board of Trustees Lease No.
2628 for public purposes. The 99-year lease expires January 3, 2072. A vast majority of the lease
area is subleased to the City of Pembroke Pines (City), under Sublease No. 2628-14. The City
sublease, which expires on June 30, 2051, was provided to establish and operate a Health-Care
Park (Park) on the site. The City, in turn, sub-subleases portions of the Park to a diverse group of
providers, offering services such as:

e The Village South, a residential treatment center for women participating in drug

rehabilitation where their child(ren) can reside with the parent;
e Citrus Mental Health, which operates mental health facilities for adults and juveniles;

e Ocean Breeze and Second Hand Detox Centers, residential drug detoxification facilities
and services;

e Brown’s Harbor, a residential transitional and independent living facility for young adults

e R&R Village, a City sponsored and managed program to provide dedicated housing for
veterans and their families; and,

e Youth Opportunity Investments, a secure detention facility for juvenile offenders.

Project Detail

DCF is requesting an extension of their current lease to expire on January 3, 2090. The eighteen-
year extension will assist DCF, its sublessees, and sub-sublessees to secure funding for
construction, maintenance, and improvement of program facilities. Historically, public grant
programs, as well as private and other financing programs, consider facilities with long term leases
and subleases more secure and similar to fee simple ownership. The extended lease term will
enable DCF, its sublessees, and sub-sublessees to identify and secure financing to further enhance
a wide array of services at the Park.

Public Interest

Pursuant to section 18-2.018(3)(a)1., F.A.C., the extended lease term is required to be in the public
interest. Staff is recommending the Board of Trustees make the determination that the extended
lease term is in the public interest, as it would assist DCF, its sublessees, and sub-sublessees in
providing health care services within the Park.

Comprehensive Plan
A local government comprehensive plan has been adopted for this area pursuant to section

163.3167, F.S. DEP has determined that the proposed action is not subject to the local planning
process.
