Kissimmee Prairie Preserve State Park and the Ordway-Whittell Kissimmee Prairie Sanctuary to
the west. This area is important for the Florida grasshopper sparrow, sandhill crane, mottled duck,
wood stork, crested caracara, and other imperiled wildlife species, as most the project is within a

The project lies within a wildlife corridor in the Florida Ecological Greenways Network.

Property Description

The 3,634-acre subject property consists of a globally imperiled upland habitat rich in biodiversity.
The proposed conservation easement is located in the Northern Everglades Headwaters National
Wildlife Refuge Conservation Partnership Area, in west Okeechobee County, and borders the
north boundary of Kissimmee Prairie Preserve State Park. A conservation easement over the
subject property will allow for the protection of thousands of acres of additional high-quality
habitat in an area of Florida known for its rare wildlife, imperiled habitats, and significant
hydrological resources. The proposed conservation easement will provide a buffer to the
Kissimmee Prairie Preserve State Park, which is the state’s first Dark Sky Park recognized by the
International Dark Sky Association.

The subject property contains approximately 2,100 acres of imperiled dry prairie ecosystem.
Protecting property in this area is extremely important for the survival of numerous listed species,
notably the Florida Grasshopper Sparrow. This proposed conservation easement will improve
connectivity for both wildlife and watershed, as well as provide base buffering to nearby Avon
Park Air Force Range.

Prohibited Uses

Under the proposed conservation easement, the subject property will be restricted in perpetuity by
the provisions of the easement, a summary of which includes, but is not limited to, the following
prohibited uses:

Dumping of trash, waste, hazardous materials, and soil will be prohibited;

Exploration and extraction by grantor for oil, gas, minerals, peat, muck, limestone, etc., by
means of surface exploratory and extractors operations, except as reasonably necessary to
combat erosion or flooding, or except as necessary and lawfully permitted for the conduct
of permitted activities;

e Activities detrimental to drainage, flood control, water conservation, erosion control, soil
conservation, or fish and wildlife preservation will be prohibited, unless needed for
maintenance as provided in the easement;

be prohibited;
