       a)      A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF OPA-
LOCKA,       FLORIDA,     SELECTING         A NEW CITY ATTORNEY AND                DIRECTING
STEPHEN HUNTER-JOHNSON,                  ESQ. TO NEGOTIATE FOR AND BRING BACK

THE    CITY COMMISSION; PROVIDING FOR INCORPORATION OF RECITALS;
PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE. Sponsored by City Commission

The above resolution was read by Special Counsel Hunter- Johnson.

A motion was made to nominate Greenspoon Marder LLP for the position of city attorney.

It was moved by Commissioner Kelley, seconded by Commissioner Burke for discussion.

Commissioner Kelley said he knows Greenspoon Marder LLP as they have worked for the City
before. He likes the fact that the firm is made up of a pool of attorneys with varying areas of
expertise.




Mayor Pigatt stated for the record that the Barrister Firm had been a registered agent for a
business that he incorporated and after he learned he applied he was removed from the business.
He said that when he ran for office he ran on change and at this time he is not in favor of Mr.
Geller as he has been a part of what transpired in the past.


Commissioner Burke said he understands what the mayor is saying, but in all fairness because
someone has been here before, that should not mean they are not capable of doing the work and
that goes for Mr. Geller and Ms. Norris- Weeks. There is an overflow of outside litigation and the
firms here have the experience to handle them. They should not be banned from selection
because they have been with the City before.

Mayor Pigatt said if it based on experience alone, then none of them would be sitting here today,
but it takes more than that.


There being no further discussion, the motion to select Greenspoon Marder LLP as the new city
attorney failed by a 4- 1 vote.

                        Commissioner Burke            No
                        Vice Mayor Davis              No
                        Commissioner Kelley           Yes
                        Commissioner Bass             No
                        Mayor Pigatt                  No


A motion was made to nominate Bernadette Norris-Weeks, P. A. for the position of city attorney.

It was moved by Commissioner Burke, seconded by Vice Mayor Davis for discussion.

Vice Mayor Davis said Ms. Norris- Weeks presentation was comprehensive and she was also the
lowest bidder and therefore he is in support of selecting her firm.

Commissioner Kelley said he was in favor of both Mr. Geller and Ms. Norris-Weeks as he has
worked with both of them in the past. He also appreciates that she mentioned she has no problem
