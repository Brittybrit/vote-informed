5.         RESOLUTIONS/ PUBLIC HEARINGS:


     a)   A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF OPA-
 LOCKA, FLORIDA, REAFFIRMING THE ADOPTION OF THE 2018- 2019 FISCAL
 YEAR      MILLAGE          RATE;   PROVIDING          FOR INCORPORATION OF RECITALS;
 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE. Sponsored by City Manager

 The above resolution was read by Attorney Brown.

 Mayor Pigatt opened the floor for a public hearing.

 Dorothy Johnson, 13724 NW 22nd Place, Opa-locka, FL, asked if there was no change in the
 millage rate; she asked that her question be answered at this time.


 There being no further persons to speak on the public hearing, the public hearing was closed.

 It was moved by Vice Mayor Davis, seconded by Commissioner Burke for discussion.

 Special Commission Meeting Minutes—4/ 10/ 2019                                                             1
Commissioner Burke asked why this is being done.

Acting City Manager Daughtrey said because the City made a mistake on the advertisement. In
order to receive the millage that was advertised, the City has to acknowledge the error and make
clear that there will not be an increase over what was approved. If the City does not approve this
millage rate, they will have to go back to the rollback rate and there will be nine hundred ninety
seven thousand nine hundred and eleven dollars in funds that they will not be able to spend.

Mayor Pigatt asked the manager how did this happen.


Acting City Manager Daughtrey said the numbers in the advertising were transposed, but the
total did not change. The state told them they needed to make the change because of this.

Mayor Pigatt said moving forward, something like this should not happen again.

There being no further discussion, the motion on the resolution passed by a 5- 0 vote.
