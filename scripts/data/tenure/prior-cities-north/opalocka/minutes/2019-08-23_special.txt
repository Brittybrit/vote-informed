CALL TO ORDER:

Mayor Matthew Pigatt called the meeting to order at 4:15 p.m. on Wednesday, August 23, 2019
in the Sherbondy Village Auditorium, 215 Perviz Avenue, Opa-locka, Florida.

1. ROLL CALL:

The following members of the City Commission were present: Mayor Matthew Pigatt, Vice
Mayor Chris Davis, Commissioner Joseph L. Kelley, Commissioner Alvin Burke, and
Commissioner Sherelean Bass. Also in attendance were: Assistant City Manager George Ellis,
Assistant City Attorney Michelle Pamies, and City Clerk Joanna Flores.

2. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

The Pledge of Allegiance was recited in unison.

3. INVOCATION:

The invocation was delivered by Commissioner Joseph L. Kelley.

4, PUBLIC INPUT:
Agenda Items Only

Dorothy Johnson, 13724 NW 22"! Place, Opa-locka, FL, said she was pleased with the process
for the selection of the city manager. She said she is glad the new manager will have residence
within the City limits so they can be aware of what is going on as opposed to living elsewhere.
She said she hopes the commission will consider the fact that the candidates traveled from afar
and have to relocate their families and will not use previous practices to get rid of the manager if
one or two commissioners are upset with him or her.

5. CITY COMMISSION DISCUSSION ON CITY MANAGER FINALISTS:

Colin Baenziger, Baenziger & Associates, recommended that the commission select the manager
by using the ballot process that was previously used at the last finalist meeting.

Mayor Pigatt asked if there were any objections to the process.

Commissioner Kelley said he is not a fan of the ballot process and wanted it to be done by the
resolution and the vote.

Mayor Pigatt asked the attorney if there was anything wrong with the ballot process.
Assistant Attorney Pamies said either process can be used at the discretion of the commission.

Vice Mayor Davis said he does not see the need to discuss each candidate at this point because it
is already narrowed down. They just need to take a vote.

Commissioner Bass said she is uncomfortable voting for a manager at this time. She has stated
before that the process should not be complete in one day, but she is for narrowing the selection

down to two.

Mr. Baenziger proceeded with the ballot process and each commissioner made their selection of
one or two candidates and checked their name at the bottom.

The selections were captured as follows:

Commissioner Bass: John Pate and Lawrence McNaul
Commissioner Burke: John Pate

Commissioner Kelley: John Pate and Lawrence McNaul
Vice Mayor Davis: John Pate and William Shanahan
Mayor Pigatt: John Pate

Commissioner Kelley stated for the record that he does not agree with this process, but since the
assistant attorney said it was legal and proper, he will go along with it.

Mayor Pigatt said Robert’s Rules of Order states that voting can be done several ways including
this one.

The assistant attorney was asked to read the resolution into the record.
6. RESOLUTION:
