1. CALL TO ORDER:

Mayor Myra L. Taylor called the meeting to order at 10:05 a.m. on Thursday, June 28, 2012 on
the 2™ Floor of the Opa-locka Municipal Complex, 780 Fisherman Street, Opa-Locka, Florida.

2. ROLL CALL:

The following members of the City of Opa-locka City Commission were present: Vice Mayor
Dorothy Johnson, Commissioner Timothy Holmes, Commissioner Gail Miller, and Mayor Myra
L. Taylor. Also in attendance were: City Attorney Joseph S. Geller and Interim City Clerk
Joanna Flores. Commissioner Rose Tydus arrived at 10:09 a.m.

3. INVOCATION:

The Invocation was delivered by Mayor Myra L. Taylor.
4, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

The Pledge of Allegiance was recited in unison.

was unfair.

Commissioner Holmes said he would like to make a recommendation for Mr. Baker to start his
