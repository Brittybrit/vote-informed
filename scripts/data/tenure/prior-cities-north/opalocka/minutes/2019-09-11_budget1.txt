b) AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF OPA-LOCKA,
FLORIDA, APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE CITY’S GENERAL FUND BUDGET FOR
THE FISCAL YEAR 2019-2020, COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2019 THROUGH SEPTEMBER
30, 2020; PROVIDING FOR THE EXPENDITURE OF FUNDS ESTABLISHED BY THE
BUDGET; AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO TAKE CERTAIN ACTIONS;
PROVIDING FOR APPROPRIATION OF ALL BUDGET EXPENDITURES; PROVIDING FOR
FEES CONSISTENT WITH APPROPRIATION AND AMENDMENT; PROVIDING FOR
INCORPORATION OF RECITALS; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICT AND REPEALER;
PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE. Sponsored by
City Manager

The above ordinance was read by Attorney Norris-Weeks.

Mayor Pigatt opened the floor for a public hearing. There being no persons to speak on the public hearing,
the public hearing was closed.

It was moved by Commissioner Burke, seconded by Vice Mayor Davis for discussion.

Commissioner Kelley asked the manager to clarify the projections in the budget that the state did not
allow and moved in to the reserves. He asked if the funds will be available in the future.

Acting City Manager Daughtrey said the state requires the City to give them specific documentation for
each request that is made. The City did not comply and asked the state to provide the specifics what they
are requesting. Once the information is provided, the City will come into compliance.

Vice Mayor Davis said Section 8 refers to a Finance Director and the city currently does not have a
Finance Director. He asked the manager if this was an error and needs to be amended or is there a
designee.

Acting City Manager Daughtrey said he is the designee.

Mayor Pigatt asked the manager what steps are being taken to ensure the stability of the City’s finances.
Acting City Manager Daughtrey said a written response to all of the questions raised by the state was
provided to each of the commissioners. Any changes being made needs to be done prior to second reading

on September 25". The response was also sent to the state for their approval.

Mayor Pigatt asked the manager how much of the funds were put in reserves as a result of a lack of
documentation.

First Budget Hearing Minutes — 9/11/2019 2
Acting City Manager Daughtrey said that the state did not cut anything out of the budget. They just said
they did not feel comfortable with the way the funds were being spent.

Mayor Pigatt said the City needs to account for how it is spending the funding and this needs to be done
by providing documentation to justify the spending.

There being no further discussion, the motion on the ordinance passed by a 5-0 vote.

Vice Mayor Davis Yes
Commissioner Burke Yes
Commissioner Bass Yes

The above ordinance was read by Attorney Norris-Weeks.
Mayor Pigatt opened the floor for a public hearing.

Virginia Cook, 3114 NW 204% Lane, Miami Gardens, FL, said she was told to come with her complaint
about the drainage problem so that it can be added to the budget for the upcoming fiscal year. She said
she has been before the commission several times and also spoke with Mr. Austin and met with
Commissioner Kelley regarding this. She knows it is costly, but hopes it will be included in the budget
because when it rains, it is terrible in front of her church and no one can go into the building.

Henrietta Harrison, 167 NW 45" Street, Miami, FL, said she agrees with the previous person and it is
becoming harder and harder to get into the church; it is deterring people from coming to church. Also she
is concerned with the health issues this can cause. She is a retired nurse and believes it will cause
respiratory issues for those that live in the community.

There being no further persons to speak on the public hearing, the public hearing was closed.

It was moved by Commissioner Kelley, seconded by Commissioner Burke for discussion.

Commissioner Kelley asked the manager how the City can minimize the water in the meantime while
looking for funding sources to correct the problem.

Acting City Manager Daughtrey asked the Public Works Director to come before the commission and
explain what the City intends to do about the ongoing issue.

Public Works Director Airia Austin came before the commission and stated that right now there is a

standing schedule of areas that are addressed after each rain fall. The whole situation cannot be addressed
because there is no money in the budget. The recent legislation that was passed declared a flooding
