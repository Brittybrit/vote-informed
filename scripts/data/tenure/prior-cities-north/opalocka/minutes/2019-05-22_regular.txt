1. CALL TO ORDER:

Mayor Matthew Pigatt called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. on Wednesday, May 22, 2019 at
the Sherbondy Village Auditorium, 215 Perviz Avenue, Opa-locka, Florida.

2. ROLL CALL:

The following members of the City Commission were present: Mayor Matthew Pigatt, Vice
Mayor Chris Davis, Commissioner Joseph L. Kelley, and Commissioner Sherelean Bass. Also in
attendance were: Acting City Manager Newall Daughtrey, City Attorney Vincent Brown, and
City Clerk Joanna Flores. Commissioner Alvin Burke was not in attendance.

17. CITY MANAGER’S REPORT:
Acting City Manager Daughtrey said his focus is dealing with the audit for 2017 and the CITT
audits. The second focus is the .unbilled months for utility billing and transferring the system to
Miami-Dade County. Cameras have been installed in utility billing to address the ongoing
complaints from residents regarding customer service. This is the first year of the Financial
Recovery Plan and they are working to handle the funds by the book so they are in compliance
with doing what is necessary to come out of oversight. At the end of the month, they expect to
receive the pothole patcher which will aide in maintaining the roads and streets.
Commissioner Kelley asked the manager if he is waiting for direction from the commission
regarding the report he presented to them. He said State Representative Bush went to Tallahassee

and secured funding for the City and they spoke highly of the manager. He asked if the Planning

& Zoning Board meetings are canceled, does the meeting need to be advertised or is it simply
deferred.

Acting City Manager Daughtrey said it has to be advertised at a cost of two thousand dollars
($2,000.00).

Commissioner Kelley suggested that even if there are only two people present, then can the
meeting be called and then be recessed or deferred to save on the advertising costs since you
cannot control if the members come or not.

City Attorney Brown said they will look into the matter.

Mayor Pigatt asked the clerk how often does the board meet.

City Clerk Flores said the first Tuesday of every month.

Mayor Pigatt asked who can speak on the board.

Planning & Community Development Director Gregory Gay said his staff does the scheduling
for the meetings and polls the board two weeks in advance of the meeting. They do not find out

they are not coming until minutes before the meeting.

Mayor Pigatt asked what the process was for removing someone from the board.

Acting City Manager Daughtrey said, “Yes.”

19. MAYOR/COMMISSION REPORT:

Commissioner Bass said Bingo will be held on May 31, 2019 at 6:00 p.m. and she looks forward
to seeing everyone.

Commissioner Kelley said State Representative James Bush came to the last Meet Me Monday
and spoke with residents; it was very informative. He gave Kudos to the Parks and Recreation
Department for hosting the Seniors on the Move Mother’s Day Luncheon as the seniors
appreciated it. He attended the first meeting for the 2019 Super Bowl Committee and they are
willing to come and assist with the cleanup efforts for one of the parks. The Ingram Park cleanup
project is still taking place and volunteers are needed. He said he would like to do a park summit
and get ideas from the community on what they would like to see on Ingram Park, including the
waterfront.

Mayor Pigatt asked the commission how they would feel about amending the agenda and moving
the reports before citizens forum to give residents the opportunity to receive the information in
case they may not be staying for the entire meeting; he said he will revisit it when Commissioner
Burke is present and asked the present members to give it some thought.

Acting City Manager Daughtrey said the five year recovery plan is basically what the City plans
to do to come out of the financial emergency within the next five years.
