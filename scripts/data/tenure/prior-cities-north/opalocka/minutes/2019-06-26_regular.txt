12. ACTION ITEMS:

2 (13-2). A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF OPA-
LOCKA, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO PURCHASE OFFICE
FURNITURE FROM AN APPROVED FURNITURE VENDOR OF THE FLORIDA
DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SERVICES IN AN AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED
SIX THOUSAND DOLLARS ($6,000.00); PROVIDING FOR INCORPORATION OF
RECITALS; PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE. Sponsored by City Manager

The above resolution was read by Attorney Norris-Weeks.
It was moved by Commissioner Kelley, seconded by Commissioner Burke for discussion.

Commissioner Kelley said the item says it is sponsored by the city manager, but it should be
sponsored by the commission. He also said that the City is under oversight and should not be
spending this kind of money at this time. The offices might need to be updated, but the money is
not there and there are other things the money can be spent on to benefit the City.

Mayor Pigatt said when he came in office; he was not afforded the things that previous officials
were able to obtain. The funds have been approved by the state and the commission offices and
conference room_should be presentable when handling City business and dealing with others. He
asked the manager for clarity.

Acting City Manager Daughtrey said the mayor worded it properly and at this time he will take
direction at their discretion.

Commissioner Kelley reiterated that the City is under a financial emergency and the commission
should lead by example and keep in mind that the budget process is underway and the directors
will be requesting things for their departments. Outfitting the offices is not important at this time.

A motion was made to amend the agenda to appoint someone to look through the vendor list and
find a company to order the furniture from.

Commissioner Burke said the City just hired a procurement person to handle purchasing.

17. CITY MANAGER’S REPORT:

Acting City Manager Daughtrey said they have been responding to the auditor general’s report
and have been working to resolve all of the issues. Specific policies and procedures will be put in
place to comply with all of the concerns in the future. This report is a draft, but an official report
will be provided of exactly what is needed for the City to come into compliance moving forward.
He has been pushing for staff to get this done because they will be returning to the City in the
future to observe if the new processes are being enforced.

18. OFFICIAL BOARD REPORTS:
19. MAYOR/COMMISSION REPORT:

Commissioner Kelley thanked all of the employees and departments for their well wishes on the
passing of his mother. He is taking everything day by day and he knows his mother would want
him to keep moving forward and that is what he is doing. The food distribution program was
held on last Friday as scheduled and was cohosted by Commissioner Burke; Movie Night is the
first Friday of every month and is held on the lawn of the historic city hall; and he is working on
hosting the special needs book bag giveaway that will be held in August. He received an e-mail
from the consultant who is working on the search for a city manager that the search had been
extended at the discretion of the consultant. He asked for clarity on who authorized the
consultant to arbitrarily make the decision of extending the search without direction from the
commission.

City Attorney Norris-Weeks said she did look at the contract and it was not specific or addressed
authority on who can make those kinds of decisions.
