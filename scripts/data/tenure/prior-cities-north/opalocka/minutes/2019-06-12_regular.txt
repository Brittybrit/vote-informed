17. CITY MANAGER’S REPORT:

Acting City Manager Daughtrey introduced the new City budget administrator, Ms. Dunleavy.
She has been working with all of the department heads with their individual budgets and it will
continue into next week. The summer youth employment program will begin on Monday. There
are eighty five slots and sixty six of them have been filled, they will work to register additional
youth so they can benefit from working over the summer. The CITT audit will be on the 18" and
once that is completed, it will bring additional funding into the City. Cairo Lane project is still
moving forward and is expected to be completed by September 30, 2019. The historic City Hall
project will also be moving forward so that the work that has already been completed is not
destroyed. They are working on obtaining the body cameras for the police officers and GPS for
the police vehicles. Cameras have been installed in the utility billing department so that they can
minimize the number of complaints they have been receiving from the residents regarding
customer service. Cameras have also been installed in places where there has been known
dumping so they can hold the offenders accountable for illegal dumping.

Commissioner Burke asked the manager to do whatever it takes to complete the historic City
Hall.

Attorney Norris-Weeks said she spoke with the attorney for the company that is doing the work
and they are trying to set up a meeting. After they meet, she will provide the commission with a
report at the next meeting.

Commissioner Burke asked about the Music in the Park event.

Parks and Recreation Director Charles Brown said they were waiting for the company who was
organizing the event to obtain the event insurance and once this is done they can move forward

with planning and implementing.

Commissioner Bass asked the manager how many of the youth who applied for the summer jobs
were residents.

Acting City Manager Daughtrey said they made sure all of the youth were residents.
