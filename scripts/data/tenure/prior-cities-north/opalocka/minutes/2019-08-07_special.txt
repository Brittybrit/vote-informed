Mayor Pigatt asked Mr. Baenziger to come before the commission and provide a status report on
the selection process for the city manager position.

Colin Baenziger, Baenziger & Associates, Daytona Beach Shores, FL, said the search started
about eight weeks ago through recruiting, advertising, and an e-mail list. He was not entirely
pleased with the selection at that time, so they went back out and accepted additional
applications for the position. A total of sixty seven (67) candidates from twenty (20) states
applied for the position and it was narrowed down to a dozen that were strong. There were
several applicants who have withdrawn for different reasons and left the final number of
applicants at eight (8) for the commission to consider. He presented a ballot process to the
commission and informed them that it is just a suggestion and they do not have to go along with
it.

The commission agreed to the process suggested by Mr. Baenziger and he presented them with
ballots to begin making their selections.

Mayor Pigatt thanked Mr. Baenziger for the work he has performed : as part of this selection
process; it has been very clear and transparent.

Acting City Manager Daughtrey said “No”.

Mayor Pigatt asked who negotiated the contract for the building.

Acting City Manager Daughtrey said Executive Assistant to the City Manager Sha’mecca
Lawson is currently managing the entire building and is the one who negotiated the contract. The
objective is to lease out the whole building.

Vice Mayor Davis asked for Ms. Lawson to explain how the rates were determined for leasing
the building.

Ms. Lawson came before the commission and explained that the rates were determined from the
current market rates which have gone down and since they were renting so much space, the City
worked with them on the rates per square footage.

parents.

Ms. Lawson confirmed this and also stated that there will be security provided for the school by
the school during class hours.

Commissioner Burke asked Ms. Lawson if she negotiated where the school would pay their
share of the property taxes.

Ms. Lawson said, “Yes”.

Mayor Pigatt asked what steps have taken to secure a property manager for Town Center One.
He is unsure of Ms. Lawson’s level of expertise with managing property and believes the City
needs an experienced property manager that has experience in negotiating and determining the

market. He suggested that an RFP be put out in search of a property manager.

City Attorney Norris-Weeks said she will look into what it on the books to determine if it needs
to go out for procurement, but she is sure it will need to be put out.

Acting City Manager Daughtrey said the work has already been done, but the oversight board
