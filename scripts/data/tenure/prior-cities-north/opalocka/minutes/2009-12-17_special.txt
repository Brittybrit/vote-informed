Mayor Kelley stated that there is a void in the position of City Attorney with the
resignation of Burnadette Norris-Weeks, P.A. The City Commission must fill that
position; therefore, he brought the recommendation to name an interim City Attorney. At

house City Attorney, whoever the Assistant City Attorney was at that time was supposed
to cease.

Commissioner Tydus expressed her concerns relating to a time period for the Interim
City Attorney and suggested that it be incorporated in the resolution.

Commissioner Tydus disagreed with the six (6) months because the process shouldn’t

incorporate the six months (6) time period in the resolution.

and she can go along with three (3) months, but she cannot support six (6) months.

request for permanent attorneys.

feels it wants to go out for a permanent, whether its three (3) months or six (6) months,
