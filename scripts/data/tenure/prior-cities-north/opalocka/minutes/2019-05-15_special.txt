 Special Commission   Meeting Minutes— 5/ 15/ 2019
Commissioner Kelley thanked the staff and everyone that made the partnership possible with
South Florida Workforce to bring the summer youth program back to the City.

Mayor Pigatt also thanked the agency for coming together with the City to make this initiative
happen. He asked the manager how the word will get out informing the public and local
businesses of what is going on.

Acting City Manager Daughtrey asked his Executive Assistant to the City Manager Sha- Mecca
Lawson to come before the commission and provide a response.


Ms. Lawson said once the legislation is approved, the City Manager' s office will provide flyers
to all city departments, the schools, supermarkets, and the media to inform the residents of the
summer youth program.



Mayor Pigatt encouraged his colleagues to do what is in their ability to inform the public of the
program.




Commissioner Bass said the churches should be included in the initiative as they provide weekly
announcements to the congregation.


Commissioner Burke agreed.


There being no further discussion, the motion on the resolution passed by a 5- 0 vote.
