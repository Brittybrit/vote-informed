 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has held all necessary
 14 public workshops and public hearings to consider and make changes to the annual budget
 15 submitted by the City Manager for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2010.
 16 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY
 17 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,AS FOLLOWS:
 18 Section 1. That the millage rate for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2009
 19 and ending September 30, 2010, to be levied in order to produce sufficient ad valorem
 20 taxation revenue which, when combined with other projected General Fund revenues,
 21 will be adequate to pay the appropriations made in the budget for the fiscal year
 22 beginning October 1, 2009 and ending September 30, 2010 shall be:
 23 Operating Millage Rate 4.0198
 24 Section 2. That the adopted operating millage rate of 4.0198 mills is 3.2%
 25 less than the rolled back rate of 4.1532, and is computed according to the guidelines set
 26 forth in the Florida Statutes, Section 200.065.
 27 Section 3. That this ordinance was adopted after a public hearing was held
 28 upon the subject matter hereof, as required by the City Charter and Florida Statutes
 29 200.065; notice of hearing was published as required by law, which public hearings were
