 2
 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 4 FLORIDA AMENDING THE AUTHORITY OF THE EDUCATION ADVISORY
 5 BOARD TO MAKE RECOMMENDATIONS RELATING TO SCHOOL
 6 BOUNDARIES; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICT; PROVIDING FOR
 7 SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 8
 9 WHEREAS, on February 1, 2006, the City Commission of the City of Parkland passed
 10 Ordinance 2005-30 to create an Education Advisory Board ("EAB") for the purpose of advising
 11 the City Commission on issues relating to educational matters which impact the quality of
 12 education for residents of the City of Parkland and the business community serving the City;
 13 and
 14 WHEREAS, it was the intention of the City Commission that such a committee would
 15 advise the City Commission on issues regarding all aspects of education from the preschool
 16 through and including the post-secondary educational levels; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, it was the desire of the City Commission to specifically exclude from the
 19 EAB's authority any recommendations regarding public school boundaries;
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the EAB met on June 1, 2006 and voted to request that the City
 22 Commission reconsider the prohibition of the EAB to discuss public school boundaries;
 23
 24 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 25 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA THAT:
 26
 27 SECTION 1. Ordinance No. 2005-30 Section 3 is hereby amended as follows:
 28
 29 Section 3. The board shall make recommendations to the City Commission with regard to
 30 matters relating to education in the City of Parkland, make recommendations to improve
 31 communications between schools and school populations, to recommend projects for
 32 improving school facilities, to discuss, advise and, if they desire to, make recommendations on
 33 public school boundaries and to advise the City Commission on health, safety, transportation
 34 and quality of life issues that affect students.
 35
 36 SECTION 2. It is the intention of the City Commission of the City of Parkland that
 37 the provisions of this Ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances
 38 of the City of Parkland, Florida, and that the Sections of this Ordinance may be renumbered,
 39 re-lettered and the word "Ordinance" may be changed to "Section", "Article" or such other word
 40 or phrase in order to accomplish such intention.
 41
 42 SECTION 3. If any clause, section, or other part or application of this Ordinance
 43 shall be held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or invalid, such
 44 unconstitutional or invalid part or application shall be considered as eliminated and so not
 45 affecting the validity of the remaining portions or applications remaining in full force and effect.
 46
 47 SECTION 4. All Ordinances or parts of Ordinances, Resolutions or parts of
 48 Resolutions in conflict herewith be and the same are hereby repealed to the extent of such
 49 conflict.
