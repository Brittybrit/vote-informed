 ORDINANCE NO. 2007-16
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ADOPTING A BUDGET
 FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2007
 AND ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 2008, PROVIDING FOR
 SEVERABILITY AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE
 DATE
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland according to the
 requirements of Chapter 200.065 of the Florida Statutes and House Bill lB signed into
 law on June 21, 2007, is required to adopt a Budget for the City of Parkland, Florida for
 the fiscal year 2007-2008; and
 WHEREAS, the City Manager has duly prepared and presented a Budget in
 accordance with Section 2-100(6), Code of Ordinances, City of Parkland, Florida, a copy
 of which is attached hereto and made a part hereof; and
 WHEREAS, the public has had an opportunity to be heard regarding such
 Budget:
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,AS FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. That the General Fund, Operating and Capital
 Improvements, Special Revenue Funds and Capital Project Funds Budget for the fiscal
 year 2007-2008, a copy of which is attached hereto and made a part hereof, showing a
 total of revenues and appropriated fund balance of $29,957,195 and a total expenditure
 requirements of $ 29,957,195 as set forth in detail in said Budget be and the same is
 hereby adopted as the General Fund, Operating and Capital Improvements, Special
 Revenue Funds and Capital Project Funds Budget for the City of Parkland for the fiscal
 year 2007-2008.

 Section 2. That the provisions of this ordinance shall not be deemed to be a
 limitation on the powers granted to the City Commission by the City Charter and which
 relate to the fiscal management of the funds of the City of Parkland.
 Section 3. From time to time, the City Commission may transfer funds from
 one fund, account or department to another as the necessity for same may occur without
 being required to amend the terms and provisions of this ordinance.
 Section 4. Approving the Authorized Position Cap and Regular and
 Management Pay Plan for fiscal year 2007-2008 and setting a position cap of 80 full time
 positions.
 Section 5. That all ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed to the
 extent of such conflict.
 Section 6. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, subsection of this ordinance
 is for any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect
 the validity of any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 Section 7. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon
 its passage and adoption.
