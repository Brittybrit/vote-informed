 6 (b) Enforcement of parkinq requirements for persons who have disabilities.
 7
 8 (1) Parking spaces set aside for handicapped persons, pursuant to
 9 Florida Statutes 316.1955 and 553.5041, as amended, and all
 10 parking spaces which are designated for handicapped persons in
 11 accordance with Florida law, shall only be available for parking by
 12 handicapped persons at all times. It is unlawful for any person to
 13 stop, stand or park a vehicle within, or to obstruct, any such specially
 14 designated and marked parking space unless such vehicle displays a
 15 parking permit issued pursuant to Florida Statute 320.0848, as
 16 amended or authorized under Florida Statute 316.1958, as amended,
 17 and such vehicle is transporting a person eligible for the parking
 18 permit. It is unlawful for any person to obstruct the path of tray-I
 19 to an accessible parking space, curb cut, or access aisle by
 20 standing or parking a vehicle within any such designated area.
 21 The violator is subject to the same penalties as are imposed for
 22 illegally parking in a space that is designated as an accessible
 23 parking space for persons who have disabilities. It shall be
 24 unlawful for any person to park a vehicle on the pavement
 25 marking within any such designated area.
 26
 27 (2) Any person violating this section shall be fined in an amount of
 28 $250.00 for each violation. Failure to pay the penalty within ten (10)
 29 days of the date of the issuance of the citation (Saturdays, Sundays
 30 and legal holidays being excluded for the computation) shall result in
 31 a late fee of $25.00 also being assessed against the violator.
 32
 33 (3) As set forth in Florida Statute 316.1957, when evidence is presented
 34 in any court that a motor vehicle was parked in a properly designated
 35 parking space for persons with disabilities in violation of Florida
 36 Statute 319.1955 316.1955 is prima facie evidence that the vehicle
 37 was parked and left in the space by the person, firm, or corporation in
 38 whose name the vehicle is registered and licensed according to the
 39 Division of Motor Vehicles.
 40
