 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland, Florida,
 12 pursuant to the authority vested in Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, is authorized and empowered to
 13 consider changes to its Code of Ordinances; and
 14 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland operates numerous recreational
 15 facilities in which youth leagues play a prominent role; and
 16 WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to protect the youth of the City participating in
 17 leagues at City parks; and
 18 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland implemented regulations that required background
 19 checks for all coaches participating in Sports Leagues utilizing City fields except for law
 20 enforcement officers;
 21 WHEREAS, Florida Statutes do not allow any exceptions to the background check
 22 requirements;
 23 WHEREAS, the City is proposing modifications to its regulations so the City's adopted
 24 background check requirements are in compliance with State Law; and
 25 WHEREAS, the City Commission of Parkland, Florida finds and determines that
 26 amending Chapter 11 of the City's Code of Ordinance is necessary to protect the health, safety,
 27 property, and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland.
 28 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 29 CITY OF PARKLAND, THAT:
 30 SECTION 1. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 31 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the City Code of the City of
 32 Parkland, and that the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to accomplish such intent.
 33 SECTION 2. Chapter 11, Section 80, of the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances is
 34 hereby amended as follows:
