 13 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland wishes to amend the Comprehensive Plan in
 14 accordance with the requirements of Chapter 163, Florida Statutes; and
 15 WHEREAS, a public hearing was held November 18, 2010, by the City of

 17 consider the above referenced Amendments; and

 19 public hearing on November 30, 2010, pursuant to Chapter 163, Florida Statutes, to
 20 consider transmittal of the proposed amendments to the Comprehensive Plan to the

 22 and interests and all other citizens and residents of the City of Parkland so desiring to

 26 Chapter 163, Florida Statutes during the course of the preparation and transmittal
