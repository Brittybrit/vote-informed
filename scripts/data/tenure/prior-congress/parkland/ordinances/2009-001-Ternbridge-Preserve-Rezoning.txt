 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF

 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 12 Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and
 13 the Code of Ordinances, as adopted by the City of Parkland, is authorized and
 14 empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and land development orders; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Board, after notice and public hearing, has
 17 considered the petition of the City of Parkland to zone certain property from Recreation /
 18 Open Space (OS) Zoning District to Conservation (CONS) Zoning District; and has
 19 presented its recommendation to the City Commission; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements have been satisfied; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Board of the City of Parkland has reviewed
 24 said petition on October 16, 2008 as required by Florida Statutes and has made
 25 recommendation for approval by a 7-0 vote of the members present; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony
 28 presented by the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of
 29 the various City of Parkland review agencies and staff; and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, The City Commission has determined that the proposed rezoning is
 32 consistent with the City of Parkland Comprehensive Plan designation of Conservation;
 33 and
 34
