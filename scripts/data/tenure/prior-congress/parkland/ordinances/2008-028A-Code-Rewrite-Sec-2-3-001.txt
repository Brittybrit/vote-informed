 ORDINANCE NO. 2008-28A
 2
 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AMENDING CHAPTER 2 ENTITLED
 5 "ADMINISTRATION"; RENAMING AND AMENDING SECTION 2-3
 6 ENTITLED "FEES FOR COPIES OF OFFICIAL CITY RECORDS";
 7 PROVIDING A CONFLICTS CLAUSE AND A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE;
 8 AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 11 Florida, pursuant to the authority vested in Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, is authorized
 12 and empowered to consider changes to its Code of Ordinances; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the Florida
 15 Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, the Charter of the City of Parkland, and the
 16 police powers of the City; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 163, Florida
 19 Statutes, the City Planning and Zoning board has reviewed the proposed ordinance and
 20 has determined that the proposed regulations are consistent with the City of Parkland
 21 Comprehensive Plan; and
 22
 23 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 24 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 25
 26 SECTION 1. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained
 27 that the provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the City Code
 28 of the City of Parkland, and that the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to
 29 accomplish such intent.
 30
 31 SECTION 2. Chapter 2 of the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances is hereby
 32 amended and renumbered as follows:
 33
 34 Chapter 2 ADMINISTRATION
 35
 36 Sec. 2-3. Administrative & User Fees
 37 The City Commission may set administrative and user fees by resolution or
 38 ordinance. All fees set forth by resolution shall be on file with the City Clerk.
 39
 40 SECTION 3. Should any section or provision of this ordinance or any portion
 41 thereof, any paragraph, sentence or word be declared by a court of competent
 42 jurisdiction to be invalid, such decision shall not affect the validity of the remainder
 43 hereof as a whole or part thereof other than the part declared to be invalid.
 44
 45 SECTION 4. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 46 passage and adoption.
