 ORDINANCE NO.2006115
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA
 AMENDING THE CITY CODE BY AMENDING CHAPTER 2
 "ADMINISTRATION," ARTICLE X "PENSIONS," DIVISION 2
 "THE POLICE PENSION PLAN AND TRUST FUND" BY
 AMENDING SECTION 2-226 TO PROVIDE FOR NORMAL
 RETIREMENT UPON THE COMPLETION OF TWENTY (20)
 YEARS OF CREDITED SERVICE, TO INCREASE THE
 BENEFIT FORMULA MULITPLIER TO 3% AND TO
 PROVIDE AN ANNUAL 2% COST-OF-LIVING ADJUSTMENT
 FOR RETIREES; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY;
 PROVIDING FOR CODIFICATION; AND PROVIDING AN
 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has determined that the maintenance of a police pension
 plan is in the best interests of the police officers and citizens of the City; and
 WHEREAS,the City Commission desires to provide for normal retirement upon the completion of
 twenty years of credited service, to increase the benefit formula multiplier from two percent (2%) to three
 percent(3%),and to provide an annual two percent(2%)cost-of-living adjustment.
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Commission of the City of Parkland
 as follows:
 Section 1. Recitals. The above recitals are true and correct and incorporated herein by this
 reference.
 Section 2. City Code Amended. Section 2-226"Service Retirement Benefit"of the City Code
 is hereby amended to read as follows:
 Sec. 2-226. Service retirement benefit.
 (a) A member may retire on the first day of the month coincident with or next
 following the earlier of: the date upon which the member completes twenty-five
 (25) years of credited service and attains age fifty-two (52); the date upon which
 the member completes twenty (20) years of credited service; or the date upon
 which the member attains age fifty-five (55) with ten (10) years of credited
 service. There shall be no mandatory retirement age.
 (b) Subject to the provisions of subsection (j) below, a A normal retirement
 benefit shall be determined by multiplying two (2) three (3) percent of final
 monthly compensation by the number of years of credited service.

 f Subject to provisions of subsection (j) below and effective January 1, 2007,
 and on each January 1 thereafter, anv member who has been receiving a
 retirement benefit for at least one (1) year prior thereto shall receive a cost-of-
 living adjustment ("COLA") equal to two percent (2%) of the member's
 retirement benefit.
 The annual COLA provided by subsection (i) and the three percent (3%)
 multiplier provided by subsection (b) shall be funded from the annual state
 monies received by the Retirement Plan in excess of$9,158.00. The maximum
 additional state monies that can be used in each year for these benefit
 enhancements is $69,006. The annual COLA provided for in subsection (i) and
 the three percent (3%) multiplier provided for in subsection (b) will be calculated
 by the actuary and the excess state monies (limited as stated above) shall be
 applied as follows:
 (1.) To the extent that the excess state monies received by the City are
 less than $69,006, the annual two percent (2%) COLA provided to eligible
 members shall be reduced to a level, of not less than zero percent (0%), that can
 be funded by excess state monies actually received. Any reductions in future
 cost-of-living adjustments will not affect any prior cost-of living-adjustments
 given pursuant to this paragraph.
 (2.) If, after reducing the annual COLA to zero percent (0%), the
 remaining excess state monies received by the City do not cover the full cost of
 the three percent (3%) multiplier provided for in subsection (b) above, then the
 three percent (3%) multiplier shall be reduced to a level that can be fully funded
 by the excess state monies actually received provided, however, that in no event
 shall the multiplier for any year be reduced to less than two percent (2%).
 (3.) Each member, in order to be eligible for the enhanced benefits
 provided by this subsection (j), shall execute an acknowledgment that the
 enhanced benefits are dependent upon insurance premium tax rebate money being
 received by the City pursuant to Chapter 185, Florida Statutes, in an amount
 sufficient to pay for the enhanced benefits or a portion thereof.
 Section 3. Severability Should this ordinance or any part thereof be declared
 invalid by a Court of competent jurisdiction, the invalidity of any part of this ordinance shall not
 otherwise affect the validity of the remaining provisions of this ordinance, which shall be
 deemed to have been enacted without the invalid provision.
 Section 4. Inclusion in the Code It is the intention of the City Commission that
 the provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of the City of
 Parkland, and that the word "ordinance" may be changed to "section," "article," or such other
 appropriate word or phrase in order to accomplish such intentions.
