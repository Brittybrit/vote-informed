 6 SECTION 4. The City hereby adopts the following criteria for exclusion of adult
 7 managers, coaches and any other person 16 18 years of age or older, who has any supervisory or
 8 coaching responsibility, regardless of title or designation including league board members and
 9 "buddy" volunteers (hereinafter referred to as Volunteer). Active law enforcement officers,
 10 defined as any law enforcement agency, court or prosecutor charged by law with criminal law
 11 enforcement duties, shall be exempt from undergoing a criminal background check provided
 12 they submit to the City a letter signed by the agency director on agency letterhead stating that the
 13 individual is a sworn law enforcement officer currently employed by the agency, in good
 14 standing with no active internal affairs investigations with the law enforcement officer as the
 15 target of that investigation. Any Volunteer shall be disqualified from participating as a
 16 Volunteer on City owned fields if the person has been found guilty of any one of the following
 17 crimes (guilty means that a person was found guilty following a trial, entered a guilty plea,
 18 entered a no contest plea, accompanied by a court finding of guilty, regardless of whether there
 19 was an adjudication of guilt or a withholding of guilt):
 20 Sex Offenses:
 21 All sex offenses, regardless of the amount of time since offense (examples include child
 22 molestation, rape, sexual assault, sexual battery, sodomy, prostitution, solicitation, indecent
 23 exposure or similar offenses).
 24 Felonies:
 25 All felony offenses involving violence, regardless of the amount of time since offense.
 26 All felony offenses other than violence or sexual offenses within the past ten years.
 27 Misdemeanors:
 28 All misdemeanor violent offenses within the past seven years (examples include simple
