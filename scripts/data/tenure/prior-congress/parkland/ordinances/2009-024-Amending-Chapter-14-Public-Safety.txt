 15 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland's existing code for regulating the health, safety, and
 16 general welfare does not recognize that there are devices that may be desirable to provide
 17 emergency medical treatment to City residents at certain buildings; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission adopted its original Automated
 20 External Defibrillator ("AED") Ordinance on March 28, 2007 which required and AED to be
 21 located within gymnasiums, fitness centers and indoor recreational centers in excess of one
 22 thousand five hundred (1,500) square feet; restaurants with fifty (50) or more seats including
 23 indoor, outdoor and bar; and assisted living facilities as defined by F.S. § 400.402, as
 24 amended from time to time; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission adopted an amended AED
 27 Ordinance on March 4, 2009 requiring pharmacies to be included on the list of uses to have
 28 an AED; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, it is in the best interest of the citizens of Parkland that the following
 31 requirements to the regulations governing the general welfare of the City residents be
 32 adopted.
 33
 34 WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 163, Florida Statutes, the
 35 City Planning and Zoning Board has reviewed the proposed ordinance and has determined

 1 (1) Definition of automated external defibrillators.
 2
 3 Automated external defibrillator device. A defibrillator device that 1) is commercially
 4 distributed in accordance with the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, 2) is
 5 capable of recognizing the presence or absence of ventricular fibrillation, and is
 6 capable of determining without intervention by the user of the device whether
 7 defibrillation should be performed, and 3) upon determining that defibrillation should be
 8 performed, is able to deliver an electrical shock to an individual.
 9
 10 (2) Intent.
 11
 12 (A) It is the intent of the city and the purpose of this chapter:
 13
 14 1. To promote the public health, safety, and general welfare by requiring the
 15 installation of automated external defibrillator devices within certain buildings in
 16 the city;
 17
 18 2. To provide for the inspection of each automated external defibrillator device
 19 within the city; and
 20
 21 3. To provide for the payment of automated external defibrillator inspection
 22 fees.
 23
 24 4. To provide for penalties for non-compliance with this article.
 25
 26 (3) Automated external defibrillator devices required.
 27
 28 (A) Automated external defibrillator devices shall be installed in the following
 29 locations and buildings located within the geographical boundaries of the city:
 30
 31 1. Gymnasiums, fitness centers, athletic clubs, health spa and indoor
 32 recreational centers in excess of one thousand five hundred (1,500) square
 33 feet;
 34
 35 2. Restaurants with fifty (50) or more seats including indoor, outdoor and bar;
 36
 37 3. Assisted living facilities as defined by F.S. § 400A02 429.02, as amended
 38 from time to time,,-;
 39
 40 4. Pharmacies. A pharmacy is an establishment or institution in which the
 41 practice of pharmacy is conducted; drugs, medicines or medicinal chemicals
 42 are dispensed or offered for sale, or a sign is displayed bearing the word or
 43 words 'pharmacist,' 'pharmacy,' 'apothecary,' 'drugstore,' 'druggist,' 'medicine
 44 store,' 'drug sundries,' 'prescriptions filled,' or similar words intended to indicate
 45 that the practice of pharmacy is being conducted..-;

 47 5. Biq box retail establishments. A big box retail establishment shall be

 9 (4) Installation and operation.
 10
 11 (A) The city shall inspect all automated external defibrillator devices for operation
 12 prior to being placed in service or available for use, and on an annual basis.
 13
 14 (B) Automated external defibrillator devices shall be:
 15
 16 (1) Conspicuously located in plain view of the primary public entrance, with
 17 unobstructed access;
 18
 19 (2) Housed in a cabinet with a clear window in the door, an audible alarm
 20 signaling the opening of a door, permanently affixed to a wall, and whose top is
 21 no more than forty-eight (48) inches above the floor;
 22
 23 (3) Located below a sign having a minimum area of seventy (70) square
 24 inches and containing the letters "AED" and the universally recognizable
 25 symbol, which shall be placed no more than sixty (60) inches, on center, above
 26 the floor;
 27
 28 (4) Readily accessible and immediately available when needed for on-site
 29 employees and the general public, including disabled persons; and
 30
 31 (5) Placed near the elevator(s) in the first floor lobby, if the building contains
 32 an elevator.
 33
 34 (C) Automated external defibrillator devices shall contain adult and pediatric pads
 35 and bandage scissors.
 36
 37 (D) All automated external defibrillator devices shall be used in accordance with the
 38 manufacturer's guidelines.
 39
 40 (E) It shall be the responsibility of the owner of the building to:
 41
 42 (1) Install automated external defibrillator devices.
 43
 44 (2) Provide all necessary training for appropriate use;
 45
 46 (3) Maintain automated external defibrillator devices in accordance with
 47 manufacturer's recommended maintenance requirements and as required
 48 herein.
 49
 50 (F) If an automated external defibrillator device is removed for repair, a replacement
 51 shall be provided.

 2 (G) If an automated external defibrillator device is required to be located within a

 8 (5) Fees. Fees for automated external defibrillator device permits and inspections shall be

 11 (6) Penalties. It shall constitute a violation of the City Code, punishable as provided in this
 12 section, to intentionally or willfully:
 13
 14 (A) Render an automated external defibrillator device inoperative except during such
 15 time as the automated external defibrillator device is being serviced, tested, repaired,
 16 or recharged, except pursuant to court order;
 17
 18 (B) Obliterate the serial number on an automated external defibrillator device for
 19 purposes of falsifying service records;
 20
 21 (C) Improperly service, recharge, repair, test, or inspect an automated external
 22 defibrillator device;
 23
 24 4- (D) Use the inspection certificate of another person; er
 25
 26 ? (E) Hold an inspection certificate and allow another person to use said inspection
 27 certificate number; or
 28
 29 (B F) To otherwise fail to comply with this article.
 30
 31 Failure to comply with any part of this article shall be punished by a fine not to exceed five
 32 hundred dollars ($500.00) or imprisonment for a term not exceeding sixty (60) days or by
 33 both fine and imprisonment. Each day a violation exists shall be a separate violation. In
 34 addition, the city shall also have the right to revoke, refuse to issue, refuse to reissue, refuse
 35 to transfer, or refuse to renew any business tax receipt for any facility, business, entity or
 36 establishment where the violation occurred until such time as the violation and/or fine is
 37 corrected.

 42 (A) The use and training of an AED shall occur only after a written notification, in a

 45 must contain the facility or business name, street address, specific location of the
 46 AED, the number of employees at the facility or business, the name of the AED
 47 manufacturer, and model number of each AED.
 48
