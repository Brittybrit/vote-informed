 4 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 5 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, CREATING NEW SECTION 11-143 OF THE
 6 CITY CODE RELATING TO REGULATING ACCESS TO TOT LOTS BY
 7 ADULTS NOT ACCOMPANYING A CHILD; AMENDING SECTION 11-
 8 39 (D) ENTITLED "PROPERTY OWNERS PROHIBITED FROM
 9 RENTING REAL PROPERTY TO CERTAIN SEXUAL OFFENDERS
 10 AND SEXUAL PREDATORS; PENALTIES"; CONTAINING
 11 PROVISIONS FOR IMPLEMENTATION, PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION
 12 IN THE CITY CODE; PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE;
 13 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the preservation of the health, safety and welfare of the children of
 16 the City of Parkland is one of the highest priorities for the City Commission of the City
 17 of Parkland; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland is in an area that is largely populated by
 20 families with children and the City Commission wishes to take every appropriate
 21 measure necessary to protect the safety, health and welfare of the children; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the City Commission has provided numerous playground areas for
 24 young children, often called "tot lots"; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, given the age of children who frequent "tot lots" the City
 27 Commission wishes to assure that the only adults who frequent "tot lots" are those
 28 accompanying children who are playing in the "tot lots" and to provide some
 29 protection from the children playing in the "tot lots" against sexual predators and
 30 others who might mean them harm;
 31
 32 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 33 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 34
 35 SECTION 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and
 36 made a part hereof.
 37
 38 SECTION 2. It is the intent of this Ordinance to provide for the protection of
 39 children playing at designated "tot lots" and to restrict access to said "tot lots" to those
 40 persons over 18 years old who are accompanying children playing at designated "tot
 41 lots".
 42
 43 SECTION 3. For the purpose of this Ordinance, a "tot lot" shall be deemed to
 44 be any City playground designed for use for children under the age of 13 and

 12
 13 Section D. Property Owners Prohibited from Renting Real Property to
 14 Certain Sexual Offenders and Sexual Predators; Penalties.
 15
 16 (1) It is unlawful to let or rent any place, structure, or part thereof, trailer or
 17 other conveyance, with the knowledge that it will be used as a permanent
 18 residence or temporary residence by any person prohibited from establishing
 19 such permanent residence or temporary residence pursuant to this ordinance, if
 20 such place, structure, or part thereof, trailer or other conveyance, is located
 21 within two thousand five hundred (2.500) feet of any school, designated public
 22 school bus stop, day care center, park, playground, or other place where
 23 children regularly congregate.
 24
 25 (2) Prior to letting, renting or leasing any place, structure, or part thereof,
 26 trailer or other conveyance for use as a permanent or temporary residence that
 27 is located within 2,500 feet of a school, designated public school bus stop, day
 28 care center, park, playground, or other place where children regularly
 29 congregate and annually thereafter if a rental agreement is entered into, the
 30 owner or lessor shall obtain confirmation of a nationwide search from the
 31 Broward Sheriff's Office, Parkland District or other law enforcement agency that
 32 the prospective renter, lessee or adult resident is not a registered sexual
 33 offender or sexual predator as a result of a conviction of a sexual offense as
 34 defined herein.
 35
 36 (3) A property owner's failure to comply with provisions of this section shall
 37 constitute a violation of this section, and shall subject the property owner to all
 38 code enforcement provisions and procedures as permitted in the City Code
 39 (through Section 4.5 of the City Code or through prosecution in court), or as
 40 otherwise permitted by law, including, but not limited to injunctive or other relief
 41 in a court of law in order to secure compliance with this ordinance.
