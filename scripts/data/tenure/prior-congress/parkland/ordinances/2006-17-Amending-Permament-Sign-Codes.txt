 5 TO UNDER CANOPY SIGNAGE AND WALL

 7 "DEFINITIONS" AND AMENDING SECTION 15-38
 8 ENTITLED "PERMANENT SIGNS; LOCATION" OF
 9 THE CODE OF ORDINANCE; PROVIDING A
 10 CONFLICTS CLAUSE AND A SEVERABILITY
 11 CLAUSE; AND PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the
 14 Florida Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the police powers of the
 15 City; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland deems it is in the best interest of the
 18 City's commercial businesses to amend Sections 15-2 and 15-38 of the City of
 19 Parkland Code of Ordinances to permit under canopy signs and wall signs in
 20 commercially designated areas; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland finds that the proposed regulations
 23 further the goals of the Comprehensive Plan; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 163, Florida
 26 Statutes, the Planning and Zoning Board has reviewed the proposed regulations
 27 and on June 12, 2006 recommended approval of the proposed language by a 7-0
 28 vote;
 29
 30 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 31 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 32
 33 Section 1. Section 15-2 entitled "Definitions" is hereby amended as
 34 follows:
 35
 36 ***

 38 Under canopy sign. A sign suspended beneath an awning, canopy, ceiling, roof
 39 or marquee.

 7 i. Under canopy signs shall be permitted in commercial districts subject to the
 8 following standards listed below:
 9
 10 1. Sign Area. Under-canopy signs shall not exceed four (4) square feet in
 11 area and shall not exceed one line of copy.
 12
 13 2. Mounting. Under-canopy signs shall be mounted a minimum of eight
 14 (8) feet above the surface of the walkway. Signs shall be mounted
 15 perpendicular to the facade of the adjacent storefront.
 16
 17 3. Number. One (1) under-canopy sign shall be permitted for the main
 18 entrance for each tenant, not to exceed one (1) per business for each
 19 storefront occupied.
 20
 21 4. Master Sign Plan. All under-canopy signs shall be shown on the
 22 master sign plan and shall be in conformance with the standards
 23 contained herein.
 24
 25 5. Undercanopy signs shall not be counted towards the maximum
 26 permitted sign area.
 27
 28 6. Under canopy signs shall be permitted to be located under canopies
 29 and/or awnings for pedestrian view only.
 30
 31 7. All signs under this subsection must be approved through a master
 32 sign program, except for commercial centers which possess an
 33 approved master sign plan, prior to adoption of this ordinance, the
 34 addition of the under-canopy signs can be accomplished through a no
 35 fee administrative process.
 36
 37 8. Under-canopy signs shall be uniform throughout the development in
 38 regards to shape, design, location, color and lettering.
 39
 40 j. Wall sign. A second wall sign shall be permitted on a commercial building,
 41 excluding outparcels, subject to the following standards:
 42
 43 1. The main wall sign shall be located over the entrance to the primary
 44 pedestrian access. The second wall sign may be located either over
 45 the entryway to a secondary public pedestrian access or at the rear
 46 door of the tenant space if it faces a thoroughfare or parking lot.

 1 2. Sign area. No more than one-half (1/2) of the permissible square
 2 footage is permitted on the storefront facade for each tenant and
 3 shall not exceed one line of copy.
 4 3. Letter size and font. The letter size cannot exceed that as approved
 5 in the master sign plan and the letter font must meet the
 6 specifications for the commercial development in which said tenant is
 7 located.
 8 4. Sign type.
 9 a. Prohibited. Illuminated signs, revolving or rotating signs.
 10 5. Location. A second wall sign may be only be located in the tenant
 11 space in which the tenant has signed a lease. Only one wall sign is
 12 permitted per store front.
 13 6. All signs under this subsection must be approved through a master
 14 sign program.
 15 7. The Planning Director shall have the authority to make a determination
 16 regarding the primary pedestrian access and the secondary pedestrian
 17 access.

 21 Section 2. Codification. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is
 22 hereby ordained that the provision of this ordinance shall become and be made
 23 part of the Code of Ordinances of the City of Parkland, Florida and the section of
 24 this ordinance may be renumbered to accomplish such intent.

 26 Section 3: Severability. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section
 27 of this ordinance is for any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity
 28 thereof shall not affect the validity of any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 29
 30 Section 4. Conflicts. All other ordinances in conflict herewith are repealed
 31 to the extent of the conflict.
 32
 33 Section 5. Effective Date. That this ordinance shall be effective
 34 immediately upon adoption by the City Commission of the City of Parkland.
