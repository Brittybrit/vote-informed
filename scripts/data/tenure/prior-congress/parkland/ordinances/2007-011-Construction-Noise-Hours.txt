 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 4 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA RELATING
 5 TO THE HOURS OF CONSTRUCTION, AMENDING
 6 CHAPTER 11 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND CODE
 7 OF ORDINANCES, AMENDING SUBSECTION 11-
 8 40 RELATING TO CONSTRUCTION NOISE
 9 HOURS, CASE NO. 01-ZTA-07; PROVIDING FOR
 10 SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN
 11 THE CODE; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE
 12 DATE.
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 15 Florida, pursuant to the authority vested in Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, is authorized
 16 and empowered to consider changes to its Code of Ordinances; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the Florida
 19 Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, the Charter of the City of Parkland, and the
 20 police powers of the City; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 163, Florida
 23 Statutes, the City Planning and Zoning Board has reviewed the proposed ordinance and
 24 has determined that the proposed regulations are consistent with the City of Parkland
 25 Comprehensive Plan; and
 26
 27 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 28 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 29
 30 SECTION 1. Section 11-40 of the Code of Ordinance is hereby amended as
 31 follows:
 32
 33 No person shall operate or cause to be operated any equipment used in
 34 commercial or residential construction, repair, alteration or demolition work on buildings,
 35 structures, streets, alleys, or appurtenances thereto in residential or commercial land
 36 use categories or within three hundred (300) feet of such land use categories before
 37 7:30 a.m. and after 7:30 p.m., Monday through Friday, and before 9:30 a.m. and after
 38 7:30 p.m. on Saturday and before 10:00 a.m. and after 2:00 p.m. on Sunday. This
 39 section does not apply to any land which is zoned agricultural, or classified as
 40 agricultural on the city's land use plan or county's land use plan. Noise or construction
 41 equipment used in the performance of emergency work for the immediate safety, health
 42 or welfare of individuals of the community or to restore property to a safe condition
 43 following a public calamity shall not be subject to the provisions of this section.
 44
 45 SECTION 2: All ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby
 46 repealed.
