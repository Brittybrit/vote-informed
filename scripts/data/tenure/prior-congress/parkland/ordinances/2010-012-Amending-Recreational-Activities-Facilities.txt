 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 4 FLORIDA, AMENDING CHAPTER 11 ENTITLED "OFFENSES AND
 5 MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS"; AMENDING SECTION 11-72 ENTITLED
 6 "RECREATIONAL ACTIVITIES AND FACILITIES" TO ALLOW TEMPORARY
 7 SUN-SHADE CANOPIES IN CITY PARKS; PROVIDING A CONFLICTS
 8 CLAUSE; PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
 9 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland wishes to ensure residents are able to
 12 fully enjoy City Parks; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland wishes to amend the Code of Ordinance
 15 to ensure the safety of all residents within the Parks by restricting potentially
 16 dangerous hazards and activities; and
 17
 18 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 19 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 20
 21 SECTION 1. It is the intention of the City commission and it is hereby
 22 ordained that the provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part
 23 of the City Code of the City of Parkland, and that the sections of this ordinance
 24 may be renumbered to accomplish such intent.
 25
 26 SECTION 2. Chapter 11, Section 72, of the City of Parkland Code of
 27 Ordinances is hereby amended as follows:
 28

 30

 33 Sec. 11-72. Recreational activities and facilities.
 34 The restrictions and regulations contained herein shall apply to all city parks
 35 as follows:
 36
 37 ***
 38
 39 (k) Remote controlled vehicles. Remote controlled vehicles are not
 40 permitted within any City park.
 41
 42 (I) Golf. No person shall strike a golf ball within any City park unless such
 43 activity is part of a City authorized program.
