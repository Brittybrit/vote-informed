 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ADOPTING A BUDGET FOR THE
 5 FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2008 AND ENDING
 6 SEPTEMBER 30, 2009, PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY
 7 AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE
 8
 9 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland according to the
 10 requirements of Chapter 200.065 of the Florida Statutes, is required to adopt a Budget for
 11 the City of Parkland, Florida for the fiscal year 2008-2009; and
 12 WHEREAS, the City Manager has duly prepared and presented a Budget in
 13 accordance with Section 2-100(6), Code of Ordinances, City of Parkland, Florida, a copy of
 14 which is attached hereto and made a part hereof; and
 15 WHEREAS,the public has had an opportunity to be heard regarding such Budget:
 16 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 17 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 18 Section 1. That the General Fund, Operating and Capital Improvements,
 19 Special Revenue Funds and Capital Project Funds Budget for the fiscal year 2008-2009, a
 20 copy of which is attached hereto and made a part hereof, showing a total of revenues and
 21 appropriated fund balance of $22,254,000 and a total expenditure requirements of $
 22 22,254,000 as set forth in detail in said Budget be and the same is hereby adopted as the
 23 General Fund, Operating and Capital Improvements, Special Revenue Funds and Capital
 24 Project Funds Budget for the City of Parkland for the fiscal year 2008-2009. The budget
 25 attached hereto is hereby adopted as the budget for the City of Parkland as required pursuant
 26 to Section 200.065, Florida Statutes.

 1 Section 2. That the provisions of this ordinance shall not be deemed to be a
 2 limitation on the powers granted to the City Commission by the City Charter and which
 3 relate to the fiscal management of the funds of the City of Parkland.
 4 Section 3. The City Commission may, by resolution, decrease or increase
 5 appropriations or expenditures within a fund, provided that the total of the appropriations of
 6 the fund does not change. Additionally to the extent permitted, amendments or transfers
 7 which do not increase the total budget, but simply reappropriate unspent money, record
 8 contributions or grants, and adjust carryovers may also be effected by a duly enacted
 9 Resolution. The Finance and Administrative Services Director may authorize certain budget
 10 amendments within a department, provided that the total of the appropriations of the
 11 department does not change.
 12 Section 4. That amounts allocated to Capital projects/outlay line items are
 13 specific authorizations for each line item and may be expended only for that specific
 14 purpose. The amount allocated to each line item may not be increased or decreased without
 15 specific City Commission authorization, the same being a duly enacted Resolution effecting
 16 such amendment or transfer.
 17 Section 5. The City Commission, as part of the Budget adopts the Authorized
 18 Position Cap and Regular and Management Pay Plan for fiscal year 2008-2009 and setting a
 19 position cap of 72 full time positions.
 20 Section 6. That all ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed to the
 21 extent of such conflict.
