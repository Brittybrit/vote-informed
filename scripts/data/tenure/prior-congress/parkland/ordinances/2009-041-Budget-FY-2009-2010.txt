 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ADOPTING A BUDGET
 5 FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2009
 6 AND ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 2010, PROVIDING FOR

 8 DATE
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland according to the
 11 requirements of Chapter 200.065 and Chapter 166.241 of the Florida Statutes, is required
 12 to adopt a Budget for the City of Parkland, Florida for the fiscal year 2009-2010; and
 13 WHEREAS, the City Manager has duly prepared and presented a Budget, a copy
 14 of which is attached hereto and made a part hereof and hereinafter referred as the Budget;
 15 and
 16 WHEREAS, the public has had an opportunity to be heard regarding such
 17 Budget:
 18 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 19 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 20 Section 1. The City of Parkland, City Commission hereby adopts the Budget
 21 in accordance with Section 200.065 and 166.241 of the Florida Statutes for the operations
 22 of City government for the City of Parkland. The General Fund, Capital Projects Fund,
 23 Park Improvement Fund and Capital Replacement Fund for the fiscal year 2009-2010,
 24 showing a total of revenues and appropriated fund balance of $21,784,450 and a total
 25 expenditure requirements of$21,784,450 as set forth in detail in the Budget be and the
 26 same is hereby adopted as the General Fund, Capital Projects Fund, Park Improvement
 27 Fund and Capital Replacement Fund for the City of Parkland for the fiscal year 2009-
 28 2010.
 29 Section 2. The Budget as adopted shall regulate expenditures for the City of
 30 Parkland in accordance with applicable law.
 31 Section 3. All delinquent taxes collected during the year as proceeds from
 32 levies of operation millage of prior years are hereby specifically appropriated for the use
 33 and benefit of the General Fund.
