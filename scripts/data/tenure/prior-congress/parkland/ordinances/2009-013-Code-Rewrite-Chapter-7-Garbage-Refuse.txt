 13 Sec. 7-32. Use of franchise's services required; disposal of hazardous material.
 14
 15 Waste Management shall be the exclusive collector of solid waste for all
 16 residents tenants in commercial establishments and recycling services for all residences
 17 within the city boundary, subject to the limitations set forth in the franchise agreement
 18 with Waste Management dated Jan. 28, 2008, and further subject to any limitations as
 19 set forth in applicable Florida law. The exclusive right and obligation granted by
 20 Ordinance No. 2008-06 and as set forth in these regulations does not relate to storm-
 21 generated debris that is the subject of an emergency order, construction debris,
 22 hazardous waste and hazardous substances (as defined in Florida Statute § 403.703).
 23
 24 Sec. 7-33. Residential solid waste and horticultural trash collection.
 25
 26 All persons occupying a residential unit or maintaining a place of residence within
 27 the city shall be provided regular solid waste and horticultural trash collection two (2)
 28 days per week. The owners and/or occupants shall subscribe to the solid waste
 29 collection service and have all accumulations of solid waste generated from such
 30 premises removed by Waste Management. Waste Management shall provide twice a
 31 week collection as long as all waste set out for collection is in approved containers as
 32 provided for in Ordinance 2008-06. All owners/occupants shall comply with this
 33 schedule. Containers shall be placed at curbside prior to "pickup," but no later earlier
 34 than twelve (12) hours prior to collection.
 35
 36 Residential customers who are physically or mentally challenged and for which
 37 there is no adult living in the residence who is capable of placing the containers at the
 38 curb may elect "handicapped service" at no additional charge. Waste Management shall
 39 provide an appropriate form for such residents to complete together with required
 40 documentation corroborating such status. The city shall make the final decision in the
 41 event of a dispute between Waste Management and resident over resident's right to
 42 have "handicapped service."
