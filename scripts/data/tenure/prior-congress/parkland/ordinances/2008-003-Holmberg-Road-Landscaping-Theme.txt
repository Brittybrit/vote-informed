 32 SECTION 1. Section 9-31.1 of the Code of Ordinance is hereby amended and states
 33 as follows:
 34 Sec. 9-31.1. Holmberg Road landscaping theme.
 35 (a) The Holmberg Road landscaping theme shall apply directly to land within the following
 36 general location: That portion of Holmberg Road, from NW 61st Avenue to Riverside Drive
 37 through, and including, the intersections. The landscape buffer areas abutting Holmberg Road
 38 rights-of-way north and south shall comply with the following landscaping design theme to
 39 enhance, facilitate, and encourage landscaping of Holmberg Road which has been determined
 40 by the city commission to be special and unique in this location in providing a desirable
 41 entranceway for the city. Notwithstanding any other provision in any ordinance to the contrary,
 42 the city shall not approve any permit for, or permit any removal of any tree, shrub, bush, or
 43 grass along Holmberg Road from S.R. 441 to University Drive from the edge of the roadway
 44 for a distance of twenty-five (25) feet from said edge, unless there is a direct replacement or
 45 equivalent replacement of vegetation within five (5) feet of any tree, shrub, bush, or grass
 46 removed. Replacement vegetation shall be installed in no more than ninety (90) days from the
 47 removal of existing vegetation and achieve opaqueness in no more than one (1) year from date of
 48 installation.

 3 SECTION 2. Section 9-43 of the Code of Ordinance is hereby amended and states as
 4 follows:
 5 Sec. 9-43. Arbor streets.
 6 An arbor street shall be a street designated by the city commission, as set forth herein, based upon the
 7 existence of extensive vegetation bordering the street. For designation as an arbor street, the vegetation
 8 must be dense in character, contain vegetation or trees in excess of fifteen (15) feet in height for
 9 substantial lengths of the road, and creates a rural feel for significant lengths of the road. The city does
 10 hereby find that Holmberg Road is an arbor street for its entire length within the city. Other streets may
 11 be designated as arbor streets by resolution of the city commission, provided the city makes a specific
 12 finding that the requirements set forth above have been met.
 13 (1) Where a street has been designated as an Arbor Street, no vegetation of any type whatsoever shall
 14 be removed from the right-of-way for the Arbor Street without the specific permission of the city. Said
 15 permission must be granted by the city commission after review by the planning and zoning board. The
 16 director of planning shall be responsible for developing application forms for person wishing to obtain
 17 permission for such removal. All applications shall first be reviewed by the city landscape architect,
 18 who shall make a recommendation with regard to the application. The city shall process any completed
 19 application within ninety (90) days of submittal. This provision may be waived by the city manager in
 20 cases deemed to be a public emergency. No removal shall be permitted, notwithstanding the fact that
 21 there exists in the area an exotic species which would normally be unregulated if the removal of that
 22 species or, any other species, would cause the character of the vegetation along the Arbor Street to be
 23 materially changed. The standard for granting permission for a private party to remove vegetation
 24 outside of the right-of-way shall be whether the removal of the vegetation shall cause these to be a
 25 material diminution in the amount, size, density or quality of this vegetation bordering the road and
 26 whether there are commitments to replace the vegetation which maintains the rural and vegetated feel of
 27 the road. Replacement buffer shall be installed in no more ninety (90) days from removal and
 28 achieve opaqueness in no more than one(1)year from date of installation.
