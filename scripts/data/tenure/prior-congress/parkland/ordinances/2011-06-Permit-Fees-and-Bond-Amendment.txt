 3 SECOND READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA
 4 AMENDING CHAPTER 18 ENTITLED "ENGINEERING STANDARDS," ARTICLE IV
 5 ENTITLED, "PERMITS, FEES AND BONDS," AMENDING SECTION 18-56 ENTITLED,
 6 "PERFORMANCE BOND"; PROVIDING A CONFLICT CLAUSE, PROVIDING A
 7 SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE
 8
 9 WHEREAS, Chapter 18, Article IV of the City of Parkland ("City") Code of Ordinances
 10 addresses the requirement for a performance bond to be posted for roadway construction; and
 11
 12 WHEREAS, roadway construction typically includes the installation of two lifts of asphalt
 13 each 3/" thick; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, it is desirable to install the second (or final) lift of asphalt after most of the
 16 buildings in the area have been constructed; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, there is presently no time frame on when the second or final lift is required
 19 to be constructed by the developer of the project; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the City has experienced developments that have lingered prior to their
 22 completion; thus, causing staff to question the viability of the roadway's first lift to continue to
 23 support traffic; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City Commission of Parkland, Florida finds and determines that
 26 amending Chapter 18, Article IV of the City's Code of Ordinances to provide a deadline to
 27 construct the second lift of asphalt or to halt certificate of occupancies or to allow a longer
 28 deadline to provide the second lift if the thickness of the first lift is increased is in the best
 29 interests of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland.
 30
 31 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 32 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 33
 34 SECTION 1. Chapter 18, Code of Ordinances entitled "Engineering Standards" Article
 35 VI entitled "Permits, Fees & Bonds," Section 18-56 is hereby amended to read as follows:
 36
 37 Sec. 18-56. Performance bond.
 38 The performance bond amount shall be one hundred twenty (120) percent of the
 39 estimated cost of all construction. In public and private rights-of-way, the performance bond for
 40 roadway construction may be reduced to twenty-five (25) percent of the original bond amount
 41 after the first lift of asphalt is installed and approved by the city's engineer, qualified engineering
 42 consultant, or designee. A- performance bond shall also be required for the installation of
 43 roadways within private rights of way, except single family homes' driveways. This bond shall
 44 be in an amount equal to one hundred twenty (120) percent of the cost of construction and must
 45 be delivered to the city prior to issuance of an Engineering Permit. prior to the installation of the
 46 first lift of asphalt . A performance bond is not required for driveways of single — family homes.
 47 All bonds shall be in a form that is approved by the City Attorney.
 48
 49 In planned residential developments, if from the date of issuance, the bond for the final
 50 lift of asphalt has been in place for two (2) years or more, and the first lift is less than 1.25" thick,
 51 then upon issuance of a Certificate of Occupancy for eighty (80%) percent of the buildings in
