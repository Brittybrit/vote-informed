 ORDINANCE NO. 2008-06
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA
 AMENDING APPENDIX A ENTITLED "FRANCHISES" ARTICLE III
 ENTITLED "WASTE MANAGEMENT INC. OF FLORIDA" BY REPEALING
 ORDINANCE 91-4 IN ITS ENTIRETY; ADOPTING A FRANCHISE
 AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASTE MANAGEMENT INC. OF FLORIDA
 AND THE CITY OF PARKLAND; AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER
 TO EXECUTE A CONTRACT WITH WASTE MANAGEMENT INC. OF
 FLORIDA CONCERNING THE COLLECTION AND REMOVAL OF TRASH,
 GARBAGE AND OTHER REFUSE WITHIN THE CITY OF PARKLAND;
 PROVIDING A CONFLICTS CLAUSE; PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY
 CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, there is an immediate and continuing need for the collection and disposal of
 trash, garbage and other refuse found within municipal limits of the City of Parkland; and
 WHEREAS, this is an important public service which the City wishes to provide through
 a franchise agreement; and
 WHEREAS, the City's current Franchise Agreement with Waste Management Inc. of
 Florida for the collection and disposal of trash, garbage and other refuse will expire at 12:01 a.m.
 on April 1, 2008; and
 WHEREAS, Waste Management Inc. of Florida has heretofore provided for the
 collection and disposal of trash, garbage and other refuse found within the municipal limits of the
 City of Parkland; and
 WHEREAS, service provided by Waste Management Inc. of Florida has been rendered
 with satisfactory results and adequate efficiency; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of Parkland, Florida upon investigation and inquiry,
 has determined that it would be in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland to
 continue its existing franchise with Waste Management Inc. of Florida with revisions thereto and
 as set forth herein in order to provide a needed public service.
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 Section 1: That as of the first day of April, 2008 Ordinance 91-4 is repealed in its
 entirety.
 Section 2: That Waste Management Inc. of Florida is hereby granted a franchise in
 accordance with the franchise agreement attached hereto, including every right and privilege
 appertaining thereto, and the City Manager is hereby authorized to enter into and execute the
 aforementioned franchise agreement with Waste Management Inc. of Florida.

<<PG>>
 Section 3: If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for
 any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of
 any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 Section 5: That all Ordinances or parts of Ordinances in conflict herewith are to the
 extent of said conflict, hereby repealed.
 Section 6: That this Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its passage and
 adoption.
