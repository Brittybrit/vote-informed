 19 Section 22-107.1. Intent.
 20
 21 The regulations and requirements establish general guidelines for the siting of
 22 telecommunications towers and antennas and are intended to accomplish the following
 23 purposes:
 24
 25 (a) protect and promote the public health, safety and general welfare of the
 26 residents of the City;
 27
 28 (b) minimize residential areas and land uses from potential adverse impacts of
 29 telecommunications towers and antennas;
 30
 31 (c) encourage the location of telecommunications towers in non-residential areas
 32 and to locate them, to the extent possible, in areas where the adverse impact on the
 33 community is minimal;
 34
 35 (d) minimize the total number of telecommunications towers throughout the
 36 community by strongly encouraging the co-location of antennas on new and pre-existing
 37 telecommunications tower sites as a primary option rather than construction of additional
 38 single-use telecommunications towers;
 39
 40 (e) encourage users of telecommunications towers and antennas to configure them
 41 in a way that minimizes the adverse visual impact of the telecommunications towers and
 42 antennas through careful design, siting, landscape screening, and innovative
 43 camouflaging techniques;
 44
 45 (f) minimize potential damage to property from telecommunications towers and
 46 antennas by requiring such structures be soundly designed, constructed, modified and

 10 Section 22-107.2. General Rules of Interpretation and Definitions.
 11
 12 (a) Certain terms used in this subdivision have been defined in this section. In
 13 the absence of definitions, the standard dictionary meaning shall be utilized. In any event,
 14 the director of planning shall have the right to interpret the terms contained in this
 15 subdivision. In construing the meaning of the subdivision, the following rules shall apply:
 16 (1) Words used in the present tense also include the future tense.
 17 (2) Words used in the singular number also include the plural and vice-
 18 versa.
 19 (3) The word "shall" is mandatory. The word "may" is permissible.
 20 (4) The word "development" shall refer also to "project" and the area in
 21 which a project takes place.
 22 (5) The words "used" or "occupied" shall be construed to include
 23 arranged, designed, constructed, altered, converted, rented, leased or
 24 intended to be used, intended to be occupied.
 25 (6) The word "lot" shall refer also to plot, parcel, tract and premises.
 26 (7) The word "building" shall refer also to structure, mobile home,
 27 dwelling and residence.
 28 (8) The words "area" and "district" may indicate and include the
 29 meaning "zone."
 30 (9) Except where specified, the provisions of this article shall be
 31 construed to mean the minimum standards, requirements and regulations
 32 adopted in pursuit of the purposes of this subdivision.
 33
 34 (b) Definitions. As used in this Ordinance, the following words, terms and
 35 phrases, when used in this subdivision shall have the meanings set forth below, and for
 36 the purpose of this Ordinance shall control over any other definitions contained in the
 37 City's Code of Ordinances. Words not defined shall be given their common and ordinary
 38 meaning.

 40 "Accessory Use" means a secondary use including a use that is not related to,
 41 incidental to, subordinate to and subservient to the main use of the property on which an
 42 antenna and/or telecommunications tower is sited.
 43
 44 "Alternative Tower Structure" means a design mounting structure that
 45 camouflages or conceals the presence of an antenna or telecommunications tower, for
 46 example, flag poles, man made trees, clock towers, bell steeples, and similar alternative

 5 "Antenna" means a transmitting and/or receiving device used in wireless
 6 telecommunications services that radiates or captures electromagnetic waves, digital
 7 signals, analog signals, radio frequencies, wireless communications signals and other
 8 communications signals including directional antennas such as panel and microwave dish
 9 antennas, and omni-directional antennas such as whips, but excluding radar antennas,
 10 amateur radio antennas and satellite earth stations.

 12 "Applicant" means any party submitting an Application within the meaning of
 13 this Ordinance.
 14
 15 "Application" means any proposal, submission or request to construct, operate, or
 16 maintain a telecommunications tower, equipment facility, wireless communications
 17 facility, or Antenna within the City or seeks any other relief from the City pursuant to this
 18 Ordinance.
 19
 20 "Array" means a group of up to twelve (12) Antennas that are either (i) mounted
 21 or side mounted on the rooftop of a building or rooftop structure(s); or (ii) directly or
 22 indirectly mounted on a telecommunications tower.
 23
 24 "Backhaul network" means the lines that connect personal wireless service
 25 facilities to one (1) or more cellular telephone switching offices, and/or long distance
 26 providers, or the public switched telephone network.
 27
 28 "Broadcasting Facility" means any telecommunications tower or Antenna built
 29 primarily for the purpose of broadcasting AM, FM or television signals.
 30
 31 "Building Code" means the Florida Building Code, as amended, the National
 32 Electrical Code, as amended, the National Electrical Safety Code, as amended, FCC
 33 regulations, as amended, and any other applicable federal, state, and local building code.
 34
 35 "Building-Permit Review" means a review for compliance with building
 36 constructions standards adopted by the City and does not include a review for compliance
 37 with land development regulations.
 38
 39 "Camouflaged Facility"means a facility that is disguised, hidden, part of an
 40 existing or proposed structure, or placed within an existing or proposed structure in a
 41 manner that makes it not readily identifiable as a wireless communications facility. A
 42 camouflaged facility may or may not have a secondary function (e.g., bell tower, spire,
 43 flag pole, etc.). This term shall be synonymous with "stealth facility."
