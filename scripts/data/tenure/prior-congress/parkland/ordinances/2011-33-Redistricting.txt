 1 ORDINANCE NO.2011-33
 2
 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA
 4 REDISTRICTING THE CITY INTO FOUR CITY COMMISSION
 5 DISTRICTS, PURSUANT TO THE CITY CHARTER SECTION 3.02,
 6 PROVIDING FOR THE REDISTRICTING OF THE CITY INTO FOUR
 7 COMMISSION DISTRICTS AS NEARLY AS PRACTICABLE ON AN
 8 EQUAL POPULATION BASIS BY CONTIGUOUS BOUNDARIES,
 9 CONTAINING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE, CONTAINING AN
 10 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 11
 12 WHEREAS, Section 3.02 of the City Charter thereof provided for the redistricting of the City
 13 of Parkland into four (4) Commission Districts to be drawn as nearly as practicable, on an equal
 14 population basis by contiguous boundaries as required by the City Charter; said districts to be effective
 15 for the municipal elections to be held in November 2012 and November 2014; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the City has retained the services of a consultant to draw districts as required
 18 by the City Charter, Section 3.02; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, to provide for proper redistricting to be effective for the elections to be held in
 21 November of 2012, the City Commission has considered alternatives and determined a redistricting
 22 plan as provided for in the map (Exhibit A), attached hereto and made a part of;
 23
 24 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 25 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,that:
 26
 27 SECTION 1. The above wh ereas clauses are adopted herein as part of this ordinance.
 28
 29 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland does hereby divide the City of
 30 Parkland into four (4) Commission Districts. The Commission Districts shall be divided and shall
 31 be represented by the numbers given to each district as indicated on the attached map (Exhibit A);
 32 these districts shall be effective for the municipal elections to be held in November 2012 and
 33 November 2014.
 34
 35 SECTION 3. Should any section, paragraph, sentence, or word of this Ordinance be
 36 declared unenforceable or unconstitutional that shall not affect the remainder thereof which shall
 37 remain in full force and effect.
 38
 39 SECTION 4. This Ordinance sh all become effective immediately upon passage.
 40
 41 PASSED this 5th day of October 2011, upon first reading.
 42
 43 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 19th day of October 2011, upon seco d final reading.
