 31 SECTION 1. Section 22-4 entitled "Definitions" of the Code of Ordinances of
 32 the City of Parkland is hereby amended by adding the following definitions:
 33
 34 Beekeeping - The practice of intentional maintenance of honeybee hives.
 35
 36 Boarding Stable - A facility that is used for the feeding, housing, training, breeding
 37 and exercising of horses whether owned by the property owner or by another for
 38 which the owner of the premises receives compensation for such when not owned
 39 by the owner.
 40
 41 Botanical Garden - A public or private facility for the demonstration and observation
 42 of the cultivation of flowers, fruits, vegetables or ornamental plants.
 43
 44 Cattle or stock raising - The breeding, feeding and general care of livestock on a
 45 farm.
 46
 47 Corn Raising — The growing or production of corn.

 1
 2 Farm - The land, buildings, support facilities, machinery, and other appurtances
 3 used in the production of farm and aquaculture products.
 4
 5 Floriculture - The cultivation of flowering and ornamental plants.
 6
 7 Forest - An ecosystem characterized by a more or less dense and extensive tree
 8 cover consisting of over ten (10) acres often consisting of stands varying in
 9 characteristics such as species, composition, structure, age, class and associated
 10 processes and commonly including meadows, streams, fish and wildlife.
 11
 12 Forestry - The profession embracing the science, art, and practice of creating,
 13 managing, using, and conserving forests over ten (10) acres and associated
 14 resources for human benefit and in a sustainable manner to meet desired goals,
 15 needs, and values.
 16
 17 Greenhouse - An accessory structure or enclosed building, permanent or portable,
 "18 which is used for the growth of plants consisting of a glass or hard plastic
 19 enclosure used to protect plants from insects, heat, cold and exposure to the sun.
 20
 21 Grove - A group of five or more trees planted and cultivated for the production of
 22 fruit or nuts.
 23
 24 Horticultural farming - The use of land for the growing or production of flowers and
 25 nursery stock, including ornamental plants and cultured sod.
 26
 27 Hydroponic Garden - A plot of land used for the cultivation of plants in nutrient
 28 solution, rather than in soil.
 29
 30 Nursery - Any grounds or premises on or in which nursery stock is grown,
 31 propagated, or held for wholesale sale or distribution, except where aquatic plant
 32 species are tended for harvest in the natural environment.
 33
 34 Pasture - Land that is used for grazing livestock.
 35
 36 Plants and plant products - Trees, shrubs, vines, forage and cereal plants, and all
 37 other plants and plant parts, including cuttings, grafts, scions, buds, fruit,
 38 vegetables, roots, bulbs, seeds, wood, lumber, and all products made from them,
 39 unless specifically excluded by the rules of the department.
 40
 41 Produce Farm —A farm business that grows fruits and vegetables for sale to the
 42 fresh market or to processors for use in manufacturing food products.
 43
 44 Raising of poultry or sheep — The breeding, feeding and general care of poultry or
 45 sheep.
