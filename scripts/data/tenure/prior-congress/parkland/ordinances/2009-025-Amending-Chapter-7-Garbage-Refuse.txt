 12 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland finds that the storage of
 13 abandoned vehicles outside of residences and businesses constitutes a blight on neighborhoods,
 14 is unsightly, and constitutes a threat to the health, safety and welfare of the citizens; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland wishes to adopt reasonable regulations relating to
 17 abandoned vehicles to assure that abandoned vehicles will not be stored outside of residences
 18 and businesses in the City;
 19
 20 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 21 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 22
 23 SECTION 1. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 24 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the City Code of the City of
 25 Parkland, and that the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to accomplish such
 26 intent.
 27
 28 SECTION 2. Chapter 7 of the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances is hereby amended
 29 as follows:
 30
 31 Chapter 7 Garbage and Refuse
 32
 33 Section 7-4 Regulating the Placement of Abandoned Vehicles
 34
 35 a. An abandoned vehicle shall be deemed to be any vehicle which is wrecked, inoperative,
 36 or partially dismantled and shall include motor vehicles, trailers, and boats which are in
 37 said condition. A motor vehicle, trailer or boat for which there is no valid license tag
 38 affixed to the vehicle, or current registration shall constitute an abandoned vehicle for
 39 the purposes of this ordinance.
 40
 41 b. Inoperative vehicle means any wrecked or partially dismantled vehicle that is
 42 parked or stored without having all the wheels mounted, or is in a condition of
 43 substantial disrepair, or which is parked or stored without having tires inflated, or
 44 other similar condition for a period in excess of (72) seventy-two hours.
 45
 46 c. It shall be unlawful for any person to store abandoned vehicles outside of an enclosed
 47 building; provided that nothing in this section shall be deemed to apply to an abandoned

 1 vehicle which is authorized to be left on private business property properly operated,
 2 licensed and zoned in the City of Parkland for the purposes of accepting such vehicles.
 3
 4 d. When an enforcement officer of the City of Parkland determines that an abandoned
 5 vehicle is being stored in violation of this ordinance, said enforcement officer shall
 6 cause a notice to be placed on an abandoned vehicle in a form containing the following
 7 information:
 8
 9 1. Notification of violation of this ordinance, containing the ordinance number or
 10 code section, describing the nature of the violation.
 11
 12 2. A statement of the location of the abandoned vehicle.
 13
 14 3. A statement that the abandoned vehicle must be removed within three (3) days
 15 from the date of the notice, otherwise it will be presumed to be abandoned and
 16 the matter will be brought before the Code Enforcement Special Master or the
 17 County Court as a violation of the City code.
 18
 19 4. The signature of the Code Enforcement Officer.
 20
 21 5. Such other information as is deemed appropriate by the Code Enforcement
 22 Officer.
 23
 24 e. Additionally, the Code Enforcement Officer shall make a reasonable effort to ascertain
 25 the name and address of the owner of the abandoned vehicle, and if such information is
 26 reasonably available, the Code Enforcement Officer shall mail, by certified mail, a copy
 27 of the notice to the owner. This shall occur prior to the posting of the notice on the
 28 vehicle. The Code Enforcement Officer shall also mail, by certified mail, a copy of the
 29 above described notice to the owner of the real property upon which the abandoned
 30 vehicle is located as shown by the real estate tax records used by the Broward County
 31 Property Appraiser.
 32
 33 f. If, at the end of the three (3) day period after posting of the notice as set forth above,
 34 the owner or any person interested in such abandoned vehicle has not removed same,
 35 or caused said vehicle to be in a condition where it is no longer defined as an
 36 abandoned vehicle, then the matter shall proceed to enforcement action before either
 37 the Code Enforcement Special Master of the City or the County Court as a municipal
 38 ordinance violation.
 39
 40 g. There shall be a fine imposed for violation of this ordinance in the amount not to exceed
 41 $250 per day for each day the abandoned vehicle is permitted to remain on the property
 42 in violation of this ordinance.
 43
 44 SECTION 3: All ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby
 45 repealed.
