 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, RELATING TO ARTICLE
 5 V. OF THE CODE OF ORDINANCES ENTITLED
 6 "PROPERTY MAINTENANCE" BY ADDING SECTION 4-63
 7 ENTITLED "GENERAL ROOF MAINTENANCE";
 8 PROVIDING FOR AA SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; AND
 9 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the Florida
 12 Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, the Charter of the City of Parkland; and
 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland ("City Commission") finds
 14 it in the best interest of the City to amend Article V. of the Code of Ordinances entitled
 15 "Property Maintenance; and
 16 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds it in it's best interest to add Section 4-63
 17 entitled "General Roof Maintenance" to enhance roof maintenance regulations for all
 18 property owners and to regulate the removal of damaged, broken and loose roof tiles in a
 19 timely manner to be enforced at any time of the year; and
 20 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds it in the best interest of the City to enable
 21 Code Enforcement to act in a proactive manner to better prepare for hurricane season and
 22 the possibility of violent high wind events; and
 23 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that the reasonable regulations contained
 24 herein will protect the health, safety, and welfare of the residents of the City: and
 I
 25 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 26 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 27
 28 SECTION 1. Section 4-63 entitled "General Roof Maintenance" of the Code of
 29 Ordinances of the City of Parkland is hereby amended by adding the following:
 30
 31 Section 4-63 General Roof Maintenance.
 32 (1) Every roof shall be free of holes, breaks, loose and/or damaged roof tile, shingles or
 33 rotting boards or timber and other conditions which might admit rain water or dampness to the
 34 interior.

 1 (2) Every roof shall be watertight, weather tight and rodent-proof.
 2 (3) Every roof shall be structurally sound and kept in a good state of repair.
 3 (4) Every roof with wood surfaces, other than decay resistant wood, shall be protected from
 4 the elements and decay by paint or other protective covering or treatment.
 5 (5) Every roof should be so designed and maintained to provide proper drainage or rain water
 6 and prevent standing water.
 7 (6) Gutters and downspouts, where in existence shall be maintained in a good state or repair
 8 and properly maintained.
 9 (7) Every roof shall be free of debris, trash and refuse at all times.
 10 (8) Rubbish, brush, weeds, broken glass, stumps, roots, obnoxious growths, filth, garbage,
 11 trash and debris shall not be disposed or permitted to remain on any property. This subsection
 12 shall not apply to garbage, trash and debris, which is containerized in approved receptacles for
 13 appropriate collection and removal.
 14 (9) Dead and/or dying trees and limbs or other natural growth which constitute a health or
 15 safety hazard to persons or property shall be removed and replaced if required by City Code
 16 requirements. Trees shall be kept pruned and trimmed to prevent the occurrence of a health or
 17 safety hazard as provided by section 9-34 of the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances.
 18
 19
 20 SECTION 4. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that
 21 the provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of
 22 the City of Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance to accomplish such intent.
 23
 24 SECTION 5. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for
 25 any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity
 26 of any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 27
 28 SECTION 6. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 29 passage and adoption.
