 41 resolution or ordinance) shall be for terms of two (2) years beginning on the third

 43 in section 2-40. The terms of all board members serving as of the date of
 44 adoption of this section shall terminate on the third Wednesday of November
 45 2008; except that if a successor is not appointed, then that board member shall
 46 serve until an appointment is made. The board appointments of an individual

 22 (e) Members of the board shall be eligible for reappointment.

 24 Sec. 2-40. Vacancies.
 25 (a) Upon a vacancy occurring in any board appointment, the vacancy shall be
 26 filled by the commissioner who made the appointment or a majority of the city
 27 commissioners attending a meeting for at large appointments. If the city
 28 commissioner who made the appointment is no longer serving, his/her successor
 29 shall make the appointment, or if the commission vacancy has not yet been filled
 30 as proscribed by the Charter, the city commission shall appoint the replacement
 31 by a majority vote of the city commissioners attending the meeting; said
 32 appointment shall be valid until the successor city commissioner is appointed or
 33 elected. For all individual appointments, the term of the appointee shall be
 34 deemed to end when the appointing commissioner leaves office, thereby creating
 35 a vacancy to be filled as proscribed herein.
 36
 37 Ib) Appointments to the board to fill vacancies due to other than expiration of
 38 terms shall be in each instance for the unexpired remainder of the term, except
 39 as set forth above.
 40
 41 (c) Any member who is absent for more than two (2) regularly scheduled
 42 meetings in either the first or second year of his service, commencing with the
 43 date of appointment, shall be deemed to have vacated his position. The vacancy
 44 so created shall be placed on the next city commission agenda for appointment.
 45 The member who has vacated his position shall be eligible for reappointment.

 2 Sec. 2-42. Compensation.
 3 The city manager shall provide appropriate staff support for the operation of any
 4 advisory board. Members shall receive no compensation except that, if members
 5 undertake actions on behalf of the city which require them to incur costs, they may
 6 apply to the city manager for reimbursement of those expenses prior to undertaking any
 7 such services. It shall be within the discretion of the city manager to provide
 8 reimbursement to such member.
 9
 10 Sec. 2-43 Oath.
 11 Before entering upon his or her official duties, every person appointed to an advisory
 12 board shall qualify for office by taking the oath prescribed for officeholders of the city.
 13
 14 Sec. 2-44 Organization; rules of procedure.
 15 (a) Annually at its first regular meeting after the city commission has appointed
 16 board members, the board shall elect from its membership a chairperson, and a
 17 vice chairperson, each to serve until qualification of a successor, elected in the
 18 year following. Officers of the board so elected shall be eligible to succeed
 19 themselves.
 20
 21 (b) The board shall adopt reasonable rules for the conduct of its business
 22 consistent with this article and other applicable law, and such rules shall be
 23 published and be made available to the public.
 24
 25 (c) Meetings shall be held at the call of the chairperson or the City Manager or
 26 his/her designee, at such other times as the board may determine, or as
 27 otherwise provided by law. Special meetings shall not be held unless at least
 28 twenty-four (24) hours notice is given to each member and the city manager.
 29
 30 (d) Members shall notify the board liaison if they are unable to attend a meeting.
 31 The board liaison shall notify the alternate that his/her attendance is required. If
 32 a quorum will not be present, the board liaison shall notify the chairperson, who
 33 may cancel the scheduled meeting.
 34
 35 (e) All meetings shall be open to the public.
 36
 37 (f) Minutes shall be kept of the proceeding at each meeting which shall record the
 38 official actions taken by the board. An audio tape recording of the proceedings
 39 may be available.
