 ORDINANCE NO. 2008 - 11
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 PROVIDING FOR ENFORCEMENT OF VIOLATIONS RELATED TO
 WATER RESTRICTIONS, PROVIDING FOR ENFORCEMENT BEFORE
 THE SPECIAL MASTER, PROVIDING FOR PROCEDURES AND
 IMPOSITION OF PENALTIES; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY;
 PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE, PROVIDING FOR
 AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has determined that the most
 efficient manner for enforcingviolations of water shortage regulations would be through the Code
 g g g
 Enforcement Special Master; and
 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 162.03, the City is permitted to adopt an alternate code
 enforcement system; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to do so for water restriction violations;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 Section 1. This Ordinance shall apply to the violation of any restriction on the use of
 water, or violations of Section 11-86 through 11-96 (collectively referred to as Water
 Restrictions).
 Section 2. Code Inspector shall mean any authorized agent or employee of the City of
 Parkland who has been assigned the duty of code compliance by the City Manager or City
 Commission. A Broward County Sheriff's officer or deputy shall be considered a Code Inspector.
 Section 3. A Code Enforcement Special Master appointed by the City Commission of
 the City of Parkland shall have authority to hear alleged violations of water restrictions and
 impose the fines as authorized by City Ordinance or as otherwise authorized by applicable law
 upon a finding of a violation of any water restriction.
 Section 4. Code Inspectors shall have authority to cite persons in violation of water
 restrictions pursuant to the procedures set forth herein. Upon a determination that a violation of a
 water restriction exists, the Code Inspector shall provide a notice to the person violating the water

 a. Certified mail, return receipt requested, to the owner of the property as listed in the
 Tax Collector's Offices for Tax Notices; or
 b. Hand delivery by a Law Enforcement Officer or Code Inspector; or
 c. Leaving the notice at the location where the violation occurred with any person
 above fifteen (15) years of age residing at the location, provided the person is informed of the
 contents of the notice; or
 d. Where certified mail, return receipt method is used (as set forth above in
 subparagraph a.) and said notice is returned as unclaimed or refused, notice may be served by
 publication or posting as follows:
 i. The notice shall be published once during each week for four consecutive
 weeks in a newspaper of general circulation in Broward County; or
 ii. In lieu of publication as set forth above, the notice may be posted at least
 fourteen days prior to any hearing to be held on the violation in at least two locations, one of
 which shall be on the property upon which the violation is alleged to exist, and the other which
 shall be at City Hall.
 For a posted notice, there shall be a proof of posting by affidavit by the person posting the
 notice, which affidavit shall include a copy of the notice posted and the date and places of its
 posting.
 e. In a case of commercial premises, leaving the notice with the manager or other
 person in charge.
 Section 5. All violation notices shall state the nature of the violation, the date it
 occurred, and shall provide the date, time and location of a hearing before the Special Master at
 which the Special Master shall make a determination as to whether a violation exists. The notice
 shall also state that the Respondent shall have the right at the hearing to provide all defenses and
 relevant evidence related to the charge.
 Section 6. All hearings shall be scheduled by the Code Enforcement Inspector in
 conjunction with the Special Master. Each case before the Special Master related to water
 restrictions shall be presented by the Code Inspector or an attorney selected by the City for the
 purposes of presenting the case. If the City prevails in prosecuting a case before the Special
 Master, it shall be entitled to recover all costs incurred in prosecuting the case. Such costs may be
 included in the lien provided pursuant to the procedures set forth herein.
 Section 7. A Special Master shall hear the cases set on the agenda for the particular
 date. All testimony shall be under oath and shall be recorded by electronic device or stenographer.
 The Special Master shall take testimony from the Code Inspector, the alleged violator, and any
 persons they deem to possess relevant evidence. The formal rules of evidence shall not apply, but

 fundamental due process shall be observed and shall govern the proceedings. At the conclusion of
 the hearing, the Special Master shall make a determination as to whether a violation exists, and if
 so, the amount of the fine. The findings shall be reduced to a written order. If the fine is not paid
 within ninety (90) day of the written order, the City may record a copy of the order in the public
 records, the recordation of which shall constitute a lien on the Respondent's property. The City
 may attach an affidavit to the order, as part of a Notice of Code Lien, which shall include the
 amount of costs incurred in the prosecution (including overtime costs), together with the amount
 of the fine; the Notice of Code Lien shall include the costs as set forth in the affidavit.
 Section 8. It is declared to be the intent of City Commission of the City of Parkland,
 Florida, that if any section, subsection, sentence, clause, or provision of this Ordinance shall be
 declared invalid, the remainder of this Ordinance shall be construed as not having contained said
 section, subsection, sentence, clause, or provisions and shall not be affected by such holding.
 Section 9. It is the intent of the City Commission that the provisions of this Ordinance
 shall become and be made a part of the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances, and that the sections
 of this Ordinance may be renumbered or re-lettered and the word "ordinance" may be changed to
 "section," "article," "regulation," or such other appropriate word or phrase in order to accomplish
 such intentions.
 Section 10. That this Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon its adoption
 on second reading.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED on first reading on March 5 , 2008.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED on second reading on April 2 , 2008.
