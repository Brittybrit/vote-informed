 33 Sec. 11-86. Definitions.
 34
 35 ***
 36 Water shortage: When insufficient water is available to meet the requirements of
 37 the South Florida Water Management District permit system; or when conditions are
 38 such as to require temporary reduction in total use within the area to protect water
 39 resources from serious harm; or when it is determined that a public water system has
 40 become seriously affected by lack of plant capacity to treat water demanded, saltwater
 41 intrusion, lack of water pressure due to mechanical failure or lack of supply; or where
 42 the SFWMD declares an emergency related to a water shortage; or where sufficient
 43 water is not available to meet present or anticipated needs for persons using the water
 44 resource; or any other manifestation of insufficient water availability.
