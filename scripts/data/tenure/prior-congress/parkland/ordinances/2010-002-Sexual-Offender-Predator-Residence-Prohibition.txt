 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 4 FLORIDA, AMENDING SECTION 11-39 C (5) OF THE CITY CODE
 5 REGARDING SEXUAL OFFENDER AND SEXUAL PREDATOR RESIDENCE
 6 PROHIBITION; PENALTIES; EXCEPTIONS; PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN
 7 THE CITY CODE; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING FOR AN
 8 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland wishes to amend Section 11-39
 11 C (5) of the City Code entitled "Sexual Offender and Sexual Predator Residence Prohibition;
 12 Penalties; Exceptions";
 13
 14 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 15 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 16
 17 SECTION 1. That Section 11-39 C (5) is hereby amended to read as follows:
 18
 19 Section C. Sexual Offender and Sexual Predator Residence Prohibition;
 20 Penalties; Exceptions.
 21
 22 (1) It is unlawful for any person who has been convicted of a violation of
 23 §§794.011, 800.04, 827.071, or 847.0145, Florida Statutes, or a similar crime
 24 in another jurisdiction, regardless of whether adjudication has been withheld,
 25 in which the victim of the offense was less than 16 years of age, to establish a
 26 permanent residence or temporary residence within 2500 feet of any school,
 27 designated public school bus stop, day care center, park, public playground
 28 or the multi-purpose trail system, as depicted on Exhibit A.
 29
 30 (2) For purposes of determining the minimum distance separation, the
 31 requirement shall be measured by following a straight line from the outer
 32 property line of the permanent residence or temporary residence to nearest
 33 outer property line of a school, designated public school bus stop, day care
 34 center, park, public playground or the multi-purpose trail system, as depicted
 35 on Exhibit A.
 36
 37 (3) A person who violates this section shall be punished by a fine not exceeding
 38 $500.00 or by imprisonment for a term not exceeding 60 days, or by both
 39 such fine and imprisonment; for a second or subsequent conviction of a
 40 violation of this section, such person shall be punished by a fine not to exceed
 41 $1,000.00 or imprisonment in the county jail not more than 12 months, or by
 42 both such fine and imprisonment. Each day that the offense occurs shall be
 43 deemed a separate violation. A person found to be in violation of this section
 44 shall be subject to arrest or issued a Notice to Appear and shall appear at the
 45 prearranged court date to answer the charge. Once notified of the residency
 46 restriction, the person shall have two (2) weeks to vacate the premises. A
 47 person who fails to vacate the premises within two (2) weeks after receiving
 48 notice of the residency restriction shall be subject to arrest.

 1 (4) As a supplement or alternative to the enforcement mechanism set forth
 2 above, the City may seek an injunction in Broward County Circuit Court to
 3 enforce this law.

 5 (5) Exceptions. A person residing within 2500 feet of any school, designated
 6 public school bus stop, day care center, park, public playground or the multi-
 7 purpose trail system, as depicted on Exhibit A, does not commit a violation of
 8 this section if any of the following apply:
 9
 10 (i) The person established the permanent residence prior to July 1,
 11 2005 and committed the offense prior to that time.
 12
 13 (ii) The person was a minor when he/she committed the offense and
 14 was not convicted as an adult.
 15
 16 (iii) The person is a minor.
 17
 18 (iv) The school, designated public school bus stop or day care center
 19 park, public playground or portion of the multi-purpose trail within
 20 2500 feet of the person's permanent residence was opened after
 21 the person established the permanent residence.
 22
 23 SECTION 2. All ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict herewith be and the same
 24 are hereby repealed.
 25
 26 SECTION 3. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 27 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of the
 28 City of Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to accomplish
 29 such intent.
 30
 31 SECTION 4. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for
 32 any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of
 33 any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 34
 35 SECTION 5. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 36 passage and adoption.
