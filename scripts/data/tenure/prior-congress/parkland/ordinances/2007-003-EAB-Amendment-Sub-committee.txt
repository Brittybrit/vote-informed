 ORDINANCE NO. 2007- 03
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AMENDING
 ORDINANCE NO. 2006-20, RELATING TO THE
 EDUCATIONAL ADVISORY BOARD.
 WHEREAS, the education of the children of Parkland is of prime importance to the City
 Commission; and
 WHEREAS, as a result of said belief the City established an Educational Advisory
 Board(EAB); and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that student safety should be an essential
 element of the work of the EAB;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. Section 1 of Ordinance No. 2006-20 is hereby amended as follows:
 Section 1. The City Commission hereby establishes a Parkland
 Educational Advisory Board. The Board shall consist of five (5) regular members
 and two (2) at large members. All members of the board shall be residents of the
 City of Parkland. Each member shall serve a term co-extensive with the
 Commissioner appointing that member; the two (2) at large members shall serve
 two (2) year terms. There shall be two (2) alternates serving two (2) year terms.
 The Board shall also sit as a Special Safety Committee to consider matters
 relating to student safety and make recommendations directly to the full Board on
 said matters. The City Commission shall also appoint three (3) members who are
 not on the Board to serve on the Special Safety Committee; these three (3)
 members shall either live or work in the City.
 Section 2. The board shall meet as regularly as is deemed necessary; provided that the
 Special Safety Committee shall meet at least twice a year. The City Manager shall provide
 appropriate staff support as deemed appropriate in his discretion. The board shall operate
 pursuant to Robert's Rules of Order unless it adopts other specific rules of procedure. A
 majority of the members of the board shall constitute a quorum. The board shall appoint a

<<PG>>
 chairman and co-chairman from among their membership.
 Section 3. This amendment shall be valid for a two (2) year period unless extended
 by the City Commission by resolution; if no extending resolution is adopted the provisions of
 this amendment shall lapse and be of no further force and effect.
 Section 4. The board shall make recommendations to the City Commission with regard
 to matters relating to education in the City of Parkland, make recommendations to improve
 communications between schools and school populations, to recommend projects for improving
 school facilities, to discuss, advise and, if they desire to, make recommendations on public
 school boundaries and to advise the City Commission on health, safety, transportation and
 quality of life issues that affect students.
 PASSED ON FIRST READING ON THIS 17 DAY OF January , 2007.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED ON SECOND READING THIS 91 DAY OF
 February , 2007.
