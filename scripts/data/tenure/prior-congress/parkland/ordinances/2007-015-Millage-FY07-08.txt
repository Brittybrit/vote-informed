 ORDINANCE NO. 2007-15
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE
 MILLAGE RATE TO BE LEVIED FOR THE FISCAL YEAR
 BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2007 AND ENDING
 SEPTEMBER 30, 2008 AND STATING THE PERCENTAGE
 INCREASE IN PROPERTY TAXES AS SUCH INCREASE IS
 DEFINED IN FLORIDA STATUTES, SECTION 200.065, AND
 HOUSE BILL 1B, PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY, AND
 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has held all necessary
 public workshops and public hearings to consider and make changes to the annual budget
 submitted by the City Manager for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2008.
 WHEREAS, in compliance with House Bill 1B that was passed in the Legislature
 June 14, 2007 and signed into law on June 21, 2007 the City of Parkland is required to
 reduce its millage rate from roll-back by 5%.
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. That the millage rate to be levied in order to produce
 sufficient ad valorem taxation revenue which, when combined with other projected
 General Fund revenues, will be adequate to pay the appropriations made in the budget for
 the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2007 and ending September 30, 2008 shall be:
 Operating Millage Rate 3.4083
 Section 2. That the operating millage rate of 3.4083 mills will result in a 5%
 decrease in City property taxes. This decrease is the percent by which
