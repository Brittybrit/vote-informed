 ORDINANCE NO. 2008 - 15
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND,FLORIDA,AMENDING SECTION 4-53(1)OF THE CITY
 CODE RELATED TO REQUIRED LIGHTING; PROVIDING THAT
 ALL OUTDOOR LIGHTING INSTALLED SUBSEQUENT TO
 JANUARY 1, 2009 ON COMMERCIALLY ZONED PROPERTIES BE
 SOLAR POWERED; PROVIDING FOR IMPLEMENTATION;
 REQUIRING EXISTING LIGHTING TO COMPLY WITH THE
 SOLAR LIGHTING REQUIREMENT BY JUNE 18,2018;PROVIDING
 FOR WAIVERS;PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE;
 PROVIDING FOR A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE;PROVIDING FOR AN
 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS,the City Commission of the City of Parkland believes that as the governmental
 entity most directly involved with regular development in the City, it should take the lead in
 stewardship of the environment when approving development and when permitting development
 within the City; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that requiring solar powered pole lighting for
 outdoor commercial lighting will enhance and preserve the environment, reduce the demands on
 other resources, and contribute to the public welfare; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission has determined that it will require solar lighting for
 outdoor commercial lighting for all new development and, to provide an appropriate Aphase in®
 period, will require existing commercial development to comply with this requirement within ten
 years from the date of this ordinance;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 SECTION 1. Section 4-53(1) relating to required lighting is hereby amended to read as
 follows:
 (1) Required lighting. No light source shall illuminate the awning of any building nor
 shall the use of flood lights be permitted.
 (1) Pole lights shall be required to illuminate all parking lots located in the rear of
 buildings to an average of one(1)footcandle measured at the surface of the pavement on all property
 that has not received site plan approval on or before August 10, 2005.
 (2) Wall mounted lights shall be required on all structures which have received

 site plan approval prior to August 10,2005 and which had no exterior parking lot lighting. The wall
 mounted lights shall illuminate rear entrances and the adjacent areas to a minimum of one (1)
 footcandle measured one (1) foot above grade. Wall mounted lighting at rear entrances and the
 adjacent area for these facilities must be in place on or before December 1, 2005.
 (3) All outdoor pale lighting installed on commercially zoned property,pursuant
 to a building permit issued subsequent to January 1, 2009, except for pole lighting in a
 development constructed pursuant to a site plan or master plan approved and active as of July
 2, 2008 shall be 100% solar powered lighting. In all cases where light fixtures on commercially
 zoned property are replaced subsequent to January 1,2009,the replacement lighting shall be 100%
 solar powered lighting. In all cases where the design of pole lighting fixtures in a commercial
 zoning district is altered, or where a pole light fixture is replaced subsequent to January 1,2009,
 said lighting fixtures shall be subject to community appearance approval in accordance with and said
 lighting shall be required to comply with this section and shall be 100% solar powered. All pole
 lighting on commercially zoned property shall be required to be 100%solar powered by June
 18,2018. Where,because of restrictions set forth in the Florida Building Code,or other provisions
 of the City of Parkland Land Development Regulations,or other circumstances beyond the owners
 control,it would be an undue hardship to install 100%solar powered lighting,the City Commission
 may waive the requirement of this section in whole or in part upon application to the community
 development department,and after a review and recommendation by the planning and zoning board.
 01 Routine lighting fixture maintenance, such as changing lamps or light
 bulbs, ballast, starter, photo control, housing, lenses and other similar components shall not
 constitute replacement and shall be permitted provided such changes do not result in a higher
 lumen output or structural alteration of outdoor pole light fixture.
 Other illumination standards and provisions,including but not limited to
 internally illuminated signs,flag poles,statues,monuments,landscape,security lighting,and
 other exterior lighting which are not a part of this ordinance may be included in other
 ordinances such as the sign ordinance.
