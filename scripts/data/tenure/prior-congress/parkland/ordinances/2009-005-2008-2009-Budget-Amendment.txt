 15 WHEREAS, on September 24, 2008, the City of Parkland adopted a final budget for the
 16 Fiscal Year beginning October 1, 2008, and ending September 30, 2009; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 166.241, Florida Statutes, the City of Parkland may, by
 19 ordinance, amend its budget and wishes to do so to reflect additional revenues and
 20 appropriations;
 21
 22
 23 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 24 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 25
 26 Section 1. That Ordinance 2008-20, as amended, which Ordinance did adopt the
 27 budget of the City of Parkland for the 2008/09 fiscal year, and the budget adopted
 28 thereby be and the same is hereby amended to cause and reflect the increase of a net
 29 total of $522,959 of revenues and appropriations to said Operating Budgets as set
 30 forth on the attached Exhibit A.
 31
 32 Section 2. That all Ordinances or parts of Ordinances, Resolutions or parts of
 33 Resolutions in conflict herewith be and the same are hereby repealed to
 34 the extent of such conflict.
 35
 36 Section 3. Should any section or provision of this Ordinance or any portion
 37 thereof, any paragraph, sentence or word be declared by a Court of
 38 competent jurisdiction to be invalid such decision shall not affect the
 39 validity of the remainder of this Ordinance.
