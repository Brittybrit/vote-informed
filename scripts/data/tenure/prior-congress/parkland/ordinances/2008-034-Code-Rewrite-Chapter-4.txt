 14 Chapter 4 BUILDINGS AND BUILDING REGULATIONS*
 15
 16 Sec. 4-1. Adoption of the Florida Building Code.
 17 (a) The Florida Building Code, as amended, as adopted by the county
 18 commission be and the same is herewith adopted as the official building code of
 19 the city.
 20
 21 (b) References to the South Florida Building Code in the Code of Ordinances
 22 of the City of Parkland shall mean the Florida Building Code, inclusive of Chapter
 23 1, the Broward County Administrative Provisions, as amended from time to time.
 24 The Florida Building Code together with all local amendments thereto is hereby
 25 adopted by reference.
 26
 27 Sec. 4-2. Fees.
 28 The building permit fees shall be as adopted and amended by the commission from
 29 time to time which shall be on file in the clerk's office.
 30
 31 Sec. 4-3. Permit.
 32 Required before construction. The owner or general contractor, if the owner is not his
 33 own builder, shall first obtain his building permit and pay the fee called for in section 4-2
 34 before commencing any construction, alteration, repair or any other activity regulated by
 35 this chapter or the Florida Building Code, of any building or other improvement on the
 36 land for which a building permit and fee is required.
 37
 38 Sec. 4-6. Potable water source and septic tank setbacks.
 39 (a) Any person installing a potable water well must first obtain a permit to do so
 40 from the Broward County Health Department. Any potable water well must be set
 41 back from the owner's property line at least fifty (50) feet.
 42
 43 (b) Any person desiring to install a septic tank within the city limits must first
 44 obtain a permit to do so from the Broward County Health Department. Any septic
 45 tank must be set back fifty (50) feet from the owner's property line.
