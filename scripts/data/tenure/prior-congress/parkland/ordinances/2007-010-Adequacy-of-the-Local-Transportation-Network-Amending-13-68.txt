 35 (6) Adequacy of local roads.
 36
 37 a. The applicant shall demonstrate that the local streets and roads which
 38 will serve the development will provide safe and adequate access
 39 between the regional transportation network and the proposed
 40 development and shall have the capacity to serve the proposed
 41 development at the level of service established by the city.
 42
 43 b. The capacity of the local street and road network shall be determined
 44 based upon sound standards and principles of traffic engineering
 45 considering to the extent available:

 38 the improvements that would otherwise be required in paragraph
 39 d.1 . above.

 42 section shall be maintained by the city and shall be subject to the

 45 necessary for the construction of the necessary improvements.

 14 h. Proportionate Fair-share Mitigation Program
 15
 16 1. In order to establish a method whereby the impacts of
 17 development on transportation facilities can be mitigated by the
 18 cooperative efforts of the public and private sectors, there shall be
 19 a program known as the Proportionate Fair-Share Mitigation
 20 Program (the "Proportionate Fair-share Mitigation Program"), as
 21 required by and in a manner consistent with §163.3180(16), Florida
 22 Statute. The Fair-Share Mitigation Program shall apply to all
 23 developments in the City that have been notified of a lack of
 24 capacity to satisfy City transportation concurrency. The
 25 Proportionate Fair-Share Program does not apply to developments
 26 of regional impact (DRIs) using proportionate fair-share under
 27 §163.3180(12), Florida Statute.
 28
 29 2. Determining Proportionate Fair-Share Obligation.
 30
 31 (a) Proportionate fair-share mitigation for concurrency
 32 impacts may include, without limitation, separately or
 33 collectively, private funds, contributions of land, and
 34 construction and contribution of facilities.
 35 (b) A development shall not be required to pay more than its
 36 proportionate fair-share. The fair market value of the
 37 proportionate fair-share mitigation for the impacted facilities
 38 shall not differ regardless of the method of mitigation.
 39 (c) The methodology used to calculate an applicant's
 40 proportionate fair-share obligation shall be as provided for in
 41 Section 163.3180 (12), Florida Statute, as follows:
 42 "The cumulative number of trips from the proposed
 43 development expected to reach roadways during peak hours
 44 from the complete build out of a stage or phase being

 1 approved, divided by the change in the peak hour maximum
 2 service volume (MSV) of roadways resulting from
 3 construction of an improvement necessary to maintain the
 4 adopted level of service (LOS), multiplied by the construction
 5 cost, at the time of developer payment, of the improvement
 6 necessary to maintain the adopted LOS."
 7 (d) For the purposes of determining proportionate fair-share
 8 obligations, the cost shall include all improvements and
 9 associated costs, such as design, rights-of-way acquisition,
 10 planning, engineering, inspection, and physical development
 11 costs directly associated with construction at the anticipated
 12 cost in the year it will be incurred. The cost shall be based
 13 upon the cost estimate contained in the CIE as determined
 14 by the Public Services Department. Where such information
 15 is not available, improvement cost shall be determined using
 16 the following method:
 17 1. If the City has accepted an improvement project
 18 proposed by the applicant, then the value of the
 19 improvement shall be based on an engineer's certified
 20 cost estimate provided by the applicant and approved
 21 by the Planning Director.
 22 (e) If the City has accepted right-of-way dedication for the
 23 proportionate fair-share payment, credit for the dedication of
 24 the non-site related right-of-way shall be valued on the date
 25 of the dedication at 120 percent of the most recent assessed
 26 value by the County property appraiser or, at the option of
 27 the applicant, by fair market value established by an
 28 independent appraisal approved by the City and at no
 29 expense to the City. The applicant shall supply a drawing
 30 (both hard copy and electronic version) and legal description
 31 of the land and a certificate of title or title search of the land
 32 to the City at no expense to the City. If the estimated value
 33 of the right-of-way dedication proposed by the applicant is
 34 less than the City estimated total proportionate fair-share
 35 obligation for that development, then the applicant must also
 36 pay the difference. Prior to purchase or acquisition of any
 37 real estate or acceptance of donations of real estate
 38 intended to be used for the proportionate fair-share, public or
 39 private partners should contact the FDOT for essential
 40 information about compliance with federal law and
 41 regulations.

 1 (3) Proportionate Fair-Share Agreements ( "Agreements").
 2
 3 (a) No proportionate fair share agreement will be effective
 4 until approved by the City Commission. The City
 5 Commission shall approve proportionate fair share
 6 agreements if it is in the best interest of the public safety,
 7 health and welfare of the City of Parkland.
 8
 9 (b) Upon the applicant's execution of a proportionate fair-
 10 share agreement, project approval and issuance of the first
 11 development order, the applicant shall receive a Certificate
 12 of Concurrency. Should the applicant fail to apply for a
 13 building permit within 18 months of the execution of the
 14 Agreement, then the fair-share agreement shall be
 15 considered null and void, and the applicant shall be required
 16 to reapply.
 17
 18 (c) Payment of the proportionate fair-share contribution is
 19 due in full prior to issuance of the final development order or
 20 recording of the final plat and shall be nonrefundable unless
 21 a development order is abandoned following approval of an
 22 applicant's formal request to rescind approval of the
 23 applicant's preliminary or final development order, or the
 24 application expires, and the funds paid for the proportionate
 25 fair share contribution, including any impact fee credits, have
 26 not been used for their intended purpose, and no
 27 development has occurred on the applicant's proposed
 28 development site.
 29
 30 (d) All developer improvements authorized under this
 31 ordinance must be completed prior to issuance of the first
 32 development permit, or as otherwise established in a binding
 33 agreement that is accompanied by a security instrument that
 34 is sufficient to ensure the completion of all required
 35 improvements. Any required improvements shall be
 36 completed before issuance of building permits or certificates
 37 of occupancy.
 38
 39 (e) Dedication of necessary right-of-way for facility
 40 improvements pursuant to a proportionate fair-share
 41 agreement must be completed prior to issuance of the first
 42 development order or recording of the final plat.
 43
 44 (f) Any requested change to a development project
 45 subsequent to a development order may be subject to
 46 additional proportionate fair-share contributions to the extent

 4 (5) Appropriation of Fair-Share Revenues.
 5 (a) Proportionate fair-share revenues shall be placed in the
 6 appropriate project account for funding of scheduled
 7 improvements in the City's CIE, or as otherwise established
 8 in the terms of the proportionate fair-share agreement. At the
 9 discretion of the City, proportionate fair-share revenues may
 10 be used for operational improvements prior to construction of
 11 the capacity project from which the proportionate fair-share
 12 revenues were derived.
 13 (b) In the event a scheduled facility improvement is removed
 14 from the CIE, then the revenues collected for its construction
 15 may be applied toward the construction of another
 16 improvement within that same roadway that would mitigate
 17 the impacts of development pursuant to the following
 18 requirements:
 19 1. If the funds allocated for the five-year
 20 schedule of capital improvements in the City's
 21 CIE are insufficient to fully fund construction of
 22 a transportation improvement required by the
 23 CMS, the City may still enter into a binding
 24 proportionate fair-share agreement with the
 25 applicant authorizing construction of that
 26 amount of development on which the
 27 proportionate fair-share is calculated if the
 28 proportionate fair-share amount in such
 29 agreement is sufficient to pay for one or more
 30 improvements which will, in the opinion of the
 31 governmental entity or entities maintaining the
 32 transportation facilities, significantly benefit the
 33 impacted transportation system. The
 34 improvement or improvements funded by the
 35 proportionate fair-share component must be
 36 adopted into the five-year capital
 37 improvements schedule of the comprehensive
 38 plan at the next annual capital improvements
 39 element update.
