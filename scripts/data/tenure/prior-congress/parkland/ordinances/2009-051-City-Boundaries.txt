 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SETTING
 4 FORTH THE REVISED BOUNDARIES OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND
 5 MUNICIPAL CITY LIMITS.
 6
 7 WHEREAS, over the past several years, the municipal boundaries of the City of Parkland
 8 have been substantially revised based upon lawful annexation of lands into the municipal
 9 boundaries of the City of Parkland; and
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City wishes to have on record, in ordinance form, a complete description
 12 of the boundaries of the City of Parkland; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the description as set forth on the attached Exhibit A reflects the complete
 15 boundary of the municipal and corporate limits of the City of Parkland;
 16
 17 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 18 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 19
 20 SECTION 1. The above representations are true and correct and made a part hereof.
 21
 22 SECTION 2. The City Commission specifically finds that the legal description attached
 23 on Exhibit A does set forth the lawful and legally binding description of the municipal and
 24 corporate limits and boundaries of the City of Parkland. All lands lying within said legal
 25 description are within the City of Parkland and governed by the laws of the City of Parkland.
 26
 27 SECTION 3.The City of Parkland does hereby adopt the attached legal description as
 28 the legal description of the corporate limits and boundaries of the City of Parkland, Florida. All
 29 other ordinances and resolutions setting forth the boundaries of the City are amended
 30 accordingly.
