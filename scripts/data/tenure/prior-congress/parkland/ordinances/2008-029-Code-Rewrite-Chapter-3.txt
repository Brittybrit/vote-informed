 1 (1) For the purposes of this section, "vicious dog" shall be defined as any
 2 dog known to have, unwarrantly and without provocation, aggressively
 3 bitten or attacked and to have caused severe injury to a person or
 4 domestic animal on public property or to a person or domestic animal or
 5 agricultural animal lawfully on private property.
 6 (2) A "proper enclosure" means that the dog is securely confined indoors
 7 or in a securely enclosed and locked pen or structure suitable, or fenced
 8 or walled area, to prevent the dog from escaping, as well as to prevent the
 9 entry of young children.
 io (3) For the purpose of this subsection, a person is lawfully upon private
 11 property when he is on such property in the performance of any duty
 12 imposed upon him by the laws of this state or by the laws of the postal
 13 regulations of the United States or when he is on such property upon
 14 invitation, expressed or implied, of the owner thereof.
 15 (b) Restrictions.
 16 (1) It shall be unlawful for the owner of a vicious dog to permit the dog to
 17 be outside a proper enclosure, unless the dog is muzzled to the extent
 18 that the dog is not harmed or rendered unable to breathe or see, but is
 19 prevented from biting another person or domestic or agricultural animal,
 20 and restrained on a chain or leash not exceeding six (6) feet in length and
 21 under the control of a competent person.
 22 (2) It shall be unlawful to maintain or keep any dog within the city limits
 23 which has twice bitten or attacked any person, domestic animal, or
 24 agricultural animal where such attack occurred off the property of the
 25 owner or person with custody thereof. Upon receipt of the second dog bite
 26 report by the city involving the same dog, the dog shall be declared a
 27 public nuisance and the code enforcement officer shall immediately
 28 commence proceedings against the owner of the dog, seeking an order
 29 from the code enforcement beard special magistrate to have the dog
 30 permanently removed from the city limits; in the alternative, the city may
 31 initiate a municipal prosecution in the county court for any violation of this
 32 section. The city may also seek injunctive relief in the circuit court to
 33 secure removal of the dog.
 34 (3) This section does not apply to dogs used by law enforcement officials
 35 for law enforcement work.
 36 (c) Penalty. Any violation of this section shall be punishable by a five-hundred-
 37 dollar fine. Each incident or day the violation occurs shall be deemed a separate
 38 violation.
