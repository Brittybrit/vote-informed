 35 Sec. 20-3. General Parking Restrictions.
 36
 37 (a) Parking restrictions on designated public rights-of-way. There shall be no
 38 parking of motor vehicles, trailers, boats, or water craft of any kind in the dedicated public right-
 39 of-way areas within the limits of the city (as set forth below), except in those right-of-way areas
 40 which may be specifically reserved and marked by signage for parking purposes. This prohibition
 41 shall apply seven(7) days a week, and shall apply to:
 42
 43 (1) All of the following roadways: University Drive, Holmberg Road, Hillsboro
 44 Boulevard, Parkside Drive, Riverside Drive, Pine Island Road and Trails End;
 45 (2) Any public streets located within or contiguous to multifamily zoned properties; or

 2 (3) Any public street or portion thereof where the city commission finds that a
 3 temporary or permanent restriction on parking is necessary to protect the
 4 safety of the public, as evidenced by a resolution and where a "No Parking"
 5 sign is erected by the City.
 6
 7 (b) Motor vehicle. Any self-propelled vehicle not operated upon rails or gridway,
 8 including but not limited to automobiles, trucks, motorized scooters, motorcycles, mopeds, golf
 9 carts and electric personal mobility devices.
 10
 11 (c) Park or parking. The standing of a motor vehicle, whether occupied or not, other
 12 than temporarily for the purposes of and while actually engaged in loading or unloading of
 13 passengers or objects.
 14
 15 (d) Parking restrictions on unimproved private property. It shall be unlawful for any
 16 person, natural or corporate, to park any motor vehicle upon any unimproved private property
 17 unless that person shall have the consent of the legal owner of said property. In any prosecution
 18 or trial of any person charged with violating this section, there shall be a rebuttable presumption
 19 that the alleged violator did not have the consent of the property owner in engaging in the
 20 prohibited conduct.
 21
 22 (e) Except as otherwise provided in this section, every motor vehicle stopped or
 23 parked upon a two-way roadway shall be so stopped or parked with the right-hand wheels parallel
 24 to and within twelve (12)inches of the right-hand curb or edge of the roadway.
 25
 26 (f) Every motor vehicle stopped or parked upon a one-way roadway shall be so
 27 stopped or parked parallel to the curb or edge of the roadway, in the direction of authorized
 28 traffic movement, with its right-hand wheels within twelve (12) inches of the right-hand curb or
 29 edge of the roadway, or its left wheels within twelve (12) inches of the left-hand curb or edge of
 30 the roadway.
 31
 32 (g) It is unlawful to park a motor vehicle on any roadway, parking lot or right-of-way
 33 in such a way as to interfere with the free flow of traffic.
 34
 35 (h) Whenever a motor vehicle is disabled on any street or highway within the city or
 36 for any reason obstructs the regular flow of traffic, the driver shall move the vehicle so as not to
 37 obstruct the regular flow of traffic or, if the individual cannot move the motor vehicle alone,
 38 solicit help and move the motor vehicle so as not to obstruct the regular flow of traffic.
 39
 40 (i) It is unlawful to park a motor vehicle, which, due to its size, shape, contents or
 41 location, creates an obstruction or public safety hazard, or which cannot be contained within a
 42 single designated parking space. The above notwithstanding, this shall not preclude the parking
 43 of commercial vehicles within commercial business districts if parked in compliance with all other
 44 provisions of the City Code so long as there is no obstruction of the flow of traffic or no public
 45 safety hazard.

 26 Sec. 20-5. Enforcement and penalties.
 27
 28 (1) Violations: liability for payment of parking ticket violations. The owner of a motor
 29 vehicle cited for violation of this chapter shall be responsible and liable for payment of any parking
 30 tickets unless the owner can furnish evidence that the vehicle was, at the time of the parking
 31 violation, in the care, custody, or control of another person. In such instances the owner of the
 32 motor vehicle is required, within a reasonable time after notification of the parking violation to
 33 furnish to the appropriate law enforcement authorities the name and address of the person or
 34 company who leased, rented, or otherwise had the care, custody, or control of the motor vehicle.
 35 The owner of a motor vehicle is not responsible for parking ticket violations if the vehicle was, at
 36 the time, stolen or in the care, custody, or control of some person who did not have permission of
 37 the owner to use the vehicle.
 38
 39 (2) Ticketing procedure. Motor vehicles parking in violation of this chapter shall be ticketed
 40 by any duly authorized law enforcement officer, said ticket to be affixed to the offending vehicle.
 41
 42 (3) Refusal to accept and sign citation or "notice to appear." Any person who willfully
 43 refuses to accept and sign a citation or "notice to appear" issued pursuant to this section shall be
 44 guilty of a separate violation and that person may be arrested by any duly authorized law
 45 enforcement officer.
