 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AMENDING THE FUTURE LAND USE MAP OF
 5 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, AS DESIGNATED IN EXHIBIT "A"
 6 CONSISTING OF 80.42 ACRES FROM RESIDENTIAL 1 DU/1 AC ("R-
 7 1") TO RESIDENTIAL IRREGULAR 2 DU/1 AC ("IR 2"); CONTAINING
 8 A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; CONTAINING AN EFFECTIVE DATE;
 9 CASE 02-LUPA-09.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has received an
 12 application from Richard G. Coker, Jr., Esquire of Coker & Feiner on behalf of the
 13 property owner, for a land use amendment to change the current land use from City of
 14 Parkland Residential 1 DU / 1 AC ("R-1.0") to City of Parkland Residential Irregular 2 for
 15 approximately 80.42 acres for the parcels generally located east of University Drive,
 16 west of NW 84th Avenue and North of NW 72nd Street; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland finds it in the best
 19 interest of the City to amend the Future Land Use Map City of Parkland Residential 1
 20 DU / 1 AC ("R-1.0") to City of Parkland Residential Irregular 2 for approximately 80.42
 21 acres for the parcels generally located east of University Drive, west of NW 84th Avenue
 22 and North of NW 72nd Street as designated in Exhibit A; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the applicant and City have negotiated an Annexation Agreement on
 25 (collectively "The Agreement") which includes the subject
 26 property; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Board, sitting as the Local Planning
 29 Agency, after notice and public hearing, has considered the appropriateness of the
 30 proposed future land-use designation for the property which is the subject of this
 31 Ordinance on May 3, 2010, and has presented its recommendation to the City
 32 Commission; and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, the City Commission, after notice and public hearing, voted to
 35 transmit this proposed amendment to the Florida Department of Community Affairs for
 36 written comment on May 5, 2010 and has otherwise complied with applicable provisions
 37 of the Florida Statutes governing amendments of local comprehensive plans; and
 38
 39 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements for Comprehensive Plan
 40 amendments, as set forth in the Florida Statues, have been satisfied; and
 41
 42 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the recommendations of the
 43 Local Planning Agency, and has also considered the appropriateness of the proposed
 44 future land-use designation for the property that is the subject of this Ordinance; and

 1 WHEREAS, The Agreement recorded on which contain
 2 commitments relating to infrastructure and other matters and has further voluntarily
 3 agreed to a restrictive covenant relative to maximum potential development and other
 4 matters as set forth in the staff report; and
 5
 6 WHEREAS, based upon the above and the conditions set forth in The
 7 Agreement, the City Commission believes that the proposed Amendment is consistent
 8 with the Goals, Objectives and Polices of the Comprehensive Plan;
 9
 10 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 11 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 12
 13 SECTION 1. The area identified in Exhibit A consisting of 80.42 acres shall be
 14 amended from Parkland Residential 1 DU / 1 AC ("R-1.0") to City of Parkland
 15 Residential Irregular 2.
 16
 17 SECTION 2. The City Manager is hereby authorized and directed to make the
 18 appropriate amendments to the Future Land Use Map of the City of Parkland
 19 Comprehensive Plan, in accordance with this Ordinance.
 20
 21 SECTION 3. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance
 22 is for any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the
 23 validity of any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 24
 25 SECTION 4. It is hereby ordained that the Future Land Use Map of the City of
 26 Parkland be amended and do hereby direct the Director of Planning and Zoning to
 27 amend the Future Land Use Map.
 28
 29 SECTION 5. All other ordinances in conflict herewith are repealed to the extent
 30 of the conflict.
 31
 32 SECTION 6. The effective date of this plan amendment shall be the date a final
 33 order is issued by the Department of Community Affairs or Administration Commission
 34 finding the amendment in compliance in accordance with Section 163.3184, Florida
 35 Statutes, whichever occurs earlier or thirty days from the date of adoption if no action is
 36 taken by the Department of Community Affairs and shall be subject to recertification by
 37 the Broward County Planning Council. No development orders, development permits, or
 38 land uses dependent on this amendment may be issued or commence before it has
 39 become effective. If a final order of noncompliance is issued by the Administration
 40 Commission, this amendment may nevertheless be made effective status, a copy of
 41 resolutions shall be sent to the Department of Community Affairs, Bureau of Local
 42 Planning, 2555 Shumard Oak Boulevard, Tallahassee, Florida 32399-2100.
 43
 44 PASSED AND ADOPTED ON FIRST READING THIS 5th OF May , 2010.
