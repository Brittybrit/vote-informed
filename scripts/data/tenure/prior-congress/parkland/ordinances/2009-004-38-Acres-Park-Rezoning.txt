 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, REZONING APPROXIMATELY 38 ACRES
 5 FOR THE PARCEL KNOWN AS 38 ACRE PARK, CASE NO. 04-Z-08,
 6 FROM RECREATION / OPEN SPACE (OS) ZONING DISTRICT TO
 7 CONSERVATION (CONS) ZONING DISTRICT; CONTAINING A
 8 SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; AND CONTAINING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 11 Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and
 12 the Code of Ordinances, as adopted by the City of Parkland, is authorized and
 13 empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and land development orders; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Board, after notice and public hearing, has
 16 considered the petition of the City of Parkland to zone certain property from Recreation /
 17 Open Space (OS) Zoning District to Conservation (CONS) Zoning District; and has
 18 presented its recommendation to the City Commission; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements have been satisfied; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Board of the City of Parkland has reviewed
 23 said petition on October 16, 2008 as required by Florida Statutes and has made
 24 recommendation for approval by a 7-0 vote of the members present; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony
 27 presented by the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of
 28 the various City of Parkland review agencies and staff; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, The City Commission has determined that the proposed rezoning is
 31 consistent with the City of Parkland Comprehensive Plan designation of Conservation;
 32 and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland finds it in the best
 35 interest of the City to rezone the property described in Exhibit A; and
 36
 37 NOW, THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 38 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 39
 40 Section 1. The zoning designation for the property attached on Exhibit "A" is
 41 herby changed from Recreation / Open Space (OS) Zoning District to Conservation
 42 (CONS) Zoning District.
 43
 44 Section 2. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence of this ordinance is for any
 45 reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity
 46 of any remaining portions of this ordinance.
