 ORDINANCE NO. 2008 - 08
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, RELATING TO A
 MANDATORY RECYCLING PROGRAM, CREATING
 SAID PROGRAM, PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN
 CHAPTER 7 OF THE CITY CODE OR AS OTHERWISE
 DETERMINED FOR THE PURPOSE OF CODIFICATION,
 IMPLEMENTING A PROGRAM OF MANDATORY
 RECYCLING IN THE CITY OF PARKLAND; PROVIDING
 FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE; PROVIDING FOR
 A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING FOR AN
 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland enacts this Ordinance pursuant to Article VIII of the
 Florida Constitution and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and pursuant to the authority as provided
 in the Charter of the City of Parkland; and
 WHEREAS, these regulations are also an exercise of the authority provided for, and in
 order to further the policies as set forth in Chapter 403, Florida Statutes; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that it is in the best interests of the citizens of
 Parkland and the citizens of Broward County that there be a definite policy providing for
 separation of recyclable materials in order to further a valid public purpose;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND,AS FOLLOWS:
 SECTION 1. A new Section 7-51 of the Code of Ordinances of the City of Parkland is
 hereby created as follows:
 Section 7-51. Mandatory Commercial Recycling.
 It is determined by the City of Parkland that it is appropriate to require that commercial
 establishments separate commercial material. The City adopts the findings of the Florida
 Legislature that the failure and inability to economically recover material and energy resources
 from solid waste results in the unnecessary waste and depletion of our natural resources and
 therefore warrants that the maximum resource recovery from solid waste and the maximum
 recycling and reuse of such resources must be considered a goal of the City, as well as the State
 of Florida. In order to further these purposes, the City hereby requires that all commercial

 establishments shall be required to separate recovered materials to make them available for
 recycling.
 (a) Definitions.
 For the purposes of this Ordinance, the definitions as set forth in Section 403.703 of the
 Florida Statutes shall apply.
 A commercial establishment shall mean any public or private place, building or
 enterprise devoted in whole or part to a commercial enterprise, whether for profit or not for
 profit, or any enterprise that is required or does have a city occupational license. This definition
 shall be deemed to exclude any residents or other operation otherwise excluded by state law.
 (b) Mandatory Recycling.
 All commercial establishments shall source separate recovered materials at the location
 where the recovered materials are generated and make said materials available for commercial
 haulers who are registered with the City, for the purpose of collecting and properly disposing of
 said recycled materials. The commercial establishment shall be permitted to choose a
 commercial hauler of its choice, so long as said hauler is registered with the City and properly
 complies with all state and city regulations.
 (c) Collection and Disposal.
 Recyclable materials collected pursuant to this Ordinance shall transport the collected
 recyclable materials to a licensed and permitted processing center designated by the hauler. The
 hauler shall take title to the recyclable materials upon collection from the commercial
 establishment and shall be responsible for the sale and disposal of such recyclable materials in a
 manner consistent with state law. The hauler shall insure that all recyclable materials are
 processed for future use to the extent possible and shall not be disposed of as solid waste.
 Corrugated cardboard shall be segregated from all other solid waste material by all
 commercial solid waste customers and must be flattened and placed into a recycling dumpster.
 Material shall be free of food, dirt, grease, tape, or liquid and other contaminants. Wax coated
 cardboard pizza boxes, shoe boxes, cereal boxes and cake mix boxes are acceptable and shall be
 placed into the same designated dumpster as corrugated cardboard. Recycling dumpsters for
 corrugated cardboard and cardboard boxes shall be supplied by the owner of the commercial
 establishment where the commercial operations are conducted.

 (d) Containers.
 All recyclable materials shall be placed in an appropriate industry standard container.
 Where garbage containers are used, they shall be placed at the location or at such other singular
 collection point as may be agreed on between the hauler and the customer and in compliance
 with all city codes and requirements. All containers shall be kept in a safe, accessible, clean
 location as designated or approved by the city and agreed to by the hauler and the customer. The
 city may impose reasonable requirements on the location where said containers are placed. If a
 hauler provides a recyclable materials container to its customers, the hauler will be responsible
 for the proper maintenance of the container. Customers that acquire their own containers from
 any other source are responsible for the proper maintenance of the container, except that damage
 done by the hauler shall be the responsibility of the hauler; and for ensuring that the container
 can be serviced by the registrant's equipment.
 (e) Proof of Participation.
 A commercial establishment, generator of recyclable materials or owner of a
 commercially-collected property shall produce proof of a valid and current contract with a
 registrant or receipts for delivery of recyclable materials to an approved site, upon request of the
 city manager or designee.
 SECTION 2. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of the
 City of Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to accomplish
 such intent.
 SECTION 3. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for
 any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of
 any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 SECTION 4. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effective (30) thirty days
 from its passage and adoption.
