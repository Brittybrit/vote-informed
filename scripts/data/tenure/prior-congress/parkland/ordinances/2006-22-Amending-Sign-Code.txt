 4 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 5 PARKLAND, FLORIDA RELATING TO SIGNAGE; AMENDING SECTION
 6 15-40 ENTITLED "ELECTION SIGNS AND FREE EXPRESSION SIGNS"
 7 OF THE CODE OF ORDINANCE; PROVIDING A CONFLICTS CLAUSE
 8 AND A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; AND PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE
 9 DATE.
 10
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland, Florida,
 13 pursuant to the authority vested in Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, is authorized and empowered to
 14 consider changes to its land development regulations; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the Florida
 17 Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, the Charter of the City of Parkland, and the police
 18 powers of the City; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 163, Florida Statutes, the
 21 City Planning and Zoning Board has reviewed the proposed ordinance and has determined
 22 that the proposed regulations are consistent with the City of Parkland Comprehensive Plan.
 23
 24 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 25 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 26
 27 SECTION 1. Section 15-40(A) of the Code of Ordinance is hereby amended and states
 28 as follows:
 29
 30 (a) Election signs. Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter to the contrary, election
 31 signs shall be allowed on private property in all zoning districts subject to the following
 32 limitations as to number, size (area), height, setback and duration:
 33
 34 (1) Number: For any parcel, there shall be a limit of one (1) election sign per candidate and
 35 per issue. No more than one (1) election sign per candidate and one (1) election sign
 36 per issue shall be permitted on any one (1) private lot or parcel property unless it is a
 37 corner lot, in which case two (2) signs per candidate and per issue may be placed, so
 38 long as there is no more than one (1) sign per street frontage and the signs are no
 39 closer than twenty-five (25) feet from the corner or intersection. No election sign shall be
 40 allowed to be located within a public or private right-of-way.
 41 (2) Size: The size (area) of an election sign shall be limited to six (6) square feet.
 42 (3) Height: The height of an election sign shall be limited to six (6) feet above the ground.
 43 (4) Setback: Election signs under three (3) square feet shall be setback a minimum
 44 distance of five (5) feet from the public or private right-of-way. Election signs over three
 45 (3) square feet shall be setback a minimum distance of ten (10) feet from the public or
 46 private right-of-way. The city shall not remove or interfere with the placement of political
 47 signs at a polling place, which otherwise complies with state law, on an election day,
 48 unless necessary for public safety.

 1 (5) Duration: Election signs may be placed on a parcel no earlier than sixty (60) days prior
 2 to an election and shall be removed within seven (7) calendar days following the
 3 election that pertains to the candidate or the issue that is the subject of the election
 4 sign. All signs shall be discarded in a proper manner so as to prevent litter and trash
 5 from accumulating within the city.
 6 (6) The prohibition contained in this subsection shall in no way apply to election
 7 announcement signs posted by the city, announcing each election to be held in the city.
 8 (7) Election signs shall not be placed upon property without the permission of the owner of
 9 the property or other person in legal control of the property.
 10 (8) It is unlawful to attach election signs to rooftops, trees, shrubs, fences, walls, or utility
 11 or similar poles.
 12
 13 SECTION 2: All ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby
 14 repealed.
 15
 16 SECTION 3: Should any section or provision of this Ordinance or any portion thereof,
 17 any paragraph, sentence or word be declared by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid,
 18 such decision shall not affect the validity of the remainder hereof as a whole or any part to be
 19 declared invalid.
 20
 21 SECTION 4. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its adoption.
