 9 WHEREAS, the Code of Ordinances contains several provisions that
 10 authorize the imposition of administrative and/or user fees charged to individuals
 11 who are provided with City of Parkland services; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, approval of administrative and/or user fees charged to
 14 individuals who are provided with City services are accomplished through various
 15 methods including ordinances, resolutions and motions; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, fire review and inspection fees are contained with the City's
 18 Code of Ordinances so an Ordinance is required to modify the fees; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the proposed fee modifications are necessary to cover the
 21 City's costs in undertaking fire reviews and inspections;
 22
 23 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 24 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
