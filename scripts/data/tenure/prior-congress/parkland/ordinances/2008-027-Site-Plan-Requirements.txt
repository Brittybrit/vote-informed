 1 ORDINANCE NO. 2008-27
 2
 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AMENDING SECTION 13-66(k)
 5 OF THE CITY CODE, SITE PLAN REQUIREMENTS;
 6 PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE;
 7 PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING FOR AN
 8 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to insure continued maintenance of
 11 property developed pursuant to its site plan approval process;
 12
 13 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 14 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 15
 16 SECTION 1. Section 13-66(k) of the City of Parkland City Code is hereby
 17 amended to read as follows:
 18
 19 (k) All requirements incorporated in any approval of a site plan shall be deemed
 20 requirements of this Code, as well as all improvements and landscaping depicted or
 21 described in any site plan; such requirements and depictions or descriptions shall be
 22 binding and all representations, drawings, elevations, or other submissions made as
 23 part of an approved site plan shall be deemed code requirements. The violation or
 24 failure to maintain a site plan in strict compliance with the above, or the failure to
 25 maintain all landscaping and building facades in good condition and in conformance
 26 with the site plan and all submittals related thereto shall be a violation of this Code. All
 27 landscaping and decorative materials placed in public medians and swales or privately
 28 owned buffers as part of a site plan shall also be maintained as part of the legal
 29 requirements set forth herein. In cases where a homeowner's association has
 30 succeeded the original developer of a site plan, the homeowner's association shall be
 31 responsible for the maintenance required in this section. Each day a violation exists
 32 shall be deemed a separate violation punishable by a fine up to five hundred dollars
 33 ($500.00).
 34
 35 SECTION 2. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained
 36 that the provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the City Code
 37 of the City of Parkland, and that the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to
 38 accomplish such intent.
 39
 40 SECTION 3. Should any section or provision of this ordinance or any portion
 41 thereof, any paragraph, sentence or word be declared by a court of competent
 42 jurisdiction to be invalid, such decision shall not affect the validity of the remainder
 43 hereof as a whole or part thereof other than the part declared to be invalid.
 44
 45 SECTION 4. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 46 passage and adoption.
