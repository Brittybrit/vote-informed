 36 ARTICLE XXVII. CONS CONSERVATION DISTRICT
 37
 38 Section 22-382. Purpose of district.
 39
 40 The conservation district is intended to protect natural functions of environmentally
 41 sensitive land and other open space which the City determines is appropriate for
 42 protection from development. No construction is permitted in these areas except for
 43 minimal structures and improvements required to provide safe access and essential
 44 support functions for permitted uses.

 1 Section 22-383. Uses permitted.
 2
 3 The uses permitted within the conservation district include
 4 (1) Wildlife management.
 5 (2) Wetland and upland mitigation areas and banks.
 6 (3) Natural resources based parks such as wildlife sanctuaries, feeding stations,
 7 nature centers, trails, outdoor research stations, walkways or aquifer recharge
 8 areas.
 9 (4) Environmental restoration/preservation.
 10 (5) Accessory uses which support the above uses.
 11
 12 Section 22-384. Building coverage.
 13
 14 The building coverage shall not exceed five percent (5)/0). Only structures which
 15 support the purposes of the district or necessary utilities shall be permitted.
 16
 17 Section 22-385. Floor area ratio.
 18
 19 The floor area ratio shall not exceed five percent (5%)
 20
 21 Section 22-386. Height.
 22
 23 No building or structure or part thereof shall be erected or altered to a height exceeding
 24 twenly-five (25) feet.
