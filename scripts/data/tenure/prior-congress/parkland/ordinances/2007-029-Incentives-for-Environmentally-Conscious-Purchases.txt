 ORDINANCE NO. 2007 - 29
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, CREATING A
 PROGRAM PROVIDING FOR INCENTIVES FOR
 ENVIRONMENTALLY CONSCIOUS PURCHASES;
 PROVIDING FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF THE
 PROGRAM; ADOPTING PROGRAM CRITERIA AND
 LIMITATIONS; PROVIDING FOR REGULATIONS;
 CONTAINING A PROVISION FOR INCLUSION IN THE
 CITY CODE; CONTAINING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE;
 CONTAINING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has on its logo the words "Environmentally Proud";
 and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has always taken the lead in preservation of the
 environment and wishes to continue to do so as the city, county, state and nation face mounting
 issues with regard to energy and the environment; and
 WHEREAS, the City believes that by encouraging environmentally conscious activities
 within the City of Parkland, not only the citizens of Parkland will benefit, but the citizens of our
 state and nation will receive a benefit; and
 WHEREAS, the City believes a valuable public purpose will be served by providing
 incentives for people to undertake environmentally conscious activities and purchases; and
 WHEREAS, the City believes that this will be a direct benefit to its citizens and will
 enhance the environment in the City of Parkland; and
 WHEREAS, this program is unrelated to the ad valorem taxes paid by anyone who
 qualifies for the rebate and the funds for the program shall not be taken from ad valorem tax
 revenues;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 SECTION 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are incorporated herein and
 express the legislative intent for this ordinance.
 SECTION 2. The City intends to implement a program to reward Parkland residents for
 taking extra steps to be environmentally conscious. Funds for this purpose are limited, and the

 City reserves the right to discontinue the program at any time, or to limit the payments made
 under this program when determined to be in the best interest of the City. Inasmuch as this
 program is funded by the City, no person or entity shall be eligible for a payment under this
 program unless that person or entity pays ad valorem taxes on property within the City or resides
 in or owns a business located in the City; persons or entities leasing property within the City
 shall qualify.
 SECTION 3. The City of Parkland hereby adopts a program for energy conscious
 incentives (hereinafter referred to as Incentive Payments) as set forth on the attached Exhibit A
 (The Criteria). No one will be eligible for Incentive Payments unless they meet The Criteria.
 The City Manager is authorized to provide appropriate application forms and require information
 as necessary to implement this program. No person or entity will be eligible for an energy
 incentive or Incentive Payment unless they have fully completed the application requirements as
 approved by the City Manager. Only purchases after the effective date of this ordinance shall
 qualify. Only completed applications received by May 1 of each year shall qualify for payment,
 which payment shall be made during the next fiscal year. The City reserves the right to reject
 any application, to limit payments made pursuant to this program, to adopt rules and regulations
 regarding the timing and method of payments (by resolution), or altogether suspend said
 payments when determined to be in the best interests of the City by the City Commission. The
 limitations set forth in The Criteria shall be binding.
 SECTION 4. Each fiscal year the City Manager shall set up a separate account to
 finance the Incentive Payments. The first payments shall be made during the 2008-2009 fiscal
 year. The account shall be funded from revenues or payments from the following sources:
 landscaping permit fees and cellular tower revenue; these funds shall be placed in a separate
 budget line item. The maximum funding for any fiscal year shall be $50,000; provided that the
 City Commission may increase this amount by resolution. When the funds from that line item
 are exhausted, the program will be suspended for that fiscal year unless additional non-ad
 valorem funds are deposited in the line item account. No payments in excess of that amount
 shall be made. No payments shall be made from ad valorem revenues. If no funds are placed in
 the account, then no incentive payments shall be made.
 SECTION 5. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of the

 any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of
 any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 SECTION 7. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 passage and adoption.

 EXHIBIT 'A'
 CITY OF PARKLAND ENERGY CONSCIOUS
 INCENTIVES PROGRAM
 APPLICATION PROCESS
 1. An application can be obtained on the City's web site or in person at City
 Hall.
 2. Completed applications will be accepted for processing from February 1st
 to April 30th of each year.
 3. One approved application per resident or business.
 4. No person or entity will be eligible for an energy incentive or incentive
 payment unless they have fully completed the application requirements as
 approved by the City Manager.
 5. Only completed applications received by May 1st of each year shall
 qualify for payment the following fiscal year.
 6. Applicants to successfully obtain an approved application will be eligible
 to receive an Energy Conscious Incentive Reward; payment shall be made
 during the next fiscal year.
 7. Each application will state the following: The City reserves the right to
 reject any application, to limit payments made pursuant to this program, or
 altogether suspend said payments when determined to be in the best
 interests of the City by the City Commission. The limitations set forth in
 The Criteria shall be binding.
 8. Residents must allow 6-8 weeks, after October 1st of each new fiscal year,
 for the refund award to be administered by the Finance Department.
 9. One application will apply to each program and each application will
 provide specific information applicable to the individual program.
 GENERAL PROGRAM LIMITATIONS
 1. No one person, residence or business may receive the same incentive
 twice.
 2. Upon exhaustion of funding the program will be suspended and no
 incentives paid.

 PROGRAM 1 —Hybrid Cars
 ➢ PROGRAM CRITERIA-
 1. Applicant must reside or own a business in the City of Parkland.
 a. If applicant is a resident then the vehicle must be registered in the City
 of Parkland.
 b. If applicant is a business then the vehicle must be registered to a
 business located in the City of Parkland.
 2. The applicant will be required to provide proof of purchase, vehicle and owner
 information with the application submittal.
 3. Upon completion of the approved application, the resident owning a hybrid car
 can make an appointment with staff, come to City Hall showing the vehicle and
 providing current proof of vehicle registration.
 ➢ PROGRAM REWARD AMOUNT- $200 incentive reward per resident or business.
 PROGRAM 2 — Replace inefficient incandescent light bulbs with ENERGY STAR
 qualified compact fluorescent lights (CFLs)
 ➢ PROGRAM CRITERIA-
 1. The installation of 100% Energy Star approved CFLs throughout the home or
 business will be inspected by City staff to certify the application; Building must
 be located within the City of Parkland.
 2. Required inspections will be scheduled to take place no later than four weeks
 from the application submittal.
 3. The applicant is required to provide proof of purchase with the application
 submittal.
 4. City staff shall have the right to exempt locations if more efficient light bulbs
 are not available or applicable for that location.
 ➢ PROGRAM REWARD AMOUNT- $100 incentive reward per resident or business.
 PROGRAM 3—Solar Power Landscape Lighting
 ➢ PROGRAM CRITERIA-
 1. The applicant is required to provide location of the residence or business
 (located within the City of Parkland) and obtain an inspection, by City staff, to
 determine the percentage of solar powered outdoor landscape lighting on the
 property. This inspection will determine the amount of the refund.
 2. Required inspections will be scheduled to take place no later than four weeks
 from the application submittal.
