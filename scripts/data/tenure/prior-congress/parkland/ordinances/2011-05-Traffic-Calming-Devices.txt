 ORDINANCE NO. 2011-05
 SECOND READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 FLORIDA, AMENDING CHAPTER 18, CODE OF ORDINANCES ENTITLED
 "ENGINEERING STANDARDS," ARTICLE VII ENTITLED "TRAFFIC
 CALMING"; AMENDING SECTION 18-161 ENTITLED "POLICY FOR
 INSTALLATION OF TRAFFIC CALMING DEVICES IN RESIDENTIAL
 NEIGHBORHOODS"; PROVIDING A CONFLICTS CLAUSE; PROVIDING A
 SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE
 WHEREAS, Chapter 18, Article VII of the City's Code of Ordinances addresses the installation
 of traffic calming devices; and
 WHEREAS, traffic calming devices are typically a function of the City's Engineering Division;
 and
 WHEREAS, staff desires to clarify the related section of the code to eliminate confusion,
 provide consistency, and better streamline the process; and
 WHEREAS, it is the recommendation of staff that Chapter 18, Article VII of the City's Code of
 Ordinances be amended to provide for such clarifications; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of Parkland, Florida finds and determines that amending
 Chapter 18, Article VII of the City's Code of Ordinances is in the best interests of the citizens and
 residents of the City of Parkland.
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1: That Chapter 18, Code of Ordinances entitled "Engineering Standards,"
 Article VII entitled "Traffic Calming" is hereby amended to read and provide as follows:
 ARTICLE VII. TRAFFIC CALMING
 Sec. 18-161. Policy for installation of traffic calming devices in residential neighborhoods.
 I. Use of traffic calming devices. Potentially acceptable traffic calming devices to be used in the city
 include, but are not limited to speed tables, traffic circles/roundabouts, traffic islands and traffic chokers.
 The use of speed humps/bumps shall be prohibited. The use of temporary speed tables shall also be

<<PG>>
 prohibited. The design and appearance of permanent speed tables shall be standardized throughout
 the city and shall meet applicable FDOT and Broward County standards. While permanent speed
 tables are considered a potentially acceptable type of traffic calming device, it shall be the policy of the
 city to encourage any neighborhoods seeking to utilize such devices to first explore the use of
 educational measures to help address speeding concerns. Such measures might include, but not be
 limited to seeking increased law enforcement of speeding; the use of portable speed radar units; and
 implementing a neighborhood speed watch program.
 II. New subdivisions. The city shall require that traffic calming devices/measures which do not have a
 significant impact on emergency vehicle response such as but not limited to the use of curvilinear street
 alignments; entry islands; roundabouts; chokers; and center island narrowing; be built into the design of
 all new subdivisions as feasible and appropriate.
 III. Existing subdivisions/neighborhoods. The city shall use the following standards in order to
 determine whether to install traffic calming devices in streets in existing residential neighborhoods.
 A. Eligible streets. A street will be considered for installation of traffic calming devices subject to
 satisfying the criteria in the most recent version of the Broward County Neighborhood Traffic
 Management Manual.
 B. Application/petition to request installation of traffic calming devices. An engineering review
 application must be submitted to the city's planning department of public works and engineering by the
 duly authorized representative of a neighborhood's homeowner's association (HOA) bearing the
 signatures of at least sixty-seven (67) percent of the current lot owners within the impact area where
 the proposed calming device(s) is to be located. The delineation of the boundaries of the impact area
 shall be determined by whomever the HOA hires to perform the required traffic study described below
 subject to ultimate verification/modification by the city's engineer, qualified engineering consultant, or
 designee.
 In the event the neighborhood does not have an active HOA, a petition must be submitted bearing the
 signatures of at least sixty-seven (67) percent of the current lot owners located within the impact area
 (to be determined as noted above) where the proposed traffic calming device(s) is to be located).
 The application shall include the following:
 (1) A drawing showing the street and the specific locations in the street where installation is
 requested.
 (2) A statement of the reasons for the traffic calming device.
 (3) A traffic study indicating that the criteria set forth in subsection III. A. above has been met. The
 applicant may shall employ a private qualified traffic engineering firm approved by the city
 to perform the requisite study which shall be signed and sealed by a registered professional engineer in
 the State of Florida equest that the city perform the study and shall be
 (4) Signatures from lot owners located at sixty-seven (67) percent of the addresses within the impact
 area followed by the signer's printed name, address, and the telephone number. Only one (1) person
 per address will be counted toward meeting this criteria.
 (5) A processing fee of five hundred dollars ($500.00). which is in addition to the cost of performing
 the required traffic study.
 (6) If the application or petition is returned as set out below, the city shall return the portion of the
 processing fee that has not been used.
 If a petition, the following additional information shall be supplied:
 (7) The name, address and telephone number of the signer who will represent the petitioners and
 whom the city staff will contact to implement this policy.
 (8) The name, address and telephone number of at least five (5) signers, including the signer
 identified above, who will constitute a committee of the petitioners to work with city staff to implement
 this policy.

 D. Procedure to install traffic claming calming devices.
 (a) On hearing the agenda item, the city commission shall determine whether to approve the
 installation of traffic calming devices, and if so, the type and location thereof. The city commission may
 approve, modify, condition or deny any recommendation or proposal for traffic darning calming devices,
 regardless of the extent of public support for the recommendation or proposal.
 (b) If the city commission approves the installation of traffic claming calming devices, and the
 applicant/petitioners wish to install the devices as so approved, the applicant/petitioners or their
 contractor shall obtain an engineering permit from the city for the work. The applicant/petitioner shall
 have prepared standard plans and construction cost estimate signed and sealed by a registered
 professional engineer in the State of Florida to be incorporated into the permit. The
 applicant/petitioners shall submit the appropriate engineering permit fee in accordance with Section 18-
 47 of the city code.
 (c) The applicant/petitioners shall bear all costs associated with of the issuance of permits,
 construction, inspection and perpetual maintenance of the approved traffic calming devices. Such costs

 E. Code enforcement jurisdiction. The code enforcement board shall have concurrent jurisdiction over
 violations of this policy.
 F. Upon completing the installation of traffic calming devices, the HOA shall sign a hold harmless
 agreement with the city exempting the city from any liability resulting from the use of the traffic claming
 calming devices. The hold harmless agreement shall be permanently recorded in the county's records.
