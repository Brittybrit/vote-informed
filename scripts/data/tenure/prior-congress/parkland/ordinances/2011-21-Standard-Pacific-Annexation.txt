 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, PROVIDING FOR THE ANNEXATION INTO
 5 THE CORPORATE LIMITS OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND OF
 6 APPROXIMATELY 297.357 ACRE PROPERTY KNOWN AS STANDARD
 7 PACIFIC OF FLORIDA, GENERALLY LOCATED APPROXIMATELY
 8 3.9 MILES WEST OF PARKSIDE DRIVE ON LOXAHATCHEE ROAD,
 9 PROPERTY CONTROL NUMBER 474129010010; CONTAINING
 10 PROVISIONS FOR IMPLEMENTATION AND FILINGS WITH THE
 11 APPROPRIATE GOVERMENTAL AGENCIES; PROVIDING FOR
 12 INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE; PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY
 13 CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 14
 15 WHEREAS, Standard Pacific of Florida, the owner of the property with the property
 16 control number 474129010010 (said land described on Exhibit A), has submitted a petition to
 17 annex its property into the City of Parkland, pursuant to a voluntary annexation; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the Land described in Exhibit A, is the Land owned by Standard Pacific of
 20 Florida, and shall hereinafter be referred to as "The Land"; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the map attached as Exhibit B reflects the general location of the Land; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the Land is located in unincorporated Broward County contiguous to the
 25 City of Parkland; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the requirements of Section 171.044, Florida Statutes, pertaining to
 28 voluntary annexations, have been complied with in that:
 29
 30 A. The Land is contiguous to the City of Parkland and is reasonably compact
 31 and does not create an enclave;
 32
 33 B. The petitioner, the sole owner of the Land, has sought annexation by a
 34 Petition for Annexation and approves the annexation of the Land into the City of Parkland;
 35
 36 C. This ordinance has been advertised as required by Section 171.044,
 37 Florida Statutes;
 38
 39 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that annexation of the property is in the best
 40 interest of the City of Parkland and the City of Parkland is prepared to provide all necessary
 41 municipal services to the Land; and
 42
 43 WHEREAS, the annexation of the Land into the City of Parkland will promote orderly
 44 planning and the efficient provision of the municipal services to the Land; and
 45

 1 WHEREAS, the City has determined that the Petition for Annexation bears the signature
 2 of the owner(s) of the property of the area proposed to be annexed and constitutes consent to this
 3 annexation;
 4
 5 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 6 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 7
 8 SECTION 1. The above "WHEREAS" clauses are true and correct and made a part
 9 hereof.
 10
 11 SECTION 2. The Land is hereby annexed into the corporate boundaries of the City of
 12 Parkland, Florida, along with all public rights of way lying within the Land and all public rights
 13 of way lying directly adjacent to the Land with the exception of Loxahatchee Road.
 14
 15 SECTION 3. The boundaries of the City of Parkland are re-defined to include the Land
 16 and all public rights of way lying within the Land and all public rights of way lying directly
 17 adjacent to the Land with the exception of Loxahatchee Road.
 18
 19 SECTION 4. The current land use and zoning remain unchanged by this Ordinance and
 20 shall be as provided in Section 171.062(2), Florida Statutes, until such time as the City of
 21 Parkland adopts a comprehensive plan that includes the annexed property.
 22
 23 SECTION 5. The City shall take all necessary actions before the Broward County
 24 Legislative Delegation to comply with all local acts relating to annexation and this annexation is
 25 conditioned upon approval of the Delegation.
 26
 27 SECTION 6. Within seven (7) days after its adoption a copy of this Ordinance shall be
 28 filed with the Clerk of the Circuit Court of Broward County for recordation. Within seven (7)
 29 days of adoption, this Ordinance shall be filed with the Broward County Administrator and with
 30 the Florida Department of State. Within seven (7) days after approval by the Delegation, the City
 31 shall record a Notice of Delegation Approval to which the approved ordinance is attached.
 32
 33 SECTION 7. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 34 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of the
 35 City of Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to accomplish
 36 such intent.
 37
 38 SECTION 8. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for
 39 any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of
 40 any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 41
 42 SECTION 9. This Ordinance shall be effective immediately upon adoption but the
 43 annexation provided herein shall become effective on 12:01 a.m. September 15, 2012; at such
 44 time the Land along with all public rights of way lying within the Land and all public rights of
