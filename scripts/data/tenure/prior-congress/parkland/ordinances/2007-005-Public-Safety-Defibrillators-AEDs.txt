 35 Section 2. Chapter 14 of the Code of Ordinances of the City of Parkland
 36 shall be amended to read as follows:
 37
 38 Section 14-2. Automated external defibrillators.
 39
 40 (1) Definition of automated external defibrillators.
 41
 42 Automated external defibrillator device. A defibrillator device that 1) is
 43 commercially distributed in accordance with the Federal Food, Drug, and
 44 Cosmetic Act, 2) is capable of recognizing the presence or absence of
 45 ventricular fibrillation, and is capable of determining without intervention by,
 46 the user of the device whether defibrillation should be performed, and 3)

 1 upon determining that defibrillation should be performed, is able to deliver
 2 an electrical shock to an individual.
 3
 4 (2) Intent.
 5
 6 (A) It is the intent of the city and the purpose of this chapter:
 7
 8 1. To promote the public health, safety, and general welfare by
 9 requiring the installation of automated external defibrillator devices
 10 within certain buildings in the city;
 11
 12 2. To provide for the inspection of each automated external
 13 defibrillator device within the city; and
 14
 15 3. To provide for the payment of automated external defibrillator
 16 inspection fees.
 17
 18 (3) Automated external defibrillator devices required.
 19
 20 (A) Automated external defibrillator devices shall be installed in the
 21 following buildings located within the geographical boundaries of the city:
 22
 23 1. Gymnasiums, fitness centers and indoor recreational centers in
 24 excess of one thousand five hundred (1,500) square feet;
 25
 26 2. Restaurants with fifty (50) or more seats including indoor,
 27 outdoor and bar;
 28
 29 3. Assisted living facilities as defined by F.S. § 400.402, as
 30 amended from time to time.
 31
 32 (4) Installation and operation.
 33
 34 (A) The city shall inspect all automated external defibrillator devices for
 35 operation prior to being placed in service or available for use, and on an
 36 annual basis.
 37
 38 (B) Automated external defibrillator devices shall be:
 39
 40 (1) Conspicuously located in plain view of the primary public
 41 entrance, with unobstructed access;
 42
 43 (2) Housed in a cabinet with a clear window in the door, an
 44 audible alarm signaling the opening of a door, permanently affixed
 45 to a wall, and whose top is no more than forty-eight (48) inches
 46 above the floor;

 2 (3) Located below a sign having a minimum area of seventy (70)
 3 square inches and containing the letters "AED" and the universally
 4 recognizable symbol, which shall be placed no more than sixty (60)
 5 inches, on center, above the floor;
 6
 7 (4) Readily accessible and immediately available when needed for
 8 on-site employees and the general public, including disabled
 9 persons; and
 10
 11 (5) Placed near the elevator(s) in the first floor lobby, if the
 12 building contains an elevator.
 13
 14 (C) Automated external defibrillator devices shall contain adult and
 15 pediatric pads and bandage scissors.
 16
 17 (D) All automated external defibrillator devices shall be used in
 18 accordance with the manufacturer's guidelines.
 19
 20 (E) It shall be the responsibility of the owner of the building to:
 21
 22 (1) Install automated external defibrillator devices.
 23
 24 (2) Provide all necessary training for appropriate use;
 25
 26 (3) Maintain automated external defibrillator devices in
 27 accordance with manufacturer's recommended maintenance
 28 requirements and as required herein.
 29
 30 (F) If an automated external defibrillator device is removed for repair, a
 31 replacement shall be provided.
 32
 33 (5) Fees. Fees for automated external defibrillator device permits and
 34 inspections shall be set by resolution.
 35

 37 provided in §1-8.1, to intentionally or willfully:
 38
 39 (A) Render an automated external defibrillator device inoperative except
 40 during such time as the automated external defibrillator device is being
 41 serviced, tested, repaired, or recharged, except pursuant to court order;

 44 device for purposes of falsifying service records;
