 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AMENDING SECTION 2-143.9 (C) OF THE
 5 CITY CODE RELATING TO PURCHASING, CONTAINING A
 6 PROVISION FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE; CONTAINING A
 7 SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; CONTAINING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 8
 9 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland wishes to revise Section 2-143.9
 10 of the City Code relating to purchasing,
 11
 12 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 13 PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 14
 15 Section 1. Section 2-143.9 of the City Code is hereby amended to read as
 16 follows:
 17 Sec. 2-143.9. Exempt purchases.
 18 The following purchases are exempted from this article and do not require sealed
 19 bidding, or requests for proposals, due to the nature of the goods or service. These
 20 include, but are not limited to the following:
 21 (a) Payment of utility bills for normal monthly service such as Florida Power & Light
 22 (electric), A T & T, Solid Waste Authority (solid waste disposal fees), and any other
 23 utility service.
 24 (b) Payment of payroll related deductions or liabilities, such as FICA/Medicare, Federal
 25 Withholding Taxes, Pension or (457) Contributions, Credit Union, US Savings
 26 Bonds, IRS and Child Support Garnishment.
 27 (c) Piggy-back bids—If the goods to be purchased have been approved by the City
 28 Manager and are included in the annual budget, the user department or City may
 29 "piggyback" any currently authorized public agency bid of the federal govenme -,
 30 government
 31 agencies and/or not for profit purchasing cooperatives contract. The purchase order
 32 for these goods must refer to the current public agency bid number and the goods
 33 must have the same specifications as those in the bid documents. A copy of the
 34 contract must be provided to the finance department before the purchase order can
 35 be approved.
 36 (d) Payment to service providers needed to maintain the City operations such as
 37 plumbers, electricians, temporary employment services, computer consultants, or
 38 air conditioning service. (This should only pertain to emergencies, not routine
 39 maintenance and does not include the replacement of major capital equipment or
 40 components).
 41 (e) Payment of subscriptions, memberships, dues, education, and travel expenses.

<<PG>>
 42 (f) Payment of insurance billings, postage, and postage meter permits.
 43 (g) Payment of authorized debt service.
 44 (h) Payment of authorized donations, or scholarships.
 45 (i) Sole source—A contract within the award authority of the City Manager may be
 46 awarded for supplies, services or construction without competition when determined
 47 that there is only one (1) source or one (1) reasonable source for the required
 48 supply, service or construction.
 49 a) Professional services or contracts otherwise required by law.
 50 (k) Contracts with other governmental entities or agencies for public services.
 51 (I) Franchises to provide municipal or other public services.
 52
 53 Section 2. This ordinance shall be made a part of the City of Parkland City Code
 54 and the provisions herein may be renumbered for such purpose.
 55
 56 Section 3. If any word, clause, sentence or section of this ordinance is for any
 57 reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of
 58 any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 59
 60 Section 4. This ordinance shall become effective upon adoption by the City
 61 Commission.
