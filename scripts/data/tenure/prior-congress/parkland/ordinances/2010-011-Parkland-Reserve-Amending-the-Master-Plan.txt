 12 WHEREAS, on June 15, 2005, the City of Parkland adopted Ordinance No.
 13 2004-22 which approved the rezoning of land described on Exhibit A (the Property),
 14 attached hereto and made a part hereof, from A-1 to Planned Residential District and
 15 Planned Commercial District; and
 16 WHEREAS, said Ordinance contained conditions of approval and a master plan
 17 for the project known as Parkland Village now known as Parkland Reserve; and
 18 WHEREAS, on March 18, 2009, the City subsequently adopted Ordinance No.
 19 2009-12 which amended the conditions of approval as set forth in Ordinance No. 2004-
 20 22 (both Ordinances collectively referred to as the PRD Ordinance); and
 21 WHEREAS, on December 19, 2009, the City subsequently adopted Ordinance
 22 No. 2009-52 which amended the conditions of approval as set forth in Ordinance No.
 23 2009-12; and
 24 WHEREAS, Ordinance No. 2009-52 (the PRD Ordinance) is presently the
 25 Ordinance that governs the PRD; and
 26 WHEREAS, the owners of the Property have applied for an amendment to the
 27 PRD Ordinance which amendment contains revisions to the conditions of approval; and

 1 WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Board at its meeting of March 11, 2010,
 2 recommended to deny the application of the owner which seeks to amend the PRD

 4 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that it is in the best interests of the City to

 8 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 9 Section 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are hereby ratified and
 10 confirmed as being true and correct, and are hereby incorporated herein and made a
 11 part hereof. Ordinance Number 2009-52 is hereby amended in full as set forth herein
 12 and this Ordinance shall govern the PRD.
 13 Section 2. Application number 01-MPA-09 is hereby approved subject to the
 14 conditions set forth herein. All development on the Property shall be consistent with the
 15 conditions of approval and master plan as set forth on Exhibit B and C. Where the
 16 conditions of approval refer to conditions as set forth in the PRD Ordinance or
 17 Resolutions related thereto, those conditions shall be deemed a part of this Ordinance
 18 and shall also govern all development on the Property to the extent not inconsistent
 19 herewith.
 20 Section 3. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence of this Ordinance if for any
 21 reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity
 22 of any remaining portions of this Ordinance.
