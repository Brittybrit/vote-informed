 4 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 5 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, CREATING A NEW SECTION 3-38
 6 ENTITLED "SUPERVISION AND CONFINEMENT;
 7 TETHERING"; PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY
 8 CODE; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR
 9 SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE
 10
 11 WHEREAS, P.A.W.S of Westglades Middle School has informed the City Commission of the
 12 dangers faced by animals that are confined by tethering or by other means in private or public yards in
 13 the City of Parkland; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, P.A.W.S of Westglades Middle School has informed the City Commission that
 16 animals that are improperly or extensively confined or tethered are more likely to cause injury or harm
 17 to people and other animals than are ones that are not improperly or extensively confined; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland recognizes that limiting and
 20 regulating the confinement of animals would benefit the citizens of Parkland and contribute to the good
 21 quality of life for all residents of Parkland;
 22
 23 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 24 OF PARKLAND,AS FOLLOWS:
 25
 26 SECTION 1. The foregoing Whereas clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true
 27 and correct and are hereby made a part of this Ordinance.
 28
 29 SECTION 2. Section 10-13 entitled "Supervision and Confinement; Tethering" of the Code of
 30 Ordinances of the City of Parkland is hereby created and shall read as follows:
 31
 32 Sec. 3-38. Supervision and Confinement; Tethering.
 33
 34 A. The following terms, as used in this section, are defined as set out below:
 35
 36 Animal: A multicellular organism of the kingdom Animalia, differing from plants in
 37 certain typical characteristics such as capacity for locomotion, nonphotosynthetic metabolism,
 38 pronounced response to stimuli, restricted growth, and fixed bodily structure.
 39
 40 Tether: The act of constraining an animal's movement by securing a leash (or other
 41 similar device) which is holding a dog to some object which is attached to the ground.
 42
 43 B. Any person who owns or any person who is in charge of any dog, cat or animal kept as a
 44 pet is prohibited from confining or tethering that dog, cat or animal outdoors during periods of extreme
 45 weather or when a hurricane warning has been issued.
 46

 1 C. Any person who owns or any person who is in charge of any dog, cat or animal kept as a
 2 pet who confines that animal outdoors on private property must provide the dog, cat or animal with:
 3
 4 (1) Sufficient and appropriate food and potable water; and
 5 (2) Adequate shelter from cold, heat, sun, and rain; and
 6 (3) Periodic supervision, which shall mean that each animal shall be attended to at
 7 least once during every twelve consecutive hours; and
 8 (4) Adequate space for exercise, which shall mean no less than 150 square feet
 9 per animal; and
 10 (5) A fenced yard, an electronic fence, or an enclosure, which has at least one
 11 translucent side, that is sufficient to prevent the animal from escaping; or
 12 (6) A tether under the following circumstances:
 13
 14 a. Any person who owns or any person who is in charge or in control of
 15 any dog, cat, or animal that is under the age of six months shall not tie,
 16 chain, or otherwise tether a dog, cat or animal kept as a pet that is
 17 under the age of six months outdoors.
 18
 19 b. Any person who owns or any person who is in charge or in control of
 20 any animal that is over the age of six months shall not tie, chain, or
 21 otherwise tether a dog, cat or animal kept as a pet outdoors between
 22 the hours of 10:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. except that an animal kept as a
 23 pet of any age may be temporarily tethered, tied, or chained outdoors
 24 for a total time period not exceeding fifteen minutes. At other times, it
 25 is permitted to tie, chain, or otherwise tether an animal kept as a pet
 26 that is over the age of six months outdoors if the pet is collared by a
 27 properly fitting, non-choke type collar or a body harness that is
 28 fastened to either:
 29
 30 i. A tether that is at least three times the body length of the dog, cat, or
 31 animal measured from the dog, cat, or animal's nose to back of the
 32 hindquarters, and that is free from entanglement; or
 33
 34 ii. An overhead run, which is a leash or chain attached to an overhead
 35 wire at least ten feet long, that allows the dog, cat, or animal to move
 36 unheeded, when total weight of the collar together with the chain,
 37 rope, or tether does not exceed 1/16 the body weight of the animal.
 38
 39 D. Nothing in this ordinance shall be construed to prohibit an owner or keeper from
 40 controlling a dog, cat or animal kept as a pet by the handheld use of a rope, leash, or chain, when total
 41 weight of the collar together with the chain, rope, or tether does not exceed 1/16 of the body weight of
 42 the animal.
