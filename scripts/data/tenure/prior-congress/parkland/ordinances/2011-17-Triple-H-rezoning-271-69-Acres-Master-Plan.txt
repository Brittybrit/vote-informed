 12 Section 1. The above Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a part
 13 hereof.
 14
 15 Section 1. The zoning of the Land is hereby changed from Palm Beach
 16 County Agricultural Residential District ("PBC AR") to Planned Residential Development
 17 District ("PRD"). This rezoning is subject to compliance with the Master Plan and all
 18 development conditions as set forth on Exhibit B (Development Conditions) as well as
 19 compliance with all applicable development regulations. The Master Plan and
 20 Development Conditions shall govern all development of any kind, which occurs on the
 21 Land. Failure to comply with any part of the Master Plan or development conditions
 22 shall result in the City withholding development permits.
 23
 24 Section 2. The Master Plan as substantially set forth herein shall be the basis
 25 for any site plan application. The approval of a site plan in substantial conformity with
 26 the Master Plan shall be a precondition of any development on the subject property.
 27 Such site approval shall only be valid after a plat is approved in conformity with the
 28 approved site plan.
 29
 30 Section 3. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence of this ordinance is for any
 31 reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity
 32 of any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 33
 34 Section 4. It is the intention of the City Commission of the City of Parkland,
 35 Florida, and it is hereby ordained that the Zoning Map of the City of Parkland be
 36 amended and do hereby direct the Director of Planning and Zoning to amend the map
 37 accordingly.
 38
 39 Section 5. All other ordinances in conflict herewith are repealed to the extent
 40 of the conflict.
 41
 42 Section 6. That this ordinance shall be effective immediately upon adoption by
 43 the City Commission of the City of Parkland and shall be recorded in the public records
 44 of Broward County and run with and bind the land.
 45
 46 FIRST READING HELD ON THIS 6th DAY OF July, 2011.

 5 1. The term "Owner" shall refer to Triple H Ranch Property, Ltd. and any
 6 other person who undertakes development of the Property or any portion

 9 2. All development on the Property shall be governed by and be consistent
 10 with the Master Plan prepared by Unger Engineering Services, Inc. and
 11 Winningham & Fradley, Inc. dated received by the Planning and Zoning
 12 Department on May 20, 2011. The Master Plan is incorporated by
 13 reference into this ordinance and made a part hereof. The maximum
 14 number of single family dwelling units approved as part of the Master Plan
 15 shall not exceed 570 units.
 16
 17 3. All development on the Property shall be governed by the Master Plan and
 18 the additional restrictions as set forth herein. Both the Master Plan and the
 19 restrictions as set forth in this PRD ordinance shall be binding upon the
 20 Property and all development of the Property shall conform with the terms
 21 and conditions of the Master Plans and this PRD ordinance.
 22
 23 4. The Owner shall provide drainage and utility easements to interconnect
 24 the school sites into the water management tracts. The location of such
 25 easements shall be determined by the City of Parkland, School Board of
 26 Broward County and the Owner prior to the issuance of the first building
 27 permit for the construction of a principal building.
 28
 29 5. All required landscape buffers adjacent to trafficways and medians
 30 contained therein shall be identified on the plat and maintained by a
 31 Property Owners Association with control over said buffers. The buffers
 32 shall be approved by the City Commission after review by the Planning
 33 and Zoning Board. The buffers shall comply with all land development
 34 code requirements. Buffers shall be completed prior to the issuance of a
 35 building permit for a principal building on the adjoining pod. Prior to any
 36 building permit the Owner shall provide Homeowners documents,
 37 acceptable to the City Attorney, which bind the Homeowners Association
 38 to undertake such maintenance and permit the City to enforce this
 39 obligation.
