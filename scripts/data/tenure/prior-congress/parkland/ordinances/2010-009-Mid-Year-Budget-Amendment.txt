 18 WHEREAS, on September 23, 2009, the City of Parkland adopted a final budget for the
 19 Fiscal Year beginning October 1, 2009, and ending September 30, 2010: and

 21 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 166.241, Florida Statues, the City of Parkland may, by
 22 ordinance, amend its budget and wishes to do so to reflect additional appropriations:

 24 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 25 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 26
 27 Section 1. That Ordinance 2009-41, as amended, which Ordinance did adopt the
 28 budget of the City of Parkland for Fiscal Year 2009/2010, and the budget adopted thereby be and
 29 the same is hereby amended to cause and reflect the increase of a net total of $972,100 of
 30 revenues and expenditure appropriations to said General Fund and reflect the decrease of a net
 31 total of$932,000 of revenues and expenditures appropriations to the said Capital Projects Fund
 32 as set forth in Exhibit A.
