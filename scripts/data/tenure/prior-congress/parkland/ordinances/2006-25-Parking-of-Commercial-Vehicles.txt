 21 WHEREAS, the City Commission recognizes that the parking of commercial vehicles on
 22 residential parcels creates traffic impacts and may negatively affect the appearance of
 23 residential property; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City Commission seeks to adopt definitions of "commercial vehicle"
 26 and "heavy commercial vehicle" as the basis for reasonable commercial vehicle parking
 27 regulations of parking of commercial vehicles throughout the City.
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to adopt reasonable commercial vehicle
 30 parking regulations in order to promote an orderly and aesthetically appealing appearance
 31 throughout the residential zoning districts of the City; and

 21 Heavy commercial vehicle: any vehicle in excess of one of the following: (a) fifteen
 22 thousand (15,000) pounds gross vehicle weight ratio; or (b) eight (8) feet in width; or (c) eight
 23 (8) feet in height, or (d) twenty-two (22) feet in length. It may or may not have outside
 24 lettering or logos displaying information identifying a business. Heavy commercial vehicle
 25 also shall mean other vehicles used for non-personal use of any kind that may not precisely
 26 meet the above criteria, including but not limited to:
 27 a. Tractor crane;
 28 b. Power shovel;
 29 c. Well driller and other such vehicles so constructed and designed as a tool
 30 and not a hauling unit, even if such equipment may be used on the roads and
 31 highway incidental to the purposes for which designed;
 32 d. Wrecker/tow truck;
 33 e. Semi-trailer;
 34 f. Flat bed truck;
 35 g. Except in the A-1, AE-1 and AE-2 zoning districts motor vehicles modified,
 36 altered or customized for the purposes of a commercial business, so that
 37 supplies, equipment or materials are visible, including but not limited to
 38 plumbing, irrigation, mirror and glass, bucket trucks and other trade vehicles.
 39 Racks installed on vehicles in excess of twelve (12) inches above permanent
 40 roof line or vehicle bed topper will be classified as equipment. Vehicle beds or
 41 bed toppers that are made to permit side accessibility to tools, supplies,
 42 equipment, etc. even though these enclosed items are not visible are
 43 considered heavy commercial vehicles.
 44 h. Except in the A-1, AE-1 and AE-2 zoning districts Cube, box and step vans;
 45 i. Except in the A-1, AE-1 and AE-2 zoning districts Dump truck, dump truck
 46 body, or rack truck; and
 47 j. Any other vehicle meeting the above definition.

 2 SECTION 5. Section 22-137 of the Code of Ordinances is hereby amended to state as
 3 follows:
 4
 5 Sec. 22-137. Parking of commercial vehicles.
 6
 7 (a) Off-street parking facilities supplied by the owner or operator to meet the
 8 requirements of this article shall not be used by commercial vehicles owned, operated or
 9 used in the business of such owner during regular hours of business. Additional parking
 10 spaces shall be provided to accommodate commercial vehicles during regular hours of
 11 business and shall be located as far away as possible from a public right-of-way. Parking of
 12 commercial vehicles shall not be used for advertising purposes between the hours of 7:00
 13 p.m. and 8:00 a.m. Such advertising purpose shall be demonstrated by the following: (a) the
 14 sign copy (as defined in the sign regulations) is greater than five (5) percent of the size of the
 15 sum of the outside surface of the two (2) sides and rear of the vehicle; and (b) the vehicle is
 16 positioned so that the copy is readily visible from a roadway.
 17
 18 (b) Parking of commercial vehicles and heavy commercial vehicles within residential
 19 zoning districts (AE-1, AE-2, RS-1, RS-2, RS-3, RS-4, RS-6, RM-5 to RM-10, PUD, and
 20 PRD).
 21 1. Parking of commercial vehicles and heavy commercial vehicles within public and
 22 private rights-of-way. Commercial vehicles and heavy commercial vehicles shall be
 23 permitted to park or stop within public and private rights-of-way within the residential
 24 zoning districts of the City only when such vehicles are actively engaged in the
 25 process of loading or unloading.
 26 2. Overnight parking of heavy commercial vehicles; exception for permitted
 27 construction sites. Heavy commercial vehicles may be parked overnight in a
 28 residential zoning district, provided the vehicle is not operating between the hours of
 29 7:00 p.m. to 6:00 a.m. Monday through Saturday and 6:00 p.m. to 9:00 a.m. Sundays
 30 and national holidays; and only if such overnight parking is limited to no more than two
 31 (2) times per year per parcel unless the vehicle is used in the ordinary course of a
 32 bona fide farm operation or a business which possesses a valid City of Parkland
 33 occupational license to conduct business on the property. This restriction shall not
 34 apply while construction is ongoing on a residential parcel pursuant to an active City
 35 building permit.
 36 3. Screening of commercial vehicles and heavy commercial vehicles.
 37 a. Enclosure of commercial and heavy commercial vehicles. Within residential
 38 zoning districts, commercial vehicles with or without trailers and heavy
 39 commercial vehicles shall be parked entirely within a garage, a carport, or
 40 within or surrounded by another opaque structure, or within an area in which the
 41 vehicle is screened by an opaque wall, fence, hedge, or vegetation in
 42 accordance with Section 9-31(h) of the City Code of the City of Parkland to fully
 43 block the vehicle from view of nearby public or private rights-of-way and
 44 adjacent properties.
 45 b. Additional screening options for commercial vehicles. The following options
 46 for screening are available to commercial vehicles, as defined in this section.
 47 i. Screening of commercial vehicle with opaque vehicle cover. Within
 48 residential zoning districts, commercial vehicles may be parked on a
 49 paved driveway provided that the vehicle is covered entirely by an

 1 opaque cover. Said cover shall be constructed of sound material and
 2 shall be maintained in good condition, such that there is no fading,
 3 tearing, or holes in the cover. Vinyl or canvas tarpaulin, or other covers
 4 not designed for use as a vehicle cover, shall be prohibited. All vehicle
 5 covers shall be constructed to permit all license registrations to be visible
 6 from public and private rights-of-way. This option may not be utilized to
 7 screen heavy commercial vehicles as defined herein.
 8 ii. Coverage of commercial vehicle logo with opaque magnetic logo or
 9 lettering cover. A commercial vehicle may meet the screening
 10 requirement of this subsection by completely obscuring commercial
 11 lettering or signage with a magnetic cover, provided it is the same color
 12 as the vehicle. This option does not apply to heavy commercial vehicles,
 13 and is available only to those vehicles that are considered commercial
 14 vehicles because of the presence of permanent or temporary signage on
 15 the vehicle.
