 33 14. Mixed Use (MU) — The Mixed Use designation is designed for new development or
 34 redevelopment of existing sites which are characterized by a variety of integrated land
 35 use types. The intent of the district is to provide for a mixture of uses on single parcels
 36 in order to develop or redevelop sites which are sensitive to the surrounding uses,
 37 obtain the desired character of the community, and ensure the availability of capacity of
 38 public facilities to serve proposed developments. This designation is also intended to
 39 foster infill development, to deter urban sprawl and to lessen the need for additional
 40 vehicular trips through the internalization of trips within a neighborhood or project. The
 41 minimum criteria established below are to be used for development of sites designated
 42 Mixed Use.
 43
 44 1. Mixed use development shall include a minimum of two of the following
 45 land uses described in this element. The type of land uses within a mixed
 46 use development are the following:

 4 4. The minimum land area for a Mixed Use development shall be 5 acres
 5 and the maximum land area shall be 60 acres.
 6
 7 5. The following are additional standards affecting land allocations for the
 8 Mixed Use designation:
 9
 10 (A) Residential must be one of these land uses, unless it is
 11 determined by the City Commission that the proposed
 12 development meets the criteria to waive the residential
 13 requirement.
 14
 15 (B) Either Commercial or Office land use is required as an
 16 element of Mixed Use development but not both.
 17
 18 (C) Special Exception approval shall be required to be obtained
 19 in addition to Site Plan approval.
 20
 21 6. The percentage of property that can be utilized for residential purposes
 22 shall be based upon the following factors which include but is not limited
 23 to: the gross acreage of a site; the total gross square feet that can be
 24 constructed based upon floor area ratio; the proposed number of
 25 nonresidential gross square feet of a development; the amount of
 26 remaining gross square feet; parking requirements; and drainage
 27 requirements. Additional factors shall be based on the requirements as
 28 set forth in the applicable zoning district.
 29
 30 7. The maximum building coverage for the entire site shall be 35%.
 31
 32 8. The maximum floor area ratio shall be 40%, except the maximum floor
 33 area ratio for the parks and conservation land use categories shall be 5%.
 34
 35 9. The individual uses, buildings or pods within Mixed Use developments
 36 shall include interconnecting pedestrian ways and plazas.
