 6 ARTICLE VII. PARK REGULATIONS
 7
 8 ***
 9
 10 Section 11-74. Nuisances.
 11
 12 The restrictions and regulations contained herein shall apply to all city parks as follows:
 13
 14 (a) Intoxicating beverages. Drinking, possession of or bringing alcoholic
 15 beverages into any city park is prohibited except during a special event
 16 conducted and authorized by the City of Parkland.
 17
 18 (b) Firearms; weapons. Posscssion or discharge of any instrument that fires a
 19 potentially dangerous projectile, gas or liquid is prohibited.
 20
 21 (c) (b) Fireworks and explosives. Possessing, exploding, discharging or
 22 burning within or bringing into any park area any fireworks or other potentially
 23 dangerous inflammable material, including model rockets, is prohibited except
 24 during a special event conducted and authorized by the City of Parkland.
 25
 26 (d) (c) Domestic animals. Domestic animals shall be excluded from all city
 27 parks except the Parkland Equestrian Center at Temple Park. Domestic animals
 28 may run freely (provided they are under human control) at the Parkland
 29 Equestrian Center at Temple Park except during Special Events.
 30
 31 (e) (d) Public nuisance and disorderly conduct. Whoever commits such acts
 32 as are of a nature to corrupt the public morals or outrage the sense of public
 33 decency or affect the peace and quiet of persons who may witness them or
 34 engages in brawling or fighting or engages in such conduct as to constitute a
 35 hazard to the health, safety or welfare of other park visitors shall, upon due
 36 notice, of violation, be classed as a trespasser and ordered to leave the park by
 37 any authorized city representative.
 38
 39 (f) (e) City buildings or pavilion. No hoofed animals shall be permitted in or
 40 upon city buildings or pavilion located at the Parkland Equestrian Center at
 41 Temple Park.
