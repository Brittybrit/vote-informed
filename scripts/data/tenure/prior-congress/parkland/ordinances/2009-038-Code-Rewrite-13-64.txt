 32 Sec. 13-64. Consistency with comprehensive plan and density permitted.
 33 (a) No real property within the city shall be rezoned, and no amendment to the
 34 zoning code shall be adopted and no site plan or plat (or changes to notes on
 35 plats) approved which is not consistent with the comprehensive plan of the city.
 36
 37 (b) Density permitted by zoning shall not exceed the numbers of units permitted
 38 by the land use plan for every parcel of land; the city's land use plan contains the
 39 maximum density permitted in considering a rezoning request. To increase
 40 zoning density, the city shall, along with any other site specific concerns, review
 41 the considerations set forth in Chapter 22, Article XXI, as amended from time to
 42 time, and these considerations shall guide the city's determination on rezoning
 43 requests.

 11 existing land uses, including occupied residential areas. In addition, where there
 12 are approved plats, existing land uses, including occupied residential areas, in
 13 close proximity to the area where the development permit which is under
 14 consideration, then, the city shall consider whether the proposed development
 15 permit, including the uses to be made thereof, is compatible with those areas.
 16 For the purpose of this consideration, and in all instances where compatibility is
 17 used herein, compatibility means land uses (which shall include the structure to
 18 be constructed and the use to be made of the property) that are congruous,
 19 similar and in harmony with one another because they do not create or foster
 20 undesirable health, safety or aesthetic effects arising from direct association of
 21 dissimilar, contradictory, incongruous, or discordant activities or structures,
 22 including the impacts of density, intensity of use, traffic, hours of operation,
 23 aesthetics, noise, vibration, smoke, offensive odors, mass, shadow effect, the
 24 location of structures in close proximity to residential dwellings and other land
 25 use conditions or conditions which are likely to have a detrimental impact on the
 26 existing uses on lands adjacent to the site in question.

 31
 32 ***
 33
 34 (b) Criteria for site plan approval.
 35
 36 (1) General design evaluation. The city shall evaluate the site plan as it
 37 relates to the city's codes and ordinances, the goals, policies and
 38 objectives of the comprehensive plan and conformity with the
 39 requirements of this article and shall consider vehicular circulation,
 40 pedestrian ingress and egress, conformity with the character of the
 41 surrounding area, general layout of the site and whether the development
 42 as presented will enhance the quality of life in the city and promote the
 43 health, safety and welfare of its citizens and promote the city's village
 44 atmosphere. Commercial development shall not be designed or developed
 45 as strip shopping centers and shall specifically be designed to avoid the

 1 appearance, function, or layout of a strip shopping center. In all
 2 commercial developments, designs which provide only a straight row of
 3 stores/restaurants/office or other establishments and do not create or
 4 contain design elements distinguishing the development from a strip
 5 center shall be prohibited. Design elements creating a parklike
 6 atmosphere and which encourage pedestrian usage shall be required.
 7 Retail stores/restaurants/office or other establishments shall be designed
 8 not in straight rows, but in other configurations such as clusters around
 9 plazas or face to face configurations to facilitate easy pedestrian usage
 10 and interaction between uses and a sense of a parklike shopping facility.
 11 The use of plazas integrating the various stores/restaurants/office or other
 12 establishments shall be an essential element to create the small town
 13 village atmosphere. Big box retail establishments are determined to be
 14 inconsistent with the goals of this section and shall only be permitted as
 15 consistent with the regulations set forth in Chapter 22, Article XIII, as
 16 amended from time to time. The city shall also consider the contents of the
 17 administrative report and the impact analysis report. In the event that the
 18 site plan is for a portion of land owned or controlled by the applicant within
 19 the city or the adjacent unincorporated area, the city shall consider the
 20 relationship of the site plan to the development potential of the remainder
 21 of the applicant's land which is the subject of the application. In no event
 22 may a big box exceed fifty thousand (50,000) square feet in size
 23 measured from inside the exterior wall.
