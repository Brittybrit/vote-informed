 19 Section 1. The above Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a part
 20 hereof.

 22 Section 2. The zoning of the Land is hereby changed for approximately
 23 368.999 acres from Palm Beach County Special Agricultural District ("PBC SA") to
 24 Planned Residential Development District ("PRD") and for approximately 10.678 acres
 25 from Palm Beach County Special Agricultural District ("PBC SA") to Planned
 26 Commercial Development District ("PCD"). This rezoning is subject to compliance with
 27 the Master Plan and all development conditions as set forth on Exhibit B (Development
 28 Conditions) as well as compliance with all applicable development regulations. The
 29 Master Plan and Development Conditions shall govern all development of any kind,
 30 which occurs on the Land. Failure to comply with any part of the Master Plan or
 31 development conditions shall result in the City withholding development permits. The
 32 Development Conditions shall be considered part of the Master Plan.
 33
 34 Section 3. The Master Plan as substantially set forth herein shall be the basis
 35 for any site plan application. The approval of a site plan in substantial conformity with
 36 the Master Plan shall be a precondition of any development on the subject property.
 37 Such site approval shall only be valid after a plat is approved in conformity with the
 38 approved site plan.
 39
 40 Section 4. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence of this ordinance is for any
 41 reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity
 42 of any remaining portions of this ordinance.
