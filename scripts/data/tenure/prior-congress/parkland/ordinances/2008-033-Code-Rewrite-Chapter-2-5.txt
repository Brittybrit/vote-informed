 12 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 13 Florida, pursuant to the authority vested in Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, is authorized
 14 and empowered to consider changes to its Code of Ordinances; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the Florida
 17 Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, the Charter of the City of Parkland, and the
 18 police powers of the City; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 163, Florida
 21 Statutes, the City Planning and Zoning Board has reviewed the proposed ordinance
 22 and has determined that the proposed regulations are consistent with the City of
 23 Parkland Comprehensive Plan; and
 24
 25 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 26 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 27
 28 SECTION 1. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained
 29 that the provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the City Code
 30 of the City of Parkland, and that the provisions of Article II Helistop Landing Areas shall
 31 be placed within Chapter 13 and may thereafter be renumbered or placed in a different
 32 section per the direction of the City Clerk.

 34 SECTION 2. Chapter 13 of the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances is hereby
 35 amended as follows:

 29 Sec. 13-176 Exemptions re permits.
 30 The sections of this article requiring approval for a variance as provided in
 31 Section 22-37, Section 22-57 and Chapter 22, Article XXII_of this Code shall not apply
 32 to helicopters landing within the city limits in case of a valid emergency landing required
 33 to preserve life or property or to duly authorized law enforcement officers landing a
 34 helicopter during the conduct of official business, or to helicopters operated by
 35 government or quasi-government agencies landing within the city limits pursuant to
 36 written authorization by the city manager; provided, government and quasi-government
 37 agencies are required to provide the information specified in Section 13-174, and hold
 38 the city harmless from liability, whenever landing helicopters in the city under
 39 authorization from the city manager.
 40
 41 Sec. 13-177 Temporary landing permit.
 42 Any person who wishes to land a helicopter within the city on a temporary,
 43 nonemergency basis must apply in writing to the city manager, and must provide the
 44 information specified in Section 13-174. The City Manager_shall place the application
 45 on the agenda for city commission approval. Approval may be granted by the
 46 commission only upon a clear showing that the public health, safety and welfare are
 47 served thereby. City or City sponsored events shall be exempt from this requirement.
