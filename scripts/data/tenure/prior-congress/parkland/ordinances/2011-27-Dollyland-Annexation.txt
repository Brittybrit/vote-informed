 4 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 5 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, PROVIDING FOR THE ANNEXATION INTO
 6 THE CORPORATE LIMITS OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND OF
 7 APPROXIMATELY 62.20 ACRE PROPERTY KNOWN AS DOLLYLAND,
 8 GENERALLY LOCATED APPROXIMATELY 4.3 MILES WEST OF
 9 PARKSIDE DRIVE ON LOXAHACHEE ROAD, PROPERTY CONTROL
 10 NUMBER 474119010020; CONTAINING PROVISIONS FOR
 11 IMPLEMENTATION AND FILINGS WITH THE APPROPRIATE
 12 GOVERMENTAL AGENGIES; PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE
 13 CITY CODE; PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING
 14 AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 15
 16 WHEREAS, Dolly Fiterman, the owner of the property with the property control number
 17 474119010020 (said land described on Exhibit A), has submitted a petition to annex its property
 18 into the City of Parkland, pursuant to a voluntary annexation; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the Land described in Exhibit A, is the Land owned by Dolly Fiterman, and
 21 shall hereinafter be referred to as "The Land"; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the map attached as Exhibit B reflects the general location of the Land; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the Land is located in unincorporated Broward County contiguous to the
 26 City of Parkland; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the requirements of Section 171.044, Florida Statutes, pertaining to
 29 voluntary annexations, have been complied with in that:
 30
 31 A. The Land is contiguous to the City of Parkland and is reasonably compact
 32 and does not create an enclave;
 33
 34 B. The petitioner, the sole owner of the Land, has sought annexation by a
 35 Petition for Annexation and approves the annexation of the Land into the City of Parkland;
 36
 37 C. This ordinance has been advertised as required by Section 171.044,
 38 Florida Statutes;
 39
 40 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that annexation of the property is in the best
 41 interest of the City of Parkland and the City of Parkland is prepared to provide all necessary
 42 municipal services to the Land; and
 43
 44 WHEREAS, the annexation of the Land into the City of Parkland will promote orderly
 45 planning and the efficient provision of the municipal services to the Land; and

 2 WHEREAS, the City has determined that the Petition for Annexation bears the signature
 3 of the owner(s) of the property of the area proposed to be annexed and constitutes consent to this
 4 annexation;
 5
 6 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 7 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 8
 9 SECTION 1. The above "WHEREAS" clauses are true and correct and made a part
 10 hereof.
 11
 12 SECTION 2. The Land is hereby annexed into the corporate boundaries of the City of
 13 Parkland, Florida, along with all public rights of way lying within the Land and all public rights
 14 of way lying directly adjacent to the Land with the exception of Loxahatchee Road.
 15
 16 SECTION 3. The boundaries of the City of Parkland are re-defined to include the Land
 17 and all public rights of way lying within the Land and all public rights of way lying directly
 18 adjacent to the Land with the exception of Loxahatchee Road.
 19
 20 SECTION 4. The current land use and zoning remain unchanged by this Ordinance and
 21 shall be as provided in Section 171.062(2), Florida Statutes, until such time as the City of
 22 Parkland adopts a comprehensive plan that includes the annexed property.
 23
 24 SECTION 5. The City shall take all necessary actions before the Broward County
 25 Legislative Delegation to comply with all local acts relating to annexation and this annexation is
 26 conditioned upon approval of the Delegation.
 27
 28 SECTION 6. Within seven (7) days after its adoption a copy of this Ordinance shall be
 29 filed with the Clerk of the Circuit Court of Broward County for recordation. Within seven (7)
 30 days of adoption, this Ordinance shall be filed with the Broward County Administrator and with
 31 the Florida Department of State. Within seven (7) days after approval by the Delegation, the City
 32 shall record a Notice of Delegation Approval to which the approved ordinance is attached.
 33
 34 SECTION 7. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 35 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of the
 36 City of Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to accomplish
 37 such intent.
 38
 39 SECTION 8. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for
 40 any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of
 41 any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 42
 43 SECTION 9. This Ordinance shall be effective immediately upon adoption but the
 44 annexation provided herein shall become effective on 12:01 a.m. September 15, 2012; at such
 45 time the Land along with all public rights of way lying within the Land and all public rights of

 PETITION
 FOR
 ANNEXATION OF REAL PROPERTY
 TO
 CITY OF PARKLAND,BROWARD COUNTY,FLORIDA
 Dolly Land, Inc. hereby petitions the City of Parkland for annexation of the parcel described in
 Exhibit "A" attached hereto, pursuant to Section 171.044, Florida Statutes. I qualify that I am
 the president of Dolly Land, Inc. and that Dolly Land, Inc. is the Owner of Record of said parcel,
 pursuant to Exhibit "A." I attest that this property, to the best of my knowledge and
 understanding, is adjacent to the existing City of Parkland boundary and satisfies all other
 criteria under law for annexation. I would also seek to have all rights-of-way adjacent to the
 described parcel annexed at a minimum to the centerline of the right-of-way. It is desire for said
 property to be annexed into the corporate boundaries of the City of Parkland and this Petition
 should be deemed a request for and consent to said annexation.
 Further, the undersigned hereby authorizes the City of Parkland to undertake those actions and
 procedures necessary so that the subject property becomes annexed and part of the City of
 Parkland.
 The undersigned further warrants and represents that all of the persons, firms or corporations
 who own fee simple title to the subject property described in Exhibit "A" have executed this
 Petition and have affixed their names as their ownership interest appears on the title to said
 property.
