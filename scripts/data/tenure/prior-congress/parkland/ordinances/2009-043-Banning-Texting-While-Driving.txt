 4 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 5 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, PROHIBITING THE ACT OF TEXTING
 6 WHILE DRIVING; PROVIDING FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF THE
 7 PROHIBITION; PROVIDING FOR A PENALTY; PROVIDING FOR A
 8 SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 9
 10
 11 WHEREAS, a study by the Virginia Tech Transportation Institute has concluded that
 12 people who send text messages while driving are 23 times more likely to be in a crash; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, in crashes or near crashes, texting takes a driver's focus away from the road
 15 for an average of 4.6 seconds, which is enough time to travel the length of a football field at 55
 16 miles an hour; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, a test by Car and Driver Magazine found that in some cases, a person's
 19 reaction time was harmed more by texting while driving than while driving under the influence
 20 of alcohol; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, in January of 2009, the National Safety Council called on state and
 23 federal lawmakers to ban the use of cell phones and text messaging devices while driving; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the Secretary of the United States Department of Transportation has stated
 26 that, "If it were up to me, I would ban drivers from texting immediately"; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, in a recent poll by the Lingo, a mobile voice application company, found
 29 that 26% of the people admit to driving while texting; and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, a new poll by the AAA Foundation for Traffic Safety showed that 80% of
 32 Americans regarding texting or e-mailing while driving as a very serious safety hazard; and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, according to the New York Times, more than 110,000,000,000 text
 35 messages were sent in the United States during the month of December 2008 alone, a tenfold
 36 increase in just 3 years; and
 37
 38 WHEREAS, texting and portable email are valuable to consumers, businesses, and
 39 private individuals throughout the United States, but those services also create an extreme risk
 40 when used by individuals while operating motor vehicles; and
 41
 42 WHEREAS, a 2008 study by Nationwide Insurance found that 20 percent of drivers in
 43 the United States send text messages while operating motor vehicles; and
 44
 45 WHEREAS, another study by the University of Utah found that college students using
 46 a driving simulator were 8 times more likely to have an accident while texting; and

 2 WHEREAS, the City Commission is not unmindful of provisions within Section 316,
 3 Florida Statutes, which limit the ability of municipalities to regulate the use of commercial
 4 mobile radio services and other electronic communications devices while in a motor vehicle;
 5 and
 6
 7 WHEREAS, notwithstanding this provision, the City Commission believes that,
 8 pursuant to Section 316, the specific act of texting while operating a motor vehicle may be the
 9 legitimate subject of municipal regulations; and
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that the threat to the health, safety and
 12 welfare of the citizens of the City of Parkland from this activity is so great that it demands
 13 action by the City Commission;
 14
 15 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 16 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 17
 18 SECTION 1. Definitions.
 19
 20 (a) "Text message" means a message sent or received via a process using wireless
 21 handsets. For the purposes of this section, an email shall be considered a"text message."
 22
 23 (b) "Wireless handset" means a portable electronic device capable of transmitting or
 24 receiving data in the form of a text message.
 25
 26 SECTION 2. No person shall use a wireless handset to compose, send or read text
 27 messages while driving a motor vehicle (which term shall include, but not be limited to,
 28 motorcycles) in the City of Parkland. Any person driving a motor vehicle who is operating a
 29 wireless handset while such vehicle is in motion in the City of Parkland shall be presumed to be
 30 composing, sending, or reading a text message. The presumption created by this subdivision
 31 shall be rebuttable by evidence showing that the driver was not composing, sending or reading a
 32 text message.
 33
 34 Not withstanding the provisions herein, this section shall not be construed to prohibit the
 35 use of a wireless handset inside a motor vehicle to compose, send or read a text message by:
 36
 37 (a) any (1) law enforcement, public safety or police officers, emergency services, (2)
 38 first aid, or emergency medical technicians or personnel, or (3) fire safety officials, while
 39 performing officials duties;
 40
 41 (b) a driver using a wireless handset to contact an individual listed in paragraph one
 42 of this subdivision to report an emergency situation; or
 43
 44 (c) a driver using a wireless handset inside a motor vehicle while such vehicle is
 45 parked, standing or stopped and is removed from the flow of traffic, in accordance with
 46 applicable laws or rules, or is stopped due to the inoperability of such vehicle.

 3 SECTION 3. Any person violating this ordinance shall be guilty of a fine of $100.
 4 Violations of this ordinance shall be enforced in the same manner as other violations of Section
 5 316, Florida Statutes, on municipal roads.
 6
 7 SECTION 4. The prohibitions set forth herein shall apply to the act of texting while
 8 operating a motor vehicle as set forth above, where said vehicle is operating on roads located
 9 within the City of Parkland.
 10
 11 SECTION 5. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 12 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of the
 13 City of Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to accomplish
 14 such intent.
 15
 16 SECTION 6. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for
 17 any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of
 18 any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 19
 20 SECTION 7. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 21 passage and adoption.
