 28 SECTION 1. Chapter 11, Article VI. of the Code of Ordinance is hereby amended and
 29 states as follows:
 30 Sec. 11-60. Definitions.
 31 (a) Alarm system means any mechanical or electrical device which is designed or used for the
 32 detection of an unauthorized entry into a building, structure, facility or enclosed area, or for
 33 alerting others of the commission of an unlawful act within a building structure, facility or
 34 enclosed area, or both, and which is designed to emit an audible siren and/or alert a monitoring
 35 party of either the unauthorized entry into the property; a medical emergency; the presence of a
 36 fire; or other emergency requiring the assistance of law enforcement, fire department or
 37 emergency medical personnel. Alarm systems include, but are not limited to, telephone alarm
 38 devices, audible alarms and proprietor alarms. Excluded from the definition of alarm systems
 39 are devices which are not designed or used to register alarms that are audible, visible or
 40 perceptible outside of the protected building, structure, facility or enclosed area; or auxiliary
 41 devices installed by telephone companies to protect telephone systems from damage or
 42 disruption of service.
 43 (b) Audible alarm means a device designed for the detection of unauthorized entry or premises
 44 and/or a medical emergency; the presence of a fire; or other emergency requiring the assistance
 45 of law enforcement, fire department or emergency medical personnel, which generates an
 46 audible sound when it is activated.
 47 (c) City means Parkland, Florida.

 1 (d) Alarm signal means the audible sound or a transmission of a signal or a message as the
 2 result of the activation of an alarm system or an audible alarm.
 3 (e) False alarm means the activation of an alarm system's audible siren and/or the notification
 4 of a monitoring party when there is no unauthorized entry into the property; there is no medical
 5 emergency; there is no fire; and there is no other emergency requiring law enforcement, fire or
 6 medical personnel, through mechanical failure, malfunction, improper installation or the
 7 negligence of the owner or lessee of an alarm system or of his employees or agents. Such
 8 terminology does not include, for example, alarms caused by hurricanes, tornados, earthquakes
 9 or other violent conditions.
 10 (f) Notice, unless otherwise specified, means written notice, given either by certified mail,
 11 return receipt requested, by personal service upon the addressee or posted on the property.
 12 (g) Person means any natural person or individual, or any firm, partnership, association,
 13 limited partnership, sole proprietorship or corporation or any business entity whatsoever.
 14 (h) Law enforcement officer means the Broward Sheriff's Office.
 15 (i) Telephone alarm device means any device which, when activated, automatically transmits
 16 by telephone line a recorded alarm message or electronic or mechanical alarm signal to any
 17 telephone instrument installed at a law enforcement office or to any telephone instrument
 18 installed at a facility as part of the 911 system.
 19 (j) Alarm business Any person engaged in the selling, leasing, installing, maintaining or
 20 servicing of alarm systems who sells, leases installs, maintains or services alarm systems
 21 located within the City limits.
 22 (k) Alarm user Any person, business, firm, partnership, corporation, association, organization,
 23 company or other entity deemed by the City to be an alarm user that possesses and/or uses
 24 and/or operates an alarm system within the City limits.
 25 (1) Excessive false alarms A third or subsequent false alarm occurring during the calendar
 26 year, which was not caused by extreme weather conditions.

 28 Sec. 11-61. False Alarm Fees.
 29 (a) False Alarms:
 30 1. First three false alarms free of charge
 31 2. 4th false alarms $100.00
 32 3. 5th and above false alarms .$250.00
 33
 34 (b) Failure to pay within 30 days, service fee each $35.00
 35
 36 Sec. 11-62. Prohibition of certain telephone alarm devices.
 37 (a) Except for telephone alarm devices utilized by governmental units or agencies, it is
 38 unlawful for any person, firm, corporation or association to install, maintain, own, possess,
 39 operate or use any telephone alarm device regulated or programmed to make connection with:
 40 (1) Any telephone or telephone line installed in any facility of any law enforcement office;
 41 (2) Any telephone or telephone line installed in any facility as part of the 911 system.
 42 (b) Telephone alarm devices are permitted when not connected directly to a law enforcement
 43 office or any facility as part of the 911 system, but they are subject to all other provisions of
 44 this article.
 45 (c) Any alarm system which automatically telephones a law enforcement agency over
 46 telephone lines exclusively used by the public or request emergency services or information.
 47 (d) Any alarm systems which do not have an auxiliary power supply which activates in the
 48 event of power failure or electrical outage.

 1 Sec. 11-63. Removal of unlawful systems.
 2 The law enforcement officer, when there is probable cause to believe that there has been a
 3 violation of section 11-62(a)(1) or (2) of this article, shall order the owner, operator or lessee to
 4 disconnect and cease operation of the system within seventy-two (72) hours of receipt of the
 5 order. Notice shall be by personal service. If personal service cannot be made, notice shall be
 6 by certified mail, return receipt requested. Any telephone alarm device installed prior to the
 7 effective date of the ordinance from which this article is derived shall have been removed
 8 within thirty (30) days of such date. It shall be unlawful for any person as defined in this article
 9 to fail to comply with the provisions of the article.
 10
 11 Sec. 11-64. Deactivation of audible alarms within thirty minutes.
 12 It is unlawful to maintain any alarm system or audible alarm that does not deactivate within
 13 thirty (30) minutes of its activation.
 14
 15 Sec. 11-65. Intentional activation of alarms prohibited if no emergency exists.
 16 It is unlawful for any person to intentionally activate any alarm system or audible alarm except
 17 in the event of an actual or attempted felony, misdemeanor, fire or medical emergency or to
 18 periodically test the alarm equipment after giving advance notice to the law enforcement
 19 officer.
 20
 21 Sec. 11-66. False alarms.
 22 For a law enforcement agency, fire department, or emergency medical personnel response to an
 23 incident of a false alarm as the result of the activation of any alarm system or audible alarm, the
 24 following fees and procedures shall apply:
 25 (1) For a response to premises at which no other false alarm has occurred within the preceding
 26 six (6) months, no fee shall be charged; but the person having or maintaining the alarm system
 27 or audible alarm shall, within three (3) working days after notice to do so, complete a written
 28 report to the law enforcement officer on forms prescribed by the law enforcement officer,
 29 setting forth the cause of the false alarm if known; the corrective action taken; whether the
 30 alarm system has been inspected by a licensed alarm service company and such other
 31 information as the law enforcement officer may reasonably require to determine the cause of
 32 such false alarm and the necessary corrective action.
 33 (2) No fee for a second or third response to premises within six (6) months after a first
 34 response shall be charged; the law enforcement officer shall be authorized to cause the alarm
 35 system at the premises to be inspected during reasonable hours by an independent alarm service
 36 company or a duly authorized agent of the law enforcement office with a written report
 37 furnished to the law enforcement officer by the inspector within seventy-two (72) hours.
 38 Written notice shall be either issued to an adult occupant of the residence, structure or building
 39 or in no such person is available shall be left in a prominent place at the residence, structure or
 40 building_ The law enforcement officer shall prescribe necessary corrective action, and shall
 41 give notice to the person having or maintaining the alarm system of the conditions and
 42 requirements of this article. All costs of such inspections, including the alarm service
 43 company's fee, shall be paid by the person who owns or maintains the alarm system or audible
 44 alarm, as the case may be.
 45 (3) For a fourth response to premises by City's law enforcement agency, fire department, or
 46 emergency medical personnel within twelve (12) months after a third response (as described in
 47 Section (2) above), and for all succeeding responses within six (6) months of the most recent
 48 response, a fee of one hundred dollars ($100.00) shall be charged, and if the fourth false alarm

 28 fine of one hundred dollars ($100.00).
 29 Furthermore, if there has been more than one (1) false alarm within the past six (6) months,
 30 your alarm system is subject to inspection at your expense by an independent alarm service
 31 company or by this department.

 33 your alarm system to be disconnected, and may subject you to a fine, incarceration or both.
 34
 35 Sec. 11-67. Exception for new installations.
 36 All newly installed alarm systems shall be exempt from the regulations of section 11-66 above,
 37 in-order to allow alarm installers and users to adjust the system. New installations shall be
 38 subject to the regulations of section 11-66 above, thirty (30) days after installation is complete.
 39
 40 Sec. 11-68. Penalties.
 41 Violation of the provisions and prohibitions of this article shall constitute an offense and
 42 violation of a city ordinance. Any person who is convicted of a violation by a court of
 43 competent jurisdiction shall be punished by a fine not to exceed five hundred dollars ($500.00)
 44 or by imprisonment in the county jail not to exceed sixty (60) days or by both such fine and
 45 imprisonment.
