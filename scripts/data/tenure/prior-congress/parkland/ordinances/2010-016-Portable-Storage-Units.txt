 41 Sec. 22-382. Portable/Temporary Storage Units.
 42
 43 (1) Portable/temporary storage units (units used for portable storage,
 44 excluding construction trailers) may be located in the community facilities
 45 zoning district.

 1 (2) Prior to commencing business within the City, any company or vendor
 2 that provides the portable/temporary storage units to properties within the
 3 City must obtain a business tax receipt from the city outlining the obligations
 4 and requirements prior to conducting business within the city.
 5
 6 (3) The property owner must provide a cash bond or letter of credit to the
 7 City in the amount of $500.00 for each portable/temporary storage unit prior
 8 to the placement of the unit on site, to ensure the timely removal of the
 9 unit(s) and compliance with this Section unless such fee is waived by the City
 10 Commission.
 11
 12 (4) The maximum cumulative square footage for all portable/temporary
 13 storage units on any site shall not exceed one hundred thirty (130) square
 14 feet in area.
 15
 16 (5) Portable/temporary storage units shall only be placed in a site location
 17 as approved by the Planning and Zoning Division and shall meet all required
 18 setbacks for the applicable zoning district.
 19
 20 (6) No portable/temporary storage unit shall remain at a site in excess of six
 21 (6) consecutive months.. Notwithstanding the time limitations as stated
 22 above, all units shall be removed immediately upon the issuance of a
 23 hurricane watch by a recognized governmental agency. The removal of the
 24 unit(s) shall be the responsibility of the provider of the unit(s) or the property
 25 owner.
 26
 27 (7) Prior to placing a portable/temporary storage unit on any site, the
 28 provider of the unit(s) and/or the property owner or renter must apply for a
 29 site permit. The site permit application shall be in a form provided by the City
 30 and issued by the Planning and Zoning Division. The exterior of the storage
 31 unit shall have a weatherproof clear pouch which must display the site
 32 permit.
 33
 34 (8) The provider and/or property owner or renter of the portable/temporary
 35 storage unit shall be responsible to ensure that the unit is in good condition,
 36 free from evidence of deterioration, weathering, discoloration, rust, ripping,
 37 tearing or other holes or breaks. When not in use, the unit shall be kept
 38 locked. No unit shall be utilized to store illegal or hazardous materials.
 39
 40 (9) It shall be unlawful for any person to place or permit the placement of a
 41 portable/temporary storage unit on property which he or she owns, rents,
 42 occupies or controls without first obtaining a site permit from the Planning
 43 and Zoning Division.
 44
 45 (10) Failure to comply with any part of this Section shall be punished by a
 46 fine not to exceed five hundred dollars ($500.00) or imprisonment for a term
