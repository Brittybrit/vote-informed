 17 DIVISION 4. PLANNING AND ZONING BOARD*
 18
 19 Sec. 2-53. Established.
 20 There is hereby created and established as an agency of the city a board
 21 to be known as the planning and zoning board, hereinafter called the "board,"
 22 with membership, powers, duties and responsibilities as set forth hereinafter.
 23
 24 Sec. 2-54. Composition.
 25 (a) The board shall be comprised of seven (7) members, five (5) of the
 26 members appointed to membership individually by each commissioner in
 27 accordance with Ordinance No. 90-21 of the city, and the sixth and
 28 seventh members to be nominated by any member of the commission and
 29 appointed by a majority vote of the commission. There shall also be two
 30 (2) alternate members on the planning and zoning board to be appointed
 31 in the same manner as the sixth and seventh members.
 32
 33 (b) A quorum shall consist of four (4) members. For any item to be
 34 recommended to the city commission for approval, a majority vote of the
 35 members of the board is necessary. In case of a tie vote, the item is to be
 36 considered to be not recommended to the city commission for approval.
 37
 38 Sec. 2-55. Powers and duties.
 39 The board shall have the following powers and duties:
 40
 41 (1) It shall serve as the local planning agency of the city and in the
 42 manner and to the extent provided by Section 163.3161 et seq.,
 43 F.S., and amendments thereto.

 1 (2) It shall review and thereafter submit to the commission with
 2 recommendations thereon proposals initiated by itself or by others
 3 for changes in the zoning regulations or in the official zoning map of
 4 the city.
 5
 6 (3) It shall review plats of land subdivisions lying wholly or partly
 7 within the city prior to submittal of the plats to the commission for its
 8 approval, under the established procedures for plat approval, prior
 9 to recordation; and the board shall transmit its recommendations
 10 regarding such plats to the commission prior to final decision by the
 11 commission.
 12
 13 (4) On its own initiative or when requested to do so by the
 14 commission, the board shall report to the commission its findings
 15 and/or recommendations pertaining to any matter within the
 16 purview of comprehensive planning for the development and/or
 17 improvement of the city.
 18
 19 (5) To serve as a site review committee and submit its
 20 recommendations to the commission.
 21
 22 (6) To hear and decide appeals when it is alleged that there is an
 23 error in any order, requirement, decision or determination made by
 24 an administrative official in the enforcement of any zoning
 25 ordinance or regulation of the city, county or state.
 26
 27 (7) To authorize upon appeal such variance from the terms of the
 28 chapter as will not be contrary to the public interest when, owing to
 29 special conditions, a literal enforcement of the provisions of the
 30 chapter would result in unnecessary and undue hardship. In order
 31 to authorize any variance from the terms of the chapter, the
 32 planning and zoning board must find that all variance standards
 33 and conditions have been met pursuant to article XXII.
 34
 35 (8) The board shall perform such other duties as may be assigned
 36 to it from time to time by the commission, by ordinance or
 37 otherwise.
 38
 39 Sec. 2-56. Annual report.
 40 On or before October 1 each year, the board shall submit to the
 41 commission a written report of its activities since its last preceding report,
 42 together with its recommendations, if any, concerning development and
 43 improvement of the area subject to jurisdiction of the city.

 3 Sec. 2-57. Designation.
 4 Pursuant to Section 163.3167, F.S., the planning and zoning board of the
 5 city is hereby designated and established as the local planning agency of the
 6 city.
 7
 8 Sec. 2-58. Function.
 9 (a) The community development department of the city is hereby directed
 10 to prepare the comprehensive plan and any amendments thereto, and
 11 forward same to the local planning agency for their review.
 12
 13 (b) The functions of the local planning agency are to review, revise, and
 14 or propose any amendments to the comprehensive plan of the city after
 15 hearings to be held upon due public notice and to make recommendations
 16 to the commission regarding the adoption of amendments to the plan, or
 17 elements or portions thereof.
 18
 19 Sec. 2-59. Rules of procedure; membership; staff.
 20 The rules of procedure, composition, officers, financial support and
 21 staffing for the local planning agency shall be the same as the rules of procedure
 22 presently in effect for the planning and zoning board except as noted below.
 23
 24 Consistent with Section 163.3174 of the Florida Statutes a representative
 25 of the Broward County School Board shall be permitted to serve on the City of
 26 Parkland's Local Planning Agency in a nonvoting capacity to the extent
 27 authorized by any existing interlocal agreement between the two (2) jurisdictions.
 28
 29 Sec. 2-60. Meetings.
 30 (a) All meetings of the local planning agency shall be public meetings,
 31 and the records of the same shall be public records.
 32
 33 (b) It is the intention of the commission that the planning and zoning
 34 board's hearings and procedures shall give the widest opportunities for
 35 public comments and discussion.
