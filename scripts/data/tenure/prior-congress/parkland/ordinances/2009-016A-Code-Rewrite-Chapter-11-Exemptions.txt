 33 Sec. 11-57. Exemptions.
 34
 35 (1) Open burning will be allowed for the outdoor noncommercial preparation of
 36 food in a stove, oven, barbecue pit or fireplace for the purpose of cooking,
 37 provided the fire is properly contained and is not used to burn yard waste, house
 38 refuse, garbage, recyclable materials or commercial debris.
 39
 40 (2) Open burning will be allowed when any outdoor heating device using
 41 approved fuel is used for the purposes of frost protection.
 42
 43 (3) Open burning will be allowed for the flaring of waste gases for reasons of
 44 safety, as long as excessive visible emissions do not exceed the allowable
 45 limitations.

 1 (4) Open burning will be allowed for agricultural purposes on properties over
