 1 ORDINANCE NO. 2010-03
 2
 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA
 4 AMENDING APPENDIX A ENTITLED "FRANCHISES" ARTICLE III ENTITLED
 5 "WASTE MANAGEMENT INC. OF FLORIDA" ADOPTING THE AMENDED AND
 6 RESTATED FRANCHISE AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASTE MANAGEMENT INC.
 7 OF FLORIDA AND THE CITY OF PARKLAND; EXTENDING THE FRANCHISE
 8 FOR FIVE (5) YEARS; AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO EXECUTE A
 9 CONTRACT WITH WASTE MANAGEMENT INC. OF FLORIDA CONCERNING
 10 THE COLLECTION AND REMOVAL OF TRASH, GARBAGE AND OTHER
 11 REFUSE WITHIN THE CITY OF PARKLAND, EXTENDING THE TERM OF THE
 12 FRANCHISE; PROVIDING FOR COLLECTION OF THE FRANCHISE FEE AS A
 13 SPECIAL ASSESSMENT AND OTHERWISE AMENDING THE FRANCHISE
 14 AGREEMENT; PROVIDING A CONFLICTS CLAUSE; PROVIDING A
 15 SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 16
 17 WHEREAS, there is an immediate and continuing need for the collection and disposal of trash,
 18 garbage and other refuse found within municipal limits of the City of Parkland; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the City in 2008 adopted a new Franchise Agreement with Waste Management Inc.
 21 of Florida for the collection and disposal of trash, garbage and other refuse; and
 22 WHEREAS, service provided by Waste Management Inc. of Florida has been rendered with
 23 satisfactory results and adequate efficiency; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City would like the costs for service provided by Waste Management Inc. of
 26 Florida to be placed on the ad valorem tax billing; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the City and its residents are obtaining benefits by providing for the cost on the ad
 29 valorem tax billing; and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, the 2008 Franchise Agreement did not provide for ad valorem tax billing so
 32 amendments to the Franchise is required; and
 33
 34 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 35 PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 36
 37 Section 1: That the City Commission hereby approved the Amended and Restated Franchise
 38 Agreement Between City of Parkland and Waste Management Inc. of Florida, which is attached hereto
 39 and the City Manager is hereby authorized to enter into and execute the aforementioned Amended and
 40 Restated Franchise Agreement with Waste Management Inc. of Florida.
 41
 42 Section 2: Appendix A, Article III of the Code of Ordinances is hereby repealed, and the
 43 attached Amended and Restated Franchise Agreement Between City of Parkland and Waste
 44 Management Inc. of Florida, as may be amended from time to time, is hereby adopted by the City
 45 Commission.
 46
 47 Section 3: If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for any reason
 48 held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of any remaining
 49 portions of this ordinance.
