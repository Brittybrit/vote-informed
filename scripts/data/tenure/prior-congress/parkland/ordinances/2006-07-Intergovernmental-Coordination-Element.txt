 4 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 5 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, RELATING TO ADOPTION OF A NEW
 6 POLICY WITHIN THE INTERGOVERNMENTAL COORDINATION
 7 ELEMENT REGARDING COORDINATION WITH FEDERAL,
 8 STATE, AND LOCAL AGENCIES IN MANAGING NATURAL
 9 AREAS AND OPEN SPACE; PROVIDING A REPEALER
 10 CLAUSE; PROVIDING A SAVINGS CLAUSE; AND PROVIDING
 11 AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the Florida
 14 Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, the Charter of the City of Parkland, and the
 15 police powers of the City; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has determined that a new Comprehensive
 18 Text Amendment to require coordination between groups in managing natural and open
 19 area is necessary; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that the creation of a new regulation to
 22 further coordination between groups will further the goals of the Comprehensive Plan;
 23 and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that the reasonable regulations contained
 26 herein will protect the health, safety, and welfare of the residents of the City; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 163, Florida
 29 Statutes, the City Planning and Zoning Board, acting as the Local Planning Agency, has
 30 reviewed the proposed text amendment on February 9, 2006 and by a vote of 7-0 has
 31 determined that the proposed language is consistent with the City of Parkland
 32 Comprehensive Plan.
 33
 34 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 35 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 36 SECTION 1: Intergovenmental Coordination Element Policy 1.4.5 is hereby
 37 added:
 38
 39 Policy 1.4.5 The City shall coordinate with other federal, state and local agencies or
 40 nonprofit organizations in managing natural areas or open space.
 41
 42 SECTION 2. The City Manager is hereby authorized to transmit this proposed
 43 comprehensive plan amendment to the Florida Department of Community Affairs for
 44 review pursuant to Chapter 163, Florida Statutes.
