 1 ORDINANCE NO. 2009- 39
 2
 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, PROVIDING FOR EXEMPTION OF CERTAIN
 5 CITY PROJECTS FROM DEVELOPMENT REVIEW REQUIREMENTS
 6 WHERE SAID PROJECTS ARE FOR RECREATIONAL FACILITIES THAT
 7 DO NOT CONTAIN AN ENCLOSED BUILDING; AMENDING SECTION 13-
 s 63 TO CREATE A NEW SUBSECTION 16 TO PROVIDE FOR A LIMITED
 9 EXEMPTION OF CERTAIN CITY PROJECTS; PROVIDING FOR
 10 INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE; PROVIDING FOR A SEVERABILITY
 11 CLAUSE; PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13 WHEREAS, in the State of Florida, cities may exempt themselves from all
 14 development review requirements for City governmental projects; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland wishes to apply this requirement in a limited
 17 fashion for recreational facilities that do not contain an enclosed structure; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that, in order to promote efficiency, a
 20 limited exemption for certain City projects from site plan and development review
 21 requirements is appropriate;
 22
 23 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 24 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 25
 26 SECTION 1. Section 13-63 of the City Code, Land Development Regulations, is
 27 hereby amended to include the following subsection 16:
 28
 29 16. Developments on City owned property which are for recreational
 30 purposes and do not contain an enclosed building shall be exempt from all
 31 requirements of Chapter 13 and Chapter 22 of the City Code.
 32
 33 SECTION 2. Should any section or provision of this ordinance or any portion
 34 thereof, any paragraph, sentence or word be declared by a court of competent
 35 jurisdiction to be invalid, such decision shall not affect the validity of the remainder
 36 hereof as a whole or part thereof other than the part declared to be invalid.
 37
 38 SECTION 3. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 39 passage and adoption.
 40
 41 PASSED this 19th day of August 2009, upon first reading.
 42
 43 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 7th day of October 2009, upon second and final
 44 reading.
