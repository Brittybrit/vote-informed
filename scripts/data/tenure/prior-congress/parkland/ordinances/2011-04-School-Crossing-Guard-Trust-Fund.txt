 1 There is hereby established a surcharge on parking fees and imposed a "school
 2 crossing guard revenue account". The City of Parkland may impose and collect a
 3 parking fine surcharge in the amount of five dollars ($5.00) upon each civil penalty
 4 imposed for violation of Section 20-4 of the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances which
 5 shall be deposited in said revenue account when received, as provided in Florida
 6 Statute 318.21(11), as amended. These funds shall be used only to defray the cost of
 7 the school crossing guard program, and any start-up, administrative, or
 8 implementation costs.
