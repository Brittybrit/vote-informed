 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA RELATING TO PERMITTING VALET
 5 PARKING WITHIN THE CITY'S LIMITS; AMENDING CHAPTER
 6 22, ARTICLE VI ENTITLED "OFF-STREET PARKING AND
 7 LOADING" OF THE CODE OF ORDINANCES; PROVIDING A
 8 CONFLICTS CLAUSE; PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE;
 9 AND PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE, CASE NUMBER 01-
 10 ZTA-08.
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 13 Florida, pursuant to the authority vested in Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, is authorized and
 14 empowered to consider changes to its Code of Ordinances; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the Florida
 17 Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, the Charter of the City of Parkland, and the
 18 police powers of the City; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 163, Florida Statutes,
 21 the City's Planning and Zoning Board by a 6-0 vote reviewed the proposed ordinance and
 22 determined that the proposed regulations are consistent with the City of Parkland
 23 Comprehensive Plan; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, several businesses within the City of Parkland have undertaken or
 26 wish to provide motor vehicle valet parking services for their patrons; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, providing valet parking services normally requires the use of parking
 29 aisles to receive motor vehicles, park motor vehicles, or both; and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, the City Commission specifically finds that the regulations contained
 32 in this ordinance are essential in the best interest of the public health, safety and welfare.
 33
 34 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 35 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 36
 37 SECTION 1. Chapter 22, Article VI of the Code of Ordinances relating to valet
 38 parking is hereby amended by adding Sections 22-140 through 22-150 as follows:
 39
 40 Sec. 22-140. Definitions.
 41
 42 As used in this article, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them in
 43 this section, unless context clearly indicates otherwise:
 44
 45 Business shall mean any commercial enterprise, whether for profit or not for profit or any
 46 operation having or required to have an occupational license.

 1 Permittee shall mean the persons or entities who have received a revocable use permit
 2 allowing for the operation of a valet service and shall include the owner of the business
 3 seeking valet parking service as well as the provider of the valet parking service if
 4 different.
 5
 6 Staging area shall mean the area in which the employees of the valet parking service will
 7 receive motor vehicles for passenger loading and unloading.
 8
 9 Valet service or valet parking service shall mean the use of employees of a business, or
 10 the use of an independent contractor by a business, to provide patrons of the business
 11 and others with a motor vehicle reception point and parking service, which may be
 12 provided at no charge or on a for-compensation basis.
 13
 14 Sec. 22-141. Prohibition.
 15
 16 It shall be unlawful for a business located within a commercially zoned district in the City
 17 to provide valet parking services in connection with the conduct of business absent
 18 obtaining a permit pursuant to the provisions of this article.
 19
 20
 21 Sec. 22-142. Permits.
 22
 23 The owner or operator of any business located within a commercially zoned district in the
 24 City may provide valet parking service in connection with the conduct of that business
 25 after obtaining a permit pursuant to this section. All valet parking service shall operate in
 26 a manner consistent with this section. Although valet parking service for a business may
 27 be provided by an independent contractor, the owner of the business must be a joint
 28 permittee.
 29
 30 Sec. 22-143. Permit expiration unless revoked.
 31
 32 Permits shall expire on September 30 of each calendar year and shall be issued for that
 33 portion of the year remaining from the date of issue to September 30. All permits must be
 34 renewed annually.
 35
 36 Sec. 22-144. Determination of valet services.
 37
 38 No more than 25% of the total required parking; per commercial development; shall be
 39 utilized for valet services. Valet service parking shall be reviewed based on a first come,
 40 first serve basis. Parking spaces for valet services shall be at a location deemed by the
 41 City, in its sole discretion, to create the least imposition on the operation of businesses at
 42 the center.
 43
 44 The location of the valet parking spaces shall be indicated on a site plan, submitted to the
 45 Planning and Zoning Department by the person or entity owning the commercial
 46 development where the business requesting the valet services to be located, the person
 47 or entity owning the business that will use the valet service as well as the person or entity

 1 actually providing the valet service. The process for identifying such valet parking spaces
 2 shall be submitted as a revocable valet parking services permit with all associated fees.
 3
 4 There shall be no signage on site for the valet parking spaces that restrict such parking
 5 spaces to "valet only", however such spaces may be blocked off by orange construction
 6 cones during the hours of valet service as approved per the valet parking services permit.

 8 The valet parking service operation shall not interfere with the accessibility of handicap
 9 parking spaces.

 11 Sec. 22-145. Applications.
 12
 13 k)plications for a revocable valet parking services permit shall be submitted to the City's
 14 Planning and Zoning Department accompanied by a non-refundable $250.00 application
 15 filing fee for each business. The application shall meet the following standards:
 16 (1) The application must be jointly submitted by the person or entity owning the
 17 commercial development where the business requesting the valet services is
 18 located, the person or entity owning the business that will use the valet service as
 19 well as the person or entity actually providing the valet service, if not an employee
 20 of the owner of the business;
 21 (2) The form of the application shall be supplied by the City and the applicants
 22 shall provide all information requested. Such application shall not be reviewed by
 23 the Planning and Zoning until such time as all required information is submitted
 24 and complete;
 25 (3) The application shall be accompanied by a schematic plan, which shall clearly
 26 depict the location of the staging area and the business proposed to be served.
 27 The schematic must show the exact number of parking spaces the applicants
 28 anticipate will be used by the business and valet service and the current parking
 29 regulations at the locations intended to be used. The applicant may request
 30 signaqe associated with the staging area to identify valet parking;
 31 (4) The application shall also include a schematic traffic circulation plan, which
 32 shall provide the following information:
 33 a. The primary route between the staging area and the vehicle parking
 34 areas;
 35 b. The hours of operation of the valet services;
 36 c. The number of valet parkers intended to be employed and the period(s)
 37 of the day during which they shall be employed;
 38 d. The projected number of trips from the staging area to the parking
 39 areas, and from the parking areas to the staging area during the normal
 40 operation hours of the valet service;
 41 e. The location of any additional objects associated with the valet parking
 42 service, to include, but not be limited to, key box and chairs; and
 43 f. A clear pathway, parallel with the street or parking lot, with a minimum
 44 width of five (5) feet shall be maintained for through pedestrian traffic. If
 45 such five (5) foot clear pathway cannot be maintained, no permit shall be
 46 issued. A greater width may be required as a condition of approval.
 47 (5) The applicant shall provide insurance documentation that names the City as
 48 an additional insured;

 16 Sec. 22-146. Staging of motor vehicles in parking aisles.
 17
 18 The revocable valet parking service permit will specify the terms, conditions or
 19 prohibitions applicable. When the staging area will utilize parking aisles, the following
 20 shall apply:
 21 (1) All traffic control devices shall be strictly adhered to.
 22 (2) All applicable statutes, ordinances and regulations concerning movement and
 23 parking of motor vehicles shall be adhered to.
 24 (3) The issuance of a valet parking service permit will give the permittee no
 25 special rights with respect to the use of the parking aisles, different from the rights
 26 of the general public, other than the limited right to temporarily use a staging area
 27 to receive motor vehicles.
 28 (4) At any time the City Manager or his / her designee should determine that the
 29 use of the staging area by the permittee is creating a situation which is adverse to
 30 the public health, safety and welfare or, the use of the parking aisle by the public in
 31 general, the valet service permit may be further conditioned, by the City Manager
 32 or his / her designee, so as to eliminate adverse impacts, or the permit may be
 33 revoked.
 34 (5) Staging areas shall be restricted to the staging areas permitted pursuant to
 35 the valet parking service permit. There shall be no storage of vehicles in the area
 36 used for staging. A vehicle will be considered stored if it remains in the staging
 37 area for more than five minutes. Staging shall not occur in any location other than
 38 in the area provided for staging as indicated in the permit. Egress and ingress of
 39 the commercial development shall not be obstructed by unattended vehicles
 40 waiting to be parked. At no time shall unattended vehicles be left in the staging
 41 area.
 42
 43 Sec. 22-147. Employees.
 44
 45 A valet parking service shall require employees and independent contractors of the valet
 46 parking service to meet the following requirements:
 47 (1) All employees which operate motor vehicles shall have a valid state driver's
 48 license and shall follow all traffic regulations.

 1 (2) All employees shall display a name tag identifying the valet company, or in
 2 the event that the valet parkers are employees of the business the name tag shall
 3 display the name of the business, and the individual's name.
 4 (3) All employees shall be in a similar uniform.
 5 (4) All employees shall comply with all applicable laws, statutes that relate to
 6 traffic safety.
 7
 8 Sec. 22-148. Inspection.
 9
 10 The City Manager, his / her authorized designee, or a code enforcement officer of the
 11 City shall be entitled to inspect the operation of the valet service with such frequency as
 12 he / she shall deem warranted in order to determine that the permittee is complying with
 13 the terms of this article, as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and regulations. By
 14 accepting a valet service permit, the permittee agrees to allow inspections to take place
 15 and shall cooperate fully with the City in the conduct of the inspection to assist the City to
 16 obtain such information as is required in order to determine compliance by permittee.
 17
 18 Sec. 22-149. Penalties.
 19
 20 Any business or valet found to be in violation under the terms of their permit shall be
 21 fined in the following manner:
 22 (A) Minor offenses, include but is not limited to, the following:
 23 (1). Operating valet parking service business without a valid City of
 24 Parkland Business Tax Receipt;
 25 (2) Employees of the valet parking service business who are not wearing a
 26 uniform or identification tag.
 27 (3) Penalty:
 28 (a) Fine shall be a minimum of $25.00 per offense.
 29 (B) All other offenses, include but is not limited to, the following:
 30 (1) Operation without a valet parking service permit;
 31 (2) No insurance coverage
 32 (3) Penalty:
 33 (a) First offense . . . $100.00
 34 (b) Second offense . . . 250.00
 35 (c) Third offense . . . 500.00
 36 Any fine imposed hereunder, if contested, shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the city's
 37 Special Master.
 38
 39 Sec. 22-150. Revocation or modification of permit by city.
 40
 41 The City may revoke or modify any permit issued pursuant to this article for failure of the
 42 valet service to adhere to any standard or requirement of this article; for the violation of
 43 any standards or requirements of this Code, State Statutes, or regulations of any other
 44 governmental entity; said revocation to occur subsequent to three fines having been
 45 imposed and sustained as set forth above. In addition, said permit may be revoked on a
 46 determination that the operations of the permittee conflict with the rights of the public to
 47 use the parking aisles or that the permittee's valet service adversely impacts the business
 48 or property of others.

 1
 2 The City shall follow the following revocation process:
 3 (1) The permittee shall be given written notice of a violation, the action necessary
 4 to correct the same or the modifications required with reference to the permittee's
 5 valet operations. Notice may be hand delivered, posted, or mailed by U.S.
 6 registered mail, return receipt requested. Hand delivery shall include handing the
 7 notice to any employee of the permittee. Posting shall be in an observable location
 8 at the place of business providing the valet service.
 9 (2) The notice shall specify the time for the valet service to correct the violation or
 10 implement modification, in those circumstances where such corrective action is
 11 possible. Should the valet service fail to correct the violation or implement
 12 modifications within the time given, the revocable valet service permit shall be
 13 canceled and shall be of no further force and effect. Once canceled, the permittee
 14 shall cease valet services.
 15 (3) Within five days of the date of the revocation of the permit, the permittee may
 16 appeal the revocation by submitting a written application using the form provided
 17 by the Planning and Zoning Department. All applications shall be accompanied by
 18 the processing fee and other supporting evidence pertaining to the appeal and the
 19 grounds or reasons therefore. The appeal shall be filed with the Planning and
 20 Zoning Department. The Planning and Zoning Department may request
 21 supplemental material in writing.
 22 (4) Review by the planning and zoning board. The planning and zoning board
 23 shall review and decide appeals from any person who has been adversely affected
 24 by a decision of an administrative official in the interpretation or application of the
 25 land development regulations. The planning and zoning board may modify, reverse
 26 or affirm the administrative official's decision interpreting or applying the provisions
 27 of the land development regulations.
 28 (5) Processing.
 29 (1) All applications for administrative appeals shall be scheduled for a
 30 hearing before the planning and zoning board by the planning and zoning
 31 department.
 32 (2) The applicant shall present the appeal at a public hearing before the
 33 planning and zoning board.
 34 (3) The administrator subject to the appeal may present reasons and
 35 documentation supporting the initial decision.
 36 (4) The concurring vote of four (4) of the five (5) board members shall be
 37 necessary to reverse any order, requirement, or decision of the city
 38 administrative staff.
 39 (5) Upon a decision, the planning and zoning board shall issue a written
 40 order to be forwarded to the city clerk that shall be sent to the applicant and
 41 become a part of the public records.
 42 (6) Any decision by the planning and zoning board may be appealed to the
 43 city commission by the applicant or the city administrative staff.
 44
