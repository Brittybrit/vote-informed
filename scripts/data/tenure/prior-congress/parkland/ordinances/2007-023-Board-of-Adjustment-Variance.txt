 29 ARTICLE XXII. PROCEDURES AND REQUIREMENTS FOR VARIANCES AND APPEALS
 30 OF DECISIONS OF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICIALS
 31
 32 Sec. 22-321. Purpose.
 33
 34 It is the purpose of this article to provide for the establishment of procedures and guidelines for
 35 the hearing of appeals from any decision of an administrative official relative to the
 36 enforcement of the requirements of the city's zoning regulations and the authorization of
 37 variances from the requirements of the city's zoning regulations.
 38
 39 Sec. 22-322 Variance Application Process
 40
 41 (a) An application for a variance may be filed at the initiation of the city commission, city
 42 manager or the owner of a property which is the subject of the variance.
 43 (b) Applications for a variance shall be scheduled for consideration by the planning and zoning
 44 board within forty-five (45) days after the City Manager or his designee has determined
 45 whether all pertinent information has been provided.
 46 (c) The applicant shall present the variance proposal before the Planning and Zoning Board
 47 and City Commission after the city has notified the applicant of the date set for the hearing.
 48 (d) At the time of the hearing on the application, the city shall allow both proponents and
 49 opponents of the application to be heard, in order to determine if the variance complies with

 1 the standards set forth in this chapter.
 2 (e) After receiving all relevant materials and information, the Planning and Zoning Board
 3 shall, by vote of simple majority of members present and voting, make a recommendation on

 7 hold a quasi-judicial hearing on the matter and render a decision within seven (7) days of the

 10 Sec. 22-323. Variance standards, guidelines and review criteria
 11
 12 (a) A variance from the terms of this chapter shall not be granted unless and until the City
 13 Commission determines that:
 14 (1) Special conditions and circumstances exist affecting the land, structure or building
 15 involved preventing the reasonable use of said land, structure or building.
 16 (2) The circumstances which cause the hardship are peculiar to the property or to such
 17 a small number of properties that they clearly constitute a marked exception to other
 18 properties in the district.
 19 (3) The literal interpretation of the provision of this chapter would deprive the applicant
 20 of a substantial property right that is enjoyed by other property owners in the district. (It
 21 is of no importance whatever that the denial of the variance might deny to the property
 22 use in a more profitable way or to sell it at a greater profit than is possible under the
 23 terms of this chapter).
 24 (4) The hardship is not self-created or the result of mere disregard for or ignorance of
 25 the provisions of this chapter.
 26 (5) The variance is the minimum variance that will make possible the reasonable use
 27 of the property, and that the variance will be in harmony with the general purposes and
 28 intent of this chapter and will not be injurious to the neighborhood or otherwise
 29 detrimental to the public welfare.
 30 (b) In granting a variance, the City Commission may prescribe appropriate conditions and
 31 safeguards as are, in their opinion, necessary to protect the public interest. Violations of such
 32 conditions and safeguards, when made a part of the terms under which the variance is
 33 granted, shall be deemed a violation of this chapter.
 34 (c) Under no circumstances shall the City Commission grant a variance to permit a use not
 35 generally permitted in the district involved or any use expressly or by implication prohibited by
 36 the terms of this chapter in said district or grant a variance increasing the residential density
 37 permitted in said district.
 38
 39 Sec. 22-324. Variance Application requirements
 40
 41 (a) Variance requests shall contain information sufficient in detail to reasonably apprise the
 42 City Manager, the planning and zoning board and the city commission of the nature and
 43 substance of the proposed variance.
 44 (b) Variance requests shall be filed by written application to the Planning and Zoning
 45 Department, which application shall contain the following:
 46 1. A legal description of the subject property with a current sealed land
 47 survey prepared by a registered land surveyor.
 48 2. A detailed description and justification of the proposed variance stating
 49 how the criteria for a variance have been met and be accompanied by

 13 Sec. 22-325. Variance Notification requirements.
 14
 15 Variance notification shall be provided by the city to all property owners within five hundred
 16 (500) feet of the perimeter of the property. The city shall prepare a listing of property owners
 17 and addresses. The listing shall contain all property owners whose address is known by
 18 reference to the latest ad valorem tax records. The letter of notification shall set forth a
 19 description of the particular variance being requested and the date, time and place of the
 20 hearing before the planning and zoning board. Letters shall be sent by first-class mail and shall
 21 be postmarked no later than ten (10) days prior to the city commission hearing.
 22
 23 Sec. 22-326. Expiration of variance.
 24
 25 The approval of a variance shall be void after a period of one (1) year unless a building permit
 26 has been issued and construction (pouring of the foundation) has begun.
 27
 28 Sec. 22-327. Administrative Variance
 29
 30 Notwithstanding the other provisions of Article XXII, the Planning Director may issue
 31 administrative variances pursuant to the following conditions:
 32 (1) Structural encroachments into setbacks of no more than five (5) percent. The Planning
 33 Director may issue an administrative variance for structural encroachments into a setback of
 34 no more than five (5) percent of the setback provided the structural encroachment does not
 35 encroach upon an easement.
 36 (2) Conditions. The Planning Director may impose such conditions in a development order
 37 granting an administrative variance as are necessary to accomplish the goals, objectives and
 38 policies of the Comprehensive Plan and this section, including but not limited to limitations on
 39 size, bulk, location, requirements for landscaping, buffering, lighting, and provision of adequate
 40 ingress and egress.
 41 (3) Standards for granting administrative variances. When granting an administrative
 42 variance, the Planning Director shall make one or more of the following findings:
 43 (a) Special and unique conditions exist which are peculiar to the petitioner's case and
 44 which are not generally applicable to the property located in the zoning district;
 45 (b) The special and unique conditions are not directly attributable to the actions of the
 46 petitioner;
 47 (c) The literal interpretation of this chapter, as applied to the petitioner, would deprive
 48 the petitioner of rights commonly enjoyed by the owners of other property in the zoning
 49 district;

 1 (d) The variance granted is the minimum variance necessary for the petitioner to make
 2 reasonable use of the property;
 3 (e) Granting the variance is not detrimental to the public welfare, or injurious to
 4 property or improvements in the zoning district or neighborhood involved; and,
 5 (f) Granting the variance is not contrary to the objectives of the comprehensive plan of
 6 the City.
 7 (4) Expiration of administrative variances. An administrative variance granted by the Planning
 8 Director shall automatically expire under the following conditions:
 9 (a) The variance shall expire one year from the date of the written determination of the
 10 City Manager granting the administrative variance if a building permit has not been
 11 issued in accordance with the plans and conditions upon which the administrative
 12 variance was granted; and,
 13 (b) The administrative variance shall expire if a building permit issued in accordance
 14 with the plans and conditions upon which the administrative variance was granted
 15 expires and is not renewed pursuant to the applicable provisions regarding renewal of
 16 building permits.
 17 (5) Appeal of Planning Director's decision. A petitioner may appeal the determination of the
 18 Planning Director to the planning and zoning board.
 19
 20 Sec. 22-328. Administrative Appeals
 21
 22 (a) Review by the planning and zoning board. The planning and zoning board shall review
 23 and decide appeals from any person who has been adversely affected by a decision of an
 24 administrative official in the interpretation or application of the land development regulations.
 25 The planning and zoning board may modify, reverse or affirm the administrative official's
 26 decision interpreting or applying the provisions of the land development regulations.
 27 (b) Filing. Administrative appeals shall be filed by written application using the form provided
 28 by the department of community development. All applications shall be accompanied by the
 29 processing fee and other supporting evidence pertaining to the appeal. All appeals shall be
 30 filed with the city within thirty (30) days of the act to which the appeal is directed.
 31 (c) Processing.
 32 (1) All applications for administrative appeals shall be scheduled for a hearing before
 33 the planning and zoning board by the planning and zoning department.
 34 (2) The applicant shall present the appeal at a public hearing before the planning and
 35 zoning board.
 36 (3) The administrator subject to the appeal may present reasons and documentation
 37 supporting the initial decision.
 38 (4) The concurring vote of six (6) of the seven (7) board members shall be necessary
 39 to reverse any order, requirement, or decision of the city administrative staff.
 40 (5) Upon a decision, the planning and zoning board shall issue a written order to be
 41 forwarded to the city clerk that shall be sent to the applicant and become a part of the
 42 public records.
 43 (6) Any decision by the planning and zoning board may be appealed to the city
 44 commission by the applicant or the city administrative staff.
