 ORDINANCE NO. 2007 - 14
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AMENDING
 SECTIONS 11-86, 11-87, 11-88, 11-89, 11-91 AND 11-95 RELATIVE TO
 WATER RESTRICTIONS, ENFORCEMENT AND PENALTIES;
 REPEALING SECTION 11-90; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY;
 PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE, PROVIDING FOR
 AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to refine its regulations relative to water
 restrictions during water shortages;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 Section 1. Section 11-86 of the City Code is hereby amended to read as follows:
 Sec. 11-86. Definitions.
 For the purposes of this article, the following terms, phrases, words and their derivations
 shall have the meaning given herein. When not inconsistent with the context, words used in the
 present tense include the future; words in the plural include the singular; and words in the singular
 include the plural. The word shall is always mandatory and not merely directory:
 City: The City of Parkland, Florida
 District: The South Florida Water Management District (SFWMD or the District).
 Emergency: A situation where plans implemented in response to a water shortage are
 insufficient to protect the public health, safety or welfare, including fire protection for human and
 animal life and real properties, fish or aquatic life, a public water supply or recreational,
 commercial, industrial, agricultural or other reasonable uses.
 Person: Any person, firm, partnership, association, corporation, company or organization
 of any kind.
 Water shortage: When sufficient water is available to meet the requirements of the South
 Florida Water Management District permit system; or when conditions are such as to require
 temporary reduction in total use within the area to protect water resources from serious harm; or
 when it is determined that a public water system has become seriously affected by lack of plant
 capacity to treat water demanded, saltwater intrusion, lack of water pressure due to mechanical
 failure or lack of supply; or where the SFWMD declares an emergency related to a water shortage;
 or where sufficient water is not available to meet present or anticipated needs for persons using the
 water resource; or any other manifestation of insufficient water availability.

 Section 2. Section 11-87 of the City Code is hereby amended to read as follows:
 Sec. 11-87. Jurisdiction.
 The provisions of this article apply to all persons using water within the corporate limits of
 the City when the district City or SFWMD declares a water shortage or water shortage
 emergency.
 Section 3. Section 11-88 of the City Code is hereby amended to read as follows:
 Sec. 11-88. Application of article.
 (a) Water conservation measures may shall be invoked by the city commission by
 resolution where
 (1) The district SFWMD determines and declares a water shortage or
 determines that a water shortage emergency exists within a geographical area that includes the
 city; or
 (2) The City Commission determines by resolution that a reduction in water
 consumption is necessary. Said resolution of the city commission shall be reduced to writing and
 shall designate the items of section 11-89 below which shall be included in the restrictions or
 curtailment to the extent necessary to alleviate the specific water shortage or water shortage
 emergency.
 (b) Where the city commission declares that a reduction in consumption is necessary
 pursuant to subsection (a)(2) above, the regulations promulgated pursuant to such resolution (see
 section 11-89 below) shall be limited f r apply to all city consumers of water.
 Section 4. Section 11-89 of the City Code is hereby amended to read as follows:
 Upon a determination that a water shortage condition exists, the city commission may
 order the restriction or curtailment of the use of water to the extent necessary to alleviate the water
 shortage condition and may implement any or all of the following:
 (1) Restricted uses. Uses of water to be restricted or curtailed include but are not
 limited to the following:
 a. The sprinkling, watering or irrigating of shrubbery, trees, lawns, grass,
 ground covers, plants, vines, gardens, vegetables, including the specification of certain days, hours
 or periods of time as well as place limitations, when lawns and shrubbery may be watered.
 b. The washing of automobiles, trucks trailers, railroad cars, mobile homes,
 campers, boats or any other type of mobile equipment.

 c. The washing of sidewalks, driveways, filling station aprons, porches,
 exteriors of homes, apartments or other outdoor surfaces.
 d. The operation of any ornamental fountain or other structure using water
 with or without a recirculating system.
 e. The operation of air-conditioning without a recirculation system.
 f. Swimming and wading pools not using a filter and recirculating system.
 g. The escape of water through defective plumbing which means to knowingly
 allow water to escape through a system that is in disrepair.
 h. Hotels, motels and restaurants shall be restricted to the minimum amount of
 water necessary to conduct operations and shall curtail or prohibit any use of water, including but
 not limited to the use of water to serve a customer in a restaurant unless requested by a customer.
 i. Miscellaneous uses of water from hydrants for construction purposes, fire
 drills, dust control or any purpose other than firefighting.
 j. Other restrictions required by the district SFWMD shall be applicable to the
 use of water in the City.
 (2) Additionally, all restrictions as declared by SFWMD which are applicable to the
 City of Parkland shall immediately be effective in the City of Parkland and any violation of these
 restrictions shall be a violation of this ordinance.
 (2)(3) Extension of water service. A restriction on applications to begin a new use or
 modify an existing use requiring water service connections, meters, service lines, pipeline
 extensions, mains or other water service facilities.
 Section 5. Section 11-90 of the Parkland Code of Ordinances is hereby repealed in its
 entirety.
 Section 6. Section 11-91 of the City Code is hereby amended to read as follows:
 Sec. 11-91. Daily restrictions.
 The South Florida Water Management District restrictions shall be the restrictions for all
 water consumers in the city unless specified otherwise and all provisions of Florida Administrative
 Code, Chapter 40E-21, as amended from time, shall be applicable to the City of Parkland. Any
 violation of that Chapter or any rule or order promulgated by the SFWMD shall be a violation of
 this ordinance.

 Section 7. Section 11-95 of the City Code is hereby amended to read as follows:
 Sec. 11-95. Penalties.
 Violation of any provision of this article shall be subject to the following penalties: A fine not to
 exceed five hundred dollars ($500.00) per incident; City Resolution No. 2007-58 is hereby
 incorporated to provide a graduated scale of fines.
 Section 8. It is declared to be the intent of City Commission of the City of Parkland,
 Florida, that if any section, subsection, sentence, clause, or provision of this Ordinance shall be
 declared invalid, the remainder of this Ordinance shall be construed as not having contained said
 section, subsection, sentence, clause, or provisions and shall not be affected by such holding.
 Section 9. It is the intent of the City Commission that the provisions of this Ordinance
 shall become and be made a part of the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances, and that the sections
 of this Ordinance may be renumbered or re-lettered and the word "ordinance" may be changed to
 "section," "article," "regulation," or such other appropriate word or phrase in order to accomplish
 such intentions.
 Section 10. That this Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon its adoption
 on second reading.
