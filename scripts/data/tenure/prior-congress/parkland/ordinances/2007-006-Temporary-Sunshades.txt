 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 4 AMENDING SECTION 11-72 (a) OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND CODE OF ORDINANCES
 5 ENTITLED "RECREATIONAL ACTIVITIES AND FACILITIES" TO ALLOW TEMPORARY SUN-
 6 SHADE CANOPIES IN CITY PARKS; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR
 7 SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 8
 9 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland, Florida,
 10 pursuant to the authority vested in Chapter 166, Florida Statues, is authorized and empowered to
 11 consider changes to its Code of Ordinances; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland's existing code prohibits any tent, canopy, shelter or
 14 structure of any kind within a City park; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, staff recommends allowing temporary sun-shade canopies within City Parks
 17 under certain conditions in order to assist in the prevention of dehydration, heat stroke and
 18 cancer prevention; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the Parks and Recreation Advisory Board reviewed the proposed ordinance
 21 on March 5, 2007 and unanimously recommended its approval; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, it is in the best interest of the citizens The City of Parkland that the following
 24 amendments to the regulations governing the general welfare of the City residents be adopted;
 25 and
 26
 27
 28 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 29 FLORIDA:
 30
 31 SECTION 1. Section 11-72 (a)of the Code of Ordinance is hereby amended as follows:
 32 Sec. 11-72. Recreational activities and facilities.
 33
 34 The restrictions and regulations contained herein shall apply to all city parks as follows:
 35
 36 (a) Tents. No person shall erect, maintain, use or occupy in any area any tent, canopy,
 37 shelter or structure of any kind within a city park, with the exception of a temporary sun-
 38 shade canopy.
 39
 40 (1). Temporary sun-shade canopies shall be defined as not exceeding ten (10)feet by
 41 ten (10)feet in size, open sided, containing no hanging items and must be securely
 42 fashioned to the ground through the use of tent stakes in each leg.
 43
 44
 45 (2). No person shall erect a temporary sun-shade canopy earlier than thirty(30) minutes
 46 prior to scheduled activity start times and must be removed within thirty(30) minutes
 47 following the activity.
 48
 49 (3). Placement of temporary sun-shade canopies is limited to the sidelines of athletic
 50 fields during officially scheduled City recognized events.
 51
 52 (4). Placement of temporary sun-shade canopies may not impede the visibility of other
 53 spectators as determined by the Leisure Services Director or his/her designee.
 54
 55 (5). Temporary sun-shade canopies shall not be attached to another temporary sun-
 56 shade canopy and shall not placed next to permanent or temporary structures.
