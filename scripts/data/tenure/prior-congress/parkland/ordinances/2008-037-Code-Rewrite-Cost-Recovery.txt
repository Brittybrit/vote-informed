 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA AMENDING CHAPTER 2 ENTITLED

 10 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of
 11 Parkland, Florida, pursuant to the authority vested in Chapter 166, Florida Statutes,
 12 is authorized and empowered to consider changes to its Code of Ordinances; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the
 15 Florida Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, the Charter of the City of
 16 Parkland, and the police powers of the City; and
 17
 18 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 19 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 20
 21 SECTION 1. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby
 22 ordained that the provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part
 23 of the City Code of the City of Parkland and may be renumbered by the City
 24 Clerk by inclusion.
 25
 26 SECTION 2. Chapter 2 of the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances is
 27 hereby amended and renumbered as follows:
 28
 29 Chapter 2 ADMINISTRATION
 30
 31 ARTICLE XI. COST RECOVERY PROGRAM
 32
 33 Sec.-2-234 Definitions.
 34 For the purposes of this article, the terms used herein are defined as follows:
 35
 36 Review request: An application, submission or request concerning
 37 development, utilization or improvement of realty in the city.
 38
 39 Special cases: Those review requests and other development-related
 40 reviews or procedures defined as such in section 13-6.
 41
 42 Sec. 2-235 Recovery of costs of administrative review and processing.
 43 (a) Imposed. There is hereby imposed an administrative fee for the
 44 various costs of the city's administrative processing and review of special
 45 cases in the city. Such fee shall be equal in amount to the city's actual
 46 costs, in terms of staff and consultant time expended in such review and

 (c) Project account. When the petitioner pays the initial deposit that shall
 12 reflect the estimated expenses of review, a financial account for said
 13 petitioner's review request (the project account ) will be opened and
 14 maintained throughout the entire review process until all certificates of
 15 occupancy have been issued, or the city determines that no further city
 16 action is necessary for the review and processing of the review and
 17 processing of the review request; at either of which time, the project
 18 account will be closed, and any remaining funds therein shall be refunded
 19 to the petitioner depositing the same no later than two (2) months after the
 20 project account's closing date. The project account will be monitored on a
 21 monthly basis. Whenever the account balance falls below twenty-five (25)
 22 percent of the original balance, a supplemental deposit determined by the
 23 same method set forth in Resolution No. 89-20, Section 4, shall be
 24 required before any further review or processing will continue. The
 25 petitioner making the initial deposit shall be notified when the
 26 supplemental deposit shall be required.
 27
 28 (d) Record maintenance; applicable hourly rate. The staff of the various
 29 departments of the city and all consultants retained by the city who are
 30 involved in the review and processing of review requests shall maintain
 31 records of the time expended and tasks conducted regarding each
 32 request. The project account will be debited based upon the time
 33 expended at the applicable hourly rate set forth in the estimate provided
 34 for in Resolution No. 89-20. No debits for the time of outside consultants
 35 shall be made unless the city first provides the applicant with the
 36 opportunity to review the invoices or bills of said consultants.
 37
 38 (e) Building permit fees required. This authorized program shall not
 39 replace the imposition and collection of building permit fees.
 40
 41 (f) Deposit and fees per Resolution No. 89-20. The amount of the initial
 42 deposit for the different types of processing or review requests shall be
 43 established as provided for in Resolution No. 89-20. The cost recovery
 44 fees provided herein shall be set and imposed as provided for in
 45 Resolution No. 89-20 or any amendments thereto. It is the expressed
 46 intent of the city commission and the mayor in enacting this cost recovery

 2 review requests, classified as special cases, shall be borne by the
 3 petitioner initiating the review request.
 4
 5 (g) Notification. Except as provided herein, any notification provided in
 6 this section for supplemental deposits from the city to a petitioner initiating
 7 a review request shall be deemed sufficient if made by a telephone call to
 8 such petitioner or his agent with a confirmation letter to follow. It shall be
 9 the duty of the petitioner initiating review requests to provide a
 10 continuously updated address and telephone number where said
 11 petitioners or their agents can be reached for purposes of such
 12 notification. If an attempt to notify a petitioner initiating a review request or
 13 his agent is frustrated because such furnished phone numbers or
 14 addresses are not correct or up-to-date when the notification attempt was
 15 made, such frustrated attempt shall be deemed sufficient notice for
 16 purposes of this section.
 17
 18 (h) Records available upon request. The city shall make a available all
 19 records and invoices upon which cost recovery charges are made to
 20 applicants upon request of the applicant.
 21
 22 Sec. 2-236 Failing to make deposits.
 23 Should any applicant fail to make the deposits provided for herein, the city shall
 24 cease processing all applicant's applications for the subject site (including, but
 25 not limited to, applications for certificates of occupancy) until the proper deposits
 26 are made.
 27
 28 Sec. 2-237 Severability.
 29 Should any section, paragraph, sentence, clause or phrase or other part of this
 30 article be declared by a court of competent jurisdiction to be not valid, such
 31 decision shall not affect the validity of the article as a whole or any portion or part
 32 thereof, other than the part so declared to be not valid.
 33
 34 SECTION 3. Chapter 4, Article II, Cost—Recovery Program of the City of
 35 Parkland Code of Ordinances is hereby deleted from Chapter 4 and recodified in
 36 Chapter 2.
 37
 38 SECTION 4. Should any section or provision of this ordinance or any
 39 portion thereof, any paragraph, sentence or word be declared by a court of
 40 competent jurisdiction to be invalid, such decision shall not affect the validity of
 41 the remainder hereof as a whole or part thereof other than the part declared to
 42 be invalid.
 43
 44 SECTION 5. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately
 45 upon its passage and adoption.
