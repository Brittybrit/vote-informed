 1 ORDINANCE NO. 2009-10
 2
 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, RELATING TO ARTICLE
 5 IX OF THE CODE OF ORDINANCES ENTITLED
 6 "PERSONNEL RULES AND REGULATIONS" BY
 7 REPEALING THE EXISTING RULES AND REGULATIONS,
 8 AND REPLACING THEM WITH NEW RULES AND
 9 REGULATIONS, DELETING SECTION 2-64 ENTITLED
 10 "EMPLOYEE'S HANDBOOK"; PROVIDING FOR THE
 11 APPLICATION OF NEW RULES AND REGULATIONS TO
 12 CURRENT CITY OFFICIALS AND EMPLOYEES;
 13 PROVIDING FOR RULES AND REGULATIONS
 14 GOVERNING EMPLOYMENT ADVANCEMENT,
 15 PAYMENT, DISCIPLINE AND OTHER RELATED
 16 EMPLOYMENT MATTERS AND AUTHORIZING
 17 AMENDMENTS TO THE RULES AND REGULATIONS TO
 18 BE ACCOMPLISHED BY RESOLUTION; PROVIDING A
 19 CONFLICTS CLAUSE AND A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE;
 20 AND PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 21
 22 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the Florida
 23 Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes,the Charter of the City of Parkland; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City's Personnel Rules and Regulations were adopted in 1990; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City's Personnel Rules and Regulations were last revised by staff on
 28 June 24, 1996; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the City's Employee Handbook was revised June 2003; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS,rules and regulations let the employees know what is expected of them
 33 and so that the City may provide for equal and fair treatment of all employees; and
 34
 35 WHEREAS, there exists changed conditions since the adoption of the Personnel Rules
 36 and Regulations and Employee Handbook including legal decisions and laws that are
 37 applicable to the City; and
 38
 39 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland finds it in the best interest of
 40 the City to amend the City's Rules and Regulations and to delete the Employee Handbook; and
 41
 42 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that the reasonable regulations contained
 43 herein will protect the health, safety, and welfare of the residents of the City: and
 44
 45 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 46 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 47
 48 SECTION 1. Section 2-161 is hereby amended as follows:
 1

 2 The present rules and regulations governing employees and dated June 24, 1996 are hereby
 3 repealed, and the attached personnel rules and regulations dated March 18, 2009 are hereby
 4 adopted by the city commission as the official rules and regulations governing employment
 5 with the city. These rules and regulations may be amended by resolution of the city
 6 commission. These rules and regulations shall be considered part of this article and, therefore,
 7 a law of the city.
 8
 9 SECTION 2: Section 2-64 of the City of Parkland Code of Ordinance is hereby deleted
 10 in its entirety and this section reserved.
 11
 12 SECTION 3. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that
 13 the provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of
 14 the City of Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance to accomplish such intent.
 15
 16 SECTION 4. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for
 17 any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity
 18 of any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 19
 20 SECTION 5. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect as of April 1, 2009.
