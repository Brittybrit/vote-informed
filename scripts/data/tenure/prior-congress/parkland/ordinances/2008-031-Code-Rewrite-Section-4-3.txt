 21 Sec. 4.3-4.. 2-32. Enforcement procedure.
 22 (1) Except as provided in subsections (2) and (3), if a violation of the codes is found,
 23 the code inspector shall notify the violator and give him or her a reasonable time to
 24 correct the violation. Should the violation continue beyond the time specified for
 25 correction, the code inspector shall schedule a hearing. The code inspector, through its
 26 clerical staff, shall schedule a hearing, and written notice of such hearing shall be hand
 27 delivered or mailed as provided in Section 162.12, Florida Statutes, to said violator. At
 28 the option of the code inspector, notice may additionally be served by publication or
 29 posting as provided in Florida Statutes Section 162.12. If the violation is corrected and
 30 then recurs or if the violation is not corrected by the time specified for correction by the
 31 code inspector, the case may be presented to the special master even if the violation
 32 has been corrected prior to the hearing, and the notice shall so state.
 33
 34 (2) If a repeat violation is found, the code inspector shall notify the violator but is not
 35 required to give the violator a reasonable time to correct the violation. The code
 36 inspector, upon notifying the violator of a repeat violation, shall request a hearing before
 37 the special master magistrate. The special master magistrate, through its clerical staff,
 38 shall schedule a hearing and shall provide notice pursuant to Florida Statutes Section
 39 162.12. The case may be presented to the special master magistrate even if the repeat
 40 violation has been corrected prior to the hearing, and the notice shall so state. If the
 41 repeat violation has been corrected, the special master retains the right to schedule a
 42 hearing to determine costs and impose the payment of reasonable enforcement fees
 43 upon the repeat violator. The repeat violator may choose to waive his or her rights to
 44 this hearing and pay said costs as determined by the special master magistrate.

 25 transfer.

 27 A failure to make the disclosures described in paragraphs (a), (b), and (c) before
 28 the transfer creates a rebuttal presumption of fraud. If the property is transferred
 29 before the hearing, the proceeding shall not be dismissed, but the new owner
 30 shall be provided a reasonable period of time to correct the violation before the
 31 hearing is held.

 33 Sec. 41.3 5. 2-33 Conduct of hearing.
 34 (1) Upon request of the code inspector, or at such other times as may be necessary,
 35 the special master shall schedule a hearing. Minutes shall be kept of all hearings and all
 36 hearings and proceedings shall be open to the public. The local governing body shall
 37 provide clerical and administrative personnel as may be reasonably required by the
 38 special master magistrate.
 39
 40 (2) Each case before the special master magistrate shall be presented by the local
 41 governing body attorney or by a member of the administrative staff of the local
 42 governing body. If the local governing body prevails in prosecuting a case before the
 43 special master, it shall be entitled to recover all costs incurred in prosecuting the case
 44 before the special master magistrate and such costs may be included in the lien
 45 authorized under Section 162.09(3), Florida Statutes.
