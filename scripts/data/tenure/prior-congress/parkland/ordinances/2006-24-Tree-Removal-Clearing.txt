 1 ORDINANCE NO. 2006-24
 2
 3
 4 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 5 PARKLAND, FLORIDA RELATING TO THE MAINTENANCE OF VACANT
 6 RESIDENTIAL PARCELS; AMENDING SECTION 9-21 "GENERAL
 7 REQUIREMENTS" OF ARTICLE II "TREE REMOVAL AND CLEARING";
 8 PROVIDING A CONFLICTS CLAUSE AND A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE;
 9 AND PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the accumulation of weeds, grass, or underbrush, upon vacant residential
 12 lots within the City of Parkland constitutes a nuisance and menace to the public health and
 13 safety of the City and its inhabitants; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to establish reasonable lot mowing
 16 requirements for vacant residential parcels, in order to reduce nuisance conditions and
 17 improve the appearance of properties with the City.
 18
 19 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 20 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 21
 22 SECTION 1. Section 9-21 of the Code of Ordinances is hereby amended to state as
 23 follows:
 24
 25 Section 9-21 "General Requirements."
 26
 27
 28
 29 (m) Limitation on land clearing permits.
 30 (1) Any person who receives a land clearing permit pursuant to this article shall
 31 remove the debris from land clearing and/or any mulched debris generated by said land
 32 clearing permit within thirty (30) days of the land clearing activity.
 33 (2) Should any person desire a variance from the provisions of section 9-21(1) above,
 34 he may appeal to the city commission. The city commission may grant a variance from
 35 the strict provision of subsection (m)(1) above when it finds that the alleged hardship is
 36 not self-imposed.
 37
 38 SECTION 2: All ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby
 39 repealed.
 40
 41 SECTION 3: Should any section or provision of this Ordinance or any portion thereof,
 42 any paragraph, sentence or word hereof be declared by a court of competent jurisdiction to be
 43 invalid, such decision shall not affect the validity of the remainder hereof that is not declared
 44 invalid.
 45
 46 SECTION 4. This Ordinance shall take effect 45 days after the date of adoption.
 47
 48 PASSED this 6th day of September 2006, upon first reading.
