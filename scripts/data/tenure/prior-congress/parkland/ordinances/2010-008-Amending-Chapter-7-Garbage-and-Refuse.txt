 ORDINANCE NO. 2010-08
 SECOND READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 FLORIDA, AMENDING CHAPTER 7, CODE OF ORDINANCES ENTITLED
 "GARBAGE AND REFUSE," ARTICLE II ENTITLED "SOLID WASTE
 COLLECTION AND DISPOSAL"; AMENDING SECTION 7-31 ENTITLED
 "DEFINITIONS"; AMENDING SECTION 7-32 ENTITLED "USE OF
 FRANCHISE'S SERVICES REQUIRED; DISPOSAL OF HAZARDOUS
 MATERIAL"; AMENDING SECTION 7-33 ENTITLED "RESIDENTIAL SOLID
 WASTE AND HORTICULTURAL TRASH COLLECTION"; AMENDING
 SECTION 7-34 ENTITLED "COMMERCIAL SOLID WASTE COLLECTION";
 AMENDING SECTION 7-35 ENTITLED "BULK COLLECTION"; AMENDING
 SECTION 7-36 ENTITLED "COMPLIANCE WITH FRANCHISE"; AMENDING
 SECTION 7-38 ENTITLED "RATES"; PROVIDING A CONFLICTS CLAUSE;
 PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE
 WHEREAS, the City renegotiated and amended its Franchise Agreement with Waste
 Management to provide for additional services and franchise fees in exchange for the non-ad valorem
 tax billing of residential solid waste collection and recycling services; and
 WHEREAS, pursuant to the amendment of the Franchise Agreement between the City and
 Waste Management, changes to Chapter 7 are necessary to maintain consistency within the City's
 Code of Ordinances as it applies to solid waste collection and recycling services; and
 WHEREAS, it is the recommendation of staff that Chapter 7 of the City's Code of Ordinances
 be amended to provide for such consistency; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of Parkland, Florida finds and determines that amending
 Chapter 7 of the City's Code of Ordinances is in the best interests of the citizens and residents of the
 City of Parkland.
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1: That Chapter 7, Code of Ordinances entitled "Garbage and Refuse,"
 Article II entitled "Solid Waste Collection and Disposal" is hereby amended to read and provide
 as follows:

 ARTICLE II. SOLID WASTE COLLECTION AND DISPOSAL*
 *Editor's note: Ord. No. 2008-05, §§ 1--8, adopted Jan. 16, 2008, repealed the former Art. II, §§ 7-31-
 -7-36, and enacted a new Art. II as set out herein. The former Art. II pertained to similar subject matter.
 For complete derivation see the Code Comparative Table at the end of this volume.
 Cross references: Waste Management, Inc. of Florida franchise, App. A, Art. Ill.
 Sec. 7-31. Definitions.
 Biomedical waste shall have the meaning as set forth on Section 403.703, Florida Statutes, and
 applicable regulations under the Florida Administrative Code.
 Bulk waste shall mean large items that normally exceed weight (fifty (50) pounds) or size
 restrictions (four (4) foot in length or too large to fit in rollout cart) including, but not limited to, white
 goods, furniture, and large branches. Bulk waste does not include any form of matter or debris resulting
 from land clearing, land development, or building demolition debris. Additionally, bulk waste does not
 include automobiles, automobile components, boats, internal combustion engines, hazardous waste, or
 biomedical waste. Bulk waste must be of a size and weight that can be managed by contractor's
 grapple truck.
 Commercial container shall mean any portable, nonabsorbent, enclosed container with a close
 fitting cover, or doors, approved by the contractor, which is used to store large volumes of refuse
 utilizing containers of two (2) cubic yards, or more. A commercial container must be capable of being
 serviced by the mechanical equipment of the contractor.
 Commercial establishment shall mean any public or private place, building, or enterprise
 devoted in whole or in part to business purposes whether for profit or not for profit; except where such
 place, building, or enterprise constitutes a residence.
 Construction/demolition debris shall mean materials generally considered to be not water
 soluble and non-hazardous in nature, including, but not limited to, steel, glass, brick, concrete, asphalt
 roofing material, pipe, gypsum wallboard, and lumber, from the construction or destruction of a
 structure as part of a construction or demolition project, and including rocks, soils, tree remains, trees
 and other vegetative matter which normally results from land clearing or land development operations
 for a construction project as defined in Section 403.703(21), Florida Statutes, as amended from time to
 time. Mixing of construction and demolition debris with other types of solid waste, including material
 from a construction or demolition site which is not from the actual construction or destruction of a
 structure, will cause it to be classified as other than construction and demolition debris. However, the
 inclusion of incidental solid waste generated attendant to construction or demolition activity shall not
 classify the same as other than construction and demolition debris.
 Curb service shall mean a single collection point within five (5) feet of the hard-topped road.
 Garbage shall mean and include all waste, fruit or vegetable matter that attends or results from
 the preparation, use, handling, cooking, serving or storage of meats, fish, fowl, fruit, vegetable matter,
 of any nature whatsoever, which is subject to decay, putrefaction and the generation of noxious and
 offensive gases or odors, or which may serve as breeding or feeding materials for flies and other
 insects. Garbage does not include biomedical waste as described herein.

<<PG>>
 Handicapped service shall be service of household garbage at rear or side of house for persons
 as defined herein and approved by the contractor.
 Hazardous waste shall mean wastes that are hazardous by reason of their pathological,
 explosive, radiological or toxic characteristics, or any other material that has been classified as
 hazardous by a governmental agency with the authority to regulate such material.
 Horticultural trash shall mean grass clippings, shrubbery cuttings or clippings, palm fronds,
 small tree branches, bushes or shrubs, or other matter usually created as refuse in the care of hedges,
 bushes and trees, except large branches, trees, stumps or bulky or noncombustible materials.
 Processing center shall mean a location offering purchase, sorting and preparation of recyclable
 materials for resale.
 Recyclable materials shall mean newspapers, magazines, inserts, catalogs, cereal boxes, junk
 mail, office paper, steel cans, aluminum and bi-metal cans, milk and juice cartons, drink boxes, clear
 glass, brown and green glass, plastic containers (#1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6), and such other recyclable
 materials as the parties may mutually designate in writing.
 Residence shall mean and include:
 (1) Single-family residence - A detached single-family structure designed for occupancy
 by one (1) person or by one (1) family.
 (2) Duplex residence - Shall mean and include an attached two-family structure
 designed or intended for occupancy by two (2) families or by one (1) person in one (1) or
 both units.
 (3) Multifamily residence - Shall be three (3) dwelling units or more.
 Residential container shall mean and include a thirty-two (32) gallon garbage can with handles
 and lid or a tied plastic garbage bag.
 Rollout cart shall mean and include:
 (1) For garbage/trash - A contractor issued ninety six (96) gallon or sixty four (64)
 gallon wheeled rollout cart, green in color with a green lid used for the collection of solid
 waste, horticultural trash and trash only, not exceeding 96 gallons in size with front grab
 bar suitable for mechanical collection.
 (2) For recyclables - A contractor issued sixty-four (64) gallon wheeled rollout cart,
 white in color with a green lid and clearly labeled for recyclable materials only.
 Solid waste shall mean garbage, trash and horticultural trash as defined herein. This definition
 shall not include biomedical waste, hazardous waste or construction debris as the definitions are
 defined herein.
 Trash shall mean every residential or commercial waste accumulation of metal, metal products,
 glass, crockery, rubber (excluding tires), lumber, sawdust, or other materials.
 White goods shall mean discarded refrigerators, ranges, stoves, washers, dryers, water
 heaters, and other similar domestic appliances.
 (Ord. No. 2008-05, § 1, 1-16-2008)

 Sec. 7-32. Use of franchise's services required; disposal of hazardous material.
 Waste Management or successors and assigns under the franchise agreement with the City
 (hereinafter the Franchise) shall be the exclusive collector of solid waste for all residents +14 and
 commercial establishments and provider of recycling services for all residences within the city
 boundary, subject to the limitations set forth in the Franchise agreement with Waste Management (as it
 may be amended from time to time) and further subject to any limitations as set forth in applicable
 Florida law. The exclusive right and obligation granted by the Franchise Ordinance No. 2008 06 and as
 set forth in these regulations does not relate to storm-generated debris that is the subject of an
 emergency order, construction debris, hazardous waste and hazardous substances (as defined in
 Florida Statute § 403.703). The terms of the Franchise shall be deemed as part of this Ordinance and
 codified as Appendix A, Article III in the City Code.
 (Ord. No. 2008-05, § 2, 1-16-2008)
 Cross references: Waste Management, Inc. of Florida franchise, App. A, Art. III.
 Sec. 7-33. Residential solid waste and horticultural trash collection.
 All persons occupying a residential unit or maintaining a place of residence within the city shall
 be provided regular solid waste and horticultural trash collection two (2) days per week. The owners
 and/or occupants shall subscribe to the solid waste collection service and have all accumulations of
 solid waste generated from such premises removed by Waste Management the Franchisee. Waste
 Management The Franchisee shall provide twice a week collection as long as all waste set out for
 collection is in approved containers as provided for in Ordinance 2008 06 the Franchise. All
 owners/occupants shall comply with this schedule. Containers shall be placed at curbside prior to
 "pickup," but no later than twelve (12) hours prior to collection.
 Residential customers who are physically or mentally challenged and for which there is no adult
 living in the residence who is capable of placing the containers at the curb may elect "handicapped
 service" at no additional charge. Waste Management The Franchisee shall provide an appropriate form
 for such residents to complete together with required documentation corroborating such status. The city
 shall make the final decision in the event of a dispute between Waste Management the Franchisee and
 resident over resident's right to have "handicapped service."
 (Ord. No. 2008-05, § 3, 1-16-2008)
 Sec. 7-34. Commercial solid waste collection.
 All persons occupying a commercial establishment or maintaining a place of business within the
 city shall be provided regular solid waste collection a minimum of one (1) time per week. To the extent
 permitted by law, the city shall require the owners and/or occupants to subscribe to the solid waste
 collection service and have all accumulation of solid waste generated from such premises removed by
 Waste Management the Franchisee.
 In the event of a dispute between Waste Management the Franchisee and a commercial
 establishment as to the level of service pursuant to a contract for service between the two (2) parties,
 then either party may take the matter to the city manager for resolution. The decision of the city
 manager shall be final.
 (Ord. No. 2008-05, § 4, 1-16-2008)
 Sec. 7-35. Bulk collection service.
 Waste Management The Franchisee shall provide a city-wide bulk pickup service six-(6) twelve
 (12) times per contract year at no additional cost to the city or residents. Bulk pickup schedules shall be
 established annually and shall be scheduled in twe one-month intervals. The bulk pickup shall be at
 curbside, the same as the normal pickups and shall include, but not be limited to, bulky household
 items, discarded furniture, etc. Bulk pickups shall not include construction debris, tree stumps or mature
 trees unless reduced to size and weight that can be managed by Waste Management the Franchisee's
 grapple truck.

<<PG>>
 The following set-out rules apply to bulk pickups:
 • Bulk waste shall be placed curbside in a neat and orderly manner.
 • Cut tree trunks, logs, and large limbs into sections not to exceed four (4) feet and stack neatly
 at the curb.
 • Cut lumber, metal poles and other items of this nature into sections not to exceed four (4) feet.
 • Bulk waste set out for collection must not be placed close to a mailbox, fire hydrant, cable box,
 utility pole, fence or car.
 • White goods shall have chlorofluorocarbons (CFC's) removed prior to set out.
 Additional bulk pickups may be provided by Waste Management the Franchisee or city at additional
 cost to residents - the cost to be determined by the city.
 (Ord. No. 2008-05, § 5, 1-16-2008)
 Sec. 7-36. Compliance with franchise.
 All persons, entities and properties which are subject to the exclusive collection agreement
 Franchise as set forth in Ordinance No. 2008 06 Appendix A, Article Ill of the City Code shall comply
 with the provisions therein and shall be obligated to comply with the provisions stated herein and as set
 forth in said Ordinance No. 2008 06 for the exclusive collection of the solid waste set forth therein by
 Waste Management, Inc the Franchisee.
 (Ord. No. 2008-05, § 6, 1-16-2008)
 Sec. 7-37. Solid waste and recyclable materials.
 With regard to all solid waste and recyclable materials, all residences shall comply with the
 collection schedule as designated by the City Manager of the City of Parkland. The collection schedule
 shall be set forth in a written order from the city manager; a copy of said order shall be on file with the
 city clerk and shall be on the City of Parkland web site. This schedule is subject to change by further
 written order of the city manager. All residences and businesses are required to comply with the
 procedures for collection of solid waste and recyclables as set forth herein.
 (Ord. No. 2008-05, § 7, 1-16-2008)
 Sec. 7-38. Rates.
 At the City's option, all single family and duplex residents shall be billed for services provided
 herein through a non-ad valorem assessment on their tax bill and shall be subject to the provisions
 thereof. All business owners, multifamily residences, and other persons serviced as provided herein
 shall establish an account and pay the rates as charged by Waste Management the Franchisee for the
 services set forth herein. Single family and duplex accounts, whose tax bills do not reflect a non-ad
 valorem assessment for solid waste and recycling services, for any reason will be charged an annual
 fee directly by Franchisee which will be pro-rated accordingly, under the proper circumstances. Such
 properties will be placed on the next year's tax bill as a non-ad valorem assessment. The failure to pay
 Waste Management, the Franchisee, in a timely fashion as determined by the city shall be deemed a
 violation of this article. The Rate Schedule as provided for in Appendix A, Article Ill and attached
 hereto as Exhibit A are hereby adopted and imposed together with any automatic adjustments
 provided therein.
 (Ord. No. 2008-05, § 8, 1-16-2008)
 Secs. 7-39--7-49. Reserved.
