 38 Section 9-1. Intent and objectives.
 39
 40 (a) Intent. The intent of these regulations is to protect, preserve and enhance
 41 the natural environment and beauty of the city by providing for landscaped green
 42 spaces, trees and other plants and arranging them in a pleasing manner in
 43 relation to paved areas, structures and abutting properties.
 44
 45 (b) Objectives. The objectives of this chapter are as follows:

 15 Sec. 9-31.1. Holmberg Road landscaping theme.
 16
 17 ***
 18
 19 (a) The Holmberg Road landscaping theme shall apply directly to land within the
 20 following general location: That portion of Holmberg Road, from NW 61 st Avenue
 21 to Riverside Drive through, and including, the intersections. The landscape buffer
 22 areas abutting Holmberg Road rights-of-way north and south shall comply with
 23 the following landscaping design theme to enhance, facilitate, and encourage
 24 landscaping of Holmberg Road which has been determined by the city
 25 commission to be special and unique in this location in providing a desirable
 26 entranceway for the city. Notwithstanding any other provision in any ordinance to
 27 the contrary, the city shall not approve any permit for, or permit any removal of
 28 any tree, shrub, bush, or grass along Holmberg Road from S.R. 441 to University
 29 Drive from the edge of the roadway for a distance of twenty-five (25) feet from
 30 said edge, unless there is a direct replacement or equivalent replacement of
 31 vegetation within five (5) feet of any tree, shrub, bush, or grass removed.
 32 Replacement vegetation shall be installed in no more than ninety (90) days from
 33 the removal of existing vegetation and achieve opaqueness in no more than one
 34 (1) year from date of installation.
 35
 36 (b) An application for a building permit within the above-described area shall
 37 provide a landscaping plan which shall conform with the requirements of a
 38 parklike setting as defined in this chapter [section 9-30], as amended from time to
 39 time, and also provide a landscape buffer area of at least twenty (20) feet
 40 minimum and, in addition, shall meet the following requirements along the area
 41 abutting Holmberg Road:
 42
 43 (1) Berms. A berm shall be constructed along the entire length of the
 44 landscape buffer and shall be a minimum of four (4) feet above the crown
 45 of the adjacent roadway.
