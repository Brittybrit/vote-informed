 28 SECTION 1. Section 13-66(a) of the Code of Ordinance is hereby amended as
 29 follows:
 30
 31 Sec. 13-66. Site plan requirements.
 32
 33 (a) Generally: when required. A site plan shall be required as a precondition to
 34 issuance of a building permit or the undertaking of any of the following
 35 activities:
 36
 37 (1) Construction of any building other than a single-family homed
 38 residential community in which a plat was approved that created two
 39 (2) or more lots or an attached two-family home; or
 40
 41 (2) Construction of any addition or alteration to any building not exempted
 42 in (1) above where said addition or alteration alters any portion of the
 43 building, any structures on an approved site plan, or causes the
 44 reduction in any landscaping on an approved site plan; or
 45
 46 (3) Construction or alteration of any entrance features to a residential
 47 development. An entrance feature shall be considered to be any signs,
 48 gates, walls, fences or other structures, and any landscaping used to
