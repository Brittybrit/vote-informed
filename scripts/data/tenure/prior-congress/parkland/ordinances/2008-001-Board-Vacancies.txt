 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, REPEALING SECTIONS
 5 2-39, 2-40 AND 2-41 OF THE CITY CODE RELATING TO

 8 WITH REGARD TO APPOINTMENT OF BOARD
 9 MEMBERS, VACANCIES AND REMOVAL, PROVIDING
 10 THAT THESE PROVISIONS SHALL APPLY TO ALL
 11 BOARD MEMBERS; CONTAINING A PROVISION FOR
 12 INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE; CONTAINING A
 13 SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; CONTAINING AN EFFECTIVE

 16 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland wishes to establish a
 17 comprehensive system to govern board appointments to delineate terms, vacancies and removal
 18 procedures;
 19 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 20 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 21 Section 1. Sections 2-39, 2-40, and 2-41 of the City Code are hereby repealed in their
 22 entirety and replaced by following new sections:
 23 Sec. 2-39.
 24 All board appointments (notwithstanding any language in the enabling
 25 resolution or ordinance) shall be for terms of two years beginning on the third
 26 Wednesday of November 2008; provided that vacancies shall occur as set forth in
 27 Section 2-40. The terms of all board members serving as of the date of adoption
 28 of this ordinance shall terminate on the third Wednesday of November 2008;
 29 except that if a successor is not appointed, then that board member shall serve
 30 until an appointment is made. The board appointments of an individual
 31 commissioner shall automatically be appointed upon nomination by the
 32 appropriate commissioner; at large appointees must be approved by a majority of
 33 those members of the commission in attendance at the meeting. All appointments
 34 shall serve until a successor is appointed; notwithstanding any language herein to
 35 the contrary.

 1 Sec. 2-40. Vacancies
 2 Upon a vacancy occurring in any board appointment, the vacancy shall be
 3 filled by the commissioner who made the appointment or a majority of the city
 4 commissioners attending a meeting for at large appointments. If the city
 5 commissioner who made the appointment is no longer serving, his/her successor
 6 shall make the appointment, or if the commission vacancy has not yet been filled
 7 as proscribed by the Charter, the city commission shall appoint the replacement by
 8 a majority vote of the city commissioners attending the meeting; said appointment
 9 shall be valid until the successor city commissioner is appointed or elected. For
 10 all individual appointments, the term of the appointee shall be deemed to end
 11 when the appointing commissioner leaves office, thereby creating a vacancy to be
 12 filled as proscribed herein.
 13 Sec. 2-41. Removal
 14 All individual board appointees shall serve at the pleasure of the
 15 appointing commissioner. Said board members may be removed at any time by
 16 the appointing commissioner through the appointment of a replacement. At large
 17 board members shall serve at the pleasure of the city commission and said
 18 members may be removed and replaced at any time by a majority vote of the
 19 members of the commission in attendance at a commission meeting.
 20
 21 SECTION 2. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 22 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of the
 23 City of Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to accomplish
 24 such intent.
 25
 26 SECTION 3. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for any
 27 reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of any
 28 remaining portions of this ordinance.
 29 SECTION 4. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 30 passage and adoption.
