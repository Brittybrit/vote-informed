 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, AMENDING SECTION 13-68 OF THE CITY OF
 5 PARKLAND LAND DEVELOPMENT REGULATIONS OF THE CITY
 6 CODE RELATING TO IMPACT FEES, PROVIDING FOR THE EXISTING
 7 USE OF IMPACT FEES AND OTHER FINANCIAL REQUIREMENTS;
 8 CONTAINING A PROVISION FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE;
 9 CONTAINING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; CONTAINING AN
 10 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the Florida Impact Fee Act imposes certain requirements on cities
 13 with regard to collection and use of impact fees; and
 14
 1s WHEREAS, the City of Parkland impact fee regulations comply with the
 16 requirements of Florida Impact Fee Act but the City wishes to expand upon the present
 17 language in the City Code regarding same,
 18
 19 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 20 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 21
 22 SECTION 1. Section 13-69 of the City of Parkland City Code hereby amended to
 23 include the following new section (c) (4):
 24
 25 All impact fees collected pursuant to the City of Parkland
 26 Development Regulations shall be placed in separate funds
 27 for each specific type of impact fee. The finance director
 28 shall annually provide an accounting of all collections and
 29 expenditures within each separate accounting fund. Impact
 30 fees collections shall be segregated from other money. The
 31 City shall annually provide for an audit of impact fee funds,
 32 revenues and expenditures in a manner consistent with
 33 Florida law and annually the chief financial officer shall
 34 execute and affidavit that the City has complied with the
 35 annual requirements of the Florida Impact Fee law.
 36
 37 SECTION 2. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance
 38 is for any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the
 39 validity of any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 40
 41 SECTION 3. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 42 passage and adoption.
