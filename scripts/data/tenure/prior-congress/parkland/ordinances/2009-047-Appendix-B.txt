 34 SECTION 1. ARTICLE I TITLE AND INTENT
 35
 36 18.1 Title.
 37 This body of regulations shall be known as "The City of Parkland Minimum Construction
 38 and Design Engineering Standards", and may be referred to as the "Minimum
 39 Standards" or "City Standards", as is done hereinafter.
 40
 41 18.2 Intent.
 42 The intent of the Minimum Standards is to provide for the safe and orderly use and
 43 development of public and private rights-of-way and easements within the City limits of
 44 the City of Parkland, as well as for lands developed for public or commercial/industrial
 45 use, to the end that the needs and interest of the public are served as fully and
 46 efficiently as possible.
