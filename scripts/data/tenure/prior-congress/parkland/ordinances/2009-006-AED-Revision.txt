 13 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland's existing code for regulating the health, safety,
 14 and general welfare does not recognize that there are devices that may be desirable to
 15 provide emergency medical treatment to City residents at certain buildings; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, it is in the best interest of the citizens of Parkland that the following
 18 requirements to the regulations governing the general welfare of the City residents be
 19 adopted.

 24 Section 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed
 25 as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon
 26 adoption hereof.

 28 Section 2. Chapter 14 of the Code of Ordinances of the City of Parkland shall be
 29 amended to read as follows:
 30
 31 Section 14-3. Automated external defibrillators.
 32
 33 (1) Definition of automated external defibrillators.
 34
 35 Automated external defibrillator device. A defibrillator device that 1) is
 36 commercially distributed in accordance with the Federal Food, Drug, and
 37 Cosmetic Act, 2) is capable of recognizing the presence or absence of ventricular
 38 fibrillation, and is capable of determining without intervention by the user of the
 39 device whether defibrillation should be performed, and 3) upon determining that
 40 defibrillation should be performed, is able to deliver an electrical shock to an
 41 individual.
 42
 43 (2) Intent.
 44
 45 (A) It is the intent of the city and the purpose of this chapter:

 1 1. To promote the public health, safety, and general welfare by requiring
 2 the installation of automated external defibrillator devices within certain

 11 (3) Automated external defibrillator devices required.
 12
 13 (A) Automated external defibrillator devices shall be installed in the following
 14 buildings located within the geographical boundaries of the city:
 15
 16 1. Gymnasiums, fitness centers and indoor recreational centers in
 17 excess of one thousand five hundred (1,500) square feet;
 18
 19 2. Restaurants with fifty (50) or more seats including indoor, outdoor and
 20 bar;
 21
 22 3. Assisted living facilities as defined by F.S. § 400.402, as amended
 23 from time to time;
 24
 25 4. Pharmacies. A pharmacy is an establishment or institution in which
 26 the practice of pharmacy is conducted; drugs, medicines or medicinal chemicals are
 27 dispensed or offered for sale, or a sign is displayed bearing the word or words
 28 'pharmacist,' 'pharmacy,' 'apothecary,' 'drugstore,' 'druggist,' 'medicine store,' 'druq
 29 sundries,' 'prescriptions filled,' or similar words intended to indicate that the practice of
 30 pharmacy is being conducted.
 31
 32 (4) Installation and operation.
 33
 34 (A) The city shall inspect all automated external defibrillator devices for
 35 operation prior to being placed in service or available for use, and on an annual
 36 basis.
 37
 38 (B) Automated external defibrillator devices shall be:
 39
 40 (1) Conspicuously located in plain view of the primary public entrance,
 41 with unobstructed access;
 42
 43 (2) Housed in a cabinet with a clear window in the door, an audible alarm
 44 signaling the opening of a door, permanently affixed to a wall, and whose
 45 top is no more than forty-eight (48) inches above the floor;

 2 inches and containing the letters "AED" and the universally recognizable

 4 above the floor;

 12 (C) Automated external defibrillator devices shall contain adult and pediatric
 13 pads and bandage scissors.
 14
 15 (D) All automated external defibrillator devices shall be used in accordance with
 16 the manufacturer's guidelines.
 17
 18 (E) It shall be the responsibility of the owner of the building to:
 19
 20 (1) Install automated external defibrillator devices.
 21
 22 (2) Provide all necessary training for appropriate use;
 23
 24 (3) Maintain automated external defibrillator devices in accordance with
 25 manufacturer's recommended maintenance requirements and as required
 26 herein.
 27
 28 (F) If an automated external defibrillator device is removed for repair, a
 29 replacement shall be provided.

 31 (5) Fees. Fees for automated external defibrillator device permits and inspections
 32 shall be set by resolution.

 39 repaired, or recharged, except pursuant to court order;
 40
 41 (B) Obliterate the serial number on an automated external defibrillator device for
 42 purposes of falsifying service records;
 43
 44 (C) Improperly service, recharge, repair, test, or inspect an automated external
 45 defibrillator device;
