 19 WHEREAS, the Mayor and City Commission of the City of Parkland are
 20 deeply concerned about the numerous recent occurrences in our state and
 21 elsewhere, whereby convicted sex offenders who have been released from custody
 22 repeat the unlawful acts for which they had originally been convicted; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the Mayor and City Commission of the City of Parkland find from
 25 the evidence the recidivism rate for released sex offenders is alarmingly high,
 26 especially for those who commit their crimes on children; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the City is becoming an increasingly attractive place of residence
 29 for younger families with small children; and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, the Mayor and City Commission of the City of Parkland desire to

 33 the City of Parkland; and

 4 SECTION 1. That a Section 11-39(B) is hereby amended to read as follows:
 5
 6 The following words, terms and phrases, when used in this article, shall have
 7 the meanings ascribed to them in this section, except where the context clearly
 8 indicates a different meaning:
 9
 10 "Child"means a person under the age of 16 years.
 11
 12 "Child care facility" means day nurseries, family day care homes licensed
 13 by the Department of Children and Families.
 14
 15 "Permanent residence" means a place where the person abides, lodges,
 16 or resides for 14 or more consecutive days.
 17
 18 "Park"means a county or municipal park.
 19
 20 "Playground" means an area set aside by a homeowners association of
 21 the City for use by minors to play or an area set aside by a governmental agency
 22 devoted to active recreational uses for the primary use of preschool or elementary
 23 school age children.
 24
 25 "School" means a public or private kindergarten, elementary, middle or
 26 secondary (high) school.
 27
 28 "Sexual offender or sexual predator" shall have the meaning ascribed to
 29 such term in Section 775.21, Florida Statutes.
 30
 31 "Temporary residence" means a place where the person abides, lodges,
 32 or resides for a period of fourteen (14) or more days in the aggregate during any
 33 calendar year and which is not the person's permanent address, or a place where
 34 the person routinely abides, lodges, or resides for a period of four (4) or more
 35 consecutive or nonconsecutive days in any month and which is not the person's
 36 permanent residence.

 20 SECTION 4. A new Section 11-39(E) is adopted as follows:
 21
 22 Section E. Sexual Offender and Sexual Predator Access to Parks,
 23 Playgrounds and Child Care Facilities Restricted; Penalties
 24
 25 (1) It is unlawful for a sexual offender or sexual predator convicted
 26 of a sexual offense, as defined in the section above, to knowingly be
 27 present in a municipal park or a playground, when a child under the
 28 age of sixteen (16) years is present, unless the sexual offender or
 29 sexual predator is the parent or legal guardian of a child present in the
 30 park or playground. Prior to any citation for a violation under this
 31 section, the alleged violator shall be given an opportunity to promptly
 32 leave the park or playground.
 33
 34 (2) It is unlawful for a sexual offender or sexual predator convicted
 35 of a sexual offense, as defined herein above, to knowingly enter or
 36 remain in a child care facility ("facility") or on its premises unless the
 37 sexual offender or sexual predator:
 38
 39 (a) Is dropping off or picking up a child registered at the facility
 40 and is the parent or guardian of said child; and
 41
 42 (b) Remains under the supervision of a facility supervisor or his
 43 or her designee while on the facility premises.
