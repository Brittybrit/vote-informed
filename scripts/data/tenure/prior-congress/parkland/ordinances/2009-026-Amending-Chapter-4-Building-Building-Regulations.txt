 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland finds that open air storage
 12 outside of residences and businesses constitutes a blight on neighborhoods, is unsightly,
 13 and constitutes a threat to the health, safety and welfare of the citizens; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland wishes to adopt reasonable regulations relating
 16 to open air storage to assure that junk, trash or abandon property will not be stored
 17 outside of residences and businesses in the City;
 18
 19 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 20 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 21
 22 SECTION 1. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained
 23 that the provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the City Code
 24 of the City of Parkland, and that the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to
 25 accomplish such intent.
 26
 27 SECTION 2. Chapter 4 of the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances is hereby
 28 amended as follows:

 7 SECTION 4-64 OPEN AIR STORAGE.
 8 a. Open-air storage in residential-zoned and commercial-zoned districts is subject
 9 to the following provisions:
 to b. It shall be unlawful to deposit, store, or maintain, or to permit to be deposited,
 t t stored, or maintained, junk as defined in this Ordinance, in or on any lot, parcel
 12 or tract of land or body of water in any zoning district, The deposit of junk in a
 13 location authorized for waste collection is exempted from this section, provided
 14 the junk is not or does not become a nuisance, and provided the junk is collected
 15 by a City authorized commercial waste collector.
 16 c. Junk property which would be visible, at ground level, from a street or other
 17 public or private property but for the concealment of such junk property behind a
 18 wall, fence, hedge or other plant material or by the use of a vehicle cover,
 19 plastics, fabrics or other materials to form a tent, partition or similar makeshift
 20 structure or device, shall be subject to this section.
 21 d. It shall be the responsibility of the property owner to maintain their property in
 22 accordance with the provisions of this section. Where applicable, tenants or
 23 lessees may receive enforcement notices in connection with enforcement;
 24 however, the property owner is ultimately responsible for compliance.
 25 e. The City may enforce this ordinance through the code enforcement Special
 26 Master or, in its sole discretion. The City may undertake remedial
 27 measures where there is an imminent threat of harm to the life, safety, or
 28 health of the public, but in no event shall the City be obligated to take such
 29 remedial action. Any costs incurred by the City shall be documented and
 30 when an affidavit of said costs is filed in the public records of Broward
 31 County it shall constitute a lien of superior status to any mortgage.
 32 f. Open-air storage in residential-zoned and commercial-zoned districts including
 33 but not limited to the following items and materials is prohibited:
 34 1. Junk, as defined in this Ordinance.
 35 2. Merchandise or manufacturing materials.
 36 3. Multiple boxes of uniform appearance bearing shipping labels,
 37 4. Multiple articles of similar type in unused condition, including without
 38 limitation raw materials for manufacturing furniture and computer
 39 components;
 40 5. Pallets containing multiple boxes;
