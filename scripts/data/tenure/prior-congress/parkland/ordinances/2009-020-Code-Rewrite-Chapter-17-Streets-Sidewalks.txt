 33
 34 Sec. 17-15 Sidewalks, Multi-Use Paths/Trails, and Bicycle Lanes
 35
 36 No construction before permit. No construction of sidewalks, multi-use
 37 paths/trails, and/or bicycle lanes shall be started until a permit for the proposed
 38 installation has been granted by the city's engineer, qualified engineering
 39 consultant, or designee. Violation of this ordinance is a misdemeanor, punishable
 40 as provided by law.
 41
 42 a) Sidewalks shall be constructed in compliance with the design standards of
 43 any agencies having jurisdiction including but not limited to, the American
 44 with Disabilities Act (ADA), Florida Department of Transportation, Broward
 45 County, and the City of Parkland as may be amended from time to time.

 2 b) Minimum sidewalk width shall be five(5)feet.
 3
 4 c) Minimum construction standards: All sidewalks shall be constructed of
 5 Portland concrete (3,000 psi) and not less than four inches (4") thick
 6 except at driveways which shall be not less than six inches (6") thick.
 7
 8 d) Sidewalk widths may be required to be greater than five (5) feet when
 9 large pedestrian volumes are anticipated or in accordance with the City's
 10 Comprehensive Plan or adopted Multi-use Trails Plan as may be
 11 amended from time to time.
 12
 13 e) Sidewalks shall incorporate pedestrian cross-access at intersections in
 14 compliance with the American with Disabilities Act (ADA), Broward
 15 County, Florida Department of Transportation, City of Parkland and/or any
 16 other agency(ies) having jurisdiction.
 17
 18 f) Multi-Use paths/trails shall be constructed in compliance with the design
 19 standards of any agencies having jurisdiction indudinq but not limited to,
 20 the American with Disabilities Act (ADA), Florida Department of
 21 Transportation, Broward County, and the City of Parkland as may be
 22 amended from time to time.
 23
 24 g) A multi-use pedestrian/bicycle path with a minimum width of six (6) feet
 25 and constructed within an adjacent public or private right-of-way may be
 26 used in lieu of the required sidewalk.
 27
 28 h) Multi-Use paths/trails shall be constructed in accordance with Sections
 29 17.15(c) and (e) above.
 30
 31 i) Bicycle lanes on new and existing roadways shall be constructed in
 32 accordance with Florida Department of Transportation, Broward County,
 33 and City of Parkland standards.
 34
 35 j) Parking, stopping, or standing of motorized vehicles within bicycle lanes
 36 for any purpose other than routine maintenance or approved construction
 37 activities is prohibited.
 38
