 13 Sec. 16-23. Dedication of drainage easements.
 14
 15 (a) As a condition precedent to obtaining any building permit for any lot,
 16 or upon sale or transfer of any lot, an owner shall dedicate to the city such
 17 drainage easements as hereinafter required, and shall pay any special
 18 assessment certified against said owner's lot as hereinafter provided.
 19
 20 (b) Side drainage easements, five (5) feet in width with minimum slopes of
 21 0.3%, extending from the side property lines of each lot, and running in an
 22 east and west direction on both the north and south lot lines, shall be
 23 required.
 24
 25 For corner lots, no side drainage easement will be required along any
 26 lot line adjacent to a street. If an owner wishes to construct one residence
 27 on two (2) or more contiguous lots, then such combined lots shall be
 28 treated as one lot for purposes of drainage plans and dedication of side
 29 drainage easements; however, no such combination of lots will affect the
 30 requirements for dedication of main drainage easements in respect to
 31 each lot (as per the unrecorded plat of Pine Tree Estates).
 32
 33 (d) Any future subdivision of such combined lots shall be subject to
 34 dedication of additional side drainage easements as required.
 35
 36 (b)(e) A rear drainage easement, seventeen and five-tenths (17.5) feet in
 37 width with minimum slopes of 0.3%, extending from the rear property line
 38 of each lot, and running in a north and south direction along the rear
 39 property line, shall be required.
