 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AMENDING SECTION 13-67 ENTITLED
 5 "RESERVED" BY RENAMING SECTION 13-67 TO BE ENTITLED
 6 "PUBLIC SCHOOL CONCURRENCY"; REQUIRING THAT FUTURE
 7 LAND DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS WITH A RESIDENTIAL
 8 COMPONENT MEET REQUIREMENTS FOR PUBLIC SCHOOL
 9 CONCURRENCY; PROVIDING FOR CODIFICATION; PROVIDING FOR
 10 CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR
 11 AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 14 Florida, pursuant to the authority vested in Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, is authorized
 15 and empowered to consider changes to its Code of Ordinances; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the Florida
 18 Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, the Charter of the City of Parkland, and the
 19 police powers of the City; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 163, Florida
 22 Statutes, the City Planning and Zoning Board has reviewed the proposed ordinance
 23 and has determined that the proposed regulations are consistent with the City of
 24 Parkland Comprehensive Plan; and
 25
 26 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 27 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 28
 29 SECTION 1. Section 13-67 of the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances is
 30 hereby amended to read as follows:
 31
 32 Section 13-67 Reserved. Public school concurrency requirements.
 33
 34 All plats, replats, plat note amendments, findings of adequacy, or site plans with a
 35 residential component shall be subject to the public school concurrency requirements as
 36 set forth in Section 5-182(m) of the Broward County Land Development Code, as
 37 amended from time to time, which regulations and requirements are incorporated herein
 38 by reference. The burden shall be on the applicant to ensure compliance with this
 39 section, and the applicant shall provide the necessary documentation from Broward
 40 County demonstrating satisfaction of these requirements.
 41
 42 SECTION 2. That the text incorporated herein will mandate that any future
 43 development with a housing component will be required to meet the requirements set
 44 forth in the Broward County Code of Ordinances, Chapter 5, Article IX.
