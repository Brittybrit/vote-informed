 ORDINANCE NO. 2010—07
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA FOR WRITING PROCEDURES FOR
 IMPLEMENTATION OF THE COMPETITIVE CONSULTANT'S
 NEGOTIATONS ACT PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTES
 SECTION 287.055 AND PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE
 CITY PROCUREMENT CODE IN SUBSECTION 2-146,
 OBTAINING PROVISION FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE
 CONTAINING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; CONTAINING AN
 EFFECTIVE DATE
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland wishes to have specific procedures in place to
 implement the provisions of Section 287.055 (Competitive Consultant's Negotiations Act
 or CCNA); and
 WHEREAS,the City wishes those procedures to be fair and equitable and place a
 premium on a review by those with expertise in the areas in question;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA THAT:
 Section 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are hereby ratified and
 confirmed as being true and correct, and are hereby incorporated herein and made a part
 hereof.
 Section 2. A new section 2-146 is hereby created as follows:
 COMPETITVE CONSULTANT'S NEGOTIATIONS
 ACT PROCEDURES
 The following procedures shall apply where the City procures services regulated
 by Section 287.055, Florida Statutes, referred to as Consultant's Competitive
 Negotiations Act(CCNA).
 1. The following procedures apply for existing contracts where more than
 one firm has been retained to provide said continuing services:

 A. City staff should review the qualifications of approved contractors
 to determine which firm best meets the needs of the project and should attempt to
 equitably distribute the work to the various contractors retained.
 B. City staff shall provide documentation as to the basis for utilizing
 the particular contractor together with a justification as to why the particular contractor
 was selected and file with the City Clerk.
 C. The chosen firm shall provide a complete task authorization "or
 work authorization" which describes the scope of the project, the services to be provided
 and a detailed breakdown of the consideration together with a total cost estimate with
 expenses, if possible.
 D. If the value of the work exceeds $25,000.00 the City Commission
 must approve the work authorization.
 2. The following procedures shall comply with the CCNA procedures for
 obtaining a new consultant covered by the Act:
 A. The purchasing division or other division authorized by the City
 Manager shall issue a request for qualifications for an RFQ to obtain qualifications from
 potential consultants from which the selection will be made.
 B. An evaluation committee comprised of staff as appointed by the
 City Manager shall be established; the City Manager shall appoint at least one member
 from the using agency and others who have expertise which will assist them in the
 necessary evaluation.
 C. The RFQ shall provide for a City staff member to receive all
 responses. When the deadline for responses has been reached that staff person shall
 distribute the responses to all committee members who shall review the responses.
 D. The committee shall have a minimum of three members. All
 meetings of the selection committee shall be conducted in a manner consistent with the
 Sunshine Law and all applicants shall receive notice by either mail, fax or e-mail. A
 Quorum shall be a majority of members except that if there are only three members all
 three must be present. To participate the members of the selection committee must be
 physically present. All members shall be free of any conflicts of interest as set forth in
 Chapter 112, Florida Statutes. The selection committee shall then set forth the procedure
 for reviewing the applicants. The selection committee shall reduce the number of firms
 to a short list of a minimum of three (provided at lease three firms applied). In short-
 listing firms, the committee shall use the criteria set forth in the RFQ and attempt to
 select the best qualified firms for the particular project. The committee shall then hold
 discussions with all short-listed firms. This may be undertaken at the same meeting or a
 meeting scheduled by the committee.

 E. After discussions are held with the short-listed firms, the voting
 members of the selection committee may discuss the presentations and the qualifications
 of each firm further and shall rank the firms based upon which firms at least will best
 serve the City based upon the factors set forth in the RFQ. The firms shall be ranked in
 order of preference. This ranking and a report of the committee's analysis shall be
 provided to the City Commission who shall make the final decision with regard to the
 firms that should be chosen. The City Commission may approve the rankings as set forth
 by the selection committee or, upon the request of a majority of the City Commissioners
 present at the meeting, re-rank the applicants based upon the criteria and the report and
 rankings from the selection committee.. Prior to re-ranking the applicants based upon the
 criteria, the City Commission must undertake a majority vote to indicate that it may wish
 to rank the applicants in an order different from those established by the selection
 committee.
 F. Upon the City Commission approving a ranking, negotiations shall
 be undertaken with the number one ranked firm. The City or its manager or his/her
 designee shall undertake said negotiations of the City Commission may appoint a
 negotiations committee. If the City Manager or his/her designee is unable to negotiate a
 satisfactory contract with the first ranked firm, negotiations with that firm shall be
 formally terminated in a writing sent to the firm. Upon termination of said negotiations,
 negotiations shall then be undertaken with the second ranked firm, with this process
 being repeated until an agreement is reached which is then approved by the negotiator
 and formally approved by the City Commission or until the short-list is exhausted in
 which case a new request for qualifications shall be undertaken.
 G. All procedures set forth above shall be applied in a manner
 consistent with the CCNA and in compliance therewith.
 3. Contract Administration — The City Manager shall appoint a contract
 administrator or project manager for each contract to assure compliance with the contract
 and applicable law. The contract administrator or project manager shall review and
 approve all pay requests or deny same as required.
 Section 3. It is the intention of the City Commission of the City of Parkland,

 of Parkland's Code of Ordinances. The sections of this ordinance may be re-numbered or
 relettered and the word "ordinance" may be changed to "section," "article," or such other
 appropriate word or phrase in order to accomplish such intentions.
