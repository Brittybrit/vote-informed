 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, REPEALING SECTIONS 4.2-1 THROUGH 4.2-
 5 3 RELATING TO "CABLE COMMUNICATIONS"; PROVIDING A
 6 CONFLICTS CLAUSE AND A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; AND
 7 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 8
 9 WHEREAS, the State of Florida has preempted most municipal regulatory
 10 authority over cable communications;
 11
 12 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 13 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 14
 15 SECTION 1. Sections 4.2-1 through 4.2-3 of the City of Parkland Code of
 16 Ordinances relating to Cable Communications is hereby repealed.
 17
 18 SECTION 2. Should any section or provision of this ordinance or any portion
 19 thereof, any paragraph, sentence or word be declared by a court of competent
 20 jurisdiction to be invalid, such decision shall not affect the validity of the remainder
 21 hereof as a whole or part thereof other than the part declared to be invalid.
 22
 23 SECTION 3. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 24 passage and adoption.
 25
 26 PASSED this 15th day of October 2008, upon first reading.
 27
 28 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 5th day of November 2008, upon
 29 second and final reading.
