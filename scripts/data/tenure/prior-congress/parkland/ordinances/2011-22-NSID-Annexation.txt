 4 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 5 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, PROVIDING FOR THE ANNEXATION INTO
 6 THE CORPORATE LIMITS OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND OF
 7 APPROXIMATELY 7.72 ACRE PROPERTY KNOWN AS NORTH
 8 SPRINGS IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT RIGHT OF WAY, GENERALLY
 9 LOCATED APPROXIMATELY 3.5 MILES WEST OF PARKSIDE DRIVE
 10 ON LOXAHATCHEE ROAD, PROPERTY CONTROL NUMBER
 11 474129010013; CONTAINING PROVISIONS FOR IMPLEMENTATION
 12 AND FILINGS WITH THE APPROPRIATE GOVERMENTAL
 13 AGENCIES; PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE;
 14 PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE
 15 DATE.
 16
 17 WHEREAS, North Springs Improvement District, the owner of the property with the
 18 property control number 474129010013 (said land described on Exhibit A), has submitted a
 19 petition to annex its property into the City of Parkland, pursuant to a voluntary annexation; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the Land described in Exhibit A, is the Land owned by North Springs
 22 Improvement District, and shall hereinafter be referred to as "The Land"; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the map attached as Exhibit B reflects the general location of the Land; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the Land is located in unincorporated Broward County contiguous to the
 27 City of Parkland; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the requirements of Section 171.044, Florida Statutes, pertaining to
 30 voluntary annexations, have been complied with in that:
 31
 32 A. The Land is contiguous to the City of Parkland and is reasonably compact
 33 and does not create an enclave;
 34
 35 B. The petitioner, the sole owner of the Land, has sought annexation by a
 36 Petition for Annexation and approves the annexation of the Land into the City of Parkland;
 37
 38 C. This ordinance has been advertised as required by Section 171.044,
 39 Florida Statutes;
 40
 41 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that annexation of the property is in the best
 42 interest of the City of Parkland and the City of Parkland is prepared to provide all necessary
 43 municipal services to the Land; and
 44

 1 WHEREAS, the annexation of the Land into the City of Parkland will promote orderly
 2 planning and the efficient provision of the municipal services to the Land; and
 3
 4 WHEREAS, the City has determined that the Petition for Annexation bears the signature
 5 of the owner(s) of the property of the area proposed to be annexed and constitutes consent to this
 6 annexation;
 7
 8 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 9 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 10
 11 SECTION 1. The above "WHEREAS" clauses are true and correct and made a part
 12 hereof.
 13
 14 SECTION 2. The Land is hereby annexed into the corporate boundaries of the City of
 15 Parkland, Florida.
 16
 17 SECTION 3. The boundaries of the City of Parkland are re-defined to include the Land.
 18
 19 SECTION 4. The current land use and zoning remain unchanged by this Ordinance and
 20 shall be as provided in Section 171.062(2), Florida Statutes, until such time as the City of
 21 Parkland adopts a comprehensive plan that includes the annexed property.
 22
 23 SECTION 5. The City shall take all necessary actions before the Broward County
 24 Legislative Delegation to comply with all local acts relating to annexation and this annexation is
 25 conditioned upon approval of the Delegation.
 26
 27 SECTION 6. Within seven (7) days after its adoption a copy of this Ordinance shall be
 28 filed with the Clerk of the Circuit Court of Broward County for recordation. Within seven (7)
 29 days of adoption, this Ordinance shall be filed with the Broward County Administrator and with
 30 the Florida Department of State. Within seven (7) days after approval by the Delegation, the City
 31 shall record a Notice of Delegation Approval to which the approved ordinance is attached.
 32
 33 SECTION 7. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 34 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of the
 35 City of Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to accomplish
 36 such intent.
 37
 38 SECTION 8. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for
 39 any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of
 40 any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 41
 42 SECTION 9. This Ordinance shall be effective immediately upon adoption but the
 43 annexation provided herein shall become effective on 12:01 a.m. September 15, 2012; at such
 44 time the Land shall become a part of the City of Parkland, to be governed by the City and subject
 45 to the laws and regulations of the City.

 PETITION FOR ANNEXATION OF REAL PROPERTY
 TO
 CITY OF PARKLAND, BROWARD COUNTY, FLORIDA
 The North Springs Improvement District, a unit of Special Purpose Government and Public
 Corporation of the State of Florida, hereby petition the City of Parkland for annexation of the
 parcel described in Exhibit "A" attached hereto, pursuant to Section 171.044, Florida Statutes.
 I/We qualify that I am/We are the Owner(s) of Record of said parcel, pursuant to Exhibit "A"
 [Deed or other Proof of Ownership]. I/We attest, that this property to the best of my/our
 knowledge and understanding, is adjacent to the existing City of Parkland boundary and satisfies
 all other criteria under law for annexation. I/We would also seek to have all rights-of-way
 adjacent to the described parcel annexed at a minimum to the centerline of the right-of-way. It is
 our desire for said property to be annexed into the corporate boundaries of the City of Parkland
 and this petition should be deemed a request for and consent to said annexation.
 Further, the undersigned hereby authorize(s) the City of Parkland to undertake those actions and
 procedures necessary so that the subject property becomes annexed and part of the City of
 Parkland.
 The undersigned further warrant(s) and represent(s) that all of the persons, firms or corporations
 who own fee simple title to the subject property described in Exhibit "A" have executed this
 Petition and have affixed their names as their ownership interest appears on the title to said
 property.
