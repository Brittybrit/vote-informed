 22 WHEREAS, effective February 29, 2004 the City created the Parkland Police Officers
 23 Retirement Plan (the "Plan"); and
 24 WHEREAS, subsequent to the creation of the Plan, the City contracted with the
 25 Broward Sheriffs Office for law enforcement and related services; and
 26 WHEREAS, the purchase of annuities from a third party insurance company will
 27 transfer investment and related actuarial risks from the City to the annuity provider; and
 28 WHEREAS, the payment of the plan's 2% COLA and related enhancements adopted in
 29 2007 are contingent on the receipt of sufficient Chapter 185 premium tax revenue; and
 30 WHEREAS, the City Commission has received and reviewed an actuarial impact
 31 statement regarding these changes for the City.
 32 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Commission of the City of
 33 Parkland as follows:
 34 Section 1. Recitals. The above recitals are true and correct and incorporated herein
 35 by this reference.

 1 Section 2. City Code Amended. Section 2-226 (a), (i) and (j), "Service Retirement
 2 Benefit", are hereby amended as follows:
 3
 4 (a) A member may retire on the first day of the month coincident with or next following the
 5 earlier of: the date upon which the member completes twenty-five (25) years of credited
 6 service and attains age fifty-two (52); the date upon which the member completes
 7 twenty (20) years of credited service; or the date upon which the member attains age
 8 fifty-five (55) with ten (10) years of credited service. Notwithstanding anything to the
 9 contrary, the sole remaining member of the plan shall be permitted to retire without any
 10 penalty for early retirement based on existing credited service as of June 15, 2011.
 11 There shall be no mandatory retirement age.

 16 Section 3. City Code Amended. Section 2-233(f), "Miscellaneous", is hereby
 17 created:
 18
 19 Sec. 2-233. Miscellaneous.
 20
 21 (f) At a time of its choosing, the City shall have the authority to purchase annuities
 22 to cover the value of the "base benefit"under the Plan, prior to the adoption of
 23 Ordinance 2006-45 which increased the multiplier from 2%to 3% and created a
 24 2% COLA funded with premium tax revenue. After the annuities are purchased,
 25 the Board shall be responsible for paying the benefits adopted by Ordinance
 26 2006-45, with available premium tax revenue, consistent with state law. By
 27 purchasing annuities from a reputable third party insurance company, the City
 28 intends to reduce all unfunded actuarial liabilities and minimize the risk of
 29 financial market volatility. Nevertheless, the City recognizes that it is ultimately
 30 responsible as the plan sponsor to guarantee payments, in the event of default or
 31 insolvency by the annuity provider. For this reason, the City reserves all rights
 32 to receive a credit against future Chapter 185 revenue in the event of future
 33 amendments to Chapter 185.

 35 Section 4. Severability. Should this ordinance or any part thereof be declared
 36 invalid by a Court of competent jurisdiction, the invalidity of any part of this ordinance shall
 37 not otherwise affect the validity of the remaining provisions of this ordinance, which shall be
 38 deemed to have been enacted without the invalid provision.

 40 Section 5. Inclusion in the Code. It is the intention of the City Commission that

 42 Parkland, and that the word "ordinance" may be changed to "section," "article," or such other
 43 appropriate word or phrase in order to accomplish such intentions.
 44
 45 Section 6. Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective immediately
 46 upon its passage.
