 4 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 5 PARKLAND, FLORIDA RELATING TO FLAG POLES AND FLAGS;
 6 AMENDING SECTION 15-9 ENTITLED "FLAG POLES AND FLAGS IN ALL
 7 ZONING DISTRICTS" OF THE CODE OF ORDINANCES; PROVIDING A
 8 CONFLICTS CLAUSE AND A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; AND PROVIDING
 9 AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland, Florida,
 13 pursuant to the authority vested in Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, is authorized and empowered to
 14 consider changes to its land development regulations; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the Florida
 17 Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, the Charter of the City of Parkland, and the police
 18 powers of the City; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 163, Florida Statutes, the
 21 City Planning and Zoning Board has reviewed the proposed Ordinance and has determined
 22 that the proposed regulations are consistent with the City of Parkland Comprehensive Plan.
 23
 24 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 25 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 26
 27 SECTION 1. Section 15-9 of the Code of Ordinance is hereby added and states as
 28 follows:
 29
 30 (a) Except for residential lots of record which are over two (2) acres in size, there shall be one (1)
 31 flag pole, allowed per parcel or lot of record for the display of flags. For residential parcels
 32 greater than two (2) acres, two (2) flag poles shall be permitted. Commercial parcels and
 33 community facilities, excluding government owned parcels, shall be permitted to have no more
 34 than one (1) flag pole and the location of the flag pole shall be identified on the site plan prior to
 35 receiving a building permit.
 36 (b) Except as set forth herein, the maximum height of the flag pole in any residential zoning district
 37 shall not exceed twenty-five (25) feet. Flagpoles in nonresidential zoning districts shall not
 38 exceed the allowed height of the zoning district or seventy (70) feet, whichever is less.
 39 (c) The maximum dimensions of any flag shall be proportional to the flag pole height. The hoist
 40 side of the flag shall not exceed 20% of the vertical height of the pole. In addition, flags are subject
 41 to the following dimensional limitations:
 42 Pole Height Maximum Flag Size
 43 Up to 25 feet 24 total square feet
 44 25 to 39 feet 40 total square feet
 45 40 to 49 feet 60 total square feet
 46 50 to 59 feet 96 total square feet
 47 60 to 69 feet 150 total square feet
 48 70 feet 216 total square feet
 49 (d) The setback of the flag pole from all property lines shall be equal to the height of pole. The
 50 maximum number of flags allowed per flag pole shall be three (3).

 1 (e) In addition to the display of flags on a flag pole, one (1) flag may be displayed on a pole
 2 mounted on a bracket at a building entrance.
 3 (f) In the event that a flag pole is proposed to camouflage a cellular tower as part of a proposal to
 4 limit the adverse visual appearance of a lattice or other noncamouflaged cellular tower, the location
 5 of which is otherwise necessary, the city commission may allow a flag pole to reach a height greater
 6 than set forth herein and may allow for a flag size greater than set forth herein.
 7
 8 SECTION 2: It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 9 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of
 10 the City of Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance to accomplish such intent.
 11
 12 SECTION 3. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for
 13 any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of
 14 any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 15
 16 SECTION 4. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 17 passage and adoption.
