 11 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland wishes to amend the Comprehensive Plan in
 12 accordance with the requirements of Chapter 163, Florida Statutes; and
 13 WHEREAS, a public hearing was held November 18, 2010, by the City of
 14 Parkland Local Planning Agency in compliance with Chapter 163, Florida Statutes, to
 15 consider the above referenced Amendments; and
 16 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida held the first
 17 public hearing on November 30, 2010, pursuant to Chapter 163, Florida Statutes, to
 18 consider transmittal of the proposed amendments to the Comprehensive Plan to the
 19 State review agencies under Chapter 163, Florida Statutes, at which time the parties
 20 and interests and all other citizens and residents of the City of Parkland so desiring to
 21 participate had an opportunity to be and were heard; and
 22 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida has determined
 23 that the public has adequately participated in the planning process as required by
 24 Chapter 163, Florida Statutes during the course of the preparation and transmittal
 25 process; and
 26 WHEREAS, the reviewing agencies authorized by Chapter 163 may make
 27 objections, recommendations and comments (ORC Report) to the City; and
 28 WHEREAS, the City may consider said ORC Report and adopt the amendments
 29 with changes; and
 30 WHEREAS, the local governing body shall hold at least two public hearings on
 31 the proposed amendment; one at the transmittal stage and one at the adoption stage in

 34 documents are hereby incorporated as referenced; and

 1 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the application in its entirety,
 2 staff reports, minutes of meetings, findings of fact and support documents and
 3 determines the request is in the best interests of the City;
 4 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 5 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 6 Section 1: That the foregoing application recitals, findings of fact, minutes of
 7 meetings and all staff reports and documents are true and correct, and are hereby
 8 incorporated by reference and transmitted.
 9 Section 2: That the attached amendments to the Comprehensive Plan, generally
 10 addressing revised data and analysis, goals, objectives and policies and plan
 11 implementation procedures, having been recommended for approval by the Local
 12 Planning Agency on November 18, 2010 are hereby adopted as amendments to the
 13 Comprehensive Plan by the City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida.
 14 Section 3: That the public hearing was held on November 18, 2010 by the Local
 15 Planning Agency in compliance with Section 163, Florida Statutes, to consider the
 16 above referenced Comprehensive Plan Amendments.
 17 Section 4: That these amendments to the Comprehensive Plan shall be
 18 transmitted to the Florida Department of Community Affairs in compliance with Chapter
 19 163, Florida Statutes.
 20 Section 5: That in the event any provision or application of this Ordinance shall
 21 be held to the invalid, it is the legislative intent that the other provisions and applications
 22 hereof shall not be thereby affected.
 23 Section 6: That all Ordinances or parts of Ordinances in conflict herewith are to
 24 the extent of said conflict, hereby repealed.
 25 Section 7: That this Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its passage
 26 and adoption.
