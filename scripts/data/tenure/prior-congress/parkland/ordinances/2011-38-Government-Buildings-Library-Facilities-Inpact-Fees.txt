 14 SECTION 2. Section 13-68 (a) 11 of the City Code is hereby amended to read as
 15 follows
 16
 17 (11) (a) Adequacy of government buildings and library facilities.
 18 (i) Government buildings include: administrative buildings, public works
 19 buildings, maintenance buildings, storage buildings and similar facilities. Adequate government
 20 buildings are essential to the proper administration of municipal services. The buildings and land
 21 which house the municipal administration and legislative staff and personnel must be sufficient
 22 to adequately serve the proposed development considering any increase in staff requirements
 23 necessary to serve the proposed development. Adequacy of such government buildings is a
 24 precondition to the approval of any development permit.
 25
 26 (ii) Libraries are an essential part of the educational and cultural enrichment
 27 of the community and facilities must be adequate to serve the entire population of residents,
 28 including new residents created by proposed development. These facilities must be sufficient to
 29 meet the needs of residents who will reside in new development as a condition of any
 30 development permit.
 31
 32 (b) Findings and Necessity of Impact Fee. The provision of adequate general government
 33 buildings and library facilities is essential for the existing/achieved level of service (LOS) for the
 34 citizens of the City of Parkland. Without the imposition of an impact fee, the City will either
 35 have to utilize additional general fund revenues to serve the demands of new development or
 36 experience a declining level of service as the current inventory of infrastructure serves additional
 37 new development. The impact fees set forth herein are based upon the study conducted by
 38 Tindale-Oliver & Associates, Inc., dated November 9, 2011, and is based upon recent and
 39 localized data. The impact fees are designed to represent the new development's proportionate
 40 share of the capital costs associated with providing general government buildings and library
 41 facilities infrastructure capacity to serve the new development. The Florida Legislature has
 42 found in Section 163.31801, Florida Statutes, as amended from time to time that impact fees are
 43 an important source of revenue for local government to use in funding the infrastructure
 44 necessitated by new growth. The purpose of this impact fee is to assure new development pays
 45 its fair share for infrastructure which is necessitated by new growth and that the fees paid will
 46 benefit the new growth.

 1 (c) For the purposes of the government building and library impact fees a building shall
 2 be considered any structure having a roof and four walls or used or built for the shelter or
 3 enclosure of person, animals, chattels, or property of any kind.
 4
 5 (d) General Government Buildings Impact Fees
 6
 7 A general government buildings impact fee is hereby imposed against all residential and
 8 non-residential buildings constructed in the City of Parkland. Any application for a building
 9 permit in the City of Parkland subsequent to the effective date of this ordinance shall be subject
 10 to the assessment of this fee. The impact fees shall be paid prior to receiving any building
 11 permits for a residential or non-residential building in the City of Parkland and shall be paid
 12 based upon the schedule attached hereto as Exhibit A. The fees shall be paid by the party
 13 applying for the building permit. No building permit shall be issued unless an impact fee
 14 imposed herein has been paid. Unless otherwise specifically provided for herein, the fees are not
 15 refundable.
 16
 17 (e) Library Services Impact Fees
 18
 19 A library services impact fee is hereby imposed against all residential and non-residential
 20 buildings constructed in the City of Parkland. Any application for a building permit in the City of
 21 Parkland subsequent to the effective date of this ordinance shall be subject to the assessment of
 22 this fee. The impact fees shall be paid prior to receiving any building permits for a residential or
 23 non-residential building in the City of Parkland and shall be paid based upon the schedule
 24 attached hereto as Exhibit B. The fees shall be paid by the party applying for the building
 25 permit. No building permit shall be issued unless an impact fee imposed herein has been paid.
 26 Unless otherwise specifically provided for herein the fees imposed herein are not refundable.
 27
 28 (f) Exemptions
 29
 30 The following shall be exempted from the obligation to pay general government
 31 buildings and library facilities impact fees:
 32
 33 (i) Alteration, expansion or replacement of an existing non-residential
 34 building where the use of the structure is not changed or interior square footage is not increased;
 35 or the alteration, expansion or replacement of any existing residential building where interior
 36 square footage is not increased by more than 33% for a residential building.
 37
 38 (ii) Government or public facilities owned by the Federal, State, County or
 39 City government or the South Florida Water Management District where the building or facility
 40 is related to the operation of those entities.
 41
 42 (iii) Full or partial replacement of buildings destroyed by fire or act of God.
 43

 1 (i) The City Manager shall annually adjust the general government buildings
 2 and library services impact fees schedule provided herein by indexing the schedule to inflation
 3 as defined by the Engineering News Record, "Construction Cost Index", but no increase shall
 4 exceed five (5%) percent in any year without a recalculation of fees and amendment to this
 5 ordinance. The first indexing calculation adjustment shall occur during 2012-2013 budget year
 6 using the Construction Cost Index for the previous budget year. The adjusted Impact Fee
 7 Schedule shall be on file with the Department of Planning and Zoning and City Clerk.
 8
 9 (ii) Unless otherwise directed by the City Commission, any adjustments to
 10 the general government buildings and library facilities impact fees made pursuant to this section,
 ii. shall be effective the first of October each calendar year.
 12
 13 (iii) If the Construction Cost Index is discontinued or revised, such other
 14 government index or computation with which it is replaced shall be used in order to obtain
 15 substantially the same result as would be obtained if the index had not been discontinued or
 16 revised.

 18 (g)Appeals
 19
 20 Any decision relative to the imposition of any impact fee may be appealed by the
 21 feepayer to the City Commission where it is claimed that the fee or any expenditure violates this
 22 ordinance, any state or federal law or a constitutional or other established right. The appeal shall
 23 be filed within thirty (30) days of the act which is the subject of the appeal. The appeal shall be
 24 filed in writing and delivered to the City Clerk prior to the end of the thirty (30) day period set
 25 forth above. The appeal shall set forth all the facts, argument and law believed to be relevant to
 26 the appeal and the specific relief requested. The City Commission shall hear the appeal within
 27 forty-five (45) days of delivery and may either grant the relief requested, deny the relief
 28 requested or fashion such relief as is deemed just and proper.
 29
 30 (h) Accounting
 31
 32 The City shall provide for an annual accounting in reporting of all impact fee collections
 33 and expenditures. Said revenues and expenditures shall be contained in a separate accounting
 34 fund specifically created for the purpose of infrastructure needs and capital equipment for the
 35 specific impact fee collected. Any administrative charges related to the collection of impact fees
 36 shall be solely based upon actual cost.
 37
 38 ( i) Audit
 39
 40 The annual audit of financial statements of the City of Parkland that are submitted to the Auditor
 41 General shall include an affidavit signed by the Chief Financial Officer of the City stating that
 42 the City has complied with the requirements of Section 163.31801, Florida Statutes, as amended
 43 from time to time.

 1 (j) Expenditure of Funds.
 2
 3 The City shall expend, or commit for expenditure, any impact fees received within seven
 4 (7) years of payment; if not expended or committed within that time frame the fees shall be
 5 returned to the feepayer upon request of the feepayer provided said request is made within three
 6 (3) months of the conclusion of the seven (7) year period and provided said refund contains an
 7 affidavit that the person requesting the refund is the owner of the property on behalf of which the
 8 impact fee was paid, together with evidence of payment of the fee. All funds expended shall be
 9 used to benefit the development which paid the funds and for the purpose for which the
 10 particular impact fee was paid (libraries for library facilities fee and government buildings for
 11 government buildings fee). Funds shall only be used for the acquisition, expansion or
 12 improvement of buildings, structures or real property, capital facilities, or equipment made
 13 necessary by the new development and needed to mitigate the impact of new development.
