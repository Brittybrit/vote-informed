 32 Plan is specifically subject to and condition upon an amendment to the restrictive

 34 townhouse units from the underlying plat.

 1 8. All regulations pertaining to the Multi-Family shall be determined during the site plan

 3 9. The applicant shall be responsible for the construction of the sidewalk in front of the
 4 fire station / archeological parcel which includes, but is not limited to, the hiring of all
 5 required professionals to monitor the site for the City and Broward County and shall
 6 commence within sixty (60) days from the date of the City Commission approval.
 7 The Broward County Historical Commission requests archaeological monitoring of
 8 all ground disturbance activities adjacent to the Margate-Blount property along the
 9 Trails End ROW. Monitoring should be conducted by a qualified professional
 10 archaeologist. The Broward County Historical Commission shall be notified upon
 11 selection of the archaeologist and shall be notified at least 24 hours prior to
 12 commencing of field work. The monitor shall be on-site during all ground
 13 disturbance activities and will be responsible for submitting a monitoring report to
 14 the Historical Commission for review.

 17 i. LED exterior lighting;
 18 ii. Entry signs solar lighting:
 19 iii. Sustainable lighting:
 20 iv. Floor sheathing from renewable sources;

 23 11.There is nothing in the submitted and approved applications that will vest the right
 24 to increase density over and above the approved Land Use Plan Amendment and
 25 PRD Zoning approval.
 26 12.The Applicant shall make an effort to build all proposed parallel parking spaces
 27 (intended for guests parking) using permeable asphalt. In lieu of implementing such
 28 a technology at this time, the Applicant shall commit to build not less than 50% of
 29 the roadways in Pod B utilizing permeable asphalt. This item shall not be the
 30 subject of any variance request, whether the work is done by Centerline Homes or
 31 another Developer.
 32 13.The Applicant shall make an effort to install LED-based signage and roadway
 33 markers throughout the community. In lieu of implementing such a technology at
 34 this time, the Applicant shall commit to install LED-based signage and roadway
 35 markers throughout the entire Pod B community. This item shall not be the subject
 36 of any variance request, whether the work is done by Centerline Homes or another
 37 Developer.
 38 14.All previously approved Conditions of Approval shall remain in full force and effect.
 39 15. Per Section 22-132 (b) of Parkland's Code, parallel parking spaces shall be 10 feet
 40 in width and 25 feet in length. On the Site Engineering Plans, please revise the 42
 41 proposed parallel guest parking spaces located along the inner loop roads of Pod A
 42 to adhere to the Code.

 44 shall be designed with bumper guards, wheel stops, or contiguous curbing. On the

 1 17.Please provide plan-view detail of the 42 proposed parallel guest parking spaces
 2 located along the inner loop roads of Pod A on the Site Engineering Plans. The

 4 18.On Sheet 6 of the Site Engineering Plans, please provide dimensions and details
 5 regarding the proposed roundabout. In particular, please graphically depict the
 6 turning radius of an AASHTO Single Unit (SU) design vehicle, consistent with a
 7 garbage truck.
 8 19. It appears that parallel parking spaces are being proposed along the East/West
 9 Road near the Pine Island Road intersection. These parallel parking spaces appear
 10 in the Landscape Architecture Plans and the cover page of the Master Site Plan
 11 Amendment Plan but do NOT appear in the Site Engineering Plans. If the spaces
 12 are to be included, please maintain consistency between the submitted documents
 13 and provide appropriate dimensions and details.
 14 20. Due to traffic safety and sight distance concerns, it is recommended that the two
 15 parallel parking spaces located immediately south of Unit 12 in Block E as well as
 16 the two parallel parking spaces located immediately north of Unit 22 in Block L be
 17 eliminated.
 18 21.Please note that the engineering design for the proposed roundabout may differ
