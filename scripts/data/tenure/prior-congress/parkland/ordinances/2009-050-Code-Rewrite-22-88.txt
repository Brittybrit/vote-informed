 31 Section 22-364. Location of trailers.
 32
 33 Trailers (and any other associated storage/dumpster containers) shall be located
 34 as far away from any abutting public right-of-way or adjoining residential property as
 35 practical given the extent and location of the improvements to be constructed on the
 36 site. The location of the trailer shall be approved by the planning director and city
 37 engineer, qualified enqineerinq consultant, or desiqnee.
 38
 39 SECTION 3. Should any section or provision of this ordinance or any portion
 40 thereof, any paragraph, sentence or word be declared by a court of competent
 41 jurisdiction to be invalid, such decision shall not affect the validity of the remainder
 42 hereof as a whole or part thereof other than the part declared to be invalid.
 43
 44 SECTION 4. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 45 passage and adoption.
