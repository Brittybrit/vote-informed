 ORDINANCE NO. 2008-25
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AMENDING
 CHAPTER 7, CODE OF ORDINANCES ENTITLED "GARBAGE AND
 REFUSE," ARTICLE I ENTITLED "IN GENERAL," BY ADDING SECTION 7-4
 ENTITLED "MUNICIPAL AUTHORITY"; PROVIDING A CONFLICTS CLAUSE;
 PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE
 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 403.706(1) of the Florida Statutes, Cities have the primary
 responsibility to provide for the collection and transportation of solid waste from their jurisdictions to a
 solid waste disposal facility; and
 WHEREAS, the City has a responsibility,to protect the public health, safety and welfare of its
 citizens and residents; and
 WHEREAS, the accumulation of post-disaster debris on roads, rights-of-way, and utility and
 ingress/egress easements within the City can create a health hazard and hinder the City's ability to
 respond to the emergency needs of it's residents and citizens and to provide municipal service; and
 WHEREAS, the authority to enter private communities within the City to remove the
 aforementioned debris is critical to ensuring the health, safety, and welfare of Parkland citizens and
 residents in the event of a disaster; and
 WHEREAS, it is the recommendation of staff that Chapter 7 of the City's Code of Ordinances
 be amended to provide for such authorization; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of Parkland, Florida finds and determines that amending
 Chapter 7 of the City's Code of Ordinances is necessary to protect the health, safety, property, and
 welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland.
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1: That Chapter 7, Code of Ordinances entitled "Garbage and Refuse," Article I
 entitled "In General" is hereby amended by adding Section 7-4 entitled "Municipal Authority" to
 read and provide as follows:

<<PG>>
 Sec. 7-4. Municipal authority.
 (a) Pursuant to F.S. § 403.706(1), the City has the primary responsibility for the collection and
 transportation of solid waste from its jurisdiction to a solid waste disposal facility.
 (b) The City has the responsibility to protect the public health, safety and welfare and in order to
 discharge this responsibility shall have the authority to enter upon and clear debris from public
 and private streets, rights-of-way, and utility and ingress/egress easements within the City in the
 event of a declared natural or man-made disaster.
 (c) The franchise requirements and other standards created by this chapter are necessary and
 appropriate to protect the public health, safety and welfare, and to ensure that the solid waste
 and debris generated by the City of Parkland is collected and disposed in a safe, timely and
 responsible manner.
 Section 2: It is the Intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that
 the provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of
 the City of Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance to accomplish such intent.
 Section 3: If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for
 any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of
 any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 Section 4: That all Ordinances or parts of Ordinances in conflict herewith are to the
 extent of said conflict, hereby repealed.
 Section 5: That this Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its passage and
 adoption.
