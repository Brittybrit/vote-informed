 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland believes that development
 should pay its proportionate share of the capital costs associated with providing additional
 infrastructure capacity for public safety services,to wit: fire and police services; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission has retained the firm of TischlerBise to do a public
 safety impact fee study; and
 WHEREAS, said study has been completed and is on file with the City Clerk and is
 dated June 23, 2010. Said study provides for the calculation of impact fees based upon recent
 and localized data and the study has indicated that without the impact fees, the City will either
 have to utilize additional general fund revenues to serve the demands of new development or
 experience a declining level of service as a current inventory of infrastructures serves additional
 new development;
 WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to assure that all impact fees are collected,
 accounted for and spent in accordance with applicable law;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND,AS FOLLOWS:
 SECTION 1. The above referenced "Whereas" clauses are true and correct and made a
 part hereof.

 SECTION 2. Section 13-68 (7) of the City Code is hereby amended to read as follows:
 (7) Adequacy of fire protection service. Fire protection service is adequate to
 protect people and property in the proposed development; or fire protection service is not
 available, but there exists a fiscally feasible plan which shall include collection of impact
 fees) to expand fire protection service to provide for the needs of the proposed
 development; and all development permits will be granted on the condition that the
 proposed development be restricted to coincide with the implementation of such plan. A
 finding that adequate fire protection service is available shall be based on a
 demonstration that adequate fire personnel and apparatus and an adequate water supply
 will be available to service the fire protection needs of the new development and that the
 location of the city's fire stations will provide fire service to the development within
 acceptable response time.
 (83 Adequacy of police protection service. Police protection is adequate to
 protect people and property in the proposed development; or police protection service is
 not available, but there exists a fiscally feasible plan (which shall include collection of
 impact fees) to expand police protection service to provide for the needs of the proposed
 development; and all development permits will be granted on the condition that the
 proposed development be restricted to coincide with the implementation of such plan.
 SECTION 3. A new Section 13-68(8) is hereby created as follows:
 (8) Police and Fire Impact Fees
 fpj Findings
 The provision of adequate fire and police protection is essential for the
 health and safety of the public and citizens of the City of Parkland.
 Without the imposition of an impact fee, the City will either have to utilize
 additional general fund revenues to serve the demands of new
 development or experience a declining level of service as the current
 inventory of infrastructure serves additional new development. The
 impact fees set forth herein are based upon the study conducted by
 TischlerBise, dated June 23, 2010, and is based upon recent and localized
 data. The impact fees are designed to represent the new development's
 proportionate share of the capital costs associated with providing
 additional fire and police infrastructure capacity to serve the new
 development. The Florida Legislature has found in Section 163.31801,
 Florida Statutes, that impact fees are an important source of revenue for
 local government to use in funding the infrastructure necessitated by new
 growth. The purpose of this impact fee ordinance is to assure new
 development pays its fair share for infrastructure which is necessitated by
 new growth and that the fees paid will benefit the new growth.

 (b,1 Definitions
 Building shall mean any structure having a roof or used or built for the
 shelter or enclosure of person, animals, chattels, or property of any kind.
 (c) Fire Impact Fees
 A fire impact fee is hereby imposed against all residential and non-
 residential buildings constructed in the City of Parkland. Any application
 for a building permit in the City of Parkland subsequent to the effective
 date of this ordinance shall be subject to the assessment of this fee. The
 impact fees shall be paid prior to receiving any building permits for a
 residential or non-residential building in the City of Parkland and shall be
 paid based upon the schedule attached hereto as Exhibit B. The fees shall
 be paid by the party applying for the building permit. No building permit
 shall be issued unless an impact fee imposed herein has been paid.
 (d) Police Impact Fees
 A police impact fee is hereby imposed against all residential and non-
 residential buildings constructed in the City of Parkland. Any application
 for a building permit in the City of Parkland subsequent to the effective
 date of this ordinance shall be subject to the assessment of this fee. The
 impact fees shall be paid prior to receiving any building permits for a
 residential or non-residential building in the City of Parkland and shall be
 paid based upon the schedule attached hereto as Exhibit A. The fees shall
 be paid by the party applying for the building permit. No building permit
 shall be issued unless an impact fee imposed herein has been paid.
 Lej Exemptions
 The following shall be exempted from the obligation to pay fire and police
 impact fees:
 i) Alteration, expansion or replacement of an existing non-
 residential building where the use of the structure is not changed or
 interior square footage is not increased; or the alteration, expansion
 or replacement of any existing residential building where interior
 square footage is not increased by more than 10% for a residential
 building.

 SECTION 4. Appeal.
 Any decision relative to the imposition of any impact fee may be appealed by the
 feepayer to the City Commission where it is claimed that the fee or any expenditure violates this
 ordinance, any state or federal law or a constitutional or other established right. The appeal shall
 be filed within 30 days of the act which is the subject of the appeal. The appeal shall be filed in
 writing and delivered to the City Clerk prior to the end of the 30 day period set forth above. The
 appeal shall set forth all the facts, argument and law believed to be relevant to the appeal and the
 specific relief requested. The City Commission shall hear the appeal within 45 days of delivery
 and may either grant the relief requested, deny the relief requested or fashion such relief as is
 deemed just and proper.

 SECTION 5. Accounting.
 The City shall provide for an annual accounting in reporting of all impact fee collections
 and expenditures. Said revenues and expenditures shall be contained in a separate accounting
 fund specifically created for the purpose of infrastructure needs and capital equipment for the
 specific impact fee collected (fire or police). Any administrative charges related to the collection
 of impact fees shall be solely based upon actual cost.
 SECTION 6. Audit.
 The annual audit of financial statements of the City of Parkland that are submitted to the
 Auditor General shall include an affidavit signed by the Chief Financial Officer of the City
 stating that the City has complied with the requirements of Section 163.31801, Florida Statutes.
 SECTION 7. Expenditure of Funds.
 The City shall expend, or commit for expenditure, any impact fees received within seven
 (7) years of payment; if not expended or committed within that time frame the fees shall be
 returned to the feepayer upon request of the feepayer provided said request is made within three
 (3) months of the conclusion of the seven (7) year period and provided said refund contains an
 affidavit that the person requesting the refund is the owner of the property on behalf of which the
 impact fee was paid, together with evidence of payment of the fee. All funds expended shall be
 used to benefit the development which paid the funds. Funds shall only be used for the
 acquisition, expansion or improvement of buildings, structures or real property, capital facilities,
 or equipment made necessary by the new development and needed to mitigate the impact of new
 development.
 SECTION 8. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that

 Parkland, and that the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to accomplish such intent.
