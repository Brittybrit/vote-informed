 15 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the Florida
 16 Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, the Charter of the City of Parkland("City"); and
 17 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission("City Commission") finds it in the
 18 best interest of the City to amend Chapter 9 of the Code of Ordinances entitled Landscaping
 19 and Vegetation to reflect changed conditions within the City; and
 20 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds it in it's best interest to add definitions to
 21 clarify the distinction between exotic invasive plant species, exotic plant species and South
 22 Florida Native plant species; and
 23 WHEREAS,the City Commission finds it in it's best interest to reduce the negative
 24 impacts of invasive plant species that invade native plant communities by prohibiting their use
 25 and encouraging the use of native plants; and
 26 WHEREAS, the City has determined it necessary to amend the City's landscaping
 27 general requirements to add State of Florida Category I invasive exotic plant species that have
 28 the greatest impact on the natural areas; and
 29 WHEREAS, the City has determined it necessary to amend the City's landscaping
 30 general requirements to add specific exotic invasive plant species removal requirements prior to
 31 the issuance of a Certificate of Occupancy for new development; and

 9 SECTION 1. Section 9.2 entitled "Definitions" of the Code of Ordinances of the City
 10 of Parkland is hereby amended by adding the following definition:
 11
 12 Exotic species. A species introduced to Florida, purposefully or accidentally, from a
 13 natural range outside of Florida.
 14
 15 Native Species. A species whose natural range included Florida at the time of European
 16 contact (1500 AD).
 17
 18 Invasive exotic. An exotic that not only has naturalized but is expanding on its own in
 19 Florida plant communities.
 20
 21 Category I invasive exotics. Invasive plants that are altering native plant communities
 22 by displacing native species, changing community structures or ecological functions, or
 23 hybridizing with natives.

 10 (c)Application.
 11
 12 h. As a condition of any development permit, the eradication of nuisance vegetation
 13 (invasive exotic species) is required on all sites, including abutting rights-of-way,
 14 during the lot clearing permit process and prior to the issuance of a certificate of
 15 occupancy. Privately owned natural areas shall be included in this requirement. All
 16 areas obtaining a development permit after the effective date of this ordinance
 17 amendment shall be maintained free of nuisance species. This shall include natural
 18 areas within developed property. Any property which has already received site plan
 19 approval as of the effective date of this ordinance amendment shall be exempt from the
 20 application of this section provided said site plan approval remains valid and is not
 21 amended.
 22 i. A landscape plan and landscape permit application are required for any additions or
 23 property improvements over 50% of the existing square footage, as determined by the
 24 Building Director, which impact existing required landscaping on the property. The
 25 landscape plan shall indicate the eradication of nuisance vegetation (invasive exotic
 26 species) required on all sites, including abutting rights-of-way, during the landscape
 27 permit process and prior to the issuance of a certificate of occupancy.
