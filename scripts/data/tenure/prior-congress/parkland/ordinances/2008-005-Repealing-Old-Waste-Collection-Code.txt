 19 WHEREAS, there is an immediate and continuing need for the collection and disposal of
 20 trash, garbage and other refuse found within municipal limits of the City of Parkland; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, this is an important public purpose and service which the City is unable to
 23 provide internally so it wishes to provide said service through a franchise agreement; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, Waste Management Inc. of Florida has the ability to provided for the
 26 collection and disposal of trash, garbage and other refuse found within the municipal limits of the
 27 City of Parkland; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the City Commission through Ordinance No. 2007-24, has authorized a

 31 Management); and

 33 WHEREAS, the City wishes to implement this franchise agreement to provide regulations

 37 CITY OF PARKLAND,AS FOLLOWS:
 38 SECTION 1. Definitions.
 39 Biomedical Waste shall have the meaning as set forth on Section 403.703, Florida Statutes,
 40 and applicable regulations under the Florida Administrative Code.
 41
 42 Bulk Waste shall mean large items that normally exceed weight (fifty pounds) or size
 43 restrictions (four foot in length or too large to fit in rollout cart) including, but not limited to, white
 44 goods, furniture, and large branches. Bulk Waste does not include any form of matter or debris

 1 resulting from land clearing, land development, or building demolition debris. Additionally, bulk

 6 Commercial Container shall mean any portable, nonabsorbent, enclosed container with a
 7 close fitting cover, or doors, approved by the CONTRACTOR, which is used to store large
 8 volumes of refuse utilizing containers of two (2) cubic yards, or more. A commercial container
 9 must be capable of being serviced by the mechanical equipment of the CONTRACTOR.

 11 Commercial Establishment shall mean any public or private place, building, or enterprise
 12 devoted in whole or in part to business purposes whether for profit or not for profit; except where
 13 such place, building, or enterprise constitutes a residence.
 14
 15 Construction/Demolition Debris shall mean materials generally considered to be not
 16 water soluble and non-hazardous in nature, including, but not limited to, steel, glass, brick,
 17 concrete, asphalt roofing material, pipe, gypsum wallboard, and lumber, from the construction or
 18 destruction of a structure as part of a construction or demolition project, and including rocks, soils,
 19 tree remains, trees and other vegetative matter which normally results from land clearing or land
 20 development operations for a construction protect as defined in Section 403.703(21), Florida
 21 Statutes, as amended from time to time. Mixing of construction and demolition debris with other
 22 types of solid waste, including material from a construction or demolition site which is not from
 23 the actual construction or destruction of a structure, will cause it to be classified as other than
 24 construction and demolition debris. However, the inclusion of incidental solid waste generated
 25 attendant to construction or demolition activity shall not classify the same as other than
 26 construction and demolition debris.
 27
 28 Curb Service shall mean a single collection point within five (5) feet of the hard-topped
 29 road.
 30
 31 Garbage shall mean and include all waste, fruit or vegetable matter that attends or results
 32 from the preparation, use, handling, cooking, serving or storage of meats, fish, fowl, fruit,
 33 vegetable matter, of any nature whatsoever, which is subject to decay, putrefaction and the
 34 generation of noxious and offensive gases or odors, or which may serve as breeding or feeding
 35 materials for flies and other insects. Garbage does not include Biomedical Waste as described
 36 herein.
 37
 38 Handicapped Service shall be service of household garbage at rear or side of house for
 39 persons as defined herein and approved by the CONTRACTOR.
 40
 41 Hazardous Waste shall mean wastes that are hazardous by reason of their pathological,
 42 explosive, radiological or toxic characteristics, or any other material that has been classified as
 43 hazardous by a governmental agency with the authority to regulate such material.
 44
 45 Horticultural Trash shall mean grass clippings, shrubbery cuttings or clippings, palm
 46 fronds, small tree branches, bushes or shrubs, or other matter usually created as refuse in the care

 4 SECTION 3. Residential Solid Waste and Horticultural Trash Collection. All
 5 persons occupying a residential unit or maintaining a place of residence within the City shall be
 6 provided regular solid waste and horticultural trash collection two (2) days per week. The owners
 7 and/or occupants shall subscribe to the solid waste collection service and have all accumulations of
 8 solid waste generated from such premises removed by Waste Management. Waste Management
 9 shall provide twice a week collection as long as all waste set out for collection is in approved
 10 containers as provided for in Ordinance 2008-06. All owners/occupants shall comply with this
 11 schedule. Containers shall be placed at curbside prior to "pickup," but no later than twelve (12)
 12 hours prior to collection.
 13 Residential customers who are physically or mentally challenged and for which there is no
 14 adult living in the residence who is capable of placing the containers at the curb may elect
 15 "handicapped service" at no additional charge. Waste Management shall provide an appropriate
 16 form for such residents to complete together with required documentation corroborating such
 17 status. The City shall make the final decision in the event of a dispute between Waste
 18 Management and resident over resident's right to have "handicapped service."
 19 SECTION 4. Commercial Solid Waste Collection. All persons occupying a commercial
 20 establishment or maintaining a place of business within the City shall be provided regular solid
 21 waste collection a minimum of one time per week. To the extent permitted by law, the City shall
 22 require the owners and/or occupants to subscribe to the solid waste collection service and have all
 23 accumulation of solid waste generated from such premises removed by Waste Management.
 24 In the event of a dispute between Waste Management and a commercial establishment as to
 25 the level of service pursuant to a contract for service between the two parties, then either party may
 26 take the matter to the City Manager for resolution. The decision of the City Manager shall be
 27 final.
 28 SECTION 5. Bulk Collection Service. Waste Management shall provide a city-wide
 29 bulk pickup service six (6) times per contract year at no additional cost to the City or residents.
 30 Bulk pickup schedules shall be established annually and shall be scheduled in two (2) month
 31 intervals. The bulk pickup shall be at curbside, the same as the normal pickups and shall include,

 1 but not be limited to, bulky household items, discarded furniture, etc. Bulk pickups shall not
 2 include tree stumps or mature trees unless reduced to size and weight that can be managed by
 3 Waste Management's grapple truck or construction debris.
 4 The following set-out rules apply to bulk pickups:
 5 • Bulk waste shall be placed curbside in a neat and orderly manner.
 6 • Cut tree trunks, logs, and large limbs into sections not to exceed 4 feet and
 7 stack neatly at the curb.
 8 • Cut lumber, metal poles and other items of this nature into sections not to
 9 exceed 4 feet.
 10 • Bulk waste set out for collection must not be placed close to a mailbox, fire
 11 hydrant, cable box, utility pole, fence or car.
 12 • White Goods shall have chlorofluorocarbons (CFC's) removed prior to set
 13 out.
 14 Additional bulk pickups may be provided by Waste Management or City at additional cost
 15 to residents—the cost to be determined by the City.
 16 SECTION 6. All persons, entities and properties which are subject to the exclusive
 17 collection agreement as set forth in Ordinance No. 2008-06 shall comply with the provisions
 18 therein and shall be obligated to comply with the provisions stated herein and as set forth in said
 19 Ordinance No. 2008-06 for the exclusive collection of the solid waste set forth therein by Waste
 20 Management, Inc.
 21 SECTION 7. With regard to all solid waste and recyclable materials, all residences shall
 22 comply with the collection schedule as designated by the City Manager of the City of Parkland.
 23 The collection schedule shall be set forth in a written order from the City Manager; a copy of said
 24 order shall be on file with the City Clerk and shall be on the City of Parkland web site. This
 25 schedule is subject to change by further written order of the City Manager. All residences and
 26 businesses are required to comply with the procedures for collection of solid waste and recyclables
 27 as set forth herein.
 28 SECTION 8. All residents, business owners, and other persons serviced as provided
 29 herein shall pay the rates as charged by Waste Management for the services set forth herein. The
 30 failure to pay Waste Management in a timely fashion as determined by the City shall be deemed a
 31 violation of this ordinance.
