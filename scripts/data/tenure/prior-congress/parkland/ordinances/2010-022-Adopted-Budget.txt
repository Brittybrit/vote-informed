 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ADOPTING A BUDGET
 5 FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2010
 6 AND ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 2011, PROVIDING FOR
 7 SEVERABILITY AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE
 8 DATE
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland according to the
 11 requirements of Chapter 200.065 and Chapter 166.241 of the Florida Statutes, is required
 12 to adopt a Budget for the City of Parkland, Florida for the fiscal year 2010-2011; and
 13 WHEREAS, the City Manager has duly prepared and presented a Budget, a copy
 14 of which is attached hereto and made a part hereof and hereinafter referred as the Budget;
 15 and
 16 WHEREAS, the public has had an opportunity to be heard regarding such
 17 Budget:
 18 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 19 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 20 Section 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and made
 21 a part hereof.
 22 Section 2. The City of Parkland, City Commission hereby adopts the Budget
 23 in accordance with Section 200.065 and 166.241 of the Florida Statutes for the operations
 24 of City government for the City of Parkland. The General Fund, Capital Projects Fund,
 25 and Capital Replacement Fund for the fiscal year 2010-2011, showing a total of revenues
 26 and appropriated fund balance of $22,681,000 and a total expenditure requirements of
 27 $22,681,000 as set forth in detail in the Budget be and the same is hereby adopted as the
 28 General Fund, Capital Projects Fund, and Capital Replacement Fund for the City of
 29 Parkland for the fiscal year 2010-2011.
 30 Section 3. The Budget as adopted shall regulate expenditures for the City of
 31 Parkland in accordance with applicable law.
 32 Section 4. All delinquent taxes collected during the year as proceeds from
 33 levies of operation millage of prior years are hereby specifically appropriated for the use
 34 and benefit of the General Fund.

 1 Section 5. That the provisions of this ordinance shall not be deemed to be a
 2 limitation on the powers granted to the City Commission by the City Charter and which
 3 relate to the fiscal management of the funds of the City of Parkland.
 4 Section 6. That all ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed to the
 5 extent of such conflict.
 6 Section 7. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, subsection of this ordinance
 7 is for any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the
 8 validity of any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 9 Section 8. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon
 10 its passage and adoption.
