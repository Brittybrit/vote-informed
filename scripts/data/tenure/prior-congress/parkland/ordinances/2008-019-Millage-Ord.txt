 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has held all necessary
 14 public workshops and public hearings to consider and make changes to the annual budget
 15 submitted by the City Manager for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2009.
 16 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 17 THE CITY OF PARKLAND,AS FOLLOWS:
 18 Section 1. That the millage rate to be levied in order to produce sufficient
 19 ad valorem taxation revenue which, when combined with other projected General Fund
 20 revenues, will be adequate to pay the appropriations made in the budget for the fiscal year
 21 beginning October 1, 2008 and ending September 30, 2009 shall be:
 22 Operating Millage Rate 3.4083
 23 Section 2. That the operating millage rate of 3.4083 mills will result in a 5%
 24 decrease in City property taxes. This decrease is the percent by which the millage rate to be
 25 levied is less than the rolled back rate of 3.6070, which is computed according to the
 26 guidelines set forth in the Florida Statutes, Section 200.065
 27 Section 3. That this ordinance was adopted after a public hearing was held upon
 28 the subject matter hereof, as required by the City Charter and Florida Statutes 200.065;
 29 notice of hearing was published as required by law, which public hearings were held on
