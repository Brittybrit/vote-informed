 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 4 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, PROVIDING
 5 FOR THE CITY COMMISSION TO EXTEND BY
 6 RESOLUTION THE APPLICATION SUBMITTAL
 7 CYCLE FOR THE ENERGY INCENTIVES PROGRAM
 8 FOR ENVIRONMENTALLY CONSCIOUS
 9 PURCHASES PER ORDINANCE 2007-29 AND
 10 CODIFIED IN CHAPTER 4.5 OF THE CITY CODE OF
 11 ORDINANCES; CONTAINING A SEVERABILITY
 12 CLAUSE; AND CONTAINING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has always taken the lead in preservation of the environment
 15 and wishes to continue to do so as the city, county, state and nation face mounting issues with regard
 16 to energy and the environment; and
 17 WHEREAS, the City believes that by encouraging environmentally conscious activities within
 18 the City of Parkland, not only the citizens of Parkland will benefit, but the citizens of our state and
 19 nation will receive a benefit; and
 20 WHEREAS, the City Commission adopted an energy incentive program through Ordinance
 21 2007-29; and
 22 WHEREAS, the 2008 application submittal cycle has ended for this program; and
 23 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that by extending the application submittal cycle
 24 that additional Parkland residents and businesses will apply for the energy incentive program;
 25 WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to extend the 2008 and possibly future application
 26 submittal cycle to provide the longest possible opportunity for Parkland residents and businesses to
 27 undertake environmentally conscious activities and purchases; and
 28 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 29 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 30 SECTION 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are incorporated herein and express the
 31 legislative intent for this ordinance.
 32 SECTION 2. Section 3 of Ordinance 2007-29 codified in Section 4.5 of the City's Code of
 33 Ordinance is hereby amended as follows:
 34 The City of Parkland hereby adopts a program for energy conscious incentives (hereinafter
 35 referred to as Incentive Payments) as set forth on the attached Exhibit A (The Criteria). No one will
 36 be eligible for Incentive Payments unless they meet The Criteria. The City Manager is authorized to
 37 provide appropriate application forms and require information as necessary to implement this program.

 1 No person or entity will be eligible for an energy incentive or Incentive Payment unless they have
 2 fully completed the application requirements as approved by the City Manager. Only purchases after
 3 the effective date of this ordinance shall qualify. Only completed applications received by May 1 of
 4 each year shall qualify for payment, which payment shall be made during the next fiscal year;
 5 provided that the City Commission may amend the application submittal date deadline by resolution.
 6 The City reserves the right to reject any application, to limit payments made pursuant to this program,
 7 or altogether suspend said payments when determined to be in the best interests of the City by the City
 8 Commission. The limitations set forth in The Criteria shall be binding.
 9 SECTION 3. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 10 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of the City of
 11 Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to accomplish such intent.
 12 SECTION 4. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for any
 13 reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of any
 14 remaining portions of this ordinance.
 15 SECTION 5. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 16 passage and adoption.
