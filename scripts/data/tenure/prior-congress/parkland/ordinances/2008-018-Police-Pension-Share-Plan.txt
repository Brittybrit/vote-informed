 20 Section 2-234. Police Officer's Share Plan.
 21
 22 (1) Creation;purpose.
 23
 24 Pursuant to F.S. §185.35(1)(b), the City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby
 25 creates a trust fund to be known as the "City of Parkland Police Officer's Share
 26 Plan." The purpose of this share plan is to place additional premium tax revenues
 27 pursuant to F.S. §§185.08 and 185.35 and earnings generated therefrom into a
 28 separate trust fund to pay extra benefits to police officers of the City of Parkland
 29 who are participants in the City of Parkland Police Officer's Retirement Plan as of
 30 the effective date set forth herein. In accordance with the provisions of F.S.
 31 §185.35(1), this share plan has been approved by a majority of such participants.
 32 The share plan hereby created shall be in addition to any other benefits under the
 33 City of Parkland Police Officer's Retirement Plan and nothing herein shall in any
 34 way affect any other benefits that now or hereafter exist.

 37 (3) Funding; allocation to accounts.
 38
 39 (a) Initial allocation of additional premium tax revenues. The retirement
 40 plan has heretofore received additional premium tax revenues that were
 41 paid to and collected by the state in calendar year 2007. Upon
 42 implementation of this share plan, the board shall allocate to each
 43 participant's or eligible beneficiary's account an equal share of the
 44 additional premium tax revenues received prior to the implementation of
 45 the share plan.
 46
 3

 1 (b) Subsequent allocation of additional premium tax revenues. For
 2 allocations after the initial allocation of additional premium tax
 3 revenues, the board shall allocate to each participant's or eligible
 4 beneficiary's account each year an equal share of the additional
 5 premium tax revenues it receives under the provisions of F.S. § 185.10.
 6 The board shall make the allocation of the additional premium tax
 7 revenues it receives to the share plan as soon as possible but in no event
 8 later than five days after its receipt of such additional premium tax
 9 revenues. Thereafter, the board shall forthwith allocate to each
 10 participant's or eligible beneficiary's account an equal share thereof.
 11 The city shall not be required to levy any additional taxes on its
 12 residents or to make any contributions to the share plan.
 13
 14 (c) Allocations of forfeitures. As of the annual valuation date in each share
 15 plan year, all amounts forfeited under section 5 since the preceding
 16 annual valuation date shall be prorated and credited to the accounts of
 17 the individual participants and eligible beneficiaries in the same manner
 18 as the allocations under subparagraph (b) above.
 19
 20 (d) Allocation of net investment return. The board shall invest the share
 21 plan assets together with the assets of the retirement plan. Except for
 22 the initial allocation of additional premium tax revenues, the board shall
 23 value the share plan's assets as of each annual valuation date and shall
 24 allocate to the participant's or eligible beneficiary's account his or her
 25 allocable share of the change in the fair market value of the share plan's
 26 assets, where such allocable share shall be based on the participant's or
 27 eligible beneficiary's account balance, if any, as of the preceding
 28 October 1.
 29
 30 (e) Payment of costs, expenses and fees. All costs, expenses and fees of
 31 administering the share plan shall be paid from the assets of the share
 32 plan in such fashion as the board shall determine. Any allocation to a
 33 participant's or eligible beneficiary's account shall be net of the
 34 participant's or eligible beneficiary's share of the plan's costs, expenses
 35 and fees of administering the share plan.
 36
 37 (f) Exclusive benefit rule. No part of the assets of the share plan shall be
 38 used for or diverted to any purpose whatsoever other than for the
 39 exclusive benefit of participants and beneficiaries thereof in accordance
 40 with the terms hereof and paying the expenses of the share plan as
 41 provided in subsection (e) above. No person shall have any interest in
 42 or right to any part of the assets of the share plan except as and to the
 43 extent expressly provided in the share plan.
 44
 45 (g) Custody of fund assets. The board shall hold all assets of the share plan
 46 in trust solely for use in paying the benefits provided by the share plan in

 11 (4) Participation.
 12
 13 (a) Establishment of share plan account. An account shall be established
 14 for each participant and eligible beneficiary.
 15
 16 (b) Termination of participation. Every individual who is a participant as of
 17 the effective date shall remain a participant until death. Every
 18 individual who is an eligible beneficiary after the effective date shall
 19 remain an eligible beneficiary only for the period of time described in
 20 subsection 2(h) above.
 21
 22 (5) Benefits;forfeitures.
 23
 24 (a) Distribution. A participant shall receive a distribution of benefits from
 25 this share plan upon the participant's commencement of retirement or
 26 disability benefits under the retirement plan and as of each valuation
 27 date thereafter. An eligible beneficiary shall receive a distribution of
 28 benefits from this share plan upon the participant's death and as of each
 29 valuation date thereafter so long as the eligible beneficiary remains an
 30 eligible beneficiary. The participant's or eligible beneficiary's
 31 distribution shall be equal to the balance in his account at the payment
 32 date. However, no participant or eligible beneficiary shall receive a
 33 benefit from the share plan in excess of the amount allocated to his or
 34 her account.
 35
 36 (b) Death. The board may require and rely upon such proof of death and
 37 such evidence of the right of any eligible beneficiary to receive the value
 38 of the account of a deceased participant as the board may deem proper
 39 and its determination of the right of that beneficiary to receive payment
 40 shall be conclusive.
 41
 42 (c) Forfeiture for cause. Notwithstanding anything in the plan to the
 43 contrary, if a participant or eligible beneficiary is convicted of a
 44 specified offense as set forth in F.S. §112.3173, as same may be
 45 amended from time to time, the provisions of F.S. §112.3173, as same
 46 may be amended from time to time, shall apply and the participant or
 47 eligible beneficiary shall forfeit all rights to receive a benefit from the

 5 (d) Payment of benefits. Benefits under this section 5 shall be payable as

 12 (e) Required distributions. In no event shall the provisions of this share
 13 plan operate so as to allow the distribution of a participant's account to
 14 begin after the later of April 1st following: (i) the calendar year in which
 15 the participant attains age 70'/2, or (ii) the calendar year in which the
 16 participant terminates employment with the City (the "required
 17 beginning date").
 18
 19 (f) Distribution limitation. Notwithstanding any other provision of this
 20 share plan, all distributions from this share plan shall conform to the
 21 regulations issued under IRC section 401(a)(9), including the incidental
 22 death benefit provisions of IRC section 401(a)(9)(G). Further, such
 23 regulations shall override any provision of this share plan that are
 24 inconsistent with IRC section 401(a)(9).
 25
 26 (g) Direct rollover of certain distributions. Notwithstanding any provision
 27 of this share plan to the contrary that would otherwise limit a
 28 distributee's election under this subsection (g), a distributee may elect,
 29 at the time and in the manner prescribed by the board, to have any
 30 portion of an eligible rollover distribution paid directly by the share plan
 31 to an eligible retirement plan specified by the distribute in a direct
 32 rollover. The following definitions apply to the terms used in this
 33 subsection (g):
 34
 35 (1) "Eligible rollover distribution" means any distribution of all or any
 36 portion of the balance to the credit of the distributee, except that an
 37 eligible rollover distribution does not include any distribution to
 38 the extent such distribution is required under IRC section
 39 401(a)(9) and the portion of any distribution that is not includible
 40 in gross income;
 41
 42 (2) "Eligible retirement plan" means an individual retirement account
 43 described in IRC section 408(a), an individual retirement annuity
 44 described in IRC section 408(b), an annuity plan described in IRC
 45 section 403(a), a deferred compensation plan described in IRC
 46 section 457, or a qualified trust described in IRC section 401(a),
 47 that accepts the distributee's eligible rollover distribution.

 9 (6) Miscellaneous provisions.
 10
 11 (a) Non-alienation of benefits. The benefits provided by this share plan
 12 shall not be subject to garnishment, attachment; execution of any other
 13 legal process, except the board may authorize the deduction of alimony
 14 and child support, in accordance with F.S. §185.05(6).
 15
 16 (b) City's responsibilities. The city shall have no responsibility for the
 17 operation of this share plan except those specified herein.
 18
 19 (c) Facility of payment. If the board finds that a participant or other person
 20 entitled to a benefit is unable to care for his or her affairs or is a minor
 21 or is legally incapacitated, the board may direct that any benefit due such
 22 individual shall be paid to the individual's duly appointed legal
 23 representative. Any payment so made shall be a complete discharge of
 24 the liabilities of this share plan for that benefit.
 25
 26 (d) Information. Each participant, beneficiary or other person entitled to a
 27 benefit, before any benefit shall be payable to such individual or credited
 28 to the participant's account under the fund, shall file with the board the
 29 information that it shall require to establish the individual's rights and
 30 benefits under the plan.
 31
 32 (e) Amendment. The provisions of the plan are intended to meet the
 33 requirements of a qualified profit sharing plan under IRC section 401(a)
 34 and to be tax-exempt under IRC section 501(a). Should any changes be
 35 required to this share plan for it to comply or to continue to comply with
 36 the provisions of IRC sections 401(a) and 501(a), the board shall
 37 recommend to the City Commission any such required changes to the
 38 share plan.
 39
 40 (f) Severability of provisions. If any provision of this share plan is for any
 41 reason held unconstitutional, inoperative or void, such holding shall not
 42 affect the remaining provisions of this share plan. It is the intent of the
 43 City Commission to create this share plan without such unconstitutional,
 44 inoperative or void provision and the remainder of the share plan, after
 45 the exclusion of such provision, shall be deemed and held to be valid as
 46 if such provision had not been included herein.
