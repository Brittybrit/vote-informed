 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AMENDING CITY CODE SECTION 13-66(K)
 5 RELATING TO THE REQUIREMENTS FOR MAINTAINING PROPERTY

 7 REQUIREMENT FOR COMPLYING WITH ANY AGREEMENT RELATING
 8 TO MAINTENANCE OF AREAS, INCLUDING MEDIANS AND

 10 PROVISION FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE, CONTAINING

 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, in approving site plans,
 14 reviews all elements site plan, including all representation with regard to the condition of
 15 medians and swales adjacent to the approved site plan; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the maintenance of these medians and swales is of great importance
 is to the City of Parkland as it improves the aesthetic appearance of the development
 19 approved and softens the impact of the development upon the community at large; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, at times, the City will enter into maintenance agreements with the
 22 developer, and/or the association to assure maintenance of certain swales and medians;
 23 and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that these agreements are, in fact, a part
 26 of the development approval process and critical to the development approval process and
 27 wishes to make those agreements binding not only as agreements but as code provisions;
 28
 29 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 30 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:

 32 SECTION 1. The above referenced "Whereas" clauses are true and correct and
 33 made a part hereof.

 35 SECTION 2. Section 13-66(k) is hereby amended to read as follows:

 37 (k) All requirements incorporated in any approval of a site plan shall be
 38 deemed requirements of this Code, as well as all improvements and landscaping
 39 depicted or described in any site plan; such requirements and depictions or
 40 descriptions shall be binding and all representations, drawings, elevations, or other
 41 submissions made as part an approved site plan shall be deemed code
 42 requirements. The violation or failure to maintain a site plan in strict compliance with
 43 the above, or the failure to maintain all landscaping and building facades in good
 44 condition and in conformance with the site plan and all submittals related thereto
 45 shall be a violation of this Code. All landscaping and decorative materials placed in
 46 public medians and swales or privately owned buffers as part of a site plan shall
