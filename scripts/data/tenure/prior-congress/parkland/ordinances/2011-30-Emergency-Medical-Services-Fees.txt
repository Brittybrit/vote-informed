 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ADOPTING FEES FOR EMERGENCY

 7 CODE; PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN

 10 WHEREAS, through a contract, the City of Coral Springs Fire Department provides emergency
 11 medical services to its citizens; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS; these services are of several types, including basic life support services and
 14 various types of advanced life support services as well as inter-facility transfers; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland pays the City of Coral Springs for these emergency medical
 17 services (EMS) and wishes to impose a reasonable charge on residents that receive the benefits of these
 18 services;
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the City Commission has reviewed the charges set forth herein and finds that they
 21 are reasonable and appropriate;
 22
 23 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 24 OF PARKLAND,AS FOLLOWS:
 25
 26 SECTION 1. The above reference "Whereas" clauses are true and correct and
 27 made a part hereof.
 28
 29 SECTION 2. Chapter 14, Section 4 is hereby added and states as follows:
 30
 31 Sec. 14-4. Emergency Medical Services Fees
 32
 33 a. The City of Parkland does hereby impose the following charges for emergency medical
 34 services (EMS) rendered to persons within the corporate boundaries to persons within the City of
 35 Parkland and to any parties in unincorporated Broward County that the City of Parkland may
 36 serve by virtue of an inter-local with Broward County:
 37
 38 1. BLS Service $461.00
 39 2. ALS1 Service . $520.00
 40 3. ALS2 Service $663.00
 41 4. Inter-Facility Transfers $520.00
 42 5. Mileage $10.40
 43 6. EMS Standby Fee (3 hr minimum)
 44 per hour per person $57.00
 45 Each additional hour or portion thereof

 4 b. It is the intent of the City Commission to transfer the costs of certain City services to the
 5 individuals who are requesting the services from the City. The administrative and/or user fees
 6 charged under this section shall be set forth in a Resolution adopted by the City Commission.
 7
 8 c. The listing of administrative and/or user fees in this chapter may not be all inclusive and
 9 therefore, other applicable Code of Ordinance chapters and/or provisions may apply in addition
 10 to the fees designated in this chapter.
 11
 12 d. All fees included in this chapter shall be permitted to be increased yearly, without City
 13 Commission approval, no greater than the Consumer Price Index ("CPI"). Upon approval by the
 14 City Commission, through a Resolution, an administrative and/or user fee can be increased above
 15 the CPI.
 16
 17 e. All fees adopted per this Chapter shall be increased on October 1st of each respective year
 18 unless noted otherwise.
 19
 20 SECTION 3. The City Manager is authorized to take all necessary steps in order to
 21 collect these fees.
 22
 23 SECTION 4. The fees set forth herein may be amended by the City Commission at any
 24 time by adoption of a Resolution.
 25
 26 SECTION 5. It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 27 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of the
 28 City of Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered to accomplish
 29 such intent.
 30
 31 SECTION 6 If any word,phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for any
 32 reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of any
 33 remaining portions of this ordinance.
 34
 35 SECTION 7. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 36 passage and adoption.
