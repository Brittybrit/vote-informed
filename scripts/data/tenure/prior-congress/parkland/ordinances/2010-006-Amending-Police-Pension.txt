 25 WHEREAS, in 2009 the Florida Legislature enacted Chapter 2009-97, Law of Florida,
 26 which mandates certain amendments to Chapter 185 municipal police pension plans, including the
 27 City of Parkland Police Officers Retirement Plan (the "Plan"); and
 28 WHEREAS, these recent legislative amendments to Chapter 185 require several technical
 29 amendments to the Plan in order to maintain continued eligibility for receipt of state premium tax
 30 revenues; and
 31 WHEREAS, the trustees of the Plan have requested and approved such an amendments,
 32 along with several newly permissible amendments, as being in the best interests of the participants
 33 and beneficiaries as well as improving the administration of the Plan; and
 34 WHEREAS, the City Commission has received and reviewed an actuarial impact
 35 statement regarding these changes, reflecting no actuarial impact for the City.

 9 Sec. 2-223. Administration of the retirement plan.
 10
 11 (b) The board of trustees shall consist of five (5)persons; two (2) of whom shall be legal
 12 residents of the city and who shall be appointed by the city commission. The city appointees may
 13 be members of the city commission, as service on the board is deemed an additional duty of the
 14 office of city commissioner. Two (2) members of the board of trustees shall be police officers
 15 elected by a majority of the police officers who are active members of the plan. A fifth member of
 16 the board shall be chosen by a majority of the other four(4)trustees, and such person's name shall

 18 member selected by the other four(4)trustees as a ministerial duty. Should no active police officer
 19 members be available to serve on the board of trustees,retired members of the plan may serve on
 20 the board if so elected by membership. If there are no active or retired police officers remaining in
 21 the plan or capable of serving, the remaining board members may elect an individual to serve in
 22 the active police officer member seat. Upon receipt of such person's name,the city commission
 23 shall, as a ministerial duty, appoint such person to the board.
 24
 25 (c) All trustees shall serve a term of twe four(2 4)years. If a vacancy shall occur prior to the
 26 expiration of a member's term, a replacement member shall be chosen in the same manner as the
 27 person who has left office. A replacement trustee shall serve a full term measured from the date of
 28 replacement. All trustees shall serve until their replacements are selected. A trustee who misses
 29 two (2) consecutive meetings without an excuse satisfactory to the board shall be subject to
 30 removal for neglect of duty, as permitted by Section 112.501, Florida Statutes.

 22 (h) A member entitled to a normal or early service retirement benefit shall have the right at any
 23 time prior to the date upon which the first payment is received to elect to have the benefit payable
 24 under one of the options provided in this plan. A member shall be permitted to revoke any such
 25 election and to elect a new option at any time prior to the receipt of the first payment. Each

 8 Section 5. City Code Amended. Section 2-231(b), "Amendment or termination of the
 9 system", is hereby amended to read as follows:
 10
 11 Sec. 2-231. Amendment or termination of the system.
 12
 13 (b) In the event of termination or partial termination of the plan, each participant's accrued pension
 14 benefit shall become nonforfeitable (one hundred (100)percent vested)to the extent funded. At
 15 such time,the funds shall be appropriated and distributed in accordance with the provisions of
 16 Chapter 185, [F.S.]. In the event that the plan is terminated,the assets of the plan shall first be
 17 distributed to retired members and their beneficiaries. If there is any asset value remaining after the
 18 apportionment to retired members and their beneficiaries, apportionment shall next be made to
 19 each member in the service who has completed at least ten(10) of credited service and has
 20 contributed to the fund for at least ten(10) years and who is not otherwise eligible to retire. If there
 21 is any asset value after the apportionments to retirees and their beneficiaries and to vested
 22 members of the plan, apportionment shall lastly be made in respect of each member in the service
 23 of the city in an amount not to exceed the total value of the member's contributions. In the event
 24 that there is any asset value remaining after full apportionment to all members and beneficiaries of
 25 the plan, the excess, if any, shall revert proportionately to the city and the State of Florida on the
 26 basis of contributions to the plan.
 27 Pursuant to Section 185.37, Florida Statutes, in the event of plan termination, the board shall
 28 determine the asset value required to fully fund all nonforfeitable benefits. The board shall inform
 29 the City if additional assets are required, in which event the City shall continue to financially
 30 support the plan until all nonforfeitable benefits have been fully funded.

 32 Section 6. City Code Amended. Section 2-232(d), "Distribution of marital interests
 33 in the plan", is hereby amended to read as follows:
 34
 35 Sec.2-232. Distribution of marital interests in the plan.
 36
 37 (d) The board of trustees may, upon written request by a retiree, or by a dependent, when
 38 authorized by the retiree or the retiree's beneficiary, authorize the plan administrator to withhold
 39 from the monthly retirement payment those funds that are necessary to pay for the benefits being
 40 received through the governmental entity from which the employee retired, to pay the certified
 41 bargaining agent of the governmental entity, and to make any payments for child support or
 42 alimony. The board may, upon the written request of a retiree, authorize the plan administrator to
 43 withhold from the retiree's retirement payment those funds that are necessary to pay for premiums
 44 for accident, health, and long-term care insurance for the retiree and the retiree's spouse and
 45 dependents. The pension plan, the board and the City shall not incur any liability for actions taken
 46 in good faith, at the retiree's written request.
