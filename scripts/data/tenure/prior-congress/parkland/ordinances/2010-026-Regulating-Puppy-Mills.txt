 9 WHEREAS, a puppy mill has been described as a large scale, for profit facility that
 10 usually operates under sub-standard conditions concerning the well-being of dogs and their
 11 care; another description has been that a breeder at a puppy mill "produces puppies hands-
 12 over-fist with no breeding program, little attention to puppy placement and probably little or
 13 no health care or socialization practices"; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, puppy mills are often over crowded, females are often bred too often
 16 without checks for hereditary diseases or other standards normally employed by more
 17 reputable operations; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the documented problems of puppy mills include over breeding, in-
 20 breeding, minimal veterinary care, poor quality of food and shelter, lack of socialization with
 21 humans, over crowded cages and the killing of unwanted animals; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, it is estimated that there are more than 4,000 puppy mills in the United
 24 States producing more than a half-million puppies each year; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, consumers, unaware of the conditions and the problems that could arise
 27 from puppy mills find themselves faced with an array of immediate or future veterinary
 28 problems or a puppy which harbors genetically borne diseases and/or defects which do not
 29 appear until years later, from puppies purchased at puppy mills; and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, the Animal Welfare Act (7 U.S.C. Section 2131) requires, among other
 32 things, the licensing of certain breeders of dogs and cats and requires breeders to maintain
 33 minimum health, safety and welfare standards for animals in their care; and
 34
 35 WHEREAS, according to the City of West Hollywood, a review of state and U.S.D.A.
 36 inspection reports from more than 100 breeders who sold animals to the nation's largest
 37 retail pet store chain revealed that more than 60% of the inspections found serious violations
 38 of basic animal care standards, including sick or dead animals in their cages, lack of proper
 39 veterinary care, inadequate shelter from weather conditions and dirty unkempt cages that
 40 were too small; and
 41
 42 WHEREAS, a 2005 undercover investigation of California pet stores revealed that
 43 nearly half of the pet shops visited displayed animals that showed visible signs of illness,
 44 injury or neglect, and nearly half of the stores also sold animals showing clear symptoms of
 45 psychological distress; and

 29 Section 3-49. Puppy Mills.
 30
 31 A. A puppy mill is hereby defined as a facility where dogs are bred for the purpose of
 32 selling them and where any two of the following conditions are found to exist:
 33
 34 1. More than 20 puppies (under the age of 12 weeks) are kept at a single time;
 35
 36 2. No genetic (heredity) health testing appropriate for the breed is conducted;
 37
 38 3. No long-term (over one year) guarantees are offered;
 39
 40 4. A single female is bred every cycle;
 41
 42 5. A single female is bred more than five times;
 43
 44 6. There are no records of the dogs' parents;

 3 B. It shall be unlawful for any person or entity to establish, operate or maintain a
