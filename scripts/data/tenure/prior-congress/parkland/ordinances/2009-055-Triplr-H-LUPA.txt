 5 LAND USE ELEMENT

 7 1.5.11 Policy:
 8 By December 2011 the City will adopt ordinances to address
 9 the following:
 10 • Limitation of all lawn and ornamental irrigation to the
 11 hours and days specified in rule 40E-24.201 F.A.C.
 12 • Landscape ordinance meeting the Florida Friendly
 13 Landscape requirements set forth within Section
 14 373.185(2)(a)-(f), F.S. to create and maintain
 15 attractive, low maintenance, low impact landscapes
 16 which protect the City's and County's natural
 17 resources.
 18 • Installation of ultra-low volume plumbing fixtures in all
 19 new construction.
 20 • Inclusion of rain sensor device or automatic shut off
 21 with the purchase and installation of an automatic lawn
 22 sprinkler system.
 23
 24 1.6.7 Policy:
 25 The City will allocate 200 of the available residential flexibility
 26 and reserve units for mixed-use developments located within
 27 one quarter mile of the west right-of-way line of SR 7. The
 28 City will work with property owners who apply to develop a
 29 mixed-use development in that area to incorporate these
 30 residential units as part of the development plan. The mixed-
 31 use development shall be consistent with the City's
 32 comprehensive plan and land development regulations.
 33
 34 1.6.8 Policy:
 35 As part of the zoning and site plan approval of lands
 36 contained in land use plan amendment 01-LUPA-09, the City
 37 shall require a multi-family residential development consisting
 38 of a minimum of 250 units on 25 gross acres adjacent to the
 39 commercial parcel and County Line Road. Said multi-family
 40 residential development shall be connected to the adjacent
 41 commercial parcel by, at a minimum, the multipurpose trail
 42 system. Said commercial parcel shall be limited to a
 43 maximum of 110,000 square feet.
