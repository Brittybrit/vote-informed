 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 4 FLORIDA RELATING TO FLAG POLES AND FLAGS; AMENDING SECTION
 5 15-9 ENTITLED "FLAG POLES AND FLAGS IN ALL ZONING DISTRICTS" OF
 6 THE CODE OF ORDINANCES; PROVIDING A CONFLICTS CLAUSE AND A
 7 SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; AND PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 8
 9 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland, Florida,
 10 pursuant to the authority vested in Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, is authorized and empowered to
 11 consider changes to its land development regulations; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, this Ordinance is enacted pursuant to Article VIII of the Florida
 14 Constitution, Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, the Charter of the City of Parkland, and the police
 15 powers of the City; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 163, Florida Statutes, the
 18 City Planning and Zoning Board has reviewed the proposed Ordinance and has determined
 19 that the proposed regulations are consistent with the City of Parkland Comprehensive Plan.
 20
 21 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 22 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, that:
 23
 24 SECTION 1. Section 15-9 of the Code of Ordinance is hereby amended as follows:
 25
 26 Section 15-9. Flag poles and flags in all zoning districts.
 27
 28 (a) Except for residential lots of record which are over two (2) acres in size, there shall
 29 be one (1) flag pole, allowed per parcel or lot of record for the display of flags. For
 30 residential parcels greater than two (2) acres, two (2) flag poles shall be permitted. The
 31 maximum size of any flag is twenty-four (24) square feet. Flags attached to an angled
 32 pole attached to a building shall not exceed fifteen (15) square feet. No flags of any
 33 commercial nature may be displayed within any residential districts.
 34
 35 (b) Except as set forth herein, the maximum height of the flag pole in any residential
 36 zoning district shall not exceed twenty-five (25) feet.
 37
 38 (c) Within all commercial, industrial, open space / recreation, and community facility
 39 zoning districts, buildings under forty thousand (40,000) square feet in size and located
 40 on a plot under five (5) acres, up to two (2) flagpoles may be erected per property owner
 41 containing one (1) flag per flagpole only or one (1) flagpole containing no more than two
 42 (2) flags may be erected. The maximum height of a flag pole shall not exceed twenty
 43 (20) feet. The property owner can substitute the flagpole(s) and attach no more than
 44 two (2) flags to the property owner's building. The maximum size of any one (1) flag
 45 shall not exceed twenty-four (24) square feet. Flags attached to an angled pole attached
 46 to a building shall not exceed fifteen (15) square feet. The location of any flag pole or
 47 flag must be identified on a site plan prior to receiving a building permit and may be no

 11 flag shall not exceed twenty-four (24) square feet. Flags attached to an angled pole

 13 pole or flag must be identified on a site plan prior to receiving a building permit and may
 14 be no higher than the roofline of the first floor of the building. No flags of any
 15 commercial nature may be displayed within these districts.
 16
 17 Within all parcels containing governmental institutions, including public or private
 18 schools, up to three (3) flagpoles may be erected per property owner containing one (1)
 19 flag per pole only. If only one (1) flagpole is erected, two (2) flags may be flown. The
 20 property owner can substitute the flagpole(s) and attach no more than two (2) flags to
 21 the property owner's building. The maximum height shall not exceed thirty-five (35) feet.
 22 The maximum size of any one (1) flag shall not exceed sixty (60) square feet.
 23
 24 (d) The maximum dimensions of any flag shall be twenty-four (24) square feet.
 25
 26 (e) The minimum setback of the flag pole from all property lines shall be equal to the
 27 height of pole or the required setback for the zoning district, whichever is greater.
 28
 29 (f) In the event that a flag pole is proposed to camouflage a cellular tower as part of a
 30 proposal to limit the adverse visual appearance of a lattice or other noncamouflaged

 32 allow a flag pole to reach a height greater than set forth herein and may allow for a flag
 33 size greater than set forth herein. If a flag pole is proposed to camouflage a cellular

 3 (g) All flag poles are required to obtain site plan approval in addition to obtain a building
 4 permit.
 5
 6 (h) Any existing flag pole with a valid building permit is excluded from the height and
 7 setback requirements as set forth herein.
 8
 9 (i) All flags shall be in a good condition when being flown as determined by the City
 10 Manager and/or his/her designee. If a flag has been determined to be in poor
 11 maintenance condition, the property owner shall have three (3) days to replace the
 12 poorly maintained flag with a flag in good condition.
 13
 14 (j) Penalties. It shall constitute a violation of the City Code, punishable as provided in
 15 this section, to intentionally or willfully:
 16
 17 (1) Fly a flag that is torn, tattered, ripped, faded, worn, damaged or not maintained;
 18
 19 (2) Fly a flag in violation of the United States Code, as may be amended from time
 20 to time.
 21
 22 (3) To otherwise fail to comply with this article.
 23
 24 Failure to comply with any part of this article shall be punished by a fine not to exceed five
 25 hundred dollars ($500.00) or imprisonment for a term not exceeding sixty (60) days or by both
 26 fine and imprisonment. Each day a violation exists shall be a separate violation. In addition,
 27 the city shall also have the right to revoke, refuse to issue, refuse to reissue, refuse to transfer,
 28 or refuse to renew any business tax receipt for any facility, business, entity or establishment
 29 where the violation occurred until such time as the violation and/or fine is corrected.
 30
 31 SECTION 2: It is the intention of the City Commission and it is hereby ordained that the
 32 provisions of this ordinance shall become and be made a part of the Code of Ordinances of
 33 the City of Parkland, Florida, and the sections of this ordinance to accomplish such intent.
 34
 35 SECTION 3. If any word, phrase, clause, sentence, or section of this ordinance is for
 36 any reason held unconstitutional or invalid, the invalidity thereof shall not affect the validity of
 37 any remaining portions of this ordinance.
 38
 39 SECTION 4. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect immediately upon its
 40 passage and adoption.
 41
 42 PASSED this 6th day of January 2010, upon first reading.
 43
 44 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 3rd day of February 2010, upon second and final
 45 reading.
