 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF

 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland is a family oriented community with thousands of children
 12 under the age of 18 years old; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the City wishes to protect these children and provide for a secure environment
 15 for said children by assuring that persons recently convicted of sexual activity with minors may not
 16 have ready access to minors while said minors are at school, at school bus stops, day care centers,
 17 parks or playgrounds.
 18
 19 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 20 CITY OF PARKLAND,AS FOLLOWS:
 21
 22 SECTION 1. The above reference "Whereas" clauses are true and correct and made a
 23 part hereof.
 24
 25 SECTION 2. Section 11-141 of the City Code is hereby amended to read as follows:
 26
 27 Sec. 11-141. Sexual offender and sexual predator residence prohibition; penalties; exceptions.
 28
 29 (1) It is unlawful for any person who has been convicted of a violation of § 794.011, 800.04,
 30 827.071, 847.0135 or 847.0145, Florida Statutes, or a similar offense in another jurisdiction,
 31 regardless of whether adjudication has been withheld, in which the victim of the offense was
 32 less than sixteen (16) years of age, to establish a permanent residence or temporary residence
 33 within two thousand five hundred (2,500) feet of any school, designated public school bus stop,
 34 day care center, park or playground. Persons convicted of a violation of Section 794.05,
 35 Florida Statutes within the previous 5 years shall be subject to the same restrictions.
 36
 37 (2) For purposes of determining the minimum distance separation, the requirement shall be
 38 measured by following a straight line from the outer property line of the permanent residence
 39 or temporary residence to nearest outer property line of a school, designated public school bus
 40 stop, day care center, park or playground.
 41
 42 (3) Penalties. A person who violates this section shall be punished by a fine not exceeding

 44 by both such fine and imprisonment; for a second or subsequent conviction of a violation of

 1 ($1,000.00) or imprisonment in the county jail not more than twelve (12) months, or by both

 4 (4) Exceptions. A person residing within two thousand five hundred (2,500) feet of any
 5 school, designated public school bus stop, day care center, park, playground, or other place
 6 where children regularly congregate does not commit a violation of this section if any of the
 7 following apply:
 8
 9 (i) The sexual offender or sexual predator established the permanent residence prior to July 1,
 10 2005.
 11 (ii) The sexual offender or sexual predator was a minor when he/she committed the offense
 12 and was not convicted as an adult.
 13 (iii) The sexual offender or sexual predator is a minor.
 14 (iv) The school, designated public school bus stop or day care center within two thousand five
 15 hundred (2,500) feet of the sexual offender or sexual predator's permanent residence was
 16 opened after the person established the permanent residence.
