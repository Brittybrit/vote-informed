 ORDINANCE NO. 2007-01
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 FLORIDA,AMENDING ARTICLE XI OF CHAPTER 11,CREATING SECTION 11-
 118 OF THE CITY CODE RELATIVE TO CONDUCT IN COMMERCIAL
 ESTABLISHMENTS AND PROHIBITING SPECIFIED ACTIVITIES OR
 EXPOSING SPECIFIED ANATOMICAL AREAS IN ESTABLISHMENTS SELLING
 OR DISPENSING ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES;REVISING SAID REGULATIONS,
 TO INCLUDE A DEFINITION OF NUDE OR SEMI-NUDE ENTERTAINING AND
 OTHERWISE AMEND THE REGULATIONS CONTAINED THEREIN;
 PROVIDING EXCEPTIONS AND DEFINITIONS; PROVIDING FOR A
 SEVERABILITY CLAUSE;PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE;
 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland may regulate certain activities within
 establishments dealing in alcoholic beverages in order to avoid the negative effects of certain conduct; and
 WHEREAS,the City Commission of the City of Parkland takes notice of the decisions and opinions
 in the following cases: New York State Liquor Authority v. Bellanca, 452 U.S 714(U.S. 1981);El Morocco
 Club, Inc. v. Richardson, 746 A.2d 1228(2000);Ben's Bar, Inc. v. Village of Somerset, 316 F.3d 702(7th
 Circuit 2003); Blue Canary Corporation v. City of Milwaukee, 251 F.3d 1121 (7th Circuit 2001); Grand
 Faloon Tavern,Inc. v. Wicker, 670 F.2d 943(11th Circuit 1982);Lanier v. City ofNewton,Alabama, 842 F.2d
 253 (11th Circuit 1988);Sammy's of Mobile, LTD v. City of Mobile, 140 F.3d 993 (11th Circuit 1998); Wise
 Enterprises,Inc. v. Unified Government ofAthens-Clarke County, Georgia, 217 F.3d 1360(11 th Circuit 2000);
 Toy Box,Inc. v. Bay County, 989 F.Supp. 1183 (Northern District of Florida 1997);the Report of the Attorney
 General's Working Group on the Regulation of Sexually Oriented Businesses, State of Minnesota (1989);
 Report to the American Center for Law and Justice on the Secondary Impacts of Sex Oriented Businesses
 (1996); and the Final Report to the City of Garden Grove on the Relationship between Crime and Adult
 Business Operations on Garden Grove Boulevard(1991), all of which are on file with the Clerk; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission notes that based upon these decisions there is documented
 evidence that adult entertainment establishments have a deleterious effect on existing businesses and
 neighborhoods around them; and
 WHEREAS,the combination of alcohol and adult entertainment establishments produces negative
 secondary effects for the surrounding communities; and
 WHEREAS, in attempting to avoid the negative secondary effects of the sale or consumption of
 alcoholic beverages in adult entertainment establishments, the City Commission does not wish to regulate
 speech or artistic endeavors or in any way regulate the message conveyed by any type of public nudity; and
 WHEREAS,the City Commission finds that the sale or consumption of alcoholic beverages on the
 premises of adult entertainment establishments exacerbates the deleterious secondary effects of such businesses
 on the community, including a negative effect on property values, and begets undesirable behavior; and
 WHEREAS,the City Commission wishes to amend its previous Ordinance on this subject to further
 define terms;
 NOW THEREFORE,BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA,AS FOLLOWS:

 SECTION 1. Article XI of Chapter 11, Section 11-118 of the City Code entitled AAlcoholic
 Beverages in Commercial Establishments@ is hereby amended as follows:
 Sec. 11-118. Alcoholic Beverages in Commercial Establishments.
 I. Definitions.
 The following words,terms and phrases,when used in this article,shall have the meanings ascribed to
 them in this section, except where the context clearly indicates a different meaning:
 Advertisement means a display, notice or other information designed to attract public attention,
 including,but not limited to handbills,signs,billboards,soundtracks,placards,signboards and written notices.
 Alcoholic beverages means all beverages containing more than one-half of one percent of alcohol by
 weight, including beer and wine.
 Areola means the darkened ring surrounding the nipple of a female breast.
 Commercial means operated for pecuniary gain,which shall be presumed for any establishment which
 imposes a charge for entrance to view entertainment, or to remain on the premises, or to obtain any goods,
 items, food, drink or service. For purposes of this article, operating for pecuniary gain shall not depend on
 actual profit or loss.
 Establishment means a physical plant or location, or the commercial activities or operations being
 conducted, or both together, as the context of this article may require.
 Nude or Semi-Nude Entertainment means any activity where specified anatomical areas are exposed or
 displayed in public view,or where specified anatomical activities are displayed or performed in public view;
 this shall include the display of motion pictures which permit the audience to view such areas or activities.
 Premises means a physical plant or location, which is enclosed by walls or any other enclosing
 structural device, or which is covered by a single roof or with a single shared entrance from outside.
 Specified anatomical areas is defined as:
 (1) Less than completely and opaquely covered:
 a. Human genitals and pubic region; or
 b. Cleavage of the human buttocks; or
 c. That portion of the human female breast encompassed within an area
 falling below the horizontal line one would have to draw to intersect a point
 immediately above the top of the areola, including the areola; this definition shall
 include the entire lower portion of the human female breast, but shall not include a
 portion of the cleavage of the human female breast exhibited by a dress,blouse,skirt,
 leotard,bathing suit or other wearing apparel,provided the areola is not so exposed;
