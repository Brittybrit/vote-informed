 3 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, RELATING TO PURCHASING GUIDELINES;
 5 CREATING A NEW SECTION 2-143.9 (M) PROVIDING FOR WAIVER
 6 OF COMPETITIVE BIDDING REQUIREMENTS AND CREATING A
 7 NEW SECTION 2-143.5 (2), PROVIDING FOR A LOCAL PREFERENCE
 8 TO PARKLAND CONTRACTORS; CONTAINING PROVISIONS FOR
 9 IMPLEMENTATION, PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY
 10 CODE; PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN
 11 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland wishes to amend its Procurement
 14 Code to provide flexibility for certain situations where the City Commission deems it in the best
 15 interest of the City to waive competitive bidding requirements; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, to support local businesses, the City wishes to provide certain provisions for local
 18 preferences to Parkland contractors;
 19
 20 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 21 CITY OF PARKLAND,AS FOLLOWS:
 22
 23 SECTION 1. A new Section 2-144.1 of the City Code is hereby created and shall read
 24 as follows:
 25
 26 Sec. 2-143.9 (m). Waiver of procurement requirements.
 27
 28 The City Commission may waive the procurement requirements set forth in Sections 2-
 29 141 through 2-144 in such cases the Commission finds that it is in the best interests of the City
 30 of Parkland to do so, and that the waiver of competitive bid requirements will not inure to the
 31 financial disadvantage of the City. In no case may competitive bidding requirements be waived
 32 where said waiver would constitute a violation of state law.
 33 SECTION 2. A new Section 2-143.5(2) of the City Code is hereby created and shall
 34 read as follows:
 35
 36 Sec. 2-143.5 Local preference.
 37
 38 Sec. 2-143.5. Award of bid.
 39
 40 Bids shall be awarded by the city commission to the "lowest responsible and responsive bidder"
 41 submitting a responsive bid. In determining the lowest responsible and most responsive bidder,
 42 in addition to the price, the following may be considered:
 43
 44 (a) The ability, capacity, and skill of the bidder to perform the contract.
 45 (b) Whether the bidder can perform the contract within the time specified, without delay or
 46 interference.
 1

<<PG>>
 1 (c) The character, integrity, reputation,judgment, experience and efficiency of the bidder.
 2 (d) The quality of performance on previous public or private contracts of a similar type.
 3 (e) The previous and existing compliance by the bidder with laws and ordinances relating to
 4 the contract.
 5 (f) The ability of the bidder to provide future maintenance and service.
 6 (g) Tie bids. The tie may be broken and the successful vendor selected by the following
 7 criteria presented in order of importance and consideration:
 8 1. Quality of the items or services if it is ascertainable
 9 2. Time of delivery if provided in the bid
 10 3. If the above criteria are impossible to determine with any reasonableness or do not
 11 resolve the issue, the award will be given to that vendor whose bid was received first as
 12 indicated by the time stamp on the envelope containing the bid.
 13
 14 Bids shall be unconditionally accepted without alteration or correction, except as authorized in
 15 this policy. Bids shall be evaluated based on the requirements set forth in the invitation for bids,
 16 which may include additional criteria to determine suitability for a particular purpose.
 17
 18 2. Except where prohibited by federal, state or city law, for bid solicitations where the bid is to
 19 be awarded to the lowest qualified, responsible and responsive bidder without a numerical
 20 grading the City may grant a preference in the amount of five (5) percent of any bid to the
 21 lowest qualified, responsible and responsive local bidder. If said local bidder is within 5% of
 22 the lowest responsible and responsive bid, then the lowest, responsible, responsive local bidder
 23 shall be permitted to match the lowest bid within three business days of notice from the City.
 24 For all other competitive solicitations in which objective factors are used to numerically
 25 evaluate the responses from vendors by the selection committee, and price is one of several of
 26 the criteria for award among otherwise qualified vendors, where a non-local business is the
 27 highest ranked proposer and the ranking of a local proposer is within five percent (5%) of the
 28 ranking obtained by the non-local proposer, the highest ranked local proposer (within 5%) shall
 29 have the opportunity to proceed to negotiations with the City upon approval of such ranking by
 30 the City Commission (or staff if the award does not need commission approval). If the City
 31 determines that it is, in its sole and exclusive discretion, unable to negotiate an acceptable
 32 contract, then it shall proceed to the negotiate with the next highest ranked proposer, whether
 33 local or non-local.
 34
 35 This section shall not apply to professional services procured pursuant to the State of Florida
 36 Consultants Competitive Negotiations Act, nor to "cooperative" purchasing plans and proposals
 37 that the city participates in with other governmental agencies or"piggyback" bid purchases.
 38 To qualify as a local vendor/bidder/proposer/supplier, an eligible bidder or proposer must
 39 satisfy the criteria set forth below. The City has sole discretion in determining whether a
 40 business meets the criteria to qualify for a local business preference and reserves the right to
 41 revoke this preference at any time if the City determines the business no longer meets the
 42 following criteria:
 43 a.. Its principal place of business is located in the City Parkland and
 44 b. the business has held a valid City business license for at least one (1) year prior to the date of
 45 application; and
 46 c. It maintains its status as a local bidder throughout the term of the contract; if it fails to do so
 47 the contract shall entitle the City, in its sole discretion, to terminate the contract.
