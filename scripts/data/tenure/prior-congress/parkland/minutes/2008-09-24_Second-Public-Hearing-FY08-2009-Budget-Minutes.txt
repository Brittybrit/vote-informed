A. PRESENTATION BY STAFF ON THE 2008-2009 BUDGET 
 
Mayor Udine recalled the previous presentations made, and he asked that it be waived at this 
meeting. There were no objections from the Commission. 
 
B. PUBLIC INPUT 
 
 C. ORDINANCE NO. 2008-19: ADOPTING THE MILLAGE RATE 
 
 SECOND READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF 
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE MILLAGE RATE TO BE 
LEVIED FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2008 AND 
ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 2009 AND STATING THE PERCENTAGE 
INCREASE IN PROPERTY TAXES AS SUCH INCREASE IS DEFINED IN 
FLORIDA STATUTES, SECTION 200.065, AND HOUSE BILL 1B, 
PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY, AND PROVIDING FOR AN 
EFFECTIVE DATE. . 
 
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the second reading of Ordinance No. 2008-19. 
 
In accordance with Florida statutes, City Attorney Andrew Maurodis read information 
concerning the budget and operating millage rate of 3.4083, which will result in a five percent 
decrease in property taxes. He noted that the public may comment on either. 
 
Mayor Udine opened to the public. There were no comments or questions, and he closed to the 
public. 
 
Commissioner Smith commented this was his third budget year, and he noted that the City had 
been able to lower the millage rate for the third year in a row. He thanked staff for an 
outstanding job. 
 
MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to approve the second reading of Ordinance No. 
2008-19, Adopting the Millage Rate of 3.4083. Seconded by Vice Mayor Moskowitz. 
 
ROLL CALL VOTE: 
 
Commissioner Smith Yes 
Vice Mayor Moskowitz Yes 
Commissioner Rosenof Yes 
Commissioner Weissman Yes 
Mayor Udine Yes 
 
MOTION passed unanimously.
