 Impact Fee Requirements
 • Authorized by Florida Statutes 163.31801 ("Florida
 Impact Fee Act")
 • One-time payments paid at time of building permit
 • Represent new development's proportionate share of
 capital facility needs
 • Can only be used for capital projects that add or expand
 capacity and can only be spent on categories for which
 they are collected
 • Cannot be used for maintaining or replacing existing
 capital facilities
 • "Rational Nexus" — demand, proportionality, benefit
 • Must be assessed in a non-discriminatory manner
 Tischler if- ;

<<PG>>
 Calculation Methodologies
 • Best measure of the demand created by new
 development for additional infrastructure capacity
 - Past = Buy-in
 - Present = Incremental Expansion
 - Future = Plan-base
 • "Level-of-Service" (LOS) — units of infrastructure per
 demand unit
 • Police and Fire Impact Fees utilize the incremental
 expansion methodology

 Impact Fee Assessment Options
 • Assessed against both residential and nonresidential
 land uses
 • Three options for assessing single family detached units
 - Option 1 : one impact fee amount for all single family
 detached units
 - Option 2: different impact fee amounts based on number of
 bedrooms
 - Option 3: different impact fee amounts based on number
 of square feet of building
 • Impact fee amounts for multi-family units and
 nonresidential land uses are the same under each option

 Impact Fee Assessment Options (cont.)
 • All three approaches are defensible
 • All three approaches are used by nearby jurisdictions
 • No one approach generates more revenue than another
