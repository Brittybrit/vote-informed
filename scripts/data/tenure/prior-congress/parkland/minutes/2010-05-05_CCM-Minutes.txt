ADDED AGENDA ITEM: DISCUSSION REGARDING BSO NON-

EMERGENCY PHONE LINE

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young explained that the City’s own non-emergency telephone
number that BSO answers is 954-753-5050, which goes directly to the Public Safety Building
during operations when that building is open. She said that after operations, the telephone call
goes to the officer-in-charge or to the sergeant in charge, who carries a cell phone to answer these
calls while they are out on patrol, since there is no administrative staff after hours. She added
that this arrangement was agreed to by BSO and the City when police services transferred to BSO
in 2004.

Commissioner Rosenof spoke about his ride-along with BSO where he witnessed the deputy
trying to answer his cell phone and taking emergency calls, while he was taking dispatch calls off
the radio, and while he was pulling drivers over. Commissioner Rosenof said he feels that after
hours, calls to the 753-5050 number should be transferred to BSO dispatch and if it is a true
emergency, it would then get dispatched through the normal channels. He stated that he would
like the Commission’s opinion about changing the current policy.

Commissioner Weissman commented that he would like to maintain the 753-5050 number for
non-emergency use and not have calls transferred to downtown, even after hours. He said the
