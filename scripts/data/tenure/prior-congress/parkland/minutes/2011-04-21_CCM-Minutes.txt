Mayor Udine moved out of the regular order of the agenda to resume the hearing that was continued
from the April 6, 2011 Regular City Commission Meeting. He reminded everyone that this is still a
quasi-judicial proceeding and all parties were present. He stated they were at the point of the hearing
where the City Commission asked to engage an attorney to review the issues raised by the residents
regarding the bankruptcy issue.
City Attorney Maurodis stated the part of the hearing is at the end where the City Commission may
ask additional questions of the report.
Mayor Udine invited Attorney Chad Pugatch to the podium explaining that he has been engaged to
offer legal opinion on the WCI Bankruptcy and if the City has any options.
Attorney Pugatch briefly summarized the opinion. He explained that this is a very clear issue under
bankruptcy law and not one that could be open to reasonable debate. He stated the question was
whether there is any basis as a result of the Chinese Drywall claims that were outstanding and made at
the time of the WCI bankruptcy proceedings. He explained that the Chinese Drywall claims were
directed into a Trust — that Trust receiving treatment and benefits under thc WCI plan and the Trust
and the Plan being approved by the Bankruptcy Court in Delaware and the Bankruptcy Plan, the order

confirming the Plan and the Bankruptcy Code provides that once creditor’s claims are dealt with in a
Plan, the creditors or anyone on behalf of the creditors will be precluded from taking any action that
would not be in accordance with the Plan that was confirmed by the Court. In this particular case, the
claims of the Chinese Drywall claimants were channeled into a Plan into the Trust — that Trust gives
them certain property rights and remedies, it preserved all the insurance claims, and equity interest in
the New WCI and if they are successful this translates into dollars for those people who arc found in
the Chinese Drywall class action. He also stated that Section 11-41 of the Bankruptcy Code says you
are bound as a creditor by the Plan whether anyone agrees with it or not. He stated interestingly the
Chinese Drywall claimants class approved the plan by about a 98% vote in the WCI Bankruptcy case.
Other than the remedies in the plan, no one can take action that would violate either the confirmation
order or the discharge that the debtors got. In this case, the rights were transferred to New WCI
through the plan and both the confirmation order and the plan specifically provide that WCI and its
affiliates take free and clear of any of the claims that were in the plan.

That not only is a court order, but by law it constitutes an injunction from anyone interfering with
their actions going forward on account of those claims that were dealt with in the plan. And the
violation of that injunction can not only subject individuals who violate to contempt of court, but can
subject individuals and entitics to monctary sanctions for violating the terms of the confirmation order
and injunction.

Attorney Pugatch recited several cases in case law and the statutory provisions, recited specific
provisions of the WCI plan and specific provisions of the confirmation order that deals with these
matters as back-up to the opinion letter that was given to the City Attorney Andrew Maurodis. He
stated it would be inappropriate for the City and subjecting the City and individuals acting on behalf
of the City to potential liability to take any action against New WCI strictly because of the fact that
this bankruptcy occurred and the Chinese Drywall claims occurred within it.

He stated there was an issue raised concerning the potential of fraud in terms of agents of WCI who
knew or covered up these matters. He pointed out in the letter that even fraud claims are claims and if
anyonc has issues with the Plan, they have to bring the claims up as part of Bankruptcy and those
claims are either dealt with or overruled, but they are dealt with in the Bankruptcy and they don’t
create any separate right or separate ability to come back after the fact due to that issue. Even
regarding fraud on the court, there is a 6 month limitation after a bankruptcy plan is confirmed for a
creditor to come back and try to undo the confirmation of a plan because of fraud on the court. It is
past that stage and no one has pointed out that anything that occurred rises to the level of fraud on the
court as opposed to potentially fraud on claimants. In that regard, there is no ability at this point to go
back and take any action strictly because the Chinese drywall claims and their impact to the
Bankruptcy case.

There is a provision of the Bankruptcy Code Section 525 that with regard to any debtor getting a
discharge whether it is an individual or a corporation, there is a specific statute that states you cannot
discriminate against someone just because they filed bankruptcy and got a discharge in bankruptcy.
Mayor Udine asked about the relevance of unclean hands in this case.

Attorney Pugatch replied Bankruptcy court is a court of equity. If someone has unclean hands, it is
completely proper to raise objections based upon those unclean hands but it would have had to be
done in Bankruptcy court. Based on review of the docket, they did not see any objection to
confirmation filed raising this issue and the order states that all objections were either resolved or
overruled.
Mayor Udine asked even if it could be brought up, would the City Commission be the body to bring it
up.
Attorney Pugatch stated if there are Chinese Drywall claimants who feel they were wrongfully treated,
if they think they have remedies against New WCI, they certainly have the right to bring them —
however difficult it may be. He does not see where the City, indirectly through the land use process,
is the appropriate vehicle to do this.
Mayor Udine stated that two years ago when the Plan was being contemplated by the Bankruptcy
Court, the City engaged counsel in Delaware and tried to extend the timeframe to file a claim.
Attorney Maurodis explained that the evidentiary portion of the proceeding is closed and is open to
