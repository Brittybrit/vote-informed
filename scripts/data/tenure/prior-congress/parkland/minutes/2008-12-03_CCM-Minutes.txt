2. RESOLUTION NO. 2008-121: PARKLAND RESERVE 
 ANTI-MONOTONY 
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE 
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, FOR ANTI-MONOTONY 
APPROVAL FOR PARKLAND RESERVE SINGLE FAMILY 
RESIDENTIAL DWELLING UNIT ELEVATIONS, CASE 
NUMBER 14-CAB-08, LOCATED WITHIN THE PARKLAND 
RESERVE COMMUNITY (FORMERLY KNOWN AS 
PARKLAND VILLAGE). 
 (quasi-judicial) 
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2008-121, Parkland 
Reserve Anti-Monotony and advised this was a quasi-judicial hearing. 
Mr. Robert Stiegele, on behalf of Centerline Homes, waived quasi-judicial proceedings. 
Planning & Zoning Director, Ms. Roberta Moore gave a brief explanation. Ms. Moore advised 
the Planning & Zoning Board had recommended approval of the petition unanimously 5-0 based 
on staff request for a condition of approval. Staff had requested to limit the number of building 
permits to the existing 50 foot lots until such time as the replatt goes through and the City 
Commission approves the modifications requested. 
Mayor Udine clarified for the record that the request was for an approval of floor plans and 
elevations only for Centerline’s marketing purposes and will not affect density or offer any 
future guarantees. There will be a condition added to the resolution. 
MOTION made by Vice Mayor Weissman to approve Resolution No. 2008- 121, Parkland 
Reserve Anti-Monotony as amended. Seconded by Commissioner Moskowitz. 
Mayor Udine opened for discussion. 
Commissioner Smith questioned if price points were known at this time. 
Mr. Stiegele advised Centerline will require the City Commission’s decision in order to 
determine sales prices. 
Commissioner Smith noted his opinion on elevations, color choices and architectural features. 
Mr. Stiegele offered to review the plans with Commissioner Smith in more detail at a later time.

Commissioner Moskowitz advised he intended to support this item and offered comments for the

mixed use property and noted this would be the first planned residential community in Parkland

 1. RESOLUTION NO. 2008-122: AMERICAN BODY WORKS 
 SPECIAL EXCEPTION 
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE 
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, FOR SPECIAL 
EXCEPTION APPROVAL FOR AMERICAN BODY 
WORKS LOCATED AT THE SOUTHWEST CORNER OF 
STATE ROAD 7 AND LOXAHATCHEE ROAD IN THE 
WATERWAYS SHOPPES, CASE NO. 02-SE-08. 
(quasi-judicial) 
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2008-122, American Body 
Works Special Exception and advised this was a quasi-judicial hearing. 
Ms. Hope Calhoun, on behalf of American Body Works, waived the quasi-judicial proceedings. 
Planning & Zoning Director, Ms. Roberta Moore gave a brief explanation. Ms. Moore advised 
City staff notified residents within 500 feet of this location per code requirements and the 
Planning & Zoning Board recommended approval of this petition unanimously 6-0 vote. She 
advised this request is for approval of a 5,441 sq. ft, 24 hour gymnasium to operate within an 
existing shopping center (the Waterways). 
Ms. Moore advised that City of Parkland staff, BSO staff and the applicant have reviewed the 24 
hour nature of the facility and concerns for public safety. The applicant agreed to limit hours of 
operation from 5:00 AM to 12:00 Midnight. 
Commissioner Smith questioned if there is a real need for a gymnasium that is open until 
midnight. 
Ms. Calhoun responded that American Body Works is new to South Florida and they feel that 
there is a need for this service and gave examples such as Police Officers and Students, etc. 
Commissioner Smith questioned BSO Police Chief O’Connell if additional security would be 
necessary with these hours of operation. 
Chief O’Connell responded that after discussion with the applicant and City staff, they felt 24 
hour operation did not fit for the City of Parkland. He cited crime statistics show that 15% of 
crimes occur between the hours of 12:00 Midnight and 5:00 AM. He suggested closing at 12:00 
Midnight would mitigate that percentage. 
Commissioner Rosenof requested clarification if notifications had been sent to residents 
regarding this application.
