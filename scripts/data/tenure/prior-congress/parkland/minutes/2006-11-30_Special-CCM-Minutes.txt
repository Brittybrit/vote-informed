II PURPOSE:

1. CONSIDERATION OF SHORT LIST FOR CONTINUING
ENGINEERING SERVICES:

ENGINEERING & CONSULTING SERVICES
BID # 2006-11

Mayor Udine summarized that staff had considered the proposals in order to short list them for
Commission consideration, and the Commission's possible addition of others. He noted that the
engineering presentations would take place at 5 :00 p.m. preceding the next Regular Commission
meeting.

City Manager Mark Lauzier noted that the back-up material contained the committee's
selections, which were relatively consistent in their evaluation of qualifications and expertise.
The process was that the committee would provide a minimum of eight firms; there were nine for
the Commission to consider. The Commission could also determine additional firms to be
interviewed. It was originally contemplated that interviews would be 10-minute presentations
with a 5-minute question and answer period but, given the number, staff suggested limiting the
presentations to five minutes, thereby allowing some leeway before the next meeting. A letter
would be sent to the firms under consideration outlining their specific times for December 6th.

Mayor Udine agreed with the staff committee's selections, but asked that Bill Keith's firm and
CDM be added to the short list.

MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to add CDM/Keith & Associates to the short list.
Seconded by Vice Mayor McGilvray.

ROLL CALL VOTE:

Commissioner McGilvray Yes
Commissioner Weissman Yes
Commissioner Smith Yes
Vice Mayor Moskowitz Yes
Mayor Udine Yes

MOTION passed unanimously.

Mayor Udine summarized that 10 firms would comprise the short list for presentations. There
were no other additions.

MOTION made by Commissioner McGilvray to short list as amended. Seconded by Vice
Mayor Moskowitz.

ROLL CALL VOTE:

Commissioner McGilvray Yes
Commissioner Weissman Yes
Commissioner Smith Yes
Vice Mayor Moskowitz Yes
Mayor U dine Yes

MOTION passed unanimously.

Mr. Lauzier offered that he had spoken with the City Attorney that, as the processes are refined
for the future, a short list of this magnitude is probably not the most efficient handling. He
suggested that staff recommend a short list of three to five firms, with the Commission then
having the opportunity to review.
