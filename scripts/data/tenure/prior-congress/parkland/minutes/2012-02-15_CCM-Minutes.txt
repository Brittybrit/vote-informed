 Program Details
 The Y-Fit Physical Activity program will be open to students 6-12 to ensure the
 success of the initial measurable outcomes. This evidence based program will have
 measurable outcomes to demonstrate the impact of increased physical activity.
 The measurements will include (but may not be limited to) time, flexibility,
 cardiovascular endurance, muscular strength and nutrition education. (BMI testing
 will NOT be used.)
