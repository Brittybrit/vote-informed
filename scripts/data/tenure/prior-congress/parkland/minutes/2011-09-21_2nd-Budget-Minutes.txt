City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of the second reading of Ordinance No.
2011-31: Adopting Millage.

City Attorncy Andrew Maurodis read into the record the following: “The Millage Rate for the fiscal
year beginning October 1, 2011 and ending September 30, 2012 to be levied in order to produce
sufficient ad valorem taxation revenue which when combined with other projected general fund
revenues will be adequate to pay appropriations made in the budget for the fiscal year beginning
October 1, 2011 and ending September 30, 2012 shall be the operating millage rate of 4.0198. The
adopted millage rate of 4.0198 mils is 2.34% more than the roll back rate of 3.9279 and is computed
according to the guidelines set forth in Section 200.065. So therefore, we are here to adopt the final
millage rate of 4.0198 which is 2.34% increase over the rollback rate 3.9279.”

Mayor Udine recognized Ms. Edith Lederberg from Aging and Disability Resource Center and
Ms. Kris Sever from Family Central.

Mayor Udine expressed that the City of Parkland funded both agencies grant requests and each
agency is credited with giving back to the Parkland community.

Ms. Edith Lederberg thanked the City Commission for its support. She stated that this is the
25™ consecutive year the City of Parkland has given them the full fair share amount requested.
Kris Sever at Family Central thanked the City Commission for its support as well.
Commissioner Weissman pointed out that the dollars the City of Parkland donates is multiplied
by 57, which helps the organization obtain additional dollars to fund their initiatives.

Stuart Krantz, Pine Tree Estates, expressed his feelings about why the Parkland millage should
be 5.1 mills and why the $500,000 dollars should be spent on West Glades Middle School and
Stoneman Douglas High School traffic patterns. He also had a concern about the ethics of the
TRIM notice process and he spoke about the library’s carpet.

Mayor Udine commented that by State law there has to be a maximum number placed on the
TRIM Notices before they are sent out to residents. He then deferred to City Attorney Andrew
Maurodis who explained the process. Mr. Maurodis explained that as opposed to other
municipalities, the City of Parkland sets its maximum at a public hearing through a resolution.
City Attorney Maurodis addressed the issue Mr. Krantz raised regarding ethics with the TRIM
process. Mr. Maurodis explained that there is categorically no issue with regards to an ethics
violation with the TRIM process.

Mayor Udine opened to the public. There was no one present and the Mayor closed to the
public.

2. ORDINANCE NO. 2011-32: ADOPTING BUDGET
COMMENTS: SECOND READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY

COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
FLORIDA, ADOPTING A BUDGET FOR THE FISCAL
YEAR BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2011 AND ENDING
SEPTEMBER 30, 2012, PROVIDING FOR
SEVERABILITY AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE
DATE.

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of the second reading of Ordinance No. 2011-

32: Adopting Budget. She explained that the budget being proposed is a balanced budget based

on the fire assessment rate that was set at the September 14, 2011 mccting. All the changes that

were requested from the City Commission during the two public hearings have been reflected in

the budget as being presented.

Mayor Udine opened to the public.

Mr. Stuart Krantz expressed concerns about more money going to Parks and Recreation and very

little going to the Library. He is displeased with the $522,000 that is being rolled in the budget.

He thinks it could be spent on other things. He stated the sound system at the Library was bad

and the Friends of the Library had to purchase the sound system. He does not think the Library is

being properly taken care of in this budget. He gave many suggestions to spend the $522,000.

Mayor Udinc stated that the Parkland Library gets a lot of use and the residents love it.

There were no others present and the Mayor closed to the public.

MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to approve on second reading Ordinance No.

2011-32: Adopting Budget. Seconded by Vice Mayor Rosenof.
