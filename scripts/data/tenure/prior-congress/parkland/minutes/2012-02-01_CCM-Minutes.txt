II PUBLIC INPUT
Ms. Sheryl Woods, 1632 Old Thyme Court, resident of Parkland Golf & Country Club and President
and CEQ of YMCA of Broward County introduced Mr. Jeff Keller as the new Executive Director of
the Parkland YMCA and Ms. Michelle Brown, Board Member of the Parkland YMCA. Ms. Brown
took a few minutes to speak about the SK Run to raise money for children’s programs and to help
childhood obesity. The event will take place on March 4, 2012 and she invited all to attend.
Cliff Kaufman, 6011 NW 62™ Court, Country Pointe Estates strongly recommended talking to other
cities who have red light cameras and have tried to drop the service unsuccessfully. He spoke of
penalties that the cities incurred. He also complained that BSO officers are blocking the left turn lanes
into the subdivision for Country Pointe.
There were no other comments. Mayor Udine closed to the public.

Vice Mayor Weissman stated that the City has talked about acquiring the Beaty parcel for some time
now and if feasible, the City should move forward with the purchase.
Commissioner Moskowitz clarified that the money to purchase the Beaty parcel would come from the
City’s reserves and asked would there be enough monies left in reserves to cover cleanup cost should a
hurricane occur. He agrees that it makes sense to move forward with the purchase of the property.
Commissioner Rosenof agrees with moving forward with the purchase of the Beaty parcel.
The consensus of the City Commission was to direct the City Manager to move forward with the
purchase of the Beaty parcel.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young stated she will move forward with a letter of commitment.

1. PRESENTATION: MPO PRESENTATION OF NORTHWEST

BROWARD STUDY

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young referred to Ms. Roberta Moore, Planning & Zoning Director for a
brief presentation and explanation of the above item. She explained that as part of the removal of
University Drive when the Wedge piece was annexed into Broward County, a number of studies were
required one of which was the NW Broward Study. She then introduced Mr. James Cromar, Livability
Planner with the Broward Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) who gave a brief presentation
on the findings of the study. (Sec attached presentation for greater details of the study.)

2. DOG PARK UPDATE
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young referred to Mr. Scott Davidoff, Community Services Director for
an update and brief presentation on the dog park. (See attached presentation)
Commissioner Moskowitz detailed many projects that were paid for by developer dollars. He
addressed several items within the presentation that he felt were areas of concern. He stated he would
like sod replaced between Barkland and the Library; Additional parking is necessary at Liberty Park;
Specimen Oaks instead of Palms for shade trees; and additional garbage cans from the parking lot to
the dog park.
Vice Mayor Weissman favors waiting for development dollars to come in to pay for additional
improvements to the dog park instead of taking it from the City’s coffers because he feels that once the
City pays for it, it is more difficult to get a developer to pay for it.
Commissioner Rosenof stated he would like to see the benches and trees paid for by dollars received
from sponsorship opportunities. He feels the biggest priority is keeping the sod green along Holmberg
Road and along the sidewalk between the Library and Barkland. He also is in favor of irrigation being
installed to keep grass green along Holmberg.

Mayor Udine commented that Broward County Commissioner Stacy Ritter has available dollars in the

Broward County Park fund that needs to be expended and he suggested that staff contact her

accordingly. He stated that he is ok with holding off on additional parking, however he is confident

that monies can be raised through donations or if need be, taken from reserves.

Commissioner Moskowitz commented that he is ok with delaying the addition of parking, but if staff

can find dollars, then the parking can be done. He stated he would like to move forward with

installing sod by the Library and installing irrigation or Bahia sod along Holmberg Road. He would

also like to move forward with installing shade trees at Barkland. He suggested expending some

dollars now and funding the project in September when monies become available in the City’s budget.

He would like to do everything except parking, benches and the dog equipment.

MOTION made by Commissioner Moskowitz to approve installation of the Specimen Oak shade

trees, and installing sod along Holmberg Road and between the Library and Barkland.

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young informed the City Commission that a mid-year budget transfer

may be necessary if there are not enough funds in the City’s budget to cover the cost of expenditures.

Seconded by Vice Mayor Weissman.

Mayor Udine opened to the public.

Mr. Jim Weiss suggested installing a donation box to fund some of the Barkland initiatives.

There were no further comments and the Mayor closed to the public.

Commissioner Moskowitz initiated a discussion regarding establishing a “Friends of Barkland”

committee.

Further discussion ensued.

All were in favor of the motion.

MOTION passed by those present.
