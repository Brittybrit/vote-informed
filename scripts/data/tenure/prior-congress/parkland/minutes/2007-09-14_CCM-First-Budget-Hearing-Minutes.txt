C. ORDINANCE NO. 2007-15: ADOPTING THE MILEAGE RATE
FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF
PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE MILEAGE RATE TO BE
LEVIED FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2007 AND
ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 2008 AND STATING THE PERCENTAGE
INCREASE IN PROPERTY TAXES AS SUCH INCREASE IS DEFINED IN
FLORIDA STATUTES, SECTION 200.065, AND HOUSE BILL 1B,
PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY, AND PROVIDING ,FOR AN
EFFECTIVE DATE.
ACTION: MOTION ON THE DECISION OF THE COMMISSION
Mr. Maurodis read the first reading of Ordinance No. 2007-15.
As statutorily required, Mr. Maurodis read into the record the pertinent points of the Ordinance.
He explained that millage rates were levied in order to produce sufficient ad valorem revenue
which, when combined with other projected General Fund revenues, will be adequate to pay the
appropriations made in the budget for the Fiscal Year beginning October 1, 2007, and ending
September 30, 2008. The operating millage shall be 3.4083 mils. This operating millage rate
will result in a five percent decrease in property taxes. This decrease is a percentage by which
the millage rate to be levied is less than the rolled back rate of 3.5877, computed in accordance
with Florida Statutes 200.065.

Commencing her PowerPoint presentation, Ms. Hastings thanked Mr. Maurodis for reading the
required information into the record and. noted the inclusion of the five percent decrease required
by House Bill 1B. She briefly reviewed the tax reform budget highlights and recapped the
General Fund. She explained that the August TRIM Notice had been mailed to taxpayers based
upon the 3.58 millage adopted in July. With subsequent receipt of further information, staff had
presented a budget with a millage of 3.4083. With this rework of the budget and based upon the
input received from the Commission at their last meeting, the fire rescue assessment had reverted
to last year's rate of $151. Additional funding for the Holmberg Road capital project had been
pulled. Ms. Hastings reviewed a sample property tax bill.
As to revenues, Ms. Hastings indicated that the City would experience a $1.95 million reduction
in property taxes due to the decrease in the millage to 3.4083. With the sluggish housing market,
revenues for building and related permits had also been reduced by $1.1 million. The ad valorem
revenue had increased from last year by $763,000 due solely to new construction; $386 million in
new construction was unaffected by House Bill 1B.
Regarding expenditures, Ms. Hastings stated that fire rescue had increased $825,000 with the
addition of the Western Fire Station and 13 firefighters. Public safety costs with BSO had
increased $250,000 with the addition of the motorcycle officer and one supervisor shift; she
noted that crossing guards and code enforcement costs were also included.
Ms. Hastings advised that the total budget for the City for Fiscal Year 2007-2008 would be $29.9
million; the General Fund represented $22.2 million and the Capital Projects Fund represented
$6.9 million. Without transfers or carry forwards, total revenues were $19.8 million and total
expenditures were $19.7 million. The City would have a $140,000 excess. The Fund Balance of
the General Fund as of September 2006 was $4.03 million. Staff has proposed to keep that Fund
Balance, and it was projected that the same would remain as of September 2008. As to its
adequacy, Ms. Hastings commented that in the past three months of expenditures were
considered adequate, which would be $4.8 million for the City. In recent years with the
hurricanes, it became apparent that three months would not be sufficient, and she hoped to work
toward building it up.
Ms. Hastings .indicated total revenues in the Capital Projects Fund were $2.5 million, with total
expenditures of $6.9 million. Funds were being used from the Capital Projects Fund Balance, as
well as $2 million moved from the General Fund. It was projected that $l.l million would
remain in September 2008; much would rely on transfers from the General Fund and care must
be exercised.
Mayor Udine thanked Ms. Hastings and staff for doing an excellent job in preparing this fiscally-
responsible budget. He noted that the City must determine another funding source for major
capital projects in the future.

E. RESOLUTION NO. 2007-105: ANNUAL FIRE RESCUE ASSESSMENT
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, RELATING TO THE
PROVISION OF FIRE RESCUE SERVICES, FACILITIES
AND PROGRAMS IN THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
FLORIDA; REIMPOSING FIRE RESCUE ASSESSMENTS
AGAINST ASSESSED PROPERTY LOCATED WITHIN
THE CITY OF PARKLAND FOR THE FISCAL YEAR
BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2007; APPROVING THE
RATE OF ASSESSMENT; APPROVING THE
ASSESSMENT ROLL; AND PROVIDING AN
EFFECTIVE DATE.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2007-105. She commented
that the fire rescue assessment rate had remained the same.
Mayor Udine recalled that, at the time that the TRIM Notice was sent, there had been discussions
about raising the fire assessment fee by $30 per year, which was believed a reasonable amount.
He noted that the repaving of a section of Holmberg Road would be delayed by one year, which
had allowed the fire fee to remain the same. He was proud of the Commission and staff for this
solution and noted that the City was one of the few cities to keep the assessment at the same rate.
Commissioner Smith noted that another city had raised its fire assessment by $200 per home to
make up for lost tax revenue and that a chart of taxes by city within .the county included the
City's millage as one of the lowest five. He commended the Commission and staff.
Mayor Udine opened to the public.
Robert Mayersohn, 6742 N.W. 80t" Manor, thanked the Commission and staff for their efforts to
keep the fire assessment fees at the same rate. He understood that next year's budget may
contain some shortfalls, and he felt it would be prudent for the Commission and staff to prepare
residents for cost increases if revenues were not found elsewhere.
Mayor Udine agreed. He commented that the City had tried to notify residents of these meetings,
and the property tax issue had been at the forefront of every meeting for the past six months. He
noted that poor attendance had been consistent throughout the process, and he believed it a
shame that interest was not shown.
There were no further comments or questions, and Mayor Udine closed to the public.
MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to approve Resolution No. 2007-105, Annual Fire
Rescue Assessment. Seconded by Commissioner Smith.
