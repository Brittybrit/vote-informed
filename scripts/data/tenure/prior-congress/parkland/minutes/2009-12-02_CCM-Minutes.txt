III PUBLIC INPUT
Mr. Zach Fertal, 6440 NW 98t Lane, and Shane Randall, stated they were hosting a convention
at their High School, Marjory Stoneman Douglas. It is co- hosted by the FASC, Florida
Association of Student Councils. The will be hosting about 700 students at the High School on
April 16, 17 & 18. The requested the use of the Parkland Amphitheatre at Park Trails Park for
their candle lighting service and dance on the Sunday evening.
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young directed Mr. Fertel to contact the Parks & Recreation
Director Mr. Scott Davidof to make sure the Amphitheatre was available on those days and he
would explain the process in order to use the space.

Mr. Brian Archer explained that Parkland held its' second annual Plastic Bag Recycling Contest
on November 21, 2009 in recognition of America Recycles Month. He said that 9,000 bags were
collected and brought to the local Publix for recycling. He announced the winners were from
Heron Heights Elementary, which also hosted the event. Mr. Archer explained the categories and
announced the . winners as follows:
The contest was divided into two categories; 1 r and 3 place individual winners, and 1 2 and
3 place group winners.
1 place individual winner was Kaylin Baer (1,483 bags)
2 place Samantha Bishop (269 bags),
3 place Mariana Lopez (180 bags) all students from HHE.
1 place group winner was Ms. Henthome's HHE class (1,008 bags),
2 place winners were Ms. Taubenkimel's HHE class (952 bags),
3 place winners were Ms. McIntyre's HHE class (796 bags).
