City Attorney Andrew Maurodis stated that a review committee made up of City staff reviewed the
nine proposals submitted and shortlisted the five firms that are before the Commission. He
explained the process by which the Commission would be scoring the firms and determining a final
overall ranking after each firm is given the opportunity to provide a brief introduction.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young called on each firm and introductions were made as follows:

e Bamaco — not present

e Ceres Environmental Services - Matt Sharp, Project Manager

e Crowder Gulf — Eric Paul, Debris Reduction Manager

oD & J Enterprises, Inc. — Jason Sanders

e DRC Emergency Services — Valennie Katz
There was a brief discussion regarding how the rating should be done.
Commissioner Moskowitz said that he put together a spreadsheet with all of the pricing of the firms
and he spoke about DRC’s low price, noting that this is not always a good thing. He also
commented about other issues of concern with some of the other proposals.
Mayor Udine stated that he paid special attention to Robert Madoo’s rankings because he is from
the Public Works Department, which will be particularly involved with debris management.
City Attorney Maurodis suggested that the Commission read their rankings into the record, the
points will then be added up, and the firm with the most points will be the one the Commission
awards the contract to.
The Mayor and Commissioners read their total points given to each of the five firms and the
resulting rankings as follows:

City Attorney Maurodis said the preliminary results indicate that D & J Enterprises received four

first place point totals.

Commissioner Moskowitz commented that the overall total points given to each firm should be

reflected in the record and asked about making the second place firm a secondary or back-up

contractor.

City Attorney Maurodis explained that there was no provision for a secondary contractor, but if the

Commission would like to do this, a special contract with the firm would need to be done, with the

understanding that they are back-up only if the first firm can not provide the contractual services.

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the overall total points in the order of highest to lowest:

2. RESOLUTION NO. 2010-53: EMERGENCY DEBRIS MANAGEMENT
& DISASTER RECOVERY ASSISTANCE
BID AWARD
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE

CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO RANK THE SHORT
LISTED DEBRIS MANAGEMENT FIRMS TO PROVIDE
EMERGENCY DEBRIS MANAGEMENT AND DISASTER
RECOVERY ASSISTANCE IN THE EVENT OF A
NATURAL DISASTER AND TO AUTHORIZE THE CITY
MANAGER TO EXECUTE A CONTRACT WITH THE TOP
RANKED FIRM; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND
PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to approve Resolution No. 2010-53, Emergency

Debris Management & Disaster Recovery Assistance Bid Award, as amended, including authorizing

execution of a contract with the top ranked firm, D & J Enterprises, and with the second ranked

firm, Crowder Gulf, solely as a backup. Seconded by Vice Mayor Smith.

City Attorney Andrew Maurodis advised that the language in the resolution would be changed to

reflect the back-up contract with the number 2 firm.
