3. ORDINANCE NO. 2011-15: PARKLAND GOLF & COUNTRY CLUB
MASTER PLAN AMENDMENT
COMMENTS: FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY

COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA
AMENDING ORDINANCE NUMBER 2010-23 RELATING
TO THE PROJECT KNOWN AS PARKLAND GOLF AND
COUNTRY CLUB; AMENDING THE MASTER PLAN TO
ALLOW AN INCREASE IN DWELLING UNITS AS SET
FORTH IN SAID ORDINANCE FOR THE PROPERTY
GENERALLY LOCATED SOUTH OF COUNTY LINE
ROAD, WEST OF UNIVERSITY DRIVE, AND ADJACENT
TO TRAILS END ON THE NORTH AND SOUTH SIDE;
PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING FOR AN
EFFECTIVE DATE; CASE NUMBER 02-MPA-11.

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Ordinance No. 2011-15: Parkland Golf &

Country Club Master Plan Amendment. Mr. Dennis Mele, on behalf of the applicant, waived the

quasi-judicial procedure.

Ms. Roberta Moore, Planning & Zoning Director gave a brief presentation on the item being

presented. She stated staff is recommending approval.

Mayor Udine expressed that this item has already gone through Planning & Zoning (P&Z) and

already approved. He asked to see Pod 9 in connection with the school and several other points of

interest.

Commissioner Moskowitz spoke about the need to increase the number of units in PGCC in order

to make it a viable community to live in relation to the monthly fees. He explained this is a

different time in that what was planned to be built years ago is not a workable solution today. The

density in PGCC must be increased. He said he will vote in favor of this Ordinance because the

developers need to figure out how to make the community sustainable.

Commissioner Weissman stated he agrees with the economics of Commissioner

Moskowitz’ discussion, but not with the necessity for the increased density. He stated he was going

to vote against this item as it would be an unnecessary strain on the residents. He is not in favor of

increasing 58 units to enrich the developer.

Vice Mayor Rosenof questioned the financial impact of the increased dwelling units. He requested

a more accurate breakdown of the impact fees, taxes, and other factors before second reading. He

said he does not want to kill it, but he does not want to vote for it today.

Further discussion ensued.

7. RESOLUTION NO. 2011-35: MISTY MEADOWS - CONTRACT TO
PURCHASE
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE

CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE
PURCHASE OF CERTAIN PROPERTY LOCATED AT
15040 LOXAHATCHEE ROAD.

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2011-35: Misty Meadows —

Contract to Purchase. She explained staff was able to contact several property owners in the Wedge

Piece. Misty Meadows has 53 acres that is contiguous to the Standard Pacific Parcel. She

explained according to the Comprehensive Plan the City is required to provide park level of service,

After consulting with the Parks & Recreation Director, Scott Davidoff, it was decided to have a

larger park to accommodate the number of children in the area and the additional residents coming

into the Wedge Piece. In addition, due to the housing bust, staff felt it was necessary to purchase

the parcels now since the value of property is much lower. She explained there is a letter of

9. CONSIDERATION: BROWARD COUNTY ORDINANCE ON ETHICS
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young explained that the voters in Broward County decided they
wanted an Ethics Ordinance that would apply to Broward County as well as to local municipalitics.
She explained Broward County adopted their own Ordinance and is working on an Ordinance that
would be applicable to local governments. The League of Cities has taken Broward County’s
Ordinance and has underlined and crossed out language and has asked the municipalities to provide
comments to them and the League of Cities will provide those comments to Broward County.
Further discussion ensued.

VIII COMMENTS BY THE CITY ATTORNEY
City Attorney Maurodis commented about road blocks they are experiencing with respect to
obtaining agreements for the medians on County Line Road and Pine Island Road. Currently, there
is cooperation from the Developer who is willing to place the burden on the Association. However,
due to the roads belonging to the County, the City is not able to progress. The County is trying to
put the ultimate responsibility on the City in the event the HOA does not live up to its maintenance
agreement.
