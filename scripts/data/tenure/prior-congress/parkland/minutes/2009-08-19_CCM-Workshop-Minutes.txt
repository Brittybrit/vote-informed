II PURPOSE:
1. DISCUSSION: 2009 -2010 CITY OF PARKLAND BUDGET
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young gave a brief introduction and noted that the emphasis of the
proposed budget is to reduce expenditures while maintaining a level of service that the residents

are accustomed to. She reviewed the budget highlights and then introduced Interim Finance
Director Nancy Morando who gave a PowerPoint presentation (a copy of which is attached
hereto and incorporated herein as part of these minutes).
Mayor Udine questioned the comment by City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young regarding cuts to
training and travel, and confirmed local training is acceptable but travel for training out of state
has been cut. Mayor Udine spoke about when the Commission met on the fire contract and being
frustrated because of the lack of information from Coral Springs; at that time, the Commission
budgeted the same amount for next year that was paid this year. Mayor Udine said now the
actual numbers have come in and he feels that the Commission should adjust the budget to
include the 2% increase.
Commissioner Moskowitz said that he had just spoken with City Manager Levinson at Coral
Springs and he was told that Coral Springs' numbers have gone up 2.2% and Parkland went up
2.1%. Commissioner Moskowitz stated he feels that with this slight of an increase, the City
should move forward and pay for the services for this coming year.
Vice Mayor Weissman said he is in agreement regarding the 2% increase, but there is a much
larger disparity in what the residents of Coral Springs pay per household than what is being paid
in Parkland. He added that this issue, together with the lack of opportunity to review the
numbers, including the reimbursement Coral Springs gets on fire rescue, is disconcerting to him.
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young noted that staff would adjust the numbers accordingly
pursuant to this discussion.
Mayor Udine commented about the repaving of the temporary parking lot at Pine Trails Park, and
said he feels that area should not be paved and the money for the repaving could be pulled out
and used elsewhere in the budget. Mayor Udine complimented staff for the cost reductions in the
departments under City control.
Commissioner Rosenof agreed with Mayor Udine and thanked staff for their cuts. He spoke
about proposed budget enhancements and said things he would like to see in the budget are:
• Equipment replacement
• Additional sidewalk on the south side of Holmberg Road
• Comcast simulcast or webcast of Commission meetings
• Infrastructure maintenance
• Seed money for a public relations committee and/or public relations in lieu of the
Parkland Pipeline
• Furlough money for the Building Department is fine so long as they stay revenue neutral
• BSO motorcycle shift
Commissioner Rosenof concluded by agreeing that paving a temporary parking lot now is not
needed.

Commissioner Smith agreed with Mayor Udine and Commissioner Rosenof about the temporary
parking lot. He said that he feels more police officers, in particular motorcycle officers, are
needed and he likes the donation to Parkland schools.
Commissioner Moskowitz asked about reserves and said he remembers the decision to put
money in reserves every year. He spoke about Comcast service and Parkland having its own
radio station and webcast, which could be utilized to broadcast Commission meetings as well as
used in emergencies. Commissioner Moskowitz commented about the Parkland Pipeline and
said he would like to keep it because it is widely read by the residents. Regarding discussion of
the fourth police zone, Commissioner Moskowitz stated that although it is said a fourth zone is
needed and the evidence is there based on the population, crime is decreasing and adding a zone
would be very expensive. He suggested perhaps paying overtime to a motorcycle officer from
another area.
Chief O'Connell confirmed that a motor officer sends a very powerful message and is a deterrent
to continued speeding.
Commissioner Moskowitz said maybe a motorcycle officer could be brought in occasionally,
perhaps during holidays when there are high traffic periods or during the beginnings of school,
but he feels a full time officer is not needed right now.
Vice Mayor Weissman questioned whether it would be possible to share a motorcycle officer
with another city. Regarding the public relations proposal by Commissioner Rosenof, Vice
Mayor Weissman said he would be against it if it is used as advertisement for realtors. He added
that he is fully supportive of the Parkland Pipeline and said if the Commission is not willing to
stay at the 4.15 millage rate, then the enhancements need to be fully scrutinized.
Mayor Udine said he feels there is a need for a fourth police zone, but the City can not afford it;
the priority among the proposed enhancements would be funding a motor officer. He stated that
surfacing the temporary parking lot should be taken out and he agrees with not changing the
Building Department furloughs for now. Mayor Udine said that the donation to the Parkland
schools should be tied to FCAT or some kind of school improvement and caution should be
exercised in making donations to public schools. He added that the money set aside for EAB
should continue and he feels it is important to make sure there is no travel in the budget. Mayor
Udine spoke about webcasting and the radio station. He said he has no problem with webcast,
but he does not want to spend significant dollars now because this is not the year to do it. Mayor
Udine reiterated that the park impact fees can only be used for improvement on parks, so that is
the rationale for another artificial field.
Commissioner Moskowitz stated the $400,000 for the sidewalk is grant money and that money
can only be used on a sidewalk. He then made additional comments about the cost of equipment
for, and utilization of, a radio station and webcast, and said he feels that if it is possible to get the
same technology that will simulcast the meetings on the interne and the radio for the same cost,
it makes sense to include it.

Mayor Udine commented on the amount for outside legal services and questioned whether it
needs to be budgeted that high.
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young said part of those fees are to cover the City Attorney's
monthly retainer and dollars were included for the Florida Highway Products case, based on the
figures provided by Stuart Michelson. She then explained the rationale for inclusion of
additional amounts. After further discussion, it was suggested that Florida Highway Products be
put as a separate line item.
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young stated that this is a bare bones budget and if there are any
changes made during the year, funds would have to come out of reserves.
Mayor Udine spoke about reserves, next year's property values, home sales in Parkland, and
increased revenues coming into the City in the future. He noted that Family Central and Area
Agency on Aging asked for a minimal amount and he is glad to see it in the budget.
Commissioner Moskowitz asked Chief O'Connell about bike patrol in Parkland's parks or
commercial facilities and Chief O'Connell said its visibility is effective, but mobility is a
problem. Chief O'Connell noted that it is designed to patrol in a very small geographic area.
Commissioner Moskowitz added that he would like some visibility of the mounted unit in
Parkland.
Mayor Udine asked if the budget funds a School Resource Officer in all schools and the school
crossing guards for the entire year and City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young responded in the
affirmative.
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young confirmed that the amount for the additional sidewalk on
the south side of Holmberg Road is approximately $118,000
Mayor Udine opened to the public.
Stuart Krantz commented on the following topics:
• The 96% satisfaction rate from the citizen survey
• Budgeting for pulling out Brazilian Pepper
• Paving Pine Tree roads
• Budgeting for berm issue on Pine Island between Park Trails and Pine Tree Trails
• Expenditures for the library
• $4,000 for Russ Klenet
• A 20 -year plan for pulling out invasives
There were no further comments or questions and the Mayor closed to the public.
