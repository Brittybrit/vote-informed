 II PURPOSE:
 1. CONSIDERATION: SETTLEMENT OF FABERMAN
 LAWSUIT
 City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young said this meeting is in reference to a potential settlement
 offer from Mr. Faberman and introduced Kevin Markow, Becker & Poliakoff, P.A., to present
 the offer.
 Mr. Markow said he feels the offer is a good settlement and commented as follows:
 • The draft settlement agreement has been principally agreed to bythe Faberman entities.
 g p p Y g
 • The proposed settlement is a great result for the City in that it achieves the primary
 objective in filing the litigation, which was to bring uniformity and consistency to the
 Ranches in connection with the lawn maintenance business.

 • The agreement provides that lawn maintenance activities are going to cease no later than
 60 days after the settlement agreement is signed.
 • The primary method through which compliance will be achieved is through the sale of the
 property, but the closing is not a contingency of the settlement agreement.
 • The City will be able to recoup $6,500 of its costs in enforcing the code in the Ranches,
 which is a good result particularly in light of the defenses and the counterclaims which
 have been raised.
 Commissioner Moskowitz asked about obtaining a release and Mr. Markow responded that there
 is release language which was added to the agreement and approved by the Fabermans' attorney,
 but a separate document in the form of an acceptable mutual general release could be attached to
 the agreement as well.
 Vice Mayor Weissman asked about prevailing party attorney's fees if this matter should go
 further in litigation. Mr. Markow stated that in the event of non-compliance with the agreement,
 there is a prevailing party attorney's fee provision; if there are any further costs of enforcement,
 whether from achieving compliance or recouping the $6500, the City will have a right to recover
 attorney's fees.
 Mr. Markow added that the City is obligated to provide an estoppel letter and release the liens
 prior to the closing so clear title of the property can be conveyed.
 Commissioner Rosenof stated he will not vote for this because he feels if it really was a willful
 act, he has a hard time settling for such a small amount, and if it was not willful, he has a hard
 time dealing with the fact that Mr. Faberman has not expressed his position in a public forum.
 MOTION made by Vice Mayor Weissman to approve the settlement agreement between the
 City and the Fabermans subject to a general release being executed. Seconded by Commissioner
 Moskowitz.
 Commissioner Moskowitz said he feels the City is saving money by avoiding a lawsuit.
 Mayor Udine commented that if this went to full blown litigation, it would cost the City
 hundreds of thousands of dollars.
 Commissioner Smith said he agrees with both sides of the Commission's discussion.
 ROLL CALL VOTE:
 Vice Mayor Weissman Yes
 Commissioner Smith Yes
 Commissioner Moskowitz Yes
 Commissioner Rosenof No
 Mayor Udine Yes
