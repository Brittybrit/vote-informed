1. CONSIDERATION: DOUGLAS HIGH SCHOOL LACROSSE
FIELD USAGE
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young informed the City Commission that at the last Parks and Recreation
Board Meeting, the issue came up about the use of the city’s facility by Douglas High School Lacrosse
team for practice and games. She explained that she would like to proceed with allowing usage of the
fields for practice which will meet their immediate need and in January, staff will get language from the
Parks & Recreation Board to finalize usage of the fields for their games.
Mayor Udine commented that he thinks this is a win-win situation. He explained that although he is
supportive of the usage taking place at the City’s field, it really should be held at the high school.
Commissioner Moskowitz expressed that there has been a continual problem between the principal of
Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School and the City of Parkland for numerous reasons. He explained that
it is the right thing to do for the kids, but it is bad policy for the City.
Mayor expressed that he does not want the kids to get caught in the middle of this situation. He feels the
decision will benefit the kids in the long run.
Commissioner Moskowitz expressed that he will support the use now and in the future.
There was unanimous consent amongst the City Commission allowing the City’s fields to be used for
Lacrosse practice.
