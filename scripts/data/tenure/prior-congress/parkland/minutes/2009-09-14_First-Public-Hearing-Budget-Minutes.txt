II PURPOSE: 2009 -2010 BUDGET
1. ORDINANCE NO. 2009 -40: ADOPTING MILLAGE
COMMENTS: FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY
COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE MILLAGE RATE TO BE
LEVIED FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING
OCTOBER 1, 2009 AND ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 2010
AND STATING THE PERCENTAGE INCREASE IN
PROPERTY TAXES AS SUCH INCREASE IS DEFINED
IN FLORIDA STATUTES, SECTION 200.065, AND
HOUSE BILL 1B, PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY,
AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young read the title of Ordinance No. 2009 -40, Adopting Millage,
and explained the millage. She noted that approximately 63% of the City's revenue comes from
ad valorem taxes. City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young said that back in July, the Commission
decided to use the rollback rate to submit to the property appraiser for the TRIM notice due to the
forecast loss in revenue as a result of the decrease in housing assessed values. She stated that
since then, staff received additional information and now recommends a millage rate of 4.0198,
which is less than the rollback rate.
Mayor Udine said that when the Commission sent in the rollback rate for the TRIM notice, they
wanted to ensure they had the maximum flexibility at budget time. He added that he is seeing
most cities are going back to the rollback rate or have increased their rate because of the
reduction in property values. Mayor Udine noted that Parkland has lowered the millage for the
past three years rather than increasing the rate as property values were rising. He said that the
problem now is the valuation of properties, which has resulted in decreased revenue and the City
needs sufficient dollars to provide services the residents demand.
City Attorney Andrew Maurodis made the following statement: the present Ordinance before the
Commission states the millage rate to be levied in order to produce sufficient ad valorem taxation
revenue, which when combined with other projected funds, will be adequate to pay the
appropriations made in the budget for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2009 and ending
September 30, 2010, and shall be an operating millage rate of 4.0198; the Ordinance also states
for the record the percentage decrease in property taxes by which the millage rate to be levied is
less than the rollback rate of 4.1532 and it is computed according to the guidelines set forth in
Florida Statutes 200.065.
Vice Mayor Weissman thanked staff for working so diligently because it was not easy this year.
MOTION made by Vice Mayor Weissman to approve the millage rate at 4.0198, which is a
reduction from the TRIM notice rate of 4.1532.

3. RESOLUTION NO. 2009-61: FIRE RESCUE ASSESSMENT
COMMENTS:
A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, RELATING TO THE
PROVISION OF FIRE RESCUE SERVICES, FACILITIES
AND PROGRAMS IN THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
FLORIDA; REIMPOSING FIRE RESCUE ASSESSMENTS
AGAINST ASSESSED PROPERTY LOCATED WITHIN
THE CITY OF PARKLAND FOR THE FISCAL YEAR
BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2009; APPROVING THE
RATE OF ASSESSMENT; APPROVING THE
ASSESSMENT ROLL; AND PROVIDING AN
EFFECTIVE DATE.
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young read the title of Ordinance No. 2009 -61, Fire Rescue
Assessment, and introduced Interim Finance Director Nancy Morando, who gave a brief
presentation.
Mayor Udine explained that the City hires a company to assess the cost of fire services and the
amount that could be charged for the fire rescue assessment. He noted that the $151 fee has
remained since 2004 and is well below what could be charged. Mayor Udine said the City now
has two fire stations and a professional fire department, and staff is recommending an increase in
the fire assessment fee for a rate of $195.00.
Vice Mayor Weissman stated that he spoke with City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young and he
believes a $161 fire fee could work utilizing revenue from the new millage rate and further
reductions in some of the contingency items.
Interim Finance Director Morando clarified that this would result in an approximate $244,000
reduction in fire assessment fees, and with the $60,000 gain that City Manager Caryn Gardner-
Young spoke about, an overall reduction of approximately $180,000.
There was discussion about which contingency item would be sacrificed to make up the decrease
in the fire assessment fee.

City Attorney Andrew Maurodis suggested that before the public hearing is opened for this item,
the Commission amend the tentative budget so that at the Second Budget Hearing, City Manager
Caryn Gardner -Young can present the tentative budget in line with the fire assessment.

elimination of the ambulance.
MOTION made by Vice Mayor Weissman to reconsider the budget. Seconded by
Commissioner Smith.
