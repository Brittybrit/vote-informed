MOTION passed unanimously.
III PUBLIC INPUT
Mr. Arthur Franke, 6511 Arlington Lane in Cypress Head, addressed the Commission regarding
the Cypress Head front entry project. He felt the project was not meeting the plan specification
and what had been approved by the City Commission. He stated he hoped the Commission
would instruct staff to reevaluate the project.
Mr. Bob Etingoff, 5877 NW 73` Court, President of Parkwood 9 Home Owner's Association
and Terramar Masters Association, requested the Commission's assistance with an issue
regarding two restaurants located in the Riverstone Shoppes. Mr. Etingoff noted problems with
garbage stacked in the alleyway, restaurant workers loitering and playing football in the alleyway.
Police had been called to investigate and had advised the noise ordinance was in effect after
11:00 PM. He said they has an agreement with the developer would be a good neighbor and not
allow these types of issues.
Mayor Udine requested this information to be given to the code enforcement officer to ensure
they are complying with the code and developer orders.
Mr. Steve Carney, 7322 NW 58 Way, also commented he had similar concerns with the above
issue and noted there was loud use of foul language as well coming from workers in the
alleyway.
There were no further comments or questions, and Mayor Udine closed to the public.

1. UPDATE: ELEMENTARY SCHOOL Z
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young introduced Planning & Zoning Director, Roberta Moore to
give an update regarding Elementary School Z.
Ms. Moore advised Mr. Sam Doggart, Senior Vice President of Skanska was present to give a
short power point presentation.
Mr. Doggart introduced part of the project team as Ricardo Santana Project Executive, Jose
Capelin Project Manager, Dana Nelson Project Engineer and Gianet Giangreco School Board
Representative.
Mr. Doggart reviewed highlights from the presentation such as photos reflecting project's
progress, noted Skanska's desire to make this safest project to date, be good neighbor
philosophy, green elements, documented recycling, maintain dust control, noise abatement and
project safety training.
Commissioner Rosenof questioned if the school would be a LEED certified building.

Mr. Doggart responded that it has green elements but will not be a fully certified LEED building.
At the questioning of Mayor Udine, Mr. Doggart advised the goal was to have the Certificate of
Occupancy by mid to end of July 2009.
Ms. Moore advised they would like to come back in three months to provide the City
Commission with another update.
Mr. Doggart spoke about the Skanska newsletter they create quarterly to give residents updates
on what is going on in their community.
Mayor Udine and City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young agreed to assist distributing the
newsletters via City Hall, the City' web site and the City's electronic newsletter system.
Vice Mayor Weissman questioned when and where would the incoming kindergarteners for
Elementary School Z register for the upcoming 2009 -2010 school year.
Gianet Giangreco, Construction Project Manager for the Broward County School Board advised
she will need to request this information and respond back to the City as soon as possible.
Ms. Gardner -Young advised the City would make this information known via the City's web site
and email blast as soon as it is received.
2. UPDATE: PINE ISLAND & TRAILS END TRAFFIC LIGHT
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young gave a brief introduction and invited Engineering Project
Manager Horacio Danovich to give an update.
Mr. Danovich invited WCI representative Maurice Berry to present a progress report on the
project.
Mr. Berry advised the plans were approved by Broward County on December 1 2008 and due
to other issues he expected work to begin in approximately 2 months.
Mayor Udine questioned if this project had been approved by the bank under WCI's bankruptcy
status.
Mr. Berry responded that this project had internal approval and the project did not require the
bank's approval in this case.
Mr. Berry clarified the intent was to have the traffic light operational by the new school year
opening August 24 2009.
