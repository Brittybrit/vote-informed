1. BUDGET
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young asked if there were any objections to starting the Budget
Workshop at 6:00 p.m. on August 17, 2011. Mayor Udine deferred to the other members of the City
Commission and there was consensus to start the Budget Workshop at 6:00 p.m.
1A. BUDGET PRESENTATION
Nancy Morando, Finance Director gave a brief overview of the proposed budget. See presentation
attached.
Commissioner Weissman asked for an explanation of the Pine Tree roadways improvements. Ms.
Morando explained that this project is a $7 - $10 million undertaking and each road will be assessed
separately provided they can obtain a certain percentage of property owners to approve the
assessment. :
Vice Mayor Rosenof clarified that the $10 million is not coming out of the City’s funds.
Commissioner Weissman clarified that it is an assessment and has no fiscal impact. He also stated
that it will be a total cost recovery and any costs associated with obtaining a loan will be built into
the repayment plan.
Vice Mayor Rosenof verified that the sunshades are being replaced at Terramar Park and the new
sunshades were for Pine Trails Park and is within the same budget. He also asked if any money was
being put away for the air conditioner system in City Hall. City Manager Gardner-Young stated that
funds are in reserves to cover expenditures for the air conditioner unit at City Hall.
Mayor Udine commented that up until 2004, the City of Parkland never had a reserve fund. He
explained that the former City Manager felt it was important to have reserves to cover expenditures
from hurricanes due to the time it took to get reimbursement from FEMA.

VIIT COMMENTS BY THE CITY MANAGER
+ She received a phone call from the owners of the parcel with the antennas in the Wedge
piece, Beasley Communication. They indicated their willingness to come into the City under
the condition that the City enter into an annexation agreement and waive the engineering
inspections requirements. She asked for direction from the City Commission.
Commissioner Weissman stated he has no objection to waiving the engineering fees. He stated there
was also a discussion with regard to inspection fees. He stated Beasley Communication had an
objection to paying $1,000 per tower, per antenna. Commissioner Weissman stated that it is his
understanding that Beasley Communication will save $600 in taxes by annexing into Parkland. He
proposes that the City enter into an annexation agreement with them and cap the fees at $500.
Beasley will sce a savings of $100 from what they are currently paying and they would be annexed in
the City.
City Manager Gardner-Young explained that the City of Parkland is the only city in Broward County
including Broward County, the FAA, and the FCC that requires an engineering report on the safety
of the tower and inspection.
Mayor Udine stated rather than do the $100 savings that Commissioner Weissman mentioned, he
stated he would like to inter into an annexation agreement with Beasley Communication putting
them in the same position monetarily as they are currently in. He would like to see this parcel come
into the city like the other parcels. He stated that if it is less than a few thousand dollars, he would
like staff to figure out a way to make it work.
¢ She introduced Mr. Esmond Scott as the new Public Works Director and he took a few
minutes to address the City Commission.
