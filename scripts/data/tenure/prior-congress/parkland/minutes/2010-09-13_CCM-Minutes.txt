1. RESOLUTION NO. 2010-70: HERON BAY PLAT BUFFER
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, FOR SITE PLAN
AMENDMENT APPROVAL FOR THE LANDSCAPE
BUFFER ALONG THE BUFFERS OF Nw 122"°
AVENUE IN HERON BAY NORTH PLAT 3; THE EAST
BUFFER ALONG SAWGRASS BAY ADJACENT TO
THE LAKE PARCEL B-10; ALL CUL-DE-SACS IN
SAWGRASS BAY AND SOMERSET
NEIGHBORHOODS; THE BUFFER ALONG THE
NORTH SIDE OF THE HERON BAY BOULEVARD
NORTH ROUNDABOUT AND THE ROUNDABOUT AT
THE TERMINUS OF NW 122° AVENUE; CASE
NUMBER 05-SPA-10.
ACTION: MOTION ON THE DECISION OF THE COMMISSION

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2010-70: Heron Bay Plat

Buffer and introduced Planning & Zoning Director, Roberta Moore who informed the City

Commission that this was a quasi judicial hearing and asked the applicant, Mr. Scott Davidson to

come forward.

Mr. Scott Davidson representing WCI Communities waived the quasi judicial procedures.

Roberta Moore, Planning & Zoning Director gave a brief explanation of the item being presented

and asked if the City Commission would like to hear a presentation from the applicant.

Commissioner Moskowitz asked for a global strategy for Heron Bay and Parkland Golf and

Country Club from WCI.

Scott Davidson, Davidson Design Studios and Agent for WCI Communities informed the

Commission that there are two signage applications that have been submitted for Sawgrass Bay

and Somerset. A Community Appearance Board (CAB) application will be submitted for both

Sawgrass Bay and Somerset in the next few weeks.

Tim Oak, Senior Vice President of Florida Homebuilding and Tower for WCI explained that they

intend to go forward with building 75+ single family homes in Sawgrass and 125+ at Somerset.

Currently, the Parkland Golf and Country Club (PGCC) Community is under contract and

because he is under a confidentiality agreement, he cannot discuss anything further. Mr. Oak

confirmed that WCI’s intention is to sell Parkland Golf and Country Club.

Zs RESOLUTION NO. 2010-71: ~~ BISHOP VARIANCE
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, GRANTING A
VARIANCE APPLICATION FOR PROPERTY LOCATED
AT 7286 NW 127™ WAY (CASE NO. 01-V-10).
ACTION: MOTION ON THE DECISION OF THE COMMISSION

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2010-71: Bishop Variance.

Mayor Udine disclosed that he lives in one of the 54 homes in the Colony. He met with them

and have visited the location and wanted to clarify if there would be a conflict that would prevent

him from voting. Andrew Maurodis, City Attorney advised that Mayor Udine could vote and

there was no conflict.

Planning & Zoning Director, Roberta Moore informed the City Commission that this is a quasi

judicial hearing. Mr. Bishop questioned the quasi judicial definition and process.

Andrew Maurodis, City Attorney provided a definition of quasi judicial and explained the

procedures.

After receiving the explanation, Mr. Bishop waived his quasi judicial rights.
Ms. Roberta Moore proceeded with a brief explanation about the Bishop Variance being
presented. She informed the City Commission that based on the required standards, staff is
recommending denial of the application.
Commissioner Moskowitz questioned what had not been done by the applicant that would make
staff deny the application.
Ms. Moore replied that the Bishops have not been able to prove a hardship for not being able to
meet the requirements for the setback.
Mayor Udine stated for the record that he spoke to the property owner directly across the lake
from subject site on the cul-de-sac and that neighbor did not have a problem with the work to be
done. Mayor Udine expressed that the work being done is actually an improvement and not an
eyesore and no one can use the property otherwise.
Mayor Udine said he was going to vote in favor of the variance. He has spoken to many of the
neighbors and no one had a problem with the work to be done.
Commissioner Moskowitz questioned if the two homeowners on each side objected.
Ms. Moore clarified that staff sent out notifications for the Planning & Zoning Board and
tonight’s Commission meeting. Staff did not receive any phone calls for or against the
application.
Mayor Udine clarified that the neighbor next door sent a letter of support.
Jay Bishop, 7286 NW 127" Way, (property owner) explained that the home to the left gave a
letter of approval. The neighbor to the right are rarely at their property and there is a 25” Ficus
along the side of the property and could not be seen at all. Mayor confirmed that the property
owner directly behind the subject property called and he has no problem with it either.
MOTION made by Vice Mayor Smith to approve Resolution No. 2010-71 as amended with all
the staff conditions for approval. The gavel was passed to Deputy Vice Mayor David Rosenof.
Seconded by Mayor Udine.

Vice Mayor Smith

Mayor Udine
MOTION passed 3-1.

Further discussion ensued regarding the number of votes required for a variance. City Attorney
Andrew Maurodis took a brief moment to check the Code of Ordinance.
Commissioner David Rosenof commented that he had a problem with granting the variance in
theory only. He feels the variance procedure was put in place for a reason and aesthetics was not
a valid reason to ask for a variance. He feels a financial hardship or constructability issue would
be appropriate for a variance.
It was determined by City Attorney Andrew Maurodis after reviewing the Code of Ordinance that
a super majority vote is required, therefore the motion passed.
