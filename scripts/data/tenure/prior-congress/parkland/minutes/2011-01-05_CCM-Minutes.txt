3. RESOLUTION NO. 2010-103: SPORTS POLICY AMENDMENTS
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AUTHORIZE THE
SIXTH AMENDMENT TO THE APPROVED SPORTS
POLICY TO ESTABLISH SPORTS LEAGUE RULES AND
REGULATIONS FOR USE OF CITY PARK FACILITIES;
PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2010-103: Sports Policy
Amendments. She then turned it over to Scott Davidoff, Parks and Recreation Director. Mr.
Davidoff gave a brief explanation of the item being presented.
Commissioner Weissman clarified with Mr. Davidoff that the recommendation is to approve the
amendment with the restricted use of the synthetic turf fields only. He further clarified that the
Parks & Recreation Department would be able to administer and maintain the current cost levels
within the current budgetary guidelines.

Commissioner Moskowitz clarified staff’s recommendation that users would pay a non-resident
sports fee and a usage fee for the turf field. He raised concerns that other clubs would come to
the City for the same usage and where would it end.
Mayor Udine expressed that there will be a meeting on January 6, 2011 with School Board
Superintendent Notter that will address these and other concerns. He further commented that the
Lacrosse team should be playing and practicing at Douglas High School. He feels the
unfortunate problem is that the kids are being placed in the middle of a bureaucratic situation.
Mayor Udine agrees with Commissioner Moskowitz that there needs to be a policy and amending
the sports policy is not the right way to do this.
Mayor Udine asked if the Commission could vote on the Sports Policy with an exception.
Commissioner Weissman felt an exception for one club would only make them have to hear it
again when some other club wants the same thing. He stated he would rather leave it to staff to
determine usage based on availability.
Mayor Udine suggested that staff get rid of the non-resident card and allow them to pay the fees
for the field space so the policy does not have to be changed.
Mr. Davidoff stated that the Lacrosse Team would receive a benefit that the other kids do not
receive.
Commissioner Moskowitz stated since there are only 3 or 4 games, the Commission can make
the exception, give the special events permit and if it becomes an issue later, they would look at
the policy as a whole. The bottom line is that everyone wants the kids to play.
Vice-Mayor Rosenof had comments about non-resident sports cards fee and the actual dollar
amount for the field usage fee.
Further discussion ensued.
MOTION made by Commissioner Moskowitz to approve Resolution No. 2010-103: Sports
Policy Amendments. Seconded by Commissioner Weissman.

Ed Deutch, expressed that he thinks the non-resident fees are ridiculous.

4. ORDINANCE NO. 2011-02: LOBBYIST REGISTRATION
COMMENTS: FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY

COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
FLORIDA, CREATING NEW CHAPTER 2.5 OF THE
CITY CODE RELATING TO LOBBYIST
REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS; CONTAINING
PROVISIONS FOR IMPLEMENTATION; PROVIDING
FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE; PROVIDING A
SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE
DATE.

City Manager, Caryn Gardner-Young read the first reading of Ordinance No. 2011-02: Lobbyist

Registration. Ms. Gardner-Young gave a brief explanation of the item presented.

Ms. Gardner-Young stated this was a request from Commissioner Weissman and it was drafted

by City Attorney Maurodis.

Commissioner Weissman explained this was patterned after Broward County which requires

lobbyist to pay a $150 registration fee.

Mayor Udine questioned if this was taken from the City of Deerfield Beach or Broward County.

City Manager Gardner-Y oung replied she was not sure which entity was used.

Commissioner Weissman stated that he asked City Attorney Maurodis to look at Broward

County so that if something is enacted for the cities that it is likely to be something they are

living under themselves and it won’t have to be amended. If it does have to be amended, the City

of Parkland will have something in place and will also start to generate some fees.

Mayor Udine explained that the problem he has with this item being presented is that Broward

County is going to be passing a new ordinance that codifies what was voted on and it is not what

is presented before them today. Another problem he has with the ordinance is the definition of a

lobbyist. He proceeded to read the definition listed in the Ordinance and determined that all the

parents would have to be registered as a lobbyist.

Commissioner Moskowitz stated he is more than happy to vote on this item on First Reading

however he agrees with the Mayor's comments that the City’s policy needs to the same as the

County’s.

Mayor Udine stated he is willing to pass it on first reading and asked if it can be brought back on

second reading after it is enacted by Broward County.

MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to approve Ordinance No. 2011-02 with the

comments that it be passed on first reading. Second reading will be delayed until further review

ITEMS ADDED TO THE AGENDA:

1. Discussion of the City of Parkland partnering with businesses to increase revenue.
Commissioner Weissman gave a handout to each of the Commissioners. He gave a brief
recount of past partnerships i.e. the Farmer's Market and the Concerts where the City has
received funding in one form or another from business advertising. He reminded his
colleagues about the approval of the carwash at the Chevron station where the City would
receive revenues to be given to the Parks & Recreation Department.

The carwash owner is asking the City to place a coupon advertisement on the City’s website
or other media communication such as the Pipeline that would give a $2.00 carwash discount
to the residents and each time a coupon is redeemed, the owner would contribute $2.00 to the
Parks & Recreation Department.

Commissioner Weissman feels this is a way for businesses within the City’s boundaries to
partner with the City. Commissioner Weissman asked for feedback from the other
Commissioners.

Commissioner Moskowitz added that he has no problem with advertising through the blast
emails to residents and also in the Pipeline, but he thinks the City’s website is inappropriate.
He asked how the City would track the $2.00 donation to the Parks Department.
Commissioner Weissman replied that it would be a good faith arrangement.

Vice Mayor Rosenof felt that it is a good idea, but it needs to be thought through a little
further.
