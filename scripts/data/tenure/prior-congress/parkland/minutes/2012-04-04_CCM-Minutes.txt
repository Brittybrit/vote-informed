2. RESOLUTION NO. 2012-26: DEBUYS PLAT APPROVAL
COMMENTS: A 'RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
PARKLAND, FLORIDA FOR APPROVAL OF THE DEBUYS PLAT;
APPROXIMATELY 368.999 NET ACRES GENERALLY LOCATED
AT THE NORTHEAST CORNER OF FUTURE NOB HILL ROAD
AND COUNTY LINE ROAD, ALSO KNOWN AS CASE NUMBER
06-P-11.(quasi-judicial)
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2012-26: Debuys Plat Approval. Ms,
Roberta Moore, Planning and Zoning Director explained that this was quasi-judicial and asked the applicant if
they wished to waive the quasi-judicial hearing. Mr. Dennis Mele on behalf of the applicant waived the quasi-
judicial procedure. Ms. Moore explained that previously the City Commission approved the rezoning master
plan and site plan for the project. She explained that the plat mirrors what the master plan and rezoning
approved which is 526 single family homes, 250 multi-family, and 110,000 square feet of commercial and
park uses. She explained that the Planning & Zoning Board is recommending approval as well as staff and
that all documentation be included as part of the record.
Mayor Udine opened to the public.
Mr. Dennis Mele, Applicant Representative pointed out a technical issue on condition #32. He explained that
at a previous meeting in January the City Commission asked that a provision be added indicating that
maintenance would be done by the commercial property owner. He indicated that the provision was added,
but the provision was not reconciled with other conditions within the resolution. Mr. Mele asked that
language be added to provision #32 making it clear that the responsibility of the maintenance provision
applies to commercial property and if the commercial property owner does not maintain it, their building
permits would be withheld. He stated that the building permits would not be withheld from residential as long
as they are in compliance with the development order.
Commissioner Moskowitz disclosed that he and Mr. Mele spoke regarding the commercial property
maintenance provision and all lobbying forms were filed accordingly.
Discussion ensued.
Commissioner Moskowitz asked for details about the buffer around the City. Mr. Craig Unger of Unger
Engineering explained that this came to them during the site plan approval process and as approved it shows a
4 ft. high buffer with 4 to 1 slopes. Mr. Brian Archer, Public Works Director nodded in the affirmative
agreeing with Mr. Unger’s remarks.
Commissioner Rosenof had concerns about the vacant property item. He asked what the current ordinance is
regarding undeveloped land.
Ms. Gardner-Young explained that if the property owner has not done any development on the site, they are
entitled to leave it in its natural state.
Commissioner Rosenof stated it was his understanding that the City Commission agreed that they wanted
something fore pristine than its natural state. He feels the City does not have enforcement rights to make the
owner maintain the property.
Dennis Mele referred to condition #32 on page 6 of 7 in the back-up material, explaining that language states
that the property owner is bound to maintain the property. He merely wants a statement added that if the
