replacement.

been with BSO since 1988, and hopes he will be able to meet the same level of service provided
by Lt. Rosende.

1. PRESENTATION: AUDIT
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young introduced Finance Director Nancy Morando, who noted
that the Comprehensive Annual Financial Report being presented is for the fiscal year ending
September 30, 2009. She introduced Ron Bennett, Nowlen, Holt & Miner, P.A., City auditor,
who stated that the auditor’s opinion rendered on the financial statements was a clean opinion,
that is, an unqualified opinion, and he then spoke about changes in the City’s revenues,
expenditures, and ad valorem taxes.
Mayor Udine said that regarding expenditures, the Commission has tried to reduce the amount of
money spent over the last several years to deal with the current economic environment, which is
something the residents are asking them to do. He noted that the survey results show the top two
issues are taxes and schools, and he feels taxes are a main issue insofar as residents want to see

their taxes go down and by implication, they want to see less government spending. He added

that the Commission has been holding steady with the ad valorem taxes and believes they are

being very responsive to the residents and to the economic situation that exists.

Mayor Udine asked Mr. Bennett if the audit revealed any problems and Mr. Bennett responded

that there were only a couple prior period adjustments needing to be made. He spoke about

comments in the management letter and explained the two types of auditing applicable to

governmental entities.

Commissioner Rosenof questioned the significant decrease in interest income from 2008 to 2009

and Mr. Bennett said this was attributable to the severe drop in interest rates.

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young commented that in addition to falling interest rates, this

time period coincided with the sub-prime mortgage situation and with the money the City had in

the state fund.
