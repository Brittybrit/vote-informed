I PURPOSE:

1. DISCUSSION: PARKLAND GOLF AND COUNTRY CLUB
Ms. Gardner-Young noted that some residents had previously expressed concerns about
ongoing construction at Parkland Golf and Country Club. Ms. Gardner-Young said that Ms.
Mellgren, the City’s planning consultant, would provide a brief update this evening.
Ms. Mellgren made staff's PowerPoint presentation, a copy of which is attached hereto.
Mayor Udine inquired whether Toll Brothers was implementing designs and other details
which were consistent with City of Parkland ordinances and other requirements. Ms. Mellgren

said that Toll Brothers was presently in compliance with all City development-related
requirements at the Parkland Golf and Country Club.

Mayor Udine said that most of the people he had spoken to were very happy with the home
products being constructed and sold at Parkland Golf and Country Club. Noting that the City
Commission did not traditionally enter public comments at workshop meetings, Mayor Udine
inquired whether there were any members of the public who wished to comment. No members
of the public indicated an interest in addressing the subject matter of ongoing development at
Parkland Golf and Country Club.

Mayor Udine said the City Manager had inquired whether or not to send letters of notification
about this evening’s workshop meeting. Mayor Udine stated that the City of Parkland could
not get into the practice of piecemeal notifications outside of those required by State statute or
City of Parkland regulations.

Commissioner Willis said he had received an email eatlier today from Valerie Verger about
guards on windows of some of the newer homes. Building Official Steve Pizzillo explained
that certain window heights and specifications triggered Code requirements for the installation
of window guards and was related to life-safety matters.

Vice Mayor Weissman inquired whether sales in Parkland Golf and Country Club were
meeting or exceeding Toll Brothers projections for this community. Bill Schmidt, representing
Toli Brothers, said his firm was pleased with sales in Parkland Golf and Country Club to date.
Mayor Udine said residents of Parkland Golf and Country Club were telling him that they
loved the club and it was a thriving residential community.
