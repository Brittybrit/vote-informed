II PURPOSE: RE-RANKING OF WESTERN FIRE STATION BID 
 
 1. RESOLUTION NO. 2012-77: WESTERN FIRE STATION – BID AWARD 
 
 COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE 
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO APPROVE THE 
RANKING OF THE TOP FIVE PROFESSIONAL 
SERVICES FIRMS TO PROVIDE DESIGN AND 
CONSTRUCTION ADMINISTRATION SERVICES 
RELATED TO THE WESTERN FIRE STATION AND TO 
AUTHORIZE THE CITY MANAGER TO EXECUTE A 
CONTRACT WITH THE TOP RANKED FIRM; 
PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING 
FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE. 
 
City Attorney Maurodis gave a brief explanation of how the ranking process would work.

He explained that only the top two proposers will be presenting, however all top five firms will 
be ranked by the City Commission. He also explained that the same criteria must be used that is 
in the RFQ which is the assignment of points in the various categories. 
 
He asked that the City Commission approve the policy. 
 
MOTION made by Commissioner Moskowitz to adopt as the City’s official policy, that the 
st 
company which receives three (3) 1 place votes from the City Commission automatically 
becomes the top ranked firm. 
 
Michelle Klimkaun, Attorney with Becker and Poliakoff stated that they respectfully disagree 
with the process that the Parkland City Commission is taking. Although it is permissible under 
the City’s Code of Ordinance and there has not been an official opinion that this type of 
procedure violates the Broward County ethics code, she feels it goes against the spirit of what the 
ethics code allows which is keeping politics out of the selection process. 
 
Further discussion ensued. 
 
Mr. Chris Zimmerman, CPZ Architects gave a brief presentation of their experience and 
qualifications. See PowerPoint presentation. He also commented that he distributed three (3) 
letters of recommendations from three (3) fire chiefs who think they did a good job in designing 
fire stations. 
 
Commissioner John Willis asked how many fire stations had they designed; Was any work done 
in Parkland; How long has Mr. Zimmerman been in business and how long has he worked as an 
architect? 
 
Mr. Zimmerman replied that they have designed ten (10) fire stations all in Broward County. 
They did the most recent fire station three (3) years ago in Coral Springs - Fire Station #80. He 
has been in business for ten (10) years and an architect for 20 years; and he has never done any 
work in Parkland. 
 
Commissioner Moskowitz agreed that station #80 is fantastic and looks great. 
 
Mayor asked if there had been any litigation against the company. 
 
Mr. Zimmerman replied, no and stated that CPZ has never been threatened with litigation. 
 
Ms. Cindy Baldwin, Principal with ACAI Architects gave a ten (10) minute presentation. She 
spoke about the fire station on Parkside Drive that was built in 2007 in Parkland. She 
highlighted some of the features and technology used that helped to bring the project in under 
budget. 
 
Discussion ensued about the ranking process. 
 
Comments from the City Commission:

II PURPOSE: 
 
 1. RESOLUTION NO. 2012-90: SALTA PRE-ANNEXATION AGREEMENT 
 
 COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE 
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING AN 
ANNEXATION AGREEMENT WITH SALTA PROPERTIES 
RELATING TO PROPERTY BEING ANNEXED INTO THE 
CITY OF PARKLAND. (tabled from 9/12/2012) 
 
 
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2012-90: Salta Pre-Annexation 
Agreement. 
 
City Attorney Maurodis explained that the agreement provides for 1) a different schedule for the 
payment of the park impact fees which is set forth on page 8; and 2) it provides for a specific donation 
of $30,000 for an emergency education fund prior to the issuance of the first building permit for the 
land. 
 
Mayor Udine commented that they received a spreadsheet that this applicant made the required 
concessions as the previous applicants have done on pre-annexation agreements. He stated that he 
does not think they should be calling the $30,000 an emergency education fund. He thinks it should 
simply be called and “Education Fund.” 
 
Vice Mayor Weissman read into the record what Salta Properties is committing to: 
 51.36 acres 
 105 dwelling units 
 $303,625 in Park impact fees 
 $647,000 in school impact fees 
 $2,900 for Lox Road Guardrail 
 $6,800 for street lighting 
 4,500 for landscaping 
 $30,000 
 
Vice Mayor Weissman thanked the applicant for these concessions. 
 
Dennis Mele, Applicant Representative for Salta Properties commented that he had a conversation 
with Commissioner Weissman after the last meeting and at the request of Commissioner Weissman, 
they added the payment for the education fund. 
 
MOTION made by Vice Mayor Weissman to approve Resolution No. 2012-90: Salta Pre-Annexation 
Agreement. Seconded by Commissioner Willis. 
 
Mayor Udine opened to the public. There were none present and he closed to the public.
