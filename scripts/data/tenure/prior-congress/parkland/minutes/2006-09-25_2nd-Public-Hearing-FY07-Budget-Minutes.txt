 B. PUBLIC INPUT
 Mr. Stuart Krantz, 6348 NW 62nd Terrace, said he has previously asked past Commissions to go
 with a higher millage rate of 5.1 and he is asking this Commission now. He gave the following
 reasons for his request. He feels the western fire station should be funded immediately. He feels
 there should be water hydrants on the east side of town. He feels the library should be funded
 for expansion. He asked why there was no line item for environmental resources or community
 wildlife money. He asked why there was no funding for sidewalks on Holmberg Road.
 Mayor Udine closed to the public.
