Ms. Natalie Bigio, 6877 NW 66 Avenue, Pine Tree Estates, spoke on two items:
• On behalf of M.U.S.T., she presented the City with a check for the Riverside Trail
amenities in the amount of $5,645.65 since the group had been dissolved and noted she
and Lauri Giafaglione plan to participate on the City of Parkland Trail Committee.
• On behalf of the Parkland `Friends of the Library', she requested approval to place a
banner advertising their book sale at a location within the City. Ms. Bigio advised that in
the past they had been allowed to place a banner at Old City Hall, however that location
was now under construction and without proper advertisement their book sales may not
produce the revenue required to fund the group and continue their grants to the City.
Kevin Bolling, Parkland resident, discussed public record request procedures and requested
further information regarding the $84,000 that had been awarded to replace a resident's bridge
on Trotter's Lane.
Mayor Udine clarified that some of Mr. Bolling's requests were for information and not for
actual documents that exist and therefore do not fall under public records request laws, however
staff had responded to his requests accordingly and would continue to do so in the future. He
also advised that records pertaining to the litigation case would be available after the release date
had passed.
Mr. Bolling questioned if the water had been tested at the NW 83 Terrace canal and stated he
has seen balls of tar in the water.
Mayor Udine responded that the matter had been discussed at several City Commission meetings
and contractors are in the process of testing the water and results may be requested through the
appropriate agencies.
There were no further comments or questions and the Mayor closed to the public.

4. DISCUSSION: SAFETY LIGHTING AT TERRAMAR PARK
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young introduced Parks & Recreation Director, Scott Davidoff to
present safety lighting options at Terramar Park.
Mr. Davidoff reviewed the options from the memo provided before the Commission which
discussed both electric and solar powered light source possibilities and the related costs.
Vice Mayor Weissman understood the City's budgetary concerns; however he felt that the lack
of adequate lighting at Terramar Park, especially after day light savings time goes into affect,
created a critical safety issue for their residents.

<<PG>>

Mayor Udine questioned if funding could be utilized from park impact fees collected.
Ms. Gardner -Young advised that the answer was yes, however, the project would need to be put
out for bid as it may total over $25,000.00 and the cost would depend upon how many lights are
required and which type of power source is used (solar vs. electric).
The Commission directed staff to put the project out for bid, requesting proposals for both
electric and solar power options, to be decided upon for the 2009 -2010 fiscal year budget.
