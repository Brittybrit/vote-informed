 II PURPOSE: 2006-2007 BUDGET
 A. PRESENTATION BY STAFF ON THE 2006-2007 BUDGET
 Mr. Lauzier gave a brief presentationexplaining adjustments ex lainin the adjustments that were made from the
 Budget workshop. He said they set out to achieve three goals in the budget process, a balanced
 budget required by law, millage neutral or slight decrease and continue with operating reserves
 but also to fund some CIP project commitments that are in the budget including Pine Trails Park.
 The following are the main points made by Mr. Lauzier:
 • Millage rate of 3.95 mils
 • Fire Assessment remains the same at$151
 • Police and Fire Enhancements mid year
 • Public Safety expansion for Western Fire Station and a fourth patrol zone—mid year
 • Addition of 4 leisure service aids in the Parks
 • Included a 3% COLA for all employees
 • Recommending expansion of Pine Trails Park to include a Phase III element.
 Mr. Lauzier continued reviewing all other changes and recommendations by staff.
 B. PUBLIC INPUT
 Kenneth Nejib, 6615 NW 75 Place, Whittier Oaks, and a candidate for District 4. He asked
 about the fire service fee, and if the rate for commercial has changed. He also asked about the
 out sourcing of the city engineer duties would be a big savings.
 Mr. Lauzier said there were no changes at all to the fire assessment and went on to explain that
 the savings anticipated in the out sourcing of the city engineer was $50,000. Some money was
 budgeted back in the engineering area for consulting work.
 Mr. Nejib also questioned if there was any thought on leasing the fire truck rather than make the
 $450,000 outlay in purchasing it.
 Mr. Lauzier said they did look into leasing and found that the 6 to 7% carrying cost would add a
 $20,000 increase to the purchasing cost.
 Cindy Watts, a representative of Family Central, gave a brief presentation about Family Central.
 She thanked the Commission for their proposed grant money.
 MaRhea Lascano, 6670 NW 76th Court, also a candidate for District 4. She questioned the
 decrease in positions and an increase in the salaries.
 Mr. Lauzier explained how he calculated this area.

 MOTION made by Commissioner Smith to reconsider the terminating of the City Bus.
 Seconded by Commissioner Moskowitz and for the record would like his reasons noted. He
 stated that the bus vendor was not notified before hand when this item appeared for termination
 on the last agenda and he feels the City should give the vendor his just due.
 Vice Mayor McGilvray asked what happens to the 60 days notice that has been given to cancel.
 Mr. Maurodis stated that if this vote passes, it will cancel the previous vote to terminate. A new
 notice would have to be sent out again if the City chooses to terminate again.
 Mr. Lauzier said that since that vote will be null, he will take money out of the hurricane reserve
 to fund another$15, 000 to $20,000 to pay for the bus for the rest of the year.
 Vice Mayor McGilvray said she would vote yes, but would like to see this on an agenda as soon
 as possible because delaying this item will cost the City money.
