2, ADMINISTRATION OF OATHS OF OFFICE TO ELECTED
OFFICIALS BY JUDGE LOU SCHIFF
VICE MAYOR/COMMISSIONER MARK WEISSMAN
COMMISSIONER DAVID ROSENOF
Judge Schiff administered the oaths of office to Commissioner Weissman and Commissioner
Rosenof.
Commissioner Rosenof noted that this was his third, and perhaps last term, to serve as a Parkland
City Commissioner, due to term limits for elected officials. Commended City staff for their fine
work and consistent efforts to do more with less. Commissioner Rosenof also thanked the
members of his family, especially his wife Rhonda for her support and understanding of his role
as an elected official. Commissioner Rosenof also expressed appreciation to all of the residents
of Parkland for the opportunity to serve as an elected official.
Vice Mayor Weissman expressed grateful appreciation to everyone for their support in
yesterday’s City of Parkland election. He noted that the City of Parkland was envied by many,
often copied, and never duplicated. Vice Mayor Weissman made special note of the most

Noting the large number of people in attendance for this evening’s special City Commission
meeting, Mayor Udine moved the proclamation honoring Anthony Rizzo from this evening’s
regular meeting to the special meeting. Mayor Udine invited his fellow City Commissioners to
join him in presenting the proclamation to Mr. Rizzo. He noted that Mr. Rizzo was a lifelong
Parkland resident, was a sixth round draft choice of the Boston Red Sox and would be the
starting first baseman for the Chicago Cubs beginning in 2013. Mayor Udine read the
proclamation in its entirety. He invited all interested persons to participate in a walk to benefit
the Anthony Rizzo Foundation, this Sunday morning, at Pine Trails Park.

Mr. Rizzo, noting that he was a lifetime Parkland resident, expressed gratitude for the
tremendous honor of being recognized by the Mayor and City Commission this evening,

Mayor Udine invited all in attendance to join himself and the City Commission for light
refreshments in honor of the recently elected members of the Parkland City Commission.
