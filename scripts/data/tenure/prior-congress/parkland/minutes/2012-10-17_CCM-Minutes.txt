v COMMENTS BY MAYOR AND COMMISSION

Mayor Udine requested that his colleagues join him for the presentation of a proclamation recognizing, and
expressing appreciation for the service of Commissioner Jared Moskowitz to the residents of Parkland. Mayor
Udine noted that this cvening’s meeting was Commissioner Moskowitz final meeting as a Parkland City
Commissioner. Mayor Udine expressed his best wishes to Commissioner Moskowitz and commended him for
all of his efforts, including the establishment of the City’s Dog Park, “Barkland”,

Comments by Vice Mayor Weissman:

Noted that it seemed like only yesterday that he was campaigning with Commissioner Moskowitz.

Also stated that the citizens of Parkland owed Commissioner Moskowitz a debt of gratitude for his service to the
community. He also expressed confidence that Commissioner Moskowitz would do a very good job of
representing his Parkland constituents in Tallahassee.

Comments by Commissioner Willis:

Stated that he had first met Commissioner Moskowitz on the same day that he found out about the Chinese
drywall issue. Noted that he found Commissioner Moskowitz to be a very caring person who has worked hard
for those impacted by Chinese drywall concerns and other matters of interest throughout the community.
Comaments by Commissioner Moskowitz:

Stated that it had been his honor to serve the residents of the City of Parkland. He also expressed appreciation
to City staff, and to the City Manager for her patience and assistance. Commissioner Moskowitz also expressed
gratzful appreciation to Mayor Udine, City Attorney Andy Maroudis, and his wife.

Noted that the City of Parkland had achieved a great deal for a smaller city, including protecting the future of
Parkland residents by having the University Drive extension project taken off the County’s master roadway plan
network. He also said that other noteworthy achievements included the expansion of parks facilities, including
accommodations for special needs children, the addition of a new fire station in Parkland city limits, repaving of
Holmberg Road and the opening of the first “green” school in Broward County, Heron Heights Elementary
School. Commissioner Moskowitz also said that the City of Parkland had spearheaded County-wide efforts to
increase driver education about the dangers of texting while driving. He also noted that the City of Parkland

had maintained one of the lowest millage rates in Broward County by achieving efficiencies throughout City
operations. Commissioner Moskowitz also emphasized the importance of continuing to hold developers
accountable for maintaining higher levels of aesthetics, creativity and craftsmanship relative to the quality of
their developments.
