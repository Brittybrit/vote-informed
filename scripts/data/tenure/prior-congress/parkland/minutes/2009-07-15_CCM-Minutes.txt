ADDED ITEM: TROTTERS LANE CLEANUP:
Commissioner Moskowitz gave a brief background on the situation from the beginning to present
and said Mayor Bockhold requested he put the issue of water quality on the agenda for August,
but he planned to discuss it tonight. Commissioner Moskowitz noted the water was tested
previously and to this day, the road material is not in the wells of The Ranches. Working with
the State, we will be doing an 18 -month monitoring program in which intermittently the water
will be tested to determine if the material has seeped into the wells. Commissioner Moskowitz
said he believes some residents have done their own studies and, if so, asks them to share those

City Manager, Caryn Gardner -Young read the title of Resolution No. 2009 -52, Fire Rescue
Assessment Rate For FY 2009 -2010 and introduced Interim Finance Director, Nancy Morando to
provide a brief presentation.
Ms. Morando gave an overview of the fire assessment from its inception in the City and noted
that the rate has not increased in five years, yet the fire cost has more than doubled. Ms.
Morando said staff is recommending the fire fee be raised to $215, the highest rate that can be
charged per resident based on the study conducted in 2004, which is still only 34% of the total
Fire Rescue budget. She stated the General Fund revenue has been covering the shortfall, while
as revenues are going down, the impact is hitting the City hard. The rate adopted at this meeting
will go on the Trim Notices, but will not be final until the rate is adopted in September.
Commissioner Smith said the Commission probably should have raised the fire fee last year, and
even by increasing it this year, only a small portion of the difference in the costs is being covered.
Commissioner Rosenof said it is easier to explain to a resident how the fire assessment fee is
calculated than it is to explain a millage rate. He added he is in favor of moving to the $215 rate,
at least for the Trim Notices.
Vice Mayor Weissman said he is philosophically against a fire rescue fee and is opposed to a fire
fee increase.
Mayor Udine said he is opposed to going to a $215 fee, but he understands that there are more
dollars needed to continue the level of service. He stated he is in favor of raising the fee, but not
that high. He added that he can see going to $195.
Commissioner Smith asked if any other scenario numbers have been run.
City Manager, Caryn Gardner -Young stated staff was able to calculate the return on various
options and whatever the Commission sets here tonight will be the highest they can go. She said
that even raising the fee to the limit will only cover 34% of the Fire budget.
MOTION made by Commissioner Rosenof to raise the fire assessment fee to $215. MOTION
died for lack of a second.
MOTION made by Commissioner Smith to raise the fire assessment fee to $195. Seconded by
Mayor Udine (who passed the gavel to Vice Mayor Weissman).
