6. DISCUSSION: THE WEDGE MASTER PLAN OPTIONS
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young gave a brief explanation of the item being presented. Ms.
Roberta Moore, Planning & Zoning Director gave an update on master plan options and requested
direction from the City Commission if any additional action nceds to be taken now that the Wedge
Master Plan is completed.
Ms. Moore explained that the final Master Plan as presented by Keith & Associates necded a few
minor changes that staff will be working on with Keith & Associates.
Mayor Udine commented that he sits on the planning council along with some other staff and he feels
the City of Parkland is far ahead in that land is set aside for schools, parks and roads through
donations. The Planning Council members can’t understand how the City of Parkland has
accomplished this.

Mayor Udine expressed that he was in favor of a combination of options. First option being #6

which is to do nothing. He has no problem with conducting additional traffic modeling.

Commissioner Moskowitz also agreed but wanted to know if the developers had to do the traffic study.

He asked why shouldn’t the developers be responsible for the traffic study or reimburse for the study.

Commissioner Moskowitz agreed there is no need for an overlay district. He asked if a traffic study

had already been done.

City Attorney Maurodis explained that the new Growth Management Act makes Department of

Community Affairs almost non-existent. It is no longer necessary to go to Tallahassee and explain

why the City is not putting in low income housing developments in thc middle of Parkland.

City Attorney Maurodis clarified that if the City Commission went with option #6 which is to do

nothing and it remains as a conceptual document, that at a later point and time we can decide to adopt

a master plan.

Commissioner Weissman asked Ms. Moore what the estimated cost to be.

Ms. Moore replied $15,000

Ms. Dodie Keith stated with regard to the Traffic Study the reason for the their recommendation to

review that is because the preliminary numbers that showed significant challenges on the east and

south end of town as you combine all the traffic studies together. She also explained that the

Commission could come up with a program to have the new developments pay for improvements so

Hilsboro may be extended to Parkside with dollars that the traffic study could support.

COMMENTS: SECOND READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY
COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
CREATING NEW CHAPTER 2.5 OF THE CITY CODE
RELATING TO LOBBYIST REGISTRATION
REQUIREMENTS; CONTAINING PROVISIONS FOR
IMPLEMENTATION; PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE
CITY CODE; PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE;
PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE. (first reading 1/5/11 &
tabled from 4/21/11)
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of the Second reading of Ordinance No. 2011-02:
Lobbyist Registration.
City Attorney Andrew Maurodis gave a brief explanation of the item being presented.
Commissioner Weissman expressed a concern with delaying approval of this Ordinance while waiting
for the League of Cities to work with Broward County regarding passage of an ordinance on ethics
requirements, He stated he secs no advantage to Parkland waiting. He is in favor of passing this
Ordinance and amending it in the future to incorporate language countywide or from another
municipality if need be.
Mayor Udine was reluctant to vote for this Ordinance based on past experiences in other municipalities
with elected officials where the “gotcha” approach occurred. He further stated the Ordinance canbe
adopted subject to the City Attorney broadening its language.
