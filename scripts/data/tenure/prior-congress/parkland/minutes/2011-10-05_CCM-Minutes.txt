Ms. Roberta Moore gave a brief presentation on the three applications. She explained that. the
Planning & Zoning Board recommended approval of the three applications and on first reading the
City Commission recommended approval for the rezoning and master plan.
Commissioner Moskowitz asked for an update on the school site since there were some pending issues
with the Broward County School Board.
Mr. Dennis Mele responded that the Master Plan shows the two school sites at the corner of Nob Hill
and County Line Road. He explained that thc School Board has authorized its staff to negotiate an
agreement to acquire the sites and Mr. Craig Unger has met several times with School Board staff and
is moving forward. He updated the City Commission that the School Board has done the appraisals
and the appraisals are currently being reviewed. They are aggressively working to get the appraisals on
the School Board's agenda and to have the sites dedicated as part of the Plat.
Vice Mayor Rosenof asked if the configuration of the schools site plans were final.
City Attorney Maurodis added that staff wants to get final approval of the impact fee agreement. He
reported to the City Commission the status of the agreement with Standard Pacific. He explained that
Standard Pacific wanted the neighboring parcel and Triple H came up with the conception for a park
impact fee agreement. Since Triple H will be providing both school sites, then the neighboring parcel
of Standard Pacific will take over the park impact fee obligation for Triple H. Therefore the City will
be receiving the park impact fee for Triple 11, but fees will be paid by Standard Pacific. The only
contingency formed is that if for some unexpected happenstance the school site does not get dedicated,
then Triple H will dedicate the ninc acre site.
MOTION made by Commissioner Moskowitz to approve on second reading the revised Ordinance
No. 2011-17: Triple H Ranch -- Rezoning & Master Plan Approval. Seconded by Vice Mayor
Rosenof.
Mayor Udine opened to the public.
Stuart Krantz, Pine Tree Estates resident, objected to the gated community development because it
does not have bike pedestrian access.

option to the Ordinance.
