SPECIAL PARKLAND CITY COMMISSION MEETING
FEBRUARY 15, 2012
6:30 P.M.
1 CALL TO ORDER 6:34 PM
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
Mayor Udine called Brannon and Alex Willis to the podium to lead everyone in the
Pledge of Allegiance.
Mayor Udine expressed that he invited Ms. Sharon Smith (former City Commissioner Jay
Smith’s wife) to attend the Swearing-In ceremony, however, it was a bit too emotional for
her to attend. She sent well wishes to John Willis and expressed that she was confident he
would continue to represent the City of Parkland in the same manner that Commissioner
Jay Smith would have.

I PURPOSE:
1. RESOLUTION NO. 2012-08: CERTIFICATION OF ELECTION
RESULTS
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ACCEPTING THE
RESULTS OF THE SPECIAL ELECTION HELD ON
JANUARY 31,2012.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young explained that on the dais was notification from Brenda C.
Snipes, Broward County Supervisor of Elections indicating that the election has been certified
and that Mr. John A. Willis was the successful candidate for the District 1 position.
MOTION made by Vice Mayor Weissman to accept the certification results from the Broward
County Supervisor of Elections Office. Seconded by Commissioner Moskowitz.
All were in favor by the sign of the I's.
MOTION passed by those present.
2. SWEARING IN OF ELECTED OFFICIAL BY JUDGE FRENCH
Mayor Udine invited the Honorable Judge David French and the Honorable Commissioner-Elect
John A. Willis and his family to the podium for the oath of office and swearing-in to the office of
the District 1 Commissioner seat.
COMMISSIONER JOHN A. WILLIS
Commissioner John Willis thanked his family and all who showed up for his swearing-in and
thanked those who were involved with his campaign.
m ADJOURNMENT
MOTION made by Commissioner Willis to adjourn the February 15, 2012 Special Commission
Meeting. Seconded by Vice Mayor Weissman.
MOTION passed by those present.
