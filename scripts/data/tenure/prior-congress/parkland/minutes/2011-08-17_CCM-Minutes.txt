Sheryl Woods, YMCA Executive Director for the City of Parkland to provide $60,000 in funding to
offset operational deficits for next year. Last year, the City Commission granted $75,000 to the
YMCA. He explained that the proposed budget which was presented on July 19, 2011 does not
include the request of $60,000, therefore, the requested funds would need to come out of reserves.

would only approve the $60,000 grant if it is used exclusively to reduce the membership fees of new
members that live in Parkland who join the YMCA.

against the grant of $75,000 last year because he does not feel monies should come out of reserves
and be given to private organizations to fund their operations. He stated he would likc to amend his

10. DISCUSSION: LIBRARY/PARKS PROGRAM & EVENT ATTENDANCE
Caryn bricfly explained that during Strategic Planning there was discussion about offering City
programs at no charge. Staff has expressed concern that since there is no charge, registrants who
decide not to attend are not accountable to notify the City and as a result people on the wait list do not
have the chance to participate.
Mr. Scott Davidoff, Parks & Recreation Director, explained that pre-registration is required for many
of the Library as well as Parks & Recreation events due to cap limits based on facility size and
resources. Those events include the Ginger Bread House Decorations, Hay Ride, and the Annual Egg
Hunt. He explained that around 14% - 20% of the participants fail to show for these events and the
wait list can be anywhere from 10 to 50 families. For Library events typically 15% - 20% fail to show
after registering. He detailed 2 options that could possibly solve the problem:
Option 1
If someone registers for an event and fails to cancel within 48 hours, they will not be able to
participate in another event or program for 3 months.
Option 2
Implement a $5.00 fee if someone registers and fails to cancel.
Further discussion ensued.
After further discussion by the City Commission, it was decided that no changes will be made and
staff will handle each issue accordingly.
