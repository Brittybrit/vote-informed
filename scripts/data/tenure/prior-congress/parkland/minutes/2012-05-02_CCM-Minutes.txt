City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Ordinance No. 2012-03: Adoption of Unified

Land Development Code Amendments. She expressed that at first reading it was recommended to

proceed to second reading. Roberta Moore, Planning & Zoning Director gave a brief explanation of

the item presented. She explained that some of the changes that staff made from first reading includes

specific definition changes of a consignment store. She stated additional language was added in the

Comprehensive Plan for consistency with the Broward County Land Use Plan. She stated that staff is

proposing, in addition to the new definition, to no longer allow sccond hand stores, but permit

consignment store. She went through each of the changes that were included in the amendment.

Vice Mayor Weissman thanked staff for putting the City Commission approvals back in place. He

asked the applicant what was the square footage of her store. The applicant replied approximately

4,000 s.f. Vice Mayor Weissman confirmed that she would need a special exception.

Commissioner Moskowitz reiterated that the lady who spoke on public comment was advocating that

her store is not just a consignment store, but more of a luxury consignment store.

Discussion ensued.

Ms. Roberta Moore read the proposed definition of consignment. She cited “Stores which sell artwork,

used musical instruments, antiques, or a particular class of items that can be fairly characterized as

vintage due to their association with a particular time in history or period of production.”

COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY

OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, FOR SPECIAL EXCEPTION
APPROVAL FOR ANYTIME FITNESS LOCATED AT THE
SOUTHWEST CORNER OF STATE ROAD 7 AND
LOXAHATCHEE ROAD IN THE WATERWAYS SHOPPES,
CASE NO. 01-SE-12. (quasi-judicial)

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2012-35; Anytime Fitness Special

Exception. Ms. Bonnie Miskell, Applicant Representative, 5355 Town Center Road, Boca Raton, FL

stated that she would like to waive the quasi-judicial procedure. Ms. Roberta Moore gave a brief

update on the item being presented. She explained that there were a number of special exception

regulations in the City’s Code that staff reviewed along with the Broward Sheriff’s Office who played

an integral part in its review. Ms. Moore clarified that Ms. Miskell is now in agreement with the

restriction of the hours from 24 hours to closing at midnight and reopening at 5 AM.

Ms. Moore explained that the Broward Sheriff's Office had a number of concerns, but with the change

in the hours, the Police Chief’s concerns were addressed.

Ms. Miskell affirmed that they now agree with staff’s recommendation and are amending their request

to limit the hours to closing between midnight and 5 AM.

Commissioner Moskowitz had no problem approving Anytime Fitness to be open 24-hours, but with a

condition that in 90 days after opening, a report be given from BSO on criminal activity as a result of

midnight to 5 AM at the sole discretion of the City Commission.

from 24 Hours back to midnight to 5 AM, however the Police Chief will provide a crime report within
