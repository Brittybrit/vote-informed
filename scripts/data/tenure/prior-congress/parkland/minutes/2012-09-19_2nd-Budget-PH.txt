Ir PURPOSE: 2012-2013 BUDGET

1 ORDINANCE NO. 2012-16: ADOPTING MILLAGE
COMMENTS: SECOND READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE

CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE MILLAGE RATE TO BE
LEVIED FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING
OCTOBER 1, 2012 AND ENDING SEPTEMBER 30,
2013; AND STATING THE PERCENTAGE CHANGE IN
PROPERTY TAXES AS SUCH CHANGE IS DEFINED
IN FLORIDA STATUTES, SECTION 200.065;
PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY, AND PROVIDING
FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE. (first hearing 9/12/12)

City Attorney Andrew Maurodis read the following into the record:

As an ordinance of the City Commission of the City of Parkland adopting the millage rate to be

levied for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2012 and ending September 30, 2013 and stating the

percentage change in property taxes and such change is defined in Florida Statutes 200.065. Whercas

the adopted millage rate of 3.999 mils is a 1.59% increase over the roll back rate of 3.9372 and is

characterized in the Florida Statues 200.065 as a percentage increase in property taxes that the millage

rate to be levied for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2012 and ending September 30, 2013 to be

levied in order to produce sufficient ad valorem taxation revenuc which when combined with other

projected general revenues will be adequate to pay the appropriations made in the budget for the fiscal

year beginning October 1, 2012 and ending September 30, 2013 shall be operating millage rate of

3.999; that the adopted millage rate of 3.999 mils is 1.59% more than the roll back rate of 3.9372 and

is computed according to the guidelines that is set forth in Florida Statutes 200.065.

Mayor Udine opened to the public. There were none present and he closed to the public.

MOTION made by Commissioner Rosenof to approve on Second Reading Ordinance No. 2012-

16: Adopting Millage. Seconded by Vice Mayor Weissman.

Commissioner Willis

Mayor Udine

MOTION passed unanimously.
