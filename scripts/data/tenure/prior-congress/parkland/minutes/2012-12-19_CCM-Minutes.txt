III PUBLIC INPUT 
 
Mayor Udine acknowledged that State Representative Jim Waldman was in attendance at this evening’s 
City Commission meeting. He noted that Representative Waldman’s district had recently been 
re-boundaried to include all residents of the City of Parkland. 
 
IV COMMENTS BY MAYOR AND COMMISSION 
 
Comments by Commissioner Rosenof: 
 
 Extended a welcome to all Broward Sheriff’s Office employees in attendance this evening. 
 Expressed happy holiday wishes to all. 
 Wished Mayor Udine and his family a safe trip to Israel. 
 
Comments by Vice Mayor Weissman: 
 Extended best wishes to Mayor Udine and his family for a safe trip to Israel 
 Expressed happy and safe holidays to all. 
 
Comments by Mayor Udine: 
 Noted that he would be traveling to Israel during the next few weeks. 
 Also made note that he would be meeting with Israeli soldier representatives during his visit and 
would proudly be presenting City of Parkland t-shirts to the soldiers. 
 Stated that the City of Parkland had consistently funded a School Resource Deputy in all of the

in support of the Zweig family’s “Justice for Jacob” cause. He noted that interested persons 
could learn more about the matter by visiting <a href="http://www.justiceforjacobnow.com">www.justiceforjacobnow.com</a>.

 MASTER PLAN AMENDMENT 
 
COMMENTS: SECOND READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY

APPROVAL OF THE MASTER PLAN AMENDMENT, BY 
STANDARD PACIFIC OF FLORIDA, TO RELOCATE THE

NORTH SIDE OF COUNTY LINE ROAD, ½ MILE WEST OF NOB 
HILL ROAD; CASE NUMBER 03-MPA-12 (first reading 11/7/12)(quasi) 
 
Ms. Gardner-Young read the title of Item 1a, Ordinance 2012-23, into the meeting record and noted that Ms. 
Mellgren would be making a single presentation for all three items (Items 1a, 1b and 1c), so long as there was no 
objection from the Mayor and Commission. There was no objection from the Mayor and Commission. 
 
Mayor Udine noted that the first item, second reading of Ordinance 2012-23, was a quasi-judicial matter. Dennis 
Mele, the applicant’s representative, waived the need for a quasi-judicial process on behalf of his client for all 
three elements (1a, 1b and 1c) of this application. 
 
Michele Mellgren made staff’s presentation for all three items, regarding the applicant’s proposal to increase the 
size of the project’s recreation amenities and related matters. She requested that City staff’s presentation and all 
appropriate documents be made a part of the meeting record. Ms. Mellgren noted that the Planning and Zoning 
Board had unanimously recommended approval of all three items. 
 
There were no public comments. 
 
MOTION made by Vice Mayor Weissman to approve Ordinance 2012-23 on second reading. Seconded by 
Commissioner Rosenof.
