City Attorney Andrew Maurodis stated for the record that at the County level as part of the
Land Use Plan Amendment for the 700-acres, the applicant is recording a restrictive covenant
that contains the 40-acre high school site. The language was taken directly from the
Annexation Agreement with the City.
Commissioner Moskowitz wanted clarification about what could be built on the 40-acre site in
terms of a school.
Ms. Moore explained that the Annexation Agreement with Hendrix and the City specifically
calls for a high school site. She explained that the middle school site would be on the Triple H
& Debuys parcel which is 15 — 20-acres and the Bruschi parcel has 8 — 12-acres for a school
site.
Discussion ensued.
Rob Feiner, Representative for the applicant Hendrix confirmed that the Annexation
Agreement restrictions have not been recorded at the County level.
Andrew Maurodis, City Attorney stated that before the Agreement is recorded, City staff will
need to remove a few words from it regarding the school site. He expressed that the change
would need to be taken care of immediately.
MOTION made by Vice Mayor Smith to approve Resolution 2010-10; Hendrix Land Use
Amendment with the conditions that the declaration of restrictions covenants filed at Broward
County for the entire Land Use Plan Amendment is changed to remove the specific reference to a
high school. Seconded by Commissioner Weissman.
