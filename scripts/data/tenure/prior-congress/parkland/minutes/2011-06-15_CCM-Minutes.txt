5. RESOLUTION NO. 2011-48: HUMAN RESOURCE SERVICES
BID AWARD
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING THE
PROPOSERS’ RANKINGS PER EXHIBIT A AND
AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE A
CONTRACT WITH THE MANAGEMENT STRATEGY
GROUP FOR HUMAN RESOURCES SERVICES.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2011-48: Human Resource Services Bid
Award. She explained two years ago, the City of Parkland went out to bid for HR services. At the time, they
realized there may be additional changes necessary to the services being provided particularly with additional
hours. The existing contractor indicated he could not provide the level of service at the rate originally agreed
upon. Not knowing what the best price would be for these services, the City went out for bid. Two firms
submitted bids, however, Management Strategy Group was the lowest bidder.
Mayor Udine expressed that the City was not losing any employees. He stated that after a year, staff will
revisit the process to determine if it works better for the City as is or discuss if HR services need to be
brought in-house.

6. RESOLUTION NO. 2011-38: DOG PARK SITE PLAN APPROVAL
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE

CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, FOR SITE PLAN
AMENDMENT APPROVAL FOR THE DOG PARK
LOCATED ADJACENT TO LIBERTY PARK; CASE
NUMBER 10-SPA-11. (quasi-judicial)

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2011-38: Dog Park Site Plan Approval.

She explained that this is a quasi-judicial hearing and the City waives its own quasi-judicial process.

Mayor Udine expressed that no ad valorem tax revenue would be used to build the dog park and it was being

funded by a third party.

Brian Archer, Management Analyst explained that the Planning & Zoning board voted unanimously

recommending approval of the dog park site plan. He then gave a brief presentation explaining the item

being presented.

Commissioner Moskowitz wanted to point out that with the buffer, the dog park will be opaque.

He stated he believed it would be a very busy park and questioned if there would be ample parking.

Mr. Archer explained it was identical to what is at the Liberty Park site.

Commissioner Moskowitz asked if they could squeeze in more parking spaces and expressed a concern that

cars would park on the side of the road.

Ms. Gardner-Young explained that if additional parking goes in, the City would have to pay for it.

Commissioner Moskowitz asked how many additional spaces could be added.

Mr. Archer replied approximately 4 or 5.

Vice Mayor Rosenof asked if there would be improvements between the berm and Holmberg Road or if it

would remain as is.

Mr. Archer replied it would remain as is.

Vice Mayor Rosenof asked if irrigation would be placed in that area.
