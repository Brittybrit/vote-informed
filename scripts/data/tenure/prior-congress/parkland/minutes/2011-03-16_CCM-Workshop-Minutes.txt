1. PRESENTATION: WEDGE PIECE MASTER PLAN
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young gave a brief introduction then introduced Ms. Dodie Keith-
Lazowick of Keith & Associates, Inc. Ms. Keith-Lazowick introduced the team of people involved
with the master planning stages of the Wedge. She then gave a presentation detailing specifics of the
Wedge Piece Master Plan. See attached presentation.
Commissioner Weisman asked Mr. Dennis Mele, representative for the Standard Pacific Developer to
approach the podium. Commissioner Weissman asked about impact credits on the infrastructure as
well as the concrete slab for the fire stations. He stated if construction does not take place within two
years, perhaps a monetary contribution would be an alternative to pay for the slabs. Mr. Mele stated

Commissioner Weissman asked Mr. Mele if the Developer has agreed to these terms and if it would be

Mr. Mele replied that a site dedication agreement would probably be needed which will indicate what
is dedicated, the improvements that are made, and when it is dedicated.

Commissioner Rosenof asked if the design would be included.

Mr. Mele replied, no, the Developer’s work would include filling, de-mucking, grading, stubbing out
water and sewer to the property line and drainage pipe connection to the lake.

Ms. Keith-Lazowick continued with the presentation.

Mayor Udine asked if the park site was 42 acres, then compared it to the size of Terramar Park, which
is 40 acres.

Mayor Udine asked Chris Akagbosu, Director of Broward County School Board Growth Management
Department if the concept for an elementary and middle school on the 25 acre site is favorable to the
School Board.

Mr. Akagbosu stated the School Board is in complete support of the arrangement to have the
elementary and middle schools on the 25 acre parcel.

Mayor Udine inquired about the code requirements for the Parkland Library.

Commissioner Weissman informed Ms. Lazowick that the City Commission is in favor of Standard
Pacific’s proposal regarding using the Southwest corner site for the fire station. There was consensus
by the City Commission.

Caryn thanked everyone involved with the Wedge Piece Master planning.
