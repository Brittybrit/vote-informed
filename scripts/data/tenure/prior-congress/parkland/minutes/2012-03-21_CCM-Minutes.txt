2. CONSIDERATION: BUDGET TRANSFER FOR DOG PARK
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young gave an update on the additional improvements that will take place at
the dog park. She also stated that on April 18, 2012, Broward County Commissioner Stacy Ritter will be
at City Hall to present approximately $9,000 to the City. Staff recommends approval,
Commissioner Moskowitz clarified that this is the first time that the City is allocating funds for the dog
park and he explained that much of the project was completed without city dollars. He also expressed that
staff is still looking for other funds to subrogate some of the city funds which is what Broward County
did. He stated that staff worked hard to come in under budget and utilize the funds allocated, however,
there were additional improvements like benches and shade trees that could not be done. He also stated
that now it is good that the City is supporting the dog park with some dollars.
Mayor Udine concurred with the comments made by Commissioner Moskowitz.
MOTION made by Commissioner Moskowitz to approve the Budget transfer for the Dog Park. Seconded
by Commissioner Weissman.

3. DISCUSSION:  50™ ANNIVERSARY PLANS
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young explained that staff is looking for direction on how the City
Commission wants them to proceed. She explained that the actual anniversary date is July 10, 2013,
however, it is very hot during that time of year. She stated that since the Farmer’s Market is over in April
and the Equestrian Center will be available after that time, staff is suggesting to hold the anniversary
celebration in the month of May.
Commissioner Moskowitz suggested since the anniversary is not going to be on the exact date, then
moving the date up a few months is not going to hurt anything to make it more enjoyable.
Mayor Udine expressed that because it is such an important event, he would like staff to piggyback on a
few events throughout the year. He suggested a joint event with Douglas High School homecoming
parade, a carnival at one of the parks, a concert at Terramar Park, a volunteer appreciation evening, bring
back some of the former Mayors, get the Historical Society and Women’s Clubs involved.
