II PURPOSE:

1. RESOLUTION NO. 2006-111: CERTIFICATION OF ELECTION
RESULTS

COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ACCEPTING THE
RESULTS OF THE SPECIAL ELECTION HELD ON
NOVEMBER 7,2006.

City Manager Mark Lauzier read the title of Resolution No. 2006-111. He commented that the
City Clerk had just received the certified election results, copies of which had been placed on the
dais.

MOTION made by Vice Mayor McGilvray to accept the election results. Seconded by
Commissioner Moskowitz.

ROLL CALL VOTE:

Commissioner Smith Yes
Commissioner Moskowitz Yes
Vice Mavor McGilvray Yes
Mayor Udine Yes

MOTION passed unanimously.

2. SWEARING IN OF ELECTED OFFICIAL:

COMMISSIONER MARK WEISSMAN

Following Mayor Udine's introduction, County Court Judge Louis Schiff swore In
Commissioner-Elect Weissman as the new commissioner for District 4.

Commissioner Weissman ascended the dais.

Commissioner Weissman stated that he had been humbled by the election results and
overwhelmed by the support he had received. He congratulated his opponents for running the
type of race - well run and without animosity -- which the City should have in its future. He
recognized and thanked his partner, Roberta, his mother, his children, his campaign consultant,
and his friends and neighbors. The Commissioner pledged his availability to the needs of
residents and hoped to be their voice in the future. He also thanked Judge Schiff for so patiently
waiting for the results.
