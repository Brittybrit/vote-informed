REGULAR AGENDA
1. PRESENTATION: BROWARD COUNTY SUPERVISOR OF
ELECTIONS OFFICE - UPDATE
Mr. Gino Herring, Director of Voter Education for the Broward County Supervisor of Elections Office,
noted that there were three (3) ways for registering to vote in the November 6, 2012 general election, as
follows: voting by mail, early voting, and voting in-person on election day. Mr. Herring stated that
early voting would be available from October 27th through November 3", between 7:00 a.m. and
7:00 p.m. Mr. Herring responded to questions from the Mayor and City Commission regarding
anticipated ballot length, requests for absentee ballots, and early voting.
