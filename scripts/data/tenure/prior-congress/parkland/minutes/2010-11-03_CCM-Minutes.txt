Vv COMMENTS BY MAYOR AND COMMISSION

Comments by Commissioner Weissman:

o Expressed that the solar lighting which was installed adjacent to Terramar Park on Hillsboro
Boulevard looks great. It lights up an area that was completely dark at night for walkers and
joggers. Commissioner Weissman expressed that tonight he is going to ask the City Commission
to add an item to the agenda to expend the already budgeted funds to complete the solar lighting
from the entrance of Terramar Park east to the no parking area. He explained that currently, you
have to go past the entrance to Terramar Park to see the solar lighting.

Comments by Commissioner Moskowitz:

e Thanked the residents of Parkland for re-electing them even though it was by default.

e Congratulated Coral Springs who has a new Mayor. He mentioned that Scott Brook was term
limited. Commissioner Larry Vignola was elected who happens to be a month younger than he
is. He commented that it is good to see young people getting elected.

e  Congratulated Claudette Bruck for being re-elected.

e Requested that a letter be sent to Congressman Klein thanking him for his service and for
representing the City of Parkland.

e Requested that a letter be sent to Congressman Ted Deutch and Congressman-Elect Alan West
inviting each of them to a City Commission meeting so they can be familiar with issues in
Parkland. Commissioner Moskowitz expressed that the only issue Washington should care about
is the Chinese Drywall issue. He commented that people are not looking for money. Instead
they are looking for a protocol to remediate. He expressed that people are still looking for the
Federal stamp that the right thing is being done and there are no long term health effects. He felt
it might be nice to educate both Congressman-Elect West and Congressman Deutch on these
issues.

o Expressed that the one clear message from the November 2, 2010 election dealt with taxes and
spending. Parkland’s City Commission has lowered taxes twice and has spent accordingly. This
is something that clearly the voters and residents still want to see, which is something the City
Commission should be mindful of each budget year.

Comments by Vice Mayor Smith:

e Thanked the residents for having faith in him over the last 4 years and for the next 4 years.

e Congratulated Commissioner Moskowitz and Mayor Udine for being re-elected.

o Congratulated everyone who won their race on November 2, 2010.

e Congratulated Roy Gold for becoming the Mayor of the City of Coral Springs.

o Thinks the City Commission has done a great job in keeping taxes at a very reasonable level. He
mentioned that he looked up some tax milage rates in his home town of Connecticut and the
average was about 32 mils. Parkland is at 4.01. He posed a question asking the residents to
imagine paying four times their taxes.

Comments by Commissioner Rosenof:

e Congratulated those who were elected and re-elected.

o Reminded everyone of the Farmer’s Market on Sunday, November 7, 2010 which is the 1%
Farmer’s Market of the year.

Comments by Mayor Udine:

o Asked everyone to get the word out about the Farmer’s Markets because the residents love them.

e Met with Mr. Irving Schatz and his grandson this week. They are trying to come up with more
ideas for solar initiatives that Mr. Shatz will fund for the City of Parkland. Mayor explained that
these are grant dollars and the City does not have to pay anything for the solar projects. He
explained that Mr. Shatz does not live in Parkland and has taken a liking to the City of Parkland
and what it is doing. Mr. Shatz and his grandson specifically mentioned a quote from
Commissioner Moskowitz that they saw in the paper a number of years ago. Commissioner
Moskowitz stated, “If the Federal government is not going to do anything, we’re going to have to
do something on our own.” Mr. Shatz felt that if this is a small city trying to do something and
they can’t get any help, then he wants to assist them.

e As for the elections, he agrees that congratulatory letters should be sent out as mentioned by
Commissioner Moskowitz.

e Mentioned that he has spoken to Congressman-Elect Alan West and his campaign about the
Chinese Drywall issue. Congressman-Elect West is an accountability type of person and wants
the residents to get the help they need. Mayor agrees that it is important to invite Congressman-
Elect West to a future City Commission meeting.

o Congratulated everyone who ran in the elections whether they won or not. He stated that it takes
a lot of guts to throw your hat in the ring and run for election. He mentioned that some of his
candidates won and some did not, but the great thing about an election is after the election takes
place, everyone can try and work together at least for a few days.

the additional solar lighting that would go from the entrance of the park south. Ms. Gardner-
Young explained that they completed the entrance to the west. Ms. Gardner-Young stated that
the reason staff budgeted the monies was under the belief they would be able to get a grant so it

commented that truthfully he has not seen it yet and asked if this was something that could be placed on

the next meeting to vote on because before he votes to spend an additional $45,000, he would like to

know what he is voting on. He commented that staff does not have a presentation, he hasn’t seen any

pictures of the lights — he will go out and take a look at it. Commissioner Moskowitz explained that he is

not going to vote against it if there are three (3) votes to do it now. He thought it would make sense if he

could educate himself before he votes to spend money.

Commissioner Rosenof clarified that the dollars are in the budget to spend, but he asked staff if the

reimbursement was also in the budget. City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young explained that on the

IX COMMENTS BY THE CITY ATTORNEY
e With regard to the annexation, Attorney Maurodis stated that Caryn Gardner-Young and Roberta
Moore have done a good job lining up a large part of the Wedge for annexation. At the last
meeting, the City Commission unanimously asked staff to try to get annexation agreements with
each one of those parties. Given the status, there were a number of practical difficulties: 1) An
elderly woman who owns Dolly Land is not going to want to retain an attorney 2) given the
nature of some of these parties and the nature of our planning at this point, staff does not know if
some of parcels will be commercial or residential or what to ask for. Staff believes they will not
be able to accomplish this for the City Commission. A decision will have to be made — whether
you want to limit the annexation to parties we have an agreement with or wait until their land use
plan time - the decision does not have to be made tonight. Attorney Maurodis stated staff
acknowledges the desire for an annexation agreement and understands Commissioner
Weissman’s comment that this is the best negotiating time; however staff does not feel confident
to be able to do that. Commissioner Weissman asked when is the last opportunity to pull these
parcels out of the agreement. Ms. Gardner-Young answered, “January 5, 2010 is the public
hearing.”
e Commissioner Weissman stated he would like to put this item on the agenda for the first meeting
in December. Attorney Maurodis suggested November 30, 2010.
e Commissioner Weissman suggested that in the meantime staff sends letters certified mail to three
land owners whose properties totaled over 100 acres.
e Attorney Maurodis recommended a phone call instead.
e Commissioner Moskowitz reminded staff of the conversation at the last meeting that Caryn was
to invite the land owners and meet with them.
