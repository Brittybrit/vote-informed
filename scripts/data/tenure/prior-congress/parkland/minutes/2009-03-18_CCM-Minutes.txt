the purchase of the right of way and the addition of a turn around area.

4. CONSIDERATION: PINE TRAILS PARK CONCERT PROPOSAL
City Manager, Caryn Gardner -Young gave a brief explanation of the item and introduced Parks
& Recreation Director, Scott Davidoff to present the Pine Trail Park Concert Proposal.
Mr. Davidoff explained Entertainment Express, Inc. (EXI), was under consideration as a
potential promoter for large scale /national acts at the Pine Trails Park amphitheater. He advised
that he met with the promoter, Doug Isaac along with Planning & Zoning Director, Roberta
Moore and BSO Police Chief , Paul O'Connell, to discuss the City's expectations for such
events.

<<PG>>

Mr. Isaac, Parkland resident and promoter for EXI, advised the Commission he felt there is a
need for this type of venue in Broward County and the band "New Found Glory ", who are
previous Parkland residents, would be interested in playing this venue during their November
tour. He noted he would like to begin with a limited series of about 4 -5 shows a year worked
around the other City events.
He added his company would be responsible for all aspects of the event such as fencing, food &
drinks, security and off site parking and that they are looking to do everything in an upscale
manner. He offered that he was willing to cover all the upfront costs and take all the risk
initially.
Commissioner Moskowitz stated he would like to test the proposal and see how it turns out. He
questioned Mr. Isaac as to what size event would be his goal?
Mr. Isaac responded his goal would be to have a large act such as the "Allman Brothers" with
approximately 7,500 people in attendance but would start with smaller events to get the
community acclimated to this type of event.
Mayor Udine noted a few concerns such as; residents had been used to small, no fee events and
the parking limitations at the park.
Commissioner Moskowitz inquired what the potential ticket cost would be, questioned potential
damage to the field and would the City have to sign a three event requirement deal?
Mr. Isaac responded he thought the base ticket price would be around $50.00 per person with
some VIP seating prices, damage should be minimal with set up and take down is done in 24
hours and he would like to sign a one event deal and see how well it does.
Commissioner Smith requested if ticket sales could be available to Parkland residents before the
general public.
Mr. Isaac responded that they could offer pre -sales for residents and also added Ticketmaster'
had agreed to install a ticketing machine at the event the day of the show for convenience.
The Commission discussed the item further, reviewing topics such as; clarifying logistics,
obtaining legal agreements, insurance liabilities, possible sale of alcohol, Parkland resident
discounts and child discounts and/or family package pricing.
Mayor Udine questioned the Commission if they desired developing the amphitheater into a
revenue source for the City? He received an affirmative response and advised they would like to
review a final proposal from Mr. Isaac and EXI.
Ms. Gardner -Young reiterated for clarification that the City Commission wanted Staff to prepare
a final agreement for review.

5. PRESENTATION: PARK SURVEY
City Manager, Caryn Gardner -Young gave a brief explanation of the Park Survey and introduced
Parks & Recreation Director, Scott Davidoff.
Mr. Davidoff explained the Parks & Recreation department were the test group for the survey
process and worked with the Finance Department to create the survey utilizing the online survey
service surveymonkey'. The survey was distributed via email and online with 404 responses
received. The results indicated an overall high rating of 91.3% (satisfied /very satisfied) rating and
will be used in updating the Parks & Recreation department's action plan.
Mayor Udine commented on the City's Senior and Children's programs, he has received
numerous letters, emails and comments from the residents stating how much they enjoy the
Leisure Services programs. He also noted he was very satisfied with the survey results and
believes this department is working on a very high service level.
The Mayor thanked Mr. Davidoff for his presentation.
