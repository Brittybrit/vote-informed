A. PRESENTATION BY STAFF ON THE 2008-2009 BUDGET 
 
Finance & Administrative Services Director Barbara Hastings made a presentation. She 
indicated that the Commission’s recommended reductions had been made. Except for Police and 
Fire, the departments reflect a two percent or greater decrease in their budgets. 
 
Total taxable value in the City of Parkland stands at $3.7 billion. The current rollback rate is 
3.6070, and staff proposed that the Commission adopt an operating millage rate of 3.4083, which 
had been presented at the first workshop. Ms. Hastings noted this represented a five percent 
reduction in the rollback rate. 
 
Ms. Hastings presented a breakdown of a resident’s tax bill, noting that the City receives just 17 
percent of the taxes collected; the City’s rates were compared to other municipalities. She 
reviewed budget highlights: the General Fund total at $19,030,000 with the removal of $267,000 
from reserves; revenues and expenditures; and capital projects. 
 
In accordance with Florida statutes, City Attorney Andrew Maurodis read information 
concerning the millage rate into the record. He noted that the Second Public Hearing on the 
budget would be held September 24, 2008, at 7:00 p.m.

 D. ORDINANCE NO. 2008-20: ADOPTING THE BUDGET 
 
FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF 
THE PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ADOPTING A BUDGET FOR THE FISCAL 
YEAR BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2008 AND ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 
2009 PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY AND PROVIDING FOR AN 
EFFECTIVE DATE. 
 
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the first reading of Ordinance No. 2008-20. 
 
Mayor Udine opened to the public. There were no comments or questions, and the Mayor closed 
to the public. 
 
Finance & Administrative Services Director Barbara Hastings commented that the budget book 
had been presented to the Commission for their review. She noted that an employment cap of 72 
full-time employees and a financial reserve policy of 20 percent of the City’s annual budget, or 
2.4 months, were included within the budget. 
 
MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to approve the first reading of Ordinance No. 
2008-20, Adopting the Budget for Fiscal Year 2008/2009. Seconded by Vice Mayor Moskowitz. 
 
Vice Mayor Moskowitz believed it important to point out the energy conservation initiatives to 
power City Hall air-conditioning and water heating at the new Fire Station. 
 
The Vice Mayor asked that information be provided about the trees impacted in the Holmberg 
Road improvements prior to second reading. He suggested that beautification funds be allocated, 
particularly to the area between The Ranches and University Drive.
