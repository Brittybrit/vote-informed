4. RESOLUTION NO. 2011-24: TERNBRIDGE SIGNAGE CAB
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE

CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, FOR COMMUNITY
APPEARANCE BOARD APPROVAL FOR THE
INSTALLATION OF CORONADO STONE VENEER OVER
THE EXISTING MASONRY STUCCO WALLS ON THE
TERNBRIDGE MONUMENT ENTRY WALLS,
GUARDHOUSE WALLS, AND BRIDGE WALLS LOCATED
AT THE HILLSBORO ROAD ENTRANCE TO THE
TERNBRIDGE COMMUNITY, CASE NUMBER 02-CAB-11.
(quasi-judicial)

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2011-24: Ternbridge Signage

CAB. She turned it over to Ms. Roberta Moore, Planning & Zoning Director. Ms. Susan Dobson,

Ternbridge HOA President, waived the quasi-judicial rights. Ms. Moore gave a brief explanation of

the staff report being presented and stated that staff is recommending approval of the CAB

Application.

Commissioner Weissman asked about another sign that used to say Terramar and the City

Commission approved putting Ternbridge on the sign. He asked if that was included in this request.

Ms. Dobson replied in the affirmative.

Vice Mayor Rosenof asked what the opposing vote was from the Planning & Zoning (P&Z) Board

meeting.

Ms. Moore replied that they indicated a concern about the entry wall and the shade of the stone. The

P&Z Board wanted the Ternbridge Homeowners Association Appearance Review Board to have

approved it prior to being submitted to the City. Ms. Moore also asked that the staff report and all

back-up be included as part of the record.

MOTION made by Commissioner Smith to approve Resolution No. 2011-24: Ternbridge Signage

CAB. Seconded by Commissioner Weissman.

ROLL CALL VOTE:

Vice Mayor Rosenof

Mayor Udine

9. ORDINANCE NO. 2011-06: AMENDING CHAPTER 18
“ENGINEERING STANDARDS”
COMMENTS: SECOND READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY
COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
AMENDING CHAPTER 18 ENTITLED “ENGINEERING
STANDARDS,” ARTICLE IV ENTITLED, “PERMITS, FEES
AND BONDS,” AMENDING SECTION 18-56 ENTITLED,
“PERFORMANCE BOND”; PROVIDING A CONFLICT
CLAUSE, PROVIDING A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE;
PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE. (first reading 4/6/11)
Ms. Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of the second reading of Ordinance No. 2011-06: Amending
Chapter 18: “Engineering Standards”. She explained there were no requested changes from first
reading and this is to address the City’s concern regarding the second lifts of asphalt applied to streets
that were done by the developer. This will ensure the streets are provided additional strength during
the time period of the phase of development and the developer has to provide a second lift of asphalt
if 80% of the homes receive a Certificate of Occupancy.
Mayor Udine opened to the public. There was no one present and he closed to the public.
MOTION made by Vice Mayor Rosenof to approve on second reading Ordinance No. 2011-06:
Amending Chapter 18: “Engineering Standards”. Seconded by Commissioner Weissman.
