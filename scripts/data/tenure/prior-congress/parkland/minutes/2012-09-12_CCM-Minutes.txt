6. REQUEST FOR WAIVER OF CONFLICT: KEITH & ASSOCIATES
Commissioner Moskowitz asked the City Commission to look at what is being proposed. He explained that the
referenced parcel adjacent to the roadway next to the temple in front of the Ranches — there is three storied
townhome assisted living facility being proposed to be built. He stated he does not have a problem with the use,
but has a problem with where it is proposed to be built. For the record, he stated that he is fine with the waiver
being granted, but in his opinion that parcel is the wrong place for the project.

The question was posed if any of the Commissioners had a problem with Ms. Dodi Keith Lazowick speaking to
the land owner.
There was a concensus among the Commission to grant the waiver of conflict.

1. RESOLUTION NO. 2012-90:  SALTA PRE-ANNEXATION AGREEMENT
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING AN
ANNEXATION AGREEMENT WITH SALTA PROPERTIES
RELATING TO PROPERTY BEING ANNEXED INTO THE CITY
OF PARKLAND.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2012-90: Salta Pre-Annexation
Agreement. Mr. Maurodis explained what this item was about.
Mayor Udine explained that the City wanted in the Annexation Agreements with the major land owners
a portion of land to be set aside for additional school sites.
City Attorney Maurodis stated this is not contained in the agreements.
Vice Mayor Weissman stated that he looked at three of the sites and the most they were able to gain
from the land owners was about 9% of their land mass that was dedicated for school sites. He would
like to put that amount into this agreement.

<<PG>>

Mr. Dennis Mele representing Salta confirmed that they will do a community project. He explained that
the agreement provides for park impact fee, street light fee, landscaping along Lox Road fee, guardrail
fee, and all impact fees. He stated that Salta recognizes there will be a contribution for schools as was
done with the other land owners.

Vice Mayor Weissman stated that while they may get a commitment for schools, if there becomes a
subsequent owner later and Mr. Mele does not represent them, then there would not be that commitment
down the road from a subsequent land owner and they would be more limited once the agreement is
signed. He suggested tabling until the next meeting,

Mr. Mele asked that the City Commission approve the agreement and stated that this agreement does not
bind the City to two (2) units per acre.

Vice Mayor Weissman asked if the agreement could be approved prior to its signing to negotiate the
dollar amount of the 9% land mass.

Commissioner Moskowitz commented that 9% makes a big difference on a large parcel vs a small
parcel.

Mr. Tom Roberts expressed his concern stating that Salta helped the City in three legislative sessions to
get the Wedge approved. They worked with the City for several months and now at the last minute the
City is trying to squeeze them for something else. He expressed that he does not think the manner in
which this is being handled is fair.

Mr. Dennis Mele also expressed that each time a fee was asked for, Mr. Roberts agreed to pay it and
these were fees others were not asked to pay.

Commissioner Moskowitz stated that he would make a motion for one acre.

Mr. Mele agreed to come back next week.

City Attorney Maurodis stated that the idea is to table in conjunction with staff to attempt to work out
some sort of dedication for public school facilities to be held by the City and the City would then
cooperate with the School Board.

Mr. Mele made an additional comment in which he asked the City Commission to take into account.
When the other property owners dedicated school sites, they received a dollar for dollar credit for school
impact fees.

Salta is going to be paying those fees 100%, so they want to make sure that the City is comparing apples
to apples.
