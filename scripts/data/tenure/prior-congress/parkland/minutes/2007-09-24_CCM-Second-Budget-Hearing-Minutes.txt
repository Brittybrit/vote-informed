City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young offered that staff would make a presentation, if desired by
the Commission.. She noted that no changes had been made.
Commissioner Weissman commented that the City had supported two organizations for many
years: Family Central and the Area Agency on Aging, which had a representative present. He
noted that the City's contributions were approximately $700 less than in previous years, and he
asked that they be funded to the full level.
MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to amend the budget to include full funding of the
contributions to Family Central and the Area Agency on Aging.
Vice Mayor Moskowitz recalled that the City had fully funded the organizations' requests last
year; however, this year the organizations had asked for an increase and the City had not
complied. Ms. Gardner-Young agreed, indicating that the first inclination had been to eliminate
the contributions due to the property tax situation but, after discussion, approximately $4,000 had
been allocated between the two. Since the budget was fully balanced, she noted that the $700
must be pulled from elsewhere.
Commissioner Weissman again stated his MOTION, believing that government had
responsibility toward children, the elderly, and the disabled before anyone else. While he
suggested that the $700 could be taken from the Education Advisory Board, if that was the only
place, he believed the City Manager should make the recommendation.
At the Mayor's request for discussion, Commissioner Weissman stated that he did not want to
limit the contributions to $700 since the figures were approximate; he wanted assurance that the
two organizations would be fully funded.
Vice Mayor Moskowitz recalled discussion regarding a discretionary fund for other use. Ms.
Gardner-Young confirmed that $5,000 had been set aside, with the potential to allocate
approximately $2,400 for the Historical Society and approximately $500 in additional monies for
EAB programs since their request had not been fully funded. She noted that a separate $5,000
had been set aside for the EAB.
Mayor Udine suggested that $2,600. was then unencumbered. He seconded Commissioner
Weissman's MOTION that the $700 be taken from that fund..
City Attorney Andrew Maurodis asked that the MOTION be held as by statute the first
substantive item of business must be the adoption of the millage rate.
Commissioner Weissman indicated that he would bring back the MOTION.
MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to waive the budget presentation. Seconded by
Commissioner Smith.
MOTION passed unanimously.

C. .ORDINANCE NO. 2007-15: ADOPTING THE MILEAGE RATE
SECOND READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF
PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE MILEAGE RATE TO BE
LEVIED FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2007 AND
ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 2008 AND STATING THE PERCENTAGE
INCREASE IN PROPERTY TAXES AS SUCH INCREASE IS DEFINED IN
FLORIDA STATUTES, SECTION 200.065, AND HOUSE BILL 1B,
PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY, AND PROVIDING FOR AN
EFFECTIVE DATE.
City Attorney Andrew Maurod.is read the second reading of Ordinance No. 2007-15.
Mr. Maurodis stated this was being done in compliance with Florida Statute 200.065. The
millage rate to be levied in order to produce sufficient ad valorem taxation revenue, which when
combined with other projected General Fund revenues, will be adequate to pay the appropriations
made in the budget for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2007, and ending September 30,
2008, shall be 3.4083 as the operating millage rate. The 3.4083 mils will result in a five percent
decrease in property taxes. This decrease is the percentage by which the millage rate to be levied
is less than.. the rolled-back rate, which is 3.5877, which is computed according to the guidelines
set forth in Florida Statute 200.054.
Mayor Udine opened to the public. There were no comments or questions, and the Mayor closed
to the public.
Mayor Udine recalled that the fire assessment fee had remained the same as last year.
MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to approve the second reading of Ordinance No.
2007-15, Adopting the Millage Rate. Seconded by Commissioner Smith.
ROLL CALL VOTE:
Vice Ma or Moskowitz Yes
Commissioner Weissman Yes
Commissioner Smith Yes
Mayor Udine Yes
 
MOTION passed unanimously.
