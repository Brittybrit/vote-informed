6. DISCUSSION: FIELD USE PERMITTING
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young said at the last commission meeting, Commissioner
Moskowitz brought up this issue and asked that staff bring back additional information for further
discussion. She added that Parks & Recreation Director Scott Davidoff is present to provide that
information.
Mayor Udine commented that Commissioner Moskowitz’ concern is shared by many on the
Commission and that issue is the father/son rule or mother/daughter rule. He said he would not
vote for a wholesale gutting of the current permitting process, but he feels some flexibility is
desired insofar as, for example, when six young children are playing softball that they do not get
snared in the bureaucracy and can not use a field.
Commissioner Moskowitz agreed with Mayor Udine and said he would like to keep the permitting
in place, but exempt children under the age of 18. He stated he feels that the Commission is
concerned that if there is no permitting, there will be out of town teens and adult leagues coming to
Parkland, which he agrees with. He noted that his concern is kids wanting to play a pickup game,
so if they are exempt, he feels the City will not have the problem in the future that it is having now.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young clarified that the permitting applies when there are more than
10 players, so if there were six 15 year olds who went on the field, they would not be kicked off.
Commissioner Moskowitz commented that this principle is getting lost in translation and there are
stories going back and forth of less than 10 being kicked off the fields. He stated that without
getting into the details of the stories, if the City exempts kids who want to have unorganized
recreational play, whether there are four or 11 kids who want to go out and play tag football, that
would not have anything to do with the leagues and adults coming to Parkland who are still
required to get a permit.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young reiterated that it would be the father and son rule that would
apply and discretion would need to be used.
Commissioner Moskowitz said that he would like to amend the policy and issue an exemption for
kids.
Discussion was held regarding how to differentiate ages and police field users.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young commented that from staff's perspective, allowing adults
unpermitted play is difficult insofar as these are the league people who are coming and utilizing the
fields, who are being told to not wear their league shirts and their coaches are acting like they are
participants in the play.
