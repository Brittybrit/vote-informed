II PURPOSE: 
 
 1. DISCUSSION: GRANT TO YMCA FOR OPERATIONS 
 
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young explained that at the last City Commission meeting, staff brought forth 
a request from the YMCA (“Y”) for a $60,000 grant. Staff was asked to come back with documentation 
from the “Y” indicating their plans to spend those dollars. 
 
Ms. Cheryl Woods, Parkland resident and YMCA, President and CEO along with Valerie Tolson, 
Chairperson of the YMCA Parkland Board and Parkland resident, and Dave Cash, CFO were present to 
answer any questions regarding the grant request. 
 
Commissioner Weissman commented that he received the information as to where the proposed dollars 
would go, however, he would still like to recommend that the additional dollars benefit only new members 
for the first year only as opposed to existing members who are ongoing and full paying. Ms. Woods agreed 
to that request. 
 
Commissioner Moskowitz thanked Ms. Woods for providing the report. He stated that he voted against it 
the last time it was presented, but since it is allocated to Parkland residents and the requested amount has

Ms. Donna Grace, lives at 11009 NW 81 Manor, in Heron Bay and commented that she opposes the 
construction of the water reuse plant in the City of Parkland. Her concerns are for the location, odor, and 
property values. She referenced an article in the Sun-Sentinel newspaper quoting Alan Garcia as being 
opposed to the water re-use plant being constructed in the City of Parkland. She feels if a water reuse plant 
is going to be constructed in Parkland, the residents should have a say in the matter. 
 
Mayor Udine explained that these are parcels of land that are now going to be in the boundary of the City of 
Parkland. He explained that the landowners are voluntarily agreeing to come into the City of Parkland.

 Commissioner Weissman thanked staff for working with the Broward County School Board to secure school
 bus service for the kids that live in the Whittier Oaks neighborhood who attend Riverglades Elementary
 School.
 He commented that there is currently a vacancy on the Police Pension Board. He stated that he read
 somewhere that the City Commission will not appoint someone to the board until the open City Commission
 seat is filled. He stated he would prefer the vacancy be filled since the annuity is in place and the Board has
 to meet four times a year. He mentioned that there is an upcoming Pension Board meeting scheduled for
 September 26,2011 and he will be out of town.
 Further discussion ensued.
 MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to appoint Mayor Udine to the Police Pension Board in the
 n interim until the seat is filled. Seconded by Commissioner Moskowitz.
 MOTION passed unanimously.
 Commissioner Weissman stated that he received a request from the family members of Daniel Agami to
 create a Veteran's park. Commissioner Weissman informed Ms. Agami that she would have to raise funds
 through personal or private donations to erect whatever monument she wanted.
 Commissioner Weissman also commented that there are redistricting maps available. City Manager Caryn
 Gardner-Young clarified that redistricting will be discussed at an upcoming Workshop on September 14,
 2011 at 6:00 PM.
 Commissioner Moskowitz acknowledged the amount of time Commissioner Weissman spent as well as the
 Education Advisory Board members specifically Richard French and David Offstein for their efforts in
 securing bussing for the kids that live in Whittier Oaks who attend Riverglades Elementary School.
