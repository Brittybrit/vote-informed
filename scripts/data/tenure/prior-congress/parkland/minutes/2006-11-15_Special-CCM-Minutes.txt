4. RESOLUTION NO. 2006-88: VICE MAYOR APPOINTING PROCEDURE

COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SETTING FORTH A
PROCEDURE FOR APPOINTING A VICE MAYOR.

ACTION: MOTION ON THE DECISION OF THE COMMISSION

Mayor Udine noted that the purpose of this meeting was to have sworn in Commissioner Mark
Weissman. The City was advised on this date by the Supervisor of Elections that, technically,
they had not yet certified the County election rolls. In an abundance of caution, the Mayor stated
that he had been advised by the City Attorney that the City should not proceed with the actions
scheduled for this meeting and instead would call a Special Meeting for Friday, November 17,
2006, at 5 :05 p.m.

III ADJOURNMENT

Commissioner Moskowitz.

MOTION passed unanimously.
