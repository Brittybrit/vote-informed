III PUBLIC INPUT

Mayor Udine opened to the public.

Jenny Diaz, 6528 NW 103™ Lane, expressed her concern about the scheduled time for practices of
the Parkland Little League team. She also asked what City event is taking place on April 23, 2011
that would allow all the Minor League Baseball games to be cancelled.

Barry Spiegel, 6808 NW 116" Avenue, asked if the City would assist in getting WCI to install a
berm at Old Brooke and Heron Bay.

Stuart Krantz, Pine Tree Estates, commended City staff for the northern sidewalk project. He
expressed concern for residents having to pull into the ditches during emergency vehicle response.
He feels the curbs on the sidewalk and around the trees are too sharp. He complained that the
medians need to be cleared of debris that was left on the Ternbridge Trail.

Annette Friedman, 7272 NW 65% Terrace, representing 19 other residents in the Pine Tree north area
received a code violation for non-maintenance of a drainage ditch behind their homes. She
explained residents were never notified that they were responsible for the maintenance of the
drainage ditch. She stated Pine Tree Water Control District has maintained the area in the past as
well as the City of Parkland. She also explained there is no safe way for the residents to maintain the
area due to the very steep slope. It would also require numerous lawn companies to go through a
private gated community to get to the area to maintain it. She asked the City to look into this matter.

There were no other comments and Mayor Udine closed to the public.

In response to the comment by Ms. Friedman, City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young stated she spoke
with Mr. Ken Cassell of Pine Tree Water Control District regarding the maintenance of the drainage
ditch. Mr. Cassell is willing to bring it before the Pine Tree Water Control District Board to see if
there is any interest in taking over the maintenance.

1. RESOLUTION NO. 2011-13: HELM VARIANCE APPROVAL
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF
THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, DENYING A
VARIANCE REQUEST FROM SECTION 18-354 OF
THE CODE OF ORDINANCES FOR THE PROPERTY
LOCATED AT 7213 NW 65 TERRACE; CASE
NUMBER 03-V-10. (quasi-judicial)
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young explained that Mr. Helm is requesting a 90-day postponement.
She requested advisement from the City Commission whether to proceed with the postponement or
proceed with the actual petition.
It was deferred to Commissioner Weissman because it concerned his district and he recommended
that the item be heard this evening.
Discussion ensued.
Mayor Udine addressed the public and asked if anyone was present who was in favor of the
application to approve the variance.
There was no one present in favor of approval of the variance.
Mr. Helm did not wish to waive his quasi-judicial rights. He stated at the Planning & Zoning Board
meeting, there was a 2-2 deadlock and questions were raised. Neither he nor his team has had a
chance to address the concerns which is why he is asking that this item be tabled until June.
MOTION made by Commissioner Smith to table this item for 90 days. There was no second.
MOTION failed.
Commissioner Weissman stated he was not in favor of approving the variance.
Mayor Udine opened to the public.
Wenz Fonzalefed, 7271 NW 65" Terrace feels there is not enough space to create a swale and the
Ordinance should remain unchanged.
Azita Behmardi, City Engineer, gave a brief history on the development of the property and why it
was recommended for denial.
Mayor Udine closed to the public.

2. RESOLUTION NO. 2011-17: AGREEMENT WITH TRIPLE H
AND DUBUYS
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE

CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA APPROVING AN
AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY OF PARKLAND AND
TRIPLE H RANCH PROPERTY, LTD AND DEBUYS
PROPERTY INVESTMENT GROUP, LTD TO PROVIDE
THAT TRIPLE H RANCH PROPERTY, LTD AND DEBUYS
PROPERTY INVESTMENT GROUP, LTD WILL BEAR THE
COSTS FOR ALL CITY ACTIONS TO USE ITS EMINENT
DOMAIN POWERS TO ACQUIRE AND CONSTRUCT NOB
HILL FROM COUNTY LINE ROAD TO LOX ROAD.

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2011-17: Agreement with

Triple H and Dubuys. She then turned it over to City Attorney Andrew Maurodis who introduced

Mitch Bernstein, Special Council and eminent domain expert. Mr. Bernstein gave a brief explanation

of the eminent domain process and explained what the agreement presented represents.

Dennis Mele, representing Triple H and Dubuys explained that the agreement presented includes the

costs for the acquisition of the Right of Way and design of the four lane road and language that says

they will build whatever road Broward County deems necessary.

Commissioner Weissman had a concern about the cost to construct a four lane highway

futuristically. He asked the developer to put money in escrow to cover the cost to convert from a two

lane highway to a four lane highway.

Mr. Mele further explained why the language should be as indicated in the agreement.

Commissioner Weissman maintained his position on development of the roadway. He explained he

does not want the City of Parkland to incur the expense futuristically.

MOTION made by Mayor Udine to approve Resolution No. 2011-17: Agreement with Triple H and

build a 4 lane highway, they will build the 4 lanes and everything else the Developers have already
agreed to. Either way, the Developer has to eminent domain for the 4 lanes and design for the 4
lanes. If the County tells them from their point of view they only have to pave the two lanes, then
the City will get it from the other developers. Seconded by Commissioner Smith.
