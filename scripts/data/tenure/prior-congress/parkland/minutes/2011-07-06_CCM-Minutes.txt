1. RESOLUTION NO. 2011-50: PARK IMPACT FEE ARRANGEMENT
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING PARK IMPACT FEE
ARRANGEMENT BY AND BETWEEN STANDARD PACIFIC OF
FLORIDA, TRIPLE H RANCH PROPERTY LTD. AND THE CITY
OF PARKLAND.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2011-50: Park Impact Fee Arrangement.
City Attorney Maurodis stated that staff is finalizing the agreement and the resolution will be amended to
read approved substantially in the form as attached subject to final approval by the City Attorney. He
explained the resolution provides for the dedication of a second school site in conjunction with the first
school site on the Triple H property. Ie recalled there was a provision for a school site subject to working
the details out with the Broward County School Board. He stated that Triple I is working cooperatively with
the City and the Broward School Board and is prepared to dedicate both school sites on the Triple H
property. In return, Triple H wants Standard Pacific to pay their public park impact fee as a trade-off which
the City is ok with. The agreement provides for a number of security provisions to ensure this takes place.
He stated that right now Standard Pacific is not in the City, so separate arrangements are required to bond
and sccure the obligation until such time as they do come into the City. He stated that Triple H originally
requested the agreement not to be effective until that happens. The City was not in favor of this so the
revised agreement will indicate that the agrecment is effective immediately. If the School Board ever rejects
the second school site, which the City does not think will happen, Triple H will dedicate the school site to the
City for anything the City wants to build instead of paying the park impact fees. He stated that staff is
recommending the resolution subject to final approval by the City Attorney as to the final form.

City Manager Caryn Gardncr-Young read the title of the second reading of Ordinance No. 2011-15: Parkland
Golf & Country Master Plan. She asked the applicant if he wished to exercise or waive the quasi-judicial
process. Mr. Dennis Mele, Applicant Representative, waived the quasi-judicial procedure on behalf of the
applicant. Ms. Roberta Moore, Planning & Zoning Director gave a brief overview of the Parkland Golf
schematic. She explained the Master Plan Amendment is to increase the total dwelling units from 820 units to
878 units. The increase would be specifically affecting Pods 9,11,13,19 & 20 which is currently vacant. She
explained Pod 9 is currently approved for 40 lots and the applicant is proposing 112 lots changing the size from
200x200 to 65x200. Pod 11 is currently approved for 60 multi-family lots and the applicant is proposing 29
single family lots. Pod 13 is currently 13 single family and the applicant is proposing 20 lots at various widths
and depths. Pod 19 is approved for 6 lots at 130x95 and the applicant is proposing 10 lots at 85x195. Pod 20
currently has 53 lots approved and the applicant is proposing 59 lots with a decrease in the lot sizes. Ms. Moore
explained the notification requirements of the Code of Ordinance for the Planning & Zoning Board and the City.
She stated that the Code of Ordinance does not require mailing notifications, but does require advertisement
notifications. She stated that advertisement is required to be 10 days prior to the public hearing. The Planning
& Zoning Board met on May 12, 2011 and the City notified all residents within 500 feet. Although not required,
the courtesy was provided on April 29, 2011. The Code of Ordinance also requires advertisement in the
newspaper which was done on April 27, 2011 in the Sun-Sentinel. For first reading, the Code does not require
this type of petition to be noticed or advertised in the newspaper. For tonight’s meeting, no mailing was
required and the Code required 10 days notification in the newspaper. The City provided a courtesy notification
to residents within 500 feet of the property line sent out on June 24, 2011 and advertised in the Sun-Sentinel on
June 26, 2011. Staff is recommending approval of the Ordinance based on the attached conditions of approval
in the back-up. The Planning & Zoning Board approved the item based on staff’s recommendation and the
conditions. On first reading, the City Commission recommended approval subject to providing a financial
impact analysis as well as the elevations for Pod 9.
Vice Mayor Rosenof explained that a study was done on the overall economic impact and staff as well as the
applicant agreed to a number of $165,480.00. Nancy Morando, Finance Director explained that a consultant

explain the breakdown of the methodology for the additional revenue.
