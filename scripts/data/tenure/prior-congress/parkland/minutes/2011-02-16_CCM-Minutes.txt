1 PRESENTATION: SOUTH FLORIDA WATER MANAGEMENT
WATER SHORTAGE
Carol Morris with the South Florida Water Management District (SFWMD) gave a brief presentation
on the current water conditions. She explained the SFWMD has activated their emergency operations
system for water shortages. They have issued water shortage warnings in certain areas throughout
Florida. She explained there is a need for water conservation now. She encouraged municipal
partners to make the “water shortage watch button” link available on its websites so residents and
constituents can easily access information on the current water shortage along with water
conservation tips.
2, DISCUSSION: THE WEDGE MASTER PLAN
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young gave a brief explanation about the item being presented then
introduced Ms. Dodie Lazowick of Keith & Associates who gave an update.
Ms. Lazowick updated the City Commission on where they were so far in the process of the Wedge
Master Planning. She stated since being brought on board in mid-December 2010, she was able to
hold a kickofT meeting with City staff. She explained that she followed up with NSID for another

kickoff meeting in January 2011. The kickoff meetings allowed them to identify seven (7) goals.
They are as follows:

1. Density - Livable Communities and Neighborhoods

2. Transportation Infrastructure — Access and mobility of the area

3. Public Safety

4. Utilities & Public Facilities

5. Parks, Open Space, & Recreational Uses

6. Education Facilities

7. Environmental Consideration in the area
She mentioned there was an open house at City Hall in which they invited all stake holders, land
owners, HOAs, and others who expressed interest regarding the wedge. She will provide a report in
March 2011 on the feedback from written and verbal comments received as a result of the open
house.
Ms. Lazowick explained they have met with other governmental agencies such as Broward County,
Broward County School Board, Palm Beach County, South Florida Water Management District,
Army Corp of Engineers, Parkland Utilities, FPL and FDOT.
She mentioned her plan is to come back before the City Commission by the March 16, 2011 City
Commission Workshop. They are looking for input and direction from the Commission.
Mayor Udine acknowledged that Chris Akagbosu, School Board of Broward County Growth
Management Department Director was present in the audience.
Commissioner Weissman asked if Ms. Lazowick reached out to all of the landowners who were not
included in the local bill this year. He asked if there were any discussions with the landowners about
being included in the local bill next year. Ms. Lazowick responded they have not been pressing the
annexation rather they have focused on the master planning and intended uses.
Commissioner Moskowitz asked what is the timeline for all unincorporated areas to be annexed. He
asked should the City be working with Broward County about forcing the rest of the properties to be
annexed into Parkland.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young responded as more parcels come into Parkland, it makes it
harder for Broward County to provide services. It then becomes easier for the City to request a letter
from Broward County stating they want parcels to be annexed in. The letter can then be taken to the
Broward Delegation to recommend the rest of the landowners be annexed in.
Commissioner Moskowitz asked how many homes were anticipated to be built in the wedge.
Ms. Lazowick responded if the homes were built to the maximum which is 2 units per acre, it would
be approximately 4,200 - 4,500 homes; however it is not likely due to the tracts of land with large
lakes or commercial components. It also depends on the type of units being built i.e. multi-family or
single family.

Commissioner Moskowitz also questioned if there was going to be a recommendation to move the fire

station to the wedge.

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young replied there will be a recommendation to move the fire station

to the wedge piece so there will be sufficient coverage. Staff is working very closely with the Coral

Springs Fire Chief so three stations can be constructed instead of four.

Commissioner Moskowitz wanted clarification of what he has heard that the Broward County School

Board will only build an elementary school and a high school — not 2 middle school in the wedge.

Ms. Lazowick stated the School Board indicated to her that the high school is required as part of the

Hendrix annexation parcel. She mentioned Hendrix is not looking to develop for many years. So the

School Board's position is that they will not make a determination until the Hendrix come in and at

the time of platting.

Chris Akagbosu, School Board of Broward County Growth Management Director stated they have

not taken this specifically to the School Board for formal acceptance, but will be doing so. He stated

School Board staff have expressed all three (3) school sites will be pursued.

Commissioner Moskowitz asked what is the status of the 10-acre parcel next to Parkland Goif &

Country Club.

Ms. Gardner-Young replied because the market is so bad, the School Board is not planning to sell at

this time.

Commissioner Moskowitz also asked what other municipal buildings might be needed.

Ms. Lazowick replied there will be a BSO substation and maintenance facilities at the Parks.

Otherwise there is no indication that the City needs to expand any other city facilities into the wedge.

Vice Mayor Rosenof thanked Ms. Lazowick for the great job at the Charette. He asked if there was

going to be a move to do any kind of covenants within the Master Plan i.e. tree types, street sign types

to bring continuity to the different developments.

Ms. Lazowick replied that it was not in their scope of services at this time.

Vice Mayor Rosenof said he would also like to see smaller pedestrian friendly parks within each

development.

6. ORDINANCE NO. 2011-03: HANDICAPPED PARKING
COMMENTS: FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY

COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
AMENDING CHAPTER 20 ENTITLED “TRAFFIC AND
MOTOR VEHICLES”; AMENDING SECTION 20-4
ENTITLED “STOPPING, STANDING, OR PARKING
PROHIBITED IN SPECIFIED PLACES”; ADDING
SECTION 20-4(B) ENTITLED “ENFORCEMENT OF
PARKING REQUIREMENTS FOR PERSONS WHO HAVE
DISABILITIES”; CONTAINING A PROVISION FOR
INCLUSION IN THE CITY CODE, CONTAINING
SEVERABILITY CLAUSE, CONTAINING AN
EFFECTIVE DATE.

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of the First Reading of Ordinance No. 2011-03:

Handicapped Parking. She then turned it over to Ms. Roberta Moore, Planning & Zoning Director

who gave a brief explanation of the item being presented. Ms. Moore briefly detailed what the new

Ordinance proposes.

Mayor Udine clarified for the public's point of view if there is a handicapped parking violation, the

City can still site now, but it has to be sited under State statute.

Mayor Udine asked that Staff keep track of how much the City receives as a result of the fines for

violation of the Handicap Ordinance. He wants the Commission to consider using some of the

generated revenues to enhance the Buddies program.

Mayor Udine opened to the public.

Karen Van Ash, 5864 NW 74™ Street commented that she works for parking with a different

municipality. She stated the majority of the handicapped parking tickets get dismissed due to the

parking placards not being put up in vehicles. She stated that the courts have dismissed the

handicapped violations very quickly. She suggested that the City place in its Ordinance that no one

can park on the markings which the courts must uphold by State statute.

There were no other comments and Mayor Udine closed to the public.

MOTION made by Commissioner Moskowitz to approve on first reading Ordinance No. 2011-03:

Handicapped Parking. Seconded by Commissioner Weissman.
