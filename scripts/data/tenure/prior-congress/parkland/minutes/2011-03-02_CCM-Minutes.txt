IN THE MATTER OF CITY OF PARKLAND V. FLORIDA
HIGHWAY PRODUCTS.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2011-14: Florida Highway Products
Settlement. She then introduced the outside counsel Attorney Stuart Michelson. He gave a brief explanation
of the item being presented.

<<PG>>

Mayor Udine gave a brief history of how the lawsuit came into effect. He explained there was an alleged
defect in the cold mix process that was used to pave a road in the Ranches. This process caused some
seepage into the canals and the City had to spend significant dollars to clean the canals, As a result, the City
commenced a lawsuit against the company that paved the road. After some mediation, it was determined that
the road needed to be taken out and replaced using the traditional hot asphalt method. Through negotiations,
Florida Highway Products agreed to fix the road using the traditional more expensive method at no cost to
the City. In addition, a monetary amount of $150,000 of which $100,000 will be paid out over the next two
years in equal monthly payments and the balance of $50,000 will be paid to the City within five years of
today’s meeting date. Attorney Michelson stated the contractor is obligated to start the road repaving within
21 days of March 2, 2011.

6. PRESENTATION: LIBRARY SURVEY

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young turned it over to Mr. Scott Davidoff, Parks & Recreation Director who
gave a brief presentation on the results of the Library Survey. He explained that the online survey consisted
of 28 questions regarding programming, library services, computer usage and demographics. He stated a
