II PURPOSE:
SWEARING IN DISTRICT III COMMISSIONER
DAVID ROSENOF
Mayor Udine invited Commissioner -Elect David Rosenof and Judge Louis Schiff to the podium.
Judge Schiff administered the Oath of Office to Commissioner Rosenof, and he took his place on
the dais.
County Commissioner Ilene Lieberman first apologized for the absence of Representative Stacey
Ritter, who would have liked to attend. Commissioner Lieberman spoke regarding the dedication
of the City's elected officials and staff, and she believed that Commissioner Rosenof would join a
great group of people. She presented him a proclamation declaring this date as "David Rosenof
Appreciation Day" in Broward County, signed by the entire Broward County Commission.
Congratulating him, Commissioner Lieberman welcomed him to the Parkland family.
Commissioner Rosenof spoke regarding the solemn words of his Oath of Office, which he had
approached with humility, reverence, and excitement. He expressed his appreciation to his wife,
his daughters, and friends, and to previous and present Commission members for their support
and encouragement. He promised passionate, impersonal debates with the best interests of the
City in mind, and he hoped for creative compromises and new solutions. He thanked City staff
for their help, and he looked forward to working with all. While he acknowledged the differences
of the east side, the Commissioner assured that more interests were shared: the importance of the
City's children and the schools, public safety, and continuing to lead by example with
environmental issues. He pledged to be part of the solutions to tight budgetary constraints.
Commissioner Rosenof stated that he would not take his incumbency for granted and would use
the next ten months to get as much accomplished as possible.
III ADJOURNMENT
