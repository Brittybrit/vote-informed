2. RESOLUTION NO. 2009-04: CITY HALL WINDOWS
BID AWARD
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE
AWARD OF BID NO. 200843 TO WIN -TECH PRODUCTS,
INC., FOR THE PURPOSE OF REMOVAL AND
REPLACEMENT OF DAMAGED WINDOW AND DOORS AT
THE CITY HALL BUILDING IN THE AMOUNT OF
$328,116.77, PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young read the title of Resolution No. 2009 -04, City Hall
Windows Bid Award and introduced Public Works Director, Jim Berkman and Engineering
Project Manager, Horacio Danovich to represent this item.
Mr. Berkman gave a brief explanation of the issues with the City Hall windows and advised Win -
Tech was the lowest responsible and responsive bidder on the project.
Commissioner Moskowitz advised that he wanted to be on record stating that he had an issue
with the changing costs for the project and subsequent 33% increase in the quote. He stated
he was not happy with the situation.
Mayor Udine commented he understood that there were some critical windows that need to be
replaced though he noted concern regarding replacing all the windows at a cost of $328,116.77.
He suggested using the monies received from the insurance company to replace the critical
windows and delay the remainder.
Commissioner Rosenof stated he felt all the windows should be replaced now since they were all
from the same factory in order to avoid future damages.
Mayor Udine requested staff to clarify what can be done with the amount received from the
insurance company settlement.
Mr. Berkman reviewed the situation and noted there are 41 critical windows and doors. He stated
that the insurance settlement would cover this part of the project. At the Mayor's question, Mr.
Berkman advised that there is a de- lamination problem which affects the windows' ability to
withstand an impact during a storm. Some of the most critical windows have water intrusion and
wood rot.
