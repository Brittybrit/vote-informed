1. PRESENTATION: PARK SURVEY RESULTS
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young introduced Mr. Scott Davidoff, Parks & Recreation Director
to give a brief presentation on the results of the Annual Park Survey. He explained that during
November and December, 2010, the City issued the 3™ Annual participant survey for Parks and
Recreation users. The survey sought online responses about facilities, customer service, and
demographics. He explained that out of a total of 242 responses received, the overall result
showed a 90.7% very satisfied/satisfied rating.
He reported that the number of participants went down overall partly due to it being the third year
of sending out the survey and people are used to seeing it; 81% of the survey completion was
done by Parkland residents along with other survey results in different categories.
Mayor Udine commented that he was amazed at the number of responses to the survey, He said
it has been a marked improvement as a direct result of the artificial turf fields.
Commissioner Moskowitz also commented that past issues regarding the parks are seemingly
non-existent and he feels the City has a much better handle on the parks in general and that is in
part due to Mr. Davidoff’s leadership. Commissioner Moskowitz commented about the T-ball
fields being converted to softball or baseball fields at Pine Trails Park.
Commissioner Moskowitz also commented that he attended a City Commission meeting at the
City of Hallandale and they discussed the location for a dog park. He stated that he hopes a dog
park is something the City of Parkland can provide to its residents by the end of 2011.
Commissioner Smith congratulated Mr. Davidoff and the Parks & Recreation staff for the fine
job they arc doing.
