3. DISCUSSION: CITY OF PARKLAND - 5K RACE NAME AND CHARITY

City Manager deferred to Mr. Scott Davidoff, Parks & Recreation Director for a brief update. He
explained that at the May 2, 2012 Regular City Commission Meeting, Mr. Ron Madally representing
the Northwest Broward Road Runners Club spoke regarding distribution of funds for the City of
Parkland 5K Race. Mr. Davidoff explained that the City Commission provided some direction and
indicated that a portion of the funds would go towards Barkland — Parkland’s Dog Park. Mr.
Davidoff further explained that Mr. Madally sent a request that a specific charity be named as the
main benefactor to generate additional revenue.
Mr. Madally explained the rationale for the selected charity. The Tri-County Humane Society was
selected because it deals with disease research, dog adoptions, etc. which is directly related to the
Dog Park.
Commissioner Moskowitz expressed that he is happy that the City is doing something for a no kill
shelter. He commented on an email that provided some proposed percentages of distribution of
funds. There was consensus of the Commission that 30% would be distributed to the Humane
Society, 20% to the dog park, and 50% to the Education Advisory Board.
Mayor Udine stated, for the record, that he is a member of the Northwest Broward Road Runners
Club.
Commissioner Rosenof posed a question if it was necessary to determine the split now.
Discussion ensued.
MOTION made by Commissioner Moskowitz that approximately 30% to the Humane Society, 20%
to the dog park and 50% to the City to determine at a later time further distribution. Seconded by
Vice Mayor Weissman.
Mayor Udine opened to the public. There were no comments and he closed to the public.

RFP AWARD
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING THE
PROPOSERS’ RANKINGS PER EXHIBIT A AND
AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE A CONTRACT
WITH CRW SYSTEMS FOR LAND DEVELOPMENT
(PERMITS AND CODE) SOFTWARE SYSTEM.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2012-56: Land Development IT
Software RFP Award. Mr. Steve Pizzillo, Building Official explained that the Development Services
Department is currently using a software program that is 17 years old and is not being fully maintained
by the developer. He stated that this software would bring the Development Services Department on
the cutting edge of its permit review process and staff recommends its approval.
Commissioner Moskowitz had several concerns about the return on investment.
Mr. Pizzillo explained that currently over 200 hundred cities across the country utilize the program,
and staff physically visited Pine Crest in Dade County and Lantana in Palm Beach County for a site
visit of its use. Ms. Gardnér-Young also explained that this software will eliminate property owner
complaints about not knowing the status of their permits and will allow 24-hour viewing. Mr. Pizzillo
explained that a time study was performed and utilizing the program would save approximately 1-1/2
hour per day on an Inspector and over a year’s time, it approximates to well over a six figure savings.
Commissioner Moskowitz expressed that the cost savings should be the justification in the analysis.
MOTION made by Vice Mayor Weissman to approve Resolution No. 2012-56: Land Development IT
Software RFP Award. Seconded by Commissioner Rosenof.

OF THE COMPREHENSIVE PLAN TO COMPLY WITH
FLORIDA STATUTES AND THE FLORIDA
ADMINISTRATIVE CODE AND TO ENABLE
CONSISTENCY WITH STATE, REGIONAL AND LOCAL
REGULATIONS; PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
(first reading 3/7/12)
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title on second reading of Ordinance No. 2012-06:
Amending Comprehensive Plan Public School Facilities Element, She explained that this Ordinance is
State mandated and the City of Parkland has to be in compliance with the Broward County School
District requirements. This instrument was sent to all the necessary entities and the City of Parkland
received no objections and staff is recommending its adoption.
Mayor Udine opened to the public. There were no comments and he closed to the public.
MOTION made by Vice Mayor Weissman to approve on second reading Ordinance No. 2012-06:
Amending Comprehensive Plan Public School Facilities Element. Seconded by Commission Willis.
ROLL CALL VOTE:
Commissioner Rosenof Yes
Vice Mayor Weissman Yes
Commissioner Willis
Mayor Udine
MOTION passed 4-1.
