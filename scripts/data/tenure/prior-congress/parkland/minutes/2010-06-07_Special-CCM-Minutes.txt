City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young introduced Landscape Architect Planner Brian Archer, who
gave a brief overview of the process the selection committee went through in reviewing this bid
proposal.
Landscape Architect Planner Archer’s comments included:
e The selection committee reviewed the 11 proposals for compliance with the proposal
format.
e The committee performed a thorough review of each proposal and unanimously
determined that the proposals had significant omissions.
e The omissions were so varied that the committee was unable to put together a consistent
set of proposals for the Commission to consider.
e It was a unanimous decision of the committee to recommend rejection of all 11 proposals
and to re-issue the RFP.
MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to reject all proposals submitted under RFP
#2010-03, Emergency Debris Management and Disaster Recovery Assistance. Seconded by
Commissioner Rosenof.
City Attorney Andrew Maurodis said the Commission had been given the RFPs and had an
opportunity to look at them; this is the recommendation of staff and he feels it is appropriate,
given the circumstances.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young stated that all of the proposers were notified of this meeting
and none of them are in the audience.
ROLL CALL VOTE:
Vice Mayor Smith
Mayor Udine
MOTION passed unanimously by those present.
Discussion was held regarding the process and timetable for re-issuing the RFP, short-listing by
the selection committee, possible interviews and determination by the Commission, and debris
services during the interim period.
Mayor Udine clarified that the Commission would not lock into a date for when they will
consider the RFP submittals; it would either be at a special meeting on June 30" or, if there is not
sufficient time, at the regular meeting on July 7h

City Attorney Andrew Maurodis stated that the City had just received a request from the Broward
County Planning Council for confirmation that the City has no objection to three trafficways plan
amendments regarding the Triple H/Debuys land use plan amendment. He advised that, in
accordance with Charter provisions, the Commission must unanimously approve taking this
matter up before any discussion or action is taken.

MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to discuss trafficways issues for the Triple
H/Debuys land use plan amendment. Seconded by Vice Mayor Smith.

MOTION passed unanimously.

City Attorney Maurodis described the three trafficways plan amendments as follows: the
addition of Loxahatchee Road from State Road 7 to the area east of the levees, known by
Broward County as the Urban Area, at a width of 94 feet; the addition of University Drive from
County Line Road to Loxahatchee, at a width of 94 feet; and the addition of Nob Hill Road from
County Line Road to Loxahatchee, at a width of 94 feet.

City Attorney Maurodis advised that the Commission is not obligated to make a decision at this
meeting, but if there is no objection, City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young will sign the letter to
the Planning Council.

Mayor Udine spoke briefly about the three trafficways and asked if any of the Commissioners felt
uncomfortable having City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young sign the letter.

There were no objections.

City Attorney Maurodis confirmed that the Commission had given the city manager the
authorization needed.

II ADJOURNMENT

MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to adjourn the June 7, 2010 Special City
Commission meeting at 6:30 p.m. Seconded by Commissioner Rosenof.

MOTION passed unanimously.
