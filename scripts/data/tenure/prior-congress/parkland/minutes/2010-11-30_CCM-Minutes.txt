e The City’s position on the Pine Hollow issue regarding the lights is that lights above 10 feet are
not allowed which is why a code enforcement case was launched. He commented that staff
would have to be consulted regarding the submission of a lighting site plan because he is not
involved in the Special Master process. In regards to the height issue, he explained that an
outside attorney gave an opinion that the height was allowed by the Farm Act which is what staff
went by. The City has no interest in committing resources to try to file a lawsuit against Mr.
Dvorkin to take down the arena unless the City is going to seek another opinion to reverse the
opinion on record.

Comments by Mayor:

e Mayor said the attorney came twice to the City to give an update on Pine Hollow.

e Mayor addressed comments made previously regarding Pine Hollow. He stated that the red
carpet was not rolled out to Mr. Dvorkin to build this structure at Pine Hollow. He stated this
was first denied by staff and that is when Mr. Dvorkin engaged counsel. He resubmitted his
documents and it went before our independent attorney. He commented that the lights were over
the allowed height and now it is a code case. If a site plan was submitted and it is over the
allowed requirement, it will be denied.

Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of the second reading of Ordinance No. 2010-23: Parkland Golf &
Country Club Amending Ordinance No. 2002-04.
The applicant, Ms. Cammy Corbit, representing Toll Brothers, waived their quasi-judicial rights.
Roberta Moore then gave a brief presentation of the item presented.
Mayor Udine stated he was going to vote “no” on the approval since the dialogue between Toll Brothers
and the Equity Owners never took place.
Commissioner Moskowitz suggested that this item be tabled to give time to resolve any issues rather than
voting “no”.
MOTION made by Commissioner Weissman to table the item until the first meeting in January.
Seconded by Commissioner Smith.
Motion was withheld.
Further discussion ensued.
Ms. Corbit asked Ron Blum to give the City Commission an update on what Toll Brothers has done so
far.
Ron Blum, representing Toll Brothers, stated that they have met with approximately 150 members for a |
meet & greet and discussions about what they are planning to do in the community. He stated he would
like to see the amendment approved to avoid delays.
Mayor Udine explained to Ms. Corbit that Toll Brothers should have a Town Hall Meeting at the
Parkland Golf and Country Clubhouse and let the residents and equity members have a forum to ask
questions and get answers before it comes back to the City Commission for a vote.
Further discussion ensued.

Market there were 1,200 compared to 800 the year before. .

: participate.

e The City Commission advised Parkland Reserve that on Pine Island from Trails End to Nob Hill
that they are responsible for installation and maintenance of that area. Under the development
order, the City cannot issue building permits if the median maintenance agreement is not
resolved — the Median Maintenance Agreement is a tri-party agreement between the County, City
and the HOA. The County has been a stumbling block for the City. She feels the City should
continue to issue building permits because it is in the resident’s best interest as well as the City.
There may come back and discuss further if the issues can’t be resolved.
