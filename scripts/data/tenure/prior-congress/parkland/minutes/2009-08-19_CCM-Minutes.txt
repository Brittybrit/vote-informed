1. DISCUSSION: CODE ENFORCEMENT OVERVIEW
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young said there was a request at a previous Commission meeting
for an overview of the code enforcement services provided by BSO and she introduced Chief
Paul O'Connell, BSO Police Chief, who gave a presentation.
Chief O'Connell thanked the Commission and introduced Code Inspector Delontay Dumas and
Code Enforcement Supervisor Lt. Ed Rosende. Chief O'Connell provided a power point
presentation along with background information and an overview of what the Broward Sheriff's
Office has done since the BSO contract for services was amended to include code enforcement.
Chief O'Connell's comments included the following:
• Before BSO took over code enforcement, between April 2005 and March 2007, there
were a total of 86 cases brought before the Special Master or the Code Board.
• After BSO took over, from May 2007 to April 2009, 831 cases were brought before the
Special Master; 921 cases through the end of July 2009
• Commercial vehicle enforcement was undertaken; Phase I involved the main roads and
Phase II focused on the Ranches.
• During the summer of 2008, 152 properties within the Ranches were inspected and
photographed to ensure any licensed business was done in compliance with City
Ordinances.
• Identified 33 different properties that were conducting businesses; five properties were in
non - compliance.
• Subsequent to notification, three of the five properties came into compliance; two of the
five ended up in litigation. Of those two, one was settled and one is still in litigation.
• Upon receipt of complaints of ongoing non - permitted use following the initial inspection,
covert surveillance was conducted by BSO's detective bureau
• Several properties, businesses, and residents of the Ranches were identified as doing non-
permitted use and are now in the process of coming into compliance.
• The goal is to get everyone in compliance; that is an ongoing process and does not stop.
• BSO code enforcement unit has also been tasked with enforcement of swale maintenance
in the Pine Tree Estates area; each property has been inspected twice.
• 741 properties were identified as non - compliant with the code; presently, five of those
properties are still non - compliant and have active liens against them. Forty of the
properties are still non - compliant, but have been given extensions because of the wet
season.

5. CITY MANAGER REVIEW
Mayor Udine said he feels City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young is doing a very good job as city
manager, but there should be no increase in compensation this year because of the economic
times and the fact that other employees did not get an increase. Mayor Udine said she does a
great job and is an extremely hard worker; she has saved the City a lot of money, especially by
doing the work on the employee handbook.
Mayor Udine commented that City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young attends many City functions
and is very visible in the community; she attends Chamber of Commerce meetings and is very
involved with the schools. He added that there are sometimes difficult issues with employees,
but she has their respect and there is good teamwork. Mayor Udine said he wishes the
Commission could give her a raise or bonus, but this is not the year.
Vice Mayor Weissman said in reviewing the evaluations, he rated City Manager Caryn Gardner-
Young the highest. He stated that these are tough times and when you are laying people off and
employees are not getting increases, it is hard to give her a raise. Vice Mayor Weissman spoke
about the difficult residents and said City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young does an excellent job
with them and with all the Commissioners. He added that he hopes when things are better
financially the Commission remembers this and compensates accordingly.
Commissioner Smith thanked City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young for all the time she takes to
help him and for making the most of City revenues and human resources.
Commissioner Rosenof spoke about his experience with reviews and said his criticisms of City
Manager Caryn Gardner -Young need to be taken with a grain of salt. He added he knows she
wants to improve, no matter how good she is.
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young thanked the Commission and said she believes she has a
very good working relationship with them. City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young added she
