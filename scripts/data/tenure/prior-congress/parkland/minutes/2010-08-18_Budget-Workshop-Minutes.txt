II PURPOSE:

1. DISCUSSION: 2010-2011 CITY OF PARKLAND BUDGET
Mayor Udine said the order of business is the Budget Workshop and he turned the meeting over
to City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young said this is the City Manager’s proposed budget to the City
Commission. The workshop is the opportunity for the City Commission to give staff additional
guidance in regards to how they would like to see next year’s budget amounts be expended and
look at the revenues that are being proposed. Staff is present for questions from the Commission.
Finance Director Nancy Morando has prepared a short overview of the budget.
Ms. Gardner-Young reported that the budget is balanced. The budget does not reflect an increase
in the TRIM rate. The direction of the Commission was followed for the Fire and Waste
Management assessments and maintaining the millage rate.
Ms. Gardner-Young commented that she met with Commissioner Rosenof prior to the meeting to
discuss some of his concerns and comments. Commissioner Rosenof emphasized on the capital
aspect of the budget. There are two unfunded projects that he indicated he would like the
Commission to consider. The first is the Australian Pines located at the Old City Hall site that
are adjacent to the cell tower. They could be removed for a cost of $6,000. His concern is that
there could be a problem with them during a hurricane because they are not hardy trees. The
second thing is there is no signage going north on State Road 7 on the medium to indicate Mecca
Blvd. or 76" Place. The City contacted FDOT and requested signs, they indicated they would
not provide, however they stated that if the City was interested they would allow the City to place
signs on FDOT property. That would cost the City $7,000 to accomplish.
Commissioner Weissman said that he does not have a problem removing the invasives.
However, he commented on the signage saying that this is a state road and the cost will not just
be $7,000 because it will cost the City to replace the signs if the signs are damaged or knocked
down however many times that may occur. He feels since it is not one of the City roadways, the
City should not have to go to that expense.
Mayor Udine stated that there are locator signs approaching Holmberg Road. He agreed about
the Australian Pines, however he felt one of the reasons that the Commission choose the tree cell
tower was for it to blend in. He said it will not blend in if those trees come down so the trees
would need to be replaced. He said they would need to look at the whole cost then.
Ms. Gardner-Young said that staff would get those numbers. Ms. Gardner-Young turned the
meeting over to Ms. Morando.
Ms. Morando preceded with her presentation and overview. (see attached power point ). She
said she would be covering the TRIM and millage rate, the Fire Assessment, the Solid Waste

The following are the City Commission questions and comments:

Mayor Udine asked for the budget presentation be placed on the website. He feels that staff has
done a great job again this year in trying to work with what there is and understanding the fiscal
environment that all governments are in.

Mayor Udine commented about a decrease in funding in the library from last year to this year.
He explained that the Friends of the Library were holding off on making a donation to the library
this year. He said he would like to go to the Friends of the Library and offer that if they come up
with a donation maybe the City could come up with some money to match their donation. He
feels that it would send the right message about the City education priorities and to those that use
the library.

Mayor Udine added that as he looks through the different departments most departments show a
decrease in year over year operating costs. He said if there are any increases it is because of
health insurance increases prorating through the specific departments. He said he was glad the
City could pick up the increase in the Health Insurance. He said in this economic climate the
employees did not receive any type of increases again this year. He said that staff is being asked
to do more with less people and they are doing a good job. The Mayor added that the City was
proactive in the last couple of years with eliminating positions and therefore Parkland is in a
better position than most cities who are being forced to increase their millage rates. Mayor Udine
commented on an idea Commission Moskowitz had about offering incentives to staff to be cost
conscious and saving City dollars when able to.

Commissioner Weissman commented in regards to the Mayor's comments that the Commission
set aside $60,000 to be used toward rewarding staff for finding ways to save the City money.
Mayor stated that he just wants to make sure the City Manager’s office is able to come up with
and monitor a process to be used.

Mayor Udine commented for the public, that in the past the City did not have the reserves that
have been put in place over the last couple of years. The City is trying to do the right thing to set
aside a reserve in case of an emergency like a hurricane.

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young added a comment about the money being taken from
reserves and how she challenged staff saying the more staff saved during the fiscal year, less
money would have to come from reserves. She added that she has a great staff and they did a
great job with wants vs. needs and they have saved a considerable amount over the years. The
dollars saved over those years is the excess dollars being expended now.

Mayor Udine also pointed out that the City has been doing a good job on all levels of going out
and finding grant money that is out there and making very good use of the grant and private
funding being received for capital projects.

He said that they have put together a fiscally conservative budget and they have not had to touch
services.

Mayor Udine commented that there should be no need for another scheduled budget workshop,
however mentioned again the resident budget meetings scheduled for August 23 at 10 am and
August 24 at 6:30 pm for the public to meet with the City Manager and Finance Director for
whatever questions they may have.
