11 PURPOSE: 2010-2011 BUDGET
Mayor Udine opened the meeting and turned the meeting over to the City Manager.
City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young stated the Finance Director Nancy Morando created a
presentation that would encompass the millage, the fire assessment and the solid waste as well as
the budget. With the City Commission approval, they could first hear the presentation and then
the Ordinances could be read and voted on.
Ms. Nancy Morando gave a brief overview of the Budget.

Commissioner Rosenof asked about the $260,000 million dollars that was lost because of the

Chinese drywall valuations and wanted to know if when those homes are remediated and

reassessed how long will it take before the City sees those dollars back in the tax roll.

Ms. Morando said there is no easy answer however if a home was remediated in the next couple

months and once it goes through the building permit process and the County reviews and

approves it, the dollars would not show in the tax rolls until next year.

Mayor Udine clarified by explaining that in the middle of the City preparation of this year’s

budget, the Florida legislature passed a law that allowed a home that had Chinese drywall to let

the building component be assessed at zero for tax purposes. That was a huge hit on the City

budget.

Vice Mayor Smith commented about the amount of home based businesses in Parkland and

wondered how many actually had occupational licenses.

Mayor said that is done through code enforcement and has to be constantly checked on.

1. ORDINANCE NO. 2010-21: ADOPTING MILLAGE RATE
COMMENTS: FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY

COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE MILLAGE RATE TO BE
LEVIED FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING
OCTOBER 1, 2010 AND ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 2011;
AND STATING THE PERCENTAGE CHANGE IN
PROPERTY TAXES AS SUCH CHANGE IS DEFINED IN
FLORIDA STATUTES, SECTION 200.065; PROVIDING
FOR SEVERABILITY, AND PROVIDING FOR AN
EFFECTIVE DATE.

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the first reading of Ordinance No. 2010-21, Adopting

the Millage Rate.

City Attorney Andrew Maurodis read into the record the following:

“With the City of Parkland as the taxing authority the millage rate for the fiscal year beginning

October 1, 2010 and ending September 30, 2011 to be levied in order to produce sufficient ad

valorem taxation revenue which when combined with other projected general fund revenues will

be adequate to pay the appropriations made in the budget for fiscal year beginning October 1,

2010 and ending September 30, 2011 shall be the proposed operating millage rate is 4.0198. The

proposed adopted millage rate of 4.0198 mils is 8.31% less than the roll back rate of 4.3841 and

is computed according to the guidelines set forth in Florida Statute 200.065.”

Mayor Udine opened to the public.

Stuart Krantz, Pine Tree Estates, said he objected to the millage rate as it stands. He would
object to the roll back rate as well. He said his total tax dollars went up $600 this year which is
about 15%. He said the rest of the Parkland residents may be happy but he is not. He does not
think the City can afford this. He feels the Western Fire Station permanent building needs to be
built. He asked the Commission how many years are they not going to give staff raises and still
keep moral up. He talked about the new conference room and the lack of public meeting space.
He spoke about the YMCA and he resents the $400,000 the City has given them over the years.
He also spoke about invasives.
There were no further comments from the public. Mayor Udine closed to the public.
Commissioner Moskowitz addressed some of the comments made by Mr. Krantz. He said the
City has not given the YMCA $400,000. He clarified that the City has not charged the Y rent.
He said the Y can not afford the rent and the City can not afford to operate the community
center.
Commissioner Moskowitz said he agreed with Mr. Krantz about the fire station and the
Commission continues to raise money from developers for this. He added that staff is in the
process of drafting a long term invasives plan, however this can not happen over night because
you can not just remove the trees, you have to replace them. He spoke about staff not getting a
cost of living adjustment in the last two years and wanted to talk to the Commission about doing
some kind of COLA.
Vice Mayor Smith spoke about the economy today and that people are happy to have a job. He
also spoke about the City picking up the 8% increase in the health insurance plan.
MOTION made by Vice Mayor Smith to approve Ordinance No. 2010-21 Adopting the Millage
Rate as stated. Seconded by Commissioner Moskowitz.
ROLL CALL VOTE:

Vice Mayor Smith

Mayor Udine
MOTION passed unanimously by those present.

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2010-75, Fire Rescue
Assessment.
Mayor Udine explained that this is the City’s Fire Rescue Assessment, which went out on the
TRIM notice at $210 per household.
Commissioner Moskowitz said he felt that it should be lowered to $199 and they could do
another raise next year in order to split the increase rather than doing it in one year.
Commissioner Rosenof stated that staff has done an admirable job of balancing this budget in
light of the current economic circumstances and feels they should stay with the Fire Assessment
Fee that keeps the budget balanced at this point.
MOTION by Vice Mayor Smith to approve Resolution No. 2010-75, Fire Rescue Assessment at
the rate of $210. Seconded by Commissioner Rosenof.
Mayor Udine opened to the public. There were no comments or questions from the public.
Mayor Udine closed to the public.
Mayor Udine said that he will vote for the $210.00 this year. He said the City has not raised the
fee since it was implemented and he feels this is the year it needs to be done. He added that
Parkland is one of nine cities that have not increased the millage rate and have one of the lowest
tax rates of any municipality.
ROLL CALL VOTE:

Vice Mayor Smith
:

Mayor Udine
MOTION passed by a vote of 3 to 1.
