DISCUSSION: WASTE MANAGEMENT
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young explained there had been previous concern from Parkland
residents as well as by Waste Management in reference to the size and type of refuse containers
utilized by the City. Ms. Gardner -Young introduced Mr. Tony Spadaccia of Waste Management
to begin the discussion.
At a recent Commission meeting the Commission stated its concerns regarding provisions
contained in the City's existing contract with Waste Management. Specifically, desires to re-
evaluate the ability of residents to utilize larger trash cans than currently allowed. Under the
existing contract, residents can place at curbside as many 30 gallon trash cans or plastic bags as
they need.
Mr. Spadaccia offered a compromise and explained that by equipping their trucks with automatic
cart tippers, they are able to automatically lift the pails. He stated that the Agreement provides
for 32 gallon maximum refuse containers. He commented on the safety of their employees and
explained the installation of cart tippers would relieve the need for manual lifting. He said this
way they can accommodate those that prefer the larger cans as well as the residents that prefer
the 32 gallon cans.
There was some discussion of automating garbage collection and placing the billing for trash
collection on the residents' property tax bill. He commented about their issue with the delinquent
bills. He stated that there is approximately 6% of City not paying their waste bills.
There was a consensus of the Commission to direct staff to begin working on the process to add
the waste bill to the annual tax bill.

3. DISCUSSION: FIRE CONTRACT
At the April 15 Commission meeting, the Commission directed staff to begin negotiations with
the City of Coral Springs regarding the fire services since the City of Parkland's contract was set
to expire on September 30, 2010 with one year's notice of termination to be provided. Also, staff
was directed to contact BSO to research the option of fire services through them. The Sage
