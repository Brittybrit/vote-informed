REGULAR AGENDA
1. DISCUSSION: FIRE CONTRACT
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young introduced Mr. Steven Savia, the fire consultant.
Mr. Savia, was present and offered a very brief presentation. He explained that he was engaged to
research Fire Services options for the City of Parkland.
Commissioner Moskowitz was to be present by phone; however the connection was unable to be
made.

Mr. Savia explained that he would report on two aspects of what he was asked to work on. The
first is the status of negotiations with Coral Springs and the second will be other options that may
be available for fire service.
Mr. Savia explained that he spent most of the summer trying to get information and trying to
schedule negotiations with Coral Springs with great difficulty. He said he and Nancy Morando,
Finance Director, finally met with the Coral Springs fire and budget department management in
mid September. He said the result of the meeting was they all agreed the current contract should
not be continued because of multiple problems with it. They did agree a new contract needed to be
crafted and they would continue to meet to negotiate a new one. He named the following staff that
attended this meeting: Coral Springs Deputy City Manager Ellen Liston, Budget Director, Bob
Goehrig, Fire Chief Curran, Deputy Fire Chief Babnick, and union representatives Powell and
Metannot. Also present was Parkland Interim Finance Director Nancy Morando.
Mr. Savia then presented four available:options
• Establish a City of Parkland Fire Department (he would not recommend)
• Negotiate new contract with the City of Coral Springs
• Establish an independent Fire District with Margate and Coconut Creek
• Contract with Palm Beach County for fire services
Mr. Savia gave the pros and cons of each of the above.
Vice Mayor Weissman asked Mr. Savia for his opinion on which would be the best long term
option for the residents of the City of Parkland. Mr. Savia said he would recommend the
independent Fire District if they were talking about 5 to 10 years out and he explained why.
Vice Mayor Weissman asked about the first year costs for the independent Fire District and about
long term costs. Mr. Savia stated that if he was asked to recommend short term he would have to
recommend Coral Springs.
Vice Mayor Weissman asked about getting the EMS reimbursement number from Coral Springs
and that it has been an ongoing problem in the past.
Commissioner Rosenof asked about the shared control in the independent Fire District and
questioned if Coral Springs would want to join the Fire District in the future and then further erode
the control. Mr. Savia answered that he would be surprised if more cities did not want to join the
District. Commissioner Rosenof questioned that if Parkland did contract with Coral Springs,
could the Coral Springs/Parkland Fire Department join the independent Fire District together and
then questioned if Parkland would have more or less control over that.
Mayor Udine wanted to clarify that an independent Fire District is a separate taxing entity that has
thier own Board and sets their own millage rate and the taxing district controls that agency. Each
City would have a representative on that Board. Mr. Savia said that the cities would still have
input on the policy making.

Commissioner Rosenof asked what would happen if Parkland would want a higher level of service.
The answer was all the entities involved would have to agree.
Commissioner Rosenof commented that he felt they should proceed on a dual approach, negotiate
with Coral Springs but at the same time proceed with the research for the independent Fire District.
He added that he felt that Coral Springs, except for the accountability issue, is the right choice for
the City. He is ok with going with Coral Springs but would like to see an audit provision in it. He
said the reason he would want to go with a dual approach is that if the Coral Springs City Manager
does not want to agree to an audit provision in the contract then the City does not have a partner in
the negotiation and then maybe some of the suspicions are correct.
Mayor Udine commented about the option 2 & 3 and that he agrees with Commissioner Rosenof
about pursuing negotiating with Coral Springs. He added that Parkland residents are satisfied with
the level of service provided by Coral Springs. He agreed that an independent Fire District would
have many more cities interested but it would take time to establish. He asked what could be done
in the interim. He feels in the long term the independent Fire District would be good for all cities.
Mr. Savia added that the smaller cities are not going to be able to do things the way they are
currently doing it. Mayor Udine added that he would prefer that time is not wasted on Options 1 &
4.
Vice Mayor Weissman said he has not seen any indication that Coral Springs is interested in a Fire
District and therefore if Parkland is interested in a Fire District in the near future then he does not
suggest a long term contract with Coral Springs. He asked what the time table was for the
negotiations.
Mr. Savia stated that if he can not come back to the Commission by December with a contract then
there is a problem.
Mayor Udine said he would like to see the negotiations continue with Coral Springs and see if an
appropriate contract can be reached by the December date, if not they will have to come up with
another option until the fire district can be formed.
City Manager Caryn Gardner -Young stated that the City of Margate and the City of Coconut Creek
also need to decide whether they want to go forward with a Fire District. She added that both cities
are interested in creating an RFP to go out sometime in January. If Parkland is interested in
joining, it would involve expenditure of funds and that would have to be decided.
Vice Mayor Weissman asked if Mr. Savia felt that the decision maker was present at the meeting
yesterday. Mr. Savia stated that Chief Curran told him that Ellen Liston was authorized to make
decisions.
Chief Curran stepped to the podium and stated that Ms. Liston was acting on behalf of the City
Manager. He said that the City Manager has guaranteed Parkland that there will be no gap in the
service no matter what the decision is. Chief Curran added that more was accomplished in three

ADDED AGENDA ITEMS: NSID ROADS
Vice Mayor Weissman said that he understands that NSID is looking to dedicate some roads to the
City that NSID is currently responsible for maintaining the medians on. His opinion is that NSID
built the roads and NSID is taxing the residents for those roads and it his feeling that NSID should

SECOND ADDED AGENDA ITEM: ANNEXATION DISCUSSION
Vice Mayor Weissman commented about the letter received from Dick Coker the attorney
representing the Hendrix's family. He said there were some items in the letter he had a problem
with. He stated that the annexation agreement is the time they have the greatest flexibility as to the
requirements so this is the time to put in the most stringent requirements. He feels that the net
overall density should be based on the net acreage not the gross acreage. He is concerned that they
want to annex in but give no guarantee for the County Line road right of way. He said County Line
road is vital to develop the east/west route. He also stated that the initial landowners that annexed
in 600 acres committed to approximately 15 and 20 acres for a school. He feels this annexation
should commit no less then 20 acres to the City to be used for school first and if the school board
decides not to build a school then the City can decide what else to use it for. He also feels that
park impact fees should be in addition to that, not included. He said if the Commission is in
agreement with that, then that needs to be transmitted back to them.
City Attorney, Mr. Andrew Maurodis said that if that is the consensus of the Commission, he will
bring that back, however he is pretty sure that will be unacceptable from previous conversations he
