III PUBLIC INPUT
Mayor Udine opened to the Public.
Mayor Udine recognized Mr. Ron Ernst - Jones, a former Mayor of Coral Springs, who spoke on
the new urgent care medical facility recently opened by Baptist Hospital. Mr. Jones advised the
facility is new to Broward County and is located at 6264 West Sample Road, Coral Springs. He
explained this facility was a non- competing health care center working with the Broward Health
and Northwest Regional Hospitals to provide intermediate urgent care to the citizens of Broward
County.
There were no further comments or questions and the Mayor closed to the public.

thanked the Commission, City staff and the residents of Parkland for all their continued
support.

REGULAR AGENDA
1. UPDATE: ELEMENTARY SCHOOL "Z"
Mayor Udine invited Mr. Kenny King, the previous Assistant Principal at Park Trails Elementary
and now the new principal of Elementary School "Z ", to provide a brief update regarding the
school.
Mr. King advised they have an unofficial name of Heron Heights for the school and are awaiting
approval by the Broward County School Board. He noted the school was now fully staffed with
teachers and they will have their first faculty meeting tomorrow to review moving into the school
for an on time opening on August 24 2009. He also noted that the parent groups had been
formed for the school and introduced Mrs. Stacy Udine who had been elected as the PTA
President.
Mr. King introduced Mr. Marshall Washington, School Board Liason Project Manager and Mr.
Ricardo Santana, Jose Capana and Dana Nelson from Skanska.
Mr. Santana gave a brief power point presentation on the new school's features specifically
noting the green building elements as well as the fact the school will have a wildlife habitat.
City Manager, Caryn Gardner -Young asked Mr. Santana to confirm for the record that the school
had not been built using any Chinese Drywall.
Mr. Santana confirmed for the record that they did not use Chinese drywall in the construction of
Elementary School `Z'.
Commissioner Moskowitz questioned placement of the solar panels and how much of the school
would the panels provide power for?
Mr. Santana advised they will be placed on the roof of the cafeteria which is a slanted roof and
the power the panels generate will supplement power for the entire school.
Mayor Udine commented that Skanska had been a great company to work with on this project
and asked if the students could be educated on some of the green elements used throughout the
school.
Mr King responded it may be incorporated into the school's curriculum by the school staff in
reference to green building and energy efficiency.

COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING AN
AGREEMENT WITH PARKLAND RESERVE, LLC
WITH REGARD TO DENSITY AND AGE
RESTRICTIONS
City Manager, Caryn Gardner -Young read the title of Resolution No. 2009 -39, Parkland Reserve,
LLC Density and Age Restrictions and introduced Planning and Zoning Director, Roberta Moore
to provide a brief presentation
Ms. Moore gave a presentation explaining the agreement with Centerline Homes/Parkland
Reserve LLC.
Mayor Udine clarified that this agreement would not in any way allow for any additional density;
it would only allow Parkland Reserve LLC to go before Broward County first and then they
would come back before the Parkland City Commission for the final decision.
City Attorney, Andrew Maurodis added that Parkland Reserve LLC were seeking to have the
restrictive covenant lifted by Broward County and that he had drafted an agreement to ensure
against any implication that the City had allowed additional density added to the agreement.
MOTION made by Commissioner Moskowitz to approve Resolution No. 2009 -39, Parkland
Reserve, LLC Density and Age Restrictions. Seconded by Vice Mayor Weissman.

3. RESOLUTION NO. 2009-40: PARKLAND RESERVE MEDIAN
MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING TWO
AGREEMENTS WITH PARKLAND RESERVE, LLC FOR
MEDIAN MAINTENANCE.
City Manager, Caryn Gardner -Young read the title of Resolution No. 2009 -40, Parkland Reserve
Median Maintenance Agreement and provided a brief explanation.
Ms. Gardner -Young explained the proposed agreement would be a Tri -party agreement with the
City of Parkland, NSID and Centerline Homes to maintain the median from Trails End, between
Nob Hill Road and Pine Island Road.
Vice Mayor Weissman noted his concern to clarify that this agreement included the proper
language that would ensure the Parkland Reserve HOA, once created, would maintain the median
to their satisfaction and requested the City Attorney, Andrew Maurodis, to review the agreement
before final approval.
Commissioner Moskowitz stated he would prefer the agreement does not revert to NSID and the
agreement should not include that situation as an option.
Mr. Robert Stiegele of Centerline Homes, stating they were currently revising their proposed
budgets in order to accommodate median maintenance agreements and indicated they would
provide median maintenance to the City's satisfaction.
MOTION made by Commissioner Moskowitz to approve Resolution No. 2009 -40, Parkland
Reserve Median Maintenance Agreement as amended pending the City Attorney's approval.
Seconded by Commissioner Smith.
ROLL CALL VOTE:
MOTION passed unanimously.
