3. RESOLUTION NO. 2012-77: ~~ WESTERN FIRE STATION —- BID AWARD
COMMENTS: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE

CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO APPROVE THE
RANKING OF THE TOP FIVE PROFESSIONAL SERVICES
FIRMS TO PROVIDE DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION
ADMINISTRATION SERVICES RELATED TO THE
WESTERN FIRE STATION AND TO AUTHORIZE THE CITY
MANAGER TO EXECUTE A CONTRACT WITH THE TOP
RANKED FIRM; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND
PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

This item was heard out of the normal order of the agenda without ohjeciion.

City Manager Caryn Gardner-Young read the title of Resolution No. 2012-77: Western Fire Station.

Mr. Brian Archer gave a bricf update of the item presented. He explained that in accordance with the

City’s purchasing ordinance, staff advertised for an RFQ for the design of the Western Fire Station.

The committee short listed from twelve firms down to five firms and they were ranked accordingly.

He then gave the City Commission three options. Option #1) to accept the committee’s ranking;

Option #2) Reject the committee’s ranking and start the process all over again; or Option #3) the City

Commission could re-rank by a 4/5 vote based on the Purchasing Ordinance. The top five firms were

present.

Commissioner Moskowitz commented that approximately two years ago, the City Commission

awarded the design of the fire station, but due to economic conditions and less revenues received,

monies were never allocated to the design phase. Since that time, there was a change in location of the

fire station and a significant amount of time had lapsed since the award, therefore the project had to be

rebid. As a result of the rebidding, the company initially awarded the project has now become number

two in the ranking. Commissioner Moskowitz expressed that the number two ranked firm has

experience with building fire stations in the City of Parkland and he would like the project to be

awarded to them. He stated that he has reviewed the Code of Ordinance which has been changed as a
result of new ethics rules and has determined that the selection committee can be overruled by a 4/5
vote, then the City Commission acts as the selection committee in which they will review the
proposals and rank the firms.

City Attorney Maurodis recommended that the City Commission review all bid proposals and come
back at the next meeting for presentations.

Vice Mayor Weissman inquired about the closeness of the rankings.

Mr. Brian Archer replied that there was a 7% difference between the number 1 and 2 ranked firms.
Rosenof asked what the criteria was for the scoring. He asked who was on their teams as far as
engineering team etc.

In response to Commissioner Rosenof’s questions, Mr. Chris Zimmerman with CPZ Architects
introduced his team of professionals.

In response to Commissioner Rosenof’s questions, Ms. Cindy Baldwin, Principal with ACAI
Associates introduced her team of professionals.

Mayor Udine expressed his concern for the way in which business is conducted with the bid process.
He stated he would vote to re-rank.

MOTION made by Commissioner Moskowitz to reevaluate all proposals and at the next meeting,
rank each firm based on same criteria that the evaluation committee use. Seconded by Commissioner
Willis.

Mr. Neil Schiller with Becker & Poliakoff, 3111 Stirling Road on behalf of CPZ Architects asked if
public comment was permitted despite the fact that there was a cone of silence. Mr. Maurodis replied
that there is no public comment and no requirement to do so unless the City Commission in their
discretion wishes to allow public comment.

Mr. Neil Schiller expressed his concern for the process. He made reference to three things under the
City Code of Ordinance that allows the City Commission to effectively re-rank (the criteria, the report
included in the backup, and the actual rankings). He also feels that if the City Commission re-rank,
review and evaluate the proposals, then the City Commission is doing all the firms a disservice
because the Selection Committee had presentations and the ability to interact with the proposers.

City Attorney Maurodis recommended that the City Commission offer each firm the opportunity to
make a presentation. Mr. Maurodis addressed the concern about the Broward County Ethics Code and
stated that the right to re-rank is reserved in the selection process. The Selection Committee’s ranking
is not final and the process is not over until the City Commission affirms the rankings or decides to re-
rank.
