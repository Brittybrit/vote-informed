PARKLAND CITY COMMISSION AGENDA
OCTOBER 28, 2008 SPECIAL CITY COMMISSION MEETING
PAGE 2
Mayor Udine explained that he called this special meeting pursuant to the rules of the City of
Parkland Charter. The purpose of the meeting was to discuss preparing a formal request to
Governor Charlie Crist and Supervisor of Elections, Brenda Snipes to extend the early voting
hours from 7 am to 7 pm in order to allow enough time for everyone to vote due to the very long
lines. Since this special meeting was called, the newspapers have begun questioning whether or
not extending the hours was allowed or who had the authority to make the decision to extend the
hours. In the interim, the Governor has issued the executive order extending the early voting
hours from 7 am to 7 pm. for the remainder of early voting including longer hours on the
weekends. The Mayor stated that he feels the purpose of this meeting has been addressed and
that the Governor made a smart move.
Commissioner Weissman requested to see a resolution on the next agenda supporting an amended
statute that would require the extended hours and electronically transmit the resolution to other
cities.
Mayor Udine agreed but suggested waiting for a few meetings.
Vice Mayor Moskowitz also wanted to commend the Governor for acting on extending the early
voting hours. He commented that this Governor has a record of standing up for voting rights. He
added that this meeting is now moot as far as what this Commission was trying to achieve.
Commissioner Smith asked if this was effective immediately.
III ADJOURNMENT
Motion made by Commissioner Rosenof to adjourn the October 28, 2008 Special City
Commission Meeting at 7:15 PM. Seconded by Commissioner Weissman.
Motion passed unanimously.
