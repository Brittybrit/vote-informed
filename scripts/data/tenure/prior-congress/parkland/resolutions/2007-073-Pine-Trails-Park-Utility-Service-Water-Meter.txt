 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-73
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY
 4 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 5 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING
 6 THE PURCHASE AND INSTALLATION
 7 OF A THREE INCH WATER METER
 8 FROM NORTH SPRINGS
 9 IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT (NSID)
 10 NECESSARY TO PROVIDE POTABLE
 11 WATER TO PINE TRAILS PARK PHASE
 12 II IN THE AMOUNT OF $34,009.00,
 13 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 14
 15
 16 WHEREAS, monies were budgeted in the City of Parkland Fiscal
 17 Year 2007 Budget for the purpose of constructing a Community Center
 18 and Amphitheater at Pine Trails Park; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, a 3" water meter is required for the purpose of
 21 providing potable water service to the site; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the cost of the 3" water meter including installation and
 24 system connection fee is $34,009.00; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, Staff recommends the installation of the water meter
 27 for the aforementioned purpose; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes the
 30 installation of the 3" water meter is in the best interest of the citizens of
 31 the City of Parkland; and
 32
 33 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 34 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 35
 36 SECTION 1. The City Manager is hereby authorized to
 37 purchase a 3" water meter including installation and system connection fee
 38 from the North Springs Improvement District (NSID) in the amount of
 39 $34,009.00.
 40
 41 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and
 42 effect immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 43
 44 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 5 DAY OF July
 45 2007.
