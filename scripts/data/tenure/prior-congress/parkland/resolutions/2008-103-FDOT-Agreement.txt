 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-103
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING THE
 MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT FOR THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND STATE ROAD 7 BEAUTIFICATION PROJECT
 BETWEEN THE CITY OF PARKLAND AND THE STATE OF
 FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION;
 PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR
 SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE
 DATE.
 WHEREAS, the STATE OF FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF
 TRANSPORTATION ("FDOT") has jurisdiction over State Road 7 as part of the State
 Highway System; and
 WHEREAS, the CITY OF PARKLAND seeks to install and maintain certain
 landscape improvements within the right-of-way of State Road 7; and
 WHEREAS, as part of the continual updating of the State of Florida Highway
 System, the FDOT, for the purpose of safety, protection of the investment, and other
 reasons, has constructed and does maintain the highway facility as described in Exhibit A,
 attached hereto and incorporated by reference herein, within the corporate limits of the
 CITY OF PARKLAND; and
 WHEREAS, the CITY OF PARKLAND is of the opinion that said highway
 facilities within the CITY OF PARKLAND'S limits that contain landscape
 improvements to areas outside the travel way to the right of way line shall be maintained
 by periodic pruning, mowing, fertilizing, weeding, litter pick-up, necessary replanting and
 irrigation repair as needed: and
 WHEREAS, the parties hereto mutually recognize the need for entering into an
 Agreement designating and setting forth the responsibilities of each party; and
 WHEREAS, the CITY OF PARKLAND by Resolution No.2008-103 desires to
 enter into this Agreement and authorizes its officers to do so,

 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND FLORIDA,THAT;
 Section 1: The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached hereto are
 hereby incorporate herein.
 Section 2: The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves the
 Maintenance Agreement with the State of Florida Department of Transportation for the
 City of Parkland State Road 7 Beautification Project.
 Section 3: The appropriate City officials are hereby authorized and directed to
 execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this resolution.
 Section 4: All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are
 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 Section 5: If any clause, section, other part or application of this Resolution is
 held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or invalid, in part of
 application, it shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions or applications of this
 Resolution.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 1 DAY OF October , 2008.
 CITY AND,)ORIDA
