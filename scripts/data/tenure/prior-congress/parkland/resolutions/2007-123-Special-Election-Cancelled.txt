 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-123
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, DECLARING THE SPECIAL MUNICIPAL
 ELECTION SCHEDULED FOR JANUARY 29, 2008 FOR THE PURPOSES
 OF FILLING THE VACANT DISTRICT III COMMISSION SEAT
 CANCELLED.
 WHEREAS, by virtue of the resignation of Commissioner Tracey McGilvray, there was a
 vacancy in District III, and
 WHEREAS, under newly adopted Charter Amendments, the City Commission could not appoint
 a new Commissioner and therefore were required to hold a Special Election to fill the vacant Commission
 seat; and
 WHEREAS, a Special Election was scheduled for January 29, 2008 to fill the vacant District III
 seat through Resolution No. 2007-102; and
 WHEREAS, the qualifying period for candidates began on November 2, 2007 and ran through
 November 9,2007; and
 WHEREAS, during this qualifying period only one candidate qualified for the vacant District III
 seat; and
 WHEREAS, David Rosenof was the qualified candidate and since he was unopposed, there is no
 need to hold the Special Election;
 WHEREAS, David Rosenof, as the only qualified candidate, will fill the remaining term of the
 District III Commission seat.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 SECTION 1. The above Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a part hereof.
 SECTION 2. That the remaining term of the District III Commission seat has been filled by
 unopposed candidate, Mr. David Rosenof who shall take office as of January 30, 2007 or when sworn in
 thereafter.
 SECTION 3. The special election hereby set for January 29, ,108, is hereby cancelled.
