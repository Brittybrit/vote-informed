 INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT FOR
 ENGINEERING & ENGINEERING INSPECTION SERVICES
 This is an Interlocal Agreement, made and entered into by and between: CITY
 OF PARKLAND, a municipal corporation existing under the laws of the State of Florida,
 hereinafter referred to as "PARKLAND,"
 AND
 CITY OF COCONUT CREEK, a municipal corporation existing under the laws of
 the State of Florida, hereinafter referred to as "COCONUT CREEK."
 WHEREAS, this Agreement is entered into pursuant to Sec. 163.01, Florida
 Statutes, also known as the "Florida Interlocal Cooperation Act of 1969"; and
 WHEREAS, COCONUT CREEK maintains a Department of Utilities &
 Engineering, which includes an Engineering Division that performs various engineering
 services, including engineering inspections; and
 WHEREAS, PARKLAND is desirous of procuring from COCONUT CREEK
 engineering and engineering inspection services within its municipal boundaries; and
 WHEREAS, COCONUT CREEK, through its Engineering Division is willing to
 perform such services pursuant to the terms and conditions hereafter set forth.
 NOW, THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION of the mutual terms, conditions,
 promises, covenants and payments hereinafter set forth, PARKLAND and COCONUT
 CREEK agree as follows:
 ARTICLE I - SCOPE OF SERVICES
 1.1 PARKLAND agrees to grant to COCONUT CREEK the authority to perform
 services in accordance with the terms herein and pursuant to the direction of
 PARKLAND.
 1 .2 COCONUT CREEK shall perform engineering and engineering inspection
 services through its Engineering Division, or any successor division as may be
 designated by the COCONUT CREEK City Manager.
 1.3 PARKLAND shall issue permits and retain fees; COCONUT CREEK shall charge
 an hourly rate in accordance with Article 3 below for providing appropriate staffing for
 the duration of this Agreement.
 1.4 For all service requests, COCONUT CREEK will prioritize and respond based on
 availability of resources. It is understood that COCONUT CREEK is not adding any

<<PG>>
 additional personnel for this Agreement. However, all services provided for herein shall
 occur within a reasonable time frame and during the course of normal business hours.
 No overtime shall be authorized for work provided to PARKLAND unless prior approval
 of PARKLAND is obtained.
 ARTICLE 2- FUNCTIONS AND DUTIES TRANSFERRED TO COCONUT CREEK
 2.1 It is specifically understood and agreed that all rights and powers as may be
 vested in PARKLAND pursuant to Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, or any other law or
 ordinance or Charter provision of PARKLAND not specifically addressed by this
 Agreement, shall be retained by PARKLAND. It is further understood and agreed that
 this Agreement is primarily intended to address the following functions or activities:
 • Engineering Inspections
 • Site Plan / Development Review and related Engineering.
 • Development Review Meetings
 • Commission/Board Meetings (as may be required from time
 to time)
 This Agreement may, upon written request (including e-mail) of PARKLAND City
 Manager or designee and acceptance of COCONUT CREEK, be extended to include
 the following functions or activities on an as needed basis:
 • General Civil Engineering
 • Stormwater Management/Drainage
 • Services requiring City Engineer's seal/signature
 • Any other related services
 2.2 In the event PARKLAND desires to have COCONUT CREEK provide any
 additional non-engineering related services, a separate agreement or an amendment
 shall be required between PARKLAND and COCONUT CREEK.
 ARTICLE 3 - COMPENSATION
 3.1 COCONUT CREEK shall provide services in this Agreement at the rate of Sixty-
 Five Dollars ($ 65.00) per hour for an Engineering Inspector, Seventy-Five Dollars ($
 75.00) per hour for Engineer I, Ninety Dollars ($ 90.00) per hour for Senior Engineer,
 and One Hundred Twenty Five Dollars ($ 125.00) for City Engineer. Overtime, when
 approved by PARKLAND, shall be at one and one half the normal hourly rate. All
 charges shall be billed for a minimum of one hour and in increments of thirty (30)
 minutes. When scheduling engineering inspections within PARKLAND, COCONUT
 CREEK shall schedule said inspections in the most efficient manner possible to ensure
 that all inspections performed on any given day are completed without having to make
 multiple trips to and from PARKLAND each day. Any overtime, when required, shall be
 governed by the COCONUT CREEK Civil Service Code.
 2

<<PG>>
 3.2 All costs shall be properly documented and said documentation provided to
 PARKLAND with the monthly invoices. Documentation shall include Name of
 Employee, Position of Employee, Employee Rate Charged, Time Started, Time
 Completed, Specific Task Performed, and Location of Task. COCONUT CREEK shall
 invoice PARKLAND on a monthly basis for the services requested for the preceding
 month. PARKLAND shall reimburse COCONUT CREEK within thirty (30) days of the
 date of the invoice.
 3.3 The amounts set forth above shall be adjusted annually by COCONUT CREEK
 by eight percent (8%) to address increases in operating and labor costs. Any additional
 request above the aforementioned eight percent (8%) shall be requested in writing no
 less than ninety (90) days prior to the end of the PARKLAND fiscal year (September
 30th of each year). Said request shall include any and all documentation necessary to
 justify the additional increase. Increases shall take effect on October 1 , 2007 and every
 October 1st thereafter if the Agreement is extended.
 ARTICLE 4 - TERM OF AGREEMENT
 4.1 This Agreement shall be deemed to have commenced upon approval by the
 COCONUT CREEK and PARKLAND CITY COMMISSIONS, and shall continue in full
 force and effect for one-year period unless it is mutually extended by both parties. This
 Agreement may be extended in one-year increments upon acceptance and approval of
 said extension by both COCONUT CREEK City Manager and the PARKLAND City
 Manager.
 4.2 This Agreement shall remain in full force and effect through the termination date
 or any extended termination date, as set forth above, unless written notices of
 termination by COCONUT CREEK or PARKLAND is provided pursuant to Section 7,
 TERMINATION.
 ARTICLE 5 — RESPONSIBLE GOVERNMENTAL IMMUNITY
 PARKLAND and COCONUT CREEK are municipal corporations existing under
 the laws of the State of Florida. Each agrees to be fully responsible for acts and
 omissions of their employees to the extent permitted by law. Nothing herein is intended
 to serve as a waiver of sovereign immunity by any party to which sovereign immunity
 may be applicable. Nothing herein shall be construed as consent by a state agency or
 political subdivision of the State of Florida to be sued by third parties in any matter
 arising out of this Agreement or any other contract.
 ARTICLE 6 - INSURANCE
 COCONUT CREEK is a member of South East Risk Management Association
 (SERMA) and carries insurance as described in Exhibit A. COCONUT CREEK shall add
 PARKLAND to their policy as an additional insured and shall maintain PARKLAND as
 an additional insured for the term of this Agreement and all authorized extensions.

 ARTICLE 9 - MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
 9.1 ASSIGNMENT: COCONUT CREEK shall perform engineering and engineering
 inspection services provided for in this Agreement exclusively and solely for
 PARKLAND which is a party to this Agreement. Neither party shall have the right to
 assign this Agreement.
 9.2 ENGINEERING & ENGINEERING INSPECTION STANDARDS: Unless
 specifically adopted in PARKLAND Code of Ordinances or Policy, all engineering and
 engineering standards shall be performed pursuant to COCONUT CREEK Code of
 Ordinances and Engineering Standards. In case of conflict, PARKLAND standards
 shall be applied. In cases where no PARKLAND standard exists, COCONUT CREEK
 standards shall apply. In cases where no standard exists in either municipality,
 standards as determined by the City Engineer of COCONUT CREEK shall apply.
