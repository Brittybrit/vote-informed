 9 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,

 16 zoning designation Community Facility ("CF"); and

 19 purpose of providing master planning and conceptual development planning of the Pine Island
 20 Park (now known as Pine Trails Park); and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City Commission has determined that changes to the Pine Trails Park
 23 Master Plan is necessary; and
 24

 26 approval of the petition by a 7-0 vote; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in the Code of
 29 Ordinances have been satisfied; and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony
 32 presented by the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of the
 33 various City of Parkland review agencies and staff; and
 34
 35 WHEREAS, the City Commission has made the following findings of fact:
 36
 37 1. Environmental Considerations: No adverse impacts to the natural environment are
 38 expected since the property has already been
 39 disturbed.
 40
 41 2. Comprehensive Plan The proposed project is consistent with the Land Use
 42 Considerations: Element of the Comprehensive Plan.
 43
 44 3. Zoning Considerations: The proposed project is consistent with the zoning
 45 requirements of the Code of Ordinances.
 46
 47 4. Community Appearance The proposed project is consistent with the
 48 Considerations: community appearance standards in the Code of
 49 Ordinances as the site is a park.
