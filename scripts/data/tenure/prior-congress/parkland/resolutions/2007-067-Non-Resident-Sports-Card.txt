 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO IMPLEMENT AND CHARGE A FEE FOR
 5 A NON-RESIDENT SPORTS CARD FOR NON-RESIDENTS TO
 6 PARTICIPATE IN CITY RECOGNIZED SPORTS LEAGUES;
 7 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 8
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City recognizes eleven (11) City Recognized Sports
 11 Programs; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, within the eleven (11) programs, six hundred forty seven (647)
 14 non-residents participate; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, since non-residents do not pay taxes for the upkeep and
 17 maintenance of City facilities, an additional non-resident charge for City sports was
 18 considered; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the City's Sports Policy requires non-residents who wish to
 21 participate in City Recognized Sports League to purchase a non-resident sports card;
 22 and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the Parks and Recreation Advisory Board ("Board") believes that
 25 implementation of the non-resident sports card and associated fee will assist in
 26 ensuring that City Recognized Sports Leagues are primarily comprised of City
 27 residents; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the Board believes that instituting this fee will best provide the
 30 participants of City sports programs with a positive experience; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes assessing this fee
 33 is in the best interest of the citizens of Parkland; and
 34
 35 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 36 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 37
 38 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified
 39 as being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein. All exhibits attached
 40 hereto are hereby incorporated herein.
 41
 42 SECTION 2. Non-residents of the City of Parkland shall be required to
 43 purchase a non-resident sports card from the City at a fee of one hundred fifty five
 44 dollars ($155.00) per family per fiscal year in order to participate in City Recognized
 45 Sports Programs.
