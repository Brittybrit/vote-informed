 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-29
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING CITY
 OFFICIALS TO AWARD A CONTRACT FOR THE
 PINETREE ESTATES INTERSECTION IMPROVEMENTS
 PROJECT (CITY CONTRACT #2006-01) TO RANGER
 CONSTRUCTION SOUTH, CONTAINING AN EFFECTIVE
 DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida, is authorized
 to award a contract for the Pinetree Estates Intersection Improvements Project (City
 Contract#2006-01); and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland received four (4) bids from the following
 bidders: Ranger Construction South, Florida Blacktop, Asphalt Consultants, and All-Rite
 Paving; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has fully reviewed the bids
 submitted and has determined that the bid of Ranger Construction South., is the lowest
 responsible and responsive bid which best meets the criteria set forth in the bid packet
 and that, based upon the base bid price of$128,500.00 plus Add Alternate # 1 at a price
 of$225,355.60 for a grand total of$353,855.60 and other factors to be considered, it is in
 the best interest of the City to accept said bid.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That Ranger Construction South, is hereby awarded a contract in
 the total amount of $353,855.60 for the Pinetree Estates Intersection Improvements
 Project(City Contract#2006-01).
 Section 2. That the Funds shall be taken from the Public Road Resurfacing
 line item# 305-701.351-506031.
