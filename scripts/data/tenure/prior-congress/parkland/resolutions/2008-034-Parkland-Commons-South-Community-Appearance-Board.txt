 10 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 11 Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the
 12 Code of Ordinance is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and
 13 land development orders; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the parcel possesses a Future Land Use Designation of Commercial
 16 with a zoning designation of Planned Commercial District ("POD"); and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony
 19 presented by the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of the
 20 various City of Parkland review agencies and staff; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City Commission has made the following findings of fact:
 23
 24 1. Environmental Considerations: No adverse impacts to the natural environment are
 25 expected since the property has already been
 26 disturbed.
 27
 28 2. Utilities: The proposed water, sanitary, sewer and drainage
 29 systems will meet code requirements subject to
 30 final permitting.
 31
 32 3. Comprehensive Plan The proposed project is consistent with
 33 Considerations: the Land Use Element of the Comprehensive Plan.
 34
 35 4. Zoning Considerations: Site area, height restrictions, and landscaping all
 36 meet or exceed Code standards.
 37
 38 5. Community Appearance The building fits within the character of area and
 39 Considerations: the adjacent shopping centers, and includes
 40 pedestrian amenities.
 41
 42 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 43 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:

 46 true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein.
