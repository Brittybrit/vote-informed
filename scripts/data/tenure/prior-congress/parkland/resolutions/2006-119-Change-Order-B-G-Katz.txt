 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-119
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION
 4 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 AUTHORIZING A CHANGE ORDER IN THE
 6 AMOUNT OF $87,000.00 TO THE
 7 AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY AND B.G.
 8 KATZ NURSERIES, INC. TO PROVIDE
 9 ADDITIONAL SWALE REGRADING AND
 10 STABILIZATION RELATED TO THE CANAL
 11 AND SWALE DEBRIS REMOVAL PROJECT,
 12 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 13
 14
 15 WHEREAS, on May 24, 2006 the City Commission approved Contract
 16 #2006-04 between the City of Parkland and B.G. Katz Nurseries, Inc. providing
 17 for the removal of hurricane debris to prevent flooding within the City in
 18 accordance with a United States Department of Agriculture Natural Resources
 19 Conservation Service (NRCS) grant; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, during the course of work it was discovered that additional
 22 swale regrading and bank stabilization would be necessary within the swale that
 23 lies between NW 69th Way and NW 71st Terrace in Pine Tree Estates to prevent
 24 flooding in the amount of $87,000.00; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, NRCS has authorized the $87,000 funding increase to the
 27 initial grant for the aforementioned purpose; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, staff recommends the approval of a change order to the
 30 Agreement with B.G. Katz Nurseries, Inc. in the amount of $87,000.00; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes approval of
 33 the change order is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 34
 35 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 36 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 37
 38 SECTION 1. The above representations are true and correct and
 39 made a part hereof.
 40
 41 SECTION 2. The City Commission does hereby authorize a change
 42 order increasing the amount of the Agreement between the City and B.G. Katz,
 43 Nurseries, Inc. by $87,000.00.
 44
