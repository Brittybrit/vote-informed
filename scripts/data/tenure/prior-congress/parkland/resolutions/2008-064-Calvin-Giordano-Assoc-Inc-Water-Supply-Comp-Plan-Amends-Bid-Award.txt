 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-64
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AWARDING A CONTRACT TO CALVIN,
 5 GIORDANO & ASSOCIATES, INC. FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF
 6 A WATER SUPPLY COMPREHENSIVE PLAN AMENDMENTS
 7 AND WATER SUPPLY FACILITIES WORK PLAN FOR THE CITY
 8 OF PARKLAND.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland requested proposals from companies / firms for
 11 the development of a Water Supply Comprehensive Plan Amendments and Water Supply
 12 Facilities Work Plan; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the City received four (4) proposals and a selection committee
 15 appointed by the City Manager reviewed said proposals and ranked the proposals
 16 received; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the number one ranked firm was Calvin, Giordano & Associates, Inc.;
 19 and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the City Commission agrees with the ranking by the committee and
 22 wishes to authorize execution of an agreement with Calvin, Giordano & Associates, Inc.
 23 upon negotiation of an acceptable contract by the City Manager;
 24
 25 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 26 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 27
 28 SECTION 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and
 29 made a part hereof.
 30
 31 SECTION 2. The City Commission hereby affirms the ranking (1 through 4) of the
 32 evaluation committee as to the proposals received for the development of a Water Supply
 33 Comprehensive Plan Amendments and Water Supply Facilities Work Plan. Calvin,
 34 Giordano & Associates, Inc. is deemed the top ranked firm and the City Manager is hereby
 35 authorized to enter into negotiations to develop a contract, which contract shall first be
 36 approved by the City Attorney as to form and then submitted to the City Commission for
 37 final determination. The City Commission will then make a final decision on approving or
 38 not approving the contract. If the City Commission does not approve the contract, or if the
 39 City Manager is unable to negotiate an acceptable contract with Calvin, Giordano &
 40 Associates, Inc., then the City Commission may direct that negotiations be undertaken
 41 with the number two (2) ranked firm and the process shall be followed with the number two
 42 (2) ranked firm and so forth until such time a contract has been reached. If the City is
 43 unable to reach a contract, or in the alternative, at any time the City may decide to reject
 44 all proposals and repeat the process. No contract or award shall be final until such time
 45 as the City Commission approves a final contract.
 46
 47 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
 48
 49 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 18 day of JUNE , 2008.
