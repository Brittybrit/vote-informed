 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-101
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO AUTHORIZE THE
 5 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE
 6 ATTACHED CONTRACT FOR SURVEYING SERVICES
 7 FOR HOLMBERG ROAD AND PARKSIDE DRIVE;
 8 PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR
 9 SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE
 10 DATE.
 11
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City received Broward County approval for a grant to
 14 install a sidewalk on the north side of Holmberg Road; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City Commission has asked staff to provide conceptual
 17 drawing of Holmberg Road as a three lane roadway; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the City Commission has asked staff to provide an opinion on
 20 whether a bicycle lane is possible on Parkside Drive; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, to complete these projects requires the City to survey
 23 Holmberg Road from State Road 7 to Parkside Drive and Parkside Drive from
 24 Holmberg Road to Lox Road; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, no City employee is licensed to perform surveys; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, City staff has investigated the possibility of hiring an outside
 29 consultant to perform the surveying work; and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, the City Commission already approved a contract with Calvin
 32 Giordano & Associates, Inc. to perform engineering services for the City; and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best
 35 interest of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City
 36 of Parkland to enter into an contract with Calvin Giordano & Associates to
 37 perform the surveying work; and
 38
 39 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 40 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 41
 42 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 43 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits
 44 attached hereto are hereby incorporate herein.
