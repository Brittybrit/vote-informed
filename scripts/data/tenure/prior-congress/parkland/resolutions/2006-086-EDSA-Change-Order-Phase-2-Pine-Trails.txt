 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION
 4 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 AUTHORIZING A CHANGE ORDER IN THE
 6 AMOUNT OF $98,474.30 FOR DESIGN AND
 7 CONSTRUCTION ADMINISTRATION
 8 RELATED TO THE PINE TRAILS PARK
 9 PHASE II PROJECT, PROVIDING AN
 10 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 11
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City requested the addition of various park improvements
 14 to be designed into the Pine Trails Park Phase II project; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, said elements included a restroom building, picnic pavilions,
 17 a native hammock area, dock, boardwalk, fishing pier, landscape treatment,
 18 basketball courts and an emergency generator; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the South Florida Water Management District required the
 21 City to perform a water use study prior to issuing a permit; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the City requested the deletion of a tennis complex and
 24 perimeter landscaping from the design process; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the aforementioned contract changes resulted in a net
 27 increase to the design and construction administration in the amount of
 28 $98,474.30; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, staff recommends the approval of a change order to EDSA in
 31 the amount of $98,474.30; and
 32
 33 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes approval of
 34 the change order is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 35
 36 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 37 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 38
 39 SECTION 1. The above representations are true and correct and
 40 made a part hereof.
 41
 42 SECTION 2. The City Commission does hereby authorize a change
 43 order in the amount of $98,474.30 for design and construction administration costs
 44 to EDSA and authorizes the City Manager to execute a change order in that
 45 amount pursuant to the contract between the City and EDSA.
