 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-29
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION
 4 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 AUTHORIZING THE APPROPRIATE CITY
 6 OFFICIAL TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT
 7 WITH MALCOLM PIRNIE, INC. TO PROVIDE
 8 POST-DISASTER DEBRIS MONITORING
 9 SERVICES ON AN AS NEEDED BASIS,
 10 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 11
 12
 13 WHEREAS, City staff advertised RFP #2007-01 attached hereto as
 14 Exhibit A and entitled "Post—Disaster Debris Monitoring Services" for the purpose
 15 of having a qualified firm to provide post-disaster debris monitoring services; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, a selection committee consisting of the Assistant City
 18 Manager, Public Services Director, Public Services Operations Manager, and
 19 Finance & Administrative Services Director read, reviewed, and ranked five (5)
 20 firms in accordance with the Consultants' Competitive Negotiation Act (CCNA)
 21 and FEMA recommendations; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, Malcolm Pirnie, Inc. was the highest ranked firm among the
 24 five (5) firms; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, staff recommends that the City enter into an Agreement with
 27 Malcolm Pirnie, Inc. for the purpose of providing post-disaster debris monitoring
 28 services; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission supports the ranking
 31 and believes an Agreement with Malcolm Pirnie, Inc. is in the best interest of the
 32 citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 33
 34 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 35 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 36
 37 SECTION 1. The Agreement with Malcolm Pirnie, Inc. is hereby
 38 approved and the appropriate City Official is hereby authorized to execute the
 39 Agreement.
 40
 41 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 42 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 43
 44 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 21 DAY OF February , 2007.
