 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-66
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING ADD ALTERNATE #2 TO THE
 PARKLAND CITY HALL RE-ROOF PROJECT (CITY CONTRACT #
 2006-02) IN THE AMOUNT OF $30,355.28 TO INSTALL NEW WINDOWS,
 SILL PANS AND FLASHING IN THE CUPOLA; PROVIDING AN
 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City has executed a contract, pursuant to competitive bids, with
 Welling Construction Inc., to install a new roof on City Hall; and
 WHEREAS,the contract price is $295,500; and
 WHEREAS, after Welling Construction Inc. removed the test window in
 accordance with the contract documents, it was determined these windows, sill pans and
 flashing must be replaced; and
 WHEREAS, the contract documents included add alternate #2 in the amount of
 $30,355.28 to cover the costs associated with the installation of new windows, sill pans
 and flashing in the cupola;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 SECTION 1. The above representations are true and correct and made a part hereof.
 SECTION 2. The City Commission does hereby authorize a change order in the amount
 of $30,355.28 for the Parkland City Hall Re-Roof Project (City Contract No. 2006-02) and
 authorizes the City Manager to execute a change order in that amount pursuant to the contract
 between the City and Welling Construction Inc..
 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective upon passage.
