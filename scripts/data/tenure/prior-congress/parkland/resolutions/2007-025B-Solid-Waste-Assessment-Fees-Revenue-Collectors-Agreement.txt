 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-25B
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE PROPER
 CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT FOR THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND TO REIMBURSE THE BROWARD
 COUNTY DEPARTMENT OF FINANCE AND
 ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES FOR PROVIDING THE
 UNIFORM COLLECTION AND ENFORCEMENT OF NON-AD
 VALOREM ASSESSMENTS; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE
 DATE.
 WHEREAS, the agreement is intended to conform with the requirement of
 section 197.3632, Florida Statutes, that the City and the Tax Collector enter into a written
 agreement providing for reimbursement of necessary administrative and actual costs
 incurred as a result of the use of the Uniform Method; such administrative and actual
 costs include, but are not limited to those costs associated with personnel, forms,
 supplies, data processing, computer equipment,postage and programming
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That an Agreement between the City of Parkland and Broward
 County providing for the Broward County Finance and Administrative Services
 Department to implement the uniform method of collection for the City's Solid Waste
 non-valorem assessment, a copy of which Agreement is attached hereto and incorporated
 by reference as if set forth in full, is hereby approved.
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials are hereby authorized to execute
 said Agreement between the City of Parkland and Broward County.
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS DAY OF , 2007.
 CITY OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA
 MICHAEL UDINE, MAYOR
