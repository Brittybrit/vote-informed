 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-109
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, OPPOSING THE BROWARD COUNTY
 5 COMMISSION'S PROPOSED RETAIL ESTABLISHMENT
 6 PARKING SECURITY ORDINANCE, MANDATING RETAIL
 7 BUSINESSES TO INSTALL SECURITY SURVEILLANCE
 8 CAMERAS THAT WOULD COVER THE ENTIRE PARKING LOT
 9 TWENTY-FOUR HOURS A DAY, SEVEN DAYS A WEEK.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the Broward County Commission has proposed a Retail Establishment
 12 Parking Security Ordinance mandating that retail businesses install security surveillance
 13 cameras to survey the entire parking lot area for twenty-four hours a day, seven days a
 14 week unilaterally imposes a requirement on Broward County municipalities in violation of
 15 Home Rule authority ; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland while not taking a position on the merits of the
 18 proposed Retail Establishment Parking Security Ordinance wishes to preserve its home
 19 rule authority; and
 20
 21 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 22 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 23
 24 SECTION 1. That the City of Parkland opposes the Broward County Commission's
 25 proposed enactment of the Retail Establishment Parking Security Ordinance based upon
 26 the preservation of its Home Rule authority.
 27
 28 SECTION 2. The City Clerk shall forward this Resolution to all appropriate
 29 persons and parties
 30
 31 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 32 adoption.
 33
 34 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 5 day of November , 2008.
