 17 WHEREAS, Florida Stature 200.065 (TRIM — Truth in Millage) requires all
 18 municipalities, within 35 days of receipt of the city's certification of taxable value (on or
 19 around July 1), to advise the Property Appraiser of an estimated millage rate to be included
 20 in the TRIM notice sent to each taxpayer; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, an estimate and tentative millage rate should be proposed which would
 23 allow the City Commission latitude in meeting Fiscal Year 2008 budgetary requirements:
 24 and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, Florida Statute 2000.065 requires that a hearing date, other than that
 27 scheduled by the Broward County Commission and the Broward School Board, be set by
 28 each municipality be included in the TRIM Notice sent to each taxpayer; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the City Commission recommends that the estimated millage rate be
 31 set at 3.58 mills for Fiscal Year 2008, and that the public budget hearing be set at 7:00 p.m.
 32 on September 14, 2007 and 7:00 p.m. on September 24, 2007, in the City Commission
 33 Chambers, 6600 University Drive, Parkland, Florida 33067; and
 34
 35 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida deems it to be in
 36 the best interest of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland to allow the City
 37 Manager to send an estimated and tentative millage rate of 3.58 mills to the Property
 38 Appraiser and to set a date, time and place for the first and second public hearing.
 39
 40 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 41 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 42
 43 Section 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are hereby ratified and
 44 confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution.
 45
 46 Section 2. The City Manager is authorized to send to the Property Appraiser an
 47 estimated millage of 3.58 mills, this is an estimate and constitutes the maximum millage and
 48 the actual rate approved may be lower.
