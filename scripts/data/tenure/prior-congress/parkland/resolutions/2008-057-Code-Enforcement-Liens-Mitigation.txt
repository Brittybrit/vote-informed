 RESOLUTION NO. 2008 - 57
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, PROVIDING FOR
 MITIGATION OF CODE ENFORCEMENT LIENS;
 REPEALING RESOLUTION NO. 2004-32; SETTING
 FORTH THE PROCEDURES TO MITIGATE CODE
 ENFORCEMENT LIENS; PROVIDING FOR
 CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND
 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland wishes to provide an
 efficient fair method to mitigate code enforcement liens where there has been compliance
 with code enforcement board orders; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes this procedure to ensure that, at a
 minimum, the City's out-of-pocket cost is recovered, and that individuals do not profit by
 virtue of their non-compliance;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a
 part hereof.
 Section 2. Resolution No. 2004-32 is hereby repealed in its entirety.
 Section 3. The following process shall be used where there is a code enforcement
 lien filed of record and a property owner wishes to have said fine reduced in amount:
 A. The owner of the property shall submit a letter to the City Manager of the City
 of Parkland, or his/her designee, indicating the ownership interest, attaching a copy of the
 recorded lien, and setting forth the basis for the request that the lien be reduced. The request
 should contain a certification of compliance. If the request is made in conjunction with a
 proposed sale of a property, the sale price shall be disclosed, as well as the amounts of any
 mortgages or liens on the property.

<<PG>>
 B. The City Manager, or his/her designee (where the City Manager is not
 available), shall review the request in conjunction with the City Attorney and make a
 recommendation as to the application. The recommendation shall be in writing to the City
 Commission (with a copy to applicant) and state the amount of the reduction, if any. Any
 City Commissioner may, by written notice to the City Manager, request that the proposed
 reduction be brought before the Commission before becoming final. Upon said request, the
 reduction shall not become final, and the matter shall be set on a future City Commission
 agenda for a determination. The determination by the City Commission shall be final with
 regard to any proposed reduction and any reduction granted shall be terminated (and the
 original fine reinstated) if the reduced fine is not paid within fifteen(15) calendar days or at
 a closing on the sale of the subject property, provided said closing occurs within forty-
 five (45) days.
 C. If no City Commissioner makes the above referenced request within two (2)
 business days of the issuance of the notice set forth above,then in that event,the reduction of
 the lien shall be considered final,provided that the applicant pays the reduced amount within
 fifteen (15) days of written notice from the City Manager, or his/her designee, that the
 proposed reduction is final in accordance with the procedures set forth above or at a closing
 on the sale of the subject property, provided that closing occurs within forty-five (45)
 days. If said amounts are not paid to the City, then the lien shall remain as originally filed.
