 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING A CHANGE ORDER IN

 6 THE PURPOSE OF FURNISHING AND INSTALLING TWO LIGHT

 12 WHEREAS, On July 9, 2008 via Resolution No. 2008-75 the City Commission
 13 authorized the City Manager to piggyback a Palm Beach County Contract with West
 14 Construction, Inc., in for the purpose of constructing improvements at Pine Trails Park Phase III;
 15 and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the initial multipurpose field design intended to use two existing light poles
 18 that were to be relocated from the T-Ball fields; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the light requirements for the multipurpose field require more light fixtures
 21 than the existing poles are designed to carry; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, new poles and additional fixtures will be necessary to complete the project
 24 and meet the necessary lighting requirements; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, Staff recommends a Change Order with West Construction, Inc. in the
 27 amount of$88,846.54 for the purpose of installing the required light poles and fixtures; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the cost for the aforementioned poles and fixtures will be reimbursed to the
 30 City by Florida Towers LLC as part of their Agreement with the City; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes that a change order in the
 33 amount of$88,846.54 for the purpose of installing required light poles and fixtures at Pine Trails
 34 Park is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 35
 36 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 37 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 38
 39 SECTION 1. The City Commission hereby authorizes the City Manager to execute a
 40 change order with West Construction, Inc. in the amount of $88,846.54 for the purpose of
 41 furnishing and installing two additional light poles and associated fixtures AT Pine Trails Park
 42 Phase III.
