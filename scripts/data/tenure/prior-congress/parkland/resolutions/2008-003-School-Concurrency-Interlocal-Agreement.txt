 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-03
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ADOPTING AND APPROVING AN
 5 INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH THE BROWARD COUNTY
 6 SCHOOL BOARD RELATING TO SCHOOL CONCURRENCY.
 7
 8 WHEREAS, the Florida Legislature recently mandated that public school concurrency
 9 be adopted by all Florida school boards, counties and non-exempt municipalities by the end
 io of 2008 however Broward County and the municipalities within Broward County are
 11 mandated to adopt public school concurrency no later than February 1, 2008; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, school concurrency requires the coordination of planning efforts between
 14 the School Board of Broward County, Broward County and local governments to ensure that
 1s residential development orders are issued and conditioned upon the availability of public
 16 school facilities; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the Interlocal Agreement will establish a mechanism for coordinating the
 19 Public School Facilities Element of the local governments; establishes a process for the
 20 School Board and the local governments to agree on the amount, type, and distribution of
 21 population growth; allows for the School Board and local governments to share information
 22 about the planning of new public schools and new residential developments; establishes a
 23 process for school siting and encourages the siting of schools near urban residential areas
 24 as well as near parks, libraries, and other public facilities; specifies districtwide Level of
 25 Service (LOS) standards for public schools and the process for modifying these LOS
 26 standards; establishes a financially feasible Public School Facilities Program and a process
 27 for including the Public School Capital Facilities Program in the Comprehensive Plan of the
 28 local governments; a definition of the geographic application of school concurrency;
 29 establishes a uniform procedure for the implementation of school concurrency within the
 30 district; and includes a rule for terminating, suspending, and amending the interlocal
 31 agreement.
 32
 33 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 34 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 35
 36 Section 1 : That the appropriate City officials do hereby approve entering into an
 37 Interlocal Agreement with the School Board of Broward County, Broward County and the
 38 municipalities within Broward County for the development and enforcement of the Public
 39 Schools Facility Element.
 40
 41 Section 2: The appropriate City Officials are hereby authorized to sign the Interlocal
 42 Agreement that relates to School Concurrency.
 43
 44 Section 3: This Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately upon its
 45 passage and adoption.
 46
 47 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 16 day of January 2008.
