 AGREEMENT
 THIS AGREEMENT is entered into by and between the CITY OF PARKLAND (City), a
 municipal corporation and COUNTRY POINT MASTER HOME OWNERS ASSOCIATION
 (Association), as follows:
 WITNESSETH:
 WHEREAS, Association maintains certain medians in the City and has an interest in the
 beautification of medians in public right-of-ways adjacent to its community; and
 WHEREAS, the City is prepared to enter into the attached maintenance agreement with the
 Florida Department of Transportation (FDOT), which agreement is hereinafter referred to as the
 Joint Participation Agreement; and
 WHEREAS, the City and FDOT will provide for the beautification of certain public right-
 of-ways, more particularly described on Exhibit A (the Beautified Property); and
 WHEREAS, the Association is located adjacent to the Beautified Property and wishes to
 assure that it is continually maintained; and
 WHEREAS, the City also recognizes the need to continually maintain the Property and to
 comply with its Joint Participation Agreement with FDOT (FDOT Agreement); and
 WHEREAS, in order to enable the City to justify the expenditure of funds for the
 landscape improvements to the Beautified Property, as set forth in the FDOT Agreement, the City
 is seeking assurances from the Association that it will maintain the Beautified Property; and
 WHEREAS, Association is prepared to give said assurances and the purpose of this
 Agreement is to memorialize this undertaking;
 NOW THEREFORE,be it agreed by and between the parties as follows:
 Section 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a part
 hereof
 Section 2. The City will undertake the installation of certain landscape on the Beautified
 Property pursuant to the FDOT Agreement. Subsequent to completion of said improvements, the
 City will provide notice to Association that the improvements are complete.
 Section 3. Upon receipt of said notice, the Association shall be responsible for the
 maintenance and care of the landscaping installed on the Beautified Property ("Maintenance
 Services"). This will include mowing the lawn, maintaining plant beds and plants, as well as
 shrubs and trees. The Maintenance Services to be provided by the Association are as follows:

<<PG>>
 (a) To maintain, which means proper watering and fertilization of all plants and
 keeping them as free as practicable from disease and harmful insects; and
 (b) To properly mulch the plant beds; and
 (c) To keep the Beautified Property free of weeds and litter; and
 (d) To mow and cut the grass to a proper length; and
 (e) To properly prune all plants, including the (1) removal of dead or diseased parts of
 plants, and the (2) pruning of such parts thereof that present a visual hazard to those using
 the roadway; and
 (f) To remove and replace dead or diseased plants in their entirety, or remove and
 replace those plants that fall below the standards outlined in Exhibit B attached hereto and
 incorporated herein; and
 (g) All plants removed for whatever reason shall be replaced by plants of similar size
 and grade as specified in the original plans and specifications.
 (h) Plants shall be those items which would be scientifically classified as plants and
 include but are not limited to trees, grass, groundcovers, and shrubs.
 (i) It is understood by the parties that Maintenance Services shall not include the
 maintenance or repair of any median curbing, curb and gutter, road surface, or roadway
 drainage facilities.
 (j) To the Beautified Property; Association shall be responsible for the maintenance,
 repair, and/or replacement of such utilities (irrigation systems/electricity), as necessary to
 keep utilities in good working order, including the costs and expense associated therewith.
 (k) The Association shall provide, and be solely responsible for all costs and liabilities
 that are associated with or arise out of, the Maintenance Services for the Beautified
 Property as provided in his Agreement.
 (1) The maintenance shall be provided by the Association in strict compliance with all
 governmental entities' and agencies' permits, requirements, rules, acts, statutes, ordinances,
 orders, regulations and restrictions, including but not limited to the following entities, if
 applicable, (a) the North Springs Improvement District; (b) the Florida Department of
 Transportation; (c) Broward County, Florida; and (d) the City of Parkland, Florida.
