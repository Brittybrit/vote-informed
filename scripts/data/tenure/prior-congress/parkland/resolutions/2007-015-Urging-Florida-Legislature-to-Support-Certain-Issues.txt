 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 4 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, URGING
 5 MEMBERS OF THE FLORIDA LEGISLATURE TO
 6 SUPPORT THE FOLLOWING ISSUES DURING THE
 7 2007 LEGISLATIVE SESSION.
 8
 9 WHEREAS, municipal self-determination and local self-government are
 10 constantly under attack from a variety of public and private interests; and
 11
 12 WHEREAS, numerous counties have recently proposed revisions to their charters
 13 that would adversely impact the Home-Rule powers of municipalities within those
 14 counties; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, a majority of Florida's municipalities have experienced negative
 17 impacts to infrastructure, housing stock and employment base from a series of natural
 18 disasters; higher than expected increases in the cost of building products; double-digit
 19 increases in the cost of property insurance; and inequities in the existing property-tax
 20 system; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, Florida's continuing population increase has presented a challenge
 23 to cities struggling to meet the infrastructure needs required to accommodate new growth
 24 and development without an infrastructure revenue source dedicated to municipalities;
 25 and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, various proposed revisions to the property-tax system would have
 28 substantial adverse impacts for all municipalities in Florida should they be adopted; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, Florida's municipalities are dependent on other governmental
 31 entities for financial stability and under the current system the state, counties, and school
 32 districts, control all funding sources authorized for implementing capital improvements
 33 and complying with concurrency requirements; and
 34
 35 WHEREAS, Gov. Charlie Crist and Florida's House and Senate leaders have an
 36 opportunity to address the unique concerns of municipalities during the upcoming 2007
 37 legislative session.
 38
 39 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 40 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 41
 42 SECTION 1. The City of Parkland urges Gov. Charlie Crist and members of
 43 the Florida Legislature to support legislation during the 2007 legislative session that:
 44
 45 Supports a tax structure that is fair and equitable, and is competitively neutral and
 46 allows municipalities the flexibility to provide adequate services in their communities;

<<PG>>
 1
 2 Preserves the dedicated documentary stamp fees as a funding source for the State
 3 and Local Government Housing Trust Fund (Sadowski Trust Fund), eliminates the cap
 4 placed on fund distribution, and prohibits any of these trust fund dollars from being
 5 diverted to the General Revenue Fund;
 6
 7 Provides a comprehensive and uniform workers' compensation system for all
 8 municipal employees, regardless of employee classification, at a reasonable cost to
 9 employers;
 10
 11 Provides a dedicated and recurring source of revenue, including municipal local-
 12 option fuel taxes, rental-car surcharges, or other taxes or fees, for municipalities to fund
 13 transportation projects such as TRIP, urban arterial and other projects. Furthermore, the
 14 existing distribution formula for local-option transportation taxes imposed by a county
 15 should be revised to allow for a more equitable distribution to municipalities and provides
 16 for indexing of local-option fuel taxes;
 17
 18 Requires legislative authorization prior to the Florida Department of
 19 Transportation removing or delaying any project that is contained in the FDOT Five-Year
 20 Work Plan;
 21
 22 Preserves municipal authority to franchise cable and video services.
 23
 24 SECTION 2. A copy of this resolution shall be provided to Gov. Charlie
 25 Crist, Senate President Ken Pruitt, House of Representatives Speaker Marco Rubio and
 26 members of the Florida Legislature.
