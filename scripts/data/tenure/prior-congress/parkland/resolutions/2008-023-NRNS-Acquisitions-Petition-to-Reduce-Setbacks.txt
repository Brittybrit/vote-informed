 12 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of
 13 Parkland, Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166,
 14 Florida Statutes, and the Code of Ordinances, is authorized and empowered to
 15 consider petitions related to zoning and land development orders; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, a proper Notice of Public Hearing was sent to property
 18 owners of record within 500 feet of the subject property and was published in a
 19 newspaper qualified to carry legal advertisements; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, at a regular meeting of the Planning and Zoning Board, taking
 22 place on February 21, 2008, a public hearing was held to hear testimony and
 23 receive exhibits for and against the Petition of N.R.N.S. Acquisition IV, LLC
 24 (owners) requesting a variance from Section 22-34 in the Code of Ordinances, as
 25 adopted by the City of Parkland; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, at a regular meeting of the City Commission, taking place on
 28 April 2, 2008, a public hearing was held to hear testimony and receive exhibits for
 29 and against the Petition of N.R.N.S. Acquisition IV, LLC (owners) requesting a
 30 variance from Section 22-34 in the Code of Ordinances, as adopted by the City
 31 of Parkland; and
 32
 33 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and
 34 testimony presented by the Applicant and other interested parties and the
 35 recommendations of the various City of Parkland review agencies and staff.
 36
 37 NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the City Commission of the City of
 38 Parkland, Florida, that;
 39
 40 Section 1. After due consideration of all testimony and exhibits and
 41 review of the standards for granting a variance as set forth in Article XXII, Section
 42 22-323 of the Code of Ordinances, all necessary standards for granting a
 43 variance have not been satisfied to reduce the eastern building setback by 40
 44 feet for the wood horse stables, 39 feet for the tack room, 39 feet for the paddock
 45 and 35 feet for the wood stalls on the property located at 6740 NW 82 Terrace;
 46 and

 1 Section 2. The variance request of N.R.N.S. Acquisitions IV, LLC,
