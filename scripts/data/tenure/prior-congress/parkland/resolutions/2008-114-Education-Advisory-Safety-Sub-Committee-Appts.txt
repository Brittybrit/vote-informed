 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-114
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPOINTING THREE (3)
 MEMBERS TO THE EDUCATIONAL ADVISORY BOARD,
 SPECIAL SAFETY(SUB) COMMITTEE
 WHEREAS, in accordance with the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances, the
 Educational Advisory Board shall also sit as a Special Safety (Sub) Committee to
 consider matters relating to student safety and make recommendations directly to the full
 Board on said matter.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission shall also appoint three (3) members who are
 not on the Board to serve on the Special Safety(Sub) Committee.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. The Parkland City Commission hereby appoints three (3) members
 to the Educational Advisory Board Special Safety (Sub) Committee as follows:
 Special Safety (Sub) Committee Board Member Adam Zola
 Special Safety (Sub) Committee Board Member Al Ortenzo
 Special Safety(Sub) Committee Board Member Peter P. Ochinko
 Section 3. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 19 DAB OF November , 2008.
