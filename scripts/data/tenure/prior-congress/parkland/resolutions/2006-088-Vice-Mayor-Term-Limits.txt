 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-88
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SETTING FORTH A PROCEDURE
 FOR APPOINTING A VICE MAYOR.
 WHEREAS, Section 3.01 of the City Charter provides that the City Commission
 shall appoint a Vice Mayor from among the members of the Commission; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to transition to a procedure for
 appointing the Vice Mayor on a yearly basis;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. The City Commission shall appoint a Vice Mayor in November
 2006. The City Commission shall thereafter consider the appointment of a Vice Mayor
 annually in November.
 Section 2. If the Commission fails to timely appoint a successor Vice Mayor
 pursuant to the above process, the Commissioner serving as Vice Mayor shall continue to
 serve until a new Vice Mayor is appointed.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 17 DAY OF November , 2006.
