 RESOLUTION NO. 2006- 77
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, DECLARING A SPECIAL MUNICIPAL
 ELECTION ON NOVEMBER 7, 2006 FOR THE PURPOSES OF FILLING
 A VACANCY FOR THE COMMISSION SEAT FOR DISTRICT IV AND
 SETTING THE QUALIFYING PERIOD
 WHEREAS, by virtue of the resignation of Vice Mayor Mimi Ribotsky, there is a
 vacancy in District IV; and
 WHEREAS, no action has been taken to fill the vacancy within thirty (30) days of its
 creation; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland therefore desires to hold a
 special election, concurrent with the general election of November 7, 2006, for the purpose of
 filling said vacancy;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 SECTION 1. The above Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a part hereof.
 SECTION 2. A special election is hereby set for November 7, 2006, for the purpose of
 filling the vacancy for District IV. The person elected shall fill the unexpired term for District IV,
 as that term is set forth in the City Charter, including any amendments thereto.
 SECTION 3. The qualifying period for candidates shall open at 12:00 noon on
 September 1, 2006, and close at 12:00 noon on September 8, 2006. Qualifying shall be at
 Parkland City Hall, 6600 University Drive, Parkland, Florida in the City Clerk's Office.
 SECTION 4. The City Clerk shall undertake the necessary measures for notice of
 qualifying and of the election and coordinate with the Supervisor of Elections. The Supervisor of
 Elections is hereby authorized to take all actions necessary for the purposes of holding the special
 election for the vacancy in District IV. The City Clerk is hereby given authority to undertake all
 necessary measures with regard to the Supervisor of Elections and the Department of State for the
 purposes of holding said election and providing notices therefore and for the appropriate
 advertising of the election. The City Clerk shall undertake all necessary measures as required by
 Florida law for these purposes.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 9 day of AUGUST , 2006.
