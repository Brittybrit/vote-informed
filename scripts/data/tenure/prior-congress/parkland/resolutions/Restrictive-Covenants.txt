 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-56B
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING THE
 INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT FOR THE ACQUISITION,
 IMPROVEMENT, ENHANCEMENT, OPERATION AND
 MANAGEMENT AND DECLARATION OF RESTRICTIVE
 COVENANTS FOR GREEN SPACE SITE GS-464 QUIGLEY
 PARK ADDITION, TRACT G, PARKLAND LAKES P.U.D.,
 BETWEEN THE CITY OF PARKLAND AND BROWARD
 COUNTY; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING
 FOR SEVERABLILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, The City entered into a contract with TEP Holdings, Inc. to
 purchase 5 acres along Parkside Drive; and
 WHEREAS, The property will be used as a passive park, the City approached
 Broward County to see if it would be interested in assisting in the purchase of the site;
 and
 WHEREAS, The County agreed that the purchase qualified under its bond
 program; and
 WHEREAS, An Interlocal Agreement (ILA) and Declaration of Restrictive
 Covenants (DRC) are required under the bond program for the City to be reimbursed for
 the purchase of Quigley Park Addition; and
 WHEREAS, The City Commission of the City of Parkland approves the
 Interlocal Agreement and the Declaration of Restrictive Covenants with Broward County
 for the purpose of acquiring, improving, enhancing, operations and management of the
 Quigley Park Addition site within the City of Parkland; and
 WHEREAS, Included with the Interlocal Agreement is the Declaration of
 Restrictive Covenants; and
 WHEREAS, The property is to be added to Quigley Park and utilized as a passive
 park trailhead and overflow parking; and
 WHEREAS, The City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best interest
 of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland to
 execute the Interlocal Agreement and Declaration of Restrictive Covenants for Tract G,
 Parkland Lakes P.U.D with Broward County.; and

 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND FLORIDA, THAT;
 Section 1: The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached hereto are
 hereby incorporate herein.
 Section 2: The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves the
 Interlocal Agreement and Declaration of Restrictive Covenants with Broward County for
 the acquisition, improvement, enhancement, operation, management and preservation of
 the Quigley Park Addition.
 Section 3: The appropriate City officials are hereby authorized and directed to
 execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this resolution.
 Section 4: All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are
 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 Section 5: If any clause, section, other part or application of this Resolution is
 held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or invalid, in part of
 application, it shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions or applications of this
 Resolution.
