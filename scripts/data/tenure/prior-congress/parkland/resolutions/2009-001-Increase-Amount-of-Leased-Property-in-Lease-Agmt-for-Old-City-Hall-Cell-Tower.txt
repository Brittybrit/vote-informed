4 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
5 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING AN INCREASE IN
6 THE AMOUNT OF LEASED PROPERTY COVERED IN THE
7 LAND LEASE AGREEMENT BETWEEN FLORIDA TOWERS
8 LLC AND THE CITY OF PARKLAND FOR PROPERTY AT
9 6500 PARKSIDE DRIVE.
10
11 WHEREAS, Florida Towers LLC (Lessee) entered into a Land Lease Agreement
12 with the City of Parkland to install a cellular tower at Old City Hall located at 6500
13 Parkside Drive; and
14
15 WHEREAS, it was determined that the individual cellular telephone carriers
16 would like to operate their own generators in case there is a loss of power; and
17
18 WHEREAS, this will require additional property to be included in the leased
19 property; and
20
21 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission feels it is in the best interest
22 of operations of the Old City Hall location that the leased property be increased to allow
23 the individual carriers to operate their own generator;
24
25 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
26 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS;
27
28 SECTION 1. The Land Lease entered into between Florida Towers LLC and the
29 City of Parkland is hereby amended to according to the attached First Amendment to the
30 Land Lease Agreement.
31
32 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 7 day of Jan , 2009.

LAND LEASE AGREEMENT
THIS AMENDMENT TO LAND LEASE AGREEMENT (hereinafter referred to as
"Amendment ") is executed this 20 • #'da of 40-44..A , 2009, by and
between FLORIDA TOWERS, LLC, a Florida limited liabilitompany (hereinafter
referred to as "Lessee ") and CITY OF PARKLAND, a municipality (hereinafter
referred to as "Lessor ").
WHEREAS, Lessee and Lessor entered into that certain Land Lease Agreement dated
August 15, 2007 (hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement ") whereby Lessor Leased to
Lessee a certain portion of the real property located at 6500 Parkside Drive, City of
Parkland, County of Broward, State of Florida (hereinafter referred to as the "Leased
Property"); and
WHEREAS, Lessor and Lessee desire to amend the Agreement to allow additional
property to be leased for installation of a generator; and .
NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency
of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties hereto covenant, agree and bind themselves
to the following modifications to the Agreement:
1. Section 1. Premises. Lessor owns a parcel of land ( "Land ") located in the
City of Parkland, County of Broward, State of Florida, commonly known as Old
Town Center (Folio # 474135024710). The Land is more particularly described
in Exhibit A annexed hereto. Subject to the provisions of Paragraph 2 below
( "Effective Date/Due Diligence Period "), Lessor hereby leases to Lessee and
Lessee leases from Lessor approximately 1,595 4 square feet (30' x 50')
square -feet of the Land and all access and utility easements necessary or
desirable therefor ( "Premises "), as may be described generally in Exhibit B
annexed hereto.
2. Exhibit B is hereby deleted in its entirety and replaced with the attached revised
Exhibit B.
3. This Amendment will be governed by and construed and enforced in accordance
with the laws of the state in which the Property is located without regard to
principles of conflicts of law.
