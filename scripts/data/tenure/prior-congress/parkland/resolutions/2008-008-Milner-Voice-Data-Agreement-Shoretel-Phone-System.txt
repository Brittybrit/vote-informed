 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-08
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AUTHORIZE THE CITY MANAGER
 5 TO EXECUTE A CONTRACT WITH MILNER VOICE AND DATA
 6 FOR A SHORETEL TELEPHONE AND VOICE PROCESSING
 7 SYSTEM AND GREATAMERICA LEASING CORPORATION FOR
 8 LEASING OF THE SYSTEM; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY;
 9 AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, City of Parkland ("City") staff advertised an RFP for the purpose of
 12 awarding a contract to provide the City with a telephone and voice processing system; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, a selection committee consisting of the Finance and Administrative
 15 Services Director, Information Systems Manager, Parks and Recreation Director, Library
 16 Director, City Clerk and Permit Technician (reflecting staff members from all affected
 17 departments), reviewed the 10 submittals with presentations; and selected the top four (4)
 18 ranked companies; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, during the December 19, 2007 meeting, the City Commission ranked the
 21 four (4) top companies, and determined that the Shoretel system proposed by Milner Voice
 22 and Data provided the latest technology at the most cost effective price; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, staff was instructed to negotiate a contract with the City Commission's
 25 first choice — Milner Voice and Data
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best interest of
 28 the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland for the
 29 City to enter into contract with Milner Voice and Data for a Shoretel telephone and voice
 30 processing system and leasing with GreatAmerica Leasing Corporation;
 31
 32 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 33 PARKLAND, FLORIDA THAT;
 34
 35 SECTION 1. The foregoing 'WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as being
 36 true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached hereto are hereby
 37 incorporate herein.
 38
 39 SECTION 2. The City Manager is hereby authorized to execute a contract with Milner
 40 Voice and Data for a telephone and voice processing system and for leasing of the system
 41 from GreatAmerica Leasing Corporation for City Hall, Library and Amphitheater.
 42
 43 SECTION 3. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are hereby
 44 repealed to the extent of such conflict.
