 FIRST AMENDMENT TO
 FACILITY CONSTRUCTION AGREEMENT
 T IS FIRS AMENDMENT TO THE AGREEMENT (the "Amendment") dated
 as of A.\ , 2006 is entered into by and between the CITY OF
 PARKLAN , a municipal corporation organized and existing under the laws of the State
 of Florida("City") and WCI COMMUNITIES, INC., a Delaware corporation("WCI").
 WITNESSETH
 WHEREAS, the City and WCI entered into that certain Facility Construction
 Agreement for a community recreation building at Pine Trails Park ("Community
 Center") dated October 7, 2002 (the "Agreement") concerning Facility Construction for a
 community known as Heron Bay North, developed by WCI located within the City;
 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 4 of the Agreement, WCI is required to pay for
 the cost of construction of the Community Center, obtaining performance bonds therefore
 and design fees up to a maximum amount of Three Million and no/100 Dollars
 ($3,000,000.00) ("Maximum Amount");
 WHEREAS, WCI wishes to contribute an additional amount to the City in the
 amount of Three Hundred Fifty-Nine Thousand Five Hundred Ninety-Three and 82/100
 Dollars ($359, 593.82) which the City may use in its sole and absolute discretion towards
 the Community Center or its parent property, Pine Trails Park;
 WHEREAS, the City advised WCI the cost of construction of the Community
 Center substantially exceeds WCI's Maximum Amount contribution; and
 WHEREAS, the parties have agreed to fulfill their obligations under the
 Agreement in an alternative manner by converting WCI's construction obligation into a
 monetary obligation, and as a result, the City, in its sole and absolute discretion, can
 spend the money provided pursuant to this Amendment towards the Community Center
 or its parent property, Pine Trails Park.
 NOW, THERFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and agreements
 herein contained and other good and valuable consideration, City and WCI agree as
 follows:
 1. The above recitals are true and correct and are incorporated herein by this reference.
 2. The total amount WCI shall pay pursuant to the Agreement and this Amendment is
 Three Million Three Hundred Fifty-Nine Thousand Five Hundred Ninety-Three and
 82/100 Dollars ($3,359,593.82), of which $489,115.00 (consisting of$360,000 in design
 fees and $129,115.00 in accrued performance bond fees) has already been paid by WCI.
 Pursuant to this Amendment the net amount due and owing to the City is Two Million
 Eight Hundred Seventy Thousand Four Hundred Seventy-Eight and 82/100 Dollars

 ($2,870,478.82) ("Final Amount"). The effect of this paragraph is to increase the
 payment made to the City pursuant to the Agreement
 3. WCI shall have the option, in its sole and absolute discretion, to pay the Final Amount
 either (1) in one lump sum payment paid within sixty (60) days of the date both parties
 sign this Amendment; or (2) in four (4) equal quarterly installments of Seven Hundred
 Seventeen Thousand Six Hundred Nineteen and 70/100 Dollars ($717,619.70). WCI
 shall not deduct any further performance bond fees from the Final Amount regardless of
 the manner in which it is paid. If WCI pays the Final Amount in one lump sum, upon
 receipt of said Final Amount, the City shall provide WCI with a letter authorizing WCI to
 terminate the performance bond securing the Maximum Amount. If WCI elects to make
 quarterly payments as provided for herein, upon receipt of a quarterly payment, the City
 shall provide WCI with a letter authorizing WCI to reduce its performance bond securing
 the Maximum Amount in accordance with the schedule attached hereto as Exhibit "A"
 and incorporated herein.
 4. At such time as WCI pays to the City the entire Final Amount, WCI shall have no
 further duties and obligations under the Agreement.
 5. This paragraph pertains solely to local park impact fee credits. If WCI pays the Final
 Amount in one lump sum pursuant to Section 3.(l) of this Amendment, WCI or its
 assigns shall immediately and irrevocably receive a credit in the amount of Three Million
 and no/100 Dollars ($3,000,000.00) to be applied against local park impact fee credits
 assessed to the Project (as defined in the Agreement) or to any other real property located
 west of University Drive within the City which is owned by WCI, now or in the future, as
 provided for in Section 13. of the Agreement ("Impact Fee Credits"). If WCI elects to
 pay the Final Amount in quarterly installments pursuant to Section 3.(2) of this
 Amendment, WCI or its assigns shall immediately and irrevocably receive said Impact
 Fee Credits as set forth in Exhibit"A".
 6. The Impact Fee Credits shall be subject to the same restrictions and conditions as set
 forth in Section 13. of the Agreement.
 7. In the event of any conflict between this Amendment and the Agreement, this
 Amendment shall control. In all other respects, the Agreement shall remain in full force
 and effect.
 8. This Amendment shall be binding on and shall inure to the benefit of the parties, their
 respective heirs, successors and assigns.
