 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-97
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SUPPORTING SENATE BILL 1115
 5 BEFORE THE UNITED STATES CONGRESS KNOWN AS THE
 6 "ENERGY EFFICIENCY PROMOTION ACT" AUTHORIZING
 7 IN SECTION 605 OF IMPROVED INITIATIVES AIMED AT
 8 IMPROVING ENERGY EFFICIENCY AND REDUCING
 9 GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS, AS WELL AS
 10 IMPLEMENTING LEGISLATION ON A NATIONAL LEVEL.
 11
 12 WHEREAS, Senate Bill 1115 is a giant step toward the reduction of greenhouse
 13 gases that substantially contribute to global warming, and initiating through financial
 14 incentives energy efficiency as a National Policy; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS,the House is considering companion legislation; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, Senate Bill 1115 is modeled after the Housing and Urban
 19 Development (HUD) Community Development Block Grant Programs that have been
 20 successful in the past.
 21
 22 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 23 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 24
 25 Section 1. That the City of Parkland welcomes and supports Senate Bill 1115
 26 that shall progressively encourage programs promoting energy efficiency and reducing
 27 global warming on a local and on a national level by providing financial assistance
 28 toward the reduction of greenhouse gases.
 29
 30 Section 2. That the City Clerk is herby directed to transmit a copy of this
 31 Resolution to the President of the United States, the Speaker of the House in Congress,
 32 the Governor of the State of Florida, the President of the Senate, the Speaker of the
 33 House, each member of the Broward County Federal Legislative Delegation, the Broward
 34 County Commission, the Mayor of each municipality in Broward County, the Florida
 35 League of Cities, and the Broward League of Cities.
 36
 37 Section 3. That this resolution shall be in full force and effect immediately
 38 upon its passage and adoption.
 39
 40 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 9 D O September , 2007.
