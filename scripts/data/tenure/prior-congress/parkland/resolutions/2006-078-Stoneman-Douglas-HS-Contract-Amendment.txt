 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-78
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA FOR APPROVAL OF THE
 FIRST AMENDMENT TO THE CONTRACT ENTERED INTO
 WITH THE SCHOOL BOARD OF BROWARD COUNTY TO
 PROVIDE STADIUM IMPROVEMENTS FOR THE
 STONEMAN DOUGLAS HIGH SCHOOL AND PROVIDING
 AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland entered into a contract with the School Board
 of Broward County (SBBC) via Resolution 2005-22 to provide lighting and a track for
 the Stoneman Douglas High School stadium; and
 WHEREAS, since execution of the contract, an additional $300,000 in funding
 has been committed to the project by the School Board of Broward County (SBBC);
 and
 WHEREAS, SBBC and Stoneman Douglas High School desires to utilize the
 additional monies toward the Stadium Improvements Project in order to expand the
 contract to provide for the completion of the stadium bleachers and pressbox; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes it is in the best interests of the City
 residents to amend the contract to allow the City to continue our project procurement
 relationship with the SBBC; and
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 SECTION 1. The City Commission authorizes the City Manager or his
 designee to approve the first amendment to the contract between the School Board
 of Broward County and the City to provide for the appropriation of additional monies,
 increasing the total SBBC financial commitment to the project from $321,211 to
 $621,211, and to amend the contract to complete the bleachers and pressbox as
 part of the Stadium Project.

<<PG>>
 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 adoption.
