 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-27
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ACCEPTING THE
 RESULTS OF THE MUNICIPAL GENERAL ELECTION
 HELD ON MARCH 14, 2006.
 WHEREAS, the results of the Municipal General Election held on March 14,
 2006, have been canvassed by the Broward County Canvassing Board in accordance with
 the provisions of the general election law.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the City Commission accepts the results of the Municipal
 General Election held on March 14, 2006.
 Section 2. That Michael Udine is declared elected to the office of the Mayor
 At Large.
 Section 3. That Jay D. Smith is declared elected to the office of the
 Commission for District 1 and ran unopposed.
 Section 4. That Jared E. Moskowitz is declared elected to the office of the
 Commission for District 2.
 Section 5. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 22 DAY OF MARCH ,2006.
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA
 ROBERT A. MARKS, MAYOR
 ATTEST:
 SANDRA COUZZO, CMC L41
 CITY CLERK
