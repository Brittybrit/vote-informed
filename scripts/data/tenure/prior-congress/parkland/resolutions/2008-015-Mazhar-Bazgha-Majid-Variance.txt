 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA DENYING MAZHAR & BAZGHA
 5 MAJID VARIANCE PETITION FROM SECTION 22-54 OF THE
 6 CODE OF ORDINANCES TO REDUCE THE SIDE BUILDING
 7 SETBACK FROM TWENTY-FIVE (25) FEET TO EIGHT (8)
 8 FEET FOR A STORAGE SHED ON THEIR PROPERTY AT
 9 7167 NW 62 TERRACE, AS MORE PARTICULARLY
 10 DESCRIBED HEREIN; AND PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE
 11 DATE; CASE NO. 03-V-07.
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of
 14 Parkland, Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166,
 15 Florida Statutes, and the Code of Ordinances, is authorized and empowered to
 16 consider petitions related to zoning and land development orders; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, a proper Notice of Public Hearing was sent to property
 19 owners of record within 500 feet of the subject property and was published in a
 20 newspaper qualified to carry legal advertisements; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, at a regular meeting of the Planning and Zoning Board, taking
 23 place on January 10, 2008, a public hearing was held to hear testimony and
 24 receive exhibits for and against the Petition of Mazhar and Bazgha Majid
 25 (owners) requesting a variance from Section 22-54 in the Code of Ordinances, as
 26 adopted by the City of Parkland, and the Planning and Zoning Board
 27 recommended denial (7-0); and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, at a regular meeting of the City Commission, taking place on
 30 February 6, 2008 and April 16, 2008, a public hearing was held to hear testimony
 31 and receive exhibits for and against the Petition of Mazhar and Bazgha Majid
 32 (owners) requesting a variance from Section 22-54 in the Code of Ordinances, as
 33 adopted by the City of Parkland; and
 34
 35 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and
 36 testimony presented by the Applicant and other interested parties and the
 37 recommendations of the various City of Parkland review agencies and staff.
 38
 39 NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the City Commission of the City of
 40 Parkland, Florida, that;
 41
 42 Section 1. After due consideration of all testimony and exhibits and
 43 review of the standards for granting a variance as set forth in Article XXII, Section
 44 22-323 of the Code of Ordinances, the City Commission finds that no hardship
 45 exists and that circumstances causing the application were self-created.
