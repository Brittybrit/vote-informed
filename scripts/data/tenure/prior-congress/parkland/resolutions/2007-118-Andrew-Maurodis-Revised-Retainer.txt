 RESOLUTION NO. 2007— 118
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING A REVISED
 RETAINER WITH CITY ATTORNEY ANDREW MAURODIS.
 WHEREAS, Andrew Maurodis has served as City Attorney for the City of Parkland
 since June of 1988; and
 WHEREAS, while his monthly retainer has been adjusted during that period, his hourly
 rate has not and is no longer close to reflecting current market conditions; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to provide for a fair and equitable retainer
 agreement for its City Attorney;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. The attached retainer letter dated October 22, 2007 from the Law Offices
 of Andrew S. Maurodis is hereby approved effective for the billing month beginning November
 1, 2007.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 7 DAY OF November , 2007.
 CITY OF D FL RIDA
 M C AEL INE, MAYOR
 ATTEST:
 SANDRA COUZZO, CM Y CLERK
