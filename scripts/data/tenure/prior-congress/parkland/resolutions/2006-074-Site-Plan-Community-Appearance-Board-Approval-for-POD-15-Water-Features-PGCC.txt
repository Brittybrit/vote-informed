 3 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:

 6 SECTION 1. The Site Plan and Community Appearance Board petitions of WCI
 7 Communities Inc for Pod 15 Water Features within Parkland Golf & Country Club is hereby
 8 APPROVED, subject to the conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition to the
 9 general requirements otherwise provided by ordinance:
 10
 11 1. This approval is based upon the plans entitled "POD 15 - Buffer Features"
 12 dated June 26, 2006 and received by the Planning and Zoning Department
 13 on August 4, 2006.
 14 2. As set forth in Ordinance 2002-04 and 2005-23, all conditions of the
 15 Parkland Golf & Country Club not specifically amended by this site plan
 16 shall govern the development.
 17 3. The City shall inspect all landscaping surrounding the water features on a
 18 periodic basis to insure that they are being properly maintained and are
 19 growing in at a normal rate. The City may require the applicant to add
 20 additional landscaping material to any water feature area that is not
 21 growing in at a normal rate as would be expected based on the approved
 22 landscaping plans. Failure to maintain the landscaping and water feature
 23 or respond to a City request as set forth herein shall constitute a violation
 24 of this approval and the City Code of Ordinances.
 25 4. The party responsible for the water feature area shall employ a full-time
 26 grounds/building maintenance person to maintain the water feature area as
 27 required herein.
 28 5. The petitioner shall ensure all airbourne particles associated with the
 29 construction of the POD 15 Water Feature does not negatively affect
 30 neighboring properties.
 31 6. The petitioner shall notify all affected adjacent neighboring property owners
 32 of the schedule for the construction of the POD 15 Water Feature.
 33 7. If any damage occurs to water features, the petitioner shall repair the
 34 damage within a reasonable period of time, as determined by the City.
 35 8. The water features for POD 15 shall receive a final certificate of occupancy
 36 no later than eighteen (18) months from issuance of a building permit. If
 37 the Project is not completed as evidenced by the Certificate of Completion
 38 or other statement of completion by the City within eighteen (18) months of
 39 the date of the issuance of a building permit, then in that event, the site
 40 plan and building permit referencing the project shall be null and void and
 41 Owner shall be required to apply for a new site plan approval and new
 42 building permits. Additionally, the City shall not allow inspections and plans
 43 review on such project to occur. The applicant is required to enter into an
 44 agreement with the City of Parkland, as prepared by the City Attorney, that
 45 addresses all appropriate penalties if the eighteen (18) month deadline is
 46 not met.
 47 9. Any substitution of materials shall require City approval prior to the
