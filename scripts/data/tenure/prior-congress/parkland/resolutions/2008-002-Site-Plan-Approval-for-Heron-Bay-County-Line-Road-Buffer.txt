 9 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,

 20
 21 WHEREAS, with this application, WCI Communities Inc., is requesting site plan

 31 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 32 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 33
 34 SECTION 1. The community appearance board application for the County Line Road
 35 landscape buffer of the Heron Bay residential development is hereby APPROVED with the
 36 following conditions:
 37
 38 1. This approval is based upon the Landscape Plan L-1 through L-6 entitled
 39 "County Line Road Buffer Heron Bay" by Parker Yannette Design Group, Inc.,
 40 dated July 24, 2006 and stamp-dated and received by the Planning and Zoning
 41 Department on September 30, 2007.
 42 2. Root barriers shall be installed for all trees located within five (5) feet of a
 43 sidewalk or roadway.
 44 3. The City shall inspect the landscape buffer on a periodic basis to insure that it is
 45 being properly maintained and growing in at a normal rate. The City may require
 46 the applicant to add additional landscaping material to any area that is not
 47 growing in at a normal rate as would be expected based on the approved
