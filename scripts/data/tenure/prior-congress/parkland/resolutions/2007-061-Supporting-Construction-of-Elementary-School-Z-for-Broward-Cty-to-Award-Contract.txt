 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF

 5 SUPPORTING THE CONSTRUCTION OF
 6 ELEMENTARY SCHOOL Z AND REQUESTING THE

 12
 13 WHEREAS, the student population of the City of Parkland ("City")
 14 supports the construction of a third elementary school within the City; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City was able to convince two developers within the City
 17 to donate land to the Broward County School District ("School District") for
 18 construction of an elementary school ; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the Parkland Commons site (School Z) was the first site to be
 21 available for construction; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the School District budgeted monies for construction of
 24 School Z with an opening date of August 2008; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the Broward County School Board in October of 2006
 27 decided to delay the construction of School Z due to lower than expected
 28 enrollment projections; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the School District has now determined that an additional
 31 elementary school is necessary within the City but it is deciding whether to
 32 proceed with construction of School Z or the Parkland Village school (School A3);
 33 and
 34
 35 WHEREAS, the Parkland Educational Advisory Board recommended
 36 twice to the City Commission that the City should support construction of School
 37 Z; and
 38
 39 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 40 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 41
 42 SECTION 1. The City of Parkland supports the construction of elementary
 43 School Z with an opening date no later than January 2009.
