 4 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 5 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 6
 7 SECTION 1. The Site Plan and Community Appearance Board Application of WCI
 8 Communities, Inc. for the construction of an entry feature for the Somerset entrance located within
 9 Heron Bay is hereby APPROVED on the real property as described in Exhibit A, subject to the
 10 conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition to the general requirements
 11 otherwise provided by ordinance:
 12
 13 1. This approval is based upon the Plans H-1, H-2 and L-1 entitled "Somerset
 14 Entrance" by Parker Yannette Design Group, Inc., dated August 15, 2007 and
 15 revised on March 3, 2008 and March 6, 2008 and stamp-dated and received by the
 16 Planning and Zoning Department on April 23, 2008.
 17 2. Root barriers shall be installed for all trees located within five (5) feet of a sidewalk
 18 or roadway.
 19 3. The City shall inspect the landscape buffer on a periodic basis to insure that it is
 20 being properly maintained and growing in at a normal rate. The City may require
 21 the applicant to add additional landscaping material to any area that is not growing
 22 in at a normal rate as would be expected based on the approved landscaping plan.
 23 Failure to maintain the landscaping and entry area or respond to a City request as
 24 set forth herein shall constitute a violation of this approval and the City's Code of
 25 Ordinances.
 26 4. The party responsible for the landscape buffers shall employ a full-time grounds /
 27 building maintenance person to maintain the entry area as required herein.
 28 5. All conditions and approvals as outlined in Ordinances 2002-11, 2003-06, 2003-7
 29 and Resolution 2005-96 not specifically amended by this application shall govern
 30 the development.
 31 6. Any substitution of materials shall require City approval prior to the installation of
 32 such material. For recordation purposes, a waiver of site plan application shall be
 33 submitted as long as the material proposed to be substitute is determined to be
 34 comparable as that approved. If such material is determined to not be comparable,
 35 a site plan amendment application shall be submitted with all applicable fees.
 36 7. The entry features for Somerset shall receive a final certificate of occupancy no
 37 later than eighteen (18) months from issuance of a building permit. If the Project is
 38 not completed as evidenced by the Certificate of Completion or other statement of
 39 completion by the City within eighteen (18) months of the date of the issuance of a
 40 building permit, then in that event, the site plan and building permit referencing the
 41 project shall be null and void and Owner shall be required to apply for a new site
 42 plan approval and new building permits. Additionally, the City shall not allow
 43 inspections and plans review on such project to occur. Additionally, Owner shall be
 44 required to pay a fee equal to double the standard site plan review fee and double
 45 the building permit fee paid for the original site plan approval and the original
 46 building permit.
