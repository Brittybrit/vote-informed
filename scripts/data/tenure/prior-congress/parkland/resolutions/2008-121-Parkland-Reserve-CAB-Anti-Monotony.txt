 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, FOR ANTI-MONOTONY
 5 APPROVAL FOR PARKLAND RESERVE SINGLE FAMILY
 6 RESIDENTIAL DWELLING UNIT ELEVATIONS, CASE
 7 NUMBER 14-CAB-08, LOCATED WITHIN THE
 8 PARKLAND RESERVE COMMUNITY (FORMERLY
 9 KNOWN AS PARKLAND VILLAGE).
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 12 Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the
 13 Code of Ordinance is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and
 14 land development orders; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in the Code of
 17 Ordinances have been satisfied; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the parcel possesses a Future Land Use Designation of Residential 3
 20 with a zoning designation of Planned Residential Development (PRD); and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the Code of Ordinance requires City Commission approval of elevations
 23 to avoid similar homes built adjacent to each other; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the petitioner has provided five (5) single home models with three (3)
 26 elevations and eight (8) exterior color packages for the Parkland Reserve project; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony
 29 presented by the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of the
 30 various City of Parkland review agencies and staff; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Board on November 13, 2008 voted to
 33 recommend approval of the petition by a 5-0 vote; and
 34
 35 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 36 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 37
 38 SECTION 1. The Community Appearance Board petition of Parkland Reserve is
 39 hereby APPROVED, subject to the conditions of approval contained herein, which are in
 40 addition to the general requirements otherwise provided by ordinance:
 41
 42 1. This approval is based upon the Centerline Homes plans stamped dated
 43 November 3, 2008 by the Planning & Zoning Department.
 44 2. All conditions and approvals of the Parkland Village project not specifically
 45 amended by this application are still in effect and shall govern the
 46 development.
