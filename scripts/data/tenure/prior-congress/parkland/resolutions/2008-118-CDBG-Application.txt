 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AUTHORIZE THE
 5 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE
 6 ATTACHED COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT BLOCK GRANT
 7 APPLICATION
 8
 9 WHEREAS, in 2006 and 2007 the City Commission authorized staff to submit a
 10 Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) application to begin a mature adult program;
 11 and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City was successful in obtaining the grant in the amount of
 14 approximately $66,000 in 2006, $52,922 in 2007 and $49,408 in 2008; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the program has been extremely successful, where all the trips planned
 17 have been filled to capacity; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the CDBG is a yearly program that the City must reapply for; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS,based upon the success of the in house lecture/field trip format, staff is
 22 recommending submitting for the 2009-2010 CDBG format program; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, staff has conducted public research and received public feedback and
 25 input in conformance with CDBG requirements to obtain the views of citizens or community
 26 development and housing needs, and on the proposed activities; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has the legal jurisdiction and authority to create,
 29 operate and maintain the mature adult program; that the City of Parkland agrees to conform
 30 with the regulations, statutes, terms and conditions described in the CDBG Application, that
 31 the City of Parkland commits to providing the matching funds as proposed in the CDBG
 32 application; and
 33
 34 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 35 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 36
 37 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 38 being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached hereto are
 39 hereby incorporate herein.
 40
 41 SECTION 2. The appropriate City officials are hereby authorized and directed to
 42 execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this resolution.
 43
 44 SECTION 3. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are hereby
 45 repealed to the extent of such conflict.
