 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-82
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AUTHORIZE THE CITY MANAGER
 5 TO EXECUTE A CONTRACT WITH VERMONT SYSTEMS THE
 6 TOP RANKED FIRM FOR RECREATION SOFTWARE SYSTEM;
 7 PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
 8 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, City staff advertised RFP for the purpose of awarding a contract to
 11 provide Finance and Recreation Software; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, a selection committee consisting of the Finance and Administrative
 14 Services Director, Business and Strategic Planning Manager, Planning Director, Information
 15 Systems Manager, Leisure Services Director, and Environmental Resources Director, have
 16 reviewed the only response submitted for Recreation Software; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, based upon staff input the City Commission finds that Vermont Systems
 19 the sole responder, is capable of providing the needed software and wishes to enter into a
 20 contract with Vermont Systems for Recreation Software; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City Commission authorizes the City Manager to enter into a
 23 contract with Vermont Systems Inc. at the price not to exceed $30,226; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best interest of
 26 the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland for the
 27 City Manager to enter into such contract; and
 28
 29 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 30 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 31
 32 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as being
 33 true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein. All exhibits attached hereto are hereby
 34 incorporated herein.
 35
 36 SECTION 2. The City Manager is hereby authorized to enter into a contract for
 37 Recreation software system with Vermont Systems at a price not to exceed $30,226, subject
 38 to approval of form by the City Attorney.
 39
 40 SECTION 3. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are hereby
 41 repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 42
 43 Section 4. If any clause, section, other part or application of this Resolution is held by
 44 any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or invalid, in part of application, it shall
 45 not affect the validity of the remaining portions or applications of this Resolution.
