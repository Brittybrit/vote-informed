 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-13
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE
 CITY OFFICIALS TO ENTER INTO A LANDSCAPING AND
 MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND AND THE LANDINGS OF PARKLAND
 HOMEOWNERS ASSOCIATION, INC.; PROVIDING FOR
 CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND
 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the CITY desires to undertake a beautification program at the
 intersection of Holmberg Road and University Drive in the City of Parkland; and
 WHEREAS, to undertake the Project, the CITY requires an easement to install
 landscaping, decorative walls, and other hardscape features on property owned by the
 ASSOCIATION; and
 WHEREAS, ASSOCIATION is desirous of granting an easement to CITY for the
 purposes of the Project, provided the CITY agrees to maintain all features placed on the
 Property; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commissioners believes that entering into
 this agreement is in the best interest of the citizens of Parkland.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials are hereby authorized to enter
 into the agreement between the City of Parkland and the Landings of Parkland
 Homeowners Association, Inc.
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 17 DAY OF January , 2007.
