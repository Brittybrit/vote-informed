 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 11 Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the
 12 Code of Ordinance is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and
 13 land development orders; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the petitioner desires to obtain approval for a Master Sign Plan for the
 16 Parkland Commons South development; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in Code of Ordinances,
 19 have been satisfied; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony
 22 presented by the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of the
 23 various City of Parkland review agencies and staff; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City Commission has made the following findings of fact:
 26
 27 1. Environmental Considerations: No adverse impacts to the natural environment are
 28 expected since the property has already been
 29 disturbed.
 30
 31 2. Utilities: The proposed request will not affect existing water,
 32 sanitary, sewer and drainage systems.
 33
 34 3. Comprehensive Plan The proposed development is consistent with the
 35 Considerations: Land Use Element of the Comprehensive Plan.
 36
 37 4. Zoning Considerations: The property has obtained Master Plan Approval.
 38 Each outparcel is required to submit site plans for
 39 review to ensure the Code of Ordinance standards
 40 are met or exceeded.
 41
 42 5. Community Appearance The buildings shall fit within the character of the
 43 Considerations: area and the adjacent shopping centers, and
 44 includes pedestrian amenities. Each building shall
 45 be reviewed during the site plan process.
 46
 47 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 48 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:

 15 during the Site Plan and Community Appearance Board application process.

 23 a part hereof.
 24
