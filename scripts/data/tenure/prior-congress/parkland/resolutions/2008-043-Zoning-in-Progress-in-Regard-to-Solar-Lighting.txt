 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008—43
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA DECLARING A ZONING
 5 IN PROGRESS WITH REGARD TO REQUIREMENTS FOR
 6 SOLAR LIGHTING ON ALL ILLUMINATING SIGNS.
 7
 8 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland ("City") is dedicated to
 9 preserving the environment of the City of Parkland, Broward County and the State of
 10 Florida; and
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City wishes to undertake reasonable regulations to further the goal
 13 of preservation of the environment; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the City has previously adopted regulations with regards to solar
 16 lighting, requiring said lighting to be solar powered; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the City Commission will consider adopting regulations with regards to
 19 all illuminated signage to be solar powered; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the City Commissioner wishes to place persons on notice of its
 22 intentions in this regard;
 23
 24 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 25 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 26
 27 Section 1. The above Whereas clauses are true and correct and are incorporated
 28 herein.
 29
 30 Section 2. The City of Parkland City Commission does hereby declare a zoning
 31 in progress with regard to its sign regulations and lighting regulations for 180 days from
 32 adoption of this resolution. The purpose of the zoning in progress is for the City to consider
 33 regulations to require all illuminated signage to be one hundred percent (100%) solar
 34 powered. The City will be reviewing regulations during the next 180 days with the idea of
 35 adopting regulations in that regard by abiding by the 180 day period. All persons and
 36 property owners are on notice of the City's intention in this regard and no approval for
 37 illuminated signage during this period shall be granted which does require compliance with
 38 any regulations adopted during this period. The City Manager shall place a notice of this
 39 requirement at the Planning Department and Building Department.
 40
 41 Section 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its passage
 42 and adoption.
