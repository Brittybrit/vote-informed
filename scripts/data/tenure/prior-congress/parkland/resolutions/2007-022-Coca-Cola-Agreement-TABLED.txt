 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AUTHORIZE THE APPROPRIATE CITY
 5 OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE ATTACHED PROFESSIONAL
 6 SERVICE AGREEMENT TO PROVIDE FOR EXCLUSIVE
 7 BEVERAGE VENDING SERVICES FOR THE CITY OF PARKLAND;
 8 PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY;
 9 AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland desires to offer healthy alternatives to soft
 13 drinks
 14
 15 WHEREAS, this can be accomplished through a beverage vending services
 16 agreement;
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland investigated possible companies to offer this
 19 service;
 20
 21 WHEREAS, Florida Coca-Cola Bottling Company will enter into a five (5)
 22 year agreement to provide vending services in exchange for pricing reductions,
 23 commission revenue and scoreboards for the City Of Parkland in exchange for
 24 exclusivity; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes signing this agreement
 27 is in the best interest of the citizens of Parkland; and
 28
 29 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 30 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 31
 32 SECTION 1. The foregoing"WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified
 33 as being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached
 34 hereto are hereby incorporate herein.
 35
 36 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves the
 37 Agreement between the City and Florida Coca-Cola Bottling Company, a copy of which
 38 is attached hereto and incorporated by reference as if set forth in full.
 39
 40 SECTION 3. The appropriate City officials are hereby authorized and directed to
 41 execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this resolution.
 42
 43 SECTION 4. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are
 44 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
