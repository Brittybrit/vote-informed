 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO AUTHORIZE THE APPROPRIATE
 5 CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE YEAR 2006
 6 TRANSPORTATION ENHANCEMENT GRANT APPLICATION
 7 TO CONSTRUCTION A MULTI-USE PATH WITHIN THE
 8 FLORIDA POWER AND LIGHT EASEMENT FROM THE
 9 SAWGRASS EXPRESSWAY TO WILES ROAD WITHIN THE
 10 COUNTRY ACRES SUBDIVISION
 11
 12 WHEREAS, County administers a grant program entitled"Year 2006 Transportation
 13 Enhancement";
 14
 15 WHEREAS,there are grant funds allocated and available from this program to assist the
 16 City in enhancing transportation-related facilities and activities within the City;
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland, Florida desires to construct a multi-use path
 19 within the Florida Power and Light easement from the Sawgrass Expressway to Wiles
 20 Road within the Country Acres subdivision;
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City demonstrates it has sufficient staff resources, technical expertise,
 23 and/or experience with similar projects to carry out the proposed program; and
 24
 25 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 26 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 27
 28 Section 1. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves the Year
 29 2006 Transportation Enhancement Grant application for the construction of a multi-use
 30 path within the Florida Power and Light easement from the Sawgrass Expressway to
 31 Wiles Road within the Country Acres subdivision and authorizes the appropriate City
 32 officials to execute it.
 33
 34 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
