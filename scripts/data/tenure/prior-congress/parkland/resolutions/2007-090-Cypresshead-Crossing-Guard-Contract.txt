 AGREEMENT
 THIS AGREEMENT is entered into by and between the CITY OF PARKLAND
 (PARKLAND) and CYPRESS HEAD CLUB, INC. (CYPRESS HEAD), as follows:
 WITNESSETH:
 WHEREAS, PARKLAND has previously funded a school crossing guard within the
 CYPRESS HEAD COMMUNITY; and
 WHEREAS, because of budgetary constraints, PARKLAND will no longer be able to
 continue this practice; and
 WHEREAS, CYPRESS HEAD has requested that PARKLAND provide a school
 crossing guard within the development with CYPRESS HEAD paying the full cost; and
 WHEREAS, PARKLAND wishes to work with its citizens and is prepared to work with
 CYPRESS HEAD in this regard to accomplish the goal of providing a school crossing guard
 within CYPRESS HEAD at the expense of CYPRESS HEAD;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT AGREED BY AND BETWEEN THE PARTIES AS
 FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. The above Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a part hereof as
 if fully set forth herein.
 Section 2. PARKLAND shall retain and provide a school crossing guard to serve
 within CYPRESS HEAD during the public school term, to serve on the same basis and during
 the same hours as in prior years.
 Section 3. CYPRESS HEAD hereby agrees to reimburse PARKLAND for the cost of
 providing said crossing guard. The cost is estimated to be $6,250.00 per school year. The exact
 cost will be provided to CYPRESS HEAD at least four (4) weeks prior to Oct. 1st of each year.
 The fee shall be paid by check or cash at least two (2)weeks prior to Oct. 1st of each year.
 Section 4. CYPRESS HEAD does hereby agree to defend, indemnify and hold
 PARKLAND harmless from any and all claims, causes of action, judgments, penalties, costs
 (including attorney's fees) or other liability in any way resulting from the stationing of the
 crossing guard within CYPRESS HEAD.
 Section 5. PARKLAND may terminate this agreement at any time for convenience
 upon thirty(30) days notice to CYPRESS HEAD, provided that if the monies due under this
 agreement are not paid at least two (2) weeks prior to Oct. 1st of each year then PARKLAND
 may cancel this agreement with three (3) business days notice.

<<PG>>
 Section 6. CYPRESS HEAD may terminate this agreement for convenience at any
 time upon thirty(30) days notice to PARKLAND.
 Section 7. This is the entire agreement between the parties and this agreement may
