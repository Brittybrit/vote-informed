 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008 - 01
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, PROVIDING FOR A
 5 POSITION OF DEPUTY VICE MAYOR.
 6
 7 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Charter provides for a Mayor of the City of
 8 Parkland and for a Vice Mayor of the City of Parkland; and
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the Charter provides that the Vice Mayor is to be elected by members of the
 11 Commission; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, there is presently no provision for situations where both the Mayor and the
 14 Vice Mayor are absent; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, without creating a City Charter provision, the City Commission wishes to
 17 provide for situations where both the Mayor and the Vice Mayor are absent from Commission
 18 meetings to assure that there is a member of the Commission designated to run the meetings in
 19 their absence;
 20
 21 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 22 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 23
 24 Section 1. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby creates the position
 25 of Deputy Vice Mayor. The Deputy Vice Mayor shall not be considered a Charter position and
 26 this resolution shall not be deemed to amend or alter the Charter in any way. The Deputy Vice
 27 Mayor's sole responsibility shall be to chair a City Commission meeting should both the Mayor
 28 and Vice Mayor be absent.
 29
 30 ADOPTED THIS 16 DAY OF January ,2008.
