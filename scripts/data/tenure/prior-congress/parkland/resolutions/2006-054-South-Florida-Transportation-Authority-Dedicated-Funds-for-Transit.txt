 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 4 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA SUPPORTING
 5 A REFEREDUM TO BE PLACED ON THE
 6 NOVEMBER 2006 BALLOT TO CONSIDER THE
 7 METROPOLITIAN PLANNING ORGANIZATION'S
 8 REQUEST FOR A ONE-CENT PENNY SURTAX
 9 FOR TRANSPORTATION TO SUPPORT AN
 10 EXPANDED MASS TRANSIT SYSTEM IN
 11 BROWARD COUNTY; PROVIDING FOR
 12 CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY;
 13 AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 14
 15
 16 WHEREAS, South Florida, comprised of Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm
 17 Beach Counties, is the fifth largest urbanized area in the United States, and its
 18 population is expected to continue to increase over the next twenty (20) years;
 19 and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the Broward County's population is expected to increase by
 22 forty percent (40%) to approximately 2.4 million residents by the year 2030; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the Broward County Board of County Commissioners and the
 25 Broward County Metropolitan Planning Organization ("MPO") have identified the
 26 need for an efficient, reliable, and affordable regional public transit system, as
 27 one of the most critical issues facing the residents of Broward County; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the MPO has adopted a Year 2030 Long Range
 30 Transportation Plan that identifies the future transit system for the County;
 31
 32 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland supports the need for a mass transit
 33 system and roadway improvements and supports Broward County and the
 34 MPO's commitment to identify a one-cent penny surtax to be used to match
 35 federal funds and finance future transit
 36
 37 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 38 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 39
 40 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 41 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein.
 42
 43 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby supports
 44 a referendum to be placed on the November 2006 ballot to consider the
 45 Metropolitan Planning Organization's proposal of a one-cent penny tax for
 46 transportation for an expanded mass transit system within Broward County.
