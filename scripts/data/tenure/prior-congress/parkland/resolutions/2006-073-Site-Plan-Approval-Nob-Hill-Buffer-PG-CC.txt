 9 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 10 Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the

 14 WHEREAS, the parcel possesses a Future Land Use Designation of Residential 3

 18 City Commission; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, with this application, WCI Communities Inc., is requesting site plan
 21 approval for the Nob Hill Road buffer within the Parkland Golf and Country Club residential
 22 development; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in Code of Ordinances,
 25 have been satisfied; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony

 29 various City of Parkland review agencies and staff; and

 31 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 32 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 33
 34 SECTION 1. The Site Plan for the Nob Hill Road landscape buffer of the Parkland
 35 Golf and Country Club residential development is hereby APPROVED with the following
 36 conditions:
 37
 38 1. This approval is based upon the Landscape Plan P-1 through P-7 entitled "Nob
 39 Hill Buffer Parkland Golf and Country Club Landscape Buffer" dated June 23,
 40 2006 and stamp-dated and received by the Planning and Zoning Department on
 41 August 4, 2006.
 42 2. Root barriers shall be installed for all trees located within five (5) feet of a
 43 sidewalk or roadway.
 44 3. The City shall inspect the landscape buffer on a periodic basis to insure that it is
 45 being properly maintained and growing in at a normal rate. The City may require
 46 the applicant to add additional landscaping material to any area that is not
 47 growing in at a normal rate as would be expected based on the approved

 1 landscaping plan. Failure to maintain the landscaping and entry area or respond
 2 to a City request as set forth herein shall constitute a violation of this approval
 3 and the City's Code of Ordinances.
 4 4. The party responsible for the landscape buffers shall employ a full-time grounds
 5 / building maintenance person to maintain the entry area as required herein.
 6 5. If any damage occurs to pedestrian amenities on site, the petitioner or its
 7 assigns shall repair the damage within a reasonable period of time, as
 8 determined by the City. Documentation shall be provided to the City of Parkland
 9 as it relates to whom the assigns is referring to.
 10 6. All required landscape buffers adjacent to trafficways shall be identified on the
 11 replat and maintained by a Property Owners Association with control over said
 12 buffers. The buffers shall be approved by the City Commission after review by
 13 the Planning and Zoning Board. The buffers shall comply with all land
 14 development code requirements. Buffers shall be completed prior to the
 15 issuance of a certificate of occupancy for a principal building on the adjoining
 16 pod.
 17 7. Any substitution of materials shall require City approval prior to the installation of
 18 such material. For recordation purposes, a waiver of site plan application shall
 19 be submitted as long as the material proposed to be substitute is determined to
 20 be comparable as that approved. If such material is determined to not be
 21 comparable, a site plan amendment application shall be submitted with all
 22 applicable fees.
 23 8. The Nob Hill Road buffer will be constructed and maintained as the other buffers
 24 that are adjacent to the Parkland Golf and Country Club.
 25 9. As set forth in Ordinance 2002-04 and 2005-23, all conditions of the Parkland
 26 Golf & Country Club not specifically amended by this site plan shall govern the
 27 development.

 29 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
