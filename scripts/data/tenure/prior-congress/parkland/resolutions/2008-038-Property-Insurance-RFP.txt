 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-38
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AWARDING A CONTRACT TO
 PREFERRED GOVERNMENT INSURANCE TRUST FOR
 PROPERTY INSURANCE FOR THE CITY OF PARKLAND.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has competitively bid property insurance for the
 City; and
 WHEREAS, the City contracted with insurance specialist, Sid Weber, from Inter
 Risk Corporation, to assist in the process of bidding and analysis of the bids; and
 WHEREAS, after receipt of bids, Inter Risk Corporation and the City Finance
 Department find that the lowest responsible and responsive bidder is Preferred
 Government Insurance Trust (PGIT) submitted by Wells Fargo Insurance Company;
 and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that accepting this bid would be in the
 best interests of the citizens of the City of Parkland;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 SECTION 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and
 made a part hereof.
 SECTION 2. The City Commission hereby awards the contract for City of
 Parkland property insurance to PGIT as submitted by Wells Fargo Insurance Company.
 The coverage is to be provided for 16 '/2 months in the amount of $190,466.00. The
 City Manager is authorized to execute any necessary documents to effectuate this bid
 award and to secure the necessary coverage pursuant to the bid process.
 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 7 day of MAY , 2008.
