 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA APPROVING THE SITE PLAN
 5 AMENDMENT AND COMMUNITY APPEARANCE BOARD
 6 APPLICATIONS TO RELOCATE REAR LIGHTING FROM THE
 7 SEAWALL TO THE BACK OF BUILDINGS B AND C AT THE
 8 WATERWAYS SHOPPES LOCATED AT 7499 STATE ROAD 7 AS
 9 DESCRIBED MORE PARTICULARLY HEREIN AND PROVIDING
 10 AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland, Florida,
 13 pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the Code of
 14 Ordinance is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and land development
 15 orders; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the petitioner desires to relocate rear lighting from the rear seawall to the back
 18 of Buildings B and C at the Waterways Shoppes located at 7499 State Road 7; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in Code of Ordinances, have
 21 been satisfied; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, on February 9, 2006, the Planning and Zoning Board unanimously voted (7-0)
 24 to recommend approval of the petitioner's request;
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony presented by
 27 the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of the various City of Parkland
 28 review agencies and staff, and
 29
 30 WHEREAS,the City Commission has made the following findings of fact:
 31
 32 1. Environmental Considerations: No adverse impacts to the natural environment are
 33 expected since the property has already been disturbed.
 34
 35 2. Utilities: The proposed request will not affect existing water,
 36 sanitary, sewer and drainage systems.
 37
 38 3. Comprehensive Plan Considerations:The proposed development is consistent with the Land
 39 Use Element of the Comprehensive Plan.
 40
 41 4. Zoning Considerations: The proposed sign meets the requirements of Chapter 15
 42 of the Code of Ordinances.
 43
 44 5. Community Appearance Considerations: The lighting fits in the character of the area and is in
 45 good scale and proportion to the building.

 1 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 2 PARKLAND,FLORIDA that:
 3
 4 SECTION 1. The Site Plan Amendment and Community Appearance Board Application of
 5 Simon and Douglas for the relocation of rear lighting from the seawall to the rear of Buildings B and C
 6 of the Waterways Shoppes is hereby APPROVED on the real property as described in Exhibit A, subject
 7 to the conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition to the general requirements
 8 otherwise provided by ordinance:
 9
 10 1. This approval is based upon the Photometrics Plan entitled "Waterways Shoppes at Parkland"
 11 dated March 6, 2001 and revised May 25, 2004 and February 2, 2005, Electrical Plan E-1
 12 dated March 6, 2001 and revised March 28, 2003, July 3, 2003, December 22, 2003, May 25,
 13 2004, March 2, 2005 and May 19, 2005, Electrical Plan ES-1 dated March 6, 2001 and revised
 14 May 24, 2004 and February 2, 2005 and Elevations dated March 5, 2001 and revised May 1,
 15 2001, February 20, 2003, July 3, 2003, October 26, 2004, February 21, 2005 and April 19,
 16 2005 which were all received by the Planning and Zoning Department on March 10, 2006.
 17 2. All conditions of the Waterways Site Plan not amended by this site plan shall apply.
 18 3. A decorative architectural feature shall be placed upon each seawall column.
 19
 20 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
