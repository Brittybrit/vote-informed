 RESOLUTION NO. 2007—06
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, REQUESTING BROWARD COUNTY TO
 CONSTRUCT A SIDEWALK ON LOX ROAD IN THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND FROM STATE ROAD 7 TO PARKSIDE DRIVE.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland believes in encouraging pedestrian access to all parts of the City
 through the provision of sidewalks; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes that a sidewalk on Lox Road from
 State Road 7 to Parkside Drive is an important part of a City wide pedestrian network; and
 WHEREAS,the City of Parkland wishes for Broward County to construct a sidewalk on Lox Road
 from State Road 7 to Parkside Drive
 NOW,THEREFORE,BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND,AS FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. The above Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a part hereof.
 Section 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland does hereby formally request the Broward
 County Commission to authorize the necessary funds and actions to construct a sidewalk on Lox Road
 connecting State Road 7 and Parkside Drive.
 Section 3. The City Clerk is authorized to provide copies of this Resolution to all appropriate Broward
 County officials.
 Section 4. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 3 DAY OF January ,2007.
