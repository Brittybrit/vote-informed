 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-30
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING CITY
 OFFICIALS TO AWARD A CONTRACT FOR THE CITY
 HALL RE-ROOF (PROJECT #2006-02) TO WELLING
 CONSTRUCTION, INC., CONTAINING AN EFFECTIVE
 DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida, is authorized
 to award a contract for the City Hall Re-Roof Contract(Project#2006-02); and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland received two (2) bids from the following
 bidders: Welling Construction, Inc., and Pritts Incorporated; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has fully reviewed the
 bids submitted and has determined that the bid of Welling Construction, Inc., is the
 lowest responsible and responsive bid which best meets the criteria set forth in the bid
 packet and that, based upon the base bid price of $210,500 and other factors to be
 considered, it is in the best interest of the City to accept said bid.
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That Welling Construction, Inc., is hereby awarded a contract in
 the total amount of$210,500 for the City Hall Re-Roof(Project#2006-02).
 Section 2. That the funds shall be taken from the City Hall Re-Roof Project,
 line item numbers 305-701.502-506.030 and 305-701.502-506.031.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 22 DAY OF March , 2006.
