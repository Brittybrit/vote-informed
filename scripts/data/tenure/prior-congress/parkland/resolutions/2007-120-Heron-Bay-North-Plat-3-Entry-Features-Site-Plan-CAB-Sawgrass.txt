 1 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 2 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 3
 4 SECTION 1. The Site Plan and Community Appearance Board Application of WCI
 5 Communities, Inc. for the construction of an entry feature for the Sawgrass Bay entrance located
 6 within Heron Bay is hereby APPROVED on the real property as described in Exhibit A, subject to
 7 the conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition to the general requirements
 8 otherwise provided by ordinance:
 9
 10 1. This approval is based upon the Plans H-1, H-2 and L-1entitled "Sawgrass Bay
 11 Entry Feature" by Parker Yannette Design Group, Inc., dated April 3, 2007 and
 12 revised on July 16, 2007 and August 16, 2007 and stamp-dated and received by
 13 the Planning and Zoning Department on September 30, 2007.
 14 2. Root barriers shall be installed for all trees located within five (5) feet of a sidewalk
 15 or roadway.
 16 3. The City shall inspect the landscape buffer on a periodic basis to insure that it is
 17 being properly maintained and growing in at a normal rate. The City may require
 18 the applicant to add additional landscaping material to any area that is not growing
 19 in at a normal rate as would be expected based on the approved landscaping plan.
 20 Failure to maintain the landscaping and entry area or respond to a City request as
 21 set forth herein shall constitute a violation of this approval and the City's Code of
 22 Ordinances.
 23 4. The party responsible for the landscape buffers shall employ a full-time grounds /
 24 building maintenance person to maintain the entry area as required herein.
 25 5. All conditions and approvals as outlined in Ordinances 2002-11, 2003-06, 2003-7
 26 and Resolution 2005-96 not specifically amended by this application shall govern
 27 the development.
 28 6. Any substitution of materials shall require City approval prior to the installation of
 29 such material. For recordation purposes, a waiver of site plan application shall be
 30 submitted as long as the material proposed to be substitute is determined to be
 31 comparable as that approved. If such material is determined to not be comparable,
 32 a site plan amendment application shall be submitted with all applicable fees. The
 33 entry features for Sawgrass Bay shall receive a final certificate of occupancy no
 34 later than eighteen (18) months from issuance of a building permit. If the Project is
 35 not completed as evidenced by the Certificate of Completion or other statement of
 36 completion by the City within eighteen (18) months of the date of the issuance of a
 37 building permit, then in that event, the site plan and building permit referencing the
 38 project shall be null and void and Owner shall be required to apply for a new site
 39 plan approval and new building permits. Additionally, the City shall not allow
 40 inspections and plans review on such project to occur. Additionally, Owner shall be
 41 required to pay a fee equal to double the standard site plan review fee and double
 42 the building permit fee paid for the original site plan approval and the original
 43 building permit.
