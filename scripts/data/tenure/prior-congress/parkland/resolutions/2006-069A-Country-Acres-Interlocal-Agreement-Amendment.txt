 11 WHEREAS, the Country Acres residential subdivision is located in unincorporated
 12 Broward County; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, all Broward County unincorporated properties are required to be
 15 incorporated into a local jurisdiction by 2010; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the residents of each unincorporated subdivision must decide which
 18 jurisdiction they want to be annexed to; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the residents of Country Acres had a choice of being annexed into the
 21 City of Parkland or the City of Coral Springs; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, by vote, the residents of Country Acres elected to annex into the City of
 24 Parkland; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the annexation does not become effective until September 15, 2006;
 27 and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, there must be a process and understanding as to what services
 30 Broward County and the City of Parkland will provide through September 30, 2006; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, the City Commission adopted Resolution 2006-69 on July 5, 2006 which
 33 contained an Interlocal Agreement expressing the process and understanding as to what
 34 services Broward County and the City of Parkland will provide; and
 35
 36 WHEREAS, the executed Interlocal Agreement as attached in Resolution 2006-69
 37 was forwarded to Broward County for counter signature; and
 38
 39 WHEREAS, Broward County did not agree to the terms and conditions of Paragraph
 40 10 as outlined in the Interlocal Agreement since its attorneys determined that federal law
 41 prohibited the County from meeting the obligations of this paragraph; and
 42
 43 WHEREAS, Paragraph 10 of the Interlocal Agreement has been amended per
 44 Broward County's request;
