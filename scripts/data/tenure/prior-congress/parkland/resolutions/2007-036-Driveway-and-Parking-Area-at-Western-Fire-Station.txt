 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-36
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION
 4 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO
 6 PIGGYBACK A CITY OF NORTH MIAMI
 7 BEACH CONTRACT WITH ARROW ASPHALT
 8 AND ENGINEERING INC. FOR THE PURPOSE
 9 OF INSTALLING AN ASPHALT DRIVEWAY
 10 AND PARKING AREA TO ACCOMMODATE
 11 THE TEMPORARY WESTERN FIRE STATION
 12 IN THE AMOUNT OF $134,163.80,
 13 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 14
 15
 16 WHEREAS, monies were budgeted in the City of Parkland Fiscal Year
 17 2007 Budget for the purpose of constructing a temporary western fire station
 18 facility; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, it is necessary to install a driveway and parking area to
 21 accommodate the temporary facility; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, Staff has identified a piggybacking opportunity to install the
 24 driveway and parking area utilizing a City of North Miami Beach contract with
 25 Arrow Asphalt and Engineering, Inc; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes the installation
 28 of the driveway and parking area to accommodate the temporary western fire
 29 station is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 30
 31 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 32 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 33
 34 SECTION 1. The Interim City Manager is hereby authorized to
 35 piggyback the City of North Miami Beach contract with Arrow Asphalt and
 36 Engineering, Inc. for the purpose of installing a driveway and parking area to
 37 accommodate the temporary western fire station in the amount of $134,163.80.
 38
 39 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 40 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 41
 42 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 7 DAY OF March , 2007.
