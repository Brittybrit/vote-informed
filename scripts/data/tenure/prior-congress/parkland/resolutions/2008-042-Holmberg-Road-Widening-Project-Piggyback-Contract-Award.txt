 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO
 5 PIGGYBACK A BROWARD COUNTY CONTRACT #HZ-05-376-B1
 6 WITH WEEKLEY ASPHALT PAVING, INC., FOR THE PURPOSE OF
 7 ADDING TWO (2) 4'-WIDE BICYCLE LANES ALONG HOLMBERG
 8 ROAD BETWEEN STATE ROAD 7 AND UNIVERSITY DRIVE,
 9 RESURFACING HOLMBERG ROAD BETWEEN NW 62ND
 10 TERRACE AND RIVERSIDE DRIVE, AND INSTALLING SIXTEEN
 11 (16) FIRE HYDRANTS ALONG HOLMBERG ROAD TO SERVICE
 12 THE PINETREE ESTATES SUBDIVISION IN THE AMOUNT OF
 13 $2,718,086, PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 14
 15 WHEREAS, monies were budgeted in the City of Parkland Fiscal Year 2008
 16 Budget for the purpose of adding bicycle lanes, installing fire hydrants and resurfacing
 17 various portions of Holmberg Road between State Road 7 and University Drive; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the bicycle lanes on Holmberg Road from State Road 7 to University
 20 Drive will provide a safer path for bike riders, promote energy efficient transportation,
 21 and encourage fitness; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, it is necessary to resurface Holmberg Road from NW 62 Terrace to
 24 University Drive as a function of the City's on-going street maintenance program; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the addition of fire hydrants will greatly enhance fire response
 27 services within the Pinetree Estates subdivision; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, Staff has identified a piggybacking opportunity to construct the
 30 aforementioned improvements utilizing a Broward County Contract #HZ-05-376-B1 with
 31 Weekley Asphalt Paving, Inc; and
 32
 33 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes that piggybacking
 34 Broward Contract #HZ-05-376-B1for the purpose of adding bicycles lanes, resurfacing,
 35 and installing fire hydrants along Holmberg Road in the amount of $2,718,086 is in the
 36 best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 37
 38 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 39 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 40
 41 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 42 being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein.
 43
 44 SECTION 2. The City Manager is hereby authorized to piggyback the Broward
 45 County Contract with Weekley Asphalt Paving, Inc. for the purpose of adding bicycle
