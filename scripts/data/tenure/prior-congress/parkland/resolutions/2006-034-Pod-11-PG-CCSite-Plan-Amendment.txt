 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA FOR APPROVAL OF WCI COMMUNITIES,
 5 INC'S SITE PLAN AMENDMENT AND COMMUNITY
 6 APPEARANCE BOARD PETITIONS TO REMOVE THE POOL,
 7 CABANA, AND SPA, REDESIGN THE PLAZA FEATURE, AND
 8 MODIFY THE ENTRY AND FOUR PLEX BUILDING ELEVATIONS
 9 FOR WITHIN POD 11 OF THE PARKLAND GOLF & COUNTRY
 10 CLUB AS MORE PARTICULARLY DESCRIBED HEREIN AND
 11 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 14 Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the
 15 Code of Ordinance is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and
 16 land development orders; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the parcel possesses a Future Land Use Designation of Residential 3
 19 with a zoning designation of Planned Residential District ("PRD"); and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the Pod 11's Site Plan was adopted on January 19, 2005; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, WCI Communities, Inc is requesting to modify the previously approved
 24 Pod 11 Site Plan to delete the pool, cabana, spa, redesign the plaza feature and modify the
 25 entry and four plex building elevations to meet market demands; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in the Code of
 28 Ordinances have been satisfied; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony
 31 presented by the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of the
 32 various City of Parkland review agencies and staff; and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, the City Commission has made the following findings of fact:
 35
 36 1. Environmental Considerations: No adverse impacts to the natural environment are
 37 expected since the property has already been
 38 disturbed.
 39
 40 2. Utilities: The proposed water system will meet code
 41 requirements subject to final permitting.
 42
 43 3. Comprehensive Plan Considerations: The proposed project is consistent with the
 44 Land Use and Transportation Elements of the
 45 Comprehensive Plan.

 7 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 8 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 9
 10 SECTION 1. The Site Plan Amendment and Community Appearance Board petitions
 11 of WCI Communities Inc for modifications to the Site Plan for Pod 11 within the Parkland Golf
 12 and Country Club are hereby approved, subject to the conditions of approval contained
 13 herein, which are in addition to the general requirements otherwise provided by ordinance:
 14
 15 1. This approval is based upon the Common Area Plan View Sheets L1-L7 entitled
 16 "Pod 14 Parcel B-4, B-6, and/or B-5 Parkland Golf & Country Club" dated July 25,
 17 2005 and revised January 9, 2006 stamp-dated and received by the Planning and
 18 Zoning Department on January 13, 2006.
 19 2. This approval is based upon the Site and Landscape Plans Reissued entitled
 20 "POD 11" dated October 20, 2004 revised December 1 , 2004 and January 13,
 21 2006 and stamp-dated received by the Planning and Zoning Department on
 22 January 18, 2006.
 23 3. This approval is based upon the Architectural Plans entitled "4-PLEX at POD 11"
 24 dated January 12, 2006 and stamp-dated received by the Planning and Zoning
 25 Department on January 18, 2006
 26 4. Any conditions as set forth in Ordinance 2005-06 not amended by this
 27 Resolution shall remain in full force and affect Master Plan not specifically
 28 amended by this site plan shall govern the development.
 29
 30 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
