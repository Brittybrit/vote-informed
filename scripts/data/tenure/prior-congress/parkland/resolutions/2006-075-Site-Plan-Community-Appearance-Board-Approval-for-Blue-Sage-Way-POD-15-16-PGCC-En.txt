 2 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 3 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 4
 5 SECTION 1. The Site Plan Amendment and Community Appearance Board Application of
 6 WCI Communities, Inc. for the construction of an entry feature for the Blue Sage Way Rotary
 7 located within Parkland Golf and Country Club is hereby APPROVED on the real property as
 8 described in Exhibit A, subject to the conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition
 9 to the general requirements otherwise provided by ordinance:
 10
 11 1. This approval is based upon the plans entitled "Blue Sage Way Rotary
 12 Entrance Features" dated April 1, 2006 and received by the Planning and
 13 Zoning Department on August 4, 2006.
 14 2. As set forth in Ordinance 2002-04 and 2005-23, all conditions of the Parkland
 15 Golf & Country Club not specifically amended by this site plan shall govern
 16 the development.
 17 3. The City shall inspect all landscaping surrounding the entry features on a
 18 periodic basis to insure that they are being properly maintained and are
 19 growing in at a normal rate. The City may require the applicant to add
 20 additional landscaping material to any entry feature area that is not growing in
 21 at a normal rate as would be expected based on the approved landscaping
 22 plans. Failure to maintain the landscaping and entry features or respond to a
 23 City request as set forth herein shall constitute a violation of this approval and
 24 the City Code of Ordinances.
 25 4. Materials used on the walls will be constructed as stone walls and not stucco.
 26 5. The entry features for the Blue Sage Way Rotary shall receive a final
 27 certificate of occupancy no later than eighteen (18) months from issuance of a
 28 building permit If the Project is not completed as evidenced by the Certificate
 29 of Completion or other statement of completion by the City within eighteen
 30 (18) months of the date of the issuance of a building permit, then in that
 31 event, the site plan and building permit referencing the project shall be null
 32 and void and Owner shall be required to apply for a new site plan approval
 33 and new building permits. Additionally, the City shall not allow inspections and
 34 plans review on such project to occur. Additionally, Owner shall be required
 35 to pay a fee equal to double the standard site plan review fee and double the
 36 building permit fee paid for the original site plan approval and the original
 37 building permit.
 38 6. The applicant is required to enter into an agreement with the City of Parkland,
 39 as prepared by the City Attorney, that addresses all appropriate penalties if
 40 the eighteen (18) month deadline is not met.
