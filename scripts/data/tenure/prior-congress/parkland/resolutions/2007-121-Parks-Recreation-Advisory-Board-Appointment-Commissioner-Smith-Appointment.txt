 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-121
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPOINTING ONE (1) REGULAR
 MEMBER TO THE PARKS & RECREATION ADVISORY
 BOARD.
 WHEREAS, in accordance with the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances, the
 City Commission shall appoint members to the Parks &Recreation Advisory Board; and
 WHEREAS, a vacancy was created when Board Member Jerry Neptune
 submitted his resignation as Commissioner Smith's appointment on the Parks &
 Recreation Advisory Board on November 7, 2007; and
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That Commissioner Smith hereby makes his individual
 appointment to the Parks &Recreation Advisory Board as follows:
 Commissioner Smith shall appoint Mr. Phil Thornburg
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 5 DAY OF December , 2007.
