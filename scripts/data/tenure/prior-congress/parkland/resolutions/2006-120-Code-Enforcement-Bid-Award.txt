 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-120
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AUTHORIZE THE
 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO NEGOTIATE A
 CONTRACT OR CONTRACT AMENDMENT FOR CODE
 ENFORCEMENT SERVICES; PROVIDING FOR
 CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND
 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, Chapter 4.5 entitled "Code Enforcement" of the Code of Ordinances
 provides the City the authority to perform code enforcement responsibilities; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission determined it may be in the City's best
 interest to contract these services to a private company; and
 WHEREAS, the City Staff advertised RFP# 2006-12 entitled "City of Parkland
 Request for Proposals for Code Enforcement Services" for the purpose of obtaining
 bidders to perform code enforcement services; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland received two (2) proposals from the following
 companies: Broward County Sheriff's Office and Code Services Inc.; and
 WHEREAS, Staff has reviewed the two proposals and provided the City
 Commission its recommendation; and
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein.
 Section 2. The City Commission hereby awards the bid for code enforcement
 services to Broward Sheriff and authorizes and directs the appropriate City Officials
 to execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this
 Resolution.
 Section 3. That the Funds shall be taken from the Code Enforcement Budget.
 Section 4. This resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately
 upon passage and adoption
