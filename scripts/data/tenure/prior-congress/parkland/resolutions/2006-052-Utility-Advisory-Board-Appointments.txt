 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-52
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPOINTING THREE (3)
 REGULAR MEMBERS TO THE UTILITY ADVISORY BOARD.
 WHEREAS, in accordance with the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances, the
 City Commission shall appoint members to the Utility Advisory Board.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That Mayor Udine, Commissioner Moskowitz and Commissioner
 Smith hereby make their individual appointments to the Utility Advisory Board as
 follows:
 Mayor Udine Harvey Rudich
 Commissioner Moskowitz Thomas McDonald
 Commissioner Smith Eric Young
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 3 DAY OF MAY , 2006.
