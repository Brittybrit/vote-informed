 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-25
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, WAIVING THE REQUIREMENTS
 OF BIDS AND OBTAINING A SOLE SOURCE PROCUREMENT
 FOR THE PURCHASE OF ATHLETIC FIELD LIGHT
 REPLACEMENT PARTS AT TERRAMAR PARK.
 WHEREAS, Hurricane Wilma caused significant damage to the athletic
 field lights at Terramar Park; and
 WHEREAS, the Parks & Recreation Department has evaluated the damage to the
 athletic field lights and is requesting approval to purchase replacement parts for the
 athletic field lights at Terramar Park; and
 WHEREAS, Musco Lighting is the sole source to purchase these replacement
 parts; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commissioners believe approving the
 funds for this purchase is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials do hereby approve the funds for
 athletic field light replacement parts for Terramar Park.
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 22 DAY OF March ,2006.
