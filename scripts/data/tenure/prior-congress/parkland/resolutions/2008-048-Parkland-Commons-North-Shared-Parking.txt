 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of
 12 Parkland, Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166,
 13 Florida Statutes, and the Code of Ordinances, as adopted by the City of Parkland,
 14 is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and land
 15 development orders; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in the Code
 18 of Ordinances, as adopted by the City of Parkland, have been satisfied; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, a Future Land Use Designation of Commercial with a
 21 compatible Planned Commercial Development District Zoning Designation was
 22 assigned to the subject property; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, a Site Plan permitting a 46,031 square foot Publix; five (5)
 25 retail buildings totaling 39,897.30 square feet; one (1) 4,339 square foot restaurant
 26 building; and a 2.07 acre lake area was approved by the City Commission on April
 27 25, 2005 through Resolution 2005-19; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the Petitioner is requesting a Shared Parking Study to allow for
 30 the mixture of tenants that will be open during different times; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, on May 8, 2008, the Planning and Zoning Board reviewed said
 33 petition and voted 7-0 to recommend approval of the modifications sought by the
 34 applicant, subject to specified conditions; and
 35
 36 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and
 37 testimony presented by the Petitioner and other interested parties and the
 38 recommendations of the various City of Parkland review agencies and staff.
 39
 40 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 41 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 42
 43 SECTION 1. The Shared Parking Study for Parkland Commons North, which legal
 44 description is described in Exhibit A, is hereby APPROVED with the following
 45 conditions:

 1 1. This approval is based upon the plans entitled "Publix at Parkland
 2 Commons" signed and sealed on April 30, 2008 and received by the
 3 Planning and Zoning Department on April 30, 2008 and the Shared
 4 Parking Study by Walter H. Keller, Inc. dated February 7, 2008.
 5 2. All conditions and approvals for the Parkland Commons project not
 6 specifically amended by this application shall govern the development.
 7 3. The property owner shall be responsible for providing updated parking
 8 calculations with each proposed tenant during the building permit
 9 process to ensure adequate parking capacity is available pursuant to
 10 the Shared Parking Study.
 11 4. If the uses identified in the shared parking study substantially change as
 12 determined by the City, the City shall require the shopping center owner /
 13 developer to provide an updated shared parking study to be reviewed by
 14 staff, the City's traffic consultant, at the applicant's expense, and
 15 approved by the City Commission.
 16 5. The uses are restricted to those identified on the site plan associated with
 17 Resolution Number 2008-47.
 18 6. No fast food restaurants shall be permitted. A fast food restaurant shall
 19 be deemed any restaurant which is part of a chain of restaurants which
 20 generally have "drive through" service even though the particular
 21 restaurant in Parkland Commons does not have such service.
 22
 23 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 24 adoption.
