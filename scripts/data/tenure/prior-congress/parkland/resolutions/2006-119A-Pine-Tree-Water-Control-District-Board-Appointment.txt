 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-119A
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE
 CITY OFFICIALS TO MAKE AN APPOINTMENT TO THE
 PINE TREE WATER CONTROL DISTRICT BOARD.
 WHEREAS, the former Vice Mayor Ribotsky has previously served as a City
 representative on the Pine Tree Water Control District Board.; and
 WHEREAS, Commissioner Weissman will replace former Vice Mayor Ribotsky;
 and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission unanimously approves the appointment of
 Commissioner Mark Weissman to represent the Commission on the Pine Tree Water
 Control District Board.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials do hereby approve the
 appointment of Commissioner Mark Weissman to the Pine Tree Water Control District
 Board.
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 6 DAY OF December , 2006.
