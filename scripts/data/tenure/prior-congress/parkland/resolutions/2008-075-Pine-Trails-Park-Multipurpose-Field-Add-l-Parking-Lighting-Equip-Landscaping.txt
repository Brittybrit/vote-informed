 AGREEMENT
 THIS AGREEMENT is entered into by and between the CITY OF PARKLAND
 (CITY)and WEST CONSTRUCTION, INC. (CONTRACTOR), as follows:
 WITNESSETH:
 WHEREAS, by virtue of Resolution No. 08-42, the CITY approved a contract in the
 maximum amount of$1,305,923.00 for site work, lighting, paving and related improvements to
 Pine Trails Park-Phase III; and
 WHEREAS,this was approved as a piggyback bid for Palm Beach County Contract No.
 R2007-0948, based upon Bid No. R2007-0948, submitted by CONTRACTOR to Palm Beach
 County; and
 WHEREAS, by virtue of the CITY piggybacking on the Palm Beach County Contract
 No. R2007-0948 (which includes the unit prices for the work as set forth therein attached as
 Exhibit A and hereinafter referred to as the County Contract) the CITY and CONTRACTOR
 agree that the CITY may avail itself of all of the rights and benefits of the Palm Beach County
 under the County Contract; and
 WHEREAS, it is the intention of the parties that by virtue of this award through the
 piggyback process, that the CITY shall be entitled to all the rights and benefits of the County
 under the Palm Beach County Contract by virtue of this agreement with CONTRACTOR; and
 WHEREAS, CONTRACTOR wishes to obligate itself to CITY in the same manner as it
 obligated itself to the County and to grant CITY all the rights, privileges and benefits of the
 County Contract including, but not limited to, price provisions and other covenants running in
 favor of the County; and
 WHEREAS, the purpose of this agreement is to provide for these rights, benefits and
 obligations running to the CITY as set forth in the Palm Beach County Contract;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT AGREED BY AND BETWEEN THE PARTIES AS
 FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. The above Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a part hereof as
 if fully set forth herein.
 Section 2. The Palm Beach County Contract (Exhibit "A") is attached hereto and
 made a part hereof and referred to as the Palm Beach County Contract.
 Section 3. All references to the County shall be deemed to apply to the CITY, thus
 granting the CITY all rights, benefits and privileges of Palm Beach County with regard to the
 implementation of this contract between the CITY and CONTRACTOR. Contractor shall be
 obligated to CITY in the same manner and to the same extent as it is obligated under the County
 Page 1 of 5

<<PG>>
 Contract. This document and the attachments are to be interpreted in a manner so as to be
 consistent. Where one document offers greater rights, benefits, or protections to CITY, that
 document shall prevail.
 Section 4. The CONTRACTOR hereby affirms all the price quotations and other
 obligations and covenants running to the County as being valid and applying to the CITY. All
 representations made to Palm Beach County shall apply to the CITY and be binding elements of
 this contract and the parties hereby incorporate the Palm Beach County Contract herein; all
 references in the Palm Beach County Contract to CITY shall be deemed to apply to CITY.
 Section 5. It is understood and agreed that all unit prices as set forth in the Palm
 Beach County Contract shall apply to CITY. Further, it is agreed by the parties that, while the
 maximum amount of this Contract is $1,305,923.00, the CITY may, by subsequent work order
 reduction, authorize work in a lesser amount based upon its determination of the needs of the
 particular situation. For the purposes of this Contract, the plans and specifications for the Pine
 Trails Park - Phase III improvement project dated June 2008 (and subsequent revisions), as
 contained in the file in the office of the City Clerk of the CITY and specifically referenced as the
 Pine Trails Park - Phase III (the Project). The unit prices set forth in the Palm Beach County
 Contract shall apply to the work to be performed on the Project provided that the maximum price
 to complete the Project is agreed to be$1,305,923.00.
 Section 6. The CITY reserves the right to reduce the scope of the Project and to limit
 work to less than the full amount of the Project by written work order reduction.
 CONTRACTOR however shall provide labor, material and services for the purpose of
 accomplishing the entire Project pursuant to the unit prices as set forth in the Palm Beach County
 Contract,unless specifically directed to undertake a lesser scope of services by the CITY through
 a written work order reduction executed by the City Manager directed to CONTRACTOR. The
 parties agree that based upon the unit prices set forth in the Palm Beach County Contract, when
 applied to the plans and specifications for the project (as referred to above) shall provide for a
 maximum contract price of$1,305,923.00 (maximum price)to fully complete the Project. In the
 event the unit prices exceed the cost to complete the Project the maximum price shall control,
 should the unit prices be less than the maximum price,then the unit prices shall control.
 Section 7. No change in the scope of services beyond the maximum amount as set
 forth herein shall be approved, except by a written change order executed by the City Manager of
 the CITY and by the CONTRACTOR.
 Section 8. The CITY shall have the right to terminate this Contract for convenience
 as set forth in the Palm Beach County Contract.
 Section 9. All work shall be undertaken by the CONTRACTOR in a professional
 workman like fashion and an expeditious manner.
 Section 10. The time for completion of the Project from date of the notice to proceed
 shall be 120 days. No extensions of time shall be granted for the completion of the Project, as
 set forth in the plans and specifications referred to herein, unless executed in writing by the

 parties and based upon circumstances beyond the control of the CONTRACTOR. No claim for
 damages or any claim other than for an extension of time shall be made or asserted against the
 CITY by reason of any delays. The CONTRACTOR shall not be entitled to an increase in the
 contract sum or payment or compensation of any kind from the CITY for direct, indirect,
 consequential, impact or other costs, expenses or damages, including but not limited to costs for
 acceleration or inefficiency arising because of delay, disruption, interference or hindrance from
 any cause whatsoever, whether such delay, disruption, interference or hindrance was avoidable
 or unavoidable; provided however, that this provision shall not preclude recovery of damages by
 the CONTRACTOR for hindrances or delays due solely to fraud, bad faith or active interference
 on the part of the CITY or its agents. Otherwise, the CONTRACTOR shall be entitled only to
 extensions of the contract time as the sole and exclusive remedy for such resulting delay in
 accordance with and to the extent provided for herein.
 Section 11. CONTRACTOR specifically acknowledges and states that he has
 reviewed the plans and specifications, that he has inspected the site of the work, and is fully
 aware of the scope and nature of the Project and made sure of the conditions at the Project site,
 and that the sum as set for herein is sufficient to enable it to complete the Project in the time
 period set forth herein based upon the unit prices set forth in the Palm Beach County Contract
 and that no other funds shall be due and owing by the CITY for completion of the Project in
 accordance therewith.
 Section 12. The CONTRACTOR also agrees that it will warrant the work and make
 all necessary repairs for any defects in the work for a period of two years from the date of the
 final completion as certified by the CITY.
 Section 13. The CONTRACTOR shall be required to post a Performance Bond
 • equivalent to 120% of the estimated costs of construction. The Performance Bond shall be
 irrevocable and shall indemnify the City of Parkland against costs of restoring or maintaining the
 rights-of-way or easements due to, or arising from, failure of the CONTRACTOR to complete
 the work properly, to pay fully for labor, material and/or equipment supplied for the project, or
 for a failure in the rights-of-way or easements related to the permitted installation for a period of
 two (2) years after the permitted work is completed and approved by the Public Works
 Department,Engineering Division. All bonds must first be approved by the City Attorney.
 Section 14. The CONTRACTOR shall be required to post a Maintenance Bond. The
 maintenance bond amount shall be for a period of two (2) years at a bond amount of twenty-five
 (25%) percent of the cost of all related construction. A maintenance bond covering work
 performed in public rights-of-way and easements shall commence upon final release of the
 Performance Bond. A payment and Performance Bond for the entire cost of the work, in a form
 acceptable to the City Attorney, shall be provided by Contractor prior to commencement of
 work.
 Section 15. The CONTRACTOR shall indemnify and hold the CITY harmless from
 any causes of action, claims, penalties or any other liabilities in any way related to the execution
 of this Contract or any work undertaken pursuant to this Contract by CONTRACTOR, its
 subcontractors, employees, agents and representatives. This shall be a full and complete
