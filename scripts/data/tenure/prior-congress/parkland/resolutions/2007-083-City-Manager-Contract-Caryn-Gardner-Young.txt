 EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT
 THIS AGREEMENT made and entered into this ,E,z day of July 2007, by and between
 the City of Parkland, State of Florida, a municipal corporation, hereinafter called "Employer" or
 "City" as party of the first part, and Caryn Gardner-Young hereinafter called "Employee," as
 party of the second part, both of who understand as follows:
 WITNESSETH:
 WHEREAS, Employer desires to employ the services of said Caryn Gardner-Young as
 City Manager of the City of Parkland, as provided by the City Charter; and
 WHEREAS, it is the desire of the Governing Board, hereinafter called "Commission," to
 provide certain benefits, establish certain conditions of employment and to set working
 conditions of said Employee; and
 WHEREAS, it is the desire of the Commission to (1) secure and retain the services of
 Employee and to provide inducement for her to remain in such employment, (2) to make possible
 full work productivity by assuring Employee's morale and peace of mind with respect to future
 security, (3) to act as a deterrent against malfeasance or dishonesty for personal gain on the part
 of Employee, and (4) to provide a just means for terminating Employee's services at such time as
 he may be unable fully to discharge his duties due to age or disability or when Employer may
 otherwise desire to terminate his employ; and
 WHEREAS, Employee desires to accept employment as City Manager of City of
 Parkland;
 NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein contained, the
 parties agree as follows:
 Section 1. Term of Employment
 A. This agreement shall be effective upon execution and shall continue until terminated
 by either party as set forth herein.
 B. Nothing in this agreement shall prevent, limit or otherwise interfere with the right of
 the Employee to resign at any time from his position with Employer, subject only to the
 provisions set forth in Section 3 of this agreement.
 C. Nothing in this agreement shall prevent, limit or otherwise interfere with the right of
 the Commission to terminate the services of Employee at any time, subject only to the provisions
 set forth in Section 4 of this agreement.

 Section 2. Salary, Performance Evaluation and Miscellaneous Provisions
 A. Salary. Employer agrees to pay Employee for his services rendered pursuant hereto
 an annual base salary of one-hundred fifty thousand dollars ($150,000) payable in installments at
 the same time as other employees are paid. Employee shall be entitled to an annual cost-of-
 living wage increase each year in the same amount and at the same time as such increases are
 provided to other City employees. Employee's initial annual salary shall be adjusted by such
 increases beginning with the next annual COLA following the effective date of the Agreement.
 B. Performance Evaluations. The City Commission shall in good faith perform an
 evaluation of Employee on or before October 1 of each year or at any earlier time period if the
 parties agree. Based on the review and evaluation of the Employee's performance, the
 Commission will consider a merit increase in salary, bonus and/or benefit enhancement as they
 deem appropriate in their sole discretion.
 C. Additional Employment. Employee agrees to neither accept other employment nor to
 become employed by any other employer until services of the Employee are terminated per
 Section 3 or 4 of this agreement. The term "employed" shall not be construed to include
 teaching, writing, or consulting services performed on a part-time basis during the Employee's
 time off and with the knowledge of the City Commission.
 D. Compliance. In effecting the provisions of this Section, the Commission and
 Employee mutually agree to abide by the provisions of applicable law.
 Section 3. Voluntary Resignation by Employee
 In the event Employee voluntarily resigns his position with Employer, then Employee
 shall give Employer thirty (30) days written notice in advance, unless the parties otherwise agree.
 There shall be no severance (separation) pay to Employee in the case of a voluntary resignation.
 Lump sum payments for accumulated leave time shall not be considered severance pay
 Section 4. Involuntar_y Termination and Severance Pay
 A. The Employee shall serve at the pleasure of the City Commission and may be
 terminated at any time, without cause, subject only to the serveance provisions set forth below.
 B. In the event Employee is terminated by the City Commission during such time that
 Employee is willing and able to perform the duties of City Manager, then in that event the
 Employer agrees to pay Employee's then current compensation package for a six (6) month
 period, which shall be deemed to be a consulting fee. "Compensation package" shall be
 inclusive of salary, retirement and all benefits, such as, but not limited to, health insurance. The
 payment of the current compensation package shall continue for a period of six (6) months if
 Employee is terminated by City under any circumstances (except as set forth below) or at any
 time during her employment with the City.
 C. In the event Employee is terminated because of his conviction of any felony or any
 Page 2 of 9

<<PG>>
 crime involving moral turpitude, then, in that event, Employer shall have no obligation to pay
 any severance pay described in paragraph A.
 D. Compliance. Termination shall be subject to the provisions and proceedings of the
 City Charter and Code of Ordinances as well as the Statutes of the State of Florida.
 Section 5. Benefits
 A. Retirement: Employer agrees to provide Employee an International City
 Management Association Retirement Corporation (ICMA-RC) retirement plan which provides
 for a fifteen percent (15%) contribution of Employee's base salary to a 401(a) defined
 contribution plan. Contributions by the Employer shall be tax deferred and shall not require any
 match by the Employee. Contributions by the Employer shall be adjusted with changes in salary
 and shall be paid in equal proportionate amounts each pay period. Contributions shall be made
 directly to the account established on behalf of the Employee at the same time as the Employee
 is paid and will be considered vested immediately upon receipt into the account. Retirement plan
 payments will be effective with the first day of employment, which will include all retirement
 plan payments made by Employee since February 2005 and the Employer shall expeditiously
 execute any documents and take any legal or administrative action required by Charter and the
 ICMA-RC for implementation of the plan. The City shall also make contributions on behalf of
 the Employee to the retirement plan in effect for all City employees at the same levels applicable
 to general administrative employees and agrees to transfer ownership of same to Caryn Gardner-
 Young upon resignation or discharge.
 B. Health Insurance: City shall provide Employee and her husband health insurance
 coverage (to include a dental and vision plan) as is available to general employees at no cost to
 Employee. Where several options are available to general employees, Employee may choose
 any option from those offered to all City employees in her sole discretion.
 C. Disability: The Employer agrees to put into force and to make required premium
 payments for short term and long term disability coverage for the Employee equal to that for the
 highest ranking non-uniform employee. If Employee is permanently disabled or is otherwise
 unable to perform his duties because of sickness, accident, injury, mental incapacity or health for
 a period of four (4) successive weeks beyond any accrued sick and vacation leave, or for twenty
 (20) working days over a thirty (30) working day period, Employer shall have the option to
 terminate his employment subject to the termination and consulting fee requirements of Section
 4. Additionally, Employee shall be compensated for any accrued sick leave, vacation, holidays,
 and other accrued benefits as set forth above.
 D. Automobile Allowance: In recognition of the 24-hour, on call status of the Employee
 and the requirement that Employee shall use his own personal vehicle for travel performed as
 part of City business, Employer will reimburse Employee at flat rate of five hundred seventy five
 dollars ($575) per month. Alternatively, Employee may request the sole and exclusive,
 unrestricted use of an automobile for business and personal use within the State of Florida at no
 cost to Employee. The lease terms of the chosen vehicle shall be no more than $575 per month.
 Vehicle shall be a full-size, 4-door sedan of the Employee's choice. Employer shall be
 Page 3 of 9

<<PG>>
 responsible for paying for liability, property damage, and comprehensive insurance and for the
 purchase or lease, operation/gasoline, maintenance, repair, and replacement of said automobile.
 E. Vacation and Sick Leave; Holidays; Personal Days: Employee shall accrue vacation
 leave at a rate of twenty (20) days per year (1.67 days per month). Employee shall accrue sick
 leave at the same rate as other employees based on length of service with the City and in
 accordance with City Ordinances and Personnel Rules and Regulations governing
 unrepresented/exempt management employees. At a minimum, sick leave shall accrue at a rate
 of at least one day per calendar month. Upon resignation or termination of Employee as
 described and subject to the terms of Section 3 or 4 of this agreement, Employee shall be paid a
 lump sum payment of all accumulated vacation and sick leave. There shall be no limit to the
 amount of Vacation and Sick Leave hours that may be accumulated by the Employee. Employee
 shall be provided the same holidays and same quantity of personal days, if any, as are granted to
 other employees of the City.
 F. Life Insurance: As an inducement to Employee to become City Manager, the
 Employer shall pay the amount of premium due for term life insurance in the amount of two (2)
 times the Employee's annual base salary, including all increases in the base salary during the life
 of this agreement. The Employee shall name the beneficiary of the life insurance policy.
 Section 6. Hours of Work
 It is recognized by both Employer and Employee that the duties of City Manager require
 a great deal of time outside of normal office hours. It is also recognized by the parties that
 Employee is required to devote the amount of time and energy necessary to carry out those duties
 with the highest amount of professionalism possible. This position is exempt from overtime as
 outlined in the Fair Labor Standards Act. That being the case, the parties recognize that
 Employee may choose to take personal time off or"Administrative Leave" during business hours
 when it is appropriate and when her duties allow. Although this personal time off is not
 considered vacation, neither is it to be considered as compensatory time for time spent by
 Employee in carrying out her duties outside of normal office hours, as the parties agree that
 Employee must devote the amount of time necessary to fulfill those duties. Employee shall not
 be entitled to payment of personal time off (Administrative Leave) upon resignation or
 termination.
 Section 7. Dues and Subscriptions
 Employer agrees to budget an appropriate amount annually to pay for the professional
 dues and subscriptions of Employee necessary for her continuation and full participation in
 national, regional, state and local associations necessary for her continued professional
 participation, growth and advancement, and for the good of the Employer. This shall include but
 not be limited to dues for the American Planning Association ("APA"), Broward County City
 Manager's Association ("BCCMA"), Florida City and County Manager's Association
 ("FCCMA") and the International City/County Management Association ("ICMA"). Local civic
 club membership shall be limited to one paid by Employer.

 Section 8. Training and Professional Development
 Employer hereby agrees to budget appropriate amounts annually to pay the reasonable
 travel and subsistence expenses of Employee for professional and official travel, meetings and
 occasions necessary to continue the professional development of Employee. This shall include
 but not be limited to Employee maintaining her American Planning Association AICP status and
 for Employee to obtain ICMA "Credentialed Manager" status and to adequately pursue
 necessary official and other functions for Employer, including but not limited to the Annual
 Conference of the International City/County Management Association, the Florida City/City
 Management Association annual conference, the Florida or national American Planning
 Association annual conference, the Florida League of Cities annual conference or any other
 seminars, workshops, institutes, or short courses offered by national, regional, state and local
 governmental groups and committees providing professional development that will benefit both
 the Employer and Employee.
 Section 9. General Expenses
 Parties recognize that the nature of Employee's duties on behalf of City necessitate
 Employee's frequent and continuing contacts with employees, members of the public,
 civic/homeowners'/professional associations, governmental officials, tradesman and
 professionals wishing to solicit City business, and the like. City encourages Employee, in her
 discretion, to encourage said contacts in such manner as may be in the best interest of the public.
 Recognizing that, many times, Employee may be in circumstances whereby it would be
 beneficial to meet or entertain said persons, in limited manner, in social settings at receptions,
 luncheon and dinner meetings. Employer hereby agrees to reimburse or to pay said reasonable
 general expenses where related to the performance of her job and subject to the limitations of
 state law or any municipal ordinance. The Finance Director is hereby authorized to disburse such
 monies_upon receipt of duly executed expense or petty cash vouchers, receipts, statements or
 personal affidavits.
 Section 10. Indemnification
 Employer shall defend, save harmless and indemnify Employee against any tort,
 professional liability claim or demand or other legal action, whether groundless or otherwise,
 arising out of an alleged act or omission occurring in the performance of Employee's duties as
 City Manager. Legal representation, provided by Employer for Employee, shall extend until a
 final determination of the legal action, including any appeals brought by either party. The
 Employer shall indemnify Employee against any and all losses, damages, judgments, interest,
 settlements, fines, court costs and other reasonable costs and expenses of legal proceedings,
 including attorney's fees and any other liabilities incurred by, imposed upon, or suffered by such
 Employee in connection with or resulting from any claim, action, suit, or proceeding, actual or
 threatened, arising out of matters in connection with and within the scope of her job as City
 Manager. Any settlement of any claim must be made with prior approval of the Employer in
 order for indemnification, as provided in this section, to be available and shall relate only to
 matters in connection with and within the scope of her job as City Manager.

 Employee recognizes that Employer shall have the right to compromise any claim or suit;
 unless said compromise or settlement is of a personal nature to Employee. Further, Employer
 agrees to pay all reasonable litigation expenses of Employee throughout the pendency of any
 litigation to which the Employee is a party or witness, where the action relates to matters in
 connection with and within the scope of her job as City Manager. Such expense payments shall
 continue beyond Employee's service to the Employer as long as litigation is pending. If a
 judgment is issued against Employee in her personal capacity for actions outside the scope of her
 employment as City Manager or for actions which are malicious or in bad faith, Employee shall
 reimburse the Employer for all legal fees and costs associated with the defense and shall be
 responsible for said judgment. Notwithstanding any obligations set forth above, the Employer
 shall have no obligation to Employee relating to matters not in connection with or within the
 scope of her job as City Manager.
 Section 11. Bonding
 Employer shall bear the full cost of any fidelity or other bonds required of the Employee
 under any law or ordinance.
 Section 12. Notices

 Section 13. General Provisions
 A. The text herein shall constitute the entire agreement between the parties.
 B. This agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the heirs at law and
 executors of Employee.
 C. This agreement shall become effective commencing immediately.
