 RESOLUTION NO. 2007 -02
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 FLORIDA, DECREASING THE TOTAL NUMBER OF FULL-TIME POSITIONS
 WITHIN THE 2006-2007 BUDGET FROM 90 FULL-TIME POSITIONS TO 87
 POSITIONS; APPROVING AMENDMENTS TO THE PAY PLAN; APPROVING
 THE ASSOCIATED BUDGET ADJUSTMENTS; AND PROVIDING AN
 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, approved the annual
 operating budget via Ordinance No. 2006-30; and
 WHEREAS, the authorized total number of full-time positions contained in the
 approved budget was 90; and
 WHEREAS, the total position count must be reduced to 87 to reflect the contracting
 out of code enforcements services to the Broward Sheriff's Office along with a reduction of
 one position in Leisure Services; and
 WHEREAS, budget funds must be transferred from one department to another in
 order to accomplish the contracting out of code enforcement services to the Broward
 Sheriff's Office which changes the character of said funds from personal services to
 contractual services; and
 WHEREAS, the City Manager's Office has completed organizational performance
 reviews and is responsible for ensuring the appropriate titles, classifications and personnel
 are in place to successfully implement the Strategic Goal of "High Performing City
 Organization Providing Superior Services;" and
 WHEREAS, key organizational changes as well as reclassifications and title changes
 are necessary to more accurately reflect organizational assignments, duties and
 responsibilities which will necessitate updates to the City's pay plan; and

<<PG>>
 WHEREAS, it would be in the best interests of the City of Parkland to approve said
 modifications to the pay plan to implement organizational performance and customer service
 needs;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1 . That the City of Parkland City Commission hereby authorizes an
 decrease in the personnel cap by a net total of two positions as detailed in Exhibit A attached
 hereto and made a part hereof.
 Section 2. That the City of Parkland City Commission hereby authorizes updates to
 the pay plan to reflect organizational needs as detailed in Exhibit B attached hereto and
 made a part hereof.
 Section 3. That the 2006-2007 Budget, as established by Ordinance No. 2006-30
 shall be amended to the inter-department budget transfer in accordance with Exhibit C
 attached hereto.
 Section 4. That all Resolutions or parts of Resolutions in conflict herewith are
 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 Section 5. That this Resolution shall be in full force and effect immediately upon it
 passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 3 DAY OF nu , 2007
 MICHA L UDINE, MAYOR
 Attest:
 SANDRA COUZZO, C.M.C., 4cCLERK
