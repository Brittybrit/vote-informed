 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO DECLARE A WATER SHORTAGE
 5 EMERGENCY AND TO ASSIST IN THE IMPLEMENTATION AND
 6 ENFORCEMENT OF SFWMD PHASE III ORDER ENTITLED,
 7 "DECLARATION OF MODIFIED PHASE III WATER SHORTAGE
 8 RESTRICTIONS", ISSUED DECEMBER 13, 2007 BY THE
 9 SOUTH FLORIDA WATER MANAGEMENT DISTRICT ORDER
 10 NO. 2007-870-DAO-WS; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS;
 11 PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
 12 EFFECTIVE DATE.

 14
 15 WHEREAS, the South Florida Water Management District ("SFWMD")
 16 determines and declares a water shortage or determines that a water shortage
 17 emergency exists within the City of Parkland ("City"); and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland adopts said declaration and finds a water
 20 shortage (as defined in Section 11-86 of the City Code) to exist; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, during such water shortage conditions the amount of surface
 23 and ground water supplies may become insufficient to meet current or
 24 anticipated demands; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, upon the existence of such conditions it has become
 27 imperative to the publics well being that certain uses of water be restricted or
 28 curtailed and that available water resources be allocated; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, SFWMD has the primary responsibility for regulating water
 31 use and allocating available water supplies during periods of water shortage; and
 32
 33 WHEREAS, on December 13, 2007 the SFWMD issued a Phase Ill water
 34 shortage Order No. 2007-870-DAO-WS, which became effective January 15,
 35 2008; and
 36
 37 WHEREAS, SFWMD requested every city, county and appropriate
 38 governmental entities to assist in the implementation and enforcement of the
 39 water shortage area; and
 40
 41 WHEREAS, Section 11-88 of the Code of Ordinances of the City requires
 42 that the City Commission act by resolution to declare an emergency water
 43 shortage whenever SFWMD declares such a shortage or emergency; and
 44
 45 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines the water
 46 conservation measures to be necessary and in the best interest of the health,
 47 safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City; and
 48

 1 WHEREAS, violations of any provision of this Section 11-95 can be
 2 subject to a fine not to exceed five hundred dollars ($500.00) per incident; and
 3
 4
 5 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 6 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 7
 8 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified
 9 as being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein. All exhibits attached
 10 hereto are incorporated herein.
 11
 12 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby declares a
 13 water shortage emergency pursuant to Order of the South Florida Water
 14 Management District and shall assist the SFWMD in the implementation and
 15 enforcement of the Phase III Water Shortage Order. The provisions of said order
 16 are hereby declared to be binding and violations of said order shall be considered
 17 a violation of law punishable as set forth in section 11-95 of the City Code. A
 18 copy of the Order is hereby incorporated by reference as Attachment A.
 19
 20 SECTION 3. The City Commission acknowledges that additional orders
 21 related to the water shortage may be forthcoming from the SFWMD and that the
 22 City shall take the necessary action to assist in the implementation of any further
 23 orders related to the water shortage.
 24
 25 SECTION 4. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are
 26 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 27
 28 SECTION 5. If any clause, section, other part or application of this
 29 Resolution is held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or
 30 invalid, in part of application, it shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions
 31 or applications of this Resolution.
 32
 33 SECTION 6. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 34 adoption.
 35
 36 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 6th DAY OF FEBRUARY, 2008.
