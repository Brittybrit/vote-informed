 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-112
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO AUTHORIZE THE APPROPRIATE
 5 CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE ATTACHED AGREEMENT
 6 BETWEEN THE CITY AND BROWARD COUNTY FOR A
 7 SENIOR RECREATION PROGRAM UNDER THE COMMUNITY
 8 DEVELOPMENT BLOCK GRANT PROGRAM
 9
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland, Florida applied to Broward County for
 12 a Community Development Block Grant ("CDBG") to implement a mature
 13 adult program; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, Broward County has determined that the City's application
 16 meets all requirements for issuance of the CDBG grant; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has the legal jurisdiction and authority
 19 to create, operate and maintain the mature adult program; that the City of
 20 Parkland agrees to conform with the regulations, statutes, terms and
 21 conditions described in the CDBG Agreement, and that the City of Parkland
 22 commits to providing the matching funds as proposed in the CDBG
 23 Agreement; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland must enter into the attached
 26 Agreement to finalize the funding and administration requirements of the
 27 CDBG grant program; and
 28
 29 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 30 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 31
 32 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified
 33 as being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached
 34 hereto are hereby incorporate herein.
 35
 36 SECTION 2. The appropriate City officials are hereby authorized and directed
 37 to execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this
 38 resolution.
 39
 40 SECTION 3. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are
 41 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 42
 43 SECTION 4. If any clause, section, other part or application of this Resolution
 44 is held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or invalid, in part of
 45 application, it shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions or applications of
 46 this Resolution.
 47
 48 SECTION 5. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 49 adoption.
