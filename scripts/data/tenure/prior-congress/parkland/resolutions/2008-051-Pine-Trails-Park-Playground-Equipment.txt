 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-51
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AUTHORIZING THE
 5 CITY MANAGER TO PIGGYBACK THE CITY OF
 6 JACKSONVILLE STATE CONTRACT SC-0511-06 WITH
 7 PLAYMORE RECREATIONAL PRODUCTS FOR THE
 8 PURPOSE OF PURCHASE OF PLAYGROUND
 9 EQUIPMENT FOR PHASE III OF PINE TRAILS PARK IN
 10 AN AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED $299,947.52 AND
 11 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland is committed to providing 1st class leisure
 14 and cultural amenities; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland is committed to providing facilities
 17 responding to Parkland's community needs; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, monies were budgeted in the City of Parkland Fiscal Year 2008
 20 Budget for the purpose of completing Phase III at Pine Trails Park; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, Phase Ill at Pine Trails Park includes the construction of a
 23 playground between the basketball courts and the concession stand; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, Staff has identified a piggybacking opportunity to construct the
 26 playground utilizing the City of Jacksonville State contract #SC-0511-06 with
 27 Playmore Recreational products; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, Playmore Products has designed a playground that best suits
 30 the needs of the City of Parkland; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes that it is in the
 33 best interest of the residents of the City of Parkland to piggyback the City of
 34 Jacksonville State contract with Playmore for the purpose of constructing a
 35 playground at Pine Trails Park; and
 36
 37 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 38 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 39
 40 SECTION 1. That the City Manager is hereby authorized to piggyback the
 41 City of Jacksonville State Contract #SC-0511-06 with Playmore Recreational
 42 Products for the purpose of constructing a playground at Pine Trails Park in an
 43 amount not to exceed $299,947.52.
 44
 45 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately
 46 upon its passage and adoption.
