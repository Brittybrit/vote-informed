 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-99
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION
 4 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 ADOPTING A CITY OF PARKLAND
 6 COMPREHENSIVE EMERGENCY
 7 MANAGEMENT PLAN (CEMP) IN
 8 ACCORDANCE WITH THE NATIONAL
 9 INCIDENT MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (NIMS),
 10 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 11
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland and other municipalities within the State
 14 of Florida have been victims of several hurricanes over the past two years; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the response and recovery efforts to said hurricanes has
 17 revealed shortfalls in management and coordination efforts between the various
 18 levels of government; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, it has been determined that response and recovery efforts
 21 would be greatly improved through written emergency management plans that
 22 are common to all levels of government; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the Federal Government has instituted the National Incident
 25 Management System (NIMS) which provides a consistent nationwide template
 26 that enables governments at all levels to work together more effectively and
 27 efficiently in all aspects of emergency management ; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, adoption of NIMS is a requirement in order to receive future
 30 Federal emergency preparedness funding; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, the Parkland Comprehensive Emergency Management Plan
 33 has been prepared in accordance with Broward Emergency Management
 34 Agency ordinance, FEMA emergency planning criteria, and Florida Statutes
 35 Chapter 252.38(2); and
 36
 37 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes having a
 38 comprehensive emergency management plan is in the best interest of the
 39 citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 40
 41 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 42 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 43

<<PG>>
 44 SECTION 1. The Parkland Comprehensive Emergency
 45 Management Plan and National Incident Management System (NIMS) are hereby
 46 adopted.
 47
 48 SECTION 2. The City manager or designee is hereby authorized to
 49 maintain and update the Comprehensive Emergency Management Plan as may
 50 be necessary from time to time.
 51
 52 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 53 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
