 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, VACATING AND ABANDONING
 5 A 10 FOOT PORTION OF THE 25 FOOT UTILITY AND
 6 DRAINAGE EASEMENT OF LOTS 1 AND 2 OF RAYEL

 8 THEREFORE; AND DIRECTING THAT A COPY HEREOF BE
 9 FILED WITH THE PROPERTY APPRAISER'S AND COUNTY
 10 RECORDER'S OFFICES IN AND FOR BROWARD COUNTY,
 11 FLORIDA. CASE NO. 01-AB-06.

 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission is authorized to cause any street, waterway, alley
 14 or public highway to be vacated when the same may be necessary; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, is City Commission has determined that the vacation of the herein
 17 described property as a utility and drainage easement is necessary and in the public interest
 18 and said property is no longer needed; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, Rayel Estates Plat 1 was approved on November 14, 2004.
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the Plat included a 25 foot easement for Drainage and Utility purposes;
 23 and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the petitioner desires to vacate a portion of the easement to increase the
 26 area available for pool, patio and deck designs for the parcels; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, all utility and drainage easement holders have submitted a letter of no
 29 objection; and
 30
 31 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 32 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 33
 34 SECTION 1. That this City Commission does hereby find, after a public hearing was
 35 duly held with publication required by law, said public hearing held June 6, 2007, that all the
 36 property as described in Section 2 of this Resolution is no longer needed for public use, and the
 37 vacating / abandoning of same is necessary and in the public interest.
 38
 39 SECTION 2. That the utility and drainage easement property on Lot 1 Rayel Estates
 40 Plat 1 of approximately 3,770 square feet described on Exhibit A is hereby vacated and
 41 abandoned.
 42
 43 SECTION 3. That the utility and drainage easement property on Lot 2 Rayel Estates
 44 Plat 1 of approximately 3,770 square feet described on Exhibit B is hereby vacated and
 45 abandoned.
