 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA GRANTING THE VARIANCE PETITION
 5 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND FROM SECTION 15-5 OF THE
 6 CODE OF ORDINANCES TO PERMIT A REVOLVING OR
 7 ROTATING SIGN AT OLD CITY HALL, AS MORE
 8 PARTICULARLY DESCRIBED HEREIN; AND PROVIDING AN
 9 EFFECTIVE DATE; CASE NUMBER 02-V-08.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of
 12 Parkland, Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166,
 13 Florida Statutes, and the Code of Ordinances, is authorized and empowered to
 14 consider petitions related to zoning and land development orders; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, a proper Notice of Public Hearing was sent to property
 17 owners of record within 500 feet of the subject property and was published in a
 18 newspaper qualified to carry legal advertisements; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, at a regular meeting of the Planning and Zoning Board, taking
 21 place on May 8, 2008, a public hearing was held to hear testimony and receive
 22 exhibits for and against the Petition of the City of Parkland (owners) requesting a
 23 variance from Section 15-5 in the Code of Ordinances, as adopted by the City of
 24 Parkland; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, at a regular meeting of the City Commission, taking place on
 27 May 21, 2008, a public hearing was held to hear testimony and receive exhibits
 28 for and against the Petition of the City of Parkland (owners) requesting a
 29 variance from Section 15-5 in the Code of Ordinances, as adopted by the City of
 30 Parkland, and the Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval (7-0); and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and
 33 testimony presented by the Applicant and other interested parties and the
 34 recommendations of the various City of Parkland review agencies and staff.
 35
 36 NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the City Commission of the City of
 37 Parkland, Florida, that;
 38
 39 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 40 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein.
 41
 42 SECTION 2. After due consideration of all testimony and exhibits and
 43 review of the standards for granting a variance as set forth in Article XXII, Section
 44 22-323 of the Code of Ordinances, all necessary standards for granting a
 45 variance have been satisfied to permit a revolving or rotating sign on the property
 46 known as Old City Hall, located at the northeast intersection of Holmberg Road
 47 and Parkside Drive; and
