 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION
 4 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 AUTHORIZING THE APPROPRIATE CITY
 6 OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
 7 WITH AQUAGENIX FOR THE PURPOSE OF
 8 PERFORMING MONTHLY CANAL AND LAKE
 9 MAINTENANCE SERVICES WITHIN THE
 10 RANCHES SUBDIVISION AND AT VARIOUS
 11 CITY FACILITIES IN THE AMOUNT OF $2825
 12 PER MONTH ($33,900 PER YEAR),
 13 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 14
 15
 16 WHEREAS, monies are budgeted annually for the purpose of contracting
 17 canal and lake maintenance services within the Ranches Subdivision and at
 18 various City facilities; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the City currently utilizes two (2) separate companies for the
 21 purpose of providing the aforementioned services; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the City has identified a piggybacking opportunity to provide
 24 the aforementioned services with Aquagenix utilizing a Village of Pinecrest
 25 contract; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City will realize an annual savings of approximately 5% by
 28 piggybacking the Village of Pinecrest contract with Aquagenix; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, staff recommends that the City execute an Agreement with
 31 Aquagenix for the purpose of providing canal and lake maintenance services
 32 within the Ranches Subdivision and at various City facilities; and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes the
 35 Agreement with Aquagenix is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of
 36 Parkland; and
 37
 38 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 39 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 40
 41 SECTION 1. The City Manager is hereby authorized to execute an
 42 Agreement with Aquagenix for the purpose of providing canal and lake
 43 maintenance services within the Ranches Subdivision and at various City facilities.
