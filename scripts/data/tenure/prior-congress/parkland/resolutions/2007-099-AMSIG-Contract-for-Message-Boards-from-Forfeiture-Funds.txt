 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING A CONTRACT BETWEEN
 5 THE CITY OF PARKLAND AND AMSIG TOWARDS THE
 6 PURCHASE OF TWO MESSAGE BOARDS AT A COST NOT TO
 7 EXCEED $29,000; FUNDING TO BE VIA THE POLICE FORFEITURE
 8 FUND
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the Parkland Police Chief and City Manager recommend that $29,000 from
 11 the Police Forfeiture Fund be utilized toward the purchase of two message boards; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, in the past Parkland/BSO was able to obtain similar message boards from
 14 other BSO Districts but the ability to borrow them has diminished due to demand and needs in
 15 other Districts; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, Parkland Police Chief acknowledges that the two message boards are
 18 needed and it is his recommendation that monies are used from the police forfeiture fund for the
 19 purchase; and
 20
 21 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 22 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 23 SECTION 1. The City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida hereby authorizes
 24 the appropriate City official to enter into a contract with Amsig for the purchase of two message
 25 boards not to exceed the amount of$29,000.
 26
 27 SECTION 2. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are hereby repealed
 28 to the extent of such conflict.
 29
 30 SECTION 3. If any clause, section, other part or application of this Resolution is held by
 31 any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or invalid, in part of application, it shall
 32 not affect the validity of the remaining portions or applications of this Resolution.
 33
 34 SECTION 4. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately
 35 upon its passage and adoption.
