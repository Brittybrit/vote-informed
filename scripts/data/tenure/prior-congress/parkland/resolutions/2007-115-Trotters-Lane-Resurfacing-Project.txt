 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007- 115
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO
 5 PIGGYBACK A PALM BEACH COUNTY CONTRACT (PROJECT
 6 #2007054) WITH FLORIDA HIGHWAY PRODUCTS, INC., FOR THE
 7 PURPOSE OF INSTALLING 3" COLD MIX OPEN GRADED BITUMINOUS
 8 PAVEMENT ON TROTTERS LANE IN THE AMOUNT OF $129,209.00,
 9 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11
 12 WHEREAS, monies were budgeted in the City of Parkland Fiscal Year 2008 Budget for
 13 the purpose of making road improvements to NW 72n Street (Trotters Lane); and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, Staff recommends utilizing cold mix open graded bituminous pavement to
 16 stabilize the road surface; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, stabilizing the road surface will provide a safer and more durable road
 19 condition; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, Staff has identified a piggybacking opportunity to install the aforementioned
 22 pavement utilizing a Palm Beach County contract with Florida Highway Products, Inc; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes it is in the best interest of the
 25 citizens of the City of Parkland to contract with Florida Highway Products, Inc. for the purpose
 26 of stabilizing Trotters Lane; and
 27
 28 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 29 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 30
 31 SECTION 1. The City Manager is hereby authorized to piggyback the Palm Beach
 32 County contract with Florida Highway Products, Inc. for the purpose of stabilizing Trotters Lane in
 33 the amount of $129,209.00.
 34
 35 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately
 36 upon its passage and adoption.
 37
 38
 39 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 5 DAY OF Decembe , 2007.
