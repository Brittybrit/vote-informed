 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-63
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO ADOPT THE FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF
 5 ELDER AFFAIRS COMMUNITIES FOR A LIFETIME INITIATIVE AND
 6 FOR CITY STAFF TO IMPLEMENT THE PROGRAM
 7
 8 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland supports the Florida Department of Elder Affairs' Communities
 9 for a Lifetime initiative to make Florida a friendlier place to live for people of all ages, that they may be
 10 as independent as long as possible, and remain in their homes and in the communities they love; and
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the State of Florida has the highest percentage of elders of any state in the nation
 13 and our elder population will continue to increase, reaching numbers without precedent in the first part
 14 of the XXI Century; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, in order to allow all residents to maintain their dignity, security and independence,
 17 communities must evaluate, assess and modify their infrastructures to become a Community for a
 18 Lifetime; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland and the Florida Department of Elder Affairs share the vision
 21 and responsibility to improve the life of all citizens so they can prepare for and enjoy aging in place
 22 throughout their life; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, in order to achieve our mutual goals, cities and counties should begin to build
 25 together a place free of physical, emotional, and social barriers, and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland will make every effort to first evaluate and later remove
 28 barriers to become a Community for a Lifetime.
 29
 30 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 31 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 32
 33 Section 1. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby adopts the Florida
 34 Department of Elder Affairs' Communities for a Lifetime Initiative.
 35
 36 Section 2. City staff is hereby authorized to implement the Communities for a Lifetime Initiative
 37 in accordance with the established guidelines.
 38
 39 Section 3. That this Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
