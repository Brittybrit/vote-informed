 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-87
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION
 4 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO
 6 APPROVE CONSTRUCTION AND DESIGN
 7 RELATED CHANGE ORDERS TO THE PINE
 8 TRAILS PARK PHASE II PROJECT,
 9 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11
 12 WHEREAS, monies were budgeted in the City of Parkland Fiscal Year
 13 2006 Capital Improvement Budget for the purpose of constructing Phase II of
 14 Pine Trails Park; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, Construction and design related change orders are not
 17 uncommon to projects of this scope and can cause project delays; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, it is recommended that the City Manager be authorized to
 20 approve change orders in an effort to minimize project delays; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, said authorization shall not be permitted to exceed the
 23 approved project budget and no single change item shall exceed twenty five
 24 thousand dollars ($25,000); and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes the City
 27 Manager's ability to approve said change orders is in the best interest of the
 28 citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 29
 30 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 31 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 32
 33 SECTION 1. The appropriate City officials do hereby approve the City
 34 Manager to approve change orders related to the Pine Trails Park Phase II project.
 35
 36 SECTION 2. The City manager's approval authority shall not exceed
 37 the project budget and shall not exceed twenty five thousand dollars ($25,000) for
 38 any single item.
 39
 40 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 41 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 42
 43 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 23 DAY OF AUGUST , 2006.
