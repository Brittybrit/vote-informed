 9 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,

 35 Bay North Plat 3 residential development is hereby APPROVED with the following conditions:

 43 2008.

 45 sidewalk or roadway.

 4 4. The party responsible for the landscape buffers shall employ a full-time

 22 material is determined to not be comparable, a site plan amendment
 23 application shall be submitted with all applicable fees.
 24 8. The NW 122nd Avenue Phase II Landscape buffer will be constructed

 26 Bay residential community. Failure to maintain the landscaping or
 27 respond to a City request as set forth herein shall constitute a violation of
 28 this approval and the City's Code of Ordinances.
