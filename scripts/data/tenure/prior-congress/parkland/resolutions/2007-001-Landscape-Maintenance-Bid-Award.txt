THIS AGREEMENT is entered into this by and between the CITY OF PARKLAND 
(CITY), a municipal corporation operating and existing under the Laws of the State of Florida, 
and TOP BRANCH ENVIRONMENTAL SERVICE, INC., (CONTRACTOR) as follows: 
WITNESSETH: 
WHEREAS, the CITY has advertised for a bid for Landscape Maintenance. Services 
pursuant to the specifications as attached hereto and made a part hereof (hereinafter referred to as 
the Specifications); and 
WHEREAS, CONTRACTOR has been declared the lowest responsible and responsive 
bidder; and 
WHEREAS, the parties wish to enter into a contract for Landscape Maintenance 
Services according to the Specifications; and 
WHEREAS, CONTRACTOR has reviewed the Specifications and is fully prepared to 
undertake the services in strict compliance with the Specifications; 

<<PG>>
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT AGREED TO BY AND BETWEEN THE PARTIES 
AS FOLLOWS: 
Section 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a 
part hereof. 
Section 2. The Specifications are hereby adopted as part of this contract. The 
contract shall consist of this document, together with the Specifications. To the extent that this 
document conflicts with the Specifications, this document shall control, otherwise, the 
Specifications shall control the services between the parties. 
Section 3. The CONTRACTOR hereby affirms that it has fully read the 
Specifications, is familiar with the CITY, and has done the necessary investigations to assure that 
it is capable of performing the services as set forth in the Specifications for the compensation as 
set forth therein. 
Section 4. The bid sheet for Landscape Maintenance Services as contained on page 
14 of the Specifications shall constitute the basis for payment. One percent (1%) of said sum 
shall constitute compensation for the indemnity set forth in Section 5.8 of the Specifications. 
Invoices shall be provided to the CITY monthly on the first week of each month for the work 
done in the previous month. All undisputed invoices shall be paid by the CITY within thirty (30) 
days of receipt, provided all appropriate documentation is provided. The Public Services 
Director may provide specific forms for payment after four (4) invoices at its sole option. If such 
forms are promulgated by the Public Services Director, they shall be used by the 
CONTRACTOR in order to receive payment. Any documentation required by the Public 
Services Director as is reasonable to enable him to make a judgment on the invoice shall be 
provided by the CONTRACTOR.

LANDSCAPE MAINTENANCE SERVICES 
SPECIFICATIONS 
GENERAL: The City of Parkland is interested in obtaining proposals from qualified 
Contractors, for the purpose of providing landscape maintenance services within City 
medians and rights-of-way. 
The work consists of furnishing all labor, materials, equipment, tools, permitting, service 
and supervision necessary to properly complete the work in a safe, effective, and 
efficient manner. The work shall be performed by a licensed landscape contractor and 
crew with at least five (5) years of verifiable full-time experience with landscape 
maintenance work of similar scope and cost. 
ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS 
1.0 ANSI: American National Standards Institute. 
1.1 
DEBRIS & LITTER: Objects that are unsightly or present obstacles to mowing and other 
grounds maintenance operations including but not limited to, rocks in lawn areas, wind 
and storm damage items; paper, glass, trash, or debris deposited or blown onto the 
sites; palm fronds and tree limbs; and illegally posted signs within the public rights-of- 
way, are to be removed by Contractor, at Contractor's expense. 
1.2 EDGING: The cutting of overhanging or overgrown vegetation away from a hard surface 
such as curbing, sidewalks, driveways, roadways, etc., and/or the maintenance of a 
defined manicured edge around tree rings, landscape borders, monuments, signs, or 
other physical elements protruding from the landscape maintenance area. 
1.3 FDOT: Florida Department of Transportation 
1.4 IRRIGATION: All of the plumbing and electrical devices involved in the distribution of 
irrigation water. The plumbing begins at, but does not include, the main supply and ends 
with the drain valve or sprinkler head. The electrical begins at, and includes, the 
electrical controller (including box) and ends with the electrical solenoid on a valve or 
sprinkler head. 
1.5 MSDS: Material Safety Data Sheet 
1.6 MULCH: Shall mean shredded melaluca or eucalyptus. Mulch shall be red in color 
unless otherwise authorized by the City. 
1.7 OSHA: Occupational Safety and Health Administration 
1.8 PEST CONTROL: The use of integrated pest management techniques to control 
outdoor pests such as weeds, rodents, insects and plant diseases. 
1.9 SAFETY: Freedom from the occurrence or risk of injury, danger, or loss.
