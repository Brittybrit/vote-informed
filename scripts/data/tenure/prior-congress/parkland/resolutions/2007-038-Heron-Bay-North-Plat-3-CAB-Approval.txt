 3 SECTION 1. The Community Appearance Board petition of WCI Communities Inc for
 4 the Heron Bay North Plat 3 is hereby APPROVED, subject to the conditions of approval
 5 contained herein, which are in addition to the general requirements otherwise provided by
 6 ordinance:
 7
 8 1. This approval is based upon the plans entitled "40' Wide Product Heron
 9 Bay North POD 3" dated December 5, 2006, "50' Wide Product Heron
 10 Bay North POD 3" dated December 5, 2006 and "60' Wide Product Heron
 11 Bay North POD 3" dated December 5, 2006 and received by the Planning
 12 and Zoning Department on January 4, 2007.
 13 2. All conditions and approvals as outlined in Ordinances 2003-06, 2003-07,
 14 and 2005-96 not specifically amended by this application shall govern the
 15 development.
 16 3. A site plan shall be required for all landscape buffers, free standing signs,
 17 utility structures, and entry features.
 18 4. No residential dwelling unit building permit shall be issued under this
 19 approval until the certificate of occupancy is issued for the second
 20 recreational facility within the Heron Bay development and until the
 21 certificate of completion issued for the basketball courts, pool and
 22 accessory buildings and uses adjacent to the second recreational facility.

 24 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
