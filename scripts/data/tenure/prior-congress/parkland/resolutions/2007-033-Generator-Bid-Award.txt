 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION
 4 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 APPROVING THE AWARD OF BID NUMBER
 6 2007-03 TO CRISTIANO ELECTRIC INC. FOR
 7 THE INSTALLATION OF AN EMERGENCY
 8 GENERATOR AT CITY HALL IN THE AMOUNT
 9 OF $91,361.24, PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE
 10 DATE.
 11
 12
 13 WHEREAS, monies were budgeted in the City of Parkland Fiscal Year
 14 2007 Budget for the purpose of installing emergency generators at City Hall; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, Staff prepared and issued Bid Number 2007-03 for the
 17 purpose of obtaining a qualified contractor to install the aforementioned
 18 generator; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the City received three (3) responses to the aforementioned
 21 Bid; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, Cristiano Electric Inc. submitted the lowest responsive and
 24 responsible bid; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, Staff recommends that the bid be awarded to Cristiano
 27 Electric Inc. in the amount of $91,361.24; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes the award of
 30 Bid Number 2007-03 to Cristiano Electric Inc. for the installation of a generator at
 31 City Hall in the amount of $91,361.24 is in the best interest of the citizens of the
 32 City of Parkland; and
 33
 34 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 35 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 36
 37 SECTION 1. The appropriate City officials do hereby approve the
 38 award of Bid Number 2007-03 to Cristiano Electric Inc. in the amount of
 39 $$91,361.24.
 40
 41 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 42 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 43
 44
 45 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 7 DAY OF March , 2007.
