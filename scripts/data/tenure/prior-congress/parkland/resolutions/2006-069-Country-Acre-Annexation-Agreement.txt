 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006- 69
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND FLORIDA AUTHORIZING THE APPROPRIATE CITY
 5 OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE ATTACHED INTERLOCAL
 6 AGREEMENT WITH BROWARD COUNTY IN REGARD TO THE
 7 ANNEXATION TRANSITION PROCESS RELATED TO THE
 8 SEPTEMBER 15, 2006 ANNEXATION OF COUNTRY ACRES.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the Country Acres residential subdivision is located in unincorporated
 11 Broward County; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, all Broward County unincorporated properties are required to be
 14 incorporated into a local jurisdiction by 2010; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the residents of each unincorporated subdivision must decide which
 17 jurisdiction they want to be annexed to; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the residents of Country Acres had a choice of being annexed into the
 20 City of Parkland or the City of Coral Springs; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, by vote, the residents of Country Acres elected to annex into the City of
 23 Parkland; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the annexation does not become effective until September 15, 2006;
 26 and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, there must be a process and understanding as to what services
 29 Broward County and the City of Parkland will provide through September 30, 2006; and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 32 Florida, finds and determines that the City needs an Interlocal agreement to outline the City
 33 of Parkland's and Broward County's responsibilities regarding the transition period related
 34 to the September 15, 2006 annexation of Country Acres; and
 35
 36 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 37 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 38
 39 SECTION 1. That the appropriate City Officials are hereby authorized to execute the
 40 attached Interlocal Agreement with Broward County in regard to the annexation
 41 transition process related to the September 15, 2006 annexation of Country Acres.
 42
 43 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
