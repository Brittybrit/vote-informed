 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, CONDEMNING ALL HATE
 5 CRIMES PARTICULARLY THE RECENT INCIDENT AT THE
 6 CHABAD IN PARKLAND AND DECLARE THERE WILL BE
 7 ZERO TOLERANCE FOR THESE TYPE OF ACTIONS IN THE
 8 CITY OF PARKLAND.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland is a diverse community; and
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland supports racial and religious freedom for
 13 everyone, regardless of country of origin, native language or chosen path to find and
 14 worship their God; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has a responsibility to take a leadership role in
 17 help creating a climate of tolerance and understanding with the various ethnic, religious
 18 and nationality groups and people within the City of Parkland; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland chooses to be a formidable leader in promoting
 21 religious freedom and calls on all leaders to promote tolerance and decry violence and
 22 acts of hatred in their communities; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland strongly condemns racial or religious
 25 harassment and holds that racial and religious hate crimes are contrary to the very
 26 principal upon which the United States and the City of Parkland is founded; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS prejudice and discrimination based on religion have caused untold
 29 human suffering throughout recorded history; and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, the most recent statistics published by the Federal Bureau of
 32 Investigation indicate that crimes against Jews and Jewish institutions comprise the
 33 overwhelming majority of religiously-based hate crimes in the United States; and
 34
 35 WHEREAS, we owe it to the victims of the Holocaust, to its survivors and to
 36 those who fought and died for their liberation, never to forget its lesson and to be ever
 37 vigilant against the scourge of religious intolerance in all its forms, including anti-
 38 Semitism and
 39
 40 WHEREAS, the recent incident involving the desecration of the Chabad of
 41 Parkland is deplorable and reflects badly upon every resident of Parkland
 42
 43 WHEREAS, these type of hate crimes will not be tolerated in the City of
 44 Parkland; and
 45
 46 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland residents deserve to live in a peaceful and
 47 charitable community where differences are not only accepted but celebrated; and

 2 WHEREAS, the Mayor and the Parkland City Commission would like it to be
 3 declared that the City of Parkland condemns these type of actions and will not tolerate
 4 any type of hate crimes in this City.

 6 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 7 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 8
 9 Section 1. The Mayor and City Commission condemns the actions that took
 10 place at the Chabad of Parkland and of all hate crimes and wishes for it to be known that
 11 these type of crimes will not be accepted in the City of Parkland and will be prosecuted to
 12 the fullest extent of the law.
 13
 14 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in 1 full force and effect
