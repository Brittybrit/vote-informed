 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO APPROVE THE
 5 READOPTION OF THE EVALUATION AND APPRAISAL
 6 REPORT FOR THE CITY OF PARKLAND.
 7
 8 WHEREAS, the Florida Legislature intends that local planning be a continuous
 9 and ongoing process; and
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland formally adopted the
 12 Goals, Objectives and Policies of each Element within its Comprehensive Plan on
 13 June 17, 1992 and amended it on March 16, 1994, December 15, 1999 and June 5,
 14 2002; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, Section 163.3191, Florida Statutes, directs local governments to
 17 adopt needed amendments to ensure that the Plan provides appropriate policy
 18 guidance for growth and development; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Board and City Commission at a joint
 21 meeting on September 21, 2005 reviewed the draft Evaluation and Appraisal Report,
 22 held an advertised public hearing, provided for participation by the public in the
 23 process, approved the draft Evaluation and Appraisal Report which identified the
 24 major issues and transmitted it to the Department of Community Affairs; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the City Commission according to a scheduled published by the
 27 Department of Community Affairs was required to adopt a final copy of the Evaluation
 28 and Appraisal Report prior to January 2006; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the Department of Community Affairs provided written comments
 31 to the City's adopted Evaluation and Appraisal Report after the required adoption
 32 date; and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, the City and the Department of Community Affairs agreed that
 35 changes are required to the Evaluation and Appraisal Report so the City is proceeding
 36 with the process of readopting its final Evaluation and Appraisal Report; and
 37
 38 WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Board at its July 25, 2006 meeting
 39 reviewed the draft Evaluation and Appraisal Report, held an advertised public hearing,
 40 provided for participation by the public in the process and recommended approval;
 41 and
 42
 43 WHEREAS, the City Commission has reviewed the final Evaluation and
 44 Appraisal Report, held an advertised public hearing and provided for comments and
 45 public participation in the process in accordance with the requirements of state law
 46 and the procedures adopted for public participation in the planning process; and

 13 SECTION 3. All Resolutions and parts of Resolutions in conflict herewith are
