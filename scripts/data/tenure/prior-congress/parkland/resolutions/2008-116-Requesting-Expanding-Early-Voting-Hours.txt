 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-116
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, URGING THE BROWARD
 5 COUNTY SUPERVISOR OF ELECTIONS TO EXPAND
 6 EARLY VOTING SITES.
 7
 8 WHEREAS, many Broward County residents took advantage of the opportunity to
 9 participate in early voting for the elections in November 2008; and
 10
 11 WHEREAS, turnout was so high that in many cases there were lines that were two or
 12 three hours long; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland believes that opportunities for
 15 voting should be expanded and that early voting provides an opportunity to increase voter
 16 turnout; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, in order to enhance this opportunity, the City Commission believes that
 19 centers for early voting should be expanded and suggests that, within the City of Parkland, the
 20 City of Parkland Community Center, a facility with more space than the City of Parkland
 21 Library, should be considered as a site for early voting;
 22
 23 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 24 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 25
 26 SECTION 1. The City of Parkland does hereby urge the Supervisor of Elections to
 27 expand sites for early voting, to choose larger sites to accommodate the popularity of this
 28 alternative; and to consider the City of Parkland Community Center as a future site for early
 29 voting.
 31
 32 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 19 day of November , 2008.
