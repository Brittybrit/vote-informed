 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA APPROVING THE SITE PLAN
 5 AMENDMENT AND COMMUNITY APPEARANCE BOARD
 6 APPLICATIONS TO DELETE THE CARPORTS AT THE
 7 WINNER'S CIRCLE MULTI-FAMILY RENTAL COMMUNITY
 8 LOCATED AT 5851 HOLMBERG ROAD AS DESCRIBED MORE
 9 PARTICULARLY HEREIN AND PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE
 10 DATE.
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland, Florida,
 13 pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the Code of
 14 Ordinance is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and land development
 15 orders; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the petitioner desires to remove the carports at the Winner's Circle Multi-
 18 Family Rental Community at 5851 Holmberg Road; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in Code of Ordinances, have
 21 been satisfied; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony presented by
 24 the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of the various City of Parkland
 25 review agencies and staff; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS,the City Commission has made the following findings of fact:
 28
 29 1. Environmental Considerations: No adverse impacts to the natural environment are
 30 expected since the property has already been disturbed.
 31
 32 2. Utilities: The proposed request will not affect existing water,
 33 sanitary, sewer and drainage systems.
 34
 35 3. Comprehensive Plan Considerations:The proposed development is consistent with the Land
 36 Use Element of the Comprehensive Plan.
 37
 38 4. Zoning Considerations: A carport is not required for an multi-family rental
 39 community and the deletion of the carports will not
 40 affect the number of required parking spaces.
 41
 42 5. Community Appearance Considerations: The carports were not maintained properly and were
 43 severely affected by the past year's hurricane. The
 44 removal of the dilapidated carports would improve the
 45 aesthetics of the development.

 1 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 2 PARKLAND,FLORIDA that:
 3
 4 SECTION 1. The Site Plan Amendment and Community Appearance Board Application of
 5 Winners Circle Realty Holding, Inc. ("Winners Circle") for the deletion of the carports within the
 6 Winners Circle Multi-Family Rental Community is hereby APPROVED on the real property as
 7 described in Exhibit A, subject to the conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition to
 8 the general requirements otherwise provided by ordinance:
 9 1. Plans. This approval is based upon the plans entitled "Carport Removal/Landscape Betterment
 10 Plan for the Winner's Circle" dated November 30, 2005 and received by the Planning and Zoning
 11 Department on December 21, 2005.
 12
 13 2. Western Landscape Buffer. Winners Circle shall install new native landscaping in accordance with
 14 the "Typical 100' section" prepared by the Environmental Resources Director and stamp dated April
 15 14, 2006. The typical 100' section shall be installed for a distance of approximately 1,600 lineal feet
 16 of Winners Circle along the western boundary line of the development("Western Landscape Buffer").
 17 Winners Circle shall install the Western Landscape Buffer within one hundred and eighty(180) days
 18 from approval of this Resolution, and shall maintain the landscaping in accordance with the approved
 19 plan. Upon installation, petitioner shall call the Environmental Resources Director to schedule
 20 courtesy inspections. To the extent any permits are needed to complete the Western Landscape
 21 Buffer,City agrees to expedite the issuance of such permits.
 22
 23 3. The Trail. Within sixty (60) days of approval of this Resolution, Winners Circle shall remove
 24 the fallen trees, tree stumps and debris ("Clearing Activities") in the area described as the 17-foot
 25 wide multi-purpose trail located immediately west of the Winners Circle Property (the "Trail").
 26 Winners Circle shall exercise due care to minimize any damage to the meandering asphalt walkway
 27 located in the Trail which may result from the execution of the Clearing Activities. City shall be
 28 responsible for any repairs to the asphalt walkway which results from the Clearing Activities. Upon
 29 completion of the Clearing Activities described in this paragraph, the City shall assume all future
 30 maintenance of the Trail and Winners Circle's obligation to maintain the Trail shall cease. To the
 31 extent any permits and/or inspections are needed to complete the Clearing Activities, City agrees to
 32 expedite same.
 33
 34 4. Northern Landscape Buffer (along Hillsboro Boulevard). Winners Circle shall upgrade the existing
 35 landscape buffer along Hillsboro Boulevard to comply with the City's current code requirements
 36 (with respect to landscape plant materials only) ("Northern Landscape Buffer"). City staff agrees to
 37 work with Winners Circle to develop a mutually acceptable landscape plan to complete this condition.
 38 If City staff and Winners Circle cannot reach an agreement regarding the landscape plan for the
 39 Northern Landscape Buffer, then this issue shall be brought before the City Commission for final
 40 determination. Within ninety(90)days of approval of the landscape plan(by either City staff or City
 41 Commission), Winners Circle shall install the Northern Landscape Buffer. To the extent any permits
 42 and or inspections are needed to install the Northern Landscape Buffer, City agrees to expedite same.
 43 Winners Circle shall be responsible for maintaining the Northern Landscape Buffer in accordance
 44 with the approved plan.
 45
 46 5. Eastern Landscape Buffer (U.S. 441). Winners Circle shall install adequate additional
 47 landscaping along U.S. 441 to fill in only those areas immediately adjacent to the Winners Circle
 48 monument sign ("Eastern Landscape Buffer). City staff agrees to work with Winners Circle to
 49 develop a mutually acceptable landscape plan to complete this condition. If City staff and Winners
 50 Circle cannot reach an agreement regarding the landscape plan for the Eastern Landscape Buffer, then
