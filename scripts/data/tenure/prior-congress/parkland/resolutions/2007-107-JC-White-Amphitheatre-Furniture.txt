 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO
 4 APPROVE UTILIZATION OF THE FLORIDA STATE CONTRACT # N061060 FOR THE
 5 PURCHASE OF FURNITURE FOR THE AMPHITHEATER LOCATED AT PINE TRAILS PARK.

 7
 8 WHEREAS, The City of Parkland will be opening a new Amphitheater facility within Pine
 9 Trails Park; and
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the Parks and Recreation Department offices will be located at the
 12 Amphitheater; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS; furniture is required within the facility for staff and for the public conference
 15 room; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the City published an advertisement for interested companies; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, three companies responded to the advertisement but only one submitted a
 20 proposal —JC White; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, JC White is the largest office furniture company in South Florida with over
 23 twenty five years experience; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the requested funds have been budgeted for this project; and
 26
 27
 28 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 29 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 30
 31 SECTION 1. That the state contract amount of $40,936.56 is hereby accepted and that
 32 purchase order for said furnishings in that amount is hereby approved to JC White.
 33
 34 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
