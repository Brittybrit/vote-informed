 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-50
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ADOPTING AN INVESTMENT POLICY
 5 FOR THE CITY OF PARKLAND TO PROVIDE GUIDELINES FOR
 6 INVESTMENT OF CITY FUNDS
 7
 8 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 218.415, Florida Statutes, the City is required to
 9 adopt an investment policy; and
 10
 11 WHEREAS,the City wishes to adopt a policy that addresses issues such as liquidity,
 12 risk diversification, safety of principal, market rate of return, maturity, and investment
 13 quality, as well as qualifications of investment dealers and issuers, to assure the proper care
 14 of public funds; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, it is the intention of the City of Parkland to adopt the policies as are
 17 attached hereto and to comply with applicable state law with regard to investment policies;
 18
 19 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 20 THE CITY OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 21
 22 SECTION 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and made
 23 a part hereof.
 24
 25 SECTION 2. The attached City of Parkland investment policy, dated May 21, 2008,
 26 is hereby adopted by the City Commission of the City of Parkland. The City Manager or
 27 his/her designee and all appropriate officials of the City shall comply with said investment
 28 policies, except to the extent as preempted by Florida law. The investment policy as set
 29 forth herein shall govern the actions of the City of Parkland with regard to all investment
 30 policies as required by state law.
