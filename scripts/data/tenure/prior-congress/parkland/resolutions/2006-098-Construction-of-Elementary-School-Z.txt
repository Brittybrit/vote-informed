 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-98
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SUPPORTING THE CONSTRUCTION OF
 ELEMENTARY SCHOOL Z AND REQUESTING THE SCHOOL BOARD OF
 BROWARD COUNTY IMMEDIATELY APPROVE THE AWARDING OF
 THE CONSTRUCTION CONTRACT WITH A TARGET COMPLETION
 DATE OF AUGUST 2008.
 WHEREAS, the School Board of Broward County has determined a short delay is needed in
 the,construction of the elementary school known as School Z due to lower than expected enrollment,
 and
 WHEREAS,the school is designed and the contract is ready to be let, and
 WHEREAS, the Parkland Education Advisory Board recommends to the City Commission
 that the Commission support the construction of School Z for opening by August 2008, and
 WHEREAS, the Educational Advisory Board expects the enrollment numbers to steadily
 increase within the next two years, and should the School Board need any additional demographic data
 from the City in order to proceed, such data will be supplied promptly, and
 WHEREAS, this resolution needs to be sent in time for the School Board's October 17, 2006
 meeting.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials do hereby support the construction of
 elementary school Z with an opening date of August 2008.
 Section 2. That the City Clerk forward this resolution to the Superintendent of Schools and
 the School Board of Broward County in time for their October 17, 2006 meeting.
 Section 3. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately upon its
 passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 4th DA. October ,2006.
