 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, URGING
 4 MEMBERS OF THE FLORIDA LEGISLATURE TO SUPPORT
 5 THE FOLLOWING ISSUES DURING THE 2008 LEGISLATIVE
 6 SESSION.
 7
 8 WHEREAS, as Florida's population continues to increase, cities are
 9 challenged to meet the service demands and infrastructure needs to account for
 10 new growth; and
 11
 12 WHEREAS, a majority of Florida's municipalities are challenged to meet
 13 the increasing infrastructure, affordable housing, transportation, and water
 14 quality needs of their citizens; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the state has imposed greater accountability in
 17 comprehensive planning and fiscal feasibility standards on municipalities; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, Florida's municipalities are dependent on other governmental
 20 entities for financial stability and presently the state, counties, and school
 21 districts, control every funding source that has been authorized for implementing
 22 growth management capital improvements and complying with concurrency
 23 requirements; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, municipal self-determination and local self-government are
 26 constantly under attack from a variety of public and private interests; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the role and function of municipal government is constantly
 29 evolving from new demands from its citizens and businesses.
 30
 31 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY OF PARKLAND:
 32
 33 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 34 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits
 35 attached hereto are hereby incorporate herein.
 36
 37 SECTION 2. That the City of Parkland urges Governor Charlie Crist and
 38 members of the Florida Legislature to support legislation that:
 39
 40 • Reauthorizes the affordable housing trust funds, preserves the
 41 documentary stamp fee as the trust funds' funding source and removes
 42 the legislative cap on fund distribution;
 43 • Enhances public notice of and participation in state environmental
 44 permitting processes;
 45 • Fully funds state growth management mandates and provides
 46 municipalities with state and local option revenue sources to meet
 47 financial feasibility requirements and infrastructure demands;

<<PG>>
 1 • Allows municipalities and counties to reclassify businesses, professions,
 2 and occupations and establish new rate structures for local business
 3 taxes on a periodic basis;
 4 • Enhances municipal control over municipal employee pension plan
 5 management and funding;
 6 • Preserves and protects property rights of mobile home owners, when
 7 mobile home parks are rezoned, but not in a manner that requires
 8 removal costs to be the responsibility of local governments;
 9 • Broadens municipal home rule and other authority to issue tax-increment
 10 financed bonds and other forms of indebtedness without approval by
 11 referendum;
 12 • Prohibits the Florida Department of Transportation from eliminating,
 13 deferring, or delaying capacity improvement projects contained in the 5-
 14 year Work Plan when it negatively impacts the comprehensive plan of the
 15 local government;
 16 • Authorizes the use of electronic traffic infraction detectors at traffic
 17 intersections; and
 18 • Maintains or increases funding of the state Water Protection and
 19 Sustainability Trust Fund.
 20
 21 SECTION 3. That a copy of this resolution shall be provided to Governor
 22 Charlie Crist, Senate President Ken Pruitt, House Speaker Marco Rubio
 23 and members of the Florida Legislature.
 24
 25 SECTION 4. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 26 adoption.
 27
 28 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 16 DAY OF January , 2008.
