 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007- 91
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 4 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, REQUESTS
 5 THE GOVERNOR'S CONSIDERATION DURING
 6 THE COMPACT DISCUSSIONS THE IMPACTS
 7 UPON TRANSPORTATION FOR PARKLAND
 8 RESIDENTS AND EFFECTS UPON FIRST
 9 RESPONDERS IF THE COCONUT CREEK CASINO
 10 IS APPROVED
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the Seminole Tribe ("Tribe") has developed plans to
 13 transform the Seminole Casino Coconut Creek into a destination resort; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the Tribe's proposal includes a 24 story, 1,500 room hotel,
 16 160,000 square foot casino, 2,500 seat concert venue, and 150,000 square feet
 17 of shops, restaurants and offices; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the hotel would have three times as many rooms as the
 20 Seminole Hard Rock Hotel & Casino near Hollywood; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the complex's nine story parking garage would be five stories
 23 higher than the next tallest building in the City of Coconut Creek; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the proposed complex would require 5,000 employees and
 26 likely generate up to 20,000 visitors a day; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the construction of the proposed expanded casino would
 29 result in a substantial number of vehicles on roadways within and adjacent to the
 30 City of Parkland; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, the proposed development would have a negative impact
 33 upon the first responder agreement that the City entered into with Coral Springs
 34 and Broward County;
 35
 36 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 37 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 38
 39 SECTION 1. The City of Parkland requests the Governor's consideration
 40 during the compact discussions of the impacts that the City of Parkland will need
 41 to address including transportation of Parkland residents and its first responder
 42 agreement with the City of Coral Springs and Broward County.
 43
 44 SECTION 2. The City Clerk forward this Resolution to the Governor
 45 immediately.
