 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-23
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AUTHORIZING A WORK
 AUTHORIZATION WITH CRAVEN THOMPSON AND
 ASSOCIATES, INC. FOR PROFESSIONAL SERVICES
 ASSOCIATED WITH THE DESIGN, PERMITTING, AND
 CONSTRUCTION OF PINE TRAILS PARK PHASE III IN
 AN AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED $264,540; PROVIDING
 FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, Edward D. Stone and Associates (EDSA) is currently under
 contract to the City for the design, permitting and construction administration for
 Pine Trails Park Phase II; and
 WHEREAS, City staff requested a change order from EDSA for the
 purpose of design, permitting, construction administration, and associated
 services for Pine Trails Park Phase III; and
 WHEREAS, EDSA submitted a proposal for $345,000 to perform the
 aforementioned services; and
 WHEREAS, City staff additionally requested a quote from Craven
 Thompson and Associates, Inc., one of the City's pre-approved engineering
 services firms, for the purpose of comparison and maintaining competitiveness;
 and
 WHEREAS, Craven Thompson and Associates, Inc. submitted a proposal
 in the amount of $264,540 to perform the aforementioned services; and
 WHEREAS, City staff recommends that it would be in the best interest of
 the City to issue a work authorization in the amount of $264,540 to Craven
 Thompson and Associates, Inc. to perform the design, permitting, construction
 administration and associated services for Pine Trails Park Phase III; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best
 interest of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City
 of Parkland to issue such work authorization; and
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 SECTION 1. The City Manager is hereby authorized to issue a work
 authorization to Craven Thompson and Associates, Inc. in the amount of $264,540

<<PG>>
 for the purpose of providing design, permitting, construction administration and
 associated services related to Pine Trails Park Phase III.
 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 7 DAY OF February , 2007.
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA
