 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-99
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AMENDING THE
 5 STREET NAME OF "TRAILS END" TO INCLUDE THE
 6 SUBTITLE "DARA TORRES BLVD" IN HONOR OF THE
 7 NINE TIME OLYMPIC GOLD MEDALIST AND RESIDENT
 8 OF PARKLAND, PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 9
 10
 11 WHEREAS, this past summer Parkland resident Dara Torres traveled to Beijing, China
 12 and competed in her fourth Olympic Games; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, inspiring the world at age forty, Dara Torres won three silver medals to
 15 bring her career total to nine Olympic medals overall; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to honor Dara Torres by co-naming a City
 18 street after her; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, staff recommends adding "Dara Torres Blvd" as a subtitle to "Trails End";
 21 and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes that honoring Dara Torres by
 24 amending the street name of "Trails End" to include the subtitle Dara Torres Blvd" is in the best
 25 interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 26
 27 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 28 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 29
 30 SECTION 1. The above representations are true and correct and made a part
 31 hereof.
 32
 33 SECTION 2. The City Commission does hereby approve the amendment of the
 34 street name "Trails End" to include the subtitle "Dara Torres Blvd."
 35
 36 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately
 37 upon its passage and adoption.
 38
 39 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 1 DAY OF October , 2008.
