 3 A RESOLUTION OF CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA SUPPORTING THE EFFORTS OF
 5 GOVERNOR CHARLIE CRIST AND THE SOUTH FLORIDA WATER
 6 MANAGEMENT DISTRICT TO ACQUIRE LAND SOUTH OF LAKE
 7 OKEECHOBEE FOR THE PROTECTION AND RESTORATION OF
 8 AMERICA'S EVERGLADES AND FLORIDA'S COASTAL
 9 ESTUARIES
 10
 11 WHEREAS, America's Everglades and the South Florida ecosystem is a
 12 unique expanse of habitats, whose environmental health is critical to the economy
 13 and quality of life of South Florida; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, on June 24, 2008, Governor Charlie Crist unveiled a
 16 momentous strategy to bring about the largest environmental land acquisition in
 17 Florida's history and restore America's Everglades in a way never before
 18 anticipated; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the Governor announced that the South Florida Water
 21 Management District will begin negotiating an agreement with United States Sugar
 22 Corporation to purchase nearly 300 square miles of land spanning four counties in
 23 South Florida; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the acquisition of as much as 187,000 acres of land south of
 26 Lake Okeechobee would provide the "missing link" needed to better protect the St.
 27 Lucie and Caloosahatchee rivers and estuaries, and revive, restore and preserve
 28 one of America's greatest natural treasures — the Everglades; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the South Florida Water Management District and United States
 31 Sugar Corporation signed a "Statement of Principles" that provides the framework
 32 for the potential acquisition of property and assets; and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, through successful negotiations and strategic land swaps, vast
 35 tracts would be used to reestablish a part of the historic connection between Lake
 36 Okeechobee and the Everglades through a managed system of storage and
 37 treatment; and
 38
 39 WHEREAS, dedicating significantly more land in the Everglades Agricultural
 40 Area to restoration will build upon and enhance the 30-year state-federal
 41 Comprehensive Everglades Restoration Plan, offering the opportunity and flexibility
 42 to store and clean water on a scale never before contemplated; and
 43
 44 WHEREAS, benefits from the land acquisition include huge increases in the
 45 availability of water storage, significantly reducing the potential for harmful
 46 discharges from Lake Okeechobee to Florida's coastal rivers and estuaries; and
 47

 1 WHEREAS, the acquisition will prevent thousands of tons of phosphorus
 2 from entering the Everglades every year and eliminate the need for "back-pumping"
 3 water into Lake Okeechobee; and
 4
 5 WHEREAS, the acquisition also provides the potential opportunity to sustain
 6 remaining agriculture in the region by eliminating environmental concerns;
 7 enhancing the availability of water supply; and fostering green energy production;
 8 and
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the Governor has identified the State of Florida's Rural
 11 Economic Development Initiative as a resource for working with United States
 12 Sugar Corporation, local governments, area businesses, state agencies and the
 13 South Florida Water Management District on an economic transition plan for the
 14 area; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, saving the Everglades and protecting Florida's coastal estuaries
 17 is an environmental priority for City of Parkland; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the South Florida Water Management District's initiative to
 20 purchase 187,000 acres of land in the Everglades Agricultural Area is a watershed
 21 event for restoring a beloved national treasure — the Everglades;
 22
 23 NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the City of Parkland applauds
 24 and supports the landmark efforts of Governor Charlie Crist and the South Florida
 25 Water Management District to negotiate the acquisition of significant lands in the
 26 Everglades Agricultural Area for the purposes of restoring and protecting the
 27 northern and southern Everglades and Florida's coastal estuaries.
 28
 29 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 20 day of August 2008.
