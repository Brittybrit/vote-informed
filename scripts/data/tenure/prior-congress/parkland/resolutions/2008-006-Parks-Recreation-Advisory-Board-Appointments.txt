 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-06
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPOINTING ONE (1) AT LARGE
 MEMBER AND ONE (1) ALTERNATE # 2 MEMBER TO THE
 PARKS & RECREATION ADVISORY BOARD.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances states that the Parks and
 Recreation Advisory Board shall consist of seven (7) regular members and two (2)
 alternates members who shall be residents of the City; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances states that each member of
 the City Commission shall have the right to appoint one (1) member to the Board and
 there shall be two (2) At Large appointments and two (2) Alternate At Large
 appointments; and
 WHEREAS, Board Member Pete Gado submitted his resignation from his At
 Large seat on the Parks & Recreation Advisory Board on January 31, 2008; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission needs to fill the At Large vacancy.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the City Commission hereby appoints the following At Large
 position as follows:
 At Large Eric Young
 Section 2. That by appointing Eric Young to the At Large position, there is
 now available an Alternate # 2 At Large position that the City Commission hereby
 appoints as follows:
 Alternate#2 - At Large Howard Wyner
 Section 3. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
