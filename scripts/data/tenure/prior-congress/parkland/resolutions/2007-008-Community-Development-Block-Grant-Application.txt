 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AUTHORIZE THE
 5 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE
 6 ATTACHED COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT BLOCK GRANT
 7 APPLICATION
 8
 9 WHEREAS, in 2006 the City Commission authorized staff to submit a Community
 10 Development Block Grant(CDBG) application to begin a mature adult program; and
 11
 12 WHERAS, the City was successful in obtaining the grant in the amount of
 13 approximately$66,000; and
 14
 15 WHERAS, the program has been extremely successful, where all the trips planned
 16 have been filled to capacity; and
 17
 18 WHERAS, the CDBG is a yearly program that the City must reapply for; and
 19
 20 WHERAS, based upon the success of the in house lecture / field trip format, staff is
 21 recommending submitting for the 2007-2008 CDGB that same format program
 22
 23 WHEREAS, staff has conducted public research and received public feedback and
 24 input in conformance with CDBG requirements to obtain the views of citizens or community
 25 development and housing needs, and on the proposed activities; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has the legal jurisdiction and authority to create,
 28 operate and maintain the mature adult program; that the City of Parkland agrees to conform
 29 with the regulations, statutes, terms and conditions described in the CDBG Application, that
 30 the City of Parkland commits to providing the matching funds as proposed in the CDBG
 31 application; and that Mark Lauzier, City Manager is authorized to submit this application to
 32 Broward County, on behalf of the City of Parkland, to act on behalf of the City of Parkland
 33 and to provide such additional information as may be required; and
 34
 35 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 36 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 37
 38 Section 1. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves the
 39 CDBG application for the creation of a City mature adult program and authorizes the
 40 appropriate City officials to execute it.
