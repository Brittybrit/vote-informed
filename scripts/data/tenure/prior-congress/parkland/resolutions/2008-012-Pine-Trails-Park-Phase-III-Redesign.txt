 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 4 FLORIDA, TO AUTHORIZE A DESIGN CHANGE TO PHASE III OF PINE TRAILS
 5 PARK; TO AUTHORIZE THE APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIAL TO TERMINATE
 6 THE CONTRACT FOR PHASE III WITH WEST CONSTRUCTION; AND
 7 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 8
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland is committed to providing first class leisure and cultural
 11 amenities;and
 12
 13 WHEREAS,the City of Parkland is committed to providing facilities responding to Parkland's
 14 community needs;and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland is committed to providing ample sports and athletic facilities
 17 for games and practices;and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes authorizing a design change to Phase
 20 III of Pine Trails Park is in the best interest of the citizens of Parkland;and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, The City of Parkland entered into a contract for the construction of Phase III of Pine
 23 Trails Park with West Construction on January 14,2008;and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, due to reduced revenue beyond the control of the City, it is necessary to terminate the
 26 West Construction contract and to redesign Phase III of Pine Trail Park;and
 27
 28 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 29 CITY OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA:
 30
 31 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as being true
 32 and correct and are hereby incorporated herein. All exhibits attached hereto are hereby incorporated
 33 herein.
 34
 35 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby authorizes a design
 36 change to Phase III of Pine Trails Park to include a multipurpose field,playground,pavilion and parking.
 37
 38 SECTION 3. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby wishes to terminate the
 39 contract with West Construction for construction of Phase III of Pine Trails Park.
 40
 41 SECTION 4. The appropriate City officials are hereby authorized and directed to execute the
 42 necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this resolution.
