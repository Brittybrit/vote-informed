 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-28
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AWARD THE BID
 FOR THE CITY OF PARKLAND TREE REPLACEMENT
 PROGRAM (RFP/DSD 2006-01) TO REPLACE CITY OF
 PARKLAND TREES LOST DURING HURRICANE WILMA,
 AND TO AUTHORIZE THE APPROPRIATE CITY
 OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE ATTACHED CONTRACT,
 AND CONTAINING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida, is authorized
 to award a contract for the City of Parkland Tree Replacement Program (RFP/DSD 2006-
 01); and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland received five (5) bids from the following
 bidders: Conco Horticultural Inc., Quinn Design, Inc. DBA Design Landscaping &
 Nursery, Superior Landscaping & Lawn Service, Inc., All Green Nursery Grower &
 Landscaper, Landscape Service Professionals, Inc., and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has fully reviewed the
 bids submitted and has determined that the bid from Superior Landscaping & Lawn
 Service, Inc., is the lowest responsible and responsive bid which best meets the criteria
 set forth in the bid packet and that, based upon the base bid price of $104,405.00 and
 other factors to be considered, it is in the best interest of the City to accept said bid.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herin.
 Section 2. That Superior Landscaping & Lawn Service, Inc., is hereby
 awarded a contract in the total amount of $104,405.00 for the City of Parkland Tree
 Replacement Program (RFP/DSD 2006-01).
 Section 3. The appropriate City Officials are hereby authorized and directed
 to execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this
 Resolution.
 Section 4. That the Funds shall be taken from the Disaster Clean-up Fund
 Line Item # 001.800.519 506.992.
