 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-26
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPOINTING THE FINANCE &
 ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES DIRECTOR AS THE
 REPRESENTATIVE OF THE GOVERNING BODY TO SERVE
 ON THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF SERMA, AND THE CITY
 MANAGER AS THE ALTERNATE, AND PROVIDING FOR AN
 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, on the 4th day of January, 1995, the City Commission of the City of
 Parkland approved that certain Interlocal Agreement establishing the South East Risk
 Management Association("SERMA"); and
 WHEREAS, Article VI a., thereof provides that the governing body of each
 municipality comprising SERMA shall appoint one person to represent the body on the
 Board of Directors for a specified term and another person to serve as an alternate;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials hereby appoint the Finance &
 Administrative Services Director as the representative of the governing body to serve on
 the Board of Directors of SERMA for an indefinite term and the City Manager as the
 Alternate.
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 7 DAY OF FEbruary , 2007.
