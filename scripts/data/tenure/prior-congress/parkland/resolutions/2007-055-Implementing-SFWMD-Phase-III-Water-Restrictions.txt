 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO DECLARE A WATER SHORTAGE
 5 EMERGENCY AND TO ASSIST IN THE IMPLEMENTATION AND
 6 ENFORCEMENT OF SFWMD PHASE III ORDER ENTITLED,
 7 "DECLARATION OF MODIFIED PHASE III WATER SHORTAGE
 8 RESTRICTIONS", ISSUED MAY 10, 2007 BY THE SOUTH
 9 FLORIDA WATER MANAGEMENT DISTRICT; PROVIDING FOR
 10 CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND
 11 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the South Florida Water Management District ("SFWMD")
 15 determines and declares a water shortage or determines that a water shortage
 16 emergency exists within the City of Parkland ("City"); and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland adopts said declaration and finds a water
 19 shortage (as defined in Section 11-86 of the City Code) to exist; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, during such water shortage conditions the amount of surface
 22 and ground water supplies may become insufficient to meet current or
 23 anticipated demands; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, upon the existence of such conditions it has become
 26 imperative to the publics well being that certain uses of water be restricted or
 27 curtailed and that available water resources be allocated; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, SFWMD has the primary responsibility for regulating water
 30 use and allocating available water supplies during periods of water shortage; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, on May 10, 2007 the SFWMD issued a Phase III water
 33 shortage order which became effective May 16, 2007; and
 34
 35 WHEREAS, SFWMD requested every city, county and appropriate
 36 governmental entities to assist in the implementation and enforcement of the
 37 water shortage area; and
 38
 39 WHEREAS, Section 11-88 of the Code of Ordinances of the City requires
 40 that the City Commission act by resolution to declare an emergency water
 41 shortage whenever SFWMD declares such a shortage or emergency: and
 42
 43 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines the water
 44 conservation measures to be necessary and in the best interest of the health,
 45 safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City; and
 46

 1 WHEREAS, violations of any provision of this Section 11-95 can be
 2 subject to a fine not to exceed five hundred dollars ($500.00) per incident; and
 3
 4
 5 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 6 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 7
 8 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 9 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein. All exhibits
 10 attached hereto are incorporated herein.
 11
 12 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby declares
 13 a water shortage emergency pursuant to Order of the South Florida Water
 14 Management District and shall assist the SFWMD in the implementation and
 15 enforcement of the Phase III Water Shortage Order. The provisions of said order
 16 are hereby declared to be binding and violations of said order shall be
 17 considered a violation of law punishable as set forth in section 11-95 of the City
 18 Code. A copy of the Order is hereby incorporated by reference as Exhibit A.
 19
 20 SECTION 3. The City Commission acknowledges that additional orders
 21 related to the water shortage may be forthcoming from the SFWMD and that the
 22 City shall take the necessary action to assist in the implementation of any further
 23 orders related to the water shortage.
 24
 25 SECTION 4. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are
 26 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 27
 28 SECTION 5. If any clause, section, other part or application of this
 29 Resolution is held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or
 30 invalid, in part of application, it shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions
 31 or applications of this Resolution.
 32
 33 SECTION 6. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 34 adoption.
