 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-62
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING A CHANGE ORDER IN THE
 AMOUNT OF $85,000 FOR THE PARKLAND CITY HALL RE-ROOF
 PROJECT (CITY CONTRACT # 2006-02); PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE
 DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City has executed a contract, pursuant to competitive bids, with
 Welling Construction Inc.,to install a new roof on City Hall; and
 WHEREAS,the contract price is $210,500; and
 WHEREAS, due to a scrivener's error in the reproduction of construction plans
 associated with this project,the cost of the roof replacement is increasing by $85,000; and
 WHEREAS, the bid of $210,500 shall still be the lowest responsible and
 responsive bid with this change order; and
 WHEREAS, it is of utmost importance to the health and safety of the persons
 occupying City Hall that the re-roof project be undertaken as soon as possible;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 SECTION 1. The above representations are true and correct and made a part hereof
 SECTION 2. The City Commission does hereby authorize a change order in the amount
 of $85,000 for the Parkland City Hall Re-Roof project (City Contract No. 2006-02) and
 authorizes the City Manager to execute a change order in that amount pursuant to the contract
 between the City and Welling Construction Inc..
 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective upon passage.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 7 day of MAY,, , 2006.
