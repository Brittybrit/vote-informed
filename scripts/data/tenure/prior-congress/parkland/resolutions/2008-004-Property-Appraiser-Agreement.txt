 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO AUTHORIZE THE
 5 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE AN
 6 AGREEMENT WITH BROWARD COUNTY PROPERTY

 14 WHEREAS, the City is required to create and maintain a fire assessment
 15 non-ad valorem assessment roll; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the City has utilized the Broward County Property Appraiser's
 18 Office to provide these services; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the Property Appraiser's Office has notified all cities that it is
 21 terminating any previous agreements due to increases in costs in providing the
 22 stated services; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best
 25 interest of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City
 26 of Parkland to continue to contract with the Property Appraiser's Office regarding
 27 the creation and implementation of a non-ad valorem assessment roll; and
 28
 29 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 30 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 31
 32 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 33 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits
 34 attached hereto are hereby incorporate herein.
 35
 36 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves
 37 the Agreement with Broward County Property Appraiser's Office relating to the
 38 creation and implementation of a fire assessment non-ad valorem assessment roll,
 39 a copy of which is attached hereto and incorporated by reference as if set forth in
 40 full.
 41
 42 SECTION 3. The appropriate City officials are hereby authorized and
 43 directed to execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent
 44 of this resolution.
