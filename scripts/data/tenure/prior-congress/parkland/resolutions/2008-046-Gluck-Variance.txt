 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA GRANTING IN PART AND DENYING IN
 5 PART A VARIANCE APPLICATION FOR PROPERTY LOCATED AT
 6 6061 N.W. 69TH WAY (CASE NO. 01-V-08).
 7
 8 WHEREAS, the owners of the property located at 6061 N.W. 69th Way
 9 (the Property) have applied for a variance to Sections 22-53 and 22-54 of the
 10 City of Parkland Code of Ordinances; and
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City Commission has heard all argument and evidence of
 13 the applicant and other information provided by staff at a public hearing; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the applicant has applied for variances with regard to the
 16 following matters:
 17
 18 ▪ A request for a 7 foot fence (fence variance).
 19
 20 A variance relative to the height of a light pole (light pole variance).
 21
 22 A variance related to the location of the shed (shed variance).
 23
 24 ▪ A variance with regard to the existing carport (carport variance).
 25
 26 A variance with regard to the location of the basketball court
 27 (basketball court variance);
 28
 29 WHEREAS, after a full hearing and consideration of all relevant evidence,
 30 the City Commission determined that with regard to the fence variance, the light
 31 pole variance, and the shed variance, there was no showing of an undue
 32 hardship and that any hardship was self created; and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, with regard to the carport variance and basketball court
 35 variance, the City Commission found that there was an undue hardship to
 36 support the variances, subject to the petitioner obtaining a valid building permit
 37 for both;
 38
 39 NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the City Commission of the City of
 40 Parkland, Florida, as follows:
 41
 42 Section 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct.
 43
 44 Section 2. Based upon the City Commission's findings as set for above,
 45 the City Commission finds that the applicant did not adequately demonstrate that
 46 it met the requirements for a variance under Sections 22-57 and Section 22-323
 47 of the City Code for the fence variance, the light pole variance and the shed
