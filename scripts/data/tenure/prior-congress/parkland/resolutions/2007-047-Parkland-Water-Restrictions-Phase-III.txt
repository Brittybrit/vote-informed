 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO DECLARE A WATER SHORTAGE
 5 EMERGENCY AND TO ASSIST IN THE IMPLEMENTATION AND
 6 ENFORCEMENT OF ORDER NO. 2007-034-DAO-WS,
 7 "DECLARATION OF MODIFIED PHASE I MODERATE WATER
 8 SHORTAGE RESTRICTIONS", ISSUED MARCH 15, 2007 BY
 9 THE SOUTH FLORIDA WATER MANAGEMENT DISTRICT;
 10 PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR
 11 SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the South Florida Water Management District ("SFWMD")
 15 determines and declares a water shortage or determines that a water shortage
 16 emergency exists within the City of Parkland ("City"); and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, during such water shortage conditions the amount of surface
 19 and ground water supplies may become insufficient to meet current or
 20 anticipated demands; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, upon the existence of such conditions it has become
 23 imperative to the publics well being that certain uses of water be restricted or
 24 curtailed and that available water resources be allocated; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, SFWMD has the primary responsibility for regulating water
 27 use and allocating available water supplies during periods of water shortage; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, on March 15, 2007 the SFWMD issued order 2007-034-DAO-
 30 WS which became effective March 22, 2007; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, SFWMD requested every city, county and appropriate
 33 governmental entities to assist in the implementation and enforcement of the
 34 water shortage area; and
 35
 36 WHEREAS, Section 11-88 of the Code of Ordinances of the City requires
 37 that the City Commission act by resolution to declare an emergency water
 38 shortage whenever SFWMD declares such a shortage or emergency; and
 39
 40 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines the water
 41 conservation measures to be in the best interest of the health, safety and welfare
 42 of the citizens and residents of the City; and
 43
 44 WHEREAS, violations of any provision of this Section 11-95 can be
 45 subject to a fine not to exceed five hundred dollars ($500.00) per incident; and
 46

 1 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 2 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 3
 4 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 5 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein. All exhibits
 6 attached hereto are incorporated herein.
 7
 8 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby declares
 9 a water shortage emergency pursuant to Order No. 2007-034-DAO-WS of the
 10 South Florida Water Management District and shall assist the SFWMD in the
 11 implementation and enforcement of the Water Shortage Order. The provisions of
 12 said order are hereby declared to be binding and violations of said order shall be
 13 considered a violation of law punishable as set forth in section 11-95 of the City
 14 Code. A copy of the Order is hereby incorporated by reference as Exhibit A.
 15
 16 SECTION 3. The City Commission acknowledges that additional orders
 17 related to the water shortage may be forthcoming from the SFWMD and that the
 18 City shall take the necessary action to assist in the implementation of any further
 19 orders related to the water shortage.
 20
 21 SECTION 4. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are
 22 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 23
 24 SECTION 5. If any clause, section, other part or application of this
 25 Resolution is held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or
 26 invalid, in part of application, it shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions
 27 or applications of this Resolution.
 28
 29 SECTION 6. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 30 adoption.
 31
 32 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 18 DAY OF April , 2007.
