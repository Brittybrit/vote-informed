 4 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 5 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE
 6 CITY OFFICIALS TO ADD CONTINUED SUPPORT TO
 7 AND PARTICIPATE IN THE STATE ROAD 7
 8 PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland joined the State Road 7 I U.S. 441
 11 Collaborative ("Collaborative") on May 16, 2001 through Resolution No. 2001-35; and
 12 WHEREAS, The City of Parkland's Comprehensive Plan encourages
 13 intergovernmental coordination; and
 14 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland's eastern border is State Road 7 / U.S. 441.
 15 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has been actively involved in the Collaborative
 16 by attending meetings and holding a charrette on the State Road 7 Master Plan; and
 17 WHEREAS, The City of Parkland has benefited from its involvement in the
 18 Collaborative; and
 19 WHEREAS, the Collaborative is seeking a revised agreement to signify the
 20 cooperative intent of the parties to leverage in-kind services, private funds, grant funds,
 21 foundation funds, and/or funds from the individual signatories to further the work of the
 22 Collaborative and to continue to share in the mutual benefits and rewards of the
 23 collaborative effort; and
 24 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 25 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 26 Section 1: The appropriate City Officials are hereby authorized to sign the
 27 State Road 7 Partnership Agreement to add support and participate in the State Road 7
 28 Partnership Agreement.
 29 Section 2: That this Resolution shall be in full force and effect immediately
 30 upon its passage and adoption.
 31
 32 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 7 day of November2007.
