 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-51
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPOINTING FIVE (5) REGULAR
 MEMBERS AND TWO AT LARGE MEMBERS AND TWO
 ALTERNATE AT LARGE MEMBERS TO THE PARKS &
 RECREATION ADVISORY BOARD.
 WHEREAS, in accordance with the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances, the
 City Commission shall appoint members to the Parks & Recreation Advisory Board.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That Mayor Udine, Vice Mayor Ribotsky, Commissioner
 McGilvray, Commissioner Moskowitz and Commissioner Smith hereby make their
 individual appointments to the Parks & Recreation Advisory Board as follows:
 Mayor Udine Howard Davis
 Vice Mayor Ribotsky Lynda J. Rusinowski
 deferred
 Commissioner McGilvray
 Commissioner Moskowitz Pete Manso
 Commissioner Smith Jerry E. Neptune
 Section 2. That the City Commission hereby appoints the following At Large
 and At Large Alternate positions as follows:
 At Large Pete Gado
 Tracy Koch
 At Large
 Alternate# 1 - At Large Jeb Niewood
 Alternate# 2 - At Large Stephen Wind
 Section 3. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 3 DAY OF NAY , 2006.
