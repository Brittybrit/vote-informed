 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-56
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE
 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE
 CONTRACT FOR THE SALE AND PURCHASE BETWEEN
 THE CITY OF PARKLAND AND TEP HOLDINGS, INC. FOR
 TRACT G, PARKLAND LAKES P.U.D.; PROVIDING FOR
 CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABLILITY; AND
 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, TEP Holdings, Inc. owns the property known as Tract G, Parkland
 Lakes P.U.D.; and
 WHEREAS, The City of Parkland is looking to purchase the property known as
 Tract G, Parkland Lakes P.U.D.; and
 WHEREAS, The property is to be added to Quigley Park and utilized as a passive park
 trailhead and overflow parking; and
 WHEREAS, TEP Holdings, Inc. is looking to sell Tract G, Parkland Lakes
 P.U.D. to the City of Parkland for $2.6 million; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best interest
 of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland to
 execute the Contract for Sale and Purchase of Tract G, Parkland Lakes P.U.D with TEP
 Holdings, Inc.; and
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND FLORIDA, THAT;
 Section 1: The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached hereto are
 hereby incorporate herein.
 Section 2: The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves the
 Contract between the City and TEP Holdings, Inc., a copy of which is attached hereto and
 incorporated by reference as if set forth in full.
 Section 3: The appropriate City officials are hereby authorized and directed to
 execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this resolution.
