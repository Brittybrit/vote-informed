 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-56A
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE
 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE
 AMENDED CONTRACT FOR THE SALE AND PURCHASE
 BETWEEN THE CITY OF PARKLAND AND TEP
 HOLDINGS, INC. FOR TRACT G, PARKLAND LAKES
 P.U.D.; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR
 SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE
 DATE.
 WHEREAS, TEP Holdings, Inc. owns the property known as Tract G, Parkland
 Lakes P.U.D.; and
 WHEREAS, The City of Parkland is looking to purchase the property known as
 Tract G, Parkland Lakes P.U.D.; and
 WHEREAS, The property is to be added to Quigley Park and utilized as a passive
 park trailhead and overflow parking; and
 WHEREAS, The Seller requested that the City of Parkland revise the Contract
 for Sale and Purchase to eliminate paragraph 18 in its entirety and to include an "as is"
 clause which releases them from liability; and
 WHEREAS, The City of Parkland requested that the Seller revise the Contract for
 Sale and Purchase to eliminate paragraph 13 in its entirety; and
 WHEREAS, TEP Holdings, Inc. is looking to sell Tract G, Parkland Lakes
 P.U.D. to the City of Parkland for an amended cost of$2,553,600.00; and
 WHEREAS, The City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best interest
 of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland to
 execute the Amended Contract for Sale and Purchase of Tract G, Parkland Lakes P.U.D
 with TEP Holdings, Inc.; and
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND FLORIDA, THAT;
 Section 1: The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached hereto are
 hereby incorporate herein.
