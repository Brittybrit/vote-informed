 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-101
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO AUTHORIZE THE
 5 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIAL TO ENTER INTO A
 6 LEASE AGREEMENT WITH THE YMCA OF BROWARD
 7 COUNTY TO OPERATE THE COMMUNITY CENTER
 8 LOCATED IN PINE TRAILS PARK; PROVIDING FOR
 9 CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND
 10 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland is committed to providing programs and
 13 facilities of outstanding quality; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland desires to provide wellness, family and
 16 community programs to residents; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, The YMCA is a nationally and worldwide recognized organization
 19 dedicated to providing programs of the highest quality for over 150 years; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the YMCA of Broward County is interested in providing
 22 programming and services for youth, teens, adults and seniors within the City's newly
 23 built Community Center; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, based upon the cost and uncertainty to operate the Community
 26 Center and the YMCA's expertise in such operations, the City Commission desires the
 27 YMCA to operate the City's newly build Community Center; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes signing this
 30 agreement is in the best interest of the citizens of Parkland; and
 31
 32 NOW, THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 33 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 34
 35 SECTION 1. The foregoing"whereas" clauses are confirmed and ratified as being
 36 true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein.
 37
 38 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves the
 39 Agreement between the City and YMCA of Broward County, a copy of which is attached
 40 hereto and incorporated by reference as if set forth in full.
 41
 42 SECTION 3. The appropriate City officials are hereby authorized and directed to
 43 execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this resolution.
 44
 45 SECTION 4. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
