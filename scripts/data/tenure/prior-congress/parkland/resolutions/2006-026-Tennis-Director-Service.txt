 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-26
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF AN
 INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT WITH KEN COLLINS
 AND NICHOLAS HALL FOR TENNIS SERVICES FOR THE PARKS
 AND RECREATION DEPARTMENT.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida, desires to hire
 and independent contractor to take on the responsibilities of the Tennis Director position
 for the City; and
 WHEREAS, Ken Collins and Nicholas Hall wish to enter into an Agreement with
 the City of Parkland to provide a comprehensive tennis program; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes entering into this
 Agreement is in the best interest of the citizens of Parkland; and
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 SECTION 1. That the appropriate City officials do hereby authorize the
 execution of an independent Contractor Agreement with Ken Collins and Nicholas Hall.
 SECTION 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 22 DAY OF March , 2006.
