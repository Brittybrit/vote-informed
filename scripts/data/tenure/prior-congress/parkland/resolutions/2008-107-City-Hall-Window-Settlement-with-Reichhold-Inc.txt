 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF

 10 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland purchased Jeld-Wen windows when constructing
 11 City Hall located at 6600 University Drive, Parkland, Florida; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, during the warranty period of the windows, the City noted that the resin
 14 in the windows were not working properly; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City notified Jeld-Wen of the issue and requested that the problem
 17 be corrected; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, Jeld-Wen was sued by various parties that suffered the same or similar
 20 damage that the City experienced; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, Reichhold, Inc. the resin manufacturer was then contacted by Jeld-
 23 Wen to honor its work; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, Jeld-Wen and Reichhold, Inc. were parties in a lawsuit which was
 26 settled; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, According to the lawsuit settlement, Reichhold Inc. assumed all
 29 responsibility for the warranty work required under the Jeld-Wen windows: and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, Reichhold, Inc. conducted a review of the City's windows and offered a
 32 settlement to the City; and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes it is in the City's best interest to accept
 35 the settlement rather than litigate the issue since the windows are causing damage to the
 36 City Hall building;
 37
 38 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 39 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 40
 41 SECTION 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and
 42 made a part hereof.
 43
 44 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves
 45 the Settlement Agreement with Reichhold, Inc., a copy of which is attached hereto
 46 and incorporated by reference as if set forth in full.
 47
 48 SECTION 3. The appropriate City officials are hereby authorized and directed
 49 to execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this
 50 resolution.
