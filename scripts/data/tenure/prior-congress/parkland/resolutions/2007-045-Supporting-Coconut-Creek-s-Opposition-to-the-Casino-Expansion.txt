 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-45
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 4 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 SUPPORTING THE CITY OF COCONUT CREEK'S
 6 OBJECTION TO THE SEMINOLE TRIBE'S
 7 APPLICATION FOR THE DESIGNATION OF AN
 8 ADDITIONAL 44 ACRES OF LAND AS BEING
 9 SOVEREIGN LAND
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the Seminole Tribe ("Tribe") has developed plans to
 12 transform the Seminole Casino Coconut Creek into a destination resort; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the Tribe's proposal includes a 24 story, 1,500 room hotel,
 15 160,000 square foot casino, 2,500 seat concert venue, and 150,000 square feet
 16 of shops, restaurants and offices; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the hotel would have three times as many rooms as the
 19 Seminole Hard Rock Hotel & Casino near Hollywood; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the complex's nine story parking garage would be five stories
 22 higher than the next tallest building in the City of Coconut Creek; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the proposed complex would require 5,000 employees and
 25 likely generate up to 20,000 visitors a day; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the Seminole Casino Coconut Creek site sits on five acres of
 28 sovereign land deeded to the Tribe two decades ago by Broward County in
 29 exchange for Hollywood tribal property used for road easements; and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, the Tribe opened the Casino in 2000 and has since bought
 32 up an additional forty-four (44) acres of surrounding land, which are subject to
 33 city, county and state regulations; and
 34
 35 WHEREAS, the Tribe submitted an application to the Bureau of Indian
 36 Affairs requesting that the 44 acres of surrounding land be declared sovereign so
 37 the land would not be subject to city, county and state regulations; and
 38
 39 WHEREAS, the City of Coconut Creek has objected over granting the
 40 Tribe the sovereignty since it was not provided sufficient time to evaluate its
 41 concerns with the Tribe's controls over such a massive development;
 42
 43
 44 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 45 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 46

<<PG>>

 2 objection and is opposed to the Tribe's application for designation of an
 3 additional forty-four acres of land to be sovereign land.
