 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-49
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPOINTING THREE (3)
 REGULAR MEMBERS AND TWO AT LARGE MEMBERS AND
 TWO ALTERNATE AT LARGE MEMBERS TO THE
 PLANNING& ZONING ADVISORY BOARD.
 WHEREAS, in accordance with Section 13-12 of the Code of Ordinances, the
 City Commission shall appoint members to the Planning & Zoning Advisory Board
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That Mayor Udine, Commissioner Moskowitz and Commissioner
 Smith hereby make their individual appointments to the Planning & Zoning Board as
 follows:
 Mayor Udine Lynne Fenoglio
 Commissioner Moskowitz Murray Zweig
 Commissioner Smith Larry S. Litow
 Section 2. That the City Commission hereby appoints the following At Large
 and At Large Alternate positions as follows:
 At Large David Frand
 At Large Mario Mangone
 Alternate # 1 - At Large Ryan Johnson
 Alternate #2 - At Large deferred
 Section 3. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 3 DAY OF MAY , 2006.
