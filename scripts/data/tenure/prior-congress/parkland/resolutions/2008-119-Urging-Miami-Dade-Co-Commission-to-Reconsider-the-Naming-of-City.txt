 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008- 119
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, URGING THE MIAMI DADE COUNTY
 5 COMMISSION TO RECONSIDER THE NAMING OF A NEW CITY
 6 IN MIAMI DADE COUNTY THE CITY OF PARKLAND
 7
 8 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland is a City located in Broward County chartered by the Florida
 9 Legislature in 1963 as a municipality; and
 10
 11 WHEREAS, since that time, the City of Parkland has been guided by leaders desiring to
 12 develop a small town community with open and green space, parks and a family atmosphere; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, these goals have been met by the City of Parkland and the City's leaders and
 15 residents have worked hard to develop a brand which represents high quality semi-rural living and a
 16 family atmosphere; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the citizens of the City of Parkland are proud of their City and the efforts of its
 19 leaders and citizens past and present to develop a quality municipality in Broward County; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission has learned that Miami Dade County is
 22 considering the creation of a City in Miami Dade County and naming that City Parkland; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has no desire to interfere with the home rule authority of Miami
 25 Dade County, but believes that it would be confusing and unfair for a neighboring county to create a
 26 City with the same name as the City of Parkland;
 27
 28 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 29 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 30
 31 SECTION 1. The City Commission of the City of Parkland respectfully requests that Miami Dade
 32 County reconsider the naming of any new municipality created as a City of Parkland or as a municipality
 33 which contains Parkland in its name.
 34
 35 SECTION 2. The City Clerk is authorized to provide copies of this Resolution to the Miami Dade
 36 County Commission, the Administrator for Miami Dade County, the Department of Community Affairs and
 37 any other agencies deemed appropriate.
 39
 40 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 19 day of November , 2008.
