 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-121
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND FLORIDA AUTHORIZING THE FIRST AMENDMENT TO THE
 5 2006-2007 FISCAL YEAR CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PLAN TO ESTABLISH
 6 A NEW PROJECT ENTITLED "UNIVERSITY DRIVE/HOLMBERG ROAD
 7 INTERSECTION BEAUTIFICATION"; AUTHORIZING THE CITY
 8 MANAGER, CITY ATTORNEY AND CITY CLERK TO TAKE THE STEPS
 9 NECESSARY TO IMPLEMENT THE INTENT OF THE PROJECT; AND
 10 AUTHORIZING THE REQUIRED BUDGET ADJUSTMENTS TO
 11 IMPLEMENT THE PROJECT.
 12
 13 WHEREAS,the City Commission has maintained a sustainable budget philosophy; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, on September 25, 2006, the City Commission adopted the budget at second
 16 hearing which included a Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) to address capital improvement projects
 17 proposed during the 2006-2007 fiscal year; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the City encountered delays in the construction of the safety element involving a
 20 new traffic signal at the intersection project at University Drive and Holmberg Road which was
 21 required as part of a development order to be completed by the Developer, WCI; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the safety element of the intersection project was the first of two phases of the
 24 public infrastructure improvement project and the Developer and City are both desirous of shifting
 25 the project management and control of the beautification element from the Developer to the City in
 26 order to avoid delays and lack of control; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the Developer has agreed to fully fund the beautification phase in honor of their
 29 prior commitment and City staff is recommending the full accountability of all revenue and
 30 expenditure activity associated with the project by establishing a new project entitled "University
 31 Drive-Holmberg Road Intersection Beautification"; and
 32
 33 WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to amend its Capital Improvement Plan to include
 34 the "University Drive-Holmberg Road Intersection Beautification" Project;
 35
 36 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 37 PARKLAND,FLORIDA:
 38
 39 SECTION 1. The 2006-2007 CIP for the City of Parkland is hereby amended to recognize $407,000
 40 of Developer funds toward the"University Drive-Holmberg Road Intersection Beautification" Project
 41 and to allocate sources and uses of revenues as shown in Exhibit A.
 42
 43 SECTION 2. The City Manager, City Attorney and City Clerk are authorized to implement, execute
 44 and record documents required to effectuate the intent of the project which shall be attached hereto upon
 45 their completion as follows:
 46 Exhibit B—Landscape/Hardscape Final Plan
 47 Exhibit C—WCI Letter of Understanding
 48 Exhibit D—Easement Agreement with Grand Cypress Estates HOA
 49 Exhibit E—Easement Agreement with The Landings HOA
