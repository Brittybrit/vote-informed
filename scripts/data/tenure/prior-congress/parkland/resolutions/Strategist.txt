 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-85
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING AN AGREEMENT
 BETWEEN THE CITY OF PARKLAND AND DUTKO POOLE
 McKINLEY TO PERFORM LOBBYIST/STRATEGIST
 SERVICES.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes the City requires
 representation before the 2006-2007 Legislature Session and any special sessions that
 may arise during the year; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes the City requires
 representation before the Broward and Palm Beach County Delegations and County
 Commissions as well as other governmental entities and private interests in regards to all
 annexation and local government matter; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes the City requires
 professional services to prepare legislation, meeting with elected officials and staff to
 further the City's position, and testifying before all appropriate groups as needed both
 locally and in Tallahassee.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes this agreement is in
 the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials do hereby approve the attached
 agreement between the City of Parkland and Dutko Poole McKinley.
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 4 DAY OF October , 2006.

 AGREEMENT BETWEEN
 CITY OF PARKLAND
 AND
 Dutko Poole McKinley
 THIS AGREEMENT made and entered into this 1 day of_October , 2006,
 by and between:
 CITY OF PARKLAND, a municipal corporation of the State of
 Florida, whose address is 6600 University Drive, Parkland, Florida
 33067, hereinafter referred to as "CITY-,
 and
 _Dutko Poole McKinley , whose principal place
 of business is located at _106 E. College Ave., Suite 1100,
 Tallahassee, FL 32309 , hereinafter referred to as "AGENT."
 WITNESSETH:
 1. SERVICES TO BE PERFORMED.
 A. AGENT shall perform services such as but not limited to:
 1. Representing the CITY before the Legislature and Executive Branch during
 the 2007-2008 Florida Legislative Session and any and all special sessions
 that may arise during the year.
 2. Representing the CITY in front of all appropriate legislative and executive
 entities as directed by City.
 3. Preparing legislation, meeting with elected officials and staff to further the
 CITY's position.

<<PG>>
 AGENT will represent those positions presented to it by the City Manager or City
 Attorney as they may be directed by the City Commission.
 B. AGENT shall maintain offices in Tallahassee. Professional staff in the
 Tallahassee location shall be available to attend meetings and represent positions of the CITY as
 they occur. The AGENT shall make available to the CITY various e-mail or other subscriptions
 to on-line legislative bill history services and statewide clipping services. AGENT shall fax or
 mail promptly to the CITY any articles from the statewide press that professional staff deems of
 interest to the CITY.
 2. COMPENSATION, PAYMENT SCHEDULE,EXPENSES.
 CITY agrees to pay AGENT consulting fees of Six Thousand Five Hundred Dollars
 ($6,500.00) monthly due and payable by the fifteenth day of the month commencing with the
 month of October 1, 2006. CITY also agrees to reimburse AGENT legislative expenses, i.e.:
 travel, entertainment, not to exceed Three-Hundred Dollars ($300.00) monthly. Appropriate
 receipts shall document all legislative expenses incurred with each monthly invoice submitted by
 AGENT.
 3. INDEMNITIES.
 In consideration of payment of the sum of $10.00 by the CITY to AGENT, receipt of
 which is hereby acknowledged, AGENT shall indemnify the CITY, its Officers, Agents and
 Employees and hold it harmless from and against any and all claims, liabilities, or damages
 arising from the services provided by AGENT under this agreement.

<<PG>>
 4. TERM OF AGREEMENT.
 The term of this agreement shall be for the twelve months, commencing October 1, 2006
 and terminating September 30, 2007, unless earlier terminated. The agreement may be extended
 (in writing)for an additional one-year period by the City Commission at its sole discretion.
 5. TERMINATION.
 This agreement may be terminated by CITY for convenience upon thirty(30) days notice
 to AGENT. AGENT may not terminate this agreement except for breach, after fifteen (15) days
 notice to CITY with an opportunity to cure. In the event of termination by CITY, CITY shall be
 liable to AGENT for the proportionate amount of any prior installment(s) due. Such amount
 shall be paid in full no later than ten days after the effective date of termination. In the event of
 termination by AGENT, AGENT shall be liable to CITY for the unearned portion of any
 advance payment made by the CITY of the most recent installment due, and such amount shall
 be refunded not later than ten days after the effective date of termination.
 6. NO CONTINGENCY AGREEMENT.
 AGENT warrants that they have not employed or retained any company or person, other
 than a bona fide employee working solely for AGENT, to solicit or secure this agreement and
 that it has not paid or agreed to pay any person, company, corporation, individual or firm, other
 bona fide employee working solely for AGENT any fee, commission, percentage, gift, or other
 consideration contingent upon or resulting from the award or making of this agreement. For the
 breach or violation of this provision, CITY shall have the right to terminate the agreement
 without liability at its discretion, to deduct from the contract price, or otherwise recover, the full
 amount of such fee, commission,percentage, gift or consideration.

<<PG>>
 7. ETHICS.
 AGENT shall comply with any State and Local ethics laws as may be applicable. If any
 other employment relationship of agent could be deemed to create a conflict of interest, or the
 appearance of a conflict of interest, Agent shall immediately notify CITY.
 8. NOTICE.
 All notices required herein shall be in writing and either hand delivered or mailed
 certified, return receipt requested, to the following person at the address listed unless changed by
