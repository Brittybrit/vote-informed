 12 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of
 13 Parkland, Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida
 14 Statutes, and the Code of Ordinances, is authorized and empowered to consider petitions
 15 related to zoning and land development orders; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in Code of
 18 Ordinances, as adopted by the City of Parkland, have been satisfied; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the subject site has been approved on the City of Parkland's Future
 21 Land Use Map as Commercial and possesses a zoning designation of Business -2; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the proposed Special Exception use was reviewed by the City of
 24 Parkland's Planning and Zoning Board at a public hearing conducted on April 20, 2006
 25 and it recommended approval of the petition; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony
 28 presented by the Applicant and other interested parties and the recommendations of the
 29 various City of Parkland review agencies and staff; and
 30
 31 WHEREAS,the City Commission has made the following findings of fact:
 32
 33 1. Surrounding Use Considerations: The proposed 4,465 square foot
 34 Friendly's Restaurant will not affect the surrounding properties since the restaurant will
 35 be located adjacent to the State Road 7 and away from any residential developments and
 36 will be heavy screened by a buffer and berm, which will exceed code requirements.
 37
 38 2. Access, traffic generation and road capacity: The proposed 4,465 square
 39 foot Friendly's Restaurant will not result in the adjacent roadways being over capacity
 40 and the traffic generation is similar in characteristics of other uses permitted in the same
 41 zoning district.
 42
 43 3. Utilities: The proposed 4,465 square foot Friendly's Restaurant can be
 44 serviced by the service providers of water, sanitary sewer, fire, police and drainage
 45 systems.

 5 NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the City Commission of the City of
 6 Parkland, Florida, that;
 7
 8 SECTION 1. The Special Exception Case No. 01-SE-06, the petition of NL Ark
 9 Development, LLC requesting a Special Exception to allow the construction of a 4,465
 10 square foot Friendly's Restaurant on the described real property described in Exhibit A, is
 11 hereby approved subject to the conditions of approval contained herein, which are in
 12 addition to the general requirements otherwise provided by ordinance:
 13
 14 1. This approval is based on a Site Plan entitled " Friendly's Restaurant" prepared by
 15 Keith and Schnars, P.A., dated April 14, 2006 and received by the Planning and
 16 Zoning Department on May 22,2006.
 17
 18 2. This approval is limited to a 4,465 square foot Friendly's Restaurant; should
 19 petitioner desire any other type of use, a new Special Exception application must be
 20 reviewed and approved by the City Commission.
 21
 22 3. The hours of operation for the 4,465 square foot Friendly's Restaurant shall be
 23 limited to Sunday through Thursday, 7:00 am to 11:00 pm. and Friday and Saturday
 24 7:00 am to midnight.
 25
 26 3. The subject parcel is part of a larger development approved in Resolution 2000-
 27 56 and known as the Waterways Shopping Center ("Resolution"). The property
 28 subject to this approval is therefore part of a larger parcel and shall be subject to the
 29 requirements related to the whole parcel to the extent set forth in the Resolution.
 30 Further, the requirements set forth in the site plan and plat approval for the subject
 31 parcel or the larger parcel shall apply to this Resolution. The subject property may
 32 not be developed in a manner inconsistent with the Resolution.
 33
 34 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
