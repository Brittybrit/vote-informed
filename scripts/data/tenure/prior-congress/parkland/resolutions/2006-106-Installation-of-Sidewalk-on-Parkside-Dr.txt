 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-106
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION
 4 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO
 6 PIGGYBACK A CITY OF WESTON
 7 CONTRACT WITH TENEX ENTERPRISES
 8 INC. FOR THE PURPOSE OF INSTALLING AN
 9 8' WIDE CONCRETE SIDEWALK ALONG THE
 10 EAST SIDE OF PARKSIDE DRIVE IN THE
 11 AMOUNT OF $317,652.00, PROVIDING AN
 12 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 13
 14
 15 WHEREAS, monies were budgeted in the City of Parkland Fiscal Year
 16 2006 Budget for the purpose of installing an 8' wide sidewalk along the east side
 17 of Parkside Drive as part of the City's Multi-Use Trail Plan; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, Staff has identified a piggybacking opportunity to install the
 20 sidewalk utilizing a City of Weston contract with Tenex Enterprises, Inc; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes the installation
 23 of the sidewalk is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 24
 25 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 26 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 27
 28 SECTION 1. The City Manager is hereby authorized to piggyback
 29 the City of Weston contract with Tenex Enterprises, Inc. for the purpose of
 30 installing a sidewalk along Parkside Drive in the amount of $317,652.00.
 31
 32 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 33 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 34
 35 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 15 DAY OF November , 2006.
