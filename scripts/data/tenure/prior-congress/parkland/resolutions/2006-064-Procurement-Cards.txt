 1 RESOLUTION NO.2006-64
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO AUTHORIZE THE CITY OFFICIALS
 5 TO ENTER INTO A PROCUREMENT CARD AGREEMENT
 6 WITH SUNTRUST BANK AND EXECUTE DOCUMENTS
 7 NECESSARY TO IMPLEMENT THE PROGRAM.
 8
 9 WHEREAS, City staff believes it would be in the best interest of the City to have
 10 the ability to purchase goods/services and pay bills through a procurement card system;
 11
 12 WHEREAS, City staff has investigated the various institutions that offer such a
 13 program to local municipalities;
 14
 15 WHEREAS, City staff has determined that SunTrust Bank offers the best terms to
 16 the City of Parkland;
 17
 18 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 19 THE CITY OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 20
 21 Section 1. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves staff to
 22 enter into a procurement card program and authorizes the appropriate City officials to
 23 execute the necessary documents with SunTrust Bank to implement a procurement card
 24 system.
 25
 26 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
 27
 28
 29 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 5 DAY OF JULY 2006.
