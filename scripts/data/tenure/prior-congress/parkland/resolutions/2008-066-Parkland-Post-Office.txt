 1 RESOLUTION NO.2008-66
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SUPPORTING THAT A POST
 5 OFFICE OR POST OFFICE TYPE FACILITY BE LOCATED
 6 WITHIN THE CITY'S MUNICIPAL BOUNDARIES AND TO
 7 NAME IT AFTER PFC. DANIEL AGAMI OR IF A NEW
 8 FACILITY CANNOT BE BUILT THAT AN EXISTING POST
 9 OFFICE OR POST OFFICE TYPE FACILITY BE NAMED
 10 AFTER PFC.DANIEL AGAMI
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has grown in population from 9,000 residents in 1990 to
 13 over 23,000 in 2008; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the need of a Post Office to service the increased growth of Parkland's
 16 population is necessary; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, Pfc. Daniel Agami, who was a resident of Parkland, gave his life in Iraq to
 19 keep the United States safe; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland feels that it would be an appropriate honor to name a
 22 Post Office or Post Office type facility after Pfc. Daniel Agami to recognize his sacrifice; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland and the City Commission desire the United States Postal
 25 Services to locate a Post Office or Post Office type facility within its municipal limits; and
 26
 27 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 28 CITY OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA,THAT:
 29
 30 SECTION 1. The City of Parkland hereby requests the United States Postal Service to
 31 locate a Post Office or Post Office type facility within the municipal limits of the City of Parkland
 32 and for the Post Office facility to be named after Pfc. Daniel Agami, who gave his life in Iraq to
 33 ensure the safety of the United States. If it is determined that a Post Office facility cannot be located
 34 within the City's municipal boundaries then we request the United States Postal Service to name an
 35 existing Post Office facility in a nearby jurisdiction for Pfc. Daniel Agami.
 36
 37 SECTION 2. The City Clerk is directed to forward this Resolution to the United States
 38 Post Master General, Congressman Robert Wexler and Congressman Ron Klein.
 39
 40 SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately upon
 41 its passage and adoption.
