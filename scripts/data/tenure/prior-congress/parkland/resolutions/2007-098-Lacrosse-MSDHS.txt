 1 RESOLUTION NO.2007-98
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA SUPPORTING THE REQUEST FOR A
 5 CHANGE IN STATUS OF THE MARJORY STONEMAN
 6 DOUGLAS LACROSSE CLUB TO A VARSITY PROGRAM.
 7
 8 WHEREAS, the Marjory Stoneman Douglas Lacrosse Club ("Club") was created on
 9 October 28, 2004, as a booster club for the purpose of sponsoring and supporting the sport of
 10 lacrosse of both boys and girls at Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School ("School"); and
 11
 12 WHEREAS, at the time the Club was formed, lacrosse was not yet a recognized sport
 13 by the Broward County Public Schools; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the Club has been self supporting and has assisted with the development of
 16 the Parkland Redhawks Youth Lacrosse Program; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the Parkland Redhawks Youth Lacrosse Program has grown to almost three
 19 hundred(300)participants; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, both the Club and the School are desirous of having School lacrosse teams
 22 considered varsity rather than club teams; and
 23
 24 NOW, THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 25 OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 26
 27 SECTION 1. The City of Parkland supports the change in status of Marjory Stoneman
 28 Douglas lacrosse program from club to varsity.
 29
 30 SECTION 2. The City Clerk is hereby directed to forward this Resolution to Ann
 31 Kowalski, Principal of Stoneman Douglas High School, Dave Grad, Athletic Director of Stoneman
 32 Douglas High School and Damian Huttenhoff,Broward County Athletic Association Commissioner.
 33
 34 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately upon its passage
 35 and adoption.
