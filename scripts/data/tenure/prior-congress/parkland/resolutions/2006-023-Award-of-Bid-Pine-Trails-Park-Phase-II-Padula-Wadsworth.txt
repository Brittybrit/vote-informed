 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-23
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING CITY
 OFFICIALS TO AWARD A CONTRACT FOR THE PINE
 TRAILS PARK PHASE 2 CONSTRUCTION (PROJECT
 #2005-02) TO PADULA & WADSWORTH CONSTRUCTION,
 INC., CONTAINING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida, is authorized
 to award a contract for Pine Trails Park Phase 2 Construction (City Contract #2005-02);
 and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland received three (3) bids from the following
 bidders: Padula & Wadsworth Construction, Inc.; The Tower Group, Inc.; and The
 Redland Company, Inc.; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has fully reviewed the
 bids submitted and has determined that the bid of Padula & Wadsworth Construction,
 Inc., is the lowest responsible and responsive bid which best meets the criteria set forth in
 the bid packet and that, based upon the base bid price of$10,480,000 and other factors to
 be considered, it is in the best interest of the City to accept said bid.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That Padula & Wadsworth Construction, Inc., is hereby awarded a
 contract in the total amount of $10,480,000.00 for the construction of Pine Trails Park
 Phase 2 onstruction(Project#2005-02).
 Section 2. That the Funds shall be taken from the Pine Island Park Project,
 line item # 305-800573-506195.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 1 DAY OF MARCH ,2006:
