 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SUPPORTING THE
 5 BROWARD COUNTY EXTENSION EDUCATION DIVISION;
 6 AND URGING THE BROWARD COUNTY BOARD OF
 7 COMMISSIONERS TO CONTINUE ITS PARTNERSHIP
 8 WITH THE UNIVERSITY OF FLORIDA NECESSARY TO
 9 FUND THE EFFORTS OF THE EXTENSION EDUCATION
 10 DIVISION; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING
 11 FOR SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the Broward County Extension Education Division was formed
 14 through a partnership between Broward County Government and the University of Florida to
 15 provide research-based information to Broward County residents and businesses for
 16 improved quality of life and business profitability; and
 17 WHEREAS, the Extension Education Division is responsible for administering
 18 necessary and valued education programs such as the Broward County Tree Trimmer
 19 Licensing Program, training and testing for the Pest Control industry, training in Best
 20 Management Practices (BPMs) for the Green industry, and the Master Gardener Volunteer
 21 Program; and
 22 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has directly benefited from the extensive services
 23 provided by the Broward County Extension Education Division, including but not limited to
 24 the above mentioned programs; and
 25 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland supports the efforts of the Broward County
 26 Extension Education Division;
 27 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 28 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 29
 30 SECTION 1: That the City Commission hereby declares its support of the
 31 Broward County Extension Education Division and their efforts to improve the quality of
 32 life for Broward County residents through valued educational programming aimed at
 33 promoting environmental consciousness and developing family, business, and
 34 community.
 35
 36 SECTION 2: That the City Clerk is hereby authorized and directed to forward
 37 true copies of this Resolution to the Broward County Board of Commissioners and any
 38 other interested persons or agencies having interest or jurisdiction over the matter.
