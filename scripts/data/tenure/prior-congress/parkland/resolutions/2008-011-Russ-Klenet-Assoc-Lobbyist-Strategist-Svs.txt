 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO EXTEND FOR ONE
 5 YEAR AND TO AUTHORIZE THE ASSIGNMENT OF THE
 6 DUTKO POOLE MCKINLEY AGREEMENT TO RUSS
 7 KLENET AND ASSOCIATES FOR PROFESSIONAL
 8 SERVICES RELATED TO LOBBYIST/STRATEGIST
 9 SERVICES; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING

 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that the City requires
 14 representation before the State Legislature, Broward County Commission as well
 15 as other governmental entities and private interests in regards to all annexation
 16 and local government matters; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that the City requires
 19 professional services to prepare legislation, meet with elected officials and staff
 20 to further the City's position and testify before all appropriate groups as needed
 21 both locally and in Tallahassee; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the City Commission entered into an agreement with Dutko
 24 Poole McKinley to perform these services on October 1, 2006; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the Dutko Poole McKinley has requested that their
 27 agreement be assigned to Russ Klenet and Associations; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best
 30 interest of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City
 31 of Parkland to extend its agreement with Dutko Poole McKinley for one (1) year
 32 and to assign the agreement to Russ Klenet and Associates;
 33
 34 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 35 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 36
 37 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 38 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein. All exhibits
 39 attached hereto are hereby incorporated herein.
 40
 41 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves
 42 the extension of agreement between the City and Dutko Poole McKinley and to
 43 assign the agreement to Russ Klenet and Associates.
 44
 45 SECTION 3. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are
 46 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
