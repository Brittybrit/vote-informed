 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-87
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AWARD THE BID
 FOR THE CITY OF PARKLAND TERNBRIDGE PRESERVE
 REPLANTING PROJECT (RFP NO. DSD 2007-11) FOR THE
 REPLANTING OF 145 NATIVE TREES IN THE
 TERNBRIDGE PRESERVE ON PARKSIDE DRIVE, AND TO
 AUTHORIZE THE APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO
 EXECUTE A CONTRACT, AND CONTAINING AN
 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida, is authorized
 to award the bid for the City of Parkland Ternbridge Preserve Replanting Project (RFP
 No. DSD 2007-11); and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland received seven (7) qualified bids from the
 following bidders: Broward Landscape, Inc, Lamaco Enterprises, Inc., Conco
 Horticultural Inc., Maia Lawn & Land. Services, Inc., Superior Land. & Lawn Service,
 Inc., Stiles Landscape Co. and Mainguy Industries, Inc.; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has fully reviewed the
 bids submitted and has determined that the bid from Broward Landscape, Inc. is the
 lowest responsible and responsive bid which best meets the criteria set forth in the bid
 packet and that, based upon the base bid price of $28,800.00 and other factors to be
 considered, it is in the best interest of the City to accept said bid.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herin.
 Section 2. That Broward Landscape, Inc. is hereby awarded the bid in the
 total amount of $28,800.00 for the City of Parkland Ternbridge Preserve Replanting
 Project(RFP No. DSD2007-11).
 Section 3. The appropriate City Officials are hereby authorized and directed
 to execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this
 Resolution.
 Section 4. That the Funds shall be taken from the Public Property
 Beautification Arbor Day Capital Improvements Line Item# 305 701.551 505.350.
