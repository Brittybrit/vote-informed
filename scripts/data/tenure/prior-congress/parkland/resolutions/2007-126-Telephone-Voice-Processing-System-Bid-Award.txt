 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-126
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO RANK THE FOUR (4) SHORT
 5 LISTED FIRMS TO PROVIDE A TELEPHONE AND VOICE
 6 PROCESSING SYSTEM; AND TO AUTHORIZE THE
 7 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIAL TO NEGOTIATE A CONTRACT
 8 WITH THE FIRST RANKED FIRM; PROVIDING FOR
 9 SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, City staff advertised an RFP for the purpose of awarding a contract to
 12 provide The City of Parkland with a Telephone and Voice Processing System; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, a selection committee consisting of the Finance and Administrative
 15 Services Director, Information Systems Manager, Parks and Recreation Director, Library
 16 Director, City Clerk and Premit Technician, have reviewed the 10 submittals with
 17 presentations; and selected the top four (4) ranked companies; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, upon final scoring and ranking, the City Commission authorizes the
 20 appropriate City Official to negotiate a contract with the first ranked firm to provide the
 21 aforementioned services; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, if the City Manager or her designee is unable to negotiate such a
 24 contract with the first ranked company, the City Manager is authorized to negotiate with the
 25 next ranked company until a contract is approved by the City Commission; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best interest of
 28 the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland for the
 29 City Manager to enter into such a contract;
 30
 31 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 32 PARKLAND, FLORIDA THAT;
 33
 34 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as being
 35 true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached hereto are hereby
 36 incorporate herein.
 37
 38 SECTION 2. The City Commission hereby ranks the four (4) short listed companies.
 39
 40 SECTION 3. The City Manager is hereby authorized and directed to negotiate a
 41 contract for Telephone and Voice Processing systems with the top ranked firm and if unable to
 42 do so negotiate a contract with the next ranked firm until a contract is approved.
 43
 44 SECTION 4. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are hereby
 45 repealed to the extent of such conflict.
