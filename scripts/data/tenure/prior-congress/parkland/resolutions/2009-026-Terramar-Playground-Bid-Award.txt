City of Parkland
InterOffice Memorandum
To: Caryn Gardner - Young, City Manager Date: April 7, 2009
Thru: James Berkman, Director of Public Services
From: Horacio Danovich, Engineering Projects Manager RE: Terramar Park Playground
Project
Issue:
Award RFP No. 2009 -05 to Leadex Corporation, for the purpose of designing and constructing a
new playground at Terramar Park in the sum of $164,925.00.
Recommendation /Action:
Award RFP No. 2009 -05 to Leadex Corporation.
Backeround:
Staff submitted prior to the 2008 -2009 budget a Florida Recreation Development Assistance
Program (FRDAP) grant to perform repairs and replacements at. Terramar Park. Included in the
grant was the replacement of the 5 -year to 12 -year old playground. The playground needs to be
replaced immediately as Staff cannot obtain required parts (equipment is so old manufacturer
does not make them anymore) and the playground equipment grows smaller and smaller as areas
have to be closed due to lack of parts. The grant is for $200,000.
The Terramar Park Playground Project entails providing and installing all play equipment, shade
structures, surfacing material, landscaping, removal and disposal of existing playground
equipment and other necessary improvements to complete a new playground at Terramar Park
pursuant to the drawings and specifications provided to the applicants.
The specifications require acceptance of a qualified proposal within sixty (60) days of receipt of
proposals and the applicants are ranked using the following criteria: price (40 points), expertise
of designated staff (10 points), proposed design (30 points), firm's credentials (10 points), and
past performance (10 points).
Initially, the project was advertised on November 16, 2008 as RFP No. 2008 -14. For this RFP,
the City received five (5) qualified proposals. At the time, the lowest proposal was submitted by
ARC Florida for $158,775.60. As per the specifications, the City needed to make a determination
as to either accept a proposal or reject all proposals within sixty (60) days of the deadline for
receipt of proposals. Due to financially - related concerns, the City was unable to award a contract
within sixty (60) days of receiving the proposals. As a result, the bids were officially rejected via
Resolution 200946. With the rejection, Staff hoped that the bid amounts would be lowered and
with the introduction of new play equipment that there would be better proposals. Additionally,
