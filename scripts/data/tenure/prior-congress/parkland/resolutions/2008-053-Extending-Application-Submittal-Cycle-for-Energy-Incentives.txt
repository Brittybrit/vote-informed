 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-53
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO EXTEND THE APPLICATION
 5 SUBMITTAL CYCLE FOR THE ENERGY INCENTIVES
 6 PROGRAM FOR ENVIRONMENTALLY CONSCIOUS
 7 PURCHASES PER ORDINANCE 2008-17; PROVIDING FOR
 8 CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND
 9 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has always taken the lead in preservation of the
 13 environment and wishes to continue to do so as the city, county, state and nation face
 14 mounting issues with regard to energy and the environment; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City believes that by encouraging environmentally conscious
 17 activities within the City of Parkland, not only the citizens of Parkland will benefit, but
 18 the citizens of our state and nation will receive a benefit; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the City Commission adopted an energy incentive program through
 21 Ordinance 2007-29; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the 2008 application submittal cycle has ended for this program;
 24 and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that by extending the application
 27 submittal cycle that additional Parkland residents and businesses will apply for the
 28 energy incentive program; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to extend the 2008 and possibly future
 31 application submittal cycle to provide the longest possible opportunity for Parkland
 32 residents and businesses to undertake environmentally conscious activities and
 33 purchases; and
 34
 35 WHEREAS, Section 3 of Ordinance 2007-29 codified in Section 4.5 of the
 36 City's Code of Ordinance has been amended providing that the City Commission may
 37 amend the application submittal date deadline by resolution; and
 38
 39
 40 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 41 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 42
 43 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 44 being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached hereto are
 45 hereby incorporate herein.

 1 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves
 2 Resolution 2008-53 to extend the application submittal cycle for the Energy Incentives
 3 Program for environmentally conscious purchases to close as of business on July 31, 2008.

 10 application, it shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions or applications of this
 11 Resolution.
 12
 13 SECTION 5. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
