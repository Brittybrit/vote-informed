 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-18
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE EXPENDITURE OF
 5 UP TO $750.00 FOR A NOISE METER TRAINING SEMINAR;
 6 PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR
 7 SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 8
 9
 10 WHEREAS, The Law Enforcement Trust Fund for the City of Parkland is
 11 to provide funds for Law Enforcement purposes; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, in order to enforce the City of Parkland's noise ordinance
 14 regulations, City Staff and the Broward Sheriff's Office need training on use of
 15 the City's sound noise meter; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, this purpose conforms with the State Statute for Law
 18 Enforcement Trust Funds and is in the best interest of the City of Parkland; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the City's Chief of Police recommends this expenditure as
 21 necessary for law enforcement purposes;
 22
 23 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 24 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 25
 26 SECTION 1. The above findings are true and correct and made a part
 27 hereof.
 28
 29 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby
 30 authorizes the expenditure of up to $750.00 for a Sound Level Meter Training
 31 Seminar from the Law Enforcement Trust Fund proceeds. The City Manager is
 32 authorized to take all necessary actions to effectuate the purpose and intent of this
 33 resolution.
 34
 35 SECTION 3. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are
 36 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 37
 38 SECTION 4. If any clause, section, other part or application of this
 39 Resolution is held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or
 40 invalid, in part of application, it shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions
 41 or applications of this Resolution.
 42
 43 SECTION 5. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 44 adoption.
