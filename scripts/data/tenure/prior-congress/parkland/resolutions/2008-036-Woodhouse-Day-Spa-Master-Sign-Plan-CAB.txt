 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, FOR COMMUNITY
 5 APPEARANCE BOARD APPROVAL FOR THE
 6 MODIFICATION TO THE MASTER SIGN PROGRAM FOR
 7 THE BAKER AND ZIMMERMAN OFFICE BUILDING
 8 LOCATED AT 6991 N. STATE ROAD 7, CASE NUMBER 01-
 9 CAB-08.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 12 Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the
 13 Code of Ordinance is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and
 14 land development orders; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in the Code of
 17 Ordinances have been satisfied; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the parcel possesses a Future Land Use Designation of Commercial ("C")
 20 with a zoning designation of Broward County Business District; ("BCB-3"); and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the petitioner desires to modify the master sign plan for the Baker and
 23 Zimmerman Office Building located at 6991 N. State Road 7; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City Code of Ordinances for the City of Parkland requires the petitioner
 26 to obtain Community Appearance Board approval when proposing to modify an existing master
 27 sign plan; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Board on April 10, 2008 voted to recommend
 30 approval of the petition by a 6-1 vote; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony
 33 presented by the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of the
 34 various City of Parkland review agencies and staff;
 35
 36 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 37 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 38
 39 SECTION 1. The Community Appearance Board Application of Woodhouse Day Spa for
 40 the modification to the existing master sign plan for the Baker and Zimmerman Office Building
 41 for the addition of two (2) wall signs for Woodhouse Day Spa is hereby DENIED based upon
 42 failure to comply with the requirements of original site plan approval and applicant's
 43 representations and unsatisfactory color schemes (Code Section 13-79.4).
 44
 46 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
 47
 48 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 7th day of May , 2008.
