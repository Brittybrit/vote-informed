 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA FOR APPROVAL OF
 5 AN AMENDMENT TO THE PREVIOUSLY APPROVED
 6 SITE PLAN FOR PARKLAND VILLAGE SQUARE TO
 7 ALLOW THE SITE PARKING TO BE CALCULATED
 8 BASED UPON SHARED PARKING GUIDELINES; CASE
 9 NUMBER 01-SPS-07.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of
 12 Parkland, Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166,
 13 Florida Statutes, and the Code of Ordinances, as adopted by the City of Parkland,
 14 is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and land
 15 development orders; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in the Code
 18 of Ordinances, as adopted by the City of Parkland, have been satisfied; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, a Future Land Use Designation of C (Commercial) with a
 21 compatible PCD (Planned Commercial Development District) Zoning Designation
 22 was assigned to the subject property; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, a Site Plan permitting seventy (70) age restricted condominium
 25 units, 44,966 square feet of commercial with ten (10) dwelling units, twelve (12)
 26 acres for a school site, and one hundred seventy seven (177) single family
 27 dwelling units was approved by the City Commission on July 6, 2005 through
 28 Resolution 2005-42; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, on December 13, 2007, the Planning and Zoning Board
 31 reviewed said petition and voted 5-1 to recommend approval of the modifications
 32 sought by the applicant, subject to specified conditions; and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and
 35 testimony presented by the Petitioner and other interested parties and the
 36 recommendations of the various City of Parkland review agencies and staff.
 37
 38 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 39 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 40
 41 SECTION 1. The Site Plan Amendment of the Parkland Village Green, which legal
 42 description is described in Exhibit A, is hereby AMENDED with the following
 43 conditions:
 44
 45 1. The uses are restricted to those identified on the site plan associated with
 46 Resolution Number 2007-129.

 1 2. As it relates to fast food restaurants, the property owner shall limit the
 2 leased space to restaurants that have a limited product line excluding a
 3 McDonalds, Burger King and the like.
 4 3. If the uses identified in the shared parking study substantially change as
 5 determined by the City, the City shall require an updated shared parking
 6 study to be reviewed by staff, the City's traffic consultant, at the
 7 applicants expense, and approved by the City Commission.
 8 4. There shall be sufficient parking reserved for the required 10,000 square
 9 feet (maximum) of restaurant building(s) in Phase II. The parking
 10 reservation shall remain in full force until such time as Phase II has
 11 obtained site plan approval.
 12 5. Provide clarification in the shared parking study by indicating "Medical"
 13 refers to "Medical Office".
 14
 15 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 16 adoption.
