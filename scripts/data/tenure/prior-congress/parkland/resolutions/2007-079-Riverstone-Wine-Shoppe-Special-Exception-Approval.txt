 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, FOR SPECIAL EXCEPTION APPROVAL
 5 OF THE RIVERSTONE WINE SHOPPE, LOCATED AT THE
 6 SOUTHWEST CORNER OF STATE ROAD 7 AND MECCA
 7 BOULEVARD, CASE NO. 01-SE-07.
 8
 9 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of
 10 Parkland, Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166,
 11 Florida Statutes, and the Code of Ordinances, is authorized and empowered to
 12 consider petitions related to zoning and land development orders; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in the Code
 15 of Ordinances, as adopted by the City of Parkland, have been satisfied; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the subject site has been approved on the City of Parkland's
 18 Future Land Use Map as Commercial and possesses a zoning designation of
 19 Business 2 (B-2); and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the proposed Special Exception use was reviewed by the City
 22 of Parkland's Planning and Zoning Board at a public hearing conducted on June
 23 14, 2007 and it recommended approval of the petition by a 4-2 vote; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and
 26 testimony presented by the Applicant and other interested parties and the
 27 recommendations of the various City of Parkland review agencies and staff; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the City Commission has made the following findings of fact:
 30
 31 1. Surrounding Use Considerations: The proposed 2,200 square foot
 32 wine and cheese store will not affect the surrounding properties since the
 33 proposed use will be located entirely within a building previously approved for the
 34 Riverstone Shopping Center which contains appropriate buffers and setbacks to
 35 address any adverse impacts.
 36
 37 2. Access, traffic generation and road capacity: The proposed 2,200
 38 square foot wine and cheese store will not result in the adjacent roadways being
 39 over capacity and the traffic generation is similar in characteristics of other uses
 40 permitted in the same zoning district.
 41
 42 3. Utilities: The proposed 2,200 square foot wine and cheese store
 43 can be serviced by the service providers of water, sanitary sewer, fire, police and
 44 drainage systems.
 45
 46 4. Comprehensive Plan Considerations: The proposed 2,200 square
 47 foot wine and cheese store conforms to and complies with the stated goals,
 48 objectives, policies and land uses established by the City's Comprehensive Plan.

 1 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 2 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 3
 4 SECTION 1. The Special Exception Case No. 01-SE-07, the petition of
 5 Coral Management Association (DBA Riverstone Wine Shoppe) requesting a
 6 Special Exception to allow a proposed 2,200 square foot wine and cheese store
 7 within Bay 7391 of Building C within the Riverstone Shoppes is hereby approved
 8 subject to the conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition to the
 9 general requirements otherwise provided by ordinance:
 10
 11 1. This approval is based on a Site and Master Plan entitled "Riverstone
 12 Shoppes formerly Millennium Plaza" prepared by Synalovski Gutierrez
 13 Architects, Inc.
 14
 15 2. This approval is limited to a 2,200 square foot wine store with the following
 16 uses:
 17 • Wines sold for consumption off premises.
 18 • Free wine tastings and educational events (guest speakers,
 19 sommeliers).
 20 • Free ad hock tastings.
 21 • Customer purchases a bottle of wine, and then drinks it in the store.
 22 • Paid tastings and educational events.
 23 • Wines sold by the glass and consumed on premises.
 24 • A flight (small tastes of several wines from the same region or of the
 25 same varietals (paid for by the consumer).
 26 • Cheeses, breads and pre-made deserts will be available for purchase
 27 and consumption on site.
 28
 29 Should the petitioner desire any other type of use, a new Special
 30 Exception application must be reviewed and approved by the City
 31 Commission.
 32
 33 3. In the event that any problems arise as a result of the operation of this
 34 establishment, such as noise, parking, traffic and/or other nuisances, the
 35 applicant will make all improvements required to mitigate these nuisances
 36 so as not to negatively impact adjacent commercial/residential areas.
 37
 38 4. The applicant shall obtain proper approvals such as Certificate of
 39 Occupancy and Occupational License from the City prior to operations
 40 and a State of Florida Liquor License.
 41
 42 5. The Shared Parking Study shall be amended to reflect the new use within
 43 Bay 7391 within Building C.
 44
 45 6. The total number of tables shall not exceed four (4) and the total
 46 number of chairs shall not exceed eight (8).
