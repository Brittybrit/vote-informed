 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 14 Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the
 15 Code of Ordinance is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and
 16 land development orders; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the petitioner desires to add a second wall sign to the east elevation of
 19 the existing SunTrust Bank building located at 5801 Hillsboro Blvd. ; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in Code of Ordinances,
 22 have been satisfied; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony
 25 presented by the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of the
 26 various City of Parkland review agencies and staff; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the City Commission has made the following findings of fact:
 29
 30 1. Environmental Considerations: No adverse impacts to the natural environment are
 31 expected since the property has already been
 32 disturbed.
 33
 34 2. Utilities: The proposed request will not affect existing water,
 35 sanitary, sewer and drainage systems.
 36
 37 3. Comprehensive Plan The proposed development is consistent with the
 38 Considerations: Land Use Element of the Comprehensive Plan.
 39
 40 4. Zoning Considerations: The proposed sign meets the requirements of
 41 Chapter 15 of the Code of Ordinances.
 42
 43 5. Community Appearance The sign fits in the character of the area
 44 Considerations: and is in good scale and proportion to the SunTrust
 45 Bank building.

 1 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 2 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 3
 4 SECTION 1. The Site Plan Amendment and Community Appearance Board
 5 Application of the SunTrust Bank within an outparcel of the Shoppes of Parkland is hereby
 6 APPROVED on the real property as described in Exhibit A, subject to the conditions of
 7 approval contained herein, which are in addition to the general requirements otherwise
 8 provided by ordinance:
 9
 10 1 . This approval is based upon the Elevation plans entitled "SunTrust Bank — Parkland
 11 Branch Outparcel #3, US441 + Hillsboro Blvd. Parkland, Florida" dated November
 12 28, 2005 and received by the Planning and Zoning Department on November 29,
 13 2005.
 14 2. A sign permit shall be required to be submitted and approved prior to installation of
 15 the wall sign on the east side elevation of the SunTrust Bank building.
 16 3. The sign letter heights shall be no larger than twenty inches and consist of green
 17 Rohm & Haas channel letters of Helvetica style.
 18 4. All conditions of the Resolutions 2002-60 not amended by this site plan shall apply.
 19 5. The petitioner shall bring its adjacent perimeter buffers into compliance with the
 20 approved landscape plans for the Shoppes at Parkland and/or the SunTrust Bank
 21 prior to issuance of the sign building permit.
 22
 23 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
