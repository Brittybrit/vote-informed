 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO AUTHORIZE THE
 5 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE AN
 6 INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH BROWARD COUNTY
 7 RELATING TRANSFERRED PROPERTY AND PUBLIC
 8 DEBT FOR GUARDRAILS; PROVIDING FOR
 9 CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND
 10 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 11
 12
 13 WHEREAS, House Bill 1315 was introduced which moved the Broward
 14 County line from County Line Road to Loxahatchee Road; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, House Bill 1315 was approved by the Florida House and
 17 Senate unanimously in the 2007 Legislative Session; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, Governor Charlie Crist signed approval of House Bill 1315 on
 20 August 26, 2007; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, House Bill 1315 requires that Palm Beach County and
 23 Broward County execute an interlocal agreement no later than September 30,
 24 2007 agreeing to the payment or apportionment of any public debt; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, House Bill 1315 states that all public roads or rights-of-ways
 27 lying with Palm Beach County and in the affected property are transferred from
 28 Palm Beach County to Broward County or the City of Parkland ("City"); and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, Palm Beach County has stated that guardrails along
 31 Loxahatchee Road are part of their debt service and Palm Beach County is
 32 seeking reimbursement; and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, since a portion of the affected property is being annexed into
 35 the City directly, Broward County is seeking contribution from the City; and
 36
 37 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best
 38 interest of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City
 39 of Parkland to enter into an interlocal agreement with Broward County regarding
 40 the debt service; and
 41
 42 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 43 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
