 Parkland Commons- North Site Plan Amendment
 (02-SPA-08 & O1 -SPS-08)
 Revised Permitted Use Definitions
 SITE PLAN AMENDMENT
 The following uses have been requested to be added to the
 approved use list for the Planned Commercial Development
 known as Parkland Commons North which is located on the
 northwest corner of Trails End and University Drive.
 The applicant, at the request of the Planning and Zoning Board,
 was to modify the definitions for "Fast food restaurant" and
 "Food take-out only" to further clarify the intent of the uses and
 for ease of regulation over time. The Applicant has since
 decided to no longer seek "Fast Food Restaurant" approval
 instead expanding the existing Restaurant Use to include a
 specific variety of eateries.
 The Applicant has provided the document in a strikethrough and
 underline format in order to show what was previously proposed
 for the definitions and the final verbiage.
 • Animal hospital / emergency clinic: Establishment engaged
 in providing medical care and treatment for domestic
 animals incidental to a veterinarian facility.
 • Restaurant: An establishment that sells a limited variety of
 food and beverages that generally are prepared to order
 and consumed on-premises, and are typified by ice cream
 shops, health food shops, coffee shops, doughnut and bakery
 shops, delicatessens, and the like subject to the City's Land
 Development Code and other applicable regulations. Drive-
 though windows are prohibited. Outdoor seating may be
 provided subject to the City's Land Development Code and
 other applicable regulations.
 • Food take-out only (Take-Out Restaurant): Restaurants /
 Eating establishments with on-site cooking facilities where
 food and beverages are to be consumed off-premises, (i.e.
 express-style eateries). Drive-through windows are prohibited.
