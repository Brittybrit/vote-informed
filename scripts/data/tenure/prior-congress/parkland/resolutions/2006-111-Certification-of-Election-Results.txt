 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-111
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ACCEPTING THE
 RESULTS OF THE SPECIAL ELECTION HELD ON
 NOVEMBER 7, 2006.
 WHEREAS, the results of the Municipal Special Election held on November 7,
 2006, have been canvassed by the Broward County Canvassing Board in accordance with
 the provisions of the general election law.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the City Commission accepts the results of the Municipal
 Special Election held on November 7, 2006.
 Section 2. That Mark Weissman is declared elected to the office of the
 Commission for District 4.
 Section 3. That Parkland Charter Amendments #1, 3, 4, 5 and 7 were
 approved by the electors at the November 7, 2006 election and the Charter is deemed
 amended as set forth in those questions. Questions 2 & 6 were not approved by the
 electors and shall have no force or effect.
 Section 4. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 17 DAY OF November , 2006.
 CITY OF,-E A L RIDA
 GMI AEL UDINE, MAYOR
