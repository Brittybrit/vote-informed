 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-72
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AUTHORIZE THE CITY MANAGER
 5 TO EXECUTE A CONTRACT WITH SUNGARD/H.T.E. THE TOP
 6 RANKED FIRM FOR INTEGRATED MUNICIPAL. FINANCE
 7 SOFTWARE SYSTEM; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND
 8 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, City staff advertised RFP for the purpose of awarding a contract to
 11 provide Finance and Recreation Software; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, a selection committee consisting of the Finance and Administrative
 14 Services Director, Business and Strategic Planning Manager, Planning Director, Information
 15 Systems Manager, Leisure Services Director and Environmental Resources Director, have
 16 read and reviewed five (5) submitted proposals for Finance Software; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the two (2) top ranked firms Sungard/H.T.E. and Tyler Technologies
 19 Munis were provided the opportunity to introduce their team and provide an oral
 20 presentation to the selection committee for ranking; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, upon demonstration of the software and reference checking, City Staff
 23 has recommended that a contract be entered into with Sungard/H.T.E as the top ranked firm
 24 with Tyler Technologies Munis being the second ranked firm; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the City Commission accepts said rankings and authorizes the City
 27 Manager to enter into a contract with Sungard/H.T.E at the price not to exceed $380,000;
 28 and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best interest of
 31 the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland for the
 32 City Manager to enter into such contract; and
 33
 34 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 35 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 36
 37 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as being
 38 true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein. All exhibits attached hereto are hereby
 39 incorporated herein.
 40
 41 SECTION 2. The City Manager is hereby authorized and directed to enter into a
 42 contract for Municipal Finance software system with the top ranked firm of Sungard/H.T.E. at a
 43 price not to exceed $380,000, subject to approval of form by the City Attorney.
 44
 45 SECTION 3. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are hereby
 46 -repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 47
