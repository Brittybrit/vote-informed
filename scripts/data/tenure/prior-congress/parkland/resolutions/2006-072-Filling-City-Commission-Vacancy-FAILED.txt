 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-72
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, FILLING THE VACANCY IN THE
 CITY COMMISSION SEAT FOR DISTRICT 4 UNTIL THE
 NOVEMBER 7, 2006 GENERAL ELECTION.
 WHEREAS, on March 9, 2004 Commissioner Mimi Ribotsky was elected
 to the City Commission as the District 4 representative; and
 WHEREAS, on July 6, 2006, Vice Mayor Mimi Ribotsky submitted her
 resignation, which was effective immediately; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission solicited qualified candidates to fill the
 vacancy pursuant to Article 4.06 of the City Charter; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines iit to be in the best
 interest of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland that the vacant City
 Commission position be filled by appointment; and
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby
 appoints to thevacant District 4 City Commission
 seat.
 Section 2. That. this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS DAY OF , 2006.
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA
 MICHAEL UDINE, MAYOR
 ATTEST:
 SANDRA COUZZO, CMC
