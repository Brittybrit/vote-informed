 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-35
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE CITY
 OFFICIALS TO APPOINT A REPRESENTATIVE TO THE
 POLICE PENSION BOARD.
 WHEREAS, the Police Pension Plan requires a Board of Trustees which shall
 consist of five (5) persons; two (2) of whom shall be legal residents of the City of
 Parkland and who shall be appointed by the City Commission; and
 WHEREAS, former Commissioner Tracey McGilvray was one of the
 Commission appointments to the Police Pension Board; and
 WHEREAS, since Commissioner McGilvray's resignation from the City
 Commission, the Commission has the option to replace their appointment to the Police
 Pension Board; and
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials do hereby approve the following
 Parkland Police Retirement Plan representative: Commissioner Mark Weissman.
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 28 AY OF March , 2007.
