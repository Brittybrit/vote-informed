 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-102
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, DECLARING A SPECIAL MUNICIPAL
 ELECTION ON JANUARY 29, 2008 FOR THE PURPOSES OF FILLING A
 VACANCY FOR THE COMMISSION SEAT FOR DISTRICT III AND
 SETTING THE QUALIFYING PERIOD
 WHEREAS, by virtue of the resignation of Commissioner Tracey McGilvray, there is a vacancy
 in District III; and
 WHEREAS, no action has been taken to fill the vacancy within thirty (30) days of its creation;
 and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland therefore desires to hold a special
 election, concurrent with the Presidential Primary election of January 29, 2008, for the purpose of filling
 said vacancy;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 SECTION 1. The above Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a part hereof.
 SECTION 2. A special election is hereby set for January 29, 2008, for the purpose of filling the
 vacancy for District III. The person elected shall fill the unexpired term for District III, as that term is set
 forth in the City Charter, including any amendments thereto.
 SECTION 3. The qualifying period for candidates shall open at 12:00 noon on November 2,
 2007 and close at 12:00 noon on November 9, 2007. Qualifying shall be at Parkland City Hall, 6600
 University Drive,Parkland,Florida in the City Clerk's Office.
 SECTION 4. The City Clerk shall undertake the necessary measures for notice of qualifying
 and of the election and coordinate with the Supervisor of Elections. The Supervisor of Elections is hereby
 authorized to take all actions necessary for the purposes of holding the special election for the vacancy in
 District III. The City Clerk is hereby given authority to undertake all necessary measures with regard to
 the Supervisor of Elections and the Department of State for the purposes of holding said election and
 providing notices therefore and for the appropriate advertising of the election. The City Clerk shall
 undertake all necessary measures as required by Florida law for these purposes.
