 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-10
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA; SUPPORTING THE CITY OF
 5 PARKLAND'S APPLICATION TO OBTAIN THE NATIONAL
 6 WILDLIFE FEDERATION CERTIFICATION AS A COMMUNITY
 7 WILDLIFE HABITAT; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS;
 8 PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
 9 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the National Wildlife Federation's Backyard Wildlife Habitat
 13 Program promotes a Community Wildlife Habitat certification program; and
 14 WHEREAS, the Community Wildlife Habitat project promotes the
 15 provision of food, water, cover and spaces where wildlife can raise their young
 16 from individual backyards to multiple locations throughout the community; and
 17 WHEREAS, the Community Wildlife Habitat project benefits the entire
 18 community of people, plants and wildlife through the creation of sustainable
 19 landscapes that require little or no pesticides, fertilizers, or excess watering; and
 20 WHEREAS, habitat landscapes can serve to beautify the City of Parkland
 21 areas and provide our residents pride in their neighborhoods; and
 22 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland will strive to become certified as a
 23 National Wildlife Federation Community Wildlife Habitat;
 24
 25 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 26 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 27
 28 SECTION 1: The City Commission hereby supports the City of Parkland's
 29 application to the National Wildlife Federation for Certification as a Community
 30 Wildlife Habitat.
 31 SECTION 2: The Department of Development Services is hereby
 32 directed to support the application for certification
 33 .
 34 SECTION 3. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are
 35 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 36
 37 SECTION 4. If any clause, section, other part or application of this
 38 Resolution is held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or
 39 invalid it shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions or applications of this
 40 Resolution.
