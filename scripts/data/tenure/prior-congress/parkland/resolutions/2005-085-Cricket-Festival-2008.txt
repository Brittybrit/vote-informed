 RESOLUTION NO. 2005-85
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SUPPORTING THE BOARD OF
 COMMISSIONERS OF BROWARD COUNTY IN THEIR BID TO
 HOST THE GOLDEN OLDIES WORLD CRICKET FESTIVAL
 IN 2008.
 WHEREAS, the Cricket Festival was established over twenty years ago and
 takes place every two years; and
 WHEREAS, the Cricket Festival has historically included over 50 different
 teams from nations all over the world; and
 WHEREAS, the Broward County Board of Commissioners desires to submit a
 bid to host the Cricket Festival in 2008; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland desires to show support to Broward County
 and wish them well in their bid efforts.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials do hereby support the Board of
 Commissioners of Broward County in their bid to host the Golden Oldies World Cricket
 Festival in 2008.
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 16 DAY OF November , 2005.
