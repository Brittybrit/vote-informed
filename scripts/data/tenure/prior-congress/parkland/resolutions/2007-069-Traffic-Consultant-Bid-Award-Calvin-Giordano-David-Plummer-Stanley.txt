 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-69
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO RANK THE SEVEN (7) LISTED
 5 TRAFFIC CONSULTING COMPANIES TO PROVIDE
 6 PROFESSIONAL TRAFFIC CONSULTING SERVICES ON A
 7 CONTINUING BASIS AND TO AUTHORIZE THE INTERIM CITY
 8 MANAGER TO EXECUTE A CONTRACT WITH THIE THREE (3)
 9 TOP RANKED FIRMS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERAI3ILITY; AND
 10 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 11
 12 WHEREAS, City staff advertised RFP for the purpose of having a list of pre-qualified
 13 firms to provide traffic consulting on a continuing "as needed" basis; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, a selection committee consisting of the Interim City Manager, Planning
 16 Director, Environmental Resources Director and Capital Projects Manager read and
 17 reviewed seven (7) submitted proposals in accordance with the Consultants' Competitive
 18 Negotiation Act ("CCNA"); and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the seven (7) firms were provided the opportunity to introduce their team
 21 and provide an oral presentation to the City Commission for ranking; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, upon final scoring and ranking, the City Commission authorizes the
 24 Interim City Manager or her designee to negotiate and enter into a contract with the top
 25 three (3) ranked firms to provide the aforementioned continuing traffic consulting services;
 26 and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, if the Interim City Manager or her designee is unable to negotiate such a
 29 contract with any of the three (3) top ranked companies, the Interim City Manger is
 30 authorized to negotiate with the next ranked company until three (3) contracts are entered
 31 into; and
 32
 33 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best interest of
 34 the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland for the
 35 Interim City Manager to enter into such contracts; and
 36
 37 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 38 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 39
 40 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as being
 41 true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached hereto are hereby
 42 incorporate herein.
 43
 44 SECTION 2. The City Commission hereby ranks the seven (7) firms according to the
 45 order as outlined in Exhibit "A".
