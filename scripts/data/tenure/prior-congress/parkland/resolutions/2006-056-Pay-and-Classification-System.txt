 17 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland approved the
 18 annual operating budget via Ordinance No, 2005-17; and
 19 WHEREAS, the approved annual operating budget included a total of
 20 eighty (80) positions; and
 21 WHEREAS, the total number of positions was subsequently increased
 22 from eighty (80) positions to eighty-one (81) positions through Resolution 2005-
 23 95 and then increased from eighty-one (81) positions to eighty-two (82) positions
 24 through Resolution 2006-15;
 25 WHEREAS, the City Manager has reviewed the organization of the City
 26 and desires to adopt a new pay and classification system to provide for a modern
 27 compensation and performance system allowing the City to achieve the goal of
 28 hiring and retaining qualified employees; and
 29 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 30 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 31 Section 1. That the existing pay and classification system is hereby
 32 repealed and replaced with Exhibit A which is attached hereto and made a part
 33 hereof.
 34 Section 2. That the City of Parkland City Commission hereby authorizes
 35 an increase in the authorized personnel cap by one position (82 to 83) with the
 36 classifications and authorized position counts as detailed in Exhibit A attached
 37 hereto and made a part hereof.
