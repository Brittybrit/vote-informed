 "STATE ROAD 7 PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT"
 I. Purpose
 The Purpose of this Partnership is to provide a framework to support the signatory
 agencies, organizations and governments, within Broward, Miami-Dade, and Palm
 Beach Counties to work collaboratively to facilitate the aesthetic and economic
 revitalization of the State Road 7 / US 441 corridor. This document is not
 intended to be a contract. It is recognized that each party has different interests,
 priorities, and responsibilities. However, this document shall signify the
 cooperative intent of the parties to leverage in-kind services, private funds, grant
 funds, foundation funds, and/or funds from the individual signatories to further the
 work of this Partnership and to continue to share in the mutual benefits and
 rewards of this collaborative effort.
 II. Agreement
 WHEREAS, the signatory agencies, organizations and governments that
 execute this agreement will be collectively referred to as signatories; and,
 WHEREAS, jurisdictions along the State Road 7 / US 441 corridor have
 expressed their desire to enter into and participate in a cooperative undertaking to
 further facilitate ongoing areawide comprehensive, functional and best-planning
 practice activities along the State Road 7 / US 441 corridor; and,
 WHEREAS, the State Road 7 / US 441 corridor is a heavily traveled
 corridor extending from Palm Beach County in the north, through Broward County,
 to Miami-Dade County in the south; and,
 WHEREAS, each of the signatories to this agreement bears responsibility
 for or has involvement in some aspect of community planning and redevelopment
 along the State Road 7 / US 441 corridor; and,
 WHEREAS, each of the signatories to this agreement understands that
 revitalization of the State Road 7 / US 441 corridor will generate social, economic,
 and environmental benefits to the region; and,
 WHEREAS, there is a need to address current land use/transportation
 issues along the State Road 7 / US 441 corridor and increase effective
 intergovernmental cooperation; and,
 WHEREAS, the participation and involvement of area local governments,
 stakeholders, and agencies is critical to the future regional mobility and enhanced

 quality of life for area residents, along with economic stability and effective
 extrajurisdictional coordination of planning and development activity; and,
 WHEREAS, the problems of growth and development often transcend the
 boundaries of individual units of local general-purpose government, and often no
 single unit can formulate plans or implement policies for their solution without
 affecting other units in their geographic area; and,
 WHEREAS, the regional planning council is recognized as a multipurpose
 regional entity that is in a position to plan for an coordinate intergovernmental
 solutions to growth-related problems on greater-than-local issues, provide
 technical assistance to local governments, and meet other needs of the
 communities in each region; and,
 WHEREAS, there is a need for the South Florida Regional Planning
 Council ("SFRPC") to assist local governments to resolve their common problems,
 engage in areawide comprehensive and functional planning, administer certain
 federal and state grants-in-aid, assist with activities designed to promote and
 facilitate economic development, and provide a regional focus in regard to multiple
 programs undertaken on an areawide basis; and,
 WHEREAS, it is the intent of this agreement to facilitate community
 involvement in the planning of future transportation alternatives to support local
 development opportunities, promote residential infill, further employment
 opportunities, reduce urban sprawl pressures, foster increased intergovernmental
 coordination, and coordinate supporting infrastructure improvements; and,
 WHEREAS, this agreement shall serve as a commitment of cooperation,
 realizing the mutual benefit to be derived from a close working relationship among
 all participating entities and shall be reviewed an amended as necessary; and,
 WHEREAS, it is not the intent of this agreement to specify all specific areas
 that can or should be effectively coordinated.
 WHEREAS, each of the signatories to the agreement is committed to the
 following guiding principles related to the revitalization of the State Road 7 / US
 441 corridor:
 • Coordinating the implementation of existing statutes, ordinances,
 regulations, applicable programs, and other efforts that will assist
 with local revitalization measures and maximize their efficiency and
 effectiveness; thereby, reducing the need for state review of
 amendments to local government comprehensive plans;
 • Approaching revitalization of State Road 7 / US 441 corridor in the
 context of broader area-wide and local planning, economic

 development, neighborhood development, and urban revitalization
 efforts;
 • Recognizing that the economic development, efficient and practical
 transportation systems, and community land use at appropriate
 densities and intensities are linked and that these activities should be
 well coordinated and thoughtfully planned;
 • Providing a good quality of life for citizens working or living adjacent

 NOW, THEREFORE, this agreement is established to create a framework
 for coordinating efforts and extrajursdictional impacts relating to the revitalization
 of the State Road 7 / US 441 corridor.
 Focus of Cooperative Activities
 The signatories to this agreement share mutual interest in the following issues and
 will use this agreement to expand their cooperation to focus their efforts on the
 coordination of vital infrastructure including multi-modal transportation for people
 and goods, water and wastewater, sustainable schools, design and development
 of public spaces and the development of advanced communications technology.
 1. Actively participate in the State Road 7 / US 441 Partnership Committee.
 This Committee considers and collaborates on matters concerning State
 Road 7 / US 441, and makes recommendations to the signatories and other
 stakeholders. This Committee is subject to the Public Records and
 Sunshine Laws of Florida.
 2. Maintain, implement and update the State Road 7 / US 441 Master Plan.
 Develop and complete workplans for future periods, as appropriate, for the
 life of this agreement.
 3. Develop, maintain, and update an inventory of existing planning and
 implementation efforts along the State Road 7 / US 441 Corridor. This
 inventory provides a basis to coordinate consistency between each
 community's revitalization efforts, and as a way to identify strategies for
 successful implementation.
 4. Undertake an initiative to promote a collective vision for the urban design of
 the State Road 7 / US 441 corridor that shall uniquely apply in each
 jurisdiction to the extent that each jurisdiction chooses. The purpose is to
 create a sense of place, and promote pedestrian-oriented safe
 neighborhoods and town centers to enhance individual community identity.
 5. Undertake an initiative to market the State Road 7 / US 441 corridor for
 redevelopment, including but not limited to the preparation of marketing

 materials, promoting public outreach, and development of an effective
 marketing tagline to identify corridor investment opportunities.
 6. Review local comprehensive plans, land development regulations, and
 capital improvement programs to help ensure that these instruments will
 move the community toward its vision. Support the amendment of local
 comprehensive plans that further locally developed visions developed
 through individual Charrettes for corridor sectors and to insure conformance
 with the State Growth Management Act.
 7. Collaborate for the development and submission of competitive applications
 for recognition awards and to enhance efforts to secure State, Federal or
 other grant funding for physical development and/or the provision of
 technical assistance to support the activities of the State Road 7 / US 441
 Collaborative.
 8. Work together in support of comprehensive and inclusive sustainable
 development practices throughout the State Road 7 / US 441 corridor.
 Encourage the application of green construction techniques including the
 development of buildings eligible for LEED certification or meet the building
 principles of the Florida Green Building Coalition or U.S. Green Building
 Council.
 9. Coordinate with the other SR 7 /US 441 Partnership signatory entities and
 the SFRPC in the development and implementation of local planning and
 development activities.
 10.Collaborate with the encourage the coordination of current projects and
 planning efforts along the corridor including, but not limited to,
 Transportation and Transit, Land Use, Regional Water Supply, Economic
 Development, Aesthetics, Intergovernmental Coordination, Schools, and
 other vital infrastructure.
 11.Cultivate a link between redevelopment, access, mobility, and the
 expansion of public transit systems. Actively seek the development of
 multiple transportation modes to move people and goods. Ensure that
 redevelopment and new development along the corridor is accessible to
 transit and provides connectivity to adjacent neighborhoods.
 12.Support redevelopment activities that are consistent with principles of and
 best practices in urban infill and development. These principles and
 practices include, but are not limited to:
 i. infill development at appropriate densities and intensities,
 ii. quality urban design that is pedestrian- and transit- oriented,
 iii. urban school construction standards, multi-story, smaller acreage,
 and/or school reconstruction,
 iv. crime prevention through environmental design (CPTED),
 v. urban greenways and public open spaces,
 vi. mixed uses,
 vii. providing a variety of housing types for all income levels,
 viii. Actively seeking the involvement of the wide range and diversity of
 stakeholders in public participation in the planning and
 implementation of specific projects.

 Ill. Programming, Budgeting, Funding and Reimbursement
 Signatories are not obligated to expend appropriations, obligate funds or enter into
 any contract or agreements. This agreement is neither a fiscal nor a funding
 obligation. Any transfer of funds between signatories may take place through
 applicable laws, agreements, existing authorities and procedures.
 The Partnership has leveraged significant benefits from the active participation
 and dedication of staff and inkind resources from each of the signatory members.
 Continued participation is critical to achieving the established goals of this
 Partnership. Signatories are encouraged to:
 1. Provide a Designee and Alternate to serve on the Steering Committee of
 the State Road 7 / U.S. 441 Collaborative,
 2. Participate in activities and events that support public involvement and
 market and promote the economic enhancement of the State Road 7 / US
 441 corridor,
 3. Advocate for issues affecting the State Road 7 / US 441 corridor,
 4. Identify funding and grant opportunities available to promote Collaborative
 goals,
 5. Provide letters of support for funding and grant requests that promote the
 goals and activities of the State Road 7 / US 441 Collaborative,
 6. Identify issues and opportunities affecting the State Road 7 / US 441
 corridor,
 7. Participate in working groups and offer technical assistance to Collaborative
 communities,
 8. Amend local land uses, land development regulations, redevelopment
 plans, and capital improvement plans to promote redevelopment of the
 State Road 7 / US 441 corridor,
 9. Share insights on the effectiveness of local efforts to promote
 redevelopment and aesthetic improvements of the State Road 7 / US 441
 corridor; and
 10.Work cooperatively with adjacent municipalities and agencies.
 IV. Shareholders
 Recognizing that the core functions of the Collaborative require dedicated financial
 support, Signatories, at their discretion, may contribute funds to sustain and further
 the core activities of the Collaborative. Signatories of this Agreement may at their
 option also become shareholders at the suggested levels:

 1. SFRPC: $5,000 per annum
 Jurisdictions:
 < 25,000 population: $2,500 per annum
 > 25,000 population: $5,000 per annum
 2. The Collaborative will apply for and may accept grant funding to support
 core functions
 3. Failure of the Collaborative to match or exceed the total financial
 contributions provided by a shareholder will not obligate shareholders to
 future funding obligations.
 4. Core activities will be limited by funding availability as determined by the
 Steering Committee.
 5. The Collaborative may accept funds from non-governmental entities that
 promote the mission and activities of the Collaborative.
 6. Whenever possible, any grant funds awarded to the Collaborative for
 capital projects will be allocated first to any qualifying shareholder up to
 the shareholder's contribution. Remaining grant funds will be distributed
 between all qualifying signatories.
