 Date"). Beginning on the Effective Date and continuing until the Term Commencement Date as defined in Paragraph 3 below("Due
 Diligence Period"), Lessee shall only be permitted to enter the Land for the limited purpose of making appropriate engineering and
 boundary surveys, inspections, and other reasonably necessary investigations and signal, topographical, geotechnical, structural and

 commencement of the Due Diligence Period as to its intent to proceed with the terms of this Lease. Failure to so notify Lessor will be
 deemed Lessee's intent to cancel this Lease and this Lease will automatically become null and void and of no further force and effect.
