 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, FOR SITE PLAN AND
 5 COMMUNITY APPEARANCE BOARD APPROVAL FOR
 6 THE BANYAN ISLES AND SABLE POINTE ENTRY
 7 FEATURES LOCATED WITHIN THE HERON BAY
 8 COMMUNITY; CASE NO. 04-SP-06.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland, Florida,
 11 pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the Code of
 12 Ordinance is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and land development
 13 orders; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the petitioner desires to construct an entry feature for the Sable Point
 16 community located within Heron Bay and to construct an entry feature for the Banyan Isles
 17 community located within Heron Bay; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in Code of Ordinances, have
 20 been satisfied; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony presented by
 23 the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of the various City of Parkland
 24 review agencies and staff; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS,the City Commission has made the following findings of fact:
 27
 28 1. Environmental Considerations: No adverse impacts to the natural environment are
 29 expected since the property has already been disturbed.
 30
 31 2. Utilities: The proposed request will not affect existing water,
 32 sanitary, sewer and drainage systems.
 33
 34 3. Comprehensive Plan Considerations:The proposed development is consistent with the Land
 35 Use Element of the Comprehensive Plan.
 36
 37 4. Zoning Considerations: Site area, setbacks to the wall, height restrictions, and
 38 landscaping all meet or exceed Code standards.
 39
 40 5. Community Appearance The wall and sign fits within the character of the area.
 41 Considerations:
 42
 43 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 44 PARKLAND,FLORIDA that:
 45
 46 SECTION I. The Site Plan Amendment and Community Appearance Board Application of
 47 WCI Communities, Inc. for the construction of an entry feature for the Sable Point community located
 48 within Heron Bay and for the construction of an entry feature for the Banyan Isles community located
 49 within Heron Bay is hereby APPROVED on the real property as described in Exhibit A, subject to the

 1 conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition to the general requirements otherwise
 2 provided by ordinance:
 3
 4 1. This approval is based upon the plans entitled "The Sable Pointe Entrance" and "The
 5 Banyan Isles Entrance" dated March 6, 2006 and received by the Planning and Zoning
 6 Department on June 14, 2006.
 7 2. The maximum height of the signage letters for the Sable Pointe and Banyan Isles
 8 entrances shall not exceed 14".
 9 3. All conditions of the Sable Pointe Site Plan and Banyan Isles Site Plan not amended
 10 by this Site Plan and Community Appearance Board application shall apply.
 11
 12
 13 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
