 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-63
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE A
 5 CONTRACT WITH THE MCKINLEY FINANCIAL SERVICES INC. FOR
 6 BROKER SERVICES FOR THE EMPLOYEE HEALTH CARE AND
 7 WELFARE BENEFITS PROGRAMS.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland requested proposals from agents/brokers for services for
 11 the City's employee health care and welfare benefits programs; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City received eight (8) proposals and a committee appointed by the City
 14 Manager reviewed said proposals and ranked the proposals received; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City Commission based upon reviews of the proposals and the criteria
 17 set forth in the RFQ has ranked the proposers as follows: Number One: McKinley Financial
 18 Services Inc., Number Two Gehring Group, Number Three: Rhodes Insurance; and
 20
 21 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 22 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 23
 24 SECTION 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a
 25 part hereof.
 26
 27 SECTION 2. The City Commission hereby adopted the ranking as set forth above.
 28 McKinley Financial Services Inc. is deemed the number one ranked proposer and has submitted
 29 the proposal most advantageous to the City. The City Manager is hereby authorized to enter into
 30 negotiations to develop a contract with McKinley Financial Services Inc., which contract shall first
 31 be approved by the City Attorney as to form and then submitted to the City Commission for final
 32 determination. The City Commission will then make a final decision on approving or not
 33 approving the contract. If the City Commission does not approve the contract, or if the City
 34 Manager is unable to negotiate an acceptable contract with the number one ranked proposer,
 35 then the City Commission may direct that negotiations be undertaken with the number two
 36 ranked firm. No contract or award shall be final until such time as the City Commission approves
 37 a final contract.
 38
 39 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
 40
 41 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 9 day of JULY , 2008.
