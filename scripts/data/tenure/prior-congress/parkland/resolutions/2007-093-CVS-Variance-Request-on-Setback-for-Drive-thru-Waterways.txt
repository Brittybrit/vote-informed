 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-93
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA GRANTING PETITION OF CVS
 5 CORPORATION FOR VARIANCE APPROVAL FROM SECTION
 6 22-226(1) TO REDUCE THE EASTERN BUILDING SETBACK
 7 FROM THE SECOND DRIVE-THRU CANOPY BY 14 FEET ON
 8 PROPERTY LOCATED WITHIN THE WATERWAYS SHOPPES,
 9 AS MORE PARTICULARLY DESCRIBED HEREIN; AND
 10 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE; CASE NO. 02-V-07.
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of
 13 Parkland, Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166,
 14 Florida Statutes, and the Code of Ordinances, is authorized and empowered to
 15 consider petitions related to zoning and land development orders; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, a proper Notice of Public Hearing was sent to property
 18 owners of record within 500 feet of the subject property and was published in a
 19 newspaper qualified to carry legal advertisements; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, at a regular meeting of the City Commission, taking place on
 22 September 19, 2007 , a public hearing was held to hear testimony and receive
 23 exhibits for and against the Petition of CVS Corporation (owners) requesting a
 24 variance from Section 22-226(1) in the Code of Ordinances, as adopted by the
 25 City of Parkland; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and
 28 testimony presented by the Applicant and other interested parties and the
 29 recommendations of the various City of Parkland review agencies and staff.
 30
 31 NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the City Commission of the City of
 32 Parkland, Florida, that;
 33
 34 Section 1. After due consideration of all testimony and exhibits and
 35 review of the standards for granting a variance as set forth in Article XXII,
 36 Section 323 of the Code of Ordinances, all necessary standards for granting a
 37 variance have been satisfied to reduce the eastern building setback for the
 38 second drive-thru canopy; and
 39
 40 Section 2. The variance request of CVS Corporations, owners, is hereby
 41 approved for the eastern building setback of the second drive-thru for the CVS
 42 Pharmacy to be reduced by a maximum of fourteen (14) feet.
 43
 44 Section 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 45 adoption.
