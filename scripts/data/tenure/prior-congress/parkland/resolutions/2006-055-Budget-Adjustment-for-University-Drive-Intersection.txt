 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-55

 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE THIRD AMENDMENT TO THE
 5 2005-2006 FISCAL YEAR CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PLAN TO FUND THE
 6 CONSTRUCTION OF ADDITIONAL ENHANCEMENT FEATURES FOR THE
 7 SIGNALIZATION OF HOLMBERG ROAD AND UNIVERSITY DRIVE AND
 8 TO AUTHORIZE THE REQUIRED BUDGET ADJUSTMENTS NECESSARY
 9 TO ESTABLISH A NOT-TO-EXCEED $200,000 CITY PROJECT BUDGET.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission has maintained a sustainable budget philosophy; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission has adopted a strategic plan for the City of Parkland; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, on September 21, 2005, the City Commission adopted the budget which
 16 included a Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) to address capital improvement projects proposed during
 17 the 2005-2006 fiscal year; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, on May 3, 2006 the City Commission provided direction to the City Manager
 20 regarding the final selection of materials for the enhancement/beautification component of the traffic
 21 signalization project being funded and installed by WCI Communities, Inc., and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to make the project the "signature gateway"
 24 intersection adjacent to the Municipal Complex and costs of both the signalization component and
 25 enhancement/beautification component will exceed the anticipated budget, and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City staff is recommending the appropriation of $200,000 in unexpended
 28 surplus Capital Improvement Program funds in order to provide for the necessary budget changes to
 29 achieve a signature gateway, and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, the City staff is recommending a City contribution to the
 32 enhancement/beautification component of the traffic signalization project being funded and installed
 33 by WCI Communities, Inc. in order to achieve the desired quality, and
 34
 35 WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to adjust its Capital Improvement Plan to include
 36 the additional Enhancement Project;
 37
 38 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 39 PARKLAND,FLORIDA that:
 40
 41 SECTION 1. The 2005-2006 CIP for the City of Parkland is hereby amended to transfer$200,000 of
 42 City Capital Improvement Funds currently budgeted in the Reserve for Capital Improvements account
 43 toward the University Drive and Holmberg Road Intersection Enhancement Project as shown in Exhibit
 44 A.
