 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-05
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION
 4 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 AUTHORIZING THE APPROPRIATE CITY
 6 OFFICIAL TO ENTER INTO A
 7 PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT
 8 WITH KIMLEY-HORN AND ASSOCIATES, INC.
 9 TO PROVIDE ENGINEERING AND RELATED
 10 SERVICES ON AN AS NEEDED BASIS,
 11 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13
 14 WHEREAS, City staff advertised RFQ #2006-07-2 entitled "Professional
 15 Engineering and Miscellaneous Consulting Services" for the purpose of having a
 16 list of pre-qualified firms to provide services on a continuing "as needed" basis;
 17 and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, a selection committee consisting of the City Manager,
 20 Assistant City Manager, Public Services Director, Planning Director, and
 21 Environmental Resources Director read, reviewed, and short listed nine (9) firms
 22 in accordance with the Consultants' Competitive Negotiation Act (CCNA); and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the nine (9) short listed firms were provided the opportunity to
 25 introduce their team and provide an oral presentation to the City Commission at
 26 a December 6, 2006 Special City Commission Meeting; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, at the Regular City Commission Meeting on December 6,
 29 2006, the City Commission ranked the top five (5) firms and authorized the City
 30 Manager to negotiate contracts with the top three (3) firms; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, Kimley-Horn and Associates, Inc. was one of the top three (3)
 33 firms; and
 34
 35 WHEREAS, staff recommends that the City enter into an Agreement with
 36 Kimley-Horn and Associates, Inc. for the purpose of providing professional
 37 engineering and miscellaneous consulting services on an ongoing basis; and
 38
 39 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes an Agreement
 40 with Kimley-Horn and Associates, Inc. is in the best interest of the citizens of the
 41 City of Parkland; and
 42
 43 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 44 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 45

<<PG>>
 46 SECTION 1. The Agreement with Kimley-Horn and Associates, Inc.
 47 is hereby approved and the appropriate City Official is hereby authorized to
 48 execute the Agreement.
 49
 50 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 51 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
