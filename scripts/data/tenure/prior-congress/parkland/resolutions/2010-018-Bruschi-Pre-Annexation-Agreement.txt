RESOLUTION NO. 2010 - 18 
A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE 
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING AN 
ANNEXATION AGREEMENT WITH THE BRUSCHI 
FAMILY RELATING TO PROPERTY BEING ANNEXED 
INTO THE CITY OF PARKLAND. 
WHEREAS, the Bruschi Family (Owners) own certain property in unincorporated 
Broward County in the area known as the Wedge; and 
WHEREAS, the Bruschi Family desires to have their property annexed into the 
corporate limits of the City of Parkland; and 
WHEREAS, the parties have negotiated the attached Annexation Agreement 
which deals with some, but by no means all of the development issues that will affect 
the Owners' property; 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE 
CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS: 
SECTION 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and 
made a part hereof. 
SECTION 2. The attached Annexation Agreement between the City of Parkland 
and the Bruschi Family is hereby approved.

AGREEMENT 
BETWEEN 
CITY OF PARKLAND, 
AND 
THE AMERIGO BRUSCHI FAMILY 
LIMITED PARTNERSHIP, 
A FLORIDA LIMITED PARTNERSHIP, 
AND 
DALE ALAN BRUSCHI 
AND 
DEAN CURTIS BRUSCHI 
THIS AGREEMENT is entered into by and between THE AMERIGO BRUSCHI 
FAMILY LIMITED PARTNERSHIP, a Florida limited partnership, and DALE ALAN 
BRUSCHI, and DEAN CURTIS BRUSCHI ("OWNERS") and the CITY OF PARKLAND 
("PARKLAND"), a municipal corporation of the State of Florida, as follows: 
WITNESSETH: 
WHEREAS, OWNERS own undeveloped land formerly located in Palm Beach County, 
which lands are separately described on Exhibit "A" and shall hereinafter be referred to 
collectively as the "Land"; and 
WHEREAS, pursuant to state legislation, the Land is now located within Broward 
County, Florida in an area referred to as the Wedge; and 
WHEREAS, the OWNERS wish to annex the Land into the corporate limits of the City 
of Parkland, and PARKLAND is willing to support such annexation if it receives certain 
assurances from OWNERS, as set forth below; and 
WHEREAS, the parties wish to set out certain understandings as to land use issues; and 
WHEREAS, the OWNERS have reviewed the level of actual developed densities within 
PARKLAND as well as the quality of the development approved and constructed in 

<<PG>>
PARKLAND, and wish to develop the Land in a manner which is consistent and compatible with 
the existing development in PARKLAND, and to ensure that any future development of the Land 
will be of the same high quality and overall density as the existing development in PARKLAND; 
and 
WHEREAS, PARKLAND is desirous of ensuring that the features which are associated 
with present PARKLAND development, to wit: high quality amenities, lushly landscaped 
berms, roadways and public spaces, low densities, and high quality architectural and design 
details for commercial development, are continued on the Land; and 
WHEREAS, the OWNERS agree with these goals of PARKLAND for any development 
of the Land, and also understand that any development will require additional parks, open space, 
public facilities, utilities, public educational facilities, and roadways and multipurpose trails; and 
WHEREAS, in order to undertake such development, the OWNERS will require 
appropriate land use changes by Broward County or PARKLAND followed by rezoning, should 
the property be placed within the municipal boundaries of PARKLAND; and 
WHEREAS, the parties wish to set forth some general parameters of the expectations 
and limitations which will govern any future development of the Land; and 
WHEREAS, the parties understand that no land use change can be undertaken except 
through the process as set forth under Florida law, after public hearing and appropriate findings 
to support any such change and the parties understand that Broward County must consent to any 
land use change; and 
WHEREAS, this Agreement shall not be construed to require any action by 
PARKLAND or any other government body with regard to any Land Use Plan or other 
development applications, as the parties recognize and agree that any development application

WHEREAS, OWNERS understand that the conditions and requirements set forth herein 
by PARKLAND are not in any way intended to be all inclusive or exhaustive; 
NOW THEREFORE, be it agreed by and between the parties as follows: 
Section 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a 
part hereof 
Section 2. Upon the effectiveness of the annexation, PARKLAND shall permit 
OWNERS to apply for an Amendment to the City of Parkland Land Use Plan related to the 
Lands, to a category of two (2) units per acre (gross). Said application would also require a Land 
Use Plan change incorporating the Land into PARKLAND, and would also require amendments 
to the Broward County Land Use Plan. The actual approval of any Land Use Plan Amendments 
shall require public hearings and appropriate findings as to infrastructure, transportation, roads, 
schools, utilities (which shall be deemed to include potable water, sanitary sewer and drainage as 
well as necessary power service). The approval of the Land Use Plan Amendment is not 
mandated by this Agreement, nor is any other development approval mandated, promised or 
required. It is understood by the Parties that, notwithstanding any land use change which may 
occur, the total number of residential units will be determined by the Parkland City Commission 
at the rezoning and site plan public hearings. Any land use category shall constitute a maximum 
density; the actual density shall be lower than the land use density and shall provide for a 
development intensity and quality as described in the "whereas" clauses set forth above and as 
are existing in PARKLAND presently and as is reasonably determined by PARKLAND to be 
compatible with development in the area. OWNERS agree that no multi -family units will be 
proposed or constructed on the Land.

Section 3. OWNERS agree that any development approvals for the Land shall 
require provision of parks, open space, public facilities, utilities (as defined above), public 
educational facilities and roads (collectively referred to as Development Infrastructure), as well 
as compliance with all applicable regulations existing at the time of development including any 
lawful development exaction or impact fee regulations existing at the time of development. 
Section 4. The OWNERS recognize that the present infrastructure is not sufficient to 
support development on the Land (as contemplated by the above land use plan amendment) and 
that actual construction or provision of said Development Infrastructure must take place 
concurrent with any development. The OWNERS further recognize that PARKLAND will not 
provide water and wastewater service, nor will it construct the necessary roads to access and 
service the Land, and that OWNERS shall be required to make separate arrangements to assure 
that these two infrastructure elements meet applicable standards. Moreover, OWNERS shall be 
responsible for providing or paying their fair share of any other portion of the Development 
Infrastructure not specifically provided for herein. 
Nothing contained herein shall limit the authority of PARKLAND or any other 
appropriate governmental authority to require additional road dedications or construction where 
necessary based upon the impact of any proposed development or the need for safe and adequate 
access. 
Section 5. OWNERS specifically recognize the need for additional recreational 
facilities and open space. The OWNERS shall be required to provide the necessary funds and/or 
land dedications to provide such facilities. The OWNERS recognize and accept the five (5) acre 
per thousand residents public parks and open space standard adopted by PARKLAND, as well as 
the additional minimum five (5) acres per thousand residents private open space requirement.

The OWNERS also recognize the importance and applicability of the City-wide multi-purpose 
trail system and agree that all development shall contain adequate connections to the system, and 
that this shall require dedication of appropriate easements and construction of trails (by 
OWNERS) within and/or adjacent to development, as appropriate to create a City-wide trail 
system; any such dedication shall be in addition to the public parks and open space dedication 
referred to above. OWNERS recognize and agree that these requirements will benefit their 
development. 
Section 6. The OWNERS recognize that existing public education facilities will not 
be satisfactory to support the future development which will appear on the Land, and that new 
facilities must be made available concurrent with any development on the Land. Prior to any 
land use change on the Land as contemplated in Section 2 and then again prior to any rezoning 
on the Land, the OWNERS shall work with the Broward County School Board and PARKLAND 
to identify and donate an appropriate school site acceptable to the Broward County School Board 
(School Board). The size of the required donation to be determined as follows: total approved 
development 300 units or less — 8 acres; total approved development 301 to 350 acres — 10 acres; 
total approved development 351 units or greater — 12 acres. The donation shall occur prior to 
recordation of any plat and shall be a condition of any rezoning ordinance. The OWNERS shall 
cooperate to the fullest extent possible to locate a school site adjacent to any selected school site 
on adjacent parcels; it is the intent of the parties to combine, if feasible, the dedication required 
in the annexation agreement between PARKLAND and Triple H Ranch Property, LTD and 
Debuys Property Investment Group, LTD dated (by PARKLAND) April 20, 2007 and any 
amendments thereto. If such a school site is not required by the School Board at the time of any 
rezoning on the Land, as a result of a lack of student demand or any other reason, and provided

the Land Use Plan Amendment as contemplated in Section 2 has been approved, OWNERS shall 
deed marketable title to 4 contiguous acres, at a location acceptable to PARKLAND, for any use

any approval by PARKLAND of a plat for any portion of the Land. This 4 acre donation shall

herein or otherwise existing at the time of rezoning and shall not be a credit against any school 
impact fee or mitigation requirement or any impact fee of any kind. The determination as to the 
need for the site for a school shall be made prior to the approval of any rezoning for any part of 
the Land. If the School Board does not request the donation or dedication of a school site prior 
to the adoption of a rezoning ordinance, the OWNERS shall then be required to deed marketable 
title to the 4 acre parcel described above to PARKLAND prior to the recordation of any plat for 
any portion of the Land and this requirement shall be a condition of any rezoning ordinance or 
plat approval. The parcel shall undergo a Phase 1 environmental review (and Phase 2) to assure 
its environmental suitability; if not found suitable an alternate site shall be dedicated as 
acceptable to PARKLAND. This requirement shall be included in a restrictive covenant 
approved by the City Attorney for PARKLAND and the County Attorney and recorded in the 
public records within ten (10) days of approval of the land use plan amendment.
