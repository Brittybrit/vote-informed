 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 12 Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the
 13 Code of Ordinance is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and
 14 land development orders; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in the Code of
 17 Ordinances have been satisfied; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the parcel possesses a Future Land Use Designation of Community
 20 Facilities ("CF"), Residential 3 ("R-3), Commercial Business("B") and Office ("0") and a Zoning
 21 Designation of Community Facility ("CF"); and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the petitioner desires to construct a double-sided freestanding sign at the
 24 entrance to Pine Trails Park and a wall sign on the Community Center; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Board on January 10, 2008 voted to recommend
 27 approval of the petition by a 6-1 vote; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony
 30 presented by the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of the
 31 various City of Parkland review agencies and staff; and
 32
 33 WHEREAS, the City Commission has made the following findings of fact:
 34
 35 1. Environmental Considerations: No adverse impacts to the natural environment are
 36 expected since the property has already been
 37 disturbed.
 38
 39 2. Comprehensive Plan The proposed project is consistent with the Land
 40 Considerations: Use Element of the Comprehensive Plan.
 41
 42 3. Zoning Considerations: The existing landscaping meets or exceeds the Code
 43 standards as reviewed by the Environmental
 44 Resources Director. The freestanding sign meets the
 45 intent of the sign code. The wall sign does not meet
 46 the Code standards as it relates to the maximum sign
 47 area permitted however a variance application is
 48 being reviewed and processed concurrently.

 1 4. Community Appearance The double-sided freestanding sign shall match in
 2 Considerations: appearance with the signage at Holmberg Road and

 5
 6 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 7 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 8
 9 SECTION 1. The Site Plan Amendment and Community Appearance Board Application
 10 for a double-sided freestanding sign at the entrance to Pine Trails Park and a wall sign for the
 11 Community Center is hereby APPROVED on the real property as described in Exhibit A, subject
 12 to the conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition to the general requirements
 13 otherwise provided by ordinance:
 14
 15 1. This approval is based upon the signs drawn by Baron Sign Manufacturing.
 16 2. The stone materials on the freestanding sign shall match the stone material, font
 17 style and colors on the signs located at the intersection of Holmberg Road and
 18 University Drive.
 19 3. If additional landscape is required once the freestanding sign is constructed, the
 20 YMCA will be responsible for all associated costs.
 21
 22 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
