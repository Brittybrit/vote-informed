 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO APPROVE THE
 5 ADOPTION OF ADMINISTRATIVE AND USER FEE AND
 6 CHARGE SCHEDULES FOR SERVICES, ACTIVITIES AND
 7 USE OF FACILITIES PROVIDED BY THE CITY OF
 8 PARKLAND; PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, Chapter 19, Section 19-11 of the Code of Ordinances authorizes the
 11 City Commission to adopt administrative and user fees and charges; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that the costs associated with certain
 14 City programs, activities and/or facilities should be borne by the person(s) utilizing the
 15 service or should be commensurate with the cost incurred by the City to perform the
 16 services;
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland offers a variety of services to the residents,
 19 businesses and visitors, for which the City charges a minimal fee or has not charged a fee;
 20
 21 WHEREAS, it is the intent of the City Commission to transfer the costs of these
 22 City services to the individuals who are requesting the services from the City; and
 23
 24 NOW, THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 25 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 26
 27 SECTION 1. The foregoing "whereas" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 28 being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein.
 29
 30 SECTION 2. That the City Commission of the City of Parkland approves the
 31 attached administrative and/or user fee schedules, which copies are attached hereto and
 32 incorporated as if set forth in full as exhibits.
 33
 34 SECTION 3. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are hereby
 35 repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 36
 37 SECTION 4. If any clause, section, other part or application of this Resolution is
 38 held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or invalid, in part of
 39 application, it shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions or applications of this
 40 Resolution.
 41
 42 SECTION 5. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 43 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
