 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-32
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 4 FLORIDA TO APPROVE UTILIZATION OF THE FLORIDA STATE CONTRACT
 5 # 070-001-01-1-7 FOR THE PURCHASE OF THREE (3) FLEX FUEL PICK-UP
 6 TRUCKS IN AN AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED $50,000.
 7
 8 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland is committed to protecting and
 9 preserving the environment; and
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the Leisure Services Department will be providing necessary
 12 maintenance and janitorial services to enhance the level of service provided to
 13 residents at City parks through the creation of Leisure Service Aides; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS; there are eleven (11) Leisure Service Aides that will be
 16 operating at multiple parks simultaneously that will require vehicles to transport
 17 staff and materials; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, staff has researched the availability and pricing of
 20 environmentally friendly vehicles; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the use of flex-fuel vehicles utilizing ethanol produce fewer
 23 emissions than traditional unleaded fuel vehicles; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the Ford F150 Flex Fuel is the best available vehicle and is
 26 available through state contract # 070-001-01-1-7; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the Finance Department has verified that the requested funds
 29 are available through the contingency capital account; and
 30
 31 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 32 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 33
 34 SECTION 1. That the state contract amount of $16,646 per vehicle for
 35 three (3) Ford F150 Flex Fuel trucks is hereby accepted and that purchase orders
 36 for said vehicles in that amount is hereby approved.
 37
 38 SECTION 2. Finance and Leisure Services Directors are hereby authorized
 39 to take any and all such actions to make changes as may be necessary to
 40 accomplish the purpose of this resolution.
 41
 42 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 43 adoption.
