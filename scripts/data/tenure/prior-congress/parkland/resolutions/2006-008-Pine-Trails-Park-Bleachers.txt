 RESOLUTION NO. 2006- 08
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING FUNDS TO BE USED
 TO PURCHASE 16 BLEACHERS AT PINE TRAILS PARK.
 WHEREAS, Hurricane Wilma caused significant damage to the
 bleachers at Pine Trails Park; and
 WHEREAS, the Parks & Recreation Department is requesting approval to
 replace 16 bleachers in the spectator area of the soccer fields at Pine Trails Park; and
 WHEREAS, this item has been previously bid by the City of Jacksonville,
 Florida, Bid# SC-0549-03, which the City of Parkland will piggyback off of; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commissioners believe these replacement
 bleachers are in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials do hereby approve the funds for
 16 bleachers for Pine Trails Park.
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 1 DAY OF February , 2006.
