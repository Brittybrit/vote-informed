 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-72
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING THE STATE
 OF FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
 JOINT PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT FOR THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND STATE ROAD 7 BEAUTIFICATION PROJECT
 BETWEEN THE CITY OF PARKLAND AND THE STATE OF
 FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION;
 PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR
 SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE
 DATE.
 WHEREAS, the Florida Department of Transportation (FDOT) and the City of
 Parkland (City) are desirous of having the City make certain improvements in connection
 with Financial Management (FM) Number 421952-1-58-01 for landscape, hardscape
 and/or irrigation improvements at State Road 7 (US 441) from State Road 869 (Sawgrass
 Expressway) (M.P. 22.645) to 254 Feet South of Holmberg Road (M.P. 23.500) Parkland,
 Florida. Refer to Exhibit "A" for a detailed Scope of services and Exhibit "B" for a set of
 Production Plans attached hereto and made a part hereof; and,
 WHEREAS, the improvements are in the interest of both the City and FDOT and
 it would be more practical, expeditious, and economical for the City to perfoini such
 activities; and,
 WHEREAS, the City is prepared to enter into the attached maintenance
 agreement with the FDOT, which agreement is hereinafter referred to as the Joint
 Participation Agreement; and
 WHEREAS, the City and FDOT will provide for the beautification of certain
 public right-of-ways, more particularly described on Exhibit A (the Beautified Property);
 and
 WHEREAS, the City also recognizes the need to continually maintain the
 Property and to comply with its Joint Participation Agreement with FDOT (FDOT
 Agreement); and
 WHEREAS, in order to enable the City to justify the expenditure of funds for the
 landscape improvements to the Beautified Property, as set forth in the FDOT Agreement,
 the City is seeking assurances from the Association that it will maintain the Beautified
 Property; and

 WHEREAS, Association is prepared to give said assurances and the purpose of
 this Agreement is to memorialize this undertaking;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND FLORIDA, THAT;
 Section 1: The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached hereto are
 hereby incorporate herein.
 Section 2: The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves the
 Joint Participation Agreement with the State of Florida Department of Transportation for
 the City of Parkland State Road 7 Beautification Project.
 Section 3: The appropriate City officials are hereby authorized and directed to
 execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this resolution.
 Section 4: All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are
 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 Section 5: If any clause, section, other part or application of this Resolution is
 held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or invalid, in part of
 application, it shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions or applications of this
 Resolution.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 18 DAY OF JUNE , 2008.
