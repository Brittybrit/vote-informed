 1 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 2 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 3
 4 SECTION 1. The Site Plan Amendment Application of HB Partners I, LLC for the addition
 5 of four (4) light fixtures to the existing driving range located within Heron Bay is hereby
 6 APPROVED on the real property as described in Exhibit A, subject to the conditions of approval
 7 contained herein, which are in addition to the general requirements otherwise provided by
 8 ordinance:
 9
 10 1. This approval is based upon the Photometric Plan entitled "Heron Bay Driving
 11 Range Lighting Design with Full Spill-Glare Control" by Techline Sports Lighting
 12 dated September 15, 2008 and stamp-dated and received by the Planning and
 13 Zoning Department on November 5, 2008; "3 Fixture Crossarm Assembly" by
 14 Techline Sports Lighting dated February 27, 2003 and stamp-dated and received by
 15 the Planning and Zoning Department on November 5, 2008; "Integral Ballasted
 16 Luminaire Spill Control Optics" by Techline Sports Lighting stamp-dated and
 17 received by the Planning and Zoning Department on November 5, 2008.
 18 2. Additional landscape materials are required to be planted adjacent to the lights to
 19 conceal their appearance in addition to the north side and west side of the golf
 20 course to shield the view from the adjacent residential properties. The applicant
 21 shall submit a Landscape Plan to the Environmental Resources Department in
 22 conjunction with the submittal of the building permit for the light fixture to ensure the
 23 proposed landscape plan screens the lights and provides addition buffer to the
 24 residential properties.
 25 3. The lights are to be shut off no later than 10:00 p.m. unless a Special Events
 26 Permit application, with associated fee, has been submitted to the City of Parkland
 27 to allow for the use of the lights after this time.
 29 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediat:ly upon adoption.
