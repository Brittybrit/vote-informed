 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-24
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA FOR APPROVAL OF THE PARKLAND
 5 FIRE STATION NO. 2 PLAT; APPROXIMATELY 4.907 ACRE
 6 PARCEL LOCATED ON THE NORTH SIDE OF TRAILS END
 7 BETWEEN PINE ISLAND ROAD AND NOB HILL ROAD; CASE
 8 NO. 03-PT-07.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 11 Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the
 12 Code of Ordinances, as adopted by the City of Parkland, is authorized and empowered to
 13 consider petitions related to zoning and land development orders; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, at a regular meeting of the City Commission a Public Hearing was held to
 16 hear testimony for and against the petition of the City of Parkland for approval of the Parkland
 17 Fire Station No. 2 Plat consisting of a 1.12 acre fire station parcel and a 3.787 acre
 18 archeological site known as Margate Blount; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the proposed plat was reviewed by the Planning and Zoning Board on
 21 February 21, 2008 which voted 7-0 to recommend approval/denial of the petition; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds the proposed plat to be in conformance with all
 24 applicable regulations including the City's concurrency and other land development
 25 regulations.
 26
 27 NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the City Commission of the City of Parkland,
 28 Florida, that:
 29
 30 The Parkland Fire Station No. 2 Plat is approved with the following conditions:
 31
 32 1. This approval is based on the plat entitled; "Parkland Fire Station No. 2 Plat",
 33 prepared by Paul E. Brewer and Associates, Inc. and dated received by the
 34 Planning and Zoning Department on February 5, 2008.
 35
 36 2. All applicable conditions set forth in the final County Staff Report must be
 37 incorporated in the final version of the plat to be recorded.
 38
 39 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 19th day of March 2008.
