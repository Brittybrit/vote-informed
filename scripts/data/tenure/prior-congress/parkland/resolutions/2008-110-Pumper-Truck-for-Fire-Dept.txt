 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING THE PURCHASE OF A
 5 PIERCE DX 3000 GALLON KENWORTH COMMERCIAL TANKER
 6 IN THE AMOUNT OF $167,517.00, PIGGYBACKING LAKE COUNTY
 7 CONTRACT #08-0803, PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 8
 9 WHEREAS, monies were budgeted in the City of Parkland Fiscal Year 2009 Budget
 10 for the purpose of purchasing a fire tanker truck; and
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the identical vehicle was competitively bid by Lake County with a
 13 resulting price of$175,117.00; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the bid is open for other municipalities to use in a cooperative manner;
 16 and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, Staff has identified a piggybacking opportunity to purchase the new fire
 19 tanker truck utilizing Lake County Bid Contract#08-0803; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes the purchase of the fire
 22 tanker truck is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 23
 24 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 25 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 26
 27 SECTION 1. The appropriate City officials do hereby approve the purchase of a
 28 Pierce DX 3000 gallon Kenworth Commercial tanker piggybacking Lake County contract
 29 #08-0803 at a price of$167,517.00.
 30
 31 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately upon
 32 its passage and adoption and the City Manager is authorized to take such actions as necessary
 33 to consummate said purchase.
