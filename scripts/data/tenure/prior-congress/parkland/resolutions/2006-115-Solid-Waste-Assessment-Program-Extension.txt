 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-115
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA
 REQUESTING THAT THE BROWARD COUNTY REVENUE
 COLLECTOR AND BROWARD COUNTY PROPERTY APPRAISER
 AGREE TO THE ALTERNATE MARCH 1st DEADLINE UNDER
 FLORIDA STATUTE 197.3632(3)(a) FOR ADOPTION OF THE
 RESOLUTION OF INTENT TO USE THE UNIFORM METHOD OF
 COLLECTING NON-AD VALOREM SPECIAL ASSESSMENTS FOR
 SOLID WASTE AND RECYCLING SERVICES; PROVIDING FOR THE
 MAILING OF THIS RESOLUTION; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland, Florida (the "City") is contemplating
 the imposition of special assessments for the provision of solid waste and
 recycling services; and
 WHEREAS, the City intends to use the uniform method for collecting
 non-ad valorem special assessments for the cost of providing solid waste and
 recycling services to property within the incorporated area of the City as
 authorized by section 197.3632, Florida Statutes, as amended, because this
 method will allow such special assessments to be collected annually
 commencing in November 2007, in the same manner as provided for ad valorem
 taxes; and
 WHEREAS, the City does not have sufficient time to duly advertise for
 four consecutive weeks our intent to adopt a resolution at a public hearing per
 section 197.3632(3)(a), Florida Statutes, prior to January 1st; and
 WHEREAS, section 197.3632(3)(a), Florida Statutes, provides an
 alternative deadline of March 1st for adoption at public hearing of the resolution
 of intent, a preliminary draft copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit A,
 provided said alternative deadline is agreed to by the Broward County Tax
 Collector and Broward County Property Appraiser's Office; and
 WHEREAS, the City, the Broward County Property Appraiser's Office
 and the Broward County Tax Collector have worked closely on non-ad valorem
 assessment projects and have a coordinated process including a ready database
 already in place that will allow for easy implementation of a solid waste non-ad
 valorem assessment without creating an undue burden on any party by the
 requested time extension;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:

 Section 1. The City of Parkland respectfully requests that the
 Broward County Property Appraiser and Broward County Tax Collector agree to
 allow the City of Parkland to submit the approved resolution of intent by March
 1st as provided under section 197.3632(3)(a), Florida Statutes.
 Section 2. Upon adoption, the City Clerk is hereby directed to send a
 copy of the Resolution by United States mail to the Broward County Tax
 Collector and the Broward County Property Appraiser.
 Section 3. The City Manager is hereby directed to secure the
 approval of the Broward County Tax Collector and the Broward County Property
 Appraiser in writing or by e-mail in response to the request herein.
 Section 4. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
