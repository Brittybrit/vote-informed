 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007- 41
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO AUTHORIZE THE
 5 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE
 6 ATTACHED PRE-ANNEXATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN
 7 THE CITY OF PARKLAND AND THE PROPERTY
 8 OWNERS OF APPROXIMATELY 700 ACRES OF LAND
 9 WITHIN THE WEDGE PIECE; PROVIDING FOR
 10 CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND
 11 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the Hillsboro Canal separates the Wedge Piece from the rest
 15 of Palm Beach County and serves to isolate the Wedge Piece from Palm Beach
 16 County; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, this separation from Palm Beach County makes it difficult for
 19 the Wedge Piece to be serviced by Palm Beach County and creates difficulties in
 20 development for the property owners; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, based upon the above stated conditions, the property owners
 23 of the Wedge Piece are desirous of revising the Broward County/Palm Beach
 24 County boundary to reflect these conditions and to include the Wedge Piece
 25 within the jurisdiction of the City of Parkland rather than Palm Beach County; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland is desirous of ensuring that the features
 28 which are associated with present City development, to wit: high quality
 29 amenities, lushly landscaped berms, roadways and public spaces, low densities,
 30 and high quality architectural and design details for commercial development,
 31 are continued on the Wedge Piece; and
 32
 33 WHEREAS, the property owners of approximately 700 acres agree with
 34 the City of Parkland goals for any development of their property and desire a
 35 Pre-Annexation Agreement; and
 36
 37 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best
 38 interest of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City
 39 of Parkland to enter into a Pre-Annexation agreement with the property owners
 40 of approximately 700 acres to ensure that the Wedge property is properly
 41 developed.
 42
 43 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 44 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
