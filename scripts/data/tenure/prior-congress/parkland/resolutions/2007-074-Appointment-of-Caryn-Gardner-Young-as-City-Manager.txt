 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-74
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPOINTING CARYN GARDNER-
 5 YOUNG AS CITY MANAGER AND AUTHORIZING THE
 6 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO NEGOTIATE AN
 7 AGREEMENT FOR CITY MANAGER SERVICES BETWEEN
 8 THE CITY OF PARKLAND AND CARYN GARDNER-YOUNG.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds it necessary to retain the services of a
 11 City Manager for the City of Parkland to provide the City with professional City
 12 Manager services; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, Caryn Gardner-Young has been selected by the City Commission as
 15 the person to provide said services; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that Caryn Gardner-Young is fully
 18 qualified to provide said services.
 19
 20 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 21 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 22
 23 Section 1. That Caryn Gardner-Young has been appointed as City Manager
 24 and the appropriate City officials are herby authorized to negotiate compensation terms
 25 for the City Manager services between the City and Caryn Gardner-Young.
 26
 27 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 28 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 29
 30 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 20 DAY OF June , 2007.
