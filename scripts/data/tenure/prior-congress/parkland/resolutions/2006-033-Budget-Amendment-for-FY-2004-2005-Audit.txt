 RESOLUTION NO. 2006- 33
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING A SUPPLEMENTAL BUDGET
 ADJUSTMENT WITHIN THE 2005-2006 BUDGET AS ESTABLISHED
 BY ORDINANCE NO. 2005-17; AUTHORIZING THE TRANSFER OF
 FUNDS WITHIN THE GENERAL, PARK IMPROVEMENTS AND
 CAPITAL PROJECTS FUNDS; AND AUTHORIZING AMENDMENTS
 BASED ON THE AUDITED FUND BALANCES RESULTING IN BUDGET
 ADJUSTMENTS TOTALING $3,121,303.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, in accordance with
 Ordinance No. 2005-17, Section 3, recognizes the necessity for the transfer of funds
 from one fund, account or department to another to provide and maintain a proper
 accounting for all financial transactions; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland authorizes the transfer
 of funds within the General, Park Improvements and Capital Projects Funds; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland authorizes the
 amendment to the General, Park Improvements and Capital Projects Funds to reflect
 the audited financial report for the fiscal year ended September 30, 2005.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the City of Parkland hereby authorizes the transfer of funds
 and changes to fund balance within the General, Park Improvements & Capital
 Projects Funds as described in Exhibit A attached hereto and made a part hereof.

<<PG>>
 Section 2. That the 2005-2006 Budget, as established by Ordinance No.
 2005-17 shall be amended to reflect the changes in accordance with Exhibit A attached
 hereto.
 Section 3. That all Resolutions or parts of Resolutions in conflict herewith are
 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 Section 4. That this Resolution shall be in full force and effect immediately
 upon it passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 6 DAY OF April , 2006
 MICHAEL U INE, MA OR
 Attest:
 SANDRA COUZZO, C.MCITY CLERK
