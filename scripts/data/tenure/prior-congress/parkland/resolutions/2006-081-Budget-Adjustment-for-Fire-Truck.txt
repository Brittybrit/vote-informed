 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-81
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING A BUDGET ADJUSTMENT IN
 THE AMOUNT OF $170,000 WITHIN THE 2005-2006 BUDGET AS
 ESTABLISHED BY ORDINANCE NO. 2005-17; AUTHORIZING THE
 APPROPRIATION OF ADDITIONAL REVENUES AND THE
 ALLOCATION OF THOSE REVENUES FOR THE PURPOSE OF
 PURCHASING A FIRE ENGINE; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY;
 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, in accordance with
 Ordinance No. 2005-17, recognizes the need for periodic adjustment of the budget to
 provide and maintain for the City's needs; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland authorizes the
 appropriation of additional unanticipated revenue within the General Fund; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland authorizes the
 amendment of the General Fund to increase the original appropriation of $280,000 for
 the purchase of a rescue/mini-pumper to $450,000 for the purchase of a fire
 engine/pumper,
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the City of Parkland hereby authorizes the budget increase in
 the General Fund of $170,000 as described in Exhibit A attached hereto and made a
 part hereof.

<<PG>>
 Section 2. That the 2005-2006 Budget, as established by Ordinance No.
 2005-17 shall be amended to reflect the changes in accordance with Exhibit A attached
 hereto.
 Section 3. That all Resolutions or parts of Resolutions in conflict herewith are
 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 Section 4. That this Resolution shall be in full force and effect immediately
 upon it passage and adoption.
