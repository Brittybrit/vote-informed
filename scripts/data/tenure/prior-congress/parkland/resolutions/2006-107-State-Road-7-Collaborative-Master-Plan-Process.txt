 RESOLUTION NO. 2006 - 107
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SUPPORTING THE STATE ROAD 7
 COLLABORATIVE MASTER PLAN PROCESS.
 WHEREAS, the Treasure Coast Regional Planning Council and South Florida
 Regional Planning Council have created a State Road 7 Collaborative Master Plan (the
 Master Plan); and
 WHEREAS, the Master Plan has been the product of many meetings and much
 citizen input, including a charrette held at Cypress Hammock Park in Coral Springs; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has reviewed the
 Master Plan and supports many aspects of the Master Plan; and
 WHEREAS, however, the City of Parkland City Commission believes that for its
 residents, further study is appropriate before a full endorsement of the Master Plan;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and made
 a part hereof
 Section 2. The City of Parkland supports the Master Plan process and the
 efforts of the South Florida Regional Planning Council and the Treasure Coast Regional
 Planning Council. The City Commission also believes that several aspects of the Plan are
 worthy of endorsement, but it is not prepared to endorse the document as a whole. The
 City Commission believes that further study is needed from the standpoint of the City of
 Parkland and commits to conducting that further study and responding to the South
 Florida Regional Planning Council and the Treasure Coast Regional Planning Council as
 part of this worthy effort.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 1 DAY OF November , 2006.
