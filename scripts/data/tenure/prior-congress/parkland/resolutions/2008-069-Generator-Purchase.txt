 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-69
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING THE
 5 PURCHASE OF A GENERATOR IN THE AMOUNT OF
 6 $35,316, PIGGYBACKING FLORIDA SHERIFFS
 7 ASSOCIATION BID AWARD #07-15-0827, PROVIDING
 8 AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 9
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City entered into a lease Agreement with Florida Towers, LLC for the
 12 purpose of constructing and operating a cell tower at Pine Trails Park; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the aforementioned Agreement requires Florida Towers, LLC to provide
 15 a generator to the City for the purpose of powering the Amphitheater in the event of power
 16 failure; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, Florida Towers, LLC will be providing the City the monies necessary to
 19 purchase the generator; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, City staff has identified an opportunity to purchase a generator utilizing
 22 Florida Sheriffs Association Bid Award #07-15-0827 in the amount of $35,316; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes the purchase of a
 25 generator is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 26
 27 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 28 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 29
 30 SECTION 1. The City Manager or designee is hereby authorized to piggyback Florida
 31 Sheriffs Association Bid Award #07-15-0827 and purchase a generator in the amount of
 32 $35,316.
 33
 34 SECTION 2. The purchase is contingent upon and subject to Florida Towers,
 35 LLC providing funding to the City in the amount of$35,316.
 36
 37 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately
 38 upon its passage and adoption.
 39
 40 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 2 DAY OF JULY 2008.
