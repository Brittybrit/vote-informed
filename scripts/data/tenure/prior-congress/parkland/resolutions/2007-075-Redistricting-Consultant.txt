 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-75
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AWARD ONE CONSULTANT OR
 5 CONSULTING FIRM FOR PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FOR
 6 REDISTRICTING THE COMMISSION DISTRICTS FOR THE CITY
 7 OF PARKLAND AND TO AUTHORIZE THE CITY MANAGER TO
 8 EXECUTE A CONTRACT WITH SCOTT RICHAFtDS AND
 9 MAUREEN TARTAGLIONE; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS;
 10 PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
 11 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13 WHEREAS, City staff advertised a request for the purpose of having a list of a
 14 minimum of three firms to provide professional services for the redistricting of the
 15 commission districts per the Charter of the City of Parkland; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, in order to comply with the requirements of the City Charter, staff also
 18 solicited proposals from universities within the State of Florida; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the City Commission reviewed the proposals from the three responses
 21 in addition to the proposals from faculty of the University of Miami and a letter of interest
 22 from a University of Florida faculty member; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the City Commission authorizes the City Manager or her designee to
 25 negotiate a contract with Scott Richards and Maureen Tartaglione to provide the
 26 aforementioned professional services; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best interest of
 29 the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland for the
 30 City Manager to enter into such contract; and
 31
 32 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 33 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 34
 35 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as being
 36 true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein.
 37
 38 SECTION 2. The City Commission hereby hires Scott Richards and Maureen
 39 Tartaglione as a consultant to provide professional services for redistricting the Commission
 40 Districts for the City of Parkland.
 41
 42 Section 3. The City Manager is hereby authorized and directed to negotiate and enter
 43 into a contract with Scott Richards and Maureen Tartaglione.
