 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO RANK THE THREE
 5 (3) SHORT LISTED FIRMS TO PROVIDE GRANTS
 6 MANAGEMENT SERVICES AND TO AUTHORIZE THE
 7 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIAL TO NEGOTIATE A
 8 CONTRACT WITH THE FIRST RANKED FIRM;
 9 PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR
 10 AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 11
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the existing contract for grants management services expired
 14 on December 6, 2007; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, City Staff advertised an RFQ for the purpose of having a
 17 company provide grant management services; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, a selection committee consisting of the City Manager, Parks
 20 & Recreation Director, Planning & Zoning Director, Environmental Resources
 21 Director and Parks & Recreation Program Manager read and reviewed three (3)
 22 submitted proposals and following presentations scored the three (3) companies
 23 in accordance with the Consultants' Competitive Negotiation Act ("CCNA"); and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, upon final scoring and ranking, the City Commission
 26 authorizes the appropriate City Official to negotiate a contract with the first
 27 ranked firm to provide the aforementioned parks master planning services; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, if the City Manager or her designee is unable to negotiate
 30 such a contract with the first ranked company, the City Manager is authorized to
 31 negotiate with the next ranked company until a contract is approved by the City
 32 Commission; and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best
 35 interest of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City
 36 of Parkland for the City Manager to enter into such a contract; and
 37
 38 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 39 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 40
 41 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 42 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits
 43 attached hereto are hereby incorporate herein.
