 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-63
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION
 4 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO
 6 PIGGYBACK A BROWARD COUNTY
 7 CONTRACT WITH SOUTHEAST
 8 UNDERGROUND UTILITIES CORP. FOR THE
 9 PURPOSE OF INSTALLING A NEW TRAFFIC
 10 SIGNAL HEAD AT THE INTERSECTION OF
 11 STATE ROAD 7 AND HOLMBERG ROAD IN
 12 THE AMOUNT OF $28,868.00, PROVIDING AN
 13 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 14
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland desires the installation of a second left
 17 turn lane on Holmberg Road at the intersection of State Road 7; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, a condition of the Parkland Commons Site Development Plat
 20 requires the Developer, Monroe Prestige Group, Inc. to assume the costs
 21 associated with the addition of the left turn lane; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, Florida Department of Transportation requires that an
 24 existing traffic signal head be replaced with a new one that will support a new left
 25 turn lane on Holmberg Road at the intersection of State Road 7; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the aforementioned Developer has agreed to reimburse the
 28 City for the full cost of the traffic signal replacement; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, Staff has identified a piggybacking opportunity to install the
 31 new traffic signal head utilizing a Broward County contract with Southeast
 32 Underground Utilities Corp. in the amount of $28,868.00; and
 33
 34 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes that
 35 piggybacking the Broward County Contract and utilizing Southeast Underground
 36 Utilities Corp. to replace the existing traffic signal head is in the best interest of
 37 the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 38
 39 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 40 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 41
 42 SECTION 1. The Interim City Manager is hereby authorized to
 43 piggyback the Broward County contract with Southeast Underground Utilities Corp.
 44 for the purpose of installing a new traffic signal head in the amount of$28,868.00.
 45
 46 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 47 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
