 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-120

 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 4 FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING A CHANGE ORDER WITH WEEKLEY ASPHALT
 5 AND PAVING, INC. NOT TO EXCEED $70,000.00 FOR THE PURPOSE OF
 6 REPLACING EXISTING ASPHALT SIDEWALKS ALONG THE SOUTH SIDE
 7 OF HOLMBERG ROAD WITH CONCRETE SIDEWALK, PROVIDING AN
 8 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, On May 21, 2008 via Resolution No. 2008-42 the City Commission authorized
 11 the City Manager to piggyback a Broward County Contract with Weekley Asphalt and Paving, Inc.,
 12 for the purpose of constructing improvements along Holmberg Road between State Road 7 and
 13 University Drive; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the Contractor was able to complete the project under budget; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, Staff recommends utilizing the additional funds available to replace as much of
 18 the remaining sections of asphalt sidewalk along the south side of Holmberg Road between State
 19 Road 7 and Riverside Drive as possible with concrete sidewalk; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, Staff recommends approval of a change order to Weekley Asphalt and
 22 Paving, Inc. not to exceed $70,000.00 for the purpose of replacing the aforementioned asphalt
 23 sidewalk, and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes that a change order to replace
 26 as much of the existing asphalt sidewalk on the south side of Holmberg Road between State Road
 27 7 and Riverside Drive is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 28
 29 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 30 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 31
 32 SECTION 1. The City Commission hereby authorizes the City Manager to execute a
 33 change order increase with Weekley Asphalt and Paving, Inc., not to exceed $70,000.00 to replace
 34 as much of the existing asphalt sidewalk on the south side of Holmberg Road between State Road 7
 35 and Riverside Drive as possible with concrete sidewalk.
 36
 37 SECTION 2. The aforementioned Change Order shall not exceed the total funds available
 38 within the Project Budget.
 39
 40 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
 41
 42 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 19 DAY OF November , 2008.
