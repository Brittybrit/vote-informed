FIRST AMENDMENT
TO
LAND LEASE AGREEMENT
THIS AMENDMENT TO LAND LEASE AGREEMENT (hereinafter referred to as
"Amendment ") is executed this q,.24 day of , 2009, by and
between FLORIDA TOWERS, LLC, a Florida limited liabih company (hereinafter
referred to as "Lessee ") and CITY OF PARKLAND, a municipality (hereinafter
referred to as "Lessor ").
WHEREAS, Lessee and Lessor entered into that certain Land Lease Agreement dated
May 19, 2008 (hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement ") whereby Lessor leased to
Lessee a certain portion of the real property located at 10555 Trails End, City of
Parkland, County of Broward, State of Florida (hereinafter referred to as the "Leased
Property "); and
WHEREAS, Lessor and Lessee desire to amend the Agreement to correct the Site
Number and Site Name, modify the requirements regarding the existing light poles,
modify the after park hours accessibility, modify the requirements regarding the
generator, dugouts and concrete sidewalk and eliminate the requirement of a removal
bond.
NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency
of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties hereto covenant, agree and bind themselves
to the following modifications to the Agreement:
1. Site No. and Site Name located in the footer of page 1 of the Agreement is hereby
amended as follows:
Site No.: FL0205
Site Name: Pine Trails Park
2. Paragraph 6. Facilities; Utilities; Access. (a) of the Agreement is hereby
amended to reflect that the requirement for Lessee to relocate the original two
light poles located on the T -ball field to a proposed multi -use field shall instead be
performed by Lessor at a new location as determined by Lessor. Lessee shall only
be responsible for reimbursing Lessor for the costs which shall not exceed Eighty -
Three Thousand Seven Hundred Seventy -Three Dollars and Eighty -One Cents
($83,773.81). Upon receipt of said amount, Lessee shall be released from any and
all obligations concerning the original two light poles.

<<PG>>
3. Paragraph 6. Facilities; Utilities; Access. (c) of the Agreement is hereby
amended to replace the first sentence of this subsection with the following:
Lessee, Lessee's employees, agents and contractors shall have access to the
Premises during park hours; after park hours Lessee, Lessee's employees, agents
and contractors shall be permitted access by use of the key issued by Lessor and
provided that the Leased Property is properly secured upon departure. Further,
Lessee shall be responsible for any keys lost or damaged while in Lessee's
possession and for any losses or damages resulting from Lessee's possession of a
key. In the event it is determined by Lessor that Lessee, Lessee's employees,
agents and contractors are not properly securing the Lessors property as a result of
accessing Lessors property after hours, Lessor may terminate after hours access
by key upon thirty (30) days written notice to Lessee. In the event Lessor
exercises its right to terminate after hours access by key, Lessor shall make
available a city employee upon reasonable notice to provide after hours access
and any cost(s) to Lessor of providing such access shall be paid by Lessee.
4. Paragraph 6. Facilities; Utilities; .Access. (g) of the Agreement is hereby
amended as follows:
In lieu of Lessee's requirement to purchase and install a generator powerful
enough to service the Lessee's Facilities and the Amphitheater located on
Lessor's Land, Lessor has agreed to accept Lessee's payment of Thirty -Five
Thousand Three Hundred Sixteen and no /100 Dollars ($35,316.00) paid on July 2,
2008. Having received said payment, Lessor releases Lessee from any and all
obligations concerning the generator. Lessor shall be responsible for the purchase
and installation of the generator including providing the transfer switch and
connecting the generator to the commercial power source including any costs
involved with such connection.
5. Paragraph 6. Facilities; Utilities; Access. (h) of the Agreement is hereby
amended as follows:
In lieu of Lessee's obligation to design and build eight (8) dugouts at the T -ball
fields and installation of a concrete sidewalk, Lessor has agreed to accept
Lessee's dugout payment of Fifteen Thousand and no /100 Dollars ($15,000.00)
paid on December 18, 2008 and Lessee's concrete sidewalk payment of
approximately Thirty Thousand and no /100 Dollars ($30,000.00) paid on
September 29, 2008. Having received said payments, Lessor releases Lessee from
any and all obligations concerning the dugouts and concrete sidewalk.

6. Paragraph 11. Removal Process and Bond for. Removal. of the Agreement is
hereby deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:
Paragraph 11. Removal Process. Upon termination of this Agreement for any
reason, Lessee shall remove Lessee Facilities located on the Premises and restore
the Premises to its condition prior to this Agreement as set forth in Paragraph 6
within thirty (30) days of the termination.
7. This Amendment shall be governed by and construed and enforced in accordance
with the laws of the state in which the Property is located without regard to
principles of conflicts of law.
8. Except as specifically set forth in this Amendment, the Agreement is otherwise
unmodified and remains in full force and effect and is hereby ratified and
reaffirmed. In the event of any inconsistencies between the Agreement and this
Amendment, the terms of this Amendment shall take precedence.
9. This Amendment shall be effective as of the date last executed by both parties.
