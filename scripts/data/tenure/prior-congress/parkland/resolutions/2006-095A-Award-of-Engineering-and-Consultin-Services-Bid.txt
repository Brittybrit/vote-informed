 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-95A
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO RANK THE TEN
 5 SHORT LISTED ENGINEERING COMPANIES TO
 6 PROVIDE PROFESSIONAL ENGINEERING SERVICES
 7 ON A CONTINUING BASIS AND TO AUTHORIZE THE
 8 CITY MANAGER TO EXECUTE A CONTRACT WITH THE
 9 THREE (3) TOP RANKED FIRMS; PROVIDING FOR
 10 SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE
 11 DATE.
 12
 13
 14 WHEREAS, City Staff advertised an RFP for the purpose of having a list
 15 of pre-qualified firms to provide engineering services on a continuing basis; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, a selection committee consisting of the City Manager,
 18 Assistant City Manager, Planning Director, Public Services Director and
 19 Environmental Resources Director read and reviewed sixteen (16) submitted
 20 proposals and picked their top eight (8) companies in accordance with the
 21 Consultants' Competitive Negotiation Act ("CCNA"); and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the nine staff picked and one City Commission short listed
 24 engineering companies were provided an opportunity to introduce their team and
 25 provide an oral presentation to the City Commission for ranking; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, upon final scoring and ranking, the City Commission
 28 authorizes the City Manager or his designee to negotiate and enter into a
 29 contract with the top three (3) ranked firms to provide the aforementioned
 30 continuing engineering services; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, if the City Manager or his designee is unable to negioate
 33 such a contract with any of the three top ranked companies, the City Manager is
 34 authorized to negotiate with the next ranked company until three contracts are
 35 entered into; and
 36
 37 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best
 38 interest of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City
 39 of Parkland for the City Manager to enter into such contracts; and
 40
 41 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMUISSION
 42 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 43
 44 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 45 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits
 46 attached hereto are hereby incorporate herein.
