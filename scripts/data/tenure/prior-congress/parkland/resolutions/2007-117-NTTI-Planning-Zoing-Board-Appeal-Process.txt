 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, DENYING AN APPEAL
 5 FILED BY NATIONAL TREE TRIMMING, INC,
 6 CONCERNING THE PLANNING AND ZONING BOARD'S
 7 DECISION TO AFFIRM THE PLANNING AND ZONING
 8 DIRECTOR'S DECISION TO DENY THE ISSUANCE OF
 9 AN OCCUPATIONAL LICENSE I BUSINESS TAX
 10 RECEIPT (CASE NO. 02-AA-07) PURSUANT TO
 SECTION 22-328 OF THE CITY CODE OF ORDINANCES;

 14 WHEREAS, on June 25, 2007 National Tree Trimming Inc., ("NTTI") submitted
 15 an application for an Occupational License / Business Tax Receipt for forestry ( the
 16 "Application"); and
 17 WHEREAS, the property subject to the Occupational License / Business Tax
 18 Receipt Application, Administrative Appeal and Planning and Zoning Board Appeal is
 19 located at 6770 NW 84 Avenue, Parkland, Florida 33067 (the "Property"); and
 20 WHEREAS, the parcel size of the subject Property is 2.73 acres; and
 21 WHEREAS, the Future Land Use designation of the subject Property is E .4. and
 22 the zoning designation of the subject Property is AE-1; and
 23 WHEREAS, on July 19, 2007 a determination was made by the Planning and
 24 Zoning Director that the Application did not meet the City of Parkland Code of
 25 Ordinances (the "City Code") for the issuance of a forestry Occupational License /
 26 Business Tax Receipt; and
 27 WHEREAS, on August 15, 2007 NTTI submitted an Administrative Appeal
 28 application appealing the Planning and Zoning Director's decision (Petition No. 01-AA-
 29 07) ;and
 30 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 22-330 of the City Code, the Planning and
 31 Zoning Board heard an Administrative Appeal of the Application on September 20,
 32 2007 and subsequently issued a Final Order (attached as Exhibit "A") affirming the
 33 denial of the Application and Occupational License / Business Tax Receipt ; and
 34 WHEREAS, NTTI submitted a Planning and Zoning Board Appeal application on
 35 October 11, 2007 pursuant to Section 22-328 of the City Code ; and

 1 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission (the "City Commission")
 2 affirms the decision of the Planning and Zoning Board pursuant to Section 22-328 of
 3 the City Code by a 4-0 vote.
 4 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 5 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 6 Section 1: The application for the Appeal of the Planning and Zoning Board's
 7 decision is hereby denied and the Occupational License / Business Tax Receipt for
 8 NTTI is denied.

 10 its passage and adoption.
