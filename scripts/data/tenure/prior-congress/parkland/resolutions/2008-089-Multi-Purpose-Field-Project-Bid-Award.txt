 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-89
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE AWARD OF BID NO.
 5 2008-11 TO PRO GRASS, LLC, FOR THE PURPOSE OF
 6 CONSTRUCTING A MULTI-PURPOSE FIELD AND INSTALLING
 7 PRO GRASS SYNTHETIC GRASS AT PINE TRAILS PARK IN THE
 8 AMOUNT OF $416,000.00, PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, monies were budgeted in the City of Parkland Fiscal Year 2008
 11 Budget for the purpose of constructing field improvements at Pine Trails Park; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, Staff determined that a Multi-Purpose field constructed with
 14 synthetic turf would best meet the City's current needs; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, on August 3, 2008, the City advertised Bid # 2008-11 and on August
 17 12, 2008, the City opened three submitted bids; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, Pro Grass LLC, submitted the lowest responsible and responsive
 20 bid in the amount of$416,000.00; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, Staff reviewed and determined that the bid submitted by Pro Grass
 23 LLC, is in compliance will all the applicable requirements set forth in the bid package;
 24 and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes that utilizing Pro
 27 Grass, LLC to construct a Multi-Purpose Field and install synthetic grass at Pine Trails
 28 Park is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 29
 30 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 31 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 32
 33 SECTION 1. The City Commission hereby awards Bid No. 2008-11 to Pro
 34 Grass, LLC in the amount of$416,000.00.
 35
 36 SECTION 2. The appropriate city officials are hereby authorized and directed to
 37 execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this resolution.
 38
 39 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
 40
 41 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 20 DAY OF August , 2008.
