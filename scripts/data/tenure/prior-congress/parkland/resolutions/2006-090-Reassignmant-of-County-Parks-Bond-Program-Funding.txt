 RESOLUTION 2006-90
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SUPPORTING THE
 REASSIGNMENT OF BROWARD COUNTY PARKS AND
 OPEN SPACE BOND PROGRAM LAND ACQUISITION
 FUNDING FROM THE BEATY PARCEL TO A QUIGLEY
 PARK EXPANSION PROJECT.
 WHEREAS, the Comprehensive Plan for the City of Parkland requires the City
 Commission to consider the acquisition of additional parks and open space lands for the
 benefit of the general public; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland ("City") approved of
 the Broward County Board of County Commissioners' ("County") action to allocate
 County Parks and Open Space Bond Program funding toward acquiring the
 approximately 9-acre Beaty parcel as part of the City's Greenbelt Expansion project; and
 WHEREAS, the County has undertaken lengthy efforts to acquire the Beaty
 parcel and has now determined that the owner of the Beaty parcel is no longer willing to
 sell the parcel within the limitations of the County Bond Program; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that it is critical for the residents and
 citizens of Northwest Broward County to be able to receive the benefits of the funding
 allocated from the Bond Program for an alternative, eligible project in the same
 geographic area of Broward County;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials do hereby approve and recommend that
 the Broward County Board of County Commissioners reallocate the funding reserved for
 the Beaty green space land acquisition project to an alternative, approximately 5-acre
 Quigley Park open space land acquisition project in order to expand public park facilities
 benefiting all residents of Northwest Broward County.
 Section 2. That the City Clerk is directed to provide a copy of this Resolution to the
 Broward County Board of County Commissioners, County Administrator, and County
 Land Preservation Advisory Board.
 Section 3. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately upon
 its passage and adoption.
