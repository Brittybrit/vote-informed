 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-80
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, PROVIDING FOR SPECIAL
 MASTER PROCEDURES; PROVIDING FOR ASSESSMENT OF
 CODE ENFORCEMENT ADMINISTRATIVE COSTS;
 PROVIDING FOR ASSESSMENT OF SPECIAL MASTER
 ADMINISTRATIVE COSTS; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS;
 PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
 EFFECTI9VE DATE.
 WHEREAS, pursuant to Ordinance No. 2006-01 of the City of Parkland, the
 City appointed Special Masters to hear code enforcement matters brought pursuant to the
 authority of Chapter 162, Florida Statutes; and
 WHEREAS, the Code Enforcement Special Master possesses the jurisdiction and
 authority to hear and decide alleged violations; and
 WHEREAS, each case before the Special Master is presented by the local
 governing body attorney or by a member of the administrative staff of the local
 governing body. If the local governing body prevails in prosecuting a case before the
 Special Master, it shall be entitled to recover all costs incurred in prosecuting the case
 before the Special Master and such costs may be included in the lien authorized under
 Section 162.09(3), Florida Statutes.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 SECTION 1. The foregoing"WHEREAS"clauses are confirmed and ratified
 as being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached hereto
 are hereby incorporate herein.
 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves
 Resolution 2008-80 where the Special Master shall assess an administrative cost of$150.00
 for each case, due to lack of compliance is scheduled on the Special Master Code
 Enforcement Agenda. The Special Master, in his/her discretion, may reduce said
 administrative cost where he/she finds the$150.00 does not represent the City's costs.
 SECTION 3. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are hereby
 repealed to the extent of such conflict.

 SECTION 4. If any clause, section, other part or application of this Resolution is
 held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or invalid, in part of

 SECTION 5. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
