 18 Preserve;

 21 Hurricane Wilma;
 22

 24 and/or experience with similar projects to carry out the proposed program; and
 25
 26 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 27 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 28
 29 Section 1. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby authorizes the City
 30 of Parkland to enter into a grant agreement between Broward County and the City of
 31 Parkland in the amount of $10,740.00 and authorizes the appropriate City officials to
 32 execute it.
 33
 34 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
