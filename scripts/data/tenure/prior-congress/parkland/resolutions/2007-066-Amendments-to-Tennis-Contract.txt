 FIRST
 AMENDMENT
 TO
 INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT FOR PROVIDING A TENNIS
 PROFESSIONAL
 BY AND BETWEEN
 THE CITY OF PARKLAND AND
 UNITED STATES TENNIS FEDERATION
 THIS FIRST AMENDMENT is made and entered into this /3 day of
 , 2007 by and between the City of Parkland, a municipal corporation organs ed and
 existing under the laws of the State of Florida, which municipality is fully located within the
 boundaries of Broward County, Florida (hereinafter referred to as "CITY") and United States
 Tennis Federation (hereinafter referred to as "USTF").
 WITNESSETH:
 WHEREAS, on or about May 17, 2006 the CITY contracted with USTF to provide
 tennis related instruction and related services to the public through a comprehensive tennis
 program; and
 WHEREAS, the May 17, 2006 agreement is hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement";
 and
 WHEREAS, the parties are desirous of amending the hours that USTF administrative
 staff are available and USTF will pay for forty percent(40%) of court maintenance costs; and
 NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the sums hereinafter set forth and for other
 good and valuable consideration, the receipt and legal sufficiency of which are hereby
 acknowledged, it is agreed as follows:
 1. The above recitals are true and correct and incorporated herein.
 2. Section 2 2.01 (13) of the Agreement shall be amended as follows:
 (13) Except as hereinafter provided, it shall be the responsibility
 of CONTRACTOR to supervise all tennis courts for public use, and keep
 the tennis courts open to the public according to the following schedule:
 Monday-Friday 8.00 AM - 12:00 PM
 4:00 PM - 10:00 PM
 Saturday and Sunday 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
 The tennis pro shop and administrative staff will be open and available
 open to the public according to the following schedule:
 1

<<PG>>
 Monday, Tuesday, 8:00 AM — 11:00 AM
 Thursday
 Monday—Friday 4:00 PM— 7:00 PM
 Saturday and Sunday 8:00 AM— 12:00 PM
 Hours of supervision may be expanded beyond these prescribed times when
 busy periods require additional supervision. These additional hours will be
 at the discretion of CONTRACTOR. Decisions regarding closing the
 tennis shop due to inclement weather,which includes the Thorguard
 Lightning Prediction System, will be made by the CONTRACTOR, or his
 designee. CONTRACTOR is required to abide by the rules,regulations
 and policies of the Parks &Recreation Department of the City of Parkland
 governing the use of the CITY'S recreational facilities.
 3. The following shall be added to Section 2 2.01 as (30) of the Agreement:
 (30) The contractor shall be required to pay for forty percent (40%) of court
 maintenance costs (net replacement, rollers, court lights, windscreens,
 etc.)within thirty(30) days of being presented a bill by the CITY.
 4. The following shall be added to Section 2 2.01 as (31) of the Agreement:
 (31) The Contractor shall be liable for any damages to the facility or any
 equipment during hours of operation where the damage is the result of the
 failure of Contractor to use reasonable care in supervising the activities at
 the facility.
 5. Section 5 5.01 (1) of the Agreement shall be amended as follows:
 (1) Maintenance of the tennis facilities and its surrounding areas will be
 the responsibility of CITY. These maintenance duties include building
 maintenance (electrical, plumbing, painting, ceilings, walls, floors, roof
 and public restrooms, etc.); sixty percent (60%) of court maintenance
 (net replacement, rollers, court lights, windscreens, resurfacing etc.);
 resurfacing and general maintenance (shrubbery and lawn care, garbage
 pick-up etc.).
 5. Except as amended herein, all other provisions of the Agreement shall remain in full
 force and effect.
