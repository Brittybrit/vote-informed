 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-125
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE CITY OFFICIALS TO
 APPOINT A REPRESENTATIVE TO THE POLICE PENSION BOARD.
 WHEREAS, the Police Pension Plan requires a Board of Trustees which shall consist
 of five (5) persons; two (2) of whom shall be legal residents of the City of Parkland and who
 shall be appointed by the City Commission and the fifth member who is chosen by the
 majority of the other four Trustees and this person's name shall be submitted to the City
 Commission for appointment; and
 WHEREAS, Vice Mayor Weissman and Commissioner Smith currently serve on the
 Police Pension Board as the legal residents and the City Commission's appointments to the
 Police Pension Board; and
 WHEREAS, Mr. Michael Merlob currently serves on the Police Pension Board as the
 fifth member; and
 WHEREAS, the terms for Vice Mayor Weissman, Commissioner Smith and Mr.
 Merlob have expired on the Police Pension Board; and
 WHEREAS, the Parkland City Commission wishes to reappoint Vice Mayor
 Weissman and Commissioner Smith as their appointments to the Police Pension Board and
 the City Commission wishes to re-appoint Mr. Merlob should the Trustees submit his name
 for re-appointment.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials do hereby approve the following
 Parkland Police Retirement Plan representative: Vice Mayor Mark Weissman and
 Commissioner Jay Smith.
 Section 2. That the appropriate City officials do hereby approve the appointment
 of Mr. Merlob should the Police Pension Board Trustess submit his name for reappointment.
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately
 upon its passage and adoption.
