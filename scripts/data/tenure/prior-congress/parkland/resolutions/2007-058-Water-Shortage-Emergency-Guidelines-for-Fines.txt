 RESOLUTION NO. 2007 - 58
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SETTING FORTH A SCHEDULE OF
 ACCEPTED PENALTIES FOR VIOLATION OF WATER SHORTAGE
 EMERGENCY GUIDELINES AS SET FORTH IN SECTIONS 11-86
 THROUGH 11-95 OF THE CITY CODE.
 WHEREAS, Sections 11-86 through 11-95 of the City Code provide regulations for
 restrictions on water usage during water shortages and similar emergencies; and
 WHEREAS, Section 11-95 contains a maximum fine of$500 per incident for violation of
 water shortage regulations; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland wishes to set forth an accepted set of penalties for
 violation of the regulations contained in Sections 11-86 through 11-95 (collectively referred to as
 the Regulations); and
 WHEREAS, this shall serve as guidance to the City Attorney for prosecution of water
 shortage violations and place on record with the County Court the City's recommended penalties;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 SECTION 1. The above representations are true and correct and made a part hereof
 SECTION 2. The following is a schedule of acceptable penalties for violation of the
 Regulations, if the fine is paid within thirty (30) days:
 First Violation $125 Fine
 Second Violation $250 Fine
 All Violations thereafter $500 Fine
 SECTION 3. Where fines are mentioned in any notices of violations, this schedule may
 be referred to as acceptable settlement of the notice. In addition, in cases of specific
 circumstances of any case, unusual or special circumstances, or where evidentiary matters dictate,
 the City may settle cases for other amounts.
 SECTION 4. This Resolution shall become effective upon passage.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 16 day of May , 2007.
