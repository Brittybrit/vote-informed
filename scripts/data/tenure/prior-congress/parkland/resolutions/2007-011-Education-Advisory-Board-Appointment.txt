 RESOLUTION NO. 2007- 11
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPOINTING ONE (1) REGULAR
 MEMBER AND ONE ALTERNATE AT LARGE MEMBER TO
 THE EDUCATIONAL ADVISORY BOARD.
 WHEREAS, in accordance with the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances, the
 City Commission shall appoint members to the Educational Advisory Board.
 WHEREAS, there are two (2) regular Advisory Board seats vacant, one to be
 appointed by Commissioner Weissman with the other to be filled by the District 3
 Commissioner which seat is presently vacant.
 WHEREAS, Alternate Board Member Christine Hunschofsky was appointed as a
 Standing Board Member, leaving a vacancy for the Alternate 2 seat.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That Commissioner Weissman hereby makes his individual
 appointment to the Education Advisory Board as follows:
 Commissioner Weissman: William D. Matthewman
 Section 2. That the City Commission hereby makes a temporary District 3
 appointment to the Education Advisory Board as follows:
 District 3 appointment: Kristina Stein
 Section 3. That the City Commission hereby appoints the following Alternate
 2 position as follows:
 Alternate 2: Richard French
 Section 3. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS A 18 I OF March , 2007.
