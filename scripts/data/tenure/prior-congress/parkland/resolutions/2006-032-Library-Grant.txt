 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-32
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SUPPORTING THE AWARD OF A
 GRANT FOR A CHILDREN'S BILINGUAL STORY PROGRAM
 WHEREAS, The Parkland Library offers a diverse schedule of programs for the
 residents, and
 WHEREAS, The State Library of Florida via the Pinellas Public Library
 Cooperative, Inc. offered a grant for a celebration called El dia de los ninos/E ldia de los
 libros, and
 WHEREAS, this event fits with our annual Week of the Young Child programs
 during the first week of April, 2006,
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA: that we accept the
 award of a $500 grant from the Pinellas Public Library Cooperative, Inc. to be used for a
 bi-lingual (Spanish-English) story program and related activities.

 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
