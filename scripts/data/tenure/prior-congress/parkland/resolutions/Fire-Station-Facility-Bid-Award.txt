 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-88
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 4 FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE AWARD OF BID NO. 2008-09 TO
 5 BRADANNA, INC., FOR THE PURPOSE OF CONSTRUCTING A PUBLIC
 6 SERVICES/FIRE STATION FACILITY IN THE AMOUNT OF $1,949,000,
 7 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 8
 9 WHEREAS, monies were budgeted in the City of Parkland Fiscal Year 2008 Budget for the
 10 purpose of constructing a public services/fire station facility at the Old City Hall site; and
 11
 12 WHEREAS, Bradanna, Inc., submitted the lowest responsive and responsible bid in the
 13 amount of $1,949,000; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the bid submitted by Bradanna, Inc. is in compliance with all the applicable
 16 requirements set forth in the bid package; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes that awarding the bid to
 19 Bradanna, Inc. for the purpose of constructing a public services/fire station facility at the Old City
 20 Hall site is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 21
 22 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 23 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 24
 25 SECTION 1. The City Commission hereby awards Bid No. 2008-09 to Bradanna, Inc. for
 26 the purpose of constructing a public services/fire station facility in the amount of$1,949,000.
 27
 28 SECTION 2. The City Manager is hereby authorized and directed to execute an Agreement
 29 with Bradanna, Inc. for the aforementioned purpose.
 30
 31 SECTION 3. The City Manager is hereby authorized to approve individual change orders
 32 provided the sum of all change orders do not exceed the project budget.
 33
 34 SECTION 4. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
 35
 36 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 20 DAY OF August , 2008.
