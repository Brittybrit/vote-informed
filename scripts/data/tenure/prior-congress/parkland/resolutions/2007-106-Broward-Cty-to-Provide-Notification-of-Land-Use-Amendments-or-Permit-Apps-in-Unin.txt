 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, REQUESTING BROWARD COUNTY TO
 5 PROVIDE ADVANCE NOTIFICATION TO THE CITY OF
 6 PARKLAND OF ANY LAND USE PLAN AMENDMENTS OR
 7 DEVELOPMENT PERMIT APPLICATIONS IN
 8 UNINCORPORATED AREAS ABUTTING THE CITY OF
 9 PARKLAND.
 10
 11
 12 WHEREAS, there is significant acreage in unincorporated Broward County
 13 abutting the City of Parkland, which acreage is located so that Parkland would be the only
 14 municipality to which said acreage could be annexed; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, any development in these areas (hereinafter referred to as the
 17 unincorporated areas, depicted on Exhibit A) would have a significant effect upon the
 18 City of Parkland; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland wishes to be apprised of any proposed land use
 21 plan amendments or development permit applications for these unincorporated areas;
 22
 23 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 24 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 25
 26 SECTION 1. The above representations are true and correct and made a part
 27 hereof.
 28
 29 SECTION 2. The City of Parkland hereby requests that Broward County provide
 30 advance notification to the City of Parkland of any land use plan amendments or
 31 development permit applications in the unincorporated areas.
 32
 33 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 19 day of September , 2007.
