 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 12 Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the
 13 Code of Ordinance is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and
 14 land development orders; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the petitioner desires to amend the previously approved site plan for the
 17 Parkland Village Green located within the Parkland Village development; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in Code of Ordinances,
 20 have been satisfied; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony
 23 presented by the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of the
 24 various City of Parkland review agencies and staff; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the City Commission has made the following findings of fact:
 27
 28 1. Environmental Considerations: No adverse impacts to the natural environment are
 29 expected since the property has already been
 30 disturbed.
 31
 32 2. Utilities: The proposed request will not affect existing water,
 33 sanitary, sewer and drainage systems.
 34
 35 3. Comprehensive Plan The proposed development is consistent with the
 36 Considerations: Land Use Element of the Comprehensive Plan.
 37
 38 4. Zoning Considerations: Site area, building setbacks, height restrictions,
 39 and landscaping all meet or exceed Code
 40 standards.
 41
 42 5. Community Appearance The building fits within the character of the area
 43 Considerations: and the adjacent shopping centers, and includes
 44 pedestrian amenities.

 1 SECTION 1. The Site Plan Amendment and Community Appearance Board
 2 Application of RM Parkland Village Square, LLC for the modification of the Parkland Village
 3 Square within the Parkland Village development is hereby APPROVED and the Master Sign
 4 Plan is hereby APPROVED on the real property as described in Exhibit A, subject to the
 5 conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition to the general requirements
 6 otherwise provided by ordinance:
 7
 8 1. This approval is based upon the plans entitled "Parkland Village Square"
 9 signed and sealed on November 5, 2007 and received by the Planning and
 10 Zoning Department on November 30, 2007.
 11 2. All conditions and approvals as outlined in Ordinance Numbers 2004-21 and
 12 2004-22 and Resolution Numbers 2005-41 and 2005-42 not specifically
 13 amended by this application shall govern the development.
 14 3. The Master Sign Plan shall permit the same font and same color except for
 15 Federally trademarked logos that may be permitted as long as they fit within
 16 the permitted sign band area. City Commission approval shall be required if
 17 any further changes are requested from the approved Master Sign Plan.
 18 4. Root barriers shall be installed for all trees located within five (5) feet of a
 19 sidewalk or roadway.
 20 5. The City shall inspect the landscape buffer on a periodic basis to insure that it
 21 is being properly maintained and growing in at a normal rate. The City may
 22 require the applicant to add additional landscaping material to any area that is
 23 not growing in at a normal rate as would be expected based on the approved
 24 landscaping plan. Failure to maintain the landscaping and entry area or
 25 respond to a City request as set forth herein shall constitute a violation of this
 26 approval and the City's Code of Ordinances.
 27 6. The party responsible for the landscape buffers shall employ a full-time
 28 grounds / building maintenance person to maintain the entry area as required
 29 herein.
 30 7. This approval is based upon the application and associated color schemes
 31 provided by the applicant which include the following:

 2 8. Any substitution of materials shall require City approval prior to the installation of
 3 such material. For recordation purposes, a waiver of site plan application shall
 4 be submitted as long as the material proposed to be substitute is determined to
 5 be comparable as that approved. If such material is determined to not be
 6 comparable, a site plan amendment application shall be submitted with all
 7 applicable fees. The project shall receive a final certificate of occupancy no later
 8 than eighteen (18) months from issuance of a building permit. If the Project is not
 9 completed as evidenced by the Certificate of Completion or other statement of
 10 completion by the City within eighteen (18) months of the date of the issuance of
 11 a building permit, then in that event, the building permit referencing the project
 12 shall be null and void and Owner shall be required to apply for new building
 13 permits. Additionally, the City shall not allow inspections and plans review on
 14 such project to occur. Additionally, Owner shall be required to pay a fee equal to
 15 double the building permit fee paid for the original building permit.
 16 9. The applicant is required to enter into an agreement with the City of Parkland,
 17 as prepared by the City Attorney that addresses all appropriate penalties if the
 18 eighteen (18) month deadline is not met.
 19 10.The three (3) parking spaces adjacent to the Mixed Use Building 2 are to be

 25 Use Building 1 connecting the parking area to the existing sidewalk.
