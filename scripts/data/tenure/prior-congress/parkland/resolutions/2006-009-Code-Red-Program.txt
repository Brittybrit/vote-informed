 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-09

 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO AUTHORIZE THE
 5 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE
 6 ATTACHED AGREEMENT FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF A
 7 RESIDENT EMERGENCY NOTIFICATION SYSTEM;
 8 PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR
 9 SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE
 10 DATE.
 11
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City before, during and after Hurricane Wilma was unable
 14 to inform to its residents of the City's status; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, Emergency Communications Network, Inc. operates a
 17 emergency notification system known as CodeRed; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the CodeRed system will allow the City to place prerecorded
 20 telephone calls at high speed to City residents in the need of an emergency; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City Commission approved in its 2005-2006 budget,
 23 expenditure for such a system and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best
 26 interest of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City
 27 of Parkland to enter into an agreement with Emergency Communications
 28 Network, Inc. to ensure that City residents are notified of events occurring within
 29 the City ; and
 30
 31 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMUISSION
 32 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 33
 34 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 35 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits
 36 attached hereto are hereby incorporate herein.
 37
 38 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves
 39 the Agreement between the City and Emergency Communications Network, Inc., a
 40 copy of which is attached hereto and incorporated by reference as if set forth in
 41 full.
 42
 43 SECTION 3. The appropriate City officials are hereby authorized and
 44 directed to execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent
 45 of this resolution.
