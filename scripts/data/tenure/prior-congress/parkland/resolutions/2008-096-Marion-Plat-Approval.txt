 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA FOR APPROVAL OF THE
 5 MARION ESTATES PLAT; APPROXIMATELY 2.63
 6 ACRES GENERALLY LOCATED ON THE SOUTH SIDE
 7 OF NW 72ND STREET, WEST OF NW 87TH AVENUE,
 8 ALSO KNOWN AS CASE NUMBER 02-PT-07.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland,
 11 Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the
 12 Code of Ordinances, as adopted by the City of Parkland, is authorized and empowered to
 13 consider petitions related to zoning and land development orders; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, at a regular meeting of the City Commission a Public Hearing was held
 16 to hear testimony for and against the petition of Mikki Ulrich of Deni Land Surveyors, Agent
 17 and Alexander and Linda Gonzalez, Property Owner requesting approval of the Plat to
 18 subdivide a portion of Tract 16, Section 3, Township 48 South, Range 41 East, Florida Fruit
 19 Lands Company's Subdivision No. 2 into two (2) parcels each larger than one (1) acre in
 20 which the property is zoned AE-2; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the proposed plat was reviewed by the Planning and Zoning Board on
 23 September 11, 2008 which voted 7-0 to recommend approval of the petition; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds the proposed plat to be in conformance with
 26 all applicable regulations including the City's concurrency and other land development
 27 regulations.
 28
 29 NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the City Commission of the City of Parkland,
 30 Florida, that:
 31
 32 The Marion Estates Plat is approved with the following conditions:
 33
 34 1. This approval is based on the plat entitled; "Marion Estates Plat", prepared by Deni
 35 Land Surveyors, Inc. and dated received by the Planning and Zoning Department on
 36 August 21, 2008.
 37
 38 2. All applicable conditions set forth in the final County Staff Report must be
 39 incorporated in the final version of the plat to be recorded.
 40
 41 3. Prior to the issuance of the first building permit for each individual lot, the owner shall
 42 pay the appropriate park impact fee.
 43
 44 4. Any development of the lots shall conform to the City's tree preservation and
 45 landscaping requirements for single-family residences.
