 4 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 5 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 6
 7 SECTION 1. The Site Plan Amendment for the Parkland Commons North commercial
 8 project is hereby APPROVED on the real property as described in Exhibit A, subject to the
 9 conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition to the general requirements
 10 otherwise provided by ordinance:
 11
 12 1. This approval is based upon the plans entitled "Publix at Parkland Commons"
 13 signed and sealed on April 30, 2008 and received by the Planning and Zoning
 14 Department on April 30, 2008 and the Shared Parking Study by Walter H. Keller,
 15 Inc. dated February 7, 2008.
 16 2. All conditions and approvals for the Parkland Commons project not specifically
 17 amended by this application shall govern the development.
 18 3. The property owner shall be responsible for providing updated parking
 19 calculations with each proposed tenant during the building permit process to
 20 ensure adequate parking capacity is available pursuant to the Shared Parking
 21 Study.
 22 4. If the uses identified in the shared parking study substantially change as determined
 23 by the City, the City shall require the shopping center owner / developer to provide
 24 an updated shared parking study to be reviewed by staff, the City's traffic
 25 consultant, at the applicant's expense, and approved by the City Commission.
 26 5. The uses are restricted to those identified on the site plan associated with
 27 Resolution Number 2008-47.
 28 6. No fast food restaurants shall be permitted. A fast food restaurant shall be deemed
 29 any restaurant which is part of a chain of restaurants which generally have "drive
 30 through" service even though the particular restaurant in Parkland Commons does
 31 not have such service.
 32
 33 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediatel upon adoption.
