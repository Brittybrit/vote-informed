 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006- 103
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA REQUESTING THAT
 5 THE BROWARD COUNTY BOARD OF COUNTY
 6 COMMISSIONERS AUTHORIZE A ONE YEAR
 7 EXTENSION TO THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY
 8 OF PARKLAND AND BROWARD COUNTY FOR
 9 COMPLETION OF THE RECREATIONAL FACILITIES AT
 10 PINE TRAILS PARK UNDER THE CHALLENGE GRANT
 11 PREVIOUSLY APPROVED BY THE PARTIES;
 12 PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR
 13 SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE
 14 DATE.
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland and Broward County ("County") entered
 17 into an Agreement for funding of certain recreational facilities within the City's
 18 Pine Trails Park under the County's Challenge Grant program; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the Challenge Grant Agreement requires the approved City
 21 recreational facilities to be completed on or before January 3, 2007; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, the recreational facilities completion date has been delayed
 24 due to a myriad of challenges including a change of scope of services, hurricane
 25 impacts, and increase in costs of materials, and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City has determined that the recreational facilities cannot
 28 be completed within the time period provided; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines that a one year
 31 extension of the Challenge Grant deadline is necessary to complete the
 32 recreational facilities; and
 33
 34 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 35 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 36
 37 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 38 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits
 39 attached hereto are hereby incorporate herein.
 40
 41 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby requests
 42 Broward County Commission to approve an Amendment to the Agreement
 43 between the City and itself to provide monies under the Challenge Grant until
 44 January 3, 2008. .
