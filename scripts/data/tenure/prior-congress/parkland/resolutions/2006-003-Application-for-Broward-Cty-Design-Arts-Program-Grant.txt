 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO AUTHORIZE THE APPROPRIATE
 5 CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE BROWARD CULTURAL
 6 COUNCIL DESIGN ARTS PROGRAM GRANT APPLICATION
 7
 8 WHEREAS, Broward County maintains a grant program entitled "Design Arts
 9 Program";
 10
 11 WHEREAS, there are grant funds allocated and available from this program to
 12 assist the City in improving or upgrading the aesthetics of the physical environment
 13 within the City;
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland, Florida desires to create a community
 16 wide sign design for the City of Parkland;
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland desires a coordinated and community
 19 cased planning approach to the design and identity for signage throughout the
 20 City;
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City demonstrates it has sufficient staff resources, technical
 23 expertise, and/or experience with similar projects to carry out the proposed program;
 24 and
 25
 26 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 27 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 28
 29 Section 1. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves
 30 the Broward County Council Design Art Program Grant application for the
 31 creation of a community wide sign design for the City of Parkland and authorizes
 32 the appropriate City officials to execute it.
 33
 34 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 35 adoption.
