 13 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland, Florida, pursuant
 14 to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the Code of Ordinance is authorized
 15 and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and land development orders; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the petitioner desires to remove non-conforming parking lot lighting structures and
 18 install new parking lot lighting poles and fixtures in compliance with the Code of Ordinances at 5901
 19 Hillsboro Road; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, on February 9, 2006, the Planning and Zoning Board voted unanimously (7-0) to
 22 recommend approval of the petitioner's request with additional conditions of approval;
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in the Code of Ordinances, have been
 25 satisfied; and

 29 agencies and staff; and

 32 implementation of the conditions as set forth herein):

 1 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 2 PARKLAND,FLORIDA that:
 3
 4 SECTION 1. The Site Plan Amendment and Community Appearance Board Application of Magnolia-
 5 Star Parkland LP to remove the non-conforming parking lot lighting structures and to install new parking lot
 6 lighting poles and fixtures in compliance with the Code of Ordinances within the Shoppes of Parkland is
 7 hereby APPROVED on the real property as described in Exhibit A, subject to the conditions of approval
 8 contained herein,which are in addition to the general requirements otherwise provided by ordinance:
 9
 10 1. This approval is based upon the Photometrics Plan entitled "Shoppes of Parkland" dated November
 11 30, 2005 and Parking Luminaires Plans undated but both received by the Planning and Zoning
 12 Department on January 4, 2006.
 13 2. All lighting shall conform to Section 4-53 of the City's Code of Ordinances. If the petitioner fails to
 14 comply, upon notice from the City of Parkland, the deficiency shall be corrected within sixty (60)
 15 days from notice.
 16 3. All conditions of the Shoppes of Parkland Site Plan or Site Plan Amendments not amended by this
 17 site plan shall apply.
 18 4. The lights poles to remain on site shall be in compliance with the existing City Code of Ordinance by
 19 being no higher than twenty (20) feet in height as evidenced by the City's Planning and Zoning
 20 Department receipt of a land surveyor's certification.
 21 5. Petitioner shall bring the site into compliance with all previous site plan approvals, for those items
 22 that do not require a Site Plan Amendment approval within ninety(90) days from the date of this Site
 23 Plan Amendment approval.
 24 6. Petitioner shall bring the site into compliance with all previous site plan approvals, for those items
 25 requiring site plan amendment approval within six (6) months from the date of this Site Plan
 26 Amendment approval.
 27 7. Petitioner shall provide the Planning and Zoning Department a certified cost estimate with a letter of
 28 credit for all necessary site work prior to the City Commission approval.
 29 8. Failure to comply with these conditions shall constitute a violation of this approval and the City Code.
 30
 31 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
