 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-116
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO
 5 PIGGYBACK A CITY OF NORTH MIAMI BEACH CONTRACT WITH
 6 ARROW ASPHALT AND ENGINEERING, INC., FOR THE PURPOSE
 7 OF INSTALLING AN 8'-WIDE SIDEWALK ALONG THE EAST SIDE
 8 OF PINE ISLAND ROAD FROM TRAILS END TO NOB HILL ROAD
 9 IN THE AMOUNT OF $99,950, PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City is required to install an 8'-wide sidewalk along the east side
 12 of Pine Island Road between Trails End and Nob Hill Road as part of the Pine Trails
 13 Park Phase II project; and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the County as a condition of the plat approval, requires that the
 16 sidewalk be constructed prior to allowing occupancy of the Community Center and
 17 Amphitheater; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, funding for said project is available within the capital improvement
 20 budget; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, Staff has identified a piggybacking opportunity to install the 8'-wide
 23 sidewalk along the east side of Pine Island Road from Trails End to Nob Hill Road
 24 utilizing a City of North Miami Beach contract with Arrow Asphalt and Engineering, Inc;
 25 and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes that installing an 8'-
 28 wide sidewalk along the east side of Pine Island Road from Trails End to Nob Hill Road
 29 is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 30
 31 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 32 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 33
 34 SECTION 1. The City Manager is hereby authorized to piggyback the City
 35 of North Miami Beach contract with Arrow Asphalt and Engineering, Inc. for the purpose
 36 of installing an 8'-wide sidewalk along the east side of Pine Island Road between Trails
 37 End and Nob Hill Road in the amount of$99,950.
 38
 39 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 40 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
