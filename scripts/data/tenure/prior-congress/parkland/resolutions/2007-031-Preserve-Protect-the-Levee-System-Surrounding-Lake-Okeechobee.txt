 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-31
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 4 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, URGING THE
 5 FEDERAL GOVERNMENT AND THE
 6 LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF FLORIDA TO
 7 TAKE ANY AND ALL ACTION NECESSARY TO
 8 PRESERVE AND PROTECT THE LEVEE SYSTEM
 9 SURROUNDING LAKE OKEECHOBEE;
 10 PROVIDING FOR CONFLICT; PROVIDING FOR
 11 SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
 12 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 13
 14 WHEREAS, in 2005, as result of Hurricane Katrina, the United States was
 15 exposed to the severity of the damage and devastation that could result from the
 16 failure of a levee system; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, Lake Okeechobee is surrounded by the 140-mile long
 19 Herbert Hoover Dike, and failure of the dike could have a catastrophic impact on
 20 the surrounding areas including, but not limited to Hendry County and the rest of
 21 South Florida; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, in May 2006 an engineering report stated that the dike posed
 24 a grave threat to the people and environment of South Florida; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, in October 2006 the Army Corps of Engineers released a
 27 plan calling for an increase in the stability of the dike; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, in 2006 member of the Florida Congressional Delegation
 30 urged Congress to reclassify the dike as a dam, which would trigger additional
 31 engineering studies and federal funding to ensure protection of Lake
 32 Okeechobee and preservation of the surrounding areas; and
 33
 34 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 35 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 36
 37 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 38 being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached
 39 hereto are hereby incorporate herein.
 40
 41 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby urges
 42 the Federal Government and the Legislature of the State of Florida to take any and
 43 all action necessary to preserve and protect the dike surrounding Lake
 44 Okeechobee. The City Commission further urges the State and Federal
 45 Government to take all steps necessary to ensure the survival of. Lake
 46 Okeechobee and to safeguard the communities surrounding the lake.
