 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 4 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, URGING THE
 5 BROWARD COUNTY BOARD OF COUNTY
 6 COMMISSIONERS TO SUPPORT ALTERNATIVE
 7 B1c IN THE PROPOSED EXPANSION OF FORT
 8 LAUDERDALE/HOLLYWOOD INTERNATIONAL
 9 AIRPORT; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICT;
 10 PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND

 12
 13 WHEREAS, the Fort Lauderdale/Hollywood International Airport is a major
 14 economic engine of Broward County with a $2.9 billion economic impact,
 15 providing 18,000 direct jobs and impacting an additional 28,000 jobs; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the Fort Lauderdale/Hollywood International Airport currently
 18 accommodates 84 aircraft operations an hour, with an average delay of six
 19 minutes; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the Fort Lauderdale/Hollywood International Airport must
 22 accommodate 107 aircraft operations an hour to meet expected transportation
 23 demands in the year 2020; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the Fort Lauderdale/Hollywood International Airport will see
 26 delays of an average 400 percent by the year 2020 if infrastructure
 27 improvements are not made; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the Fort Lauderdale/Hollywood International Airport is a vital
 30 asset to all Broward County citizens.
 31
 32 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 33 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 34
 35 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 36 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein.
 37
 38 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida,
 39 supports the efforts to expand the Fort Lauderdale/Hollywood International Airport.
 40
 41 SECTION 3. The City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida, hereby
 42 urges the Broward County Board of County Commissioners to support the
 43 preferred Alternative Bic, which will meet capacity needs through 2020, does not
 44 displace any homeowners, will allow for the decommission of the diagonal runway,
 45 and will decrease air traffic over John U. Lloyd State Park.
