 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO AUTHORIZE THE APPROPRIATE
 5 CITY OFFICIALS TO REPLACE TREES IN CITY PARKS, PUBLIC
 6 FACILITIES AND PUBLIC STREETS; TO ADOPT THE CITY OF
 7 PARKLAND'S TREE REPLACEMENT PROGRAM INCLUDING A
 8 NEIGHBORHOOD ASSOCIATION GRANT; FOR CONFLICTS;
 9 PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
 10 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 11
 12
 13 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland desires to replace trees that were lost
 14 during Hurricane Wilma in public parks, on public facilities and public streets;
 15 and
 16 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland desires to assist Home Owners
 17 Associations in replacement of trees in rights of way; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland desires to implement this through a City
 20 of Parkland Tree Replacement Program, as attached as Exhibit A; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland desires to have the contractor that is
 23 awarded the tree replacement bid to also honor the same costs for City of
 24 Parkland Residents to replace trees lost during Hurricane Wilma, and
 25
 26
 27 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and
 28 testimony presented at a public meeting; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines this Program to be in
 31 the best interest of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of
 32 the City of Parkland; and
 33
 34 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 35 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 36
 37 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 38 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits
 39 attached hereto are hereby incorporate herein.
 40
 41 SECTION 2. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves
 42 the City of Parkland Tree Replacement Program, a copy of which is attached
 43 hereto and incorporated by reference as if set forth in full.
 44 .
 45 SECTION 3. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are
 46 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
