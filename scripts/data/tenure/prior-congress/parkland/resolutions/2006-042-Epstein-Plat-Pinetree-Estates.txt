 9 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland, Florida,
 10 pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the Code of
 11 Ordinances, as adopted by the City of Parkland, is authorized and empowered to consider petitions
 12 related to zoning and land development orders; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, at a regular meeting of the City Commission a Public Hearing was held to hear
 15 testimony for and against the petition of Paul E. Brewer, Agent and Abby Epstein, Property Owner
 16 requesting approval of the Plat to subdivide Lot 242 in the Pine Tree Estates subdivision into two (2)
 17 parcels each larger than one (1) acre; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the proposed plat was reviewed by the Planning and Zoning Board on May 11,
 20 2006 which voted 7-0 to recommend approval of the petition; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds the proposed plat to be in conformance with all
 23 applicable regulations including the City's concurrency and other land development regulations.
 24
 25 NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida,
 26 that:
 27
 28 The Epstein Plat is approved with the following conditions:
 29
 30 1. This approval is based on the plat entitled; "Epstein Plat", prepared by Paul E. Brewer &
 31 Associates, Inc. and dated received by the Planning and Zoning Department on March 28,
 32 2006.
 33
 34 2. All applicable conditions set forth in the final County Staff Report must be incorporated in the
 35 final version of the plat to be recorded.
 36
 37 3. Prior to the issuance of the first building permit for each individual lot the owner shall pay the
 38 appropriate park impact fee.
 39
 40 4. Any development of the lots shall conform to the City's tree preservation and landscaping
 41 requirements for single-family residences.
 42
 43 5. The petitioner is required to notify the Planning and Zoning Department which option it
 44 chooses and meet one of the following options prior to final certificate of occupancy:
