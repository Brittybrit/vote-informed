 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-56C
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPROVING THE
 OBLIGATIONS OF THE CONCEPTUAL
 PARK/RESOURCE MANAGEMENT PLAN FOR THE
 PROJECT WITHIN THE CITY'S 5 YEAR CAPITAL
 IMPROVEMENT PROGRAM FOR GREEN SPACE SITE
 GS-464 QUIGLEY PARK ADDITION, TRACT G,
 PARKLAND LAKES P.U.D.; PROVIDING FOR
 CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND
 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, The City of Parkland is participating with Broward County in the
 acquisition of a parcel of property known as Site #464 Quigley Park Addition located
 west of Parkside Drive and south of Lox Road in the City of Parkland which is on the
 Broward County Green Space Inventory; and
 WHEREAS, The City of Parkland simultaneously with this Resolution is
 entering into an Interlocal Agreement with Broward County which "Agreement" and will
 be recorded in the public records of Broward County; and
 WHEREAS, The City of Parkland and Broward County have prepared a
 Conceptual Park Management Plan attached hereto as Exhibit "A"; and
 WHEREAS, The City of Parkland wishes to provide the necessary assurances to
 Broward County as part of the Agreement that the obligations set forth within the
 Conceptual Park Management Plan will be complied with by City of Parkland; and
 WHEREAS, The City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best interest
 of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland to
 approve the Conceptual Park Management Plan and to included it within the City's 5
 Year Capital Improvements Program as set forth within the timeline contained in the
 Conceptual Park Management Plan;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND FLORIDA, THAT;
 Section 1: The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits attached hereto are
 hereby incorporate herein.
