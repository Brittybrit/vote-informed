 "BETTER PLACE" SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT
 THIS AGREEMENT, dated this r day of N04egyooer , 2006, is entered into by
 and between the Florida League of Cities, Inc. ("the League"), 301 South Bronough
 Street, Ste. 300, Tallahassee, Florida 32302, and the City of Parkland("the Subscriber)
 6600 University Dr., Parkland, FL 33067.
 The Subscriber desires to subscribe to the "BetterPlace" Complaint/Request
 Tracking System. The Subscriber understands that this service is a collaborative effort
 between the League and Mainline, Inc. The League has the license to give your agency
 the right to use BetterPlace.
 The "BetterPlace" Complaint/Request Tracking software is owned and operated
 by Mainline, Inc. as an Application Service Provider (ASP). This means that the
 development software is accessed through the Internet with no additional software or
 hardware being required at the Subscriber site. The only requirement is that the
 Subscriber has access to the Internet. The software is designed to provide the ability for a
 Subscriber to track complaints and/or requests from citizens generated by a telephone call
 or received through the Internet. The content is stored at a remote facility owned and
 operated by Mainline, Inc. (the "Host"), again eliminating the need for extensive
 computer software or hardware at the Subscriber site. Because the software and data is
 remotely located, the Subscriber can access and update their information from any
 location that has Internet access, assuming proper authority (security).
 1. Responsibilities of the Florida League of Cities
 A. The League will provide the interface between the Subscriber and the
 vendor, Mainline, Inc.
 B. The League will provide training opportunities for the Subscriber, which
 may be on a fee for service basis.
 C. The League will provide implementation and set-up assistance to the
 Subscriber.
 D. The League will monitor and evaluate services provided by the vendor on
 a continuous basis in order to ensure service meets level agreements and to
 improve the application.
 E. The League is responsible for billing the Subscriber and paying hosting
 fees.
 2. Responsibilities of the Subscriber
 A. The Subscriber will designate a coordinator and give the coordinator
 sufficient time to be trained and maintain the system's tables on the
 Subscriber's behalf. The Subscriber understands that the time it takes to
 administer the system will vary depending upon the amount and
 complexity of the information.

 5. Term and Termination of this Agreement
 A. This is an 11-month subscription commencing on the first day of
 November 1, 2006 and terminating on the last day of September 2007.
 This contract will automatically renew on each subsequent October 1
 unless notified by the terms contained in section 5C.
 B. The League will notify the Subscriber 45-60 days prior to the termination
 of this agreement and provide a new agreement with the then existing
 subscription rate. If a new agreement is signed and returned to the League
 by the termination date, service will not be interrupted. The fee for
 BetterPlace is guaranteed not to increase more than 5% a year, regardless
 of the pricing structure, provided there is no interruption of service due to
 non-payment.
 C. Either party may terminate this agreement at any time by sending written
 notification to the other. If the Subscriber terminates the agreement before
 the termination date, 50% of the remaining subscription fees become due
 immediately. If the League terminates the agreement before the
 termination date, the League will return 50% of all funds paid under this
 agreement.
 D. The League may terminate this agreement and the service for non-
 payment of subscription fees. The League will provide 30 days notice to
 the Subscriber prior to terminating the service. If the subscription fee is
 paid within that 30-day period, the service will not be interrupted.
 6. Statement of Ownership
 A. The Subscriber owns their data. The Subscriber may obtain an exported
 data file (in comma delimitated format) of their data from the system at
 anytime. This data is owned by the Subscriber and may be used for any
 purpose they see fit.
 B. Mainline, Inc. owns the actual software code, templates, and appearance
 of the "Better Place" site. The agreement does not grant the right of the
 Subscriber to use Mainline, Inc. or "Better Place" trademarks, trade
 names, or other designations in any promotion of publication without prior
 written consent.
 C. The Subscriber understands that the "Better Place" product is copywrited
 by Mainline, Inc. and will take due care to protect it as a trade secret.

 7. Warranty and Remedies
 A. The Subscriber has had a minimum of 30 days to test the subscription
 service and to determine its usefulness to their particular organization.
 The League does not warrant this service to be fit for any particular
 purpose.
 B. In no event shall either party be liable for consequential, incidental,
 special or indirect damages, as a result of this agreement.
 C. Regardless of the form of action, the League's direct damages under this
 agreement are limited to amounts actually received for services in the
 previous six-month period.
 8. Choice of Law.
 In the event of a dispute between the parties under this Agreement, the parties
 agree to submit to mediation pursuant to Florida law. The mediation will take
 place in Broward County, Florida. The mediator shall be selected from the
 list of certified circuit court mediators in the 17th Judicial Circuit of Florida
 by the parties' mutual agreement. The mediation shall comply with the legal
 requirements for court-ordered mediation in Florida. The agreement shall be
 reduced to writing and provide for its enforcement consistent with Florida
 law. In any event, this Agreement and attachments or schedules thereto shall
 be governed by and construed and enforced in accordance with the laws of the
 State of Florida, and venue for any action shall be Broward County, Florida.
