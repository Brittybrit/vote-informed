 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-114
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPOINTING ONE (1) AT LARGE
 MEMBER TO THE EDUCATIONAL ADVISORY BOARD.
 WHEREAS, in accordance with the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances, the
 City Commission shall appoint members to the Educational Advisory Board.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That Board Member Tiavette Collins-Biggett submitted her
 resignation from her At Large seat on the Education Advisory Board.
 Section 2. That the City Commission hereby appoints the following At Large
 position as follows:
 At Large Christine Hunschofsky
 Section 3. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 6 DAY OF December , 2006.
