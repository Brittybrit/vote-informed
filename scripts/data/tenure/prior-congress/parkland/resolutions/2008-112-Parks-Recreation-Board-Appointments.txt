 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-112
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 FLORIDA, APPOINTING FIVE (5) REGULAR MEMBERS, TWO AT LARGE
 MEMBERS AND TWO ALTERNATE AT LARGE MEMBERS TO THE PARKS &
 RECREATION ADVISORY BOARD.
 WHEREAS, in accordance with the City of Parkland code of Ordinances, the City
 Commission shall appoint members to the Parks&Recreation Advisory Board; and
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That Mayor Udine, Vice Mayor Weissman, Commissioner Smith,
 Commissioner Moskowitz and Commissioner Rosenof hereby make their individual
 appointments to the Parks&Recreation Advisory Board as follows:
 Mayor Udine Howard Davis
 Vice Mayor Weissman Lynda J.Rusinowski
 Commissioner Smith Phil Thornburg
 Commissioner Moskowitz Pete Manso
 Commissioner Rosenof Stephen Ross
 Section 2. That the City Commission hereby appoints the following At Large and
 At Large Alternate positions as follows:
 At Large Eric Young
 At Large Jeb Niewood
 Alternate# 1 -At Large Terry Crowley
 Alternate#2 -At Large Howard Wyner
 Section 3. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately
 upon its passage and adoption.
