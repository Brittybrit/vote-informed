 Recitals
 A. Licensee has an interest in licensing the Property for use as a communications site for the receipt and
 transmission of wireless communications signals;and
 B. In order for Licensee to determine the viability and feasibility of the Property as a communications site, it is
 necessary for employees, agents or independent contractors of Licensee to enter upon and inspect the Property and/or
 temporarily locate communications equipment on the Property to conduct short term radio propagation tests;and
 C. Licensor and Licensee desire to provide for the entry upon, inspection and/or testing activities, concerning
 the Property pursuant to the terms contained in this Agreement.
 NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises, covenants, undertakings, and other
 consideration set forth in this Agreement,Licensor and Licensee agree as follows:
 1. Consent. Licensor consents and agrees that Licensee, its employees, agents and independent contractors
 ("Authorized Parties") may enter upon the Property to conduct and perform some or all of the following activities
 ("Permitted Activities"): surveys, soil borings, installation of temporary groundwater monitoring wells, phase I and
 phase II environmental assessments,asbestos surveys,lead-based paint surveys,wetlands inspections and delineations,
 boundary surveys, radio propagation studies,and such other tests and inspections of the Property which Licensee may
 deem necessary or advisable. Licensee agrees to be responsible for any and all costs related to the Permitted
 Activities,including installation on and operation and removal of equipment on the Property.
 2. Filings. Licensor consents and agrees that the Authorized Parties may make and file applications on
 Licensor's behalf to such local,state and federal governmental entities whose approval may be necessary or advisable
 to enter the Property for Permitted Activities.
 3. Access. Licensor agrees that the Authorized Parties may enter upon the Property during normal business
 hours to perform the Permitted Activities upon execution of this Agreement and may have access to the Property for
 thirty(30)days following the Effective Date.
 4. Signal Interference. The parties acknowledge that the purposes of this Agreement are subject to overriding
 interests of Licensor in avoiding interference with Licensor operations or compromise of site security. Licensee shall
 not interfere in any manner with Licensor operations. Licensee hereby grants to Licensor the right to shut down any
 Licensee equipment and terminate any transmission/reception test which, in the sole opinion of Licensor, results in
 interference with Licensor's signal transmission /reception as well as the authorization to do so by any means and
 without notice.
 5. Removal of Equipment. Licensee agrees that it will, upon the conclusion of the term of this Agreement,
 remove any equipment installed on the Property as a part of the Permitted Activities,repair any damage to the Property
 that might have been caused in connection with any of the Permitted Activities, all to the satisfaction of Licensor,and
 will return the Property to the condition it was in before Licensee's entry onto the Property within ten (10) business
 days after the termination of this Agreement. In the event any equipment installed on the Property by Licensee is not
 timely removed,Licensor will have the right to remove such equipment and Licensee agrees to be responsible for the
 reasonable costs of such removal.
 6. Indemnity. Licensee shall indemnify Licensor from any and all causes of action, liability, claims,penalties
 or other responsibility in any way related to the activities of Licensee pursuant to this agreement or which otherwise are
 undertaken by Licensee on the Property. Licensee proceeds at its own risk in undertaking the testing on the Property.
 Nothing contained herein shall constitute a waiver of the City's sovereign immunity.
 7. Investigation Derived Hazardous Waste Disposal. If any samples or materials(including, but not limited
 to, samples, purge water, cuttings, or boring residues containing Hazardous Substances) taken from the Property for
 investigation or testing purposes are required to be disposed as hazardous waste, Licensor agrees to supply its existing
 EPA I.D.Number or apply for an EPA I.D.Number and execute any hazardous waste manifests required for the lawful
 disposal of such wastes. Licensee agrees that all such disposal will be at Licensee's sole cost and expense.
 8. Insurance. Licensee shall maintain commercial general liability("CGL") insurance covering bodily injury
 and property damage with a combined single limit of at least One Million and 00/100 Dollars ($1,000,000.00) per
