 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-59
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AWARD THE BID
 FOR THE CITY OF PARKLAND TERNBRIDGE PRESERVE
 HURRICANE CLEAN-UP (RFP# 2006-05) TO REMOVE
 LEANING AND DEAD TREES AND DEBRIS AS A RESULT
 FROM HURRICANE WILMA, AND TO AUTHORIZE THE
 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE
 ATTACHED CONTRACT, AND CONTAINING AN
 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the CityCommission of the Cityof Parkland Florida, is authorized
 to award a contract for the City of Parkland Ternbridge Preserve Hurricane Clean-up
 (RFP#2006-05); and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland received•seven (7) bids from the following
 bidders: Sherwood Forest Arbor Care, Contract Site Services, Inc., Phil's Expert Tree
 Service, Inc., BG Katz Nurseries, Inc., Stiles Landscape Company, Man-Con,
 Incorporated, and Arbor Tree & Land, Inc.; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has fully reviewed the
 bids submitted and has determined that the bid from Sherwood Forest Arbor Care is the
 lowest responsible and responsive bid which best meets the criteria set forth in the bid
 packet and that, based upon the base bid price of $44,450.00 and other factors to be
 considered, it is in the best interest of the City to accept said bid.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herin.
 Section 2. That Sherwood Forest Arbor Care is hereby awarded a contract in
 the total amount of $44,450.00 for the City of Parkland Tree Replacement Program
 (RFP#2006-05).
 Section 3. The appropriate City Officials are hereby authorized and directed
 to execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this
 Resolution.
 Section 4. That the Funds shall be taken from the Disaster Clean-up Fund
 Line Item# 001.800.519 506.992.
