 1 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 2 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 3
 4 SECTION 1. The Site Plan and Community Appearance Board petitions of WCI
 5 Communities Inc for Pod 14 Water Features within Parkland Golf & Country Club is hereby
 6 APPROVED, subject to the conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition to
 7 the general requirements otherwise provided by ordinance:
 8
 9 1. This approval is based upon the Common Area Plan View Sheets L1-L7 entitled
 10 "Pod 14 Parcel B-4, B-6, and/or B-5 Parkland Golf & Country Club" dated July 25,
 11 2005 and revised January 9, 2006 stamp-dated and received by the Planning and
 12 Zoning Department on February 14, 2006.
 13 2. As set forth in Ordinance 2002-04 and 2005-23, all conditions of the Parkland
 14 Golf & Country Club not specifically amended by this site plan shall govern the
 15 development.
 16 3. The City shall inspect all landscaping surrounding the water features on a
 17 periodic basis to insure that they are being properly maintained and are growing in
 18 at a normal rate. The City may require the applicant to add additional landscaping
 19 material to any water feature area that is not growing in at a normal rate as would
 20 be expected based on the approved landscaping plans. Failure to maintain the
 21 landscaping and water feature or respond to a City request as set forth herein shall
 22 constitute a violation of this approval and the City Code of Ordinances.
 23 4. The party responsible for the water feature area shall employ a full-time
 24 grounds/building maintenance person to maintain the water feature area as
 25 required herein.
 26 5. If any damage occurs to water features, the petitioner shall repair the damage
 27 within a reasonable period of time, as determined by the City.
 28
 29 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
 30
 31 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 3rd day of May, 2006
