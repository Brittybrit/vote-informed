 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-07
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND FLORIDA TO ADOPT AND
 5 APPROVE PHASE II OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND'S
 6 MULTI-USE TRAIL SYSTEM MASTER PLAN
 7
 8 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of
 9 Parkland, Florida, pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166,
 10 Florida Statutes, and the Code of Ordinances, as adopted by the City of Parkland,
 11 is authorized and empowered to consider matters related to transportation and
 12 recreation; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the City's Comprehensive Plan under the Transportation
 15 Element states that the City will provide a multipurpose trail system for non-
 16 motorized vehicles; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, on April 28, 2005, the Planning and Zoning Board reviewed the
 19 proposed Multi- Use Trail system and voted 4-0 to recommend approval of the
 20 proposed routes; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, on August 10, 2005, the City Commission considered the
 23 evidence and testimony presented and the recommendations of the various City
 24 of Parkland review agencies and staff; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the City Commission considered and approved the proposed
 27 master multi-use trail system plan, but also determined that the proposed multi-
 28 use trail system did not address all possible trails located within the City; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the City Commission provided staff guidance as to additional
 31 trails to consider and requested that staff bring back to the City Commission an
 32 amended/update of the City wide multi-use trail system.
 33
 34 WHEREAS, on January 11, 2006, the City Commission considered the
 35 amended/updated trail system plan at a workshop;
 36
 37 WHEREAS, on January 18, 2006, the City Commission considered the
 38 evidence and testimony presented and the recommendations of the various City
 39 of Parkland review agencies and staff;
 40
 41 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 42 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 43
 44 SECTION 1. That the City Commission hereby approves and adopts Phase
 45 II of the master multi-use trail system attached as Exhibit A.
 46
 47 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 48 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 August 17, 2005
