 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-121A
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPOINTING ONE (1)
 ALTERNATE #2, AT LARGE MEMBER TO THE PARKS &
 RECREATION ADVISORY BOARD.
 WHEREAS, in accordance with the City of Parkland Code of Ordinances, the City
 Commission shall appoint members to the Parks&Recreation Advisory Board; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission received an email from Parks & Recreation Alternate
 # 2 Board Member, Stephen Wind stating that he was unable to commit the time necessary to
 fully serve on the Parks&Recreation Board; and
 WHEREAS, Steve Wind stated in his email that if someone else was interested, that he
 was willing to step down; and
 WHEREAS, the City has received several applications from residents interested in
 serving on the Parks&Recreation Board; and
 WHEREAS, this vacancy is an At Large position to be appointed by a motion of the
 Commission.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the City Commission accepts Steve Wind's resignation as the
 Alternate#2,At Large member on the Parks&Recreation Advisory Board.
 Section 2. That the City Commission hereby appoints the following Alternate # 2,
 At Large member as follows:
 Alternate#2 -At Large Mr. Eric Young
 Section 3. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately
 upon its passage and adoption.
