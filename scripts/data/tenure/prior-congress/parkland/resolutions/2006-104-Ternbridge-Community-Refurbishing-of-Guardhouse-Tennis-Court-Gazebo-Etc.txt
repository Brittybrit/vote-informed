 3 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 5
 6 SECTION 1. The Community Appearance Board Application of Ternbridge Homeowners
 7 Association, Inc. for the repainting of the exterior wall along Hillsboro Boulevard, repainting of the
 8 guardhouses at both entrances (Hillsboro Boulevard and Parkside Drive), repainting of the tennis
 9 court gazebo that is located within the community and the addition of three landscape lighting
 10 fixtures within the landscaped area at the entrance located on the east side of Hillsboro
 11 Boulevard, replacing the marble on the entry sign with travertine tile and repainting the dark
 12 brown lettering with a bronze lettering color located within Ternbridge is hereby APPROVED,
 13 subject to the conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition to the general
 14 requirements otherwise provided by ordinance:
 15
 16 1. This approval is based upon the application and associated color schemes provided
 17 by the applicant stamp-dated July 12, 2006 to include the following:
 18
 19 a. Repaint the Exterior Wall with a terracotta as the base color for the wall, a
 20 sand beige for the trim on the top of the wall and bronze as the accent
 21 stripe on the wall.
 22 b. Repaint the Guardhouse with a terracotta as the base color, a sand beige
 23 for the trim on the top of the guardhouses and bronze as the accent stripe.
 24 c. Repaint the Tennis Court Gazebo with terracotta as the base color, a sand
 25 beige for the trim and bronze as the accent stripe.
 26 d. Provide three landscape lighting fixtures on the east side of the Hillsboro
 27 Boulevard entrance.
 28 e. Replace marble on the Ternbridge entry sign with travertine tile.
 29 f. Repaint dark brown lettering on entry sign with a Bronze color.
 30 g. Obtain approval from the Terramar Master Homeowners Association to
 31 modify the Terramar sign to replace the marble on the Terramar sign with
 32 travertine tile and create the same appearance as the Ternbridge sign.
 33
 34 2. All conditions of the Bennington aka Ternbridge site plan not specifically
 35 amended by this application shall govern the development.
 36
 37 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
