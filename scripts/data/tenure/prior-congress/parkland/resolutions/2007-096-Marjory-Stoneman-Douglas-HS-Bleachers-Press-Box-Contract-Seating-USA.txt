 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-96
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO
 5 EXECUTE A CONTRACT AGREEMENT WITH SEATING
 6 CONSTRUCTORS USA FOR THE PURPOSE OF INSTALLING
 7 ALUMINUM HOME BLEACHERS WITH PRESS BOX FOR THE
 8 STONEMAN DOUGLAS HIGH SCHOOLS FOOTBALL STADIUM IN
 9 THE AMOUNT OF $515,799.37, PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS;
 10 PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
 11 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13 WHEREAS, it is the desire of the School Board of Broward County, the City, and
 14 the Eagle Fund Committee to install aluminum bleachers and a press box to
 15 accommodate spectators, scorekeepers, and the press at Stoneman Douglas High
 16 School; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the School Board of Broward County has identified a piggybacking
 19 opportunity utilizing a Marion County contract with Seating Constructors USA to install
 20 the aluminum bleachers and press box; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the Stoneman Douglas High School has obtained the necessary
 23 funds for this project through private donations and fund raising activities conducted by
 24 the Eagle Fund Committee; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the City agreed to assist the School Board of Broward County and
 27 the Eagle Fund Committee with the financial management of the project; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the Eagle Fund Committee shall deposit the contract amount into a
 30 designated City of Parkland bank account and prior to the execution of the contract
 31 approved herein; and
 32
 33 WHEREAS, the proposed stadium improvements are consistent with the
 34 schedule of improvements listed in Exhibit B of the First Amendment to Lease and
 35 Funding Agreement for Recreational Facilities at Stoneman Douglas High School,
 36 between the School Board of Broward County, Florida and the City of Parkland
 37 approved on March 24, 2005 (the original agreement and first amendment referred to as
 38 the ILA); and
 39
 40 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes it is in the best
 41 interests of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of
 42 Parkland to enter into an Agreement with Seating Constructors USA for the purpose of
 43 constructing bleachers and a press box at Stoneman Douglas High School.

 1 WHEREAS, the execution of any contract shall be subject to receipt of all
 2 necessary funds by the City through donation and approval of the project by the School
 3 Board;
 4 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 5 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 6
 7 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 8 being true and correct and rare hereby incorporated herein. All exhibits attached hereto are
 9 hereby incorporate herein.
 10
 11 SECTION 2. Subject to the conditions set forth in Section 4 below, the City
 12 Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves the agreement between the City of
 13 Parkland and Seating Constructors USA, a copy of which is attached hereto and
 14 incorporated by reference as if set forth in full.
 15
 16 SECTION 3. The appropriate city officials are hereby authorized and directed to
 17 execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this resolution
 18 upon compliance with the conditions set forth in Section 4.
 19
 20 SECTION 4. The appropriate City officials shall not execute the documents until
 21 such time as the Broward County School Board approves this project in writing pursuant to
 22 the ILA; the entire amount of the contract is deposited into the designated City account as
 23 outlined in the Donation Agreement as approved by the City Attorney and the Broward
 24 County School Board provides written authorization for the project; If both conditions do not
 25 occur within 45 days this Resolution and the authorization provided herein shall be void.
 26
 27 SECTION 5. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are hereby
 28 repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 29
 30 SECTION 6. If any clause, section, other part or application of this Resolution is
 31 held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or invalid, in part of
 32 application, it shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this Resolution.
 33
 34 SECTION 7. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
 35
 36 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 22 DAY OF August , 2007.
