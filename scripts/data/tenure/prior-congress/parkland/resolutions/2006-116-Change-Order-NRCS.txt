 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-116
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION
 4 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 AUTHORIZING AN AMENDMENT TO THE
 6 AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY AND THE
 7 U.S. DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE
 8 NATURAL RESOURCES CONSERVATION
 9 SERVICE INCREASING FUNDING BY $87,000
 10 FOR THE PURPOSE OF STABILIZING A
 11 SWALE WITHIN THE PINE TREE ESTATES
 12 SUBDIVISION, PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE
 13 DATE.
 14
 15
 16 WHEREAS, on February 15, 2006 the City Commission approved an
 17 Agreement between the City of Parkland and the United States Department of
 18 Agriculture Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) providing for the
 19 removal of hurricane debris to prevent flooding within certain sections of the City;
 20 and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, during the course of work it was discovered that additional
 23 swale regrading and bank stabilization would be necessary within the swale that
 24 lies between NW 69th Way and NW 71st Terrace in Pine Tree Estates to prevent
 25 flooding; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, NRCS is authorizing an $87,000 funding increase to the initial
 28 grant for the purpose of repairing the aforementioned swale; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the City would be responsible for matching 25% of said
 31 increase; and
 32
 33 WHEREAS, staff recommends the approval of an amendment to the
 34 Agreement with NRCS in the amount of $87,000.00; and
 35
 36 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes approval of
 37 the amendment is in the best interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland; and
 38
 39 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 40 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 41
 42 SECTION 1. The above representations are true and correct and
 43 made a part hereof.
 44

<<PG>>
 45 SECTION 2. The City Commission does hereby authorize an
 46 amendment increasing the funding amount of the Agreement between the City and
 47 NRCS by $87,000.00.
 48
 49 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 50 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
