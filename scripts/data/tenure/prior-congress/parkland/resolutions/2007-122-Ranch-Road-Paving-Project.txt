 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-122
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,
 4 FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO PIGGYBACK BROWARD
 5 COUNTY CONTRACT #H-Z-05-376-B1 WITH WEEKLEY ASPHALT PAVING
 6 INC. FOR THE PURPOSE OF RESURFACING A PORTION OF RANCH ROAD
 7 FROM NW 81ST TERRACE TO NW 87TH AVENUE AND CONSTRUCTING A
 8 DRIVEWAY AT THE EQUESTRIAN CENTER IN THE AMOUNT OF
 9 $42,683.20, PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, monies were budgeted in the City of Parkland Fiscal Year 2008 Budget for
 12 the purpose of resurfacing a portion of Ranch Road from NW 81st Terrace to NW 87th Avenue;
 13 and
 14
 15 WHEREAS, the aforementioned resurfacing includes the construction of an asphalt
 16 driveway for the Equestrian Center entranceway; and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, Staff has identified a piggybacking opportunity to construct the driveway and
 19 resurface Ranch Road from NW 81st Terrace to NW 87th Avenue utilizing Broward County
 20 contract #H-Z-05-376-B1 with Weekly Asphalt Paving, Inc; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes that it is in the best interest of
 23 the citizens of the City of Parkland to piggyback the Broward County contract with Weekly
 24 Asphalt Paving, Inc. for the purpose of constructing a driveway at the Equestrian Center and
 25 resurfacing a portion of Ranch Road; and
 26
 27 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 28 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 29
 30 SECTION 1. The City Manager is hereby authorized to piggyback Broward County
 31 contract #H-Z-05-376-B1 with Weekly Asphalt Paving Inc. for the purpose of constructing a
 32 driveway at the Equestrian Center entranceway and resurfacing Ranch Road between NW 81st
 33 Terrace and NW 87th Avenue in the amount of $42,683.20.
 34
 35 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately upon
 36 its passage and adoption.
 37
 38 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 19 DAY OF December , 2007.
