 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-21
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SUPPORTING AN ANTI-
 BULLYING CAMPAIGN.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has adopted Schools of Excellence as a
 strategic goal;
 WHEREAS, an objective of that goal is "Safe learning environment for
 children";
 WHEREAS, the City is concerned regarding the impact of bullying, harassment
 and intimidation in schools as well as off premises;
 WHEREAS, school bullying greatly reduces a student's ability to both achieve
 and surpass academic standards;
 WHEREAS, school bullying, harassment and intimidation can directly affect a
 student's health and well-being;
 WHEREAS, with the help of local school boards, parents, concerned individuals
 and the City of Parkland, school bullying can be addressed;
 WHEREAS, the Education Advisory Board recommends a proactive stance to
 educate residents and students on what is bullying, what anti-bullying programs are
 available and how to identify and work against bullying in the schools, playgrounds and
 sports fields in Parkland.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials do hereby support the
 establishment of an Anti-Bullying Awareness Campaign and November 15-21, 2008 as
 Anti-Bullying Week in Parkland.
