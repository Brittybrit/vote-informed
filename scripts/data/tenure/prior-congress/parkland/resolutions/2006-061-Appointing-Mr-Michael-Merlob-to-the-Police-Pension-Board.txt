 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-61
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE
 CITY OFFICIALS TO APPROVE MR. MICHAEL MERLOB
 AS THE NEW PENSION BOARD REPRESENTATIVE FOR
 THE POLICE PENSION PLAN AND TRUST FUND.
 WHEREAS, the City Manager, Mark R. Lauzier is resigning his position on the
 Police Pension Board; and
 WHEREAS, according to Ordinance No. 2004-09 creating the Police Pension
 Board, the fifth member of the Police Pension Board, currently held by Mr. Lauzier, is
 chosen by the majority of the other four Trustees and this person's name shall be
 submitted to the City Commission for appointment; and
 WHEREAS, the Police Pension Board, at their May 15, 2006 meeting,
 unanimously agreed to submit the name of Mr. Merlob to the City Commission for
 appointment to the Police Pension Board.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the appropriate City officials do hereby approve the following
 Parkland Police Pension Plan and Trust Fund representative: Mr. Michael Merlob.
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 7 DAY OF MAY , 2006.
