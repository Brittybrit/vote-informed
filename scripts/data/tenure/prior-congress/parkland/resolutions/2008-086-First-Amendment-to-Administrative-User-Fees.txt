 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-86
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO APPROVE THE FIRST
 5 AMENDMENT TO THE ADOPTION OF ADMINISTRATIVE
 6 AND USER FEE AND CHARGE SCHEDULES FOR
 7 SERVICES, ACTIVITIES AND USE OF FACILITIES
 8 PROVIDED BY THE CITY OF PARKLAND; PROVIDING
 9 FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, Chapter 19, Section 19-11 of the Code of Ordinances authorizes the
 12 City Commission to adopt administrative and user fees and charges; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that the costs associated with certain
 15 City programs, activities and/or facilities should be borne by the person(s) utilizing the
 16 service or should be commensurate with the cost incurred by the City to perform the
 17 services;
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland offers a variety of services to the residents,
 20 businesses and visitors, for which the City charges a minimal fee or has not charged a fee;
 21
 22 WHEREAS, it is the intent of the City Commission to transfer the costs of these
 23 City services to the individuals who are requesting the services from the City; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, on September 19, 2007 the City Commission approved the adoption
 26 of administrative and user fee, and charge schedules for services, activities and use of
 27 facilities; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, staff conducted a yearly evaluation of user fees and services and is
 30 proposing changes to the previously adopted schedule including increasing fees and
 31 adding new fees; and
 32
 33 NOW, THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 34 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 35
 36 SECTION 1. The foregoing "whereas" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 37 being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein.
 38
 39 SECTION 2. That the City Commission of the City of Parkland approves the
 40 attached amended administrative and/or user fee schedules for Parks and Recreation and
 41 miscellaneous, which copies are attached hereto and incorporated as if set forth in full as
 42 exhibits. This schedule does not affect existing Building Department fees.
 43
 44 SECTION 3. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are hereby
 45 repealed to the extent of such conflict.
