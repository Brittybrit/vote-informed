 RESOLUTION NO. 2006 -15
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING AN INCREASE IN THE TOTAL
 NUMBER OF FULL-TIME POSITIONS WITHIN THE 2005-2006 BUDGET
 FROM 81 FULL-TIME POSITIONS TO 82 POSITIONS.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, approved the annual
 operating budget via Ordinance No. 2005-17; and
 WHEREAS, the authorized total number of full-time positions contained in the
 approved budget was 80; and
 WHEREAS, the total position count of 80 represented a reduction in the position
 cap of four positions from the prior year; and
 WHEREAS, the authorized total number of full-time positions contained in the
 approved budget was subsequently increased to 81 by Resolution 2005-95 in order to
 add a position in the City Manager's Office; and
 WHEREAS, the promotion of the Development Services Department Director to
 the position of Assistant City Manager requires the replacement of a key department
 head position needed to manage the development services and Comprehensive Plan
 functions required under State Law; and
 WHEREAS, it would be in the best interests of the City of Parkland to increase
 the authorized staffing level of the City by the creation of the position of Planning
 Director;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:

<<PG>>
 Section 1. That the City of Parkland City Commission hereby authorizes an
 increase in the personnel cap by a net total of one position as detailed in Exhibit A
 attached hereto and made a part hereof.
 Section 2. That the 2005-2006 Budget, as established by Ordinance No.
 2005-17 and amended by Resolution 2005-95 shall be amended to reflect the increase
 in the personnel cap in accordance with Exhibit A attached hereto.
 Section 3. That all Resolutions or parts of Resolutions in conflict herewith are
 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 Section 4. That this Resolution shall be in full force and effect immediately
 upon it passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 1 DAY OF February , 2006
