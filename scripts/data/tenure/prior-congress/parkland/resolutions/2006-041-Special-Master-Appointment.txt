 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-41
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPOINTING SPECIAL MASTERS
 FOR CODE ENFORCEMENT MATTERS.
 WHEREAS, pursuant to Ordinance No. 2006-01 of the City of Parkland, the
 City is to appoint Special Masters to hear code enforcement matters brought pursuant to
 the authority of Chapter 162, Florida Statutes; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has reviewed the qualifications of Eugene
 Steinfeld and Mitchell Kraft and find that they satisfy the requirements for Special
 Masters and are capable of effectively implementing the procedures set forth in
 Ordinance No. 2006-01.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA:
 Section 1. The City Commission hereby appoints Eugene Steinfeld and
 Mitchell Kraft as code enforcement Special Masters to undertake the duties as set forth in
 ordinance No. 2006-01. The appointments set forth herein shall be for a term of two
 years.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 3 DAY OF NAY , 2006.
