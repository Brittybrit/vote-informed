 RESOLUTION NO. 2006- 11
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, URGING THE BROWARD COUNTY SCHOOL
 BOARD TO INCLUDE LIGHTNING PROTECTION AT ALL SCHOOL
 RECREATIONAL FACILITIES.
 WHEREAS, unexpected lightning strikes can result in serious injury or death to children
 using school recreational facilities; and
 WHEREAS, lightning sensors and other lightning protection devices have been proven
 to be useful in warning children of the onset of lightning and can save lives;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,AS FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. The City of Parkland City Commission does hereby urge the Broward
 County School Board to include permanent fixed lightning protection/warning devices in all new
 school recreational facilities and to begin a program of retrofitting existing schools with
 permanent fixed lightning protection/warning devices such as Thor Guard.
 Section 2. The City Clerk is hereby authorized to provide a copy of this Resolution to
 the Broward County School Board and to the Broward County Commission.
 ADOPTED THIS 1sT DAY OF FEBRUARY, 2006.
