 RESOLUTION NO. 2004 - 32
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, PROVIDING A PROCEDURE FOR
 MITIGATION OF CODE ENFORCEMENT LIENS OR FINES.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland wishes to provide a procedure for considering
 mitigation of code enforcement liens or fines; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to do this without reevaluating the
 appropriateness of code enforcement action, but to provide for exceptional circumstances
 related to the imposition of the fine and related lien where some relief is warranted;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. A person or entity subject to a code enforcement lien, may obtain
 partial or full mitigation of said lien through the process as set forth below:
 (A) The person or entity subject to the lien shall make application to the
 City Manager or his designee, requesting relief The application shall identify the code
 enforcement lien at issue, the amount of the lien, and the circumstances that would justify
 mitigation.
 (B) The City Manager or his designee shall forward the application to
 the Code Enforcement Board, which will schedule a hearing to make a recommendation
 on the request for mitigation. The hearing shall not be for the purposes of reevaluating
 the appropriateness or correctness of the original code enforcement order. The original
 order shall be deemed correct and valid. The hearing shall be solely for the purpose of
 determining whether there are any mitigating circumstances. The mitigating
 circumstances shall consist of circumstances or events which were not the fault or cause
 of the person or entity seeking relief, which would render the imposition of the lien in the
 amount set forth therein to be unjust and to impose an undue hardship upon the person or

<<PG>>
 entity seeking relief. No evidence previously presented to the Code Enforcement Board
 shall be considered, nor shall the Board hear any evidence related to the guilt or
 innocence of the applicant or the merits of the finding of guilt or imposition of lien. City
 staff may present evidence on the application.
 (C) The Code Enforcement Board shall then make a recommendation
 which shall be forwarded to the City Manager who shall forward the recommendation to
 the City Commission (the Notice).
 (D) If no member of the City Commission submits a written request for
 review of the recommendation within ten (10) days of the date of the Notice, the
 recommendation shall be deemed to be final. If any member requests a review, it shall be
 placed on a future City Commission agenda. The City Commission shall then review the
 application and recommendation and make a determination based upon the criteria set
 forth above.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 21 DAY OF April , 2004.
