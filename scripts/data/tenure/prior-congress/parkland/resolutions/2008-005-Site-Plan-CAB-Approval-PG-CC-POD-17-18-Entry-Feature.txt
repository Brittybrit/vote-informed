 5 SECTION 1. The Site Plan and Community Appearance Board Application of WCI
 6 Communities, Inc. for the construction of an entry feature for the Pod 17/18 entrance located
 7 within Parkland Golf and Country Club is hereby APPROVED on the real property as described
 8 in Exhibit A, subject to the conditions of approval contained herein, which are in addition to the
 9 general requirements otherwise provided by ordinance:
 10
 11 1 . This approval is based upon the Plans H-1, H-2, H-3 and H-4, entitled "Pod 17/18
 12 Entry Feature Parkland Golf and Country Club" by Krent Wieland Design, Inc.,
 13 dated May 31 , 2006 and revised on March 23, 2007 and June 1, 2007 and August
 14 20, 2007 and stamp-dated and received by the Planning and Zoning Department
 15 on October 25, 2007; Plans P-1 and P-2 entitled "Pod 17/18 Entry Feature
 16 Parkland Golf and Country Club" dated March 23, 2007 and revised on June 1 ,
 17 2007 and August 20, 2007 and received by the Planning and Zoning Department
 18 on October 25, 2007.
 19 2. Root barriers shall be installed for all trees located within five (5) feet of a sidewalk
 20 or roadway.
 21 3. The City shall inspect the landscape buffer on a periodic basis to insure that it is
 22 being properly maintained and growing in at a normal rate. The City may require
 23 the applicant to add additional landscaping material to any area that is not growing
 24 in at a normal rate as would be expected based on the approved landscaping plan.
 25 Failure to maintain the landscaping and entry area or respond to a City request as
 26 set forth herein shall constitute a violation of this approval and the City's Code of
 27 Ordinances.
 28 4. The party responsible for the landscape buffers shall employ a full-time grounds /
 29 building maintenance person to maintain the entry area as required herein.
 30 5. All conditions and approvals as outlined in Ordinance Numbers 2002-4, 2005-23,
 31 and 2006-08, and Resolution Number 2002-28 not specifically amended by this
 32 application shall govern the development.
 33 6. Any substitution of landscape materials and building materials shall require City
 34 approval prior to the installation of such material. For recordation purposes, a
 35 waiver of site plan application shall be submitted as long as the material proposed
 36 to be substitute is determined to be comparable as that approved. If such material
 37 is determined to not be comparable, a site plan amendment application shall be
 38 submitted with all applicable fees.
 39 7. The entry feature for Pod 17/18 of Parkland Golf and Country Club shall receive a
 40 final certificate of occupancy no later than eighteen (18) months from issuance of
 41 the first building permit. If the Project is not completed as evidenced by a Certificate
 42 of Completion or other statement of completion by the City within eighteen (18)
 43 months of the date of the issuance of the first building permit, then in that event,
 44 the site plan and building permit referencing the project shall be null and void and
 45 Owner shall be required to apply for a new site plan approval and new building
 46 permits. Additionally, the City shall not allow inspections and plans review on such
 47 project to occur. Additionally, Owner shall be required to pay a fee equal to double
