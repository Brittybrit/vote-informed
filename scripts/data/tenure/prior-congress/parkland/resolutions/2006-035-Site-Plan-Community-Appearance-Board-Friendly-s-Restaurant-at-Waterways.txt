 11 WHEREAS, the City Commission, as the governing body of the City of Parkland, Florida,
 12 pursuant to the authority in Chapter 163 and Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, and the Code of
 13 Ordinance is authorized and empowered to consider petitions related to zoning and land development
 14 orders; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the petitioner desires to construct a 4,465 square foot Friendly's Restaurant at
 17 the Waterways Shoppes; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the notice and hearing requirements as provided in Code of Ordinances, have
 20 been satisfied; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony presented by
 23 the Petitioner and other interested parties and the recommendations of the various City of Parkland
 24 review agencies and staff; and
 25
 26 WHEREAS,the City Commission has made the following findings of fact:
 27
 28 1. Environmental Considerations: No adverse impacts to the natural environment are
 29 expected since the property has already been disturbed.
 30
 31 2. Utilities: The proposed request will not affect existing water,
 32 sanitary, sewer and drainage systems.
 33
 34 3. Comprehensive Plan Considerations:The proposed development is consistent with the Land
 35 Use Element of the Comprehensive Plan.
 36
 37 4. Zoning Considerations: Site area, building setbacks, height restrictions, and
 38 landscaping all meet or exceed Code standards.
 39
 40 5. Community Appearance The building fits within the character of the area and the
 41 Considerations: adjacent shopping centers, and includes pedestrian
 42 amenities.
 43
 44 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 45 PARKLAND,FLORIDA that:

 1 SECTION 1. The Site Plan Amendment and Community Appearance Board Application of
 2 NL Ark Development, LLC for the construction of a Friendly's Restaurant within theWaterways
 3 Shoppes is hereby APPROVED on the real property as described in Exhibit A, subject to the conditions
 4 of approval contained herein, which are in addition to the general requirements otherwise provided by
 5 ordinance:
 6
 7 1. This approval is based upon the plans entitled "Friendly's Restaurant" dated April 14,
 8 2006 and received by the Planning and Zoning Department on May 22, 2006.
 9 2. Fire Hydrants shall be services by 8" water main.
 10 3. Fire Hydrants shall be installed at the main entrance and every 500 feet thereafter.
 11 4. All conditions of the Waterways Shoppes Site Plan or Site Plan Amendments not
 12 amended by this site plan shall apply.
 13 5. According to Parkland Ordinance, the finished floor elevation is required to be 6" above
 14 the `Crown of the Road' as approved by the City Engineer. Finished floor elevation is
 15 shown on these documents as 0.6' lower than that of the building on the adjacent lot, and
 16 lower than the street elevations as well. Raise finished floor, or provide justification for
 17 this selection.
 18 6. The hours for use of the loading zone shall be limited to 8:00 am to 6:00 pm.
 19 7. The hours for refuse pickup shall be limited to six(6) days a week, 8:00 am to 6:00 pm.
 20
 21 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
