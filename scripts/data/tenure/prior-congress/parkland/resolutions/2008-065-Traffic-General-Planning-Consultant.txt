 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2008-65
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO RANK THE SIX (6) LISTED
 5 TRAFFIC AND GENERAL PLANNING CONSULTANT
 6 COMPANIES TO PROVIDE PROFESSIONAL TRAFFIC
 7 AND GENERAL PLANNING CONSULTING SERVICES ON
 8 A CONTINUING BASIS AND TO AUTHORIZE THE CITY
 9 MANAGER TO EXECUTE A CONTRACT WITH THE FOUR
 10 (4) TOP RANKED FIRMS; PROVIDING FOR
 11 SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE
 12 DATE.
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland requested proposals from companies / firms for
 15 traffic and general planning consultant services; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, the City received eight (8) proposals, two (2) of the eight (8) proposals
 18 did not qualify and a selection committee appointed by the City Manager reviewed (6) six
 19 said proposals and ranked the proposals received; and
 20
 21 WHEREAS, the top four (4) ranked firms were Keith and Schnars, P.A.; Keith and
 22 Associates, Inc.; Kimley-Horn and Associates; and Calvin, Giordano and Associates; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the City Commission agrees with the ranking by the committee and
 25 wishes to authorize execution of an agreement with Keith and Schnars, P.A.; Keith and
 26 Associates, Inc.; Kimley-Horn and Associates; and Calvin, Giordano and Associates upon
 27 negotiation of acceptable contracts by the City Manager;
 28
 29 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 30 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 31
 32 SECTION 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and
 33 made a part hereof.
 34
 35 SECTION 2. The City Commission hereby affirms the ranking (1 through 6) of the
 36 evaluation committee as to the proposals received for traffic and general planning
 37 consultants services. Keith and Schnars, P.A.; Keith and Associates, Inc.; Kimley-Horn and
 38 Associates and Calvin, Giordano and Associates is deemed the top four (4) ranked firms
 39 and the City Manager is hereby authorized to enter into negotiations to develop a contract,
 40 which contract shall first be approved by the City Attorney as to form and then submitted to
 41 the City Commission for final determination. The City Commission will then make a final
 42 decision on approving or not approving the contract. If the City Commission does not
 43 approve the contract, or if the City Manager is unable to negotiate an acceptable contract
 44 with Keith and Schnars, P.A.; Keith and Associates, Inc.; Kimley-Horn and Associates and
 45 Calvin, Giordano and Associates, then the City Commission may direct that negotiations be
 46 undertaken with the number five (5) ranked firm. No contract or award shall be final until
 47 such time as the City Commission approves a final contract.
 48
 49 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
 50
 51 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 18th day of June , 2008.
 52
 Resolution No.2008-65
