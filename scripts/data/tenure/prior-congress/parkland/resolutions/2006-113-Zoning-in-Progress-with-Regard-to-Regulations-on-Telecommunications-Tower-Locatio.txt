 RESOLUTION NO. 2006— 113
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA DECLARING A ZONING IN
 PROGRESS WITH REGARD TO REGULATIONS RELATING
 TO THE LOCATION OF TELECOMMUNICATIONS TOWERS,
 ANTENNAS AND SIMILAR FACILITIES; PROVIDING NOTICE
 TO ALL PERSONS THAT SAID REGULATIONS ARE BEING
 REVIEWED AND WILL BE CONSIDERED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION WITHIN A 180 DAY PERIOD; PROVIDING FOR
 AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland is currently reviewing its wireless telecommunications
 ordinance relating to towers, antennas and similar facilities; and
 WHEREAS, the City has actively considered revised regulations with regard to said
 facilities; and
 WHEREAS, the City wishes to put all persons on notice of this action so that all persons
 will be aware that any applications submitted will be required to comply with any regulations
 adopted within the 180 day period set forth herein; and
 WHEREAS, the City believes that this is of significant importance for the health, safety
 and welfare of the public and that the regulations being considered will further that goal;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. The above representations are true and correct and made a part hereof
 Section 2. All persons are hereby placed on notice of the consideration of revised
 regulations as described in this resolution and that the regulations will relate to cell towers,
 antennas and other wireless communications facilities and will be implemented within 180 days

<<PG>>
 of the date of this resolution. All applications for said facilities shall be required to be consistent
 with the regulations adopted during this zoning in progress.
 Section 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its passage and
 adoption.
