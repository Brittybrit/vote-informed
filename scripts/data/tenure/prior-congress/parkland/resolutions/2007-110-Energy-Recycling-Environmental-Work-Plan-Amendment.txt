 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO AMEND THE CITY OF PARKLAND
 5 ENVIRONMENTAL WORK PLAN BY AMENDING THE
 6 SECTION ENTITLED "RECYCLING" SECTION; AND ADDING
 7 A SECTION ENTITLED "ENERGY"; PROVIDING FOR
 8 CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND
 9 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland desires to amend the Environmental Work Plan
 12 to further promote resident awareness and education, energy efficiency, protect the urban
 13 natural areas and continue to establish the conservation of biological diversity and
 14 cultural resources; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to further address the challenges of
 17 preserving the ecological integrity of Parkland's character; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to address, through the Environmental
 20 Work Plan, a Recycling Action Plan, which addresses residential and commercial
 21 recycling needs, promotion, education, awareness and incentive programs for residents
 22 and local business; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to address, through the Environmental
 25 Work Plan, a Energy Action Plan, which addresses programs that provide education,
 26 awareness and incentives for residents to easily and effectively save energy and lower
 27 utility bills; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, the City staff desires to provide valuable opportunities for citizen
 30 enjoyment by promoting Environmental Awareness and Education to City residents; and
 31
 32 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland desires to provide effective environmentally
 33 safe treatment of recycling materials for the sole purpose of preservation of the City's
 34 valuable natural resources; and
 35
 36 WHEREAS, the City Commission has considered the evidence and testimony
 37 presented at a public meeting; and
 38
 39 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines that amendments to the
 40 Environmental Work Plan to be in the best interest of the health, safety and welfare of the
 41 citizens and residents of the City of Parkland; and
 42
 43
 44 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 45 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
