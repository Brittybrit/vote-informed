 RESOLUTION NO. 2006- 89
 A RESOLUTION BY THE MAYOR, ON BEHALF OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, DECLARING A STATE OF LOCAL
 EMERGENCY.
 WHEREAS, the National Hurricane Center recognizes the danger to coastal residents of
 Florida from Hurricane Ernesto by posting a Hurricane Watch/Warning from August 28, 2006 to
 August 30, 2006.
 WHEREAS,Broward County has declared a State of Emergency; and
 WHEREAS, Hurricane Ernesto has the potential for causing extensive damage to public
 utilities,public buildings,public communication systems,public streets and roads,pubic drainage
 systems, commercial and residential buildings and area as well as causing life threatening
 conditions for the residents;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT, I, MAYOR MICHAEL
 UDINE ON BEHALF OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND,FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the above whereas clauses are incorporated herein.
 Section 2. That Hurricane Ernesto poses a serious threat to the lives and property of
 the residents of the City of Parkland and that a state of Local Emergency shall be declared
 effective immediately, for all areas within the corporate limits of the City of Parkland. The City
 requires the ability to respond promptly to these conditions.
 Section 3. The City Manager is hereby authorized to take all action including but
 not limited to execution of contract, enforcement of regulations, and as otherwise may be
 necessary to respond to the emergency conditions to the fullest extent permitted by all
 applicable laws or emergency declaration.
 Section 4. Be it further resolved that the Mayor, on behalf of the City, hereby
 exercises his authority and waives the procedures and formalities required by law of a political
 subdivision and declares a state of Local Emergency. All declarations of emergency by the state
 and federal authorities are herby incorporated and adopted.
