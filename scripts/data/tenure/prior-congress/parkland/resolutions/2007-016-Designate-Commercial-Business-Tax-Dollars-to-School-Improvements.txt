 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-16
 2
 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, ENCOURAGING PARKLAND
 5 COMMERCIAL BUSINESSES TO CHECK THE BOX ON THEIR
 6 TAX RETURN TO DESIGNATE TAX DOLLARS TO BE USED
 7 TOWARDS SCHOOL IMPROVEMENTS FOR TECHNOLOGY
 8 AND TEACHER TRAINING.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, Florida schools are ranked at or near the bottom in school
 11 funding; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, Florida commercial businesses are entitled to a collection
 14 allowance on their tax return; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, Florida commercial businesses are now entitled to elect to
 17 donate their collection allowance to the Educational Enhancement Trust Fund
 18 ("Fund"); and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the revenue deposited in this Fund is required to be used for
 21 school improvements for technology and teacher training; and
 22
 23 WHERAS, there is a concern that Parkland commercial businesses are
 24 not aware of this option which can assist Parkland schools;
 25
 26 WHEREAS, the Broward School Board and Broward County have
 27 supported the tax remittance be permitted to be used to purchase classroom
 28 technology and teacher training; and
 29
 30 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 31 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 32
 33 Section 1. That the City of Parkland supports the donation of
 34 commercial businesses' collection allowance to the Educational Enhancement
 35 Trust Fund.
 36
 37 Section 2. That a copy of this Resolution shall be sent to Broward
 38 County, Broward County School District, Parkland Chamber of Commerce, and
 39 all Parkland businesses
 40
 41 Section 3. This Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 42 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
