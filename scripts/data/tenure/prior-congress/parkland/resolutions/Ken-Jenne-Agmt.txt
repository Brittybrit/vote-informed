 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA TO VOID THE SECOND
 5 AMENDMENT TO THE AGREEMENT BY AND BETWEEN
 6 KENNETH C. JENNE, II, SHERIFF OF BROWARD COUNTY,
 7 FLORIDA AND CITY OF PARKLAND FOR LAW
 8 ENFORCEMENT SERVICES ADOPTED FEBRUARY 7, 2007
 9 THROUGH RESOLUTION 2007-27.
 10
 11 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland reviewed various options for providing
 12 police services to its citizens; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, on February 4, 2004, the City Commission awarded a
 15 contract with the Broward Sheriff's Office ("BSO") for law enforcement services;
 16 and
 17
 18 WHEREAS, the Agreement By and Between Kenneth C. Jenne, II, Sheriff
 19 of Broward County, Florida and the City of Parkland for Law Enforcement
 20 Services ("Agreement") was entered into; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City Commission adopted the Second Amendment to the
 23 Agreement to address outstanding issues; and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, BSO never approved the Second Amendment; and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, BSO and the City have settled all disputes under different
 28 terms so Resolution 2007-27 must be voided; and
 29
 30 WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best
 31 interest of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland that the Second
 32 Amendment to the Agreement adopted on February 7, 2007 be voided; and
 33
 34 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 35 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT;
 36
 37 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 38 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporate herein. All exhibits
 39 attached hereto are hereby incorporate herein.
 40
 41 SECTION 2. The Second Amendment to the Agreement By and Between
 42 Kenneth C. Jenne, II, Sheriff of Broward County, Florida and the City of Parkland
 43 for Law Enforcement Services ("Agreement") and adopted February 7, 2007
 44 through Resolution 2007-27 is hereby voided.
