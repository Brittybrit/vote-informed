 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 4 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING A
 5 BUDGET AMENDMENT IN THE AMOUNT OF $472,000
 6 WITHIN THE 2006-2007 BUDGET AS ESTABLISHED BY
 7 ORDINANCE NO. 2006-30; AUTHORIZING THE
 8 APPROPRIATION OF ADDITIONAL REVENUES AND
 9 ALLOCATION OF SAID EXPENDITURES; AND
 10 PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, in accordance
 13 with Ordinance No. 2006-30, recognizes the need for periodic adjustment of the
 14 budget to provide and maintain for the City's needs; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland authorizes the
 17 appropriation of additional unanticipated revenue within the General Fund; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland authorizes the
 20 amendment of the General Fund and Capital Projects Fund to
 21 increase/decrease the original appropriations.
 22
 23 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 24 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 25
 26 Section 1. That the City of Parkland hereby authorizes the budget
 27 amendment to increase the General Fund by $65,000 and an increase in the
 28 Capital Projects Fund of $407,000 as described in Exhibit A attached hereto and
 29 made a part hereof.
 30 Section 2. That the 2006-2007 Budget, as established by Ordinance
 31 No. 2006-30 shall be amended to reflect the changes in accordance with Exhibit
 32 A attached hereto.
 33 Section 3. That all Resolutions or parts of Resolutions in conflict
 34 herewith are hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 35 Section 4. That this Resolution shall be in full force and effect
 36 immediately upon it passage and adoption.
