 18 WHEREAS, Florida Stature 200.065 (TRIM — Truth in Millage) requires all
 19 municipalities, within 35 days of receipt of the city's certification of taxable value (on or
 20 around July 1), to advise the Property Appraiser of an estimated millage rate to be included
 21 in the TRIM notice sent to each taxpayer; and
 22
 23 WHEREAS, an estimate and tentative millage rate should be proposed which would
 24 allow the City Commission latitude in meeting Fiscal Year 2009 budgetary requirements:
 25 and
 26
 27 WHEREAS, Florida Statute 200.065 requires that a hearing date, other than that
 28 scheduled by the Broward County Commission and the Broward School Board, be set by
 29 each municipality be included in the TRIM Notice sent to each taxpayer; and
 30
 31 WHEREAS, the City Commission recommends that the estimated millage rate be
 32 set at 3.4083 mills for Fiscal Year 2009, and that the public budget hearing be set at 7:00
 33 p.m. on September 15, 2008 and 7:00 p.m. on September 24, 2008, in the City Commission
 34 Chambers, 6600 University Drive, Parkland, Florida 33067; and
 35
 36 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida deems it to be in
 37 the best interest of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland to allow the City
 38 Manager to send an estimated and tentative millage rate of 3.4083 mills to the Property
 39 Appraiser and to set a date, time and place for the first and second public hearing.
 40
 41 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 42 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 43
 44 Section 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are hereby ratified and
 45 confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution.
