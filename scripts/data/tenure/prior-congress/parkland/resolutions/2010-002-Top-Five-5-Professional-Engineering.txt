RESOLUTION NO. 2010-02 
A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, 
FLORIDA TO RANK THE TOP FIVE (5) PROFESSIONAL ENGINEERING 
FIRMS TO PROVIDE ENGINEERING SERVICES ON AN AS-NEEDED BASIS 
AND TO AUTHORIZE THE CITY MANAGER TO NEGOTIATE AND EXECUTE 
CONTRACTS WITH THE FOUR (4) TOP RANKED FIRMS; PROVIDING FOR 
SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE. 
WHEREAS, City Staff advertised Request For Qualifications (RFQ) #2009-13 for the purpose 
of having a list of pre-qualified firms to provide various engineering services on an as-needed basis ; 
and 
WHEREAS, a review committee consisting of the City Manager, Public Works Director, and 
Chief Engineer read and reviewed thirty seven (37) submitted proposals to ensure compliance with 
the requirements of the RFQ; and 
WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of the RFQ, the aforementioned review 
committee short listed thirty four (34) firms based on the criteria set forth in the RFQ; and 
WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of the RFQ, the City Commission is required 
to rank the top five (5) firms in order or preference for the purpose of having a final list ranking the top 
five (5) firms in order; and 
WHEREAS, Staff recommends that the City utilize the services of at least four (4) engineering 
firms for the purpose of ensuring the City is in the best position to meet various engineering needs; 
and 
WHEREAS, upon final ranking, the City Commission authorizes the City Manager to negotiate 
and execute contracts with the four (4) top ranked firms as indicated on the attachment hereto to 
provide the aforementioned engineering services; and 
WHEREAS, the City Commission hereby determines it to be in the best interest of the health, 
safety and welfare of the citizens and residents of the City of Parkland for the City Manager to enter 
into such contracts; and 
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF 
PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT; 
SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as being true and 
correct and are hereby incorporated herein. All exhibits attached hereto are hereby incorporated 
herein.

SECTION 3. The City Manager is hereby authorized to negotiate and execute professional 
engineering services agreements with the top four (4) ranked firms on Exhibit "A" attached hereto. 
SECTION 4. In the event that the City Manager and any of the top four (4) ranked firms are 
unable to mutually agree on terms, conditions and fees, the City Manager is hereby authorized to 
discontinue negotiations with such firm and begin negotiations with the next highest ranked firm. 
SECTION 5. All agreements shall be reviewed and approved by the City Attorney prior to 
City's execution. 
SECTION 6. If any clause, section, other part or application of this Resolution is held by any 
court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or invalid, in part of application, it shall not affect 
the validity of the remaining portions or applications of this Resolution. 
SECTION 7. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
