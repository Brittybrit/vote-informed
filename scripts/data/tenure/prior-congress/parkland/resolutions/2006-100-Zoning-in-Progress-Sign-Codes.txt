 RESOLUTION NO. 2006— 100
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA DECLARING A ZONING IN
 PROGRESS WITH REGARD TO SIGN CODE REGULATIONS
 AND REVISIONS TO THE SIGN CODE; PROVIDING FOR AN
 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has determined that it is in its
 best interest to review its present sign regulations to ensure that they are consistent with recent
 developments of the law, and to ensure that the present restrictions on the size and locations of
 signs is enforceable based upon the recent decision; and
 WHEREAS, it is the desire of the City Commission of the City of Parkland that a sign
 ordinance properly regulates signage to avoid aesthetic visual pollution, traffic distractions, and
 to otherwise enforce a sign code which is consistent with the City of Parkland that is has
 developed; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to have this review undertaken within a 180
 day period and wishes to place all parties on notice that these revisions are being considered and
 that all persons constructing signs will be required to comply with the code as revised;
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, AS FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. The above Whereas clauses are true and correct and are incorporated
 herein.
 Section 2. The City of Parkland City Commission does hereby declare a zoning in
 progress with regard to its sign regulations. This zoning in progress shall last for a period of 180
 days while the City considers revisions related to its sign regulations. The purpose and intent of

<<PG>>
 these regulations are set forth herein and the City Commission reserves the right to make any
 changes it deems appropriate and in the best interests of its citizens with regard to sign
 regulations. All persons and entities are on notice of this zoning in progress and of the intent of
 the City of Parkland in this regard and no sign permit shall be issued unless it is conditioned
 upon compliance with the regulations as adopted during the zoning in progress. The City
 Commission may, by resolution, extend this zoning in progress by an additional 30 day period.
 Section 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its passage and
 adoption.
