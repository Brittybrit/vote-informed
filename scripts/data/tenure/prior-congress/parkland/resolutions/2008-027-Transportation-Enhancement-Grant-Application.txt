 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 4 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AUTHORIZE THE APPROPRIATE CITY
 5 OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE THE YEAR 2008 TRANSPORTATION
 6 ENHANCEMENT GRANT APPLICATION TO CONSTRUCT A MULTI-USE
 7 PATH ON RIVERSIDE DRIVE FROM HOLMBERG ROAD TO TROTTERS
 8 LANE, PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 9
 10 WHEREAS, The Broward County Metropolitan Planning Organization ("MPO")
 11 administers a grant program entitled "Year 2008 Transportation Enhancement"; and
 12
 13 WHEREAS, there are grant funds allocated and available from this program to assist the
 14 City in enhancing transportation related facilities and activities within the City; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland, Florida desires to construct a multi-use path on
 17 Riverside Drive, from Holmberg Road to Trotters Lane; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the proposed multi-use path was approved on the City's Multi-Use Trails
 20 Plan; and
 21
 22 WHEREAS, the City demonstrates it has sufficient staff resources, technical expertise,
 23 and/or experience with similar projects to carry out the proposed program; and
 24
 25 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 26 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 27
 28 SECTION 1. The City Commission hereby authorizes the City Manager to execute the
 29 Year 2008 Transportation Enhancement Grant Application.
 30
 31 SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.

 33 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 19 DAY OF MARCH , 2008.
