 AGREEMENT
 BETWEEN
 CITY OF PARKLAND,
 AND
 HENDRIX FARMS
 THIS AGREEMENT is entered into by and between CHARLES HENDRIX and
 CHARLOTTE HENDRIX and RALPH V. HENDRIX and KATHY L. BAKER, and HENDRIX
 HOLDING COMPANY, LTD., a Florida limited partnership, General Partner, CVH LAND,
 LLC, a Florida limited liability company, CHARLES W. HENDRIX, III, Managing Member and
 CHARLES W. HENDRIX III, KATHY L. BAKER and RALPH V. HENDRIX, and CHARLES
 W. HENDRIX III and CHARLOTTE HENDRIX ("OWNERS") and the CITY OF
 PARKLAND ("PARKLAND"), a municipal corporation of the State of Florida, as follows:
 WITNESSETH:
 WHEREAS, OWNERS own undeveloped land presently located in Broward County,
 which lands are separately described on Exhibit "A" and shall hereinafter be referred to
 collectively as the "Land"; and
 Whereas, OWNERS desire to have the Land annexed into the City of Parkland and
 PARKLAND is willing to support such annexation if it receives certain assurances from
 OWNERS, as set forth below; and
 WHEREAS, it is the intent of the parties to set forth their respective findings with regard
 to the goals of PARKLAND and OWNERS for appropriate development on the Land; and
 WHEREAS, the OWNERS have reviewed the level of actual developed densities within
 PARKLAND as well as the quality of the development approved and constructed in
 PARKLAND, and wish to develop the Land in a manner which is consistent and compatible with
 the existing development in PARKLAND, and to ensure that any future development of the Land

<<PG>>
 will be of the same high quality and overall density as the existing development in PARKLAND;
 and
 WHEREAS, PARKLAND is desirous of ensuring that the features which are associated
 with present PARKLAND development, to wit: high quality amenities, lushly landscaped
 berms, roadways and public spaces, low densities, and high quality architectural and design
 details for commercial development, are continued on the Land; and
 WHEREAS, the OWNERS agree with these goals of PARKLAND for any development
 of the Land, and also understand that any residential development will require additional parks,
 open space,public facilities, utilities, public educational facilities, and roadways; and
 WHEREAS, in order to undertake such development, the OWNERS will require
 appropriate land use changes, followed by rezoning, platting, site planning and such other
 reviews required by Broward County or PARKLAND should the property be placed within the
 municipal boundaries of PARKLAND; and
 WHEREAS, OWNER wishes to obtain a land use plan change to the Land to 40 acres of
 commercial with 2 dwelling units per acre dashed line around the entire Land and PARKLAND
 finds that such a category is generally consistent with the surrounding area in PARKLAND; and
 WHEREAS, the parties understand that no land use change can be undertaken except
 through the process as set forth under Florida law, after public hearing and appropriate findings
 to support any such change; and
 WHEREAS, this Agreement shall not be construed to require any action by
 PARKLAND or any other government body with regard to any Land Use Plan or other
 development applications, as the parties recognize and agree that any development application
 shall be reviewed based upon its particular merits and its specific compatibility to surrounding

 areas as well as other considerations of the Comprehensive Plan and Land Development
 Regulations and the availability of required public services and facilities; and
 WHEREAS, notwithstanding the findings above, there are numerous requirements
 relating to transportation, utility service, police and fire service, schools, and other issues which
 shall be relevant and dispositive when any decision on a land use change is made; and
 WHEREAS, OWNERS recognize that the Land presently has no road system or access
 for the proposed development, nor does it have utility service or facilities, public safety or public
 works facilities to serve it, public schools, nor parks and recreation space and facilities to serve it
 and OWNERS shall be required to provide such facilities, and
 WHEREAS, any land use change must also be approved by Broward County; and
 WHEREAS, the OWNERS have indicated a desire to construct residential developments
 as planned unit development similar in density, design, landscape treatment, amenities, mix and
 quality of housing types and lot sizes and quality, to the Heron Bay development within
 PARKLAND; and
 WHEREAS, PARKLAND notes that the density, mix and quality of housing types and
 lot sizes and design, landscape treatment and amenities, and quality of Heron Bay is compatible
 with the goals of PARKLAND for the quality of residential development within PARKLAND;
 and
 WHEREAS, OWNERS are aware of, and support, PARKLAND'S rigorous community
 appearance process and standards, and requirements for"park-like development;' and
 WHEREAS, incorporation of all residential and commercial development with
 PARKLAND'S multi-purpose trail system, to include any necessary dedications, shall be

<<PG>>
 deemed a requirement for any development in addition to the park impact as set forth in
 PARKLAND regulations; and
 WHEREAS, OWNERS agree that the conditions set forth herein represent reasonable
 development requirements that are rationally related to the development levels set forth herein
 and will benefit the Land; and
 WHEREAS, based upon the commitments set forth herein, PARKLAND will support
 the annexation of the Land into PARKLAND;
 WHEREAS, the OWNERS represent that they currently lawfully operate a long standing
 and functioning agricultural business on the Land consistent with the existing ordinances and
 regulations of the Counties of Palm Beach and Broward, as applicable, and of the State of
 Florida ("OWNERS' Existing Use"), and solely as it relates to enforcement of PARKLAND
 regulations, OWNERS acknowledge and agree that the OWNERS' use of the Land shall be
 governed by the applicable existing ordinances and regulations until such time as the Land is
 converted to a non-agricultural use, though OWNERS acknowledge that PARKLAND cannot
 commit any other governmental or regulatory agency in this regard; and
 WHEREAS, OWNERS understand that the conditions and requirements set forth herein
 by PARKLAND are not in any way intended to be all inclusive or exhaustive;
 NOW THEREFORE, be it agreed by and between the parties as follows:
 Section 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a
 part hereof.
 Section 2. Upon the effectiveness of the annexation, PARKLAND shall permit
 OWNERS to apply for an Amendment to the PARKLAND Land Use Plan related to the Lands
 permitting a development with forty (40) acres of Commercial at the intersection of the proposed
 4

<<PG>>
 rights of way for University Drive and County Line Road and residential density of 2 dwelling
 units per acre dashed line around the entire Land ("Land Use Proposal"). Prior to the
 effectiveness of the annexation, the OWNERS will be filing for an amendment to the Broward
 County and Unincorporated Areas land use plans for the Land Use Proposal. Said application
 would also require a Land Use Plan change incorporating the Land into PARKLAND, and would
 also require amendments to the Broward County Land Use Plan. The actual approval of any
 Land Use Plan Amendments shall require public hearings and appropriate findings as to
 infrastructure, transportation/roads, schools, utilities (which shall be deemed to include potable
 water, sanitary sewer and drainage as well as necessary power service), public safety facilities
 and all other matters relevant to such a land use change in support thereof. The approval of the
 Land Use Plan Amendments is not mandated by this Agreement, nor is any other development
 approval.
 Section 3. The OWNERS agree that any development approvals for the Land shall
 require provision of parks, open space, recreational facilities, public facilities (including but not
 limited to public safety and public works facilities), utilities (as defined above), public
 educational facilities and roads (collectively referred to as Development Infrastructure), as well
 as compliance with all applicable regulations including any lawful development exaction or
 impact fee regulations existing at the time of development.
 Section 4. The OWNERS recognize that the present Development Infrastructure is
 not sufficient to support development on the Land and that actual construction or provision of
 said Development Infrastructure must take place concurrent with any development. The
 OWNERS further recognize that PARKLAND will not provide water and wastewater service,
 nor will it construct the necessary roads to service the Land, and that OWNERS shall be required

<<PG>>
 to make separate arrangements to assure that these two infrastructure elements meet applicable
 standards. Moreover, OWNERS shall be responsible for providing or paying their fair share of
 any other portion of the Development Infrastructure not specifically provided for herein.
 Specifically, OWNERS recognizes that dedication and construction of County Line
 Road right-of-way for a four lane divided road with a width of 120 feet (or as otherwise required
 by Broward County) shall be necessary as generally depicted on the Broward County
 Trafficways Plan and shall be a condition of any development permit for the Land. OWNERS
 also recognize that dedication and construction of a portion of University Drive abutting its
 property as a 120' right-of-way depicted on the Broward County Trafficways Plan (or otherwise
 required by Broward County) and Lox Road as a 120' right-of-way as depicted on the Palm
 Beach County Thoroughfare Right-of-Way Identification Map (as it existed prior to the
 boundary change) or the Broward County Trafficways Plan (if included at the time of dedication)
 (and as otherwise required by Broward County) shall also be necessary to support the
 development of the Land and other land within PARKLAND and shall properly be a condition of
 any development permit. OWNERS shall dedicate the right-of-way for University Drive as
 depicted on the Broward-County Trafficways Plan and Lox Road as depicted on the Palm Beach
 Thoroughfare Right-of-Way Map (or Broward County Trafficways Plan if included therein) to
 PARKLAND or Broward County (if required by Broward County) by warranty deed. These
 roadway obligations may be in addition to other roadways found necessary for safe and adequate
 access or otherwise lawfully required by other agencies. OWNERS shall dedicate the four lane
 County Line Road prior to any plat recordation for any portion of the Land. OWNERS shall
 dedicate the right-of-way for the University Drive and the Lox Road right-of-ways as described
 above prior to any plat recordation for any portion of the Land. However, in the event that, there

 the additional minimum five (5) acres per thousand residents private open space requirement.
