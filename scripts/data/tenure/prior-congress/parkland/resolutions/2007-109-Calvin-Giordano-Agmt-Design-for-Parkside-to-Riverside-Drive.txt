 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE CITY
 5 MANAGER TO EXECUTE A WORK AUTHORIZATION
 6 WITH CALVIN-GIORDANO, INC., FOR THE PURPOSE OF
 7 PROVIDING ENGINEERING DESIGN SERVICES FOR
 8 CERTAIN INFRASTRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS ALONG
 9 HOLMBERG ROAD BETWEEN STATE ROAD 7 AND
 10 PARKSIDE DRIVE IN THE AMOUNT OF $111,230.00,
 11 PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 12
 13
 14 WHEREAS, monies were budgeted in the City of Parkland Fiscal Year
 15 2008 Budget for the purpose of designing and constructing bike lanes, fire
 16 hydrants, and a sidewalk along Holmberg Road between State Road 7 and
 17 Parkside Drive; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, on January 3, 2007 the City Commission via Resolution No.
 20 2007-03 authorized a Professional Services Agreement between Calvin-
 21 Giordano, Inc. and the City of Parkland to provide engineering and related
 22 services on as needed basis; and
 23
 24 WHEREAS, it is necessary to secure engineering design services to
 25 support the aforementioned infrastructure improvements; and obtain all
 26 applicable Federal, State, and local permits; and
 27
 28 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes is in the best
 29 interest of the citizens of the City of Parkland to execute a work authorization
 30 with Calvin Giordano, Inc. to provide engineering design and permitting for the
 31 aforementioned infrastructure improvements along Holmberg Road between
 32 State Road 7 and Parkside Drive; and
 33
 34 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 35 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
 36
 37 SECTION 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and
 38 ratified as being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herein.
 39
 40 SECTION 2. The appropriate city officials are hereby authorized and
 41 directed to execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent
 42 of this resolution.
 43
 44 SECTION 3. All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are
 45 hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
 46
 47 SECTION 4. If any clause, section, other part or application of this
 48 Resolution is held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unconstitutional or
 49 invalid, in part of application, it shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions
 50 of this Resolution.
