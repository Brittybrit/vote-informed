 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-92
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO
 EXPEND PUBLIC FUNDS FOR THE PURPOSE OF ADVOCATING
 SUPPORT OF THE PROPOSED CHARTER AMENDMENTS WHICH
 ARE THE SUBJECT OF A NOVEMBER 7, 2006 REFERENDUM;
 BUDGETING APPROPRIATE FUNDS FOR SAID PURPOSE; MAKING A
 FINDING OF PUBLIC PURPOSE AND BENEFIT; AUTHORIZING THE
 CITY MANAGER TO TAKE ALL NECESSARY ACTIONS TO
 EFFECTUATE THE INTENT OF THIS RESOLUTION
 WHEREAS, the citizens of the City of Parkland will be voting on several referendum
 questions providing for amendments to the City Charter of the City of Parkland on November 7,
 2006; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland is permitted by law to expend public funds for the
 purposes of advocating passage of the referendum questions; and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland makes a finding that it is in the
 best interests of the health, safety and welfare of the residents and citizens of the City of Parkland
 to be educated as to these Charter amendments and that these Charter amendments be adopted and
 become part of the Charter; and
 WHEREAS, in order to effectuate this public purpose and benefit, the City Commission
 believes that it is appropriate and in the interest of the citizens to expend public funds to support
 passage of all of the City of Parkland Charter amendments being voted upon at the November 7,
 2006 election;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 SECTION 1. The above Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a part hereof
 SECTION 2. The City Commission finds that it is in the best interests of the citizens of
 the City of Parkland to support and advocate the approval of all of the proposed Charter
 amendments to be voted upon on November 7, 2006 by the citizens of the City of Parkland. In
 furtherance thereof, the City Commission hereby appropriates the sum of $20,000 for a public
 education program related to the proposed Charter amendments.
 SECTION 3. The City Manager is hereby authorized and directed to do all things
 necessary to effectuate the intent of this Resolution and to comply with any rules and regulations
 necessary in furtherance of this Resolution and the expenditure of funds as set forth herein.
