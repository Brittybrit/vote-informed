 AGREEMENT FOR POST-DISASTER
 DEBRIS MONITORING SERVICES
 THIS AGREEMENT is entered into by and between the CITY OF PARKLAND (City), a
 municipal corporation and Malcolm Pirnie, Inc. (Contractor), as follows:
 WITNESSETH:
 WHEREAS, on April 10, 2007, the City and Contractor entered into an agreement to
 provide for Post-Disaster Debris Monitoring Services; and
 WHEREAS, pursuant to said contract and the understanding between the parties, the
 contract is to be administered in a fashion so as to assure that the City will be eligible for all
 reimbursement programs issued by the federal government, state of Florida, or Broward County;
 and
 WHEREAS, the City wishes to amend the agreement entered into on April 10, 2007 (the
 Agreement) as set forth herein, to provide further assurances of same;
 NOW THEREFORE,be it agreed by and between the parties as follows:
 Section 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a part
 hereof.
 Section 2. The Contractor shall be required to be in compliance with the following federal
 — aid requirements as part of the Agreement with the City:
 a. FHWA Form 1273, titled Standard Federal — Aid Provisions, as attached hereto as
 Exhibit A, shall be deemed a part of the contract and shall be physically in and made a part of any
 subcontract. All references to the Contractor shall apply to Contractor in the Agreement and the
 requirements in Exhibit A shall be deemed a binding part of the Agreement. In recognition of this
 fact, each page of the attachment has been initialed by Contractor.
 b. Davis-Bacon Wages Act;
 c. By America;
 d. Disadvantage Business Enterprises;
 e. American's With Disabilities Act;
 f. Convict Labor Prohibition.
 Additionally, any repairs undertaken by the Contractor shall comply with the requirements
 of the National Environmental Policy Act.

<<PG>>
 Section 3. In all other respects the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect except
 as specifically amended herein.
 Section 4. This amendment shall take effect on the date that the last party affixes its
 signature hereto.
 Section 5. No amendment of this Agreement may be permitted except by execution of a
 written document executed with equal dignity.
