 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-86
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, TO AWARD THE BID
 FOR THE CITY OF PARKLAND NATURAL AREAS
 EXOTIC PLANT SPECIES TREATMENT (RFP NO. DSD
 2007-10) FOR THE TREATMENT AND REMOVAL OF
 EXOTIC PLANTS IN THE PARKSIDE DRIVE TRAILHEAD,
 38 ACRE HOLMBERG ROAD PRESERVE, TERRAMAR
 PARK PRESERVE AND THE MARGATE BLOUNT
 ARCHEOLOGICAL SITE, AND TO AUTHORIZE THE
 APPROPRIATE CITY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE A
 CONTRACT, AND CONTAINING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland, Florida, is authorized
 to award the bid for the City of Parkland Natural Areas Exotic Plant Species Treatment
 (RFP No. DSD 2007-10); and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland received three (3) bids from the following
 bidders: Native Technologies, Inc., Florida Best, Inc., and Tree Huggers Landscaping;
 and
 WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Parkland has fully reviewed the
 bids submitted and has determined that the bid from Native Technologies, Inc. is the
 lowest responsible and responsive bid which best meets the criteria set forth in the bid
 packet and that, based upon the base bid price of $89,088.14 and other factors to be
 considered, it is in the best interest of the City to accept said bid.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. The foregoing "WHEREAS" clauses are confirmed and ratified as
 being true and correct and are hereby incorporated herin.
 Section 2. That Native Technologies, Inc. is hereby awarded the bid in the
 total amount of$89,088.14 for the City of Parkland Natural Areas Exotic Plant Species
 treatment (RFP No. DSD2007-10).
 Section 3. The appropriate City Officials are hereby authorized and directed
 to execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this
 Resolution.
 Section 4. That the Funds shall be taken from the Environmental Work Plan
 Capital Improvement Project Line Item# 305 701.901 506.029.
