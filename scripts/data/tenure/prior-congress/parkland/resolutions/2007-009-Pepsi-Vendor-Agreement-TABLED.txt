RESOLUTION NO. 2007-09
A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE CITY COMMISSION
TO ENTER INTO A PROFESSIONAL SERVICE AGREEMENT WITH
PEPSI AMERICAS TO PROVIDE VENDING, PRICING,
SPONSORSHIP REVENUE AND SCOREBOARDS FOR THE CITY OF
PARKLAND IN EXCHANGE FOR EXCLUSIVITY.
WHEREAS, the City Commission is authorized to enter into a five (5) year
professional service agreement with Pepsi Americas to provide vending, pricing,
sponsorship revenue and scoreboards for the City Of Parkland. in exchange for
exclusivity; and
WHEREAS, this agreement will provide for two (2) new scoreboards in _the
Community Center, discounted .pricing on purchased product for the concessionaire,
sponsorship for the Annual Concert and donation of one .hundred (100) cases of product
each year; and
WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes signing this agreement
is in the best interest of the citizens of Parkland; and
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
SECTION 1. The appropriate City officials are hereby authorized. and directed to
execute the necessary documents to effectuate the purpose and intent of this resolution.
SECTION 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force .and effect
immediately upon its passage and adoption.
PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS DAY OF , 2007.
CITY OF PARKLAND
MICHAEL UDINE, MAYOR
