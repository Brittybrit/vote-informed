 4 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 5 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AWARDING A CONTRACT TO
 6 FLORIDA LEAGUE OF CITIES FOR CASUALTY
 7 INSURANCE PROGRAM FOR THE CITY OF PARKLAND.
 8
 9 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has competitively bid the casualty
 10 insurance program for the City; and
 11
 12 WHEREAS, the City contracted with insurance specialist, Sid Weber, from
 13 Inter Risk Corporation, to assist in the process of bidding and analysis of the bids;
 14 and
 15 WHEREAS, after receipt of bids, Inter Risk Corporation and the City Finance
 16 Department find that the lowest responsible and responsive bidder is Florida
 17 Municipal Insurance Trust (FMIT) submitted by Florida League of Cities; and
 18
 19 WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that accepting this bid would be in
 20 the best interests of the citizens of the City of Parkland;
 21
 22 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 23 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
 24
 25 SECTION 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct
 26 and made a part hereof.
 27
 28 SECTION 2. The City Commission hereby awards the contract for City of
 29 Parkland casualty insurance program to FMIT as submitted by Florida League of
 30 Cities. The coverage is to be provided for 12 months in the amount of
 31 $222,451.00. The City Manager is authorized to execute any necessary documents
 32 to effectuate this bid award and to secure the necessary coverage pursuant to the
 33 bid process.
 34
 35 SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
 36 adoption.
 37
 38 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 24 day of September , 2008.
