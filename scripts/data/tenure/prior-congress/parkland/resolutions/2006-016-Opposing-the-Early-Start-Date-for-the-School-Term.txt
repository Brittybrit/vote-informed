 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-16
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, SUPPORTING THE STATE
 LEGISLATURE PROPOSED HOUSE BILL AND SENATE BILL
 AMENDMENTS RELATING TO THE OPENING DATE OF THE
 SCHOOL YEAR.
 WHEREAS, the State Legislature intends to amend section 1001.42 of the
 Florida Statues by adjusting the opening date of the school year to be no earlier than 7
 days before Labor Day each year, and
 WHEREAS, the State of Florida requires an adequate school program in order to
 participate in the Florida Education Finance Program, and
 WHEREAS, the school start date will become a requirement of said Finance
 Program,
 WHEREAS, this amendment shall apply to the school year beginning with the
 2007-2008 school year unless the district school board decides to make such provisions
 applicable beginning with the 2006-2007 school year.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. That the City Commission does hereby encourage the 2006
 Broward County Legislative Delegation to approve Florida House of Representatives Bill
 177 and Florida Senate Bill 306 providing a requirement relating to the opening date of
 the school year.
 Section 2. That the City Clerk is hereby directed to provide a copy of this
 Resolution to Governor Jeb Bush, Broward County Legislative Delegation, the Broward
 League of Cities, the Florida League of Cities, the Broward County Commission & the
 Broward County School Board.
 Section 2. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect
 immediately upon its passage and adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 15 DAY OF February , 2006.
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA
