 1 RESOLUTION NO. 2007-04

 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION
 4 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA,
 5 AUTHORIZING THE APPROPRIATE CITY
 6 OFFICIAL TO ENTER INTO A
 7 PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT
 8 WITH CRAVEN THOMPSON AND
 9 ASSOCIATES, INC. TO PROVIDE
 10 ENGINEERING AND RELATED SERVICES ON
 11 AN AS NEEDED BASIS, PROVIDING AN
 12 EFFECTIVE DATE.
 13
 14
 15 WHEREAS, City staff advertised RFQ #2006-07-2 entitled "Professional
 16 Engineering and Miscellaneous Consulting Services" for the purpose of having a
 17 list of pre-qualified firms to provide services on a continuing "as needed" basis;
 18 and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, a selection committee consisting of the City Manager,
 21 Assistant City Manager, Public Services Director, Planning Director, and
 22 Environmental Resources Director read, reviewed, and short listed nine (9) firms
 23 in accordance with the Consultants' Competitive Negotiation Act (CCNA); and
 24
 25 WHEREAS, the nine (9) short listed firms were provided the opportunity to
 26 introduce their team and provide an oral presentation to the City Commission at
 27 a December 6, 2006 Special City Commission Meeting; and
 28
 29 WHEREAS, at the Regular City Commission Meeting on December 6,
 30 2006, the City Commission ranked the top five (5) firms and authorized the City
 31 Manager to negotiate contracts with the top three (3) firms; and
 32
 33 WHEREAS, Craven Thompson and Associates, Inc. was one of the top
 34 three (3) firms; and
 35
 36 WHEREAS, staff recommends that the City enter into an Agreement with
 37 Craven Thompson and Associates, Inc. for the purpose of providing professional
 38 engineering and miscellaneous consulting services on an ongoing basis; and
 39
 40 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believes an Agreement
 41 with Craven Thompson and Associates, Inc. is in the best interest of the citizens
 42 of the City of Parkland; and
 43
 44 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF
 45 THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, THAT:
