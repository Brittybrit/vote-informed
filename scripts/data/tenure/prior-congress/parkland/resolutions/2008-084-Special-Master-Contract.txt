 RESOLUTION NO.2008-84
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, APPOINTING SPECIAL MASTERS
 FOR CODE ENFORCEMENT MATTERS; CONTAINING
 PROVISIONS FOR IMPLEMENTATION; PROVIDING FOR
 SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE CITY
 CODE; PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 WHEREAS, pursuant to Ordinance No. 2006-01 of the City of Parkland, the
 City is to appoint Special Masters to hear code enforcement matters brought pursuant to
 the authority of Chapter 162, Florida Statutes; and
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland has reviewed the qualifications of Mitchell
 Kraft and Mike Cirullo and find that they satisfy the requirements for Special Masters and
 are capable of effectively implementing the procedures set forth in Ordinance No. 2006-
 01.
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY
 COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA:
 Section 1. The City Commission hereby appoints Mitchell Kraft and Mike
 Cirullo as code enforcement Special Masters to undertake the duties as set forth in
 Ordinance No. 2006-01. The appointments set forth herein shall be for a term of one (1)
 year with the City's right to extend for one (1) additional year.
