 RESOLUTION NO. 2006 -19
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE
 MAYOR TO EXECUTE A FIRST AMENDMENT TO THE
 INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH HOWARD C.FORMAN,
 CLERK OF THE COURT FOR THE SEVENTEENTH
 JUDICIAL CIRCUIT OF THE STATE OF FLORIDA TO
 PROVIDE FOR THE PROCESSING OF MUNICIPAL
 PARKING TICKETS.
 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland issues parking citations to individuals who violate the
 City's parking regulations;
 WHEREAS,Howard C.Foreman,Clerk of the Court for the 17th Judicial Circuit has offered
 his services for the processing of municipal parking tickets;
 WHEREAS, on April 19, 2005, through Resolution No. 2005-26, the City of Parkland
 entered into an Interlocal Agreement with Howard C.Foreman to provide services for the processing
 of municipal parking tickets;
 WHEREAS, the Broward League of Cities worked with Howard C. Foreman to enable
 Broward County and the City to continue to utilize the services of the Broward County Court system
 for the processing of the City's parking tickets;
 WHEREAS,State Statute has changed necessitated amendments to the previously approved
 Interlocal Agreement;
 WHEREAS,the City of Parkland finds it desirable to enter into the First Amendment to the
 Interlocal Agreement for the processing of the City's parking tickets; and
 WHEREAS,such an Amendment has been prepared and a copy thereof is on file in the City
 Clerk's Office;

<<PG>>
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 Section 1. The City Commission of the City of Parkland hereby approves the First
 Amendment to the Interlocal Agreement for the processing and collection of parking citations
 attached hereto as Exhibit A and authorizes the Mayor to execute same.
 Section 3. That this Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 15 DAY OF February ,2006.
