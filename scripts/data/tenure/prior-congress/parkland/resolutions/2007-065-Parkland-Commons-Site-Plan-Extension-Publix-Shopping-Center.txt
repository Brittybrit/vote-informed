 3 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 4 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA APPROVING THE SITE PLAN
 5 EXTENSION FOR PARKLAND COMMONS LOCATED AT
 6 THE NORTHWEST CORNER OF TRAILS END AND

 11 WHEREAS, the owner of the property subject to Resolution 2005-19 has
 12 requested an extension of its site plan approval of eighteen (18) months; and
 13
 14 WHEREAS, the City Commission approved this site plan by Resolution 2005-19
 15 on April 25, 2005; and
 16
 17 WHEREAS, said approval was valid for eighteen (18) months, and expired as of
 18 October 25, 2006; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission believe that authorizing this
 21 extension is in the best interest of the citizens of Parkland.
 22
 23 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 24 PARKLAND, FLORIDA that:
 25
 26 SECTION 1. That the appropriate City officials are hereby authorized to approve
 27 the extension of the site plan for "Parkland Commons — North Parcel" in the amount of six
 28 (6) months from the date of this Resolution.
 29
 30 SECTION 2. The owner shall obtain all Broward County approvals for the building
 31 permits no later than September 1, 2007. All applicable documentation shall be provided to
 32 the City of Parkland no later than September 1, 2007.
 33
 34 SECTION 3. All conditions of Resolution No's. 2005-15, 2005-18, 2005-19, 2005-
 35 20, shall remain and be in full force and effect.
 36
 37 SECTION 4. That this Resolution shall become in full force and effect immediately
 38 upon its passage and adoption.
