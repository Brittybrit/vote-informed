 4 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY
 5 OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING A TWELVE
 6 MONTH WAIVER OF THE MONTHLY FEE TO BE PAID BY
 7 YOUNG AT ART OF BROWARD, INC. ACCORDING TO ITS
 8 PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT WITH THE CITY
 9 OF PARKLAND.
 10
 11
 12 WHEREAS, Young At Art of Broward, Inc. (Young At Art) has entered into a
 13 Professional Services Agreement with the City of Parkland to provide art education
 14 programming at the City of Parkland Community Center; and
 15
 16 WHEREAS, because of economic times, and other factors, Young At Art needs
 17 certain relief under that Professional Services Agreement to continue providing its art
 18 education services; and
 19
 20 WHEREAS, the City of Parkland City Commission wishes to foster the activities
 21 of Young At Art at the Community Center;
 22
 23 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION
 24 OF THE CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 25
 26 SECTION 1. The City Manager is hereby authorized to waive the monthly fee
 27 due by Young At Art under the Professional Services Agreement with the City of
 28 Parkland for the period of May 9, 2007 through May 9, 2009. Thereafter, the monthly
 29 fee and all other fees required shall be due and payable per the terms of the lease by
 30 Young At Art.
