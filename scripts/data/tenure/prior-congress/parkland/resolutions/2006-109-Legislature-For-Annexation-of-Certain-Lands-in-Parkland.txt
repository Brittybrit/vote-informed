 RESOLUTION NO. 2006-109
 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF
 PARKLAND, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE FILING OF A SPECIAL
 ACT OF THE LEGISLATURE FOR ANNEXATION OF CERTAIN LANDS
 INTO THE CITY OF PARKLAND.
 WHEREAS, there are certain unincorporated lands completely surrounded by the City of
 Parkland which are appropriate for annexation into the City of Parkland; and
 WHEREAS, this property is generally described on Exhibit A (hereinafter referred to as
 the Property); and
 WHEREAS, the City has previously held discussions with the owner on the issue of
 annexation and the City believes that consistent with Broward County Legislative Delegation
 policies, and appropriate governance for the Property, that it is in the best interest to annex the
 Property into the corporate boundaries of the City of Parkland; and
 WHEREAS, the Property is completely contiguous with the City of Parkland and meets
 all requirements and prerequisites for annexation into the City;
 NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
 CITY OF PARKLAND, FLORIDA AS FOLLOWS:
 SECTION 1. The above referenced Whereas clauses are true and correct and made a part
 hereof.
 SECTION 2. The City Manager is hereby authorized to submit a local bill to the
 Broward County Legislative Delegation for the purposes of annexing the Property into the
 corporate boundaries of the City of Parkland. The City Manager is authorized to undertake all
 other necessary actions in furtherance of the spirit and intent of this resolution and the filing of the
 local bill.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 1 day of Novemb , 2006.
