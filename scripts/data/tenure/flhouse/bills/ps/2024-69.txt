C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
       The bill may have a positive impact on merchants who lose money from organized retail theft, to the
       extent that the requirements of the bill may result in more orders of restitution or orders of restitution in
       greater amounts. Restitution generally must be ordered in theft cases under current law, and the impact
       of the bill’s changes on the frequency or value of restitution orders is indeterminate.
