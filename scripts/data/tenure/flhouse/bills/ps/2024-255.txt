C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill may have a positive economic impact on the private sector to the extent that it induces victims
       of the Dozier School or the Okeechobee School to apply for compensation through the Program and
       protects them from fraud or other financial harm.
