Private Sector Impact:

             Increasing the amount of fines that may be assessed against a third-party voter
             registration organization will financially impact those organizations that fail to comply
             with statutory requirements.

             Increasing fines for certain election law violations will financially impact persons who
             fail to comply with statutory requirements.
