           Effect of the Bill

           The bill transfers lands commonly known as Wellington Preserve at the Marjory Stoneman Douglas
           Everglades Habitat and the Moncada Property from the Pine Tree Water Control District to the AID.

           The purpose of the transfer of territory is to facilitate the incorporation of the Moncada Property into the
           existing Wellington Preserve at the Marjory Stoneman Douglas Everglades Habitat, and to preserve
           public resources by allowing the Village of Wellington to direct financial resources to developing the
           area for the benefit of the public. The addition of the Moncada property to Wellington Preserve will add
           45 acres to the preserve.
