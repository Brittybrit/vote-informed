DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill may have a positive economic impact on franchised dealers in the state as it protects their
       current business model even where the licensee with whom they have the agreement expands into EV
       production and sales. However, the bill may have a negative economic impact on consumers in the
       state to the extent that it prevents them from buying a vehicle using the potentially less-expensive
       direct-to-consumer sales model.
