       The bill increases the compensation for board members of the Authority from $100 to $250 for each
       meeting attended, and increases the compensation cap from $1,200 to $3,000 per member per year.

       The bill authorizes the board to employ or terminate the employment of the airport manager, who may
       be referred to as the executive director, by a two-thirds vote of the members of the authority, with at
       least one majority vote made by a county appointee. The bill also prohibits the board members from
       interfering with the day-to-day administration of the airport manager’s duties.

       The bill updates and provides definitions for terms used in the charter.
