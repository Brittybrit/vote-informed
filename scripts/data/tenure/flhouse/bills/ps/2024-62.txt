C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The proposed public record exemption may encourage and attract the formation of new banks or trust
       companies as Florida-chartered institutions, in which case the bill would have a positive impact on
       investment, employment, economic growth, and consumer access to financial services. However, the
       impact to the private sector is indeterminate.
