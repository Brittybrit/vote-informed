     Effects of the Bill

            The bill revises the boundaries of the district to correct errors in the property description and to add an
            additional tract to the district. The additional tract increases the total acreage of the district by
            approximately 949 acres, bringing the district to a total of 3,686.495 acres.

            The Economic Impact Statement indicates the bill will raise an expected $150,000 and $172,500 in
            additional revenue in the first and second fiscal year after the bill takes effect, all of which is anticipated
            to be used for infrastructure and costs related to the jurisdictional expansion.
