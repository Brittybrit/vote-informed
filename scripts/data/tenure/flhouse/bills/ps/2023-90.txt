Private Sector Impact:

                The bill will have a fiscal impact on hospitals, ASCs, or physician offices registered for
                office surgery that fail to provide the attestation required under sections 3, 7, and 8 of the
                bill. The bill may have an indeterminate fiscal impact on persons seeking sex
                reassignment treatment and health care practitioners or facilities who provide such
                treatment.
