C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

         Health care providers previously not required to undergo a background screening will incur the cost
         associated with obtaining a background screening. These costs amount to $61.25 per person for
         applicants screened and retained within the Care Provider Background Screening Clearinghouse. This
         amount does not include additional servicing fees which may be assessed by the screening service
         provider.74
