C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill authorizes RPEs to proceed with an unsolicited proposal for a qualifying project without
       engaging in a public bidding process if certain conditions are met. Accordingly, private entities may
       experience less competition when contracting with RPEs through such means resulting in more
       economically attractive contracts for the private entities. The financial impact to the private sector is
       indeterminate at this time.
