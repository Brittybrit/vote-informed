C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
      The bill may have a positive economic impact on the private sector to the extent that it ensures an HOA
      member has knowledge of all applicable covenants, rules, and regulations by which he or she must
      live, and such knowledge leads to a financial benefit (such as the avoidance of fines and liens for rule
      violations) for the HOA member.
