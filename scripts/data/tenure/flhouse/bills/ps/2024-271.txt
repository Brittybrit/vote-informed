C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
       The bill may have a positive economic impact on a professional firm shielded by sovereign immunity
       protections due to the bill’s expansion of s. 768.28(10)(e), F.S., as its liability would be capped by the
       statutory recovery limits in s. 768.28(5)(a), F.S. However, the bill may have a negative economic impact
       on persons injured by the negligence of such a professional firm or its employees, as their recovery
       would likewise be limited by the statutory recovery limits placed in s. 768.28, F.S., absent passage of a
       claim bill.
