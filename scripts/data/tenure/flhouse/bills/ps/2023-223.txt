DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill limits the liability of appraisers and appraisal management companies by imposing statutes of
       limitations and repose on actions for damages against such persons or companies for acts or
       omissions in the performance of appraisal services or appraisal management services.
