Private Sector Impact:

               Public employers may realize a cost savings by the narrowing application of the bill to
               active federal military service of at least a minimum duration of 90 days. A
               servicemember called to active federal military service for fewer than 90 days would not
               be eligible for up to 30 days of pay by the public employer.
