DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

        To the extent that homes that participate in the MSFH Program receive mitigation credits under their
        insurance policies and are less exposed to risk as a result of mitigation retrofitting using grant funds,
        the MSFH Program will have a positive direct economic impact on homeowners. Hurricane mitigation
        contractors may also see an increase in project activity.
