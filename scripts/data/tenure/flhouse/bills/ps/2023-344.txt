       Fire Department

       The bill removes the provision establishing a fire warden, subordinate officers, and firefighters as
       employees of the Town. Instead, the bill provides that fire protection services in the Town will be
       provided by Columbia County.

       Town Auditor, Clerk, Tax Assessor, Town Treasurer, and Municipal Court

       The bill amends the duties of the Town clerk, providing that the clerk will:
           Keep the journal of the Town council’s proceedings;
           Authenticate and maintain all ordinances and resolutions of the Town council;
           Ensure that Town elections are conducted in accordance with law;
           Exercise budgetary control over every department, board, mission, and agency of the Town;
           Perform all duties and responsibilities conferred by this charter; and
           Perform all other administrative duties of the Town.

       The bill removes provisions providing for a town auditor, tax assessor, and treasurer, as these duties
       are now performed by other offices. The bill also removes references to municipal courts to reflect the
       modern structure of the judicial system.

       Fiscal

       The bill amends the fiscal year of the Town to change it from the calendar year to a fiscal year
       beginning on October 1 and ending on September 30 in order to align with other local governments.
