DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
        The bill requires warranty associations to have a reserve of $100 million, instead of $10 million. The bill
        requires motor vehicle insurers bear the entire cost of mediation. It makes various other changes that
        have an indeterminate, negative fiscal impact.
