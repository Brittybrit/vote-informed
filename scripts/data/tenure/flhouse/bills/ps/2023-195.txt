DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill prohibits a convicted person from profiting or benefitting from violating the provisions of the bill
       and provides that any such proceeds are subject to seizure and forfeiture under the FCFA. Thus, a
       person who is convicted of interfering with a sporting or entertainment event will no longer be able to
       profit or benefit from such behavior.
