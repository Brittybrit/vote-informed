Private Sector Impact:

                  To the extent that any payment settlement entities, merchant acquiring entities, or third
                  party settlement organizations violate the provisions in the bill or contest any
                  administrative fines imposed by the DACS for such violations, the bill may result in an
                  indeterminate fiscal impact on them.

                  Government Sector Impact:

                  To the extent that violators contest any administrative fines through the formal
                  administrative hearing process, the bill may result in an indeterminate fiscal impact on
                  the DACS and the state court system.

                  The DACS is unable to estimate any fiscal impact the proposed bill may have on the
                  agency.105
