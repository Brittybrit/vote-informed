Private Sector Impact:

                The bill may provide cost savings to eligible students by way of tuition assistance for
                those students who are enrolled in an eligible postsecondary institution which be eligible
                to participate in this postsecondary educational grant pursuant to the Effective Access to
                Student Education (EASE) grant program.
