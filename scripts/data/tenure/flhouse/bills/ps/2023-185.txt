DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill will require distributors of dosage form animal products to comply with Commercial Feed Law
       regulatory requirements governed by DACS, including the payment of fees and enforcement of
       penalties.

       Dosage form animal health products currently sold may need to be recalled to be accurately labelled,
       which may have a negative fiscal impact to the distributors and business owners of those products.
