Private Sector Impact:

             The ability of a financial institution to place a delay on a disbursement or transaction,
             may decrease losses to account holders who are financially preyed upon because such a
             delay may prevent the money from ever getting into the hands of the bad actor. Once the
             bad actor receives the money, it is difficult, or in some cases impossible, to ever recover
             the money.

             The bill may result in increased training and compliance costs for financial institutions.
