Private Sector Impact:

               The clarification in the bill that purchasers, transferees, and assignees of a valid pari-
               mutuel permit may hold a valid pari-mutuel permit, if approved by the commission
               before the change in ownership, eliminates an inadvertent uncertainty in current law, for
               those permitholders authorized to conduct pari-mutuel wagering who wish to sell,
               transfer, or assign a valid pari-mutuel permit and its associated licenses.
