C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
      The bill may have a positive economic impact on the private sector to the extent it leads to the
      preservation of:
          Records identifying a decedent’s beneficiaries, which may help true heirs avoid economic loss
              where real property ownership passes through probate by intestacy succession.
          A surviving spouse’s community property rights.
