Private Sector Impact:

               Because the bill prohibits operators from engaging in targeted advertising; places new
               and significant restrictions on operators’ collection and use of students’ online personal
               information; and prohibits operators from sharing, selling, or renting such information,
               operators will no longer be able to financially benefit from such activities. Additionally,
               because the bill requires operators to adhere to new baseline privacy and security
               protections in connection with students’ online personal information, operators will incur
               costs associated with implementing these measures and complying with the bill.
