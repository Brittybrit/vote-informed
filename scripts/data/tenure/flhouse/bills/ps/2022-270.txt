Private Sector Impact:

               Toll facility users who are eligible for account credits are expected to realize a positive
               fiscal impact resulting from the prepaid toll account credits. While the exact amount of
               the positive impact (both total and individually) is dependent on the number of eventual
               eligible customers and the number of paid transactions per month per eligible customer.
               Thus, the positive fiscal impact to the pool of eligible toll customers is capped at the
               amount appropriated for the program in the bill, $500 million.
