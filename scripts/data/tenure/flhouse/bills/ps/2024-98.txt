C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

         Broadband Internet providers will benefit from discounted rates for certain attachments made to
         municipal electric utility poles over the next four years. These savings may provide incentives for
         additional investment in broadband infrastructure to reach unserved areas and unserved customers in
         this state. This may result in increased economic activity in areas that currently lack access to
         broadband Internet service.
