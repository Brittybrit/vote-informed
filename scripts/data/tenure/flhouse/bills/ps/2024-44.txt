C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill may make the removal of an unlawful occupant easier for a property owner. As such, the bill
       may have a positive economic impact on the private sector based upon the quicker and more efficient
       removal of such unlawful occupants.
