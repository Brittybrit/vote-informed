DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill may have an indeterminate positive impact on the private sector as the bill authorizes FDLE to
       grant funds to county jails and sheriffs’ offices for the purchase of Rapid DNA machines and other
       necessary supplies, which may be purchased from a private entity.
