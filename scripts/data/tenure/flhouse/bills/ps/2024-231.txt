ANALYST COMMENT

Specific appropriation 2802 provides a total of $28,296,810 to the Legislative Support Services
budget entity. This line item provides funding for several joint offices including the following:
the Office of Economic & Demographic Research (EDR), Office of Program Policy Analysis &
Government Accountability (OPPAGA), Joint Administrative Procedures Committee (JAPC),
Joint Legislative Auditing Committee (JLAC), and Office of Legislative Services (OLS).

The specific appropriation also includes proviso at the beginning of the Legislative Support
Services budget which requires the EDR to conduct a study and present policy options relating to
prohibiting the collection of interchange fees on certain sales taxes.
