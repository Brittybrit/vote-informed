C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill may encourage transactions involving the purchase of water and wastewater utility systems by
       eligible utility systems. These transactions may lead to needed infrastructure improvements, cost
       efficiencies, and improvements in service quality and regulatory compliance for small or troubled utility
       systems, though this result is not required by the bill. For acquisitions by PSC-regulated utilities that
       petition to use the alternative valuation process created in the bill, the PSC may consider potential rate
       impacts and proposed improvement plans in its consideration of each petition. Use of the alternative
       valuation process may lead to higher rates for the affected customers.
