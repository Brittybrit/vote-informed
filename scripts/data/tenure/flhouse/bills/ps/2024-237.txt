C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill may have an indeterminate fiscal impact on parties involved in the dissolution process. The bill
       revises what constitutes a marital asset for the distribution of assets and liabilities and may alter the
       manner by which a court classifies certain property.
