Private Sector Impact:

                     The bill may reduce the amount of time it takes for a mobile phone service provider to
                     obtain ETC status for the Lifeline program. This bill does not alter the pool of eligible
                     consumers, nor will the fees charged to consumers for Lifeline service change.
