C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
       The bill may have a positive economic impact on the private sector to the extent that it:
           Preserves an HVAC system or system component warranty where the original consumer, or a
               subsequent purchaser, does not register the product.
           Allows a class A air-conditioning, class B air-conditioning, or mechanical contractor to complete
               the line-side procedures of an HVAC system or system component repair or replacement in a
               manner that reduces costs for a consumer.

       Further, the bill may have a negative economic impact on HVAC manufacturers, distributors, and
       retailers to the extent that such persons must honor warranties they would not have otherwise had to
       honor.
