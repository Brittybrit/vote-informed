C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

        The bill does not require the use of PG as a construction aggregate material in road construction.
        However, if the study concludes that PG is suitable for such use, and if PG is at some future time
        actually used in road construction, the private sector may benefit from the abundant supply of PG and
        may benefit from the resulting resolution of PG stacks. The fiscal impact is indeterminate.
