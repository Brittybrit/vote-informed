Private Sector Impact:

                The bill appropriates for 2022-2023 fiscal year, $1,757,982 in recurring funds from the
                Insurance Regulatory Trust Fund to the OIR with an associated salary rate of $844,464.
                The funds will be allocated as follows: $1,356,615 for Salaries and Benefits, $400,000
                for Other Personal Services Category, and $1,367 to DMS. Funds also will be used for
                recruitment and retention of personnel within the OIR.
