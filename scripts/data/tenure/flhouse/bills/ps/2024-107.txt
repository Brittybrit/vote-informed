Private Sector Impact:

             The bill increases the funds available for the MSFH Program, making it possible for more
             homes to be granted mitigation grants. Homeowners that complete mitigation projects
             funded by the MSFH Program will receive mitigation credits that reduce the cost of their
             property insurance.
