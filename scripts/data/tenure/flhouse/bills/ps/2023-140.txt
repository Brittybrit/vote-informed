DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

            Fees associated with applying for a license in a compact state would be eliminated for a
            psychologist whose home state is Florida and wants to practice in a compact state, as well as a
            psychologist whose home state is in a compact state and wishes to practice in Florida.
