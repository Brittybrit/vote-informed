DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill may, by creating guardrails for an agreement to pay a fee in lieu of a security deposit and for
       the payment of a security deposit in monthly installments while the tenant participates in the fee
       program, protect from financial loss or harm landlords and tenants who enter into such agreements.
