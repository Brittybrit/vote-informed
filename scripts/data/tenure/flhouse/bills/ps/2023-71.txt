DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Persons with mild to moderate hearing impairment will now be able to purchase OTC hearing aids
       without the supervision, prescription, involvement, or intervention of a licensed professional. This will
       likely reduce the cost of hearing aids for this specific demographic.
