LOCALBILL II. FISCAL ANALYSIS & ECONOMIC IMPACT STATEMENT

   A. FISCAL IMPACT ON STATE GOVERNMENT:

       1. Revenues:

           None.

       2. Expenditures:

           None.

   B. FISCAL IMPACT ON LOCAL GOVERNMENTS:

       1. Revenues:

           The Economic Impact Statement (EIS) estimates the bill will decrease local government revenue by
           $449,835 in Fiscal Year (FY) 2024-2025 and a $449,835 in FY 2025-2026.

       2. Expenditures:

           The EIS estimates a cost of $30,000 for external software developers and other changes impacting
           staff and operations in FY 2024-2025 and a cost of $35,000 for FY 2025-2026. The EIS also states
           other estimated costs will reduce by $220,000 per year.
