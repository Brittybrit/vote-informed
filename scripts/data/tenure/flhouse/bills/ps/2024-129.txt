C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

         The bill may have an indeterminate impact on the private sector as it creates a new civil cause of
         action, which may allow any person whose image or likeness is used without consent in a scheme to
         defraud to recover monetary damages from any entity or individual party to the suit.
