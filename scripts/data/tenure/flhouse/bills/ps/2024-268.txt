C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
       The bill may have a positive economic impact on the private sector to the extent that it preserves
       servitudes that, if extinguished, would result in a reduction in the dominant estate’s value or cause the
       beneficiary to suffer another form of economic loss.
