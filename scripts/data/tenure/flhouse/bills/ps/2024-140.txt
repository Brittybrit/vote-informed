C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill’s provisions relating to unsafe and unsound practices by financial institutions may have an
       indeterminate positive economic impact on the private sector. The bill may lead to fewer financial
       institutions suspending, terminating, or taking similar action restricting customers’ or members’ account
       access without legitimate reason.
