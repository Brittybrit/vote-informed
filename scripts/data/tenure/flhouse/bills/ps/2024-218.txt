Private Sector Impact:

                The Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles’ (DHSMV’s) direct issuance of
                a certificate of title or certificate of destruction when the owner retains a total loss vehicle
                or vessel will have an indeterminate positive impact on insurance companies due to the
                streamlined process.
