Private Sector Impact:

                Eligible applicants will be able to apply to receive a loan to implement innovative
                solutions, which will improve the quality and delivery of health care in Florida, improve
                the work environment for the state’s health care workforce, lead to lower costs, and allow
                savings to be passed on to health care consumers.
