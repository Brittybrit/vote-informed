Private Sector Impact:

                  The method for obtaining a license as a registered contractor authorized in the bill will
                  assist those contractors who chose registration in a local community rather than
                  qualification as a statewide certified contractor, to remain in the workforce when the local
                  jurisdiction determines not to issue local licenses.
