Private Sector Impact:

               The bill will have a positive fiscal impact on the selected organization, which will receive
               the $2.50 collected when a motorcycle is registered in the state. Based on the number of
               motorcycles registered in Florida, the organization will receive approximately $1,626,675
               annually.
