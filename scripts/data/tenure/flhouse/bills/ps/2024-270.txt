C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

        Once a specialty license plate goes into production, organizations authorized for a distribution from the
        proceeds of the sale of the respective license plate will benefit. Therefore, the following organizations
        may see an increase in revenues:
            Various in-state colleges and universities
            Eligible grant entities under the Historic Cemeteries Program
            The American Eagle Foundation
