DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

        Employees eligible to receive family leave insurance benefits can receive compensation they would not
        have otherwise received while unable to work due to eligible parental leave or their own or their family
        member’s serious health condition or military service. Although the paid family leave insurance is
        optional for private employers not otherwise covered under FMLA, the insurance policy would be
        funded by the employer and/or the employee.
