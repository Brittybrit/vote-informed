DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

        The bill may have a positive impact on the revenues of vendors who sell or lease speed detection
        systems to the extent that local governments may contract with them to implement a school zone speed
        detection program.

        A portion of the funds generated from authorized fines is provided for school crossing guard recruitment
        and retention programs, which may include stipends to third parties for the recruitment of new crossing
        guards. The bill may have a positive impact on any such third party’s revenue if a local government
        chooses to spend the funds in that manner.
