C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
        The inclusion of coverage for skin cancer screenings without cost sharing restrictions may positively
        impact physicians who likely will see an increased demand for their services, as well as collateral and
        ancillary medical supports such as laboratories and diagnostic offices which will be called upon to
        process additional lab slips, biopsies, and scans.
