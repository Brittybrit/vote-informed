2.   Senate Sponsor Wilton Simpson

3.   Date of Request 12/08/2019

4.   Project/Program Description
     County Line Road borders Hernando and Pasco Counties and serves as a connection between US 19 to the west and US 41 to the east.
     Currently, there are two segments of County Line Road that are only two lanes, including the segment from Cobblestone Drive (East
     Road) to Mariner Boulevard (Shady Hills Drive). Widening this segment of County Line Road from two lanes to four lanes is planned to
     take place in the 2036-2040 time frame. This request is to obtain funding for additional right-of-way and the design so a roadway
     widening project can begin before 2036.
     This project is included in the Pasco County Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) Draft 2045 Cost Feasible Plan. The project is also
     included in the Hernando/Citrus MPO Draft 2045 Cost Feasible Plan (shown as a Pasco County MPO improvement).

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      The purpose of this request is to obtain funding for right-of-way acquisition and design for the widening of County Line Road from two
      lanes to four lanes between Cobblestone Drive (East Road) and Mariner Boulevard (Shady Hills Road). Currently, there are two
      segments of County Line Road that are only two lanes and obtaining these funds will allow widening of this section to begin earlier
      than the planned 2036-2040 time frame.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Right-of-way acquisition and design for the widening of County Line Road.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Added roadway capacity and enhanced safety will be provided to citizens.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      The general population of Hernando and Pasco Counties will be served by this project. Traffic counts from FDOT show that
      approximately 19,000 vehicles per day used this segment of County Line Road in 2018. Numerous undeveloped sites along County
      Line Road would be more attractive to business owners with more traffic traveling in front of a storefront.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      This project will improve traffic flow and enhance safety for those motorists using County Line Road. Future traffic counts and
      number of crashes will be used to demonstrate the increase in traffic flow and an enhancement in safety.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      The county will reimburse state funding for failure to meet deliverables.
