13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Miami Diaper Bank seeks support to expand mobile diaper distribution in Miami-Dade and Broward County. Demand
    in these communities exceeds our current capacity, and funding will allow us to sustain monthly Mobile Pantry services
    and add two new monthly distribution points. This investment will help families keep their children clean, dry, and
    healthy, reduce financial strain, and improve pathways to stability and self-sufficiency.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Staff support for program operations and administration, purchase of wholesale diapers for mobile distribution, expand
    services to two new diaper desert locations, one in Miami-Dade and one in Broward, sustain current services at 5
    diaper desert locations, register new clients while capturing intake and post-distribution surveys.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Diapers and related supplies will be distributed directly to underserved and under-reached participants in Miami-Dade
    and Broward, links to other wrap-around services from local agencies, awareness about Miami Diaper Bank and
    accessible services will be increased across South Florida. Awareness about diaper insecurity will be increased across
    South Florida.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Miami Diaper Bank serves mostly BIPOC families with low incomes who have children in diapers or pull-ups including
    children living in foster care. Recent data collected by Miami Diaper Bank on the population of households served
    includes following: 83% earn less than $30,000 annually before taxes, 70% receive Medicaid/Medicare, 60% receive
    WIC, 43% had at least one incidence of diaper rash; 32% paid for over-the-counter medication for treatment, 30% of
    caregivers are single parents, Female: 66%, Male: 34%. Race/Ethnicity: 54% Hispanic, 29% Black/African American,
    7% White, 6% mixed, 3 % other. We expect to serve 2,000 individuals.

   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     By providing a diaper safety net, we ensure that families are better prepared to meet the physical, educational, and
    social-developmental needs of their children. 30% of diaper recipients report fewer incidences of diaper related illness
    such as severe diaper rash and UTIs; 60% increased capacity to direct limited income to other essential needs such as
    food, shelter, and childcare; 30% missing fewer days of work and an increased capacity to focus on self-sufficiency;
    and 60% reduced caregiver stress.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    The contracting agency may consider financial penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures.
