1. Project Title         Miami Dade Sheriff's Office Law Enforcement Training Center Upgrades

2. Senate Sponsor        Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request       12/10/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    The legislative funding will allow MDSO to upgrade the Sheriff's Training Center, by (1) purchasing a mobile canopy
   system, which will provide desperately-needed sanctuary from sun exposure and inclement weather allowing for law
   enforcement personnel to enjoy continued opportunities for firing range practice in any environment, and (2) upgrading
   severely eroded berms in the range causing dangerous conditions such as ricocheting rounds.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    Much-needed upgrades to the Miami-Dade Sheriff's Training Center, which will impact not only MDSO but also entities
    who use and/or train at the facility, including the Secret Service, FBI, US Military, Miami-Dade Corrections and
    Rehabilitation, and Miami-Dade College academy students.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Much-needed upgrades to the Miami-Dade Sheriff's Training Center.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
