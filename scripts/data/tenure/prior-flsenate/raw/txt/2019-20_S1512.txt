1. Title of Project: Citrus County - Inverness Airport Business Park
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 02/01/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   This request is for the design, permitting, and construction of an approximately 75-acre business-industrial park.
   Infrastructure improvements include all utilities, telecommunication/fiber, a stormwater treatment system,
   circulation roads, and an aircraft taxiway within the park. This effort also includes the reconstruction of
   approximately 3,115' of the main roadway to the business park.

11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        Design and construction of an approximately 75 acre business park and reconstruction of part of the main
        roadway leading to the Park. This Business Park will provide approximately 350 jobs in Citrus County.
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        Engineering design, permitting, and construction of an approximately 75 acre business park.
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Construction jobs during the construction of the project and approximately 350 permanent jobs.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        Residents of Citrus County. Approximately 350 residents.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
      will be measured?
        Construction of an approximately 75 acre business-industrial park which includes approximately 5,000' of
        roads, all utilities, telecommunication/fiber, reconstruction of the main access road to the park, 3,350' of
        aircraft taxiway into the park, and all associated stormwater treatment facilities. It is planned, that with the
        availability of the basic infrastructure in place, the county will market the business park to draw businesses
        which will provide jobs to the citizens of Citrus County. The number and type of jobs created will be
        documented.
   f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
        penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
        Standard contract penalties.
