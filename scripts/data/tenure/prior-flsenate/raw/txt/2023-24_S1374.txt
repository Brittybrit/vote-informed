4. Project/Program Description
    The estimated cost of the Miami Lakes Multipurpose Senior Activity and Community Center is $1,700,000. The Town is
   requesting State legislative appropriation of $500,000 (29.4%), reflecting a developer donation of the building to the Town
   valued at $1,200,000 (70.6%) to fund this Project. The senior center's construction will begin in mid-2023 and the newly
   constructed center will be donated to the Town by the developer. This appropriation will fund the utilities, electrical, fixtures,
   equipment, and interior build scope of services. This Senior Activity and Community Center will be the Town’s main, multi-
   purpose senior center, offering vital health, fitness, and wellness programs and services; civic engagement opportunities;
   language, educational, and arts programs; community resources and elderly assistance; free ADA accessible Freebee
   transportation; and vital support services to the elderly community and local senior Veterans of Miami Lakes.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to complete and operate the Miami Lakes Multipurpose Senior Activity and Community Center to enhance
    the physical and emotional well-being of Miami Lakes seniors. Funds will achieve the utilities, electrical, fixtures,
    equipment, and interior build scope of services. This Senior Activity and Community Center will be the Town’s main,
    multi-purpose senior center, providing vital health, fitness, and wellness programs and services; civic engagement
    opportunities; language, educational, and arts programs; community resources and elderly assistance; free ADA
    accessible Freebee transportation; and vital support services to the elderly community.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Activities include the utilities, electrical, fixtures, equipment, and interior build scope of services. Over 5,000 of the Miami
    Lakes adult population are seniors. The intended purpose of the Project is to provide seniors with health, fitness, and
    wellness programs and services; civic engagement opportunities; language, educational, and arts programs; and vital
    resources and elderly assistance. The Miami Lakes Multipurpose Senior Activity and Community Center will be the
    Town’s main, multi-purpose senior center, offering vital programs and services to the elderly community and local
    Veterans of Miami Lakes.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This Project will be the Town’s main, multi-purpose senior center, offering vital programs, health and wellness services,
    social services referrals, healthy nutritional meals, physical fitness activities, social enrichment and interaction,
    educational and language programs, free ADA accessible Freebee transportation, and vital support services to the elderly
    community and local Veterans of Miami Lakes.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population to be served by this project are over 5,000 Miami Lakes seniors, 630 veterans, and the general
    senior community.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The Town is a Member of the AARP Age-Friendly Communities Network and this project is a necessity for the seniors of
    Miami Lakes. The expected outcome is to complete the utilities, electrical, fixtures, equipment, and interior build scope of
    services for the senior community center. This project benefits to seniors and veterans include enhanced physical health
    through nutritional meals, fitness and wellness programs, improved mental health by connecting older adults to vital
    community services that can assist them stay healthy and independent, enriched cultural experience through providing
    peer engagement and cultural events and classes, and enhanced protection from harm through providing
    hurricane/disaster relief services; water, ice, and sandbags distribution for seniors. Methodology by which this outcome
    will be measured include enrollment forms, attendance, participation, data, and surveys.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     No payment will be made for a request for reimbursement which does not include an updated summary of the project,
    dated invoices, checks paid by the contracting agency, and proof of payments via copies of deposited checks.
