1. Title of Project: The Village Youth Housing Project
2. Senate Sponsor: Daphne Campbell
3. Date of Submission: 12/20/2017
4. Project/Program Description:
    Due to the lack of housing locations for repeat 8 to 17 year old court documented uncontrollable offenders,
    Northwest Behavioral program will bridge the gap between the Jacksonville Sheriff’s office (JSO), Family Support
    Services of North Florida (FSSNF), and the Department of Children and Families (DCF) in an effort to provide safe
    housing and social and psychological treatment activities, to include the increase of parental involvement, to help
    reduce the youth involvement in truancy, delinquency, and criminal activities.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           Due to the lack of housing locations for repeat 8 to 17 year old court documented uncontrollable offenders,
          the Northwest Behavioral program will bridge the gap between JSO, FSSNF, and DCF to provide safe housing
          and social and psychological treatment activities, including increased parental involvement, to help reduce
          truancy, delinquency, and criminal activities.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           The funding will provide emergency temporary housing for up to 72 hours while simultaneously ensuring the
          child is transitioning to a safe and secure placement. While placed, the following will occur: 1) Triage for
          medical, behavioral, substance abuse treatment, and supportive services; psychiatric and psychological
          assessments; medical checkups/management; and behavior health therapy. 2) Educational services.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          Persons with poor mental and physical health, jobless persons, economically disadvantaged persons, at-risk
         youth, homeless, drug users (in health services), grade school students, high school students, university/college
         students, currently or formerly incarcerated persons, drug offenders (in criminal justice), victims of crime,
         abused neglected, delinquent, truant youth

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          Diagnosing and treating ADHD and Anxiety with pre/post testing by triaging medical/mental health conditions,
         develop a diagnosis, treatment plan and track expected outcomes. Improve mental health by instilling 7 to 14
         cultural principles toward achieving personal and social responsibilities by doing pre/post testing following
         training sessions, and practicum activities. Improve agricultural production/promotion/education by improving
         reading and math outcomes to expected appropriate grade levels by pre/post tests following remediation
         sessions. Improve quality of education by improving reading and math outcomes to expected appropriate
         grade levels by pre/post tests following remediation sessions. Protect the general public from harm
         (environmental, criminal, etc.) by reducing truancy, delinquency and violent and criminal activities. Reduce
         recidivism, substance abuse, and divert youth from Criminal/Juvenile justice system

    g. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
       penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
        Withholding of funds.
