4. Project/Program Description
    Goodwill South Florida offers vocational education & training programs where participants are taught job skills and gain
   work experience. The appropriation will provide vocational education & training where participants are taught job skills
   and gain work experience in positions in the sewing trade. 90% of program participants will have a disability or barriers to
   work. The program will provide training opportunities for 450 individuals (new participants or retraining of current
   participants) who have the capabilities and interests to perform in a job but are in need of additional training (classroom
   and/or on-the-job training) and supportive counseling due to a specific condition that impairs their productivity or
   precludes them from achieving standard productivity levels.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Provide training opportunities for 450 individuals (new participants or retraining of current participants) who have the
    capabilities and interests to perform in a job but are in need of additional training (classroom and/or on-the-job training)
    and supportive counseling due to a specific condition that impairs their productivity or precludes them from achieving
    standard productivity levels.

   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The appropriation will allow us to invest in programs that help transition someone seeking our services from
    dependency to independence. Rather than using tax dollars to receive state services, our successful job placements
    can help convert tax users to become taxpayers by now being able to find a job and contribute to the community rather
    than depending on it.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Individualized supervision, supportive counseling, on-the-job training, and transitional employment services for
    individuals to develop their maximum potential for employment. Some general skills taught are communicating
    effectively with supervisors, co-workers, and customers, understanding and following workplace behaviors, following
    directions, and paying attention to tasks.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Goodwill South Florida will identify adults with disabilities from the following: Current participants that work in the
    Apparel Division and have a disability who a.) could benefit from supplemental training to enhance their skills or b.) are
    performing at a lower efficient/productivity rate. General recruitment activities that will focus on seniors, individuals who
    are economically disadvantage, and individuals who have been trained while in a jail reentry program.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     Providing individuals an income while receiving training for an employable skill by measuring the number of
    participants who complete the program. Trainees will be measured by their performance prior to the training. Pre-
    testing and post-testing will be conducted against key performance indicators identified to assess comprehension and
    progress.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    Corrective action plan followed by cessation of funding if corrective actions are not taken.
