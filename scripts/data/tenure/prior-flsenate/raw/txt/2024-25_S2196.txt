14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Keiser University will purchase, equipment and staff a mobile clinical training unit and KU Central Florida on-campus
    simulation lab for hospitals/clinics and students to gain required clinical hours. Facility RN's (new hires/seasoned nurses)
    can stay current with skills for low frequency/high acuity crisis situations: codes, stroke, precipitous birth, OB hemorrhage,
    cardiac arrest, respiratory arrest, providing increased access to nursing education for students and medical facilities in
    rural/metropolitan areas.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     To address the nursing shortage in Florida, the mobile training unit and the on-campus comprehensive simulation lab will
    provide training/clinical hours and specific critical competencies in nursing to address the workforce needs for faculty and
    shortage areas resulting in more prepared and credentialed nurses.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The mobile training unit and the campus based lab will be equipped with the necessary adult and pediatric simulators for
    nursing students to access for clinical rotations as required for graduation and licensing. Simulation, replacing a
    percentage of clinical hours, will allow programs to bring in additional students while still maintaining FBON and
    accreditation instructor-to-student ratios.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     High school students and University/College students.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The benefit of this project is to increase the number of graduates who will be ready to fill shortages in Florida's nursing
    workforce. The outcome will be measured by the increase in students graduating with a nursing degree and preparing for
    appropriate licenses, passing the NCLEX exams, along with other appropriate licenses and tests to fill the critical
    workforce needs.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Keiser University nursing students consistently pass their licensing exam at a rate higher than the state average. If the
    pass rates should significantly change, we would ask for a 60-day performance improvement plan using the ATI
    remediation program to correct deficiencies found in the Comprehensive Predictor exam result. Over the last two years,
    Keiser University's NCLEX pass rates have been above the state of Florida average (55.19% in 2022, 64.79% in 2021).
