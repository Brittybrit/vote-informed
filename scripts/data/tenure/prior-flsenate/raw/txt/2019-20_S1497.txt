1. Title of Project: Starkey Trail @ SR 54 Bicycle/Pedestrian Overpass
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 01/30/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   Project is a bicycle/pedestrian overpass to provide users of the Starkey Trail with a safe, grade-separated crossing of
   SR 54, a busy six-lane highway. The Starkey Trail is part of the statewide Coast-to-Coast trail network, and
   construction of the last unfinished segment south of SR 54 is scheduled for completion in the summer of 2019. The
   overpass will provide a safer, more pleasant experience to numerous bicyclists and pedestrians, including families
   from nearby neighborhoods, users of the popular Pinellas Trail that connects to the south, and long-distance bicycle
   tourists. The trail is priority #3 in the Pasco MPO's 2018 list of "regional trail" projects adopted in September 2018.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         Funds will pay for construction of a bicycle/pedestrian overpass across SR 54 to serve users of a popular trail
         that is part of Florida's Coast-to-Coast trail network.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         Design/permitting, and construction.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         The completed bicycle/pedestrian overpass will support recreation and health, transportation and tourism.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         Bicyclists, pedestrians, and others traveling by foot-powered modes (e.g. roller-blades) and mobility devices.
         Project benefits will accrue to residents of Pasco County and the Tampa Bay area and trail users from around
         Florida and across the country, as the project is on the Coast-to-Coast Trail. As this segment of trail is under
         construction now, user counts do not yet exist, however based on overpass use in similar circumstances in
         Pinellas County we anticipate the Starkey/54 overpass will attract hundreds of users each day.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         The project will enhance safety and comfort for trail users, which can be expected to attract more users to the
         trail. User volume may be collected using automatic counters and user surveys.
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         Loss of funding in the applicable fiscal year.
