11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           The funds will revitalize and provide economic investment for a community that was neglected. The project
          will impact thousands of businesses and families by providing training and jobs for the community at large.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

            The project includes building warehouses and industrial areas to create jobs in the film and theatre industries.
          The project will provide training thanks to the creation of the incubation project that will include, but is not
          limited to, manufacturing, processing, trucking, logistics, shipping & receiving. In addition, the project will hire
          experienced professionals and the on-the-job trainees to perform light manufacturing jobs and moderate
          industrial work.

d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

      The project will center around distribution. The project will provide wholesale warehousing where wholesale
     companies will be able to lease space for dried and frozen items for delayed or immediate distribution and
     transportation to the Port of Miami, the International airports, via train, trucks, or other advanced
     transportation.

e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

      The target population is mostly African-American located in the Liberty City/Model City area where 95% of the
     population are low income, and moderate income workforce families who can hardly retain their position. The
     population includes African-American, Africans, African-Caribbean's, Latinos, Asian, Hispanics who are
     descendants of immigrants from Haiti, Cuba, Puerto Rico, Jamaica, Dominican Republic, and Mexico.

f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
     will be measured?
      To eradicate poverty and blight. The project will create an economic base with sustainable jobs. There will be
     benchmarking where every participant will be followed from the time of training to the time he or she finds
     employment. Beyond this time, the participant will be followed for another two years after employment or re-
     employment. There will be construction jobs as the buildings are being erected. There will be training and jobs

    g. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
       penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
        In addition to penalties provided by the state of Florida for persons or entities who fail to deliver what was
       contracted, the willfully negligent contractors may be charged for fraud and prosecuted as such. Projects that
       are late will be taken away from the responsible contractor and will be re-assigned to a better bidder. Each
       contractor will be held accountable for their work based on a low/poor, moderate and strong/satisfactory
       performance. Only satisfactorily performed contractors will be paid and will retain their positions.
