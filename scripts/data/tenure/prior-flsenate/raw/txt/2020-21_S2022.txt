4.   Project/Program Description
     Village of Palmetto Bay is requesting funding for the construction and construction support for stormwater improvements to its sub-basin
     #61. Improvements include building additional catch basins, manholes, culverts & exfiltration to connect the catch basins within sub-basin
     #61. Improvements will reduce pollutant load contribution from 3 main pollutants from the sub-basin into Biscayne Bay Aquifer, control
     flooding during storms; such as Hurricane Irma; and improve surface water quality by limiting pollution to our waters.

11. Program Performance
 a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
     Village of Palmetto Bay is requesting funding for the construction and construction support for stormwater improvements to its sub-
     basin #61. Improvements include building additional catch basins, manholes, culverts & exfiltration to connect the catch basins within
     sub-basin #61. Improvements will reduce pollutant load contribution from 3 main pollutants from the sub-basin into Biscayne Bay
     Aquifer, control flooding during storms; such as Hurricane Irma; and improve surface water quality by limiting pollution to our waters.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
     The Village will have to secure contractor to perform the construction via our procurement process.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
     There will be no direct services provided to the citizens, but this appropriated project will benefit the residents who are directly
     impacted by the flooding taking place on the sub-basin #61 roadways.




 d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
     The target population is residents of Southern Miami-Dade. Residents traversing from southern US1 north and along our arterial
     roads will benefit. Our local residents will greatly benefit by no longer worrying about flooded roads to exit or enter their homes and
     neighborhood. We expect greater than 100 people will be served.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
     With this funding the Village of Palmetto Bay will enhance/preserve/improve environmental or fish and wildlife quality by mitigating the
     environmental hazards associated with stormwater runoff.
     We will also protect the general public from harm by controlling flooding and other hazards associated with stormwater surges. We
     will additionally improve stormwater management, groundwater quality and surface water quality by reducing the loading pollutants
     into Biscayne Bay and control flooding and other hazards associated with stormwater surges.



  f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
     penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
     Withholding of funds.
