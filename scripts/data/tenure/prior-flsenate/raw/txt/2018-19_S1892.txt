1. Title of Project: Habitat for Humanity Neighborhood Infrastructure
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 12/13/2017
4. Project/Program Description:
    Habitat for Humanity of East and Central Pasco County has received a piece of property in Dade City that has the
    potential to turn 13 local and hardworking renters into Habitat Homebuyers. However, before Habitat for Humanity
    can begin work on this new small neighborhood, there is significant infrastructure work to be done. Habitat for
    Humanity has obtained a good estimate from Superior Siteworks, located in Dade City, FL. The estimate is
    $276,783.75. Habitat for Humanity is working hard to obtain the necessary funds to begin the infrastructure work;
    however this could be a long term struggle to raise these funds.
