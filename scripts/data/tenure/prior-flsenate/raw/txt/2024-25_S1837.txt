4. Project/Program Description
    The Doral Emergency Operations Center (EOC) is responsible for strategic direction and operational decisions during any
   emergency situation, including pre/post hurricane response. The DPD EOC is a Divisional Emergency Managment Center
   responsible for 3 additional cities to provide the highest level of response. The DPD is in need of a deployable Mobile
   Command Post (CP) Vehicle that can be utilized in any critical incident to provide effective emergency response operations
   regardless of the circumstances or location. If funding is received, this initiative would allow for the acquisition of a
   deployable mobile CP that can provide support to agency resources in a variety of critical incidents and/or natural disaster
   situations. The DPD mobile CP would contain all of the necessary equipment vital to the successful operation of any law
   enforcement response to include items such as: generators, real-time monitoring technology, and a meeting area to
   coordinate the deployment of resources.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds requested are for the acquisition and retrofitting of a deployable mobile CP EOC which can be utilized
    throughout the entire City of Doral and/or deployed to any of the satellite cities that fall under the Doral Divisional EOC. In
    addition, the mobile CP will contain all of the necessary equipment to deploy effectively and provide operational and
    logistical support during a man-made or natural disaster emergency situation such as a hurricane or any industrial
    accident or fire. The mobile CP EOC will serve as an extension of the Doral Police Headquarters and the Doral Divisional
    Emergency Operations Center.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     In the event of a major disaster situation or critical incident, it is necessary for first responders and supervisors to deploy
    in order to provide an effective law enforcement response and/or relief operation. At times, especially when responding
    during post hurricane devastation, it is necessary to provide continuous 24-hour resources and assistance to various
    locations. The DPD mobile command post EOC will provide a fully equipped resource for any level emergency response
    situation in a safe, effective, and efficient manner. It should be noted that the Doral Police Department is responsible for
    the safety of numerous educational institutions, places of higher learning, and places of worship.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Although the DPD Mobile Command post EOC can be deployed as a valuable resource during a variety of critical
    incidents, it is important to highlight our agency's preparedness efforts that are required annually during hurricane
    season. As a law enforcement agency located in the most centralized point within Miami-Dade County, we are no
    strangers to the needs of our residents and business owners following a hurricane. In a post hurricane environment, it is
    critical to ensure that our personnel have the support, supplies, and capability to operate continuously with an effective
    command vehicle, before, during and after a major disaster situation to effectively protect the lives and properties of our
    Doral residents.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population served by this project are the residents, business owners and visitors of the City of Doral and the
    three neighboring satellite cities that fall within the City of Doral Divisional Emergency Operations Center which require
    assistance and lifesaving resources in the event of a disaster or critical incident.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The project will allow for the Doral Police Department to be prepared to provide extended emergency response
    throughout the City of Doral, ensuring that all personnel have access to equipment and resources during a major incident.
    The Doral Police Department will be able to reinforce our emergency response services to better help the citizens of the
    City of Doral and increase our effectiveness in securing the safety of our community. These benefits are measurable by
    calculating post emergency response times and statistics as well as the accessibility of police resources within hard to
    reach remote areas post hurricane for our residents following a hurricane and/or natural disaster.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

    In the event that the Doral Police Department is unable to utilize these funds for the purpose described herein, the funds
    will be returned to the State in full
