1. Project Title          Virginia Gardens - Phase II Central Drainage Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        2/17/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    Installation of a new storm-water drainage system to prevent stormwater ponding and flooding. The project is part of the
   Village's storm water management initiative to alleviate surface runoff, water pollution, as well as the likelihood of soil
   erosion in the central section of the Village and will allow for proper access in the vicinity during heavy rain events.
