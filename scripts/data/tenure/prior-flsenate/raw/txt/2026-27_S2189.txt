1. Project Title          Village of Virginia Gardens - Roadway Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor        Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request       11/13/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    The primary purpose of this project is to improve the safety for all road users, reduce pedestrian/bicyclist conflicts,
   provide accessible marked ADA walkways, and improve the quality of life for the residents. The project scope includes
   milling and overlaying roadways on the west side of the Village - including 41st Street - which is a shared roadway
   between the Village of Virginia Gardens and City of Miami Springs. Improvements also include removal/replacement of
   deficient sidewalks and ADA compliant crosswalks, and speeding signage installation.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Improved transportation conditions for the general public including the residents, business owners, and cut through
    traffic from the City of Miami Springs, as well as traffic flow from NW 36th Street- a main thoroughfare.
