13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Behavioral health and case management for children and their families on waitlist for services. Many referred by
    agencies, schools, or other providers. Reduce mental health and criminal risk-factors, reduce abuse and neglect,
    improve likelihood of successful societal participation for at-risk children.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Behavioral health services for children and families including parenting classes and supports to help keep families
    together and reduce short- and long-term risk factors for children such as substance abuse, violent criminality, poor
    academic performance, suicidality, and mortality. Every $1 spent saves state $7 in future uncompensated care and
    criminal justice.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Individual Behavioral Health and Case Management Services for children and their families. School Based Supports
    and Crisis Response. Six-therapy sessions reduce risk factors by 20% with improved results with continued services.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     At-risk youth, people with poor mental health, economically disadvantaged, students, and victims of crime. Additional
    150-180 children and their families on waitlist expected to be served.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     Reduction of mental health and criminal risk factors and attainment of treatment goals determined via assessments,
    recorded in electronic records, data will be populated and reported to ME. Expected reductions of abuse and neglect
    and ER visits.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     Funds reduced commensurate with unmet measures.
