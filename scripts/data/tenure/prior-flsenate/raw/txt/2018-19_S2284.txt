1. Title of Project: Sunny Isles Beach Bella Vista Bay Dock & Flooding Repairs
2. Senate Sponsor: Daphne Campbell
3. Date of Submission: 01/17/2018
4. Project/Program Description:
    The docks at Bella Vista Bay Park were destroyed during Hurricane Irma. Additionally, the entire property flooded
    during the storm and subsequently experienced significant erosion. This funding request is for the engineering and
    design of the dock repair and seawall and land stabilization to mitigate future erosion.

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          Design of sufficient plans to restore the only public dock space in the City and stabilize the park property, in
         order to provide a functional, safe park for residents and visitors.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          Residents and tourists in the area, with a large economic impact.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          The expected benefit of implementing the design plan is mitigation of storm damage, and protection of life
         and property. The outcome can be measured by the completion of the final project - restoration of the park.

    g. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
       penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
        The City will not fail to meet deliverables; however, delay in disbursement of state appropriated funds would
       be a suggested penalty.
