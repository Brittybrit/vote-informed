11. Program Performance
 a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
     The purpose is to enhance local financial capacity to address water resources, drainage, flood mitigation, sea level rise and water
     supply development.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
     The main activities will include updating the stormwater structures, installation of a new stormwater pump station at the Key Biscayne
     K-8 Community School.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
     The Project will alleviate the excessive flooding experienced in and around the immediate surrounding area of the Key Biscayne K-8
     Center Elementary, will target “sunny day flooding” caused by sea level rise.




 d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
     This project will focus on retrofitting the civil infrastructure within the whole Village to reduce the risk of flooding and flood related
     impacts.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
     Expected benefit of the Project is less frequent flooding, upgraded stormwater infrastructure, manage the sea level rise and ground
     water level movements and adapt to the climate changes.




  f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
     penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
     The Village of Key Biscayne suggests pursuing liquidated damages, collective performers bonds and keeping record of poor
     performance.
