                          Shelter Hardening Miami-Dade

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        02/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Hardening the infrastructure of the Village Hall, which houses the Police Department and other City departments; and
   serves as a voting precinct location. The Village Hall currently serves as a base operation for first responders and support
   staff during a storm event. Furthermore, funding of the project will allow for the Village Hall to serve as an emergency
   shelter for public safety/first responders and once a storm passes the residents.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Hardening of the Village Hall and Public Safety Building will allow it to be a reliable base of operations for first
    responders and support staff during a storm event. The proposed project will secure the building from a CAT 5 hurricane
    and the enclosed generator will allow for vital communication with the police and first responders.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The proposed hardening project will help secure the vital resources necessary to continue operations during a storm.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    The proposed project will ensure that police and first responders can continue to operate without interruption from a
    major weather event. This will allow them to continue to serve the residents.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The Village of Virginia Gardens 2400 residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The ability to shelter first responders and Village staff in the Village Hall/Public safety building during a storm event. And
    to allow first responders to respond to calls once the winds are reduced to <35 mph. Also serves as a location for the
    Village residents to come to and cool off and recharge.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Revocation of funding of Appropriation at end of Fiscal Year.
