1. Title of Project: Dade City Dade Oaks Stormwater Pond
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 01/30/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   The City purchased the Dade Oaks property in 2016 and would like to restore the development to what it once was:
   a pond.
   BENEFITS:
   - Alleviate flooding in areas of NE Dade City and restore this area to an acceptable level of service as defined by the
   City of Dade City Stormwater Master Plan: During large storm events, the pumping system is inadequate to keep
   up and the area begins to flood.
   -Provide a public amenity using the pond as a water feature: Add an observation pier and sidewalk along the pond.
   -Provide an improvement to water quality in the area resulting in a cleaner discharge to the Withlacoochee River
   Basin: Water quality will be added to the pond design to treat the stormwater generated in the area before it
   outfalls into the Withlacoochee River Basin.
   -Eliminate the need for stormwater pumps which are costly to operate and maintain and have failed in the past.
   The project will eliminate old pumps and allow the new pond to function naturally.

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
