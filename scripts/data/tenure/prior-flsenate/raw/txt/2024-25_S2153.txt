1. Project Title         Town of Miami Lakes NW 155th Street Roadway Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor        Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request       12/14/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The Miami Lakes NW 155th Street Roadway Improvements Project includes the design, permitting, and construction
   relocation of the existing median parking to areas along the north and south curbs, adding a left turn lane at the
   intersections of NW 79th Avenue and NW 82nd Avenue, milling and resurfacing, new stripping, and signage. The project's
   proposed goal is to reduce traffic congestion on NW 154th Street, thereby decreasing congestion on the SHS SR826 off
   ramps, as well as improve public safety access for emergency vehicles at the nearby MDFR Miami Lakes West Station 64
   (MDFR Station located at 15320 Palmetto Frontage Road in Miami Lakes). The town is requesting legislative appropriation
   of $615,000 to complete this Project.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds requested will achieve the purpose of construction relocation of the existing median parking to areas along the
    north and south curbs, adding a left turn lane at the intersections of NW 79th Avenue and NW 82nd Avenue, milling and
    resurfacing, new stripping, and signage. The goal of the project is to reduce traffic congestion on NW 154th Street,
    thereby decreasing congestion on the SHS SR826 off ramps, as well as improve public safety access for emergency
    vehicles at the nearby MDFR Miami Lakes West Station 64.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Activities and services to be provided include the design, permitting, and construction relocation of the existing median
    parking to areas along the north and south curbs and the adding of a left turn lane at the intersections of NW 79th Avenue
    and NW 82nd Avenue to reduce traffic congestion and improve public safety access.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Direct services to be provided to the citizens and general public of road users include reduced traffic congestion,
    improved transportation infrastructure and access, improved transportation conditions, and increased traffic flow.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Target population served by this project are Miami Lakes' citizens, the general public of road users, and emergency
    vehicles traveling on NW 155th Street, NW 154th Street/Miami Lakes Drive, and SHS SR826 on ramps.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcome is to complete the construction of the Miami Lakes NW 155th Street Parking and Turn Lane
    Transportation Improvements Project. Expected benefits include reduced traffic congestion, improved transportation
    infrastructure and access, improved transportation conditions, and increased traffic flow. Outcome will be measured by
    field observations. The project will create specific contractor, engineer, environmental, and consultant immediate job
    opportunities measured by contractual agreements.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     No payment will be made for a request for reimbursement which does not include an updated summary of the project,
    dated invoices, checks paid by the contracting agency, and proof of payments via copies of deposited checks.
