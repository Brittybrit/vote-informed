11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           Mitigation of tree canopy and native plant loss at designated historic public venues due to the impacts of
          Hurricane Irma.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           The following activities and services will be provided: Assessment of damage and surveys at selected historic
          venues, Recommendation for plant materials replacement, installation plan and mapping, purchase of trees
          and other native planting materials, delivery scheduling and estimate for installation costs, interpretation
          regarding importance of tree canopy and tree functionality in a healthy urban environment.

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          Citizens will benefit from having access to improved public urban historic assets and the local economy will
         benefit by providing an improved Miami urban landscape for both residents and tourists.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          4 million urban Miami residents and visitors.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          Improved public spaces, improved environmental conditions for Miamians and visitors, improved sea level rise
         and heat island mitigation
