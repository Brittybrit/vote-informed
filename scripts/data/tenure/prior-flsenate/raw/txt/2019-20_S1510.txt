1. Title of Project: HCSD- Student Computer Replacement
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 02/01/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   The Hernando County School District seeks to replace student-facing computers purchased in 2008. Outdated and
   beyond the capacity to meet instructional usefulness, the funds would be used to replace computer lab and
   individual computer machines for students. To replace all student computers puchased in 2008, the cost is $7
   million. The $2.3 million appropriation would help the district reduce the current need.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         Replacement of outdated student-facing computers purchased in 2008
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         Enhanced instructional experiences for students Reduced time spent on testing
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         Students will gain the use of computers that are capable of accessing online instructional materials, help
         students to conduct research as well as allow students to create original work.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         Students in grades K-12.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         The expected benefit is that we will be able to provide computers to students who are as dynamic as they are.
         Outcomes can be measured with a detailed report that shows the prchase of computers and where they were
         installed. The district has a prioritized list of needs to guide the technology upgrade process.
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         In the event of a delay in meeting deliverables, an action plan outlining a new timeline with an explanation of
         the reasons for delay will be provided. In the event that no deliverables are met, all funds would be returned.
