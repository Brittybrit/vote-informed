    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           This is a tennis and athletic facility to serve the community and area at large.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           The focus of the tennis center will be youth activities and play. While the center is open to all ages, reaching
          young people is the primary goal.

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          Exposure to many different people, opportunity to play a sport that can improve physical outcomes and
         attend tennis tournaments and events.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          Mission statement clarifies our goals and how to ensure all are welcome at the Sarah Vande Berg Tennis
         Center: The Tennis Center will provide tennis and education in underserved communities with a focus on youth

         annually.
