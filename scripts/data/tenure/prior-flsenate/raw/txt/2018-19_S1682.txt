4. Project/Program Description:
    Project Name: City of Miami Beach Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) and Smart Parking System Project (SPS).
    Over the past 10 years, the average daily population including tourists for Miami Beach has increased over 36%.
    Congestion has also increased significantly, resulting in nine (9) segment roadways rated as an 'F' Level of Service
    (Travel demand exceeds capacity of roadway, and is constantly in traffic gridlock). Traffic congestion adversely
    affects tourism and jobs in Miami Beach, and tourism revenue back to the State. Our mission is to create a system
    that enhances transportation through the safe and efficient movement of people, goods, and information while
    increasing fuel efficiency, lessening pollution and promoting the growth in jobs related to all industries in Miami
    Beach, through ITS and SPS.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           The Intelligent Transportation System and Smart Parking System project will help City of Miami Beach manage
          traffic congestion on a day-to-day basis.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           The activity includes the implementation of an Intelligent Transportation System and Parking Management
          System within the City of Miami Beach. This project will provide a transportation network with improved
          operating conditions, and operational efficiency; improved capacity; enhanced personal mobility; improved
          access, safety, and parking; and enhanced present and future economic productivity of individuals,
          organizations and the economy.

d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

      This project will provide a transportation network with improved operating conditions and operational
     efficiency; improved capacity; enhanced personal mobility; improved access, safety, and parking; and enhanced
     present and future economic productivity of individuals, organizations, and the economy.

e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

      Residents, visitors, and tourists in Miami Beach totaling 200,000 average daily population.

f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
     will be measured?
      Reduced fuel consumption, reduced travel reduction benefits, reduced air pollution benefits, law enforcement
     wages reduction. The total monetized benefits of this project is calculated at $142 million over 20 years.
     Outcomes were measured through a Benefit Cost Analysis.
