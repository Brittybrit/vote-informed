4.   Project/Program Description
     This request is for the design, permitting, and construction of the infrastructure for a 75+/- acre business-industrial park. Infrastructure
     improvements include all utilities, telecommunication/fiber, stormwater treatment system, circulation roads, and an aircraft taxiway within
     the park. This effort also includes the reconstruction of approximately 3,115' of the main roadway to the business park.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      Design and construction of the infrastructure for a 75+/- acre business-industrial park, which includes approximately 5,000' of roads,
      all utilities, telecommunication/fiber, reconstruction of the main access road to the park, 3,350' of aircraft taxiway into the park, and all
      required stormwater attenuation. The park will hire trade related workers during construction and upon completion, provide pad-
      ready building site(s) for an aircraft component recertification operation that expects start with at least 90 employees and employ up
      to 350 people.


 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Engineering design, permitting, and construction of the infrastructure for a 75+/- acre business-industrial park.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Construction jobs during the construction of the project and approximately 350 permanent jobs.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      Residents of Citrus County. Approximately 350 residents.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      Construction of the infrastructure for a 75+/- acre business-industrial park which includes approximately 5,000' of roads, all utilities,
      telecommunication/fiber, reconstruction of the main access road to the park, 3,350' of aircraft taxiway into the park, and all associated
      stormwater treatment facilities.
      With infrastructure in place and pad-ready sites to meet currently known demand by new business, the project will support over 350
      new jobs for the citizens of Citrus County, including accommodating an existing aircraft component recertification operation that will
      employ 90 to 120 people initially.


 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Standard contract penalties are sufficient.
