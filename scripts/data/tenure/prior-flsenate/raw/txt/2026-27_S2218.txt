13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Jimmy Graham Foundation's Aerospace & Technology Pathway introduces students to hands-on STEM learning
    through aviation, mechanical, and engineering experiences. This initiative aims to inspire youth to pursue career paths
    in the aerospace industry by engaging them in flight training, maintenance workshops, and engineering labs. Students
    gain direct exposure to aircraft operations, mechanics, and flight simulation, culminating with in-air flight experiences.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?



                                                         Page 2 of 5

     The program provides direct services to students through after-school STEM workshops, aircraft maintenance labs,
    engineering projects, flight simulation, and supervised in-air introductory flights.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The program's hands-on experiences are designed to build technical literacy, confidence, and professional direction
    for students aged 13-18. All flights are fully insured through the EAA Young Eagles Program.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Economically disadvantaged persons, at risk youth, grade school students, high school students. 101-200 people.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     1. Improved quality of education through increasing STEM scores and career readiness. This will be measured by pre
    and post assessments and teacher feedback. 2. Creation of job opportunities through internships and training
    placements. This will be measured by programming tracking and FM logbook hours. 3. Enhance economic self-
    sufficiency of graduates entering aviation training or college stem programs. This will be measured by follow-up
    surveys and placement records.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     Pro rata return of funds.
