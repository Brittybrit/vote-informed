4. Project/Program Description
    The Inland Port Intermodal Logistics Center in Miami-Dade County ("Project") will provide an additional land area for Port
   Miami's island-constrained site and its growing international container movement traffic. Approximately 200 acres will be
   used for the temporary storage of in-transit cargo and include a sustainable truck parking facility. The facility will reduce
   emissions by leveraging electrification and solar technologies, as well as additional offsite electric rubber tire gantry (eRTG)
   stackers, electric plugs for refrigerated container units, charging stations, and a solar farm. The site will require
   environmental mitigation for the majority of the land with removal of exotic invasive species of wetlands. To facilitate further
   development of the inland port, Port Miami is requesting funding to support the first phase of site development.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     As part of the Port Miami Net Zero Supply Chain Program, the purpose for the Inland Port Intermodal Logistics Center is
    to provide an additional land area for Port Miami's island-constrained site to increase the Port's economic
    competitiveness, bolster workforce development, and reduce carbon emissions associated with the movement of cargo.
    This first phase will include planning, environmental mitigation, initial site development, cargo laydown area, and truck
    parking.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     A vital component is the freight rail access and connectivity for the most fuel-efficient way to transport large volumes of
    cargo and reduce congestion and idling. The carbon neutral operation will be electrified to encompass the entire logistics
    chain. The electrified operation is anticipated to commence at Port Miami, through its docks with existing electric ship-to-
    shore gantry cranes, to an inland port powered by renewable energy, and lastly, to the consumer via a fleet of electric
    over-the-road trucks. Strategically locating one of the inland ports in South Florida allows reach from Miami to Central
    Florida, serving a population of 18-million residents and businesses.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The Project will be a significant driver of job creation and workforce development. During each construction phase,
    hundreds of construction-related jobs will be generated, providing opportunities in engineering, construction, logistics, and
    renewable energy. Beyond the construction phase, there will be ongoing operational jobs in logistics management,
    equipment maintenance, intermodal operations, and environmental technology. These roles will offer high-skill
    employment opportunities, contributing to the local economy's vitality. To ensure long-term benefits for the community,
    the full project will include training programs for the local workforce, fostering a skilled workforce ready to meet future
    industry demands.

    The project promises significant benefits for consumers by improving logistic efficiencies.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Strategically locating one of the inland ports in South Florida, allows reach from Miami to Central Florida, serving a
    population of 18-million residents and businesses. Each phase of the project adds capacity to Florida Ports. Since the
    first year of development will require significant investments in environmental mitigation and initial site development, an
    estimated 55,000 TEUs in capacity will be created. The following phases will escalate this capacity to approximately
    250,000 TEUs its second year, etc., allowing Florida ports to handle significantly larger container volumes. This directly
    increases trade revenues and supports the growing community's demands and global shipping lines served.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The Project offers substantial economic returns through a combination of operational efficiencies, increased trade
    revenues, and sustainability benefits. With the boost in cargo throughput capacity, Port Miami and other ports will be in
    the position to handle greater container volumes. This expansion directly raises trade revenues and meets the growing
    demands of global shipping lines. In addition, operational cost reductions are achieved through electrification of cargo
    handling equipment and integration with intermodal rail systems, cutting reliance on diesel vehicles and reducing fuel
    costs and emissions. Investments in renewable energy, such as solar farms, also bring long-term energy savings and
    lessen the environmental impact of logistics operations. This outcome will be measured by the increased container
    volumes.
