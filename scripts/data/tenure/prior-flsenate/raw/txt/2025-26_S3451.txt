1. Project Title           Statewide Driver's License Initiative

2. Senate Sponsor          Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request         3/18/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    The goal and purpose of this project is to address the unlicensed drivers in the State of Florida. The project would assist
   unlicensed drivers who are in a collections phase with the different Clerks of Courts, provide case plans with specific
   instructions on obtaining a valid driver's license, and in turn assist all Clerks Offices in the collection of court costs, fines
   and associated fees.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Case plans, case management and counseling services will be provided. Other services will include guidance and
    support throughout programming to assist the population in obtaining valid driver's licenses. Job Skills and Financial
    Literacy workshops will also be included as part of the project for every unlicensed driver served.

   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Case plans, case management and counseling services will be provided. Other services will include guidance and
    support throughout programming to assist the population in obtaining valid driver's licenses. Job Skills and Finacial
    Literacy workshops will also be included as part of the project for every unlicensed driver served.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The funds will provide case plans for unlicensed drivers. Services will include guidance and support throughout
    programming to assist the population in obtaining valid driver's licenses. Job Skills and Financial Literacy workshops will
    also be included as part of the project for every unlicensed driver served.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Jobless persons; economically disadvantaged persons; high school students; university/college students; currently or
    formerly incarcerated individuals; drug offender (in criminal justice).
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The funds will provide case plans for unlicensed drivers. Services will include guidance and support throughout
    programming to assist the population in obtaining valid driver's licenses. Job Skills and Financial Literacy workshops will
    also be included as part of the project for every unlicensed driver served. Measured outcomes will be calculated by
    program completion and successfully meeting collections obligations.
