11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         Construction of a 5" concrete sidewalk, crosswalks, and signage along the North side of north Avenue W.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         A new sidewalk will be constructed, approx. 2,250 linear feet.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         This project will provide a safe route for children and residents to travel to the local high school, various sports
         facilities & practice fields, performing arts theater, local elementary school, churches, and downtown.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         All residents will benefit from a new sidewalk. The new sidewalk would serve approx. 8,000 residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         This project will provide a safe corridor for children and adults alike traveling along North Avenue. Residents
         not able to currently use this unpaved area will be able to travel this area (if approved and constructed) safely
         as a direct route to the local high school, various sports facilities & practice fields, performing arts theater, local
         elementary school, churches, and downtown.
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         Penalties for not meeting the contract guidelines could include no additional sidewalk funding for 1 year.
