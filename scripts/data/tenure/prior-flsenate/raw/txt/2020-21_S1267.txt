3.   Date of Request 11/12/2019

4.   Project/Program Description
     The Village of Pinecrest Waterline Extension Project addresses the water needs of approximately 740 well-dependent homes. Funds will
     be used for the installation of 8-, 12- & 16 inch water mains and extending new service lines from the water main to the lot lines, in
     accordance with Miami-Dade County Water and Sewer Department (WASD) standards.

11. Program Performance
     a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
     This project will bring potable water lines to over 700 homes, which currently depend on wells and filtration systems. Some even
     require double filtration systems and do not have access to water during power outages.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      The construction of water lines to over 700 homes.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
     Access to clean potable water.




d.   Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
     740 well dependent homes in the Village of Pinecrest.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
     Families will have access to clean potable water because of this project. The project will be held to the standards of Miami Dade
     Water and Sewer Department.




 f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
    penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
     Defaulting of the appropriations amount.
