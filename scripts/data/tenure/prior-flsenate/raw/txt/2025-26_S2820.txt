    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will support the Human Rights Education and Advocacy Initiative to educate individuals on the importance of
    human rights and individual freedoms. The project will achieve this through workshops in schools, community centers,
    and sporting events, training educators and community leaders, and distributing educational materials. Outreach
    campaigns will raise awareness, address oppressive ideologies, and empower citizens to advocate for and protect
    fundamental rights.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The project will provide workshops in schools, community centers, and sporting events to educate on human rights and
    individual freedoms. Activities will include training educators and community leaders, developing and distributing
    educational materials, and running outreach campaigns through social media and local events. These efforts will promote
    awareness, address oppressive ideologies, and empower individuals to advocate for human rights.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The appropriation project will offer direct services such as educational workshops in schools, community centers, and
    sporting events, focusing on human rights and individual freedoms. It will provide training for educators and community
    leaders, access to educational materials, and outreach campaigns through social media and local events, empowering
    citizens with knowledge and tools to protect their rights and promote civic engagement.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population for this project includes grade school, high school, and university students, as well as educators
    and community leaders. The project aims to serve over 800 individuals, empowering them with knowledge about human
    rights, individual freedoms, and civic responsibility while fostering a culture of advocacy and engagement.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The project will increase participants ’understanding of human rights, individual freedoms, and civic responsibility while
    fostering advocacy for protecting these values. It will also raise awareness of oppressive ideologies and promote cultural
    appreciation and engagement. The outcome will be measured through pre- and post-program surveys, assessing
    participants’ increased awareness, critical thinking skills, and ability to apply these concepts in educational and
    community settings.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    The agency will not be reimbursed for project activities that do not meet the deliverables or performance measures.
