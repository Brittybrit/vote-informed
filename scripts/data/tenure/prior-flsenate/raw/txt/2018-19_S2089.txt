11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           Save the lives of hundreds of homeless dogs and cats, thereby reducing the number of them roaming the
          streets and going into restaurant garbage bins. This will help in the beautification of our City, making it more
          attractive to businesses. This will also increase the possibility of the elderly, children and families bringing a pet
          into their homes and experiencing the gratification of knowing they have saved a life.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Administer medications and care for wounds. Care for their basic physical and behavioral needs while they are
          waiting to be healthy enough for adoption.

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          The elderly, the lonely, and children and families can have the unconditional love of a pet in their homes.
         Businesses such as restaurants will see a decrease of abandoned animals rummaging through their garbage
         bins, thereby making the City more appealing to small business and providing more stability.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          Getting approximately 1500 more animals into the shelter will mean that many more available for Citizens to
         adopt and bring into their homes.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          The ability to rescue approximately 1,500 more animals into the shelter will mean there will be many more
         available for citizens to adopt and bring into their homes.

    g. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
       penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
