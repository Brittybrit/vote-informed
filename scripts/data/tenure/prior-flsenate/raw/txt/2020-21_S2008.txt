4.   Project/Program Description
     The Colombian American Chamber of Commerce of Greater Miami, Inc., mission is to promote international trade, investment, and foster
     economic development in Florida business community with bilateral trade between Florida and Colombia; to facilitate international
     commerce; and to support the needs of the small and minority business in Florida. We also assist Colombia business to enter and expand
     in the U.S. market. Colombian American Chamber of Commerce of Greater Miami, Inc., since 1976, is serving both Florida and Colombia
     in international trade.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      To promote international trade between businesses in Florida and Colombia, business development opportunities, connections, and
      establisment of contacts. Creating new sources of revenue while creating new jobs.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Provide guidance and technical assistance to companies in both countries to help facilitate the expansion of trade. Additional
      services: seminars, events, workshops, and conferences. These programs will help to introduce corporate leaders, which will help
      them facilitate business expansions.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Economic and business development activities, connections, workshops, and seminars.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      Florida business community, small and minority businesses.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      Job creation through expanded international trade. The methodology by which this outcome will be measured is providing the
      assistance needed and tracking the expansion and job creation of each endeavor in which we participate.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Financial consequences until job creation goals are met.
