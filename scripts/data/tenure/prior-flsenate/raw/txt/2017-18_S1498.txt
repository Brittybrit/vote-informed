g. Program Performance (if needed, include additional documentation):
a. How will requested funds be spent? Include supporting documentation.
North Bay Village is requesting match funding in the amount of $500,000 for the construction of an
approximately 3,400 linear foot boardwalk that runs along the Northern portion of the Village;
overlooking Biscayne Bay. This project will assist the Village in reaching two major goals: (1) increase
availability of recreational space within the Village and (1) serve as an area attraction to draw new
businesses and guests to North Bay Village; improving our local economy.
b. Identify expected program results and the expected benefit associated from the requested funds.
In addition to creating a beautiful over the water walkway around the parameter of North Bay Village that
will allow bicyclists and pedestrians to safely access local restaurants, businesses, parks, housing
condominiums, and the Village Hall; the Village proposes to install at least one public dock. This will be
the only waterfront access to the general public within the Village. The project will follow best practices in
environmental conservation and universal access for all people regardless of ability.
c. Who will benefit from receipt of State funds?
Statewide benefit from generated economic activity producing revenues to the state upon completion.
