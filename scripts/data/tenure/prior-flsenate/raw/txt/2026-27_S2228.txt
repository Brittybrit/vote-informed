4. Project/Program Description
    This project delivers direct educational and supportive services to economically disadvantaged individuals, at-risk youth,
   first-generation college students, and victims of crime. The program provides academic tutoring, college-readiness and
   navigation support, essential school supplies, and safe, structured summer programs that keep students engaged in a
   supervised learning environment. By combining year-round academic support with out-of-school enrichment, the
   program strengthens educational outcomes, promotes stability, and reduces risk factors associated with crime and
   economic hardship in underserved communities.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose of the funds requested is to provide direct, practical support that improves educational success and
    stability for economically disadvantaged individuals, at-risk youth, first-generation college students, and victims of
    crime. Funding will support tutoring, college-readiness and navigation assistance, essential school supplies, and safe,
    structured summer programs. These investments strengthen academic outcomes, support college access and
    persistence, and reduce risk factors associated with crime and economic hardship.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The program will provide academic tutoring and college-readiness support for first-generation college-bound students,
    supply essential school materials and backpacks to at-risk youth, and expand structured summer camp programming
    with safe, supervised, and enriching activities. Together, these services keep students engaged, strengthen academic
    performance, and provide critical support to families.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Funding will deliver direct support to local families and students by providing academic tutoring, college-readiness
    guidance, and essential school supplies for at-risk youth. It will also fund safe, structured summer programs that give
    students a supervised place to learn, grow, and stay engaged while school is out. These services offer immediate,
    hands-on help to residents.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The project serves economically disadvantaged individuals, with a strong focus on at-risk youth and students across
    multiple age groups, including grade school, high school, and university/college students. It also supports victims of
    crime by providing services and programs designed to promote safety, stability, and positive educational and personal
    outcomes.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The project is expected to reduce juvenile recidivism and improve public safety among participating youth. Success
    will be measured by year-over-year decreases in repeat juvenile offenses using police incident data and law
    enforcement reporting, along with lower arrest and school disciplinary rates and more positive interactions with law
    enforcement. Academic progress will be evaluated through regular review of report cards, standardized test scores,
    attendance, and graduation rates.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
