3. Project/Program Description:
The Urban Miami Neighborhood Revitalization Initiative Project: Description The purpose of requesting funds
is to enhance business resources. The goal is to provide direct financial support to the local businesses and
low-income communities to upgrade buildings and attract new businesses that will provide employment and
services for residents of the area without gentrifying the community and pricing current residents out. These
resources will allow local residents and businesses to improve their neighborhood and increase their ability to
improve their quality of life. In addition, funding will assist businesses in the purchase and/or rehab of
property.
