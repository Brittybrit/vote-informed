11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           The Police Athletic League of North Miami's (PAL of North Miami) juvenile delinquency prevention programs’
          goal is to divert the youth of the North Miami-Dade community from engaging in delinquent behaviors and
          intervening for those who have. Serving as a youth diversion program, PAL of North Miami seeks to serve an
          additional 80 “at-risk” students and increase programming within the 2018-2019 fiscal year. Having successfully
          provided services to over 3,000 students annually since their inception in 1996, PAL of North Miami seeks to
          expand their reach into North Miami-Dade, comprised of financially disadvantaged communities. With few
          exceptions, local law enforcement is responsible for arresting youth for alleged acts of delinquency in school.
          Under the leadership of the North Miami Police Department, PAL of North Miami’s officer led programs have
          enhanced the perception of law enforcement lessening social distance and improving youth's behavior and
          ultimately their scholastic values.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           The State’s funding allocation would provide funding to support an additional 80 students within four (4)
          newly developed programs, to include: the Youth Directors Program (YDP) and mentoring , Empower Lift
          powerlifting, chess, and basketball.

d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

     PAL of North Miami YDP provides students with weekly meetings facilitated by officers and staff. Students
    engage in training and informative presentations about relevant topics such as leadership, entrepreneurship,
    violence prevention, the judicial system, college, and postsecondary education readiness. Evidence-based
    research shows that providing students with a mentor is invaluable to their growth. As a result, PAL staff will
    match students with at least five (5) mentors from the Neighborhood Enhancement Division (NED) division of
    the Police Department, create a 1:8 mentor-student ratio. The Empower Lift powerlifting and weightlifting
    program will be held three days a week by certified trainers. Students will engage in daily exercise regiments
    and an on-going health and wellness training that will be tracked within the program. The final new activity is
    the chess program where students will engage in three weekly hourly sessions with a certified teacher from
    Miami-Dade Public Schools

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          PAL of North Miami expects that 80% of the student participants will maintain at least a passing GPA of 2.0. As
         well, PAL of North Miami seeks to achieve the outcomes of decreasing the number of suspensions and
         absences within the program period. An increase in the positive perception of the police, which lessens social
         distance is also expected as positive relationships are built. PAL of North Miami will evaluate the programs'
         effectiveness in attaining the aforementioned outcomes by monitoring each of these factors on a quarterly
         basis, maintained in individual data files for each participant. A series of records will be compiled inclusive of
         student report cards to review and discuss participants' Grade Point Averages (GPAs), attendance; and behavior
         measured by school suspension and conduct. Pre and post assessments will be given to all participants to
         monitor their change in perception of the Police and authorities as a whole.
