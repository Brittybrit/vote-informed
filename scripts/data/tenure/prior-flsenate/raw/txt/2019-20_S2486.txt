4. Project/Program Description:
   The Colombian American Chamber of Commerce (CAC) is an apolitical organization that promotes trade,
   investment, and free enterprise. The CAC supports the interests of Florida companies that are doing business in
   Colombia, and assists other Florida companies who want to do business in Colombia in the future. Conversely, the
   CAC also assists Colombia businesses enter and expand in the U.S. market. The efforts strengthen bilateral relations
   between the two international business communities. The CAC also sponsors mentoring programs for students who
   wish to pursue careers in international trade. The CAC organizes and sponsors trade missions between the USA and
   Colombia.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        The program promotes international trade between Colombia and businesses in Florida, creating new sources
        of revenue while creating new jobs in the State.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        (1) International trade missions, (2) Student mentoring programs, (3) Guidance and technical assistance is
        provided to companies in both countries to help facilitate the expansion of trade, (4) Seminars and networking
        events to introduce corporate leaders and officials from government agencies to help them understand the
        practical needs of each in order to more easily facilitate business expansion.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Economic and business development activities as needed by our participating member companies.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        The target population is the south Florida business community, with special emphasis on small and minority
        businesses that often lack the resources that can be provided to help expand their companies and create new
        jobs. In real terms, every resident of Miami-Dade benefits from a growing economy and job creation.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
        The primary benefit of the program is job creation through expanded international trade. The methodology by
        which this outcome will be measured is very simple: Needed assistance will be provided to track the expansion
        and job creation of each endeavor participated in.
