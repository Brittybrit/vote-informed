4. Project/Program Description:
    Jason and Elisha Merilus Youth Academy, a program designed to help children with their homework after school
    and keep them secured from any form of delinquency. With the help of Barry University students, JEM Youth
    Academy monitors the students' schooling activities and progress reports, and helps to further increase their
    academic progress. Aside from tutoring, we provide them with food and take them on field trips. The tutoring
    hours are from 3:30pm to 7:00pm.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           Our goal prior to receiving the funds will be to further enhance our after-school program so that we can better
          assist students with all of their academic needs and help them achieve highest grades in school.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           We plan to meet our intended goal by first hiring a tutor to work with the students full-time to assist them
          with their homework and help them do better in their classes. The tutor will visit the schools and meet the
          teachers to discuss their strengths and weaknesses and have a conference with the parents to discuss the
          students' academic progress. The tutor will also do a monthly evaluation to measure the students' progression.
          The students will also have access to computers, printers, and other forms of technology so they can complete
          any project-related homework. Reading textbooks at their grade level will be used to help them increase their
          knowledge to further assist them in each subject.
