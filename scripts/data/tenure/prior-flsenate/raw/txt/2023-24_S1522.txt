4. Project/Program Description
    The Florida Fallen Heroes Wall Memorial honoring the men and women in uniform that made the ultimate sacrifice upon
   the alter of freedom is currently housed at the Air Force Armament Museum at Eglin Air Force Base. The Wall Memorial
   must to relocated due to a planned FDOT and Eglin AFB (taking) road and new runway expansion/reconfiguration causing
   the museum to close and relocate in 2023-25.

   The Military Order of the Purple Heart, Eglin AFB, the Wounded Warrior Project, and FDVA agreed the Wall Memorial
   should be moved to the Florida Capitol Grounds, Purple Heart section, and is requesting an appropriation during fiscal year
   2023 in the amount of $150,000 for design, build, update, and installation of the Memorial. The project will be managed by
   DMS with direction and input by the Military Order of the Purple Heart, Tallahassee Chapter 758, Commander Rick
   Stafford, USM.
