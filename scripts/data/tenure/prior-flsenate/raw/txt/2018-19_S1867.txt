1. Title of Project: Brooksville Fire Department - Public Safety Constructive Renovations
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 12/11/2017
4. Project/Program Description:
    The purpose of this project is to update the Public Safety Facility of the Brooksville Fire Department to improve
    deficiencies with the roof structure and bay doors. To also include living quarter renovations and improve the
    protective clothing bunker room for the firefighters in the facility.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           The building renovations provide direct services to the citizens by offering secure and compliant shelter for all
          of our fire fighting personnel and rescue vehicles. The building also shelters the necessary support assets
          required to serve our citizens such as: respite facilities, stand by generation, air cascade, tools and food
          services. Lastly, this facility shelters our most valuable assets in the men and women of the Brooksville Fire
          Department that provide property protection and life saving services to the citizens of Brooksville and
          Hernando County everday

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           The Brooksville Fire Department houses ALL fire and rescue components of the City's Public Safety First
          Responders. The building serves as a shelter for the City's Fire and Rescue Staff and houses ALL of the City's
          Fire and Rescue assets. The building also serves as a "Safe Haven for Children", by providing community
          outreach services. The Brooksville Fire Department is a source of community pride that has provided fire and
          rescue services for over 137 years.

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          To provide a safe shelter for the Public Safety First Responders that provide fire and rescue services to the city
         of Brooksville residents.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          This would be a direct impact over the total population of Brooksville, over 8,000 residents benefit from the
         Fire and Rescue Services provided.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          To improve and replace a roof system that is failing, to harden up the bay doors that do not meet current wind
         load requirements, to relocate a protective clothing bunker storage room that would be self contained and
         climate controlled which will provide additional protection to the firefighters protective equipment.

    g. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
       penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
