1. Title of Project: Zephyrhills Septic to Sewer Project
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 01/30/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   Connect approximately 67 low income residents to municipal sewer system in an area immediately east of the
   Zephyrhills city limits, but within the city's utility service area, per an agreement with Pasco County. The City
   previously provided water service to this neighborhood. This project will include extending sewer force main lines,
   gravity and/or vacuum sewer lines, connection to the city municipal waste water collection system, decommission
   existing septic tanks, and construction of Armstrong Street, a dirt road, to provide access and utility service along
   that roadway (currently a private drive). .

11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        Connect sewer lines to residential subdivision and homes along 6th Avenue and Armstrong Street. Project will
        include decommission of existing septic tanks and improvement of dirt road (Armstrong). This includes 67
        existing properties and potential for additional properties in the future.
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        Connection to municipal sewer system; environmental improvements; roadway improvements. The City of
        Zephyrhills will impact fees of $2,090 to the residents for connection to the utility waste water system as a local
        match for this project. Currently, in conjunction with Pasco County Utilities, the city is in the process of
        installing a lift station within a mile of this neighborhood.
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        In addition to the improvement of the quality of life for residents in this predominately African American
        neighborhood by upgrading local utility standards, these residents will be served by the local municipality with
        both water and sewer service and decommission of existing septic tanks, many of which were installed as long
        ago as the 1950s and 1960s.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        Approximately 67 property owners/150 residents, This is a largely minority neighborhood with high
        concentration of senior citizens and low and fixed income residents.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
      will be measured?

    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard

         implementation of this project.
12. The owner(s) of the facility to receive, directly or indirectly, any fixed capital outlay funding. Include the
    relationship between the owner(s) of the facility and the entity.
    The City of Zephyrhills municipal utility system will connect these residents to its system. This system provides a
    safe, clean water supply and environmentally sound sewer system. The residents within this project area are
    located within the city's Utility Service Area Agreement with Pasco County and has provided utility services (water)
    to the area for more than 30 years.
