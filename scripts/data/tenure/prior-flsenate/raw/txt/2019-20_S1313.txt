4. Project/Program Description:
   Project is to include a well site/pumping facility, water main extension, master pump station, and a off-site force
   main. These facilities will provide the support and stimulus for economic development located near the intersection
   of Kieffer Road and Handcart Road in central Pasco County. The Area was previously identified within the proposed
   community of Villages of Pasadena Hills, and currently has approved MPUDs surrounding the area.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         Funds will pay for the design and construction of a potable water facility, off-site water main, master pump
         station, and a off-site force main that will provide service to the future Villages of Pasadena Hills
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         Design/Permitting, Construction and CEI
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         The completed facilities will provide fire protection, clean drinking water, and a reliable source of wastewater
         transmission to one of Pasco County Utilities wastewater treatment plants.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         The project will provide potable water, and wastewater services to a portion of The Villages of Pasadena Hills a
         proposed development in central Pasco county. The project will serve approximately 9,000 homes, along with a
         large commercial area.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         The project will stimulate economic growth in a currently undeveloped portion of central Pasco county. This
         will lead to an increased number of homes and businesses in the area, these developments can be monitored
         to provide measurement.
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         Loss of funding in the applicable fiscal year.
