13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Stronger Choices Initiative empowers youth, parents, and community members to make healthier, informed
    choices through drug prevention education, dialogue, and collaboration. By addressing risk behaviors such as
    substance use and misuse, and promoting wellness, legal awareness, and positive decision-making, this multi-event
    effort strengthens prevention, communication, and resilience across stake holders, community partners and
    generations in the City of Hialeah.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Youth Dialogue Day (YDD), Know the Law Parent Training (KTL). Prevention Plus Wellness Curriculum, Vaping &
    Opioid Prevention for Teens (PPW V&O). Stronger Choices Together Symposium (STS). All evidence based,
    researched practices and programs.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     YDD: Evidence based participatory method where youth discuss drug prevention awareness around community
    stakeholders. KTL: Students and parents obtain an understanding of the law to avoid unlawful behaviors. PPW V&O:
    Sessions to connect the importance of physical, emotional and social awareness to reduce substance use among
    youth. STS: culminating event including partners, stakeholder & educators
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Parents, Youth and Community, At-risk youth, Grade school students, High school students.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     400+
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     If HCC cannot comply to meet the agreed-upon deliverables, the coalition will return any funds received for those
    unmet deliverables.
