1. Project Title          Miami-Dade County - S-20 Collector Canal Everglades Wetlands
                          Restoration Project

2. Senate Sponsor        Annette Taddeo

3. Date of Request       02/02/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    Funds will be used to fill an unnecessary ditch (S-20 Collector Canal), disable two nearby unneeded culverts that impact
   regional Everglades wetlands hydrology, and restore wetlands near the coast in southern Miami-Dade County. This project
   will enhance water retention and wetland recharge potential to address salt intrusion in the Biscayne Aquifer near municipal
   wellfields, including those under the jurisdiction of Miami-Dade County and the Florida Keys Aqueduct Authority.
   This ditch is not part of the Central and South Florida Flood Control Project.
   This project will improve water management, reduce salt water intrusion and restore publicly owned wetlands in Miami-
   Dade County in the southern Everglades, a region located east of Everglades National Park.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This funding request will use locally sourced clean fill to backfill the eastern portion of the S-20 Collector Canal and re-
    create historically existing Everglades wetlands, as well as fill the nearby unneeded culverts.
    These actions will reduce over-drainage of the publicly owned wetlands and improve water management in an area
    where salt intrusion has become a critical issue.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Construction
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Reduce drainage of Everglades wetlands and restore them in order to better protect/enable continued use of drinking
    water wellfields located in South Miami-Dade County, including but not limited to wellfields under the jurisdiction of Miami-
    Dade Water and Sewer and the Florida Keys Aqueduct Authority.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The citizens of southern Miami-Dade County and the Florida Keys
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
