1. Title of Project: Acquisition of Florida Quilt Museum Building
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 01/29/2018
4. Project/Program Description:
    The Florida Quilt Museum is located in Trenton It is in Trenton's oldest commercial building - a restored 1910 dry
    goods store, adjacent to the city's historic turn of the century railroad depot. The museum serves as the
    headquarters for the Florida Quilt Trail. The funding request is seeking funds to help acquire the building.
