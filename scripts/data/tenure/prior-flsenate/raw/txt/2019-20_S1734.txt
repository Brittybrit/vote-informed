1. Title of Project: Key Biscayne K-8 Stormwater Improvement
2. Senate Sponsor: Jose Rodriguez
3. Date of Submission: 02/07/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   The Village of Key Biscayne is a barrier island south of downtown Miami. Due to its close proximity to Biscayne Bay
   and the Atlantic Ocean, the Village's stormwater management system is influenced by tidal waters, which presents
   a challenge for stormwater disposal. Flooding around the school area is a public safety issue. The K-8 Center
   currently has over 1,000 students enrolled. Many of these students walk and bike to school. The K-8 School
   Improvement project as a total area of 28.94 acres around the Key Biscayne K-9 Center. This low lying area runs
   along Ridgewood Drive, Woodcrest Drive and Glenridge Drive, West McIntyre Street and Fernwood Road. Phase I of
   the project includes the installation of a pump station with a backflow preventor on the north side of the school and
   drainage wells.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         Expected results for this project are reduced flooding in project area.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         Requested funds will be spent on the installation of a pump station with a backflow preventor at the K-8 Center
         as well as drainage wells.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         Residents of Key Biscayne will be able to safely travel to the K-8 Center during rain events. At the moment the
         area surrounding the school floods during rain events and King tide events.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         All residents of Key Biscayne will be served by the project but those most affected will be the students and
         parents of the K-8 Center. The school currently has over 1,000 students attending the school. Many of the
         students wither walk or bike to school.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         The project will be well documented by the Project Manager. Photographs will be taken throughout the
         process documenting the project. At the end of the project all As-Built plans will be certified by the certified
         project engineer. Project manager will provide signed statement by certified engineer indicating construction
         has been completed in accordance with approved designs.
