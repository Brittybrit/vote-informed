4. Project/Program Description
    The estimated cost of the Miami Lakes Loch Lomond Phase II Drainage Improvements Project is $920,000. The Town is
   requesting legislative appropriation of $736,000, reflecting a Town local share/match contribution of $184,000 to fund this
   Project. The Project consists of the construction of a new drainage system, comprised of new catch basins, manholes,
   drainage pipes, ex filtration trenches (French drains), milling and resurfacing of roadway, and signing and pavement
   markings along Burnside Way, East Troon Circle, and West Prestwick Place, connecting to an existing outfall. The Phase I
   drainage improvements are currently in the design phase. This Phase II Project continues the flood mitigation activities in
   the Loch Lomond community.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The specific goal is to construct the Project for the purpose of mitigating and reducing localized flooding and standing
    rainwater, enhancing protection of residential homes from flood events, increasing drainage capacity, reducing the
    probability of mosquito-transmitted diseases to the public, and protecting of private property and life in Miami Lakes'
    residential neighborhoods.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Flood mitigation activities and services consist of the construction of a new drainage system, including the design,
    permitting, and construction of new catch basins, manholes, drainage pipes, ex filtration trenches (French drains), milling
    and resurfacing of roadway, and signing and pavement markings along Burnside Way, East Troon Circle, and West
    Prestwick Place.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Direct services are flood mitigation activities to further reduce or eliminate the risk of flood damage in the Loch Lomond
    community, as well as, the protection of residential property and life.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Target population served by this project is the Loch Lomond residential community in Miami Lakes. The project will
    directly impact approximately 30 homes.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcome is to complete the construction of the Miami Lakes Loch Lomond Phase II Drainage
    Improvements Project. Expected benefits include improved drainage, reduced localized flooding, increased drainage
    capacity, improved storm water management, eliminated standing rainwater, reduced probability of mosquito-transmitted
    diseases to the public, reduced impact to property and life, enhanced environmental protection to the general public,
    improved transportation conditions, and improved groundwater and surface water quality. Outcome will be measured by
    field inspection and documentation of rainfall events.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     No payment will be made for a request for reimbursement which does not include an updated summary of the project,
    dated invoices, checks paid by the contracting agency, and proof of payments via copies of deposited checks.
