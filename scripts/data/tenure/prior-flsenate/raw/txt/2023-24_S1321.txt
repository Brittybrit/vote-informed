1. Project Title          Preservation of Historical Cuban Exile Materials - Miami-Dade

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        01/25/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    This project aims to protect, preserve, and enable access to the historical archives of Cuban exile materials. Preserving
   this collection ensures these records are safeguarded against deterioration and will survive for future generations. Direct
   digital access by means of digitization services will allow this collection to be viewed by citizens throughout the State of
   Florida and beyond. It will create the access needed for historical research, genealogy, and bring to life the stories from the
   past. It ensures an important part of cultural heritage is preserved and shared with citizens.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To preserve, digitize, and create access for historical records related to the Cuban American Diaspora in America.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Records preservation services to include archival stabilization, deacidification, and Mylar encapsulation. Digital imaging
    services to include scanning documents into an archival format that can be indexed and hosted to create a digital access
    point for citizens.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will have the ability to view, research, or request material electronically from anywhere and at their convenience.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Allowing and creating access to historical records has a far reaching impact on citizens. It could easily be estimated the
    1-3 million people of Cuban origin within the state of Florida and outside the state could benefit from this project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit from this project is the preservation of and direct digital access to historical records that have been
    archived and shelved for the majority of their life. The outcome will be measured by the number of records available
    electronically after the project is finished.

    If stated performance measures or deliverablesdia are not met within the agreed project time line, the contracting agency
