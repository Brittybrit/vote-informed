13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose and goal of this program is to raise awareness of the plight of the coral reefs in Miami-Dade County, work
    to establish a marine sanctuary or Protected Marine Area in Miami Dade County, and outplant coral in the water off
    Miami-Dade County to revitalize the coral reef tract in Miami-Dade County.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     For the awareness, we plan to host events throughout Miami Dade County as well as continue to finalize our
    partnerships with Dade County Public Schools and Florida International University to not only certify students as divers
    but also to create citizen scientist to help promote the plight of the reefs and how the community can help achieve this
    goal. We also work with marine stakeholders in the community for the development of a National Marine Sanctuary
    and/or Protected Marine Area to allow recovery of the coral reef in Miami Dade County. Finally, we plan to outplant
    coral to help proliferate the reef tract in Miami that has been in decline for decades.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     We plan to work with Miami-Dade Public Schools to certify students as divers to enable them to become citizen
    scientists to help with coral out plantings, monitoring, cleaning the corals as well as assist in raising awareness for
    importance of coral reefs to our community. The ability to have reduced insurance costs through reduced storm surge
    and tidal activity generated by a healthier reef system, a place where the community can appreciate the beauty of coral
    reefs and be able to study the reefs firsthand.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population are the residents of Miami-Dade County and visitors to the county. By creating a thriving and
    robust coral reef system again, all residents will be served with lower insurance rates and the ability to enjoy more
    ecological activities in the marine environment. Additionally, revenue generated for state and local businesses and
    governments as a result of eco-tourism emanating from tours of the reef will also be a benefit to all who live in the
    community.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The ultimate outcome will be a robust and healthy reef system and a return of a biodiversity to current reef system in
    Miami-Dade County. This will be measured by monitoring the growth of the coral reef system coverage over the current
    substrate as well as monitoring the different species of marine life and their numbers as it returns to the coral reef off
    the shores of Miami-Dade County. Secondarily, we will also monitor the insurance rates with specific reference on the
    impact of storm surge on the rates offered and work closely with the state to ensure all affected see the benefits in the
    insurance premiums.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    Penalties would the cessation of future funding for the project.
