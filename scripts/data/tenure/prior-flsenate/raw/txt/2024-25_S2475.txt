1. Project Title           Banyan Health System - Dade Chase Facility Renovation & Expansion

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        12/12/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The allocated funds will serve a pivotal purpose: enhancing our community's mental health support infrastructure. By
   expanding our current facility with 30 additional Level II beds, we will significantly elevate our capacity to assist vulnerable
   populations, especially those lacking financial resources. Addressing a statewide waitlist of approximately 3,000 patients,
   these funds enable us to make substantial strides in accessible, quality care, reinforcing our commitment to community
   well-being.

     The purpose of these funds to provide more mental health and substance abuse beds in the community. In DCF's 2022
    Behavioral Health Assessment the managing entities identified the need to increase access to short term residential
    programs.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     These funds are capital funds, once project is completed indivudals will be receiving primary and behavioral health care
    services.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Once the project is completed, the addition of new mental health beds will allow for the treatment of additional patients
    who need long term mental health treatment.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population for this project are low-income individuals with a mental health or substance abuse diagnosis or
    both.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Once construction is completed, we can provide mental health assistance or substance abuse assitance to those
    individuals in need. This will prevent these indivudals from ending up in the judicial system or ending up in emergency
    rooms as they will be receving the adequate care they need. We can measure this by providing data of how many of our
    patients end up in the judical system or in the emergency room after being in our residential program.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Organization should return funding if it fails to meet deliverables or performance measures.
