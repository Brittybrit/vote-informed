1. Project Title         Doral Pedestrian Bridge Safety Project

2. Senate Sponsor        Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request       03/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The proposed pedestrian bridge will span over NW 41 St/Doral Boulevard to provide residents and visitors a needed link
   between the Turnpike Trail shared-used path north and south of NW 41 St/Doral Blvd which is a heavily traveled arterial
   within the City of Doral. The pedestrian bridge will improve safety conditions for pedestrians and bicyclist.
