3.   Date of Request 01/16/2020

4.   Project/Program Description
     Today, school security means more than adding stronger locks to gates. To reach security objectives, schools require advanced
     command centers with integrated mass notification systems, access control alert mechanisms and interoperability. While those tools
     present a real cost challenge for school districts, Hernando County School District has discovered that our current network infrastructure
     will not support those advanced security tools. All funds requested through this appropriation will fund Phase II of the district’s plan to
     upgrade school security. Phase II includes installation of Cat 6 Ethernet cabling to allow for a fully scale-able security system able to grow
     with future technologies and support tools such as IP cameras, health monitoring and access control tools that will be the focus of Phase
     III slated for 2021. HCSD has invested $175,000 along with monthly recurring costs of over $3,500 to complete Phase I in 2019.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      HCSD will be able to install necessary Ethernet cabling to all schools that will form the network that allows for vital security upgrades
      using advanced technologies.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Services will include the installation of Cat 6 ethernet cabling, switches, connectors, panels and servers at all school sites




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Schools will be better secured and able to protect against threats from individuals seeking to do harm.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      Students and staff at all schools in the school district. Approximately 25,000 individuals will be served.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      The installation of the Ethernet cabling is essential to being able to purchase and fully utilize new security tools such as IP cameras,
      analytics to include aggression detection, vaping detection and device health monitoring technologies.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      In the event of a delay in the project timeline, an action plan will be provided to ensure the project is completed in accordance with the
      contract.
