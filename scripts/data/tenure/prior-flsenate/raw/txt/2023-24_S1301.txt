2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        02/08/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    This project aims to further the goal of expanding workforce education in Florida and addressing the State's health care
   workforce gaps. Based on feedback from health care industry partners, the strategies proposed seek to build interest and
   increase access to educational programs in two high-demand health care jobs: Physician Assistants and Clinical (medical)
   Laboratory Professionals. Through our state-approved and nationally accredited educational programs, students will
   receive all required classroom and clinical education, advising, financial aid, and job placement assistance. By increasing
   the enrollment and graduation of "job ready" students in these select health care programs, Barry University will contribute
   to resolving select critical workforce needs in Florida.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This request will further the goal of expanding workforce education in Florida and addressing the State's health care
    workforce gaps. Specifically, local health care organizations have voiced a desperate need for more clinical (medical)
    laboratory professionals and physician assistants as the State continues to face an increasing scarcity of physicians. The
    funds will support enrollment expansion of two long-standing, successful, and accredited educational programs housed in
    Barry University's College of Health and Wellness.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funds will be used to 1) recruit and develop additional clinical sites and required faculty, 2) enhance existing
    classroom resources to accommodate more students, and 3) provide partial tuition support and job placement assistance
    to students who commit to positions in Florida health care organizations upon graduation.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This initiative, by expanding the resources necessary to increase enrollment and graduates, will help facilitate the
    preparation of a health care workforce for the state of Florida. In doing so, it will address the current critical shortage of
    clinical laboratory professionals (https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/clinical-laboratory-technologists-and-
    technicians.htm) and physician assistants (https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/physician-assistants.htm). This will also
    help abate the impact of the shortage of physicians in the State of Florida
    (https://www.flmedical.org/florida/Florida_Public/News/2022/Florida%E2%80%99s_physician_shortage.aspx).
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This project targets current and potential Barry University (BU) health care students as well as practicing health care
    providers seeking advanced education. The BU College of Health and Wellness enrolls over 2000 students. Upon
    graduation, these health care practitioners practice in clinical facilities in urban, rural, and under-served areas of Florida,
    providing care to the state's uninsured, under-insured and disadvantaged citizens. More than 80% of graduates from the
    College's clinical programs remain in their Florida home communities, including in designated Health Professional
    Shortage Areas (HPSAs).
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcomes of this project are: 1) a 20% increase in the number of students enrolled and graduated from our
    medical laboratory and PA programs to fill critical workforce needs; 2) a 20% increase in the number of PA and
    Laboratory graduates who accept positions within the State of Florida, as reported by industry partners.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Return unused funds and require submission of an improvement plan with strategies for improving the institution's
    performance.
