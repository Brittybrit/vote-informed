11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

            (1) MS. SENIOR FLORIDA PAGEANT: A contestant must be at least 60 years old and is required to express her
          lifestyle through evening gown presentation, talent, philosophy of life and interview with judges. The winner
          goes on to represent the state of Florida in the national competition Ms. Senior America in Atlantic City,
          October 2018. (2) ROSE & CROWN CLUB: Former contestants produce an entertaining and inspiring show to
          perform at nursing homes, rehabs and assisted living communities. (3) SENIOR GOSPEL CHOIR: 60+ male and
          female choir perform at nursing homes, rehabs and assisted living communities. (4) NANA PROJECT: Nana is the
          nickname for "Grandmother." This is a senior woman volunteer mentoring program that provides a
          grandmother image and experience for under privileged children. (5) HEALTH, NUTRITION & POSITIVE
          LIFESTYLE WORKSHOPS: Workshops and presentations to enhance senior lifestyle with a focus on the
          implementation and maintenance of good health and productive senior life.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Provide several services for seniors including a support system, motivational & inspirational programs. (1) MS.
          SENIOR FLORIDA PAGEANT promotes confidence, self esteem, strength of purpose, dignity, maturity as they
          confront the rigors of aging. (2) ROSE & CROWN CLUB provides community outreach to Nursing Homes,
          Rehabs, Assistant Living Facilities, and Seniors abandoned by family. (3) SENIOR GOSPEL CHOIR is a senior male
          and female choir that performs at nursing homes, rehabs, and assisted living facilities; (4) NANA PROJECT is a
          senior women volunteer mentoring program that provides a grandmother image and experience to under

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          1. MS. SENIOR FLORIDA PAGEANT: Statewide pageant recognizing accomplishments and contributions of
         senior women to family and community. Winner represents state of Florida in 45 yr old National Pageant Ms.
         Senior America in Atlantic City New Jersey. 2. ROSE & CROWN CLUB: Former contestants and volunteer
         perform Community Outreach to Nursing Homes, Rehabs, and Assistant Living Facilities. 3. SENIOR GOSPEL
         CHOIR: Senior male and female choir perform at Nursing Homes, Rehabs, and Assistant Living Facilities
         throughout the community and ultimately nationwide. 4. NANA PROJECT (Never Abandoned, Never Alone):
         Nana is nicknamed for "Grandmother." This is a senior woman volunteer mentoring program that provides a
         grandmother image and experience for under privileged children. 5. WORKSHOPS: Workshops and
         presentations to enhance senior lifestyle with a focus on the implementation and maintenance of good health
         and productive senior life.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          Elderly persons, Persons with poor mental health, Persons with poor physical health, Economically
         disadvantaged persons, At risk youth, Physical disabled, Preschool students, Grade school students, High school
         students, University/College students

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          Improve Physical Health, Improve Mental Health, Enrich Cultural Experiences, Improve Quality of Education,
         Increase Economic Activity, Increase Tourism. METHODOLOGY : Improve Physical Health: Website survey
         evaluations and Senior satisfaction surveys. Improve Mental Health: Introduce Memory and Word games,
         puzzles and other memory enhancing tools. Registration and attendee follow up surveys. Statistics to show
         number of attendees, people in outreach program, and satisfaction surveys. Enrich Cultural Experience:
         Satisfaction Surveys, Focus group sessions, Q/A follow up, Target number of attendees by addresses and zip
         codes. Improve Quality of Education: Tracking through At-Risk Youths partnership in NANA program, tracking
         through various senior groups, youth agencies and Miami Dade Public Schools. Increase Economic Activity:
         Tracking of repetitive advertising, Satisfaction Surveys, Website and PayPal tracking. Increase Tourism:
         Residency Survey, Host Hotel evaluation and survey

    g. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
       penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
        None.
