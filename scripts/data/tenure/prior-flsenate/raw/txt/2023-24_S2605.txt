14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Complete Streets Program Adaptive Re-use of the Doral Arts District between NW 58 St-NW 54 St and between NW 87
    Ave- NW 79 Ave. The Project includes roadway reconstruction, new sidewalks, parallel parking, storm water drainage,
    bike lanes.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds will be spent for the construction of the adaptive Re-use of Doral Arts District between NW 58 St and NW 54 St
    and between NW 87 Ave and NW 79 Ave.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The Project includes roadway reconstruction, new sidewalks, parallel parking, storm water drainage, bike lanes (where
    possible), and street lighting. This will increase pedestrian safety and will also serve as a mitigation initiative to help
    prevent flood related losses.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All the residents in City of Doral. (81,000)
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Prevent/minimize flooding and ponding along roadway segment. Installation of street lighting and sidewalk installation.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Liquidated damages will be included in the contract documents. Liquidated damages will be based on construction cost.
