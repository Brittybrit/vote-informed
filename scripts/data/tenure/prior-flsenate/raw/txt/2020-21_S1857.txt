11. Program Performance
 a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
     The project will provide potable water distribution and centralized sewer improvements to stimulate economic growth in a currently
     undeveloped portion of central Pasco county. Project allows for development of job creating commercial areas along with
     approximately 9,000 new homes. According to AWWA and WEF each dollar spent on new infrastructure generates $6 in returns for a
     return of $34,500,000 for this project.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
     Construction of water distribution mains and wastewater transmission mains along the Handcart Road corridor. This area is currently
     undeveloped and has no access to these services.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
     Safe, reliable distribution of potable water and centralized sewer to homes and businesses.




 d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
     Target population is new homes and businesses in Pasco County. The project will support approximately 9,000 new homes, all of
     which will be served by the proposed infrastructure. The infrastructure will also serve commercial developments, which will in turn
     provide jobs.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
     According to AWWA and WEF each dollar spent on new infrastructure generates $6 in returns for a return of $34,500,000 for this
     project. The additional enviromental benefit is a centralized sewer with the potential to avoid approximately 600 pounds per day of
     nutrients discharged to water bodies.




  f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
     penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
     Loss of funding in the applicable fiscal year.
