1. Project Title          City of Hialeah 911 Communications Tower

2. Senate Sponsor        Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request       02/07/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The funds will be used to construct a 911 communications tower in the Hialeah Heights annexation area in order to
   improve emergency communication in areas where the current infrastructure is lacking. The area that will benefit from this
   proposal currently contains several zones where first responders called to the scene of an emergency are not able to use
   radio communications to communicate with dispatch.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will be used to construct a new 911 Communications Tower.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?


                                                       Page 2 of 4
                                              The Florida Senate
                                     Local Funding Initiative Request
                                          Fiscal Year 2023-2024                                                 LFIR # 1356

    The City will engage a vendor to construct a new 911 Communications Tower that will service the Hialeah Heights
    Annexation area.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The new 911 Communications Tower will improve communications between first responders at the scene of an
    emergency and dispatch, thus improving the service to the public and eliminating any lapses in communication during
    critical situations requiring rapid response.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The project will target no particular group, and will serve the general population of the City of Hialeah by augmenting the
    Police Department's ability to protect and serve the residents of the City. The population of the City of Hialeah is roughly
    220,000.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The benefit and measure of the same will be the servicing of areas in the Hialeah Heights community where first
    responders are currently not able to properly communicate with dispatch over their radios.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

    The City of Hialeah is willing to be bound by the standard penalties for breach of contract in the State of Florida.
    Furthermore, the City is willing to contemplate additional penalties the Agency may propose.
