14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will be used to improve the City right of way along W 32 Ave from 68 street-76 street.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

                                                       Page 2 of 5
                                              The Florida Senate

     The improvement will entail a roadway widening, installation of storm-water infrastructure and drainage improvements,
    road reconstruction, the installation of ADA compliant sidewalks, the installation of LED streetlights, upgrades to an off-
    site parking lot benefiting a school, and upgrades to school pedestrian crossings.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Flooding conditions during the rainy season will improve as a result of the drainage improvements. Furthermore, the
    remaining improvements will contribute to less traffic congestion during peak hours as a result of the streamlining of
    school drop off and pickup and the widening of the roadway. Public safety in the area will be augmented by the addition
    of LED lights.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The project will target no particular group, and will serve the general population of the City of Hialeah. The population of
    the City of Hialeah is roughly 220,000.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Traffic conditions will improve at peak hours because the widening of the roadway will reduce traffic congestion.
    Furthermore, the improvements to the sidewalks will contribute to improved mobility both on foot and by vehicle. These
    benefits could be measured by improved commute times for area residents after the improvements are installed as
    compared to the commute times today.
    The drainage improvements and installation of a storm water system will improve flooding conditions after large rain
    events, thus improving living conditions for area residents, and driving conditions for motorists. This benefit can be
    measured by the amount of rainwater that accumulates on the City right of way today, as compared to the flow of
    rainwater after the improvements are installed.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

    The City of Hialeah is willing to be bound by the standard penalties for breach of contract in the State of Florida.
    Furthermore, the City is willing to contemplate additional penalties the Agency may propose.
