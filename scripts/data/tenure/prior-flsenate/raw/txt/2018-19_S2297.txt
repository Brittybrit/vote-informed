1. Title of Project: City of Miami Beach Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA)
2. Senate Sponsor: Daphne Campbell
3. Date of Submission: 01/17/2018
4. Project/Program Description:
    Project Name: Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition System (SCADA). This is the priority project for the City of
    Miami Beach. SCADA is a software system that will operate the City’s water, sewage disposal and stormwater
    pumps through automated technology. It is important to the city because it will allow the pumps to be operated
    efficiently from a central location, and it will identify system failures and prevent public health situations such as
    failure in the sewage system, causing backup to streets and homes.
