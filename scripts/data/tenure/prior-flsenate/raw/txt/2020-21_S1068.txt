4.   Project/Program Description
     The funds will retrofit and bring into ADA compliance) a building donated to the Foundation to expand its current use by researchers, local
     and worldwide, to study the ecological, geological, archaeological and historical features of the Deering Estate property, a 445-acre, state-
     owned property in South Dade.
     The Deering property is the only part of the Everglades Restoration Project located in an urban setting which is easily accessed by the
     public. The Miami Rock Ridge located on the property forms a barrier between Biscayne Bay and the interior of the of the south Florida
     peninsula. The ridge is significant because it helps retain water and directs flow from Lake Okeechobee to the Everglades. The property
     is also home to more than 80 endangered or threatened species and has 26 known archaeological sites at which fossilized remains from
     nearly 10,000 years ago have been discovered.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      The funds will assist in retrofitting a building currently being used by worldwide researchers to study the ecologically, geologically significant features
      (including archeological and historical discoveries) of the Deering Estate property, a 445-acre state-owned property in South Dade County. When
      completed, the building will have expanded use, including a base of operations for up to 20 researchers at one time (conducting ecological,
      archaeological and geological studies) and additional educational program capacity. The facility will primarily be used to house researchers while they
      conduct important ecological, geological, archaeological and historical research of worldwide significance. The research conducted at the building can
      significantly enhance ongoing Everglades protection activities. Further, the upgraded building can also expand the programs hosted to educate the
      community and policymakers about the significant role the environment of the Deering property plays in the

 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      The Deering Estate is a unique area to study the economic interaction between the Everglades restoration and key Florida industries of Fishing,
      Agriculture and Eco-tourism. Studies will include: Miami Rock Ridge (part of the property) forms a barrier between Biscayne Bay and the interior of the
      southern Florida peninsula. It establishes a geological basin that benefits the Everglades by retaining water and directing its flow from Lake
      Okeechobee. It contains one of the largest blocks of globally endangered pine rockland habitat in the United States. Paleo-era “Hunting Grounds,”
      which is an original freshwater site, where the Everglades drain to Biscayne Way. Eight natural habitats, home to approximately 800 species on the
      Florida Natural Areas Inventory, 89 of which are threatened, endangered or commercially exploited. The 26 known archeological sites including Cutler
      Burial Mound, Cutler Fossil and Deering Estate. Fossilized remains from nearly 10,000 years ago.

  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      There will be educational opportunities for the public for the public to learn the highlights of the work that the researchers conduct through public
      events, published research papers, and events.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      The target population is varied. Academicians and researchers that are currently drawn to this property where significant discoveries have been made
      will increase. Further, persons visiting the property and attending educational programs (i.e. K-12 students and others) will have greater access to this
      area and the programs provided on the property. The Deering Estate research station provides an invaluable service to local communities and the
      country by providing unbiased scientific information and facilities to help governmental and other stakeholders tackle critical environmental issues. The
      Estate is the only portion of the Everglades Restoration Project (ERP) located in an urban setting which is easily accessed by the public. Field station
      staff and researchers often play a critical role in ensuring that environmental considerations are factored into local and regional planning and
      development decisions.


 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      As research continues on the discoveries already made at the property, the awareness of this 445-acre state-owned heritage site will grow worldwide
      through research-related publications. Additionally, educators, K-12 students and the general public will learn about the unique environmental
      significance of this property. Outcomes will be measured by the increase body of research conducted which can benefit the property, the Everglades
      and the state as a whole.

      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Standard penalties for non-compliance are adequate.
