1.   Project Title          Inverness - Septic to Sewer Highway 44 East Corridor - Lake Tsala Apopka

2.   Senate Sponsor Wilton Simpson

3.   Date of Request 11/23/2019

4.   Project/Program Description
     The Strategic Intermodal System State Highway 44 bisects the City of Inverness as the major commercial corridor for future economic
     development and redevelopment/infill. Currently the easterly extent of the corridor just beyond and within the City limits fronts the Tsala
     Apopka chain of lakes and is not served by central sewer. The City looks to extend central sewer to this area to proffer water quality/
     environmental protection and economic opportunity with the removal of residential and commercial septic tanks. This project will be the
     catalyst to further expansion of the City's septic to sewer program into lake front residential subdivisions.

11. Program Performance
 a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
     Water Quality, Environmental Protection, and Economic Development




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
     Design and construction of central sewer system to benefiting property owners.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
     Availability of central sewer and the foundation of future expansion therein to lake front residential communities.




 d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
     Current and future commercial developments and lake front residential subdivisions. Approximately 1000 peoples.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
     To remove present and future septic systems along the lake front protecting water quality and spurring economic development/infill of
