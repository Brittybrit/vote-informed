14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To provide comprehensive after school programs that focus on promoting fitness, health and discipline amongst students
    from K-12 ages. The foundation serves over 200 underprivileged families in a largely minority & multi-cultural area within
    Miami-Dade and Orange County (to initiate in 2024). Additional to after school programs, the foundation also hosts Law
    Enforcement & First Responder recognition week and field trips for terminally ill children.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     A comprehensive after school care program that will provides various educational, cultural and physical activities for
    underserved minority children ranging from grades K through 12. Thanksgiving Day bash that serves the students
    families and celebrates a diverse cultural community. The Foundation sponsors an annual field trip for students with
    terminal health conditions.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Daily After school tutoring for children from K-12; Taekwondo 3 days a week; Archery 2 days a week, High School SAT
    Prep; Dance classes for all ages 3 days a week. Self-defense classes 3 days a week. Piano classes 2 days a week.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Economically disadvantaged, K-12 youth.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

    Advanced reading capabilities and physical and mental health. We will record the children's progress in all activities and
    measure results from various local and state competitions.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Pro rata return of funds.
