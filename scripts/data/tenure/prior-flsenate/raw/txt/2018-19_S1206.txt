d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

      The Museum T.O.D. (Transit Oriented Development) mixed use development will create immediate jobs in the
     design and construction phases of the project. In addition more locals and tourists will have access and
     increased experiences in the Little Haiti neighborhood's rich Haitian culture, historic education, and the arts.

e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

      The Haitian Heritage Museum Project will service the general local population of citizens as well as visiting
     tourists upon its completion.

f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
     will be measured?
      The Museum T.O.D. (Transit Oriented Development) will enhance the walkability of the neighborhood for local
     residents and future transit users by facilitating mixed use entertainment and retail development in the area.
     Surveys will be conducted that measure the use of public transit users at museum activities, as well as
                                                    Page 3 of 4

         monitoring the attendance of neighborhood residents at the museum. In addition the project will increase or
         improve economic activity by bringing new businesses to the neighborhood, and creating jobs. New job
         creation statistics will be measured, together with a periodic economic impact report that measures economic
         outcomes of the project. Lastly, more tourists will have access and increased experiences in the Little Haiti
         neighborhood's rich Haitian culture. A record of Greater Miami Convention & Visitors Bureau's promoted
         tourist visits to Little Haiti will be maintained and compared on a quarterly basis.
