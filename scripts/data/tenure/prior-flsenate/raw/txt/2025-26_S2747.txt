4. Project/Program Description
    The funds requested are for the acquisition and retrofitting of a deployable mobile command post (CP)/Emergency
   Operations Center (EOC) which will be utilized throughout the City of Doral and/or deployed to any of the satellite cities that

   hurricane) or industrial incident.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds requested are for the acquisition and retrofitting of a deployable mobile command post (CP)/Emergency
    Operations Center (EOC) which will be utilized throughout the City of Doral and/or deployed to any of the satellite cities
    that fall under the Doral Divisional EOC. The mobile CP will contain the necessary equipment to deploy effectively and
    provide operational and logistical support during a significant critical incident, security event, man-made or natural
    disaster (i.e., hurricane) or industrial incident.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     In the event of a major disaster situation or critical incident, it is necessary for first responders and supervisors to deploy
    in order to provide an effective law enforcement response and/or relief operation. At times, especially in South Florida,
    when responding during post hurricane devastation, it is necessary to provide continuous 24-hour resources and
    assistance in various locations. This project will allow the DPD to provide a more effective response saving time and
    valuable resources.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     A mobile CP will provide the community with enhanced response capabilities which include rapid deployment, on-scene
    central command, advanced response/technology, neighborhood outreach, special event/support, and disaster response
    in a mobile workspace to better respond during emergency situation and emergencies.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The mobile Command Post will provide enhanced public safety for the community we serve and the satellite cities who
    report to the City of Doral Divisional EOC. Within the City of Doral, the proposed project will support enhanced emergency
    police response for approximately 200,000 individuals, including residents, visitors, business owners, and employees,
    ensuring a safer and more secure environment for all.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     This project will allow for the Doral Police Department to be prepared to provide extended emergency response
    throughout the City of Doral, ensuring that all personnel have access to equipment and resources during a major incident.
    The Doral Police Department will be able to reinforce our emergency response services to better help the citizens of the
    City of Doral and beyond. The benefit of a mobile Command Post can be measured by conducting a Benefit Assessment
    to include Log of deployment/incident engagement, collection of data during events (community engagement, public
    satisfaction surveys, social media analytics), review the feedback and data collected, evaluate the findings and complete
    a report with findings and recommendations during the specified time.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    In the event that the Doral Police Department is unable to utilize these funds for the purpose described herein, the funds
    will be returned.
