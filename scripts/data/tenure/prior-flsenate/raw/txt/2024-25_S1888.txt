14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Phase I is central to a wider environmental remediation and conversion of a former golf course with contaminated soil
    into a community asset, fostering recreation alongside a separately funded economic development and global innovation
    initiative. Phase I includes vital infrastructure such as solid pipe drainage, catch basins, water and sewer systems, bike
    paths, sidewalks, a wellness loop, tree planting, landscaping, electrical and various amenities.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funding for crucial enhancements to Miami Freedom Park, a City of Miami-owned property, will drive substantial
    local economic growth, including a catalyst global innovation hub and world-class soccer facility. Phase 1 focuses on key
    elements: drainage, water/sewer systems, bike paths, sidewalks, wellness loop, electrical installations, benches,
    landscaping, and amenities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This all-encompassing initiative aims to repurpose a former golf course with contaminated soil into a community asset by
    integrating essential infrastructure. As part of the broader benefit of Miami Freedom Park, it will not only foster recreation
    and economic growth but also function as a pivotal hub for global innovation in Miami and the home of Inter Miami Soccer
    Facility.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Elderly Persons, Persons with poor mental health, Persons with poor physical health, At-risk youth, Physically disabled,
    Grade school students, High school students, University/College students, General (The majority of funds will benefit no
    specific group. >800 expected target population to be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The overall Miami Freedom Park project will encompass the new Inter Miami Facility, a 58-acre green space, a new
    public administration building, entertainment facilities, retail, restaurants, and hotels, it evolves into a community asset,
    promoting recreation, economic growth, and global innovation in Miami. Utilization of park and completion of project.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
