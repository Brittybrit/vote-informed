1. Project Title          Carter Landings: A Habitat Community

2. Senate Sponsor        Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request       2/17/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    Carter Landings will be a twelve home, single-family home community in Miami Gardens, Fl. Named after President Carter,
   who was a steadfast supporter of Habitat and housing justice, the twelve homes will be built in partnership with twelve hard-
   working , low income families (at 80% or below of AMI). The land, which was a private acquisition by Habitat, requires the
   installation of roads, water and sewer connections, platting, and other pre-development Capitol expenditures. The
   appropriated dollars would be utilized for the construction of said infrastructure. Project is located at 2285 NW 170 TER,
   Miami Gardens.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    To bring water and sewer to the homesites, this making it possible for construction of the twelve homes. It will allow
    Habitat to underwrite the cost of creating an infrastructure.

   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Hiring of an engineering firm to lay out water and sewer and build the necessary roads prior to home construction.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     It will make the dream of becoming homeowners available to twelve families under 80% of Area Median Income.
