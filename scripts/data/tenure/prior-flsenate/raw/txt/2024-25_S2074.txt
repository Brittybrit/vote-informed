2. Senate Sponsor        Bryan Avila

4. Project/Program Description
    Alternative storm-water improvement project that will incorporate green infrastructure solutions to reduce the impact on
   gray infrastructure by incorporating designs that intercept runoff before it reaches paved surfaces while also filtering
   pollutants and sediments from the storm-water in the process. The proposed project will not only reduce the impacts to the
   City of Doral's gray infrastructure but will also reduce the flooding potential. The proposed project will also reduce the
   maintenance costs and construction costs of new conventional gray infrastructure. Phase 1 includes the drainage
   improvements of the storm-water system for intersections NW 56th Street and NW 102nd Ave, and NW 57th Street and
   NW 114 Ave. For the phase 1 the design will incorporate previous concrete panels as an innovative approach to better
   control, manage, and treat storm-water runoff. The proposed project will recharge the groundwater supply while reducing
   the hydroplaning potential.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will be utilized to improve the existing storm-water drainage system to mitigate and prevent flood and/or ponding,
    prevent flood related losses, and improve the level of service of the City of Doral public right-of-ways. The proposed
    project will reduce the hydroplaning effects of the corridors after heavy rainfalls by increasing the soil percolation rate
    inch/hr while filtering pollutants carried out but the stromwater runoff.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The existing stormwater system will be improved by adding new corrected pavement slopes and green infrastructure
    components such as modular precast porous concrete panels.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     An improved stormwater system that will properly handle stormwater, reduce water ponding, potential hydroplaning along
    corridors. The project will allow for proper access to adjacent communities and vehicular traffic along said corridor. Also,
    the project will improve the collection process of the stormwater runoff and its natural and cleaner infiltration to the strata.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Doral general public greater than 800 people.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit will be prevention of flooding and/or ponding. The measurement will be: no flooding / ponding
    visible during normal rain events.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Liquidated damages will be assessed based on the contract price and time loss.
