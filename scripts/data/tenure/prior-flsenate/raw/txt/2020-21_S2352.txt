11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      The funds requested are to be used to build a nature center in our local Pine Rockland Preserve. This project will provide a local learning space for the
      local schools and residents who wish to learn more about nature and it's beautiful impact on everyday lives. We are confident this project will have an
      environmental impact on all who will take advantage of learning opportunity to be provided.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      The Nature Center is anticipated to provide classes and courses in support of the local public and private schools. to enhance the learning experience,
      the center will also feature exhibit areas and outdoor classes to engage the younger population. all classes will promote environmental education and
      preservation.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Environmental education and preservation.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      Our targeted population include: elderly persons, at-risk youth, preschool students, grade school students, high school students, and University/College
      students. We expect to serve greater than 800 individuals annually.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      The project will improve physical health by providing education concerning the importance of being outdoor in nature while encouraging physical
      activity for the overall community health. This will also improve mental health through learning and feedback to be provided via participant assessment.
      We will improve agricultural production/promotion/education by providing course materials to participating individuals. This project improve quality of
      education by improving academics by allowing students to actively participate in the environment. Added benefits will be an increase to economic
      activity, increase tourism and provide economic opportunities to businesses by increased visitorship. Measured by participant feedback, on-site record
      keeping and new occupational license approvals.



 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Withholding of funds.
