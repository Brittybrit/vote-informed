9. Program Performance (if needed, include additional documentation):
a. _How will requested funds be spent? Include supporting documentation.
Currently, there is a need for Kreyol speaking attorneys and paralegals to assist residents of Miami-Dade
County fill out legal forms such as divorce forms and small claims forms. Legal Services of Greater Miami
has asked monthly basis for Kreyol speaking attorneys (volunteers) and paralegals (volunteers) to assist
them. Additionally, the law library at the Miami-Dade County Courthouse has 1 Kreyol speaking librarian
who receives requests for assistance at the library for a variety of reasons including filling out forms. The
librarian reports that there is a Spanish speaking paralegal who assists Spanish speakers with assistance at
the library.
