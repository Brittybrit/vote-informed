14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose of this project is to design, permit, construct and install a Baywalk, Kayak Launch and Boat Ramp as part of
    nature-base solutions for increasing Morningside Park resilience that will provide flood risk reduction within the park
    and/or surrounding neighborhods while providing eco-friendly recreational, economic and social benefits.Reduce
    Morningside Park costal errosion and restoration of existing natural shoreline by use of nature base solutions including
    natural, green, and hybrid infrastructure.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Design, construction and management of eco-friendly baywalk, kayak launch and boat ramp that increases the park's
    shoreline resilence to erosion, reduces flooding of useable park space and maximizes waterfront access to the public.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     No direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation for this project.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is the residents of the City of Miami's Morningside neighborhood. Morningside has a population of
    approximately 900.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     protect the general public from environmental harm by reducing shoreline erosion and flooding while providen improved
    physical health by increasing access to the waterfront for health related recreational activities. These outcomes can be
    measeued by documenting use baywalk, kayak launch and boat ramp, rate of shoreline erosion and severity of flooding
    both pre and post constuction of improvements.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables or performance measures should subject the City to an audit and depending on the results
    of the audit the City could be liable for sanctions up to revocation of funding.
