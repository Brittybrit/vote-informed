11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      The funds will be utilized to stimulate economic development in a quickly growing region of Pasco County with access via a major
      highway (I-75) and to provide the Tampa Bay Region with a large-scale multi-purpose event center that is hardened to be a hurricane
      shelter to serve residents evacuating around the state.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      The event center host events ranging from high school and college graduation ceremonies, concerts, trade shows, and athletic
      events. It will be built and utilized to accommodate almost any event requiring a large venue. The facility will also be hardened to
      provide emergency sheltering for evacuation of citizens.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Citizens will benefit from the center in that it will be located in an area easily accessed by the Tampa Bay community, provide space
      for both cultural and business related events, and be hardened to provide emergency sheltering for the evacuation of citizens.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      The Tampa Bay region and citizens evacuating for sheltering.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      Completion of the construction of the facility.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Failure to meet the deliverables would result in all state funds being returned.
