1.   Project Title          Brooksville Sewer Rehab Phase IV

2.   Senate Sponsor Wilton Simpson

3.   Date of Request 11/23/2019

4.   Project/Program Description
     The proposed Sewer Rehabilitation Project Phase IV, intends to correct aging infrastructure deficiencies in the City’s sanitary sewer
     collection system. Much of this important infrastructure is in need of repair and rehabilitation to correct structural defects, eliminate
     sanitary sewer overflows and backups caused by inflow and infiltration, and reduce the amount of water processed and treated at the
     City’s water treatment facility. Inflow is surface water being introduced in the collection system, eg., water from storm gutters, etc.
     Infiltration is groundwater leaking into the collection system.

11. Program Performance
 a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
     Rehabilitating sewer mains, laterals, manholes will eliminate costly and unwanted sewer backups to homes, businesses and lift
     stations. Cost savings in maintenance, wastewater treatment, will reduce future expenditures, and may save money to customers in
     billing.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
     Construction including open cut, directional boring, relining sewer main piping, manholes, cleanouts installed/replaced, lateral lines
     repaired/replaced.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
     Will eliminate costly and unwanted sewer backups to homes, businesses and lift stations. Will also minimize sewer overflows getting
     into stormwater conveyances, creeks, and other water bodies frequented by fish and wildlife, thus preserving and protecting animal
     life and the environment.




 d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
     Residential communities, RV parks, and local businesses within the City of Brooksville. Individuals expected to be served, including
     visitors amount to 16,708.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
     A reduction in costs associated with processing and treating wastewater, such as chlorination, electricity, wear and tear on pumps
     and equipment, and staffing for upkeep and maintenance. Monitor flows into treatment plant compared with previous, and costs
     associated with equipment, chemicals compared with previous. Monitor areas prone to sewer backups and overflows, and compare
     before and after effects.




  f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
     penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
     Withhold payment for non-service.
