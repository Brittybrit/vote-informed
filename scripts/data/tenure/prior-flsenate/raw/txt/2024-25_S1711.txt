1. Project Title           Virginia Gardens - Phase II Central Drainage Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        11/01/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Installation of a new storm-water drainage system to prevent storm-water ponding and flooding. The project is part of the
   Village's storm water management initiative to alleviate surface runoff, water pollution, as well as the likelihood of soil
   erosion in the central section section of the Village and will allow for proper access in the vicinity during heavy rain events.
