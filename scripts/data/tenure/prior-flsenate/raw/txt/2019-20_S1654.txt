1. Title of Project: Hernando Schools Vocational Program
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 02/07/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   Hernando is the only county in the Tampa Bay Region that does not have a stand-alone vocational school. In 2013, a
   $1.5 million appropriation helped to increase vocational offerings. Programs in Welding, HVAC, Automotive,
   Cybersecurity, Manufacturing, CNA, Cosmetology, ServeSafe and Life Insurance Marketing programs were created
   and located in available space on high school campuses. Hernando's Suncoast Technical Education Center, Florida's
   newest technical school, was created and achieved accreditation without a stand-alone building. Last year, the
   school district had 1,224 students who earned an industry certification, further demonstrating strong support for
   Career and Technical training within our community. The Hernando School District has a proven track record of
   success in Career and Technical Education and we believe there is broad support and community advocates for a
   vocational school that will serve both high school and adult students interested in learning a trade.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        The funds will go to the construction and start up of Hernando's first stand-alone vocational school. The school
        will be utilized by adults and high school students for training in specific trades.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        In addition to the current post-secondary offerings, Hernando County School District will look to create a full
        menu of courses within the first 3 years of operation that can be accessed by both populations. Proper
        wraparound programs available to adults will also be added.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Vocational Training to high school students and adults interested in learning a specific trade.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        According to the 2013-2017 American Community Survey, our County has 18,176 citizens without a high school
        diploma, and 53,759 citizens who only have a high school diploma. This would be the target population for this
        project. In addition to the Hernando County high school students who prefer a career and technical path in lieu
        of a college degree.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
