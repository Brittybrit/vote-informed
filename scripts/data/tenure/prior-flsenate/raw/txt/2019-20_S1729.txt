1. Title of Project: Crandon Blvd. Pedestrian / Bicycle Safety
2. Senate Sponsor: Jose Rodriguez
3. Date of Submission: 02/07/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   Miami-Dade County constructed the Rickenbacker Causeway after World War II to connect the mainland to Virginia
   Key, and Key Biscayne. The southern segment through the middle of Key Biscayne was named Crandon Blvd. The
   Miami-Dade County Public Works Department, from 1969 to 1970, reconstructed Crandon Blvd. south of Crandon
   Park into a four-lane divided parkway to ease congestion. This widening was accomplished within the 120 ft right-
   of-way corridor previously reserved by the county for an earlier abandoned 1950’s plan to widen Crandon Blvd. and
   build a causeway linking Key Biscayne and Key Largo. There have been 192 crashes on Crandon Blvd. during the five
   year period from 2013 to 1017. 19% of all crashes involved pedestrians and/or bicycles. This safety project proposes
   a number of measures that will help to increase safety for pedestrians and bicyclists.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         Increased safety for pedestrians and bicyclists.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         Requested funds will be spent on the reorganization of regulatory signage, Installation of pedestrian count
         down signals where not in place, shortening pedestrian walking distance with curb extensions, and installation
         of cycle tracks.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         A safer scenic roadway for the citizens.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         All residents of Key Biscayne, and visitors to the Bill Baggs State Park.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         The project will be well documented by the project manager. Photographs will be taken throughout the
         process documenting the project. At the end of the project, all as-built plans will be certified by the certified
         project engineer. Project manager will provide signed statement by the certified engineer indicating
         construction has been completed in accordance with approved designs.
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
