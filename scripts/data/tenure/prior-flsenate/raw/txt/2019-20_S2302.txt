4. Project/Program Description:
   The City of Crystal River is making efforts to get multi-modal transportation to the majority of the tourist areas
   within the cty limits. This is the reason for the city to request the first phase of the Fort Island Trail Project. The
   Plantation Resort and a proposed new RV community will be tied to Three Sister Springs Refuge and downtown. In
   turn it will tie the only city golf course to the multi-modal trail for the citizens of Crystal River. The city has funded
   the engineering and permitting.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         The City of Crystal River is making efforts to get multi-modal transportation to the majority of the tourist areas
         within the city limits. This is the reason for the city to request the first phase of the Fort Island Trail Project. The
         Plantation Resort and a proposed new RV community will be tied to Three Sister Springs Refuge and
         downtown. In turn it will tie the only city golf course to the multi-modal trail for the citizens of Crystal River.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         Improved access to tourists and residents of Crystal River and Citrus County.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         This is a public use multi-use path for residents and tourists
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         Citrus County and Crystal River is home to tens of thousands of tourists annualy from all ages and
         demographics.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         Crystal River has a growing tourism population and this connector to the only resort and golf course in the city
         should increase the resort's viability and make it easier for other tourist to utilize. The city will monitor resort
         occupancy and golf course usage.
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         Reversion of State Funds.
