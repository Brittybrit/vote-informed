2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

4. Project/Program Description
    The Downtown Doral Arts District will be the City of Doral’s blueprint to creating safe, convenient, and sustainable
   transportation options that meet the needs of the community and increases accessibility. The proposed roadway and
   infrastructures improvements shall include pedestrian connectivity, connectivity with the City’s bicycle network, on-street
   parking, lighting, landscaping, signing and pavement markings, signage, traffic calming improvements, stormwater
   improvements, harmonization with adjacent properties, traffic and transit analysis, and NW 84th Avenue shall include a
   Bus/Trolley route/lane.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Complete Streets Program Adaptive Re-use of the Doral Arts District between NW 58th Street - NW 54th Street and
    between NW 87 Ave- NW 79 Ave. The Project includes roadway reconstruction, new sidewalks, parallel parking,
    storm-water drainage, and additional trolley stop, bike lanes and lighting.

   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds will be spent for the construction phase of the adaptive Re-use of Doral Arts District between NW 58 St and NW
    54 St and between NW 87 Ave and NW 79 Ave.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The Project includes roadway reconstruction, new sidewalks, parallel parking, storm-water drainage, bike lanes (where
    possible), and street lighting. This will increase pedestrian safety and will also serve as a mitigation initiative to help
    prevent flood related losses.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    General (The funds will be benefiting no specific group). Doral residents and commercial area constituents and clients.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     Prevent/minimize flooding and ponding along roadway segment. Installation of street lighting and sidewalk installation.
    Flooding and ponding are not visible during major storms. Increase light for visibility and pedestrian safety. Minimize
    congestion by roadway widening and provide alternative transportation via bike paths. Decrease of traffic congestion
    and increase of bicycle traffic. Adaptive Re-Use is to improve the conditions of the Doral Arts District to increase
    tourism for locals.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     Liquidated damages will be included in the contract documents. Liquidated damages will be based on construction
    cost.
