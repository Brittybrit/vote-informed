4. Project/Program Description
    Dan Marino Foundation FloridaReady AI is an app using Artificial Intelligence (AI)-powered, human-like avatars focused on
   guidance counseling for Florida's high school students. The American School Counselor Association recommends a 250-
   to-1 ratio of students to school counselors, Florida's ratio in 23-24 was 432-to-1. FloridaReady AI integrates an assessment
   tool analyzing students' unique needs and skills to provide customized advice, and to support students in navigating their
   educational journeys. Through engaging and natural conversations with the Florida Ready AI, students will explore
   academic pathways tailored to their individual aspirations and capabilities. Leveraging AI technology, FloridaReady AI,
   builds upon the original success of FloridaReady (job interview app) to not only facilitate access to essential guidance
   services but also empowers students to take charge of their professional futures, fostering a culture of self-driven
   achievement across the state of Florida.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to equip Florida’s high school students with personalized access to academic guidance, and job interview
    training to help them make informed decisions about their educational and career choices. Supporting this goal,
    FloridaReady AI offers ongoing and individualized support to high school students as they plan and advance along their
    chosen academic and career paths, ensuring they have the guidance they need at every step of their journey.


    This initiative represents a significant investment in the youth of Florida, preparing them to contribute meaningfully to their
    communities and the broader economy by enhancing their independence and future prospects.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Comprehensive, research-informed activities will be conducted to guide the development of the FloridaReady AI
    platform, ensuring it addresses the academic and career needs of Florida’s high school students. These activities involve
    in-depth studies of best practices in career and skills assessment, academic counseling, and career guidance to inform
    the AI's design. Researchers, developers, and education professionals will collaborate to create models aligned with
    Florida’s educational standards and labor market data, ensuring the platform’s outputs reflect real-world opportunities.
    Additionally, user engagement initiatives, including focus groups, pilot testing, and community workshops, will be
    conducted to collect feedback and refine the app's functionality. This process will establish a solid foundation for
    delivering personalized academic and career guidance services once the app is fully operational.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Academic Guidance: Providing tailored support to help students identify appropriate courses, plan their educational
    journey, and align their academic objectives with future career goals.
    Skills Assessment: Measuring and tracking essential competencies to pinpoint areas for development, ensuring students
    build the proficiencies required for both academic and professional success. Career Assessment: Offering in-depth
    evaluations of each student’s interests and aptitudes, directing them toward career pathways that match their unique skill
    sets.
    Professional Communication Skills Training: Delivering targeted instruction on effective interpersonal communication and
    interview techniques, enabling students to present themselves confidently in academic settings and the workforce.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population: Dan Marino Foundation FloridaReady AI app will be distributed to a minimum of 50% of Florida’s
    Public High Schools
    Florida public high school students aged 16 and older, including neurotypical students and those receiving exceptional
    education services. A minimum of 3,000 students to download the Dan Marino Foundation FloridaReady AI app.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     Benefits: Distribution of a mobile app offering academic and career guidance to Florida’s high school population
    supporting individualized learning pathways.
    Students gain access to career and skills assessments, helping align their abilities and interests with suitable career
    paths. Foster engagement with guidance resources. Clearer understanding of academic and career requirements for
    Florida workforce.

    Methodology:
    Measure downloads, assessment completion rates and access to guidance resources through app analytics to determine
    the number of students actively using and benefiting from the platform;
    Pre, Post self-reported skills assessment and career readiness;
    Surveys and focus groups collecting qualitative feedback from students, educators, and guidance counselors to measure
    satisfaction, perceived improvements in readiness, and overall effectiveness of the model.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    Possible future funding adjustment.
