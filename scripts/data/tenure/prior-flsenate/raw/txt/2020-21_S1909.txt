4.   Project/Program Description
     Construction of Riverwalk Phase II project will enhance the downtown waterfront area and increase connectivity through a system of
     pathways linking existing public facilities, including parks, multi-use trails and public parking areas. Phase II construction will include
     development of a Permatrek concrete pier/walkway, two pedestrian bridges, lighting, landscaping, and installation of amenities including
     gateways, interpretive signage, benches, trash receptacles, and electrical outlets.
     Requested funds will be matched with $1.7 million in RESTORE Act funding received, $1.0 million of local funding, and $500,000 in
     donated property rights.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      Construction of the Riverwalk Phase II project will enhance the downtown waterfront area and increase connectivity through a system
      of pathways linking existing public facilities, including parks, multi-use trails, and public parking areas. Phase II construction will
      include development of a Permatrek concrete pier/walkway, two pedestrian bridges, lighting, landscaping, and installation of
      amenities including gateways, interpretive signage, benches, trash receptacles, and electrical outlets.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Completion of the Riverwalk will provide infrastructure that will be open to the general public for activities such as walking, strolling,
      jogging, sightseeing, shopping, restaurant going, and many others.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      The Riverwalk will be open to the public for recreational and transportation purposes. It will serve as a hub for local tourism related
      activities, connecting multiple businesses along the Kings Bay shoreline and downtown area.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      General (The majority of funds will benefit no specific group).
      Other - approximately 340,000 tourists and 100,000 event goers annually.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      Enrich cultural experience - The number of visitors can be directly measured.
      Enhance/preserve/improve environment for fish and wildlife quality - The number of visitors can be directly measured.
      Improve transportation conditions - The number of visitors can be directly measured.
      Increase or improve economic activity - The increase in taxable value and number of hotel stays can be directly measured.
      Increase tourism - The increase in taxable value and number of hotel stays can be directly measured.



 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      None.
