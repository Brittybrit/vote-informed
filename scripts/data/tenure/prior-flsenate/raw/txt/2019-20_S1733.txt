1. Title of Project: Miami-Dade Veterans Treatment Court
2. Senate Sponsor: Jose Rodriguez
3. Date of Submission: 02/07/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   Miami-Dade Veterans Treatment Court (VTC) is a problem-solving court designed to offer treatment in lieu of
   incarceration for veterans whose substance use disorders and/or mental illness appear to have contributed
   significantly to their arrest. VTC seeks to provide a means to divert eligible veteran participants from the traditional
   criminal justice system, offer support and rehabilitation through comprehensive substance abuse and/or mental
   health treatment, education and vocational programs, and community resource referrals for housing and
   transportation under court supervision.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        The funds will be used to support the MDVTC. This specialized court is intended to provide rehabilitative
        services for veterans who have been charged with non-violent crimes and have been identified as suffering
        from serious mental health conditions or substance use disorders. Veterans will be diverted from the
        traditional justice system and receive help with reintegration into the community.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

         The funds will support VTC's general daily operations; assessment of potential participants; development of
         case plan; placement in emergency shelter if needed; outpatient or residential treatment; ensure proper wrap
         around services through the VA or other community agencies; ensure frequent alcohol and other drug testing;
         and training for VTC team members to maintain current court practices.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         Intake screening and assessment, intensive case management, random alcohol and drug testing, emergency
         shelter, residential and/or outpatient treatment, and staff training.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         The justice-involved veteran population within Miami-Dade County. VTC is anticipated to serve 50-75 veterans.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         *Provide access to services that may improve quality of life by stabilization mental health: to be measured via
         evidence-based screening and assessment tools                  *Enhance individuals economic self-sufficiency: to
         be measured via monitoring compliance with VA vocational rehabilitation, work programs, and housing
         programs                                                                   *Reduce recidivism: to be measured via
         a risk-need-responsivity model and follow-up                                     *Reduce substance abuse: to be
         measured via random, consistent, and frequent alcohol and drug testing, and when appropriate, medically
         assisted treatment for veterans with opioid addiction
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         Financial consequences will be administrated to contract service providers for failure to provide quantity or
         quality services, in compliance with protocols, procedures and contract terms established by the court.
