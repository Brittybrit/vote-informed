1. Title of Project: 2022 Special Olympics USA Games
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 02/20/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   The Special Olympics USA Games will be coming to Florida in 2022, for the first time in history. This event will
   showcase the talents of over 4,000 Special Olympics athletes from all 50 states and the Caribbean. It will also be the
   largest humanitarian event in Florida's history with a projected economic impact of $69+ million to the State of
   Florida. The 2022 USA Games will position Florida as a leader in the "inclusion movement," provide health
   screenings to thousands of individuals with intellectual disabilities, and showcase Florida as a disability friendly
   destination. Total cost of putting on the event is $21 million over 4 years with approximately 85 percent coming
   from local and private sources.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        Provide operational funding to produce the largest Special Olympics USA Games in history. It will also be the
        largest humanitarian event in Florida's history. The 2022 Games will position Florida as a leader in the
        "inclusion movement," showcase Florida as a disability friendly destination, deliver a projected economic
        benefit of $69+ million to Florida, and provide individuals with intellectual disabilities with thousands of
        healthcare screenings and physician referrals at the event.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        FY 2019-20 funds will be used primarily for foundational work (e.g. planning, marketing, private fundraising,
        and logistics) required to execute an event of this magnitude which is expected to attract 125,000 people.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Please see above.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          All Floridians will benefit from the positive media exposure, contribution to the "inclusion movement," and
          direct economic benefit. In addition, approximately 4,000 individuals with intellectual and developmental
          disabilities and their families will benefit from their direct participation in the games and health screenings that
          will be provided.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
          - Improve physical health: Health screenings for individuals with intellectual disabilities and referral of the
          estimated 40 percent who will require subsequent medical attention in one or more areas. Healthcare
          professionals administer the screenings and direct athletes to physicians and specialists. The referrals are
          quantified and tracked and physician visits confirmed. Data will be entered into the Special Olympics Healthy
          Athlete Software (HAS) system which captures screening and referral data. - Improve mental health: The
          Special Olympics Strong Minds (emotional well-being) program will be provided to athletes. The HAS system
          will track the number of participants. - Improve/increase economic activity and tourism: 67,000 total hotel
          room nights and direct economic impact projected by the Central Florida Sports Commission (CFSC) of $69+
          million. Special Olympics will track participation data and will work with the CFSC to validate the projected
          economic impact.
    f.    What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
          penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
          Plan of corrective action with reduced payment for uncorrected deficiency.
