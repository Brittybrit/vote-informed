4. Project/Program Description
    Th primary purpose of this project is to Upgrade our technology infrastructure, specifically focusing on enhancing our
   communication systems. This includes the procurement of radios that can significantly improve real-time communication
   and coordination. The need for these radios stems from the necessity to ensure reliable and efficient communication,
   especially in critical situations where clear and immediate information dissemination is vital. Upgrading our communication
   infrastructure will not only enhance operational effectiveness but also ensure that we remain responsive and connected
   across various settings.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds requested for radios will be used to enhance communication capabilities within our police department. By
    procuring advanced and reliable radios, we aim to improve operational efficiency, ensure secure and clear
    communication during incidents, and enhance officer safety. This upgrade will facilitate better coordination in response
    efforts, ultimately leading to a more effective law enforcement presence in the community.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     These activities will enhance communication effectiveness and improve overall safety and response times within the
    community.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Upgraded radios will enable our police department to deliver more efficient, responsive, and community-oriented services
    to the public.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The project targets police officers of the Virginia Gardens Police Department and the residents of Virginia Gardens,
    Miami, Florida. It aims to upgrade communication capabilities, enhancing operational efficiency and emergency response
    times. This initiative will directly benefit about 30 police officers and approximately 2,500 residents, leading to improved
    safety and communication within the community.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The project aims to enhance communication capabilities within the police department, resulting in improved response
    times, enhanced safety, and more effective emergency resolution. The success of the project will be measured through
    various metrics, including Response Time, Incident Resolution Rate, Communication Success Rate, Officer Feedback
    and other metrics.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    Revocation of funding of Appropriation at end of Fiscal Year.
