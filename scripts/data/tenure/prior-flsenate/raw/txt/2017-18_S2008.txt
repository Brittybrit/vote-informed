9. Program Performance (if needed, include additional documentation):
a. How will requested funds be spent? Include supporting documentation.
Waterline Extension Project that consists or a 38-mile (20,000 linear feet) system to address the water
needs of approximately 1,500 well-dependent homes (50% of properties). This project was developed for
variousreasons; well systems in Pinecrest are vulnerable to saltwater intrusion; a lack of fire protection
via hydrants; documented cases of contamination of several wells; and the inability of properties without
generators toaccess water after hurricane disasters. Between 2007 and 2009, the Village designed the
entire Waterline Extension Project and completed Phase 1 and 2 which brought potable water to 549
homes. This project consists ofthe installation of 8-, 12- and 16-inch water mains and extending new
service lines from the water main to the lot lines, in accordance with Miami-Dade County Water and
Sewer Department (WASD) standards. It alsoincludes fire hydrants to provide fire protection. Each phase
of the system is conveyed to WASD, which will operate and maintain it. The amount requested by the
Village is to continue structing/installing water mainextensions, providing the residents and businesses of
Pinecrest
