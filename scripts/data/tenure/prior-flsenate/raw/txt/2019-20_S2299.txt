1. Title of Project: Multimodal Transit Station
2. Senate Sponsor: Jose Rodriguez
3. Date of Submission: 02/20/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   To build a multimodal station as a regional park and ride for county transit services. The park & ride will provide
   parking to support Miami-Dade County's bus rapid transit system on the county's transitway that will transport
   residents as far south as Homestead to the S. Dadeland Metrorail Station, which lacks sufficient parking to support
   the S. Dade commuter ridership. The park & ride will provide approx. 500 parking spaces, on-demand service drop-
   off/pick-up, and other amenities.

11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        To build a multimodal station as a regional park and ride for county transit services. The park & ride will provide
        parking to support Miami-Dade County's bus rapid transit system on the county's transitway that will transport
        residents as far south as Homestead to the S. Dadeland Metrorail Station, which lacks sufficient parking to
        support the S. Dade commuter ridership. The park & ride will provide approx. 500 parking spaces, on-demand
        service drop-off/pick-up, and other amenities.
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        Proposed park & ride facility supports the county's transit system for S. Dade residents from Homestead to
        Palmetto Bay, and also supports the village's efforts to reduce traffic congestion and commute times.
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Access to transit, traffic reduction, and shorter commuting time in the heavily congested Miami-Dade County.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        Our target population: elderly persons, jobless persons, economically disadvantaged persons, homeless, high
        school students, University/college students, and the general public. We expect greater than 800 people will be
        served.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
      will be measured?
        We expect to help protect the general public from harm, by enabling less carbon dioxide emissions to be
        expelled by reducing the number of cars in circulation. We expect to improve transportation conditions by
        providing shorter commutes from Homestead to downtown Miami. We expect to increase or improve
        economic activity, increase tourism, create specific immediate job opportunities, and enhance specific
        individuals economic self-sufficiency. Increased access to jobs will bring more businesses and tourism
        opportunities.
