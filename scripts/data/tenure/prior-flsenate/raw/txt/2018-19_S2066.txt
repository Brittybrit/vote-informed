11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

            The goal is to expand and remodel the interior of an existing building that the Boys and Girls Club of Citrus
          County (BGCCC) currently occupies in Inverness to meet ADA requirements. This goal will enable the local
          young people, especially those who need the BGCCC programs most, to reach their full potentials as
          productive, caring, and responsible citizens. This will be accomplihed because we will then be able to increase
          the children we serve from approximately 65 children to over 150 a day during the school year and double our
          summer camp capacity from 150 to 300 children. We are currently at capacity during the day and have waiting
          lists for our summer programs. This will help meet the needs of working parents and families that are in need of
          a safe place for their children before and after school.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Provide a world-class Club Experience that assures success is within reach of every young person who enters
          our doors, with all members on track to graduate from high school and plan for the future, demonstrating good
          character and citizenship, and living a healthy lifestyle. Our programs cover: HEALTHY LIFESTYLE CHOICES -
          EDUCATION & CAREER - THE ARTS - FITNESS & RECREATION - CHARACTER & LEADERSHIP DEVELOPMENT. All of
          these programs will be able to be enhanced with the improved environment of the remodeled facility.

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          To facilitate the expansion of the Boys and Girls club program services to local children.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          This target population are the children on the east side of Citrus County. We expect that we will be able to
         expand our services and double our capacity from 65 children to approximately 150 per day during the school
         year and 300 children in our summer programs from 165.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          The expected benefit of this project is to reach more children and families in need of the BGCCC which are
         currently going unserved. The renovations will expand and improve the use of the building and meet newer
         ADA requirements. The measurement of our success from this expansion will be judged by the number of
         adiitional children we serve while working with the school system to monitor their school attendance and
         educational progress.
