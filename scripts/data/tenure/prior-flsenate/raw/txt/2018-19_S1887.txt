11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           The project will bring healthcare resources to east Pasco County - in a local area where services are limited.
          The facility will provide urgent care, physician offices, rehab facilities and a wellness center all available to the
          public.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Urgent care operations to meet emergency medical needs of the community. Physicians on-site to provide
          both well and sick care to area residents, rehab facilities not currently available in the area and a wellness
          center to promote physical fitness through regular exersize programs with professionally managed and
          maintained equipment.

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          Medical care - urgent and regular visits. Wellness training and facility to perform necessary physical activities
         for maintaining health and re-hab activities as needed.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          The immediate area impacted represents a population of nearly 15,000 with the outer ring population
         encompassing another 25,000 who may use the facility. The immediate area is drmatically underserved in
         healthcare.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          The major benefit is providing healthcare and wellness opportunities to the community. The facility's use in
         both patient visits and wllness center daily usage statistics will serve as success measurements.
