13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The AERO (Advanced Emergency Response & Operations) Command Unit is designed to enhance situational
    awareness, crisis management, and community engagement through cutting-edge technology and rapid deployment
    capabilities. This specialized command vehicle will serve as a mobile hub for emergency operations, drone response,
    and multi-agency coordination, ensuring a more effective and proactive approach to public safety.

   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Rapid deployment to critical incidents such as natural disasters, search-and-rescue operations, hostage situations,
    and major accidents. Provides a centralized command center for real-time decision-making, improving coordination
    between responding agencies. Advanced Drone Deployment & Aerial Surveillance. Serves as a drone launch and
    monitoring platform for aerial reconnaissance, missing person searches, traffic incident management, and tactical
    response. Equips officers with real-time intelligence from aerial footage, improving operational awareness in dangerous
    or complex situations.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Deployed at community events, the AERO Unit serves as an interactive engagement platform, allowing residents to
    learn about drone technology, public safety initiatives, and emergency preparedness. Strengthens trust between law
    enforcement and the community by demonstrating transparency and innovation in modern policing.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The AERO (Advanced Emergency Response & Operations) Command Unit is designed to serve a broad spectrum of
    individuals and groups within the community, including General Public – Residents, visitors, and businesses who
    benefit from enhanced emergency response, crisis management, and public safety initiatives. Emergency & Crisis
    Victims – Individuals affected by disasters, missing persons, high-risk incidents, and large-scale emergencies. Law
    Enforcement & First Responders – Officers, firefighters, EMS personnel, and emergency management teams who rely
    on AERO for real-time intelligence, aerial surveillance, and coordinated response efforts. Community Event
    Participants – Attendees at public events, festivals, and educational outreach programs where AERO is deployed for
    engagement and safety monitoring. Vulnerable Populations – Missing persons, individuals with special needs, or those
    affected by natural disasters and hazardous incidents.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The AERO (Advanced Emergency Response & Operations) Command Unit is designed to significantly enhance public
    safety, emergency response, and community engagement through innovative technology and coordinated operations.
    The key benefits and expected outcomes include:
    1. Faster Emergency Response & Crisis Management.
    2. Enhanced Situational Awareness & Officer Safety.
    3. Increased Community Engagement & Public Trust.
    4. Improved Inter-agency Collaboration & Operational Efficiency.
    5. Reduction in Crime & Enhanced Public Safety.

    To ensure the AERO Command Unit meets its objectives, the following performance metrics and evaluation methods
    will be used: Compare emergency response times before and after AERO implementation. Track number of drone-
    assisted operations and cases where drone footage contributed to successful resolutions.
