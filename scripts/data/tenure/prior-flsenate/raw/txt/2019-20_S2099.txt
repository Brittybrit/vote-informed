1. Title of Project: Palmetto Bay Drainage Sub-Basin #61 Construction
2. Senate Sponsor: Jose Rodriguez
3. Date of Submission: 02/22/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   Palmetto Bay is requesting funding for the construction and construction support of storm-water improvements to
   its sub-basin #61. Improvements include building, additional catch basins, manholes, culverts & ex-filtration to
   connect the catch basins within sub-basin #61. Improvements will reduce pollutant load contribution from 3 main
   pollutants from the sub-basin into Biscayne bay Aquifer, control flooding during storms such as Hurricane Irma and
   improve surface water quality.

11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        Palmetto Bay is requesting funding for the construction and construction support of stormwater improvements
        to its sub-basin #61. Improvements include building, additional catch basins, manholes, culverts & exfiltration
        to connect the catch basins within sub-basin #61. Improvements will reduce pollutant load contribution from 3
        main pollutants from the sub-basin into Biscayne bay Aquifer, control flooding during storms such as Hurricane
        Irma and improve surface water quality.
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        Not applicable.
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Not applicable.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        The target population is residents of Southern Miami-Dade. Residents traversing south to north along our roads
        will benefit. Our local residents will greatly benefit by no longer worrying about flooded roads to exit or enter
        their homes and neighborhood.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
      will be measured?
        With this funding the Village of Palmetto Bay will enhance/preserve/improve environmental or fish and wildlife
        quality by mitigating the environmental hazards associated with stormwater runoff. We will also protect the
        general public from harm by controlling flooding and other hazards associated with stormwater surges. We will
        additionally improve stormwater management, groundwater quality and surface water quality by reducing the
        loading pollutants into Biscayne Bay and control flooding and other hazards associated with stormwater surges.
