1. Title of Project: Brooksville - Replacement of Fire Truck and Equipment
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 01/30/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   This project is designed to improve a City of Brooksville fire station by replacing a 2000 American Lafrance fire truck.
   Several years ago, the company "American Lafrance" went out of business making most of the fire truck parts for
   replacement, service and maintenance extremely difficult or virtually impossible to obtain. The 19 year old
   apparatus needs to be replaced as we are working to provide an Advanced life support engine for the City and
   surrounding county areas. The fire station also has a failing Mako Air Compressor and cascade system designed to
   fill self contained breathing apparatus for firefighting personnel. This machine is serviced and tested each year and
   the department has been advised that the compressor has lost ability to maintain necessary pressures consistent
   with filling air bottles at high pressures. FIrefighter's use the system to fill self contained breathing air cylinders for
   use in structural firefighting. The total cost of this project is $347,000.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        The ability to improve the fire department emergency response to the community and surrounding areas.
        Firefighting personnel will be able to actively fill self contained breathing apparatus bottles using a new Mako
        Air Compressor designed to safely perform this work.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        Firefighting personnel routinely respond to fires, emergency medical incidents, hazards and emergency
        management activity within the City limits and the surrounding county areas. Fire personnel will be available 24
        hours a day seven days a week 365 days a year to perform emergency services.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        They will be provided with firefighting personnel that are adequately prepared with proper equipment and
        ready to serve the citizens with firefighting services.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        The City of Brooksville has approximately 8000 residents and 900 business occupancies in the city. The
        department also has an Automatic/Mutual aid agreement with Hernando County to assist in the surrounding
        areas of the City.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
