11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        The purpose of the funds being requested is to build the Richloam Museum, which upon completion will be a
        unique educational opportunity for all of Central Florida and the state. The museum will serve to preserve the
        history of the early settlers of Central Florida, especially African Americans, that worked in the turpentine
        industry. The museum will also educate youth on historical tasks through hands-on workshops. Richloam
        Museum will provide scholarships to local underprivileged students.
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        The completion of rebuilding the cucumber packing house, train depot, ice house, and smoke house.
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        The museum will also educate youth on historical tasks through hands on workshops. Richloam Museum will
        provide scholarships to local underprivileged students.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        Polling shows overwhelming support within the immediate community and the greater Central Florida Region.
        There were also over 70,000 visitors to the location in the 2018-2019 year. We have acquired a letter of
        support from Hernando County Zoning and Building Department to build the museum.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
      will be measured?
        Enrich cultural experience - the measure of benefit will be how many students and families are able to visit and
        learn essential skills and the history of Old Florida. Improve agricultural education- he land around the museum
        would be used to raise crops and animals and to teach the visitors the skills needed to survive in early Florida.
        Improve quality of education- the measure of benefit would be the number of students and families that would
        visit and learn and experience life in early Florida. Improve environmental- recreating the original lifestyle in
        the Florida flatwoods will bring more agriculture and forestry care to the region, thereby enhancing and
