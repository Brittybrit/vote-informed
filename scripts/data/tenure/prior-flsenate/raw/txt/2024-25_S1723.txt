4. Project/Program Description
    Funding for this project will enable the implementation of stormwater management measures, including the construction of
   drainage and roadway enhancements along Forrest Drive, extending from NW 36 Street to South Royal Poinciana Blvd in
   the northbound direction. This initiative is aimed at mitigating water pollution risks, enhancing the road's resilience to
   flooding, and addressing the growing challenges posed by climate change. Not only will the general public's well-being be
   improved, but this project will also open doors to increased economic development opportunities within the City of Miami
   Springs.This project involves the revitalization of the Forrest Drive corridor, focusing on enhancing stormwater drainage
   infrastructure, while also prioritizing pedestrian, bicycle, and vehicular safety. Additionally, it aims to introduce landscape
   improvements. The project will assess the current conditions and employ green infrastructure for stormwater management.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funding for this project will enable the implementation of stormwater management measures, including the construction
    of drainage and roadway enhancements along Forrest Drive, extending from NW 36 Street to South Royal Poinciana
    Blvd in the northbound direction. This initiative is aimed at mitigating water pollution risks, enhancing the road's resilience
    to flooding, and addressing the growing challenges posed by climate change. The main goal of this project is the
    redevelopment of this corridor to accommodate pedestrian, bicycle, and vehicular activity and to address flooding issues
    and safety along the Forrest Drive area.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Contracted activity and services under this funding include surveying, demolition, clearing, geotechnical services, design,
    permitting, cost estimating and construction of drainage and landscape improvements.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This is a much-needed public safety, landscape and drainage improvement project as documented in several emails and
    public meetings with support by residents and elected officials. This project involves the revitalization of the Forrest Drive
    corridor, focusing on enhancing stormwater drainage infrastructure, while also prioritizing pedestrian, bicycle, and
    vehicular safety. Additionally, it aims to introduce landscape improvements. The project will assess the current conditions
    and employ green infrastructure for stormwater management. It will also enhance sidewalk connectivity to ensure
    pedestrian safety, aligning with the recommendations outlined in our Citywide Bicycle and Pedestrian Mobility Study and
    Master Plan. Furthermore, landscape designs will be implemented to reduce runoff pollution and safeguard roadways
    from erosion.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The target population of this projects are local residents along Forrest Drive, as well as pedestrian and bicyclists from the
    City and neighboring areas and visitors that will benefit with the recreational and safety features of the project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefits of this project is aimed at mitigating water pollution risks, enhancing the road's resilience to
    flooding, and addressing the growing challenges posed by climate change. As well as safety to pedestrians and
    bicyclists, with added drainage improvements to an area prone to flood. The methodology for this project includes
    structural as well as non-structural components to provide a comprehensive approach. Stormwater management
    combined with landscape improvements and public education will be the hallmark of this project success.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The contract will have standard language that will include liquidated damage, debarment and withhold payment.
