1. Title of Project: Youth Agricultural Development Center

4. Project/Program Description:
    This project will focus on youth agricultural development. Our goal is to provide leadership opportunities to youth
    through agricultural and environmental experiences. We plan to give youth the chance to learn specific agricultural
    skills that can later be applied to agriculture and science career paths. The Youth Agricultural Development Center
    will serve as the catalyst that promotes leadership in our youth.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           We will build an Agricultural Youth Development Center to provide opportunities for youth to develop
          leadership qualities and a deeper understanding and appreciation of the agriculture industry.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Youth will be given the opportunity to participate in raising livestock which is an after school activity that
          teaches teamwork, responsibility, and environmental stewardship. The animals raised would be shown
          nationwide in order to develop and understanding of varying American cultures and agricultural perspectives.
          The youth involved would be able to mentor other members of the community who are interested in
          developing a deeper understanding of the agriculture industry and members of our community will mentor
          adults and youth in best practices related to environmental stewardship and agriculture.

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          Raising livestock and fruits and vegetables, after school programs to be offered to youth (with an emphasis on
