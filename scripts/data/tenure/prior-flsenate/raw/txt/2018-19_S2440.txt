11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           The purpose is to purchase heavy equipment that will replace the current equipment that has aged and
          cannot keep up with the existing workload, much less handle the demands of a natural disaster.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           The services the equipment is intended for is for the daily clean-up of thirteen plus miles of beaches that
          include Miami beach and other municipalities; and to promptly clear beaches of debris immediately following
          hurricanes, storms and King tides.

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          The direct service is the clearing of debris on the beachfront, which in turn provides it's citizens, visitors and
         tourists, clean beaches free of layers of seaweed, dead fish, garbage left behind by users and washed up debris
         from storms and King tides.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          Residents, visitors and tourists. A minimum of 12 million tourists are currently being served. That does not
         include local residents.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          The expected benefit is to provide a clean and safe environment. Debris that remains on beaches impacts the
         environment and the economy. The economic impact not only affects the PROS Department but other
         municipalities it services.
