4. Project/Program Description
    North America Vehicle Rescue Association (NAVRA) is an education and training organization comprised of fire fighters,
   paramedics, nurses and physicians. NAVRA's mission is to “prepare today’s rescuers to succeed at tomorrow’s
   challenges.” NAVRA's focus is to provide platforms for rescue personnel throughout N. America and the world to share and
   advance rescue science and technology. NAVRA seeks to develop and enhance proficiency in rescue techniques, vehicle
   rescue, incident command and pre-hospital care. NAVRA requests funding to purchase a truck for transporting equipment
   for challenges as well as funding for two education/training sessions for first responders in order to better prepare them for
   saving lives.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     NAVRA is an education and training organization made up of fire fighters, paramedics, nurse and physicians. Our
    mission is to “prepare today’s rescuers to succeed at tomorrow’s challenges.” It is our focus to provide platforms for
    rescue personnel throughout N.America and the World to share and advance rescue science and technology. We seek to
    develop and enhance proficiency in rescue techniques, vehicle rescue, Incident Command and pre-hospital care. NAVRA
    is a leading training and networking association which brings the focus of casualty centered training & rescue to
    operational emergency responding personnel throughout North America. Our challenges introduce world class rescue
    teams who compete throughout the year in events designed to challenge emergency service personnel and raise
    awareness of the global problem of road death and injury.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Purchase of truck needed to transport equipment for challenges and education/training sessions provided to first
    responders to be better prepared to save lives here in Florida. We will also host two challenges in Florida bringing fire
    rescue teams from across the country and globe, creating economic opportunity to host cities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     NAVRA recently hosted an extrication and ALS Medical Challenge at Miami Dade Fire Rescue where 12 teams for
    extrication from fire departments across South Florida, the State and the globe. NAVRA had 15 teams participate in the
    medical challenge totaling nearly 200 participants hosted at areas hotels, dining in our communities, all while competing
    and learning better rescue techniques to save lives.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All populations in Florida are served with transfer of lifesaving techniques shared with first responders.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improved quality of education & training: In the rescue challenges we recreate realistic vehicle scenarios & insert live
    patients in the vehicles. The team are required to perform command, medical & technical skills & knowledge under timed
    conditions, to access, treat and extricate one or more patients in realistic environments. After each team completed
    evolution, they’re debriefed by leaders in the field who give learning feedback. Protection of general public: develop &
    enhance proficiency in vehicle rescue techniques, vehicle rescue, Incident Command & pre-hospital care. We aim to
    promote injury prevention through education and awareness within all groups at risk from unintentional injury. Economic
    activity: NAVRA recently hosted an extrication and ALS Medical Challenge at Miami Dade Fire Rescue with 200+
    participants traveling to the challenges.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to utilize the funds in the specified manor shall result in funds being returned to the State of Florida.
