4. Project/Program Description
    This project will provide funding for property owners to abandon septic tank systems and connect to available sanitary
   sewer infrastructure for approximately 100 homes located in low to moderate income areas. Providing financial assistance
   for private side connections will incentive timely connections, which remains the biggest barrier for property owners. This
   funding would complement the public laterals being installed by the County for properties with available and abutting sewer
   infrastructure.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project will provide funding for property owners to abandon septic tank systems and connect to available sanitary
    sewer infrastructure for approximately 100 homes located in low to moderate income areas. Providing financial
    assistance for private side connections will incentive timely connections, which remains the biggest barrier for property
    owners. This funding would complement the public laterals being installed by the County for properties with available and
    abutting sewer infrastructure.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    The project will design and construct plumbing connections from public sewer infrastructure to individual properties.
    Once a property is connected to the sanitary sewer system, the septic tank will be properly abandoned.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Property owners will be provided the service of plumbing connections and septic tank abandonment, including the
    required permitting and notifications.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This funding would support the private property connections in an area with a high density of unconnected and
    economically vulnerable properties from NW 27th Avenue to 37th Avenue between NW North River Drive, which is
    adjacent to the C-6 canal, north to NW. 71st St. This is the area where the County is installing sewer laterals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This appropriation will have social, economic, and environmental benefits including reducing costs to homeowners for
    establishing sewer connection, reduced impacts from septic systems compromised by rising groundwater including:
    • reduce nutrient effluent impacts on ground and surface waters including Biscayne Bay
    • reduce public health impacts from failing septic systems that have the potential to cause sewer backups in homes,
    impacting health and property
    • reduce risk of exposure to bacteria and pathogens in floodwaters in areas with saturated soil conditions
    The project will be measured by the number of septic tank systems removed and the removal of the associated nutrients.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The penalties for failing to meet deliverable or performance measures is the risk of not recovering reimbursement cost
    and potential delay in project progress.
