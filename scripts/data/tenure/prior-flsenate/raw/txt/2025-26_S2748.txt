4. Project/Program Description
    The purpose of this project is to support the acquisition of two drones, which will enable the Doral Police Department
   (DPD) to enhance public safety, improve emergency response and increase operational efficiency. This project will also
   support the Doral Emergency Operations Center (EOC) and the Real Time Operations Center (RTOC) by allowing the
   efficient assessment during emergency situations, such as Presidential protective details, following natural disasters,
   hazardous material incidents, critical incidents, and improved overall police response.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose of this project is to support the acquisition of two drones, which will enable the Doral Police Department
    (DPD) to enhance public safety, improve emergency response and increase operational efficiency. This project will also
    support the Doral Emergency Operations Center (EOC) and the Real Time Operations Center (RTOC) by allowing the
    efficient assessment during emergency situations, such as Presidential protective details, following natural disasters,
    hazardous material incidents, and improved overall police response.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The DPD Aerial Support Project will directly benefit the citizens of Doral. Two funded drones will be deployed in the event
    of a major disaster situation or critical incident. South Florida has been victim to severe hurricanes and storms. A drone
    can expedite the response and assessment times following a storm or critical incident, resulting in expedited police
    services to the community.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The DPD Aerial Support Program will provide the community with the enhanced response capabilities to include
    improved response times, wider security coverage from an aerial perspective, enhanced public safety, and increased
    operational efficiency.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The DPD Aerial Support Program will provide enhanced support for the community we serve. The proposed project will
    support enhanced security measures for approximately 200,000 individuals within the City of Doral, including residents,
    visitors, business owners, and employees, ensuring a safer and more secure environment for all.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The DPD Aerial Support Program will directly benefit the citizens of Doral. Two funded drones will be deployed in the
    event of a major disaster situation or critical incident. South Florida has been victim to severe hurricanes and storms. A
    drone can expedite the response and assessment times following a storm or critical incident, resulting in expedited police
    services to the community. The benefit of the DPD Aerial Support Program can be measured by conducting a benefit
    assessment to include log of deployment/incident engagement, collection of data during events, review the feedback of
    data collected and incident after action reports, evaluate the findings and complete a report with findings and
    recommendations during the specified time.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    In the event that the Doral Police Department is unable to utilize these funds for the purpose described herein, the funds
    will be returned.
