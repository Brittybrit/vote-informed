13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project will provide funding for property owners to abandon septic tank systems and connect to available sanitary
    sewer infrastructure for approximately 13 homes in the Virginia Gardens neighborhood where sewer laterals are being
    installed by the County. This project addresses priorities set by the State's Biscayne Bay Commission, which includes
    reduced stormwater and wastewater-related nutrient inputs to surface waters in order to protect the state-designated
    Biscayne Bay Aquatic Preserve and Florida’s Coral Reef.

   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The project will design and construct plumbing connections from public sewer infrastructure to individual properties.
    Once a property is connected to the sanitary sewer system, the septic tank will be properly abandoned. Providing
    financial assistance for private side connections will incentive timely connections, which remains the biggest barrier for
    property owners.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Property owners will be provided the service of plumbing connections and septic tank abandonment, including the
    required permitting and notifications.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This funding would support the private property connections in an area with a high density of unconnected properties that
    are vulnerable to rising groundwater levels, which interfere with the functionality of private septic tank systems. The
    County is installing sewer laterals in the Virginia Gardens neighborhood where the 13 homes are located. The properties
    are located on NW 37th Street between NW 58th Avenue and 62nd Avenue.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     This appropriation will have social, economic, and environmental benefits including reducing costs to homeowners for
    establishing sewer connection, reduced impacts from septic systems compromised by rising groundwater including:
    • reduce nutrient effluent impacts on ground and surface waters including Biscayne Bay
    • reduce public health impacts from failing septic systems that have the potential to cause sewer backups in homes,
    impacting health and property
    • reduce risk of exposure to bacteria and pathogens in floodwaters in areas with saturated soil conditions
    The project will be measured by the number of septic tank systems removed and the removal of the associated nutrients.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
