14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Renovation of the city's War memorial Cenotaph Monument located on the 100 block of Curtiss Parkway which was built
    in the mid fifties and is in need of a complete restoration.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This is a memorial to honor all war veterans that have given the ultimate sacrifice and should be a symbol of national
    pride which is in need of renovation and is used on various occasions during the year to honor our veterans.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
