1. Project Title         Brooksville Stormwater-Sanitary System Water Quality Improvement

2. Senate Sponsor        Wilton Simpson

3. Date of Request       02/17/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    To ensure only clean stormwater run-off be discharged to the receiving waters, and the treatment of sanitary sewer is
   completed to the highest standards, the departments of Public Works and Municipal Utilities for the City of Brooksville is
   seeking to purchase two Jetter Vac trucks to be utilized in the cleaning of our Stormwater and Sanitary Sewer systems. The
   purchase of two separate jetter-vac trucks is necessary to prevent cross contamination. One truck will be utilized for the
   cleaning of the stormwater system and the other truck will be utilized for the cleaning of the Sanitary Sewer System.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To ensure only clean stormwater run-off be discharged to the receiving waters, and the treatment of sanitary sewer is
    completed to the highest standards and prevention of cross-contamination.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     To provide the ability to improve the quality of the stormwater runoff that is discharged into the receiving waters throughr
    removal of debris, build-up, silt, trash and unwanted material from the open ditch and underground stormwater system
    and removal of grease, blockages, roots, silt, and other solid contaminates from the Sanitary Sewer system. .
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Providing for a clean and functioning stormwater system which will allow the City to ensure we are meeting all of the
    requirements MS4 permit and reduce the risk of potential contamination to our ground water supply. The discharge of a
    cleaner and higher quality effluent, the reduction in potential overflows as well as reduction in possible contamination of
    our stormwater sewer system.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All citizens of the City, population 8,191, and vistors may benefit from this project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The removal of contaminates from the system will allow the systems to function correctly thereby providing for a clean
    and functioning stormwater system and sanitary sewer system which will allow the City to ensure we are meeting all of
    the requirements MS4 permit and reduce the risk of potential contamination to our ground water supply and the reduction
    in possible contamination of our stormwater sewer system.
