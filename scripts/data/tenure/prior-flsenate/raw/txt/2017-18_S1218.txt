3. Project/Program Description:
Word and Action (W&A)’s program, Strengthening the Family aims to prevent and decrease occurrence of
sexual abuse, primarily within the Haitian-American community. The program is comprised of three
components: 1) community wide outreach; 2) cultural competency training; 3) culturally based treatment.
W&A will hire: 1 Accountant; 1 Legal Specialist?l1 Human Resources Person; 1 Administrative Assistant; 1
License Therapist; 2 Master Level Therapists; 1 License Social Workers; 2 Case managers; 1 Psychiatrist; 1
Psychologist; 1 Program Coordinator; and 1 IT Consultant. W&A will provide a survey sheet/ questionnaire in
Likert Scale, which will be studied and tabulated to measure success. W&A proposes to 1) provide one hour
therapy session per week, which equals to 12 sessions within a 3-month-period; 2) conduct 1/2 hour
evaluation at the end of the 3 months; 3) conduct one hour treatment plan review at the end of 6 months
following treatment; 4) conduct one hour therapy review with client at the end of 6 months following therapy.
Within a contract year, the program aims at reaching 100 unduplicated participants. The staff will distribute an
average of 200 brochures/ educational materials. W&A proposes to reach 30 unduplicated participants during
a 12-month-period. W&A will host, at least 30 unduplicated seminars to mental health providers. W&A will
provide 2600 therapy hours to 50 clients. Therapists will collectively conduct 50 half hour evaluations and 50
Treatment Plan Reviews.

d. What specific measures will be used to document performance data for the project, if it receives funds?
W&A will be able to measure success within a year. Social conditions that will affect the readiness of the
clients in the Initial Outcome (Raise awareness about child sexual abuse}, include but are not limited to,
language, education level, age, gender, religious belief, financial status and culture. Within the contract
year, the Community Outreach Coordinators, through the use of surveys and pre & post-test to the target
population, will tally the data obtained from the clients, participants, and families to measure increase in
knowledge about child sexual abuse. The Community Outreach Coordinators, who will also facilitate the
Parent Workshops, will once again tally the data received via pre & post-tests from the participants to
measure increase in knowledge. Facilitators conducting the seminars aimed at educating clinicians and
therapists about the culturally competent approach to child sexual abuse treatment and prevention in the
Haitian Community, will face the challenges brought on by the following social conditions: familiarity of
participants with the target population, and their level of involvement in the Haitian Community. Data
collected from the seminar participants via the pre & post-test and the surveys, will be analyzed to
determine the increase in knowledge and acceptance of participants about the importance of cultural
competency in therapy. However, it is worth noting that therapeutic treatment and/or process varies
based on the severity of the trauma as well as the client’s willingness and ability to go through the
treatment plan accordingly. Social conditions such as the ones listed above in addition to trauma level,
overall psychological state, parent’s work schedule, child’s school schedule, availability of transportation,
family income, and dual —_ diagnosis will all have an impact on the success of the intermediate outcome:
Provide therapy to children and families affected by child sexual abuse. Progress notes kept by the
clinicians/therapists collecting data from the clients and their family will track the progress of the clients
in treatment. During the sessions the data will be collected to chart and monitor the progress of the
clients. It will also be used to measure and evaluate progress at the three-month evaluation mark. This
data, at the six-month mark, will be used for the treatment plan review. The data will be protected ina
secure online computerized database system that is reinforced by scripted passwords. In addition,
surveys will be conducted when implementing its cultural based approach, “Strengthening the Family.”
The program will use the Behavior Assessment System for Children Il (BASC il) as a tool to measure client
behavior and attitude
