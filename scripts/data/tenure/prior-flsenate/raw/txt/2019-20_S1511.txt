11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         Funds will allow for the expansion of academic STEM and technology programs, and expansion of student
         success initiatives in a newly constructed facility.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         PHSC and dual enrolled students from Pasco and Hernando County will receive academic and program support
         services to obtain associate degrees and certifications in STEM and technology related fields.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         The community high school graduates, veterans, and community citizens will engage and attend academic
         programs, support services, and business related workshops to enhance their career mobility.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         The primary target population is students from the College's service district of Pasco and Hernando County.
         The Student Success, Professional Development, and Entreprenurial Center (SS-PDEC) will serve approximatel
         10,000 students and provide a variety of community services to 25,000 or more residents each year.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         The expected benefit or outcome of this project is that the students' educational needs of Pasco and Hernando
         will be appropriately served. This outcome will be measured by the number of students who enroll, retained,
         and complete the educational and degree programs offered by the (SS-PDEC).
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         The agency could place the College on a monitoring or recommend the College be evaluated by auditors for any
         violations regarding the mis-use of funds.
