11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           Vincent Academy Adventure Coast seeks funding to construct a building in Hernando County for us to
          continue to provide services, and greatly expand our services to the mentally ill in Hernando and in the
          surrounding counties. We are a Recovery Through Work Program where those with a mental illness can obtain
          stability, community involvement, and employment. Our members are less likely to have contact with law
          enforcement and the criminal justice system. We actively partner with local agencies and businesses to insure
          our members can meet the needs of employers and become an integral part of the community.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Vincent Academy Adventure Coast is a work day focused clubhouse where persons with a mental illness who
          need more services than they are currently receiving or is available locally, can come to VAAC five days a week
          as a member, become part of a community, and enter into employment where appropriate.

d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

      Citizens of Hernando and neighboring counties will receive direct services from the first certified facility for
     psychosocial rehabilitation to be opened in this area of the state. Intense daily services will be offered as well as
     some evenings and weekends, so that members with a mental illness will have the benefit of a Recovery
     Through Work program, that helps them achieve mental health stability, wellness and community involvement.
     At Vincent Academy the staff works equally alongside the members throughout the work day, teaching work
     ethic and professionalism by example.

e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

      The target population of this project is persons with a mental illness, including those with criminal justice
     involvement, who need assistance in obtaining stability, re-entering the community, and obtaining increased
     income or employment. Once a person becomes a "member" they are considered life time members and
     encouraged to be active on some level with Vincent Academy even after obtaining employment and wellness.
     Those who have been members longer then take on greater roles in the work centered day and encourage the
     new members to achieve stability and wellness.

f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
     will be measured?
       The expected benefit of Vincent Academy Adventure Coast will be: increased stability for those with a mental
     illness to include fewer hospitalizations, more positive involvment with the community, decreased contact with
     law enforcement and the criminal justice system, increased income for those who qualify, transitional
     employment, supportive employment, independent employment, partnership with Vocational Rehabilitation
     and partnerships with the local agencies and communities.
