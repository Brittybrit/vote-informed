13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal of creating a workforce training program in the construction industry is to teach the essential skills and
    techniques needed for a trade. This program focuses on a hands-on training of fundamental construction skills. By
    combining classroom instruction with real-world application, trainees gain a deep understanding of tools, materials, and
    building methods while also learning critical and safety practices.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The organization will provide a database of all individuals that complete the program and follow through with assistance
    in job placement thereby enhancing the individual’s economic self-sufficiency.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Classroom learning and hands-on training of essentials to enter the construction industry.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Jobless persons, economically disadvantaged persons or At-risk youth.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The expected benefit is to enhance economic self-sufficiency in participants by to creating immediate job opportunities
    and to increase and improve economic activity. This construction trade program in Florida would boost economic activity
    by developing a skilled workforce that meets the state’s growing demands. As more workers become trained in essential
    trades, the industry can complete more projects.
