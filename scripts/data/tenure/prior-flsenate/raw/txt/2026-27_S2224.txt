4. Project/Program Description
    Miami-Dade County aims to acquire a Mobile Command Unit (MCU) to strengthen its emergency response and disaster
   recovery capabilities. This is a new initiative, as the County’s Department of Emergency Management currently does not
   have this resource. The MCU will function as a mobile, on-scene command center, providing essential infrastructure for
   real-time coordination, communication, and operational control during emergencies. It will enhance situational
   awareness, improve interoperability among agencies, and support decision-making in dynamic environments. The unit
   will be deployed countywide for all major incidents, including hurricanes, mass casualty events, and large-scale or soft-
   target gatherings. It will also be available as a mutual aid asset to support other counties throughout Florida. Given the
   region’s high risk for natural and human-made disasters, the MCU will play a critical role in improving public safety,
   response efficiency, and regional resilience.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The requested funds will support the acquisition of a Mobile Command Unit (MCU) to provide Miami-Dade County with
    critical mobile incident management, command, and coordination capabilities at disaster scenes. The MCU will serve
    as a forward-deployed operations hub, enabling real-time decision-making, multi-agency coordination, and inter-
    operable communications during emergencies. This aligns with the Florida Senate's priorities under SB 180 and the
    state's Emergency Preparedness and Response Fund, which emphasize mobile readiness, regional coordination, and
    rapid deployment. The MCU will also be available for mutual aid to other counties, supporting Florida’s broader goal of
    statewide resilience and disaster preparedness.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Mobile Command Unit (MCU) will support a wide range of disaster response and recovery operations throughout
    Miami-Dade County. It will serve as a mobile operations center, enabling on-site incident management, multi-agency
    coordination, and real-time communication during emergencies. The MCU will be deployed to major incidents such as
    hurricanes, mass casualty events, and large-scale public gatherings. It will also support recovery operations by
    maintaining a stable command presence in affected areas. Past incidents that highlight the need for this capability
    include the Club Cup event, regional brush fires, the Surfside building collapse, and deployments to assist other Florida
    communities during Hurricanes Milton and Helene.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The Mobile Command Unit (MCU) will directly enhance public safety services for residents and visitors of Miami-Dade
    County. It will provide on-site coordination during emergencies, ensuring faster response times, improved
    communication among agencies, and more efficient deployment of resources. Citizens will benefit from more organized
    evacuations, quicker access to emergency medical services, and timely public information during disasters. The MCU
    will also support large-scale public events by improving crowd safety, traffic management, and incident response. By
    serving as a mobile operations hub, the MCU ensures that critical services reach affected communities more quickly
    and effectively, ultimately reducing harm and saving lives.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The Mobile Command Unit (MCU) will serve the approximately 2.7 million residents of Miami-Dade County, along with
    the 28 million annual visitors who travel to the region for tourism, business, and major events. This includes
    international and domestic travelers, many of whom attend high-profile events that require enhanced public safety
    coordination. The MCU will be a critical asset in managing public safety, emergency response, and interagency
    coordination during such large-scale events. It will also be available for mutual aid deployments to other Florida
    counties, further expanding its reach.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The expected outcome of this project is improved emergency response coordination, faster deployment of resources,
    and enhanced public safety during disasters and large-scale events. The Mobile Command Unit (MCU) will provide on-
    site command and control capabilities, enabling real-time decision-making, inter-operable communications, and multi-
    agency coordination. This will result in more efficient incident management, reduced response times, and better
    outcomes for affected communities. The methodology for measuring these outcomes will include tracking key
    performance indicators such as response time improvements, number of successful deployments, inter-agency
    coordination metrics, and after-action reports from incidents where the MCU is utilized. Additional evaluation will
    include feedback from emergency personnel, documentation of mutual aid support provided to other counties, and
    assessments of operational readiness during planned events and unplanned emergencies.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
