4. Project/Program Description
    In 2023-2024, Miami Diaper Bank's Mobile Diaper Pantry will connect diapers directly to families in three areas of
   significant need including Homestead, Florida City and North Miami Beach. These communities were identified, with
   assistance from longtime capacity partner The Children's Trust, using local data to determine areas with extremely limited
   access to services. As South Florida's first Mobile Diaper Pantry, this innovative diaper distribution program will fill a gap in
   services for under-served and under-reached families in Miami-Dade. To remove barriers to access and promote equity,
   diapers will be distributed directly to families who lack awareness about services, do not have access to services because
   of transportation and other challenges, and/or who do not meet the guidelines for local direct service agencies.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Miami Diaper Bank's innovative Mobile Diaper Pantry program will fill a gap in services for at-need children and parents
    in Miami-Dade by directly distributing free diapers to communities with limited or no access to services. By removing
    barriers to access for undeserved and under-reached children and their parents, Miami Diaper Bank will support improved
    community health and wellness.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     -Staff oversight and support for program operations and administration
    -Consultative support for new program evaluation
    -Purchase of wholesale diapers for mobile distribution
    -Promote mobile pantry locations and increase visibility in areas of need to ensure awareness and service are reaching
    families in need
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     -Diapers and related supplies will be distributed directly to undeserved and under-reached participants in Miami-Dade
    -Links to other accessible local services will be provided to participants in Miami-Dade
    -Awareness about Miami Diaper Bank and accessible services will be increased across South Florida
    -Awareness about diaper need will be increased across South Florida
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population served by this project is over 800 economically disadvantaged Miami-Dade children ages 0-5 and
    their parents. This includes participants who are jobless, homeless, in foster care, and have limited or no access to
    diapers.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Outcomes include measurable improvements in infant and child physical health, maternal mental health, access to
    childcare, economic self-sufficiency, and an increase in attendance at work for parents and caregivers. Outcomes will be
    measured using intake surveys administered to participants on-site at mobile distributions and electronic post-distribution
    surveys to evaluate changes that occurred. Anecdotal reports and testimonials may also be collected from participants.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The contracting agency may consider financial penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures.
