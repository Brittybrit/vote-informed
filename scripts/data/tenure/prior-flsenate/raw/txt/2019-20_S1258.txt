1. Title of Project: Morningside Drive Extension
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 02/05/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   The primary objective of the new roadway is to serve as a reliever for an additional connector road centrally located
   between and parallel to two existing east-west oriented arterial roadways: Clinton Avenue and Meridian Avenue
   (State Road 52). This will provide additional eastbound and westbound travel options for area residents, and
   visitors. The proposed roadway will also accommodate new development within the area, provide enhanced
   response times for emergency services, and serve as a corridor for extension of utility services. The proposed
   roadway will provide direct east-west access for Florida Hospital - Dade City.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         The primary objective of the new roadway is to serve as a reliever for Clinton Avenue and Meridian Avenue
         (State Road 52).
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         The City of Dade City will have a new roadway constructed.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         The direct services provided by the project to the citizens of Dade City and eastern Pasco County will be a direct
         connection to the only hospital in Dade City. This benefit and service will allow for reduced times for
         emergency services to Dade City and Pasco County residents. A secondary direct service and benefit will be a
         reduction in traffic volumes for two parallel roadways that are nearing capacity.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         All citizens and visitors will benefit from the new roadway. 9,000 individuals are expected to be served by the
         new roadway.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         The expected outcomes are reduced emergency response times to area residents and a reduction in traffic
         volumes for two parallel roadways.
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         Standard contract penalties.
