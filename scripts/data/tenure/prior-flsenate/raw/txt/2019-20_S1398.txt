1. Title of Project: Project Be Strong (Social and Emotional Wellness)
2. Senate Sponsor: Annette Taddeo
3. Date of Submission: 02/05/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   The Be Strong project is a healthy relationship education project that explains the risks associated with early risky
   behaviors. This program aims to serve youth in low socioeconomic areas that have high teen pregnancy rates, STD
   infection rates, and juvenile delinquency rates. Youth learn the tools needed to establish healthy relationships
   through self-actualization modules. Youth also learn character skills, effective communication skills, financial literacy
   skills, and resume writing skills. Parents are also encouraged through workshops the importance of talking to their
   teens and pre-teens about how to avoid risky behaviors and the dangers of unmonitored social media platforms.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        Funds will be primarily spent on youth and activities, curriculum materials and supplies. In addition, some funds
        will be used to hire program facilitators.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        The activities and services that will be provided includes 5 hours of services to 360 participants annually.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Direct Services will include: 1) Providing instruction and review of curriculum, including financial literacy, risk
        avoidance education, sexual health, and drug and alcohol intervention. 2) Providing instruction on developing
        surveys and tests for student participants, including documentation requirements under this contract. 3)
        Providing information on the evaluation process, including corrective remedies for inadequate performance
        and available refresher courses.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          360 disadvantaged or at-risk youth, between the ages of 11 and 18, residing in the Miami-Dade area.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
          Program results are as follows: 75% of program participants will show an improvement in knowledge of risks
          associated with early sexual involvement and sexually transmitted diseases. 75% of program participants will
          report an increased sense of emotional well-being upon completion of the program. 60% of program
          participants will show an increase in the awareness of important decision making skills and how it can affect
          future financial stability. 60% of program participants will show an increase in their attitudes to pursue their
          career goals. 60% of program participants will report an intent to avoid substance use. 60% of program
          participants will show an increase in understanding the risks of substance use. The benefit of this program is to
          correlate academic success and a healthy future orientation with emotional and social wellness. Pre and post
          tests are given to each participant.
    f.    What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
          penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
          Failure of Provider to complete or submit a deliverable in the time and manner specified will result in a 10%
          reduction in payment.
