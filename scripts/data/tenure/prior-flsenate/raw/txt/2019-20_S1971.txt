11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        To fund the inaugural year of operations, events, programs, salaries and benefits, State University Internships,
        website, software for computerized collections records, exhibition supplies, computer and software for
        museum library catalog, funding contract librarian to create catalog, inaugural exhibit expenses (cabinetry,
        artwork, displays), education programs for elementary through high school field trips and colleges, and capital
        costs for preservation of the historic museum building.
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        In its inaugural year, the museum will build audiences through exhibits, programs, education, and outreach; to
        provide educational tours to students K-12 and colleges; design, plan, and build the museum's inaugural
        exhibits for the public; establish a museum website, collections database and library database; preserve the
        exterior of the building by repairing wooden siding and other wooden elements and to treat the building for
        wood-boring insect infestation (tenting); and paint repaired wooden siding and elements with special resistant
        paint.
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

         Direct services include history tours, lectures, traveling exhibits, and special patriotic events. The museum hosts
         the VA hospital outreach "the Vet's Center," and counsels returning veterans. Events such as Presidents Day,
         Armed Forces Day, Memorial Day, 9/11, and Veterans Day will be observed. Special observances for African-
         American heritage, Hispanic heritage, and Purple Heart Day will be programmed.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         Florida has the third largest number of veterans in the US, 1.5 million. Also national and international tourists,
         as well as local and state visitors will benefit. Veterans, first responders, and law enforcement officers. Black,
         white, hispanic, school children, and college students will benefit and learn. Service to be provided to 30,000
         visitors, tourists, and students in the museum's inaugural year.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         Benefits/outcomes are: increase to the Florida heritage experience, improved quality of education, increased
         tourism, specific job opportunities for museum workers and college student interns, improved mental health
         for veterans who receive counseling at the Vet Center hosted in the museum with its own office space. The
         main measurement will be in numbers of visitors, tourists, and students who visit the museum or attend
         events, special exhibits, and conferences.
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         Funds are to be withheld if deliverables and performance measures are not met.
