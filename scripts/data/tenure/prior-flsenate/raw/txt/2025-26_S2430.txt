     The purpose of the Medley Lakeside Community Passive Park Project is to develop a new passive recreational park on
    annexed areas (Parcel-B) to enhance the local community. Funds requested will be used for mitigation, permitting,
    clearing and grubbing, clean fill material, spreading and grading of material, park safety, and beautification. This passive
    park is critical for health and well-being of the local community, preservation of wet land and wildlife, and the natural
    environment.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Once approved for funding, the project is ready to enter the construction phase, which may include, but not limited to
    mitigation, permitting, clearing and grubbing, clean fill material, spreading and grading of material, park security,
    beautification, and other minimal development.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The proposed project will serve as an investment in the Town of Medley and shall enhance the Lakeside and
    surroundings annexed are communities and residents. Passive recreational parks are considered critical for overall health
    and well-being of the citizens, preservation of wet land, and protection of local wildlife. The overall focus of the proposed
    park is to provide a memorable experience to all visitors in a safe, educational, and peaceful setting to promote deep
    appreciation for the natural environment, wildlife, and open spaces.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The Town of Medley residents, including the Lakeside and the surrounding communities, local workforce, and visitors,
    who possess deep appreciation for natural environment, local wildlife, and open spaces are the target population for this
    project. An estimated 800 plus individuals, including 200 at least visitors may experience this new park, each year.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The Lakeside Community Passive Recreational Park shall enhance the quality of life and living in the Lakeside
    Community and surrounding areas, and throughout the Town of Medley by extending a quiet, natural, and open space
    experience to all individuals, who value environment, wildlife and nature in a well preserved "naturalness" setting. 90% of
    all visitors surveyed may rate this park as a safe, educational, and unique environment. They may also describe having a
    deep appreciation for open spaces and a "must visit" park, when visiting south Florida.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     The Town of Medley shall comply with all provisions of the fully executed state contract, including the standard language
    describing strict compliance with certain provisions regarding performance expectations, timely deliverable, and desired
    outcomes. The Town shall comply with standard penalties, such as holding payments or possible reduction in funding for
    failing to meet any deliverables, established performance measures, or non-performance.
