11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           The goal is to promote environmental workforce sustainable job training by introducing trainees to the history
          of environmental justice, agriculture and safety by providing comprehensive instruction in OSHA/HAZWOPER,
          with academic and practicums to targeted food desert residents in attaining self-sufficiency. Up to 100
          residents will receive internship and employment upon completion of their training and certification.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Combining intensive hands-on training with lectures to reinforce environmental & safety as it pertains to
          Chemical Hazards, Respiratory Protection, Emergency Response Plan, Hazardous Waste Clean Up. This program
          provides the targeted population with a broad range of experiences, with 500 hours of course training, certified
          by the National Center for Construction Education and Research (NCCER). Graduates will be able to secure
          employment in the environmental and agriculture industry.

d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

     Our training platform will utilize the required of OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120 40-hour/HAZWOPER in the curriculum.
    Certifications earned will provide and opportunity for employment for graduates, while meeting hiring needs of
    employers in the community. Our model of "Urban Environmental Revitalization through Agriculture (UERA)
    "Training will expose trainees to clean-up, assessments, repairs, regeneration and rehabilitation of the soil and
    can be replicated.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          White Harvest 14 Acres will provide access to training, clean-up and using site as green space to grow fresh
         produce year-round. White Harvest Farm will provide required training in OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120 40-hour that
         include Hazardous Wasted Operations and Emergency Response HAZWOPER to all trainees of the program.
         Trainees will expose and trained to repair and regenerate the soil. They will experience how a community with
         many environmental justice issues, can change their community by engagement and training, while growing
         healthy food that will benefit the neighborhoods and surrounding communities. Our proposed "one stop shop"
         will introduce training to trainees with no advance training and or education. This training center will add value
         to the Farm by remediating and creating replicable training programs, while ecological repairing the farmland.
         Targeted residents can take advantage of this opportunity to become a part of the solution and decreased
         recidivism
