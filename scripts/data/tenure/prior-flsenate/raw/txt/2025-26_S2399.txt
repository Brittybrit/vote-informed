1. Project Title          Ohel South Florida Trauma Response and Resilience

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

4. Project/Program Description
    Ohel continues to offer and expand trainings to educators and professionals to meet the mental health needs of youth by
   fostering resilience and wellbeing through our social-emotional learning curricula and resources as well as substance
   abuse prevention work. Ohel has a dedicated trauma team across S Florida, specializing in crisis response, resilience and
   support services for acute traumas such as drownings, suicides, sudden deaths, fatal diagnoses, natural disasters and
   community tragedies.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Children and teachers using Ohel's resilience resources will learn skills, tools, and language to help them cope with life's
    challenges. A school that uses a trauma informed approach with their students and faculty enables children to grow
    academically, socially, and emotionally creating a stronger and more resilient next generation. Communities, families and
    individuals are strengthened when there are positive interventions for trauma and grief and substance abuse prevention.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Ohel will provide parent, teacher and student education to promote resilience, wellbeing and trauma-sensitivity to better
    meet the mental-health needs of youth and social-emotional, resilience and substance use prevention resources and
    programs for students from preschool through high school age. We will conduct trauma support and crisis intervention for
    schools, families and communities.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will receive preventative resilience education on to help strengthen and build mental capacity as well as being
    provided with crises response and trauma interventions when faced with a tragedy and substance abuse issues.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Elderly persons, persons with poor mental health, preschool students, grade school students, high school students,
    university/college students, victims of crime.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     -enhance educators and students' resilience and well-being with the understanding of resilience as a parallel process in
    building student wellbeing
    -trauma team ensures access to mental health interventions
    -enhance individual's coping and increase their ability to connect to resources in the aftermath of traumatic events
    -build community resilience
    -Ohel's Communities Confronting Substance Use & Addiction preventative programming for students, educators, parents
    and communities provides education regarding substance use prevention, with the goal of enhancing students’ refusal
    skills and understanding of the harm/dangers of substance abuse. This directly addresses the serious drug use amongst
    today's youth and the risk of overdose and death
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     A detailed spending plan and deliverables will be provided to the agency. Ohel will ensure transparency and efficient use
    of funds to serve children, families and educators in South Florida.
