1.   Project Title          US 301 - Pretty Pond Road - Medical Arts Court Intersection Improvements - Zephyrhills

2.   Senate Sponsor Wilton Simpson

3.   Date of Request 12/05/2019

4.   Project/Program Description
     The project includes the relocation of an existing signalized intersection from the shopping plaza entrance to Pretty Pond Road, a
     signalized intersection at Medical Arts Court, and all other required roadway improvements. The addition of signalized intersections at
     these locations improves the mobility of the NE section of the City of Zephyrhills.
     These improvements will serve as an economic driver, which will enable the NE and NW corners of Pretty Pond to be developed into a
     national grocery store, national chain hotel, pet supply store, and chain restaurants.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      To construct two signalized intersections at Pretty Pond Road and Medical Arts Court along US 301, which will allow for improved
      operation and safety of the arterial streets through mobility of through traffic and access to adjacent properties.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Construction of two signalized intersections along US 301, along with corresponding roadway improvements.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      In addition to the improvement of the mobility of US 301, Pretty Pond Road, and Medical Arts Court for area residents, the
      improvements will be beneficial in the development of the NE and NW corners of Pretty Pond Road/US 301.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      Target population will be all traffic the traverses the US 301 corridor in the NE quadrant of the City of Zephyrhills. The proposed
      improvements will serve thousands of vehicles each day.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      The benefit of the project will be improved traffic circulation patterns at the intersections. Economic growth at the NE and NW
      intersections with businesses added to the tax rolls and job growth.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Loss of funding.
