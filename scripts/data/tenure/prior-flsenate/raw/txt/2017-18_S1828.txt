11.   Program Performance (if needed, include additional documentation):
      a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
           The Veterans Alternative is the first center to bring research proven modalities, in a collective holistic
           manner, including Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART), Integrative Restoration (iRest), and
           camaraderie-building programs.

      b.   What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
           Retreats at the Veterans Alternative will be open to Combat Veterans and Veterans who have
           experienced Military Sexual Trauma (MST) and their spouses/significant others. Retreats will be for 6
           days and will offer evidence-based and complementary modalities to support Combat Veterans and
           spouses.

      d.   What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
           Therapy and consultation with licensed therapists and fellow veterans, including alternative treatments
           not normally provided by the VA.
      e.   Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served.
           Veterans suffering from Traumatic Brain Injury and Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder. 51-100 veterans
      f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this
           outcome will be mesasured?
           The expected benefit will be veterans who are suffering from TBI and PTSD will be better able to confront
           their symptoms and handle the transition to civilian life. Veterans who have gone through the program
           are routinely checked by the organization to ensure that they are continuing to cope and handle their
           symptoms.
      g.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
           penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
           Additional penalties would be the termination of the state's four year contract with the organization
