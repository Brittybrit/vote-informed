14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Phase II of this restoration will address the critical situation that threatens the residential area through which the canal
    passes and will 1) prevent property damage to adjacent homes caused by toppling of mature trees when embankment
    collapse undermines their roots; 2) prevent collapse of roadway that provides access to resident and emergency vehicles;
    and 3) mitigate flooding in adjacent properties due to water flow obstruction caused by debris, soil, vegetation from
    embankment degradation and canal bank collapse. Dredging, demolition, clearing , asphalt, curb & gutter, new trees,
    ballast, rip rap etc. to continue canal bank stabilization project (Phase II) in residential area per attached conceptual plan
    for whole project.

    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The nearby homeowners, motorists and pedestrians who travel on the roadways that abut the canal. 14,000 +served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The benefits will include erosion control & stabilization of canal banks, gutters, adjacent roads & mature trees as well as
    the protection and stabilization of private and public properties. In addition, there will be improvement to water quality in
    this tributary to Everglades Watershed. The project will be measured by specified deliverables including documentation of
    number of mature trees saved, number of feet of collapsing canal banks stabilized, number of linear feet of asphalt, curb
    and gutter stabilized, documentation of reduction in #and % of storm water-related flooding and pre- and post-stabilization
    of water quality metrics/analysis.
