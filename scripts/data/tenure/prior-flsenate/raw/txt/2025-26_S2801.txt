1. Project Title         Miami Dade College - Hialeah Campus Expansion

2. Senate Sponsor        Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request       2/17/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    Enrollment at the Hialeah Campus has grown by over 50% in three years, from 7,500 students in fall 2021 to over 11,600
   this term, driven by a population boom in Hialeah and nearby areas. Operating at full capacity, the campus faces
   challenges in meeting rising demand. Expanding the campus would enable more programs for career development and
   workforce training, addressing community needs and supporting regional economic growth. This investment is essential to
   maintaining accessible, high-quality education.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Miami Dade College's Hialeah Campus serves a rapidly growing student population, and expanded facilities are
    essential to meet increasing demand. Additional funding will allow the campus to enhance educational spaces, provide
    access to state-of-the-art resources, and support workforce development programs, ensuring students have the tools
    they need to succeed in their academic and career goals.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    state-of-the-art resources, and the enhancement of workforce development programs. These efforts will provide students
    with hands-on learning opportunities, updated facilities, and career-focused training to support their academic success
