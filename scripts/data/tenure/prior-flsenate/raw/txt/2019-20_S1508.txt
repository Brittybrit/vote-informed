1. Title of Project: Hidden Lake (SW-827; CFI N967)
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 02/01/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   Certain areas downstream of Hidden Lake have flooded during recent events. Areas most impacted by flooding from
   this watershed include residences near Bass Lake, Yellow Lake, and Scout Lake. In recent events, portable showers,
   etc. were provided to the residents due to the prolonged nature of the flooding. It should be noted that some of the
   residents found themselves with few personal resources to call upon. The project would provide flood relief to
   downstream properties immediately after completion. The project consists of a proposed berm to be constructed
   around Hidden Lake. The berm would allow the property to retain more water, and then discharge at a
   controlled/reduced rate. This project will, in addition, serve to provide aquifer recharge and water quality benefits.
   This flood protection project will be designed to reduce flood levels so as to remove 60+ homes currently lying
   within the flood hazard area and reduce the length of roadway flooding by approximately 4,000 feet.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         Many homes and streets have been flooded as a result of recent storm events. This project will reduce flooding
         levels in multiple neighborhoods so as to remove more than sixty (60) homes from the flood hazard area (FHA)
         and eliminate street flooding from over 4,000 linear feet of roadway. In addition, the project will improve water
         quality and increase aquifer recharge.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         This project entails the design, permitting, and construction of a storm-water impoundment pond. The Pasco
         County BCC will manage and maintain the project.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         This project will provide employment for the engineering and construction trades. Potentially, a passive
         recreational park may be built on the project site in the future.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         This project will serve the residents in and around Cranes Roost, Yellow Lake, Bass Lake Estates, and Lake
         Worrell Acres. The project will serve approximately 2,200 individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         Both house- and street flooding will be significantly reduced; flooding durations will also be reduced. The
         County will actively monitor flooding elevations.
