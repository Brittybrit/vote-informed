11. Program Performance
     a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
     Pinecrest Gardens (PG) is a nationally, state, and locally designated historic botanical and cultural arts park in the Village of
     Pinecrest. The proposed porject includes improvements to various facilities that are in line with the Village's goal of fostering an
     educational environment for all chidlren. This includes renovating the exisiting playground, bathrooms, petting zoo, nature trail,
     Inspiration (educational) Center, Sensory Garden, and connecting paths to make them ADA accessible.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      The Village proposes to renovate the existing playground, bathrooms, petting zoo, Inpsiration (educational) Center, and connecting
      paths to make them ADA accessible; it also intends to add new playground equipment and a unique, greatly expanded ADA
      accessible Senory and Learning Garden. Additionally, creating new picnic facilities, a new nature trail, and installing better lighting.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
     This project will allow Pinecrest Gardens to continue its efforts to provide inclusive recreational and educational opportunities to
     children with diverse abilities. With the addition of the new facilities, programming serving this specific population will be expanded.




d.   Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
     Elderly persons; persons with poor mental health; persons with poor physical health; developmentally disabled; physically disabled.

     Approximately 800 individuals will be served.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
     Increased access to additional education and cultural programming to residents of all abilities.




 f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
    penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
     Revocation of funding if project is not completed.
