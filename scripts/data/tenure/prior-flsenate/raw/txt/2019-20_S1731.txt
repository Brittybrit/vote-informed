2. Senate Sponsor: Jose Rodriguez
3. Date of Submission: 02/07/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   The Town of Cutler Bay is requesting $175,000 to assist with the development and implementation of the Town
   Center District Small Business Incubator. This project is located in a newly designated Opportunity Zone. The
   primary goal of the Business Incubator is to attract new high-tech, research and development (R&D) and
   manufacturing start-up companies to the Town of Cutler Bay. The town is currently in the design phase; the
   business incubator will provide promising startups in the fields of technology, research and development with
   below market rent and technical assistance in the form of educational business workshops. The Town will also
   target small business owners from low to moderate income (LMI) households living in the South Dade Community
   and service businesses with an annual income of less than $300,000. These individuals will have access to
   workshops, temporary meeting space, and office space at free or reduced rates.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        Outcome 1: Stimulate economic development by implement a strategy to attract new high-tech, R&D, and
        manufacturing start-up companies to the Town of Cutler Bay. Performance Measure: Conduct building use
        study to identify space and development needs within the Town Center Building to house the incubator. Build-
        out of business incubator. Outcome 2: Facilitate use or reuse of existing infrastructure by providing technical
        assistance/training to town staff, members of the Economic Development Council, and the business community
        to allow them to fully participate in this project. Performance Measure: Host a series public involvement
        meetings to increase awareness about the business incubator, and receive public input. Develop a marketing
        strategy to recruit new businesses.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        This project provides affordable or free office/meeting space to start-up companies in the south Miami-Dade
        community. The project is located within an Opportunity Zone in the Cutler Bay Town Center District.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Direct services include scheduling workshops through partnership with the US Small Business Administration
        and other economic development community agencies. Topics may include: time management, marketing
        strategies, HR, Ethics, and money management.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        The proposed project will provide space for up to 25 small businesses. Scheduled workshops will be open to
        the general public and may attract 100 businesses from the local community throughout the year.
