11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         The city is dealing with a flood plain resilience issues were approximately 95% of the City is below the proposed
         flood plain. Part of the plan to build a new City Hall because the current one will be approximately six feet
         below the proposed flood plain. The proposed location currently is a park which requires relocation. The City
         purchased property for this purpose in 2018. The request is to do the capital improvements to the property to
         meet resident and tourist desires.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         The park design is a linear park with a general area that focuses on children and protective measures to assure
         their safety.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         In development of a new city hall it displaces a park that focuses on children, this request will service youth of
         the City, Citrus County and tourists. In general Crystal River Parks serve the majority of the time residents from
         outside the City.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         Hundreds of children and families.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         The City lacks activities that encourage longer stays. This will add to the overall package of Eco-tourism for
         Crystal River. We will monitor activity at the park as well as tourism numbers.
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         Reversion of state funds
