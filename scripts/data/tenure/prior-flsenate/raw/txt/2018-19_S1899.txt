1. Title of Project: City of North Miami Beach - Master Force Main Installation
2. Senate Sponsor: Daphne Campbell
3. Date of Submission: 12/14/2017
4. Project/Program Description:
    The City of North Miami Beach’s regional water and wastewater utility, NMB Water, provides service to over
    180,000 customers in Northeast Miami-Dade County. This shovel ready Appropriations Project Request seeks funds
    for the installation of an alternative Master Force Main with approximately 6,000 feet of new 16-inch diameter
    ductile iron pipe, designed to alleviate the threat of sewage spills and sanitary sewer overflows. This non-recurring
    request for $670,650 mitigates the potential of a sanitary sewer spill and overflow in the neighborhoods located
    south of Hard Rock stadium. This project falls within the boundary of a Financially Disadvantaged Community. In
    addition, this project alleviates the potential economic hardships associated with a wide-spread sewage spill and
    overflows; both businesses and residents would benefit from this infrastructure upgrade, which would ultimately
    translate directly into more robust commercial investments and increased job opportunities in the area
