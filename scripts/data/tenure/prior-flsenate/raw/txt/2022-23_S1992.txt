4. Project/Program Description
    Our goal is to restore the critical Homosassa spring shed habitat and Outstanding Waterway by removing filamentous
   algae and decaying benthic detrital material, then replanting Submerged Aquatic Vegetation (SAV). The project will
   increase water clarity,oxygen levels,and habitat for fish and other wildlife, including the manatee.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Homosassa River is a first magnitude spring and designated Outstanding Florida Waterway which is identified as a
    priority waterbody by SWIM. It is listed as impaired for nutrients & filamentous algae by FDEP & USEPA. The goal is to
    restore water quality through removal of algal detrital material and planting SAV
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Contractor will provide vacuum removal of algal detrital material, planting SAV, install cages as needed to protect SAV,
    then monitor and maintain restored areas for one year post cleaning & planting
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Improvements proposed by this project to improve water quality via the restoration process will improve as well as
    maintain conditions for tourism in this area, which is one of the largest economic drivers in the region.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The serves to maintain and improve recreation and tourism, which supports local citizens of Homosassa, Citrus County,
    and the State of Florida at large as this would have a tremendous impact on the fishing industry, hotels, restaurants,
    boating, providing an economic impact as well as overall optimal environmental experience
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
