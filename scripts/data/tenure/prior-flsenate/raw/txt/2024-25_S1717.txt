4. Project/Program Description
    The Miami Lighthouse Academy provides services for students who are Blind/Visually Impaired who live within Miami-
   Dade County, Florida. The academy provides services for Miami-Dade County Public School Students who are visually
   impaired from K-2nd grade. To qualify to be in the school, students must have an updated eye report from a doctor stating
   they are legally blind, have peripheral field loss, or have a neurological visual impairment inhibiting vision. The services
   provided on site are standard curriculum instruction, braille instruction, assistive technology instruction, expanded core
   curriculum instruction, and low vision accommodations (such as large print, screen reader technology). The school capacity
   is 30 students, of which all are blind/visually impaired. The instruction includes four teachers of the visually impaired, one O
   & M Specialist, two ESE certified teachers, one Occupational Therapist, one Physical Therapist, and one Speech
   Therapist.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Miami Lighthouse Academy will utilize the requested funds to provide additional services and materials for K-2
    students who are blind/visually impaired to be given the expansive supports needed to adapt, modify, and be provided
    access to the general education curriculum and activities of daily living skills.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Providing educational opportunities for students with visual impairments requires an explicit hands-on approach that
    implements materials and requires key support personnel. Services include: direct on site services by four Teachers of
    the Visually Impaired which include braille instruction, low vision adaptations, blind specific curriculum (expanded core
    curriculum) instruction, blind specific assessments (Oregon, ILSA, FVLMA), assistive library instruction, and assistive
    technology instruction. Full day (8:30-2:40) general education teacher led instruction following Miami Dade pacing guide
    and the standard curriculum for the state of Florida. Teacher to student ratio of 3:1 to provide explicit hands-on instruction
    for each student's unique needs.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Services and materials require funding that is needed to ensure the agency can provide all these resources for
    blind/visually impaired students. Services that will be implemented include braille instruction and materials (braille paper,
    braille writers, braille note takers, tactile graphic paper), low vision adaptations (lessons in large print, screen reader
    devices and apps, slant boards, magnifiers) and bringing in manipulatives to represent real world objects.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The targeted population are Miami-Dade County students with blindness/visual impairment grades K-2. Students are
    receiving services through the Miami Lighthouse Academy. With additional funding and resources, these numbers can
    grow to support upwards of 30 students.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Students will have access to expert led instruction and materials that reduce barriers for them to receive an appropriate
    education. The agency utilizes statewide assessments (FastBridge, Fast) 2X per year, blind specific assessments
    (EVALS, Oregon Project Assessment, FVLMA) 2X per year, and data from IEP related goals to track student success
    and improvements over time. The data is quantitatively tracked to measure student growth, and benchmarks met relating
    to same age peers on a standard curriculum statewide.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures include a percentage decrease in funding for failing to
    report:
    a. Final Narrative Report of program
    b. Final breakdown and justification of budget categories including direct costs, administrative costs, student costs.
    d. Final Financial Report
    e. Final invoice for incurred expenses.
