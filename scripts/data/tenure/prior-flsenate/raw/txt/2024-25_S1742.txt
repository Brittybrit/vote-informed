14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Securing Florida's supply chain in the face of a truck driving shortage crisis.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Tuition and training grants for students to train for their CDL license so they can be gainfully employed in a high demand
    field and help boost the economy.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Tuition grants.

    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Jobless, college, economically disadvantaged, former incarcerated people.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Protection from Shortages: More truck drivers can help prevent shortages of essential goods and supplies, such as baby
    formula, by ensuring timely and efficient delivery to areas of high demand. Improved availability and reliability of essential
    goods, which can be measured through customer feedback, surveys, and sales data.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Return of a pro-rata portion of the funds if the program fails to meet the established deliverables and performance
    measures, such as the training of a sufficient number of drivers, serves as a penalty for non-compliance and ensures the
    responsible use of taxpayer dollars.
