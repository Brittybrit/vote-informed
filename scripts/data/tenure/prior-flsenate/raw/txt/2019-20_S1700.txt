1. Title of Project: Miami-Dade County Model Lands North Canal Everglades Restoration
2. Senate Sponsor: Annette Taddeo
3. Date of Submission: 02/04/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   Funds will allow filling an unnecessary ditch (Model Lands North Canal) and restoring wetlands near the coast in
   southern Miami-Dade County. Enhanced water retention and wetland recharge will address salt intrusion in the
   Biscayne Aquifer near municipal wellfields, including those under the jurisdiction of Miami-Dade County and the
   Florida Keys Aqueduct Authority. CERP projects in this area will be enhanced through better retention of CERP-
   provided extra water.

11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        Funds will allow filling an unnecessary ditch (Model Lands North Canal) and restoring wetlands near the coast in
        southern Miami-Dade County. Enhanced water retention and wetland recharge will address salt intrusion in the
        Biscayne Aquifer near municipal wellfields, including those under the jurisdiction of Miami-Dade County and
        the Florida Keys Aqueduct Authority. CERP projects in this area will be enhanced through better retention of
        CERP-provided extra water.
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        Surveying, engineering design, bidding, inspections, construction and construction management.
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Reduce drainage to wetlands and restore them in order to better protect/enable continued use of drinking
        water wellfields located in South Miami-Dade County, including but not limited to wellfields under the
        jurisdiction of Miami-Dade Water and Sewer and the Florida Keys Aqueduct Authority. CERP projects in this
        area will be enhanced through better retention of CERP-provided extra water. sewrvices
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        The citizens of southern Miami-Dade County and the Florida Keys
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
      will be measured?
        Protect/enable continued use of drinking water wellfields located in South Miami-Dade County, including but
        not limited to wellfields under the jurisdiction of Miami-Dade Water and Sewer and the Florida Keys Aqueduct
        Authority. CERP projects in this area will be enhanced through better retention of CERP-provided extra water.
        Compare water level patterns before and after construction in groundwater monitoring wells located in South
        Miami-Dade County in the Model Lands.
