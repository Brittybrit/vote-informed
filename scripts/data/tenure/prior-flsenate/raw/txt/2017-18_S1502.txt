3. Project/Program Description:
Designed as a state of the Arts multipurpose building, the African Museum of Arts and Culture (AMAC) Center
is expected to house the largest collection of African Arts and Artifacts outside of the African continent. A
bridge between the old and new, the AMAC center would serve as a repository of contemporary arts from the
Diaspora including exhibits from the Caribbean, Latin American, Europe, Asia, North America and ‘BlackArt’
from the focal community. The land for this project has been donated by the Miami Dade County Government
and the requested funding is strictly for the construction and building of the facility. An important mission of
the AMAC center is to provide a platform for educating our local community and our children about
Africa/African American history. Home to the African Language Academy (ALA) the center would be an
invaluable resource to the more than three hundred and fifty thousand students in our Miami Dade County
Public School district and would serve as a hub for culture and learning for students in neighboring Broward
and Palm Beach Schools. The AMAC project has gained the support of the local government as evidenced by
the donation of land by the Miami Dade County Board of County Commissioners and the city of Miami Gardens
City Council has unanimously passed a resolution supporting the AMAC project and last year the council
submitted a request for $1,000,000.00 (one million dollars) as part of its legislative agenda for funding the

AMAC project. The AMAC Project also received an appropriation of $110,000.00 from the 2016-2017 state

budget.
