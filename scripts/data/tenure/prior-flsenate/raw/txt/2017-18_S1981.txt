a. How will requested funds be spent? Include supporting documentation.

The funds will be utilized to commission a design consulting firm to develop a Stormwater Master Plan that include
but not limited to the following activities:

1. Review previous Stormwater Master Plans.

2. An understanding of current challenges facing the City’s infrastructure and provide recommendations on
implementing resilient components that allow first responders to react and arrive quicker at devastated
areas, restore public safety and initiate clean-up activities.

3. Detailed analysis and review of existing Stormwater management systems currently in place.

4. Review of potential new Stormwater management opportunities such as resiliency and flood proofing.

5. Areview of current and pending Stormwater legislation that may impact future development.

6. To comply with the National Flood Insurance Program — Community Rating System (NFIP — CRS).

7. Toanalyze the extent of sea level rise.

a. Evaluate flood control LOS, vulnerability, resiliency, and potential risk for the stormwater
management system and infrastructure from existing sea level conditions and tides (normal ranges
of high tides and extreme event surges generated from severe storm events.

b. Project potential ranges of sea level rise {SLR} on normal and extreme tide and surge conditions.
Utilizing SLR projects from USACE and NOAA consistent with the Southeast Florida Climate.

c. Evaluate the potential impact from high and intermediate SLR scenarios on the stormwater
management system over the different time horizons (5, 20, 25 and 100 year).

d. f€valuate the potential vulnerability and risk from SLR ranges over the time periods on public
safety, City infrastructure and operations.

e. Pian resilient, adaptable capital improvement features in to the SWMP update to mitigate impacts
through the design period (20, 50, 100 years).

c. Whowill benefit from receipt of State funds?
The proposed action established by the Stormwater Master Plan will benefit the City of Miami will benefit from recipt
of State funds in the following ways:

1. Tourism industry — The City relies heavily on tourism to support its local economy. With improved drainage
infrastructure and resilient building and road infrastructures in place, the City will respond faster in restoring
norma! activities so that commercial enterprises can return to normal business operations with less down-
time. .

2. Jobs to the local economy — The plan will establish drainage infrastructure and structural resiliency needs for
the city as a whole. Design and construction firms will be commissioned for these tasks that will stimulate
job creation for the local economy.

3. Emergency and First Responders — With less damages sustained during storm events, greater accessibility
; allows the City to be better prepared during severe storm events. Local Fire, Police and other emergency
responders will have quicker response time in addressing public safety and clean-up activities.

4. Stormwater Utility Fees — The upgraded drainage infrastructures generate additional revenue that will be
utilized for maintaining the system to ensure peak performance.

5. Environmental Protection — The Stormwater Plan will provide an action plan to counter the threat of sea level
rise and its impact on the Biscayne Aquifer (balancing ecological sustainability with economic constraints}
and the City’s drinking water supply.

6. Flood Mitigation — The Stormwater Master Plan will address flooding and sea level rise in a holistic manner
rather than a “Band-Aid” approach.

a. Property and Asset Protection — The Stormwater Master Plan will address large areas of concern
and the appropriate design of stormwater drainage system that effectively drain and convey
stormwater away from properties, alleviating or eliminating flood damages to public, private and
commercial properties.

b. Upgrading existing drainage infrastructure - The plan will identify potential flooding areas caused

by sea level rise. Upgrades to existing drainage infrastructure include and are not limited to pump
stations, backflow preventer valves and berm construction that will alleviate flood damage to
, private and public properties.

7. Asanintegral component of the National Flood Insurance Program’s Community Rating System (CNFIP-CRS),
the Stormwater Master Plan will assist the City in seeking discounted rates on insurance premiums by
reducing flood risk to property and assets.
