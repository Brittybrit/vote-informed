1. Project Title         Doral - NW 117th Avenue from NW 25 St to NW 34 St Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor        Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request       12/16/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    Reconstruction of NW 117th Avenue will accommodate the realigning and improvement of the intersection with NW 34
   St. This new intersection will be a key to a new ramp to the Turnpike expressway. The works along NW 117 Ave include
   curb and gutters, drainage improvements, new pavement, striping, lighting, signage, sidewalk, relocation of the mass
   arm at NW 25 St, and installation of new traffic signal on NW 34 St. and NW 117 Ave due to the new construction of the
   NW Turnpike ramp.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The NW 117th Avenue reconstruction will realign and upgrade the intersection at NW 34th Street, creating a critical
    connection to a new Turnpike expressway ramp. Improvements along NW 117th Avenue include new curb and gutter,
    upgraded drainage, pavement and striping, lighting, signage, relocation of the mast arm at NW 25th Street, and a new
    traffic signal at NW 34th Street.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds will be spent to improve NW 117 Ave from NW 25 St to NW 34 St, the redesigned intersection with NW 34 St,
    where a new mass arm, signal head, and sidewalks will help pedestrians safely cross the area. The existing mass arm
    at NW 25 St will be relocated.

   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The Project includes roadway improvement, stormwater drainage, new signage and street lighting, sidewalks and new
    mass arm and signal head at NW 34 St. This will increase vehicular and pedestrian safety, and improvement in cargo
    transportation and its incorporation into the Turnpike Expressway and will also serve as a mitigation initiative to help
    prevent flood related losses.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     General at large.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     This project will reduce flooding and ponding along the roadway, improving safety and reliability during major storms
    when issues are typically not visible but still impactful. The installation of new street lighting and sidewalks will enhance
    visibility, expand walkability, and significantly improve pedestrian safety. Additionally, with the construction of the new
    Turnpike ramp, a new traffic signal is essential to manage increased traffic flow and ensure safe movements; a warrant
    study has already confirmed the need for this signal.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     Liquidated damages will be included in the contract documents. Liquidated damages will be based on construction
    cost.
