4. Project/Program Description
    The Doral Police Department (DPD) Presidential/Dignitary Protective Detail Overtime Funding Initiative is a project
   intended to secure overtime funding for expenses incurred by the DPD during protective dignitary events within the City of
   Doral which may include the President of the United States (POTUS) and other protectives. This funding will enable the
   DPD to recover costs associated with providing security support to a variety of federal agencies with regards to POTUS
   and dignitary details, while supporting our national security efforts.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal of this project is to secure overtime funding for expenses incurred by the Doral Police Department during
    POTUS/dignitary related protective events when the President of the United States (POTUS) or protected visits the City
    of Doral and the Trump National Doral Resort. This funding will enable the Department to recover costs associated with
    providing security support to federal agencies with regards to POTUS/dignitary related details while supporting national
    security efforts.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Trump National Doral Resort is a focal point within the City of Doral. The venue hosts United States and
    international federally sponsored dignitary events and draws significant global attention and crowds of protesters and
    supporters alike. These funds will allow the City of Doral to support POTUS/dignitary details, as needed, within Doral and
    surrounding the resort area.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     POTUS/dignitary detail overtime funding will provide the community with enhanced public safety measures during
    POTUS/dignitary visits, ensuring a secure environment for residents, business owners, and visitors. Following multiple
    recent national security incidents, it is essential that this funding assist in providing the required national security support
    during POTUS/dignitary related events within the City of Doral.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population are the residents, visitors, and business owners within the City of Doral, Florida.
    The proposed project will support enhanced security measures for approximately 200,000 individuals within the City of
    Doral, including residents, visitors, business owners, and employees, ensuring a safer and more secure environment for
    all.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The intended benefit of this project is to provide overtime funding for police to enhance public safety by increasing police
    presence and optimizing security measures during POTUS/dignitary related protective events when the President of the
    United States or other protected visit the City of Doral. Following multiple recent national security incidents, it is essential
    that this funding assist in providing the required national security support during related details and visits.

    Each detail will be documented via an approved action plan which includes: the background of the operation,
    recommendations, intelligence/briefing, logistics and equipment needed, and budgetary considerations. Following each
    event, an after action report will be completed monitoring the outcome.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    In the event that the Doral Police Department is unable to utilize these funds for the purpose described herein, the funds
    will be returned to the State.
