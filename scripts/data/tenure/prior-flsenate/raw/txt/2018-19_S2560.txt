1. Title of Project: Surfside Biscaya Island Water Main Crossing
2. Senate Sponsor: Daphne Campbell
3. Date of Submission: 01/29/2018
4. Project/Program Description:
    This grant request for $132,500 would replace the existing shallow bury water main crossing from mainland Surfside
    to Biscaya Island with a directional drillwater main crossing at a greater depth. This additional depth and additional
    ground cover would protect the line from potential impacts, potential leakingof potable water into the Bay and
    potential loss of service to the 35 residents on the Island.
