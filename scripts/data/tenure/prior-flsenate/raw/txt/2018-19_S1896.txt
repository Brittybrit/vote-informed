11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

            The building will serve as a community center with an auditorium and smaller rooms available for short-term
          rental for events and programs and lease to community and non-profit groups. The building will provide space
          for historic, cultural, and arts programming and exhibits for residents and guests. The building is a stop on the
          Citrus County Public Transit line, facilitating access for mobility/transportation-limited for visitors. The School
          will showcase cultural growth and community investment in Hernando, even as it supports those efforts itself.
          It will also provide employment opportunities in an area slow to recover after the recent recession, as positions
          for skilled workers are created by the restoration and 2 positions for full and part-time staff are created by the
          completed project.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           The primary benefit of establishing this space is to create a permanent and centralized location for the
          community to access, utilize, enjoy, and benefit from various activities, including speaker series, cultural and
          artistic performances, educational exhibits, 4H activities, Friends of the Library, Citrus County Parks and
          Recreation, Hernando Heritage Council, and Citrus County Historical Society programs and events, as well as
          those created by other groups. The school is immediately adjacent to the Family Resource Center, making it a
          natural extension of the enrichment activities available to low-income families, particularly those with children
          who can benefit from enhanced educational and community engagement opportunities provided by the use of
          the School.
