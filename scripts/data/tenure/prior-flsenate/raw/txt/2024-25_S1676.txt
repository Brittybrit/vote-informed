4. Project/Program Description
    The Virtual Breast Cancer & Heart Disease Advocate Program (VAP) is an innovative digital opportunity providing college
   students from multiple disciplines, including the next generation of health care professionals, with curriculum covering areas
   not typically addressed in academic programs such as: the importance of leading disease deterrent lifestyles, breast cancer
   and heart disease risk factors, prevention, screening guidelines, related COVID-19 risk factors, clinical trials, and social
   determinants of health. These advocates utilize the knowledge acquired through the program by educating and informing
   their families, peers, and the greater community through social media engagement.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     In collaboration with post-secondary academic professors and institutions, The Virtual Advocate Certificate Program
    (VAP), will provide education and social media outreach opportunities to undergraduate, graduate, and medical students
    through an innovative digital curriculum covering topics such as leading disease deterrent lifestyles, disease prevention
    and early detection related to breast cancer and heart health and COVID-19 risk factors; topics not emphasized in
    existing academic programs.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Women's Breast & Heart Initiative, Florida Affiliate, Inc., will conduct educational webinars covering disease
    prevention and early detection for breast cancer, heart disease and related issues for 1,000 college students. Students
    will share their knowledge through quantifiable social media posts and engagement from those within their sphere of
    influence generating 3,000 social media impressions and 9,000 engagements to help educate others.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Virtual education will be provided to college students represented at nine (9) higher educational institutions. During this
    funding period we will expand capacity to serve an additional two (2) higher educational institutions to facilitate health
    literacy to impact present and future generations for a better today and a brighter tomorrow.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     1,000 college/university students are projected to complete the certificate program.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Colleges and universities receive curriculum support and supplementation through VAP as well as an efficient approach
    to ensure students are engaged in meaningful social media interaction, and professors will incorporate an educational
    program that does not increase their workload. Based on pre and post VAP assessments, students will demonstrate
    increased knowledge and share their gained knowledge with the community through quantifiable social media posts and
    engagement from those within their sphere of personal influence.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The Women's Breast & Heart Initiative, Florida Affiliate, Inc will return unspent funding in correlation to deliverable and/or
    performance measures not met.
