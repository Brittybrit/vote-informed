1.   Project Title          FIRST Economic Development Incubator - Land O'Lakes

2.   Senate Sponsor Wilton Simpson

3.   Date of Request 12/08/2019

4.   Project/Program Description
     The creation of an economic development incubator at Florida's Forensic Institute for Research, Security, and Tactics (FIRST) in Land
     O'Lakes to house economic development initiatives, office space for companies seeking to grow, and other functions.

     FIRST is a unique, first of its kind, training complex in Land O'Lakes that combines law enforcement with academia, private businesses,
     and other governmental entities to develop, research, and train.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      Construction of an economic development incubator building at FIRST, to enhance and expand business development, job creation,
      and retention.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Construction of an economic development incubator that can serve as a place for businesses to start, relocate, and expand.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Economic development in an area targeted by the Pasco EDC as a priority area along the US 41 corridor.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      Those seeking employment and companies seeking to expand to this location in conjunction with the training and research being
      done at FIRST.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      Business development and job creation, measured by jobs created.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      This will be for the construction of a building; penalty for not constructing building will be returning funds.
