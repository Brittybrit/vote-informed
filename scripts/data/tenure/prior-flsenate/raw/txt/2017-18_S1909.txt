9, Program Performance (if needed, include additional documentation):

a. _How will requested funds be spent? Include supporting documentation.

The funds will be spent on needed repairs and drainage improvements as identified in the Citywide
drainage assessment performed in December 2016 (see attached locations and fist of improvements
identified}.

b. _ Identify expected program results and the expected benefit associated from the requested funds.
Project results include decreased flooding caused by rainfall, seasonal tides and storm events. Currently
these flood events cause life safety issues due to limited access to and from properties, increased traffic
congestion, as well as damage to roadways and adjacent properties. The drainage improvements will
mitigate these adverse effects of flooding.
