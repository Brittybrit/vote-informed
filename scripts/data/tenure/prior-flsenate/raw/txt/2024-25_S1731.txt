    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     We aim to equip students, community members and small businesses with essential skills and provide access to the
    tools needed to thrive in a competitive job market by leveraging AI technologies. We deliver training workshops that will
    enhance the job-market competitiveness of the participants, set up state-of-the-art AI software and equipment within our
    learning spaces and forge partnerships with local businesses to disseminate innovative AI-based solutions for workforce
    development.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Activities include faculty and staff professional development, course development for AI Tools for Small Businesses,
    integration of Simucase into Social Work curriculum, student training and workshops for community members using
    Vmock platform and VR-based training for nursing students and practitioners.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Services include AI-based assistance with resumes, video mock interviews LinkedIn profiles and elevator pitches using
    VMock SMART platform delivered to students and community. We will conduct Office 365 Workshops and access to
    software for community and small business owners. We will train Social Work students and practitioners using the
    Simucast platform and train Nursing students and practitioners.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Students and local small businesses will be served with this project. The quantity of individuals is not yet determined.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     For students, a system will be put into place to analyze learners' competence, confidence, and readiness to apply AI-
    based tools in their respective fields through:
    • Pre- and post-training assessment
    • Instructor evaluation
    • Self-reporting and reflection

    For small businesses, a baseline assessment will be conducted to determine the percentage of small businesses already
    utilizing AI-based solutions for operational improvement in the target community. After the program's completion, a
    systematic tracking system will be established to monitor the adoption of AI-based solutions by small businesses.
    Program administrators and support teams will conduct regular follow-up
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     No suggested penalties
