4. Project/Program Description
    To address the mental health needs of youth, and to foster resilience and well-being, Ohel will expand trainings to
   educators and professional implementing our social-emotional learning curricula and resources as well as substance use

   natural disasters, and community tragedies.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Ohel will continue to mobilize a dedicated Trauma Team across South Florida to provide services that address acute
    trauma, such as suicide, drownings, sudden deaths, fatal diagnoses, natural disasters, and community tragedies.
    Using Ohel's social-emotional curriculum and resources, Ohel seeks to foster resilience and well-being in children and
    youth by building educators' capacity to address mental health needs. In addition, schools will be offered our substance
    use prevention programming.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Activities and service include crisis intervention for children, schools, families, and communities following unanticipated
    tragedies; parent, teacher, and student education to promote resilience toward improved mental health; and substance
    use prevention program for children and youth, grades 5-12.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Direct services to educators include resilience education to build and strengthen emotional well-being among students
    and families; crisis response and support following unanticipated tragedies; and substance use prevention
    programming, currently provided in 13 Florida Day, Middle, and High Schools.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Elderly persons, persons, with poor mental health, preschool children, grade-school students, high school students,
    university/college students, victims of crime, and youth at risk.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     Outcomes:
    Enhance educators' and students' resilience and well-being, trauma Team ensures access to mental health
    interventions as well as grief and bereavement services, enhance individuals' coping skills and increase their
    connections to resources in the aftermath of traumatic events, build community resilience, build community resilience,
    Ohel's substance use prevention programming for students, educators, parents and communities provides education
    regarding substance use prevention, with the goal of enhancing students’ refusal skills and understanding of the
    harm/dangers of substance abuse.

    Measure:
    Pre/post-surveys will track efficacy and impact of teacher trainings and school programming, track service utilization via
    number of individuals served, community Engagement numbers, feedback from referring professionals and partners
    with satisfaction surveys after events, and surveys will be administered to track the effectiveness of substance use
    prevention programming.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     A detailed spending plan and deliverables will be provided to the agency. Ohel will ensure transparency and efficient
    use of funds to serve children, families and educators in South Florida.
