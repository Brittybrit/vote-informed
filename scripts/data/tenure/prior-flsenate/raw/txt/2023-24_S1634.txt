1. Project Title          Miami Gardens Stormwater Repetitive Losses - Flooding Project

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        02/20/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    This is a Stormwater Drainage Project that will minimize the existing flooding problems of homes that were identified by
   FEMA of continuous insurance claims of internal home damages by water going inside the home during storms. Therefore,
   these repetitive area drainage projects will eliminate these flood damages and potential loss of properties and contents
   after the underground drainage piping systems are constructed. These drainage pipes will take up the Stormwater before
   entering the properties causing the home damages.

     The goal is to minimize the existing flooding problems of homes that were identified by FEMA of continuous insurance
    claims of internal home damages by water going inside the home during storms. Therefore, these repetitive area
    drainage projects will eliminate these flood damages and potential loss of properties and contents after the underground

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funds will be used for the construction of Stormwater Underground Drainage Systems around the right of Way in

    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     None
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population estimated is from 51-100.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit is to minimize the existing flooding problems of homes that were identified by FEMA of continuous
    insurance claims of internal home damages by water going inside the home during storms. Therefore, this property owner
    will have a better quality of living (peace of mind) if there is a storm events that could flood their home. By the homes not
    flooding anymore and damaging their property and contents.
