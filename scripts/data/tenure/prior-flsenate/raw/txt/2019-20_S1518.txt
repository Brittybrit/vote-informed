1. Title of Project: Septic to Sewer, District A, Phase 1
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 02/07/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   Springs in Florida have experienced severe degradation in water quality over many decades due to fertilizer use,
   inadequate Onsite Sewage Treatment and Disposal Systems (OSTDS), and other factors which increase Nitrate levels
   in ground water. Weeki Wachee is one such spring that has been significantly impacted. HCUD performed a "Septic
   to Sewer Conversion Study" dated November 2016 which produced recommendations for converting existing
   OSTDS to centralized sewer. One of the first priority locations was District A which is comprised of approximately
   900 existing residential lots. This project will provide the design and construction of a wastewater collection and
   transmission system to convert approximately half of District A (450 lots +\-) to centralized sewer.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         Converting approximately 450 existing residential lots to centralized sewer.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         Design and construction of pump stations, force mains, gravity sewer system, manholes, etc. necessary to
         retro-fit this area with a wastewater collection/transmission system. Project also includes decommissioning
         existing septic tanks and installing new service laterals for connecting existing homes.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         n/a
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         Approximately 450 existing residential lots.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         Completion of this project will result in reducing the groundwater loading of total nitrogen by about 3,800 lbs
         per year with an estimated life cycle cost of about $114 per lb of TN.
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         The Weeki Wachee BMAP mandates nitrogen reduction for existing OSTDS within the Priority Focus Area (PFA).
         Since District A, Phase I is within the PFA, there is already state mandated criteria for meeting the performance
         measures.
