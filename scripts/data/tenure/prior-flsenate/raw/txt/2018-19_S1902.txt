1. Title of Project: Bal Harbour Village – Roadway infrastructure improvements and rehabilitation
2. Senate Sponsor: Daphne Campbell
3. Date of Submission: 12/14/2017
4. Project/Program Description:
    The goal of this project is to improve traffic flow, pedestrian flow, and safety of roadways and sidewalks of Collins
    Avenue and 96th Street in Bal Harbour Village. This will entail the removal, relocation and introduction of new
    signals, intersections, curb-cuts, sidewalks and plantings. Additionally, this will significantly improve traffic flow as
    this intersection is a main ingress and egress point for evacuation routes.

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          Improvement of average traffic flow within Bal Harbour Village by as much as 15% by improving traffic flow,
         pedestrian flow, and safety of roadways and sidewalks of Collins Avenue and 96th Street. This will entail
         removal, relocation and introduction of new signals, intersections, curb-cuts, and sidewalks. Additionally, this
         will significantly improve traffic flow as this intersection is a main ingress and egress point for evacuation
         routes.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          Village residents, residents of the region who live and work in this area, as well as tourists to South Florida.
         This intersection is a critical ingress and egress point for the area evacuation route.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          Improvement of average traffic flow within the village of Bal Harbour by as much as 15%, which will be
         monitored and measured by contracted traffic studies.

    g. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
       penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
        Refund of funding allocated.
