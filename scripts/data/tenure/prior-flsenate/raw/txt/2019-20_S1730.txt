1. Title of Project: Miami Brickell Bay Drive Shoreline Design Improvements
2. Senate Sponsor: Jose Rodriguez
3. Date of Submission: 01/30/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   Brickell Bay Drive, a major financial district, experienced significant storm surges, flooding and sustained damage
   during Hurricane Irma. The goal of this project is to strengthen seawalls and introduce structural features to
   attenuate wave action, storm surges and king tides. It is the City’s long-term goal to protect the largest Financial
   District in the State of Florida. This area was the main breech point during Hurricane Irma and the enhancement of
   storm surge protection systems for this area will reduce infrastructure and economic vulnerabilities and provide the
   opportunity for a faster recovery.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        This area was the main breaching point for storm surge and flooding during Hurricane Irma. The enhancement
        of storm surge protection systems for this area and strengthening the storm drainage system will significantly
        reduce infrastructure and economic vulnerabilities and provide the opportunity for a faster recovery to resume
        regular business operations.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        The funds are for the design of a seawall system with structural features to attenuate wave action, storm
        surges and king tides. Additionally, tidal valves will be installed to existing storm drain systems, reducing tidal
        flooding to critical infrastructure, walkways, and city streets.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        A linear park will be constructed along the corridor to promote recreational opportunities for adjacent buildings
        housing residents and bank staff. Roadside kiosk are established to encourage commerce activity in the area.
        The park also serves as a drainage component.

    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         The Brickell area has residences, businesses and the State's largest financial district with an area population of
         56,700. Since 2000, the area has seen a 97.6% population increase while additional development is underway
         and expected.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         This project serves as a prototype to demonstrate resilient seawall structures and waterfront design concepts
         that will be utilized for neighboring areas such as Bayside and Edgewater. The project will develop design
         specifications to reduce infrastructure and economic vulnerabilities, to reduce insurance cost, to enhance
         property value and allow a faster recovery for the financial district to resume regular business operations.
         Measured outcomes are determined by reduction of insurance costs and reduced flooding impacts. Moreover,
         the project changes the negative perception of Miami as a "Sinking City" and promotes the City's efforts in
         combating sea-level rise, retain confidence in the local economy and attracting investments in the area.
