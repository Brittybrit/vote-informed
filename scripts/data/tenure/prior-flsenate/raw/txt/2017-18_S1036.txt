9.    Program Performance (if needed, include additional documentation):
      a. How will requested funds be spent? Include supporting documentation.
          Each participating coalition member organization will receive $30 per enrolled student
      b. Identify expected program results and the expected benefit associated from the requested funds.
          The return on investment a calculated by independent evaluator is $11.53 for every $ invested. This
          return is based on reductions in teen pregnancies, involvement in drugs and alcohol, increased graduation
          rates and reduced incidences of STIs, suicide, intimate partner violence, single parenting and other
          negative consequences of risky behaviors.
      c. Who will benefit from receipt of State funds?
          Members of the Florida Healthy Choices Coalition who have been approved by local schools or youth
          organizations to provide the full program, including pre/post tests and surveys.
      d. What specific measures will be used to document performance data for the project, if it receives funds?
