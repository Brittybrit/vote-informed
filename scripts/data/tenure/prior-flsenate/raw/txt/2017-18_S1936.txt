3. — Project/Program Description:
In an effort to reduce the amount of sewage produced, the Town of Bay Harbor Islands seeks to eliminate
groundwater infiltration into the sanitary sewer system by lining all of its laterals. To date, the Town has lined
all of its sewer mains and is currently in the process of lining all of its sewer laterals. Out of a total 824 sewer
laterals, the Town has lined a total of 149. The cost of lining the remaining 675 laterals is $2.1 million. The
Town is providing the matching $1.1 million.
