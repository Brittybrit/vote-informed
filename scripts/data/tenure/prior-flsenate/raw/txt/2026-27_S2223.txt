4. Project/Program Description
    The project is to acquire a Rapid Deployment Communication System (RDCS), also known as Communication on
   Wheels or COW) and a fixed spectrum analyzer to establish resilient, uninterrupted emergency communications and
   advanced threat detection capabilities in Miami-Dade County, safeguarding hundreds of thousands of people during
   large scale events. The spectrum analyzer will be used to triangulate the position of unauthorized radio frequency
   transmissions.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     These funds directly enhance public safety and emergency response, providing critical communication infrastructure
    that protects citizens and visitors during mass gatherings. This includes acquiring a Rapid Deployment Communication
    System (RDCS, also known as Communication on Wheels or COW) and a fixed spectrum analyzer. The spectrum
    analyzer will be used to triangulate the position of unauthorized radio frequency transmissions. These upgrades aim to
    maintain secure and reliable communications amid increased security demands.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds will cover acquisition and deployment of RDCS mobile communication units and fixed spectrum analyzer
    systems at key locations. These activities will support real-time emergency coordination, manage large crowds, detect
    security threats like unauthorized drones, and ensure reliable communication among all public safety agencies during
    the event.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens and visitors will receive enhanced public safety protections with faster emergency responses, effective crowd
    management, and real-time threat detection. The improved communication infrastructure will enable law enforcement,
    fire rescue, EMS, and transit agencies to coordinate efficiently, ensuring a safe environment day-to-day and during
    mass gatherings.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is the 2.8 million residents in Miami-Dade County and thousands of visitors for major events
    hosted in the County.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The project’s benefit is a secure, safe environment with improved emergency response and prevention of security
    disruptions such as rogue drones or signal interference. Success will be measured by monitoring communication
    system reliability, emergency response times, and security incident reports before, during, and after event activities
    through interagency evaluations.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
