14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal of the City's adaptive re-use project is to minimize flooding and ponding along the assigned roadway segments.
    also, the installation of street lighting and sidewalk will increase pedestrian safety.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds will be spent for the construction of the adaptive Re-use of Doral Arts District between NW 58 St and NW 54 St
    and between NW 87 Ave and NW 79 Ave.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The Project includes roadway reconstruction, new sidewalks, parallel parking, storm water drainage, bike lanes (where
    possible), and street lighting. This will increase pedestrian safety and will also serve as a mitigation initiative to help
    prevent flood related losses.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All of our pedestrians and commuters
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Minimize flooding and ponding along roadway segment. Installation of street lighting and sidewalk installation. Flooding
    and ponding is not visible during major storms. Increase light for visibility and pedestrian safety.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Liquidated damages will be assessed based on the contract price and time loss.
