11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           Extend Runway 1-19 from 5,000 feet to 6,500; construct 3,000 foot service road to access infield site
          development.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Allow larger aircraft to access Zephyrhills Municipal Airport (ZMA) to encourage aviation and industrial
          development with runway extension. The west side service road will open the infield to site development and
          runway connectivity.
