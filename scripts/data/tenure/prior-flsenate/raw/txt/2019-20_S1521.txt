1. Title of Project: ZooTampa Florida Panther Medical Facility & Habitat
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 02/08/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   As part of our conservation mission, ZooTampa provides a haven and medical care for Florida panthers deemed
   non-releasable by FWC. Because their numbers are growing, human/panther conflicts are becoming more frequent
   and there is a critical need for medical facilities to treat and release panthers. The funds will also allow for
   construction of 6 habitats for up to 6 panthers non releasable panthers. Currently, three non-releasable panthers
   reside at ZooTampa with outdoor habitats for only one panther. The three rotate days.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         Protect and treat the endangered Florida Panther species; enrich cultural experience; improve education of
         species,
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         Medical treatment for injured and sick Florida panthers Permanent housing/habitats for non-releaseable
         panthers
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         The funding will provide much needed space for panthers designated as "nuisance" animals (i.e, become
         dependent on household pets for food). Ability to see and learn about panthers to the more than 1,000,000
         annual visitors to ZooTampa. Enhanced education opportunities for more than 150,000 Florida students who
         participate in ZooTampa education programs and outreach annually.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         Residents and visitors to the Tampa Bay region.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         To treat injured Florida Panthers as well as care for Florida Panthers deemed non-releasable. The methodology
         would be the rise in Panther population.
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         Reversion of funds
