   advances, as well as lessons learned from our last several storm seasons. In partnership with DEM, funding would
   be distributed to the North East Florida Planning Council for distribution to the remaining 9 Regional Planning
   Councils for the study update.

11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        These funds will be used to begin to update the Regional Evacuation Studies across the state as found in
        s.163.3178 (2) (d), F.S.
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        Tasks include the design and implementation of a behavioral survey to determine the evacuation behavior of
        citizens from the most recent storm seasons of 2016-2018, and to begin work on a methodology to produce an
        updated Regional Evacuation Study including data needs and technological advances in coordination with local,
        state and federal emergency management partners.
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Valuable data for local and statewide decisions makers regarding the evacuation behavior of Floridians. This
        directly impacts hurricane preparedness efforts and helps to protect the citizens of Florida.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        The entire population of Florida is vulnerable to the effects of a hurricane, whether directly (storm surge,
        winds, flooding) or indirectly (increased congestion on roadways, lack of services, etc.).
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
