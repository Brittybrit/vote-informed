1. Project Title          Holocaust Learning Experience Education Platform Expansion

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        2/17/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    Holocaust Learning Experience, a vendor approved by FLDOE, provides an effective education platform through a
   specialized learning management system to deliver state-mandated Holocaust education that is immediately deployable
   and accessible to all school districts in Florida. It is necessary to engage the remaining 42 Florida school districts through
   the provision of semi-annual professional orientation and development to ensure continued platform utilization, meeting
   state-mandated educational requirements for Holocaust education and resiliency education FS1003.42 and anti-bullying
   instruction mandate 1006.147.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Engage the 42 remaining Florida school districts, along with the Department of Juvenile Justice, to promote the
    implementation of the Holocaust Learning Experience while continuing the serve the current 25 school districts.

   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Gather outcome measures (data) from participating districts demonstrating student knowledge of the Holocaust and how
    the curriculum fosters student character development through lessons from Holocaust resources. Ensure historically
    accurate instruction of the Holocaust throughout Florida schools, delivered directly into the classroom with the educators
    are facilitators of the curriculum resources and not the purveyors of the content. Preserve the testimonies and legacies of
    Holocaust survivors for ongoing impact on students.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Holocaust standards, mandated by the state legislature, are achieved and consistently delivered throughout the entire
    Florida's public education system.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    Students in Florida's Public School System, grades 5 to 12, 2 million students.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     Provide examination of what it means to be a responsible and respectful person, for the purposes of encouraging
    tolerance in a pluralistic society and for nurturing and protecting democratic values and institutions, including the policy,
    definition, and historical antisemitism, mitigating the language of hate, bias, and prejudice. Student surveys, quizzes, and
    classroom discussions will be offered and led by the classroom educator. Holocaust Learning Experience will collect,
    aggregate and analyze the data according to the outcomes set forth by Florida Department of Education.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    Funds will be returned to the state.
