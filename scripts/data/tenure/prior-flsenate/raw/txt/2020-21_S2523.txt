1.   Project Title          Renovation of the Saint Leo University Aquatics Center and Lap Pool

2.   Senate Sponsor Wilton Simpson

3.   Date of Request 01/21/2020

4.   Project/Program Description
     Saint Leo University will enhance its existing aquatics area by expanding to a 12 lane NCAA competition pool with viewing galleries, areas
     for teams and coaches, restroom facilities, and a digital timing/reporting and scoring system. The new competitive class aquatics venue
     will potentially support the university's championship class collegiate athletes and it is the expressed interest of Pasco County area high
     school and their swim programs to have such a facility in east Pasco County. The pool and aquatics area will enhance Pasco County's
     image as an attractive living destination for the growing Tampa Bay area population, and for families who have the expectation and desire
     of competitive swimming opportunities for their high school aged children. The Boys and Girls Club, Police Athletic League, Silver
     Sneakers and other citizens of Pasco County will have use of area; without state funding a smaller renovation project will be undertaken to
     solely meet Saint Leo University needs.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      Support the ability for Saint Leo University to host competitive swim meets, provide facilities for nine area high schools to host swim
      meets and to positively impact the economy of Pasco County through the hosting of said meets.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      A 12-lane NCAA competitive pool.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      An opportunity for use of a 12-lane lap swimming pool and the opportunity to be spectators at swim meets and other aquatic events.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      1-25000




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      Number of collegiate and high school swim meets hosted, number of guests who attend meets, number of high schools and high
      school students who reserve practice time at the lap pool.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Return of funding.
