4. Project/Program Description
    The funds being requested will be used to purchase body-worn cameras for officers. The specific goal is to enhance
   transparency, accountability, and public trust by documenting interactions between law enforcement and the community.
   The body-worn cameras will help provide accurate and objective evidence in situations involving police encounters, reduce
   complaints and misconduct, improve officer behavior, and support investigations and legal proceedings. Additionally, they
   will assist in strengthening community relations and promoting fair policing practices.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The specific purpose or goal of the funds requested for body-worn cameras (BWCs) is to enhance law enforcement
    transparency, accountability, and effectiveness. The funds will be used to purchase the cameras, implement the
    necessary technology and infrastructure, and support the training and operational costs associated with their deployment.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     To meet the purpose of the funds requested for body-worn cameras, the following activities & services will be provided:
    Procurement & Deployment of Body-Worn Cameras, Data Storage & Management System, System Integration &
    Software Development, Ongoing Training and Support, Monitoring & Evaluation. This ultimately achieves the goals of
    improving transparency, accountability, & trust amongst citizens.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Overall, the funding for body-worn cameras will directly improve the quality of interactions between law enforcement and
    the community, leading to enhanced public safety, trust, and fairness in policing including but not limited to transparency,
    accountability, faster resolutions to complaints, improved trust, support legal processes, and public accessibility.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population served by this project includes both law enforcement officers and the general public—specifically,
    the individuals with whom officers interact during the course of their duties. The project directly serves the police officers
    within the department who will be equipped with body-worn cameras. The broader community, including individuals
    involved in police encounters, will be served by the body-worn camera project. This includes citizens interacting with
    officers at any level (ranging from civil dispute to criminal stops), juveniles, and victims, witnesses, and defendants whose
    interactions with law enforcement may become part of investigations or legal proceedings. Officers with the body worn
    camera are dependent on the size of the department which in the case of Miami Springs can be 30-50. As for civilians, it
    is difficult to predict the exact number of individuals served, as this will depend on the frequency of police interactions
    (estimated 1000+ yearly).
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The expected benefit of implementing body-worn cameras (BWCs) is to increase transparency, accountability, and trust
    between the police department and the community. By capturing video footage of interactions between officers and the
    public, BWCs provide a clear, unbiased record that can help resolve disputes, improve officer behavior, better
    evidence/legal defense, and enhance public safety. These outcomes can be measured through public trust surveys,
    officer feedback, quantitative statistics related to arrests, complaint reduction, use of force incidents, recidivism rates,
    legal outcomes, etc.
