4. Project/Program Description
    Funds will support the BBharts Giving Harts Project, a one-of-a-kind performing arts scholarship program for students of
   low-income immigrant families in Unincorporated Miami-Dade County, FL by BBharts. Giving Harts currently serves over
   250 youth, at no cost, through on site classes at BBharts. Yearly, students present a public access musical theatre
   production, as well as multiple outreach performances throughout the community. Though all BBharts classes have always
   been fully inclusive, Giving Harts allows experienced students of ages 13+ to give back and serve the community by
   teaching and mentoring young performers ages 6+. The program originated from BBharts' belief that the meaning of life is
   to find your gift and the purpose of life is to give it away.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The specific goal and purpose of the funds requested is to continue to serve the population of 250+ students that the
    Giving Harts Project currently serves. This funding will make theatrical education possible for these students, helping to
    improve their social-emotional intelligence, communication and leadership skills, emotional wellbeing, as well as providing
    a sense of community for them. Giving Harts' primary goal has always been high-quality performing arts education for
    each and every student. The program also aims to counter the challenges that Hispanic immigrant students face when
    migrating to the states including: language barriers, cultural adjustment, economic difficulties, discrimination and bias,
    trauma, and violence. Giving Harts focuses on helping students manifest what they are capable of achieving.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Activities provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds include theatrical classes in acting, dance (ballet, jazz,
    contemporary, hip hop, lyrical, ballroom, acro, strength and conditioning, musical theatre, flamenco) and singing as well
    as technical theatre including stagecraft, costuming, makeup, lighting design, and stage management. Classes are taught
    by veteran BBharts students and their mentors who are experienced in their various artistic fields. Students also
    participate in fully produced musical theatre stage productions each year which they rehearse and perform in addition to
    classes. Students and instructors also participate in multiple community outreach activities yearly.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Direct services include artistic and social-emotional education. The mentorship process presents a win-win opportunity.
    On one end older students are developing their artistic, leadership, and communication skills as they are being mentored
    by teachers in order to give back. On the other end new low income kids are being taught dance and theater while
    creating bonds that help their social-emotional development.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Giving Harts serves approximately 250 Miami Dade county students, ages 6 and up, from underserved and
    underprivileged communities in Hialeah, Hialeah Gardens, and Unincorporated Miami-Dade. Students currently come
    from BBharts partner schools: Mater Gardens, Mater Lakes, Palm Springs North, Bob Graham, as well as from Miami
    Dade County public schools.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Onsite students at BBharts and their parents are given pre and post assessments and surveys in order to measure
    outcomes and satisfaction. Additionally, partner school classes are evaluated once per year to ensure satisfactory
    student progress. Student attendance is tracked carefully for all groups, and perhaps most effectively and importantly, all
    students participate in multiple theatre performances onstage each year. This is where progress and development are
    most evident. Success is defined as follows: 100% of all students will have improved in all parameters (cognition, fine
    motor skills, cooperation, self-confidence, etc.) as evidenced by pre and post assessments, 100% of students will have
    completed all rehearsals and performances throughout the session, and 100% of students and parents will be satisfied
    with the program.
