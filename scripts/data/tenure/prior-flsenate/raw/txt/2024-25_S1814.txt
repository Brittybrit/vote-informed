4. Project/Program Description
    To provide adequate capacity for the proposed flows from the Hoffman Gardens development, the four (4) 21-inch pipe
   segments between MH3070118 and MH3070014 are proposed to be upsized to 30- inches. With these improvements, the
   City's downstream gravity system is anticipated to have adequate capacity to accommodate the proposed flows from the
   Hoffman Gardens development without surcharging.
