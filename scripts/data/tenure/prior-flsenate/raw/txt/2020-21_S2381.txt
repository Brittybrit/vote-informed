1.   Project Title          Runway 1-19 Extension - Land Purchase and Skydive Drainage Canal - Zephyrhills

2.   Senate Sponsor Wilton Simpson

3.   Date of Request 11/07/2019

4.   Project/Program Description
     Zephyrhills Municipal Airport received funding in 2018-2019 to extend Runway 1-19 by 1,500' south in order to accommodate larger
     aircraft and encourage economic development. The extension project is under design and engineering, and awaiting FAA final approval.
     To facilitate the required runway protection zone (RPZ), the city needs to purchase additional acreage. Skydive City leases 15 acres from
     the city at the eastern edge of Runway 1-19 and some of its drop zone/landing areas will be impacted by the runway extension. This will
     require clearing land, tree removal, installing culverts and piping to an existing drainage ditch, and other improvements/changes with an
     estimated cost of $2 million. The City of Zephyrhills is seeking $2,000,000 to acquire 1.3 acres south of Runway 1-19 to clear/prepare
     property as part of the RPZ, and pipe the existing drainage ditch.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      Finalize and complete the 2018-2019 state appropriation project. This parcel is necessary to secure the runway protection zone
      (RPZ), a federal requirement. The drainage improvements are needed to reduce the impact to the "landing area" for Skydive City.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Purchase and clearing of property and the installation of stormwater piping and grading.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Will conclude extension of Runway 1-19 Zephyrhills Municipal Airport project, as authorized by the Florida Legislature in 2018.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      Target population consists of general aviation users (pilots, aviation an aeronautical businesses), airport tenants, and economic
      development proponents in Pasco County and west central Florida. The aviation community and citizens impacted by employment
      opportunities. May serve one thousand individuals with job growth and an improved economy for Zephyrhills and Pasco County.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      The benefit of this project is completion of the Runway 1-19 extension to 6,200' and providing optimum use and benefit of the
      Zephyrhills Municipal Airport. Economic and aviation industry growth - business/industry added to tax rolls and job growth.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Failure of the City of Zephyrhills to meet deliverables and performance measures may result in loss of future grant opportunities.
