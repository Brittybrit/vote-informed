4. Project/Program Description
    FSU is seeking $125m to construct the 137,700 square foot FSU Health Tallahassee Center (FSU Health) that is the
   cornerstone of development of the academic health center (AHC) with Tallahassee Memorial Healthcare (TMH). The center
   will co-locate FSU's medical and nursing education with clinical and translational research to serve as an innovation district
   focused on transformative clinical care, research, biomedical innovation, and education.
   Specifically, the center will:
   - Train doctors, nurses, and other healthcare providers with the most advanced technology including digital health and
   virtual reality,
   - Provide the residents of Tallahassee and region with access to the most advanced clinical care, and
   - Research emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence for a data-driven learning healthcare system.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The FSU Health Center will provide infrastructure to support expansion of new and existing programs in nursing, allied
    health, medical education, and Graduate Medical Education (GME) and foster interprofessional education to train
    doctors, nurses, and other healthcare professionals.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     FSU Health will facilitate the transformation of Tallahassee into a regional healthcare destination attracting patients from
    throughout the area and neighboring states. Patients will have access to new drugs, devices, and cutting-edge clinical
    care. The five-story building will accommodate the FSU College of Medicine's Annex Internal Medicine Residency
    program, the TMH Family Medical Residency program, the Tallahassee Regional Medical School Campus, clinics,
    simulation labs, and ample wet and dry lab research space for students.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     FSU plays an essential role in training the next generation of Florida healthcare workers with nearly 60% of its physician
    alumni practicing in Florida and over 8,000 nurse graduates. Joint planning between FSU and TMH is already underway
    for class size growth and additional residency programs in high need specialties, such as nursing, geriatrics, psychiatry,
    OB-GYN, and emergency medicine. The new space will allow FSU to prepare generations of outstanding healthcare
    professionals, many of whom will stay and practice in Florida, and ultimately raise the quality of care throughout the state
    and region.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     FSU and TMH have a shared mission to support the healthcare needs of local and regional communities. In recent joint
    planning efforts, TMH and FSU identified clinical focus areas based on the urgent need of the state and region which
    include health disparities, aging, oncology, and cardiovascular disease. Preliminary conservative estimates expect that
    FSU Health will add more than $217m annually and support more than 1,500 jobs directly and indirectly. FSU Health will
    accommodate at least 35 principal investigators, producing an estimated $17m of additional grant funding for research.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Benefit: FSU Health will be a transformative center to house research and education activities that aims to provide long-
    lasting impact on regional economic development, accelerate innovate research, facilitate the establishments of
    destination clinical programs, and educate the healthcare workforce of the future.
    Outcome: Close the gap of quality healthcare in the Big Bend region, and help mitigate the shortage of critically-needed
    healthcare workforce.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Return of funding to the state.
