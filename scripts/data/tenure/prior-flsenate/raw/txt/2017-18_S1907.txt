9. Program Performance (if needed, include additional documentation):

a. How will requested funds be spent? Include supporting documentation.

The City of North Miami experiences massive flooding within the Arch Creek North/Arch Creek South
drainage Area, The city is within the South Florida Water Management District (SFWMD) which requires
that all areas within its district meet State water quality standards, as set forth in Chapter 17-302, Florida
Administrative Code (FAC). The requested fund will be used for improvements to ensure that the
volumetric retention/detention requirements stipulated in the SFWMD Permit Volume IV are met within
the drainage basin.

b. _ Identify expected program results and the expected benefit associated from the requested funds.
Stormwater runoff picks contaminants on its path and deposit them in water bodies of the state. The
proposed drainage improvements will help filter pollutants and reduce peak flows, helping to protect
stream channels, improve roadside drainage, reduce flooding and reduce the need for roadway
maintenance.

c. Who will benefit from receipt of State funds?

The local community will benefit from a reduction in street flooding. Improving the stormwater
management system will also result in an improved quality of life to the residents living with the basin.
Flooding undermines the structural integrity of roadway pavements. Therefore, the service years and
quality of existing road networks will be improved by the proposed improvements. Access to property,
businesses and amenities are also improved for pedestrian and vehicular traffic.

d. _What specific measures will be used to document performance data for the project, if it receives funds?
Monitoring the poliutant loading after proposed improvement and comparing with that before the
proposed improvement.
