1. Title of Project: Village of Pinecrest-Pinecrest Gardens Botanical Restoration
2. Senate Sponsor: Jose Rodriguez
3. Date of Submission: 01/23/2018
4. Project/Program Description:
    Pinecrest Gardens features over 1,000 varieties of rare and exotic tropical plants and palm trees in a native tropical
    hardwood and cypress setting. It isdesignated a historic property by Miami-Dade County and it is listed on the
    National Register of Historic Places. Funds will be used to replace the plants andtrees lost due to Hurricane Irma.
