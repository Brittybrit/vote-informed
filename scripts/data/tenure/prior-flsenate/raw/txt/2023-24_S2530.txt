4. Project/Program Description
    The purpose of the Scouting-Character Education (formerly Learning for Life) funding is to enhance and improve student
   performance of low-performing, at-risk students, by providing additional learning opportunities through our local Scouting-
   Character Education programs. The program is an age-appropriate and grade level specific program for students in grades
   K-12. Character education lessons focus on the following traits: Positive Attitude, Citizenship, Cooperation, Responsibility,
   Respect, Caring, Self-Discipline, Perseverance, Trustworthiness and Integrity. The state funding will be delivered to Miami-
   Dade and Lee Public School Districts. Each council works in partnership with each school district to deliver Scouting -
   Character Education to the student population. The program’s objectives are to increase the knowledge of character and
   leadership traits of the target population and provide character education to over 63,000 students statewide.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Scouting-Character Education collaborates with specific school districts to deliver Florida mandated “Character
    Education” programs per FS 1003.42 (s) and verified by the DOE quarterly program delivery report to meet or exceed
    requirements to a set number of students. The project provides a classroom based Scouting-Character education for
    students with focus on at-risk, low performing, and Title 1 schools and to provide students with learning opportunities,
    mentorship, and life skills.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Scouting - Character Education curriculum is delivered to students through enhanced instruction, mentoring
    activities, and leadership training, and added value activities that offer additional character building opportunities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Scouting - Character Education provides 8-10 character education lessons for every student participating in the program
    in each school district. Participating schools include the state's lowest performing schools, Title 1, and at-risk student
    populations. Lessons support schools with topics on respect, citizenship, responsibility, good decision making, kindness,
    and honesty.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population are A. Grade School Students, and B. At-Risk Youth, in the public schools of Miami-Dade County
    and Lee County. Upon full funding, over 63,000 youth are served with the Scouting - Character Education program.
    (Greater than 800)
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Expected Benefit and Outcome - To Improve quality of education - In the past. performance data has included quarterly
    implementation reports, end of year evaluation reports, project deliverable forms, quarterly program activity report forms,
    verification of staff and teacher training, and pre/post surveys.
    Measurement Methodology - In each school district, the Scouting-Character Education (formerly Learning for Life)
    program collects data on students' understanding of eight core character traits, using a pre/post survey method.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Under prior Scouting / Learning for Life programs, DOE may assess performance penalties of $2 per student if the
    program does not deliver the specific number of lessons in a school year.
