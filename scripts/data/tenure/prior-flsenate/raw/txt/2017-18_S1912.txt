3. Project/Program Description:
Project Name: Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition System (SCADA). This is the priority project for the
City of Miami Beach. SCADA is a software system that will operate the City’s water, sewage disposal and
stormwater pumps through automated technology. It is important to the city because it will allow the pumps
to be operated efficiently from a central location, and it will identify system failures and prevent public health
situations such as failure in the sewage system, causing backup to streets and homes. ,
