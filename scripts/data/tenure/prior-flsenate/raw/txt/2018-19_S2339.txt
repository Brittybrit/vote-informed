1. Title of Project: Miami-Dade County Model Lands North Canal Everglades Wetlands Restoration Project
2. Senate Sponsor: Annette Taddeo
3. Date of Submission: 01/18/2018
4. Project/Program Description:
    Funds will be used to fill an unnecessary ditch and restore wetlands near the coastline in southern Miami-Dade
    County. This project will enhance water retention and wetland recharge potential to address salt intrusion in the
    Biscayne Aquifer near municipal wellfields, including those under the jurisdiction of Miami-Dade County and the
    Florida Keys Aqueduct Authority. The project will also benefit publicly owned wetlands by improving wetland
    functions and augmenting water management capabilities.
