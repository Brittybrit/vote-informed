11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      These right-of-way and road improvements will allow safe passage of traffic entering and leaving a new industrial park in Lacoochee,
      an economically depressed part of east Pasco County.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Jobs will be provided to the economically depressed population of the area.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Pasco County's Office of Economic Development estimates this new industrial park will support 500 to 700 jobs in the area. The
      improved roads will provide safety for traffic entering and exiting the industrial park.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      The target population is east Pasco County with a direct impact to the Lacoochee area. East Pasco County has approximately
      100,000 residents.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      The expected benefit is to improve access and safety to the area where the new industrial park is being built. Pasco County’s Office
      of Economic Development estimates 500-700 jobs will be created at this industrial park, lifting the economically depressed area.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      The contractor is required to achieve substantial completion of the entire work no later than 300 days from the issuance of the
      construction permit. If the contractor fails to achieve substantial completion within the stated time, the contractor will receive a $1,000
      per day fine.
