14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The training facility will help improve the performance of our law enforcements officers and first responders in high-
    pressure real-world simulations. These background of training simulations can be set to actual physical locations in our
    community like elementary schools, or government buildings. The training facility will help boost morale, retention, and
    recruitment of our public safety personnel, with modern training. Law enforcement officers can repeatedly train to improve
    reaction time and decision-making without risk of injury.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Modern training facility will include community spaces, computer labs, classroom, scenario training simulator, defensive
    tactics training, gym, and an indoor shooting range. Virtual reality form of training gives officers another way to be better
    equipped to keep the community safe. This training can be used for a variety of common, high-pressure real-world
    situations including domestic disputes, traffic stops and school shootings. Simulated weapons used during training
    include rifles, pistols, machine guns, tasers, and pepper spray.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will have an opportunity to participate and be exposed to the virtual reality simulator. Additionally, citizens will
    benefit from having law enforcement and first responders that will be substantially more equipped to handle emergency
    active shooter situations.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Police and first responders.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Virtual reality form of training gives officers another way to be better equipped to keep the community safe. This training
    can be used for a variety of common, high-pressure real-world situations including domestic disputes, traffic stops and
    school shootings. Simulated weapons used during training include rifles, pistols, machine guns, Tasers, and pepper
    spray. Law enforcement officers can repeatedly train to improve reaction time and decision-making without risk of injury.
    The project will be measured by continued monitoring of officer perfomance on the training facility and on the field.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The City is open to any proposed penalties for failing to deliver or perform.
