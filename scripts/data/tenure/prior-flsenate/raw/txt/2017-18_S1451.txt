9. Program Performance (if needed, include additional documentation):

a. How will requested funds be spent? Include supporting documentation.

Funds will be used to provide meals to elderly, low income residents who are currently food insufficient —
meaning they do not get enough to eat. For many seniors, lack of adequate nutrition compromises their
well-being, threatens their health and puts them at increased risk for institutionalization. These seniors
face the threat of hunger daily.

b. Identify expected program results and the expected benefit associated from the requested funds.
Expected benefits include providing nutritious meals to elders; helping to stabilize the health of seniors by
giving adequate nutrition; preventing the decline of seniors into frailty. These benefits will result in
savings to our State in costly institutional care.

c. Who will benefit from receipt of State funds?

The elderly, low income population

d. What specific measures will be used to document performance data for the project, if it receives funds?
Specific measures include reducing the number of underserved areas in our community and eliminating
gaps in service. The goal is to provide meals to seniors in underserved areas of our community.
