12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To assist with increased construction costs of expansion project funded in the 21-22 legislative year.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     In addition to the current post-secondary offerings, Hernando County School District will look to create a full menu of
    courses within the first 3 years of operation that can be accessed by both populations. Proper wraparound programs
    available to adults will also be added.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Vocational Training to high school students and adults interested in learning a specific trade.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     According to the 2013-2017 American Community Survey, our County has 18,176 citizens without a high school diploma,
    and 53,759 citizens who only have a high school diploma. This would be the target population for this project. In addition
    to the Hernando County high school students who prefer a career and technical path in lieu of a college degree.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
