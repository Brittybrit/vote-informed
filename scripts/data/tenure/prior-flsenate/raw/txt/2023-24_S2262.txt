14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will be used for e a full-renovation of a 5,284 sq. ft. Facility at AD Barnes Park that will be used in an innovative
    partnership between MacTown, Miami-Dade County, and the Miami-Dade County School Board to provide meaningful
    day activities for adults with disabilities who are aging out of school based services. Planned programming includes
    health activities, leisure and recreational activities including horticulture, culinary arts, nutritional services and enhanced
    living skills.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     MacTown's innovative day center will incorporate health, therapeutic programs and skills training. The development of
    this center will establish a long-term resource in Miami-Dade County to serve adults with disabilities who are aging out of
    school services. The funding will assist in completing construction of a state-of-the-art facility, where families can feel
    comfortable leaving their loved ones.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Activities and services will include social and health activities, life skills, recreational and cultural activities, such as
    culinary arts, horticulture. Will also provide general services such as skills training, development and enhancement of
    daily living activities. Social integration and counseling will be available to all participants.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is adults with disabilities who are aging out of school based services.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The anticipated benefit is that all participants will benefit from meaningful day activities and skills training. Their families
    will also benefit from the opportunity to find meaningful day activities for their loved ones. All consumers and families will
    participate in quarterly meetings to review progress, and emerging area of ability to ensure ongoing support is
    maintained. Data obtained through daily recording of progress of client skills demonstrated will be graphed and reviewed
    by staff at least monthly to determine if changes should be made in educational methods conducive to the success of the
    individual. MACtown utilizes a data based, positive person-centered approach to teaching individuals with disabilities and
    maintains a supportive and safe environment in which our consumers thrive and are encouraged to engage in activities
    that provide greater degrees of independence based on their interests and current abilities.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     MacTown regularly provides community based waiver services to individuals with disabilities and therefore is
    accustomed to being a good steward of state funds. This request contemplates using funding for capital needs, so if
    MacTown were unable to complete the project, they would return any funds provided by the state to ensure full restitution
    of the amount awarded.
