1. Title of Project: Creating Compassionate Schools
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson

4. Project/Program Description:
   In the wake of the Marjorie Stoneman Douglas School shooting, considerable attention has been paid to the
   structural safety of school campuses and to addressing the mental health needs of students. Very little attention
   has been directed toward the developmental needs of students relative to their social-emotional learning and the
   prevention of future problems. Addressing the security of school buildings and tackling mental health issues are
   certainly important. Social-Emotional Learning (SEL), however, attempts to mitigate the early learning experiences
   of young people to prevent them from developing maladaptive behaviors, anti-social traits and mental health
   problems. SEL. This proposal is for a statewide pilot to develop a protocol for the effective implementation of Social
   Emotional Learning (SEL) in Florida schools. Pasco County Schools is proposing to take the lead in conjunction with
   two other districts to create the standards necessary to implement SEL across all 67 counties.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

    This initiative is designed to create an explicit, whole child approach that encompasses both academic content
    and life readiness skills. This project will yield developmentally appropriate life readiness standards that both
    are aligned with and integrated into the standards-based academic curriculum as well as explicitly connected to
    the developmental school counseling program. The standards and associated evidence-based strategies and
    programs will support teachers and school counselors in planning for, instructing, monitoring, and responding
    to the whole child with the goal of preparing and maximizing post-secondary outcomes for all students, K-12.
    The idea is to use this project as a model for an integrated statewide social-emotional learning program in
    Florida's Pk-12 schools.
b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
    1) Partner with national experts in social-emotional learning and behavior prevention and intervention
    strategies. 2) Develop PK-12 social-emotional learning and academic readiness standards, aligned with the
    scope and sequence of the Florida Standards and other current district standards (i.e., Positive Behavior
    Interventions and Supports Benchmarks of Quality, Safer, Smarter Kids prevention curricula, Digital Literacy
    progression). 3) Incorporate developmental life readiness standards and establish a fully integrated curriculum
    that includes evidence-based strategies and programs, targets for student outcomes, common assessments,
    and explicit parent communication consistent with the learning expectations.
c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
    1) Provide life readiness standards that encompass social-emotional learning into PK-12 classrooms in Florida 2)
    Provide a fully integrated academic and behavioral core curriculum with: SEL Standards Development, common
    formative and summative assessments aligned with the scope and sequence and instructional units that include
    lessons with evidence based strategies, tools, and programs. System for recording, monitoring, and reflecting
    on student learning outcomes in PLCs. Professional development team and model for training and coaching
    skills. 3) Provide ongoing communication on social-emotional learning and the project to administrators, staff,
    students, parents, and community members.
d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
    This project proposes to support teachers and certified school counselors so they can support all PK-12
    students in Florida in instructing, monitoring, and responding to the whole child with the goal of preparing and
    maximizing successful outcomes for all students. Year 1 (Pilot) = 6 - 10 Pilot Schools Sites in Pasco County
    (district impact) Year 2 = Implement district-wide in Pasco County and add 2 additional partner school districts
    in Florida (regional impact) Year 3 = Continue Year 2 implementation and expand to other school districts in
    Florida (statewide) According to 17/18 Florida Department of Education, Final Survey 2 data: Total Teachers in
    Florida = 174,193 Total Teachers in Pasco = 5115 Total Certified School Counselors in Florida = 5941 Total
    Certified School Counselors in Pasco = 181 Total PK-12 Students in Florida = 2,833,115 Total PK-12 Students in
    Pasco = 73,682
e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
   will be measured?
    Expected benefits of this program include (1) improved academic achievement; (2) improved attitudes and
    behaviors; (3) reduction in negative behaviors; (4) decreased emotional distress for students Methodology by
    which this outcome will be measured includes: 1) Walk-through observational data indicating evidence of
    lesson planning and delivery of integrated instructional social-emotional learning content, strategies and
