1. Project Title          MACTown Main Campus Residential Hardening and Security Upgrades

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        01/03/2024

4. Project/Program Description
    Funds will be used for the hardening and security of MACtown Main Campus for individuals with intellectual and
   developmental disabilities. Scope includes re-roofing of a 56-bed residential facility and day training center, upgrade to 3-
   phase electrical service, replacement of several air conditioning units on both buildings, replacement of windows and doors
   with impact resistant products, fixing drainage issues on perimeter parking lot and install gas-powered emergency
   generator.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     To provide critical day and residential services to individuals with autism and developmental disabilities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Hardening and security of MACTown Main Campus for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities. The

     To provide critical services to 125 to 150 Individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities per day who are 18
