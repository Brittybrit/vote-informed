11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      The development of a nonprofit planned assisted living facility focused on Veterans offering room, food and daily support below
      market rates with financial assistance and a full time outreach Director coordinating VA benefits, local medical assistance and general
      daily life assistance. Veteran Village will be the first full service continuous care center for veterans and their families.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Veteran Village has partnered with Citrus County and will be constructed on the campus in Lecanto, FL that has the Veterans Service
      Facility, VA clinic and mass transit facilities. As mentioned above, the Director along with our partners at Mission United, will focus on
      ascertaining the needs of each veteran and assisting them determining their individual needs. Further, our local veteran
      organizations, over 40 of them, are the Advisory Board for Veterans Village along with our medical community will assist in identifying
      those veterans that need daily living assistance.



  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Veterans Village will provide rooms and boarding to veterans regardless of their economic situation, but it goes much further than
      that. This past year, Veterans Village and Mission United served over 200 veterans in everything from housing assistance, food,
      transportation, medical assistance, completing forms for taxes or VA to balancing their checking accounts. The one area that was
      consistent, was the need for a long term plan and companionship. By providing both, studies have shown a direct decrease in health
      cost, drug dependency and suicide.



d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      By targeting the Veterans living in Citrus and our surrounding counties, there is approximately 130,000 veterans. Veterans Village
      first phase will offer 120 apartments with the ability to expand on the 10 acres available.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      Veteran Village, as a non profit, will provide the services of an ALF and through donations and a portion of 9perating profits serve all
      veterans regardless of their economic situation, giving back to those who have served in our military. The measured outcome will be
      the immediate improvement in the lives of those veterans that could not afford the high cost of assisted-living and the venefits of that
      life style without a Veterans Village.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      With the approval of financing from USDA, once construction begins, the bank will direct payment as work is completed and
      approved.
