    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

           The project will service individuals who want to understand how prepare a yearly, quarterly and monthly
         budget; it will cover credit counseling and restoration, to individuals, as well as businesses. Many business
         owners have not been exposed to financial literacy. They don't know how to invest, save, prepare a statement
         or how to read it. They are not ready financially for the future. This program will help them make sound
         financial decisions for their daily, regular activities, as well as, during emergency/irregular times.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          The project will include participants of three different groups: 40% are high school students 30% are college
         students and 30% other adults who are opening small businesses.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          Financial education in general helps participants manage their finance better. One of our goals is to teach
         other become financial coaches to help others. Participants will receive certificates after successfully taking
         courses and passing our tests designed to measure knowledge of the graduates. We will have beginning,
         intermediate and advanced courses on budgeting, cash flow management, and tax savings strategies.

    g. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
       penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
        In addition to penalties provided by the state of Florida for persons or entities who fail to deliver what was
       contracted, the willfully negligent contractors will be charged of fraud and will be prosecuted as such. Projects
       that are being late will be taken away from the responsible contractor and will be re-assigned to a better
       bidder. Each contractor will be held accountable for their work and will be regarded based on a low/poor,
       moderate and strong/satisfactory performance. Only satisfactorily performed contractors will be paid and will
       retain their positions. We will refund any funds that were misappropriated.
