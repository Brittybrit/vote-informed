1. Title of Project: Cutler Bay-Drainage Improvement Cutler Ridge Sec 3
2. Senate Sponsor: Jose Rodriguez
3. Date of Submission: 02/07/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   The Town is requesting $300,000 to install new drainage in an area that currently consists solely of slab covered
   trenches that offer no water quality treatment. The new drainage is consistent with best management practices to
   include; installation of a 2,400 LF exfiltration trench (36" Diameter), and new catch basins. Following drainage
   installation, the roadway will be paved, striped, and signs installed for pedestrian safety. This project will be "shovel
   ready" by September 30, 2019. Project is located in a LMI community and within walking distance of Gulfstream
   Elementary School, a highly rated Title I public school with an estimated 708 students and a large special needs
   population. Flooding in this area creates a barrier to access for students and motorists.

11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        The goal of this project is to improve drainage in flood prone area with 51% low-moderate income families.
        Secondly this project will improve water quality in Miami-Dade County by treating stormwater runoff.
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        Activities and services include; (1) Design and permit drainage system; (2) install 2,400 LF of exfiltration trench
        and catch basins in accordance with the final construction design documents; (3) Conduct community
        education promoting the project and best practices for reducing water pollution.
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        This project does not provide a direct service.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        The proposed project will impact 124 structures consisting primarily of single family homes and one (1) Adult
        Living Facility for the elderly and individuals with disabilities, and one (1) Title I public school (Gulfstream
        Elementary, 708 students). The school provides special education for youth with severe disabilities living in the
        South Dade area. An analysis of the estimated loss due to street flooding in the target area, following a "minor"
        event as designated by FEMA is over $2.5 Million with a "major" event costing over $5 million. The total
        estimated value of the properties in the target area is $10 million.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
      will be measured?
