1. Title of Project: North Bay Village Boardwalk Development Project
2. Senate Sponsor: Daphne Campbell

4. Project/Program Description:
    North Bay Village is requesting $1,000,000 for construction of the Boardwalk Development Project. The Boardwalk
    is a core component of the Village's Economic Development Strategic Master Plan. North Bay Village is an island
    community surrounded by Biscayne Bay. North Bay Village proposes to develop a 3,400 linear foot multi-use
    Boardwalk/Baywalk overlooking Biscayne Bay and parallel to the Kennedy Causeway (State Road 934/NW 79th
    Street). The Boardwalk will provide residents with a beautiful local amenity and encourage tourists to visit the
    Village. This project offers the only available public access to the Village’s waterfront. This project will boost the
    Village's local economy by revitalizing the Kennedy Causeway and the Boardwalk as a "main street" that is attractive
    to new developers and retailers. FDOT Traffic Counts estimate that approximately 45,000 motorists travel the JFK
    Causeway daily.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           The Village has two major goals: (1) increase availability of recreational space within the Village and (2) attract
          new businesses and tourists to North Bay Village, improving the local economy.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           The Village is an island community with an estimated 8,949 residents (2017 BEBR), with less than 2 acres of
          parkland. Construction of the Boardwalk will create a linear park for residents and visitors. JFK Causeway
          (State Road 934/NW 79th Street) is the Village’s major commercial corridor accessible from Interstate-95.
          Approximately 45,000 motorists pass through the Village to Miami Beach daily without stopping in the Village.
          The Boardwalk is contiguous to State Road 934 and will revitalize the "main street," attracting new businesses
          and real estate developers and giving motorists a reason to stop in the Village.
