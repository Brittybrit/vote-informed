1. Project Title          Sweetwater Roadway and Drainage Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        11/14/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    To install/provide the necessary infrastructure in this area, resulting in highly-needed drainage, roadway, sidewalk and
   related infrastructure improvements. This project will enhance the overall quality of life, improve public rights of way, and
   safety in the City's Northern (Area 2) area on NW 108th & 109th Aves and lateral roads. The completion of this project will
   result in alleviati severe flooding that plagues the area (sample pictures and communications attached), prevention of
   property damage and loss, and improved traffic and public safety for residents and businesses.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose of the City of Sweetwater is to install the infrastructure of this area resulting in much-needed drainage,
    roadway, sidewalk and related infrastructure improvements. This project will enhance the overall quality of life, improve
    public rights of way, and safety. The completion of this project will result in mitigation of floods, prevention of property
    damage and loss, and improved traffic and public safety for residents and businesses.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Design and construction of infrastructure. Subsequent maintenance as well.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Improved infrastructure (i.e., roads, sidewalks, lighting). Enhanced drainage capacity.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The residents of the City of Sweetwater.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Once this projected is completed, it will protect the general public from flooding, as well as damage to homes and
    businesses that is affected by poor roadway conditions and flooded areas. It will also reduce the pollutants entering and
    could possible cause damage to our groundwater system.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Cancellation of contract and/or payment for liquidated damages. The Contractor may also be prohibited from entering
    into business with the City again in the future for a certain period of time as well.
