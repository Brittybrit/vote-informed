    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Those individuals wishing to become a part of the healthcare workforce; college and university students, unemployed
    persons, etc.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The Benefit and Outcome of this project will be too increase the healthcare workforce in Florida for nursing students
    (Registered Nurses (RNs)), (Licensed Practical Nurses (LPNs), and (Certified Nursing Assistants (CNAs)). The
    Foundation will report the number of students who enter these programs and then the number who successfully complete
    these programs.

     If deliverables are not met, and students are not served, the Department of Education may withhold funds.
