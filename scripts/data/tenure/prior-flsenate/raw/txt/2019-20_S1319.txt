1. Title of Project: Brooksville Water Service & Fire Protection
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 01/30/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   To provide uninterrupted water service and fire protection to approximately 15-20 low income residents at
   Hernando and Bayport Streets by constructing new (6) inch water lines. Existing (2) inch galvanized water lines are
   old and dilapidated, and have been repaired because of line breaks approximately 25 times over the past 5-6 years.
   Customers currently have no fire protection.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
          To provide uninterrupted water service and provide fire protection to customers.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
          Construction activity includes abandoning existing (2) inch water lines, and installation of approximately 1,600
          feet of new (6) inch PVC water lines at both Hernando and Bayport streets, along with fire hydrants for fire
          protection where currently there is none. Each resident will have connected water service to their property.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
          Uninterrupted water service with fire protection.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
          Target population is low income residential, and approximately 15 - 20 families will be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
          Uninterrupted water service with fire protection. By monitoring service to customers, and inspection and
          maintaining fire hydrants installed, ensuring fire protection service is available.
    f.    What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
          penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
          Withhold payment for non-service.
