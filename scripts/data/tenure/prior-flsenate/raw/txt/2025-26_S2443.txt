1. Project Title           Miami Springs - Phase II Stormwater and Flood Mitigation Improvements
                           on Esplanade Drive

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        2/17/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    Funding will be used for the design, permitting, and construction to complete Phase II for canal performance and capacity
   as well as stormwater management by mitigating flooding, erosion, accessibility, and future roadway failure caused by
   current and projected storm events and surges. It will benefit a service area designated as high priority along both sides of
   Esplanade, with the aim to mitigate and eliminate embankment damage caused by severe flooding due to sea level rise,
   provide safety and protection and prevent roadway collapse and potential property loss to the residents. Moreover, the
   project aims to protect and preserve critical infrastructure, including roadways for both private and public schools, libraries,
   senior citizen facilities, and historical sites.

     To mitigate flooding, erosion, accessibility, and roadway failure caused by current and projected storm events and storm
    surges and to protect and preserve critical infrastructure, including roadways for both private and public schools, libraries,
    senior citizen facilities, and historical sites.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Construction to complete Phase II for improvements to mitigate flooding, erosion, accessibility, and roadway failure
    caused by current and projected storm events and storm surges. The project will provide safety improvements of
    hardening of the Esplanade Embankment along both the east and west sides.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Enhance public safety by reinforcing the Esplanade Embankment encompassing both its eastern and western sides and
    to protect and preserve critical infrastructure, including roadways for both private and public schools, libraries, senior
    citizen facilities, and historical sites.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This project holds significant importance as it falls within a service area that the city has identified as a top priority due to
    its proximity to and aspires to safeguard critical infrastructure, encompassing educational institutions, libraries,
    recreational facilities, senior facility, and historically significant sites.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The project will provide safety improvements of hardening of the Esplanade Embankment which includes safeguarding
    against the collapse of roadways, preventing potential property losses, and preserving approximately 50 mature trees that
    face the risk of toppling due to severe erosion.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    Withhold funding.
