1. Project Title           Florida's Historical Deeds - Preserving Early Property Records

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        01/17/2024

4. Project/Program Description
    Preserving Florida's earliest property records held at various county courthouses, through the project "Guardians of
   Florida's Historical Deeds," is vital. These records hold historical significance, offering insights into the state's development
   and cultural heritage. They serve legal and genealogical purposes, aiding property disputes and family research.
   Furthermore, these records have educational and tourism value, attracting visitors and boosting local economies. Funding
   this project is essential to prevent the deterioration of these irreplaceable records due to aging and environmental factors.
   By doing so, Florida's history will be safeguarded, ensuring it remains accessible, relevant, and invaluable for future
   generations.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To preserve, digitize, and create access to the earliest property records that document Florida's founding.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Records preservation services to include archival stabilization, deacidification, and mylar encapsulation. Digital imaging
    services to include scanning documents into an archival format that can be indexed and hosted to create a digital access
    point for citizens.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will have the ability to view, research, or request material electronically from anywhere and at their convenience.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Creating and allowing access to historical records and documents has a far reaching impact on citizens and the business
    community. These early records enable the tracing of the lineage to the establishment of contemporary Florida and a
    large portion of its current citizens.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit from this project is the preservation of and direct digital access to these early founding documents
    and records. The outcome will be measured by the number of records available electronically after the project is finished.

    If stated performance measures or deliverables are not met within the agreed project timeline, the contracting agency
