    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          Additional funds are required to expand the program. More than 20 graduates of the Youth & Adult Pre-
         apprenticeship Graduates have been placed in jobs and are working in semi-skilled jobs or starting their
         Apprenticeship and Trade Skills Training. AmSkills needs additional equipment for the Apprenticeship Training
         and needs to increase marketing/recruitment efforts by conducting hands-on workshops in the community to
         encourage youth, adults & veterans to join the program. The funding will provide the opportunity to train for
         skilled jobs in Advanced Manufacturing

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          Youth, Adults and Veterans. 100+ in 2018/19

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          Well trained individuals to fill open Advanced Manufacturing Jobs
