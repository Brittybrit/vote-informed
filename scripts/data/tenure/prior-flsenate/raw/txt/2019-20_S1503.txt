11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        Will reduce costs of pumping water from the aquifer for irrigation purposes. Will reduce the need of fertilizers,
        as some nutrients, i.e. nitrogen and phosphorus, remain in the reuse water. It will reduce the demand of
        needed water from our drinking-water source, the floridan aquifer. It also provides an effective means for the
        city to reduce the amount of reclaimed water from its treatment facility.
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        Design, engineering, and construction of piping, and all other appurtenances necessary to deliver reclaimed
        water to customers for the purpose of irrigation.
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Reduction of fertilizer saves customers money, and minimizes the amount of nutrients carried away in
        stormwater runoff that eventually makes its way into the aquifer, where their drinking water comes from.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        Golf course communities that use high amounts of water for irrigation. Between 500 and 1000 persons may be
        served by this reuse water project.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
      will be measured?
        Expected benefits include a reduction in water pumped from the aquifer, thus conserving the drinking water
        supply, and a reduction in fertilizer used. Methodology is to meter the reclaimed water pumped, and the this
        amount can be calculated to determine the nutrient reduction, and cost savings to the customer.
   f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
        penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
        Withhold payment for non-service.
