                    Local Funding Initiative Request - Fiscal Year 2018-2019

1. Title of Project: Bal Harbour Village Bakers Haulover Inlet Bypassing Project
2. Senate Sponsor: Daphne Campbell
3. Date of Submission: 12/14/2017
4. Project/Program Description:
    This project will reconfigure the north jetty at the inlet to enhance sand deposition to keep sand out of the ebb and
    flood shoals. The jetty will be configured for fishing and public access. At regular intervals, the beach (updrift, to the
    north) will be dredged and sand bypassed to “downdrift” areas of the County, thereby maximizing inlet bypassing.
    The interval of dredging will be every 2-4 years.
