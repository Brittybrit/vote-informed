4. Project/Program Description
    The City of Hialeah is pursuing funding for the removal of invasive and non-native tree species located in public rights-
   of-way, with the intention of replacing them with native Florida tree species. Invasive, non-native trees can disrupt local
   ecosystems, outcompete native vegetation, and contribute to long-term degradation of green spaces. These species
   often spread rapidly, posing threats to local plants and wildlife. They can be introduced either intentionally for
   landscaping purposes or inadvertently through various human activities. Once they establish themselves, these invasive
   trees can dominate landscapes, diminish biodiversity, and modify ecological functions. Notable examples of invasive tree
   species include the Australian Pine (Casuarina equisetifolia), Brazilian Peppertree (Schinus terebinthifolia), and Chinese
   Tallow (Triadica sebifera), all of which are recognized for their rapid growth and competitive advantage over native
   species for vital resources.
