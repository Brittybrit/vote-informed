1. Title of Project: South Dade Wetlands Preserve Land Acquisition for Salt Intrusion
2. Senate Sponsor: Annette Taddeo
3. Date of Submission: 12/18/2017
4. Project/Program Description:
    Funds will be used to acquire land from willing sellers in a wetland preserve in southern Miami-Dade County to
    facilitate projects that slow salt intrusion, which threatens nearby wellfields including but not limited to the sole
    source of potable water for the Florida Keys. In conjunction with currently owned State and Miami-Dade County
    lands, these acquisitions will also enable projects that improve water management, allow for hazard mitigation and
    restore wetlands in these vulnerable areas.

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          Acquisition of wetlands in the South Dade Wetlands Preserve will facilitate regional projects that manage
         surface and groundwater to improve groundwater quality and stabilize or reverse salt intrusion a the base of
         the Biscayne Aquifer.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          Citizens of Miami-Dade County and the Florida Keys.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          Protect/enable continued use of drinking water wellfields located in South Miami-Dade County, including by
         not limited to wellfields under the jurisdiction of Miami-Dade Water and Sewer and the Florida Keys Aqueduct
         Authority. The outcome will be measured by the number of wetland acres acquired and restored near wellfields
         and saltwater intruded areas.
