1. Project Title          Miami-Dade Mobile Command Vehicle

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        2/18/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    A Mobile Command Vehicle (MCV) is essential for on-scene command and coordination for disaster response and
   recovery. The unit would provide Miami-Dade County responders enhanced situational awareness, command and control,
   improved communication, and flexibility and adaptability. The MCV would be deployed county-wide to all disasters and
   large-scale/soft-target events and may be utilized as a resource to other counties in the State of Florida. Note: The is a new
   project, the Miami-Dade County Department of Emergency Management does not currently have this capability.
