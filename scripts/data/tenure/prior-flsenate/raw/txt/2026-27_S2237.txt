4. Project/Program Description
    For the last 17 years, SFACS has been the nation's preeminent institution for the education of children and young adults
   on the more impacted end of the autism spectrum. With the full support of its authorizer MDCPS and the Legislature,
   SFACS is halfway toward its goal of expanding its geographic coverage to serve more families-in-need by opening 2 of
   the 4 contemplated schools in Miami-Dade. In order to do so, these facilities require specific safety and security
   upgrades to address the unique needs of our special population of students. This request is to do the same in Broward
   with 3 new autism-focused school transformations.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     For the last 17 years, SFACS has been the nation's preeminent institution for the education of children and young
    adults on the more impacted end of the autism spectrum. In partnership with neighboring Broward Schools and the
    local autism advocates, SFACS is expanding its geographic coverage to serve more families-in-need by working with
    BCPS to identify and serve additional communities. In order to do so, these facilities require specific safety and security
    upgrades to address the unique needs of our special population of students.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     SFACS will use the fund to upgrade safety, security and communications of the targeted campuses and hire and train
    the school leaders and administrative staff required to successful open these new schools.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     These three new schools will provide comprehensive academic and behavioral training for the students in their care.
    The schools will also provide extensive wrap-around services and trainings for their parents and the larger autism
    community, including, but not limited to, other parents, educators, clinicians, and local law enforcement and emergency
    response personnel.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     These three new schools will educate approximately 900 students in kindergarten through age 22 at maturity. All will
    be on the most impacted end of the autism spectrum.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     We will work closely with our charter authorizer to serve the children and families most impacted by autism. Our
    outcomes will be assessed through each student's annual state-mandated assessment, progress on their
    Individualized Education Program, parent satisfaction surveys and frequent audits by the State and district authorizer.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     In the unanticipated circumstance that one or more of the schools does not open or opens and then is not successful
    and loses it charter, the safety, security and communications elements funded by the requested appropriation would
    revert to district ownership per the anticipated leases. All funding not yet committed would obviously remain with the
    contracting agency.
