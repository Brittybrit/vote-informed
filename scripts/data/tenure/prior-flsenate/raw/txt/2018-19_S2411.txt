1. Title of Project: Advanced traveler Information Systems (ATIS)
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 01/22/2018
4. Project/Program Description:
    Advanced traveler information systems are the signs that suggest travelers tune their radio to receive important
    information such as road conditions or alternative routes. Florida uses these signs along most major interstates and
    the Turnpike. FDOT commissioned an independent study recently by the University of Central Florida that
    suggested continued investment in upgrading current sites to be compatible with smart phones and expanding
    along major evacuation routes would be beneficial to the traveling public. Given the number of storms that the
    state has recently experienced, and FDOT's shift to Emergency Shoulder Use (ESU), there is a case to be made that
    this type of communications technology is highly beneficial, particularly along major evacuation routes (I-10 & I-75),
    and along the Turnpike.

11. Program Performance:

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Existing sites would be upgraded to be compatible with smart phones and new sites would be established
          along Florida's major evacuation routes.
