2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson

4. Project/Program Description:
   Veterans Alternative, Inc. is a nonprofit organization that empowers Veterans by providing effective alternative
   therapies and community engagement which promotes the development of a more healthy, connected and resilient
   Veteran population. This is achieved through the five-day Accelerated Wellness Program (AWP), an integrative
   program which utilizes evidence-based therapies such as Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART), Integrative
   Restoration (iRest), adaptive yoga and art and music therapy. Veterans Alternative will host a minimum of 25 AWPs
   in Fiscal Year 2019-20 and will serve over 100 Florida Veterans.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        Veteran Alternative goal is to create a more healthy, connected, and resilient Veteran population through
        alternative therapies and community engagement. In Fiscal Year 2019-20, Veterans Alternative will host 25 or
        more Accelerated Wellness Programs that will serve over 100 Florida Veterans.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        The Accelerated Wellness Program (AWP) provides alternative therapies and community engagement to
        Veterans. Using an integrative approach, the AWP uses evidence-based therapies including ART, iRest, adaptive
        yoga and art and music therapy. The AWP external evaluation from the last 2 years shows Veterans experience
        a 46% reduction in post-traumatic stress symptoms, 61% decrease in depression, 55% decrease in anxiety, 42%
        decrease in perceived stress and more at the conclusion of the week.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Direct services will only be provided to Veterans and their spouses. Services include ART, iRest, adaptive yoga,
        and music and art therapy.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        100+ Florida Veterans.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
        Veterans who participate in the AWP will experience a reduction in PTSD, Depression, Anxiety, and Perceived
        Stress and an increase in resiliency and community engagement. Pre- and Post- assessments are conducted as
