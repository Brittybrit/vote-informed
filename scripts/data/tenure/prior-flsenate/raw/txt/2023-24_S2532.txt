4. Project/Program Description
    This funding will support the construction of security upgrades at water facilities including water treatment plants, well-
   fields, and pumping stations. Project components address recommendations from the risk assessment performed pursuant
   to the America's Water Infrastructure Act and a Department of Homeland Security Site Security Assessment.
