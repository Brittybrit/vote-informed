4. Project/Program Description
    The Institute for Freedom in the Americas supports curriculum and exhibitions at the Freedom Tower to narrate the story of
   the search for freedom, explore the plight caused by communism, and educate future generations on its historical, cultural,
   and societal impact. It fosters freedom through civic engagement, global dialogue, and a Fellowship program for students to
   learn from Latin American leaders via academic studies, workshops, and interactions with leaders from the Western
   Hemisphere. Established in 2024 under Florida Statute 1004.89, it advances democracy and human rights.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The specific purpose of the Institute for Freedom in the Americas is to educate and inspire future generations by
    narrating the search for freedom and examining the societal impacts of communism. Through its curriculum, exhibitions,
    and Fellowship program, the Institute aims to preserve the historical significance of the Freedom Tower, foster civic
    engagement, and empower emerging leaders from Latin America and the Caribbean to advance governance, human
    rights, and democratic values in the Americas.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funds will support developing curriculum and exhibitions at the Freedom Tower to explore the plight caused by
    communism. The Institute will offer a Fellowship program featuring academic studies, hands-on workshops, and
    mentorship from leaders in governance. Additionally, it will host events, foster global dialogue, and build partnerships to
    advance democratic principles and civic engagement across the Americas.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The appropriation project will provide direct services to students, including access to educational programs, exhibitions,
    and resources at the Freedom Tower. It will also offer a Fellowship program with academic studies, hands-on workshops,
    and mentorship opportunities, empowering students to engage with democratic principles, governance, and civic
    leadership. These services will prepare students for impactful roles in their communities and beyond.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The target population of 2.8 million residents in Miami Dade County in addition all students throughout the State of
    Florida.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The project aims to foster understanding of freedom and democracy, highlight the Freedom Tower’s significance, and
    empower future leaders. Outcomes will be measured through attendance records, participant surveys, Fellowship
    enrollment, alumni success tracking, and pre- and post-program evaluations.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     Withholding payments or requiring corrective action plans, to address unmet deliverables or performance measures,
    ensuring accountability and alignment with project goals.
