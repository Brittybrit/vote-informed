4.   Project/Program Description
     According to published data Pasco County has been on a trajectory of tremendous growth, with population growth of 16.1%, from 464,703
     in 2010 to 539,630 in 2018. Saint Leo University has also experienced record growth of 45% in enrollments from 2018 to 2019 at its
     Campus in Pasco County and is projecting growth of 10% more new enrollments each year for the next three to five years. Saint Leo
     University intends to support its growth by building a center of integrated, holistic health and well-being, with recreation space,
     preventative health care, counseling, and health services for its students and members of the wider Pasco County community. If state
     funding is approved for $5,000,000, to support the construction of the new wellness center, Saint Leo will continue to raise a total of $15M
     in private support for a facility that will support east Pasco County. The facility is slated to open in 2021 and without state funding will be
     built as a smaller University-only facility.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      Meet wellness needs for a growing county of Florida as well as a growing campus community. Expand recreational programs for the
      university and citizens in the surrounding communities for programming and wellness activities, summer camps, community
      recreational pool and meeting space for the use of businesses to conduct meetings in east Pasco County. Also a space for elected
      officials and government leaders to conduct public meetings and gatherings in east Pasco County.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Fitness/wellness classes and programs, medical and urgent care services, meeting spaces for public use, community access and
      summer camp venue.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Fitness facilities including workout spaces and equipment. A recreational swimming pool that will also allow for programming such as
      water aerobics. Fitness classes held by professional instructors. Meeting spaces for community gatherings. A healthcare partner
      will provide urgent care and outpatient clinical services to the campus and surrounding community with licensed medical personnel
      on staff daily.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      The target population is Saint Leo University students, faculty, staff and Pasco County citizens and their guests. Approximately
      25,000 citizens are expected to be served.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      The expected benefit is a healthier campus and east Pasco County community. The methodology for measuring this expected
      benefit will be doing pre and post surveys of users, tabulating annually wellness/fitness class attendance, group usage of meeting
      room space, as well as pool usage. The objective is a combined 30,000 annual user visits.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Loss of funding.
