1. Title of Project: Miami Dade County Extension of Water Service to Residents with Contaminated Wells
2. Senate Sponsor: Jose Rodriguez
3. Date of Submission: 12/04/2017
4. Project/Program Description:
    There are approximately 3600 properties within the service area of Miami-Dade Water and Sewer Dept. that are
    currently served by private wells. Many of these homes were built on former agricultural lands before public water
    supplies were available. Several instances of pesticide contamination have been detected by monitoring. While DEP
    provides on-site treatment systems to homes where drinking water standards are exceeded, no assistance is
    available to neighbors who may have detections but not exceedances. Extending water mains is a better solution
    but can cost about $30k per property for water main installation. State assistance will make this option more
    affordable and more completely address the public health issues while reducing DEP's costs over the long term for
    on-site treatment systems.
