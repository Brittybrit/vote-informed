4.   Project/Program Description
     Pepin Academies Foundation is a non-profit 501(c)3 organization created in 2008 to support all of the Pepin Academies campuses. Pepin
     Academies are public, tuition-free charter schools located in Tampa, Riverview and New Port Richey serving students with learning
     disabilities from third - twelfth grade. Pepin Academies also runs a transitional program for students 18-22 to teach life and work skills by
     partnering with several companies throughout Tampa Bay. The funds requested will go to outfit the new Pepin Academies campus being
     built in Pasco County. Funds will be used to purchase technology equipment, security equipment, items for their learning related skills
     lab, and occupational therapy equipment. Pepin Academies Pasco Campus is a 501c3 not for profit tuition free public charter school who
     specializes in students with learning and learning related disabilities.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      The goal of the Pepin Academies Foundation is to support all Pepin Academies campuses. The Specific goal of these funds will be to create a grant
      for the Pepin Academies Pasco Campus to outfit there school with the equipment and supplies stated above. Pepin Academies services a unique
      population of individuals. All who had to be failing in a traditional public school setting. By making sure we have the right tools and equipment
      available for our students and staff we are giving these students an opportunity to succeed. Not only to succeed, but to go on and become
      independent productive members in society. By creating an opportunity for these students to graduate we are helping to eliminate the unemployment
      rate for persons with disabilities. Thus helping to stimulate Florida's economy and our workforce.


 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Students and teachers will have access to the latest technology in the classroom. Allowing our population to have access to the tools they need to
      succeed. Students will also have access to a learning skills lab where they will acquire life skills so they can become productive independent members
      of society. The occupational therapy equipment will help our population coup with their learning related disabilities so they can better succeed in the
      classroom. All in all improving their life skills, their learning environment and allowing them to to flourish.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Creating a learning environment for students with learning and learning related disabilities.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      Grade school and high school students with learning and learning related disabilities. We expect to serve between 400-700 students.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      The benefit of this project is to create a thriving educational environment for our student population so they can have access to the tools and resources
      they need to succeed in school. We will track our success based on test scores and graduation rates.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Repayment of the funds
