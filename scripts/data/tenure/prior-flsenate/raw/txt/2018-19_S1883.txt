11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           Relieve existing/future traffic congestion at nearby I-75 interchanges (SR 52 and SR/CR 54), and improve local
          and regional traffic flow in the project area. The site of the proposal will provide critical access to the thriving
          Wesley Chapel market, recognized as one of the nation's top 40 fastest growing communities.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Improve transportation conditions by decreasing traffic at nearby interchanges. Increase or improve
          economic activity within the area of the interchange.

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          Improve vehicular, pedestrian/bicycle, transit and freight opportunities.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          Tampa Bay Region - Interchange will be expected to serve >100,000 vehicle trips per day.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome

          Decrease traffic at nearby interchanges which will be measured by traffic counts. Increase jobs in the area
