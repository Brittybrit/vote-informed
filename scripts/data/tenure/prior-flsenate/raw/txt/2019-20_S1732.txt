1. Title of Project: Cutler Bay-Wetland Observation Tower
2. Senate Sponsor: Jose Rodriguez
3. Date of Submission: 02/07/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   The Town of Cutler Bay is requesting $200,000 to construct an environment education observation tower, enabling
   citizens to learn about Biscayne Bay, the Cutler Wetlands and the unique fauna and flora associated with the bay
   and its region. This project will further the Town’s efforts to expand the availability and access to Florida’s
   greenways and natural areas. This project is aligned with the Florida Department of Environmental Protection's
   SCORP priority area of “Environmental Education” - “Public schools in Florida, in partnerships with the Department
   of Education and public park and recreation agencies, should continue to integrate environmental education for
   children and adults throughout their curricula to increase awareness of the threats to and benefits of Florida’s
   natural systems” (pg. 7-6). Four (4) schools in the Town limits have specialized environmental education programs
   that will benefit directly from this project.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        The Town is requesting $200,000 (45% of construction cost) to install an observation tower and create a bird-
        themed preserve in the Biscayne Bay Coastal Wetlands located at 97 Avenue south of SW 224 Street. The goal
        of this site is to create open space that is maintained as a natural, educational showcase, with the development
        of trails and boardwalks through the wetlands. The project also promotes pedestrian and bicycle access
        through connection to the State's Old Cutler Bike Trail.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        Four (4) of the schools located in the Town have specialized environmental programs. The development of an
        observation tower in the Cutler Bay Wetlands will allow students to practice what is learned in school; without
        having to leave their community.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Aligned with the FDEP SCORP: Schools in partnerships with the Dept. of Ed and public park and recreation
        agencies, continue to integrate environmental education for children throughout their curricula to increase
        awareness of the threats to and benefits of Florida’s natural systems. (Ch 7, pg. 6).
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        Town desires to attract students of all ages (K-12 and Post-Secondary) to use the Tower as a "lab" for their
        environmental studies. The Tower is also open to the general public and will become a part of the Town's
        network of public parks.
