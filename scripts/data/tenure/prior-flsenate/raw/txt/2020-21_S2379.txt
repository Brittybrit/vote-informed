4.   Project/Program Description
     The Fort Island Trail Multi-Use Path from Roy Thomas to Islamirada is the first of several phases of a 10-14 foot wide asphalt and
     boardwalk multi-use path project. The first phase (approx. 2.6 miles) will be from W. Roy Thomas Rd. to N. Islamirada Way. Future phase
     terminations will be determined based on funding with the ultimate goal of having the path continue to the county beach at the west end of
     Fort Island Trail, approximately 6.4 additional miles.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      The Ft. Island Tr. Multi-Use Path from Roy Thomas to Islamirada is the first of several phases of a 10-14 foot wide asphalt and
      boardwalk multi-use path project. The first phase (approx. 2.6 miles) will be from W. Roy Thomas Rd. to N. Islamirada Way.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Engineering, design, permitting, and construction of a 10-14 foot wide asphalt and boardwalk multi-use path.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Construction of a 10-14 foot wide asphalt and boardwalk multi-use path.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      Approximately 140,000 residents of Citrus County, visitors, and tourists.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      Construction of the project will include a 10 to 14 foot wide asphalt and boardwalk multi-use path separate from the roadway. The
      noted improvements along Ft. Island Tr. will increase public health, help protect bike/ped users from the hazards of roadway traffic,
      and improve environmental education, tourism, development potential, and access to local shopping. When constructed, this 2.6 mile
      path will connect to the City of Crystal River's 3 Sister's path and park, the path along US19, and will serve as the western end of a
      trail network to extend across the county. The outcome may be measured by evaluating the roadway and bike/ped capacity,
      vulnerable users crash data, and traffic volumes both pre and post construction of the path.


 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Standard contract penalties are sufficient.
