14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The main objective of this project is to construct roadway connectivity by improving existing conditions, and drainage.
    Our goal is to enhance the area for residents and visitors who are being adversely impacted by non-existing drainage and
    outdated infrastructure. Transportation and mobility and flood protection will be provided through this project.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The project proposes construction of drainage improvements and roadway repairs/restoration at the intersection area.
    This includes the addition of new stormwater inlets and French drain for treatment and disposal of stormwater;
    reconstruction, resurfacing, repair, and re-grading of the existing asphalt roadways and adjacent curb and gutter; and
    restoration and corrections to existing pavement markings.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Due to deterioration and the serious existing conditions in the project area, this project is critical for the safety of local
    residents as well as the economic growth and job creation for the Town of Medley. The Town of Medley is requesting
    funding for roadway and drainage improvements, including the reconstruction of the roadway, milling, resurfacing, and
    widening of the existing pavement to accommodate curb & gutter, signage, pavement markings as well as pedestrian
    sidewalks and bicycle lane. The project also includes drainage improvements with additional catch basins, swales and
    exfiltration trenches and other infrastructure improvements for water quality.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The general public and businesses will be served by this project. We anticipate more than 800 individuals to be served
    by this project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     There will be a reduction in flooding and improved roadway conditions. his project will improve the Town’s road network,
    will allow the easier flow of traffic, promote multimodal mobility, emergency evacuation, and improve connectivity and
    accessibility for the transportation of goods and the circulation of heavy equipment. The benefits of this project will be
    measured by improved roadway.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The contract will have standard compliance language for timely deliverables and performance measures.
