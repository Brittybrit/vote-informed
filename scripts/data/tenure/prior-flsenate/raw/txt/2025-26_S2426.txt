4. Project/Program Description
    The goal of the Medley NW 96 Street Roadway and Drainage Improvements Project (NW 87 Ave to NW S River Dr) is to
   construct a new roadway and drainage system, as identified under the Storm-Water Master Plan (SWMP), which may
   include: 26 drainage structures, one outfall control, 3,200 LF of roadway, gutters, and other improvements to improve the

   the local economy.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal of the Medley NW 96 Street Roadway and Drainage Improvements Project (NW 87 Ave to NW S River Dr) is to
    construct a new roadway and drainage system, as identified under the Storm-Water Master Plan (SWMP), which may
    include: 26 drainage structures, one outfall control, 3,200 LF of roadway, gutters, and other improvements to improve the
    traffic flow, water quality, mitigate flooding hazards, reduce nutrients loading in groundwater, and may ultimately help
    boost the local economy.

   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Town will complete the following activities and services for the proposed 96th street roadway and drainage
    improvements project, which may include, but not limited to a) new roadway; b) drainage pipes/outlets; c) trenches; d)
    curbs; e) gutters; f) outfall structure, and g) other improvements.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The proposed project will serve as a direct investment in the local community and surrounding businesses. An efficient
    and well-maintained roadway system reduces accidents, enhance emergency responses, and boosts economic growth.
    Also, an improved drainage system will prevent flooding and will give excess water a place to safely transporting runoff to
    an area better suited to hold it. Additionally, the system will enhance the current infra-structure, drainage system
    performance, reduce flood hazards, thus reduces economic loses.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The proposed improvements will enhance the aging infrastructure, water quality, mitigate or eliminate flooding hazards,
    and reduce nutrients loading in surrounding groundwater, and improve local traffic access for Medley residents,
    commuters, and local businesses. The project is expected to serve over 800 individuals and over 100 local area
    businesses.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The project will complete the design and construction phases of 96th Street roadway and drainage improvements which
    may help mitigate flooding hazards and reduce nutrient loading into surrounding groundwater. The proposed
    improvements include but not limited to a) roadway; b) drainage pipes/outlets; c) trenches; d) curbs; e) gutters; f) outfall
    structure, and g) other improvements. At least 95% of the area will have an enhanced roadway and drainage system,
    which may help reduce flooding hazards that may lead to reduction in property damage and other losses to businesses
    and may enhance water quality protection.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     The Town of Medley shall comply with all provisions of the fully executed state contract, including the standard language
    describing strict compliance with certain provisions regarding performance expectations, timely deliverable, and desired
    outcomes. The Town shall comply with standard penalties, such as holding payments or possible reduction in funding for
    failing to meet any deliverables, established performance measures, or non-performance.
