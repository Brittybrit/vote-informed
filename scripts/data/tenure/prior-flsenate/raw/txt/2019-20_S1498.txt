11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         Widen 1.7 miles of a 2 lane rural road to a 4 lane urban road with a 5 foot sidewalk and a 12 foot multi-use
         path.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         Engineering design, permitting, and construction of an urban roadway.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         Widen an existing two lane rural roadway to a four lane urban roadway with a five foot sidewalk, twelve foot
         multi-use path, and associated storm water treatment facilities.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         Residents of Citrus County. Approximately 140,000 residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         The construction project will convert a two lane rural roadway to a four lane urban (curb & gutter) roadway
         including access management (managed access, connection, and signal spacing) facilities. The project will also
         include a five foot side walk and a twelve foot multi-use path. The noted improvements within the corridor will
         increase public health, development potential, commerce, and transportation as the project is at the north end
         of the current phase of the Suncoast 2 construction project. The outcome may be measured by evaluating the
         roadway, and bike/ped capacity and traffic volumes both pre and post construction of the roadway facilities.
    f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
         penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         Standard contract penalties.
