4.   Project/Program Description
     Weeki Wachee Springs is an outstanding Florida spring that is a major attraction and destination for nearly 400,000 visitors every year.
     The spring is currently impaired for nitrate and the concentrations have been steadily increasing over the last several decades. As a
     result, the spring has experienced degradation in water quality and clarity through the growth of unwanted algae and this has
     subsequently led to other negative impacts to the sensitive aquatic ecosystem.
     As part of the Basin Management Action Plan(BMAP) for Weeki Wachee Springs, there is an imposed limit on the effluent concentration
     of nitrogen released from a wastewater reclamation facility (WRF) to be no more than 3 milligrams per liter. This project will design,
     permit, and construct necessary upgrades to the Glen WRF with the treatment processes necessary to achieve the effluent limit of 3mg/l
     total nitrogen.

11. Program Performance
 a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
     The Glen WRF is located in the Weeki Wachee BMAP. This project will result in reduced discharge of total nitrogen into the aquifer
     from the WRF.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
     Design and construct wastewater treatment plant assets necessary to achieve 3 milligrams/liter (mg/l) reclaimed water limit at the
     Glen Water Reclamation Facility (WRF). This level of treatment is required by the Weeki Wachee BMAP.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
     This project will provide citizens with improved water quality in the Floridan aquifer and in the Weeki Wachee Springs and River.




 d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
     Local citizens and tourists who visit Weeki Wachee Springs and River. Greater than 500,000 individuals will be served by improved
     water quality. Weeki Wachee State park had 384,723 visitors in FY 2015.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
     The benefit of this project is improved quality of ground water, springs, and river water. The first level of verification of outcome will be
     sampling at the WRF demonstrating reduced TN discharged to the aquifer. Gradually, FDEP monitoring of water quality in the Weeki
     Wachee system will demonstrate a reduction of TN.




  f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
     penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
     In addition to penalties already set up in the Florida Administrative Code for non-compliance with permit conditions, the agency may
     withhold funding if the project is not completed.
