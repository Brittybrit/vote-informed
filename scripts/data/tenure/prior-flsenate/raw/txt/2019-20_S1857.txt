   conservation of the natural environment. This is particularly important for younger children to learn about nature,

11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        The funds requested are to be used to build a nature center in our local Pine Rockland Preserve. This project
        will provide a local learning space for the local schools and residents who wish to learn more about nature and
        it's beautiful impact on everyday lives. We are confident this project will have an environmental impact on all
        who will take advantage of learning opportunity to be provided.
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        The Nature Center is anticipated to provide classes and courses in support of the local public and private
        schools. To enhance the learning experience, the center will also feature exhibit areas and outdoor classes to
        engage the younger population. All classes will promote environmental education and preservation.
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Environmental education and preservation.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        Our targeted population include: elderly persons, at-risk youth, preschool students, grade school students, high
        school students, and university/collage students. We expect to serve greater than 800 individuals.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
      will be measured?
        This project will improve physical health by providing education concerning the importance of being outdoor in
        nature while encouraging physical activity for the overall community health. This will also improve mental
        health through learning and feedback to be provided via participant assessment. We will improve agricultural
        production/promotion/education by providing course materials to participating individuals. This project will
        improve quality of education by improving academics by allowing students to actively participate in the
        environment. Added benefits will an increase to economic activity, increase tourism and provide economic
        opportunities to businesses by increased visitorship. Measured by participant feedback, on-site recordkeeping
        and new occupational license approvals.
