4. Project/Program Description
    Miami Lakes Drive, also known as NW 154th Street, is one of the Town's major thoroughfares, providing east to west
   transportation access and connection for road users. The Palmetto Expressway has interchanges with three roadways that
   serve Miami Lakes – SR 823 (NW 57th Avenue), NW 67th Avenue, and NW 154th Street. The Miami Lakes NW 154th
   Street To NW 77th Court Turn Lane Extension Transportation Improvements Project includes the design, permitting, and
   construction modification to the existing turn lane from NW 154th Street west bound to NW 77th Court south bound. The
   Project's proposed goal is to reduce traffic congestion on NW 154th Street (Miami Lakes Drive) from nearby shopping
   centers, restaurants, hotels, schools, and SHS SR826 on ramps.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Miami Lakes Drive, also known as NW 154th Street, is one of the Town's major thoroughfares providing east to west
    transportation access for road users. The purpose of the Project is to reduce traffic congestion on NW 154th Street
    (Miami Lakes Drive) from nearby shopping centers, restaurants, hotels, schools, and SHS SR826 on ramps. The goal of
    the Project is to construct modification to the existing turn lane from NW 154th Street west bound to NW 77th Court south
    bound to reduce traffic congestion and enhance traffic flow.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Activities and services to be provided include the design, permitting, and construction modification to the existing turn
    lane from NW 154th Street west bound to NW 77th Court south bound. The Project will reduce traffic congestion on NW
    154th Street (Miami Lakes Drive) from nearby shopping centers, restaurants, hotels, schools, and SHS SR826 on ramps.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Direct services to be provided to the citizens and general public of road users include reduced traffic congestion and
    improved transportation conditions on one of the Town's main thoroughfares NW 154th Street/Miami Lakes Drive and
    SHS SR826 on ramps.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    Target population served by this project are Miami Lakes' citizens and the general public of road users traveling on the
    Town's major thoroughfare NW 154th Street/Miami Lakes Drive and SHS SR826 on ramps.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     Expected benefits include improved transportation conditions, increased traffic flow, and reduced traffic congestion on
    one of the Town's major thoroughfares and SHS SR826 on ramps. Outcome will be measured by field observations and
    police cameras at site. The Project will create specific contractor engineer, environmental, and consultant immediate job
    opportunities measured by contractual agreements.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     No payment will be made for a request for reimbursement which does not include an updated summary of the project,
    dated invoices, checks paid by the contracting agency, and proof of payments via copies of deposited checks.
