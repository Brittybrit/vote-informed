1. Title of Project: Plant City Westside Integrated Treatment Park
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 01/05/2018
4. Project/Program Description:
    The Westside Canal currently facilitates minimal storm water treatment within the canal system. The City of Plant
    City (City) proposes to improve treatment by providing connectivity between the canal and an adjacent wetland and
    storm water ponds. In addition to providing additional storm water treatment the City proposes to incorporated
    park provisions for an overall enhanced integrated treatment park. The purpose of this project is to provide
    technical proof that by modifying the existing ponds, and restoring the wetland and canal in the project area, it will
    enhance ecological function and provide additional storm water quality treatment for the Westside Canal and
    downstream receiving waterbodies.
