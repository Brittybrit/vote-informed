1. Title of Project: El Portal - Little River Stormwater Improvements- Historic El Jardin
2. Senate Sponsor: Daphne Campbell
3. Date of Submission: 11/30/2017
4. Project/Program Description:
    This request of for the design, permitting, construction, and certification of stormwater improvements to the El
    Jardin area, which is located between North Miami Avenue and NE 2nd Avenue within El Portal. The project
    planning has been funded under the Clean Water State Revolving Fund (SRF) and is anticipated to be completed by
    May of 2018.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           Reduce exposure to health risks (virus, bacteria, controlled substances) in flood waters and groundwater.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Decreased health risks, increased quality of life, and increased property value resulting from properties with
          functioning stormwater management systems.

    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          Functioning stormwater management system.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          Village residents and property owners. A Household Income Survey completed in August 2017 found the per
         capita income for Village to be lower than the state per capita income. This phase will serve approximately 90
         homes in the historic El Jardin neighborhood.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          Reduce exposure to health risks (virus, bacteria, controlled substances) in flood waters and groundwater.
         Quantify number of septic systems failing before and after stormwater improvement construction.

    g. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
       penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
        Withdraw funding from project.
