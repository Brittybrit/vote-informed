13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Requested funds will expand health education to improve health literacy with a focus on breast cancer and heart
    disease, two of the most costly and leading causes of largely preventable deaths in Florida. These funds will empower
    targeted populations to live disease deterrent lifestyles which have been proven to save lives, reduce costs, and improve
    public health outcomes.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     To meet the intended purpose of the requested funding, digital curriculum delivery will be provided to college students
    about breast cancer, heart disease, disease prevention, early detection, clinical trials, and the SDOH, along with student
    peer group advocacy to broaden the impact. Additionally, low-income individuals are targeted to receive health education
    through community outreach engagement and health educational materials.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This appropriation project will directly:1) Provide education about breast cancer, heart disease, disease prevention, early
    detection, clinical trials, and SDOH virtual education to citizens attending post-secondary academic institutions. 2)Provide
    low-income citizens with critical health education about breast cancer, heart disease, disease prevention, and early
    detection through their participation in health educational rallies and the receipt of health educational materials which
    include sites providing low-cost preventive screenings.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Education is the Bridge to Health Literacy will educate 900 multi-disciplined college students including future healthcare
    professionals about breast cancer, heart disease, disease prevention, early detection, clinical trials, and SDOH; and 300
    low-income individuals will receive health education about breast cancer, heart disease, disease prevention, early
    detection, and clinical trials. In total 1,200 individuals will be served through virtual health education & direct community
    engagement.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit of the project is increased health literacy about breast cancer and heart disease among college
    students and economically disadvantaged individuals. Success will be measured by 1) 900 college students completing
    the virtual program with assessments documenting increased knowledge; and advocacy documented by 3,000 social
    media impressions and at least 9,000 engagements 2) 300 individuals experience increased knowledge as documented
    at health educational rallies and by surveys conducted with individuals receiving health educational packages.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     The Women's Breast & Heart Initiative, Florida Affiliate, Inc. will return any unspent funding corresponding to unmet
    deliverables and/or performance measures.
