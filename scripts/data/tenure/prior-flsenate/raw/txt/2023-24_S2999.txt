4. Project/Program Description
    Jimmy Ryce was abducted, raped and murdered on September 11, 1995, getting off of his school bus in the Redland,
   Florida. For three months his parents, Don and Claudine, led an extensive search. The Ryces always believed that if a
   bloodhound was available when Jimmy was abducted he would have been found, alive. To honor their son’s memory, they
   established the Jimmy Ryce Center for Victims of Predatory Abduction. Their goal was to donate a bloodhound to every law
   enforcement agency/police department.

     Distribute bloodhounds to law enforcement agencies and police departments, tracking and trailing seminars for
    bloodhounds and K9 officers and training programs to include travel expenses, bloodhound care and upkeep and
    maintenance, community outreach initiatives and programs including but not limited to attendance at law enforcement
    conferences, marketing including education/awareness.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Jimmy Ryce was abducted, raped & murdered in 1995, getting off of his school bus in Redland, Florida. For 3 months his
    parents, Don and Claudine, led an extensive search. The Ryces always believed that if a bloodhound was available when
    Jimmy was abducted he would have been found, alive. The Jimmy Ryce Center was established and our mission is to
    provide bloodhounds to law enforcement agencies.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     To honor their son’s memory, they established the Jimmy Ryce Center for Victims of Predatory Abduction. To date, the
    Jimmy Ryce Center has donated nearly 700 bloodhounds to law enforcement agencies. Our mission is to make the
    Ryce’s goal a reality. Our efforts will help law enforcement search to find missing children, as well as endangered adults.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Missing children and missing endangered adults state wide.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The bloodhounds will be utilized to assist law enforcement agencies to find missing children and endangered adults. The
    FBI and National Center for Missing and Exploited Children are all proponents of bloodhounds and their abilities. The
    National Center even named a training center after Jimmy Ryce
    https://ojjdp.ojp.gov/library/publications/jimmy-ryce-law-enforcement-training-center-program.
    Every September, Florida observes Missing Children's Day at the State Capital. This was started in Jimmy's memory. We
    can track number of missions bloodhounds respond to including outcomes.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to utilize the funds in the specified manor shall result in funds being returned to the State.
