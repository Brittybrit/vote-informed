1. Title of Project: Coral Gables 911 System Upgrades
2. Senate Sponsor: Jose Rodriguez
3. Date of Submission: 02/22/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   The City is seeking $300,000 in matching funds to upgrade its Public Safety Answering Point Service (PSAP). The
   City’s current PSAP solution (ComTech), will be end of life, end of support and become functionally obsolete once
   Miami-Dade County begins using E-911 functions such as text to 911 in 2019. The City and County PSAP systems
   must communicate seamlessly to provide critical public safety services to the community.

11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        Replace legacy end of life 911 PSAP with a new West (Viper) 911PAP systems that meets the county's
        interoperability requirements for next generation 911 (E-911, text to 911, etc).
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        The City is one of the County's backup PSAP for first responding and public/life safety functions. The City is also
        an emergency response HUB for several satellite cities (small municipalities in the vicinity).
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Public safety services, 911 call answering service, police, and fire emergency response.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        All City of Coral Gables residents, businesses venues and their employees, tenants, tourists, visitors, and
        transient drivers. Also, in case of disasters, the City is a direct backup of the County's 911 PSAP.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
      will be measured?
        The City's legacy 911 PSAP will be replaced. The new 911 PSAP will inter-operate with the County's 911 system,
        will be able to receive text to 911, will feature highly accurate location mapping for 911 calls originating from
        cellphones, and will be ready to support other new services required by E-911 (next generation 911). Outcome
        will be measured by analyzing response times, Ani-Ali information accuracy improvements, quality of text to
        911 features, quality of GIS mapping features, ability to inter-operate with neighboring agencies, ease of use for
        911 operators, and the systems reporting capabilities.
   f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
        penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
