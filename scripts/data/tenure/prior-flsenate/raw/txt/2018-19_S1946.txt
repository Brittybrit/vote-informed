11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           The purpose of this project is to provide mentoring to at risk girls ages 6-14 in Title 1 schools throughout
          Miami Dade County. The goals of this program are to serve as a holistic after school program for girls helping
          them focus on their academics, with a special emphasis on improving and increasing their exposure to science,
          technology, engineering, Arts and mathematics (STEAM) education, while providing a healthy outlet for
          discussions on several critical elements they experience and are exposed to in their daily lives. D.R.E.A.M
          provides a healthy environment to mold and shape young girls into future leaders and role models for their
          peers.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Daily mentoring, tutoring, STEAM and service learning activities.

f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
     will be measured?
      Enrich cultural experience. Goal: To increase exposure, interest and potential to further engage and explore
     STEAM related educational activities and career opportunities. Measurable Objective: 80% of participating in
     the after-school STEAM program will be able to: Identify at least 5 professions related to STEAM. Demonstrate
     5 ways that STEAM affects their life daily. Identify 3 women who chose a STEAM career and the significant
     contribution they made. Identify 5 ways in which STEAM has contributed to society and helped to change lives.
     Improve quality of education. Goal: To improve participant’s academic performance in the classroom.
     Participants will show a marked improvement in the classes where they are struggling as a result of them
