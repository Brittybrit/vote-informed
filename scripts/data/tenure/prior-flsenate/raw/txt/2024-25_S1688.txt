1. Project Title         MIAMI LAKES LOCH LOMOND PHASE II DRAINAGE IMPROVEMENTS

2. Senate Sponsor        Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request       11/01/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Flood Mitigation Activities: The Miami Lakes Loch Lomond Phase II Drainage Improvements Project includes flood
   mitigation activities in the Loch Lomond community and consists of the design, permitting, and construction of the new
   drainage system, including new catch basins, manholes, drainage pipes, exfiltration trenches (French drains), milling and
   resurfacing of roadway, and signing and pavement markings in Miami Lakes, along Burnside Way, East Troon Circle, and
   West Prestwick Place. The Project will directly impact approximately 30 homes. The Project will increase drainage capacity,
   reduce localized flooding, and enhance the protection of private property. The Town is requesting legislative appropriation
   of $920,000 to complete this Project.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The specific goal is to construct the Project for the purpose of mitigating and reducing localized flooding and standing
    rainwater, enhancing protection of residential homes from flood events, increasing drainage capacity, reducing the
    probability of mosquito-transmitted diseases to the public, and protecting of private property and life in Miami Lakes'
    residential neighborhoods.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Flood mitigation activities and services consist of the construction of the new drainage system, including the design,
    permitting, and construction of new catch basins, manholes, drainage pipes, exfiltration trenches (French drains), milling
    and resurfacing of roadway, and signing and pavement markings along Burnside Way, East Troon Circle, and West
    Prestwick Place. The project will directly impact approximately 30 homes.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Direct services are flood mitigation activities to further reduce or eliminate the risk of flood damage in the target
    population of the Loch Lomond residential community, as well as, the protection of residential property and life,
    benefitting approximately 30 homes in Miami Lakes.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Target population served by this Project is the Loch Lomond residential community, approximately 30 homes, in Miami
    Lakes. The Project will directly impact approximately 30 homes along Burnside Way, East Troon Circle, and West
    Prestwick Place.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Expected benefits include improved drainage, reduced localized flooding, increased drainage capacity, improved
    stormwater management, eliminated standing rainwater, reduced probability of mosquito-transmitted diseases to the
    public, reduced impact to property and life, enhanced environmental protection to the general public, improved
    transportation conditions, and improved groundwater and surface water quality. Outcome will be measured by field
    inspection and documentation of rainfall events. Project will create specific contractor, engineer, environmental, and
    consultant immediate job opportunities in stormwater and construction management measured by contractual
    agreements.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     No payment will be made for a request for reimbursement which does not include an updated summary of the project,
    dated invoices, checks paid by the contracting agency, and proof of payments via copies of deposited checks.
