4. Project/Program Description
    Project PLAY Ability will provide free, adaptive soccer programming in Miami-Dade County for 600 children and youth
   with intellectual and developmental disabilities, including autism and Down syndrome. The program delivers twice-
   weekly, 90-minute inclusive sessions over a 33-week cycle at schools, parks, and community centers, using modified
   soccer activities, sensory-friendly warm-ups, and mentorship to build physical skills, confidence, and social connection.
   State funding will also support specialized training for 24 coaches to individualize instruction and ensure safe, high-
   quality participation. Families will receive referrals to therapy, social-skills programs, and related supports, reinforcing
   long-term inclusion and well-being beyond the field.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will support Project PLAY Ability in Miami-Dade County, enabling FCC USA to deliver adaptive and inclusive
    soccer programming that provides 600 children and youth with intellectual and developmental disabilities with safe,
    meaningful opportunities to build physical skills, confidence, and social connection. State support will also allow FCC
    USA to equip 24 coaches with specialized strategies to individualize instruction, structure high-quality sessions, and
    ensure participants can fully engage and thrive.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Project PLAY Ability will serve 600 children and youth annually through twice-weekly, 90-minute adaptive and inclusive
    soccer sessions delivered over a 33-week program cycle at schools, parks, and community centers. Activities include
    modified soccer instruction, sensory-friendly warm-ups, and structured mentorship designed to support physical
    development, confidence, and social connection. Trained coaches will also provide families with referrals to therapy,
    social-skills programs, and other supportive services through established community partners.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     FCC USA will provide free adaptive and inclusive soccer programming through Project PLAY Ability, serving 600
    children and youth with intellectual and developmental disabilities, including autism and Down syndrome. Services
    include twice-weekly, 90-minute sessions delivered over a 33-week period at schools, parks, and community centers,
    led by 24 trained coaches. The program also includes family engagement activities and referrals to complementary
    community-based services.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is children and youth with intellectual and developmental disabilities, including autism and Down
    syndrome, residing in Miami-Dade County. The project is expected to directly serve 600 children and youth annually
    through adaptive and inclusive soccer programming.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The expected outcome is improved physical development, confidence, and social engagement among participating
    children and youth with intellectual and developmental disabilities. At least 70 percent of participants are expected to
    demonstrate improvement in age-appropriate motor skills or physical activity indicators from baseline to program
    completion. Outcomes will be measured using FCC USA’s proprietary SIMEON data system, which establishes
    participant baselines at program entry and tracks quarterly progress through structured coach observations and
    participation metrics.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     FCC ensures compliance with state appropriations by promptly addressing deficiencies through root cause analysis
    and targeted corrective measures, such as process adjustments, staff training, and resource reallocation. Progress is
    monitored via performance metrics and audits, with oversight from the Executive Director and Board of Directors.
    Regular updates are provided to stakeholders, reflecting FCC USA's commitment to accountability, continuous
    improvement, and impactful use of state funds.
