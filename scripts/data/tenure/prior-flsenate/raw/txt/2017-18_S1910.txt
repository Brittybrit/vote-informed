9. Program Performance (if needed, include additional documentation):
a. How will requested funds be spent? Include supporting documentation.
North Bay Village is requesting $500,000 in funding to continue the Village’s extensive Drainage
Improvement Project. The Village has been working diligently to retrofit the aged drainage system by
installing new pumping stations, repairing and replacing pipes and securing funding to install flex valves
on the Village’s fifty (50) outfall pipes. Since the Village initially began the arduous task of retrofitting our
system, we have invested a total of approximately $5 million through a combination of loans, grants and
the Village’s local match. The Village has also secured a consultant to review the current stormwater
system and develop a design for the recommended improvements. As a result of this Stormwater System
Feasibility Study, the Village has identified a phase of the project for which funding from the State will
help to ensure the success of this project. Funding from the State will be used to expand/modify the
existing stormwater collection system and continue the rehabilitation of existing outfall pipes at various
locations throughout the Village.
b. Identify expected program results and the expected benefit associated from the requested funds.
This project will help to balance and improve flood control in the Village by increasing the quantity of
water that the drainage system is able to support. We will also help to protect the verified impaired
waters of Biscayne Bay by allowing rainwater from the Village’s roadways to be treated prior to entering
directly into the Bay. Once the drainage system is repaired, we will protect this investment by installing
tide flex valves at the end of each outfall. The flex valves will prevent the outfall pipes from salt water
intrusion, and keep the water from the Bay from entering the drainage system and overflowing into the
roadways. Finally, the Village has implemented an ongoing educational component to support this
