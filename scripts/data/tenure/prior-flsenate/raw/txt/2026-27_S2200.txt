13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to provide peer to peer support so that officers who are suffering from PTSD, understand the possible
    symptoms and how to combat through these different emotions. Unlike the military, PTSD in police officers is prevalent
    because of the work they do on a daily basis and not tracked back to a specific event. Over the course of a law
    enforcement officers' career, they can experience high levels of cortisone due to adrenaline, which causing mood
    changes, depression, dread, and other such feeling which can significantly and negatively impact the officer's ability for
    a long healthy life after retirement.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Peer to peer support for police officers suffering from PTSD.
