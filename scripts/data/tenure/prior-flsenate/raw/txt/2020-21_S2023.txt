4.   Project/Program Description
     To build a multi-modal station as a regional park-and-ride for county transit services. The park-and-ride will provide parking support to
     Miami-Dade County's Bus Rapid Transit System on the county's transitway that will transport residents as far south as Homestead, to the
     S. Dadeland Metrorail Station, which lacks sufficient parking to support the S. Dade commuter ridership. The park-and-ride will provide
     approximately 500 parking spaces, on-demand service, drop-off and pick-up, as well as other amenities to be added per recent mobility
     hub feasibility study findings.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      To build a multi-modal station as a regional park-and-ride for county transit services. The park-and-ride will provide parking support to
      Miami-Dade County's Bus Rapid Transit System on the county's transitway that will transport residents as far south as Homestead to
      the S. Dadeland Metrorail Station, which lacks sufficient parking to support the S. Dade commuter ridership. The park-and-ride will
      provide approximately 500 parking spaces, on-demand service, drop-off and pick-up, as well as other amenities to be added per
      recent mobility hub feasibility study findings.


 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      The village will have to secure a vendor to perform the planning, engineering, and design then a contractor to perform the
      construction via our procurement process.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      The proposed park-and-ride facility will help to reduce traffic congestion and minimize commute times to and from work, home, and/or
      school.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      The target population: elderly persons, jobless persons, economically disadvantaged persons, homeless, high school students,
      University/College students, and the general public. Greater than 800 people are expected to be served.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      The project will help keep the general public from harm by enabling less carbon dioxide emissions to be expelled by reducing the
      number of cars in circulation. Improved transportation conditions are expected by providing shorter commutes from Homestead to
      downtown Miami. The hope is that outcomes are to increase or improve economic activity, increase tourism, create specific
      immediate job opportunities, and enhance economic self-sufficiency. Increased access to jobs will bring more businesses and tourism
      opportunities locally.



 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Withholding of funds.
