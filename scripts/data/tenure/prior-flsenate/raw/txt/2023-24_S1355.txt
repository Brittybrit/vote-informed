4. Project/Program Description
    The estimated cost of the Miami Lakes NW 59th Avenue Roadway Extension and Redevelopment Bridge Project is
   $14,100,315. The Town is requesting State legislative appropriation of $3,000,000 (21.3%); reflecting a Town local share,
   grant funds, and other contributions of $11,100,315 (78.7%) to fund this Project. The Project consists of design,
   engineering, and permitting; land purchase; construction; and construction engineering and inspection phases to construct
   a new bridge, roadway connection, and drainage system in the commercial and industrial NE quadrant of the Town,
   extending NW 59th Avenue south to NW 151st Street. The Project will provide north-south connectivity and access to the
   Town's only industrial and commercial district, as well as increase public access to businesses and commerce, jobs,
   transportation, and improved storm water management throughout the region.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Project consists of the design, engineering, and permitting; land purchase; construction; and construction
    engineering and inspection phases to construct a new bridge, roadway connection, and drainage system in the
    commercial and industrial NE quadrant of the Town, thereby increasing public access to businesses and commerce, jobs,
    transportation, and improved storm water management throughout the region. Funds requested will assist the Town to
    achieve the land purchase acquisition costs and professional engineering services costs to construct and complete this
    Project.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Project is currently in the design phase. Funds will be spent towards the land purchase costs, easement/property
    deed requirements, land ownership documentation, costs associated with the completion of the land release/sale
    process, and professional engineering services costs to construct and complete this Project.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     General Public:Currently, there is no north-south connectivity for the residential, commercial, and industrial population of
    the Town to access the industrial and commercial district located on and around NW 59th Avenue. Motorists traveling to
    or from the northeast quadrant must travel outside of the Town’s boundaries, thus leading to traffic congestion on the
    State Highway System (SHS) SR 823/NW 57th Avenue (Red Road) and diminished opportunities for increased
    commerce. The Project will provide the new bridge and drainage improvements necessary to link the NE district to the
    SE, and in-turn the rest of the Town’s residential and commercial districts. Additional services to the public include
    improved transportation conditions, infrastructure, improved stormwater management, expanded connectivity/access to
    jobs, improved distribution of traffic flows, expansion of complete streets on the NW 151st/NW 153rd Streets, and the
    creation of jobs in the construction and engineering industries.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population served by this project is the general transportation and mobility public comprised of vehicular and
    pedestrian commuters, including the residents, business owners, regional industries, and visitors of the Miami Lakes and
    Miami-Dade County. The NW 59th Avenue industrial and commercial district is home to numerous businesses, industries,
    and educational opportunities, including the Miami Lakes Education Center, Costco Wholesale, Aircraft Electric Motors,
    Evolution Lighting, health and medical suppliers, Cardinal Health, Caterpillar Inc., Alexim Air and Ocean Cargo, Miami
    Lakes Automall, Cordis Corporation manufacturer, and the Miami-Dade Police Department police station, to name a few.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcomes are to construct the new bridge and provide vital transportation and mobility infrastructure to the
    general public to improve transportation conditions, increase economic activity, increase drainage capacity, improve
    storm water management, improve groundwater and surface water quality, decrease traffic congestion on the State
    Highway System (SHS) SR 823/NW 57th Avenue (Red Road), provide mobility alternatives, improve distribution of traffic
    flows, and create specific immediate job opportunities in the construction and engineering fields. The methodology by
    which these outcomes will be measured are through contractual services agreements, trip data traffic reduction analysis,
    water quality testing and monitoring, and field inspections.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     No payment will be made for a request for reimbursement which does not include an updated summary of the project,
    dated invoices, checks paid by the contracting agency, and proof of payments via copies of deposited checks.
