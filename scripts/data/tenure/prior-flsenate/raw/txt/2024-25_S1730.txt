1. Project Title          School Bond Issuance Database

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        11/13/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    For DOE to renew a yearly subscription to a clearinghouse database which would 1) keep track of all bonds prior to vote
   and issuance by school districts, 2) allow districts to coordinate purchasing across districts, 3) allow districts to rate and
   review vendors, 4) allow districts to share project documents and 5) advertise RFPs nationwide to increase the number of
   competitors that reply to RFPs. The increase in vendor competition will help reduce cost.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Keeping track of what bonds are being discussed, proposed or issued by school districts prior to issuance. Allowing
    smaller school districts to coordinate purchases to achieve economies of scale. Increase the number of vendors
    competing for school district RFPs as a means by which to reduce cost through competition. The technology would notify
    a larger, national group of vendors of opportunities in order to increase competition, by increasing the number of
    competitors.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The clearinghouse database will keep track of what bonds are being discussed, have been proposed by a district or local
    board of education, and voted on by the public (with vote outcome) and therefore issued by school districts. Allowing
    smaller school districts to coordinate purchases to achieve economies of scale. Increase the number of vendors
    competing for school district RFPs as a means by which to reduce cost through competition. The technology would notify
    and publish to a larger, national group of vendors of the bid opportunities in order to increase competition through
    increasing the reach and number of competitors.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     No direct services will be provided to citizens.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Florida students and taxpayers.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Florida taxpayers will benefit from the increased competition and reduction in capital expenditures of local school
    districts. Students will benefit from this same reduction that will allow additional dollars to flow to the classroom. District
    activity tracking and reporting will provide data on the outcome.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The agency should renew a contract for this service as a yearly subscription. If the technology does not work after
    payment and activation, withhold future payments/renewals.
