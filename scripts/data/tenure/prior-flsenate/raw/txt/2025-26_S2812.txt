13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This facility will serve as a multi-purpose hub for police personnel, providing a secure parking structure for law
    enforcement vehicles, dedicated training areas, and an emergency staging zone for rapid deployment during critical
    incidents. By integrating essential resources into one strategic location, this facility will enhance operational efficiency,
    readiness, and response times for public safety efforts.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     To provide secure parking spaces for law enforcement vehicles, including patrol cars, emergency response units, and
    specialized vehicles. To create dedicated emergency staging areas that facilitate quick mobilization of law enforcement
    and emergency vehicles in response to critical incidents. To establish training zones and readiness spaces within the
    garage to enhance officers’ tactical skills, crisis management, and vehicle operations. To contribute to overall public
    safety by enhancing the law enforcement infrastructure necessary to protect and serve the community.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will experience faster, more coordinated emergency responses in situations such as natural disasters, public
    safety threats, and major accidents. The facility’s emergency staging area ensures law enforcement and emergency
    vehicles are readily available and quickly deployed, minimizing delays during critical moments. The presence of a state-
    of-the-art facility dedicated to readiness ensures that citizens can rely on swift, organized, and efficient emergency
    response, strengthening the community's resilience and safety during emergencies.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The facility will serve hundreds of law enforcement officers, providing them with a dedicated, centralized space for
    vehicle staging, training, and operational readiness. This will enhance the efficiency, effectiveness, and safety of officers
    across the region. Additional emergency response units (such as fire departments, EMS, and specialized tactical teams)
    who may need to coordinate with law enforcement for large-scale events or disasters. The entire community benefits from
    quicker emergency response times, improved public safety, and a more trained and prepared law enforcement presence.
    The facility indirectly serves thousands of citizens, particularly in emergencies, ensuring public safety is maintained during
    critical events or incidents.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     Faster deployment of law enforcement and emergency vehicles during critical incidents, reducing response times in
    emergencies. Law enforcement vehicles will be securely stored, regularly maintained, and mission-ready at all times.
    Improved law enforcement coordination during major incidents (natural disasters, active shooter situations, civil unrest,
    etc.). Measurement Methodology: Track multi-agency coordination drills and readiness exercises. Measure deployment
    efficiency in real emergency situations. Document after-action reports to evaluate response effectiveness.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    Increase on-site inspections, audits, or reporting requirements if quality or time-line issues arise.
