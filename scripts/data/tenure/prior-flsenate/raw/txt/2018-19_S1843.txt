1. Title of Project: U.S. Institute Against Human Trafficking - Ending Human Trafficking
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 12/12/2017
4. Project/Program Description:
    U.S. Institute Against Human Trafficking (USIAHT) requests funding for three project areas: 1) TraffickingFree Zone
    Pilot Program Expansion. This project will implement proven practices through a modular services engagement for
    county governments and communities to fight sex trafficking in their area. It is currently piloted in Pasco County.
    Requested funds will be used to expand the pilot to 7 additional counties. 2) Human Trafficking Youth Triage
    Center. This project will develop a short-term assessment center for youth victims of sex trafficking to be cared for
    over a 3-7 day period, meeting their immediate needs, and conducting the necessary assessments to determine
    their long-term care requirements and placement into long-term care. 3) University of South Florida Safe Home
    Comparison Study. This project will fund year 1 of a 3-year study with the University of South Florida to study the
    treatment and outcomes of boys in the USIAHT safe home.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           1) TraffickingFree Zone Pilot Program expansion beyond Pasco County into an additional seven counties in
          Florida. 2) Open a Human Trafficking Youth Triage Center to address the overnight care and immediate needs
          of minor victims of human trafficking over a 3 - 7 day period until long-term placement is identified. 3) Conduct
          year 1 of a 3-year Florida Safe Home comparison study by the University of South Florida to assess outcomes
          and differences between safe homes serving female minors versus male minors.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           TRAFFICKINGFREE ZONE PILOT PROGRAM EXPANSION: USIAHT will engage with local governments in a
          consulting and services engagement through this 10 module, 12 month program implementing promising
          practices from other areas of the country to fight sex trafficking in their local community. HUMAN TRAFFICKING
          YOUTH TRIAGE CENTER: USIAHT will develop a short-term care “triage center” facility in the Tampa Bay area
          for CSEC youth extracted from their HT situation, allowing for a 3-7 day stay with a shower, bed, and for
          assessments to occur on the victim’s history, current situation, medical needs, develop a short-term and long-
          term care plan, and more. USF SAFE HOME COMPARISON STUDY: The University of South Florida will partner
          with USIAHT and conduct the first year of a planned three-year study to provide a comprehensive assessment
          and comparison of service provision and youth outcomes between USIAHT's safe house for boys and one of the
          current safe houses in Florida for girls.

d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

     TRAFFICKINGFREE ZONE PILOT PROGRAM EXPANSION will provide education and training of law enforcement,
    the criminal justice system, education of high school, college and at-risk youth and education of the general
    population about human trafficking, and increase the focus on buyers and ending demand. HUMAN
    TRAFFICKING YOUTH TRIAGE CENTER will offer facilities and services for minor victims of human trafficking just
    recovered, until long-term placement can be identified that meets their needs. Services will include immediate
    medical treatment, assessment and best suited long-term care plan and placement. USF SAFE HOME
    COMPARISON STUDY will engage directly with the victims in the safe home and conduct the study of the boys
    safe home as compared to a girls safe home.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          At-risk youth, high school students, university/college students, victims of crime, and Commercially Sexually
         Exploited Children (CSEC) and minors who are victims of sex trafficking. It is expected that greater than 800
         individuals will be served.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          Improvement of physical/mental health of the CSEC victims. Quality of education increased for high school
         and college students, and education of other service providers. The public will receive an increased level of
         protection from harm through combating human trafficking. The CSEC youth will receive protection through
         the Triage Center and enhanced economic self-sufficiency by increasing successful placement rates into long
         term care. Recidivism rates will be reduced, and more habitual buyers of sex will be arrested. Substance abuse
         will be reduced in CSEC youth, with diversion away from the juvenile justice system through increased
         placements into proper care. “Best practices” will be identified in safe home operations and shared with
         others. All of the above will be measured through data gathering by USIAHT in each of the program areas.

    g. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
       penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
         USIAHT will submit a review to the state detailing it’s plan to meet deliverables in a reasonable amount of
       time and/or accept an abatement in its allocation if services provided are underutilized or not being used by
       the target population.
