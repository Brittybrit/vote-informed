11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
          This project will provide the funding for infrastructure that will allow us to expand academic opportunities and
          extracurricular activities for our student population.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
          The infrastructure will help us prepare for future campus expansion as noted above.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
          Expansion of our campus will provide educational opportunities for current and additional students.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
          The target population is elementary and secondary students. Approximately 1730 individuals are expected to
          be served by this funding.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
          The expected benefit is to continue to provide an A-rated education to current and additional students. The
          methodology by which this outcome will be measured is by monitoring and addressing our student data and
          growth.
    f.    What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
          penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
          Loss of funding.
