1. Project Title          Miami Springs - Westward Drive Roadway Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor        Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request       11/13/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    This project consists of the roadway improvements on the Westward Drive corridor from Miami Springs Circle. to
   Hammond Drive, to accommodate pedestrian, bicycle and vehicular activity and to address public safety. Construction to
   provide roadway resurfacing, re-striping to ensure safer roads, optimize traffic flow, reduce congestion, and improve
   overall safety and transportation efficiency. As well as implement safety measures, including the installation of improved
   signage, benches and expand pedestrian safety with crosswalks/sidewalks. Project to include the cost of engineering,
   CEI Services, architectural design, permitting, construction management and cost estimating.
