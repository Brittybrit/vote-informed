1. Title of Project: Miami Shores Village Storm Water Pumping Station
2. Senate Sponsor: Daphne Campbell
3. Date of Submission: 01/24/2018
4. Project/Program Description:
    Through this project, plans will be designed to create a storm water pumping station to eliminate flooding in an
    entire sub-division. During high tide, KingTides and during storms, sea water comes out of the storm drains
    connected to Biscayne Bay. In addition, when it rains during high tide events, storm waterfloods roadway surfaces
    and has intruded into homes in this area. This system will pump against the tide into the bay away from roadways
    and homes whileusing pollution control devices.
