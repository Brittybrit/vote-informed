11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           Human Trafficking, and Teen and Dating violence is affecting young people of all social economic backgrounds;
          however, it is affecting those who are predominantly in underserved communities or have low socioeconomic
          backgrounds. Human trafficking is usually presumed to be refugees from other countries; however, this is not
          the case in Miami-Dade and Broward Counties. More and more of our young girls and boys from our schools
          and communities are being lured into slavery. Domestic and Teen dating violence is a big problem affecting
          youth in every community across the nation. Girls and boys from elementary school and even up into their
          college years are being solicited. It is reported that Florida ranked #3 in the nation for human trafficking cases.
          The Florida Department of Education has taken measures to adopt and implement policies prohibiting dating
          violence and abuse on school grounds.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           With this grant we intend to continue efforts to have 3 to 4 community awareness events/rallies every year.
          We anticipate spending $30,000 on T-shirts; $15,000 on food and drinks; $10,000 on banners and flyers; $5,000
          on music; $25,000 on labor; $5,000 on janitorial services; $10,000 on salaries; $25,000 on renovation and
          remodeling of current space to provide face-to-face sessions; $25,000 on tables, chairs, and desks for classroom
          sessions; $50,000 for radio and TV shows (1/2 hour segments, 3-4 shows per month); and $100,000 for Florida
          National University and Dr. Jose Reiguiero Foundation consultants.

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          Haitian-Americans and Latin-Americans in low socio-economic and underserved populations of Miami-Dade
         and Broward Counties. Approximately 30,000 people.

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          The outcome of this project includes increased awareness and ultimately, to put an end to human trafficking
         in our community. The outcome will be measured on an annual basis by obtaining and reviewing the "State of
         Florida Human Trafficking Response" annual report provided by the Florida Attorney General's Office and also
         by visiting https://humantraffickinghotline.org/state/florida.
