    d. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?

          Flood Evacuation/Public Roadway

    e. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

          General Hernando County population living on the Withlacoochee River

    f.   What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
         will be measured?
          Improve physical health: during flooding, being able to travel from home to buy food and supplies/roadway
         for flood evacuation utilization. Increase or improve economic activity: positive access during flooding on public
         roadway will increase home values; Property tax appraiser. Increase tourism: Direct public access to
         Withlacoochee River/trips per day/tourism facilities. Create specific immediate job opportunities: constructed
         roadway/paid wages and benefits. Enhance specific individual's economic self-sufficiency: home with positive
         access during flooding/economic gain. Improve stormwater management: construction of two (2) acre
         stormwater retention pond/water quality flood abatement. Improve surface water quality: construction of two
         (2) acre DRA/compliance with SWFWMD regulatory standards. Provide flood evacuation: flood
         evacuation/access during flooding.
