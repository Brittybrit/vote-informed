4. Project/Program Description
    The purpose of the program is to buy a hardware and software for a virtual reality system to train officers in critical
   incidents and situations to better serve the public. The VR system puts the Officers, while in training, through various
   scenarios that will teach them to more effectively neutralize threats or deem incidents safe. The VR system saves on space
   as the headset allows the Officers to see and react to different scenarios and environments without leaving the classroom.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose is to buy a virtual reality system (hardware and software) that simulates critical incidents to train police
    officers on how to handle these situations more effectively in virtual reality scenarios. The City of Miami Police trainers will
    be able to achieve this goal, while being in one room. The VR system head ware will have multiple scenarios for the
    trainers to teach Officers to handle different critical incidents. This will allow Officers to feel the stress of these situations,
    so if it happens in real life they are able to their job and save the lives.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The VR system is a cost effective and space saving way to run different simulations of real world incidents to show
    Officers how stressful it can be in critical situations. The City of Miami Police Training Department will have access to
    these systems to teach police officers to handle these incidents better and learn when to escalate or deescalate in
    situations.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This system will allow officers to save lives more efficiently and effectively. They will learn to escalation and de-escalation
    techniques using different simulations inputted into the VR simulation through the software. This will allow officers to
    better serve the community.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All active City of Miami Police Officers which attend annual training.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     It will allow City of Miami state certified training Officers to teach City of Miami Police Officers how to better handle critical
    incidents and save lives. Officers will go through different scenarios through different times in their career to better learn
    how to handle pressure and better make split second decisions. They will be critiqued by the trainers and classmates on
    what they did right, wrong and what can they do to make the outcome satisfactory.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     Return money to the state.
