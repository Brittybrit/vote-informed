14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose of the funds are to install infrastructure in this area that will result in much-needed drainage, roadway, and
    sidewalk related improvements. This project will enhance the overall quality of life, improve public rights of way, and
    safety. The completion of this project will result in mitigation of floods, prevention of property damage and loss, and
    improved traffic and public safety for residents and businesses.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Engineering and construction of the infrastructure needed to improve flooding events in the area.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Improved flood mitigation and drainage.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population to be served by this project are the residents of the immediate area and the residents of
    Sweetwater as a whole who traverse the roads that would be improved should the project be funded. The population of
    the City of Sweetwater is over 19,000.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project will protect the general public from flooding by improving water drainage from rain, improve transportation
    conditions by paving roads and reducing flood damage, increase economic growth as flooding drastically affects
    economic opportunity in the city with increasing investment permits, increase tourism by clearing up the Dolphin Mall and
    other areas for hotel construction, Improve the stormwater management by managing floods to improve traffic conditions
    and public safety, improve groundwater quality by capturing pollutants, improve surface water quality by measuring the
    pollutants that end up on the surface to be captured.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Cancellation of contract and payment for liquidated damages. The Contractor may also be prohibited from entering into
    business with the City again in the future for a certain period of time.
