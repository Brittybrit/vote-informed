1. Project Title           Miami Springs Police Department Radio Communications Upgrade

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        11/13/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    The primary objective of this initiative is to upgrade the City of Miami Springs’ technology infrastructure, with particular
   emphasis on strengthening our public safety communication systems. The department’s current radios have reached
   end-of-life, are no longer manufactured, and lack ongoing support, creating significant risks to reliable communication
   and posing a direct safety risk to officers in the field. This project entails the acquisition of upgraded radios designed to
   significantly enhance real-time communication and coordination among first responders. The necessity for this
   investment is rooted in the critical need for reliable, uninterrupted, and efficient communication, especially during
   emergencies where the rapid and accurate dissemination of information is paramount.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will be used to replace the City of Miami Springs Police Department’s current radios, which are at end-of-life
    and are no longer being manufactured or supported. Upgrading to modern, reliable communication equipment will
    ensure seamless connectivity during emergencies, strengthen coordination among first responders, and enhance the
    City's overall emergency preparedness and public safety operations.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The project will include the procurement, installation, and maintenance of new radio systems and related equipment
    for the City’s police department fleet and personnel. This will involve outfitting patrol vehicles, assigning portable radios
    to officers, and ensuring that all equipment is properly installed, programmed, and maintained for long-term
    functionality.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will directly benefit from faster emergency response times, improved officer coordination, and enhanced
    public safety services. The upgraded communication system will enable first responders to share real-time information,
    respond more effectively to critical incidents, and maintain a higher level of safety and security for the community.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The primary target population served by this project is the residents, visitors, and businesses of the City of Miami
    Springs. By improving communication and coordination among police officers, the project will enhance public safety
    and emergency response for the entire community. The project is expected to directly impact all citizens within the
    city’s jurisdiction, serving an estimated population of approximately 14,500 individuals, while also benefiting
    approximately 60 Miami Springs first responders (Officers and Civilians who rely on reliable communication for their
    safety and effectiveness.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     This project will enhance officer safety and public protection by providing reliable radios, which are critical for daily
    operations and emergency response. Without functional radios, officers cannot safely or effectively perform their
    duties. Success will be measured through system reliability, response-time records, and officer feedback on
    communication performance during critical incidents.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    Revocation of funding of Appropriation at end of Fiscal Year.
