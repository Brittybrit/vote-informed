1. Project Title          Miami Springs Glenn Curtiss Memorial

2. Senate Sponsor        Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request       12/1/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    As part of the City of Miami Springs’ Centennial Celebration, the city is seeking State of Florida funding to install a
   permanent public statue honoring City founder Glenn Hammond Curtiss. Widely recognized as the Father of Naval
   Aviation, Curtiss revolutionized military flight, and his aircraft played a critical role in World War I through
   reconnaissance, training, and coastal defense. His innovations laid the groundwork for modern naval air operations and
   helped establish the United States as a leader in aviation. In addition to his national impact, Curtiss shaped the
   development and identity of Miami Springs through his vision for community planning and growth. The proposed
   memorial—a professionally crafted statue with educational elements—will celebrate his legacy, strengthen local heritage
   tourism, and provide an enduring historical landmark for residents and visitors. State support will fund the fabrication,
   installation, and related site improvements required.

     Glenn Curtiss is widely recognized as the Father of Naval Aviation. His groundbreaking advancements in aviation
    technology directly shaped the development of seaplanes and aircraft used by the United States Navy. During World
    War I, Curtiss-designed aircraft played a vital role in America’s early military aviation efforts, contributing significantly to
    reconnaissance, training, and coastal defense. His contributions laid the foundation for modern naval air operations
    and propelled the United States into global leadership in aviation. This investment will preserve an essential piece of
    Florida and American history while enhancing public spaces, promoting heritage tourism, and inspiring future
    generations to learn from the pioneering spirit of Glenn Curtiss.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The memorial will be open day and night to all residents and visitors alike with the purpose of preserving the historical
    significance of Glenn Curtiss' contributions to this country.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    The memorial will educate visitors as to the significant impact Glenn Curtiss had on this country.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    All residents and visitors to the city.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

    The benefit of this project is the preservation of the historical significance of Glenn Curtiss contribution to the country.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
