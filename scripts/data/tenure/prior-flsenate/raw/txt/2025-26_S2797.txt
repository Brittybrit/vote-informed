4. Project/Program Description
    The funds requested will contribute to the construction of a critical public safety facility for North Bay Village (Village) and
   surrounding areas. This project will at a total cost of $48,000,000 and with the village committing the majority of these funds
   establish a new Fire and Police Station Annex to enhance emergency response capabilities. The facility will improve
   service delivery and emergency response times along the critical SR-934 evacuation route.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The primary goal is to establish a Fire and Police Station Annex that will enhance emergency response capabilities and
    strengthen public safety infrastructure in North Bay Village and surrounding areas, while improving critical evacuation
    route coverage along SR-934.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The project encompasses construction of an emergency response facility, including installation of modern public safety
    equipment, dedicated spaces for first responders and support staff, emergency communications infrastructure, and a
    coordinated disaster response center.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This facility will provide improved emergency response times, enhanced disaster preparedness capabilities, better-
    coordinated public safety services, increased capacity for emergency medical services, and strengthened evacuation
    support along SR-934.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The target population served by this project is the entire population of North Bay Village, as well as surrounding areas.
    About 15,000 individuals are expected to be served.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The project will provide measurable improvements in emergency response times, enhanced emergency response
    capabilities, more efficient evacuation coordination during disasters, and expanded service coverage throughout the area.
    These outcomes will be monitored through comprehensive data collection and regular performance assessments.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     The agency will not be reimbursed for project activities that do not meet the deliverables or performance measures.
