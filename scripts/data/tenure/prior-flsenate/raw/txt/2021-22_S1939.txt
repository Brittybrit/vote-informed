12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The request is for funding to build the Richloam Museum, which upon completion will offer free admission to the public.
    The museum will serve to preserve the history of the people of Richloam, including African Americans that worked in the
    turpentine industry.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The completion of rebuilding the cucumber packing house, train depot, ice house, and smoke house.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The museum will also educate youth on historical tasks through hands on workshops. Richloam Museum will provide
    scholarships to local under privileged students.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Economically disadvantaged persons, persons with poor mental health, persons with poor physical health, at-risk youth,
    and preschool, grade school, high school, and college students. The Richloam General Store, located on the same
    property, had 75,000 guests in 2020. The museum is expected to have over 150,000 visitors annually.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Higher education and higher responsibility for the youth. Through a question and answer survey of the youth during the
    programs it can be determined if the programs are achieving the goals.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Standard contract penalties will be sufficient.
