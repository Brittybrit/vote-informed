11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      Program will allow students to continue working on projects for school STEAM related homeworks. This will place the students in the
      library after school to work with technology and equipment they might not have access to.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      The library will have staff assisting and guiding the those interested in learning and working with the Innovation Center.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      There will be guidance with the equipment to allow the users to create and work with new ideas and have opportunities to expand on
      production and design.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      While the Innovation Center will be open to all, it will be geared towards students.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      The Innovation Center will allow educators to give students work that they would be able to produce outside of school. This will keep
      them active indoors and help with their want to increase their grades.
