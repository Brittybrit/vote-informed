4.   Project/Program Description
     The Responder's First Wellness Program will provide free alternative therapies for first responders and their significant others suffering
     from career-related PTSD in a four-day wellness retreat. Participants will receive four sessions of Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART),
     three sessions of Integrated Restoration (iRest), three sessions of yoga. All expenses will be paid by Responder's First.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      The funds will be spent on a retreat and other related costs to provide free alternative therapies for first responders affected by
      career related post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) during a wellness program retreat based on a model initiated by the US Special
      Operations Command (SOCOM). Program Evaluations conducted by University of South Florida Tampa, FL Dr. Kevin E. Kip, Ph.D.,
      for Marcus Luttrell’s Lone Survivor Foundation and the Veterans Alternative has shown this model to be extremely effective with
      Veterans with PTSD.


 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Four-day retreat, passenger van for transportation.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      The participants will receive four days of alternative therapies to aid in their coping with PTSD.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      Persons with poor mental health, first responders affected by PTSD.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      The program will use evidence-based modalities which have been thoroughly researched with combat veterans that were deployed in
      Vietnam, Operation Enduring Freedom (OEF), and Operation Iraqi Freedom (OIF) and Responders First will provide these modalities
      in a proven week long retreat-based program.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Failure to meet deliverables will result in a return of funds to administering agency.
