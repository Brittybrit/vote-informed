1. Project Title          Sweetwater Mobile Command Post (Revert & Reappropriate)

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        4/7/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    Purchase of an equipped mobile command post for use by the Sweetwater Police Department. Uses will include, but not
   be limited to active shooter response, major crime scenes, catastrophic events, natural disasters, preventive operations,
   collaboration with other agencies, any other incident that may require incident command protocol to be initiated, and
   community outreach. This Post substantially bolsters the PD's ability to operate and react to situations that require stand
   alone capacity.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Substantially increase the ability and added capacity to respond to incidents that can be achieved by the acquisition of
    stand-alone post including, but not limited to: active shooters, major crime scenes, catastrophic events, natural disasters,
    outreach to the community, preventive operations, collaborations with other agencies, and several other types of
    incidents that may require incident command protocol to be initiated.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This is a public safety initiative that will greatly improve the City of Sweetwater's Police Department's communications
    and coordinated response efforts in mass casualty, natural disasters, or any other real-world situations requiring the
    coordination of the department's resources or communication with other responding agencies.

   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The residents of the City of Sweetwater will benefit from having their own police department to respond to emergencies
    throughout the city. The Mobile Command Post will augment the Police Department's response to large scale
    emergencies in the city such as natural disasters, mass casualty incidents, hostage situations, and other situations
    requiring multi-jurisdictional response.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The City of Sweeetwater is a minority-enriched community that is 95% Hispanic, a target population of 51-100 will be
    expected to be served.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     Reduced crime, ability to operate during power outages and/or extreme conditions, augmented patrolling capacity and
    police presence, more collaborative efforts with other agencies.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     Cancellation of contract, fines and/or payment for liquidated damages. The Contractor may also be prohibited from
    entering into business with the City again in the future for a certain period of time.
