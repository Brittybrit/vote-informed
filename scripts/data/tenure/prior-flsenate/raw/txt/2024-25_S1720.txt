4. Project/Program Description
    Goodwill South Florida offers vocational education & training programs where participants are taught job skills and gain
   work experience. The appropriation will provide vocational education & training where participants are taught job skills and
   gain work experience in positions in the sewing trade. 90% of program participants will have a disability. The program will
   provide training opportunities for 450 individuals (new participants or retraining of current participants) who have the
   capabilities and interests to perform in a job but are in need of additional training (classroom and/or on-the-job training) and
   supportive counseling due to a specific condition that impairs their productivity or precludes them from achieving standard
   productivity levels.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Provide training opportunities for 450 individuals (new participants or retraining of current participants) who have the
    capabilities and interests to perform in a job but are in need of additional training (classroom and/or on-the-job training)
    and supportive counseling due to a specific condition that impairs their productivity or precludes them from achieving
    standard productivity levels.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The appropriation will allow us to invest in programs that help transition someone seeking our services from dependency
    to independence. Rather than using tax dollars to receive state services, our successful job placements can help convert
    tax users to become tax payers by now being able to find a job and contribute to the community rather than depending on
    it.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Providing financial support to allow students to complete the training program will allow them to become employable and
    therefore self-sufficient.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Elderly persons, people with poor mental or physical health, jobless persons, economically disadvantaged persons. 450
    people are expected to be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Providing individuals an income while receiving training for an employable skill by measuring the number of participants
    who complete the program. Employment of participants who complete the training program by Goodwill, government, or
    the private sector by measuring the number of participants who complete the program and gain employment in the public
    or private sector based on the training provided.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Cessation of funding if the services are not provided.
