1. Title of Project: Howard Avenue Stormwater Pump Station
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 01/30/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   A significant portion of Howard Avenue between 12th and 9th streets is prone to flooding. The City requires
   frequent road closures after routine rainfall events due to street flooding along the roadway. This project includes
   upgrading the stormwater collection system, upgrading the existing pump station for increased pumping capacity,
   expansion of the current stormwater pond, and stabilization of the discharge ditch system.
   BENEFITS:
   - Alleviate flooding in areas of Howard Avenue and restore this area to an acceptable level of service as defined by
   the City of Dade City Stormwater Master Plan
   - Reduce the frequency and duration of street closures on Howard Avenue due to roadway flooding
