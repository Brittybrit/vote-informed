1. Title of Project: Pinecrest - Pinecrest Gardens ADA Accessibility Upgrades
2. Senate Sponsor: Jose Rodriguez
3. Date of Submission: 02/19/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   Pinecrest Gardens (PG) is a nationally designated historic botanical and cultural arts park in the Village of Pinecrest.
   Funds will be used for the replacement of the 26,504 square feet of trails throughout the park in order to make
   them ADA accessible and address the urgently needed safety issues. This project includes demolition of the existing
   trails, leveling the ground while protecting existing tree root structures to achieve a code compliant structural
   incline, and replacing the current path materials with a more durable material that provides safe mobility
   throughout the park and that, with proper maintenance, will last well into the next century.

11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        Replacing the trails with a long-lasting, durable material will improve safety and make the trails throughout the
        park ADA accessible.
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        Replacement of the 26,504 square feet of trails throughout the park in order to make them safe and ADA
        accessible.
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        The improvements to the trails will allow Pinecrest Gardens to continue to provide opportunities for people of
        all abilities and expand on them.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        Pinecrest Gardens hosts approximately 200,000 visitors each year. It regularly provides inclusive recreational
        and educational opportunities to children with diverse abilities.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
      will be measured?
        Pinecrest Gardens has historical designation by Miami-Dade County and is listed on the National Registry of
        Historic Places. One of the main reasons why is because it contains approximately 4.3 acres of native forested
        wetland including 3.3 acres of tropical hardwood hammock, 1 acre of native cypress slough hammock (the
