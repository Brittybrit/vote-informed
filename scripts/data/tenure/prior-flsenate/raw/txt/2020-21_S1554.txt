11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      The purpose of achieving the funds requested is to manage better the sargassum that is
      impacting the Village.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Increasing the number of sargassum hauls and integrations that the Village performs, as well
      as allowing the Village to explore new technologies and practices.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      No direct services will be provided to the citizens.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      The target population is any citizen that makes use of the beach within the Village of Key
      Biscayne.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      The Village expects to enhance/improve environmental, fish, and wildlife quality and protect
      the general public from environmental harm, by reducing the number of bacteria generated by
      sargassum on our beaches. This will be monitored through area sampling and monitoring.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      The Village suggest pursuing liquidated damages, collecting performance bond, and keeping
      a record of poor performance.
