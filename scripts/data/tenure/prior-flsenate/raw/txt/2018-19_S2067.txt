1. Title of Project: City of Plant City - Development of McIntosh Regional Park
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 01/05/2018
4. Project/Program Description:
    The preservation of this land compliments the natural resource and greenway corridor restoration goals of the
    area's Hillsborough River Greenways Task Force, of which the City of Plant City is a member. The Task Force's focus
    is the restoration, enhancement, mitigation and protection of all the water and natural resources that affect,
    directly or indirectly, the upper Hillsborough River Basin.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           Developing a trail head parking area for users; building an informational kiosk for maps and park information;
          developing a 2+ mile improved walking/biking trail through the NW portion of the property; building a wildlife
          observation platform ; providing trail signage; development of other trails as funding permits throughout the
          property.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Developing a trail head parking area for users; building an informational kiosk for maps and park information;
          developing a 2+ mile improved walking/hiking/biking trail through the NW portion of the property, building a
          wildlife observation platform; providing trail signage; development of other trails as funding permits
          throughout the property.
