13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will be used to expand the Chamber’s business support, workforce development, and community
    engagement initiatives, with the goal of strengthening local small businesses, increasing employment opportunities,
    and promoting sustained economic growth in the Town of Miami Lakes. These funds will allow the Chamber to deliver
    enhanced training programs, host outreach events, and provide resources that help businesses start, grow, and thrive.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?



                                                       Page 2 of 5

     The funds will support strengthening local businesses and expanding economic opportunities, including business
    training workshops, workforce development programs, one-on-one technical assistance, multilingual small-business
    support, community outreach events, expanded business expos, and partnerships with local organizations to connect
    businesses with resources, financing, and growth opportunities.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The funding will provide citizens with free or low-cost business training, workforce development programs, one-on-one
    consulting, multilingual assistance, resource fairs, and outreach events that connect entrepreneurs and residents with
    tools, education, and opportunities to support economic growth.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The program will serve at least 400 residents and small businesses annually, provide 10+ training workshops, deliver
    assistance to over 150 businesses, and increase participation in Chamber outreach events by at least 25%, directly
    boosting local economic activity.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The Chamber will measure outcomes through attendance records, workshop evaluations, business assistance logs,
    participant surveys, event registration data, services delivered, and growth in engagement compared to prior years.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     In addition to the agency’s standard penalties, the Chamber is willing to accept supplemental measures such as
    submitting a corrective action plan for any missed deliverables and providing increased reporting or progress reviews
    to ensure timely completion.
