4. Project/Program Description:
   For decades, the Voices For Children Foundation, Inc. (Voices) in Miami-Dade (VFC) has shared an invaluable public-
   private partnership with the Guardian ad Litem (GAL) Program. Through this partnership, VFC has been able to
   provide much needed support to the local GAL program. A major part of this support has come through the
   positions that are funded by private dollars and a State grants in aid contract. The grants in aid contract was made
   possible through a recurring appropriation of $892,656 in the Guardian ad Litem General Revenue budget that has
   been passed from GAL to Voices as part of a designated State project. Unfortunately, while the cost associated with
   providing the positions has increased over the last 10 years, the amount appropriated for the State project contract
   has remained unchanged . The Guardian ad Litem Program and Voices for Children Foundation, Inc are requesting a
   $150,000 increase in the recurring appropriation that is used to fund this State project.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        These funds will be used to increase the on-going State project contract between the Guardian ad Litem
        Program and the Voices for Children Foundation, Inc (VFC). This contract allows VFC to provide additional
        positions in the 11th Judicial Circuit. In addition to the contract dollars, VFC contributes in excess of $300,000 in
        private funds each year in the 11th Judicial Circuit to supplement this State project contract.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        The additional funds in this request will be used by the Voices for Children Foundation, Inc to continue
        providing positions to the Guardian ad Litem program in the 11th Judicial Circuit. These positions augment the
        child representation activities that are provided to the 11th Judicial Circuit and funded by the Guardian ad
        Litem Programs recurring General Revenue Salary and OPS appropriations.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        The funds will be used by Voices for Children to continue providing Child Advocate Manager and Attorney
        support to the Guardian ad Litem Program in Miami-Dade County.
