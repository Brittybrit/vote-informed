4. Project/Program Description:
   We are seeking funding for an expansion of Vincent House into Citrus County. Vincent House is an evidence based
   program that features "Recovery through Work" for the serious and persistently mentally ill. Vincent House
   Pinellas opened in 2003, Vincent House Hernando opened in 2017 and Vincent House Pasco should be open when
   construction is complete in the fall of 2019. Vincent House Citrus, if funded, anticipated opening would be late
   2020. These facilities are one proven way to address the problems regarding lack of mental health services in
   Florida and particularly in the smaller counties that have very few services of this nature. Vincent House Pinellas has
   a proven track record since 2003 and the Recovery through Work model there has resulted in the members earning
   more than $6 million in real wages at real jobs in the community.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        Funding from the Florida Senate will serve two main purposes. First, it will allow us to build a state of the art
        building that is adequate in size and modern in its amenities including a commercial kitchen. Second, it will
        allow us to carry out our successful "recovery through work" mission of step wise rehabilitation for persons
        with severe and persistent mental illness.
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        The services we will provide in Vincent House are meaningful, daytime work orientated activities. This work is
        the first step in the rehabilitation process. These activities will vary from reception, graphic arts, data
        processing, banking, computer and social media, cooking, dining, and cafe work and more. Vincent House
        Citrus will be a restorative community, based on respect and dignity, in which staff will engage members in the
        work of the Clubhouse.
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Vincent House Citrus will provide a wide array of services to our participants, called Members, that will include,
        but are not limited to the following: Employment: Vincent House will offer its members, the opportunity to
        return to paid employment through our stepwise "recovery through work" system of rehabilitation. Most
        Members begin by participating in the "work ordered day" as described above. When ready, they move to

    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         The target population of Vincent House Citrus will be residents of Citrus County who are 18 years of age or
         older, with a diagnosed Axis 1 mental illness, such as schizophrenia or bipolar disorder. We expect to serve up
         to 60 unduplicated individuals, with an average daily attendance of 15 in the first year, with a 30% increase in
         the following year.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         Expected benefits of Vincent House Citrus are twofold. Since serious menial illnesses are chronic, cyclic and
         extremly debilitating, our first goal is to provide an environment that demonstrates dignity and respect and
         focuses on each member's talents and skills. For many members, this builds the confidence to attempt to
         return to paid employment. At this stage, Vincent House provides a place to go where members feel wanted
         and needed. This environment helps to keep members healthy, gives them a purpose in life and decreases
         hospitalization, incarceration and hopelessness, all very expensive alternatives. For the members who choose
         to return to work, which is a large percentage of members, Vincent House assists members with employment
         skills, resume writing, interview skills, and other general work skills. When ready, Vincent House helps
         members return to paid employment in the community.
