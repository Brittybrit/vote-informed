11. Program Performance:

   a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
        These funds will provide for the engineering design of an overpass for pedestrian crossing above US1, with the
        intended purpose of creating a safer more direct crossing between the Metro Rail transfer station and the
        economic/living area of the city.
   b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
        Safety and welfare of all residents by providing a safe crossing at US-1 between SW 57th Avenue and SW 70th
        Street.
   c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
        Provide Safe pedestrian crossings across US-1 by constructing an elevated lighted crosswalk that is suitable for
        pedestrian, bicycle, and wheelchair access above the grade of existing traffic, thereby reducing traffic flow
        interruption due to extended signal changes to allow time for pedestrian crossing of a wide roadway.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
        Over 1,000 people daily will be served by this safe passage across a federal highway.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
      will be measured?
        Provide safe pedestrian crossings across US-1 by constructing an elevated lighted cross walk that is suitable for
        pedestrian, bicycle, and wheelchair access above the grade of existing traffic, thereby reducing traffic flow
        interruption due to extended signal changes to allow time for pedestrian crossing of a wide roadway.
   f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
        penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
        Forfeiture of funds.
