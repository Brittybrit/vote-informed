13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal of the NW 116th Way Drainage Improvement Project is to construct a new drainage system, as identified
    under the Storm-Water Master Plan (SWMP), which will include new drainage structures, valley gutters, and exfiltration
    trenches and other improvements to improve water quality, mitigate flooding hazards, reduce nutrient loading in
    groundwater, and may ultimately help boost the local economy.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The project will result in construction of a new drainage system on NW 116th Way. The proposed improvement will
    include but is not limited to a) improved roadway conditions and safety: b) mitigated flooding hazards; c) other
    improvements.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The proposed project will serve as a direct investment in the local community and surrounding businesses. An efficient
    and well-maintained roadway drainage system reduces accidents, enhances emergency responses, and boost
    economic growth. Also, an improved drainage system will prevent flooding and will give excess water a place to safely
    transport runoff to an area better suited to hold it. Additionally, the system will enhance the current infrastructure,
    drainage system performance, reduce flood hazards, thus reduces economic loses.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The proposed improvement will enhance the aging infrastructure, water quality, mitigate or eliminate flood hazards,
    and reduce nutrient loading in surrounding groundwater, and improve local traffic access for Medley residents,
    commuters and local businesses. The project is expected to serve 800 individuals and over 100 local area businesses.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The proposed project will enhance the drainage system, which may include but not limited to a) new drains; b)
    drainage pipes: c) trenches; d) gutters and e) other improvements. The new drainage system will be constructed, which
    may help improve water flow, improve storm water management, overall water quality, reduce flooding hazards, and
    reduce property damages and other cost to local business, and local economy.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     The Town of Medley shall comply with all provisions of the fully executed state contract, including standard language
    describing strict compliance with certain provisions regarding performance expectations, timely deliverables, and
    desired outcomes. The Town shall comply with standard penalties, such as holding payment or possible reduction in
    funding for failing to meet any deliverables, established performance measures, or non-performance.
