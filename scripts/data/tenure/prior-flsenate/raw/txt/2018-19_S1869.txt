1. Title of Project: Project Sea HORSE (Healing Opportunities and Resources in Secondary)
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 12/12/2017
4. Project/Program Description:
    Project SeaHORSE (Healing Opportunities and Resources in Secondary Education) is a companion mental health
    program to Project StarFISH (Facillitating Individual Success and Hope) that expands intensive mental health
    services to students identified with serious mental health concerns into two high schools demonstrating the
    greatest need. It will be a continued collaboration with BayCare, Inc. to provided the clinical counseling
    services. The two schools will be selected based on a review of suicide assessments, homicide assessments,
    baker-acts, school attendance and office discipline referrals. School staff would recommend these students to
    take an elective class for credit where they would recieive individual counseling, group counseling, life
    coaching and develop a life management person-centered plan. Students could take the course multiple times
    as part of Peer Counseling if a need existed. Qualified professionals (2) would teach the class.

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?

           Students in high school with serious mental health issues will develop coping skills and a life management plan
          that help them to graduate from high school with their cohort and assist the schools selected to maintain a
          safer and more secure school climate.

    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

           Clinical and non-clinical activities will include individual counseling, group counseling, life coaching, person-
          centered planning, academic and behavioral supports, art, music, dance and recreational therapy, conflict
          resolution, positive behavioral supports, mentorship, vocational planning and peer counseling. Family services
          will also be provided with parent education and mentoring as well as in-home clinical services when deemed
          necessary.
