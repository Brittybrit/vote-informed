1. Title of Project: Dade City Dade Oaks Stormwater Pond
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 12/11/2017
4. Project/Program Description:
    The City purchased the Dade Oaks property in 2016 and would like to restore the development to what it once was:
    a pond. BENEFITS: (1) Alleviate flooding in areas of NE Dade City and restore this area to an acceptable level of
    service as defined by the City of Dade City Stormwater Master Plan: Dade Oaks lies within a closed basin of Dade
    City that its only means of discharge is a stormwater pump. During large storm events, the pumping system is
    inadequate to keep up with the generated stormwater and the area begins to flood. (2) Provide a public amenity
    using the pond as a water feature: Add an observation pier and sidewalk along the pond. (3) Provide an
    improvement to water quality in the area resulting in a cleaner discharge to the Withlacoochee River Basin: A water
    quality component will be added to the pond design to treat the stormwater generated in the area before it outfalls
    into the Withlacoochee River Basin. (4) Eliminate the need for stormwater pumps which are costly.
