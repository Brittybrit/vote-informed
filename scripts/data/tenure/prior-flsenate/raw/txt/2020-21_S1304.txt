11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      INTERSECTIONS will support, educate, and empower lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, queer (LGBTQ) youth and community by
      mobilizing resources throughout Miami-Dade County that promotes dialogue, wellness, and increased self-esteem. The mobile center
      will provide programs and services for people living with or affected by HIV, youth, and youth at-risk of/experiencing homelessness in
      a generally unaffirming areas to improve quality of life through access to community, support, and care.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      INTERSECTIONS will provide access to peer-led support groups, recovery groups, wrap around services for homeless youth, cyber
      center, access to housing, resources and referrals to competent providers. Social activities will promote the development of
      community and safety within neighborhoods.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      Pridelines will provide HIV/STI testing. Case management, job readiness support, access to holistic therapies, linkage to housing,
      tele-medicine, and tele-PrEP.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      Elderly persons, persons with poor mental health, persons with poor physical health, jobless persons, economically disadvantaged
      persons, at-risk youth, homeless, drug users (in health services), high-school students, university/college students, currently or
      formally incarcerated persons, victims of crime, LGBTQ youth and their straight allies. Serves 200-400 persons.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      Improve physical health by connecting clients to medical providers by having a number of clients connected to Federally Qualified
      Health Centers within 24 to 48 hours. Improve mental health by increasing self-esteem by administering the Rosenberg-Self-Esteem
      Assessment; a 10 item scale that measures global self-worth by measuring the both positive and negative feelings about the self.
      Protect the general public from harm by increasing participation from community based organizations and businesses in the
      neighborhoods frequented by INTERSECTION. Reduce substance abuse by increasing participation in recovery groups. HIV testing,
      awareness and outreach by increasing access to HIV testing and provide tests and treatment within 48 hours.


 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      The standard state agency contract requirements and penalties are adequate.
