4.   Project/Program Description
     To foster the redevelopment of downtown Crystal River by providing public amenities including a public gathering area for various events
     and activities. An existing 2.7 acre parcel on the SE corner of U.S. 19 and Citrus Ave will be transformed into a much needed “Town
     Square”. Phase I, under progress, includes parking, utilities, sidewalks, and a restroom at a cost of $650,000. This request for funding for
     Phase II will include installation of decorative fountains, signage, shade pavilions, and parking.

11. Program Performance
a.    What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?
      To foster the redevelopment of downtown Crystal River by providing public amenities including a public gathering area for various
      events and activities. An existing 2.7 acre parcel on the SE corner of U.S. 19 and Citrus Ave will be transformed into a much needed
      “Town Square”. Phase II will include installation of decorative fountains, signage, shade pavilions, and parking.




 b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
      Completion of the Town Square will provide infrastructure that will be open to the general public for special events, festivals,
      picnicking, and general leisure activities all within footsteps of numerous downtown businesses.




  c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
      The Town Square will be open to the public for general recreational purposes. It will serve as a hub for local tourism related
      activities, connecting multiple businesses within the downtown area.




d.    Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
      General (The majority of funds will benefit no specific group)
      Other - Approximately 340,000 tourists and 100,000 event goers annually.




 e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
      Enrich cultural experience - The number of visitors and events.
      Increase or improve economic activity - Increase in taxable value and sales tax receipts.
      Increase tourism - Increase in bed tax receipts.




 f.   What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard
      penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?
      Reversion of funds.
