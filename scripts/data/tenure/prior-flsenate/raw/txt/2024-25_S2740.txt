1. Project Title          Village of Virginia Gardens Roadway Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        11/10/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The primary purpose of this project is to improve the safety for all road users, reduce pedestrian/bicyclist conflicts, provide
   accessible marked ADA walkways and improve the quality of life for the residents. The speed limit in the Village is 30 MPH.
   There are several side streets that empty into the north side of the Village cutting through to get on 36 Street. We would like
   to install radar speed signs with a message display.
