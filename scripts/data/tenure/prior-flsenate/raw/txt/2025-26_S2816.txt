4. Project/Program Description
    The Parks & Police 4 Kids, Inc. partners with the City of Doral Police Department, Parks Department, and local schools to
   provide structured after-school programs for at-risk students. These programs offer academic support, sports, and activities
   that boost self-esteem, foster engagement, and build positive relationships with law enforcement. Addressing the risks of
   unsupervised after-school hours, PP4K reduces dropout rates and tracks success through improved grades, graduation
   rates, and reduced arrests. The Doral Police Explorer Program adds mentorship and career pathways, while former
   participants give back through community service, reinforcing the program’s lasting impact on students and the community.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The requested funds will support PP4K’s afterschool program, providing at-risk middle school students with academic
    assistance, structured activities, and mentorship. By fostering positive self-esteem, engagement, and community
    involvement, the program helps reduce dropout rates and strengthens student success, ensuring a safe, supportive
    environment that promotes academic and personal growth.
   b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     PP4K’s afterschool program provides academic support, supervised activities, and sports to at-risk middle school
    students. Teachers and police officers facilitate structured two-hour sessions, alternating between tutoring and outdoor
    activities. Students engage in mentorship, community service, and leadership programs like the Explorer Program,
    fostering positive relationships, accountability, and future career pathways.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The appropriation project will provide at-risk middle school students with direct services, including academic tutoring,
    mentorship from teachers and police officers, supervised sports and recreational activities, and community service
    opportunities. The program ensures a safe, structured afterschool environment that fosters academic success, positive
    social development, and career exploration through initiatives like the Explorer Program.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population for this project is at-risk middle school students in the City of Doral who need academic support,
    mentorship, and structured afterschool activities. The program focuses on students vulnerable to academic
    disengagement and social challenges. Approximately 100 students per year are expected to benefit from these services,
    promoting their academic success, personal development, and community engagement.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     The expected benefits of this project include improved academic performance, increased student engagement, reduced
    dropout rates, and stronger community ties. Outcomes will be measured through student grades, graduation rates,
    participation levels, arrest records, and employment tracking. Success will also be assessed by student involvement in
    mentorship programs, community service, and the Doral Police Department’s Explorer Program.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

    Return of funds to agency.
