33 Project/Program Description:
Miami Beach Community Health Center (MBCHC) — Increasing Access to Primary Care. Funding is being
requested for MBCHSC to increase access to affordable, high-quality primary health care for 2,919 low-income,
medically underserved individuals in northeastern Miami-Dade County. MBCHC’s mission is to provide its
patients with a patient-centered medical home with high quality, affordable primary health care and specialty
services, chronic disease management, and support services. A Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC),
MBCHC is accredited for ambulatory care and laboratory services and recognized as a Primary Care Medical
Home by The Joint Commission. The National Committee for Quality Assurance (NCQA) has certified MBCHC at
the highest level as a Patient-Centered Medical Home. MBCHC is recognized by the Health Resources and
Services Administration (HRSA) as a National Quality Leader for exceeding national clinical quality benchmarks
for chronic disease management, preventive care, and perinatal/prenatal care. In 2016 MBCHC provided
services to 42,059 unduplicated patients of whom 20.76% were covered by Medicaid, 26.85% were uninsured,
and 93.2% had incomes 200% or less of the Federal Poverty Level.
MBCHC’s HRSA-defined service area (encompassing zip codes 33131, 33137, 33138, 33139, 33140, 33141,
33150, 33154, 33160, 33161, 33162, 33169, 33179, 33180, and 33181) has a low-income population of
208,925—an estimated 149,926 of which are not being served by a health center. Residents in the service area
are burdened by many health disparities such as high levels of diabetes and hypertension. In recent years,
MBCHC has experienced double-digit growth in unduplicated patients—39% from 2014 to 2016. MBCHC’s
MBCHC North location at 11645 Biscayne Boulevard, Miami, FL 33181 saw an increase in unduplicated patients
of 37% in 2016. The clinical space currently available at MBCHC North is inadequate to accommodate ina
timely manner all the patients seeking care. Patients cannot obtain timely appointments. Patients with
appointments are experiencing long wait times and sometimes leave without being seen. Walk-in patients also
sometimes leave before being seen because of the long wait times. In order to meet the demand for access to
care MBCHC is renovating and converting Suite 302/304 at MBCHC North into clinical space with 11
examination rooms to be staffed by 3.0 FTE physicians, 2.0 FTE LPN Medical Home Coordinators, and 6.0 FTE
Medical Assistants. MBCHC projects that with this new clinical site it will be able to serve 2,919 unduplicated
adult patients (1,181 of whom will be new) with 10,976 encounters in a 12-month period.
