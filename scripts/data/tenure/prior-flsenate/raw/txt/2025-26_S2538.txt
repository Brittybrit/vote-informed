13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will be used for the hardening and security of MACtown Main Campus for individuals with intellectual and
    developmental disabilities. Scope includes re-roofing of 56-bed residential facility and day training center. replacement of
    windows and doors with impact resistant products, fixing drainage issues on perimeter parking lot and install gas-powered
    emergency generator.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The replacement of the roof and windows.
