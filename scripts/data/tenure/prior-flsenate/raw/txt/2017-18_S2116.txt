11.   Program Performance (if needed, include additional documentation):
      a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
           The Academy at the Farm is seeking funding to build our agriculture program. Our 5 year goal is to build a
           state of the art agriculture facility for all students at Academy at the Farm to use. This facility would
           include a commercial kitchen, a student classroom, and 4 stalls to house animals.
      b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     We are passionate about providing a high quality agriculture program at our school because 70% of jobs
     are directly related to agriculture. We feel that this initiative will have a great impact on all of the
     students at our school no matter their academic ability. As a part of this facility we plan to teach students
     about animal science. We are committed to this so students who wish to enter careers in veterinary or
     animal science have a foundation that will allow them to enter into their chosen career path early and to
     make them more desirable to the secondary education institutions in which they apply. The students will
     be allowed the opportunity to raise animals for shows and exhibitions and keep them at the school. This
     will allow those students who do not have property for animals the opportunity learn about livestock. We
     have already built a hog shed so students are able to keep a hog at the school to raise. We have also
     established a relationship with a local dairy farmer. Students raise and learn about dairy cattle as part of
     our program. The dairy cattle industry is one that is in decline and we hope this program will inspire our
     youth to be the next generation of dairy farmers. Part of the plan includes a 1/2 acre school vegetable
     garden. This garden has already been started and emphasizes the importance of pollinators and has a
     “feed a bee” garden located within it. This garden will teach our students to be self sustaining and how to
     grow food. It will also teach them the importance or protecting our pollinating insects. Some types of
     bees have recently been added to the endangered species list which is something students need to be
     well informed about. Without pollinators the cost to grow and buy our nations food will greatly increase!
     The kitchen located within the agriculture facility will be used to teach students how to prepare and
     preserve the food that they grow. We also teach the students the benefits and dangers associated with
     biotechnology so that we are producing well informed youth for our community.
