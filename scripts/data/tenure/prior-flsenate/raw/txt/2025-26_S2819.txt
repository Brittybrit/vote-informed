1. Project Title          City of Sweetwater Police Technology Upgrades

2. Senate Sponsor         Bryan Avila

3. Date of Request        2/21/2025

4. Project/Program Description
    Due to the City of Sweetwater's recent growth, the city and its police department need to update an array of its police
   equipment to accommodate and mitigate the effects of an influx of additional residents, drivers, visitors which inevitably
   results in more crime. Therefore, the City is requesting funds to upgrade its police technology and acquire a closed-circuit
   camera and license plate reader system. This new technology will assist in enhancing crime prevention measures in the
   city.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The requested funds will enable the city to enhance its policing capabilities by updating key equipment, including a new
    closed-caption camera system and license plate reader system. These upgrades will improve crime prevention,
    investigation, and analysis by providing real-time integration with the Department's existing systems. Additionally, they will
    support intelligence sharing with neighboring agencies and enhance situational awareness.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This is a public safety initiative that will greatly improve the City of Sweetwater's Police Department's communications
    and coordinated response efforts of the department's resources and further strengthen the city’s law enforcement
    response to its growing population and increasing public safety needs.
   c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Improved delivery of public safety via faster response times, and crime deterrence as a result of smart/predictive
    technological aids.
   d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The City of Sweetwater is a minority-enriched community that is 95% Hispanic, with approximately 25,000 residents in
    total.
   e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
   be measured?

     Reduced crime augmented patrolling capacity and police presence, more collaborative efforts with other agencies.
   f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
   for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     Revocation of funding and return of funds to the state.
