4. Project/Program Description
    The Li’l Abner Foundation empowers underserved youth from 1st through 12th grade in Miami-Dade County through
   academic and physical education programs designed to break the cycle of poverty. Serving over 200 students annually, the
   foundation operates centers in Sweetwater and Palmetto Bay, areas with significant socio-economic challenges, including
   high poverty rates, low employment, and limited educational attainment. Programs include tutorials, SAT preparation,
   martial arts, dance, and archery, fostering academic success, discipline, and perseverance. Staffed by certified Florida
   teachers and volunteers from institutions like Florida International University, the foundation ensures impactful mentorship
   and guidance. Additionally, it hosts initiatives like Law Enforcement & First Responder Recognition Week and field trips for
   terminally ill children, enriching lives beyond the classroom.

13. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds requested will be used to provide comprehensive after-school programs that empower underserved youth
    through education, fitness, and discipline. Specifically, the funds will support the hiring of skilled instructors for programs
    such as dance, literacy, tutoring, archery, and Taekwondo; cover essential expenses like program equipment,
    educational materials, laptops, and competition fees; and address operational needs, including rent, utilities, background
    screenings, and administrative costs. These resources will enable the foundation to improve academic performance,
    foster personal development, and break the cycle of poverty for at-risk children in socio-economically challenged
    communities.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The foundation offers a comprehensive after-school care program designed to provide underserved minority children
    from kindergarten through 12th grade with enriching educational, cultural, and physical activities. Additionally, the
    foundation also sponsors an annual field trip, creating meaningful experiences for students with terminal health
    conditions.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The appropriation project will provide a variety of direct services to benefit citizens, focusing on the enrichment and
    development of children from kindergarten through 12th grade. These services include daily after-school tutoring to
    support academic success, high school SAT preparation to enhance college readiness, and a range of extracurricular
    activities to promote physical and cultural development. Taekwondo classes will be offered three days a week, archery
    two days a week, and dance classes three days a week for all age groups. Additionally, self-defense classes will be
    available three days a week and wrestling and conditioning classes 5 days a week ensuring a well-rounded program that
    meets the educational, physical, and creative needs of the community.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The target population served by this project is economically disadvantaged youth from kindergarten through 12th grade,
    with an expected reach of over 200 individuals annually.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit of this project is improved reading capabilities as well as enhanced physical and mental health
    among participating children. These outcomes will be measured by tracking the children’s progress in all activities and
    evaluating their performance in various local and state competitions.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for in the contract?

     Pro rata return of funds.
