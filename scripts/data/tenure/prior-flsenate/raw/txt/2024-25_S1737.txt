    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Miami Beach's investment in this initiative ensures an enduring and secure walkway, appealing to residents and the
    City's over 8.8 million visitors annually. The comprehensive Lincoln Road intersection, sidewalk and traffic flow
    improvements will support economic development, traffic improvements, and pedestrian safety in this central tourist area.
    Beach walk improvements include pedestrian safety measures such as sidewalks and safety bollards.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     For the Lincoln Road pedestrian safety and traffic calming improvements, the state funds will be used, along with City
    and private matching funds, for design and construction. For the bollard pedestrian walkway project, funds will be used
    for the analysis, design and bollard installation. The installation of bollards will enhance the visitor and resident
    experience along the pedestrian walkway.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Both area's high popularity necessitates pedestrian and cyclist safety. The Lincoln Road beach access sees
    approximately 8,000 pedestrians daily and is need of safety and functionality improvements. The bollards will deter
    vehicle access while maintaining the natural shoreline beauty. This investment will secure the walkway fostering tourism
    and local enjoyment alike.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     City of Miami Beach residents (80,000) and visitors and tourists to the area (20+ million).
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The pedestrian safety upgrades including sidewalks and bollards improves physical health by creating a safer
    environment that reduces the risk of accidents and injuries caused by vehicle intrusions. This enhanced safety
    encourages more physical activity, such as walking and jogging along the walkway, promoting a healthier and active
    lifestyle for both city residents and visitors. The city will monitor the success of this based on an increase in the overall
    number of visitors.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Terminate the contract.
