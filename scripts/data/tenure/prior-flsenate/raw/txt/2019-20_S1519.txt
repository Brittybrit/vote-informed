1. Title of Project: Hernando County Airport Water Reclamation Facility Expansion
2. Senate Sponsor: Wilton Simpson
3. Date of Submission: 02/07/2019
4. Project/Program Description:
   Weeki Wachee Springs is an Outstanding Florida Spring and also one of the main attractions for tourism in
   Hernando County. It is impaired with nitrates and is experiencing accelerated algae growth, which is causing
   adverse impacts to the delicate aquatic ecosystem. One of the largest point sources of Nitrogen to Weeki Wachee is
   the old Spring Hill Water Reclamation Facility (WRF). This wastewater treatment facility has out-of-date technology
   that does not nitrify or denitrify wastewater and the effluent is releasing high concentrations of nitrogen into the
   spring shed. Funding this project will expand the Airport Water Reclamation Facility from a permitted capacity of 3.5
   MGD to 6 MGD and allow the decommissioning of the Spring Hill (Osowaw) WRF. The Airport WRF expansion will
   meet advanced wastewater treatment standards for nitrogen (3mg/l). This project will reduce an estimated 48,000
   lb/yr of total nitrogen with a favorable estimated life cycle cost/benefit ratio of ($18/lb).

11. Program Performance:

    a. What is the specific purpose or goal that will be achieved by the funds requested?
         This project is to expand an existing water reclamation facility (WRF) achieving high level nitrogen removal.
         Expansion of the Airport WRF will allow the decommissioning of the Spring Hill WRF which is currently
         providing reclaimed water within the Weeki Wachee Priority Focus Area. This will reduce an estimated 48,000
         lb/yr of TN loading which this project alone is close to meeting the 5 year goal of the Weeki Wachee Basin
         Management Action Plan (58,560 lb/yr).
    b. What are the activities and services that will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?
         Construction of the Airport WRF expansion (3.5 million gallons per day (MGD) to 6.0 MGD) while meeting the
         advanced wastewater treatment standards for total nitrogen removal (3 mg/l).
    c. What are the direct services to be provided to citizens by the appropriations project?
         Advanced wastewater treatment to reduce nitrogen loading to Weeki Wachee Springs.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
         General Hernando County population (approximately 185,000) and tourists.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome
       will be measured?
         Expanding the Airport WRF from 3.5 to 6.0 MGD. An estimated reduction of 48,000 lb-N/yr of total nitrogen
         (TN) from the Weeki Wachee Priority Focus Area (PFA). This project alone is close to meeting the 5 year goal of
         the Weeki Wachee Basin Management Action Plan (BMAP) which is (58,560 lb-N/yr).
