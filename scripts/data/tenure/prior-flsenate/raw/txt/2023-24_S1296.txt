14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will be used by the City's Police Department to purchase a Mobile Command Vehicle.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This is a public safety initiative that will greatly improve the City of Hialeah Police Department's communications and
    coordinated response efforts in mass casualty, natural disasters, or any other real-world situations requiring the
    coordination of the department's resources or communication with other responding agencies.

    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The residents of the City of Hialeah benefit from having their own police department to respond to emergencies
    throughout the City. The Mobile Command Center will augment the Police Department's response to large scale
    emergencies in the City such as natural disasters, mass casualty incidents, hostage situations, and other situations
    requiring multi-jurisdictional response.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The project will target no particular group, and will serve the general population of the City of Hialeah by augmenting the
    Police Department's ability to protect and serve the residents of the City. The population of the City of Hialeah is roughly
    220,000.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The addition of a Mobile Command Vehicle to the City of Hialeah's Police Department will improve communications
    between Police Command Staff and officers responding to a mass casualty incident, natural disaster, or other situation
    requiring mass response coordination within the Department as well as with other pertinent agencies. The improved
    communication with other departments responding to a particular scenario will be one measure of improvement in the
    City's Police services. Furthermore, these benefits will also be measured by improved officer safety while they are in the
    line of duty or engaging a perilous real world scenario.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

    The City of Hialeah is willing to be bound by the standard penalties for breach of contract in the State of Florida.
    Furthermore, the City is willing to contemplate additional penalties the Agency may propose.
