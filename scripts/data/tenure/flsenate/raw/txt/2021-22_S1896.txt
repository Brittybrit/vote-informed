4. Project/Program Description
    The Village of Palmetto Bay proposes to improve the drainage of a residential area within sub basin 61. The capacity of
   the existing swales outfalls, and french drains is not sufficient to discharge the volume of runoff needed. The project
   proposes to improve the drainage system's capacity and discharge adequate runoff by reducing pollutant loading into
   Biscayne Bay Aquifer.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project will control flooding and other hazards associated with stormwater surges and will reduce pollutant loading
    into Biscayne Bay. Project as designed will reduce loading for total suspended solids, total nitrogen, total phosphorus,
    and other contaminants that may enter into a designated priority surface water for the Water Management District, by
    treating 0.5 inches of stormwater runoff.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This project as designed will control flooding and other hazards associated with stormwater surges while also mitigating
    the environmental hazards associated with stormwater runoff.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This project will control flooding and other hazards associated with stormwater surges.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The residents and businesses within the Village of Palmetto Bay will benefit from this project through a reduced risk of
    flooding and the associated environmental hazards contained in stormwater runoff. An additional benefit is a reduction in
    the FEMA Community Rating System (CRS) which will directly impact insurance premiums and should result in a
    reduction in those rates. Approximately 2/3 of the residents, or 18,000 people will be directly impacted.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
