4. Project/Program Description
    The Washington Center is the largest and most established college student internship program in Washington, D.C. Since
   1975, we've helped more than 60,000 students translate their majors into thriving career paths.

   Florida students from State University System schools are eligible for state scholarship support when they enroll in our
   Academic Internship Program (AIP). Students who enroll in The Washington Center’s Academic Internship Program are
   placed in internships that fit their skills, interests, and professional goals. Students also take an evening course once a
   week from approximately 20 courses offered each semester, attend our LEAD Colloquium every Friday, and build a
   portfolio throughout the program to document their professional journeys. LEAD (Leadership, Engagement, Achievement,
   and Development) instructors provide one-on-one instruction, advisement, and professional exploration to each student at
   The Washington Center.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will enable Florida public university students to spend a summer or semester in Washington, D.C. interning for
    a private company, non-profit organization, federal agency, or Member of Congress. Students also participate in a range
    of leadership and professional activities. Students will leave Washington, D.C. with a much better understanding of the
    professional world and how to successfully join the workforce post-college graduation. The program instills in students the
    values of leadership, civic engagement and professional achievement. Students return to Florida ready to be vibrant,
    contributing members of Florida’s economy and workforce. Additionally, the program will enrich students’ cultural
    experiences, improve the quality of their education, improve their economic activity, create job opportunities for students
    and enable students to have greater economic self-sufficiency.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Students will intern four days a week in Washington, DC, take an academic course, and participate on Fridays in various
    lectures, workshops, and events collectively called the "LEAD Colloquium" to expand their knowledge and cultural
    fluency.

    Students will also participate in numerous career services workshops to help prepare them for the job market during their
    time at The Washington Center.
