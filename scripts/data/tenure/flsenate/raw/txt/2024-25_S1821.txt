2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       12/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Miami-Dade County's CERT program does not meet its mission in under-served communities. Division of Emergency
   Management looks to expand on the Whole Community preparedness and response approach by increasing access
   through community engagement and preparedness efforts. The CERT program expansion will require the use of multiple
   fully equipped and deployable response trailers that will be utilized for training in all Miami-Dade County districts and
   dedicated to the worst affected areas post-disaster. In addition to the response and training equipment, Division of
   Emergency Management aims to increase participation through community training and education.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Miami-Dade County will increase its resiliency post disaster. Residents will be trained to care for and assist one another.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     First response and basic needs. First aid and light search and rescue capabilities. The focus will be on the under-served
    community.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     CERT personnel and equipment will be readily available to all of the county residents.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The 2.7 million residents of Miami-Dade County. The Initial focus will be train individuals in the under-served portions of
    Miami- Dade County.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Miami-Dade County residents will benefit from having fellow residents that are training in basic life support and search
    and rescue techniques. Emergency Management will measure the quantity of trained residents and ensure that the
    program is equitably distributed through out the county.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
