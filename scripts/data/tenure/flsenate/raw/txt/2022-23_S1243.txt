12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    Willing Seller acquisition of wetlands and abandoned agricultural fields necessary for the restoration of Biscayne Bay’s
    watershed and improvement of water quality within the C-103 Mowry and Military canal basins and the adjacent waters of
    Biscayne Bay.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Acquisition of these lands will allow for management of nutrient impairment through reduction of nutrient sources,
    maximizing ecosystem services and the creation of buffer areas that will allow retention of water prior to discharge
    allowing passive natural attenuation of nitrogen.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Acquisition of these lands will protect the Biscayne Bay watershed from further degradation resulting from nutrient
    loading. In addition, purchase of these lands will afford the protection of lands located in the Coastal High Hazard Area
    from the impacts associated with sea level rise.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The citizens of southern Miami-Dade County.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Acquisition of these lands will protect the Biscayne Bay watershed from further degradation resulting from nutrient
    loading. In addition, purchase of these lands will afford the protection of lands located in the Coastal High Hazard Area
    from the impacts associated with sea level rise.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?
