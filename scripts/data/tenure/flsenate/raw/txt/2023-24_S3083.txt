4. Project/Program Description
    The Academy offers a sequence of courses that provides coherent and rigorous content aligned with challenging academic
   standards and relevant technical knowledge and skills needed to prepare for further education and/or careers in
   construction. The Academy provides an opportunity to develop technical skill proficiency and includes competency-based
   applied learning that contributes to the academic knowledge, higher-order reasoning and problem-solving skills, work
   attitudes, general employability skills, technical skills and occupation-specific skills and knowledge of all aspects of
   repairing and maintaining buildings as well as working on plumbing, electrical masonry, painting, drywall and HVAC
   systems. The students participating in the Academy have the opportunity to earn two industry certification prior to
   graduating high school - HBI Pre-Apprenticeship Certificate Training (PACT), Core and HBI Pre-Apprenticeship Certificate
   Training (PACT), Core Green.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to empower Citrus County students with the knowledge and skills needed for high-skilled, high-demand
    construction and manufacturing careers. The students will learn residential construction focused skills, receive hand-on
    training, and master the knowledge needed to successfully earn industry certifications in construction. Students will be
    ready for direct employment or advanced training at a technical college or any of the schools in the Florida College
    System.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Citrus High School Construction Academy will provide courses with challenging standards, hands-on training,
    relevant technical knowledge and skills needed to earn industry certifications for direct employment in construction or
    advanced training at a postsecondary school to build the students' careers in construction.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The direct services will be four years of high school courses to prepare our students to be employable directly out of high
    school or to continue on to advanced training at a postsecondary facility. This will fill the skilled trade gap in our area,
    promote economic growth and establish a higher standard of living for residents of Citrus County.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The targeted population will be students from across the county who are interested in developing skills needed for
    construction careers. We expect about 160 students to have access to this opportunity annually.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     To show an increases in high school industry certifications earned, graduation rates, and essential jobs filled for
    community growth, as well as a decrease in the high school dropout rate increasing the number of students that enter into
    the workforce with high-skilled, high-wage jobs. These data points will be gathered and compared to previous year's data.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Graduated prorated contract reduction amount based on factors directly related to provider deliverables performance.
