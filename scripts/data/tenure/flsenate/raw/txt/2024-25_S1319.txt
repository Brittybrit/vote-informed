1. Project Title         Miami South Golden Pines Street Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor        Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request       11/13/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Partnering with Miami-Dade County Water and Sewer Department to upgrade drainage on SW 27th Street from SW 27th
   to SW 30th Avenue. Upgrades will include removal of existing water mains, installation of 2,688 linear feet of new 8-inch
   Ductile Iron Pipe, service connections, and fire hydrants along no-outlet roads.
   This project also includes roadway reconstruction, ADA compliance upgrade, valley gutters and landscaping (trees).
   Widening of street to reduce potential safety hazard of very narrow streets. The streets will also be widened to reduce
   potential vehicular traffic and pedestrian safety conflicts/hazards in the existing
   This project will increase flood protection and provide safe routes to schools and parks for area
   residents.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Protect home and property from the perils of flood, improve pedestrian and vehicular safety, improve the quality of life for
    the residents.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Design, construction, and ject administration.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     There will be no direct service provided to citizens.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is the neighborhood of Golden Pines in Miami with and estimated population of 15,160.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Reduced flooding on narrow streets reducing home, and property damage from seasonal flooding. Improved vehicular
    and pedestrian safety by lowering the saturation level of neighborhood streets during flooding and shortening the time
    roads are submerged.
    These benefits will be measured by comparing the number of vehicular and pedestrian accidents pre and post
    construction; comparing the number of flooding complaints before the construction is started and after the project is
    constructed by using sensors and on-site inspections.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables or performance measures should subject the City to an audit and depending on the results
    of the audit the City could be liable for sanctions up to revocation of funding.
