4. Project/Program Description
    This project will provide funding for planning and preliminary design work to develop a septic to sewer treatment and
   collection system for 1295 residents in the unincorporated area of Sumter County consisting of the Lake Panasoffkee water
   service area. This will include the required geotechnical and hydro geological work and related Florida Department of
   Environmental Protection (FDEP) facility plans.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project will provide funding for planning and preliminary design work to develop a septic to sewer treatment and
    collection system in the unincorporated area of Sumter County consisting of the Lake Panasoffkee water service area.
    The development of a septic to sewer treatment and collection system will stop existing leaching of waste from septic
    systems into Lake Panasoffkee. Further, FDEP has spent approximately $6 million dollars dredging sludge from Lake
    Panasoffkee caused by the septic tanks that will be replaced by this project. This project will prevent further expenditures
    on sludge dredging.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Wastewater treatment and collection services will replace septic systems to 1295 households in Sumter County.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Wastewater treatment and collection will be provided to 1295 households in Sumter County and waste will no longer
    leach into Lake Panasoffkee.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    Low income residential properties properties in the unincorporated area of Sumter County consisting of the Lake
    Panasoffkee water service area will be provided wastewater treatment and collection. 1295 households will be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The replacement of septic systems with a wastewater treatment and collection system will improve the health and
    welfare of 1295 households the unincorporated area of Sumter County consisting of the Lake Panasoffkee water service
    area. The replacement of the septic systems will stops leaching of waste from the septic systems into Lake Panasoffkee
    and the water table. The outcome will be measured by routine monitoring and testing of water quality in the area by FDEP
    and the county health department.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Standard contractual penalties.
