14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds are being requested to assist in the creation of a public private partnership to seed the creation of the South
    Florida Institute for Economic Research (SFIER.) SFIER will focus on providing timely and update to date information on
    the economy, the region’s most pressing challenges, and through the Beacon Councils leadership will coalesce the
    public, private educational and civic sectors to create informed solutions to these problems.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     SFIER will provide annual economic forecasts for the year, 2-4 papers on specific topics related to workforce, housing,
    poverty, and the economy and host events to discuss the finding and begin the discussions on solutions. In South Florida
    no organization exists to provide customized research for the public and nonprofit sector.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The direct services provided by SFIER are timely and accurate data, research and analysis on current trends impacting
    the economy of South Florida. With acute issues such has housing affordability, misalignment of skills and employer
    needs, the realignment of the global economy etc., local government and business community need this thought
    leadership to address these challenges.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The government, nonprofit, educational and private sector are working to create a more sustainability and equitable
    economy for the benefits of all South Florida residents. The growth of this area supports the entire state’s economy.
