4. Project/Program Description
    The funds requested would better position Ohel to continue to offer and expand upon these fundamental trainings to
   educators and youth support professionals to meet the mental health needs of youth by fostering resilience and wellbeing
   through implementation of Ohel's social-emotional learning curricula and resources. Funding would also allow for increased
   provision of trauma-based interventions and other support services to schools, children and community members after a
   traumatic event. The funds would also support current counseling services for families impacted by the war in Israel.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Children and teachers using Ohel's resilience resources will learn skills, tools, and language to help them cope with life's
    challenges. A school that uses a trauma informed approach with their students and faculty enables children to grow
    academically, socially, and emotionally creating a stronger and more resilient next generation.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Ohel will provide parent, teacher, and student education to promote resilience, wellbeing and trauma-sensitivity to better
    meet the mental-health needs of youth. Ohel will provide social-emotional, resilience and substance use prevention
    resources and programs for students from preschool through high school age. We will conduct trauma support for
    schools, families, and communities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     School faculty, students, parents, and community members will receive services such as trauma and grief interventions,
    social emotional education, substance abuse prevention, attend support groups and weekend bereavement retreats.
    Students and teachers will also receive our preschool and elementary age social emotional resources.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population for this project will be Preschool, Grade School and High School students, teachers and faculty, at-
    risk youth, people experience and acute trauma suffering from poor mental health. We expect to serve over 800
    individuals this coming year.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     -Enhance educators’ and students’ resilience and wellbeing with the understanding of resilience as a parallel process in
    building student wellbeing
    -Enhance individual's coping and increase their ability to connect to natural resources in the aftermath of traumatic events
    Ohel will administer surveys to track effectiveness of this programming
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Ohel is committed to meeting deliverables and performance standards set by DOE and will offer full transparency around
    how the funds are utilized to support schools and families in south Florida. Unless there are extraordinary circumstances,
    Ohel would expect DOE to terminate their contract and no longer offer state funding if deliverables are not being met in a
    timely
    manner.
