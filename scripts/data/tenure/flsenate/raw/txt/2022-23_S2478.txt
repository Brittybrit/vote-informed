12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The specific purposes are to:
    1) Expand the workforce training program to add culinary training in Miami-Dade and Broward Counties through the
    purchase of a mobile kitchen.
    2) Through the increased training program, increase the Food Home Delivery program serving homebound individuals,
    including seniors, by expanding Feeding South Florida’s warehouse.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Enroll, train, and place training graduates in higher wage jobs. Use the training program to provide home delivered food
    to homebound individuals in need of assistance.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     10-week culinary training program and home delivered food for homebound individuals in need of assistance.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is elderly persons, jobless persons, economically disadvantaged persons, and current or formerly
    incarcerated persons. 25-50 individuals are expected to be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
