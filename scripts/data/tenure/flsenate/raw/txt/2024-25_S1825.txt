14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Carrie P. Meek faithfully advocated for equity and opportunity for people in marginalized and underserved communities.
    In the spirit of her legacy, the program will provide workforce skills training (soft skills, hard skills, upskilling and reskilling),
    specifically in the construction trades, to low income, underemployed and/or unemployed residents of Florida City and
    South Dade and connect those residents to employment opportunities.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Skilled Trades Training will be provided utilizing the Home Builder Institute nationally renowned Pre-Apprenticeship
    Certificate Training (PACT) Core program, followed by specific trade training and certifications. It combines performance-
    based learning in the building trades with vocational and academic skills training, encompassing life skills, career
    development, and on-the-job training.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The direct services will train or upskill local adults and youth and previously incarcerated individuals via job preparedness
    training, resume prep and review, job placement and retention services and career mentoring, focusing specifically on the
    construction trades. Hands on training, combined with state-of-the-art virtual reality training will be delivered in Florida
    City and South Dade areas.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population to be served are jobless persons and economically disadvantaged persons. About 201-400
    individuals are expected to be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit is to enhance individual's economic self sufficiency by developing two areas of measurement: 1)
    number of programs and program enrollment and number of employment placements of program participants. The
    budget includes retention of Recruitment, Retention and Placement Consultant as a 3rd party evaluator. The
    methodology will be measured in two ways: How many programs/organizations are engaged and how many are enrolled
    AND the percentage of program participants who are able to secure employment. Secondarily we will track the ability of
    participants to retain employment.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables would result in financial consequences including withholding of funding or reduction in
    specified payments.
