    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     - Improve physical health: Reduces number of pedestrian and vehicular accidents cased by flooded roads. Improves
    water flow away from street. Methodology: Compare number of flooding complaints before and after the project.
    Compare level of flooding before and after the project using sensors.
    - Enhance/preserve/improve environmental or fish and wildlife quality: Reduces oils, grease and trash pollutants from

    into adjacent waterways.
    - Improve wastewater management: Reduces oils, grease and trash pollutants from street runoff to waterways and
    reduces flood events. Methodology: Compare number of cleaning sweeps by stormwater operations crews on the
    improved stormwater control structures.
