14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to offer a free high-quality after-school and summer program where children and youth, including students
    with disabilities, engage in enrichment activities that incorporate youth voice and choice. The Amigos LEAD after-school
    program will be available for 160 students in grades K-5 during the school year & 40 students in grades K-8 during the
    summer.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     170 students during the school year (K-5) and 40 students during summer (K-8) will be provided with homework
    assistance (school year only), differentiated literacy instruction (DLI) (school year only), field trips, STEM (Coding,
    Engineering, Cooking & Gardening), Sports (Flag Football, Soccer, Basketball), Art (Visual Art, Theater, Dance), and
    Service Learning Projects. Program is free to participants.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The Amigos LEAD After School Program is dedicated to providing a safe, supportive, and enriching environment for
    children and youth during their out-of-school hours. The program encompasses a wide range of activities designed to
    promote academic growth, social development, and creative expression. The program's diverse offerings are aimed at
    catering to individual interests and needs, ensuring that participants have the opportunity to explore and develop their
    talents and skills. The Amigos LEAD After School Program places a strong emphasis on creating a positive and inclusive
    atmosphere where students can thrive academically, physically, and artistically. By offering a variety of enriching activities
    and ensuring students have a voice in their learning, the program supports their holistic development, fostering well-
    rounded individuals who are prepared for future success.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    170 students during the school year (K-5) and 40 during summer (K-8) in low income neighborhoods of Little Havana,
    Allapattah, and surrounding areas.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Assessments:
    • The physical fitness level of students participating in sports enrichment is gauged using the Progressive Aerobic
    Cardiovascular Endurance Run (PACER) test.
    • Literacy levels are assessed using the Oral Reading Fluency (ORF) and the Maze assessments.
    • The qualitative assessment of enrichments is conducted through showcases, performances, and portfolios, allowing
    students to demonstrate their learning and creative growth.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Standard contract penalties.
