4. Project/Program Description
    On, July 14th 2023 the BBWMAB adopted a resolution advising the County Mayor County will develop and implement a
   comprehensive Watershed Plan (WP) encompassing a holistic approach to multifaceted water management, pollution
   mitigation, habitat restoration, land use, and community resilience challenges. The end deliverable of this project proposal
   will be a comprehensive Biscayne Bay Watershed Plan, a living document using successful watershed plan models as a
   guide, along with other relevant references. The plan will integrate and help implement local resilience strategies, such as
   adaptation action areas, as identified by by the Miami-Dade County Vulnerability Assessments and Adaptation Plan,
   partially funded by DEP.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Creating a comprehensive watershed plan is essential in protecting the ecological and socio-economic vitality of the
    region. The goal of the Watershed Plan is to strategically address priority goals including water quality and habitat
    restoration using sound science and adaptive management. Understanding the impact of activities in the bay’s watershed
    including new development, storm water runoff, aging infrastructure, septic systems, and land use is critical to achieving
    our goal.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     No activities and services will be provided with these funds.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     No direct services will be provided with these funds.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The entire population of Miami-Dade County some 2.8 million residents will benefit.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The Watershed Plan will provide recommended actions to ensure a vibrant, healthy and resilient bay which supports a
    resilient economy for the County and the State. Pollution reduction in the Biscayne Bay Water shed will improve the
    health and environment for fish and wildlife. Utilizing hydrological and ecological models to simulate scenarios and predict
    the outcomes of different management actions allowing the County to measure benefits of specific actions and prioritize
    those which provide the greatest benefit.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
