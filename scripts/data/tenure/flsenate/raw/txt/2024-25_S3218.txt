14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Removing septic tanks and connecting sewer will improve ground water by eliminating sewer leakage. Project will
    provide service to impoverished residents who could not other afford it. Repairing cracked mains will also eliminate
    leakage into water table.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Eliminating sewer leakage by septic rank removal and sewer main repairs, elimination of septic tanks, repair of sewer
    lines and installation of new sewer mains will eliminate current harm to water table.

    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Eliminating sewer leakage by septic rank removal and sewer main repairs to improve groundwater quality.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The over 15,000 residents of Florida City.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     To improve the quality of the drinking water for Florida City's residents, and we will measure this outcome of this project
    by metering the amount of sewer pumped.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Reimbursement would not be provided if our agency fails to meet deliverables.
