1. Project Title         Cutler Bay Pedestrian Bridge Project

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request       12/04/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Cutler Bay requests $3.5 Million to support the development of a Pedestrian Bridge. The project aims to improve the safety
   and accessibility of pedestrians and bicyclists using public transit. The Bridge will connect to the South Dade Transitway
   station on US-1. The project area includes a transit oriented development in the Town Center District.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    The proposed project's aims to construct a pedestrian bridge to improve the safety of bicyclists and pedestrians crossing
    US-1 to access the South Dade Transitway.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    The project has two components: (1) Gain public input on the design plans and potential pedestrian bridge, and (2)
    Construct a pedestrian bridge to improve pedestrian safety and promote the use of public transportation.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The project enhances pedestrian and bicyclists safety and accessibility to public transit.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The Town of Cutler Bay's population is 43,958 (Census 2022). The facility supports all residents including those living in
    unincorporated Miami-Dade County to the east of the Town's municipal boundary.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The project outcome is the development of the pedestrian bridge. The need for a pedestrian bridge is included as a
    recommendation in the Town's Transportation Master Plan designed to enhance pedestrian connectivity. Additionally, the
    Bridge enhances the Town Center District; a key element of the Town's economic development initiative.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The Town will not be reimbursed for project activities that do not meet the deliverables or performance measures.
