    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Lotus House Women's Shelter Education and Employment Program delivers life-changing employment and
    educational support, trainings, linkages, services, internship/employment and job placement opportunities to high special
    needs women and youth experiencing homelessness. Helping them to heal, learn, grow, thrive and experience a greater
    opportunity for themselves and their families.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     One on one education support, individual/group life skills training, computer access and instruction, employment
    supports/linkages, resume creation, job search workshop, access to professional attire, job readiness training, intensive
    vocational certification training, paid/unpaid employment internships, high school/GED completion support and other
    activities aimed at improving their lives.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     One on one education support, individual/group life skills training, computer access and instruction, employment
    supports/linkages, resume creation, job search workshop, access to professional attire, job readiness training, intensive
    vocational certification training, paid/unpaid employment internships, high school/GED completion support and other
    activities aimed at improving their lives.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All those served are high special needs women, youth, and children who are experiencing homelessness in Miami-Dade
    County and are sheltered at our facility. Most have unmet medical needs, unmet mental health needs and little to no prior
    education/employment before entering our program.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

    255 homeless women/female youth will participate in 75% (3 of 4) of the following benchmarks, annually: (1) One on
    One Education Support, (2) Life Skills Training, (3) One on One Employment Coaching and (4) Computer Access.

    255 homeless women/female youth will participate in 50% of the following benchmarks, annually: (1) Obtain employment
    or increase employment by exit, (2) Complete a professional resume (3) Job search workshop and (4) Access to work
    clothing.

    Homeless women/female youth will participate in at least 1 of 4 following benchmarks, annually: (1) Job readiness
    training, (2) Participate in on-site internship training. (3) Participate in on-site paid internship and (4) Begin or complete
    high school diploma/GED.

    Summary of service spreadsheet. Copies of individual action plans and progress notes, available on-site.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     No payment if deliverables are not met.
