3. Date of Request        12/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    PortMiami and its private sector partners in the Greater Miami metropolitan area are facing a shortage of talent in the local
   maritime and seaport environment. The shortages are limiting the Port's ability to compete globally for cruise and cargo
   business and address current supply chain disruptions. The lack of available talent is expected to become more severe as
   a result of predicted increases in cargo volume, worker retirements, and competition for skilled construction workers who
   are in also demand for residential and commercial building projects. The appropriation can help the Port mitigate these
   challenges by identifying expected future talent shortfalls and strategies for retraining and reskilling workers whose jobs
   have been impacted by technology disruptions.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     PortMiami and its private sector partners in the Greater Miami metropolitan area are facing a shortage of talent in the
    local maritime/seaport environment. The shortages are limiting the Port’s ability to compete globally for cruise and cargo
    business and address current supply chain disruptions. The lack of available talent is expected to become more severe
    as a result of predicted increases in cargo volume, worker retirements, and competition for skilled construction workers
    who are in also demand for residential and commercial building projects. The appropriation can help the Port mitigate
    these challenges by identifying expected future talent shortfalls and strategies for retraining and re-skilling workers whose
    jobs have been impacted by technology disruptions.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Development of a program for training crane operators and allow the Port to document new employees hired through this
    program.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Assist the Port in recruiting, training and retaining skilled workers.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Miami-Dade County youth from historically disadvantaged communities and areas of persistent poverty.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Through the creation of the apprenticeships and internships, the Port and its partners will be able to have additional
    talent on board quickly to address operational needs.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverable and performance measures will result in loss of funding.
