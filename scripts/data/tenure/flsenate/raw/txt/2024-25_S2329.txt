14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To complete the stormwater upgrades in the first phase of the Village's resilient infrastructure program. The first phase is
    centered on the Village's K-8 elementary school and surrounding neighborhoods. This project will reduce flooding and
    thereby improve resident safety, particularly children safety, ensure access to the school during flood events, protect
    property, and increase service reliability.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Conducting the initial phase of a construction project to upgrade the stormwater system, improve roadways and
    underground electrical and telecommunication utilities in the vicinity of the Key Biscayne K-8 Community School to
    reduce flooding, improve safety and school access, and ensure greater utility reliability.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     No direct services are being provided to citizens by this appropriation project.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The majority of funds will benefit no specific group.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Reducing flooding in roadways will result in less standing water filled with contaminants on roadways. During flood
    events, residents often interact with flood waters to leave their homes or travel within the Village, exposing them to these
    contaminants. This will be measured based on the total number of days the area is flooded over 6 inches per year.
    Flooding is an environmental threat to public safety and health. Reducing flooding will keep residents out of vulnerably
    situations, particularly children, the elderly, and those with different abilities. Reducing flooding on roadways during rain
    events ensures the transportation network in the Village is able to continue to function. The project will also involve
    raising roadways which will improve transportation conditions during rain events. Measuring this level of benefit or
    outcome will be based on the number of streets flooded during heavy rain events.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables without notification will result in financial penalties as described in the contract.
