1. Project Title         Citrus County Barge Canal Boat Ramp

2. Senate Sponsor        Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request       12/27/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The Barge Canal Boat Ramp Project is located along the west side of US19 and the north side of the Barge Canal in Citrus
   County. The project includes the construction of a 400 foot wide ramp, three gangway's with floating docks, 24 car parking
   spaces, 60 boat parking spaces and associated storm water facilities.
