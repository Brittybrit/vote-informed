4. Project/Program Description
    Amigos Strengthens Families & Communities is a community-based, culturally and linguistically relevant program for
   parents/caregivers and children designed to foster positive parent/child interactions that break the inter-generational cycle
   of child maltreatment and dysfunction. By targeting families that are at risk of abuse and neglect, the program serves as a
   primary prevention tool to increase healthy parenting knowledge and skills. The program uses the Nurturing Parenting
   Program Curriculum, an evidence-based curriculum that addresses the protective factors that help prevent child abuse and
   neglect which are a research-informed, cost-effective strategy to increase family stability, enhance child development and
   reduce child abuse and neglect. The 6 factors include: Nurturing and Attachment, Knowledge of Parenting and Youth
   Development, Parental Resilience, Social Connections, Concrete Supports for Parents, and Social and Emotional
   Competence of Children.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Amigos for Kids (AFK) seeks a direct grant to expand our parent education services. This AFK program was recognized
    by The Children's Trust as "Program of the Year". "Nurturing Parenting" is a proven model for the prevention of child
    abuse/neglect. The program curricula is the result of extensive research of high-risk parenting beliefs & behaviors. The
    AAPI – Adult-Adolescent Parenting Inventory, identifies five constructs that contribute to the maltreatment of children.
    These constructs form the curriculum. Parents & children learn new beliefs, knowledge & skills that improve their family
    functioning as skills/competencies are gained. Amigos uses a Group Based Model that is cost effective, includes parent
    to parent socialization, interactive learning, & allows both parents & children to meet in separate groups that run
    concurrently for twelve weeks. Positive family life leads to productive, employed citizens, saving taxpayers and the State
    of Florida many resources.

     The goals are to strengthen families and their children by providing relevant, effective education and support for the
    healthy growth and development of the family. During the 12 week educational workshops parents and children learn
    ways in which their chances of success in life can improve, through communication, role playing, and life skills. Important
    core values of the program include empathy, parental self-care and alternative discipline methods resulting in successful
    parenting. Children learn comparable skills at age-appropriate levels through focused educational group activities. These
    activities are designed to offer experimental learning, promote child health, and positive family interactions and
    relationships. All of the educational
    activities are delivered by staff trained in our evidence-based program curriculum delivery. In addition, wrap around
    supportive services are offered by
    our family support workers for families in need of other community services.

    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     1. Reduction in Child Problem Behavior - Parents will report 85% or higher reduction in child problem behavior after
    completion of program. This will be measured through pre- and post-assessments using the Strengths and Difficulties
    Questionnaire (SDQ).

    2. Increase in Postive Parent/Child Interaction - Parents will self-report 87% or higher increase in positive parent child
    interaction aftter completion of program. This will be measured through pre- and post-assessments using the Adult /
    Adolescent Parenting Inventory 2 (AAPI-2).
