1. Project Title          Bushnell Wastewater Facility and Collection System Upgrades

2. Senate Sponsor        Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request       02/28/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The City of Bushnell is currently served by a gravity and pressure-based collection system. The city has 19 wastewater lift
   stations, many of which are beyond their useful life. Additionally, the city owns more than 600 low pressure grinder pumps
   that are more than 15 years old and many have already failed. Due to majority of lift stations having lining degradation or
   are at or nearing capacity, it is necessary to perform upgrades to line the wet wells and to support future growth and
   development. This project will also include Flow Modeling and SCADA Analysis at Lift Stations; SSES Evaluation of Gravity
   Collection System. The City needs a new WWTF to accommodate the growth in South Sumter County. The existing facility
   is nearly at capacity and has had several overflows as a result of failing equipment and undersized tankage. The City is
   seeking additional funding to support the planning, design and construction of the new facility that will facilitate growth
   within the region.

     New/upgraded lift stations, lines, grinder pumps and the performance of Flow Modeling and SCADA Analysis at Lift

    standards and supporting future growth. This will also ensure no leaking into the aquifer. New Regional WWTF will

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Design and construction of the upgraded collections system, specifically the lift stations to include new/upgraded
    stations, grinder pumps, lines, flow modeling, SCADA and SSES Evaluation. Planning/Design/Construction of new and
    improved WWTF to meet the rapid growth of the community.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Collections system will be up to FDEP standard, support future growth, and keep citizens safe from environmental harm.
    New and improved WWTF to meet the rapid growth of the community.

    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Updated/Improved Collections System and Lift Stations. New and improved WWTF to meet the rapid growth of the

    harm. Outcome/success will be measure by FDEP permit requirements.
