   businesses, located in communities disproportionately impacted by gun violence, that feed into the MDC Real Time Crime

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Miami- Dade County, through its Police Department and its Office of Neighborhood Safety, has continuously focused on
    implementing innovative and sustainable solutions to reduce community violence, including gun violence.
    Funds will be used to achieve the following goals:
    .Support and build capacity of grassroots groups and small neighborhood-based organizations to develop and/or expand
    violence prevention and intervention programs and initiatives that positively impact the community and its residents.
    . Provide funded organization with training and technical assistance to implement programming in an effort to reduce
    community violence, specifically gun violence, and create safer neighborhoods.
    . Expand MDPD’s Project Green Light virtual patrolling via the installation of cameras that feed into to the MDPD Real
    Time Crime Center.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Violence prevention and intervention programs, activities, and initiatives that positively impact the community and its
    residents, including, but not limited to, efforts addressing food insecurity, urban gardens and education, youth
    engagement initiatives during nontraditional evening hours, youth educational, internship and employment opportunities
    and activities, bridging the digital divide, youth sports, health and wellness activities, college and post-secondary prep
    and guidance, and entrepreneurial workshops; capacity-building, and training and technical assistance workshops for
    funded organizations providing the violence prevention and intervention programming. Virtual patrolling via camera
    installation that feeds into the MDPD Real-Time Crime Center.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     . Violence prevention and intervention programs for youth, adults and communities.
    . Virtual patrolling by MDPD via the installation of cameras outside of mom & pop businesses located in communities
    impacted by gun violence. Cameras provide a direct feed into MDPD's Real Time Crime Center.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Residents and mom & pop businesses in communities disproportionately impacted by gun violence.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Residents are provided with programs and activities that positively impact them and their community. Strengthen
    MDPD's efforts to identify, deter, and solve crime via a public/private partnership that provides virtual patrolling via the
    installation of cameras that feed into the MDPD's Real-Time Crime Center. Funded community-based organizations will
    provide quarterly reports that minimally highlight the number of residents served, types of services provided, referrals
    made and outcomes of services/efforts. Analyze MDPD's data on the crime trends at and near businesses participating in
    PGL.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Funds could be provided on a reimbursement basis after services are performed.
