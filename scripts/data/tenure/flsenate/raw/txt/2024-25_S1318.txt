1. Project Title         Miami Robert King High Park Redevelopment- Phase 1

4. Project/Program Description
    The Robert King High Park redevelopment project will be completed in two phases. The funding requested is to cover a
   portion of the estimated expenses of phase I. Phase one cost include site preparation and/or demolition, design and
   construction of a trail system, camping area, kayak launch area, nature playground, parking and roadway improvements
   and drainage for park site next to Tamiami Canal and Lake Mahar near Miami International Airport.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds requested will help the City of Miami to provide safe and enjoyable spaces for various forms of recreation and
    increase accessibility to open spaces for a densely populated community near Miami International Airport.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Once completed the project will provide a Community Center, Playground Equipment, Kayak Launch, Concessions,
    Fitness Equipment, Nature Trails, Boardwalks, Campsites and various classes to enhance the recreational opportunities
    and quality of life of park users.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     No funds will be used to provide direct services to participants.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is primarily the City of Miami neighborhood locally referred to a "Blue Lagoon". An estimated
    28,000 people in this area and will be the primary beneficiaries of the Robert King High Park redevelopment.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit of the project is an increase in use of public spaces by children, families, and community
    organizations in a safer, and healthier environment than currently available. The outcome will be measured by the
    increased number of public events held on the park, number of children and families participating in organized
    recreational activities and classes on the park, documenting the decrease in criminal or safety related incidents on or
    near the park and documenting the number of days areas of the park are rendered unusable from flooding.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables or performance measures should subject the City to an audit and depending on the results
    of the audit the City could be liable for sanctions up to revocation of funding.
