14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Providing enhanced Volunteer Income Tax Assistance (VITA) and financial coaching services to working families and
    individuals throughout the state reaching under-served populations in the hardest-to-reach areas
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     United Ways and their partners will secure donated office space from community partners: non-profit, for-profit and
    governmental at which IRS-certified volunteers will help low and moderate income workers complete their federal income
    tax returns. Site coordinators will be hired to identify, secure and set up the sites, and recruit and help certify volunteer tax
    preparers, improving accuracy.

    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     More than 30,000 low-to-moderate income working Floridians assisted, saving them up to $2.7 million in tax preparation
    fees and generating more than $48 million in total tax returns, $13 million in Earned Income Tax Credits, and an
    estimated $2.75 million in state and local sales taxes. Outcomes will be quantified primarily through official IRS reports
    and supplemented with data provided by UW.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     More than 30,000 low-to-moderate income workers
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     $48 million in federal income tax returns for low-to-moderate income working Floridians, which can be measured via IRS
    reports and documentation by local United Ways
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Reduced funding based on percentage of deliverables unmet
