14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     We are requesting funding to support our Coral Reef Youth Education Program. This program places a special focus on
    youth education as an important, initial step in saving endangered marine ecosystems through two main components, our
    Discover Coral Reefs School Program (DCRSP) and our Coral Camp for Kids program.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Our Discover Coral Reefs School Program offers over a dozen marine science topics taught in interactive classroom
    presentations supplemented with question-and-answer sessions and related activities. Reef Relief also offers a unique
    educational opportunity through our Coral Camp for Kids and Reef Explorer Coral Camp Programs.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Students Pre-Kindergarten through graduate level will be empowered to make informed decisions. They will gain
    experiences and snorkel skills from visiting the coral reef. As well as life skills regarding decision making, critical thinking,
    and resource partitioning. Residents will experience better quality of life from decreased pollution. Tourists will have
    higher quality experience.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The Discover Coral Reefs School Program, spring and summer programs, target students aged Pre-Kindergarten
    through graduate level. While we will target approximately 12,000 students worldwide through in person and virtual
    learning, there will be a special emphasis on students, adults, and tourists, specifically those located in Florida counties
    directly impacted by the Florida Barrier Reef.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Participants in the Discover Coral Reefs Middle School Program and spring and summer programs are pre and post
    tested. Every year, we aim to stay in line with or increase the retention rates for both programs. This is a way to not only
    gauge the success of our programs, but to determine the effectiveness of our teachings. We are also starting to see
    program alumni and students graduate and go on to pursue marine conservation fields.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The agency will initiate an internal and monitoring plan to ensure deliverables are met the following fiscal year. If future
    actions are needed, the organization will seek external assistance to hold the agency accountable. Additional penalties
    would be determined depending on circumstance. Any funds unused will be returned to the State.
