4. Project/Program Description
    With the current statewide shortage in the medical field, the expansion of Crystal River High School's Health Academy
   would address the five pathways offered at the school: dental, EMT, biomed, nursing, and veterinary. These funds will be
   used to perform renovations on Crystal River High School's current Health Academy facilities. The funds will also be used
   to purchase curriculum, supplies, and equipment to meet industry standards.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to empower Citrus County students with the knowledge and skills needed for high-skilled, high-demand
    health related careers by expanding current health academy pathways at Crystal River High School. The students will
    learn technical health focused skills, related hands-on training, and master the knowledge needed to be successful and
    earn industry certifications in health-related fields. Students will be ready for direct employment or advanced training at
    any post-secondary school within the Florida College System.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Crystal River Health Academy will provide courses with challenging standards, hands on training, relevant technical
    knowledge and skills needed to earn industry certifications for direct employment in a health-related field or advanced
    training at a post-secondary school to build the students' careers in various health pathways.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The direct services will be four years of high school courses to prepare our students to be employable directly out of high
    school or to continue on to advanced training at a post-secondary facility. This will fill the skilled medical trade gap in our
    area, promote economic growth and establish a high standard of living for residents of Citrus County.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The target population will be students from across the county who are interested in developing skills needed for
    medically associated careers. We currently have 225 students enrolled annually in the Health Academy at Crystal River
    High School.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcome is to increase industry certifications earned, graduation rates, and essential jobs filled for
    community growth. This project will also decrease the high school dropout rate increasing the number of students
    entering the workforce with high-skilled, high-wage jobs. These data points will be gathered and compared to previous
    year's data.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Citrus County Schools is committed to addressing Florida's Health Trade shortage through innovative training. In the
    event that performance measures are not met, we will ask for a 60-day performance improvement plan.
