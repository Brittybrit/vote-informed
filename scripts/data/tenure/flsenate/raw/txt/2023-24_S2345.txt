1. Project Title         Bushnell Water Production & Quality Improvements Project

2. Senate Sponsor        Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request       02/28/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    This project is to design and construct Bushnell extension of the proposed water main to connect to Sumterville water
   system. The Sumterville Water system has a history of poor water quality and connecting it to the Bushnell system will
   provide a more reliable water supply source and water quality to the growing customer base in Sumterville. This extension
   will help open up economic opportunity to the corridor.
