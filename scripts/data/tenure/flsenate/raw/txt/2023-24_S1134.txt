1. Project Title         Coral Gables ADA Transit Stop Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor        Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request       02/03/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Implement ADA improvements at City of Coral Gables trolley stop facilities along Ponce de Leon Boulevard from south of
   San Lorenzo Avenue to northeast of Douglas Road. The proposed trolley stop features include, but are not limited to: City
   standard trolley signs, accessible benches, trash receptacles, shaded facilities (shelters), and bike racks. These
   improvements will improve rider accessibility and compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) .

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal for this project is to implement ADA improvements at City of Coral Gables trolley stop facilities along Ponce de
    Leon Blvd from south of San Lorenzo Avenue to northeast of Douglas Road. Furthermore, other features that will be
    installed to encourage mass ridership include, but are not limited to: City standard trolley signs, benches, trash
    receptacles, shaded facilities (shelters) and bike racks. These improvements will improve rider accessibility and
    compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    The objective of the proposed trolley stop enhancements is to improve rider accessibility and compliance with the
    Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and encourage ridership throughout the community.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The proposed features that will improve rider accessibility under the ADA and encourage mass ridership on the City's
    trolley service include, but are not limited to: City standard trolley signs, accessible benches, trash receptacles, shaded
    facilities (shelters) and bike racks.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The following target populations will be served by this project are the: (1) Elderly, (2) Economically Disadvantaged, (3)
    Physically Disabled, and (4) Students.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     An expected benefit increase in the City's trolley ridership numbers and accessibility. The City will conduct a before and
    after comparison of the ridership numbers and accessibility features to determine whether this outcome was met.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The City will include liquidated damages per calendar day in the contract documents to address delays.
