1. Project Title          South Miami Septic to Sewer Conversion Program

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

4. Project/Program Description
    The funds will be used to construct a new sanitary system and pump station to connect a proposed Community building in
   a priority sea-level Rise area and provide a public sanitary sewer system services and to an additional forty three (43)
   homes along the project alignment which also allows for future expansion of the Sanitary System in the area. Existing
   properties are currently served by septic tank systems that are negatively impacting the City's groundwater/drinking water.
