2. Senate Sponsor         Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request        02/16/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Analyze the County's and other publicly-available data sets in a multivariate approach spatially & temporally to look for
   patterns and trends in water quality pollution; visualize DEP Grant milestones; visualize said data analysis for
   managers/decision-makers/scientists/public; inform the County's development of a Reasonable Assurance Plan; support
   goals of the County's Biscayne Bay Task Force Report & inform development of the Biscayne Bay Watershed Management
   Advisory Board's Watershed Plan.

   Specifically, employ statistical and GIS-based analyses to analyze data sets and visualize data for spatial and temporal
   variation; use analysis to identify sources of pollution and prioritize infrastructure needs (waste- and stormwater); run
   economic analyses on importance of health of Biscayne Bay; visualization tools to support public understanding and
   education.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Analyze the County's and other publicly-available data sets in a multivariate approach spatially & temporally to look for
    patterns and trends in water quality pollution; visualize DEP Grant milestones; visualize said data analysis for
    managers/decision-makers/scientists/public; inform the County's development of a Reasonable Assurance Plan; support
    goals of the County's Biscayne Bay Task Force Report & inform development of the Biscayne Bay Watershed
    Management Advisory Board's Watershed Plan; inform managers and decision-makers of the economic importance of
    Biscayne Bay; Enhance public education through dashboard to showcase DEP grant milestones as well as status of
    Biscayne Bay health.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Support in multivariate data analysis to identify spatial and temporal trends using statistical tests and projecting results
    using data visualization tools including but not limited to GIS; interactive reports; visual explore patterns in data across
    multiple datasets at the same time.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     -Products and tools will help education the public on the health of Biscayne Bay, the status of publicly-funded grant
    milestones via dashboards and portals
    - Statistical analysis, reporting, and data visualization will assist decision-makers, managers, and academia with
    prioritizing service such as improvements to stormwater and wastewater.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    - All residents of Miami-Dade County as well as southern Broward and northern Monroe that share the watershed with
    Biscayne Bay
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     - Products and tools to support development of a Reasonable Assurance Plan in partnership with DEP and municipal
    and other state stakeholders
    - Products and tools to support the work of the state's Biscayne Bay Commission, the County's Biscayne Bay Watershed
    Management Advisory Board, and development of a watershed plan
    - Tools to promote public education and understanding of water quality health and overall health of Biscayne Bay
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

    County will have a period of time to rectify meeting deliverables by certain timeline and, if not adaptable, funding is
    withheld and County covers cost of services.
