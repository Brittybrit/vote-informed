14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To acquire a building to house a museum preserving the history of the exodus of over 14,000 children that took place
    from Dec 1960 - Oct 1962, early years of the Cuban Communist Revolution. Space suitable for historical, art, and cultural
    exhibits; instructional and meeting spaces; theatre; staff offices; archival storage for the organization's collection of Pedro
    Pan historical artifacts, photographs, periodicals, etc.; in a controlled environment where it can be preserved for future
    generations.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Historical exhibits with unique tours led by Pedro Pan children. Documentary screenings, book presentations and book
    signings, art exhibits, and musicals. Cultural events showcasing the many contributions made by Pedro Pans in music,
    art, cuisine, architecture, medicine, law, public service, etc. A hub for exhibits to tour the USA.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The museum would offer entertainment and educational services for the whole community in the form of activities
    centered around the Pedro Pan experience, such as the history of the Cold War, the evils of communism, and the Pedro
    Pan contributions in a myriad of fields. A home to gather and reunite the Pedro Pans and their descendants scattered
    throughout the USA. Increased tourism due to these scattered Pedro Pans coming to the area with their families to visit
    the museum.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The majority of the funds requested will serve elderly persons, preschool students, high school students,
    university/college students, general public, and the over 14,000 Pedro Pans whose numbers continue to grow with every
    new legacy generation and their impressive achievements.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Pedro Pan is a compelling story that teaches the importance of freedom. The exhibits have a proven record of success.
    Barry University (2022-2023) Award winning documentary shown to sold out audiences in local schools and universities
    as well as in several other states.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Return funds.
