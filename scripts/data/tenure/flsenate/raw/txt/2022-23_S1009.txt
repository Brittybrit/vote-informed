2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

4. Project/Program Description
    To provide and expand after school programs for underprivileged children in the communities of Sweetwater and the South
   Miami Height. Our goal is for no child to stay behind because of lack of funds. Our most important program is to provide
   free tutoring for any child from first through twelfth grades. For our tutoring program, we partner with the FIU Honors
   College students. We reward the students that attend the tutoring programs by allowing them to join any one of our three
   current programs promoting "Education through Physical Fitness". The three programs are: Taekwondo, Dance and
   Archery. We would also start providing a fourth program with music.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To be able to provide free or very low cost sports and educational programs that will instill confidence, promote fitness,
    health and discipline, impart skills and help propel the area's children and their families towards Educational and Financial
    Success. To enable the under served youth and families of the City of Sweetwater and South Miami Heights to have an
    equal opportunity as other children do to get ahead in life.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     For children from K-12 Grades: After school Tutoring, Taekwondo, Archery, Dance, Piano classes and College S.A.T.
    prep classes. These activities will now be offered as we expand our programs into South Miami Height as well.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Tutoring: Nearly 100 students with one teacher. We have volunteer students from FIU Honors college helping with
    tutoring but, since they come on a volunteer bases, we cannot demand their presence. NEED: 1 Assistant teacher. We
    have some old computers with old educational programs, NEED: 3 new laptops with new programs and additional
    memory. This will enable these students to develop their computer savvy. College SAT prep classes. NEEDS: Teacher
    and prep books. Physical Education programs:Taekwondo-40 athletes (10 in competition team) with one instructor.
    NEEDS: Asst. Instructor, athletes protective gear & eqpt. As we are expanding, we have a need for Instructors in each
    program stated above plus funding for 3 competitions. Dance-30 dancer(10 in competition team) NEED: Asst. teacher 7
    funds for uniform and 2 competitions. Archery-35 archers w/1teacher. NEEDS: Asst teacher & eqpt. Music-Needs: 2
    teachers & instruments. NEED: New proper multi-purpose removable safety floor for TWD/Dance.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    Children from 3-18 years old. Underserved and or at risk youth, economically disadvantaged from the Sweetwater, South
    Miami Height and their surrounding communities. We expect to serve in excess of 400 children.

    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Overall, to give economically disadvantaged children the same educational and physical fitness granted to any child. Our
    free tutoring program enables parents to afford for their children a private tutor with additional instructions or educational
    guidance. Success is measured with their academic improvement. Our 3 educational through physical fitness programs
    (Taekwondo, Dance, Archery) are designed to improve physical and mental health, self discipline, improve motor skills,
    self esteem and confidence. Although it is difficult to measure outcome, we base much of the success w/our retention
    rates & results from local & state competitions. Also, for students w/discipline problems at home or school, we measure
    conduct improvement. Our upcoming music program(piano/guitar) aims to extend these same benefits to musically-
    inclined children in our community.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

    We will maintain statistics and benchmarks on accomplishments and we can discuss with the department of education if
    we not not meet expectations of monies to be returned.
