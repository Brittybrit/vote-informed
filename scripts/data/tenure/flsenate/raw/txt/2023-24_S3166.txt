4. Project/Program Description
    Florida Responders Resiliency Foundation (FRRF) cares for those who voluntarily sacrifice their physical health and
   endanger their mental wellbeing to serve and protect others. FRRF provides first responders with resources to raise
   awareness and educate police officers and firefighters on strategies to approach, mitigate and/or cope with the stresses
   encountered on the job.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To provide metal health services to first responders that do not qualify under the current Poat Traumatic Stress Disorder
    (PTSD) statutory language in section 112.1815, Florida Statutes to ensure access to certified mental health counselor for
    first responders who are in crisis.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     24/7 phone line that connects the first responder with an operator to schedule an appointment within 24 hours either in
    person or virtual with a mental health counselor that specializes in treating first responders.
