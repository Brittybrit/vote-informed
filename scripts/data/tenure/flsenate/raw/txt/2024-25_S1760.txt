1. Project Title          Homestead Septic to Sewer Conversion

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       11/28/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    To assist customers with potential costs associated with decommissioning of existing septic tanks and subsequent
   connection to City sewer infrastructure for approximately 125-200 homes in the City of Homestead. This project will provide
   funding for property owners to abandon septic tank systems and connect to available sanitary sewer infrastructure for
   approximately 125-200 homes located in low to moderate income areas. Providing financial assistance for private side
   connections is the biggest barrier for property owners. This funding would complement the public laterals being installed by
   the City for properties with available and abutting sewer infrastructure.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Eliminate septic tanks to address the concerns of aging and failing septic tanks from discharging nutrients into the
    environment in the proposed project area, thus improving water quality. The project will protect the City's groundwater
    quality and supply by providing funding for property owners to abandon septic tank systems and connect to available
    sanitary sewer infrastructure for approximately 125-200 homes located in low to moderate income areas. Costs related to
    private side connections is the biggest barrier for property owners. This funding would complement the public laterals
    being installed by the City for properties with available and abutting sewer infrastructure.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The conversion of septic tanks to sewer lines for approximately 125-200 homes in the City of Homestead. The project will
    provide plumbing connections from public sewer infrastructure to individual properties. Once a property is connected to
    the sanitary sewer system, the septic tank will be properly abandoned.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This project will give residents access to sewer lines. Property owners will be provided the service of plumbing
    connections and septic tank abandonment.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This funding would support the private property connections in areas with a high density of unconnected and
    economically vulnerable properties for approximately 125-200 low- to moderate-income homes within the City of
    Homestead where the City is installing sewer laterals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project will have social, economic, and environmental benefits including reducing homeowner's costs, reduced
    impacts from septic systems compromised by rising groundwater including: Reduce nutrient effluent impacts on ground
    and surface waters; Reduce public health impacts from aging and failing septic systems that have the potential to cause
    sewer backups in homes, impacting health and property; Reduce risk of exposure to bacteria and pathogens in
    floodwaters in areas with saturated soil conditions. The outcome will be measured by number of homes converted to the
    sewer system.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The penalties for failing to meet deliverable or performance measures is the risk of not recovering reimbursement cost
    and potential delay in project progress.
