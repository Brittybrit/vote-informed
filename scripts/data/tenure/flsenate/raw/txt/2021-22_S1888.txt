12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The CauseClass program will educate local youth in the state of FL about global citizenship using the United Nations
    Sustainable Development Goals. (UNSDGs) The goal is to educate our youth on being a global citizen, helping them
    understand their interconnected role in the world in order to develop their own role in their local community to work with
    others to make our planet more peaceful and sustainable for all.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Virtual lessons, reflections, and community service opportunities
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Education and Community Service Opportunities
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Grade school students, high school students, university/college students, economically disadvantaged persons, at-risk
    youth are the target population. The program will be able to serve over 800 individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     An enriched cultural experience will mold global citizenship with local community change and impact measured by
    service hours completed and the improved quality of education will educate students on the UNSDGs in efforts to teach
    students how to work with others to make our planet and community more peacefull and sustainable measured by
    CuaseClass enrollments and completion data.
