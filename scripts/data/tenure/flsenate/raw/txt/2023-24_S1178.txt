2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       02/08/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    This project is about producing educational materials for students and the general populace that will empower them to act
   both before and after a sudden cardiac arrest. Through a variety of media, bystander inventions will be taught so people
   know when, how, and why to act. Warning signs will be shared to identify the 1 in 300 youth with an undiagnosed heart
   condition. Personalized stories will bring the information to life.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Many lives will be saved as people, including students, are educated on sudden cardiac arrest (SCA), learning how to
    recognize it and respond with lifesaving measures. They will learn that SCA affects people of all ages and the warning
    signs of a cardiac condition.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funds will help provide for educational materials aimed at ensuring survival from sudden cardiac arrest. A variety of
    age-appropriate materials will be created, such as activity books, training videos, flyers, posters, handouts, social media
    content, interviews, media content, informational videos, and real-life stories to personalize the message.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will be given pertinent information on how to act not only in the case of a sudden cardiac arrest but also before
    one strikes. They will be armed with and empowered by knowledge to help save a life. 25% of people find themselves at
    some point in a situation where CPR and an AED are needed to save someone's life.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     K-12 students, youth with a cardiac condition and their families, and the general populace.
    2.7 million students plus families and members of the general populace.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The educational component of these materials is paramount as they will teach real-world, life-saving skills that a high
    percentage of people will need in their own lives. Quickly-administered bystander interventions mean the difference
    between life, death and brain damage in cases of SCA. These materials will educate and empower people to act.
    Reaching students will unleash an army of lifesavers. The preventative measures being addressed will lead to better
    outcomes and appropriate treatments for those with a previously undiagnosed cardiac condition.
     Another key component is bringing awareness to the undiagnosed heart conditions which 1 in every 300 students has.
    With a diagnosis, those students can receive appropriate treatments and escape sudden cardiac death. They can also be
    advised on any necessary precautionary physical limitations to avoid sudden cardiac arrest, the #1 killer of student
    athletes.

    The outcome will be measured by views, downloads, and distribution.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Cancellation of appropriation at the end of the fiscal year.
