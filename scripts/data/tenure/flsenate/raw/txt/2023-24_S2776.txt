14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose of the funds requested are to to expand the amenities offered in the City's Carlow Park. ADA upgrades will
    be made to the park, along with the addition of exercise equipment and benches to promote use by the senior population
    in the immediate area.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Exercise equipment and other amenities will be provided to meet the intended purpose of the funds.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Expansion of the overall area of the park, exercise equipment, and improved scenery/landscaping.

    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Elderly persons, persons with poor mental health, persons with poor physical health, developmentally disabled,
    physically disabled, The City of Sweetwater is a minority-enriched community that is 95.5% Hispanic with the target
    population being >800
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The project will improve physical health of the city's population by providing more exercise equipment for residents to
    use, and improve the mental health of the city's population by providing a common, green area for the community to enjoy
    and partake in recreational activities.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Termination of project funding.
