12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The state funds will be used to continue improving quality of life in the Flagami Area, around SW 7th Street from 63rd Ct
    to SW 63rd Avenue consisting of new drainage system, paving, curbs, and pedestrian crossing improvements. impacting
    aprox. 150 residential properties, 75 residents, $165.5M, will be impacted by this project.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     These funds will be used for right-of-way improvements including new and upsized stormwater inlets and pipes and new
    exfiltration trenches.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Target population is a mix of mid to low income housing.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     mix of mid to low income housing.
