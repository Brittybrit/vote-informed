4. Project/Program Description
    Construction of a 8,500 sq ft Life Development Center designed to serve individuals with intellectual, developmental and
   physical disabilities. This project will replace a 90+ yr old structure. The organization has leased the current facility from the
   Sumter County School Board for the past 40+ years, however, because of the tremendous growth that the school district is
   experiencing, they have given notice of not renewing the lease after 2030. The Arc Nature Coast is a 50 year old
   organization with a committed board of directors and an experienced competent staff prepared to ensure successful timely
   completion of this project request if funded. The Sumter County Board of County Commissioners is providing land for this
   project and has already voted unanimously to support the project if funded.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Construction of a 8,500+ sq-ft Life Development Center in order to relocate current services from a 90+ year old facility
    that is leased from the Sumter County School Board. The organization has leased the facility from the School Board for
    the past 40+ years, however, because of the tremendous growth that the school district is experiencing, they have given
    notice of not renewing the lease after 2030.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Activities funded by this appropriation, if granted, include design, pre-development, site work and construction of a 8,500
    sq ft commercial Life Development Center at 316 Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Ave., Bushnell, FL. The land is owned by
    Sumter County Government and being made available for this project with written unanimous support by the board of
    county commissioners. The site is pre-engineered and ready for development.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This proposed Life Development Center will provide adult day treatment and respite services for individuals living with
    their families, as well as individuals residing in long term-care housing. The Arc Nature Coast has had to decline new
    referrals to this service in recent years due to insufficient space at the existing facility. This project will double the service
    capacity, allowing The Arc to accept all new referrals in a timely manner from the Agency for Persons with Disabilities.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    This center will serve individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities who are clients of the Agency for
    Persons with Disabilities (APD). This center will serve 150+ individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project will replace a 90+ year old structure that is in extremely poor condition and is currently at full capacity, thus
    ensuring improved safety and quality of service for a population of individuals with disabilities. We expect to see a
    reduction in incident/accident reports, improved goal progress reports, as well as an increase in the number of people
    being served.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Any unused appropriated state funding will revert back to the state in the case of unmet deliverables or costs that are
    below what is budgeted. If The Arc Nature Cost were to ever discontinue using the facility for its intended purpose in the
    future, the asset would transfer to Sumter County government to be used for an alternative public benefit.
