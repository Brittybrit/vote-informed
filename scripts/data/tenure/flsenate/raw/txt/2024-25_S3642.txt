1. Project Title          City of Homestead Electric Utility Building Hardening

2. Senate Sponsor         Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request        01/08/2024

4. Project/Program Description
    To build an elevated warehouse to store various electric and water materials that are critical for the continuity of operations
   and services rendered to City residents. This equipment, such as switch cabinets, transformers, wire, etc., is essential to
   resume City electrical services by storing and keeping these supplies on site. A warehouse will prevent equipment from
   being stored outdoors and deteriorating from exposure to the natural elements of the environment and during natural
   disasters. By building this new facility, the equipment that is used to restore services after a storm will be protected and
   available for use.

     To build an elevated warehouse to store various electric and water materials that are critical for the continuity of
    operations and services rendered to City residents. This equipment, such as switch cabinets, transformers, wire, etc., is
    essential to resume City electrical services by storing and keeping these supplies on site. A warehouse will prevent
    equipment from being stored outdoors and deteriorating from exposure to the natural elements of the environment and
    during natural disasters. By building this new facility, the equipment that is used to restore services after a storm will be
    protected and available for use.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Construction of an elevated warehouse to store various electric and water materials that are critical for the continuity of
    operations and services rendered to City residents. By building this new facility, the equipment that is used to restore
    services after a storm will be protected and available for use.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The direct service to the residents will be the ability of City staff and field crews to respond immediately after a hurricane
    or other storm event to restore services and continue operations rendered to City residents.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is the general population of 80,000 residents City-wide.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcome and benefit of this project is that City staff is able to respond to more quickly to restore services
    after a storm since the essential equipment will be safely stored indoors and on-site for quick access.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Revocation of funding if project is not completed.
