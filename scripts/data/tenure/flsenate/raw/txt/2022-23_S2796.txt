4. Project/Program Description
    The State Attorney’s Office, 11th Judicial Circuit (SAO 11), seeks funding to develop a Smart Justice Strategies Unit
   (SJSU). The SJSU will connect the various data silos in MDC's criminal justice system and other public institutions for the
   purposes of improving transparency, identifying areas of critical need, further developing SAO 11's data-driven and
   evidence based practices, and creating a more efficient, effective, strategic, and fair system. The SJSU will identify crime
   risk factors in the community and evaluate all aspects of the system including: current laws and processes, investigations,
   civil citations, arrests, intake, our use of risk assessments, release decisions (ie. bail/bond, pre-trial services, etc.),
   diversionary programs (including treatment courts), and sentencing practices. The SAO SJSU will document the SJSU's
   work (including all scripting) so it may be used as a statewide model and allow for easy replication.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The requested funds will allow SAO 11 to develop a Smart Justice Strategies Unit that will connect the various data silos
    in MDC's criminal justice system and other public institutions for the purposes of developing a data portal providing
    increased transparency; (2) allowing SAO 11 to analyze community/environmental data relating to known risk factors; and
    (3) draw defensible, data-driven conclusions to better deter criminal activity, rehabilitate offenders, save revenue, and
    inform the public.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The SJSU will compile and analyze community and criminal justice data, identify opportunities for improvement, and
    develop business intelligence dashboards to intuitively present key performance indicators to the public. The systems
    developed with grant funds will be fully documented to allow similar institutions across the state to replicate them at
    minimal cost.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     SAO 11 will use the data and insights to identify ways to better direct our limited resources, determine who can be
    diverted/rehabilitated and who must be incarcerated, improve our diversion programs, and facilitate more effective,
    strategic, and fair prosecutions. SAO 11 will use the Smart Justice Data Portal to provide a direct service allowing internet
    users to download and review aggregate data and SAO 11's analysis.

    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population served by this project is the 2.7 million members of the Miami-Dade County community. The
    insights gained by analyzing the data will allow SAO 11 to better serve and protect the community, particularly crime
    victims. The public data portal will be free to use and provide a direct service .
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The SJSU will create a dashboard that it can use to analyze past and current data and identifying trends. SAO will use
    the data to identify areas of need and opportunities for improvement. Tangible outcome measures include: the
    implementation of a dashboard and changes to SAO 11's allocation of resources and programming, along with a public
    data portal.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Release of funds based on failure to meet project milestones.
