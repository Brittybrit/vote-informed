1. Project Title         Key Colony Beach City Hall Complex Hurricane Irma Repair

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       01/26/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    The city is replacing a Hurricane IRMA damaged critical facility (City Hall), which includes the Emergency Operations
   Center (EOC) and Police Department. This request is to help fund the following:
   1) Elevating the structure above base flood elevation by 24-36 inches to Coastal A standards and fortifying the foundation
   against V zone wave action.
   2) Furniture, fixtures, equipment, and information technology costs related to the EOC. This equipment will include
   technology to have direct audio and video connectivity to Monroe County’s EOC.
   3) The installation of an alternate power source such as mobile generator and a battery-backup power system with solar
   input to ensure continuity of operations during and following an event.
   4) Additional soft cost/match related to architecture and engineering services (A&E).
   This scope will ensure ongoing operations by designated emergency personnel and provide space for a last-resort shelter.
   The request is less than 1/3 of total cost, a 3-1 ratio.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Providing adequate space (foundation) for public safety to include policing, marine patrol operation, and criminal holding
    space.
    Provide adequate space for building department to insure building code compliance & code safety. Provide local
    government emergency operation center for emergencies and connectivity to Monroe County EOC.
    Provide critical shelter for first responders and elected officials as well as debris contractors.
    Will also provide limited space as a shelter of last resort to stranded citizens.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Adequate space for police, building, and code departments.
    Include access for community interaction.
    Measured by improved effectiveness of police, building, and code departments including the accessibility of the general
    public, which is currently is inadequate. Today, there is no ADA access to the building department and space is restrictive
    to all activities mentioned above.
    Effectiveness of managing a storm or other crisis by being able to coordinate with Monroe County EOC to deliver goods
    and services to the community in an efficient manner.
    Protect the public health and safety and protect property.
    Shelter 1st responders and limited stranded citizens in the community where necessary.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Access to adequate critical government facilities, including full ADA access, to police, emergency operations, building,
    and code departments and general administration of the city, including wastewater and storm-water administration.
    The facility will also serve as limited shelter of last resort, if necessary.
    The facility will be directly tied into Monroe County EOC.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The target population is the citizens of Key Colony Beach (average age is 63), businesses and visitors to the City.
    Non-seasonal the population is approximately 803.
    Seasonally the population swells to approximately 3,500 - 4,000.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The benefit is to provide a foundation for the construction of a new critical facility for Key Colony Beach.
    The outcome will be measured by installation of an elevated sub-structure that will mitigate both flooding and hurricane
    wind damage for many years in the future.
    It will be constructed at least 24-36 inches above the new base flood elevation provided in FEMA's new preliminary base
    flood elevation maps, consistent with Florida's floodplain management regulations and Monroe County floodplain
    requirements.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Return of state funding, with interest, and ineligibility for future funding by recipient for 1 fiscal year
