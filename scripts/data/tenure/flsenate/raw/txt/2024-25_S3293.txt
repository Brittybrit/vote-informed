3. Date of Request       01/10/2024

4. Project/Program Description
    The city's goal is to remediate a city-owned property so that it can safely operate a waste diversion site. The site will
   function to divert green matter from landfills, and instead process and repurpose such material for a more sustainable use.
   The need for the site has grown more acute due to the recent destruction of a major waste facility in Miami-Dade County.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Our goal is to divert and redirect all green yard waste from landfills and recycle this natural material into products that
    can be used in our community. The green waste diversion will prevent and reduce the number of recyclable materials
    from going into landfills, creating incentives to use products such as compost and natural gas made from the reclaimed
    materials.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The City of Coral Gables will remediate a city-owned propoerty to be safely utilized as a waste diversion site. The city will
    work with companies to divert green yard waste from landfills and to replace conventional fertilizers with organics, living
    soil, and as a result, aid in the restoration of our community.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Green yard waste will be collected from citizens, taken to the remediated site, and composted or mulched. The end
    product can then be provided to citizens for use.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Residents of the City of Coral Gables and any other entities that receive yard waste removal services from the city.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The most beneficial outcome would be to lessen our landfill usage, emissions foot print and increase the use of
    sustainable materials.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Non reimursement of project costs.
