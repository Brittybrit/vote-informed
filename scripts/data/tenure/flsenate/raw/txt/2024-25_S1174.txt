4. Project/Program Description
    The Ocean Heroes program provides professional development to students K-12 in the "Blue Economy." The program
   promotes critical thinking, problem solving, other essential education goals using ocean-based themes and specifically
   focuses on the way educators can apply this knowledge to critical subject areas and career paths, ecotourism, fisheries,
   maritime careers, technology, conservation and environment.

   A major focus on the program is placed in highlighting extraordinary "Ocean Heroes" conservation role models, respective
   jobs, and career paths for students in STREAM (Science, Technology, Resiliency, Engineering, Arts & Math), Ecotourism,
   Fisheries, Hospitality and the Marine Industries. An additional key goal of each program is to bring public attention to
   critically important environmental issues, while engaging, education, and inspiring children and their families to help protect
   andensure the sustained well-being of marine animals and their respective habitats.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The "Ocean Heroes" program provides professional development and customized resources for communities for
    workforce development in the "Blue Economy" while inspiring these Ocean Ambassadors to become our "Leaders of
    Tomorrow”. A key goal for the program is to bring public attention to critically important environmental issues, while
    engaging, educating, and inspiring communities to help protect and ensure the sustained well-being of marine animals
    and their respective habitats.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     State-of-the-Art technology, computer sciences, coding to support citizen science, discovery and advocacy. Through the
    integration of new database technologies data sets of fish species and respective population studies are helping to
    develop practical ways to manage and protect them. This helps to provide a unique insight among our communities on
    how technology and science play a critical role in the advancement of research and conservation. Ocean Heroes citizen
    science initiatives are creating ways for communities and families to engage in conservations efforts, while also providing
    insights on career opportunities within the Blue Economy.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Celebration of the Sea has successfully aggregated a consortium of partners from non-profits, research organizations,
    educational institutions, recreation companies and retailers to champion a common mission and message to protect our
    oceans by advocating for the next leaders of the blue economy. Communities and families can engage in our citizen
    science programs within their local communities to continue their enrichment and participation in ocean conservation.

     .
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Celebration of the Sea's Ocean Heroes Program targets the next generation of leaders for the blue economy – K thru
    12th grade. The Foundation continues to offer and provide career pathway opportunities to youth in college and trade
    school, as well as job placement opportunities through the foundation supporters and partnerships.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     As part of the Ocean Heroes Program, Celebration of the Sea Foundation works closely with schools and educators to
    ensure that the curriculum provided is meeting the needs of their students and classrooms. Each of the enrolled
    educators will have semi-annual check ins with program staff of Ocean Heroes to learn what is working and what needs
    adjustment. Additionally, check ins also allow for our team to observe engagement and education advancement – both
    quantitative and qualitatively. Celebration of the Sea tracks and measures the internship enrollment, and outcomes of
    who within the programs go on to pursue careers in the blue economy.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     If Celebration of the Sea Foundation does not meet the deliverables set forth in the agreement, no additional funding will
    be awarded until all performance measures are fulfilled. Additionally, if an extension is required; no additional funding will
    be sought for a period of time equal to twice the extension duration.
