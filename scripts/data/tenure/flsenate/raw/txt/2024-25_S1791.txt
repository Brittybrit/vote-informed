2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

4. Project/Program Description
    This request is to fund growth of the Math Matrix professional learning program to improve the quality of mathematics
   teaching and learning. The Math Matrix is a comprehensive online, on-demand professional learning system for teachers in
   grades K-12. The Math Matrix offers engaging resources and evidence-based collaborative professional learning
   opportunities to improve student outcomes and expand future education and career opportunities for all learners. This
   project would expand our initial pilot to provide comprehensive school-based resources to build capacity within districts to
   sustain teachers' learning, while also extending the number of teachers who gain access to that learning.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To expand the implementation of the Math Matrix program to improve the quality of mathematics teaching and learning.
    The Math Matrix is a comprehensive online, on-demand professional development system for teachers in grades K-12.
    The Math Matrix will offer engaging resources and evidence-based collaborative professional learning opportunities to
    improve student outcomes and expand future education and career opportunities for all learners. This project would fund
    school-based support for the implementation of the Math Matrix with a greater number of educators across the state.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Florida teachers and instructional coaches will receive training to improve their knowledge, skills, and abilities in
    mathematics instruction. Teacher recruitment activities. Teacher and instructional coach enrollment. Facilitating teacher's
    participation in the courses and provision of feedback. Teacher feedback will be collected and analyzed to improve the
    program. Pre and post assessments will be conducted and analyzed to demonstrate program effectiveness. A report on
    the results will be created and disseminated.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Florida teachers and instructional coaches will receive training to improve their knowledge, skills, and abilities in
    mathematics instruction. Florida students of participants will benefit from improved math instruction.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Primary target population: teachers, coaches, and administrators involved in mathematics instruction in Florida K-12
    public and charter schools. The pilot will enroll up to 500 participants. A fully implemented version would be provided to
    hundreds more each year.
    Additional target population: all Florida K-12 public and charter students will benefit from improved math instruction.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The FL DOE identified mathematics as a critical teacher shortage for 2022-2023. By offering teacher and instructional
    coach training and professional development, the Math Matrix will help to address this shortage by equipping and
    retaining current teachers. Moreover, the inclusion of instructional coaches will improve the sustainability and long term
    impact of the professional learning as teachers will have school- or district-based support for their learning. Benefits will
    be measured by teacher retention rates. Improved instruction will yield improved student outcomes. Improved instruction
    will be measured by teacher practicum outcomes, and evaluation of teacher knowledge pre- and post-program
    participation. Student outcomes will be measured by EOC and/or FAST student data.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The standard penalties should suffice.
