14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funding will provide resources for the design of the equestrian building and site improvements.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funding will be used to complete Tropical Park - Equestrian Center and Park general site improvements in
    compliance with 40 year inspection recertification.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Provide recreational and equestrian services to residents and visitors of Miami-Dade County.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The general public and community visitors surrounding the site. Tropical Park is a county-wide park within Miami-Dade
    County serving more than 1 million visitors annually.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improve access to nature and equestrian services as well as educational programming. The method for measuring
    increased access and programing satisfaction from visitors will include surveys.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Tasks not achieved according to the performance period or specified metric would result in non reimbursement of
    services to be performed.
