4. Project/Program Description
    The Area Stage Company (ASC) was founded in 1989 on the principles of artistic excellence and education. This award-
   winning theatre organization has directed significant resources towards an innovative performance arts program
   unprecedented in the world of exceptional student education: ASC Disability Theatre Enrichment Program helps
   neurodiverse actors of all ages (five and up), as they embark on their journey towards adulthood and/or independence by
   teaching communication, team work, and performance skills (acting, dancing, singing) that, in essence, become life skills.
   To date, we have served 1,800 unduplicated students, have produced 22 full theatre productions, over 54 school
   productions, and have performed in festivals, events, and other places in front of thousands. Due to a State funding gap,
   we were only able to serve 75 of our 400 primarily minority students, ages 5+, through on-site classes at ASC's facility in
   South Miami, and at three partner schools.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     With the requested funds, we once again hope to serve its previous students and more who are unable to participate in
    the program due to the financial detriment of COVID-19. Our program went from serving 400+ students to only 75. With
    these funds, we plan to provide our services once again to be able to serve at least 375+ students on our waiting list.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Students will be able to participate in productions, outreach performances, and theatrical classes in acting, dance (ballet,
    jazz, musical theater), music, and singing. Classes are taught by trained instructors who are veterans in their various
    artistic fields as well as experienced working with neurodiverse individuals. Students also participate in two or three fully
    produced musical theater stage productions each year which they rehearse and perform in addition to classes. Students
    and instructors also participate in multiple community outreach activities yearly.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Direct services include artistic education, as well as improvement in the following areas: social-emotional intelligence,
    speech therapy, communication and leadership skills, cognition, coordination, and emotional well being. To the greater
    community, we provide education about and exposure to the neurodiverse community.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     ASC's Disability Theatre Enrichment Project is recognized as a one-of-a-kind performing arts program specifically geared
    to meet the needs of a population with developmental and intellectual disabilities with learning disabilities, autism,
    spectrum disorder, Down syndrome, and cerebral palsy. It serves students k-12 in addition to an adult population (ages
    18-42). Population: White: 58%, Black: 13%, Mix: 29%, Other: 6%. Hispanic: 82%, American: 14%. Other: 4%. English
    speakers: 100%, Spanish: 40%, Other: 2%. 100% of neurodivergent learners. Families are financially stable. Maybe 10%
    are at poverty level due to immigration status. 80% are MDC median income and 10% are above median. 400+
    individuals are expecting to be directly served in FY 2023-2024.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Onsite students at ASC, and their parents are given pre and post assess, emts and surveys in order to measure
    outcomes and satisfaction. Additionally, partner school classes are evaluated once per year to ensure satisfactory
    student progress. Student attendance is tracked carefully for all groups, and perhaps most effectively and importantly, all
    students participate in multiple theatre performances onstage each year. This is where progress and development are
    most evident. Success is defined as follows: 100% of all students will have improved in all parameters (cognition, fine
    motor skills, verbal communication, cooperation, self-confidence, ect.) as evidenced by pre and post assessments, 100%
    of students will have completed all rehearsal and performances throughout the session, and 100% of students and
    parents will be satisfied with the program.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     As per the contracting agency of these funds - The Florida Stage Agency for Persons with Disabilities, according to
    contract #ACX61, page 12, section 38 of the previous contract, instances of noncompliance will be penalized through
    financial consequences.
