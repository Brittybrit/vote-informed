1. Project Title         Monroe County Florida Keys Marine Protection Mooring Field Project

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       02/10/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The funding will provide for the design, engineering and implementation of 140 new moorings close to the densest
   population center, in compliance with the requirements in new state law (SB 1432, 2022) for Monroe County. These new
   moorings will accomplish multiple critical objectives.
   New moorings will mitigate the significant environmental impacts associated with illegal, unmanaged anchorages such as
   extensive damage to the benthic habitat, including seagrass.
   New moorings will provide an additional source of affordable housing since workforce members use vessels an affordable
   housing alternative.
   Vessels on moorings are more secure and less likely to drift or break anchor endangering themselves and others.
   Vessels on moorings are provided pump out service, and so are more likely to comply with water quality protection
   regulations.
   Vessels on moorings are less likely to be abandoned or derelict, and so mitigate against the safety hazard and costs of
   vessel dereliction and removal.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     New mooring fields will safeguard fragile resources and public safety, and comply with new state law (SB 1432, 2022)
    requirements for Monroe County. The new moorings will mitigate the significant environmental impacts associated with
    illegal anchoring and unmanaged anchorages such as extensive damage to the benthic habitat, including seagrass. The
    new moorings will also reduce vessel dereliction, ensure compliance with water quality protection regulations, and provide
    safe, secure harbor for vessels.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     We will design, engineer and implement 140 new moorings for liveaboard and recreational vessels. The new moorings
    will reduce environmental impacts and benthic damage of illegal anchoring and vessel dereliction, while improving safety
    and security for recreational boating and local liveaboard community, compliance with water quality protection regulations
    and State law. The new moorings will be in managed mooring fields that will provide mobile pump out and shoreside
    services, provided by Monroe County.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     With the new moorings, liveaboard and recreational boaters will be provided with safe, secure anchoring. Further, in the
    Keys, where affordable housing is scarce and much of the workforce is in service sector, liveaboard vessels provide a
    much needed supply of affordable housing. As such, safe and secure anchorage for our liveaboards is all the more
    critical.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The primary target served by this project are the members of our local workforce who live on their vessels as an
    affordable alternative for housing. Because our current mooring fields are often fully occupied, there is a significant need
    to additional provide safe, secure, environmentally-protective mooring options for these individuals and families.
    Recreational visiting boaters are a secondary target population. The ultimate goal is to provide safe, secure, and
    environmentally-protective options for vessel owners.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     New moorings will replace illegal, destructive anchorages, protecting the benthic environment, including valuable
    seagrass beds which are foundational to our marine ecosystem.
    Vessels on moorings are monitored for proper waste disposal protecting water quality.
    Vessels on moorings are more secure and less likely to drift or break anchor endangering themselves or others.
    Vessels on moorings are monitored for dereliction conditions, mitigating against the danger of derelict vessels, and the
    cost of derelict vessel removal.
    Additional source of affordable housing helps retains workforce members required by local businesses, increasing
    economic activity.
    Availability of safe, secure moorings attracts recreational and visiting boaters, increasing tourism.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

    Funding award can be premised on completion of project. If project not completed then funding can be reduced or
    withheld.
