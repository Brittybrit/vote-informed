4. Project/Program Description
    Citrus Health Network’s Adult Crisis Stabilization Unit (CSU) is open 24 hours/day, 7 days/week to offer brief, intensive
   services for individuals who are acutely mentally ill and experiencing a mental health crisis, with the goal of examining,
   stabilizing, and redirecting them to the most appropriate and least restrictive treatment setting they need. Citrus's
   Assessment and Emergency Services (AES) Department is open 24 hours/day, 7 days/week operating as a psychiatric
   emergency room to ensure that individuals in need of mental health services receive quality screenings, evaluations, and
   determinations of suicidality. Citrus' CSU and AES services are crucial round-the-clock and emergency mental health
   services, especially for indigent, uninsured, underinsured, and homeless populations in the South Florida Community. The
   requested funding will be used to cover the costs of staffing the CSU and AES.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Citrus Health Network’s Adult Crisis Stabilization Unit (CSU) is open 24 hours/day, 7 days/week to offer brief, intensive
    services for individuals who are acutely mentally ill and experiencing a mental health crisis. Likewise, Citrus' Assessment
    and Emergency Services (AES) Department operates 24 hours/day, 7 days/week as a psychiatric emergency room
    offering quality screening, evaluations, determinations of suicidality, and linkage to services. The funding allows the Citrus
    CSU and AES to stay open and operating at all times to continue meeting the ever-increasing mental health services
    demands in the community. CSUs are a far less expensive option for the state than a hospital or jail setting.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Citrus Health Network’s Adult Crisis Stabilization Unit (CSU) is open 24-hours-a-day, 7-days-a-week to offer brief,
    intensive services for individuals who are acutely mentally ill and experiencing a mental health crisis, with the goal of
    examining, stabilizing, and redirecting them to the most appropriate and least restrictive treatment setting they need.
    Likewise, Citrus' Assessment and Emergency Services (AES) Department operates 24 hours/day, 7 days/week as a
    psychiatric emergency room offering quality screening, evaluations, determinations of suicidality, and linkage to services.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     CSU: Brief, intensive services for individuals who are acutely mentally ill and experiencing a mental health crisis.
    Services offered include the following,as appropriate and clinically indicated: Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale
    Screening, Psychiatric Evaluation, Nursing Assessment and Nursing Services, Medication Management, Individual,
    Group, Family Therapy, Safety Planning, Care Coordination
    AES: Mental health screenings, evaluations, determinations of suicidality, and linkage to services including outpatient
    psychiatry and psychotherapy, crisis stabilization, mental health case management, and other behavioral health services.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target populations to be served by this project are persons with poor mental health, economically disadvantaged
    persons, homeless persons, and developmentally disabled persons.
    CSU: The funds from the project will be used to serve 700 individuals to provide 1,065 bed days for uninsured and
    underinsured individuals in need of crisis stabilization due to a mental health crisis.
    AES: The funds from the project will be used to serve 1,000 individuals to provide mental health screenings, evaluations,
    determinations of suicidality and linkage to services for uninsured and under insured individuals in need of assessment
    and emergency behavioral health services.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit of this project is to improve mental health. The specific measures to be used for the CSU are 1.Bed
    days provided to clients who have an unfunded day of care, 2. Readmission Rate based on clients who had multiple
    admissions during the time period, and 3. Clients with stable housing situation. The specific measures to be used for AES
    are (1) number of clients served who are uninsured or under insured (2) number of screenings and evaluations provided.
    The outcomes will be measured using Citrus' Electronic Health Record.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The Citrus Health Network Adult Crisis Stabilization Unit is licensed by both the Florida Department of Children and
    Families as a Public Baker Act Receiving Facility and the Florida Agency for Health Care Administration as a Crisis
    Stabilization Unit. Both licenses require that the Adult Crisis Stabilization Unit remain open 24/7, the adjacent
    Assessment and Emergency Services Department is also open 24/7, including to serve uninsured and underinsured
    clients. Citrus would work closely with DCF to ensure continued operations and compliance with deliverables.
