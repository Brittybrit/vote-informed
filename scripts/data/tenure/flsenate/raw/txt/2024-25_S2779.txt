1. Project Title          Miami-Dade County Satellite Pet Adoption Center

2. Senate Sponsor         Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request        12/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    To address the overpopulaiton crisis at the Miam-Dade County animal shelter these funds would be used to lease
   storefront space for a satelite adoption center in other regions of the county. This satelite location would give residents who
   do not live in close proximity to the shelter another location where they can adopt shelter pets, also it provides shelter pets
   more visibility by the public and adoption oportunities. This pilot program, if successful, would be expanded in the future.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To minimize the overpopulation crisis of cats and dogs at the Miami-Dade County Shelter. Animal Services will open
    satellite adoption centers. The adoption centers will be located in retail centers with pedestrian traffic in order to provide
    visibility to our shelter pets.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

                                                         Page 2 of 4

     Adoption Centers will be open 5-7 days per week allowing the community to have easy access to our shelter pets
    available for adoption.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Miami-Dade County residents who are searching for a pet will have easy access to adopt one of the county shelter pets.
    The adoption of shelter pets will alleviate the overpopulation at the county shelter.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The adoption centers will provide community residents with the ability to adopt shelter pets. The satellite adoption
    centers will be open to the public 5-7 days a week and it will house 10-15 dogs on a daily basis with the goal of adopting
    out 30 dogs per month.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The adoption centers will provide homeless pets with an opportunity to be adopted by community residents. The current
    shelter population will decrease with the opening of these satellite adoption centers.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
