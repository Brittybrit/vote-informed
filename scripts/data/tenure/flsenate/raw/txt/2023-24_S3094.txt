4. Project/Program Description
    This program will support and expand shellfish aquaculture in Florida by creating a grant program within the Division of
   Aquaculture, Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services, that will reimburse shellfish farmers, wholesalers,
   nurseries and hatcheries for the purchase of equipment.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The grants provided to aquaculture leaseholders via this program will achieve two goals: assist with the overall growth of
    the clam and oyster industry in Florida and also help to increase the availability of good quality clam and oyster seed
    throughout the state.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Services will be grants that are administered via DACS that will go directly to farmers, wholesalers, nurseries, and
    hatcheries
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Direct services will be provided to aquaculture farmers, and also to the citizens of Florida as a whole by increasing the
    availability of good quality clams and oysters in the state.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is clam and oyster farmers in the state. The total number of individuals served will vary based on
    the size of the grant awards. However, the 500+ leaseholders in the state will likely all benefit in some way by the
    increased availability of clam and oyster seed that will likely result from these grants.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit of this project is the increased growth of the clam and oyster industry and the increased availability
    of good quality clam and oyster seed. Individual farmers are required to report their yields to DACS, so the department
    will be able to measure the impact of the grants based on information that is already collected.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Return of funds.
