12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This program will provide much needed support for youth at risk to include, soft skills training, tutoring, staying in school
    incentive, assistance in youth receiving application and information regarding college or trade school, providing the much
    needed school supplies, provide access to computers and Internet if necessary.
    The program shall assist the youth in achieving self sufficiency and provide the much needed assistance with job
    placement including placement in trade schools.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The services provided shall include but not limited to: develop a presentation, develop a marketing plan, create a tutoring
    plan, organize events alongside community based organizations, schools, and vocational training facilities. All activities
    and services shall follow the COVID-19 guidelines set forth via the local, state, and federal governments.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The services provided shall include but not limited to: seminars, workshops, technical support training, school supplies
    giveaway, literacy event, graduation celebration, awards ceremony, family participation event and incentive, and social
    media outreach. The services shall be utilized to attract the maximum amount of youth at risk possible to provide the
    much needed services.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population will be youth at risk in disadvantaged communities throughout Miami-Dade County. FVF expects to
    service between 150 to 225 individuals to include their immediate family members.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
