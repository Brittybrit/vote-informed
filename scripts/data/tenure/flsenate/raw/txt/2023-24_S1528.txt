4. Project/Program Description
    Generation USA transforms education to employment systems to prepare, place, and support people into life-changing
   careers that would otherwise be inaccessible. We provide free bootcamp-style programs, job training, and placement
   support to unemployed and underemployed individuals, giving students access to multiple career pathways and connecting
   learners to internships, apprenticeships, continuing education, or full-time employment.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Generation USA creates impact through supporting unemployed individuals, with an emphasis on Black/Latinx/Female
    individuals, with lifelong skills training and job matching to propel them into sustainable careers. Programs are rigorous
    boot camps, focusing on both the technical skills required to be a high achiever in the role and the behavioral skills and
    mindsets needed to build a career.

    In one year, Generation will serve 150 individuals in Jacksonville, FL. 85% will graduate successfully from the training
    program, 75% will reach full-time employment 180 days post graduation.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    Workforce training programs in the technology, medical, and financial services sectors.
    Career and life coaching (i.e. resume building, mock interviews)
    Wrap around supports (i.e. transportation, childcare)
    Employment placement support through direct connection with employers.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Workforce training programs in the technology, medical, and financial services sectors.
    Career and life coaching (i.e. resume building, mock interviews)
    Wrap around supports (i.e. transportation, childcare)
    Employment placement support through direct connection with employers.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Generation serves a population of individuals for whom the existing education-to-employment system has not worked.
    They face a myriad of life challenges, including transportation, parenting and family challenges, and food and housing
    insecurity. Our participants are unemployed or underemployed, earning an average of $1,000 per month through part-
    time work or government assistance prior to joining the program, and achieving uplifts of 2-6X post-graduation.
    Furthermore, 44% of our graduates have dependents, meaning that the income gains have a generational impact on their
    households.

    We have recruited, trained, and supported more than 10,000 learners (58% female and more than 73% Black or Latinx)
    across 15 cities, and 78% of those graduates were placed in jobs within 180 days.

    In one year, Generation will serve 150 individuals in Jacksonville, FL.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     In one year, Generation will serve 150 individuals in Jacksonville, FL. 85% will graduate successfully from the training
    program, 75% will reach full-time employment 180 days post graduation. We will also track graduate income pre to post
    cohort and anticipate an average 3.5X wage growth."
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Return of unspent state funds to the state.
