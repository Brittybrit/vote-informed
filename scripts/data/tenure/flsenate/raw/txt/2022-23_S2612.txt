12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Legacy Sewer System Repair and Replacement project has the objective of reducing the amount of stormwater and
    groundwater that enters into the City’s sanitary sewer system. It is anticipated that by implementing this program the
    occurrence of the sanitary sewer overflows, which adversely affect the public and public health, will be reduced.
    Additionally, the project achieves the goal of protecting an impaired water body (Coral Gables Canal – WBID 3292) from
    contamination related to sanitary sewer. The project proposes to continue with the rehabilitation of deteriorated sanitary
    sewer system lines within the City which have an average of over 50 years and thereby decrease nutrient loading into
    water bodies. It will also assist to reducing the City's utility capital, operational, and maintenance costs.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The City's contractor will perform point repairs and cured-in-place lining of the City's gravity sewer lines that will
    rehabilitate the line and prevent stormwater and groundwater from entering the system. The improvements will also
    prevent effluent flows from escaping the line and impacting groundwater and adjacent water body quality. The contractor
    will also be performing manhole rehabilitation, frame and cover replacement, and existing liner repair and replacement.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     It is anticipated that by continuing with this program, the occurrence of the sanitary sewer overflows, which adversely
    affect the public and public health, will be reduced. Additionally, the project achieves the goal of protecting groundwater
    and adjacent water bodies from contamination related to the sanitary sewer. Furthermore, this form of rehabilitation will
    reduce the impact to the traveling public by greatly reducing the need for conventional excavations in roadways to expose
    and repair the sewer lines.

     The continued implementation of this project will benefit residents, business owners, and visitors to the City by the
    reduced likelihood of exposure to contaminated waters. The project will also reduce the City's utility capital, operational,
    and maintenance costs which will allow the City to redirect funding to further improve the resilience and efficiency of the
    sanitary sewer system. The implementation of this project will also benefit Miami-Dade County as a whole by reducing the
    likelihood of effluent flows escaping the line and impacting groundwater, adjacent water bodies, and canals that ultimately
    discharge in Biscayne Bay.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The anticipated benefits are a reduction in the infiltration of stormwater and groundwater in the sanitary sewer system,
    less frequent occurrences of the sanitary sewer overflows, and reduced impacts to the quality of groundwater and
    adjacent water bodies.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The proposed method of rehabilitating the sewer lines has been successfully implemented by the City in the past and the
    project carries a very low risk of failure. The survey performed by the City has identified the segments of sewer line in
    need of rehabilitation and the estimated quantities will be accounted for in the project's budget. The contract will include
    liquidated damages and the requirement of bid and performance bonds that will help ensure the success of the project.
