1. Project Title          Florida Trade Assistance Center Export Database Miami-Dade

2. Senate Sponsor         Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request        01/23/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The funds will be used to create a database of all companies in Florida who export their goods and/or services overseas.
   The database will allow the Florida Trade Assistance Center to match those Florida companies with companies
   participating in inbound trade missions to sell their goods and/or services to the inbound trade mission companies. This will
   lead to increased sales, revenue, and potential new jobs created by the Florida companies.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The proposed database will allow the Florida Trade Assistance Center to match those Florida companies with companies
    participating in inbound trade missions to sell their goods and/or services to the inbound trade mission companies. This
    will lead to increased sales, revenue, and potential new jobs created by the Florida companies.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funds will be used to create a database of all companies in Florida who export their goods and/or services overseas,
    creating a digital central repository of information for accessing what goods and services are available to inbound trade
    missions from Florida based businesses.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Although direct services to citizens is not an objective of this project, the requested funds are for the purpose of creating
    a centralized database to provide companies with inbound trade missions to easily and readily match Florida based
    companies with the appropriate service/goods needs which can increase revenues and provide job creation for all
    Floridians.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Businesses in all sixty-seven counties are the target population for this project request. Creation of the company
    database will lead to increased sales, revenue, and potential new jobs created by the Florida companies.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

    Increased sales, revenue, and new jobs created by Florida companies who export overseas. Exporting companies who
    make sales report those sales, revenue, and new jobs to the Florida Trade Assistance Center.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Funds can be withheld until database is completed. Standard contract penalties are sufficient.
