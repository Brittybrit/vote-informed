2. Senate Sponsor        Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request       12/19/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Due to environmental and ROW constraints the Halls River Road Multi-use Path (MUP) project typically consists of a 6 to
   12 foot wide asphalt MUP separate from Halls River Road beginning at South Riverview Circle and ending at US 19 in
   Citrus County, Florida. This 3.2 miles of roadway is currently a two lane section with flush shoulders on each side within
   100-120 feet of right-of-way. The path would connect multiple residential communities to restaurants, various businesses,
   and shopping plaza’s along the route; as well as, connect to the newly constructed MUP along US19 which will run nearly
   the entire length of Citrus County once completed by the FDOT.

   The County has already contributed $1M towards phase 1 of the project; and will contribute another $1M towards phase 2
   of the project. The County would request that phase 2 of the project continue to be managed and implemented by the
   FDOT.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Construction and Construction Administration/Inspection & Testing (CA/CEI/Testing). The 3.2 mile path would connect
    multiple residential communities to restaurants, various businesses, and shopping plaza’s along the route; as well as,
    connect to the newly constructed MUP along US19 which will run nearly the entire length of Citrus County once
    completed by the FDOT.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Construction and Construction Administration/Inspection & Testing (CA/CEI/Testing).
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Construction of a 10 to 12 foot wide asphalt Multi-Use Path.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Approximately 160,000 residents of Citrus County and our visitors and tourists.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Construction of the project will include a 6 to 12 foot wide asphalt multi-use path separate from the roadway. The noted
    improvements along Halls River Rd will increase public health, help protect bike/ped users from the hazards of roadway
    traffic, improve environmental education, tourism, development potential and access to local shopping. When
    constructed, this 3.2 mile path will connect multiple residential communities to restaurants, various businesses, and
    shopping plaza’s along the route; as well as, connect to the newly constructed MUP along US19 which will run nearly the
    entire length of Citrus County once completed by the FDOT.

    The outcome may be measured by evaluating the roadway and bike/ped capacity, vulnerable users crash data and traffic
    volumes both pre and post construction of the path.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Multi-use path will not be constructed, capacity, safety, tourism, education and improved development potential will not
    be addressed.
