4. Project/Program Description
    Funding will be used to purchase communications equipment for the City of Homestead’s Mobile Command Center (MCC).
   This request is for equipment and technology upgrades which will allow the City to communicate more effectively locally
   and regionally during times of crisis, and most importantly, provide the much-needed infrastructure and apparatus, allowing
   for interoperability communications. The equipment the City needs for the MCC includes: Microwave video receivers, Bgan
   Mobile satellite terminal, tactical cameras with Microwave link to set up remote situational awareness back to C.P. and
   ruggedized fiber optic cable. In order to deploy this technology in areas ravaged by disaster, we also intend to use funding
   for Base-X shelter systems with portable air conditioners to self-support our responding personnel who staff the Mobile
   Command Center vehicle in virtually any condition or location.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The equipment the City needs for the MCC includes: Microwave video receivers, Bgan Mobile satellite terminal, tactical
    cameras with Microwave link to set up remote situational awareness back to C.P. and ruggedized fiber optic cable.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Task force operations in investigating human trafficking, support community crime prevention efforts, youth drug
    awareness, D.U.I. checkpoints, “Mini Station” to coordinate the evacuation of the Florida Keys, support critical incidents
    such as homicide scenes, hostage negotiation, large crime scenes, and assist other agencies, hurricane preparation and
    awareness training, etc.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Task force operations in investigating human trafficking, support community crime prevention efforts, youth drug

    such as homicide scenes, hostage negotiation, large crime scenes, and assist other agencies, hurricane preparation and

     The target population is the residents and visitors of the City of Homestead who are (a) impacted by a critical incidents in
    which the mobile command post would be utilized as a mobile police operational station, (b) residents/visitors who attend
    community events in which the command post will be utilized for public crime prevention information sharing. (c)
    residents/visitors who are required to evacuate their community due to the threat of a hurricane including outlying cities,
    the command post will act as the mobile police station to coordinate such evacuations with other agencies
    (Federal/State). The command post will also be "ground zero" for police operations in the event of a large crime scene
    such as a mass shooting, hostage situation or any situation in which the command center will act as the staging area for
    law enforcement.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The ability for law enforcement operations to be conducted from within a mobile command post that will act as the
    staging area for the immediate response of law enforcement to a developing critical situation. The mobile command
    center will be the base for the coordination of Federal/State agencies that may be assisting in a critical response. The
    mobile command center will also be utilized to provide educational material/information to residents and visitors during
    community events from within the City. The information that will be disseminated includes crime prevention information,
    hurricane preparedness, firearm safety, bike safety, pedestrian safety, etc. The use of the command post during
    community events also provides the opportunity for residents to actually see the enhanced capabilities of the police
    department due to Congressional support. The command post will act as the mobile police station to coordinate such
    evacuations and disaster response with other agencies.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The penalties for non-deliverables or performance would be the withholding of funds.
