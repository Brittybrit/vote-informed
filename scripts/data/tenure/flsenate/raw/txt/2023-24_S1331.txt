1. Project Title          Palmetto Bay Veterans Park Development - Phase 1

2. Senate Sponsor         Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request        02/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    This new Village park is a blank canvas. The Village would like to begin its transformation to an honoring location for the
   men and women Veterans who have served our Country in any military branch. Phase 1 includes the construction of
   parking spaces, walking path, trees and sod to begin the erection of another Village park in Miami-Dade County for the
   public to enjoy.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The specific purpose to be achieved by the requested funds is the development of a Veteran's dedicated Park for any
    and all to enjoy in Miami-Dade County.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The new park will be used for events honoring our Veterans and public servants. It will be open to everyone and will be
    used as another gathering location for the community. This will be an educational site of historic value to publicly honor
    and recognize those who have served.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     There will not be direct services provided to citizens, but this will be another park for enjoying the outdoors. The park is
    located near South Dixie Highway, walking distance from local businesses and is at the edge of a residential community
    where many will likely take advantage to walk in shade and comfort, with the ability to enjoy nature.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     There is no specific targeted population, but all Miami-Dade County residents, workers and visitors are always welcomed
    to enjoy Veteran's Park. We estimate 300+ visitors daily as the park is located near South Dixie Highway, businesses and
    is located within a residential neighborhood.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcome of this project is to start the development of this new public space of Veterans Park to honor and
    remember those who have served our Country and additionally benefit the community at large. This outcome will be
    measured by the observation of an increased presence of patrons at this site, increased social media posts, tags and
    mentions will also help us monitor the parks success.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Withholding of funds is a fair penalty for failing to meet deliverables.
