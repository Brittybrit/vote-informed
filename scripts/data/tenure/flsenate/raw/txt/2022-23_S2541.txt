1. Project Title          Coral Gables Public Works Incinerator Site Remediation

2. Senate Sponsor        Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request       09/29/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    This project is an environmental remediation of a City Facility where an incinerator building, combustion chambers, and an
   incinerator stack were located. Structures were demolished in 2005. However, the site was never remediated
   environmentally. Environmental remediation construction/ activities will allow the city to utilize a site that is currently
   unusable. Among existing contaminants found in groundwater analysis are lead, dioxin, and dibenzifurans. By providing an
   environmentally safe facility, workers will be exercising duties in a clean air environment and groundwater contamination
   will be halted and the City will be able to more effectively provide services and utilize its existing space.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funding will be used for environmental remediation of a City facility where an incinerator building, combustion chambers,
    and an incinerator stack were located. Structures were demolished in 2005. However, site was never remediated
    environmentally. Among existing contaminants found in groundwater analysis are lead, dioxin, and dibenzifurans. By
    providing an environmentally safe facility, workers will be exercising duties in a clean air environment and groundwater
    contamination will be halted.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Environmental remediation construction/ activities will allow the city to utilize a site that is currently unusable. The site will
    facilitate storage of essential equipment that is required during major events and emergency operations - directly
    benefiting the citizens of Coral Gables.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    The remediation of the site will hold supporting facilities for the City's Fire, Police, Landscaping and Public Works
    Department which are instrumental to citizen's safety and quality of life.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The remediation of this site will service the community as a whole.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The benefit or outcome of this project will improve the physical health of city workers, who will be exercising their duties
    in an environmentally safe facility. The outcome will be measured by monitoring air quality and periodic groundwater
    laboratory analysis. This project will also benefit groundwater and surface water quality, by removing contaminated
    material and being disposed of. The city will install groundwater monitoring wells by the perimeter of the contaminated
    site, to sample groundwater and perform periodic laboratory analysis which will determine if contamination reaches above
    allowable limits.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Withholding of reimbursement should city fail to complete remediation.
