12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Continuation of capital improvements previously funded by the State of Florida to include:
    Institutional Kitchen
    Fire Safety/Sprinkler Installation
    Signage
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    The research conducted on the property is culturally-significant in educating researchers, students, and the public.
    Research will include effects of sea-level rise, climate change, and resiliency.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Visitors to the property are exposed to the varied Florida history within its boundaries as well as the 450-acre Deering
    Estate. The property provides unique opportunities for the public and researchers to interact with the natural environment
    with endangered species as well as research projects.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    University/College students
    General (the majority of funds will benefit no specific group)
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Upgrading the building will increase the number of researchers worldwide who can study the property's unique qualities.
    A higher number of visitors will raise the number of persons employed by the Estate. The outcomes will be measured
    based on the number of visitors to the property, number of K-12 learning programs attendees, research work underway
    and completed, number of "live-in" researchers, and number of papers published.
