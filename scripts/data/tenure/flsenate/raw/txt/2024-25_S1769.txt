4. Project/Program Description
    CABA Pro Bono Condominium and HOA Foreclosure Defense Pilot Project will provide free legal services and
   representation to low income residents who reside in condominiums or within homeowners associations that are facing
   foreclosure and do not have funds available to hire an attorney to provide them with legal advice and/or legal
   representation. This appropriation is separate and distinct from another appropriations request requested by Senator
   Rodriguez in the amount of $500,000 as this is unique to this particular group of individuals and families that reside within
   these communities.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     These funds will be used to hire an attorney and paralegal to provide legal counsel, services and representation to low
    income homeowners who reside in a condominium or HOA and are facing the risk of foreclosure.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Through this Project we will conduct outreach to identify homeowners in need of free legal representation and provide
    them with either legal counsel, help with drafting and submitting pro se pleadings and motions, or full legal representation.
    We will also hold office hours regularly at the Courthouse to best advise individuals showing up to their foreclosure
    hearings unrepresented and in need of legal services.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Legal Counsel and Advice, Legal services and assistance with drafting pro se motions and pleadings and legal
    representation.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population for this Project are low income households that reside in condominium or HOA communities and
    are unjustly having their properties foreclosed. We will also mainly focus on elderly members of our community that are
    being targeted and becoming victims of wrongdoing by condo/homeowners' associations. We expect to serve between
    100-250 clients during the first year of this Project. This appropriation is separate and distinct from another appropriation
    being sponsored by Senator Rodriguez in the amount of $500,000.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This Project will provide legal representation to members of our community that are currently having their properties
    foreclosed on and cannot afford legal representation. Through our Project and by providing vigorous legal representation
    to those in need we will be able to save the homes of many homeowners that are currently having their properties
    foreclosed unjustly and thus keep vulnerable members of our community in their homes. This will be achieved by
    marketing efforts and outreach to inform the community about our Project and the free legal services that we offer to
    homeowners.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     If the deliverables listed in the contract are not achieved, funds from the contract should be deducted accordingly.
