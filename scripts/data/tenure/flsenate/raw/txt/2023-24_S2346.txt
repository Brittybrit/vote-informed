1. Project Title          Sumter County - Lake Panasoffkee Community Shelter

2. Senate Sponsor        Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request       02/28/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    This project will build a hardened shelter 3.2 miles East of the I-75/C-470 interchange to support evacuees of hurricanes
   and the immediate local area of unincorporated Lake Panasoffkee and Sumterville and the City of Coleman. As
   demonstrated with prior hurricane evacuations from South Florida, many evacuees end up staying in a Sumter County
   shelter. Additionally, the housing in these areas have an abundance of mobile homes and RVs and disadvantaged
   populations that also need shelter during major wind events. This shelter would also serve as a special needs shelter
   during events. The secondary use of the facility outside of emergencies will be for emergency services training purposes.

     This project will build a hardened shelter 3.2 miles East of the I-75/C-470 interchange to support evacuees of hurricanes
    and the immediate local area of unincorporated Lake Panasoffkee and Sumterville and the City of Coleman. As
    demonstrated with prior hurricane evacuations from South Florida, many evacuees end up staying in a Sumter County
    shelter. Additionally, the housing in these areas have an abundance of mobile homes and RVs that also need shelter
    during major wind events. The secondary use of the facility outside of emergencies will be for emergency services
    training purposes.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funding will be used to construct a hardened shelter 3.2 miles East of the I-75/C-470 interchange for evacuees and also
    serve as Emergency Services Training Center outside of emergencies. All site utilities and preparation will be provided by
    Sumter County as the funds are for the facility only.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This facility would be an evacuation shelter for the vulnerable residents of Lake Panasoffkee, Coleman, Sumterville, and
    surrounding counties. Mobile homes/RVs in the area necessitate a hardened shelter to protect the special needs
    population and the general public during events. With its close proximity to the I-75/C-470 interchange, it can provide
    refuge of last resort to evacuees traveling on I-75. It will also allow for a building designed to specifically address general
    population and special needs shelter requirements.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Sumter County's population is unique in that it is comprised of 58.2% persons that are 65 years and over. This high
    percentage results in an increased demand of a special needs shelter during hurricane events. The nearby City of
    Coleman's disadvantaged population, which is low income and comprised of minorities, would greatly benefit with
    accessibility of a nearby shelter. The overall target population served is more than 800 residents and evacuees (including
    special needs individuals).
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     A hardened shelter near the I-75/C-470 interchange to support and protect evacuees of hurricanes from Sumter County,
    other adjoining counties, and the local area around Lake Panasoffkee, Coleman, and Sumterville. Because housing in
    this area has an abundance of mobile homes and RVs, a hardened shelter is necessary to protect the special needs
    population and the general public during major wind events. To measure the level of benefit or outcome, the number of
    evacuees per event that utilize the shelter and the data that is collected from emergency services training throughout the
    year.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Standard contract penalties are sufficient.
