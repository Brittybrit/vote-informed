1. Project Title          Miami-Dade Roadway Resurfacing Project - SW 160 Street

2. Senate Sponsor         Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request        12/05/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The funding will be used for milling and resurfacing the segment of SW 160 Street (Colonial Drive) from US-1 to SW 112
   Avenue. The scope of the work includes milling, resurfacing, reconstruction of areas where required, adjustment of valve
   boxes and manholes, upgrading or construction of ADA ramps and connectors as well as pavement markings and signage
   and all necessary incidental work to extend the structural integrity of these features. The project is a valuable use of
   taxpayer funds because it will improve the roadway surface and driving conditions as well as provide an increased reliability
   and safety to the corridor. Additionally, the project will mitigate recurring pavement maintenance, thus reducing
   maintenance costs. It will also improve traffic flow.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The project's goal is to improve the roadway surface and driving conditions, providing increased reliability and safety
    along the corridor. Resurfacing activities are critical to extending the service life of roadways and improving traffic flow.
    Additionally, the project will mitigate recurring pavement maintenance which takes place frequently due to the poor state
    of the asphalt.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The project includes, but is not limited to, the furnishing of all labor, required materials, tools, equipment, machinery,
    expertise, services, and all else necessary for the milling, resurfacing, reconstruction of areas where required, adjustment
    of valve boxes and manholes, upgrading or construction of ADA ramps and connectors, as well as pavement markings
    and signage and all necessary incidental work to extend the structural integrity of these features.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The project will provide much needed improvements to a roadway that serves as one of the area’s main corridors,
    connecting residents, the cyclist community, workers, tourists, schools, and businesses.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The execution of this project will improve the roadway surface and driving conditions, as well as provide an increased
    reliability and safety to all of the emergency vehicles, workers, constituents and tourists of Miami-Dade County. It is a
    highly traveled area that serves as a local collector for traffic for the County. The project will benefit the Colonial/Fairway
    Estates Civic Association. Miami-Dade County has received multiple complaints from the homeowners and the cyclists
    traversing the area. It also serves as emergency access routes during evacuations and other emergency purposes.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit is the enhanced rideability of SW 160 Street after the milling and resurfacing project. Numerous
    motorist and pedestrian safety related improvements will be implemented. Success of this project will be measured in
    terms of the longevity of the pavement and associated improvements and in terms of customer satisfaction by a
    community that has been longing for this project to correct the deteriorating pavement conditions on its most well known
    streets. Success will also be measured by the elimination of pavement maintenance which takes place frequently due to
    the poor state of the asphalt.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
