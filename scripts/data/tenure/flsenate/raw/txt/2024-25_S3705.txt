4. Project/Program Description

   Legislative Agenda and Priorities documents. It is equally important to the County's largest municipality and our boating
   and liveaboard community. Numerous public dialogues have taken place at the Board, municipal, and local levels, including
   with representatives of the State legislature and Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC).

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Boca Chica shoreside facility is essential for a functional mooring field, offering vital amenities such as shoreside
    access for mooring field occupants and waste disposal services. This facility will empower Monroe County to establish a
    well-regulated mooring field in an unmanaged anchorage, effectively addressing the issue of derelict vessels and
    associated risks. It is also instrumental in upholding water quality protection regulations and improving boater safety and
    access.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Boca Chica shoreside facility will provide a well-regulated mooring field with essential amenities. This will reduce
    negative environmental impacts
    caused by derelict vessels and illegal mooring, while enhancing water quality protection and improving recreational boater
    safety and access.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This funding will establish a safe and secure shoreside access site and waste disposal site for a mooring field that will
    benefit both recreational boaters
    and the liveaboard community. In the Keys, where affordable housing is scarce and a significant portion of the workforce
    is employed in the service sector, liveaboard vessels serve as a source of affordable housing.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The General population and Workforce members who rely on vessels as a source of affordable housing.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The shoreside facility will provide a location for proper waste disposal. Vessels on moorings are monitored for proper
    waste disposal, protecting water quality. The shoreside facility will support a new mooring field that will replace illegal,
    destructive anchoring practices, protecting the benthic environment, including valuable seagrass beds which are
    foundational to our marine ecosystem.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

    Funding award can be premised on completion of project. If project not completed then funding can be reduced or
    withheld.
