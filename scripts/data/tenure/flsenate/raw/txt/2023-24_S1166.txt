4. Project/Program Description
    It is the goal of the mentoring program to improve college readiness and retention rates and improve workforce readiness
   amongst marginalized Miami youth. The program design provides continuous opportunities for youth to engage in college
   preparatory and workforce readiness activities at a variety of sites throughout Miami-Dade County. OYC's College
   Readiness component offers effective year-round college preparation services which increase the number of youth
   enrolling in college, imparts knowledge of the college admissions and financial aid process and improves preparedness for
   post secondary success.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Overtown Youth Center College and Career Readiness program aims to increase student's knowledge of college
    and career options, providing jobs and internships and improving financial literacy. The goal is to develop youth which
    contributes to a thriving community with a vibrant workforce by ensuring each student is equipped with the tools to
    succeed, can take part in civic engagement and have the ability to impact their communities.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Services include expanding college and career readiness programming to serve youth starting in kindergarten through
    12th grade, homework assistance, ACT/SAT practice, career assessment, college preparation, financial literacy and job
    placement. The goal is to include more intensive career exploration and improve student achievement.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Coordinators provide continuous student support using individual case management to track progress towards post
    secondary goals. Services also include daily monitoring, mentoring, counseling and advocacy. Student report cards are
    collected directly from Miami-Dade County Public Schools to monitor the progress of the students in core academic
    content areas. An Individual Success Plan (ISP) is used for goal-setting and career planning 4 times per year. College
    and Career Readiness Workshops are provided on a weekly basis to engage high school students in their preparation for
    post-secondary success.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Funding serves 200 youth in grades K-12 who reside in marginalized communities throughout Miami-Dade County. Risk
    factors include poor performing schools, and under resourced communities which lack college and career readiness
    opportunities.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The Overtown Youth Center Mentoring/ Student Assistance Initiative - College and Career Readiness programs provides
    a comprehensive pathway to post-secondary success. Benefits measured improved quality of education, enriched
    cultural experiences, and improved physical health. Methods of measuring benefits and outcomes include in-school
    individual case management, counseling, advocacy, mentoring, report card reviews and pre, mid and post assessments
    to monitor student proficiency in Reading, Math and Science. Additionally, enrichment and enhancement classes,
    exposure field trips and college focused activities and agility and strength assessments.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Restitution of award funds.
