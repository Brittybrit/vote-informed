4. Project/Program Description
    Miami-Dade County's Vision Zero Strategic Plan set the goal to eliminate all fatal and serious injury crashes from our
   transportation network by 2040 & identified corridors and intersections with the highest numbers of severe crashes. NW 7th
   Street from NW 39th Ave. to NW 12th Ave. & the intersection of NW 12th Ave. & NW 11th Street were identified as part of
   the Top 100 high-injury crash locations. The Department of Transportation and Public Works (DTPW) Vision Zero team is
   identifying the appropriate safety countermeasures needed to address the safety issues. The goal is to fund the
   engineering design and construction of identified safety countermeasures to provide greater comfort for economic vitality
   and quality of life for residents and businesses. These improvements may include the installation of a pedestrian hybrid
   beacon, high-visibility crosswalks, and bike facilities, concrete bulb-outs to enhance commercial on-street parking, and
   traffic signals operation improvements.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to engineer, design and construct the identified safety countermeasures. These improvements may include
    the installation of a pedestrian hybrid beacon, high-visibility crosswalks, and bike facilities, concrete bulb-outs to enhance
    commercial on-street parking, and traffic signals operation improvements. The purpose is to provide safer roadway
    facilities and greater comfort to assist economic vitality and improve the quality of life for residents and businesses.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     All of the state appropriated funds would go towards the construction of the safety improvements. These improvements
    may include the installation of a pedestrian hybrid beacon, high visibility crosswalks, and bike facilities, concrete bulb-outs
    to enhance commercial on-street parking, and retro-reflective backplates at signals, and traffic signal operations
    improvements.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The construction of these infrastructure projects will provide area businesses and residents enhanced roadway safety
    and comfort. These safety projects can improve local economic vitality by changing the corridor from a place that people
    “drive-through” to one that they “drive-to.” With these improved facilities, the street will become a more attractive place to
    shop, eat, and play.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Residents and businesses along NW 7th Street from NW 12th Ave to 39th Ave and on NW 12th Ave to 11th Ave is the
    target population. There is more than 25,000 residents and 100 small businesses between the two areas. 22 percent of
    area residents are over the age of 65. LoanDepot Park Stadium (home of the Florida Marlins) is adjacent to the corridor,
    driving additional visitors and vehicles to area.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The direct benefits of this project include the reduction/elimination of fatal and severe crashes on the corridor and
    intersections. Addressing the safety issues will also improve economic vitality by changing the corridor from a place that
    people “drive-through” to one that they “drive-to.” Improving walking areas and providing safe crossings can make the
    street a more attractive area. With these improved facilities, a motorist is more likely to park, walk around, visit
    restaurants or shops, and enjoy the setting, benefiting the economy and public safety of the neighborhood.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Funds will not be provided until deliverables are met.
