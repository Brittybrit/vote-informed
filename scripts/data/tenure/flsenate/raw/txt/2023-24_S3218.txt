1. Project Title          Anti Violence Initiative: Second Chance Services for Returning Residents

2. Senate Sponsor         Alexis Calatayud

4. Project/Program Description
    Matching funds for the Miami-Dade County Community Violence Intervention Initiative to in part, provide support,
   resources, and interventions to residents returning from incarceration to the community in an effort to reduce recidivism,
   reduce community violence, and create safer neighborhoods. Funds will be added to an existing Request for Proposal
   process.

   In Spring and Summer of 2021, Miami-Dade County saw a sharp increase in gun violence, permeate throughout its
   communities, mirroring similar disturbing trends in major cities and counties throughout the country. In response to this
   epidemic, Miami-Dade County immediately implemented a multi-faceted response including the development of the Peace
   & Prosperity Plan and the deployment of Operation Community Shield, and the establishment of the Community Violence
   Intervention Initiative.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Miami- Dade County, through its Police Department and its Office of Neighborhood Safety, has continuously focused on
    implementing innovative and sustainable solutions to reduce community violence, including gun violence. In 2021, a
    collective strategy was developed and employed to address this crisis locally, including the Community Violence
    Intervention Initiative which, in part, provides support, resources, and interventions to residents returning from
    incarceration to the community in an effort to reduce recidivism, reduce community violence, and create safer
    neighborhoods.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This program brings together Miami-Dade County's police, corrections, and social services departments along with
    community partners to serve returning citizens. These funds will be utilized to provide reentry services (reintegration
    planning, housing, basic needs, employment, etc) to reduce recidivism and ensure community safety.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Community-based services for returning citizens including reintegration planning, family reunification, employment and
    job training and placement, vocational and educational training, housing and basic needs, substance abuse and mental
    health services.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Miami-Dade County residents returning from incarceration from local and state correctional institutions.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Residents returning into communities in the County from local state and federal correctional institutions will receive care
    planning that includes linkages to various support systems that will aid in their reintegration into the community. Funded
    community-based organizations will provide quarterly reports that minimally highlights the number of returning residents
    served, types of services provided, referrals made and outcomes of services/efforts.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Funds could be provided on a reimbursement basis after services are performed.
