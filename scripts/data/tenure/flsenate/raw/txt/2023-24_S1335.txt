4. Project/Program Description

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Chabad of Kendall/Pinecrest offers religious services and outreach, schooling, community services and operates the
    Friendship Circle- a program which provides one-of-a-kind, inclusive social programs for children and adults with special
    needs. Our fortification and expansion will ensure the Jewish community has a safe place to observe religious beliefs.
    Synagogues have been under random attack in Miami. We will also grow the number of individuals with disabilities
    served from 450 to 2,000.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Our temple will be made safer with professional security enhancements through upgraded fencing, higher walls, shatter
    resistant doors, alarms and cameras with an upgraded security booth. The entrance will face the interior of the complex
    so there is no egress or ingress from main roads.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     There will be no outside visibility to the public so all activities will only be seen from the inside corridor area. This will
    allow our children safer areas to play in, and congregants safer areas to pray in, without public visibility or access without
    security.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The Jewish community at large and those with children who have disabilities. We expect to serve almost 2000 people.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Increased safety and security for the Jewish community participating in worship and social gatherings who are the
    targets of recent anti-semitic acts. This will also protect children and adults with special needs who are often targeted by
    bullying and other harmful behavior. We will measure that by recording the number of incidents or threats against the
    Chabad, and, also separately, Jewish people living in South Miami, Dade, one year after completion of the new campus
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     They may require us to return all funds.
