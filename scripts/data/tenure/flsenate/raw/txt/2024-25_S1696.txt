4. Project/Program Description
    Miami-Dade Fire Rescue's (MDFR) goal is to provide first responders with 130 Electric Vehicle (EV) plugs and 130 fire
   blankets to effectively extinguish fires involving these vehicles caused by lithium-based power sources and for vehicle
   deactivation to safely allow extrications. A single EV fire can spread quickly and this equipment will deactivate the vehicle,
   contain the fire and control re-ignitions, allowing fire crews to move unaffected vehicles nearby.

     Miami-Dade Fire Rescue's (MDFR) goal is to provide first responders with Electric Vehicle (EV) plugs and fire blankets to
    effectively extinguish fires involving these vehicles. A single EV fire can spread quickly and this equipment will deactivate
    the vehicle, contain the fire and control re-ignitions, allowing fire crews to move unaffected vehicles nearby.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    This project's purpose is to provide MDFR with EV fire suppression equipment. The EV Plug is the fastest and safest
    method currently available to deactivate an EV during the stabilization portion on an extrication operation. Fire blankets
    will allow first responders to create a thermal barrier where the lithium batteries can safely burn and consume themselves
    without allowing EV fires to spread.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens of Miami-Dade County will benefit from this project as electric vehicles and technological advances often come
    with new risks to public safety. A single EV fire can quickly engulf other items. Fire blankets will contain fires preventing
    spread to other vehicles and nearby properties while decreasing the exposure to carcinogens produced by EV fires.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This Project will serve all communities of Miami-Dade County and the region. More than 2.7 Million residents will be
    served by this project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Emergencies involving EVs and other lithium powered systems can lead to significant loss of life and property. EVs are
    often activated by phone apps, making it difficult for first responders to disable them during extrication operations. Without
    deactivating these vehicles, extrication of injured drivers will be delayed. MDFR can measure the amount of EV fires
    exterminated by the use of EV plugs and blankets. This will allow first responders to extract people from vehicles and
    prevent further damage to other vehicles and property near the emergency scene.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
