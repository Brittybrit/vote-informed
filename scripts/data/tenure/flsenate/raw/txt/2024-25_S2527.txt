3. Date of Request       12/18/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Continued education and training are vital to the services provided for public health and safety. Hernando County Public
   Safety Departments (Departments) do not have adequate training facilities available and must travel out of the County for
   training needs. Since 2012, the Departments have been working on identifying appropriate land and funding to construct
   facilities to accommodate the ongoing training needs of their staff. While numerous training facilities are in other areas of
   the State it means our public servants are not available to respond timely or effectively due to the distances away from
   Hernando County. It also requires cooperative and sometimes complex scheduling. A suitable facility should be constructed
   to accommodate continued training requirements as well as provide new fire rescue and law enforcement candidates a
   proper environment and facilities to learn and hone their skills. Hernando County has 60 acres of land earmarked for a
   public safety training facility.

     The training center will be utilized for fire, emergency medical services and law enforcement for training of their
    personnel to prepare them for their jobs of protecting and caring for the citizens of Hernando County (County). Having the
    training center within the County alleviates the necessity to take personnel out of the county to train. This leaves them
    available, if needed, to respond to calls.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funds will be used for design, engineering and construction of the shared facility by Fire, EMS and law enforcement.
    By designing a shared compound with fire, emergency medical services, and law enforcement will be beneficial and cost
    effective by sharing the facility.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The funds will be used for design, engineering and construction of the shared facility by Fire, EMS and law enforcement.
    By designing a shared compound with fire, emergency medical services, and law enforcement will be beneficial and cost
    effective by sharing the facility.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The entire Hernando County population of 201,037. Anyone within the County who calls on fire, EMS or law enforcement
    will benefit from the skills of personnel from all agencies.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Funding will provide for the construction of the facility which in turn allows for training to be accomplished in County.
    Training will be measured by the success ooof law enforcement, firefighters and paramedics in the field while responding
    to the citizens.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The County will reimburse State Funding for failure to meet the deliverables.
