4. Project/Program Description
    The funds will be used for the construction of the Ludlam Trail. The Ludlam Trail is a 5.6-mile greenway corridor extending
   from SW 80th St to NW 7th St (between 69-70 Avenues) in Miami-Dade County. It will include dedicated pedestrian and
   bicycle pathways, shade trees, landscaping, and site furniture. It will be used for non-motorized active transportation to
   work, school, and play and connects to 5 greenways, 4 parks, 2 transit hubs, and 3 schools.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funding requested would be allocated toward the construction of the Ludlam Trail, a shared use path for non-
    motorized transportation and recreational uses. The Ludlam Trail will consist of dedicated pathways for bicyclists and
    pedestrians and includes six pedestrian bridges over roadways and canals to provide for safe crossings and connectivity.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Construction of the Ludlam Trail will provide opportunities for residents and visitors to Miami-Dade County with options
    for non-motorized transportation and connectivity to residential areas, parks, schools, transit hubs, and economic activity
    areas. Additionally, the trail will provide opportunities for recreation, increased health and wellness, and social activities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This project will improve safety for bicyclists and pedestrians along 5.6 continuous miles to schools, parks, businesses,
    and shopping. It will connect more than 40,000 people in a half-mile walkable service area to 3 greenways, 5 schools, 4
    parks, and 2 transit hubs. It will be a catalyst to increase civic participation and unite the social fabric of the community
    creating a better quality of life for all residents.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The project will serve Miami-Dade County residents and visitors of all ages and abilities. This project will improve safety
    for bicyclists, pedestrians, and joggers along 5.6 continuous miles to schools, parks, businesses, and shopping. It will
    connect more than 40,000 people living within a half-mile walkable service area of the trail to 3 greenways, 5 schools, 4
    parks, and 2 transit hubs.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
