4. Project/Program Description
    Funds will provide for the construction of a fire station serving the residents and visitors of Coral Gables. The fire station
   will enhance response times for fire rescue services to an area of the city historically challenged by traffic congestion and
   lack of access to the communities along its eastern seaboard and the University of Miami. The strategic location of the fire
   station reduces the gap between fire stations from 5 miles to 2 miles, placing a fire rescue station within a 2-mile radius
   throughout the City. The location's high elevation will serve as the primary fire station south of the Gables waterway during
   hurricanes where other fire stations must evacuate due to storm surge. The strategic location reduces response times and
   enhances access to areas of the City where major thoroughfares are limited and access to major seaside communities is
   restricted. The fire station is within a block from a major elderly nursing and rehabilitation center.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will provide for the construction of a fire station serving residents and visitors of Coral Gables. The fire station will
    house a fire engine and an ambulance but from an architectural standpoint it will not disrupt the mostly residential
    character of the neighborhood. The station will also be buffered by a verdant park to further integrate and connect the
    station to the surrounding community. The addition of this facility will enhance response times for fire rescue services to
    an area of the city historically challenged by traffic congestion and lack of access to the communities along its eastern
    seaboard and the University of Miami.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Construction services.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The construction of a new fire station will directly result in enhanced fire and emergency services for the residents and
    visitors of the City of Coral Gables. Through established mutual aid agreements, the fire station will serve all residents in
    southern Miami Dade County. The fire station will have a direct impact to the residents residing along the east coast of
    the city, a high-rise nursing home and the University.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The southern areas of the City of Coral Gables will be serviced by Fire Station 4.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Reduced response times for emergency services and improved resiliency post storm or natural disaster event.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Liquidated damages clause or not reimbursement of grant funds.
