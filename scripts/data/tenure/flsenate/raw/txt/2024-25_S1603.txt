14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will support a theatre project with the mission to educate young people about the evils of communism through art
    and storytelling. The fund will provide the ability for high school and college students to come to the show for free.
    Ensuring that every young person, irrespective of their socio-economic background, can experience this pivotal tale.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The audience will first walk through a museum exhibit that highlights how Communism ruined the once flourishing
    country of Cuba as well as the resilience of the Cuban exile community in the United States. After the exhibit, the
    audience will experience the 90 minute immersive theatre play.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This project will educate the younger generation on the evils and dangers of communism through the power of
    storytelling and immersive theatre.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is high school and university/college students. More than 500 individuals are expected to be
    served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     It will help the younger generation better understand the danger in political ideology, specifically communism, as well as
    offer a true account on how communism destroyed the family nucleus and in turn devastated an entire country. Through
    the power of storytelling the play can help ensure that history does not repeat itself.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in forfeiture of funds.
