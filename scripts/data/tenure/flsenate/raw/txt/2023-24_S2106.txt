   filtering the water and improving known poor water quality. Additionally, to expand the current beach with 3600 cubic yards

     Installing new technology, a 850-foot living seawall that includes crevices for small natural organisms to live, thereby
    filtering the water and improving known poor water quality. Expanding the current beach with 3600 cubic yards of sand to
    meet the new seawall thereby giving citizens a larger area at which to recreate.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Installing new technology, a 850-foot living seawall that includes crevices for small natural organisms to live, thereby
    filtering the water and improving known poor water quality. Expanding the current beach with 3600 cubic yards of sand to
    meet the new seawall thereby giving citizens a larger area at which to recreate.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All residents of and visitors to Pasco County could enjoy better water quality and expanded beach. >800 people.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Better water quality, fewer beach days closed, more beach users. Measured water quality parameters and periodic
    beach user counts.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     If Pasco County does not build the living seawall and expand the beach, the State of Florida will not pay Pasco County
    $2,000,000.
