12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal of the master plan is to assess the City's current stormwater system, parts of which have been in existence for
    nearly 100 years, and address current flooding problems, and/or areas where future challenges may occur. The plan will
    recommend design standards for new developments, transportation, water quality, floodplain mitigation, swales,
    inadequate structures, etc.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The plan will allow the City to plan, construct, proactively maintain and improve its stormwater infrastructure which
    directly benefits the citizens in and around the city.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Improved stormwater management including reduced flooding.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Our target population served by this project will be our community as a whole, as this project will address drainage and
    reducing flooding, etc. Individuals in, surrounding and passing Coral Gables will be served by this project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project expected benefit/outcome of this project will improve transportation conditions by providing roads free of
    flooding during or after rain events, it will facilitate citizens mobility and it will improve safety, we will measure this
    outcome by conducting visual inspections and record keeping. This project will also improve stormwater management,
    groundwater, drinking water and surface water quality, we will measure these outcomes by collecting field data, sampling
    the water and having said samples analyzed by a laboratory.
