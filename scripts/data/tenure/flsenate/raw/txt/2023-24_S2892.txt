4. Project/Program Description
    Mid Florida Community Services, Inc. Children's Advocacy Center of Hernando County (CAC) coordinates the
   multidisciplinary investigative process of child abuse cases with DCF, law enforcement and the child protection team. It is a
   child-friendly facility that responds to children who are child victims of sexual abuse, physical abuse, threatened harm,
   drug-endangerment, mental injury, neglect, and witnesses of domestic violence. Additionally, the CAC has a trauma
   treatment program for child victims of sexual abuse. Last year the CAC served more than 860 children and families, of
   which 304 were children in trauma therapy. Over the last two years, the CAC had to rent additional satellite office space
   causing services to be provided at multiple locations because the main building is no longer big enough to meet the needs
   of the child victims that we serve. It is best practice to provide all the services under one building, in one location.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will be used to purchase land and construct a 7,000 square foot building to provide a child friendly safe
    environment where children who are victims of child abuse can disclose their abuse and begin healing from the trauma
    they endured. Currently therapists are housed at different locations and sometimes if there are several children involved,
    the families have to drive to different locations to receive services for each child. Housing the entire staff at the Children's
    Advocacy Center is best for the children and their families.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     On average, the CAC provides services to over 800 children who are victims of child abuse and their family members.
    Services provided at the CAC include forensic interviews, specialized interviews, social assessments, forensic medical
    exams, multidisciplinary team staffing, advocacy, deposition and court accompaniment, case management, as well as
    individual and family therapy for child victims and their non-offending family members. The CAC also provides therapeutic
    treatment for adults who were sexually abused as children. Law enforcement and DCF observe the forensic interviews
    that take place at the CAC. The child protection team is co-located at the CAC who perform the interviews and medicals.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Services provided at the CAC include forensic interviews, specialized interviews, social assessments, forensic medical
    exams, multidisciplinary team staffing, advocacy, deposition and court accompaniment, case management, as well as
    individual and family therapy for child victims and their non-offending family members. The CAC also provides therapeutic
    treatment for adults who were sexually abused as children. Law enforcement and DCF observe the forensic interviews
    that take place at the CAC.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Children ages 0-17 who are victims of physical abuse, sexual abuse, neglect, human trafficking, mental injury, drug
    endangered, who witness domestic violence, survivors of homicide as well as adults who were sexually abused as
    children are eligible for services. Non-offending family members such as siblings, parents, or grandparents are also
    eligible for services. The CAC expects to serve over 800 children and family members this current fiscal year and expect
    that figure to grow.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit is to enhance child abuse investigations and increase prosecution rates with child abuse cases,
    protecting children from harm; improving their physical and mental health, reducing substance abuse; reducing
    recidivism. The CAC provides a multidiscipline-coordinated investigative response with DCF, law enforcement, child
    protection team and the state attorney where the outcome of the interview and medical are discussed, referrals for
    therapy made, and collectively discussing what needs the family has. Pre and post assessments are completed with
    therapy and evidence-based trauma therapy is utilized. 90% of children served will see a reduction in trauma. Cases are
    tracked to see if additional abuse reports have been called in. Criminal cases are tracked. Victims of sexual abuse and
    human trafficking often turn to substances and the CAC tracks this information during the pre and post assessments and
    provides information on substance misuse.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in a return of funds to administering agency.
