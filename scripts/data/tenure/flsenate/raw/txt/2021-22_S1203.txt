4. Project/Program Description
    From the unexpended balance of funds appropriated in Specific Appropriation 23 of Chapter 2018-9, Laws of Florida, for
   FKCC - Key West Collegiate Academy Classroom Facility & Storm Shelter for $5,000,000, $4,500,000 is requested to
   revert immediately and be re-appropriated to the College of the Florida Keys (CFK) for the CFK Academy Classroom
   Facility and EOC.
   The CFK Academy project will consist of a new building that will provide improved facility for the growth of Florida Keys
   charter high school. The school will allow for students to earn an AA degree for free alongside their high school diploma.
   On average, the school projects 5 AA degrees per year over the next 5 years. In addition, students will earn Microsoft
   Office Specialist and Adobe industry certifications. The building will house a hardened space (200MPH wind/FEMA flood
   rated) that will serve as an Emergency Operations Center during & in the recovery period following severe weather or other
   catastrophes.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will be used to construct the CFK Academy charter school classroom facility.
    The school will allow for students to earn an AA degree for free alongside their high school diploma. On average, the
    school projects 5 AA degrees per year over the next 5 years. In addition, students will earn Microsoft Office Specialist and
    Adobe industry certifications. The building will house a hardened space (200MPH wind/FEMA flood rated) that will serve
    as an Emergency Operations Center during & in the recovery period following severe weather or other catastrophes.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The CFK Academy Classroom Facility will offer Dual Enrollment programs, allowing high school students to earn a free
    AA degree alongside their high school diploma in partnership with a charter school.
    The EOC/storm shelter facility will serve as a hardened location for rescue, recovery, and staging during emergency
    situations.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Expanded educational opportunities for Monroe County School District students and a facility for use by emergency
    management personnel.
