12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The program will provide much-needed support for Youth at Risk to include soft skills training, tutoring, staying in school
    incentives, provide information regarding college or trade school, provide school supplies, provide access to computers
    and internet if necessary. The program shall assist the youth in achieving self-sufficiency and provide assistance with job
    placement (including placement in trade schools).
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Services provided shall include but are not limited to: Developing a presentation , developing a marketing plan,
    creating a tutoring plan, organizing monthly events alongside community-based organizations, schools, and vocational
    training facilities. All activities and services shall follow the COVID-19 guidelines set forth via local, State and Federal
    governments.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The services provided shall include but are not limited to: seminars, workshops, technical support training, school
    supplies giveaway, literacy events, graduation celebrations, awards ceremony, family participation event and incentive,
    and social media outreach. The services shall be utilized to attract the maximum amount of youth at risk possible to
    provide the much- needed services
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population will be the youth at risk in disadvantaged communities throughout Miami-Dade County. FVF
    expects to service between 101 - 200 individuals to include their immediate family members.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?
