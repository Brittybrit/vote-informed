4. Project/Program Description
    Request supports Village’s senior program to improve the quality of life for our adults 60 years of age and older by offering
   recreational, social and educational activities; and by providing support services and resources, including direct assistance
   for those with physical and cognitive impairments. Our senior programming is the centerpiece for 20% or about 3,000
   members of our population. The program is a gateway for our aging community to provide opportunities for our seniors to
   stay safe, active and healthy.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To enhance and expand senior programming and services for the Village’s over 60 population. With state support, the
    Village will offer inclusive and accessible recreational, educational, and social programs and events for seniors, including
    those with physical and cognitive impairments. Access to such programs and services are critical to our seniors aging-in-
    place in the community they feel most at home. Funds for the Village’s senior programs will support overall wellbeing.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Village will provide recreational, cultural, and social programming for seniors to engage with each other and the
    community.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Programs and services include the following: Painting workshops, Rock Steady Boxing Parkinson's Program, Lunch with
    an Author, Computer and Technology classes, Medicare Counseling, Pickleball, Floral Arrangement Workshops, Balance
    Chair Exercise Group, A.S.K. Book Club, Dominoes, Trips and Tours, Lectures, Annual Health Fair among other ranges
    of programs.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Target populations include elderly persons, persons with poor physical and mental health, economically disadvantaged
    persons, developmentally disabled, and physically disabled. Individuals expected to be served is approximately 1,500
    people.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Programming provided to Seniors will have a positive impact in their overall physical health. Regular physical activity
    helps seniors maintain their mobility and independence. In addition to preventing or delaying the onset of mobility issues.
    As the Village's Senior programming often involves group activities, clubs, or classes. It provides opportunities for seniors
    to socialize and interact with their peers. Social isolation is a common issue among seniors, and social engagement can
    combat loneliness and depression. Money Management Programs for Seniors to provide assistance and guidance as the
    senior population is often targeted by scammers and financial predators. Money management programs will provide
    safeguards to protect seniors from identity theft and fraud. Money management programs can help access programs to
    receive assistance they may be entitled to. This benefits and outcomes will be measured based on participant feedback
    and the number of attendees at all events.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables without notification will result in financial penalties as described in contract.
