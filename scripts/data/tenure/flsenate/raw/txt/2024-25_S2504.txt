14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funding is to provide comprehensive in-home services for persons with dementia and their caregivers which will
    reduce unnecessary hospitalizations, inappropriate involuntary psychiatric admissions, and provide a better quality of
    care. Also included is up to a 3 day out of home respite to allow for de-escalation, assessment of needs and coordination
    of care planning. A medical home model will provide whole health maintenance, caregiver support, safety and education.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Out of home respite care to allow for deescalation, assessment of needs, and coordination of care planning. Services will
    be arranged and navigated by Care Coordinators who will collaborate with family, medical and home care providers, as
    well as other supportive entities for a patient centered multidisciplinary approach to care. The funding will be utilized to
    decrease and potentially eliminate the number of preventable Baker Acts for the senior population. By providing
    comprehensive services in the home, the person with dementia will have a stable, safe, and supportive environment. The
    caretaker will also be supported and better able to experience their own improved physical and emotional health.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Services include up to 3 days out of home respite, in-home assessments to implement a safe living environment, care
    management for comprehensive wrap around services including medical and home care providers and a multidisciplinary
    team to address whole health maintenance activities, caregiver support, safety, and education. Telehealth will be
    available for additional intervention and support.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Persons with dementia and their families/caregivers in Citrus County, Florida. In 2022, it is estimated by FL Health
    Charts that 6,462 individuals (11.3%) aged 65 years or older in Citrus County with degenerative brain disease, the most
    common cause of dementia. The number of people with Alzheimer’s dementia reflects the magnitude of the burden of
    Alzheimer’s on the community and health care system. Citrus County falls into the third quartile for this measure. It is
    expected that 75 individuals with dementia and their families/caregivers will be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

    Reduction in hospitalizations among the target population, both medical and psychiatric.
    Enhanced care quality and efficiency.
    Expanded support services for persons with dementia, and their families/caregivers.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     If there is an area in which LifeStream is deficient, it is suggested that a corrective action plan be implemented and
    monitored to ensure that the deficient area(s) is/are corrected in a timely fashion. If LifeStream is not meeting the
    deliverables or performance outlined n the contract, then the State has the option to cancel the contract for these
    services.
