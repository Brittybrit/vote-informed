1. Project Title         Monroe County Emergency Operations Center

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       01/25/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Construction of the first and only designated Emergency Operations Center facility for Monroe County consisting of a
   28,321 square foot, two story hurricane hardened building located on the west end of the Marathon Airport property. The
   facility will consolidate the County's Emergency Management, Fire Rescue administration and Sheriff's Office 911
   communication center. The building is designed to meet and/or exceed FEMA's requirements for a "safe room" and meets
   the FEMA P-361 Design and Construction for Community Safe Room's guidance to provide "near-absolute protection", as
   well as ICC 500 standards.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose is completion of an Emergency Operation Center in Monroe County, a facility that will be FEMA P-361
    rated, self-sustaining for 72 hr for up to 150 emergency response staff, and outfitted with emergency communications via
    satellite phone/internet service. It will house MC Emerg Mgmt, MC Fire Rescue and the Sheriff's 911 Center. Monroe
    County provides the site, on its Marathon Airport property, as the local match. Current project costs total $36,528,100.50
    with the County having received $30,282,386.00 in State and Federal funding. The additional funds requested make up
    the difference required to complete construction and include costs associated with design and construction.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds will be utilized to complete construction of the first and only designated Emergency Operations Center for Monroe
    County. The new Emergency Operations Center will consolidate Monroe County Emergency Management, Fire Rescue
    administration, and the Sheriff's Office 911 Center within a new state-of-the-art, 28,321 square foot, 220 mph wind rated
    building providing an adequate facility for emergency activation and public response efforts. During Emergency activation,
    the EOC will support first responders and emergency management staff as they respond to and recover from any crisis
    situation. During normal (non-activation) status, the EOC will provide permanent housing for the County's Emergency
    Management Department, the Fire-Rescue Administration staff and the Sheriff's Department 911 Dispatch Center.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The County does not currently have an EOC (we convert existing office space that is not wind-rated.) The new
    Emergency Operations Center will keep emergency/disaster reponders safe and secure, enabling, ensuring an effective,
    efficient and comprehensive emergency response to future disasters. Expedient and comprehensive disaster response
    ensures the protection and safety of our residents and tourists, and a speedy restoration of the County's economic
    activity.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     There are multiple sets of beneficiaries served by this project: the entire resident and tourist population of the Florida
    Keys, benefits from an emergency management and response team that is safely and securely housed, in a wind and
    flood rated facility with updated communications. Additionally, the facility is sited on the County's Marathon Airport
    property ensuring expedient coordination of all incoming supplies and additional personnel. The safe, secure facility will
    also benefit the County's and State's emergency management responders, fire rescue, Monroe County Sheriffs Office,
    state and federal officials, FEMA, and other emergency response personnel.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Safe, secure facility to enable comprehensive emergency response to prepare for disasters/emergencies, to protect our
    disaster/emergency response personnel, residents, business owners, and tourists, and to ensure a speedy recovery post-
    disaster.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     As with our current grant agreements, failure to meet required conditions may be grounds for denial of reimbursements.
