14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will be used to construct a new public sanitary system to approximately one hundred and sixty two (162)
    residents along the project alignment in a priority sea-level rise area. Existing properties are currently served by septic
    tank systems that are negatively impacting the City's groundwater/drinking water.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The project will provide Public Sanitary Sewer facilities to serve the approximate one hundred and sixty two private
    homes along the project alignment.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Provide a sanitary system that will serve the residents of project alignment.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population for this project is over 500 people from the general population.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Expected benefits- improving physical health and protecting the general public from harm by providing public sanitary
    sewer facilities. These outcomes will be measured through the level of water contaminants after project completion, and
    the elimination of drainfield issues.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Revocation/withholding of funds.
