1. Project Title          Crystal River Ladder Truck

2. Senate Sponsor         Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request        12/19/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Funding to be used by the City of Crystal River (City) to purchase a used Aerial Ladder Fire Truck to service increasing
   building height fire and rescue requirements. The primary goal is for saving lives, property, and the safety of fire personal
   during fire and rescue operations. Due to flooding and storm surge experienced during Hurricane Idalia, flood zone
   designations, and FEMA insurance requirements, it is likely that structures in Crystal River will continue becoming taller.
   The lack of an Aerial Ladder Fire Truck in our inventory has only increased our fire rescue challenges to conduct rapid
   response, ventilation, extinguishment, and rescue in tall buildings. The nearest available Aerial Ladder Truck capability is in
   Citrus County. With a growing population and close to a million visitors annually, the City is seeking to enhance its public
   safety response with the purchase of a used Aerial Ladder Fire Truck.
