1. Project Title          Miami-Dade County St. Thomas School Sewer Service Extension and
                          Road Improvements Project

2. Senate Sponsor         Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request        12/05/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The project will assist St. Thomas the Apostle Catholic School (7303 SW 64th St, Miami, FL 33143) in their septic to sewer
   conversion, transitioning the seven septic tanks on campus to Miami-Dade County's centralized wastewater system.
   Funding will provide for the extension of the force main for this project. Additionally, the funding will provide for roadway
   improvements to alleviate the traffic concerns, currently creating a safety hazard in the vicinity of the school’s campus.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Assist St. Thomas the Apostle Catholic School (7303 SW 64th St, Miami, FL 33143) in their septic to sewer conversion,
    transitioning the seven septic tanks on campus to Miami-Dade County's centralized wastewater system.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funding will provide for the extension of the force main for this project. Additionally, the funding will provide for roadway
    improvements to alleviate the traffic concerns, currently creating a safety hazard in the vicinity of the school’s campus.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Extended service with respect to the County's wastewaster system and roadway improvements.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Individuals living, working, and attending school in the area.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project reduces potential exposure to wastewater from compromised and failing septic tank systems, will lead to
    removal of nutrient and bacterial sources of pollution from septic tank effluent, and reduces safety hazard to students and
    pedestrians in the area through roadway improvements. Benefits can be measured by number of septic tanks removed
    as well as number of accidents for the two different aspects of the project, respectively.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
