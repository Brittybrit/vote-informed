4. Project/Program Description
    Miami Arts Studio 6-12 @ Zelda Glazer was created by the School Board of Miami Dade County as a magnet school for
   the performing and visual arts in southwest Miami-Dade County. This specialized program was created due to a lack of
   access for students talented in the performing and visual arts in this region of the county to receive high-level performing
   arts and entertainment industry focused educational opportunities.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This specialized program was created due to lack of access for students talented in the performing and visual arts in this
    region of the county to receive high-level performing arts and entertainment industry-focused educational opportunities.
    Specific goals include but are not limited to 100% HS graduation rate, 80% HS graduate with honors, 100% of student
    complete 100 hours of service.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Miami Arts Studio 6-12 @ Zelda Glazer (MAS) offers a high quality and extensive academic and arts curriculum
    encompassing Middle School and High School level programs in visual art, digital art, dance, music, theater,
    broadcasting, and entertainment law. In addition to our magnet programs in grades 6-8, MAS hosts a Saturday Arts
    program for local elementary students in grades 3-5. Magnet students will receive training to complete at the local and
    state level.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Miami Arts Studio 6-12 @ Zelda Glazer offers a program of academic and artistic studies which shall be available to
    talented middle school and high school students. These skills are transferrable to the cultural capital of Miami-Dade
    County as well as to the robust visual and performing arts industry workforce. Cultural experiences and performances are
    offered to the community for no or low cost.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Economically disadvantaged persons; Grade school students; High school students. Over 800 individuals are expected
    to be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Enrich cultural experience; improvement of the quality of education and promotion; increase or improvement of economic
    activity; creation of specific job opportunities. These outcomes will be measured using contracts, revenue calculations,
    and observation of overall performance.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Denial of funds in future requests.
