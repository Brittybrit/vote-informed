4. Project/Program Description
    This project serves to provide a digital and video surveillance system, as well as a command center, to be used by
   emergency and safety personnel, to monitor and protect in real time the vital staging areas for equipment, supplies, and
   community members on the property at any given time, as well as prevent situations from entering and/or escalating on the
   grounds and surrounding areas. The proprty serves as an emergency evacuation, staging, response, reunification center,
   and event center.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Community safety during times of emergency response and during community events.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The public will be served indirectly by securing the facility being used during both emergency and cultural events.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The property serves as a primary center for evacuations, staging, storage, reunification, and shelter in times of
    emergencies to the general public. It also serves as a staging, housing, storage, and command center for municipal and
    private sector first responders.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The property serves the general populationn of Miami-Dade County year round and also is available to the residents of
    Monro County during times of evacuation through a per use agreement between the two counties. More than 1,000,000
    people visit the property annually.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Upgraded digital video surveillance of the property to be used by the Miami-Dade County Fair & Exposition, Inc., local
    police & fire professionals, and emergency agencies during times of events.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Return of unused fuinds
