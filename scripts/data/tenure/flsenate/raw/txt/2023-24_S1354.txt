1. Project Title          Key Biscayne Public Beach Access Pathway

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request       02/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Funds will be used to construct a public beach access path, located between the Commodore Club South and the Island
   House Apartments. As a barrier island community, residents without private access to the beach rely on public access of
   which there are few. The path will be ADA compliant, reinforce the dune line, and improve beach ecosystem as a sea turtle
   nesting habitat. This path is required for the Village to rejoin the the USACE Shoreline Protection Program. To date, the
   Village has completed three of the five required paths. This path is one of the two needed to ensure the Village is eligible
   for $120 million in federal investment from USACE for shoreline protection. Inclusion in this program is an integral
   component of the Village’s resiliency efforts and shoreline protection, supporting the viability of the island community for
   generations to come.

12. List the owners of the facility to receive, directly or indirectly, any fixed capital outlay funding. Include the
    relationship between the owners of the facility and the entity.

     Owner of the beach access path property is legally the Island House Apartments. The Village has secured a public
    easement agreement with Island House Apartments to convert the property into a public beach access path in perpetuity.
    The Village is responsible for maintaining, repairing, and improving the path in perpetuity. The Village of Key Biscayne
    would be the recipient of the funding and would be solely responsible for its deployment in constructing the public beach
    access path.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Construct an ADA compliant, 5 ft wide public beach access path, and remaining eligible to be included in the USAC
    Shoreline Protection Program which would provide the opportunity for the Village to receive $120M in federal funding for
    shoreline protection.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Village completed all data collection including a property survey and an initial assessment of the path. The Village
    has secured the easement required to construct the path. Village staff are responsible for subsequent phases including
    design, procurement, and construction. The Village’s Public Works Department will manage design, permitting,
    procurement and construction administration with assistance from consultants as needed.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     No direct services are provided in this project.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The entire Village population of about $15,000 residents plus all visitors.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     1. Improve public beach access for residents and visitors. Measured by number of path users. 2. Support shoreline
    protection. The dune line protects the east side of the Village from storm surge and sea level rise. Measured by reduced
    flooding events west of the dune line. 3. Restore beach ecosystem. Dunes are home to native plants that strengthen the
    dune line and are sea turtle nesting sites. Measured by health and location of dune plants and sea turtle nests. 4.
    Remaining eligible to rejoin the USACE shoreline protection program which would include $120M in federal funding.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in the forfeiture of funds or financial penalties as described in the contract.
