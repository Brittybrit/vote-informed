12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will support the Inclusion Theatre Project, a one-of-a-kind theatre training program for students with
    developmental and intellectual disabilities founded in 2014 in Coral Gables, FL. ITP currently serves over 400 youth, at
    no cost to them, through on site classes at ASC and coordinated outreach classes by ITP instructors at local partner
    schools. Yearly, students present two public access musical theatre productions, as well as multiple outreach
    performances throughout the community.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Musical theatre training (acting, voice, dance) given to developmentally challenged students by ITP trained instructors
    during 2-3 times weekly classes at both ASC and local area partner schools.
    -At least two public access, fully produced musical theatre productions yearly, performed by ITP students.
    -At least five performances and/or classes for the public
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     All services are provided to anyone who would like them from the general public, at no cost to students. ITP focuses on
    helping students manifest what they are capable of achieving through impactful artistic educational interactions which
    inspire change and foster understanding in audiences and the greater community.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     ITP serves approximately 320 Miami Dade county students, ages 6 and up, with developmental, intellectual, and
    physical disabilities. Students currently come from ITP partner schools: Crystal Academy, Great Heights Academy, the
    Marian Center School and Services, Miami Learning Experience, and Our Pride Academy, as well as from Miami Dade
    county public schools.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Onsite students at ASC, and their parents are given pre and post assessments and surveys in order to measure
    outcomes and satisfaction. Additionally, partner school classes are evaluated once per year to ensure satisfactory
    student progress. Student attendance is tracked carefully for all groups, and perhaps most effectively and importantly, all
    students participate in multiple theatre performances onstage each year. This is where progress and development are
    most evident. Success is defined as follows: 100% of all students will have improved in all parameters (cognition, fine
    motor skills, verbal communication, cooperation, self-confidence, etc.) as evidenced by pre and post assessments, 100%
    of students will have completed all rehearsals and performances throughout the session, and 100% of students and
    parents will be satisfied with the program.
