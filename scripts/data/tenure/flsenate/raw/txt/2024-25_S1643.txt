1. Project Title          Renovation of Historic Bruce Hall & Reynolds School

2. Senate Sponsor         Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request        12/03/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Renovation of two facilities in Key West, Florida owned by the Monroe County School District ('MCSD'): Reynolds School
   (1316 Reynolds St.) and Bruce Hall (1310 United St.). Through renovation of these properties, MCSD will utilize existing
   local resources, MCSD facilities not currently utilized to their maximum potential, to address one of its biggest challenges to
   effective operation of schools: decreasing levels of staff retention caused by the distinct lack of affordable housing in the
   Key West area. Renovation of these facilities would permit MCSD to vacate its current administration building at 241
   Trumbo Road for purposes of making the property available for development of affordable workforce housing for MCSD
   teachers and educational support personnel at no cost to MCSD through public-private partnership.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Making the property at 241 Trumbo Road, Key West, Florida, available for development of affordable workforce housing
    to support local teachers and other 'essential services personnel' at no cost to MCSD via public-private partnership.
    Renovation of the Bruce Hall and Reynolds School facilities will utilize existing local resources to permit relocation of the
    MCSD administration team from the Trumbo Road facility, allowing the property to be developed into affordable workforce
    housing.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The requested funds will be used to retain the services of one or more firms to renovate the Reynolds School facility to
    accommodate relocation of the MCSD Maintenance Department, and the Bruce Hall facility to accommodate relocation of
    the MCSD Administration Department. Relocation of both Departments to Bruce Hall and Reynolds School will allow
    MCSD to vacate the property at 241 Trumbo Road, making the property available for development of affordable
    workforce housing in support of MCSD personnel.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     No services will be directly provided to citizens through the appropriation project. The appropriation will provide indirect
    benefits to citizens by facilitating development of affordable workforce housing in the Key West area at no cost to
    taxpayers.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The project is expected to serve economically disadvantaged persons, at-risk youth, students, and Monroe County
    School District employees. These target populations will benefit from the increased availability of affordable housing in
    the Key West area for teachers and other district employees. The proposed affordable housing development is expected
    to contain 150 dwelling units. Therefore, it is expected that approximately 200 individuals will be directly benefited by this
    project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Completion of this project is expected to enhance teacher and employee recruitment for MCSD by reducing the high cost
    of living in the Lower Florida Keys via development of affordable workforce housing at 241 Trumbo Road. This outcome
    can be measured by a reduction is the total number of applicants who reject employment with MCSD due to lack of
    affordable housing, reduction in the total number of vacant employment positions with MCSD, as well as a reduction in
    the total number of applications for state or federal housing assistance submitted by MCSD employees.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Proportional loss of funding in the next fiscal year until MCSD has complied with deliverables and performance measures
