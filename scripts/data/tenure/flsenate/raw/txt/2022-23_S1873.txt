4. Project/Program Description
    The Coastal Resiliency Project will serve as a community engagement hub that will help train and educate students,
   visitors and volunteers on the importance of making South Florida’s coastal areas, of which Fairchild’s Lowlands reside,
   resilient sea level rise, King Tides and storm surge. The project will mitigate and harden coastal areas in South Florida and
   Fairchild, including islands, barriers, collections, trails and Garden landmarks in FTBG’s Lowlands to help prevent erosion
   of critically vulnerable assets. Fairchild’s Lowlands have a Vulnerability Score (V Score) of 4.3/5. Currently, flooding and
   erosion impact the project area after any Tropical Storm or higher with areas entirely inundated by sea level rise, King
   Tides & storm surge. By taking the lead on coastal resiliency, Fairchild will ensure that the South Florida coastal community
   can mitigate against sea level rise, King Tides and storm surge.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Coastal Resiliency Project will serve as a community engagement hub that will help train and educate students,
    visitors and volunteers on the importance of making South Florida’s coastal areas, of which Fairchild’s Lowlands reside,
    resilient sea level rise, King Tides and storm surge. The project will mitigate and harden coastal areas in South Florida
    and Fairchild, including islands, barriers, collections, trails and Garden landmarks in FTBG’s Lowlands to help prevent
    erosion of critically vulnerable assets. Fairchild’s Lowlands have a Vulnerability Score (V Score) of 4.3/5. Currently,
    flooding and erosion impact the project area after any Tropical Storm or higher with areas entirely inundated by sea level
    rise, King Tides & storm surge. By taking the lead on coastal resiliency, Fairchild will ensure that the South Florida coastal
    community can mitigate against sea level rise, King Tides and storm surge
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The South Florida Coastal Resiliency Project will: 1) Enable the development of nature-based strategies to improve
    South Florida and Fairhild’s sea level rise resilience, water quality, wildlife habitat, and other environmental challenges,
    including plant species that will help harden coastal and low-lying areas. 2) Expand community partnerships. 3) Educate
    citizens, landscape professionals, visitors, students, volunteers HOAs about mitigation techniques. 4) Enhance the
    Garden, to the benefit of residents and tourists.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Coastal Miami, where FTBG is located, faces numerous environmental threats, ranging from hurricanes to floods to sea-
    level rise and storm surge. Fairchild educates local governments and environmental groups on how to use plants to
    mitigate these threats, protecting citizens' quality of life and the region's economy. The Community Resiliency Project will
    advance these goals.
