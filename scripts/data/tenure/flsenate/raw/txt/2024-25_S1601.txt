4. Project/Program Description
    The Center for Creative Education's goal is to eliminate elementary illiteracy through creativity and the arts. Several years
   ago, CCE opened The Foundations School, an innovative, independent elementary school, which serves many of the
   poorest areas in Palm Beach County, at little to no cost to the families which attend. 89% of third grade students at The
   Foundations School are reading on or above grade level. This compared to a statewide average of 53%, a national average
   of 43%, and in the high poverty communities served by The Foundations School, only 24% of third grade students can
   read. Illiteracy is a pipeline to prison, homelessness, unemployment, and multi-generational poverty -- CCE is breaking that
   barrier through transformative approaches. Critically, literacy is the foundational pillar for all future learning. The need to
   admit more students to this unique, school choice experience is staggering, and requires more space, necessitating a new
   22,000 sq/ft. building.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The requested funds will enable the Center for Creative Education (CCE) and The Foundations School to expand space
    needed to add more students who are struggling academically and living in poverty. With a transformative education
    through creativity and the arts, our students are thriving; 89% of our third-grade students are reading on or above grade
    level, as well as 88% of our fourth graders. The need is overwhelming for this unique, independent school choice
    experience.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The goal of The Foundations School is to enroll the maximum number of students to increase the positive impact on the
    community. CCE is currently using much of its classroom space for activities and functions that will be relocated to the
    new building once finished. This will allow The Foundations School to double its capacity and expand its offerings to
    students on its 4-acre campus in West Palm Beach.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Students and families currently enrolled in The Foundations School will utilize the new space for art, STEM education
    (science, technology, engineering and math), and media arts education, as well as gallery space for student exhibits and
    performances. In addition, the facility will be available for the Center for Creative Education's many Palm Beach County-
    wide after school programs. The completion of the new space will also enable The Foundations School to double its
    enrollment by freeing up existing space for much needed (and highly demanded) additional classrooms.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     CCE and The Foundations School serve students who are behind academically, living in poverty, and are zoned to
    attend public schools in northern Palm Beach County which are chronically poor performers on state standardized tests.
    Once this building project is complete, The Foundations School will double in size to serve 180 students and 360 family
    members. CCE currently serves an additional 200 students enrolled in after school programs, and 400 family members,
    as well as 2,000 donors and community members supporting our programs and events. Finally, CCE has collaborations
    with Palm Beach Atlantic University and Palm Beach State College.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The Center for Creative Education is at the forefront of educational innovation, reform, and school choice. We
    understand that every child is unique and not all students learn by the same methods of teaching. At the Foundations
    School, individualized assessments therefore drive instruction. Our goal is that 90% of all students attending our school
    will be performing at grade level or above in reading, math, and science. CCE also has an actionable plan for recruiting
    after school students for programming, and there will be opportunities to engage the wider community with student
    projects (art galleries, films, shows, etc...).
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables without notification of good reasoning, will result in penalties as described in contract.
