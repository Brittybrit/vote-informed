4. Project/Program Description

   remediation, design, construction, costs associated with National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) compliance, pedestrian
   bridges over canals, at grade roadway crossings, and roadway crossings above grade via pedestrian bridges to create a
   5.6 mile, 18-foot wide continuous shared-use path.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Design and construction of Ludlam Trail.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    Miami-Dade County will develop 100% of the design plans and construction documents for the development of Ludlam
    Trail.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will benefit from the development of the Ludlam Trail project, which will transition a former privately-owned
    railroad corridor to a publicly-owned and accessible 5.6-mile greenway trail for non-motorized transportation and
    recreational use by bicyclists and pedestrians.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Ludlam Trail is expected to attract and be used and enjoyed by 2.7 million residents of Miami-Dade County, as well as
    visitors and tourists, and approximately 40,000 people who reside within 1/2 mile of the corridor.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcome is the design and construction of an iconic 5.6-mile long greenway trail to serve 2.7 million
    residents of Miami-Dade County, as well as visitors and tourists. It will serve as an active non-motorized transportation
    and recreation trail for public use.
