1. Project Title          Florida Keys Habitat for Humanity Affordable Housing Project

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       03/09/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    This project is a collaboration between all three Habitat for Humanity affiliates in Monroe County and will be used to
   construct a total of 12 affordable housing units in the Florida Keys to serve the community's workforce. In the Upper Keys 6
   units will be constructed, in the Lower Keys 4 units will be constructed, and in the Middle Keys land will be acquired and a
   total of two units (one duplex) will be completed.

     This project is a collaboration between all three Habitat for Humanity affiliates in Monroe County and will be used to
    construct a total of 12 affordable housing units in the Florida Keys to serve the community's workforce. In the Upper Keys
    6 units will be constructed, in the Lower Keys 4 units will be constructed, and in the Middle Keys land will be acquired and
    a total of two units (one duplex) will be completed.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds will be used to construct affordable housing units for members of the Monroe County workforce.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Funds will be used to construct affordable housing units for members of the Monroe County Workforce.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Economically disadvantaged persons, 25-50 people
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Addition of affordable housing units provided to Monroe County employees will provide localized housing options for
    individuals and families, preventing the need for lengthy commutes. Number of individuals employed within Monroe
    County who purchase completed. Increase the ability of employees to find affordable housing options in the community in
    which they work, provide employers with additional options for increasing the number of employees they hire. Units
    successfully occupied by Monroe County's workforce. Specific immediate job opportunities will be created across a
    variety of fields in the construction industry. Number of individuals employed in the construction of units at all three sites.
    Allowing qualified individuals and their families the ability to own an affordable home in the community in which they work.
    Units successfully occupied by Monroe County's workforce.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Withholding the funds.
