2. Senate Sponsor         Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request        01/25/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    Coral Way (SW 24th Street/SR 972) and Granada Boulevard is currently a span wire signalized intersection. Coral Way is
   a state road serving as a connection for commuters traveling from the western end of Miami-Dade County to the City of
   Coral Gables and the City of Miami. During hurricane Irma, the signalized intersection was inoperable. The city proposes to
   convert this intersection to a hardened span wire to maintain the historic character of the plaza while adding exclusive left
   turn lanes and safe pedestrian crossings. These improvements will help increase capacity of the roadway and to move
   traffic more efficiently through the area especially during hurricane evacuations.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project will be constructed to maintain operations during and after a storm event. It will increase capacity by the
    construction of exclusive left turn lanes and minimize pedestrian crossings of all legs of the intersection.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     With the construction of exclusive left turn lanes capacity will be increased and hardened span wire signals will maintain
    signal operations following a wind event.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will be able to shorten commutes from west Miami-Dade to downtown Coral Gables and the City of Miami by
    providing additional capacity with the construction of exclusive left turn lanes.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is commuters from west Miami-Dade to downtown Coral Gables and the City of Miami. The annual
    average daily traffic is 20,000 vehicle per day.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The benefit will be that the signal will be hardened and be able to withstand a wind event. It will put less stress on county
    resources following a storm. Additionally, delays will be decreased after the construction of an exclusive left turn lane.
    The improvements to delays can be measured through a traffic study.
