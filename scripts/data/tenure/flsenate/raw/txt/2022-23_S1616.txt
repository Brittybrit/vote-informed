4. Project/Program Description

   model facility will be replicated throughout the state in conjunction with the College of the Florida Keys. Beyond the applied
   research, the model will provide for educational and workforce training opportunities and economic development and will
   alleviate food insecurity.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Requested funds will be used to build a category 5 regional aquaponics facility in an area of critical state concern. This
    model facility will be replicated throughout the state in conjunction with the College of the Florida Keys. Beyond the
    applied research, the model will provide for educational and workforce training opportunities and economic development
    and will alleviate food insecurity.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Construction of a category 5 regional aquaponics facility.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Once completed, this facility will provide educational and workforce training opportunities, and economic development,
    and help alleviate food insecurity in the surrounding area.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This project will benefit students at the College of the Florida Keys as well as the residents in the Lower Keys.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Completed aquaponics facility that will provide the College of the Florida Keys with a new educational platform while
    serving the needs for food security for the greater Monroe County community.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Return of funds.
