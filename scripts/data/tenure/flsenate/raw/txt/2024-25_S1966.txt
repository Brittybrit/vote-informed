4. Project/Program Description
    With these funds, the Piegon Key Sustainability Upgrades Project will be used to complete the wastewater upgrades that
   were previously authorized by the Florida Legislature. These funds will also begin environmental restoration projects on the
   island that will include erosion control and shore stabalization. By upgrading wastewater facilities, it will allow for better
   access to the island and ensure that the island is avaible to vistors for years to come through environmental sustainability
   projects.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     These funds will be used to complete the wastewater upgrades previously authorized by the Florida Legislature and to
    begin environmental restoration projects on the island including shoreline stabilization and erosion control. Upgraded
    wastewater and facilities will allow for greater access to the island and environmental sustainability projects will help
    ensure the island is available to be visited by residents and visitors for years to come.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Upgraded wastewater system will allow more visitors to experience the island and its historic/environmental resources.
    Sustainability projects including shoreline stabilization will help ensure the island and its shoreline are better protected
    from future storms.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     An upgraded wastewater system and facilities will allow more visitors to visit and enjoy the island. Environmental
    restoration projects like shoreline stabilization and erosion control will help ensure that the island and its resources are
    better protected from future storms.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Monroe County residents, visitors and the state of Florida all benefit this project. More than 3 million visitors vacation in
    the Florida Keys each year.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

    Completion of the upgraded wastewater system and beginning work on sustainability upgrades outlined in the Pigeon
    Key Sustainability Plan to address necessary environmental sustainability projects including shoreline stabilization.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Withholding of funds.
