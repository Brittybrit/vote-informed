14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will provide for the repair and hardening of coastal infrastructure, which has been significantly damaged and
    deteriorated due to extreme weather events like tropical storms and hurricane that serves pedestrian and vehicular traffic
    at the Southernmost Point of the U.S.A. and also protects a structure of historical significance. Hardening will include
    increased elevation to address future sea-level rise, shoreline armoring, erosion mitigation. and installation of a new
    sheet pile seawall.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Construction activities placing rip-rap shoreline armoring and wave attenuation, installation of new sheet pile seawall,
    replacement of eroded base material, and increasing new seawall cap height to address sea-level rise will meet the
    purpose of the funds to benefit citizens, including residents and tourists.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Repair and hardening of the seawall will provide for a safe and stable infrastructure which is utilized by pedestrians and
    vehicular traffic to experience the Southernmost Point and historic Cable Hut, which is visited by thousands of people per
    day. Without repair and improvements to resiliency the sidewalk, roadway, and historic structure are in danger of eroding
    and falling away.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The general population (The majority of funds will benefit no specific group).
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The completion of the project will enrich the cultural experience of thousands of visiting tourists by providing a more
    resilient environment that is safe from flooding and storm damage, improve environmental quality through the installation
    of rip-rap to reduce wave action and local erosion, protect the public from environmental harm and improve transportation
    conditions by providing a safe roadway for vehicular and pedestrians traffic that is not impacted by waves or flooding, and
    increase economic activity and tourism by revitalizing a heavily frequented tourist destination at the Southernmost Point
    of the U.S.A.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Funding will be withheld, if project deliverables are not met.
