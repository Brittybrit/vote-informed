1. Project Title           City of South Miami Community Safety & Police Technology Updates

2. Senate Sponsor          Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request         02/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The South Miami Police Department wants to increase community safety and police accountability by incorporating
   available license plate reader technology in all its patrol vehicles and deployment of traffic trailers. They will purchase and
   install in-car fleet cameras which incorporate the latest in license plate recognition technology as well as record all traffic
   stops. In conjunction with the fleet cameras the City will deploy speed measuring trailers with advisory signs within the City
   with license plate reader technology. The license plate reader will be integrated with the Miami-Dade Police Departments
   real time crime center to provide county wide alerts of electronically captured felony vehicles. The in car fleet cameras
   license plate readers will immediately notify officers of felony vehicles within his vicinity. With this technology it is the hope
   of the SMPD to pro-actively identify and apprehend felony subjects before other crimes are committed.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The City of South Miami wishes to reduce crime by identifying felony vehicles utilizing license plate reader technology
    and increase police accountability by enhancing the recording of all traffic stops by patrol personnel.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Reduction of crime in the City of South Miami and surrounding areas and greater accountability with the recording of
    traffic stops. The in car video systems will compliment the Police Departments Axon body cameras and electronic control
    devices.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Possible reduction in crime with the apprehension of more felony vehicles and identifying felony vehicles and other
    violations within residential areas. Reduce citizen complaints and increase accountability with a different view other then
    the body camera. The in car video system provides 180 degree recording from the dash of the police vehicle as well as
    recording detention areas within the police vehicle.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Residents of South Miami and surrounding areas. Approximately 20,000 individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Increase in felony and misdemeanor apprehensions, possible reduction in crime and decreased citizen complaints. The
    comparison of previous years numbers of apprehensions, crime and complaints compared to a full year of use of
    requested equipment.

    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?
