2. Senate Sponsor        Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request       12/18/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    This project will include the milling and resurfacing of C 466 between Buena Vista Boulevard and Morse Boulevard and
   installation of thermoplastic roadway stripe. The roadway lies within the heart of the largest age-restricted development in
   the world with disadvantaged citizens in surrounding areas that are low income and burdened with housing, health, and
   climate change. This roadway has a pavement condition index ranging at and below 40 and an average daily traffic of over
   23,000 vehicles per day. Due to these conditions, Sumter County has deemed this project a high priority.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose of the requested funds is to improve the poor conditions of a roadway that lies within the heart of the largest
    age-restricted development in the world with disadvantaged citizens in surrounding areas that are low income and
    burdened with housing, health, and climate change. This roadway has a pavement condition index ranging at and below
    40 and an average daily traffic of over 23,000 vehicles per day. Due to these conditions, Sumter County has deemed this
    project a high priority.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     To meet the intended purpose of these funds, the contractor will mill and resurface the roadway, as well as install
    thermoplastic roadway striping. This will significantly improve the pavement condition index (PCI) of the roadway by
    providing a smoother and improved riding experience for the high volume of travelers of the roadway.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The direct services to be provided to citizens by the funding requested include giving the high number of roadway
    travelers, which include citizens in the largest age-restricted development in the world as well as disadvantaged citizens
    in surrounding areas, an improved roadway to travel on. This roadway will provide a smoother and enhanced riding
    experience without the adverse effects of irregularities in the roadway like deformations and cracking.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population served by this project will be the residents of the largest age-restricted development in the world
    that the roadway runs through, as well as disadvantaged citizens in surrounding areas in tract numbers 12119910100,
    12069030411, and 12069030407 that are considered low income and burdened with housing, health, and climate change
    according to the Climate and Economic Justice Screening Tool. There are thousands of individuals that are expected to
    be served as the roadway has an average daily traffic of over 23,000 vehicles per day.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     An expected benefit from this project includes improved transportation conditions. Milling and resurfacing the roadway
    will provide a smoother and improved riding quality for travelers. The level of benefit can be measured by tracking the
    improved Pavement Condition Index (PCI) of the roadway. Another expected benefit from this project is an increase in
    economic activity. More people are likely to travel the roadway if it is in better condition. There is a correlation between
    traffic volumes on a roadway and economic activity. The level of benefit can be measured by tracking the Average Daily
    Traffic (ADT) of the roadway.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Corrective action plan, punitive damages, performance improvement plan.
