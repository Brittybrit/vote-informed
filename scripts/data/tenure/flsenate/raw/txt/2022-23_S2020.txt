4. Project/Program Description
    The Town of Cutler Bay is requesting $100,000 to assist with the expansion of the Cutler Bay Active Adults Services
   Program. This project is a result of recommendations made in the Town’s adopted Communities for a Lifetime Age-Friendly
   Action Plan. The goal of this project is to assist the Town’s senior citizens in aging in place while enjoying an active lifestyle
   that will keep them connected with the local community. Funding from the State will be used as seed money to expand
   existing services. Activities include; community outings, regularly scheduled activities at the Town's new Franjo Park
   Community Center, and development of a directory to help older adults find trusted resources in the community.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal of the Active Adult Services program is to assist the Town in meeting the expressed needs of older adults in our
    community by incorporating more activities for residents 60+ years old, helping to identify available resources and
    programs for residents living in Cutler Bay (South Miami-Dade), and providing regular opportunities for socialization and
    recreation to keep residents engaged.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The project has three components: (1) Hosting at least one community outing per month, (2) providing community based
    recreation at the Town's newly opened Franjo Park Community Center, and (3) Develop a resource booklet to improve
    education and awareness of services available to them and how to access these services.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The Town's park and recreation staff will provide direct services in the form of coordinating and chaperoning community
    outings, and facilitating activities offered at the Franjo Park Community Center.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population consists of older adults age 60+ residing in the Town of Cutler Bay or surrounding communities. It
    is expected that the program will reach 75 older adults in the first year, and distribute 200 resource booklets.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Cutler Bay is the first municipality in South Florida to have a Communities for a Lifetime (CFAL) Age-Friendly Action
    Plan, approved by the Florida Department of Elder Affairs and World Health Organization. Surveys conducted during the
    development of the action plan found that seniors are highly independent and capable of managing their lives; however,
    they are unaware of the services available to them in Cutler Bay and surrounding communities. The outcome of this
    project is to increase the availability of services and awareness of services to meet the needs of the community. This will
    be measured by completion of the booklet, and the number of people reached for the available programs.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     If we fail to meet our deliverables or performance measures, we should be given notice and the opportunity to come into
    compliance. Should we fail to address the concerns, funding should be withheld until a time that the project complies with
    the requirements. Failure to meet deliverables would cancel the contract and forfeiture of the remaining balance of
    unspent appropriation.
