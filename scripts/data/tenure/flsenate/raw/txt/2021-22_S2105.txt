12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The College's BS Marine Resource Management, AAS Diving Business & Technology, and AS Marine Environmental
    Technology programs, along with four embedded college credit certificates utilize this space on a daily basis. The
    purpose of these funds is to address facility and storage concerns where the College houses, repairs, and maintains life
    support equipment for all courses which utilize dive equipment. The constant moisture, attributed to the sub-tropic
    climate, has contributed to the dire need for renovation, termite extermination, and vermin elimination. These programs
    serve approximately 225 students annually.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The College will contract for services to repair/replace flooring, implement new shelving and storage racks, replace
    conduit and mounts, install rails to secure large volume storage bottles to mitigate safety risk, install lighting, repair HVAC
    leaks, paint interior walls, replace doors, and renovate bathrooms due to mold growth from moisture exposure. In
    addition, a locked cage will be installed to secure and protect expensive diving equipment.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The College serves the Monroe County community by providing educational services for dual enrollment students, credit
    and non-credit students, youth summer camp participants, and other recreational activities which utilize this space.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?
