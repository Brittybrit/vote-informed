14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Veterans of Foreign Wars of the Dept. of Florida is comprised of 50,000 veterans who are members of 155 post
    locations throughout Florida's 67 counties. Each post is a separate Florida corporation. The VFW has trained its
    leadership in VFW bylaws and policies and procedures since 1899, there is a need and an opportunity to increase the
    growth and success of veteran business leaders by providing professional business training. Therefore, the goal for and
    purpose of this request is: To provide best practices in business management and leadership training for Florida veteran
    members of the Department of Florida's Veterans of Foreign Wars; and as a model for the future for any or all Veterans
    Service Organizations.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Florida State University's Jim Moran College of Entrepreneurship will develop a series of five seminar business
    leadership courses following best practices in the disciplines of:
    1.Business Operations
    2.Marketing – Member Recruitment and Retention
    3.Basic business law
    4.Human Resources Management
    5.Leadership
    6.Basic business accounting and finance
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The Jim Moran College of Entrepreneurship instructors will, in revolving fashion, teach each of these five business
    management and leadership courses to veterans who attend the VFW Dept. of Florida annual convention held in June
    2024 in Orlando.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The courses will be available to every veteran leader who attends the Convention (approximately 500), with significant
    focus and encouragement being given to present senior leadership of Florida's 155 VFW posts from virtually all 67
    counties.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     To provide a professional educational model for veterans toward higher levels of business management and leadership
    training principles and skills to more professionally lead their respective businesses. The impact of such training may be
    seen in:
    1. increased membership recruitment.
    2. increased annual retention of annual membership payment members.
    3. decrease in incidence of disciplinary action
    4. increase in the timely and accurate IRS filings.
    5. increase in post VFW project programs
    6. increase in post income and profitability
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?
