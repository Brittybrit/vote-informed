    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Miami Dade College (MDC) will address the teacher shortage in state-designated critical subject areas (English,
    Exceptional Student Education (ESE), Science - General, Reading, English for Speakers of Other Languages (ESOL),
    Math, and Science - Physical) by expanding programs to provide and facilitate opportunities for teacher candidates. MDC
    will expand the use and integration of teacher learning with the latest technology (AR/VR, AI, etc.) to ensure a tomorrow
    ready teacher workforce today.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Students will be given programmatic and financial support to facilitate program retention, and completion. Activities and
    services include funding to cover security clearances, Florida Teacher Certification Exam (FTCE) prep tutorials, FTCE
    exam vouchers, purchase of latest educational technology, video recording equipment, Watermark licenses, GoReact
    licenses, and travel to disseminate best practices.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Direct services will include outreach and awareness events, and activities, classroom and field placement instruction, test
    prep support, and assistance with placement; and instruction of future teacher students and current in-service teachers in
    the use and integration of latest technology into teaching to improve student learning outcomes.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is current and prospective teacher candidates enrolled in associates or baccalaureate degree
    programs in Miami Dade College School of Education. Primary populations served are first-time direct entry from high
    school; career changers; non-traditional students, and veterans.

    Individuals to be served: 3,750 a year
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcome is the expansion of the number of qualified teachers to fill positions in critical subject areas and
    teachers who are prepared to integrate the latest technology to improve learning outcomes. Success will be measured by
    the a) # of completions/graduates with degrees; (b) # of participants obtaining Florida Teacher Certification; (c) # of
    participants receiving certification in a critical shortage subject areas; (d) # of participants placed as pre-service teachers
    in local and bordering county school districts; (d) # of participants hired as full-time teachers in local, bordering county,
    and other school districts within Florida.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Standard Contract Conditions
    Release of funds based on progress toward attaining established milestones. Performance-based funding with scaling
    and/or percentage-based payback based on degree of deficit in attainment of outcomes related to
    completions/graduations, persons obtaining Florida Teacher Certification, persons receiving certification in a designated
    critical shortage subject area, and persons hired as teachers by local, bordering county, or other school districts in
    Florida.
