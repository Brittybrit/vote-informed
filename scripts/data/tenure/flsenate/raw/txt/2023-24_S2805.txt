1. Project Title          Hernando County Central Fueling Facility for Critical Services

2. Senate Sponsor         Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request        02/28/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The County does not have adequate fueling reserves for its first responder critical services (Fire Rescue, ambulances,
   Public Works, and Utilities) to respond to natural disasters or any event that may disrupt the transport and delivery of fuel.
   During Hurricane Irma, the County came close to running out of fuel and had to make tough decisions on how to allocate
   fuel. This project will construct a new fueling facility to provide critical services.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To construct a fueling facility that will allow emergency services to continue their mission to serve the citizens during a
    state of emergency; provide adequate fueling reserves.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Adequate access for fuel for emergency services
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    All emergency services, including Fire, EMS, Public Works, and Utilities, provide support functions throughout Hernando
    County in a state of emergency and could potentially impact all County residents.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    All emergency services, including Fire, EMS, Public Works, and Utilities, provide support functions throughout Hernando
    County in a state of emergency and could potentially impact all County residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Provide adequate fuel for emergency services to the public (Fire, EMS, Public Works and Utilities) during a natural
    disaster or event when there is a disruption in the transport and delivery of fuel. This will be achieved by demolition of an
    existing functionally obsolete fueling facility and construction of a new central fueling facility with additional fueling
    reserves.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Revoke funding if fueling facility is not constructed.
