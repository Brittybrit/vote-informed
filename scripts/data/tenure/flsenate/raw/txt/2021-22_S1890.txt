12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will be used for engineering design and construction of this project to alleviate the excessive flooding experienced
    in and around the immediate surrounding area of the Key Biscayne K-8 Center Elementary, will target “sunny day
    flooding” caused by sea level rise and reduce direct discharge of pollutants into Biscayne Bay.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The need for this project has been documented in the Stormwater Master Plan Update dated November 15, 2015. This
    report was prepared by EAC Consulting Inc The report was requested by the Village Council. The report prioritized all
    stormwater projects and gave a preliminary estimated costs for all the projects including the Pump Station.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will enjoy flood free public roadways.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    Elderly persons. Preschool students. Grade school students. General population.
    More than 800.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The Project will reduce direct discharge of pollutants into Biscayne Bay - Area will be monitored and sampled.
    Managing sea level rise and the impacts of climate change in form of flooding - Area will be monitored
    Improved traffic flows surrounding the school during rain events and King Tides - Area will be monitored
    Expected benefit of the Project is less frequent flooding, upgraded stormwater infrastructure, and adapt to the climate
    changes - area will be monitored.

    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?
