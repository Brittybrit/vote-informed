4. Project/Program Description
    Centro Mater is the backbone to over 600 families in the East Little Havana community with children 0-12 years of age. So
   many people reach out us for help, guidance, and assistance. State funding is requested to provide an immersive after
   school/school recess program that will aim to reduce the learning loss of this school year and enhance the social-emotional
   state of the children that have been impacted by this pandemic. Also, there will be a strong parental component in which
   parenting courses, and trainings will be offered to support and strengthen the family unit. The school recess program will be
   open from 7am to 6pm, Monday-Friday. The after school program is open from 2pm to 6pm, Monday-Friday. Field trips, 3
   meals, materials, and enhancement programs (literacy, art disciplines) are all part of this initiative. This will be offered at
   NO COST TO THE FAMILIES. Centro Mater Afterschool Program is the only program that attained the prestigious National
   Accreditation in Florida.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to provide programs and services that assure optimal educational opportunities for all children regardless of
    race, creed, religious affiliation, national origin, disability, or gender. By assisting with the educational needs of children,
    providing quality services, and referrals to needed resources, we can support the extended families. State funding is
    requested to provide an immersive after school/school recess program that will aim to reduce the learning loss of this
    school year and enhance the social-emotional state of the children that have been impacted by this pandemic. Also, there
    will be a strong parental component in which parenting courses, and trainings will be offered to support and strengthen
    the family unit.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The program will utilize a combination of: research-based curricula, reading support, activities, tutoring, homework
    assistance, in-service trainings, and differentiated instruction that responds to the needs, levels, interests and abilities of
    students. Additionally, Centro Mater will be offering field trips, 3 meals, materials, and enhancement programs (literacy,
    art disciplines) as part of the initiative. The common denominator in all activities is to reduce the learning losses of the
    children during this school year. Trainings provided for the families include: Job Preparation, Life Skills, Domestic
    Violence Awareness, Effective Parenting, Child Emotional Health, Food Safety, Health & Safety Prevention at Home,
    Promoting Literacy, and Child School Transitioning.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The program provides quality services to disadvantaged children by offering relevant developmental experiences
    conducive to enhancing the child's potential and, at the same time, empowering and strengthening the families. Children
    will have the opportunity to develop and learn on an individual basis. Emphasizing diversity is an excellent opportunity to
    bring music into underprivileged neighborhoods in the community. The center supports parent self- sufficiency by
    providing them opportunities to strengthen their family unity, prevent drug abuse, overcome domestic violence, and
    develop parenting skills. Extending support services to parents, guardians, and families allows the families to identify their
    needs and establish a goal. This grant will only fund direct services as this is a program to serve children and their
    families. Funds will cover the scholarships for the children to attend our program at no cost to them.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     With so many children and families needing our support, guidance, and assistance, our goal is to help children ages 5-12
    years of age and their families in East Little Havana. More than 90% of the children attending our program qualify for free
    meals through the child care food program. More than 95% are Spanish speaking and approximately 72% were from
    single-family homes.
    Centro Mater aims to serve 100 children 10% of whom have an identified developmental delay and 50 parents throughout
    the 52 week program. The program will also provide trainings for all families registered.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Through professional and personal development programs, Centro Mater strives to empower children and their families
    to pursue opportunities that will lead them to success and growth. In addition, the Covid-19 crisis has caused significant
    disruption to children's daily lives and increased the learning loss and disparities. Our goal is to be the catalysts that
    enable these families to rise above the poverty level, thereby ensuring our entire community reaps the rewards.
    Extending support services to parents, guardians, and families allows the families to identify their needs and establish a
    goal.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Termination of contract due to non-compliance.
