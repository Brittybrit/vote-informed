4. Project/Program Description
    Florida Early Learning Corps combines the people power of AmeriCorps with evidence-based practices to fill the
   opportunity gap in Florida through individualized literacy and numeracy intervention support to PreK students. The program
   will recruit, train and place Americorps members in the highest need zip codes to deliver a data-based, problem-solving
   model of literacy instruction to children age 3 to 5.
   Funding will expand Florida Early Learning Corps' geographic reach and impact on the early literacy and numeracy skill-
   building of our state's youngest learners. An appropriation amount of $1M will facilitate Florida Early Learning Corps'
   expansion from 120 to 145 tutors who will serve more than 2,000 at-risk students, ensuring they are prepared for success
   in Kindergarten.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funding will expand Florida Early Learning Corps' geographic reach and impact on the early literacy and numeracy skill-
    building of our state's youngest learners. An appropriation amount of $1M will facilitate Florida Early Learning Corps'
    expansion from 120 to 145 tutors who will serve more than 2,000 at-risk students, ensuring they are prepared for success
    in Kindergarten.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Early Learning Corps is a proven model that accelerates literacy skills and Kindergarten readiness for high-need
    preschoolers. Full-time tutors work with PreK teachers to implement evidence-based interventions, individualized tutoring
    plans, formative assessments at least three times per year to ensure growth, extensive data systems that provide quality
    assurance, and multi-layer coaching.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The instructional model focuses on integrating the “Big Five Essential Early Literacy Predictors outlined by the National
    Early Literacy Panel (2008) into all aspects of the daily classroom routine. The program includes an immersive “push-in”
    component, where tutors provide 1) whole-class literacy enrichment for all students, 2) targeted small group experiences,
    and 3) one-on-one interventions.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     In a state where more than 18% of children live in poverty, Florida Early Learning Corps serves those who are among
    the most at risk: 96% of Florida Early Learning Corps students are eligible to receive free and reduced price lunch, an
    indicator of poverty. This is higher than the average of Florida students overall (56.6%) (M-DCPS Statistical Highlights,
    2021- 2022). Our 145 tutors will each serve 15 students resulting in more than 2,000 students receiving individualized
    tutoring support during the 2024-25 school year.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     We expect the following outcomes for student by the end of the 2024-25 school year:
    -70% of 4-5 year old's will meet or exceed their target growth in literacy and early numeracy skills needed for
    Kindergarten
    -80% of children will complete family literacy and numeracy activities at home with a parent or guardian
    -70% of all children will meet or exceed their age-level benchmarks for each skill.
    Student outcomes are measured through the use of the PELI (Preschool Early Learning Indicators) assessment tool as
    well the EMI (Early Math Indicators) assessment tool, which are both administered to students in the fall, winter and
    spring.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The suggested penalties if deliverables or performance measures are not met are loss of funding and impact on eligibility
    for future funding.
