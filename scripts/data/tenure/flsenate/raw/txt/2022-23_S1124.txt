1. Project Title          West End Park Enhancements - Miami

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       10/18/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    The ultimate goal of this project is to enhance the current conditions of the West End Park located at 250 SW 60th Ave,
   Miami, FL. The enhancements will improve the quality of life of the citizens. The construction will include enhancements to
   the baseball field, two tennis courts, two basketball courts, a children's splash pad, the pool, a walking trail, exercise
   stations, improvements to the plaza, and security fencing and gate.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will be used to improve the park including: baseball field, walking trail, exercise stations, multi-use field, two tennis
    courts, two basketball courts, children's splash pad, the pool, and security fencing and gate.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Activities to be performed to meet the intended purpose include construction administration, construction engineering,
    inspection, and construction activities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The outcome of the project will enable the citizens to use several amenities at this location. This services will include
    sports facilities for adults and children; water facilities; green spaces; a public plaza; parking; restrooms; and shaded
    areas among other services.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     City of Miami residents and visitors.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improve physical health: measured by the number of citizens using the sport facilities. Enrich cultural experience:
    measured by the number of events coordinated at the park. Improve quality of education: measured by the number of
    students participating in educational summer/winter camps.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?
