    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     These funds will be used for construction of the Biscayne-Everglades Greenway (BEG) Segment D, a 2.5-mile segment
    of the greenway between SW 117th Ave. and SW 137th Ave. along the Mowry Canal and SW 320th St. in southern
    Miami-Dade County, east of Homestead. The Biscayne-Everglades Greenway will be the first non-motorized trail in the
    country to connect two National Parks and would have an immediate impact to Homestead and the surrounding
    communities in southern Miami-Dade County.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     These funds will contribute directly to the construction activities of the BEG, which when complete, will provide citizens
    with new access to two National Parks, as well as an alternative mode of transportation and opportunities for physical
    activity and outdoor recreation.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The BEG will provide citizens with facilities for active transportation, outdoor recreation, physical activity, and connection
    to other area trails. This project will be the first non-motorized trail in the country to connect two National Parks and would
    have an immediate economic impact to Homestead and other communities in southern Miami-Dade County.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target populations served by this project include elderly persons, persons with poor physical health, jobless persons,
    economically disadvantaged persons, at-risk youth, physically disabled, grade school students, high school students,
    university/college students, bicyclists, pedestrians, and other non-motorized transportation users. The project will be
    easily accessible to the 127,000+ residents in the immediate vicinity of the Biscayne-Everglades Greenway (based on
    2020 Census Tract data), as well as the thousands of people who take advantage of the county’s other trails that will
    connect to the Biscayne-Everglades Greenway.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project will improve physical and mental health (measured by Health Impact Assessment), benefit cultural
    experiences (measured by attendance/usage), improve environmental quality (measured by Heat Index), protect the
    general public from harm via pedestrian safety (measured by collision data), improve transportation conditions (measured
    by Traffic Impact Studies), increase economic activities (measured by RTC report), increase tourism (measured by pre-
    and post-build sales tax generated), create specific immediate job opportunities (measured by number of jobs the project
    generates).
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Nonpayment until deliverables are met.
