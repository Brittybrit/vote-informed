4. Project/Program Description
    This requested funding will be utilized to expand K-12 Red Ribbon Certified Schools in Miami Dade County. This nationally
   recognized evidence-based program currently provides year-round hands-on substance abuse prevention education and
   will be expanded by 20 additional schools. The program provides prevention initiatives to engage students, parents,
   teachers and school administrators on creating healthy behavioral change. The annual campaigns include Family Day, the
   Red Ribbon Campaign, Lock Your Meds prescription drug prevention, and Safe Homes Safe Parties.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The expansion of Red Ribbon Certified Schools will provide comprehensive year-round substance and opioid abuse
    prevention education, parent training, and school-based prevention activities. The goals of the program include improving
    school climate, enhancing protection factors and preventing opioid and substance abuse prevention by students as
    determined by quantified measurements. Measurements of achievement will be provided through evaluations conducted
    by University of Central Florida and Florida Atlantic University.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The program provides year-round hands-on prevention campaigns to engage students, parents, teachers and school
    personnel in creating healthy behaviors as measured by evaluations by University of Central Florida and Florida Atlantic
    University. Evidence based materials, information, education programs, recognitions, parent trainings and ongoing
    support are provided to each school through school mentors.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Direct hands-on services are provided to each school on an ongoing basis. Services impact students, teachers, parents
    and school personnel in 20 schools in Miami Dade County. Three areas of program impact include 1) ongoing monitors to
    support individual schools, including materials and educational information (2) events and initiatives highlighting the
    dangers of substance misuse (3) evidence based educational workshops for parents and students.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population are the students, teachers, parents and school personnel in an additional 20 Dade County schools
    k-12, more than 10,000 individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Red Ribbon Certified Schools programs improve engagement, safety and the environment within the schools. Students
    gain knowledge regarding the danger of opioid and other substance abuse. The University of Central Florida and Florida
    International University will provide evaluations of the program utilizing recognized standards of measurement for student
    and school improvement.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Cancellation of the contract.
