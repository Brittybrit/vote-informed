1. Project Title           Public Library Construction - Homestead

2. Senate Sponsor         Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request        01/27/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    The City of Homestead is seeking to enhance the Cybrarium facility with additional spaces allocated for community flex
   space that can be used to house local cultural programs, host library programs, and serve as an affordable community
   events space. The city recently completed the shell of a four story facility and is nearing completion of the final build-out of
   the first and second floors that will house the library, virtual reality cube, children's theater, book mountain, and steampunk
   teen tech lounge. The proposed enhancements in this request will be located on the third floor shell.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The City of Homestead is seeking to enhance the Cybrarium facility with additional spaces allocated for community flex
    space that can be used to house local cultural programs, host library programs, and serve as an affordable community
    events space.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The community flex space will house local cultural programs, host library programs, and serve as an affordable
    community events space.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The community flex space will house local cultural programs, host library programs, and serve as an affordable
    community events space. The free wifi and technology available also helps to bridge the "digital divide."
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This project will benefit 100% of the city's service area. The city serves areas outside of city limits (unincorporated Miami-
    Dade County). The city expects to serve more than 1,084,841 residents of Homestead and Miami-Dade County who are
    currently registered library users.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will

     The community flex space will house local cultural programs, host library programs, and serve as an affordable
    community events space. Increased cultural programming is one of the main purposes of building out the additional
    meeting and community flex space. Space usage and event attendance will be tracked to show increased availability of
    these experiences. The Cybrarium will partner with local schools to provide unique programming and enhanced
    extracurricular programming. The number and content of partnerships with local schools will be tracked and reported.
    In addition, jobs will be created because additional staff will need to be hired at the Cybrarium in order to administer this
    additional space.

     Standard contract penalties will be sufficient.
