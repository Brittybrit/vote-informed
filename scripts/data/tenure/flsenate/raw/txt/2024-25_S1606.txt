14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Stormwater Improvement Project addresses water retention challenges, the impacts of sea level rising, and an aging
    drainage system causing system erosion and inadequate capacity, causing higher storm flooding.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This project entails drainage improvements to sub-basin U35-S as studied in the Village's Stormwater Master Plan.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Reduce the number of repetitive loss claims and documented home flooding.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Residents of the Village of Pinecrest along sub basin U35-S and the nearby area.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project will improve the drainage/water retention in the affected area, reducing the number of claims, damage to
    homes, environmental impacts, and the safety of residents directly and indirectly affected. Additionally, this project will
    mitigate the damage after a storm event. The methodology that will be used to measure the outcome is the number of
    homes without flooding, number of repetitive losses in that area, flood volume reduction.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Any deemed appropriate.
