4. Project/Program Description
    Youth born into environments where resources, hope, and positive role models are lacking struggle to navigate the stress
   of everyday life. They are often forced to choose survival above the opportunity to develop academically, socially,
   emotionally, creatively, and civically. Founded in 2008, Guitars Over Guns Organization, Inc. (GOGO) believes every child
   deserves access to a fair shot in life, the opportunity to self-actualize, and to contribute meaningfully to society. Our Anti-
   Violence Music and Mentorship program currently serves 1,000 youth living below the poverty line in Miami-Dade County
   through after-school, weekend, spring break, and summer programming, all free of charge. Historically, 96% of our
   students either maintain or improve their GPA over the course of the year and 97% increase their Positive Youth
   Development Inventory. Based on projections, this program will expand available services by 35%, providing free
   programing to 350 additional youth living in poverty.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    GOGO seeks to launch three new community studios, strategically located in communities of Miami where violence is
    most rampant.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Leveraging the power of music, mentorship, and baked-in mental health interventions to create holistic change, each
    community studio will be staffed with a local, in-community music producer to provide hands-on mentorship and arts
    instruction, along with a “healing artist", much like an art therapist, to further support and foster mentees' voices. Studios
    will be open 6 days per week, including after school and Saturdays–times when youth are statistically most likely to
    become involved in violent crime. Students will access a safe space to explore, create, and find caring support systems
    on a drop-in basis.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     In addition to music instruction, academic and goal check-ins, and positive youth development, studios will specifically
    formalize pathways that guide youth toward gainful employment, and establish organizational partnerships with
    community resource-providers to address mental health, housing, and food insecurity. GOGO seeks to bridge the critical
    gap between our current programs and the needed guidance and wraparound services to take their next steps in life.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     11-21 year olds living in poverty. We expect to serve 350 youth.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     80% of youth demonstrate positive youth development gains according to survey and data collection partner
    HelloInsight. 80% of youth demonstrate positive academic progress (improving academic standing and/or maintaining at
    a 3.5 GPA or higher) according to school records. 80% of youth demonstrate increased school attendance rates progress
    (improving attendance to 85%+) according to school records. 80% of youth score above 60 on the C-GAS (Children's
    Global Assessment Scale), progress 5 to 10 points, or actively participate in more intensive support (twice monthly).
    100% of youth scoring below 60 each year will participate in individual sessions twice a month, with 80% of those
    students increasing 5 to 10 points after 10 individual sessions.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Penalties for not meeting the contracted deliverables may warrant decreased funding.
