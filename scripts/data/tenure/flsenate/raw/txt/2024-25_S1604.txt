4. Project/Program Description
    This project entails the milling and resurfacing of approximately one mile of roads that were left unrestored after the
   completion of the water installation project. These roads were adjacent to that installation, but not a direct part of the
   restoration that took place. It addresses concerns over the disintegration of roads that may lead to safety hazards.

     The milling and resurfacing of approximately one mile of roads that were left unrestored after the completion of the water
    installation project. These roads were adjacent to that installation, but not a direct part of the restoration that took place.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     It will directly serve the residents of the Village of Pinecrest that live adjacent to the roads that will be resurfaced;
    however it will indirectly serve all of the users of the roads that not only live in Pinecrest but throughout Miami-Dade
    County.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     It addresses concerns over the disintegration of roads that may lead to safety hazards. The measurement will be the
    roads built to the same specifications as those that were recently resurfaced through the water line installation project.
