4. Project/Program Description
    Funds will be used to restore and repair historic and scientific offerings on the island of Pigeon Key. It will help to preserve
   the cultural history of Pigeon Key and the Florida Keys for visitors to enjoy from around the world. This appropriation would
   fund needed repairs to several historic structures such as the Paint Foreman's Building, Assistant Bridge Tender's Building,
   Bridge Foreman's Building, Bridge Tenders Building, Kitchen Building, and Section Gang Quarters.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will be used to restore, repair, and enhance the historic and scientific offerings on the island of Pigeon Key,
    preserving the cultural history of Pigeon Key and the Florida Keys for visitors to enjoy from around the world. This
    appropriation would fund necessary repairs to several historic structures including the Paint Foreman's Building, Assistant
    Bridge Tender's Building, Bridge Foreman's Building, Bridge Tenders Building, Kitchen Building, and Section Gang
    Quarters
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds will be used as a part of this project will contribute to the daily historic tours of the island, educational programs for
    students from all around the world, and events offered throughout the year to locals and visitors alike.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Preservation and enhancement of the last congregation of Flagler Railway era buildings in South Florida.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Elderly persons, at-risk youth, physically disabled, grade school students, high school students, college/university
    students, and the general population. Serving more than 800 individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Preserving historic structures and the cultural heritage of the Florida Keys. Using annual tracking of students and visitors
    to the island. Provide high end educational programs in marine science to students from around the world. These
    students will become the next generation of biologists protecting and preserving the environment. By annual tracking of
    student visitors and programs. Research conducted on the island with many universities and organizations to study and
    improve water quality, fish stocks, and pharmaceutical drug improvements. Tracking the number of research projects as
    well as water quality around the island. Providing a safe and aesthetically pleasing experience for our tourists and locals
    alike that showcases the unique history of the island and its structures. Tracking of annual admission.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Withholding of funds.
