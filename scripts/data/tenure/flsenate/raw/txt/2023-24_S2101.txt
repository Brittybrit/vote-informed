1. Project Title          Brooksville Replacement Street Sweeper

2. Senate Sponsor        Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request       02/20/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The City of Brooksville currently utilizes a 2013 Mechanical Street Sweeper to clean the streets throughout the City. This
   unit has been used effectively used for the last 9 years; however, it has reached the end of its useful life and needs to be
   replaced with a new unit that has upgraded technology and capabilities. As the City continues grow and more garbage and
   debris are introduced to the area, new and upgraded technology is needed to maintain the cleanliness of our streets,
   environment, safety and quality of life for our residents and visitors.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The replacement of our mechanical street sweeper with a new regenerative air street sweeper will greatly increase our
    ability to remove debris and litter from city streets. This increased abilities will result in reduced potential for flooding,
    improved water quality of our stormwater runoff, reduced potential for contamination of our ground water, improved
    appearance of our community as well as improved quality of life and environment for our residents and visitors.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Purchase of new, upgraded street sweeper.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Maintaining the cleanliness of the city streets. Every piece of debris and garbage that is removed from the streets and
    disposed of properly results in a cleaner environment and more aesthetically pleasing community. The equipment can
    remove hazardous materials including metals, organics, and other harmful nutrients from the environment. Additionally,
    removal of debris and liquids provide increased safety for pedestrians, vehicles, runners and cyclist that could become
    impediments while traveling. Long term benefits in addition to preservation of the environment, include preservation of the
    pavement as well as wildlife.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All citizens and visitors of the City, population est. 9,481, and visitors may benefit from this project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improved quality of life and the environment through reduction in debris, garbage and other harmful materials. This
    benefits/outcomes will be measured by the ability to increase the maintenance of city streets with a reliable, efficient
    machine and the reduction of street flooding which reduces contaminated water runoff.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Withhold payment.
