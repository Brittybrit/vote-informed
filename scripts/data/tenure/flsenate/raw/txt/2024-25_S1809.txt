    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Integrate public datasets for multivariate statistical analysis and visual projection to identify trends, quality assure data
    sets, and create visualization tools for public eduction and decision-makers & managers. The results will inform the
    development of the Reasonable Assurance Plan and the Watershed Plan with FDEP support
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This is funding that would go to a company to help identify point source pollution. It would be similar to the effort taking
    place in Crystal River: https://www.sas.com/el_gr/news/press-releases/2022/august/florida-water-quality.html
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The data gleaned from these efforts will help support water quality improvement project outcomes and future funding
    requests.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Biscayne Bay and its watershed (Miami-Dade, Broward, and Monroe counties). Millions of individuals will be served
    indirectly.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Analyze the County's and other publicly-available data sets in a multivariate approach spatially & temporally to look for
    patterns and trends in water quality pollution; visualize DEP Grant milestones; visualize said data analysis for
    managers/decision-makers/scientists/public; inform the County's development of a Reasonable Assurance Plan; support
    goals of the County's Biscayne Bay Task Force Report & inform development of the Biscayne Bay Watershed
    Management Advisory Board's Watershed Plan
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
