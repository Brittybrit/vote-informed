1. Project Title          Homestead Electrical Backup Infrastructure

2. Senate Sponsor         Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request        11/24/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    In order to prevent any disruption in electrical power during a critical event, a second back-up electrical infrastructure
   circuit will be installed from an electrical substation about a mile away and running underground to the facility. The back-up
   will automatically switch on should the main feed experience a problem. This will provide more stability to the city’s
   electrical infrastructure and will also increase electric capacity.

     General (The majority of funds will benefit no specific group). This is a back-up circuit into a city facility that regularly
    sponsors events for the public where tens of thousands of people attend. The second circuit will provide redundancy to
    the facility and will also add electrical capacity to the area and increase reliability for approximately 2,000 residents in the
    area.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     In order to prevent any disruption in electrical power during a critical event, a second back-up electrical infrastructure
    circuit will be installed from an electrical substation about a mile away and running underground to the facility. The back-
    up will automatically switch on should the main feed experience a problem. This will provide more stability to the city’s
    electrical infrastructure and will also increase electric capacity. The outcome will be measured by the successful
    installation of the second circuit as well as by the percent of times that power is successfully switched over to the back-up
    circuit in the event of a power outage.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Revocation of funding if the project is not completed. Standard contract penalties are sufficient.
