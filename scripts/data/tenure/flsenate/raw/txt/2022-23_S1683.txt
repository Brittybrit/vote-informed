12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal of JHF is offer vocational programs to people with any type of developmental and physical disability. Through
    our programs it will allow us to discover talents and train them to be productive people in society. Within our programs we
    have, Chef and Bakery Assistant, Agronomy (farmer) Tech, Arts and IT in radio and production.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     JHF will provide technical training for people with any type of disability. Therefore, we carry out the following activities:
    - Vocational programs with face-to-face classes and trainings. - Supportive therapies within vocational programs with
    specialized personnel who support the progress and rehabilitation of students. - Education through civic engagement, to
    develop their workforce, know, respect and obey the laws of our nation, for their participation in society and their
    compliance as citizens.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Vocational Programs:
    - Each careers curriculum based on Florida laws educational department in compliance with the require hours in order to
    acquire the state certification such as Chef and Bakery Assistant, Agronomy (farmer) Tech, Arts and IT in radio and
    production.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Our target population is people with any type of mental or physical disability. We have a database of people with
    disabilities who hope to take a vocational program with JHF and we hope to serve more than 200 people.
