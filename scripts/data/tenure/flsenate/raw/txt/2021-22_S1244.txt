4. Project/Program Description
    With an economy in recession and the increased vulnerability of the older adult population to the novel coronavirus, food
   solutions specifically targeting the needs of low-income seniors are more crucial than ever. Low-income older adults that
   are homebound but that do not have physical limitations that prevent or make difficult cooking at home will receive
   deliveries of nutritionally appropriate grocery items. Our intake team will note any specific health considerations to be taken
   into account to address chronic health conditions. Miami-Dade metro area, which includes Fort Lauderdale and Palm
   Beach, has among the highest senior food insecurity rates of major metropolitan areas in the nation at 8.9%, has been
   among the hardest hit by the COVID-19 pandemic, and the demographic trends indicate that senior food insecurity will
   continue to rise in our region.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Prior to the pandemic, we knew that senior grocery deliveries were a needed service in our community. COVID
    exacerbated this need and with CARES funding due to expire, Feeding South Florida (FSF) is seeking to fill this gap in
    service more permanently. At a cost of $77 per delivery, FSF can provide a senior grocery delivery service to serve a
    maximum of 1,000 households per month or 50 deliveries per day with the support of this funding.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     1. Seniors are screened for food insecurity and level of need.
    2. Health considerations are noted and taken into account in planning.
    3. Grocery boxes are packed at FSF's facility and delivered by our fleet of vans.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     1. Nutritious food: produce, protein, dairy, bread, and shelf stable items like rice, peanut butter.
    2. Medically-tailored grocery boxes.

    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The Feeding South Florida Senior Grocery Delivery Service targets home-bound, food-insecure older adults. At an
    annual household cost of $77 per delivery, Feeding South Florida can expand our grocery delivery service to serve a
    maximum of 1,000 households with the support of this funding.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     1. Improve health: Participants will report improved progress toward target mental and physical health indicators, tracked
    in our client intake database, Oasis. 2. Improve agricultural production/ promotion/education:Increased support of Florida
    farmers by purchasing additional produce. FSF uses the warehouse management system Primarius to track the number
    of pounds of locally sourced food products that are used in the boxes as well as the number of pounds of food that are
    distributed. 3. Create specific, immediate job opportunities: FSF will create 2 new jobs immediately to staff the program.
    The number of individuals hired specifically to staff the program will be reported on by FSF Human Resources. 4.
    Enhance specific individual’s economic self sufficiency: Clients will see a reduction or elimination in overall household
    budget shortfall which will be measured by monitoring the pounds of food distributed through the program against the
    average cost per meal in South Florida.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Deliverables are calculated on a unit cost basis. Contract funds can be deducted at a unit cost rate for under-
    performance.
