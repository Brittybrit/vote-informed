4. Project/Program Description
    The Town of Cutler Bay seeks $3,000,000 to implement its Lakes by the Bay Drainage Project to improve water quality
   and mitigate flooding in a hazard area. The project area includes five lakes and experiences flooding during heavy rain
   events. An engineer's assessment conducted during the development of the Town's adopted Stormwater Master Plan
   found that existing drainage infrastructure does not treat or discharge adequate runoff to meet the desired performance
   criteria. The Town proposes to install approximately 15,135 linear feet of new drainage systems, including exfiltration
   trenches, to improve water quality in the project area. Drainage improvements will mitigate flooding leading to collector
   roadways, public transportation, and emergency evacuation routes on SW 216 Street and Old Cutler Road. Additionally,
   the project incorporates enhancements to improve accessibility under the Americans with Disabilities Act and increase the
   safety of pedestrians and bicyclists.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The proposed project aims to mitigate flooding by installing a new and larger capacity drainage system, protect the
    groundwater table by adding water quality enhancement to the drainage system, and improve emergency response by
    reducing flooding along the Town's evacuation routes and public transit corridors.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The drainage improvements include installing 15,135 linear feet of new drainage including exfiltration trenches, solid
    pipes, and drainage structures. Additionally, roadway restoration activities include milling and resurfacing, sidewalk and
    accessible ramp replacement (ADA improvements), striping, and signage. Portions of the project area will receive
    continuous valley gutters and driveway approaches on both sides of the street to manage stormwater runoff
    (approximately 2,000 linear feet).
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The proposed project is included in the Town's adopted Stormwater Master Plan. Additionally, the project is supported in
    part by the Town's American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) funding allocation. The Town hosted a series of seven public
    workshops to discuss the Town's vision and ARPA spending plan. This project was one of the priority areas as the
    community experiences regular flooding. In June 2022, Cutler Bay experienced a 100-year rain event resulting in nearly
    24" of rain. The residents in the Lakes by the Bay Community were among those hit the hardest, creating a renewed
    sense of urgency.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The project is a part of the Town of Cutler Bay's comprehensive approach to Stormwater Management and water quality
    improvement. The Town of Cutler Bay's population is 43,958 (Census 2022) The census tract in which the project falls
    has an estimated population of 10,561.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The project protects residents from environmental harm by increasing the drainage system's capacity to manage
    stormwater runoff. Planned improvements provide water quality treatment leading to a 92.7% reduction in Total
    Phosphorus, 89.5% reduction in Total Nitrogen, and 90.6% reduction in Total Suspended Solids. Finally, the project
    enhances the Town's emergency response efforts by protecting critical public infrastructure.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The Town will not be reimbursed for project activities that do not meet the deliverables or performance measures.
