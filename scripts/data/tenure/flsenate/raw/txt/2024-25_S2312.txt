2. Senate Sponsor       Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request      12/19/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The Sea Pines Neighborhood Flood Abatement Project is a flood-control project located in Hudson, Florida within the
   overall Hammock Creek Watershed, west of U.S. Highway 19, east of Old Dixie Highway and north of Gulf Way. The
   project drainage area is approximately 366 acres. The Sea Pines neighborhood suffers from flooding events due to
   inadequate conveyance and storage of stormwater. The proposed project consists of the design and construction for a new
   stormwater conveyance systems and storage ponds within the Sea Pines neighborhood.
