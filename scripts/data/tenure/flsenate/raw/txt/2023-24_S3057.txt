2. Senate Sponsor         Ana Maria Rodriguez

4. Project/Program Description
    As a local fire department, Miami-Dade Fire Rescue (MDFR) is the leading organization in Miami-Dade County regarding
   fire safety, detection, and notification. The goal of this project is to upgrade Miami-Dade Fire Rescue's (MDFR) current
   smoke and fire alarm system to a more compliant version based on current local, state, and federal fire regulations. The
   objective is to continue perfecting the detection of fire and smoke emergencies at fire stations, and protect staff accordingly
   while promoting firefighter's safety. This upgraded system will be implemented in (9) MDFR fire stations and be monitored
   by a specialized office, communicating directly with MDFR emergency response staff as needed.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The aim of this project is to upgrade Miami-Dade Fire Rescue's (MDFR) current fire station smoke and fire alarm system
    to a more compliant version based on current local, state, and federal fire regulations. The goal is to continue perfecting
    the detection of fire and smoke emergencies at these stations, and protect staff accordingly. This upgraded system will be
    implemented in (9) MDFR fire stations and be monitored by a specialized office, communicating directly with MDFR
    emergency response staff as needed.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     MDFR will utilize these funds to better protect and serve its first responders in the event of a fire emergency by
    upgrading current fire and smoke alarm systems to meet local, state, and federal standards. The new system will detect
    smoke and fire emergencies more efficiently and be monitored by an off-site office, assisting with the overall protection of
    MDFR staff members.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens of Miami-Dade County will benefit from this project as it will assist in the protection of emergency personnel
    housed at respective fire stations throughout Miami-Dade County. Emergency teams will then have more time and energy
    to focus on responding to emergency calls as opposed to dwelling on outdated fire and smoke detection equipment in
    their fire stations.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Miami-Dade County, specifically MDFR, which is about 2.7 million residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     By upgrading current fire and smoke alarm systems, MDFR can guarantee a safer environment for their personnel at
    stations in regard to fire safety and observance. Should a fire occur during staff's sleeping hours, and the alarm system
    not detect it in time, a loss of staff and millions of dollars in essential emergency equipment can transpire. Thus, causing
    unnecessary injuries, potential loss of life, and damage to emergency response equipment and resources that can total
    approximately $2 million per station. Having an upgraded and modern alarm system to detect fire and smoke occurrences
    more efficiently can also ease some of the burden MDFR staff face in regard to emotional trauma. Lastly, ensuring the
    protection of MDFR personnel during station fires or smoke emergencies, through the upgrade of new alarm systems,
    can boost efficiency and precision as MDFR staff can focus on efficient emergency response.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Funds could be provided on a reimbursement basis after services are performed.
