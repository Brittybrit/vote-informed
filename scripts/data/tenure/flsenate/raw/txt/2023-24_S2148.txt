4. Project/Program Description
    As our Pasco County community continues to grow, so do the demands for quality health and well-being programs. To
   help meet these needs, the Tampa YMCA will construct a new health and wellness family center in Wesley Chapel. The
   new Y in Wesley Chapel will include a 30,000 sq ft wellness facility as well as a 30,000 sq ft outdoor aquatic center. Over
   40,000 families live within minutes of the proposed site for this new YMCA.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Construct a new health and wellness family center in Wesley Chapel to help fill gaps in community needs. This facility
    will be designed to teach kids how to swim and be safe around water; to provide seniors opportunities to improve their
    overall health, make friends and combat loneliness; to create a path for youth to overcome summer learning loss and
    develop lifelong experiences during summer camp; to help those battling chronic diseases make gains; to offer youth
    more activites that can prevent childhood obesity; bring families together; and so much more for the communities to
    become healthier in spirit, mind, and body.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This facility will provide welcoming, fully accessible environments for people of all abilities/ages; safe, positive
    environments for more youth/teens to build character/values through afterschool/teen/sports programs; more
    opportunities for fun/safety in the water; evidence-based programs/initiatives that address chronic disease caused by
    lifestyle choices; flexible gathering spaces for community to connect; effective/high quality programs/services for first-time
    exercisers and their families; support for people of all ages in their pursuit of a healthier lifestyle; and more opportunites
    for families to bond together.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     A variety of programs for adults that promote wellness, reduce risk of chronic disease, and assistance for those battling
    disease to reclaim their health. Opportunities for youth to cultivate values, skills, and relationships that lead to positive
    behaviors, chracter development, better health and educational achievement. Innovative comunity-based solutions that
    work to prevent childhood drowning. Specifically, Livestrong at the Y for Cancer Survivors, Parkinsons Management
    programs, Diabetes Prevention programs, Swim Lessons, Youth Sports, Teen Leadership programs, Summer Day Camp,
    Before and Afterschool programming, Senior Wellness programs, Food Distribution, Community Volunteer Initiatives, etc.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Children, teens, adults, senior citizens, cancer survivors, chronically ill community members, at-risk youth, infants and
    toddlers throughout the Wesley Chapel and Pasco County.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Initially, the actual construction of the Y facility. Long-term benefits include improving the overall health and well-being of
    the Wesley Chapel community members as well as helping to close the Learning Achievement Gap in youth, providing
    programs that teach life-saving skills and help prevent drowning of youth and teens. Participants will be surveyed to
    determine growth in overall physical wellness, educational enrichment, and safety.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     If the new facility in Wesley Chapel is not constructed, the Y would have to return the funding back to the state.
