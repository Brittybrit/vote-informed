14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     These funds will ensure the protection of the Harry S. Truman Little White House, Florida's only presidential museum.
    The installation of new hurricane shutter system will provide additional security and protection for the state owned propety
    from storms. It will also ensure the preservation of the property. The museum currently applies plywood to cover windows
    during oncoming storms/hurricanes.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     A new hurricane shutter system will eliminate time spent prepping for hurricanes and assist in protecting this historic
    facility.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Preservation of the state-owned historic property, ensuring that generations to come will have this opportunity to visit and
    interact with this piece of history.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The general public.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Benefits will include enriching cultural experiences, improved economic activity, increased tourism, and immediate job
    opportunities. Ways to measure this outcome will include monitoring visitors before and after project and completion of
    project tasks.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Witholding of funds.
