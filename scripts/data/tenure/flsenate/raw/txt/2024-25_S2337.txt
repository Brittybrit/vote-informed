1. Project Title          Miami NW 13th Street Roadway Reconstruction and Drainage
                          Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request       12/14/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Located within District 1, this project encompasses reconstruction of the NW 13th Street from NW 35th Avenue to NW 37th
   Avenue, including storm water drainage improvements. The City will be partnering with Miami-Dade County Water and
   Sewer Department,to provide water main upgrades. The streets will also be widened to reduce potential vehicular traffic
   and pedestrian safety conflicts/hazards in the existing very narrow streets. This project will increase flood protection and
   provide safe routes to schools and parks for area residents.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Improve pedestrian and vehicular safety, protect home and property from the perils of flood, improve the quality of life for
    the residents.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Design, construction, and project administration of 1236 linear feet of 8-inch ductile iron water main.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This project will enhance overall pedestrian and vehicular safety.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population are the residents of NW 13th Street between 35th and 37th Avenue. The project is expected to
    serve approximately 13,000 residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improved pedestrian and vehicular safety, protect home and property from the perils of flood, improve the quality of life
    for
    the residents. This will be measured by comparing the number of vehicular and pedestrian accidents pre to post
    construction; comparing the number of flooding complaints before and after the project using sensors and on-site
    inspections.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables or performance measures should subject the City to an audit and depending on the results
    of the audit the City could be liable for sanctions up to revocation of funding.
