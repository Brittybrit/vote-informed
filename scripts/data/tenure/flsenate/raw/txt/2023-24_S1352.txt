4. Project/Program Description
    JCS Delivers is a tailored grocery delivery program, which provides food and household essentials to vulnerable,
   extremely high-risk senior clients by way of door-to-door grocery deliveries. Seniors also benefit by working with a personal
   shopper, who check in with clients regularly to reduce social isolation and identify other unmet needs.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Due to growing food insufficiency of our senior clients, JCS created and implemented a tailored grocery delivery
    program, JCS Delivers, which continues to see increased demand. This program offers up to bi-monthly grocery
    deliveries, valued at up to $200 for each delivery, delivered straight to the door of homebound and/or low-income seniors.
    The funds requested will be used to increase current staff hours and hire new staff to enroll more seniors and meet
    increased demand.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     JCS Delivers’ clients provide a list of grocery and household essentials to a personal shopper who has these items
    delivered. As an added benefit to JCS Delivers clients, personal shoppers check in and can refer clients to social workers,
    as needed, for additional support. The delivery of groceries is valued up to $200 per delivery.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     See above.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is low income and/or homebound seniors (ages 60 and older) assessed by a social worker to have
    a high nutrition risk.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The main goal of this project is to increase food security for our most vulnerable seniors. Increased food security will be
    measured using clients' self-reported data on satisfaction surveys. The total number of groceries delivered and
    unduplicated client counts will be tracked monthly.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Without access to the life sustaining, food items and household goods provided by the program, vulnerable seniors in
    Miami will not have access to adequate nutrition. Many homebound seniors rely specifically on the delivery of groceries
    as their primary source of nutrition on any given day.
