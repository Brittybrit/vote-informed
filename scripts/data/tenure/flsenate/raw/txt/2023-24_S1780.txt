1. Project Title         Card Sound Road Safety Improvement Project (Miami-Dade County)

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       02/17/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    These funds will be used to install critically needed safety improvements along Card Sound Road to help reduce the
   number of accidents and fatalities along the corridor. Card Sound Road serves as a main point of entry to the Florida Keys
   from Miami-Dade County and serves as an important evacuation route (an alternate route in the event of accidents/fires).
   More than 200 accidents and 12 fatalities have occurred on Card Sound Road in the last five years.

     These funds will be used to install critically needed safety improvements along Card Sound Road to help reduce the
    number of accidents and fatalities along the corridor. Card Sound Road serves as a main point of entry to the Florida
    Keys from Miami-Dade County and serves as an important evacuation route (an alternate route in the event of
    accidents/fires). More than 200 accidents and 12 fatalities have occurred on Card Sound Road in the last five years.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds will be used to implement the improvement plan created by traffic engineers and will include additional signage,
    lighting, striping, and other safety improvements.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will directly benefit from increased safety features along the roadway which will reduce the number of accidents
    and fatalities that occur.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The project will provide safety features for all users entering and exiting the Florida Keys.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Monitor the number of accidents before and after the installation of improvements. Survey of employees who take Card
    Sound daily to determine the impact of the improvements on drivers' mental health. Monitor the number of accidents
    before and after the installation of improvements. Calculate the number of jobs created by the project.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Funds will not be provided until deliverables are met.
