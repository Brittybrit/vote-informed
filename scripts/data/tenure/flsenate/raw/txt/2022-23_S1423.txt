12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To ensure that users of the Ludlam Trail can remain digitally connected to our modern world, Miami-Dade County seeks
    to provide high-speed fiber infrastructure along the trail to fulfill the Ludlam Trail’s promise of becoming a world-class
    recreation and connectivity hub.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The installation of high-speed fiber infrastructure along the Ludlam Trail to benefit trail users and adjacent residents.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     High-speed fiber infrastructure along the Ludlam Trail to benefit trail users and adjacent residents by providing high
    speed internet connectivity, Wi-Fi, and network connections for security cameras and other features within the Ludlam
    Trail corridor.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Trail users and adjacent residents. Approximately 32,000 residents within two miles of the corridor and a safe route to
    school for over 3,700 students.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     High-quality internet access for trail users, as well as network connectivity for security measures such as video cameras,
    emergency phones, and pedestrian counters for measuring trail use.
