4. Project/Program Description
    The City of Brooksville has been utilizing a Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) system for several years
   now which permits remote communications between our outlying assets (lift station, well sites, and water distribution sites).
   SCADA is a control system architecture comprised of computers, networked data communications and graphical user
   interfaces for high-level supervision of machines and processes. It also covers sensors and other devices, such as
   programmable logic controllers, which interface with process plant or machinery. The operator interfaces enables
   monitoring and the issuing of process commands, for instance, controller set point changes, are handled through the
   SCADA computer system. The City's current system is in dire need of technological updates as well as hardware
   replacement due to exposure to the elements.
