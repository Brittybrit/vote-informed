4. Project/Program Description
    A Stormwater Master Plan is a comprehensive stormwater management plan which addresses stormwater runoff and
   residue from rain/hurricane events. The goal of Stormwater Master Plan is to help the City understand our current state of
   the stormwater system and recommended environmentally comprehensive and resilient storm drain design to address
   current problems and/or areas where future challenges may occur. This plan will further recommend design standards for
   new development/re-development, transportation, water quality, floodplain mitigation, inadequate storm sewer and culvert
   system, cannels and swales.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The master plan will be invaluable to the City as it will be used as a guide to address flooding and stormwater
    management requirements, establish requirement on climate adaptation and recommend projects to ensure we remain
    prepared for future growth and environmental changes.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Procure a consultant to develop a Stormwater Master Plan.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     A comprehensive stormwater plan will allow for the potential to reduce flooding, improve the quality of our stormwater
    runoff, reduce the environmental impact of stormwater runoff, ensure the health and safety of our residents, prepare the
    City for future growth and expansion, and allow the City to better plan for the future.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All citizens and visitors of the City, population est. 9,481, and visitors may benefit from this project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     A comprehensive capital improvement and asset plan for the stormwater utilities. This outcomes will be measured by the
    reduction in negative environmental impacts from stormwater runoff and reduction in threats to the health and safety of
    the residents and public at large.
