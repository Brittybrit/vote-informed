1. Project Title         Bushnell Regional Interconnect

2. Senate Sponsor        Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request       01/08/2024

4. Project/Program Description
    Design and construction to interconnect the City of Bushnell and the Sumterville Water Systems. Sumter County has
   allocated ARPA funds to extend water line up CR475 to open up economic opportunity corridor. Bushnell is seeking funds
   to design and construct extension of proposed water main extension to connect to their Sumterville Water system. The
   Sumterville Water system has a history of poor water quality and connecting it to the Bushnell system will provide a more
   reliable water supply source and water quality to the growing customer base in Sumterville.
