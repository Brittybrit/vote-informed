4. Project/Program Description

   second phase will construct a 4.3 MGD BNR facility to reduce nutrient loading to water bodies and wetlands. Also, these

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project involves construction activities at the City main water reclamation facility. The project has two phases, with
    the first phase making improvements to the existing infrastructure to address aging equipment and operational issues.
    The second phase will construct a 4.3 MGD BNR facility to reduce nutrient loading to water bodies and wetlands. Also,
    these improvements will support residential/commercial growth and economic vitality within the City and Sumter County.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funds will be utilized for engineering design, permitting, and construction of the improvements at the water
    reclamation facility.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Improve wastewater management by improving hydraulic efficiency and operational reliability to help prevent sanitary
    sewer overflows in the near future do to increased flows to the plant.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Existing and future City of Wildwood utility customers. The City currently as 5,946 customers and the City projects 5,000
    new customers (residential and non-residential) in the next 5 to 7 years.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improved economic activity: The provision of wastewater services is necessary for new residential and commercial
    development. Increases in new building permits within the City's utility service area is an indicator of economic activity.
    Project improvements will include biological nutrient reduction (BNR) which will reduce the amount of total nitrogen and
    total phosphorous leaving the WRF. The improvement will reduce the pollutant loading of these parameters to ground
    water under the rapid infiltration basins (RIBs).
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     If the project deliverable were not completed on schedule then the agency could rescind the funds. If the FDEP
    standards/performance measures were not met then the City could be subject to repayment of funds. The project is also
    needed for wastewater concurrency, which will allow the necessary facilities to be constructed to meet the current growth
    and issuing of building permits.
