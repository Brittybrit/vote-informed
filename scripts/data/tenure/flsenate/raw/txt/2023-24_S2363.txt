14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Village Council holds its meetings/public hearings in a Community Center not designed for use as a Council meeting
    space. Some issues are: not enough space for all members of the public interested in attending meetings and the wear
    and tear on technology equipment due to set up and breakdown for each meeting. An old storage building is used as the
    Public Works facility. All vehicles and much equipment must be stored outside in the harsh conditions of the Florida sun
    and salt air.
    The ultimate goal is to construct a new facility, appropriate for the uses of local government meetings and a Public Works
    Facility. The funding request is for the services needed to determine the site, create a site plan, and design the facility.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     A Council Chambers will benefit the public by providing increased indoor access to meetings and can be used by school
    and other groups for education and presentations. Now, overflow crowds are accommodated outside and allowed in one-
    by-one for public comment.
    Public Works vehicles and equipment serve the entire community; indoor storage would increase equipment life span and
    save tax dollars.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Citizens will benefit from the Council Chambers with improved access to the meetings of the Village Council, Boards and
    Committees. The Village would have a place for educational and other programs in a space designed for such use. The
    Community Center will become more available for fitness classes and activities.
    All residents benefit from the Public Works facility by a savings in tax dollars.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target populations to be served by the project as it increases the availability of the Community Center include:
    elderly persons, persons with poor physical health, students of all age levels.
    The project would serve the general public with appropriate meeting space to accommodate more people interested in
    participating in the local government decision-making process and a proper Public Works facility for staff and to house
    equipment.
    The individuals expected to be served would number over 800.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Increased availability of the Community Center for fitness classes and events would result from the removal of
    government meetings from the facility. The increased number of classes and events from previous years would be
    recorded by staff.
    School groups utilizing the Council Chambers for civics classes, debates, etc. as the previous Council Chambers was
    used before being destroyed by Hurricane Wilma in 2005. The number of such events would be recorded by staff.
    Members of the public would no longer have to remain outside if the Council Chambers when 85 or more wish to attend
    the meeting. The number of meetings with over 85 public attendees would be recorded by staff.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The Village would be required to repay any prepaid costs to the State of Florida if the funded activities are not
    completed.
