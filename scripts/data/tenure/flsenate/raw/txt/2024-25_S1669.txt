14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Scholarships for the Jewish pre-school program. It will be used for the re-imagining of what pre-school services are
    beyond just a place for simply child care, more of a place that serves as the center of the community to include other
    aspects that bring in the parents, students, & community as a whole. The other aspect will be the vocational training
    program, preparing adults with disabilities & veterans aged 18 & older for skilled positions in the Kosher culinary &
    hospitality sectors.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     There will be more scholarships available to students in need. Along with programing to re-imagine preschool. Mental
    and emotional development & variety of after school programs for students & parents. The Shul's vocational education
    and training program through it on site commercial kitchen and rooftop catering venue is transformative in its ability to
    empower adults with disabilities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Scholarships, mental and emotional development for students and parents, workshops and training for parents, and a
    variety of after school-programing.
    Our tailored hands-on program provides in kitchen experiences together with our culinary team for participants with
    disabilities encompass culinary education, Food Handler Certification, social interaction skills, & community center
    operations.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Economically disadvantaged persons, developmentally disabled, physically disabled, and Pre-school students. 200
    individuals are expected to be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This will definitely improve the quality and experience of pre-school that students and parents will receive. It will also
    improve the education of the adults with disabilities in the vocational program as they will have enhanced skills that they
    did not have before. The after school programing allows for-parents to have extra time to work and to have economic self-
    sufficiency. The parent trainings also will enhance understanding of parenting and other skills that are needed long term
    for the parents. Outcomes will be assessed through data, reports, job placement rates, employer feedback, pre and post
    surveys.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The return of the funds appropriated.
