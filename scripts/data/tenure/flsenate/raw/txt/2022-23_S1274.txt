4. Project/Program Description
    DMF Employment Opportunities is a 501 (c)(3) non-profit addressing the low employment rate of 19% for individuals with
   intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) through systematic job development that integrates the candidate's
   preferences with the employer's needs to carve out a position for a person with unique abilities. Intensive job training
   ensures transition to stable employment paired with individualized long-term extended supports, including the benefits of
   on-demand counseling. Our unique approach builds on creating effective, communicative relationships with Employer
   Partners to reduce the obstacles, trepidations and fears of hiring individuals with IDD by offering expanded services beyond
   the traditional 90 & 150 day support models mandated by federal guidelines. The model follows a five-step process that
   aligns with the evidence-based practice of Customized Employment.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will specifically help sustain extended services integral to maintaining long-term employment outcomes for
    individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD). An investment in extended services yields positive
    employment outcomes for young adults with IDD, which leads to economic growth and less dependence on government
    assistance.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Supported employment services provided by DMF Employment Opportunities far exceed the 90-day to 150-day federal
    guideline standards. Training and maintenance is available to Employer Partners and employees on an ongoing basis
    with no limitations. Additional direct services include: individual and group counseling sessions by a mental health
    practitioner, monthly Power Up for Jobs peer group meetings to reinforce and address topics related to employment and
    financial independence, virtual trainings and support via Google Classrooms that promote the use of technology and
    allow participants an opportunity to connect with more frequency while increasing accessibility, unified Special Olympics
    teams that include equal parts of participants with and without disabilities, and monthly social gatherings for employees to
    network and build relationships.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Eligible citizens will receive intensive supported employment services in accordance with the Customized Employment
    model adopted by DMF Employment Opportunities. The appropriation project will specifically fund intensive job coaching
    to ensure transition into stable employment and extended services to maintain employment.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    Young adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities between the ages of 18 to 35 years old in Miami-Dade
    County, Florida. In 2022, it is projected that 20 new employment placements and 68 individuals employed for over a year
    will be served. The goal is to place at least 20 new young adults with intellectual and development disabilities each year
    while ensuring that prior placements thrive or grow in their current positions.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Young adults with an intellectual and/or developmental disability will benefit from the dignity and independence that
    comes from gainful employment. Long-term employment is the key that leads to independent living and financial
    independence. Employer Partners also gain dedicated, hard-working employees with access to customized support and
    training. Furthermore, all citizens benefit from the financial independence people with disabilities gain through stable
    employment leading to inclusion in their communities.

    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?
