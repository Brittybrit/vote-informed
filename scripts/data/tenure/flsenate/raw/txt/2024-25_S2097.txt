    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Jimmy Ryce was abducted and murdered on September 1995, getting off of his school bus in the Redland, FL. His
    parents, Don & Claudine, led an extensive search. They believed that if a bloodhound was available when Jimmy was
    abducted, he would have been found, alive. They established the Jimmy Ryce Center. The funding will be used to
    provide bloodhound to law enforcement agencies/police depts, upkeep throughout year and attend conferences and
    education/awareness and marketing.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Distribution bloodhounds to law enforcement agencies and police departments, tracking and trailing seminars for
    bloodhounds and K9 officers and training programs to include travel expenses, bloodhound care and upkeep and
    maintenance, community outreach initiatives and programs including but not limited to attendance at law enforcement
    conferences, marketing including education/awareness.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     To date, the Jimmy Ryce Center has donated nearly 700 bloodhounds to law enforcement agencies. Our mission is to
    make the Ryce’s goal a reality. Our efforts will help law enforcement search to find missing children, as well as
    endangered adults.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Statewide benefit working in partnership with state's law enforcement agencies. The bloodhounds will be utilized to assist
    law enforcement agencies to find missing children and endangered adults. The FBI and National Center for Missing and
    Exploited Children are all proponents of bloodhounds and their abilities.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The bloodhounds will be utilized to assist law enforcement agencies to find missing children and endangered adults. Law
    enforcement has done extensive studies on the accuracy of a bloodhound when finding a missing person. The FBI and
    the Center for Missing and Exploited Children places a high value on their accuracy as well as numerous local and
    national law enforcement agencies. The National Center even named a training center after Jimmy Ryce
    https://ojjdp.ojp.gov/library/publications/jimmy-ryce-law-enforcement-training-center-program. Every September, Florida
    observes Missing Children's Day at the State Capital. This was started in Jimmy's memory. We can track number of
    missions bloodhounds respond to including outcomes.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Forfeit of the funds.
