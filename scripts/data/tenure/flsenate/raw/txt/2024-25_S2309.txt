14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The SOSA team will conduct monthly decoy sprints with ICAC teams in different counties and hold monthly 3-day
    training sessions with each department. The SOSA team would train and collaborate with ICAC departments throughout
    Florida to dramatically increase their arrests of online criminals against children and teens — especially highly
    sophisticated perpetrators. Our team would help Florida ICAC units develop robust undercover operations to continue this
    work for years to come.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The SOSA team will conduct monthly decoy sprints with ICAC teams in different counties and hold monthly 3-day
    training sessions with each department. This will include initial planning with local prosecutors and team leads to
    determine the needs and parameters of the operations. During the decoy sprints, the SOSA team will message potential
    perpetrators, take video calls, edit photos, investigate and collect evidence. SOSA will provide social profiles and content
    banks of photos and videos, as well as training on how to be a believable underage decoy in future operations. Founder,
    Roo Powell, will also be available for testifying in court and working with legislators to create a bill to further protect kids
    and teens from online sex abuse in Florida.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This project will result in an increase in arrests of online and hands-on predators throughout Florida, provide critical
    training to ICAC departments, and help prevent children in Florida from being abused. SOSA will also share our work and
    press releases to our large social media following, in order to raise awareness and highlight the good work of ICAC and
    law enforcement teams we work with throughout Florida.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This project is specifically targeted to protect minor children and teens. Online and hands-on sex abuse have lasting
    psychological and health ramifications for victims and for their families. The impact of decoy sprints and ICAC trainings is
    both direct and in-direct, so can be difficult to estimate. However, each monthly 4-day sprint can result in 5+ perpetrator
    arrests, and research estimates that each perpetrator can have 100+ victims over their lifetime. Further, training we
    provide to ICAC teams can have exponential effects on a department's arrest rates.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     SOSA would like to help departments to at least double their yearly arrests of online criminal predators and have all
    adjudicated cases result in conviction. Outcomes can be measured by department’s arrests during the SOSA-lead decoy
    sprints, as well as arrests made afterwards using SOSA-provided assets and training.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     If contractual performance measures are not met, then the funds will not be renewed for additional years.
