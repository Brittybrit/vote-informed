1. Project Title          Coral Gables Citywide Bridge Repair Program

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request       02/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The City of Coral Gables owns and maintains a total of 11 reinforced concrete roadway bridges over waterways that
   primarily serve to facilitate access and allow for transportation of good and services throughout surrounding neighborhoods
   and commercial areas. This project involves repairs to eight (8) of the eleven (11) bridges that range from
   sidewalk/shoulder repairs, embankment slope protection repairs, concrete spalling and delamination repairs, repairs to
   rebars, cleaning and sealing asphalt, and concrete cracks, joint replacement, removal of excess vegetation, pressure
   cleaning, repair/replacement of bridge railings and guardrails, sign replacement and pavement resurfacing and re-striping.
   With the completion of these bridge repairs and upgrades, the City will come into compliance with DOT standards and
   extend the service life of its roadway bridge infrastructure.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To maintain safe and efficient roadway access to facilitate travel of persons and transportation of goods.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Construction services to repair 8 bridges as detailed above.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will directly benefit from this project as tens of thousands utilize these bridges daily.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is countywide with tens of thousands of individuals being served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Safe and efficient transportation of motorists, bicyclists and pedestrians. Transportation counts can be used to measure
    bridge usage.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Liquidated damages or withholding reimbursements.
