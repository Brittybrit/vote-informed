14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Improve pedestrian and vehiculer traffic safety and reduce property damage due to poor conditioned roads, sidewalks
    and street flooding.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Water main replacement along NW 30th Street from NW 17th Avenue to NW 12th Avenue through with 8 inch Ductile
    Iron Pipe along with roadway milling and resurfacing.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     No direct services will be provided to citizens by this project.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    This project is targeted to primarily serve the residents of the Allapattah and Brownsville communitye of the City of Miami
    which consist of approximately 25,000 residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improved pedestrian and vehicular safety. This will be measured by comparing the number of vehicular and pedestrian
    accidents after construction is complete to the number of accidents currently being reported at the locations using
    sensors and on-site inspections.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables or performance measures should subject the City to an audit and depending on the results
    of the audit the City could be liable for sanctions up to revocation of funding.
