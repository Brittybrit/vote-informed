2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

4. Project/Program Description
    Provide safe and sustainable facilities for the Bay of Pigs-Brigade 2506 Museum in Little Havana for the purpose of
   preserving the important history of the Cuban people's fight against communism and honoring the brave men who fought
   and died for freedom. The Museum must be updated and expanded to continue the dissemination of historical information
   long into the future to visitors; local, domestic, and foreign tourists in the Miami/Dade area. The museum has received more
   than 5,000 visitors/year and ranks #14 of 80 museums in the Miami/Dade area (As reported by Trip Advisor).

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose of the funds is to ensure a safe facility for guests and patrons, as well as ensure the sustainability of the
    museum for years to come to preserve the important history of the Cuban people's fight against communism and
    honoring the brave men who fought and died for freedom. The renovated and expanded facility will include exhibits and
    services to disseminate historical information related to the Bay of Pigs invasion and it's heroes to local visitors, also
    domestic and foreign tourists visiting the museum.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Provide historical information to visitors, foreign and domestic regarding the fight against communism and socialism in
    Cuba and honor the heroes of Brigade 2506. Educate and provide a research area for students and scholars with unique
    materials only available at the museum. Offer seminars and conferences to students and other personnel and local
    organizations.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Same as paragraph b. above. Provide historical information to the thousands of visitors, foreign and domestic. Educate
    and provide a research area for students and scholars with unique materials only available at the museum. Offer
    seminars and conferences to students, to other personnel, and local organizations.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Target population include students, veterans, local/domestic and foreign tourists that visit Miami. The museum receives
    more than 5,000 visitors/year and it is expected that this amount could increase as much as two-fold. The museum ranks
    #14 by Trip Advisor out of 80 museums in the Miami/Dade area.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     It is expected that with the upgrading and expansion of the museum, the number of visitors will increase, therefore
    offering a valuable attraction to the thousands of tourists visiting the Miami/Dade area. The successful outcome will be
    measured by the increased number of visitors to the museum and increased number of programming.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Loss of future funding
