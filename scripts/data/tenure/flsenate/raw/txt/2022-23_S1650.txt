2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       11/08/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    Provide safe and sustainable facilities for the Bay of Pigs - Brigade 2506 Museum in Little Havana for the purpose of
   preserving the important history of the Cuban people's fight against communism and honoring the brave men who fought
   and died for freedom. The museum must be updated and expanded to continue the dissemination of historical information
   long into the future to visitors, and local, domestic, and foreign tourists in the Miami/Dade area. The museum has received
   more than 5,000 visitors annually and is ranked number 14 out of 80 museums in the Miami/Dade area (as reported by Trip
   Advisor).

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose of the funds is to ensure a safe facility for guests and patrons, as well as ensure the sustainability of the
    museum for years to come in order to preserve the important history of the Cuban people's fight against communism and
    honoring the brave men who fought and died for freedom. The renovated and expanded facility will include exhibits and
    services to disseminate historical information related to the Bay of Pigs invasion and its heroes to local visitors and
    domestic and foreign tourists visiting the museum.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Provide historical information to visitors, both foreign and domestic, regarding the fight against communism and socialism
    in Cuba and to honor the heroes of Brigade 2506. This will educate and provide a research area for students and
    scholars with unique materials only available at the museum. Seminars and conferences will be offered to students, other
    personnel, and local organizations.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Provide historical information to visitors, both foreign and domestic, regarding the fight against communism and socialism
    in Cuba and honor the heroes of Brigade 2506. This will educate and provide a research area for students and scholars
    with unique materials only available at the museum. Seminars and conferences will be offered to students, other
    personnel, and local organizations.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The target population will include students, veterans, and both domestic and foreign tourists that visit Miami. The
    Museum receives more than 5,000 visitors annually and it is expected that this amount could increase by two times. The
    museum ranks number 14 by Trip Advisor out of 80 museums in the Miami/Dade area.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will

     It is expected that with the upgrading and expansion of the museum, the number of visitors will increase, therefore

    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
