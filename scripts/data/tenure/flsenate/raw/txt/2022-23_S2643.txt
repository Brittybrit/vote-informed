1. Project Title          Miami East Little Havana Flood Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor         Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request        01/26/2022

4. Project/Program Description
    The funds will address a project in the East Little Havana community of the city which experiences significant flooding
   during king-tides, rain events, and effects of the sea-level rise. These funds will be used for right-of-way improvements,
   new and upsized stormwater inlets and pipes, exfiltration trenches, gravity drainage wells, injection wells, and a new
   stormwater pump station with an outfall to the Miami River. It is estimated that 846 properties, valued at $431.1M, will be
   impacted.
