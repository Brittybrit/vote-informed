     This an applied research and teaching arm of St. Thomas University College of Law which will provide timely and integral
    research, teaching, and training workshops, seminars and symposia for faculty, students and fellows to study the
    relationships between the constitution, government authority and individual freedoms and develop and provide teachers
    and faculty with tested methods to navigate the balance of these issues with civil rights and responsibility and promote
    discourse.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Education, training, teaching and research on the intersection of liberty, laws and civics to contribute to the 1 applied
    teaching outcomes for faculty and students of the Florida.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Teaching, presentations, symposia, and workshop materials for the use to improve civil discourse based on enhanced
    knowledge of laws, liberty, and civics. Fellowships and mentoring opportunities will also be provided.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Elderly persons, High School students, and or University/College students
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The project will provide a forum dedicated to the research and application of positive outcomes in dealing with
    intersections of laws, liberty, and civics for citizens. Community engagement, workshop participation, student
    performance in classes designed to improve knowledge and application of civics, increased participation in government
    jobs, and volunteer activities, and decline in negative aspects of public debate.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The university will comply with all expenditure terms; however, if penalties are assessed the university is open to
    penalties as deemed reasonable and appropriate by the DOE.
