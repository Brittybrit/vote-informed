1. Project Title         Miami - Auburndale Roadway Drainage and Traffic Calming Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor        Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request       01/25/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    This project includes the design and construction of roadway, drainage and traffic calming improvements including a raised
   intersection at SW 32 Avenue and SW 2 Street. This will increase flood protection and pedestrian safety in the Auburndale
   Neighborhoods by reducing vehicular speeds and cut-through traffic and providing safe route to schools and parks affecting
   census tract 55.03, 0.2 square miles, 3,331 persons.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    This will increase flood protection and provide pedestrian safety for safe routes to schools and parks in the Auburndale
    Neighborhood by reducing vehicular speeds and cut-through traffic. Additionally, any associated roadway improvements
    will be addressed.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Design, Construction and Project Administration
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     None
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The residents of the Auburndale Neighborhood in the City of Miami. Census Tract 55.03, 3,331 persons, 0.2 square mile
    area.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The project will increase pedestrian safety in the Auburndale Neighborhood by reducing vehicular speeds and cut-
    through traffic. Performance will be measured by monitoring the reduction of vehicular volume and speed through a pre
    and post construction study as well as on-site inspections for the improvements of the drainage system.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables or performance measures should subject the City to an audit and depending on the results
    of the audit the City could be liable for sanctions up to revocation of funding.
