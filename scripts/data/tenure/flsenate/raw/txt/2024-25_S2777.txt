     Older Adults Technology Services, Inc. (OATS) is requesting $854,461 for the launch and operation of the Senior Planet
    Center in Miami alongside the development of a network of community-based partner sites to serve as host locations for
    Senior Planet s award-winning digital literacy programming. The Center is a technology-themed community center for
    older
    adults that will welcome residents with a complete range of mainstream technologies and a rich slate of multilingual
    programming.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This project will offer a range of tech-themed programs to individuals aged 60+ living in South Florida. OATS will offer
    technology access alongside programs designed specifically for older learners at the Senior Planet Center in Wynwood
    and at
    dozens of partner locations throughout the region. Programs are free and accessible virtually for those who are unable to
    attend.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     OATS has an extensive library of proprietary curriculum covering all aspects of technology from device fundamentals to
    social
    media, privacy, job searching, and much more. OATS will use its library of multilingual materials (English, Spanish) and is
    translating many titles into Haitian. Most programs are delivered live, in-person by trained OATS instructors and are
    comprised of multiple sessions.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population for this project is older adults living in the Miami area. More specifically, individuals aged 60 and
    older.
    With this funding, OATS anticipates serving approximately 5,000 individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Expected outcomes for older adult participants include: improving mental health (75% will feel more connected to others,
    50%
    will feel less alone); improving quality of education (90% will report satisfaction with the quality of education they receive
    through Senior Planet); protecting the public from harm (70% will feel more confident about staying safe- and recognizing
    scams when using email or the internet); enhancing individuals economic self-sufficiency (80% will feel better equipped to
    find resources online to improve their life and save money. The methodology used to measure each of these expected
    benefits and outcomes will be the delivery and analysis of participant surveys.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Corrective action plans may be required for noncompliance, nonperformance, or unacceptable performance. Financial
    penalties may be imposed for failures to implement or to make acceptable progress on such corrective action plans.
    Increments of penalty imposition shall apply and shall be based upon the severity of the noncompliance,
    nonperformance, or
    unacceptable performance that generated the need for corrective action plan.
