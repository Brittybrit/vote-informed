1. Project Title          Miami Beach Resilient Seawalls Along Collins Avenue

2. Senate Sponsor        Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request       02/14/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    This project enhances systems deployed to protect people and property by preventing flooding by installing water quality
   treatment systems for stormwater pumps for use during heavy rainfall and king tide season. This project is critical to
   prevent flooding emergencies while protecting the Biscayne Bay, designated outstanding Florida waters. Funding would go
   toward the construction of the 424 linear feet from the current 2.1 feet NAVD to 5.7 feet NAVD providing elevated protection
   from flood waters along the area.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project enhances systems deployed to protect people and property by preventing flooding by installing water quality
    treatment systems for stormwater pumps for use during heavy rainfall and king tide season. This project is critical to
    prevent flooding emergencies while protecting the Biscayne Bay, designated outstanding Florida waters.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The activity includes the construction of water quality treatment systems.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This project will allow emergency stormwater pumps to operate during flooding emergencies.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population includes Miami Beach citizens and visitors. Miami Beach is a world class destination. In addition to
    the 80,000+ residents, each year the City hosts 3.1 million overnight visitors.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Enhanced protection to our citizens, residents and property against coastal erosion and flooding. The city will monitor the
    height of the water via tidal gauges and visually inspect for any evidence of flooding after storms, high tides and
    significant rain events.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Penalties include termination of contract and potential debarment pursuant to Miami Beach City Code.
