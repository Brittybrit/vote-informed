14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The last approx. 0.5 miles of Watson Road that connects with US Hwy 41 at the south end of Inverness Airport is narrow
    and substandard. With the airport expansion, 80-acre business park, and additional industrial development happening, it
    is necessary to complete Phase II of the Watson Road construction upgrade.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The design completion and construction will be managed by the Economic Development and Public Works Departments
    to ensure project completion.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Improved ingress and egress at the Inverness Airport and surrounding area.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Citrus County residents and visitors.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The upgrade will result in increased public safety by converting the last stretch of the road to the upgraded safety,
    markings, curb and gutter, and stormwater standards consistent with the rest of the road. The project will also result in
    relocation of utility poles for increased safety and resiliency by allowing SECO Energy to loop 3-phase industrial lines.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     None a this time.
