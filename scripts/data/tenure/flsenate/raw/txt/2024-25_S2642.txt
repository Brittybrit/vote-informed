    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds shall be used to promote sustainable agriculture, advocate for educational opportunities, and create awareness of
    community priorities.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Scholarships for students enrolled in the College of Agriculture, life sciences and other community priority programs·
    Provide resources to help educators and consumers learn more about agriculture. Provide opportunities for farm families
    to support agriculture in Florida or other community priority programs. Provide educational programs for individuals with
    special needs.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     -Agricultural Extension Services: Offer extension services to provide farmers with information on best practices, new
    technologies, and pest control methods. Education Programs: Develop educational programs in schools and communities
    to raise awareness about sustainable agriculture and its benefits. Food Security Programs: Develop food security
    programs to ensure that vulnerable communities have access to nutritious and locally produced food and provide
    educational programs for individuals with special needs.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     -Economically disadvantaged persons
    -Preschool students
    -Grade school students
    -High school students
    -University/College students
    -Developmentally disabled
    Target population : >800

     The expected outcome is more sustainable agriculture awareness, more educational opportunities, and more awareness
