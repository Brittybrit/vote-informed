14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    Miami Dade County Public Schools seeks to establish an ongoing summer institute for teachers on Cuban American
    Studies. This institute is in partnership with Florida International University's Kimberly Green Latin American and
    Caribbean Center.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Content experts will address select topics each summer related to Cuban American Studies and History to provide
    pedagogical assistance to teachers with lesson plan development on this topic. Teachers will participate in a two to three
    day summer professional development institute.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Middle and High school students will ultimately benefit from the professional development teachers receive in this
    institute.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population are 100 middle and high school Social Studies teachers each summer.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcome and benefit of this project are for students to learn about Cuban American History as part of their
    Social Studies middle and high school courses. This outcome will be measured through formal evaluations and a survey
    that teachers will complete.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The agency can stop funding the Cuban American Institute.
