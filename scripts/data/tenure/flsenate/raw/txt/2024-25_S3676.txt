1. Project Title         Citrus County US 41 Widening

2. Senate Sponsor        Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request       01/17/2024

4. Project/Program Description
    Widening from 2 to 4 lanes along US 41 corridor from SR 44 to SR 200 is the no. 1 priority for the Hernando-Citrus MPO.
   This 10-mile segment has been broken up into 6 smaller segments that are being funded and constructed from south to
   north. This request is to fund construction on the next unfunded segment. Increased capacity will reduce congestion,
   improve travel time, and reduce evacuation time during emergencies. US 41 is a parallel facility to I-75 and can act as
   reliever road.
