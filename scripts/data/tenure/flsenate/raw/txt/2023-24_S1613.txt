14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project will provide funding for property owners to abandon septic tank systems and connect to available sanitary
    sewer infrastructure for approximately 150 homes in the Schenley Park Neighborhood. Providing financial assistance for
    private side connections will incentive timely connections, which remains the biggest barrier for property owners. This
    funding would complement the sewer expansion project underway.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The project will provide plumbing connections from public sewer infrastructure to individual properties. Once a property is
    connected to the sanitary sewer system, the septic tank will be properly abandoned.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Property owners will be provided funding for plumbing connections and septic tank abandonment, including the required
    permitting and notifications.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    This funding would support the private property connections in the Schenley Park Neighborhood bounded by SW 24th
    Street to the north, SW 67thAvenue to the west, the C-3 Canal to the south and, SW 57th Avenue to the east.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This appropriation will have social, economic, and environmental benefits including reducing costs to homeowners for
    establishing sewer connection, reduced impacts from septic systems compromised by rising groundwater including:
    • reduce nutrient effluent impacts on ground and surface waters including Biscayne Bay
    • reduce public health impacts from failing septic systems that have the potential to cause sewer backups in homes,
    impacting health and property
    • reduce risk of exposure to bacteria and pathogens in floodwaters in areas with saturated soil conditions
    The project will be measured by the number of septic tank systems removed and the removal of the associated nutrients.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The penalties for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures is the risk of not recovering reimbursement cost
    and potential delay in project progress.
