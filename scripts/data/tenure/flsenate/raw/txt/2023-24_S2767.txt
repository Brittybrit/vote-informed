4. Project/Program Description
    On June 24, 2021, the condominium building Champlain Towers South (CTS) in the Town of Surfside experienced an
   unprecedented partial structural collapse. The Governor of Florida issued Executive Order No. 21-148 declaring a State of
   Emergency in Miami-Dade County due to the building collapse and to assist in the response and recovery efforts as well as
   the development of mitigation plans necessary to address hazards posed by the collapse. Pursuant to this tragedy, the
   Town engaged world-renowned structural engineering firm, KCE Structural Engineers PC (KCE), to perform engineering
   support and analysis services. The Town has spent over $2 million dollars on this investigation. The Town needs
   approximately $500,000 in additional funds to finalize the work KCE has performed since 2021. Funding through this
   appropriation will assist the Town in completing this work that will bring closure to the community and provide investigative
   findings beneficial for agencies to learn from.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The appropriation funding will be utilized to cover the additional costs associated with the KCE Structural Engineers CTS
    building collapse services. KCE has been actively conducting an engineering analysis and an investigation into the
    collapse of the building since 2021. The Town has paid over $2 million dollars towards this work and is in need of
    additional funding to complete the effort.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The services provided by KCE Structural Engineers include testing, engineering analysis and related investigation
    strategies that will result in a final report with findings.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     KCE's services will not only help to provide closure for the affected families and the community as whole, but will also
    yield important findings that can be used by agencies around the world to learn from.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The entire Surfside community will benefit from this project, but in addition this will have global impact as the
    investigation will be available for agencies around the world to refer to and use for their own implementation purposes.
    Additionally, information will be benefit the entire State of Florida by better understanding how buildings were built under
    the code in the 1980s. The investigation results will assist in understanding building construction of the area and how it
    affects older buildings.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The benefit of this project is providing closure to the Surfside community and the families of the deceased. These
    community members have been waiting for almost two years to better understand what caused the collapse of the CTS
    building. In addition, agencies around the world are still interested in learning what factors led to this structural collapse.
    Such findings, as the one KCE is working on, will better inform these agencies with regards to their own building codes
    and related events. The Town will measure its performance through a completed investigation provided by KCE Structural
    Engineers. Better understanding construction of that time will provide valued information to other buildings throughout the
    State.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The Town will abide by all agency agreed upon performance measures and/or deliverables as stated in the contract
    clauses. Penalties will be determined by the contracting agency.
