    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Miami-Dade Fire Rescue (MDFR) seeks to purchase a structural collapse training prop to train the Urban Search and
    Rescue Taskforce (FL-TF1). Also, this project aims to replace MDFR's 20-year-old Technical Rescue Collapse Truck.
    The current truck constantly requires repair. A replacement truck will allow MDFR to respond to technical rescue calls that
    require the transport of large-scale equipment for emergency operations.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     MDFR requires constant training and education to remain proficient and current on emerging search and rescue
    strategies. This equipment will provide FL-TF1 and US&R personnel throughout the State of Florida a viable structural
    collapse training prop. Also, this project will provide reliable transportation of specialized heavy-duty equipment for
    technical rescue.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The training prop and technical rescue collapse truck will benefit all citizens as they provides search and rescue
    responders a hands-on training experience and equipment to respond to live scenarios, respectively. Task force
    members will be able to practice rescue techniques while experiencing the actual disaster environment and then better
    respond to live scenarios with up-to-date machinery.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This Project will serve all communities of Miami-Dade County and the Region. More than 2.7 Million residents will be
    served by this project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This training prop will better equip FL-TF1 and regional agencies with tackling search and rescue missions, such as the
    Surfside building collapse that occurred in 2021. And by replacing the existing collapse truck with one that is more reliable
    and has a greater carrying capacity, first responders are able to perform technical rescue and search and rescue
    operations in a safer manner. The benefit of this training prop can be measured by the number of personnel reporting
    injury or reporting to light duty work after participation in a search and rescue emergency event. Also, MDFR can
    measure the benefit of the new collapse truck as first responders will have access to the proper equipment to complete
    their job.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
