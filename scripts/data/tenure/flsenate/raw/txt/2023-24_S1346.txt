4. Project/Program Description
    The Septic to Sewer Conversion project addresses the concerns of old and failing septic systems discharging nutrients into
   the environment. Extending the sanitary sewer service will protect properties, health and natural areas. This project entails
   extending sanitary sewer lines to approximately 34 homes in the Village of Pinecrest that are currently on septic systems.
