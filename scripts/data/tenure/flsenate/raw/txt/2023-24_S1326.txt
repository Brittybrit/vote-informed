1. Project Title          Key Biscayne USACE Ocean and Back Bay Study Project Support

2. Senate Sponsor         Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request        02/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Tackling shoreline protection for the Village is complex and requires the assistance of the US Army Corps of Engineers.
   USACE South Atlantic Division recommended a stand-alone back bay feasibility study in addition to the ocean-side
   analysis that would look at both Bayside and Oceanside threats for the Village. The funding requested would contribute to
   the Village's 25% match of a $4.22M study and lead to hundreds of millions of dollars in resilient infrastructure investment
   in and around Key Biscayne.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will contribute to the local cost share for a backbay and oceanside study performed by the US Army Corp of
    Engineers. Completion of this study is the purpose of this funding and will result in hundreds of millions of dollars of
    investment in shoreline protection.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Activities and services to meet the intended purpose of these funds consist of data collection, analysis, and study
    execution for the oceanside and backbay of Key Biscayne's shoreline protection.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Storm surge, bayside inundation, and beach erosion threaten the life safety and property of residents, business owners,
    and visitors. The results of this study will lead to implementing bayside and oceanside shoreline protection measures that
    will protect of $9.8 billion dollars in real estate, the viability of hundreds of small businesses that are the back bone of the
    local economy, and the safety and quality of life for over 15,000 residents and visitors in the Village.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All residents (15,000), visitors, and businesses in the Village are served by this project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

    The expected outcome of this project is the completion of the study and continual partnership between USACE and the
    Village to protect Key Biscayne's oceanside and backbay shorelines from environmental threats.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in the forfeiture of funds or financial penalties as described in the contract.
