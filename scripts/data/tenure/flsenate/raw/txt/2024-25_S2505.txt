3. Date of Request       12/27/2023

4. Project/Program Description
   This project was authorized per section 10 of the 2022-2023 General Appropriations Act, but no funding was provided.
   These requested funds will be used to support the construction of the Pasco-Hernando State Corporate College. The
   Corporate College will be located in Hernando County and will house programs that will produce a greater skilled
   workforce, including nurses, construction management graduates, welders' plumbers, automotive, and aviation specialists.
