4. Project/Program Description
    According to the International Organization of Chiefs of Police, 78% of agencies have reported having difficulty recruiting
   qualified candidates. The American Trucking Association estimates a shortage of 80,000 truck drivers in 2023. An
   American Ambulance Association study found that 39% of part-time EMT & 55% of part-time paramedic positions went
   unfilled because of a lack of qualified candidates. The College intends to build a 300'x600' concrete multipurpose driving
   pad to support EMS training, Law Enforcement, and Truck Driving, providing a permanent and sustainable space to
   complete training in these critical shortage areas for our community. The project program includes classrooms, bathrooms,
   offices, support services, & a covered area. The driving pad program total is 2,125 NSF and 3,018 GSF. The college has
   partnered with FleetForce and local law enforcement agencies for the success of this project.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funding for this project will create a permanent driving pad and related classroom structure for Criminal Justice,
    Emergency Medical Services and Commercial Truck Driving programs. The college is currently leasing land for two
    programs (which is at risk of no longer becoming unavailable with limited notice) and using an existing parking lot for
    another program (which will be non-compliant with size regulations after December 2024).
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Acquisition of 10 acres of land to develop driving pads and related classroom structures to meet the training needs of the
    academic programs.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This project will ensure a pipeline of highly trained law enforcement professionals, first responders and commercial
    vehicle operators to support the needs of the community.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Target population - North Central Florida, Citrus, Levy, Marion counties and the State of Florida. Our goal is to have 100
    students enrolled and using these facilities within the first year.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project will increase, expand and enhance measurable outcomes including enrollment, graduation, completion and
    job placement rates of critically needed law enforcement, first responders and commercial drivers. This project also meets
    the needs of employer partners (such as Ocala Police Department, Marion County Sheriff's Office and logistics
    companies such as Amazon, R&L Logistics, ABCO Transportation, Cone Distributing, Fed Ex and Dollar Tree) as well as
    statewide partner FleetForce Truck Driving Company.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Reversion of funds to the State.
