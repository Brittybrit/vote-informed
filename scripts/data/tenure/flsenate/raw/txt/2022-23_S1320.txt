2. Senate Sponsor         Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request        10/04/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    Pinecrest Gardens is a national and county designated historic property. One of its iconic features is Swan Lake and the
   adjacent Lake View terrace. The National Register of Historic Places and Miami-Dade County Historic Designation Report
   both list the Swan Lake which was built in 1949.The terrace overlooks the lake and is constructed of oolitic limestone
   topped with a hipped roof of thick clay tile. The walkway is paved in brick laid in a decorative pattern. The terrace and ramp
   leading up to it are sinking and require foundation improvements, leveling of the floor, and repairing the incline of the ramp
   to adhere to ADA standards.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Historic preservation and adherence to the Americans with Disability Act.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The National Register of Historic Places and Miami-Dade County Historic Designation Report both list the Swan Lake
    which was built in 1949. This is a repair project inside a public park that hosts nearly 200,000 visitors every year.
    Programs and events educate and entertain in the areas of historic preservation, culture, and botany. Additionally, the
    ramp will be improved to meet ADA standards.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The National Register of Historic Places and Miami-Dade County Historic Designation Report both list the Swan Lake
    which was built in 1949. This is a repair project inside a public park that hosts nearly 200,000 visitors every year.
    Programs and events educate and entertain in the areas of historic preservation, culture, and botany. Additionally, the
    ramp will be improved to meet ADA standards.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is residents of Miami-Dade County, tourists, and people with physical disabilities.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Pinecrest Gardens has fostered a dedication to learning about parks and unique history and facilities. This project will
    allow for the incorporation of more tours, programs, and events. The methodology used will be the number of daily
    patrons and participants to the programs and events.
