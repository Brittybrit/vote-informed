14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goals of the Institute are to: (1) study the relationship between constitutional interpretation, governmental power, and
    individual freedom, (2) conduct and promote research on the importance of interpreting the U.S. Constitution based on
    original meaning (3) plan and host research workshops, seminars, and conferences to allow students, scholars, judges,
    and guests exchange in discussion of originalism and constitutional interpretation (4) provide fellowship and mentoring
    opportunities.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The activities and services the institute will provide include the following: (1) studying originalism and its effect on
    government, (2) holding conferences, (3) hosting seminars, (4) sponsoring fellows, (5) promoting originalist scholarship,
    and (6) similar academic and nonacademic activities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The Institute will be provide Floridians a center that will inform and promote legal scholars interpreting the U.S.
    Constitution based on original meaning. Additionally, the Institute will plan and host workshops, seminars, and
    conferences for students, scholars, judges, and guests to attend. Fellowships and mentoring opportunities will also be
    provided by the Institute.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population includes local high school students, undergraduate student, legal scholars, judges, and public at
    large.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

    The expected outcome is to be able to provide a forum for robust discussion on important constitutional ideas. The
    method of measuring outcomes will be through community engagement, participation, and performance reviews.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The University is open to penalties the Department deems appropriate.
