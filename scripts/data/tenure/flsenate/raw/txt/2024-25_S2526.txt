1. Project Title          Hernando County Canal Management Feasibility Study

2. Senate Sponsor        Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request       12/18/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Hernando County Aquatic Services and Waterways Department seeks to develop a comprehensive canal feasibility study
   to evaluate known areas of concern within residential navigable canals West of US 19 in Hernando County along with
   developing a plan to assess the potential canal dredging needs. The study will identify areas to improve overall conditions
   of the canal systems for water flow, water quality, and public accessibility for the commercial fishing trade, recreational
   fishing and boating, and tourism development.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The study will identify areas to improve overall conditions of the canal systems for water flow, water quality, and public
    accessibility for the commercial fishing trade, recreational fishing and boating, and tourism development.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

                                         Fiscal Year 2024-2025                                                 LFIR # 2526

     Removal of accumulated layers of fine-grained sediments and organic material which is restricting the recruitment of
    seagrass, reducing the amount of forage and habitat for marine life.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Canal maintenance would provide several benefits to State waters such as allowing for increased boating access and
    recreation, increased water quality by removing organic and fine-grained sediment accumulation that may contain
    contaminants, spurs economic development along underutilized corridors, increases livability and property values along
    the waterways, and may even increase jobs depending upon location.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The general population of Hernando County along with the tourists that utilize the waterways will be served by this
    project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Removal of organic detrital material will allow recruitment of seagrass within the canals, providing a food source and
    habitat for marine life. Surveys of vegetation and marine wildlife before and after organic detrital material removal will
    determine the success of the project.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The County will reimburse Sate funding for failure to meet the deliverables.
