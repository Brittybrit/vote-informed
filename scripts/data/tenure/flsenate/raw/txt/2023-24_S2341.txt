2. Senate Sponsor         Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request        03/01/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The College of Central Florida offers unique academic programs in Agribusiness, Equine Studies, Horticulture, and
   Livestock Management in an experiential learning environment at its 104-acre Vintage Farm Campus. These programs are
   the only hands-on agriculture-related learning programs offered in the Florida College System. Students learn not only
   theory, but more importantly the application of utilizing one-of-a-kind, cutting-edge technology to its students that leads to
   valuable employees who help organizations be more efficient in best practices of nutrient management and soil and water
   conservation, and other agricultural applications.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funding for this project will develop and cultivate agribusiness technology for Florida and will produce highly skilled
    graduates in the areas of: agribusiness, equine studies, horticulture, livestock management, and hemp research and
    production. Local farms and businesses are in desperate need of highly skilled professionals within the equine and
    agribusiness industries. These highly specialized positions are unique to our region and are critically important to the
    vitality of our economy and workforce.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Access to affordable hands-on learning opportunities in the equine and agribusiness industries.
