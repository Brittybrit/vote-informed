4. Project/Program Description
    Girl Power Rocks is in the process of building Mama Hattie's House. The mission of Mama Hattie's House is to provide a
   24-hour safe and stable sanctuary for girls who are aging out of foster care or who are wards of the courts, also permanent
   home for Girl Power's programs and services. Mama Hattie's House will serve as a long awaited "home base" for Girl
   Power including classrooms, a large multi-purpose room, fitness rooms, a music room, private counseling rooms, a
   reception area, administrative offices, meeting and conference spaces and, most importantly, "a place to call home."
   Located at the heart of the Overtown section of Miami, Mama Hattie's House will also be an essential anchor for the
   neighborhood, providing a dedicated workspace for local residents, fostering a sense of community engagement and
   empowerment.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Mama Hattie's House will offer a residence for girls aging out of foster care, exiting detention, or transiting from
    homelessness, abuse and neglect. Programs under the same roof will provide physical, emotional, social, academic and
    career support with an emphasis on preparing young women to lead fulfilling and productive lives. Girls will also have
    access to on-call medical and mental health services and a host of on-site support services.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    Grant writing
    Architectural design services
    Owner representation services
    Program design
    New market tax credit
    Development Director Services
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Mama Hattie's House citizen's services:
    * Affordable ground floor retail space Housing
    * Co-working and business space for young female entrepreneurs.
    * Shared workspaces for interns and volunteers.
    * A warming kitchen, bathrooms, secure storage and a reception area.
    * Retail space for tenants and partners who bring vital goods and services to the surrounding community.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     * Girls ages 11 to 17 who are transiting from homelessness, abuse and neglect, at risk of sex trafficking and/or exiting
    detention (20 girls).
    * Girls 18 to 24 who are aging out of the foster care system (20 girls).
    * Girls ages 7 to 17 who are experiencing challenges with academics, behavior and school attendance (100girls).
    Families in need of services (50 families).
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Expected benefits are reduced homelessness.
    Reduced and prevent Human trafficking of women and girls.
    Increase HS graduation.
    Increase academic performance.
    Improve slumming blight
    Reduce numbers of children in the dependancy system
