4. Project/Program Description
    Miami-Dade County’s Department of Transportation and Public Works (DTPW) Vision Zero goal is to end traffic fatalities
   and serious injuries by 2040, while increasing safe, reliable, sustainable, and equitable mobility for all. A segment of SW
   136th St. was identified in the 2021 Vision Zero Framework Plan as a high-injury vehicle crash location where residents do
   not feel safe walking, biking, or driving. This project seeks the construction of roadway safety improvements including the
   installation of retro-reflective borders for signal heads, speed feedback signs, traffic signal upgrades, protected only phase
   and dual left turn lane, and installation of high visibility crosswalks. These safety countermeasures are aimed to eliminate
   the number of fatalities and serious injuries taking place on this location near schools, businesses, and residential
   neighborhoods and seeks to improve the quality of life and economic vitality of these neighborhoods.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to engineer, design, and construct the identified safety countermeasures. These safety countermeasures are
    aimed to eliminate the number of fatalities and serious injuries taking place on this location near schools, etc. This project
    seeks the construction of safety improvements including the installation of retro-reflective borders for signal heads, speed
    feedback signs, traffic signal upgrades, protected only phase and dual left turn lane, and installation of high visibility
    crosswalks.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     All of the state appropriated funds would go towards the construction of the safety improvements. These improvements
    would save lives, eliminating fatalities and serious injuries taking place near schools, businesses, and residential
    neighborhoods. Long-term this will improve the quality of life and economic vitality of these neighborhoods.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The construction of this infrastructure project will provide the area with a safer environment for bicyclists, pedestrians,
    drivers, and local residents with high visibility crossings, walking areas, and secure access to transit. This project will
    improve mental and physical health and promote a sense of community, as well as create a safe network for all modes of
    transportation.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Local residents are the target population. This project funding request seeks to accomplish this by constructing safety
    countermeasures to eliminate the number of fatalities and serious injuries taking place on this corridor located near
    schools, businesses, and residential neighborhoods. SW 136th St. has been identified as part of the Top 100 Vision Zero
    locations that need immediate safety infrastructure in place. Allocation of funds will expedite the construction of projects
    and address a major safety concern for Miami-Dade residents and visitors.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The construction of this infrastructure project will provide the area with an improved environment for bicyclists,
    pedestrians, and local residents with safer crossings and a walking area. Creating a safe transportation system can foster
    a healthier lifestyle by promoting the use of all modes of transportation. This results in a more active lifestyle which
    reduces the risk of other health complications including diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and high blood pressure
    contributing to improved mental health. Pre- and post-ped/bike counts, tracking of transit usage, and before/after
    implementation crash data analysis are how the county will measure the success of the countermeasures.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
