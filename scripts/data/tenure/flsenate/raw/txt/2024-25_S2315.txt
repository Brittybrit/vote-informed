14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The North Brooksville Wastewater Infrastructure Improvement Project represents a unified effort to enhance the City's
    wastewater management system. This will not only addresses the City's current wastewater needs, but also anticipates
    future challenges posed by population growth and urban expansion while reducing water leaks and improving overall
    water management, leading to less resource wastage and energy consumption.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    Engineering and construction of the East Ave. Lift Station upgrade as well as engineering and construction of a new
    wastewater facility.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Simultaneously, the construction of the new wastewater treatment plant on City owned property focuses on creating an
    advanced facility with modern treatment processes. This integrated approach not only addresses the City's current
    wastewater needs, but also anticipates future challenges posed by population growth and urban expansion.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All citizens and visitors of the City, population est. 9,566, and visitors may benefit from this project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Through this comprehensive approach, optimized sewer operations are ensured, health risks are minimized, local
    ecosystems are preserved, and the environment is safeguarded, enhancing the overall well-being of the North Brooksville
    community. This will be measured by the reduction in sanitary spills that has negative environmental impacts and
    increased capacity for future growth.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Withhold payment
