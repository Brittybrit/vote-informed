14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The expansion of the current Youth Programs & meals for the elders that are provided by over fourty Churches that are
    part of the West Perrine Christian Assosication. These Youth Programs will help create communities in which the youth is
    kept in a safe environment where parents are able to complete their work days without worry for the welfare/safety of their
    children. The meals for the elders portion of the program will increase our ability to feed our most vulnerable elders.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     There will be an increase of services to our community through increased access to Youth Programs at no cost to them.
    Along with the increase in services for vulnerable elders who will have food security through our meals for elders delivery
    program.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Increase access to Youth Programs within the community and increased access to food security for vulnerable seniors
    through the increased delivery of meals to more of those seniors who are in need in our community.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This project will target around 400 of the community's elders, homeless, at-risk youths, persons with poor mental and
    physical health, economically disadvantaged persons, developmentally and physically disabled, students, the formerly
    incarcerated and victims of crime.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Keeping children and young youth away from unsafe, crime driven, or dangerous situations they would encounter after
    school unattended leads to safer communities. Furthermore, It will benefit the mental health of the overall community:
    The children will be taken care off and unsupervised; not left to their own devices which will remove causes of stress,
    anxiety and depression. The parent of these children will also feel a form of stress and mental relief knowing their children
    are well taken care off. Lastly, the elders will have food security and not be worried about food insecurity.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The penalties provided in the contract are adequate at this time.
