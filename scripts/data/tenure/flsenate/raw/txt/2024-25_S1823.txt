1. Project Title          Village of Key Biscayne - Sargassum Removal and Disposal

2. Senate Sponsor         Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request        12/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Request a continuation of FDEM’s $5 million Sargassum Reimbursement Grant to assist in the removal and disposal of
   nuisance sargassum and seaweed from our public beach. The large amount of sargassum and seaweed landing on the
   village’s shoreline poses an increasing public health, environmental and economic hazard. In addition, the disposal of the
   sargassum continues to be an unnecessary waste entering South Florida’s already overburdened landfills while the funding
   for the rising removal and disposal costs could be better invested in natural shoreline protection solutions to help mitigate
   damage from future storms.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To assist in the removal and disposal of nuisance sargassum and seaweed from our public beach. The large amount of
    sargassum and seaweed landing on the shorelines poses an increasing public health, environmental and economic
    hazard. The disposal of the sargassum continues to enter the County's already overburdened landfills while the funding
    for the rising removal and disposal costs could be better invested in natural shoreline protection solutions to help mitigate
    damage from future storms.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Activities to be provided: Removal and Disposal of Sargassum
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     No direct services will be provided to citizens.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This project will serve the general population therefore, the majority of funds will benefit no specific group.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Excess sargassum can release unpleasant and hazardous odors and chemical compounds as it decomposes, potentially
    leading to water pollution. Removing this seaweed will help maintain clean and safe water, reducing the risk of
    waterborne illnesses and skin irritations among beach-goers and swimmers. This will be measured by the number of
    swim advisories issued through water quality testing. Sargassum can negatively impact coastal ecosystems and disrupt
    marine life. By managing sargassum accumulation, we can protect and restore the ecological balance of coastal areas,
    ensuring a healthier environment for both humans and wildlife. Coastal communities such as the Village often relies on
    tourism for economic well-being. Accumulated sargassum often obstructs beach access and interferes with recreational
    activities.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in forfeiture of funds or financial penalties as described in the contract.
