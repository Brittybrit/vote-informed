4. Project/Program Description
    The Miami Project to Cure Paralysis is an internationally renowned comprehensive spinal cord and brain injury research
   program that is translating new discoverers to the clinic . Our goal is to conduct basic, translational and clinical research to
   restore functional recovery and quality of life for those suffering from paralysis.
   Our investigative teams are focusing on a number of neurological disorders including traumatic brain and spinal cord injury,
   concussion and other neurological disorders such as multiple sclerosis and other neurodegenerative disorders. The Miami
   Project has assembled an international team of scientists, physicians and therapists who conduct carefully designed and
   controlled research, and move promising neuroprotective, cell therapies and innovative rehabilitation approaches to clinical
   application in humans including an established drug discovery platform.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal of The Miami Project is to develop new therapeutic interventions to improved clinical care for those living with a
    traumatic brain and/or spinal cord injury and other disorders that lead to paralysis and loss of function.
    The proposed research programs will provide the scientific and medical community with new knowledge related to cellular
    and molecular injury mechanisms that represent new therapeutic targets for drug discovery and other strategies that
    protect and repair the nervous system after acute and chronic injury. In addition, the funds provided by the State will also
    allow our scientists to obtain critical data necessary to submit successful NIH, Department of Defense and Department of
    Veterans Affairs grant applications which are critical to moving our FDA Approved Clinical Trial programs forward.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The research that will be conducted includes basic science to further understand the pathophysiology of neurological
    disorders, translational research necessary to move these basic discoveries forward to clinical application and our clinical
    trials which are testing these therapies in research subjects to determine their safety and efficacy.
    The Miami Project has established a successful pipeline for the translation of new discoveries to the clinic that has
    already resulted in significant improvements in the care and treatment of patients
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Individuals who qualify for our clinical trials will be enrolled and able to participate in our research studies.
