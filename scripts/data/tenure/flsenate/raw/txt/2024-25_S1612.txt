1. Project Title         South Miami Bike Lanes Project

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request       11/27/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The funds will be used to connect existing bike lanes on 62nd Avenue and 57th Avenue from the Ludlam Trail to The
   Underline as well as long SW 57th Avenue from Kendall Drive (SW 88th Street) to the Underline. This project would include
   a combination of bike lanes, sharrows, and intersection safety improvements along with landscaping and the creation of a
   uniformed tree canopy on 64th Street west of 62nd Avenue.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will be used to connect existing bike lanes on 62nd Avenue and 57th Avenue from the Ludlam Trail to The
    Underline as well as long SW 57th Avenue from Kendall Drive (SW 88th Street) to the Underline. This project would
    include a combination of bike lanes, sharrows, and intersection safety improvements along with landscaping and the
    creation of a uniformed tree canopy on 64th Street west of 62nd Avenue.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The project will provide strategically place bike lanes to provide a clear and safe path for cyclists.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Bike lanes and traffic calming measures that will enhance safety and connect communities.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     More than 800/day from the general public are expected to be served by this project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Expected outcomes- improve physical health, protect the general public from harm and improve transportation
    conditions. The methodology by which these outcomes will be measured is a reduction in the amount of traffic-related
    incidents after installation of the bike lanes.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Revocation/withholding of funds.
