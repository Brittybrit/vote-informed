4. Project/Program Description
    Matching funds for the Miami-Dade County Community Violence Intervention Initiative to in part, provide support,
   resources, and interventions that disrupts violence in communities most impacted by violence, specifically gun violence.
   Funds will be added to an existing Request for Proposal process.
   In Spring and Summer of 2021, Miami-Dade County saw a sharp increase in gun violence, permeate throughout its
   communities, mirroring similar disturbing trends in major cities and counties throughout the country. In response to this
   epidemic, Miami-Dade County immediately implemented a multi-faceted response including the development of the Peace
   & Prosperity Plan and the deployment of Operation Community Shield, and the establishment of the Community Violence
   Intervention Initiative.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Miami-Dade County, through its Police Department and its Office of Neighborhood Safety, has continuously focused on
    implementing innovative and sustainable solutions to reduce community violence, including gun violence. In 2021, a
    collective strategy was developed and employed to address this crisis locally, including the Peace & Prosperity Plan and
    Operation Community Shield, and establishment of the Community Violence Intervention Initiative.
    Funds will be used to achieve the following goals:
    . Reduce violence through the intervention of mediation, cease fires, social services, community healing and
    empowerment, and building positive relationships.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This program brings together the County's police and social services departments along with the community partners to
    implement intervention strategies that identify and address potential conflict in communities. Funds will be used to embed
    credible messengers and high-risk interventionists in communities experiencing a high rate of gun violence, connect
    individuals to services, help prevent shootings, and disrupt retaliatory actions by identifying and mediating conflicts.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The funding will support local organizations to join and help expand existing County anti-violence initiatives that
    strategically focuses on identifying and interrupting violence before it occurs. Services can be offered as a safe passage
    program for youth during pre and post school hours, hospitals where victims of gun violence can receive services, and in
    communities impacted by group and other forms of violence.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Miami-Dade County communities - adults and youth who are at high-risk of being directly involved in violence.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Expected outcomes include: Conflicts are identified and addressed through best -practice interventions, and individuals
    deemed most at-risk of being directly involved in violence are connected to various forms of services. Police and medical
    examiner data will be used to monitor trends (shootings and homicides) where services are implemented and aggregate
    information will be collected regarding participants' progress.
