2. Senate Sponsor        Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request       01/12/2022

4. Project/Program Description
    A behavioral modification and training program will reduce the number of shelter dogs euthanized due to behavioral
   issues. Dogs in a shelter setting often require additional resources to make them adoption ready, especially those that have
   suffered from abuse or neglect. Training resources are critical to ensuring behaviorally challenged dogs are afforded the
   opportunity to be re-homed while greatly reducing unnecessary shelter euthanasia. Implementing this program will save
   more lives, align with Miami-Dade County's Save Charlie Act, protect staff, and increase the "No Kill" save rate.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The euthanasia rate of behaviorally challenged dogs will decrease as a result of this program being funded.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Shelter dogs with behavioral concerns will be provided with training and behavior modification to increase their
    adoptability and ensure long-term success in their adoptive homes.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Direct services provided to citizens include safety net and pet retention programs intended to preserve the human-animal
    bond by offering behavioral modification and training for pets belonging to citizens at risk of relinquishing their pets to a
    shelter due to behavioral concerns.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     2.7 million residents of Miami-Dade County will enjoy the social benefit of living in a more humane and conscientious
    environment where the unnecessary euthanasia of shelter dogs has been eliminated.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

    The expected benefit is the reduction of unnecessary canine euthanasia in a shelter setting. The outcome will be
    measured by comparing the percentage of canine shelter intake euthanized for behavioral issues year over year.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
