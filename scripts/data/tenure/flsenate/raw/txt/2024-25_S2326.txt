4. Project/Program Description
    The San Carlos Institute's 98-year-old building is a historical and architectural jewel in urgent need of repairs. Water
   intrusion and concrete spalling threaten its structural integrity. This project seeks repair to the causes of the rapid
   deterioration of the building. The San Carlos Institute, founded in 1871, is one of Florida's most beautiful and historical
   landmarks. Located in the heart of Key West, the San Carlos served as cradle of Cuba's independence movement and as
   cornerstone of Florida's first Cuban-American community. The San Carlos building encompasses a 2-story museum, a
   library, an art gallery, and a 400-seat theatre. It is a living monument to the perseverance of the Cuban people in their
   struggle for freedom. A symbol of freedom and hope for people everywhere. The facility is used by the entire Key West
   community and is the home of some of the Keys' most important educational and cultural events.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The specific objective is to stop the water penetration and concrete spalling that threatens the structural integrity of the
    building. The San Carlos building will be 100 years old in 2024. The funds will be used to ascertain the structural integrity
    of the building, sealing it to stop the water penetration that threatens the structure. The project involves sealing all the
    exterior walls, replacing the roof, installing new windows and doors throughout the building, replacement of all air
    conditioning units and humidity control units to museum, library, and theatre; making the building handicapped accessible
    by installing an elevator, and replacing rear supporting wall of theatre that shows irreversible structural damage.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The San Carlos board will provide all operational, oversight, and support services at no charge so that all state monies
    are used exclusively for construction costs. No state funds will be used for salaries, travel expenses, or other benefits.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Completion of the proposed scope of work will safeguard the structural integrity of one of Florida's most beautiful and
    historical landmarks. A building that has become one of the most important cultural and educational centers for Key West
    and the state's Cuban-American community.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Thousands of people from all over the world visit the San Carlos annually. They come to learn about its history and to
    admire the beauty of its architecture. In particular, persons of Cuban descent come to the San Carlos in a spirit of
    patriotic pilgrimage. They bring their children and grandchildren to learn of their Cuban heritage and to renew their
    commitment to the freedom of Cuba. The San Carlos is also one of Key West's most important cultural and educational
    centers. It has been the home of the Key West Literary Seminar since 1993 (except 2021 and 2022 because of
    COVID19), the Key West Film Festival and the Key West Songwriters' Festival. The San Carlos has served as downtown
    campus to the Florida Keys' College and has hosted three of the college's winter commencement exercises in recent
    years. Thousands of South Florida high school students come to the San Carlos on field trips to learn of the history of
    Cuba and Florida.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The principal benefit will be to ensure the structural integrity of this historic building by sealing its walls to prevent further
    water penetration, replace its roof, exterior windows and doors, HVAC components, make the building handicapped-
    accessible by installing an elevator. The objective is to secure this structure so that it can be enjoyed for many
    generations to come.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

    The contracting agency may supervise the work and file a lien against the San Carlos' property located at 516 Duval
    Street, Key West, Florida if the San Carlos Institute fails to meet the performance measures provided in the contract.
