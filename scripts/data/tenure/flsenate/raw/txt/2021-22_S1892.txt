4. Project/Program Description
    Pinecrest Gardens (PG) is a nationally, state and locally designated historic botanical and cultural arts park in the Village
   of Pinecrest. The proposed project includes new construction of various facilities that are in line with the Village’s goal of
   fostering an educational environment for all children. This includes new playground, bathrooms, petting zoo, nature trail,
   Inspiration (educational) Center, Sensory Garden and connecting paths to make them ADA accessible.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project will allow Pinecrest Gardens to further its efforts to provide inclusive recreational and educational
    opportunities to children with diverse abilities. With the addition of the new facilities, programming serving this specific
    population will be expanded.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This project will allow Pinecrest Gardens to further its efforts to provide inclusive recreational and educational
    opportunities to children with diverse abilities. With the addition of the new facilities, programming serving this specific
    population will be expanded.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Giving access to additional education and cultural programming to residents of all abilities.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    Residents of Miami-Dade county and tourists to the area. The park attracts over 100,000 people a year and this project
    would increase that number, specifically to an audience with limited physical abilities.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

    Give access to a broader and more diverse audience, specifically to those with limited abilities. The outcome will be
    measured through the number of cultural programs and number of attendees in those programs.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?
