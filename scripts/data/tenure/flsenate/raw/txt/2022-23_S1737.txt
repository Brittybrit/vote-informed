4. Project/Program Description
    The program will expand access to care for individuals with disabilities and their families through a broad spectrum of
   tailored services and activities. Social, recreational and life skills experiences for children and youth with disabilities ages
   five through twenty-one are delivered year round through after-school activities & enrichment camps, while these and
   additional weekend and in-home activities will offer respite to parents and caregivers. To help families navigate raising a
   child with a disability, and as a prophylactic measure to prevent challenges from turning into life-threatening ones, intensive
   case management will actively provide counseling, facilitated support groups, and access to essential services as well as a
   24/7 crisis line.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The project will equip individuals with disabilities and their families with the tools, skills, and knowledge to navigate and
    overcome adversity to live full, productive lives. In addition, funds will support and enhance a lifeline connection that is
    reactive to community-at-large crises and provides a variety of community resources and interventions.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The following activities and services will be provided on-site, in the client's home, and/or in the community: intensive
    case management and care coordination; facilitated peer support groups; social,cultural, recreational and cultural
    activities after-school and when schools are closed; volunteer recruitment and engagement; Sunday and weekday
    parent/caregiver respite; community outreach and engagement; counseling; 24/7 crisis line.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Families will receive counseling for mental health challenges as well as expert navigation/care coordination through case
    management services. Individuals with disabilities will be paired one to one with teenage mentors where they are
    immersed in a rich array of activities to broaden there horizons and develop daily living, social, and other transferable
    skills. Community service events/outreach will also be performed to raise awareness, educate, and improve civic
    involvement.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population will include elderly persons, persons with poor mental health, persons with poor physical health,
    the developmentally disabled, physically disabled, high school students, and Jewish community members.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     1. Improved mental health for individuals with special needs and their parents/givers, as measured by improved self-
    esteem, coping skills; reduced isolation and feelings of helplessness; increased self-advocacy skills; improved subjective
    appraisal. Attainment of outcome measured through periodic surveys, data analysis, and staff observation

    2. Enriched cultural experience, as measured by Improved inter-connectivity, comfort of exercising beliefs, increased
    participation in cultural and communal activities, safe access to services and programs for congregants. Attainment of
    outcome measured through periodic surveys, data analysis, and staff observation.
