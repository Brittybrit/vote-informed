4. Project/Program Description
    Matching funds for the Miami-Dade County Community Violence Intervention Initiative to provide emotional and mental
   health support, resources, and interventions in communities most impacted by violence, specifically gun violence. Funds
   will be added to an existing Request for Proposal process to expand existing mental health services and/or add new
   services and/or providers that are rooted in best-practices, culturally sensitive, and responsive to residents. In Spring and
   Summer of 2021, Miami-Dade County saw a sharp increase in gun violence permeate throughout its communities,
   mirroring similar disturbing trends in major cities and counties throughout the country. In response to this epidemic, Miami-
   Dade County immediately implemented a multi-faceted response including the development of the Peace & Prosperity Plan
   and the deployment of Operation Community Shield, and the establishment of the Community Violence Intervention
   Initiative.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Miami-Dade County, through its Police Department and its Office of Neighborhood Safety, has continuously focused on
    implementing innovative and sustainable solutions to reduce community violence, including gun violence. In 2021, a
    collective strategy was developed and employed to address this crisis locally, including the Peace & Prosperity Plan and
    Operation Community Shield, and establishment of the Community Violence Intervention Initiative.
    Funds will be used to achieve the following goal(s):
    1. Expand mental health and wellness services in communities impacted by gun violence, administered by individuals and
    entities with established credibility and community trust.
    2. Victims (survivors), family members and the community are connected to various forms of mental health services and
    wraparound services.
    3. Creation of a bi-directional referral system between organizations/government entities that provide basic needs
    assistance and community mental health providers.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This program brings together the county's police and social services departments, along with the community partners, to
    expand and/or establish new mental health and wellness services in communities disproportionately impacted by
    violence, specifically gun violence. Funds will be used to embed culturally sensitive mental health services in communities
    that are both preventative and responsive (after a violent incident), serves the whole family, provides linkages to
    wraparound services, and are rooted in best-practices.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The funding will support individual, family and group trauma-informed counseling services. Social emotional learning,
    anger management, mindfulness, identifying and managing stress and anxiety techniques, and communicating feelings
    with diplomacy are some of the focus areas that may be covered. Services can be offered in partnership with after school
    and older youth programs, community resource centers, public housing community centers, and other locations that
    ensures accessibility.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Miami-Dade County communities - adults and youth residing in communities disproportionately impacted by gun
    violence.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Expected outcomes include: Youth, adults, families and neighborhoods are connected to "culturally-sensitive" mental
    health and emotional wellness services in their community administered by trusted and credible community providers.
    Mental health services are linked to other forms of services including rental and utility assistance, drug and alcohol
    rehabilitation, workforce development, childcare, education and training, and basic needs, to name a few. Aggregate data
    will be collected regarding participants' served, outcomes, types of service provided, etc.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Funds could be provided on a reimbursement basis after services are performed.
