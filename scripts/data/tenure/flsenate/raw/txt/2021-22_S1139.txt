4. Project/Program Description
    The facility will enable the isolation of sterile cell particles called "esosomes" derived from human mesenchymal stem cells
   (MSCs; a type of adult stem cell).
   The MSC functions to dampen inflammation and it does so by production of exosomes that contain anti-inflammatory
   molecules.
   By directly isolating these exosomes and giving them to patients intravenously, we propose that they would serve as a
   more potent agent in stopping inflammation.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to expand regenerative medicine in Florida. The facility will enable isolation of sterile cell particles called
    "exosomes" derived from human mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs; a typle of adult stem cell). The MSC functions to
    dampen inflammation by the production of exosomes that contain anti-inflammatory molecules. This product is designed
    for healing and repair of human cells and organs. The initial focus will be COVID19 and infectious diseases; but also
    applies to other diseases.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     It is anticipated that the project will yield therapeutic approches to treat chronic, debilitating inflammation that occurs
    following COVID-19.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The project offers a novel therapeutic approach to treat COVID-19-related long-term effects ("long hauler" syndrome).
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All people who have been infected with COVID-19
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Persons with "long hauler" COVID-19 syndrome are debilitated.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?
