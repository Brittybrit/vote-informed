    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Global Empowerment Mission is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization responding immediately to disaster relief needs. Its
    mission is to distribute the most amount of aid to those who need it, as quickly as possible. The funds will allow us to
    purchase semi-truck, CRM system and software, dry van with lift gate, barcode scanners, volunteer and vehicle key
    management and subscription, ERP implementation, miscellaneous safety equipment and PPE, all terrain forklift, disaster
    supplies.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Our Miami warehouse is fully equipped to respond immediately to crisis, and with partners such as Good360, Amazon,
    Publix, Goya and many others, we’re able to bring aid into the most devastated areas & most vulnerable populations as
    quickly and efficiently. For example, GEM was first on the ground for Hurricane Ian since landfall actively responding to
    affected communities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     GEM is fully equipped to respond immediately to crisis and with partners such as Good360, Amazon, Publix, Goya and
    many others, we’re able to bring aid to most vulnerable population quickly and efficiently. By partnering directly with local
    community organizations and leaders, we’re able to distribute aid much more quickly and efficiently than more traditional
    disaster relief organizations.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Statewide services of vulnerable communities affected by disaster or other such event.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The funds will be used to purchase equipment that will allow GEM to better respond to disaster event or other
    emergency events. GEM not only provides immediate relief but also tends to the often-overlooked gap period and long-
    term recovery, rebuilding, and redevelopment of a crisis-hit cities and communities. GEM will measure outcome by
    number of events GEM responds to, including people served, family necessity kits (boxes with snacks, food, other items)
    distributed), total aid distributed. Volunteers and skilled workers sourced in communities.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to utilize the funds in the specified manor shall result in funds being returned to the State.
