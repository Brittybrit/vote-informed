4. Project/Program Description
    Project consists of improvements to the drainage system along Galiano St. (Madeira Ave.-Zamora Ave.) and Madeira Ave.
   (Ponce De Leon Blvd.-Galiano St.) within the City of Coral Gables. The goal of the project is to improve water quality by
   redirecting roadway stormwater runoff from discharging into the Coral Gables waterway and treating it through exfiltration
   trenches and into an underground porous limestone layer. This project will also have the added benefit of increasing the
   drainage capacity, alleviate flooding, and recharge the Biscayne Aquifer with fresh rainwater.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Project consists of improvements to the drainage system along Galiano St. (Madeira Ave. - Zamora Ave.) and Madeira
    Ave. (Ponce De Leon Blvd. - Galiano St.) within the City of Coral Gables. The goal of the project is to improve water
    quality by redirecting roadway stormwater runoff from discharging into the Coral Gables waterway and treating it through
    exfiltration trenches and into an underground porous limestone layer. This project will also increase drainage capacity,
    alleviate flooding, and help to recharge the Biscayne Aquifer with fresh rainwater.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The requested funds will be
    utilized to procure a contractor for construction of the drainage improvements along the corridors.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The project will help to improve the water quality within the Coral Gables waterway and Biscayne Bay by redirecting
    roadway stormwater runoff from discharging into the waterway and treating it through exfiltration trenches and into an
    underground porous limestone layer. Algae blooms in Biscayne Bay have been attributed to the amount of contamination
    discharged into the waterways. This project will also increase the drainage capacity, alleviate flooding, and recharge the
    Biscayne Aquifer with fresh rainwater.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The businesses owners and residents along these corridors and in the vicinity will immediately benefit from these
    stormwater improvements as a result of the reduction in the frequency and severity of flooding. Furthermore, the water
    quality improvements that will be provided by this project will benefit Miami-Dade County residents and visitors overall by
    reducing the contaminants that are discharged into the Coral Gables waterway which ultimately make their way to
    Biscayne Bay.

    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Benefits that are expected as a result of this project include improvements to water quality within the Coral Gables
    waterway where the existing drainage system discharges into, and ultimately Biscayne Bay. Furthermore, a reduction in
    the frequency and severity of flooding can be expected along the corridors as a result of the increased drainage capacity.
    Both these benefits can be measured through a contamination analysis of the water in the waterway and through a
    review of flooding reports pre and post improvements.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The City may lose or be required to reimburse State funding awarded to the project.

13. The owners of the facility to receive, directly or indirectly, any fixed capital outlay funding. Include the
    relationship between the owners of the facility and the entity.

     The public right-of-ways along Galiano St. (Madeira Ave. - Zamora Ave.) and Madeira Ave. (Ponce De Leon Blvd. -
    Galiano St.) are under the jurisdiction of the City of Coral Gables. This includes maintenance of the drainage systems
    along these corridors and also includes the Coral Gables waterway where this drainage system discharges.
