4. Project/Program Description
    The City of Miami Beach offers a variety of activities and programs for seniors ages 55+. Our programs are specially
   designed to allow every adult of any activity level to participate in a safe and inviting environment. The Parks and
   Recreation Department believes physical activity and social interaction leads to a healthier lifestyle, no matter a person’s
   age. “Be active, be social, be forever young.” Funding would provide for programming, field trips, transportation needs and
   events.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     City of Miami Beach offers a variety of activities and programs for seniors 55+. Seniors are the fastest growing
    population, in Miami Beach they make up 32.1% of our community. Regular activity contributes to the prevention and
    management of chronic diseases and mental health, our programs are specifically designed to allow every adult of any
    activity level to participate in a safe environment.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Programming for seniors 55+ includes chair and water aerobics, field trips, Tai Chi, yoga, pickleball, flower arranging,
    zumba, soccer and inter-generational activities with children and teens.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Programming will be offered throughout our park system at low or no cost directly to residents over the age of 55.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Miami Beach residents over the age of 55. Over 32% of our population, approximately 27,000.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Engaging in active programming such as physical exercise, contributes to the prevention and management of chronic
    diseases as well as prevent or lessens physical limitations. Our Parks and Recreation Department will conduct regular
    assessments to track physical improvements using parameters such as cardiovascular fitness, muscle strength, flexibility
    and balance.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Termination of contract.
