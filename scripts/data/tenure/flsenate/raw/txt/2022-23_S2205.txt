12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The production of the Miami International Agriculture, Horse & Cattle Show.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Production of the Miami International Agriculture, Horse & Cattle Show which will attract agri-business enterprises,
    business people and visitors from Latin America, the Caribbean, Europe, and the United States and which will showcase,
    promote and support the agriculture of Florida.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Local business and residents within Miami-Dade County, as well as Miami-Dade County as a whole, in addition to cattle
    farmers, agri-business enterprises and entrepreneurs, will benefit from receipt of the requested funds.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Local business and residents within Miami-Dade County, as well as Miami-Dade County as a whole, in addition to cattle
    farmers, agri-business enterprises and entrepreneurs, will benefit from receipt of the requested funds.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The 2023 Miami International Agriculture, Horse and Cattle Show is expected to improve agricultural promotion and
    education, stimulate activity, and increase tourism within Miami-Dade County, and is also expected to attract agri-
    business enterprises from Latin America, the Caribbean, and Europe, along with business people and visitors from
    throughout the United States. Statistics will be gathered and analyzed recording the gross revenues of local businesses
    during the time-frame of the show, the number of visitors attending the show, and the number of sales and new business
    relationships resulting from contacts made at the show.
