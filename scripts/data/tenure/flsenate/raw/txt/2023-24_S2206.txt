4. Project/Program Description
    VolunteerCleanup.org is an on-line web technology platform that connects volunteers to local litter cleanup opportunities
   organized by individuals or non-profit groups, and helps those groups manage their cleanups and ensure key educational
   outcomes. In Miami-Dade County, we have been immensely successful in building a large community of over 150 cleanup
   organizers who have led 3,000 cleanups, where 30,000 unique volunteers have removed over 600,000 pounds of trash
   from neighborhoods, shorelines and waterways. Cleanups provide an eye-opening experience to how much plastic is in our
   Bay and Oceans that creates an awareness and desire for individuals and businesses to be part of the long term solutions.
   Our project goal is to 1) Expand our service are into 3 other coastal Counties in Florida, 2) Develop NEW organizers by
   providing micro-grants, training, supplies and educational materials, and 3) Provide grants to EXISTING organizers in
   Miami-Dade County to fund a ongoing cleanups.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     VolunteerCleanup.org is an on-line website / technology support organization that helps organizers RECRUIT volunteers
    and MANAGE their cleanup event. We will engage and educate thousands of new citizens on the marine debris issue
    through active participation in shoreline, waterway and neighborhood cleanups. This project goal is to 1) Expand our
    service are into 3 other coastal Counties in Florida, 2) Develop NEW organizers by providing micro-grants, training,
    supplies and educational materials, and 3) Provide grants to EXISTING organizers in Miami-Dade County for ongoing
    cleanups.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     1) Expand programming in Miami-Dade County to provide more opportunities for volunteers with individual/NGO's
    already capable of organizing cleanups.
    2) Expand into 3 new Counties in coastal Florida with outreach, micro-grants, training, supplies, and educational materials
    for new cleanup groups. ie, Broward, Pinellas, Brevard, Wakulla, Escambia, or Duval.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     While cleanups provide immediate benefit to clean up our communities and environment, they more importantly provide
    a first-hand, eye-opening experience to the amount of plastic entering our ecosystem that inspires individuals and
    businesses to take actions to address the roots of the problem. We also establish a sense of pride in taking care of ones
    neighborhood, which strengthens community.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     We expect this project to serve over 20,000 new citizens throughout Florida. Cleanups are rewarding community service
    and educational events for every citizen, regardless of age or ability, and provide valuable student and court-ordered
    community service hours. Cleanups can enrich all communities, whether inland or coastal. To date in Miami-Dade, we
    have amplified the efforts of over 150 individuals and NGO's who have engaged over 30,000 unique individuals. We plan
    to identify, mentor, nurture and support 5 groups in each of 3 new target communities, who will each lead 5 cleanups and
    engage 250 citizens.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     We have already demonstrated the immense impact and value of community-led cleanups through a) metrics weight and
    volume of material collected) and b) Post-Cleanup Surveys and documented local actions. Our most recent survey results
    show "66% had an increase in knowledge about marine debris from attending a cleanup, 96% want to do more to protect
    the environment, 81% committed to attending another cleanup, and 73% committed to using fewer single-use plastics."
    One of our favorite volunteer quotes is: “People can do some extraordinary things if they work together for a cause.”
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Return unused funds provided and withhold any additional future funds remaining to be dispersed.
