1. Project Title          Zoo Miami Expansion of Sea Turtle Rescue/Rehab Center

2. Senate Sponsor         Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request        12/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Zoo Miami’s Sea Turtle Rescue/Rehab Center serves a critical need for the rehabilitation of sea turtles. It provides a
   central location in Miami-Dade County for receiving injured or sick sea turtles. Within the past year, three sea turtle centers
   serving South Florida have lost the ability to house new or long-term patients. The redistribution of their turtles and the
   increased number of new patients has placed added pressure on the facilities at Zoo Miami. An expansion of infrastructure
   and addition of required equipment would allow Zoo Miami to greatly increase the ability to provide urgent medical care and
   appropriate rehabilitation of these animals. This expansion would allow Zoo Miami's Sea Turtle Rescue/Rehab Center to
   increase carrying capacity, while improving the life support systems, a critical component to the recovery and health of sick
   and injured turtles.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds would support the design/construction for the expansion of Zoo Miami's Sea Turtle Rescue/Rehab Center, a
    central location in Miami-Dade County for receiving injured or sick sea turtles. An expansion of infrastructure would allow
    Zoo Miami to greatly increase the ability to provide urgent medical care and appropriate rehabilitation, while improving the
    life support systems, a critical component to the recovery and health of injured sea turtles.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Expansion of the Zoo's Sea Turtle Rescue/Rehab Center would greatly increase the ability of Zoo Miami Veterinarian
    staff to provide urgent medical care and rehabilitation services. These services include medical examination and
    treatment for turtles effected by boat strikes, fishing hook entanglements, and other injuries or diseases.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Zoo Miami will be able to expand service as a health care resource in the South Florida region for endangered native sea
    turtles, critical to the conservation and preservation of the environment. As three sea turtle centers serving the region
    have recently lost the ability to house new or long-term patients, the redistribution of turtles and the increased number of
    new patients is in high demand.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     General public in South Florida region - residents and annual attendance of over 1 million visitors.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Treatment, recovery and rehabilitation of numerous sea turtle species in South Florida. The methodology for measuring
    outcomes will include Sea Turtle Rescue/Rehab Center intake records, the # of sea turtle releases, and recorded
    contacts with FWC, USFWS and other organizations.
    Expansion will also allow greater opportunities to educate the community on Zoo Miami's conservation efforts to help
    wildlife found locally in Florida. Opportunities include programming with on-site students from the Miami-Dade County
    Public Schools BioTech Magnet School.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Nonpayment until deliverables are met.
