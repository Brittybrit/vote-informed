12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will be used to complete construction of 27 affordable housing units located on three sites in the Florida Keys, an
    area of critical state concern.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     These units will provide home ownership opportunities and provide critically needed affordable housing to the Monroe
    County workforce, helping to replace housing units lost in Hurricane Irma, as well as to increase the options for affordable
    housing in wake of the COVID pandemic.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The availability of 27 affordable housing units in proximity to places of employment.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Monroe County workforce including economically disadvantaged persons / families. A minimum of 27 families will be
    served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The addition of 27 affordable housing units will provide critically needed housing options for the local workforce,
    improving transportation conditions, increasing / improving economic activity, and enhancing economic self-sufficiency by
    providing housing options in proximity to places of employment in the Florida Keys. Rental and ownership units
    successfully occupied by the local workforce will be the measure of success of the project.
