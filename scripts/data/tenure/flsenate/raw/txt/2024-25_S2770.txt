4. Project/Program Description
    North America Vehicle Rescue Association (NAVRA) is an education and training organization made up of firefighters,
   paramedics, nurses and physicians. Our mission is to “prepare today’s rescuers to succeed at tomorrow’s challenges.” It is
   our focus to provide platforms for rescue personnel throughout N. America and the World to share and advance rescue
   science and technology. We seek to develop and enhance proficiency in rescue techniques, vehicle rescue, Incident
   Command and pre-hospital care. This funding will allow NAVRA to host two extrication and ALS Medical challenges in
   Florida bringing together teams from across the country and the globe.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     NAVRA is an education and training organization made up of firefighters, paramedics, nurse and physicians. Our mission
    is to “prepare today’s rescuers to succeed at tomorrow’s challenges.” It is our focus to provide platforms for rescue
    personnel throughout N.America and the World to share and advance rescue science and technology. We seek to
    develop and enhance proficiency in rescue techniques, vehicle rescue, Incident Command and pre-hospital care.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This funding will allow NAVRA to host two extrication and ALS Medical challenges in Florida bringing together teams
    from across the country and the globe. Additionally, NAVRA has a need to purchase a truck to transport equipment, as
    well as purchase some equipment. In 2025, the WORLD rescue challenge will be coming to Miami with teams from
    around the world travelling to Florida, items needed for hosting. Hosting of two rescue challenges in Florida as well as
    preparations for hosting the WORLD challenge. The truck will assist with transporting equipment for challenges and
    education/training sessions in order for first responders to be better prepared to save lives here in Florida, as well as
    other equipment. NAVRA will host two challenges in Florida bringing fire rescue teams from across the country and globe,
    creating economic opportunity to host cities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     NAVRA brings together and trains fire rescue teams from fire departments across the State of Florida. NAVRA recently
    hosted an extrication and ALS Medical Challenge at Miami Dade Fire Rescue where 12 teams for extrication from fire
    departments across South Florida, the State and the globe. Fifteen teams participated in the medical challenge totaling
    nearly 200 participants hosted at areas hotels, dining in our communities, all while competing and learning better rescue
    techniques to save lives.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     NAVRA brings together and trains fire rescue teams from fire departments across the State of Florida to train, learn and
    compete to master best techniques to save more lives.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     By having fire rescue teams participate in the Rescue Challenges they are able to put the skills they have learned or are
    learning into practice in staged scenarios based upon real life accidents/incidents and save more lives. Better knowledge
    and understanding of risks and how techniques learnt help towards reducing fatalities/ mortality and morbidity; Wider
    public understanding of real hazards and results of unsafe road use; Participants sharing of knowledge and skills learned
    with their home fire rescue agency. From an economic activity and tourism, Measurement and data of how many rooms
    are booked at partner hotels during our events, including fire rescue participants, assessors who evaluate the challenges,
    sponsors, officials and community partners.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     If deliverables are not met, funding will be returned.
