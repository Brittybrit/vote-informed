4. Project/Program Description
    Miami-Dade County’s Department of Transportation and Public Works (DTPW) Vision Zero goal is to end traffic fatalities
   and serious injuries by 2040, while increasing safe, reliable, sustainable, and equitable mobility for all. A segment of SW
   24th St. was identified in the 2021 Vision Zero Framework Plan as a high-injury vehicle crash location where residents do
   not feel safe walking, biking, or driving. This project seeks the construction of roadway safety improvements including
   adding green crosswalk markings, installation of retro-reflective borders for signal heads, bus stop consolidation,
   installation of a midblock crosswalk, and signal upgrades. These safety countermeasures are aimed to eliminate the
   number of fatalities and serious injuries taking place on this location near schools, businesses, and residential
   neighborhoods and seek to improve the quality of life and economic vitality of this neighborhood.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to design and construct the identified safety countermeasures. These safety countermeasures are aimed to
    eliminate the number of fatalities and serious injuries taking place on this location near schools, etc. This project seeks
    the construction of safety improvements including adding green crosswalk markings, installation of retro-reflective borders
    for signal heads, bus stop consolidation, installation of a midblock crosswalk, and signal upgrades.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     All of the state appropriated funds would go towards the construction of the safety improvements. These improvements
    would save lives, eliminating fatalities and serious injuries taking place near schools, businesses, and residential
    neighborhoods. Long-term this will improve the quality of life and economic vitality of these neighborhoods.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The construction of this infrastructure project will provide the area with a safer environment for bicyclists, pedestrians,
    drivers, and local residents with high visibility crossings, walking areas, and secure access to transit. These
    improvements will improve mental and physical health and promote a sense of community, as well as create a safe
    network for all modes of transportation.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Local residents are the target population. This project funding request seeks to accomplish this by constructing safety
    countermeasures to eliminate the number of serious injuries taking place on this corridor located near schools,
    businesses, and residential neighborhoods. SW 24th St. corridor has been identified as part of the Top 100 Vision Zero
    locations that need immediate safety infrastructure in place. Allocation of funds will expedite the construction of the
    project and address a major safety concern for Miami-Dade residents and visitors.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The main benefit of this project is to eliminate fatalities along this corridor. Other direct benefits include reducing high-
    injury crashes in main residential and commercial roadways, improving safety for all modes, and enhancing quality of life
    for residents by providing safer streets. Improvements in physical and mental health are also likely outcomes through the
    creation of a safe transportation system that encourages residents to take transit, walk, or bike to their schools, work,
    local shops, and restaurants; all while fostering economic development in the area. This also results in a more active
    lifestyle which reduces the risk of other health complications including diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and high blood
    pressure contributing to improved mental health. The methods used for measuring the outcome consist of analyzing
    before and after traffic/crash data, tracking construction jobs, and tracking transit, pedestrian, and bike usage/counts.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
