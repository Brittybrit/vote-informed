1. Project Title          Key Largo Fire Rescue Marine Emergency Reponse Vessels

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       01/03/2024

4. Project/Program Description
    Key Largo Fire Rescue seeks funds to purchase a marine-based emergency response vehicle in order to enhance safety
   and save lives in the Keys waters. The vessel will enable the creation of a new marine-based emergency response
   program within Monroe County's Fire Rescue Department. It will consist of one vessel manned by medically trained
   firefighters operating 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The Fire/Rescue boats will operate from strategic areas to provide the
   greatest life and property saving impact.
