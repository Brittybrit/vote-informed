1. Project Title           The Underline Multi-Use/Mobility Corridor

2. Senate Sponsor          Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request         12/20/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    The funds will be used to finalize the construction of the 10-mile off-road Underline mobility multi-modal corridor. The $3
   million requested will be used for the acquisition and installation of site furniture, drinking fountains, required signage, bike
   repair stations, and Wi-Fi and will supplement the cost of the construction of amenities not covered under other funding
   sources.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Programming, events, and activities for the communities and visitors. Recreational facilities will encourage an active
    lifestyle.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Underline will provide approximately 10-miles of separated bicycle and pedestrian paths, and intersection
    improvement along US 1. Increased safety, connectivity, and decongestion of traffic is expected as a result.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The installation of bike lanes and wider sidewalks will promote urban cycling, walking by residents, and visitors to the
    community. This will promote a more active lifestyle which reduces the risk of other health complications, including
    diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and high blood pressure, and contributes to improved mental health.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Elderly, economically disadvantaged persons, at-risk-youth, and the general population. Thousands of visitors and
    residents are expected to be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The installation of bike lanes and wider sidewalks will promote urban cycling, walking by residents, and visitors to the
    community. This will promote a more active lifestyle which reduces the risk of other health complications, including
    diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and high blood pressure, and contributes to improved mental health.
