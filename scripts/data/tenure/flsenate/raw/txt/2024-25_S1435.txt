1. Project Title          Miami-Dade Animal Services Department Spay/Neuter Initiative

2. Senate Sponsor        Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request       11/20/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Animal Services will address the dog and cat overpopulation crisis in Miami-Dade County by providing Spay and Neuter
   services throughout the community. The Spay and Neuter services will be accessible at different geographical locations
   within the county in order to make the service accessible to more residents. The Spay and Neuter services will be offered at
   a low-cost or free. The goal of this program would be to decrease the stray pet population in Miami-Dade County.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Miami-Dade County is experiencing a dog and cat overpopulation crisis as is the entire country. In order to address the
    overpopulation, Animal Services will increase the number of spay and neuter services provided throughout the
    community.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Spay and Neuter services will be provided for free or low cost in different locations throughout the county. Access to
    services will be made easier for county residents willing to spay and neuter their pets.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Spay and Neuter surgeries will be made available to pet owners.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Dog and cats are the target population. Goal to provide 1,000 surgeries per month.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The increase in spay/neuter surgeries will decrease the number of stray animals in the county. The desired result would
    be less dogs and cats in the shelter.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
