4. Project/Program Description
    As a barrier island community, the Village acutely feels the impacts of sea level rise, e.g., increased storm frequency and
   intensity. Due to its proximity to Biscayne Bay and the Atlantic Ocean, the Village’s stormwater management system is
   influenced by tidal waters that raise groundwater levels and present a significant challenge for the 50-year-old stormwater
   drainage system. These factors produce frequent flood events, keeping residents and workers from using roadways and
   accessing goods and services. This project aims to improve the function of the existing system by redesigning catch basins
   and regrading roadways to prevent debris from clogging the pipes and directing stormwater runoff to catch basins. These
   improvements are intended to reduce flooding in frequently flooded areas by ensuring our system is designed to function
   efficiently and effectively.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project purpose is to reduce flooding and stormwater system overflows that inundate the rights of way and
    eventually flow out into Biscayne Bay.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Specific flood prone areas have been identified and an inventory of catch basins and roadway conditions will inform the
    type of flood mitigation and extent of the improvement along each corridor. The specific improvements to mitigate flooding
    will be designed and then constructed. Catch basins will be curbed to prevent runoff debris from clogging the pipes,
    roadways will be repitched to better drain into the stormwater system, and green infrastructure solutions will be applied.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will not receive direct services from this appropriation project. As an infrastructure improvement project, all
    citizens and visitors of the Village will indirectly or directly benefit from reduced risk of flooding and improved conditions
    during rain events.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All residents (15,000) in the Village are the target population of this project. Since all rights-of-way to receive
    improvements are public roads, all residents have access to them and may need to use them to travel within the Village,
    even during flood events.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The stormwater runoff that pools in the roadways and on private property negatively affects public health. By reducing
    localized flooding, public health will be less impacted. This project will improve accessibility for emergency vehicles on
    roadways during flood events. This project will reduce the amount of runoff pollutants entering groundwater. Assessment
    of flood levels during rain events, reports from emergency responders, and regular monitoring of area conditions will
    provide metrics to track progress towards achieving these outcomes.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in the forfeiture of funds or financial penalties as described in the contract.
