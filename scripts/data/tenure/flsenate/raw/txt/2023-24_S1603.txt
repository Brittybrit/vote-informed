1. Project Title          Coral Gables City Hall Complex Storm Hardening

2. Senate Sponsor         Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request        02/03/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The project will include storm hardening of windows and structural elements of City Hall and the Intergovernmental Annex
   within the City Hall Complex to protect the historical integrity of the facilities and accommodate emergency services
   personnel and the public during hurricanes or any other catastrophic occurrence. The interior of the Annex will also be
   renovated to maximize its usage and create a hub for intergovernmental activity, more particularly, hosting office space for
   state and county elected officials and local school board members to facilitate the public's access to essential government
   services at all levels.

     The project has several goals, including protecting the historical integrity of the facilities. The City Hall complex dates
    back nearly 100 years and is on the National Historic Register. The project will also create a safe environment to
    accommodate emergency services personnel and the public during hurricanes or any other catastrophic occurrence.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Construction and renovation services.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will benefit from continued safe access to the City Hall Complex-allowing them to obtain essential government
    services.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Residents, the business community, permittees and others who seek government services from Coral Gables City Hall.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Measures of success may include but not be limited to, continued historic designation of the city hall and annex facilities
    and usage by employees, residents, visitors, permittees and others.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Non-reimbursement of state funds.
