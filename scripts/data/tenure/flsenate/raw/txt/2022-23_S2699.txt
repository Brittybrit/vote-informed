12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To promote a safer, healthier living environment for Miami's seniors to improve the quality of their living conditions
    including, but not limited to, targeting pandemic related threats.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds shall be earmarked for the cleaning and sanitization services referenced above targeting pandemic and other
    environmental related threats to seniors' living environment.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Cleaning and sanitization to safeguard seniors from pandemic related threats and routine maintenance to proactively
    combat COVID and other environmental related threats.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Senior citizens in Miami-Dade County. Seniors Are Not Alone hopes to target as many seniors as possible.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     To curtail and/or eliminate contamination and assist to sanitize living areas to reduce risk of infection.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     There are no penalties expected. The penalties for non-deliverables or performance would be the withholding of funds.
