4. Project/Program Description
    The New Heights Microloan Fund provides entrepreneurs in Miami-Dade County the access to capital and technical
   support required for them to create new opportunities and grow. New Heights will now make capital easier and more for
   innovators to access funding. Once these funds are paid back at an interest rate of 3%, the capital will be loaned out to new
   entrepreneurs who will benefit from the microloan fund.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Entrepreneurs will be assisted and guided through the process of filling out the micro loan applications. We will provide
    education on financial management and planning to applicants to help them grow their operations and continue providing
    and creating jobs for Miami-Dade residents.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Partners for Self-Employment, the partner for this program, will issue microloans up to $10,000 and provide technical
    assistance and mentorship, where they will offer expert advice on planning and management.

    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The direct services will include access to affordable financial support for entrepreneurs. The program will help to provide
    working capital and other financial resources to growing enterprises. The microloan program will also offer financial
    literacy and development services. The goal is to empower entrepreneurs with the skills they need to succeed and be
    self-sufficient.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     100-200 local entrepreneurs who provide jobs to residents and economic growth.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     By providing access to capital, entrepreneurs will be able to hire more employees, therefore leading to job creation. The
    success of this outcome will be measured by the number of positions created / personnel hired. A major goal is to
    empower entrepreneurs to access other funding / learning opportunities so they are self-reliable. We will measure this
    result by tracking applicants' continuous growth after applying, obtaining, and repaying this microloan.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables without notification will result in financial penalties as described in contract.
    Performance measures:
    -Issue 50 loans in two years, and on an ongoing basis
    -Repayment rate of 85% on loans issued
