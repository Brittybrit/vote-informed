1. Project Title          Jericho Road Men Shelter

2. Senate Sponsor         Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request        12/21/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Jericho Road Ministries is seeking to renovate its current 2,717 sq ft shelter facility in Brooksville, Florida that provides
   housing for male residents in its substance abuse recovery program and its transitional program for recovery graduates. It
   is the only program of its kind for men and has been operating at full capacity. The residential building requires updates to
   the roof, electric, plumbing, kitchen, bathrooms. etc. Once the facility is renovated, it will be able to operate 12 to 15
   additional program beds.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will be utilized to renovate our current property at 1090 Mondon Hill Rd. Brooksville, FL that has fallen in
    disrepair to be used for housing our male residents in our recovery program and transitional program for our male
    recovery graduates. The building will require updates to the roof, electric, plumbing, kitchen, bathrooms. etc. Once
    renovated, this would allow for 12-15 additional beds in our programs.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Renovations to our property at 1090 Mondon Hill Rd. to provide housing to our male residents of our programs
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Substance abuse recovery, job readiness training, job placement, mental health classes, life skill training, and obtaining
    healthcare.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    Persons with poor mental health, Jobless persons, Economically disadvantaged persons, Homeless, Drug users,
    Currently or formerly incarcerated persons, and Drug offenders. The program will serve 25-50 persons within a year.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Reduce substance abuse by connecting the residents to a community to keep them accountable to continue in their
    recovery. Residents in our recovery program attend 22 recovery classes and submit to random drug tests. Graduates in
    our transitional program are required to attend accountability meetings and submit to random drug tests.
    Divert from Criminal Justice System by working men enrolled in the Hernando County Drug Court program. We aid them
    in meeting the requirements and completing the program, allowing them to be in good standing with the court system.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in return of funds to the state.
