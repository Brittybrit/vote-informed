2. Senate Sponsor         Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request        02/02/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    The funds will serve to replace the structurally deficient corrugated metal pipe (CMP) culvert located at SW 64th Ct. within
   the Twin Lakes Canal in South Miami, Florida with a new bridge. A Visual inspections outlined in the Routine Inspection
   Report prepared by Marlin Engineering, Inc., dated December 16, 2019, illustrates the corrugated metal pipe (CMP) culvert
   is experiencing extreme deterioration and is structurally deficient with additional roadway pavement cracking and
   settlement above the culvert. In addition, the City of South Miami desires to provide pedestrian connectivity between the
   Manor Lane neighborhood and the exsiting metrorail and businesses in the area but due to the limited right of way adjacent
   to the existing culvert prevents the construction of the new sidewalk. A new bridge at this location would provide the desire
   connectivity and safety to the residents and visitors of the area.
