4. Project/Program Description
    The Women's Cancer Center at the Miami Cancer Institute will be a 150,000 square foot facility dedicated to expanding
   access to cancer services to women by providing a coordinated patient-centered environment created to meet growing
   patient demand and physician development needs. Services provided will include benign and high-risk imaging, medical
   and surgical oncology, plastics and reconstructive and gynecologic oncology surgery.
