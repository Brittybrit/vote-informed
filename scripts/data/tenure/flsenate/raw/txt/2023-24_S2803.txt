14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Construct new entrance and road to Whispering Pines Park from the widened US 41 N to increase tourism to a regional
    park owned by the state and leased to the City that is connected to the Florida National Scenic Trail.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Will introduce a large number of Florida residents and non-resident tourist to access the 300 acre Park and connected
    trails as current entrance is on a remote residential road. Will improve transportation by alleviating traffic from a
    residential road and currently congested intersection. Provide a new venue for all age groups to get outside and improve
    health and fitness in the park or on the trails by walking, running or cycling.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Increased parks and recreation opportunities. Economic development from the multi-billion dollar cycling industry in
    Florida.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     >800 The multi-purpose aspect of the project will appeal to a wide spectrum of age groups. Parents with small children -
    trail head park. Teens - park and trail activities. Adults / seniors - walking, jogging, dog walking and cycling.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improve physical health- Year by year comparison of emergency medical service calls. User count of the new park and
    trail.
    Improve mental health - User count of the new entrance and trails.
    Enrich cultural experience- The establishment of a western gateway to the City using public art, signage, and special
    lighting complemented by the proposed new park entrance.
    Increase tourism- Count of users accessing the regional park.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Any monies received from the state would be returned in like amounts for either percentage incomplete or in totality for
    project not commencing.
