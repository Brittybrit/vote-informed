1. Project Title           Citrus County - Inverness Airport Business Park Phase 2

2. Senate Sponsor          Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request         03/01/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    This Project request is the second (and final) of two phases for completing the construction of transportation and onsite
   water utility infrastructure for a 75+/- acre Business-Industrial Park. Infrastructure improvements includes all onsite utilities,
   telecommunication/fiber, stormwater treatment system, circulation roads, and an aircraft taxiway within the Park. This effort
   also includes the reconstruction of approximately 3,115' of the main roadway to the Business Park.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Construction of the infrastructure for a 75+/- acre Business-Industrial Park which includes internal circulation roads, all
    onsite utilities, telecommunication/fiber, reconstruction of the main access road to the Park, 3,350' of aircraft taxiway into
    the Park, and all required stormwater attenuation. The pad ready building site(s) in the Park will help attract between 350
    and 500 new permanent jobs and capital investment in aviation and other business activity, thereby diversify the County's
    economy with growth in target industries, and immediate stimulate with the hiring of trade related workers during
    construction.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Completion of the construction of the infrastructure for a 75+/- acre Business Park.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Construction jobs during the construction of the Project and approximately 350 to 500 permanent jobs.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    Residents of the State of Florida as well as Citrus County will be served by this improvement and well over 800 persons
    will be impacted.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Construction of the infrastructure for a 75+/- acre Business-Industrial Park which includes internal circulation roads, all
    onsite utilities, telecommunication/fiber, reconstruction of the main access road to the Park, 3,350' of aircraft taxiway into
    the Park, and all associated stormwater treatment facilities. With infrastructure in place, and pad ready sites to meet
    current new business facility demand, the Park will support between 350 and 500 new jobs for the citizens of Citrus
    County, including accommodating aviation and other business related activities.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Completing the infrastructure of the Business Park, creating pad ready building sites for economic development fulfills
    the contracting deliverables. With few if any other permit ready sites with infrastructure in the County, delayed
    development of the Business Park severely inhibits the potential economic growth in Citrus County.
