2. Senate Sponsor        Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request       02/21/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    As the community requests more lifesaving programs for shelter pets, last year a pilot program for 5 shelter dogs was
   developed and proved that training resources are critical to ensuring dogs with behavioral or temperamental characteristics
   are afforded the opportunity to be re-homed while greatly reducing shelter euthanasia. All 5 dogs were either taken by
   rescue and/or adopted by a family. This program increases the likelihood of adoption and improves the quality of life for
   dogs.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To create a program where monthly approximately 5 shelter dogs with behavioral or temperamental characteristics will
    be sent to training facilities to increase their opportunities to be adopted. Implementing this program will save more lives
    and reduce shelter euthanasia.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Decreasing the daily shelter population, owner surrendering their pets, and citizens returning adopted pets. This will
    improve human and animal bond as it relates to health.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     If implemented, this program will secure more adoptable shelter dogs and reduce the chances of incidents and increase
    efforts in conserving public safety.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     2.7 million residents of Miami-Dade County would benefit from living in a more humane and conscientious environment
    by reducing the euthanasia of dogs with behavioral or temperamental characteristics.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit would be reflected in the decrease of the dog euthanasia rate and increase in adoptions while also
    reducing the length of stay of these dogs at the shelter by making them more adoptable. The outcome will be measured
    by comparing the percentage of dog euthanasia rate year over year.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Standard contract penalties are sufficient.
