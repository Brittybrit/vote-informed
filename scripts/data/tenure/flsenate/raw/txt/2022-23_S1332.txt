12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Florida ranks third in the country for calls of suspected human trafficking to the National Human Trafficking Hotline. In
    2020-2021, there were 2,289 reports of alleged exploitation to the Florida Abuse Hotline (Florida Dept. of Children and
    Families 2020-2021 Annual Human Trafficking Report). To increase awareness and education to individuals with native
    languages other than English, the Florida Alliance is seeking to expand its existing training course and offer it in a variety
    of languages.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funds will be provided to expand an existing training and awareness course to a larger audience (non-English
    speaking personnel) to learn the signs and indicators of human trafficking. The Florida Legislature passed HB 851 in FY
    2019-2021 to require hospitality and tourism professionals to complete an annual human trafficking awareness course.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     If the funding request is approved, the Florida Alliance will be able to offer the training to a more broad range of
    individuals who must complete it due to their profession. (e.g. hotel and motel staff, apartment managers, lawn and
    garden landscapers, housekeepers, receptionists, bellmen, valet, etc.) To date, over 5,500 individuals have completed
    the awareness training.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Hospitality and tourism professionals who are required to take human trafficking awareness training by law and the
    general public.
    Individuals expected to be served - < Thousands

    be measured?
