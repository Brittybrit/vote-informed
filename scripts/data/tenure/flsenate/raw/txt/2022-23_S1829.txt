1. Project Title          CFK Academy Classroom Facility and EOC

2. Senate Sponsor         Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request        12/14/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    Enable the College to complete the CFK Academy Classroom Facility and EOC. In addition, the facility will be equipped
   with educational technology to support exceptional learning for CFK Academy students who are maximizing dual
   enrollment opportunities in Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM) courses and Career and Technical
   Education programs. The emergency operations center (EOC) will be used as a command center for the coordination of
   efforts to recover following severe weather or other disruptive events that may take place. The EOC will strengthen the
   College's ability to mitigate the risk of loss and theft of assets and expedite the recovery from an incident by providing an on
   campus 24/7 incident command and security facility.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The CFK Academy Classroom Facility and EOC building will be constructed and the charter school will enroll up to 200
    students. The charter school will maximize dual enrollment opportunities for students in the College's Science,
    Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM) courses and Career and Technical Education (CTE) programs. In
    addition, the facility will serve as the College's EOC.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The facility will house a charter school for grades 9-12. The charter school will maximize dual enrollment opportunities for
    students in the College's Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM) courses and Career and Technical
    Education (CTE) programs. In addition, the facility will serve as the College's EOC.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The facility will house a charter school for grades 9-12. The charter school will maximize dual enrollment opportunities for
    students in the College's Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM) courses and Career and Technical
    Education (CTE) programs.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population are high school students. Up to 200 students in grades 9-12 will be served.
