4. Project/Program Description
    We intend to purchase a transition home for women released from FL prisons, with the goal of reducing recidivism. LEAP
   had 2 homes for graduates of our in-prison program, which has been delivered for 14 years pursuant to an MOU with FDC.
   The housing program has been a success in terms of employment and recidivism. However, the homes are rentals, which
   with the housing crisis, poses challenges. We had to vacate our 2nd house due to plumbing issues and currently have a
   waiting list for housing.
   The services are part of LEAP’s comprehensive incarceration to community model of reentry for justice-involved women.
   The requested funds will specifically be used to purchase a house for the post release housing component of the
   continuum of care. Activities will include housing, classes, case management, employment. Classes cover addiction, life
   skills, anger management and parenting.

     We intend to purchase a transition home for women released from FL prisons, with the goal of reducing recidivism. LEAP
    had 2 homes for graduates of our in-prison program, which has been delivered for 14 years pursuant to an MOU with
    FDC. The housing program has been a success in terms of employment and recidivism. However, the homes are rentals,
    which with the housing crisis, poses challenges. We had to vacate our 2nd house due to plumbing issues and currently
    have a waiting list for housing.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The services are part of LEAP’s comprehensive incarceration to community model of reentry for justice-involved women.
    The requested funds will specifically be used to purchase a house for the post release housing component of the
    continuum of care. Activities will include housing, classes, case management, employment. Classes cover addiction, life
    skills, anger management and parenting.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     1. Up to 730 nights of safe housing per resident, for up to 12 residents at a time
    2. Reduced rent and savings assistance- One month free and then $450/month rent and $50/month going into saving
    account
    3 Meetings with case workers, trauma informed addiction groups
    4. Wraparound health services
    5. Post release employment at LEAP’s paid internship program
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Target population to be served: economically disadvantaged persons, jobless, persons with poor mental health,
    homeless, currently or formerly incarcerated persons, drug offenders, victims of crime. With the Transitional Housing
    program, we can house up to 12 women at one time. We expect to serve at least 20 women per year.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     PROTECT THE GENERAL PUBLIC FROM HARM: Less than 2% of residents return to prison or jail for a violent crime
    (as measured by state recidivism data. All participants will complete "Beyond Anger and Violence", an in-depth, gender
    specific, evidence based cognitive behavioral program. Attendance and completion are verified by LEAP instructors who
    deliver the course).
    CREATE SPECIFIC IMMMEDIATE JOB OPPORTUNITIES:
    1. Every resident will be employed as part of LEAP's paid technology and retail internship program for at least 3 months
    following her release from prison.
    2. 90% of participants will secure permanent employment following the paid internship. Case manager and LEAP staff
    follow up.
    REDUCE RECIDIVISM: The rate of recidivism for residents will be less than 7%. Measured by the Department of
    Corrections data.
    REDUCE SUBSTANCE USE, ENHANCE SPECIFIC INDIVIDUAL'S ECONOMIC SELF SUFFICIENCY, -Case manager
    follow up.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Being banned from potential funding for 1 year.
