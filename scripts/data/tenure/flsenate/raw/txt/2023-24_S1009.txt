1. Project Title          Town of Bay Harbor Islands Emergency Backup Generator

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       01/12/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Replacement and upgrade of forty-seven year old emergency backup generator, new and replacement wiring, and new
   transfer switches. The existing generator is not reliable for the town's police department in times of extended power
   outages. If the existing generator were to fail, the town police would not be able to operate under hurricane and emergency
   conditions.
