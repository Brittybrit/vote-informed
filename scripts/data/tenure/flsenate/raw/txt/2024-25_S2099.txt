    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     We will provide a six-week summer camp, free of charge, to low income and under-served youth between the ages of 5
    and 15, that reside in the Lake Forest community and also others in the area that attend a Title I school. These children
    are most susceptible to the effects of summer slide and delinquency. In furtherance of community outreach, we will
    provide counseling, food and clothing services to families, veterans, seniors and youth of northwest Duval County.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Comprehensive literacy program, an intense math and science curriculum and feeding program. Developed community
    programs include critical thinking; problem solving; and data collection, financial literacy programs, quarterly community
    engagement program, weekly mental health and drug prevention counseling services and homework assistance.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Campers take weekly trips that give participants the opportunity to experience new educational, cultural and
    environmental interactions that they would not ordinarily experience. All field trips are directly related to our summer
    learning program design and are free of charge to the campers. Community members receive food, counseling, financial
    literacy help, clothing and mental health support year-round.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Elderly persons, persons with poor mental health, persons with poor physical health, jobless persons, economically
    disadvantaged persons, at-risk youth, homeless, developmentally disabled, preschool students, grade school students,
    high school students, university/college students, currently or formerly incarcerated persons and victims of crime.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     We will provide a robust physical activity program, mental health counseling services to campers and their families,
    incorporate gardening and cooking classes along with math, science and literacy instruction. We will conduct follow-up
    interviews with the campers and families to assess lessons learned during the counseling sessions and address any
    necessary follow-up needed by the campers and their families. Children's knowledge will be tested at predetermined
    phases throughout the six-week program for comprehension.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     If deliverables or performance measures are not met, we will identify the weakness, place the deficient area or task
    under direct supervision of the Director/Project Lead and implement remedial measures. We will also consider expanding
    our intended audience beyond our immediate area to other parts of Duval County.
