4. Project/Program Description
    To provide after school programs for underprivileged children. Our goal is for no child to stay behind because of lack of
   funds. Our most important program is providing free tutoring for any child from first through twelfth grades. For this program
   we partner with the FIU Honors College students for the tutors. We reward the children that attend the tutoring programs by
   allowing them to join any one of our three current programs promoting "Education through Physical Fitness". The three
   programs are: Taekwondo, Dance and Archery. We would like to start providing a fourth program with music. We will be
   opening a second location by years end in South Miami Height and offering the same programs.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    The purpose is to continue, enhance and expand the services provided to the economically, disadvantage residents of
    Sweetwater and surrounding low income areas.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     For the children from K-12grades after school tutoring, Taekwondo, Dance, piano, and SAT Prep classes
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Tutoring: Nearly 100 students with one teacher. Although we have volunteer students from FIU Honors college, since
    they come voluntary we cannot demand their presence. NEED: 1 Assistant teacher. We have some old computers with
    old educational programs, NEED: 4 new laptops with new programs and additional memory. This will enable these
    students to develop their computer savvy. College SAT prep classes. NEEDS: Teacher and prep books. Physical
    Education programs: Taekwondo-40 athletes (10 in competition team) with one instructor. NEEDS: Asst. Instructor,
    athletes eqpt. plus funding for 3 competition. Dance-30 dancer NEED: Asst. teacher. Archery-35 archers w/1teacher.
    NEEDS: Asst teacher
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Children from 3-18yrs old, underserved and/or at risk youth, economically disadvantaged children from Sweetwater
    community and surrounding. We expect to serve 200 children.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Overall, to give economically disadvantaged children the same educational and physical fitness granted to any child. Our
    free tutoring program enables parents to afford for their children a private tutor with additional instructions or educational
    guidance. Success is measured with their academic improvement. Our 3 educational through physical fitness programs
