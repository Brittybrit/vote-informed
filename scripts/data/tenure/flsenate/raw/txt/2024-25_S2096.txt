4. Project/Program Description
    Operation Community Shield, a strategic initiative, led by the Miami-Dade Police Department (MDPD) in partnership with
   local, state, and federal law enforcement agencies, addresses communities most impacted by gun violence, the reduction
   of gun-related crime, and the removal of firearms from the streets. This three-pronged approach encompasses intelligence
   gathering, community engagement, and a proactive enforcement/deterrent initiative. Recent internal data analysis reveals
   that Operation Community Shield resulted in an overall reduction in shootings (49% reduction from 2020 to 2021) and a
   significant number of firearms seized.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Reduction in gun violence in Miami-Dade County
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Increased law enforcement operations - Internal data compiled reveals that Operation Community Shield resulted in an
    overall reduction in shootings (49% reduction from 2020 to 2021) and a significant number of firearms seized.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This additional funding is needed to ensure the continuity of existing and effective anti-violence measures, and to provide
    the necessary financial support to create and implement sustainable solutions to the violence that continues to threaten
    and destabilize the daily lives of our residents and the economic vitality of our County.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Persons residing in our most vulnerable high-crime communities as well as the entire Miami-Dade County population of
    2.8 million people
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The initiative involves local, state, federal, and community partners working together to identify, suppress, interrupt, and
    disrupt violent crimes with a focus on guns and group-related violence. Metrics will include statistics on incidents of violent
    crime, including but not limited to homicides by firearm, contact shootings and non-contact shootings.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
