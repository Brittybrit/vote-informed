1. Project Title          Pigeon Key Wastewater & Irrigation Upgrades

2. Senate Sponsor         Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request        11/23/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    This project will bring Pigeon Key into compliance with state mandates for wastewater treatment and provide for an
   irrigation system using gray water. The project will improve water quality within the Florida Keys National Marine Sanctuary
   while promoting the health of both marine species and vegetation on the island. The project will also highlight the steps that
   the state and Monroe County are taking to preserve the natural habitat through the Pigeon Key Foundation's public
   outreach/education.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project will bring Pigeon Key into compliance with state mandates for wastewater treatment and provide for an
    irrigation system using gray water. The project will improve water quality within the Florida Keys National Marine
    Sanctuary while promoting the health of both marine species and vegetation on the island.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funding will be used to upgrade the wastewater treatment and irrigation system on Pigeon Key.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Improvement of near shore water quality will help improve the health of both marine species and vegetation in and
    around Pigeon Key. Improvements in near shore water quality will benefit both residents and visitors in the Florida Keys.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This project will benefit both residents and visitors in the Florida Keys.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

    Upgraded wastewater treatment and irrigation system and improvement of near shore water quality which can be
    measured through analyzing water quality (before and after) in the area surrounding Pigeon Key.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
