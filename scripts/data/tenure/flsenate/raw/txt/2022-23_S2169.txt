4. Project/Program Description
    The funds will be used to assist in purchasing equipment required for an Urban Search and Rescue Team in Palm Beach
   County. The equipment purchased will be become part of a newly formed Urban Search & Rescue Team which would have
   the ability to respond to needed emergencies around the state as well as to train staff. This would include incidents such as
   the Surfside building collapse, hurricanes or other events.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    The goal is to increase the readiness and ability to respond to man made or natural disasters within the State of Florida.
    The equipment purchase will be become part of a newly formed Urban Search & Rescue Team in Palm Beach County,
    which would have the ability to respond to needed emergencies locally, regionally and around the state.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     To meet the goal of standing up a Urban Search and Rescue team, Palm Beach County Fire Rescue is building a
    training prop necessary to train for building collapse. Extensive training will be provided to produce a team ready to be
    deployed. Additionally, drills would be conducted to test the deployment readiness and adjust plans to meet any response
    requirements.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Emergency response to man made and natural disasters around the state.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The potential service population could be anyone within the State of Florida.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     State Certified Urban Search and Rescue Team. This can be measured by receiving the certification from FDEM.
