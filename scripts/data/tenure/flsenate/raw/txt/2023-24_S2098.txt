4. Project/Program Description
    Citrus County recently commissioned their new Advanced Wastewater Treatment (AWT) facility, the Southwest Regional
   Water Reclamation Facility (SWRWRF). The facility is located approximately 3.9 miles from the Chassahowitzka spring
   head, a first magnitude spring, and is in the priority focus area of the Chassohowitzka/Homosassa BMAP. The SWRWRF
   was designed and constructed to provide public-access reuse-quality effluent. The purpose of the proposed project is to
   provide additional improvements at the Southwest Regional Water Reclamation Facility so that the high quality reclaimed
   water produced at the plant can be made available for beneficial reuse. The necessary improvements include a one-million
   gallon ground storage tank, a high service pump station, a booster pump station at the Sugarmill Woods Golf Course, four
   miles of reclaimed water transmission main to connect from the plant site to the golf course irrigation system, and
   associated instrumentation and control systems.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project is part of a continuing effort conducted by FDEP and SWFWMD to restore and increase the spring flow into
    the Homosassa Springsheds by reducing the amount of groundwater pumping that causes stress on our natural
    freshwater resource as well as reducing the amount of nutrient pollution entering the springsheds. As part of the 2016
    Florida Statutes chapter 373-Water Resources Part II-Permitting of Consumptive Uses of Water, 373.250 Paragraph
    (1)(a)(b), "The encouragement and promotion of water conservation and reuse of reclaimed water are state objectives
    and considered to be in the public interest. In addition, the proposed project is consistent with the Florida Springs and
    Aquifer Protection Act.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The permitting, design and construction of a central wastewater collection system made available to the residents in the
    project area.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Consolidate the County's reclaimed water system, restore and increase water flow into homosassa, and reduce the
    amount of nutrient pollution entering homosassa.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The project will serve the many residents as well as the tourists that enjoy the natural beauty of the many springs that
    are located in the Citrus County community.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The project will provide for reduction in nutrient loading to environmentally sensitive springs and Coastal Rivers. The
    effluent of the reclaim water effluent will have TN levels of 3 mg/l or less. For groundwater quality and surface water
    quality, the project will reduce groundwater pumpage by substituting high quality reclaim water for existing groundwater
    usage.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     A financial penalty will be considered when failure to meet contract deliverables.
