14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The project will provide for the design and construction of a wastewater pump station to serve the Bird Road commercial
    corridor between SW 92nd and 97th Avenues.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funding will provide design and construction services for a wastewater pump station.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Sanitary sewer service will be made available to commercial property owners within the project area.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The pump station will provide sanitary sewer service to approximately 33 commercial properties between SW 92 Ave.
    and 97 Ave. along Bird Road. This station can also support future sewer gravity expansion to properties within the pump
    station basin, which is roughly a square mile area, as additional funding or development occurs.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Removal of septic tank systems, a source of nutrient and bacterial pollution. The number of disabled septic tank systems
    will be tracked to determine outcome. This project also supports the economic development of this area by permitting for
    additional uses and business expansion for commercial properties that connect to the sanitary sewer system. This
    outcome can be tracked by the number of building permits issued
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
