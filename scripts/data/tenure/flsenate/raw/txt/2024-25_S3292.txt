14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To respond to the housing crisis in South Dade as the cost of homes are rising, the number of people who can afford to
    purchase a home is decreasing. Furthermore, this problem has contributed to broken families, violence, and
    disenfranchised communities. Increasing the number of people who can buy homes and keep their home is a way to
    address this problem. The goal is to help 25 families become 1st time homeowners by 2025 by taking a class on
    budgeting, eliminating debt, mortgages, and savings.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Clients will take a 9 hour financial literacy course that will cover budgeting, debt elimination, building an emergency
    savings, and saving for retirement. Client will show proficiency by creating a quick budget, and completing chapter
    assessments to show understanding. A financial coach will be assigned to each participant to meet 2x's/month to develop
    a financial plan, and provide support.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     1) 9-hour Financial literacy course
    2) Pre & Post Assessment of financial literacy
    3) Monthly Financial coaching
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     General population
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Program participants will improve their financial literacy through extensive coaching on budget maintenance and credit
    calculation and improvement techniques. Program participants will be assessed on their general financial literacy both
    directly prior and directly after participation in the program. Assessments will be identical, and will test their ability to
    complete budget sheets and calculate their credit. Using personal budget template provided by Federal Trade
    Commission.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Return of funds to the state
