4. Project/Program Description
    Improvements to Deering Estate to connect the general public to bay resources, recreation, and water education which
   include targeted enhancements to the visitor and welcome center, community theatre and exhibits. Additionally, funds will
   be used for improvements to access and public use of the People’s Dock for wildlife viewing and fishing which includes
   seating and shade structures. The project will also support a youth-centered environmental education and STEM initiative

   and inclement weather protection.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Improvements to Deering Estate to connect the general public to bay resources, recreation, and water education which
    include targeted enhancements to the visitor and welcome center, community theatre and exhibits. Additionally, funds will
    be used for improvements to access and public use of the People’s Dock for wildlife viewing and fishing which includes
    seating and shade structures. The project will also support a youth-centered environmental education and STEM initiative
    by replacing two existing pavilions with one large enclosed pavilion which will provide for increased capacity as well as
    heat and inclement weather protection.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Proposed funds will be used to enclose and expand open air learning pavilions to provide for heat, rain, and lightning
    protection, while maintaining students, teachers, and parent educator access to natural settings, providing the needed
    infrastructure to deliver environmental education programs and services to residents and visitors in South Florida,
    primarily Miami-Dade County.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Direct services include nature-based recreation and environmental STEM education programs conducted in pavilions:
    field study trips, citizen science, professional development workshops, volunteer projects, scout programs, and year-
    round day camps. These supplement school and home learning and expose youth/educators to environmental
    understanding unique and critical to our region’s resiliency. Additionally, the services would include heritage tourism
    programs and nature-based recreation - passive wildlife viewing, field study trips, citizen science, lectures and film
    showings, professional development, community training, and public meeting spaces - exposing the public to unique
    environmental understanding critical to our region’s resiliency, while improving tourism, small businesses and property
    values.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is the general public and would serve over 800 individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The benefit and outcomes would be measured by the number of programs, program participants and increase in park
    attendance. This will be measured by the increased access to curated nature–based experiences promote improved
    physical, mental and social health and wellbeing.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Standard penalties for non-compliance are adequate.
