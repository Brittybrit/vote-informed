1. Project Title          West Miami Senior Activity Center Development (Phase I)

2. Senate Sponsor        Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request       11/08/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The development of a new Senior Activity Center at our current Recreation Park Location but will be divided with separate
   entrance and restrooms for the seniors. By developing this new Senior Activity Center the city will be able to provide more
   services to the low-income, under-served minority senior citizens of the city and surrounding communities. The increased
   space will allow for more outreach and opportunity to partner with local businesses and health providers to offer senior
   specific activities, learning and recreational opportunities. The home-bound meal program and daily meals provided to
   congregates will continue with nutritional programing and monthly birthday celebrations and holiday parties to allow seniors
   a greater sense of connection with their senior counterparts and the community participation.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The specific purpose and goal to be achieved with the funds is the development of a new Senior Activity Center to
    continue providing a safe, secure and fun space for the low-income senior citizens of West Miami and surrounding
    communities to gather for daily activities to encourage socializing, providing educational and health related information
    sessions, provide lunch to congregates and home-bound participants and encourage getting involved with the
    community. The Senior Activity Center also helps with loneliness for seniors who either live alone or are left home alone
    while family is away during the day.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Building a new Senior Activity Center at the Recreation Center Park location, will allow to city to significantly expand
    services by partnering with local health providers and business. More space means we can increase the number of
    participants and reach more low-income seniors who would like to be more active. Current services & activities: daily
    meals, home-bound meal program, nutritional program, health services speakers, special field trips, daily activities, Tai
    Chi, Arts & Crafts, Parchment, Knitting, Crochet, Decoupage, Dominoes and Bingo gaming. All activities are at no cost to
    participants who are low-income, under-served minorities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Direct services will be provided to the low-income under-served senior community with the funds from this appropriation.
    The Senior Activity Center is specific for the aging population of our community and they will be direct recipients to the
    increased programming and services this new facility will bring. It is our responsibility to care for our elderly and provide
    them with opportunity to continue to thrive and participate in daily activities that will keep them sharp, engaged and
    smiling.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is the over 15% senior citizen population in the City, which is increasing by roughly 25% with the
    increased construction of multi-family buildings. We currently serve over 100 registered residents and non-residents with
    congregate or homebound services. Due to COVID our attendance took a dip, but has been picking back up, with the
    attraction of a new state of the art facility with more space for increased services and more activities we expect with
    advertisement of the coming facility more low-income seniors will learn about our facility and get excited to become
    members.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcome of this phase 1 is to begin permitting and begin constructing the new Senior Activity Center. Upon
    completion the expected benefit will be increased services and programs made available to a larger number of low-
    income senior citizens.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Withholding of funds is a fair penalty for failing to meet deliverables.
