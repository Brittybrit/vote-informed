4. Project/Program Description
    The Connecting Miami project features the mile-long, 33 acre linear urban oasis, Underdeck component which will
   overcome the barrier effect of the original highway network, and create a safe, inviting passage linking residents and
   businesses in Overtown with the Arts & Entertainment district to the east. The project will transform the community,
   enhance safety, increase mobility, ensure sustainability and restore connectivity to the Overtown community.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Connecting Miami project features the mile-long, 33 acre linear urban oasis, Underdeck component which will
    overcome the barrier effect of the original highway network, and create a safe, inviting passage linking residents and
    businesses in Overtown with the Arts & Entertainment district to the east. The project will transform the community,
    enhance safety, increase mobility, ensure sustainability and restore connectivity to the Overtown community.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The operations and maintenance (O&M) phase of the Underdeck will commit $6,000,000 annually to facility upkeep and
    programming of activities. It is estimated that 260 jobs will be directly created, with another 160 indirect permanent jobs
    across the regional economy, with an average compensation of over $40,000/year. The Underdeck will provide over a
    mile of trails, playgrounds and green spaces in a 33 acre urban oasis in extending from Overtown to Biscayne Boulevard.
    While accessible and safe for all users - pedestrians, bicyclists, rollers, transit riders, and drivers alike - the Underdeck
    will facilitate a safe environment for walking and outdoor activities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Residents and tourist will be able to enjoy amenities such as fountains, trails, benches, entertainment venue, murals and
    landscape.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population are those in the immediate area of the project in the Overtown community, as well as residents of
    the City of Miami as a whole. The amount of individuals expected to be served exceeds 400,000.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit and outcome of the project is to create a common area within a developing area of the City of
    Miami for the community to enjoy the public space and related amenities. This outcome will be measured by the amount
    of residents using the park.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables or performance measures should subject the City to an audit and depending on the results
    of the audit the City could be liable for sanctions up to revocation of funding.
