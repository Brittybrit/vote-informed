14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The objective of these funds is to establish 16 additional secure housing options for children in the foster care system
    and support those who have aged out, ensuring they have a stable environment. By providing safe and stable housing,
    our aim is to protect these vulnerable individuals from the threats of human trafficking and involvement in the criminal
    justice system. Your support will empower them and create a positive impact, offering a brighter future for those in need.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The construction and furnishing of new housing opportunities tailored for aged-out foster children. We are committed to
    creating a supportive environment by not only constructing the homes but also ensuring they are equipped with essential
    appliances. Our focus is on delivering comprehensive solutions to empower these individuals as they transition into
    independent living. From the construction phase to the provision of appliances, our goal is to foster a secure and
    comfortable space for aged-out foster children to thrive in their newfound independence.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Directly serving citizens by offering two primary services: construction of new housing tailored for aged-out foster children
    and the purchase of essential appliances for these homes. Through the construction service, we aim to provide safe and
    comfortable living spaces, specifically designed to meet the unique needs of individuals who have aged out of the foster
    care system. Additionally, our appliance purchase service ensures that these homes are equipped with the necessary
    tools for daily living, fostering independence and self-sufficiency. By combining these services, we strive to make a
    tangible and positive impact on the lives of aged-out foster children, empowering them to embark on their journey towards
    independent living.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Our primary focus is on aged-out foster children who are transitioning into independent living. The target population
    comprises individuals who have reached the age where they are no longer under the care of the foster care system.
    These young adults often face unique challenges as they navigate the transition to self-sufficiency.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     These new homes will offer safe spaces, nutritious meals, and access to healthcare, addressing their basic needs and
    promoting a healthier lifestyle. Regular meals and a secure living environment are fundamental to their physical
    development, ensuring they have the necessary foundation for a better future. the sense of belonging and stability
    provided by these homes will foster emotional well-being. A supportive environment can boost their self-esteem,
    confidence, and sense of security, helping them cope with past traumas and navigate life's challenges more effectively.
    Professional counseling and mentorship programs within these homes can further enhance their mental health. Conduct
    a comprehensive assessment of the participants' current status in terms of physical and mental health, substance abuse
    issues, and economic self-sufficiency before they enter the new homes. This provides a baseline to measure progress.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Organization should return funding if it fails to meet deliverables or performance measures.
