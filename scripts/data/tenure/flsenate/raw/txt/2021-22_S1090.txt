4. Project/Program Description
    Cuban American Bar Association Pro Bono Project's mission is to change the lives of children, individuals and families in
   need by providing free legal services and representation to those who cannot otherwise afford it - regardless of race, creed,
   color, gender, sexual orientation or national origin. We focus on helping victims of Domestic Violence, Victims of Human
   Trafficking, Veterans and children. Caba Pro Bono employs a staff of 10 including 6 attorneys, 3 paralegals and 1 office
   manager.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Holistic legal Services and Representation will be provided to Veterans, Victims of Human Trafficking, Victims of
    Domestic Violence and Children that have been abused, abandoned and neglected. By providing free legal services we
    are assisting individuals and families regain personal stability, safety, join the work force and become contributing
    members of our State. The legal services that we provide empower victims, children, veterans in treatment court and
    other individuals that we represent to seek and maintain employment and/or an education. By empowering the weakest
    members in the State of Florida, the cumulative result is that we strengthen our State as a whole.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     We will provide free legal services and representation in various areas of law including: Family Law, Domestic Violence,
    Juvenile dependency and Landlord Tenant. We will be attending Veterans Treatment Court and G.R.A.C.E. Court weekly
    (G.R.A.C.E. court is specifically for victims of Human Trafficking) to offer legal representation to all veterans and victims
    of human trafficking with court proceedings. We will also conduct regular outreach across the State. Additionally, we will
    hold various CLE seminars to train and mentor volunteer attorneys that will be representing clients pro bono. These funds
    will help empower the most vulnerable members of our State through legal services so that they can become contributing
    members of our State.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Services will range from full direct court representation across multiple areas of law, to legal services with filling out
    documents to help the clients proceed pro se, to legal counsel and advice.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This program serves Victims of Human Trafficking, Victims of Domestic Violence, Veterans and Children in the Foster
    Care System. We serve the Elderly, jobless, economically disadvantaged and at-risk-youth. We expect to serve 1500 -
    1800 individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     When representing foster children, we request the appropriate support and educational placement, so that the child feels
    safe, can thrive and have a successful future. Additionally, victims are not provided with free legal services and thus do
    not usually testify against their perpetrators. By providing victims with legal representation, we are providing them with the
    support that they need to testify against the aggressor. This testimony aids the prosecution in obtaining convictions and
    keeps our State safer. By providing free legal services we are also reducing the amount of pro se litigants and increasing
    judicial economy along with the economic and social costs associated with domestic violence and human trafficking. By
    providing free legal services we veterans, we are providing support to those who have done so much for our Country and
    may currently be facing a legal family law or housing issue. Our services empower clients and reduces their future need
    for government assistance.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     If deliverables are not met, money will be deducted from the total amount due to the agency as detailed in the contract.
