4. Project/Program Description
    To secure funding for the rehabilitation of roadways as well as the extension of an existing roadway at the CSX served Rail
   Park located within the Brooksville Airport & Technology Center (BKV). The roads within the Rail Park are degraded and do
   not provide the appropriate infrastructure for heavy industrial use. Extending the roadway system to available land will
   provide for additional private investment in aviation related industries requiring runway access. The County has spent
   $919,000 in previous fiscal years towards this project. With the recent immediate interest the County is requested state
   assistance in FY 22/23. Funding was appropriated by the legislature in FY 22/23. The County has not been able to access
   the funds due to DOT requirements and asks that the money be reappropriated during FY 23/24..

     To secure funding for the rehabilitation of roadways as well as the extension of an existing roadway at the CSX served
    Rail Park located within the Brooksville – Tampa Bay Regional Airport & Technology Center (BKV). The roads within the
    Rail Park are degraded and do not provide the appropriate infrastructure for heavy industrial use. Additionally, this area
    serves as the east side access to the aircraft operations area (AOA) at BKV which is limited and built out. Extending the
    roadway system to available land will provide for additional private investment in aviation related industries requiring
    runway access.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Extending the roadway system to available land will provide for additional private investment in aviation related industries
    requiring runway access.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The rehabilitation of this roadway system in conjunction with other local investment incentives will offer the appropriate
    location for new manufacturing facilities. Additionally, this area provides direct access to the airside at BKV, and the
    extension of the existing roadway system will enhance the opportunity to recruit industries to the corporate airpark for
    future investment.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population would be current and future workforce between the ages of 18-65, as well as targeted industries
    for business recruitment and expansion.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This area provides direct access to the airside at BKV, and the extension of the existing roadway system will enhance
    the opportunity to recruit industries to the corporate airpark for future investment.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Revoke funding if project is not constructed
