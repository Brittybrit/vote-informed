14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will be used to identify and purchase land and for the construction of affordable housing for Monroe County's
    workforce in alignment with the statutory requirement the Florida Keys face as an Area of Critical State Concern to
    provide affordable housing in proximity to places of employment. This project will allow all three Habitat for Humanity
    affiliates in the Florida Keys to complete construction of new housing and identify opportunities for rehabilitating existing
    housing stock.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds will be used to provide much needed affordable housing to the workforce of the Florida Keys. Further, it will
    rehabilitate existing housing stock. These properties will allow residents to live in a community where they also work.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Development of new and rehabilitated housing for income qualified residents of the Florida Keys.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Economically disadvantaged persons and the general public. 25-50 people to be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improved physical health, improved mental health, improved quality of education, improved transportation conditions,
    increased and improved economic activity, job opportunities and enhanced economic self sufficiency. Data from
    homeowner interviews, measuring of the units occupied by individuals who would otherwise have to live outside of the
    community, number of individuals employed in construction of these sites and rental ownership success can be
    measured.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Withholding funds.
