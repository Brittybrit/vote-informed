4. Project/Program Description
    Village of Palmetto Bay is requesting funding for the construction and construction support for stormwater improvements to
   its sub-basins that need upgrading. Improvements include building additional catch basins, manholes, culverts & exfiltration
   to connect the catch basins within 3 identified Sub-Basins that require our most immediate attention. Improvements will
   reduce pollutant load contribution from 3 main pollutants from the sub-basins into Biscayne Bay Aquifer, control flooding
   during storms; such as Hurricane Irma; and improve surface water quality by limiting pollution to our waters.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Village of Palmetto Bay is requesting funding for the construction and construction support for stormwater improvements
    to its sub-basins. Improvements include building additional catch basins, manholes, culverts & exfiltration to connect the
    catch basins within sub-basins 11, 43 & 57/96. Improvements will reduce pollutant load contribution from 3 main
    pollutants from the sub-basins into Biscayne Bay Aquifer, control flooding during storms; such as Hurricane Irma; and
    improve surface water quality by limiting pollution to our waters.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Village will have to secure contractor to perform the construction via our procurement process.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     There will be no direct services provided to the citizens, but this appropriated project will benefit the residents who are
    directly impacted by the flooding taking place on the sub-basin roadways.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is residents of Southern Miami-Dade. Residents traversing from southern US1 north and along our
    arterial roads will benefit. The local residents will greatly benefit by no longer worrying about flooded roads to exit or enter
    their homes and neighborhood. We expect greater than 300 people will be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     With this funding the Village of Palmetto Bay will enhance/preserve/improve environmental of fish and wildlife quality by

    management, groundwater quality and surface water quality by reducing the loading pollutants into Biscayne Bay and
