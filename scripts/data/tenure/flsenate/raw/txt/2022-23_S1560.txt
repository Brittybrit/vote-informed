4. Project/Program Description
    Village of Pinecrest Water Line Extension Project - Pinecrest developed a Comprehensive Waterline Extension Project that
   consisted of a 38 mile system to address the water needs of approximately 1,500 well-dependent homes.This project was
   developed for various reasons: well systems in Pinecrest are vulnerable to saltwater intrusion; a lack of fire protection via
   hydrants; documented cases of contamination of several wells; & the inability of properties without generators to access
   water after hurricane disasters. Between 2007 & 2009, the Village completed Phases 1 & 2 which brought potable water to
   549 homes. This project consists of the installation of 8-,12-, & 16-inch water mains and extending new service lines from
   the water main to the lot lines, in accordance with Miami-Dade County Water and Sewer Department (WASD) standards. It
   also includes fire hydrants to provide fire protection. Each phase of the system is conveyed to WASD who will operate &
   maintain it.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project consists of the installation of 8-,12-, & 16-inch water mains and extending new service lines from the water
    main to the lot lines, in accordance with Miami-Dade County Water and Sewer Department (WASD) standards. It also
    includes fire hydrants to provide fire protection.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This project was developed for various reasons: well systems in Pinecrest are vulnerable to saltwater intrusion; a lack of
    fire protection via hydrants; documented cases of contamination of several wells; and the inability of properties without
    generators to access water after hurricane disasters.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This project will provide residents with potable public drinking water from Miami-Dade County; increase fire protection
    throughout the Village by installing fire hydrants; prevent the contamination of any of the Village’s resident’s drinking
    water (i.e. saltwater intrusion, arsenic, etc.); and, it will allow residents of Pinecrest to receive water to their homes,
    regardless of power outages during a disaster.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Approximately 740 Village of Pinecrest properties that do not have access to potable water.
