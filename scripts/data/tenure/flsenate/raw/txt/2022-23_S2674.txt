12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will address Stormwater pump stations rehabilitation including installation of cathonic protection; replacement
    of sluice gates and mechanical and electrical control panels. Scope to also include professional design services and
    construction engineering inspection services.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds will be used to address Stormwater pump stations rehabilitation including installation of cathonic protection;
    replacement of sluice gates and mechanical and electrical control panels. Scope to also include professional design
    services and construction engineering inspection services.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     No direct services will be provided directly to the citizens
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Residents and Businesses of the City. Appx. 25,410 residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     1. Improve physical health: Comparing number of flooding complaints before and after project
    2. Reduced/eliminated frequency of stormwater pump station failures.
    3. Protect the general public from harm (environmental, criminal, etc.): Compare level of streets flooded prior and after
    improvements.
