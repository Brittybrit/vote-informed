14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The overall goals will be to provide fixed building capital and nursing equipment to the School of Nursing in order to
    expand training of low-income minority nursing students. Provide a state of the art nursing education building for students
    to meet the documented need of the Nursing shortages in Florida.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Nursing Education by an accredited institution focused on training low-income minority students to serve in their
    community in Miami-Dade County and the state of Florida
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Healthcare and wellness treatment to improve the lives of Florida residents and thereby increasing their economic impact
    to the state while reducing the drain on state resources for incapacitated individuals.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Persons with poor mental health, Persons with poor physical health, economically disadvantaged persons and drug
    users.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improvement of educational quality of St. Thomas University minority students will be impacted with training opportunities
    given to students to pursue careers in medical care. Outcome measures will be performed by assessment of number of
    St. Thomas University students in the program and the number of students that pursue a career in medical treatment and
    care.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The University is open to penalties the Department deems appropriate.
