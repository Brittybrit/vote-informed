4. Project/Program Description
    Directive Health’s mission is to save lives and reduce healthcare costs. This is done by aggregating health data from not
   just medical record-keeping companies, but any available consumer health repository. With the Directive Health app, a
   healthy future is at the customer’s fingertips. Directive Health is respectfully requesting funding to improve the lives of
   children in the Florida Foster Care System by digitizing their medical records after an exploratory study of needs and
   subsequent integration and deployment phase.These easy to understand, yet secure, records will assist caregivers and
   social workers in staying organized so one of Florida’s most vulnerable populations, children in foster care, can thrive and
   meet physical and mental milestones.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The main goal of the Directive Health Foster Care Innovation Pilot Program is to digitize medical records for the 21,000
    children in the Florida foster care system. Given Florida’s focus on childhood well-being and historical ranking in the top
    five states of children in foster care, the Directive Health Pilot Program will perform an exploratory analysis and launch a
    pilot program to allow caregiver teams to have instant access to secure and streamlined client medical information. In
    addition, this program will dramatically reduce underlying fiscal expenses in currently managing Florida’s foster
    population. After a 1-3 month exploratory study of how the Directive Health application will integrate with the current
    ecosystem, there will be a 3-6 month implementation phase, to support a 6-12 month pilot program with 10% of the
    current foster care population (approx. 2100) participating.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     There will be three main activities that will be provided to the State of Florida with the proposed designated funds.
    1. The Directive Health Team will provide an exploratory study in months 1-3.
    2.The Directive Health Team will take findings from the study and implement the needed customized integrations,
    prototypes, and will focus on training and development for Department Staff in months 3-6.
    3.The Directive Health Team will on board and pilot the digitization of medical records for approximately 2100 children in
    the Florida Foster Care System. This pilot will include a dedicated care team for customer service needs.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Once funded, Directive Health’s discovery, integration, and pilot program will contribute a number of positive impacts.
    The innovation program will improve the accuracy of medical records for one of Florida’s most vulnerable populations -
    children under 18 that are in foster care. The aggregation of personal health records will increase the speed of care
    resulting in better health outcomes for participants. Particularly for children in foster care, better health outcomes
    decrease costly emergency care. Having nearly instant access to accurate medical records will allow parents, foster
    parents, social workers, and other interested parties to monitor not only primary medical information, but critical child
    development milestones related to motor, sensory, communication, and emotional skills. This program will create
    complete and accurate medical records for the foster population which will lead to expense waste reduction and fiscal
    savings for the state.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     There are over 21,000 children in Florida’s foster care system. In addition, to supporting that population there are over
    4,500 foster parents and 12,000 employees of the Florida Department of Children and Families that will benefit from the
    modernization of foster care children's medical records. Approximately 37,500 individuals will be served. The Directive
    Health pilot will provide support to approximately 2,100 foster children in Florida.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Digitizing medical records for one of Florida’s most vulnerable populations, children in the foster care system, will assist
    caregivers and key stakeholders in addressing physical and mental challenges quickly and efficiently. The Directive
    Health application will be customized for the program which will ensure that no child will fall through the cracks because
    their medical information will be easy to access, digest, and share. In addition, the optimization and streamlining of
    medical records for one of Florida's most vulnerable populations will lead to fiscal savings and expense waste reduction
    for the state.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     1. For agreed upon performance levels, we suggest creating service level agreements to ensure uptime, availability, and
    other key performance measures. 2. For contractual deliverables, we suggest a standard penalty per each deliverable on
    the agreed upon calendar basis. 3. If there are consecutive SLA/performance issues noted, Directive Health will create a
    remediation plan to get performance back to agreed upon levels. The content and activities in this plan will be agreed
    upon with the contracting agency. 4. If the remediation plan is unsuccessful, the contracting agency will have the option of
    Directive Health creating a secondary remediation plan or terminating the contract for cause.
