4. Project/Program Description
    Fit Kids of America (Fit Kids) has provided After-School and Day Camp programs to Miami-Dade County Pre-K to 8th
   grade students for more than 25 years. Its Co-Founders, Connie Sootin and Rhonda Smith have a life-long commitment of
   serving their communities and ensuring that FK offers quality academic and cultural enrichment programs to students
   through specialized youth fitness training, team sports, STEM and robotics project-based instruction, nutrition education
   programs that nurture the emotional fitness, mental health wellness and cognitive development of our youth. Fit Kids offers
   extended program hours and financial assistance throughout the year to support the parents and children who need it most.
   Fit Kids has won several prestigious awards for their work and dedication for the communities they serve, including Miami-
   Dade County Public Schools "Exemplary Partner Hall of Fame Member" and The Children's Trust 2018 Champions for
   Children "Program of the Year".

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This funding will be used to serve students Pre-K-12, with quality after-school and day camp programming in the under-
    resourced/under-served community in Miami-Dade County, Florida focusing on Holistic Engagement and Enrichment.
    Our goal is to support the development of all children who will become academically engaged, physically fit, socially
    adept, and culturally enriched and to mature into independent and successful citizens for the future. Fit Kids utilizes
    evidence-based strategies-curricula-enrichment programs; which include: Academic Success-Homework Assistance,
    FAB 5 & SPARK-SPORTS, LEGO-VEX (STEM), Meet the Masters & Arts-In-Action-Arts and Culture which align with
    state-academic-standards to provide daily homework assistance, literacy, differentiated learning instruction (DLI), fitness-
    team sports, social-emotional learning, snacks-supper. Children also receive instruction and experience a variety of
    enrichment activities such as field trips.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     1. Provide comprehensive programming to K-5 students with a focus on Arts & Culture (theatre), STEM (technology,
    mathematics) and Sports for after-school and summer camp programming.
    2. Provide comprehensive programming to 6th-8th grade students with a focus on STEM (technology, engineering) and
    Sports for summer camp programming.
    3. Provide academic programs that support school day learning. (Homework assistance, tutoring, differentiated literacy
    instruction, social-emotional learning and wellness, basic health and wellness). Nutrition Education, Snacks & Meals will
    be provided.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     1. Visual Arts will help students develop positive attitudes towards other cultures and build self-esteem and pride. They
    will produce numerous multi-media creations for which an art background is not necessary because all lesson plans are
    scripted and illustrated.
    2. Students will learn STEM through Game Design, which is as inherent to student learning as reading, writing,
    mathematics, and other core subjects. Game Design concepts can be integrated as early as Kindergarten and carry
    students through 12th grade in a planned pathway to prepare them for future success. 6-8th grade students will also build
    robots. FK is preparing students for future success in a STEM field.
    3. Students will experience improved life skills and character development through Sports activities designed to fit the
    needs of all the students FK serves.
    4. Financial assistance and support for the parents and families of all the children in our program.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The Fit Kids of America “K-12 Focused on Holistic Engagement and Enrichment" program proposes to serve 255 (K-5)
    Participants through After-School, Summer program Participants, 315 (K-5), 60 (6-8) Graders, 23% Children with
    Disabilities (CWD). Our program is fully inclusive and serves children of all abilities. SEW and REDI are woven
    throughout all curricula and activities that are provided for participants. FK serves a predominant number of first-
    generation American families. English is not the primary language spoken in the home, and over 95% of the population is
    Hispanic, with an obesity prevalence of 48.7%, at risk and vulnerable. Ten of twelve of the partner schools are Title I
    schools with enrollment of over 90% of students coming from socio-economically disadvantaged homes on the Free/
    Reduced lunch program.

     Expected benefits and outcomes of this projects:
    1. Literacy Skills: Children at the frustrational level improve to instructional or mastery level.
    2. Increase in overall knowledge and participation in health and wellness activities.
    3. Student Program Satisfaction: An average score of 3 or higher on satisfaction items captured, indicating children and
    youth generally "Agree" or "Strongly Agree" that they are satisfied with the program.
    4. Parent Program Satisfaction: an average score of 3 or higher on satisfaction items captured, indicating children and
    youth generally "Agree" or "Strongly Agree" that they are satisfied with the program.
    Impact will be measured through quantitative and qualitative data collection from students, parents, and staff. Student
    level data includes baseline data, and data from pre/post-surveys. This focus is on individual data and moving each
    student forward in incremental steps based on the program they are participating in.
