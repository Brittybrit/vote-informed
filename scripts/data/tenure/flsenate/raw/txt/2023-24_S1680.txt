4. Project/Program Description
    Miami-Dade County’s Wastewater Collection and Transmission (WCT) system is comprised of sixteen (16) utilities that rely
   on three regional wastewater treatment plants. The entire WCT system is subject to excess rain derived inflow and
   infiltration (RDII) which can result in Sanitary Sewer Overflows can affect public health and safety and the environment .
   The specific purpose is to identify root source(s) of inflow.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The project will serve to better define the root sources of Rain DerivedC and this will be used to focus limited resources
    to upgrade and/or repair the WCTS to minimize Sanitary Sewer Overflows (SSOs). By reducing SSOs, public health and
    safety will be improved.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The County will measure, evaluate, and quantify Rain Derived Inflow and Infiltration to identify root causes. Having this
    information, the County can focus limited resources to address root cuases and minimize sanitary sewer ovefrlows
    caused by rain derived inflow and infiltration.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Identifying rain derived inflow and infiltration will serve to improve the operations of the publicly owned treatment works,
    the sytsems that citizens rely on daily for public sanitation and businesses operations.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The entire County.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcome is to identify the root causes of rain derived inflow and infiltration. The measure will be quantifying
    RDII county-wide based on an annual volume; e.g., Millions of Gallons by Utility per Year. This data will be used to focus
    efforts to reduce RDII and thereby reduce SSOs. A reduction in SSOs can be measured based on monthly tabulations of
    SSO volumes for a total volume per year.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failing to meet deliverables or performance measures should result in withholding funds in direct correlation to the tasks
    not delivered or where performance measures were not satisfied. However, reasonable time shall be provided for the
    County to cure.
