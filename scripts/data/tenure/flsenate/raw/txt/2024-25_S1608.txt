   opportunity to reach their dreams by giving them the tools they need through education, learning initiatives, school supplies,
   and sports education. Our program aims to enrich the under-served children in our community through access to necessary
   school supplies and educational learning experiences.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Back packs and school supplies will be distributed to over 9,000 children in South Florida; 250+ under-served children
    will participate in free sports leagues and clinics multiple time annually; free SAT/ACT Prep Courses to high school
    students 25+ high school students; computer and/or laptops will be awarded to families and students that otherwise
    cannot afford one; and more educational opportunities.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     School supplies, back packs and uniforms will be purchased and distributed. Over 25 prep courses will be prepaid to
    offer to students that otherwise cannot pay for one. Computers or laptops will be purchased and awarded to families or
    students. Uniforms, basketballs, supplies, awards, refreshments, referees, etc. will be purchased and paid in order to
    have a successful Basketball league/clinic and be able to offer to over 250+ children.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Over 9,000 children will receive school supplies; 250+ children will learn to play basketball and be part of a team; high
    school students that need help preparing for SAT/ACT will be given the opportunity to take a prep course for free;
    computer or laptops will be awarded to families that cannot afford one; other learning opportunities can be created at no
    cost to families and children.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     We target low-income families and communities and student population eligible for free/reduced lunch and serve over
    9,000 children annually.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Students are benefiting from receiving the tools they need for their academic school year to be prepared while in school;
    students are either learning skills in a sport a given an opportunity to practice what they have learned while participating
    in a team; students are taking a prep course that will improve their scores for acceptance to college; all giving students a
    path for success and a road to higher education. Students given these tools and opportunities advance academically with
    completion of high school and apply to college and/or universities.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Restitution of amount rewarded.
