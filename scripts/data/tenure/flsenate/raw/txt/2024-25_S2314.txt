14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The extension of the 12" water main is imperative for establishing a looped water system, ensuring consistent water flow
    and pressure to residents and businesses Simultaneously, the project includes a proactive approach towards DaMac
    Estates aging water distribution to mitigate the risk of failures.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Engineering and construction of a 12" water main and Water Distribution System Upgrade at DaMac Estates.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Guarantee a continuous quality potable water supply to residents and businesses while reducing unnecessary water
    usage. This comprehensive water distribution upgrade is essential to prevent potential disruptions, enhance water quality
    and inconveniences for the community.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All citizens and visitors of the City, population est. 9,566, and visitors may benefit from this project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     By addressing these critical aspects collaboratively, the project aims to optimize operational efficiency, enhance water
    quality, and accommodate future growth and development for the City of Brooksville. This will be measure by increased
    operational efficiency and reduce the frequent need for flushing, reduction in water disruption as well as mitigating a
    failure of the entire water distribution system system.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Withhold payment.
