1. Project Title          African Museum of Arts and Culture - Miami-Dade

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       02/04/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    The African Museum of Arts and Culture will provide education, cultural, and arts programming for children in the Miami
   Gardens, Miami-Dade, and South Florida communities. It will expose the local community to ancient and contemporary
   African/African-American arts and artifacts including qualitative and quantitative assessments, arts appreciation surveys,
   and questionnaires. This project meets the state's mandate for teaching African/African American history in public school
   curriculum and increasing the knowledge of African American history for school age children.
