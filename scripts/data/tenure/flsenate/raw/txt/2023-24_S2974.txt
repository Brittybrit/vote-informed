1. Project Title         Charter School Revolving Loan Fund

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request       02/14/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Public Schools borrow at low interest rates. Charter schools don't have this option and are forced to get loans with high
   rates. A Pooled Fund can bundle a number of loans and then provide excess collateral to achieve a better rating for Charter
   Schools. The program shall be administered through DOE and a management vendor selected through competitive
   procurement. FL provides a $20 million one-time allocation. The selected vendor matches it 1:1 by philanthropy and then
   5:1 with $140 million in low-cost private debt; This would result in a $200M fund to support 10,000 high-quality seats. All
   standard State contract provisions and remedies would be applicable.
