4. Project/Program Description
    Citrus Health Network’s Adult Crisis Stabilization Unit (CSU) is open 24-hours-a-day, 7-days-a-week to offer brief,
   intensive services for individuals who are acutely mentally ill and experiencing a mental health crisis, with the goal of
   examining, stabilizing, and redirecting them to the most appropriate and least restrictive treatment setting they need. The
   CSU is a crucial provider of emergency mental health services, especially for indigent, uninsured, underinsured, and
   homeless populations.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Citrus Health Network’s Adult Crisis Stabilization Unit (CSU) is open 24-hours-a-day, 7-days-a-week to offer brief,
    intensive services for individuals who are acutely mentally ill and experiencing a mental health crisis. The funding allows
    the Citrus CSU to stay open and operating at all times to continue meeting the ever-increasing mental health services
    demands in the community. CSUs are a far less expensive option for the state than a hospital or jail setting.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Citrus Health Network’s Adult Crisis Stabilization Unit (CSU) is open 24-hours-a-day, 7-days-a-week to offer brief,
    intensive services for individuals who are acutely mentally ill and experiencing a mental health crisis, with the goal of
    examining, stabilizing, and redirecting them to the most appropriate and least restrictive treatment setting they need.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Brief, intensive services for individuals who are acutely mentally ill and experiencing a mental health crisis. Services
    offered include the following,as appropriate and clinically indicated: Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale Screening,
    Psychiatric Evaluation, Nursing Assessment and Nursing Services, Medication Management, Individual, Group, Family
    Therapy, Safety Planning, Care Coordination
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The funds from the project will be used to serve 700 individuals to provide 1,065 bed days for uninsured and
    underinsured individuals in need of crisis stabilization due to a mental health crisis. The target populations to be served
    by this project are persons with poor mental health, economically disadvantaged persons, homeless persons, and
    developmentally disabled persons.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit of this project is to improve mental health. The specific measures to be used are 1.Bed days
    provided to clients who have an unfunded day of care, 2. Readmission Rate based on clients who had multiple
    admissions during the time period, and 3. Clients with stable housing situation. The outcomes will be measured using
    Citrus' Electronic Health Record.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The Citrus Health Network Adult Crisis Stabilization Unit is licensed by both the Florida Department of Children and
    Families as a Public Baker Act Receiving Facility and the Florida Agency for Health Care Administration as a Crisis
    Stabilization Unit. Both licenses require that the Adult Crisis Stabilization Unit remain open 24/7, including to serve
    uninsured and underinsured clients. Citrus would work closely with DCF to ensure continued operations and compliance
    with deliverables.
