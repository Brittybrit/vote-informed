4. Project/Program Description
    As an island community, our residents travel far to access programs and services that cater to special needs. Establishing
   a community program will go a long way towards improving quality of life for special needs residents. Programs and staff
   training more inclusive of special needs residents are in high demand in the village. The Village seeks additional funding to
   better support our special needs residents and their families. This funding will go towards an assessment of village
   services, policies, and programming which will be used to establish a protocol that meets the unique requirements of our
   local special needs population.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Improved delivery of services and programs for people with special needs of all ages (children to seniors). Enhanced and
    expanded staff training in conflict resolution involving persons with special needs. More diverse offerings of programming
    and services that are inclusive for persons with special needs. Increased accommodations in Village facilities and at
    events for persons with special needs.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     An initial assessment of Village services and policies to determine a baseline from which to build upon.
    Recommendations for programming adjustments and additions, improved services, modifications to policies to better
    address the needs of special needs residents, and staff training in how to accommodate people with special needs.
    Implementation of recommendations via additional staff training and increased and modified programming and services.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens of all ages and abilities will receive improved services, programs, and overall engagement with Village staff,
    facilities, and events. More inclusive programming to accommodate persons with special needs will enhance the
    experience of Village offerings for everyone. The classes provided to special needs residents will have an emphasis on
    physical activity, natural movement, nutrition, and overall physical health. The Village will partner with Best Buddies
    Integrated Employment Program to assist with jobs placement for residents with special needs that are interested in
    being a part of the workforce.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All special needs residents regardless of age and ability are the target population. However, all residents regardless of
    special needs will benefit from more inclusive services, facilities, and programming.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Mental health and physical health are critical to a high quality life and these programs will offer social, physical, and
    mental stimulation to maintain a strong mental and physical outlook for special needs residents. Job placement for those
    interested in joining the workforce will lead to economic self-sufficiency and teach valuable interpersonal skills. Staff
    trained to better respond to conflicts with special needs residents will reduce the likelihood these residents will
    unnecessarily encounter the criminal/juvenile justice system. Number of job placements, resident feedback, and incident
    reports for conflicts will provide the data necessary to measure progress on these outcomes.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in the forfeiture of funds or financial penalties as described in the contract.
