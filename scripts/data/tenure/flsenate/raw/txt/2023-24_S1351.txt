4. Project/Program Description
    This request is to fund a pilot of the Math Matrix professional development program to improve the quality of mathematics

   teachers in grades K-12. The Math Matrix will offer engaging resources and evidence-based collaborative professional
   learning opportunities to improve student outcomes and expand future education and career opportunities for all learners.
   The pilot will aim to test the resources with 250 teachers across multiple counties.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To run a statewide pilot of the Math Matrix program to improve the quality of mathematics teaching and learning. The
    Math Matrix is a comprehensive online, on-demand professional development system for teachers in grades K-12. The
    Math Matrix will offer engaging resources and evidence-based collaborative professional learning opportunities to
    improve student outcomes and expand future education and career opportunities for all learners. The pilot is designed for
    250 teachers.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Florida teachers will receive training to improve their knowledge, skills, and abilities in mathematics instruction. Teacher
    recruitment activities. Teacher enrollment. Facilitating teacher's participation in the courses and provision of feedback.
    Teacher feedback will be collected and analyzed to improve the program. Pre and post assessments will be conducted
    and analyzed to demonstrate program effectiveness. A report on the results will be created and disseminated.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Florida teachers will receive training to improve their knowledge, skills, and abilities in mathematics instruction. Florida
    students of participants will benefit from improved math instruction.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Primary target population: teachers, coaches and administrators involved in mathematics instruction in Florida K-12
    public and charter schools. The pilot will enroll 250 participants. A fully implemented version would be provided to
    hundreds more each year. Additional target population: all Florida K-12 public and charter students will benefit from
    improved math instruction.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The FL DOE identified mathematics as a critical teacher shortage are for 2022-2023. By offering teacher training and
    professional development, the Math Matrix will help to address this shortage by equipping and retaining current teachers.
    Benefits will be measured by Teacher retention rates. Improved instruction will yield improved student outcomes.
    Improved instruction will be measured by teacher practicum outcomes, and evaluation of teacher knowledge pre and post
    program participation. Student outcomes will be measured by EOC and/or FAST student data.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The standard penalties should suffice.
