1. Project Title          Pasco Hernando State College - Nursing Education Support

2. Senate Sponsor         Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request        03/14/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    It is estimated that the state of Florida is nearly 25,000 nurses short of being able to appropriately manage the health care
   crisis we are currently experiencing. These funds will be used to perform renovations on PHSC's Nursing and health care
   simulation labs to educate additional nursing and health care students at the Brooksville Campus. These funds will also be
   used to recruit, hire, and supplement nursing and healthcare faculty salaries and benefits.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     These funds will be used to perform renovations on PHSC's Nursing and health care simulation labs to educate
    additional nursing and health care students at the Brooksville Campus. These funds will also be used to recruit, hire, and
    supplement nursing and healthcare faculty salaries and benefits.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Recruitment of qualified nursing faculty for the PHSC Brooksville Campus. Additional upgrades to the nursing simulations
    labs.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Education nursing services for Hernando County citizens and lab upgrades for nursing students.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is the local students in Hernando County. Several hundred nursing students are expected to
    graduate with a degree in nursing to serve the local citizens of Hernando County.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Increase in the number of graduates in the nursing field to serve Hernando County. Increase in the retention of nursing
    faculty at PHSC. The outcomes will be measured by the percentage increase of nursing graduates each year and the
    percentage increase of faculty and administrators that are retained at the College each year.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The restructuring of the College's Nursing administration and or a more rigid evaluation of all student applicants will
    occur if deliverables are not met.
