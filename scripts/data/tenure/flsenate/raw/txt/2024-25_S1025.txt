1. Project Title         CABA Pro Bono Legal Services - Free Legal Clinic

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       10/31/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Free legal assistance and representation to veterans, victims of human trafficking, victims of domestic violence and low
   income individuals and families in the area of landlord tenant, employment, bankruptcy, domestic relations, and throughout
   the state. By providing free legal services and representation we assist individuals and families regain personal and
   economic stability and safety, join the work force and become contributing members of our State. The legal services that
   we provide our clients with help them obtain employment, health insurance, driving privileges, an education, housing, and
   help them to achieve a better future. This appropriations request is separate and distinct from an appropriations request
   made to support a pilot program for legal assistance to low income residents facing foreclosure by their condo and HOA
   community.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Holistic Legal Services and Representation will be provided to Veterans, Victims of Human trafficking, Victims of
    Domestic Violence and Children that have been abused, abandoned and/or neglected By providing free legal services we
    are assisting individuals and families regain personal and economic stability and safety, join the work force and become
    contributing members of our State. The Legal services that we provide our clients with help them obtain employment, an
    education and a better future.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     We will provide free legal services and representation in various areas of law including Family Law, Domestic Violence,
    Juvenile dependency and Landlord Tenant. We will be attending Veterans treatment Court and G.R.A.C.E. Court weekly
    (GRACE Court is specifically for victims of Human Trafficking) to offer legal representation to all veterans and victims of
    human trafficking.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Legal Services provided will range from full direct court representation across multiple areas of law, to legal services with
    filling out documents to help the clients proceed pro se, to legal counsel and advice.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Elderly persons, economically disadvantaged persons, at-risk youth and neurodivergent young adults. We expect to
    serve 1,500 individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     CABA Pro Bono Legal Services will provide free legal services to at least 1500 residents of Florida for the Fiscal Year
    2024-2025. Individuals served will fill out satisfaction surveys to report on the specific benefit that they gained through the
    legal services provided. All legal services provided including Court Representation will be documented in our legal Server
    Case Management system. Additionally, files will be kept documenting all services provided and outcomes achieved.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     If deliverables are not met, money will be deducted from the total amount due as detailed under the contract.
