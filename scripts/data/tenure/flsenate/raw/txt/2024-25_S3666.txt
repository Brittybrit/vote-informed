4. Project/Program Description
    We are requesting $500,000 for a production contract that would allow for the implementation of an audio book program to
   support healthcare degrees within Florida's Community College system. Learning Ally’s Learning Through Listening grant
   has funded audio books for k-12 students as a supplemental educational tool with great success. Recognizing the pivotal
   role of education in the healthcare sector, we believe that integrating audio books into the learning process will enhance
   accessibility, flexibility, and overall educational outcomes for healthcare students. The minimum number of textbooks to be
   produced in either English or Spanish is 25 for a total cost of $497,400 (per unit textbook cost of $19,896).

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Through the funding Learning Ally will publish selected titles selected by the Community College system's healthcare
    programs as audio books. These audio books will be available for all qualifying students who have an Individualized
    Education Program (IEP). By providing access to audio books, we will create a more inclusive and accommodating
    learning environment for healthcare students with language-based learning disabilities.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Content Development: Collaborate with subject matter experts and educators to create high-quality audiobook content
    tailored to healthcare degree programs.

    Platform Integration: Partner with educational technology providers to seamlessly integrate audiobook platforms into
    existing learning management systems, ensuring ease of access for students.

    Training and Support: Implement training programs for faculty and students on effectively utilizing audiobooks as part of
    their learning experience. Provide ongoing support to address any technical or educational challenges.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     All healthcare students enrolled in the Florida Community College system will have access to their textbooks through the
    audio book solution platform. As detailed earlier in this narrative, professors, faculty, students and all who have access to
    the audio book platform will receive training on how to use the platform. Technical support will also be provided.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The target population for this project will be all students enrolled in healthcare degree programs within Florida's
    Community College system who require accommodations through a documented IEP.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Through over 70 years of service and research in the field of language-based disorders, our findings show that
    multimodal learning has proven to be an effective approach. Introducing audio books complements traditional learning
    materials, offering a multimodal approach that enhances comprehension and retention. By engaging students through
    both visual and auditory channels, we can cater to diverse learning preferences and improve overall knowledge retention
    for students who need accommodations.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

    Learning Ally will comply with all applicable state penalties levied for failing to meet deliverables or performance
    measures provided for in the contract.
