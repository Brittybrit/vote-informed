14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Florida Premier FC is a non-profit organization that supports more than 300 youth soccer teams, 3,500 youth athletes,
    5,000 families, and 240 staff members, and is growing at a rate of 30% per year. State funds will leverage private support
    to acquire and develop land in Pasco County to expand its Positive Coaching Alliance model which strives to change the
    culture of youth sports so every child, regardless of social, physical or economic circumstance, has access to a positive
    sports experience.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Expanded field options will fuel Florida Premier's local economic impact and ability to provide youth with a low-cost,
    positive youth sports experience. Florida Premier will continue to assume a large percentage of the routine and deferred
    maintenance obligations on public properties, reducing the necessary local investment.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Free community events and soccer clinics; direct economic impact to local businesses from tournaments; long-term
    planning and maintenance solutions for public fields; expansion of the Positive Coaching Alliance model so every child,
    regardless of social or economic circumstance, has access to a positive youth sports experience; expansion of
    TopSoccer for kids with diverse abilities.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Youth Athletes, Economically Disadvantaged Youth, Physically and Developmentally Disabled, and Students. More than
    800 unique individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improved physical and mental health -- Numerous studies conclude that active participation in youth sports improves the
    athlete's and family's physical and mental health. TOPSoccer provides people with diverse abilities an opportunity to play
    soccer in a structured environment that is safe, fun, supportive, and inclusive; Increased economic activity and tourism --
    Florida Premier FC will expand its tournament and team offerings, increasing the local economic impact; Creation of new
    jobs through the development of land; Reduction of recidivism and substance abuse -- Numerous studies conclude that
    active participation in youth sports reduces recidivism and substance abuse among youth participants.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Dispersed funds for deliverables not met will be returned to the state.
