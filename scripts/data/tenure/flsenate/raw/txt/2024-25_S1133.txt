4. Project/Program Description
    DMF Employment Opportunities’ target population is young adults, ages 18 to 35, with intellectual and developmental
   disabilities (IDD) who wish to gain full-time employment and become self-reliant in Miami-Dade County. The Employment
   Project currently has 90 Employer Partners and 119 participants employed in a variety of fields.

   The model implemented by DMF Employment Opportunities (DMF) follows a 5-step process that aligns with the evidence-
   based practice of Customized Employment with a customer focus on the employer and the addition of extended services
   for the employee. This approach has proven to be successful in generating stable employment while encouraging the hiring
   of individuals with intellectual and development disabilities (IDD) through long-term partnerships beyond the traditional 90
   to 150-day period.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will specifically help sustain extended services integral to maintaining long-term employment outcomes for
    individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD). An investment in extended services yields positive
    employment outcomes for young adults with IDD, which leads to economic growth and less dependence on government
    assistance.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Offer extended services to at least 160 individuals seeking employment, currently employed, or recently employed to
    obtain or maintain employment and ensure the employees thrive or grow in their current positions. This includes:
    (1) Weekly: Engage participants technologically to build work and independent living skills while preventing isolation and
    promoting mental wellness
    (2) Weekly and monthly: Peer group meetings, counseling sessions, and social skills training facilitated by a mental
    health/behavioral practitioner
    (3) Daily, weekly or biweekly job coaching based on individual needs.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Eligible citizens will receive intensive supported employment services in accordance with the Customized Employment
    model adopted by DMF Employment Opportunities. The appropriation project will specifically fund intensive job coaching
    to ensure transition into stable employment and extended services to maintain employment.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All participants served are adults with intellectual and/or developmental disabilities who seek part/full-time employment
    and self-reliance in South Florida.
    DMF expects to place participants in 30 new job opportunities during the project period.

    The 119 participants currently employed will continue to access services to maintain employment and the 47 participants
    seeking employment will receive job and life skills trainings until a job opportunity is identified that matches the
    participants skills and interests.

    It is expected that 70% of participants are matched with an opportunity within a year of referral to the program.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Activities focused on continuous support for both the employer and the employee with a disability beyond the traditional
    90 to 150-day period will be provided to ensure long-term employment outcomes for program participants.
    -Secure 15 new Employer Partners
    -Place 35 new participants in employment.
    -80% of participants will remain employed with the same Employer Partner for a minimum of 1 year 70% for over 2 years
    - Increase work hours or hourly rate for 15 new or existing participants.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Future funding will be lost.
