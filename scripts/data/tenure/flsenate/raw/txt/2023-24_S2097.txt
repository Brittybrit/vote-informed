4. Project/Program Description
    The VA provides nursing homes for veterans who are medically unable to live independently. However, a large number of
   veterans need but cannot afford some assistance with activities of daily living. They do not need in-house nursing home
   assistance. Veterans Village, a veteran focused assisted living facility (ALF), hopes to become the template for providing
   assistance to this needy group of veterans and spouses in an environment that enhances their enjoyment of the final years
   of their lives.

   In addition to typical ALF services, A Director of Veterans Services will assist veterans in obtaining available federal
   financial support, assist with moving and personal affairs and will assist them in obtaining proper medical care. Veterans
   Village plans to allow those who become unable to afford monthly costs to remain in the facility utilizing available net
   operating income. Veterans Village will not require annual federal or state support once construction is complete.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The requested funds will be added to local and private resources to build a 104-bed assisted living facility in Citrus
    County dedicated to low income veterans and their spouses who cannot afford to pay the market rate for a private
    assisted living facility. The funds will reduce annual debt service resulting in net operating income sufficient to support
    both operations and the charitable component of this assisted living facility.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The 104-bed assisted living facility will operate as a low-cost resource for qualified veterans and spouses who need
    assistance with activities of daily living but who do not need full nursing home care typically offered by the VA.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Qualified veterans and their spouses who reside in the 104-bed ALF will recieve assistance with everyday activities and
    individualized health care services based on the individuals needs. The ALF will provide residents a unique mix of
    companionship, independence, privacy, and security in a home like setting. Qualified individuals include veterans and
    their spouses who need ALF services but cannot afford to pay the market rate for a private facility.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population served is the large number of qualified veterans and spouses currently in need of assisted-living
    facilities but unable to afford them.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit of this project will accrue to deserving qualified veterans and spouses who served our country and
    who now find themselves without the economic wherewithal to afford needed assisted living. An additional outcome will
    be the demonstration of a private, not-for-profit organization that is able, with minimal governmental support, to care for
    needy veterans and spouses who find themselves in the gap between VA outpatient medical care and VA nursing home
    support. This organization could become a template for economically managing and assisting these beneficiaries. Annual
    occupancy rates and financial reports will enable measurement of the intended outcomes.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?
