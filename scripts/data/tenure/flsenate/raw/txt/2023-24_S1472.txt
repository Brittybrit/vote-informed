14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     According to the Florida after 3PM (2020) report, the demand for afterschool programs is strong, with over 1.5 million
    Florida children who would have participated in an afterschool program had it been available to them. Additionally, the
    hours between 3 and 6 p.m. are peak hours for juvenile crime and experimentation with drugs, alcohol, cigarettes, and
    sex, highlighting the need for a safe space for children to go after school. The goal of this program is to provide Florida
    youth with a safe place after school; caring adults; and warm meal while providing them with academic support;
    extracurricular opportunities; and positive adult role models, all in an effort to break the cycle of poverty for future
    generations.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The services to be provided include academic enrichment opportunities during afterschool and summer programming,
    including, but not limited to, tutoring and mentoring; homework help; academic enrichment; college and career readiness;
    service learning; physical fitness; health and wellness; as well as music, arts, sports and cultural activities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Florida’s Boys & Girls Clubs work with school-age children and youth during afterschool and summer programs with
    activities that enhance and reiterate school day learning. These programs encompass a broad range of focus areas
    including academic support; mentoring; personal enrichment; the arts; sports and recreation; and apprenticeships and
    workforce development programs.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     More than 1,000 school-age (kindergarten through twelth-grade) children will receive high-quality afterschool programs.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Boys & Girls Clubs provide structured, supervised programming outside of school hours for school-aged children and
    teens, providing academic support; mentoring; youth development; exposure to the arts; and sports and recreation.
    These programs can benefit each participant, but there are additional benefits of afterschool programs for working
    families as they provide a safe place for children after school while thier parents work. The methodology by which this
    outcome will be measured is through program evaluation and attendance.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     A complete Corrective Action Plan set by the Department of Education and a reduction of funds.
