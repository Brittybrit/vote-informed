4. Project/Program Description
    Miami-Dade County’s Department of Transportation and Public Works (DTPW) Vision Zero goal is to end traffic fatalities
   and serious injuries by 2040, while increasing safe, reliable, sustainable, and equitable mobility for all. SW 2nd St. was
   identified in the 2021 Vision Zero Framework Plan as a high-injury crash location. This project seeks the construction of
   roadway safety improvements including high-visibility crosswalks, installation of bike facilities, upgrade of curb ramps and
   transit stop facilities, and upgrade of existing traffic signal countdown timers and pedestrian push buttons. These safety
   countermeasures are aimed to eliminate the number of fatalities and serious injuries taking place on this corridor.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to engineer, design, and construct identified safety countermeasures. These safety countermeasures are
    aimed to eliminate the number of fatalities and serious injuries taking place on this corridor. This project seeks the
    construction of safety improvements including high-visibility crosswalks, installation of bike facilities, upgrade of curb
    ramps and transit stop facilities, and upgrade of existing traffic signal countdown timers and pedestrian push buttons.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     All of the state appropriated funds would go towards the construction of the safety improvements. These improvements
    include high-visibility crosswalks, installation of bike facilities, upgrade of curb ramps and transit stop facilities, and
    upgrade of existing traffic signal countdown timers and pedestrian push buttons.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The construction of this infrastructure project will provide the area with a safer environment for bicyclists, pedestrians,
    drivers, and local residents with high visibility crossings, walking areas, and safe access to transit. This project will
    improve mental and physical health and promote a sense of community, as well as create a safe network for all modes of
    transportation.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Residents and businesses along SW 2nd St. are the target population. These safety countermeasures are aimed to
    eliminate the number of fatalities and serious injuries taking place on this corridor near schools, businesses, and
    residential neighborhoods. SW 2nd St. has been identified as part of the Top 100 Vision Zero locations across Miami-
    Dade County.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The direct benefits of this project include eliminating high-injury crashes in main residential and commercial roadways, an
    improvement in safety for all modes, and an enhanced quality of life for Miami residents by providing safer streets.
    Improvements in physical and mental health by creating a safe transportation system encourages residents to take
    transit, walk, or bike to their schools, work, local shops, and restaurants. Creating a more active lifestyle reduces the risk
    of health complications such as diabetes, cardiovascular disease, high blood pressure, and contributes to improved
    mental health. Improvement in economic activity by encouraging residents to reach their local shops and restaurants as
    they will provide more comfortable sidewalk and roadway facilities. The methods used for measuring the outcome consist
    of analyzing before and after traffic/crash data, tracking construction jobs, and tracking transit, pedestrian, and bike
    usage/counts.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
