    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Replacement of two Neonatal/Pediatric critical care ambulances. Nicklaus Children’s Hospital is South Florida’s only
    licensed specialty hospital exclusively for children and includes a fleet of ambulances that are specially equipped for
    children. This specialty patient transport service allows the hospital to transport children to intensive care units,
    emergency departments, and medical surgical units, in addition to other facilities in the community.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Purchase of two Neonatal/Pediatric critical care ambulances which includes all patient compartment needs,
    communication and radios required, branding, etc. (Chassis, construction/conversion of patient care compartment and
    additional specialty options including but not limited to blanket warmer for babies/kids staying warm, wider door on side to
    accommodate two stretcher system & pediatric stretcher.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This funding would help ensure that Southern Florida families and children have access to operational vehicles equipped
    to treat the special needs presented by some babies and children. Specifically, the requested new ambulances will be
    equipped for neonatal and pediatric critical care transport. These ambulances provide an isolette to ensure newborn or
    recently born infants stay warm & secure during transport, a stretcher for moving all other sized pediatric patients. Inside,
    we ensure that there is plenty of room to bring a parent or caregiver during an extremely scary time. There are other
    unique features. Most important is a child receiving transport to facility to receive best expert care.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Our transport team serves our most critical and fragile patients being transferred from referring hospitals to Nicklaus
    Children's Hospital for a higher level of care and excellence in patient care. Our ambulances are reaching the end of their
    lifecycle and need to be replaced. Our team provided specialty ground transport for 4,700 patients in 2021. Our
    immediate service area covers from St. Lucie County to the North, Lee County to the West, and Monroe County to the
    South with all areas in between these borders. As far as patient populations, we care for all children from very small
    premature babies to newborns that need whole body cooling, to trauma transfers between hospitals, to kids with heart
    defects.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     We care for all children from very small premature babies to newborns that need whole body cooling, to trauma transfers
    between hospitals, to kids with heart defects. In 2021, our team provided specialty transport to 4700 kids, ensuring
    Floridians have access to high quality care provided by Nicklaus. Nicklaus Children’s Hospital is South Florida’s only
    licensed specialty hospital exclusively for children, is renowned for excellence in all aspects of pediatric medicine with
    many specialty programs routinely ranked among the best in the nation by U.S. News & World Report. Our transport team
    serves our most critical and fragile patients being transferred from referring hospitals.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to utilize the funds in the specified manner shall result in funds being returned to the State.
