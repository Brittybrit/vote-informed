1. Project Title         Miami-Dade County Tropical Heights Sewer Force Main Extension Project

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request       12/05/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The project will expand the sanitary sewer system to 3 multifamily condominiums with a total of 374 units. The properties
   are in the Tropical Heights neighborhood located between the Coral Gables and Snapper Creek Canals, tributaries to
   Biscayne Bay, and within the County’s Wellfield Protection Area. The buildings are served by 2 private package plants with
   a history of poor performance. Funding will provide for the extension of the force main for this project.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The project will expand the sanitary sewer system to 3 multifamily condominiums with a total of 374 units. The properties
    are in the Tropical Heights neighborhood located between the Coral Gables and Snapper Creek Canals, tributaries to
    Biscayne Bay, and within the County’s Wellfield Protection Area. The buildings are served by 2 private package plants
    with a history of poor performance. Funding will provide for the extension of the force main for this project.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Activities include the design and construction of a sewer force main through the services of an engineering design firm
    and construction contractor.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This project provides sanitary sewer infrastructure for connection by three condominium properties.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This project will provide sanitary sewer infrastructure to three condominium properties in the Tropical Heights
    neighborhood located between the Coral Gables and Snapper Creek Canals. Over 374 individuals are expected to be
    served, based on the number of condominium units in the neighborhood.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project removes non-compliant sewage treatment plants from within the wellfield protection area, which are a source
    of nutrients and pathogens. The outcome can be measured by the number of privately owned sewage treatment plants
    converted to sanitary sewer service. and the number of sanitary sewer overflows within the basin.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
