4. Project/Program Description
    Since the start of the COVID-19 pandemic, the Miami-Dade County Homeless Trust has been utilizing a property in North
   Miami to divert seniors 65+ from homeless shelters and the streets to a single location. The quarantine and isolation site
   provides specialized care to ensure the safety of this group. The Trust is now seeking to acquire and improve this facility,
   known as Mia Casa, as well as other facilities, to provide permanent housing for seniors and others experiencing
   homelessness, many of whom have complex needs.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The acquisition of Mia Casa and other facilities for persons experiencing homelessness will allow the Homeless Trust to
    further reduce sheltered and unsheltered homelessness, including the fastest growing homeless subpopulation in our
    community, senior citizens.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funding will be used to purchase and renovate Mia Casa and other facilities to house and serve persons experiencing
    homelessness. Mia Casa is currently serving as a COVID-19 quarantine and isolation site for seniors 65 years old or
    older who are experiencing homelessness. A full range of services is provided, including case management, medication
    management, health and behavioral health care, meals and accommodations.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     A full range of specialized services is currently provided, including case management, medication management, health
    and behavioral health care, meals and accommodations.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is seniors citizens and others experiencing homelessness. Mia Casa includes 65 residential units
    with 118 beds. Over than past 20 months, more than 300 seniors have been served at Mia Casa. In that same time
    period, more than 6,500 persons have been served in non-congregate shelters by Miami-Dade's Continuum of Care.
    These individual accommodations have proven to be invaluable to getting persons experiencing homelessness off the
    streets.
