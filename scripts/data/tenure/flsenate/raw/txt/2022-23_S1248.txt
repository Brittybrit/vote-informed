12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Casa Valentina addresses the abrupt transition from foster care to independent living, by filling in the gaps in external
    support. We provide safe and affordable housing, and intensive wrap-around services. We are looking to extend the life-
    skills based programming we currently offer. Staff will provide support services to youth ages 13 to 23 on the cusp of
    transitioning out of foster care and/or who are otherwise at risk.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Casa Valentina campus is where our youth reside in a live-and-learn community where life skills programming is
    delivered. These funds will go toward the following renovations: plumbing and new windows.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Renovations/updates to already existing affordable housing units.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     We are serving: Economically disadvantaged youth; at-risk youth; homeless; high school students; university/college
    students.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     We know that youth must have their basic needs for safety and security met before they're able to fully realize their
    potential with higher order tasks. We seek to increase the motivation and morale of former foster care and homeless
    youth. We anticipate that youth will improve the quality of education; enhance their specific economic self-sufficiency; and
    improve in other key areas of general life skills in order to support them as they grow.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?
