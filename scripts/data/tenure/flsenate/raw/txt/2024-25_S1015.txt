14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will assist in the re-construction of a public roadway which has deteriorated due to ocean water inundation.
    Construction will include resiliency and mitigation design which will increase the elevation of the roadway as well as tidal
    valves to the stormwater system to prevent future intrusion. The re-constructed roadway will serve pedestrians, cyclists,
    and the motoring public in a heavily used downtown business area.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Construction activities will include raising the elevation of public streets, Americans with Disabilities Act compliant
    sidewalks, addition of bicycle lanes to improve cyclist safety, installation of stormwater tidal valve to prevent seawater
    inundation, construction of roadway base which is more resilient to tidal groundwater influence, and harmonization which
    improves access to businesses.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The infrastructure will provide for safe and stable pedestrian and vehicular right-of-way which will be free from ocean
    inundation and flooding. The sidewalks and roadway will be constructed to increase multi-modal safety and decrease the
    need for future repairs, which impact the traveling public. Other design elements will also improve light and green-space,
    increasing safety and air quality.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The general population (The majority of funds will benefit no specific group).
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The project will help improve water quality for fish and wildlife by incorporating drainage systems which will reduce
    stormwater runoff impacts and increase the water quality at the point of discharge, improve transportation conditions by
    adding multi-modal facilities while also raising the road to address sea-level-rise impacts, and increase and improve
    tourism by enhancing vehicular and pedestrian traffic flows on a street serving multiple restaurants, cafes, bars, and other
    tourist destinations.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Funding to be withheld, if project deliverable are not met.
