1. Project Title          County Line Road Widening - Hernando County

2. Senate Sponsor        Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request       12/18/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    County Line Rd. (C.R. 578) borders Hernando and Pasco Counties. C.R. 578 connects from U.S. Highway 19 to the
   Suncoast Pkwy (SR 589) and to U.S. Highway 41. Two segments of road are 2 lanes. Increasing capacity to 4 lanes on
   C.R. 578 will reduce congestion, improve commuter & resident travel time, & reduce evacuation time during emergencies.
   Design, right of way acquisition, & construction of 4 lane roadway on C.R. 578 from Mariner Blvd. (Shady Hills Road) to the
   existing 4 lane at Suncoast Pkwy.

     Road widening construction improvements for commuters and residents.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Improvements to this roadway would improve operational safety to the public for everyday commuting. These
    improvements will reduce evacuation time during public emergencies. Citizens in Hernando and Pasco County will have
    an improved roadway providing enhanced mobility between US 19 and the Suncoast Parkway including pedestrian and
    bike lane facilities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Improvements to this roadway would improve operational safety to the public for everyday commuting. These
    improvements will reduce evacuation time during public emergencies. Citizens in Hernando and Pasco County will have
    an improved roadway providing enhanced mobility between US 19 and the Suncoast Parkway including pedestrian and
    bike lane facilities.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is everyone who commutes through or lives in Hernando County which is hundreds of thousands
    of people per year.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Safer roadway network. Improved level of service on the roadway. Level of service is an indication of road quality. A
    traffic engineering analysis conducted by FDOT in 2000 indicated the roadway was operating at level D. Without
    improvement the level of service is projected to be F (lowest rating on scale) in the project area by 2025. This project will
    allow for the recommended level of service, which is C. (Florida Department of Transportation, Final Traffic
    Memorandum, March 2002, p. 4-1,5-1; FDOT Topic No. 000-525-006-c).
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The county will reimburse state funding for failure to meet the deliverables.
