     To provide comprehensive afterschool programs that focus on promoting fitness, health and discipline amongst students
    from K-12 ages. The foundation serves over 200 underprivileged families in a largely minority & multi-cultural area within
    Miami-Dade and Orange County (to initiate in 2024). Additional to after school programs, the foundation also hosts an
    annual Thanksgiving day bash, Law Enforcement & First Responder recognition week, and field trips to theme parks for
    terminally ill children.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     A comprehensive after school care program that will provides various educational, cultural and physical activities for
    under served minority children ranging from grades K through 12. Thanksgiving day bash that serves the students
    families and celebrates a diverse cultural community. The Foundation sponsors an annual field trip to Florida theme parks
    for students with terminal health conditions.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Daily After school tutoring for children from K-12; Taekwondo 3 days a week ; Archery 2 days a week, High School SAT
    Prep; Dance classes for all ages 3 days a week. Self defense classes 3 days a week. Piano classes 2 days a week.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The foundation serves over 200 underprivileged families in a largely minority & multi-cultural area within Miami-Dade and
    Orange County (to initiate in 2024).
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Our programs helps improve quality of education, physical and mental health, and provides cultural enrichment
    opportunities while helping reduce substance abuse. Physical Exercise improves mental health and drastically improves
    health conditions like depression and anxiety. Music, dance and sports reduces stress. Traveling with children from
    minority and underserved areas to new cities for competitions, field trips and other cultural events, we are exposing them
    to new experiences and expanding the cultural knowledge. Our after school-tutoring program allows students to receive a
    one on one learning experience; they focus on specific subjects that they may be struggling with. Our SAT/College prep
    tutoring helps students maximize their potential and prepare them for college. Children’s report cards will be a measuring
    tool. When you offer children an opportunity to participate in educational and sports activities, you reduce the chances of
    a child exposure to illegal substances
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     We will maintain records on benchmarks of all student progress and accomplishments, as well as individual goals for
    teaching staff and support personnel. Failure to accomplish goals will result in internal corrective action plan and return
    funds to the Department of Education.
