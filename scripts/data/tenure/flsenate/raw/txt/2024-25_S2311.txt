4. Project/Program Description
    By securing funding for the intersection improvements for the Culbreath Road / Ayers Road /Hayman Road intersection, it
   improves the safety of the intersection and reduces congestion to provide better movement of vehicles. With the
   construction of a roundabout, it will enhance the resiliency of the infrastructure in Hernando County as the traffic controls
   will continue to function during emergencies with or without power.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Improvements to this intersection would not only address concerns during a disaster, but also by continuing to operate
    and provide traffic control with or without power, they would also improve operation and safety to the public for everyday
    commuting.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Construction activities to build the Hernando County designed intersection improvements. The project will be
    competitively bid to increase economic stimulus in the area.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Roundabouts improve safety, having fewer crashes, 90% fewer fatalities and 75% fewer injuries, reduce congestion, 30
    to 50% increase in traffic capacity at the intersection with less delay waiting at stops and signals, and cost savings as
    there are no costs for traffic signal and yearly maintenance. The intersection still operates in power outages eliminating
    the need for police to direct traffic.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The general population of Hernando County will be served by this project. In a 3-year period (January 1, 2016 to
    December 31, 2018) crash data review from the University of Florida's Signal 4 Analytics, a total of 22 crashes were
    reported. During the same 3-year period there were no fatalities at this intersection, however since December 31, 2018
    there has been one fatality at this intersection. Improved intersection safety and enhancement will benefit all travelers on
    this section of roadway in Hernando County.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Planned improvements to this intersection would address concerns at this intersection during a disaster by continuing to
    operate and provide traffic control with or without power. It would also improve the operations and safety to the public for
    everyday commuting. The outcome will be monitored using future crash reports for the years after opening of the
    improved intersection. The future crash reports will be compared to the report prior to the intersection improvements.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The county will reimburse state funding for failure to meet the deliverables.
