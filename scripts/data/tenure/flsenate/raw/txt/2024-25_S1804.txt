1. Project Title          Miami-Dade County - The South Dade Trail Multi-Use/Mobility Corridor

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request       12/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
   The South-Dade Trail Multi-Use/Mobility Corridor consists of improvements to the 20-mile corridor existing along the South
   Corridor. This project is part of the Miami-Dade County Parks and Open Spaces System Master Plan. The South Dade
   Trail will provide approximately 20-miles of off-road bicycle and pedestrian paths, and intersection improvement along the
   South Corridor. Increased safety, connectivity and decongestion of traffic is expected as a result. Improvements to the
   South Dade Trail are necessary to facilitate this corridor as part of the core alignment of the BRT first/last mile network.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goals are to encourage residents, students, and visitors to walk, bike and take public transit to alleviate traffic
    congestion, and improve accessibility and safety. This funding will be used to perform the required environmental
    assessments associated with contamination and future closure controls related to the project.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The $750,000 requested will be used to perform the required environmental assessments associated with contamination
    and future closure controls related to the project. The South Dade Trail will provide approximately 20-miles of off-road
    bicycle and pedestrian paths and intersection improvements along the South Corridor. Increased safety, connectivity and
    decongestion of traffic is expected as a result. Improvements to the South Dade Trail are necessary to facilitate this
    corridor as part of the core alignment of the BRT first/last mile network.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Accessibility and connectivity to public transportation with safe, off-road pedestrian and bicycle paths that includes
    lighting and signage within a 20-mile multi-modal corridor.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The funds requested are for direct services to citizens, residents and visitors including but not limited to the elderly, pre-
    school, grade school, high-school and university/college students. The numbers of individuals to be served is greater than
    250,000.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improved physical health (increased physical activity), improve mental Health (satisfaction with quality of life), enrich
    cultural experience (events and curriculum drawing residents to The South Dade Trail), education, enhance the
    environment (lower CO2 levels), protect the general public from harm (improved pedestrian and bicycle safety), improve
    transportation conditions, increase economic activity (increased property values projected at 5% to 25%), increase
    tourism, and create job opportunities. Outcomes will be measured by evaluation of attendance, Health Impact
    Assessment, measurement of property value change, reduction in pedestrian and bicyclist conflicts and increase in transit
    ridership.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
