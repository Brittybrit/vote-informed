14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To maintain the trust of our constituents and ensure organizational cyber-resilience. The project will enable Miami-Dade
    County to enhance its current cybersecurity capabilities aligned with the NIST 800-53 framework. Providing a secure and
    remote access for county employees and systems by maintaining and enhancing the county's vulnerability management
    capabilities. As well as, enhance the capacity to meet new constituents services and address novel cyber threats.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     To meet demands of new county digital services by ensuring that IT systems are identified and adequately protected.
    Enable secure, remote access for employees in a hybrid work environment and to ensure proper segmentation and
    firewall capabilities to protect county assets.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Enhance cybersecurity of new and existing online services being provided to citizens conducting business with Miami-
    Dade County.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Miami-Dade County residents. Over 2.8 million residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of county systems and data by enhancing cyber-resiliance of systems
    relied upon by constituents of Miami-Dade County. System unavailability from cyber incidents.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
