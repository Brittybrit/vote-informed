4. Project/Program Description
    The Agency for Health Care Administration (AHCA) has invested in state, local, and private health information exchanges
   (HIE) since 2006, supporting the secure exchange of clinical patient data across the patient care continuum. Historically,
   state efforts have been primarily focused on hospitals and long term care facilitates. This funding enhances and secures
   the investment made by the state to expand the adoption of HIE services for independent Medicaid providers and rural
   hospitals by using a private, community, or state exchange which provides connectivity to the medical service area up to
   the national eHealth Exchange (eHX) through a HIE vendor participant to eHX. Research supports that creating a secure
   HIE for the Medicaid population helps to improve patient outcomes, while reducing the total cost of care of the Medicaid
   population.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The proposed project will connect independent Medicaid providers and rural hospitals, creating connectivity to a medical
    service area HIE which may include: digitizing faxing communication to be HIPPA compliant, an interface to the providers
    electronic health records (EHR) which provides connectivity with the local medical service area, health information
    exchange, mandated state reporting requirements, and up to the national eHX via an eHX vendor participant which
    enables their participation in a query based infrastructure connecting with private, local, Florida HIE platforms as well as
    public institutions such as the Veterans Administration or the Social Security Administration. Funding will be provided to
    operationalize Florida HIEs to connect additional eligible Medicaid practitioners, rural hospitals, and clinics.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     3 years software licensing fees for HIE subscription service. HIE implementation to include: HIE Participant Creation &
    Project Charter; Clinical Workflow Analysis and Electronic Work Queue(s) Setup User Credentialing and Setup; Desktop
    Application Installation & Setup, Fax Number Configuration & Fax Hardware Replacement (Optional), Automated Data
    Interface Setup E HR HL7 Interface for physician practice and rural hospitals (optional); the last mile connectivity to the
    FHIE; connectivity to the national eHealth Exchange; Community Patient Lookup & Notification Setup (Optional);
    Administrative Management & Reporting Setup
    On-site System Training, Testing and Validation
    On-going Support & Account Maintenance

     There are approximately 83,000 physicians licensed in the state of Florida with at least 63% accepting new Medicaid
    patients. This funding is aimed to support at least 52,500 Medicaid providers, rural hospitals, and safety net providers.
    Proper connectivity to the health information exchange also assists in a disaster recovery in the event a physician
    practice's EHRs are inaccessible due to hurricanes and flooding.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Florida's Medicaid programs serves, as of April 2021, 4,249,167 individuals. A large percentage of the Medicaid
    population resides in Region 8 and 11. Although this is a local request, funds may be used throughout the sate.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     HIEs provide a variety of benefits that include:
    -Provides a basic level of interoperability among diverse EHRs maintained by individual physicians, hospitals, and other
    healthcare facilities.
    -Reduces health related costs.
    -Providing a vehicle for improving quality and safety of patient care by reducing medication and medical errors.
    -Eliminates redundant or unnecessary testing.
    -Improves public health reporting and monitoring.
    -Increases efficiency by eliminating unnecessary paperwork.
    -Provides caregivers with clinical decision support tools for more effective care and treatment.
    HIE benefits are heightened by the number of users. The methodology by which this outcome will be measured:
    1. Number of end users established on the HIE.
    2. Implementation of HIE subscription based services.
    3. Number of transactions on the local medical service area HIE.
    4. Replacement of antiquated faxing communication between providers.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Standard penalties in the contract's execution, e.g. not paying the invoice for services not provided within this document.
