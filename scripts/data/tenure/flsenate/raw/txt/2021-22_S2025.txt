4. Project/Program Description
    LearJet's new, state-of-the-art aerospace service center will employ 250-300 full time jobs at an average salary of $75,000
   with additional benefits averaging $30,000 annually. LearJet has entered a 30-year lease and will return the facility, with

   help to defray lease expenses associated with the new facility, which are in addition to the cost of building the service
   center, accelerating the development of the facility and supporting job growth and business travel within the region.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Efficient and reliable connectivity to global markets, between Florida's diverse regions and within regions, is essential in
    promoting the state's economic competitiveness. LearJet is building a new service center at the Miami-Opa Locka
    Executive Airport (OPF) in Miami-Dade County, which is owned by Miami-Dade County. The new service center will more
    than double the company's current customer service footprint in Florida with new capabilities, including a paint facility for
    the center's vast customer base spanning the U.S. and Latin America.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Florida's statewide economic development strategy focuses on clusters of innovation-oriented industries such as life
    sciences, aerospace and information technology.
    The new service center in Miami will be fully equipped to perform scheduled and unscheduled maintenance, aircraft
    modifications, avionics installations, and aircraft on ground support (AOG) for Bombardier/Learjet, Challenger, and Global
    aircraft. Once the facility is fully operational, the new service center will nearly double LearJet's employment in Florida to
    close to 300 employees.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The quality of transportation and economic opportunity are highly interdependent. Florida's economic competitiveness
    depends heavily on the state's ability to attract businesses, skilled workers and visitors. The facility will be located at the
    Miami-Opa Locka Executive Airport in Miami-Dade County, an important business aircraft hub in the region. The new
    service center will feature an expert team of highly-trained customer experience specialists and technicians; the center
    will employ 250-300 full time employees at an average salary of $75,000 per year with additional benefits averaging
    $30,000 annually.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Located in Miami-Dade County, the Miami-Opa Locka Executive Airport (OPF) is the largest of the county's general

    County, including the development of a comprehensive aerospace cluster and the establishment of talent development
    partnerships with local colleges.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     FDOT's study, Macroeconomic Analysis of Florida's Transportation Investments (July 2020), estimates that every dollar
    invested in transportation in Florida results in a return of $4.00 in long-term benefits to residents and businesses.
    Increased economic activity derived from this project will be measured by 300 new jobs created at the facility and
    increased travel to the region, boosting tourism, business, and service industry sectors.
