12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Renovate outdated labs and classrooms
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Updated labs and classrooms will help meet the needs of Florida's nurse shortage.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Increased programs, educational opportunities for nursing students that lead to degrees in high-demand nursing
    positions to address state nursing shortage

     150+ nursing graduate and graduate students.
