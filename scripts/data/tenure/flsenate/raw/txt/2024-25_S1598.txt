14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     In partnership with the Miami Foundation and its existing Community Grants solicitation, extend and enhance existing
    programming and/or expand the number of CBOs, faith-based, and grassroots groups facilitating mental health and
    wellness services to youth, adults, families and neighborhoods with a focus on healing in communities disproportionately
    impacted by gun-violence. Additionally, provide mental health assessments and reintegration planning in jails.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Various prevention, intervention and reentry services to support mental health of individuals in the county jails and living
    in communities disproportionately impacted by gun violence.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The funding will support individual, family and group mental health and wellness services, counseling and treatment.
    Anger management, grief, mindfulness, identifying and managing stress and anxiety techniques are some of the focus
    areas that may be covered. Other direct services and linkages to wraparound services to support reintegration and
    household stabilization will also be provided.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population includes persons with poor mental health, at-risk youth, currently or formerly incarcerated persons,
    drug offenders (in criminal justice), victims of crime, and individuals residing in communities disproportionately impacted
    by gun violence. The target population expected to be served is between 201 and 400 individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Benefits include improved access to community-based mental health and wellness services, support for a bi-directional
    referral system, increased access to action-driven community integration plan, and increased number of inmates
    connected to services post-release. Outcomes will be measured by the number of individuals receiving services;
    wraparound services linkages; interventions provided, pre and post surveys to gauge inmates readiness for community
    reintegration, and post release follow up (after 30 days).
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     A failure on the part of the receiving entity in meeting or providing deliverables or with overall non-performance will result
    in non-payment or a full return of the requested monies.
