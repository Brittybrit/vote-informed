1. Project Title          Coral Gables Citywide Sidewalk Construction and Replacement

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request       12/05/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The City of Coral Gables has a number of locations within the City limits where sidewalks need to be replaced due to
   damage caused by trees in the right-of-way and where sidewalk gaps have been identified. Additional funds provided would
   supplement the City's funding for sidewalk replacement and construction of new sidewalk projects citywide. Most of these
   areas have been identified by residents as hazardous. While the City budgets annually to address sidewalks within its
   jurisdiction, the costs of construction, the City's aging pedestrian infrastructure, and increased demand from residents and
   users who demand greater alternative mobility options, have caused the demand for sidewalks and repairs to increase
   significantly.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds will pay for construction services to build and replace sidewalks that directly service citizens and visitors.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?
