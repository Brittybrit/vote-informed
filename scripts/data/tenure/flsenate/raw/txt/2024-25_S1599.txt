1. Project Title         Community Reentry Coordination - Unite Us

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request       11/14/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The Unite Us software will support the MDC Reentry team with connecting soon to be released inmates to various
   community-based services such as employment placement, mental and substance abuse treatment, and basic needs prior
   to their release. The platform is used by multiple reentry-focused organizations and allows for case
   coordination/management, referrals, and follow-up. The tablets and air cards will eliminate the paper forms used to conduct
   reintegration planning with inmates.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Unite Us is a software that will be used for Reentry Counselors to connect and support the incarcerated individuals with
    external services upon their release.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     With the purchase of Unite Us, MDCR will be able to coordinate social services virtually. The program has a case
    management component that will be instrumental in tracking inmates (sentenced and un-sentenced), allowing reentry
    staff to identify emerging issues and needs that, when addressed, will increase reintegration success.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     MDCR's Reentry Counselors will be able to connect inmates to a myriad of critical community-based services that
    include mental health services, employment, and educational services while in custody in anticipation of their release.
    The programs and services aim to reduce the opportunity of committing a crime and support recidivism reduction through
    education opportunities and workforce development.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Currently or formerly incarcerated persons. Approximately 201-400 of individuals in population are expected to be
    served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project is aligned with MDCR's mission statement, our primary focus is to enhance individuals' skills and strategies
    for a successful return to their families and communities. The methodology of the outcome is offering a comprehensive
    range of services, collaborating with external partners, and instilling a sense of responsibility and accountability while
    striving to create a safer community for all.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
