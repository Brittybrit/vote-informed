4. Project/Program Description
    Storage Buildings for Emergency Backup Power Generators.
   The storage buildings are the final phase of Emergency Backup Power Projects for Fire Rescue and Wastewater. The
   generators will keep the wastewater system operational, preventing sewage from backing up into homes during power
   outages that occur during and after hurricanes, other weather events or other catastrophic events and keep all fire stations
   operational during the same events, enhancing the resources available for the emergency management teams to conduct
   rescue and recovery efforts.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The storage buildings for the trailer-mounted generators is the final step of the Emergency Backup Power Project
    established by FEMA and administered by the FDEM. The design for the storage buildings will be ready to competitively
    bid for construction at the start of the state budget year. The storage buildings will meet the requirement for the
    generators to be stored at a location protected against a 500-year flood event or located outside the Special Flood
    Hazard Area (SFHA) and protected against wind with a rated enclosure based on their location requirements.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Construction of two wind-rated storage buildings for emergency backup generators on Village-owned properties that
    meet the federal and state criteria of being located outside the Special Flood Hazard Area (SFHA) utilizing design plans
    to ensure the storage buildings are compliant with federal, state and local regulations to protect against wind.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The Emergency Backup Power Generators will be used to keep wastewater system, including pump stations and grinder
    pumps, and fire rescue stations operational in the event of power loss during a storm or other emergency thus preventing
    environmental hazards and improving life safety.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All segments of the population will benefit from the continuation of services after storm and other emergency events.

    The individuals expected to be served would number over 800.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The benefit of the wastewater emergency backup power project would be achieved through measurement of flow for
    treatment that continues through an emergency incident. This flow would provide a measurement of the effluent that was
    prevented from affecting the environment through spillage or damage to the system.
    The benefit of the fire rescue emergency backup power project would be achieved through reporting provided by the call
    logs and incident reports that detail the fire rescue activities. Maintenance of this information after an event will provide a
    method for measuring the level of benefit from reliance on back-up generator power.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The Village would be required to repay any prepaid costs to the State of Florida if the funded activities are not completed
    and requirement for storage buildings to house FEMA HMGP-funded generators is not met.
