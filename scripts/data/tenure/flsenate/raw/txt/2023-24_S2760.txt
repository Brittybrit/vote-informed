    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     GEM is fully equipped to respond immediately to crisis & with partners such as Good360, Amazon, Publix, Goya and
    many others, we’re able to bring aid to most vulnerable population quickly & efficiently. By partnering directly with local
    community organizations and leaders, we’re able to distribute aid much more quickly and efficiently than more traditional
    disaster relief organizations.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     These are statewide services to vulnerable populations affected by disaster or other such events. Our services therefore
    all populations in communities across our state.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     GEM not only provides immediate relief but also tends to the often overlooked gap period and long-term recovery,
    rebuilding, and redevelopment of a crisis-hit cities and communities. We team with local businesses, skilled workers, and
    volunteers who know their home community best and share the language, customs, and experience. Therefore, Number
    of events GEM responds to, including people served, family necessity kits (boxes with snacks, food, other items)
    distributed), total aid distributed. Volunteers and skilled workers sourced in communities. Also, We team with local
    businesses, skilled workers, and volunteers who know their home community best and share the language, customs, and
    experience. We hire skilled workers locally during times of disaster events and create immediate job opportunities. So we
    can measure number of jobs created. Also given our response to any disaster within hours, we can measure number of
    events we respond to.
