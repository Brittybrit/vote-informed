1. Project Title           Palmetto Bay - Coral Reef Park Trail Improvements

2. Senate Sponsor          Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request         02/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The Coral Reef Park Trail Improvements project will repair portions of the existing 2.5 mile multi-use park trail of uneven
   path, redo the existing rubberized trail that has been worn down from daily use and the elements. The added development
   of 2 trail paths leading from the perimeter to the park interior is also a part of this project. Finally, the conversion of one of
   the restrooms from septic to sewer which is a Miami-Dade County project to convert as many septic tanks to the County
   sewer system.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goals to be achieved by repairing uneven trails and rubberized portions is increased safety for disabled individuals
    and comfort for users while walking or jogging, the addition of trails will increase access from the perimeter to the interior
    with increased access to sports fields. Also, the conversion from septic to sewer will achieve the goal of saving money by
    eliminating closures due to back-ups and other septic related issues.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     To meet the intended purpose of the trail repair and with the conversion of septic to sewer will be a public bid process to
    select contractors. Purchase Orders (P.O.'s) will be created to track project expenditures and at completion, the public will
    be free to continue enjoying the park with regular restroom access and a more comfortable experience while enjoying the
    park.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The direct services will be a safer use of the park trail (asphalt & rubberized) additionally, increased regular restroom
    access.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     There is no specific targeted population beyond park goers, but all Miami-Dade County residents and visitors are always
    welcomed to enjoy Coral Reef Park. We estimate 1,000+ daily visitors as this is our most frequented park with the most
    activities located adjacent to an elementary school and within a residential neighborhood.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The benefit of repaired trails is increased safe walking on an even and newly rubberized path for comfort. The benefit of
    sewer conversion is more regular restroom access rather than dealing with closures due to back-ups and other septic
    specific issues.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Withholding of funds is a fair penalty for failing to meet deliverables.
