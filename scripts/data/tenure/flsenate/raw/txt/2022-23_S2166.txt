4. Project/Program Description
    Banyan Health seeks capital support to construct a new 100,000 +/- sq ft health center at 3850 Flagler St Miami that will
   more than double patient access to comprehensive primary & behavioral health care services and siignificantly increase
   clinical and support staffing. In addition, this new facility will expand the capacity at the sole contracted Miami-Dade County
   Crisis Intervention Receiving facility, a Mobile Response Team (deploys 24/7 to emergency behavioral health crisis
   countywide) and afford direly needed integrated health services to serve additional unmet, low income, uninsured & under-
   insured vulnerable populations. Once completed, the target population seen will include children, families and seniors from
   within the Little Havana community and surrounding areas. The area has long served as a community of Latino immigrants
   and is 90% Hispanic. The area is HRSA designated as both a Medically Underserved Area (MUA) and a Health
   Professionals Shortage Area (HIPSA).

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Banyan Health seeks capital support to construct a new 100,000 +/- sq ft health center in Little Havana (3850 Flagler St)
    Miami that will more than double patient access to comprehensive primary & behavioral health care services. It will
    expand the sole contracted MDC Crisis Intervention Receiving facility, a Mobile Response Team (deploys 24/7 to
    emergency behavioral health crisis countywide), and direly needed integrated health services to serve low income, un &
    under-insured vulnerable populations.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Banyan Health is requesting support to expand an existing constrained location by constructing a new 100,000 +/- sq ft.
    healthcare center. As such, the activities will include normal construction work, some examples of which are as follows:
    architectural design, foundation, framing, finishing, installation of window assemblies, roofing & mechanical systems and
    interior build-out among many other activities.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The requested appropriation is intended to support strictly capital construction costs and, as such, does not involve the
    provision of direct services to citizens and/or other individuals.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The intent of the requested support is to assist in the capital construction costs of building a new, expanded facility, not
    to provide direct patient services. Once completed, however, the target population will include children, families and
    seniors from within the Little Havana community and surrounding areas. This population is largely Hispanic and mostly
    low-income un & under-insured. The area is HRSA designated as both a Medically Underserved Area (MUA) and a
    Health Professionals Shortage Area (HIPSA) for both medical and dental.It is anticipated that within three years of the
    project completion we will expand as follows:
    Behavioral Health Growth:
    1. Direct Services staffing from 33 to 60
    2. Unduplicated patient count from 6,650 to 12,090
    3. Events of care from 62,123 to 112,950

    Primary care Growth:
    1. Direct Services staffing from 7 to 16.
    2. Unduplicated patient count from 5,885 to 13,440
    3. Events of care from 19,636 to 44,256
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The anticipated outcome of this request is to obtain the needed funding to enable the undertaking of the new
    construction of the Banyan Health Little Havana Health Center. Once constructed, this new facility will more than double
    capacity for direct services staffing and unduplicated patient counts consisting of low income individuals, families and
    seniors .
    The methodology undertaken will include quarterly reconciliation of detailed construction budgets in alignment with stated
    timeline(s) identifying milestones and responsible parties. Once operational, established clinical and financial measures
    and evaluation methodologies in accordance with strict HRSA 330 requirements will be adhered to in measuring
    outcomes.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Banyan Health has tremendous experience in successfully managing complex federal, state and local agency grants and
    sponsored programs. Currently, it responsibly manages a diverse portfolio of federal and state funding and has strict
    managerial controls and processes in place to assure award compliance. In the unlikely event that Banyan Health fails to
    meet deliverables or performance measures, we would be prepared to offer full restitution of the amount awarded.

13. The owners of the facility to receive, directly or indirectly, any fixed capital outlay funding. Include the
    relationship between the owners of the facility and the entity.

     Banyan Health maintains a series of affiliated organizations that maintain real estate holdings. For the requested state
    appropriation, however, Banyan Health will be directly responsible for the construction of the new expanded facility and,
    as such would be the recipient organization.
