14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Increased tourism from cyclists utilizing the Withlacoochee State Trail. Improved multi-generational healthy living/lifestyle
    for current residents and visitors . Better economic development potential for an area of low-moderate income
    households. Enhanced environmental protection for native wildlife and residential potable water wells. Wastewater main
    to eliminate existing septic tanks. Improved ground and surface water quality.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Will provide bicycle access to retail, restaurants, parks, medical arts district, and community college. Provide the
    infrastructure necessary to catalyze development of the adjacent platted subdivisions containing 1,500 single-family lots.
    introduce the large number of cyclists using the Withlacoochee State Trail to the West Inverness Gateway to the City
    featuring a new restaurant and access to the 300 acre Whispering Pines Regional Park. Will provide a new venue for all
    age groups to get outside and improve health and fitness This portion of Inverness represents an area of 49.3 % low to
    moderate income households. The connector will help these residents access employment, retail, transit, and services
    without owning an automobile.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Increased parks and recreation opportunities. Enhanced multi-modal transportation choices. Economic development
    from the multi-billion dollar cycling industry in Florida.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     >800 The multi-purpose aspect of the project will appeal to a wide spectrum of age groups. Parents with small children -
    trail head park. Teens - park and trail activities. Adults / seniors - walking, jogging, dog walking and cycling.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improve transportation conditions - Count of trail connector users and reduction or daily vehicular counts at trailheads.
    Increase or improve economic activity - Number of new permits issued for single-family homes.
    Increase Tourism - Count of users accessing the regional park and count of users on the trail connector
    Improvewastewatermanagement - Track the number of septic tanks removed.
    Enrich cultural experience - The establishment of a western gateway to the City using public art, signage, and special
    lighting complemented by the proposed new trail and park.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Any monies received from the state would be returned in like amounts for either percentage incomplete or in totality for
    project not commencing.
