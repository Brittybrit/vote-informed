1. Project Title           Coral Gables Citywide Roadway Safety Upgrades

2. Senate Sponsor         Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request        01/03/2024

4. Project/Program Description
    This project would supplement the City's funding for traffic calming initiatives within the jurisdiction, the specific locations
   have been identified and prioritized via engineering assessments. The multimodal transportation planning process identified
   citywide traffic calming solutions at locations identified by residents through public meetings and emails. Traffic calming
   solutions currently include but are not limited to speed tables, speed cushions, roundabouts, and medians.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose and goal achieved would be to create safer roads and improve pedestrian safety citywide, by slowing down
    vehicles with the installation of traffic calming devices.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Citizens will benefit from the impact such devices will have on vehicle movement-ideally resulting in lowered speed
    making for safer roads and pedestrian movement.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Tens of thousands of drivers move about the City daily. This project serves drivers by helping them to slow down, and
    neighborhoods, cyclists and pedestrians by making roads safer.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will

    Protect the general public from harm, improve transportation conditions citywide by installing devices to slow down traffic.
