                          Monroe County

2. Senate Sponsor         Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request        02/07/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Continuation of exterior hardening and restoration of the state owned Harry S. Truman Little White House and interior wall
   repair restoration. Continuing with the progress made in the previous fiscal year's capital projects, this year's request is in
   line with furthering protection of the home, not only the exterior, but the interior as well. Funding is needed to continue the
   tasks associated with the restoration projects due to funding shortfalls associated with regional limitations in South Florida.
   All tasks associated with the restoration follow the Secretary of Interior's guidelines.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Funds will be used to restore the Little White House. The Key West Harry S. Truman Foundation, Inc., exists for the
    purpose of protecting and preserving the building and grounds of the Truman Little White House in Key West, Florida.
    These repairs will allow for the continued preservation of the Truman Little White House.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Key West Harry S. Truman Foundation will work with contractors and experts in historic preservation to paint and
    preserve the exterior and interior of the historic property.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The completion of this project will protect the house and its contents from the elements and preserve the museum so that
    it can be enjoyed by generations to come.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The Truman Little White House museum is Florida's only presidential museum and its continued operation and
    preservation benefit Florida residents as well as out of state visitors.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The completion of this project will better protect the historic property from potential storms or weather threats and will
    protect from deterioration and provide for restoration of the interior rooms.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The Foundation will work with the Dept. of State on this appropriation to ensure all funds are appropriately utilized to
    complete the project and funds may be withheld.
