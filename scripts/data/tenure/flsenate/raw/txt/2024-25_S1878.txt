1. Project Title           City of Coral Gables Underline Connectivity Project - Riviera Drive Bike
                           Lanes and Sidewalks

2. Senate Sponsor         Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request        12/08/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The City has identified Riviera Drive North of US-1 as a good candidate for the addition of bicycle infrastructure to connect
   to the Underline due to the critical need to provide safe bicycle and pedestrian infrastructure from the Underline to facilities
   such as Coral Gables High School. The funds provided would supplement the City's future budget for the design and
   construction of sidewalks and bicycle infrastructure along Riviera Drive. Connectivity upgrades will provide safe travel
   north-south from the Underline to Bird Road, including access to the Design Innovation District and Coral Gables Senior
   High School.

     The City has identified Riviera Drive North of US-1 as a good candidate for the addition of bicycle infrastructure to
    connect to the Underline due to the critical need to provide safe bicycle and pedestrian infrastructure from the Underline
    to facilities such as Coral Gables High School. The funds provided would supplement the City's future budget for the
    design and construction of sidewalks and bicycle infrastructure along Riviera Drive from the Underline to Bird Road.

    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will be directly serviced by the road improvements.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The thousands of drivers, pedestrians and cyclists who utilize Riviera Drive on a daily basis.
