1. Project Title          Coral Gables Restoration of Historic Gondola Building

2. Senate Sponsor         Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request        01/25/2022

4. Project/Program Description
    Reconstruction and restoration of the Gondola Building which is listed on the Local and National Registers of Historic
   Places as part of the Biltmore Hotel Complex. Built in 1925 in the Mediterranean Revival style, the building was constructed
   to house Venetian Gondolas that would travel from the hotel through the Coral Gables Waterway to Tahiti Beach.

   Due to a recent collapse of three walls, this historically significant structure is now in desperate need of reconstruction and
   restoration. Drawings of the original structure exist to ensure it can be accurately reconstructed using salvageable elements
   from the original structure.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The historic Gondola Building which is part of the Biltmore Hotel Complex and is listed on both the Local and National
    Registers of Historic Places will be reconstructed and restored using salvageable elements from the original structure.
    The restoration will contribute to the city's commitment to honoring and protecting its past for the enjoyment of future
    generations. The restoration will contribute to the integrity of the Biltmore Hotel Complex, which was recently restored,
    and will provide a valuable learning opportunity as this is thought to be a unique structure in Florida.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    The city's Historical Resources and Cultural Arts Department has staff members experienced in historic preservation who
    will oversee the reconstruction for historical accuracy and will ensure the building is appropriately maintained in the future.
    Review and permitting will be expedited to successfully complete this project as quickly as possible.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The reconstruction will ensure this building is preserved for the enjoyment of current and future citizens who both reside
    in and visit the City of Coral Gables. This structure is thought to be unique in Florida and its accurate reconstruction will
    provide a valuable learning opportunity for both residents and students.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Reconstruction of this building will primarily serve city residents. In 2019, the population of the city was 50,000. Other
    individuals who will benefit from this project are visitors to the city, guests of the Biltmore Hotel, and students of
    architecture and history. A conservative estimate is approximately 5,000 people per annum.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project will result in the reconstruction of a significant historic structure which is thought to be unique in Florida and
    is an integral element of the Biltmore Hotel Complex. The methodology will be measured through the completion of
    phased work in a timely manner, the accuracy of the restoration, and the visitor numbers achieved on completion of the
    project.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Non-disbursement of grant funds.
