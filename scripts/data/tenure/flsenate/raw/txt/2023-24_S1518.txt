1. Project Title          Coral Gables Venetian Structural and Surface Repair and Renovation

2. Senate Sponsor         Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request        02/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Preservation of a National Historic Landmark Facility. Needed repairs and renovation will continue to preserve the facility
   and will also improve the environmental footprint of the facility. The needed renovation will also improve the natural water
   system currently utilized to fill and drain the vessel. The Surface and structural repairs will prevent leaking water loss and
   allow the water derived from the Biscayne aquifer to be returned to the aquifer.
