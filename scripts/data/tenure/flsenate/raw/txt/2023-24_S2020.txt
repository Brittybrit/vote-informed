14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The improvement proposed by the City will make the Park more accessible during the day and year round. The park will
    be safer for children and families since it will include rubber playground surface and shade structures; and less prone to
    flooding events. The improvements also call for a new water fountain to allow for proper hydration.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Park will have programs to engage children and youth giving them alternatives to criminal activity.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     There will be no direct service provided to citizens
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is the neighborhood in the Coral Way Park area, an area which is home to more than 60,000
    residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The park improvements are expected to improve physical and mental health of the community as well as reduce crime,
    improve the local environment and reduce flooding. These outcomes will be measured primarily by monitoring the number
    of park sponsored activities and participants, as well anecdotal observations.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables or performance measures should subject the City to an audit and depending on the results
    of the audit the City could be liable for sanctions up to revocation of funding.
