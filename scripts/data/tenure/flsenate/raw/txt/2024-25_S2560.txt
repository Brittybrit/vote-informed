4. Project/Program Description
    Arthur & Polly Mays 6-12 Conservatory of the Arts is a specialized 6th-12th grade Magnet Program created due to lack of
   access for students talented in the performing and visual arts/fashion design in this region of the county to receive high-
   level performing arts, fashion and entertainment industry-focused educational opportunities. It is a visual, performing and
   communicative arts school in Miami-Dade County to have a full high-school Dual Enrollment Program for all students in
   grades 9-12. It has been recognized in the U.S. News and World Report for Best High School, received the Golden School
   Award, and the Magnet Schools of America Merit Award.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This specialized 6th-12th grade Magnet Program was created due to lack of access for students talented in the
    performing and visual arts/fashion design in this region of the county to receive high-level performing arts, fashion and
    entertainment industry-focused educational opportunities. Specific goals include but are not limited to 95% HS graduation
    rate, 75% HS graduate with Honors, 60% of MS students will enroll in Acceleration, 90% of HS students will enroll in
    College and Career Acceleration.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Arthur & Polly Mays 6-12 Conservatory of the Arts offers a high quality and extensive academic and arts curriculum
    encompassing middle and high school with major art strands - Art, Band, Chorus, Dance, Entertainment Technology,
    iWrite, Strings: Orchestra and Guitar, Theatre, and Fashion Design. With new essential tools, materials, equipment, and
    instruments, students will excel in their studies and reach their full artistic potential.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Arthur & Polly Mays 6-12 Conservatory of the Arts offers a program of academic and artistic studies in the visual and
    performing arts/fashion design/entertainment industry which shall be available to all students in grades 6-12. To
    showcase the program and talent, the general public is exposed to the arts via low cost and free performances and
    exhibitions. Arthur & Polly Mays will also host and bus 8th grade students from neighboring schools to view performances
    throughout the year.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Middle and High School students in grades 6-12; 65% Economically Disadvantaged; Over 500 students will be served.
    School demographics from 2021-2022:
    5% White
    35% Black
    58% Hispanic
    2% Other
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Enhancements in mental health, a culturally enriching experience, elevated educational quality, boosted economic
    activity, and the creating of targeted job opportunities. Outcomes will be measured using contracts and documented
    overall performance.
    A minimum of 2 guest artists per program to support student learning and growth.
    A minimum of 2 invitations to local or state competitions and/or conferences
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Denial of funds in future requests.
