1. Project Title         Monroe County - Marine Emergency Response Vessels

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       10/31/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Two Marine Emergency Response vessels will provide Monroe County's Fire Rescue Department the capability to provide
   emergency medical and life-saving services for the thousands of boaters that live in and visit the Keys from all over the
   State. With these vessels, Monroe County Fire Rescue will establish a new marine-based emergency response unit
   consisting of two vessels operated by medically trained firefighters to operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. The
   emergency response vessels will operate from strategic areas throughout the Keys to provide the greatest life saving
   impact. The vessels will respond to water-based medical emergencies, fire emergencies (vessels provide 1,000 gallon per
   minute fire suppression services), can rapidly deliver crews to remote marine areas, and provide support for search and
   rescue operations. These new vessels, within the new marine-based emergency response program, will protect and save
   lives.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose of the funds is to enhance safety and save lives. The vessels purchased with these funds will provide
    advanced life support, marine fire rescue, search and rescue, and assist other agencies with emergency medical and fire
    suppression services in Atlantic Ocean and Florida Bay that are not currently present within Monroe County Fire Rescue.
    Establishing marine emergency response capabilities will enable Fire Rescue personnel to respond to water-based
    medical and fire emergencies, and provide support for search and rescue operations delivering crews with life saving
    medical care and fire suppression capabilities rapidly to remote areas and provide assistance for special water-based
    events.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The vessels will enable the creation of a new emergency response program consisting of two vessels manned by two
    medically trained firefighters operating 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The Fire/Rescue boats will operate from strategic
    areas to provide the greatest life and property saving impact. The intention is to mitigate tragedies on the water such as
    the death of Harlie A. Smith who died on August 11, 2017, in the Atlantic Ocean just a few miles from Cudjoe Key. While
    on vacation with her family of four, she was accidentally hit by a boat propeller. Medical assistance could not be provided
    in time on the water, and she died as a result. Additional resources will assist to avert these types of incidents.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The value to citizens is increased capacity to provide medical, life-saving and fire suppression services on the water.
    This will add protection for the many visiting boaters and their families, as well as our resident boaters, and waterfront
    recreational areas such as docks, marinas, and water based properties. Monroe County is surrounded by many small
    inhabited islands and fire rescue is extremely limited in its ability to reach these remote areas. These boats may also
    assist in time of evacuation of potentially impacted areas that have no connection to the roadways and provide a valuable
    resource following a disaster with entry within these areas.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population are those who work, live and visit within the Florida Keys. There are 26,500 registered vessels in
    Monroe County. Additionally, there is a significant impact from trailered boats vacationing in Monroe's waters from other
    communities, particularly the boating community of neighboring Miami-Dade County, with 73,663 registered vessels.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The primary benefit is a lwater-based lifesaving service that is currently not available to the community.
    The method will mirror that of our state incident reporting, for incident types, response times, and emergency response
    actions that are able to be performed solely through the incorporation of a marine emergency response unit.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Should the vessels not be purchased in a timely fashion, no draw down of State funds will occur.
