4. Project/Program Description
    Scoring Goals for South Florida aims to increase access to mental health and emotional wellness through soccer to 5,000
   promised youth. We use an integrated and evidence-based methodology, using a resilience- informed approach, providing

   communities.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Scoring Goals for South Florida aims to increase access to mental health and emotional wellness through Soccer to
    5,000 promised youth, this will allow for participants to proactive physical fitness, lower risks of childhood obesity while
    enhancing cognitive and emotional regulation skills in safe spaces with trauma informed Coaches and mentors.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     1. Participants ages 6- 18 will receive after school soccer programming for 35 weeks, promoting physical activity and
    emotional regulation, resilience, conflict resolution, advocacy and leadership skills and financial education. 2. Training of
    200 high schoolers to youth sports mentorship program to promote Youth Leadership and workforce development. 3.
    Provide referral linkages to any other service participants or their family might need.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The requested funds will directly provide after school recreational youth sports programming accessibility and equity to
    5,000 youth in underserved communities. They will be employed in expanding our primary prevention program- which
    consists of integrating soccer, fitness, mental health and emotional wellness services in parks, schools and community
    partner organizations.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     5,000 individuals from the following Target Populations will be served:
    -Grade School students
    -High School students
    -Students with free or reduced lunch
    -Economically disadvantaged households
    -Promised youth
    -Foster youth
    -Youth with poor physical health
    -Poor mental health
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     1. Improving Physical Health.
    2. Improve Mental Health.
    3. Protect the general public from harm (environmental, criminal, etc.)
    4. Increase or improve economic activity
    5. Create specific immediate job opportunities
    6. Enhance specific individual’s economic self sufficiency
    7. Enhance specific individual’s economic self sufficiency
    8. Reduce substance abuse.
    9. Divert from Criminal/ Juvenile Justice System


    -We will monitor participants to track changes in the incidence of preventable diseases such as childhood obesity and
    increase knowledge of substance abuse. Surveys will be conducted to measure the increase in public awareness and
    adoption of preventive health practices.
    -We will measure our outcomes through our data collection with the support of Florida International University as well as a
    software tool with Hello Insight, and our Evaluation and Monitoring System that measures the participants physical, socio-
    emotional and cognitive progress.
