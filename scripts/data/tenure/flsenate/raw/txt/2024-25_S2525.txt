4. Project/Program Description
    Petit Lane connects Calienta Street to Shoal Line Boulevard, a Hernando County collector roadway along the coast of
   Hernando County. Calienta Street via Petit Lane accesses the county boat ramp, several commercial properties (including
   sea food sales and distribution), and on the north is Hernando Beach, a coastal subdivision. Currently, stormwater and
   roadway improvements are being designed on Calienta Street and Petit Lane along with the realignment of Petit Lane to
   align with Flamingo Boulevard which will remove the major skews at the intersections of Calienta Street and Shoal Line
   Boulevard. The current skew creates severe sight issues when entering and exiting both Calienta Street and Shoal Line
   Boulevard. Both Calienta Street and Petite Lane will be reconstructed as one project.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    Completion of the construction of Calienta Street and intersection improvements for the Petit Lane realignment to
    Flamingo Boulevard, which will improve the accessibility and safety of entering and exiting traffic at Petit Lane and
    Flamingo Boulevard. The realignment will remove the major skew at the intersections of Petit Lane/Shoal Line Blvd and
    Petit Lane/Calienta Street.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    Construction activities to build the designed improvements for Calienta Street and Petit Lane/Flamingo Blvd.. The project
    will be competitively bid to increase economic stimulus in the area.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Improvements to this roadway and intersections would not only improve access for evacuation of the subdivision and the
    commercial properties during a disaster, but also improve safety during everyday operation for the public and tourists
    traveling to the commercial properties, the subdivision, and boat ramp.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The general population of Hernando County and tourists visiting coastal businesses will be served by this project.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improved access for the subdivision and commercial properties as well as enhanced evacuation route. Improved safety
    for the Hernando County citizens and tourists traveling to the commercial properties, subdivision, and the public boat
    ramp.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The county will reimburse state funding for failure to meet the deliverables.
