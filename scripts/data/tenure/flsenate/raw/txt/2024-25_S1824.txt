14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The South Miami Police Department currently operates on Miami Dade County radio systems for all its police radio
    needs. They currently operate on Miami Dade Police Department (MDPD) Kendall frequency which serves over 1 million
    residents and business community. MDPD is currently in the process of upgrading it's current radios to new Motorola
    models which provide enhanced safety features like GPS, noise cancellation, transmission playback and improved audio
    reception.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     All police services will be communicated utilizing the new described radio equipment and based on the enhanced
    technology improvements. This should provide a more efficient response to the citizens it protects and serves.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Police services.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     City of South Miami, 12,000 residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Protecting the general public from harm- Improved radio communications and enhanced officer safety with the features
    included with the new radios. The enhanced functions should provide better communication during large events, officer
    safety by geo locating officers via radio and improved general enhanced services with playback features which will reduce
    radio transmissions and radio traffic during critical situations. The methodology by which this outcome will be measured is
    conducting surveys with officers who utilize the equipment and identify increased efficiencies with responses.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Revocation/witholding of funds.
