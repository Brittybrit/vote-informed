    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The City's current system is over 50 years old, in addition to being undersized to serve the existing and projected
    population of the City, the system suffers from documented persistent leakage, as well as intermittent failures and
    ruptures causing disruptions in service, damage to infrastructure, and potential contamination. The funding will go to
    construction of the potable water replacement project in the Phase IV area of the project which is north of SW 16th St and
    west of 62nd Ave.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Engineering, design, and construction of the potable water main improvements for the Phase IV area.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Improved potable water services through increased water pressure and reduced risk of interruptions in water service or
    contamination from antiquated pipe ruptures
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This project targets approximate 25% of the City's residential population or approximately 2500 residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improved potable water services through increased water pressure and reduced risk of interruptions in water service or
    contamination from antiquated pipe ruptures. The outcome will be measure by the reduction in the estimated 150 million
    gallons of water that is wasted per year and noticeable increase in water pressure for citizens.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The City is committed to the success of this project. The deliverable on this project is fully function potable water system
    upgrade. Should the City fail to meet this deliverable, the penalty would be for the City to be responsible for the cost of
    the required improvements to meet the deliverable.
