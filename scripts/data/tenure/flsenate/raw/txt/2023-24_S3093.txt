1. Project Title         Generators for Generations

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request       03/08/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    JARC's residential group homes and Supported Living Apartments provide 24/7/365 supervision for over 100 individuals.
   Hurricanes are an unavoidable reality of life in South Florida. With this in mind, JARC has begun the process of placing
   generators at each of our facilities. To date, 7 of the homes have had generators installed. We are requesting funding to
   purchase and install a generators at the remaining 3 homes as well as our Supported Living Apartment building.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    Individuals with intellectual disabilities and autism will have the ability to feel safe during a hurricane or weather event.
    They will have the ability to remain in their home in the event of a power outage.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Planning purchase and installation of generators at 3 residential group homes and one supported living apartment
    building.
