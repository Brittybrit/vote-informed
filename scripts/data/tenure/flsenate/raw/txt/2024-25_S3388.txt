4. Project/Program Description
    The purpose of Farm-Ed is to enrich grades 6-12 education by embedding modern agricultural techniques and cutting-
   edge technologies within the curriculum, facilitating experiential learning opportunities for students. This comprehensive
   program encompasses a robust grade 6-12 curriculum developed by experts, mentorship from seasoned horticulturists,
   access to state-of-the-art agricultural tools, and intuitive data dashboards for real-time learning and analysis. Farm-Ed's
   overarching mission is to cultivate a new cadre of skilled agricultural innovators by merging classical educational models
   with immersive, project-based learning experiences that are informed by empirical data and sustainable practices. This
   forward-thinking approach not only equips students with vital skills but also instills a deeper understanding and appreciation
   for the agricultural sciences.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose is to integrate modern agricultural practices and technologies into the grades 6-12 curriculum, providing
    students with hands-on learning experiences. The program includes elements such as a full grades 6-12 curriculum,
    access to master horticulturists, the latest growing tools, and easy-to-use data dashboards. The overall goal is to educate
    the next generation of agricultural innovators, merging traditional education with hands-on project-based, data-driven
    agricultural practices.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Project-based learning [PBL] tools will be provided to cater to a wide diversity of students throughout the Tampa Bay
    area. Contracted services will be used to refine features, ensuring adaptive content and tools while consultants will
    prioritize gathering educator feedback, on-going tech support, and refinements to UI/UX. The funds will also be
    channeled to support specialized training initiatives and consistent on-site assistance, aiming to promote teacher
    retention and student academic support.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Project-based learning [PBL] initiatives centered around agriculture and gardening will benefit all participating students in
    Tampa Bay 6-12 schools, particularly addressing the specific needs of ELL, ESE, gifted, and economically disadvantaged
    students. Teachers will benefit from specialized training and continuous support, enhancing their ability to provide
    personalized instruction.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Grades 6-12 Students in the Tampa Bay Area, 800+ served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     By harnessing advanced algorithms, personalized learning pathways are enhanced and reinforced, catering especially to
    ELL, ESE, and Gifted students. This tailored approach boosts student comprehension and engagement. Anticipated
    measurable outcomes include improved standardized test performance and increased teacher retention, attributed to
    enriched instructional methods. Evaluation will center around daily student data insights provided to teachers, enabling
    real-time tracking of student growth and comprehension. Yearly comparisons of standardized test performance will further
    quantify academic improvements. Teacher retention rates will be analyzed annually, linked with their engagement in
    training and tools.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Should the organization not meet the agreed upon deliverables, a pay back process will be established.
