14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This request will allow for the Office of the Clerk and Comptroller in Miami-Dade County, to provide critical services to the
    underserved in our community. The mobile units will allow the Office of the Clerk and Comptroller to setup shop not only
    on the weekends, but in pop up locations throughout Miami-Dade County to assist our residents in resolving their court
    obligations, without having to navigate our sometimes unreliable transportation options.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Due to the vast and diverse geographic/demographic area that Miami-Dade County encompasses, the mobile Clerk of
    the Court and Comptroller's Office will be utilized to service outreach areas that are not in close proximity to our satellite
    offices or downtown courthouses to bring access to justice to the public.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     It will allow for community outreach, to educate and disseminate information on upcoming events and provide detailed
    information as to the services we provide thereby bridging the current gap that exists in the community due to lack of
    transportation, childcare and work. This will allow enhanced accessibility to legal services, providing citizens with
    convenient access to court-related information.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This mobility would allow for the much needed outreach services to Veterans, the homeless population, elderly persons,
    persons with poor physical health, jobless persons, economically disadvantaged persons, developmentally disabled,
    physically disabled, university/college students, currently or formerly incarcerated persons, drug offenders, victims of
    crime, and the general Miami-Dade County public. This is expected to serve anyone in need in Miami-Dade County >800
    citizens.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The mobile Clerk of the Court and Comptroller's Office would allow for the collection and payment of fines, fees and
    related costs by the residents via cashless interactions, thereby reducing potential driver license suspensions. In that
    same breadth, the mobility of our office would allow our presence at various educational institutions and job fairs to allow
    for people to learn about our office. The outcome will be measured by Driver's License reinstatements, tag renewals,
    payment of unpaid fines, and the enhancement of community outreach.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Return of funds
