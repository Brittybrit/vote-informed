4. Project/Program Description
    Founded in 1997, the Haitian Lawyers Association (HLA) is a 501(c)(3), non-profit voluntary bar association in the State of
   Florida. HLA’s members include judges, lawyers, law professors, and law students of Haitian and Haitian-American
   descent, as
   well as those who are not of Haitian descent but have a vested interest in issues affecting the Haitian community. The HLA
   Foundation was established in 2023 as a 501(c)(3) charitable organization with the objective of carrying out HLA's mission
   of protecting and advocating for the legal rights of the Haitian and other minority communities in the State of Florida. The
   HLA Foundation's Pro Bono Program provides free legal services to low income and other vulnerable individuals and
   families in the areas of family law, landlord tenant, and contracts.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Increased access to the court system and greater understanding and appreciation of our judicial system will be achieved
    by providing pro bono assistance to low-income individuals and families and economically disadvantaged persons
    throughout the State of Florida. Our free legal services will also help decrease the workload of the county clerks offices,
    judicial staff, and various legal aid organizations. We are assisting individuals and families regain personal and economic
    stability and safety, attain civil justice, and become or maintain their status as contributing members of our State. As a
    result of the increased awareness of our services to the community, we have received a steadily growing influx of calls
    and inquiries for assistance. By adding personnel to support staff and manage the additional workload, we will fortify our
    ability to respond to the need. The cumulative result of our pro bono services is that we strengthen our State as a whole
    by empowering its citizens.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Legal services and representation in various areas of law including: Family Law, Landlord Tenant, and Contracts. Our
    staff and volunteer attorneys provide direct legal representation to low-income individuals and families. We conduct
    regular outreach across the State and train and mentor volunteer attorneys that will be assisting or representing clients
    pro bono. These funds will help empower the most vulnerable members of our State through free legal services so that
    they can regain personal and economic stability and safety, attain civil justice, and become or maintain their status as
    contributing members of our State.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Services will range from consultation, case review and providing legal counsel and advice, to full, direct legal
    representation across multiple areas of law, including assistance with filling out legal documents to help pro se clients
    proceed with litigation. Our bilingual attorneys and staff will provide assistance to the county clerks who cannot offer legal
    advice on how to prepare certain forms such as landlord tenant and divorce forms.
    As a result of the increased awareness of the services our program offers to the community, we have received a steadily
    growing influx of calls and inquiries for assistance. By adding personnel to support staff and manage the additional
    workload, the HLA Foundation will ensure and reinforce its ability to respond to and meet the needs of the local residents
    in need of such services and strengthen its collaborative partnerships with local community organizations in reaching our
    most vulnerable residents.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Low-income individuals and families; vulnerable individuals in our communities; the elderly; jobless; and economically
    disadvantaged. We expect to serve over 250 families throughout the State of Florida.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     By providing pro bono assistance to those who need it most in our local communities, we can affect positive change,
    which will have a far-reaching impact. We intend to provide representation or assistance to an estimated 250 cases with
    filling out various forms such as divorce forms and other small claim matters. We will monitor our case representation and
    pro bono legal assistance using our case management system. In the past, we have provided clear deliverables in the
    number of cases and consultations that we can provide with funding available. We have also partnered with the Office of
    the Attorney General and various legal aid organizations such as Dade Legal Aid and Broward Legal Aid to provide
    similar
    services in the past.
