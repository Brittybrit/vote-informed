4. Project/Program Description
    Funds provided will allow Learning Ally to pilot the deployment of a literacy screener that has been localized and made
   specific to Florida's state standards for education. The screener will allow teachers to deliver precision instruction to
   students based on their learning profile. Use of the screener will help to limit the number of students who end up in special
   education by identifying early, the need for interventions, thereby making proficient readers and potentially eliminating
   struggles from dyslexia.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The specific purpose for the funding is to pilot the deployment of a literacy screener that has been localized and made
    specific to Florida's state standards for education. The goal associated with this work is to limit the number of students
    who end up in special education by enabling teachers to use the screener to deliver precision instruction based on how
    that student learns. The goal is to early identify and making proficient readers, potentially eliminating struggles from
    dyslexia.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The purpose of this request is to provide a literacy screener for all Florida's children. We have been localizing the Reach
    Every Reader K-3 screening assessment to identify children who are potentially at risk for moderate to severe word-level
    problems who are in need of early intervention. The second phase of the project entails piloting the screener in key
    districts, preparing to go statewide.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     a) Assessments are computer-adaptive. b) Whole child assessment will be conducted including a biopsychosocial
    survey, family measurement, and possibly teacher measurement to inform risk calculation. c) Learning Ally/FSU will host
    and maintain the Assessment Piloting Platform and associated native applications for the duration of this contract.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Students in grades K-3 in selected pilot locations.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The program will yield an early literacy screener guaranteed to be a valid measure of early literacy readiness for students
    in grades K-2, along with a supporting instructional program on how educators adjust their instruction based on results To
    measure outcomes, Learning Ally/FSU will conduct localized validation of the Florida Dyslexia Screener. We will work
    with the state of Florida to develop the study design including sampling and scale considerations as well as the process
    of assessment that is important to the state to consider in validating the assessment (e.g., including code, language,
    RAN, and other possible types of assessments).
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Corrective action plans may be required for noncompliance, nonperformance, or unacceptable performance. Financial
    penalties may be imposed for failures to implement or to make acceptable progress on such corrective action plans.
