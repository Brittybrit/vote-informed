14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The improvement proposed by the City will make the Park more accessible during the day and year round, allowing for
    increased neighborhood schools and resident use by reducing flooding and grass maintenance Flooding keeps the park
    from being accessible after High Peak Rains and lighting will make it accessible 24 hours. The synthetic turf will reduce
    maintenance time.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Park will have programs to engage youth giving them alternatives to criminal activity. Improved lighting will illuminate
    dark areas that may encourage criminal activity. the installation of 8 LED lights will also be more efficient reducing the
    environmental impact of lighting the park. .
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     There will be no direct service provided to citizens
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is the neighborhood of Riverside Community in Miami with and esemated population of 18,543.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The park improvements are expected to improve physical and mental health of the community as well as reduce crime,
    improve the local environment and reduce flooding. These outcomes will be measured primariy through monitoring wells,
    review of electrical current usage and monitoring the number of park sponsored actives and participants, as well
    anecdotal observations.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables or performance measures should subject the City to an audit and depending on the results
    of the audit the City could be liable for sanctions up to revocation of funding.
