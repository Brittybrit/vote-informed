1. Project Title          Homestead Wastewater Treatment UV System Replacement

2. Senate Sponsor         Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request        02/02/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    To construct a new UV (ultraviolet) disinfection system for the wastewater treatment facility. The current UV system has
   been in operation since the late 1990s and that unit was retired by the manufacturer on July 31, 2022. The project consists
   of a new straight concrete open channel to house the new UV equipment and all the necessary piping needed in order to
   connect to the current wastewater treatment facility's filters effluent pipe and the wastewater treatment facility's effluent
   discharge splitter box. The new disinfection system will replace the currently retired UV system with a more technologically
   advanced unit that will provide better energy consumption and less downtime for cleaning and maintenance, all while
   providing high level disinfection to the wastewater treatment facility's effluent.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The project will allow the wastewater treatment facility to meet the wastewater high level disinfection goals while meeting
    the local and state governments goal of protecting our most vital natural resource and recharge the Biscayne Aquifer.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The construction of a new straight concrete open channel to house the new UV equipment and all the necessary piping
    needed in order to connect to the current wastewater treatment facility's filters effluent pipe and the wastewater treatment
    facility's effluent discharge splitter box.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The new UV system is needed in order to ensure the wastewater treatment facility continues to produce high level
    disinfection to the effluent water thus protecting the environment and the recharge of the Biscayne Aquifer.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population served by this project is the general population. Over 80,000 individuals are expected to be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project will mitigate health and safety risks to the public and the environment downstream from the plant by
    providing high level disinfection to the wastewater effluent without the addition of dangerous substances and adds no
    toxic compounds to the receiving waters while recharging the Biscayne Aquifer. Outcomes will be measured by
    conducting daily effluent fecal coliform bacteria samples, quarterly monitoring wells testing and annual EPA primary and
    secondary drinking water testing parameters to ensure the receiving waters are protected.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Revocation of funding if project is not completed.
