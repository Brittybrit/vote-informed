    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     These funds will be used for the design of the Miami-Dade Regional Soccer Park (MDRSP), a 47.33-acre former landfill.
    The goal is to use funds to develop the design of the park in order to then implement the phased construction of MDRSP.
    The planning for the Park is a direct response to a community desire for a regional soccer complex expressed through
    the Building Better Communities bond program. MDRSP will serve as a year-round premier facility open to the public for
    youth/adult use.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     These funds will contribute directly to the design development of MDRSP to include preparation of Schematic Design,
    Design Development, and Construction Documents. The scope of design documents are in direct response to community
    needs for a regional soccer complex as gathered through the Building Better Communities bond program and critical
    stakeholder input during the planning process.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     In order to deliver the regional soccer park and it's amenities, the design documents will be a key milestone of meeting
    community needs. In order to achieve this goal, the funding will be used to complete this phase of the project.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Target populations served by this project include elderly persons, persons with poor mental health, persons with poor
    physical health, jobless persons, economically disadvantaged persons, at-risk youth, developmentally disabled, physically
    disabled, preschool students, grade school students, high school students, university/college students. As a regional park
    in our system, it is expected to serve the 2.7 million residents at-large and visitors to Miami-Dade County.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     We expect multiple benefits including: Improved physical activity, measured by games played and/or health impact
    assessment. Mental health improvements, measured by health impact assessment. Enrich cultural experience, measured
    through attendance counts and park usage. Improve quality of education, measured by number of program registrants.
    Enhance or improve environmental conditions for wildlife and flora/fauna, measured through heat index, tree canopy
    calculations, assessments of population increases of wildlife and flora/fauna. Protect the general public from harm,
    measured by the amount of remediated soil and increased outdoor recreational space. Increase or improve economic
    activity, measured by property value changes. Increase tourism, measured by sales revenues and hotel taxes collected.
    Create immediate job opportunities, measured by number of jobs created. Reduce recidivism, measured by
    reincarceration rates before and after.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
