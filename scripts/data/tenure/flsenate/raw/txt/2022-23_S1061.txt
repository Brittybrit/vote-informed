2. Senate Sponsor          Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request         09/21/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    The funding request is for a shovel ready design/build project for the replacement of thirty-nine (39) drainage inlets
   (estimated to be between 40 to 60 years old), the addition of eight hundred and sixty (860) linear feet of perforated PVC
   pipe, and the reconstruction of two thousand one hundred sixty-five (2165) linear feet of road reconstruction. This
   Installation will allow for a greater collection of water within the vicinity to percolate within while protecting the surrounding
   residential homes from flood damage.
