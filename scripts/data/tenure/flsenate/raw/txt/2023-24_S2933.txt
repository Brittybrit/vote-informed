1. Project Title          Hurricane IRMA Recovery for City of Key Colony Beach City Hall - Monroe
                          County

2. Senate Sponsor         Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request        01/25/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Replacement of primary local government facility (City Hall) due to substantial damage as a result of Hurricane IRMA. City
   hall had been determined not appropriate for ongoing long-term function as a result from storm surge, flooding and
   scouring of the foundation of a building built in the early 1960 and current sits 3 feet below base flood elevation. A new
   facility is necessary to perform ongoing governmental functions. Repairing our current building that is a critical government
   facility, and which is 7 feet below the new flood plain (BFE), would be irresponsible and likely illegal under current federal
   and state building codes and guidelines. FEMA requires applicable codes and standards to be used during repair of direct
   damage.We have been replenishing our disaster relief reserves following Irma, but Key Colony Beach is a small one-half
   square mile residential city that does not have the business and financial base to raise funds quickly.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Continuity of Government and public safety. The existing City Hall building is more than 50 years old and is 7 feet below
    the new flood plain (BFE). The existing city hall building was deemed uninhabitable so city operations have been moved
    to trailers. Repairing the existing city hall building is an irresponsible use of funds because it will not be able to withstand
    future storms and is likely illegal under current federal and state building codes and guidelines. FEMA requires applicable
    codes and standards to be used during repair of direct damage in accordance with their policy PAPPG Chapter 8, III
    Codes & Standards.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     To serve as City Hall, Building Dept,Police Station, and Emergency Operations Center.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Funding would provide our residents with a City Hall hardened to withstand a Cat-5 storm. City Hall houses all
    Departments including Building, Planning,Police, Public works, and Administration. During any emergency, it will also
    serve as our Emergency Operations Center, and a place of shelter of last resort.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     3800
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     A Government center with a life expectancy of 50 years to serve our residents. The methodology used would be first
    life/safety in an emergency situation. Second, would be Government continuity before and after a major event/crisis.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     We have and will continue using a monetary fines per day for late delivery.
