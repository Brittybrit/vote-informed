12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To provide a safe, elevated route for pedestrians and bicyclists to travel between the north and south sides of US-1, thus
    providing greater connectivity and promoting pedestrian safety.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Pedestrian safety.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Pedestrian safety.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The target population for this project is all individuals residing in, visiting, and traveling through the City of South Miami.
    The amount of individuals this project is expected to serve will be at least 1,000 per day.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Increase connectivity of residents living on the southwest side of US-1 with health care facilities located on the northeast
    side of US-1. Improve resident quality. Reduction in vehicle miles traveled, traffic congestion, and associated emissions.
    Reduction in incidence of vehicle pedestrian collisions. Number of individuals utilizing the infrastructure in question.
    Increase number of individuals traveling to the City of South Miami Downtown Commercial District via the South Miami
    Metrorail Station. Increase connectivity of individuals residing on the NE side of US-1 with employment opportunities
    located on the southwest side.

    Methodology: Pedestrian surveys, data collected from bikeshare programs currently offered within the city. Number of
    admins to health care facilities. Traffic flow analysis. Survey of pedestrians entering South Miami Metrorail Station.
    Comparison of average annual vehicle pedestrian collisions reported pre and post project. Resident surveys.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
