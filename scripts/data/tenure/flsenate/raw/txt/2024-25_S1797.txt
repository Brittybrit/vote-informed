1. Project Title         Coral Gables Golden Gate Stormwater Improvement Project

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request       12/05/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Provide drainage improvements to prevent localized street ponding and flooding in the residential area of the Golden Gate
   (a historically black neighborhood on the National Register of Historic Places) by replacing substandard and aged street
   drainage structures. The drainage improvements will reduce ponding by collecting and removing storm water runoff from
   streets generated from a 5 yr. storm event.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The resolution of neighborhood complications arising from ineffective stormwater management.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

                                                       Page 2 of 4
                                           The Florida Senate

                                       Fiscal Year 2024-2025                                                 LFIR # 1797

    Construction of exfiltration trench drain and drainage collection system consisting of drainage pipe, drainage inlets and
    manholes to prevent street flooding. The project will also include construction or curb/gutter and roadway
    milling/resurfacing for adequate slope grading.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens will see less localized ponding and flooding, and will no longer experience the health and travel effects on
    prolonged standing water.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The community of Golden Gate and pedestrians and drivers who utilize local roads in the neighborhood. It is difficult to
    quantify the total population served but likely more than 1000.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The benefits are listed in subsection c and will be measured by neighborhood satisfaction and feedback.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The contracting agency may withhold project funds for non performance.
