14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will serve to replace in-kind the structurally deficient corrugated metal pipe (CMP) culvert located at SW 64th
    Ct. within the Twin Lakes Canal and repair the deep erosion which shows that the edge of the limestone in cantilever at
    the bottom of the north abutment of Bridge #875400 located at SW 63ct north of SW 62nd Ave located in South Miami,
    Florida.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The culvert in-kind replacement and bridge abutment repairs will provide safety and pedestrian connectivity.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The project will provide pedestrian connectivity between the Manor Lane neighborhood and the existing Metrorail and
    businesses in the area as well as within the Twin Lake neighborhood.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is more than 800 a day. The project will serve the general population/those who use this road and
    bridge.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit of this project will be safe transportation movement over new and repaired structures, and
    improved pedestrian safety/connectivity. This outcome will be measured based on the absence of any structural
    deficiencies after the project is completed, and the removal of the weight limitation imposed by regulatory agencies.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Revocation/withholding of funds.
