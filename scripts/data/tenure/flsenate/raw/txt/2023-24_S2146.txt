14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Riverfront Education Center will serve as a visitor & education center with exhibits for the residents & visitors of
    Miami-Dade County. The closest environmental education center is Biscayne Nature Center, which is 14-miles south on
    Key Biscayne. The closest others are 40-miles south at Biscayne & Everglades National Parks. The site will host
    community events, summer camps with scholarships for lower income students & lectures by scientists from FWC, DEP
    & state universities.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    Our Riverfront Education Center will serve as a place for the community to view manatees in the wild, learn about the
    Everglades, bird migrations, impacts to wildlife, as well as about the natural resources and diverse habitats of Florida.
    Residents will watch nature videos, view exhibits, engage in games, scientific lectures and more.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    School groups will be able to learn about the habitat and the importance of protecting it. Future generations of Floridians
    will be able to appreciate nature and be inspired to be active in conservation.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Elderly persons, economically disadvantaged persons, at-risk youth, physically disabled, preschool students, grade
    school students, high school students, university/college students.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Visitors will have improved physical health by walking the natural river walk to view manatees that is adjacent the terrace
    outside the education center. Include programming relating to the benefits of being in nature. This venue will enhance
    Miami-Dade County's growing reputation as a major tourist attraction in the field of the environment. Track where visitors
    come from and why they chose to visit through surveys. Students in different grade levels will be able to learn about the
    environment in their own community. Track number of students visiting and the programming they receive. Visitors will
    learn about the diverse habitats and species of Florida as well as ways to enhance and preserve Florida's natural
    resources. Track programming for visitors. Residents of Miami-Dade County that have an interest in the environment may
    choose to gain employment (full-time, part-time, volunteer, intern) with Pelican Harbor and improve their
    knowledge/professional skills. Track new employees.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

    If deliverables are not met (i.e. construction of a native wildlife hospital and environmental education center) the FDEP
    will issue or reclaim the funds.
