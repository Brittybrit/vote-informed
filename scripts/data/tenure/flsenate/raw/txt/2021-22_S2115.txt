12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     New World School of the Arts was created by the State of Florida Legislature as a center of excellence in the academic
    and arts curriculum, providing residents with focused professional arts training in dance, music, theater and visual arts.
    NWSA is a unique institution in the State of Florida providing an intensive conservatory oriented program. It is a
    partnership of Miami-Dade County Public Schools, Miami Dade College and the University of Florida, through which the
    High School Diploma, Associate of Arts and Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Fine Arts degrees are granted. Since
    inception NWSA has received direct line item funding from the State of Florida Legislature. The target population served
    by this project is High School students’ grades, 9-12, College Students freshman -senior year.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    New World School of the Arts offers a high quality and extensive academic and arts curriculum encompassing High
    School, College and University level programs in four major arts forms—visual arts, dance, music and theater. NWSA
    Faculty teach high school arts classes not funded by FEFP.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     • A minimum of 98% of high school student will graduate.
    • 80% or higher of high school students will graduate with honors.
    • 50% or higher of college students to graduate with honors.
    • 90% of high school graduating students expected to enroll in post-secondary education.
    • Minimum of 2 guest artists per division to support student learning.
    • Minimum of 5 external awards and honors by students.
    • Minimum of 5 invitations to national or international competitions and/or conferences.
    • 80% of all 9th and 10th grade students will score a satisfactory level (3) or higher on the Florida Standard Assessment,
    English Language Arts test.
    An average of 35% of students will score a satisfactory level (3) or higher on the Florida Standard Assessment, algebra 1,
    algebra 2, and geometry.
    • 60% of high school students will enroll in at least one accelerated mechanism (AP or DE)during their high school career.
