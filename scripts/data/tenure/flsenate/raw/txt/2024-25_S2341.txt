4. Project/Program Description
    The Mitzvah Kitchen is partnering with the Chabad of Kendall and Pinecrest and Friendship Circle of Miami, along with
   other businesses and community organizations that provide services to meet the needs of our community experiencing
   food insecurities with and without kosher restrictions. The kitchen will operate with the help of children with disabilities who
   can learn and obtain certifications for on-the-job skills.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Mitzvah Kitchen program will impart practical culinary skills for individuals with special needs while also promoting
    personal growth, community engagement, long-term independence, training and certifications to enter the workforce, and
    fostering inclusivity within the local business community and through partnerships with organizations meeting the needs
    of our local food insecurity.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The Mitzvah Kitchen program will impart practical culinary skills for indivdiuals with special needs while also promoting
    personal growth, community engagement, long-term independence, training and certifications to enter the workforce, and
    fostering inclusivity within the local business community and through partnerships with organizations meeting the needs
    of our local food insecurity with a focus on the jewish community
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The Mitzvah Kitchen provides the only community-run, industrial kosher kitchen in Miami-Dade area that engages
    individuals with special needs and volunteers from various businesses who prepare and distribute restaurant-quality
    meals to individuals and families (Jewish and non-Jewish) experiencing food insecurity as it relates to kosher restrictions.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Children with disabilities, the religious jewish community and community at large.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Working in a kosher kitchen within a Jewish community center offers cultural enrichment, promoting tolerance and
    diversity, while enhancing culinary skills and understanding. Outcomes will be assessed through community involvement,
    peer and community feedback, participant reflection surveys, and cultural tolerance surveys.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Funds returned in full.
