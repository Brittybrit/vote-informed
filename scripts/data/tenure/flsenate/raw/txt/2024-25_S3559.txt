4. Project/Program Description
    Miami-Dade County Public Schools is developing an additional school choice opportunity, the first of its kind in the district.
   This program provides students with a classical education curriculum, centered around the three fundamental stages of the
   classical model: grammar, dialectic, and rhetoric. To ensure the successful transformation of a traditional public school
   model to a classical education school model, M-DCPS has contracted with the Hamilton Classical and Civic Education
   Center at the University of Florida to work closely with our District staff and school personnel, providing professional
   development, staff support, instructional materials, curriculum, and school branding/advertising.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds requested are intended to transform a traditional public school into a classical education school. These funds
    will cover various expenses, including professional development, teacher compensation for specialized courses,
    instructional materials, and promotional efforts to brand and advertise the classical education model.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funds will be used to provide specific activities and services essential for achieving our intended purpose. These
    include professional development opportunities, curriculum development, and the provision of additional resources. The
    University of Florida's Hamilton Classical and Civic Education Center will oversee these services to support our district
    staff and school personnel in implementing the classical education school model. Ultimately, students will receive
    instruction through the classical education model.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The appropriation project directly benefits students by offering an additional choice in learning opportunities within Miami-
    Dade County Public Schools. Thus, M-DCPS would afford families with a school-wide choice option. It facilitates the
    transformation of a traditional public school into a classical education school, marking the first schoolwide choice program
    of its kind in the district. This means that parents and students will have access to an alternative educational model, and
    in turn will expand school choice options. Students enrolled in the Classical Education school will receive a
    comprehensive education that includes courses in literature, grammar, reading, math, foreign language, science, social
    studies, art, and music. This program is designed to help students build a strong foundation in liberal arts, critical thinking,
    and character development, providing them with a unique and enriching educational experience.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The project's target population encompasses 350 students ranging from kindergarten through 5th grade. These students
    are primarily those who face economic disadvantages and exhibit lower proficiency rates compared to similar nearby
    schools. The project aims to serve and support students to enhance their educational opportunities and outcomes.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     To measure the outcome, the project will employ a methodology that includes assessing students' knowledge and
    understanding of essential knowledge and cultural literacy components. Additionally, proficiency levels in various subjects
    will be closely monitored using tools such as the “FAST” Florida State assessment to gauge the project's impact. The
    interdisciplinary learning and real-world applications integrated into the program will contribute to students' deeper
    understanding, preparing them for academic success.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in revocation of funding.
