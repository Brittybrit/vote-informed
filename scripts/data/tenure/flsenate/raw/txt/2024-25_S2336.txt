     The Village of Palmetto Bay is known as the "Village of Parks". The Village seeks funding to assist in the development of
    a multi-generational public Veterans Park. Veterans Park has been designed to be an uplifting and interactive park that
    honors individuals who have served the United States in the military. The park will include educational components,
    public art, gathering areas, a playground with military themes, and Gold Star Family memorial walls. The Veterans Park
    has been identified in the Village's 2023 Parks Master Plan. Furthermore, the Village Council has allocated funds based
    on the construction documents prepared by a consultant.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    The park will serve as a gathering place for all citizens and visitors to the area. The purpose of the park is to honor US
    Veterans. The park will provide areas for exercise, education, and family gatherings.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The creation of a new park in the central area of Palmetto Bay will create more opportunities for safe gathering spaces,
    reintroduction of natural areas, and further create goodwill towards veterans in the community. Veterans Park will include
    the introduction of sidewalk improvements and a crosswalk. These improvements reflect the Village's commitment to
    micromobility and alternative transportation methods. The park is also located within a less than five-minute walk from the
    South Dade Busway.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The Palmetto Bay Veterans Park will service all local and regional residents. Specifically, veterans and their families will
    find the design and programming to place to celebrate their service in the armed forces. Veterans Park will serve as
    location that is simultaneously interactive and contemplative. In 2021, the US Government Accountability Office reported
    the number of veterans receiving mental health care from the Department of Veterans Affairs increased 90% from FY
    2006-2019. It is the intention that this public park celebrate those who served the country in a positive environment
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Veterans Park will provide opportunities for individuals, groups, and families to participate in low-impact healthy
    movement through the design of walkways and a boardwalk. A play area will be built for children. The intent of the play
    area is to mimic a training obstacle course. Additionally, the Village of Palmetto Bay has a robust public art program. The
    design of Veterans Park already contemplates the inclusion of public art. Veterans Park will include informational stations
    that promote the US branches of military and US history. Opportunities will exist for events and school-age children visits.
    he Village routinely conducts surveys of park users and solicits feedback through social media, committees, and one-on-
    one interaction
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The contracting agency shall withhold funds or seek reimbursement.
