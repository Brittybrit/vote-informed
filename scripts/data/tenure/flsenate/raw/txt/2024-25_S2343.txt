4. Project/Program Description
    It has been demonstrated that land uses surrounding the Goulds Canal have been a source of pollution to Biscayne Bay &
   Biscayne National Park. The primary concern has been a landfill immediately adjacent to Goulds Canal. New pumps as
   part of USACE Everglades project are slated to pump this nutrient-laden water into wetlands. The County is requesting
   funding to implement & manage the project (including construction engineering & inspections) of this backfilling project.

     It has been demonstrated that land uses surrounding the Goulds Canal have been a source of pollution to Biscayne Bay
    & Biscayne National Park. The primary concern has been a landfill immediately adjacent to Goulds Canal. New pumps as
    part of USACE Everglades project are slated to pump this nutrient-laden water into wetlands. The County is requesting
    funding to implement & manage the project (including construction engineering & inspections) of this backfilling project.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Design, Permitting, Testing, and Survey; Project Management, Engineering, Inspections; Construction.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The limiting of pollutants from entering into Biscayne Bay and Biscayne National Park.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The entire County.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project seeks funding for the initial component of the backfilling project which will directly limit pollution from being
    conveyed to Biscayne Bay and Biscayne National Park, adjacent to underserved communities that are some of the most
    vulnerable to the effects of climate change and sea level rise. Additionally, vegetation will be planted on the plug,
    providing coastal habitat enhancement.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failing to meet deliverables or performance measures should result in withholding funds in direct correlation to the tasks
    not delivered or where performance measures were not satisfied.
