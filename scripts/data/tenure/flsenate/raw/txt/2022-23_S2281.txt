1. Project Title          Crandon Boulevard Intersection Improvements - Village of Key Biscayne

2. Senate Sponsor        Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request       10/20/2021

4. Project/Program Description
    The specific purpose of this project is to improve roadway level of service and provide a significant reduction in possible
   traffic fatalities and serious injuries on Crandon Boulevard which is a historically designated public road. A safe, secure,
   and reliable accessway will increase economic activity and tourism for Bill Baggs Cape Florida State Park and hotels in the
   Village of Key Biscayne, which collectively receive approximately 950,000 visitors annually.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to improve roadway level of service and provide a significant reduction in possible traffic fatalities and serious
    injuries while increasing economic activity and tourism.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Physical improvements to the roadway, sidewalks, bike lanes, pedestrian crossings, and traffic signals.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     A safe, secure, and reliable facility for motorists, bicyclists, and pedestrians.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All residents and visitors of the State of Florida will be served by this project. Approximately 950,000 visitors and 15,000
    village residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Increased level of service, safety, tourism, and economic activity in terms of sales tax. Level of service and safety will be
    measured by traffic counts and analysis. Tourism will be measured by annual visitor numbers, and economic activity will
    be measured by sales tax revenues.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

13. The owners of the facility to receive, directly or indirectly, any fixed capital outlay funding. Include the
    relationship between the owners of the facility and the entity.

     The roadway is owned and maintained by Miami-Dade County. Like the Village of Key Biscayne's other complete streets
    projects that are owned by Miami-Dade County, the Village of Key Biscayne completes the engineering and construction
    of the project and supplements Miami-Dade County's maintenance operations plan. This includes landscaping, street
    sweeping, sidewalk inspections and repairs, and general upkeep.
