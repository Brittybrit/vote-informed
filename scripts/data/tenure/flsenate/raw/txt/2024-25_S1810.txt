4. Project/Program Description
    Miami-Dade County’s Department of Transportation and Public Works (DTPW) Vision Zero goal is to end traffic fatalities
   and serious injuries by 2040, while increasing safe, reliable, sustainable, and equitable mobility for all. Grand Avenue and
   Douglas Road, within the City of Miami, were identified in the 2021 Vision Zero Framework Plan as high-injury crash
   locations. This project seeks the construction of roadway safety improvements including the installation of high-visibility
   crosswalks, rapid flashing beacons, intersection medians and other traffic calming measures such as concrete bulb-outs,
   upgrade of curb ramps and transit stop facilities, and upgrade of existing traffic signal countdown timers and pedestrian
   push buttons. These safety countermeasures are aimed to eliminate the number of fatalities and serious injuries taking
   place on these two corridors.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to design and construct identified safety countermeasures. These safety countermeasures are aimed to
    eliminate the number of fatalities and serious injuries taking place on these corridors. This project seeks the construction
    of safety improvements including the installation of high-visibility crosswalks, rapid flashing beacons, intersection medians
    and other traffic calming measures such as concrete bulb-outs, upgrade of curb ramps and transit stop facilities, and
    upgrade of existing traffic signal countdown timers and pedestrian push buttons.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     All of the state appropriated funds would go towards the construction of the safety improvements. These improvements
    could save lives and will enhance the safety and quality of life for the residents and businesses adjacent and passing
    through these two corridors.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The construction of these infrastructure projects will provide the area with a safer environment for bicyclists, pedestrians,
    drivers and local residents with high visibility crossings, walking areas, and safe access to transit. These projects will
    improve mental and physical health and promote a sense of community, as well as create a safe network for all modes of
    transportation.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Residents and businesses along Grand Avenue and Douglas Road are the target population. These safety
    countermeasures are aimed to eliminate the number of fatalities and serious injuries taking place on these corridors near
    schools, businesses, and residential neighborhoods. Grand Avenue and Douglas Road are also identified as part of the
    Top 100 Vision Zero locations across Miami-Dade County.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The main benefit of this project is to eliminate fatalities along these corridors. Other direct benefits of these projects
    include reducing high-injury crashes in main residential and commercial roadways, improved safety for all modes, and an
    enhanced quality of life for Miami residents by providing safer streets. Improvements in physical and mental health are
    also likely outcomes through the creation of a safe transportation system to reduce vehicle dependency, promote walk or
    bike to schools, work, local shops, and restaurants; all while fostering economic development in the area. This will also
    result in a more active lifestyle which reduces the risk of other health complications including diabetes, cardiovascular
    disease, high blood pressure and contributes to improved mental health. The methods used for measuring the outcome
    consist of analyzing before and after traffic/crash data, tracking construction jobs, and tracking transit, pedestrian and
    bike usage/counts.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
