14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     A PASER assessment was completed to help identify the conditions of each street located within City limits. Additional
    funds provided would supplement the City's funding for milling and resurfacing roadways citywide that have been
    prioritized based on the findings of the completed assessment.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     City will retain the services of a licensed Contractor to mill and resurface roadways citywide.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Improves the conditions of citywide streets that provide access daily to residents, business owners, and visitors. Will help
    maintain traffic flow and provide uninterrupted access to critical public services such as emergency fire and police.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All roadways within the City limits provide access daily to residents, business owners, and visitors. Approximately 49,000
    residents and all those that travel through the City to reach other destinations throughout Miami-Dade County.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will

     Improves the conditions of citywide streets that provide access daily to residents, business owners, and visitors. Will help

    Improve current rating provided by PASER assessment.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties

     The City will include liquidated damages per calendar day in the contract documents to address delays.
