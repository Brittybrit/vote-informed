1. Project Title          Kristi House - Renovations to Main Building for UM Child Protection Team

2. Senate Sponsor        Ileana Garcia

3. Date of Request       11/29/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Kristi House is requesting funds to reconfigure and renovate its nearly 18,000 square foot main facility to allow its
   multidisciplinary partner, the UM Child Protection Team, to co-locate within Kristi House. The request will enable Kristi
   House, the nationally accredited state designated Children's Advocacy Center for Miami-Dade County, to better serve the
   children who are survivors of sexual abuse, sex trafficking, and other forms of trauma. Having the agencies co-locate will
   allow children to get necessary services in a single location, thereby minimizing re-traumatization.

    other specialized interviews, mental health treatment, and advocacy services. Having all of this available in a single
    location limits additional trauma and and makes the services more accessible to families. Kristi House currently provides
    mental health services, case coordination, and advocacy for children who have experienced trauma, specifically sexual
    abuse and other forms of abuse. Section 39.303, F.S., requires DOH and DCF to utilize child protection teams in each of
    the service circuits of DCF. Miami's historically has been housed within the University of Miami, which is no longer able to
    provide geographically desirable space. Thus, relocation to Kristi House is necessary to allow Miami's child protection
    team to fulfill its statutory d
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The facility will allow children who are victims of sexual abuse, physical abuse and other types of trauma to come to one
    location to receive the vast majority of services they need, including forensic medical exams, forensic interviews, other
    specialized interviews, mental health treatment, and advocacy services. Having all of this available in a single location
    limits any additional trauma.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Children will be able to undergo forensic medical exams for sexual abuse, physical abuse, and other types of abuse at
    Kristi House's facility from the specialty trained physicians and nurses at the Child Protection Team. Additionally, all
    forensic interviews will be conducted at Kristi House in a child-friendly and calm environment to minimize further trauma.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     There are several target populations, including at-risk youth, students of all grade levels from preschool through high
    school, and child victims of crime. Currently, the Children's Advocacy Center sees approximately 2,000 children and
    families per year. This number is expected to increase if the UM Child Protection Team is co-located within Kristi House.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     It is expected that child victims of sexual abuse, sex trafficking, physical abuse, and other types of trauma will see
    improvements in physical health and mental health. Creating this redesigned space in Kristi House where forensic
    examinations and interviews can be performed will ensure that the Miami-based child protection team has a fully
    resourced and safe space for these evaluations, thus providing the best care to meet the needs of children's physical
    well-being. Also, co-location of Kristi House and CPT will minimize the stress associated with the required evaluations
    and optimize the children's mental health by surrounding these victims with Kristi House's mental health resources.
    Outcomes can be tracked using evidence-based assessments, the DOH databese, the Kristi House database, and
    surveys of clients and caregivers.

    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?
