12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The ultimate goal of this initiative is to improve roadway conditions and implement traffic calming improvements that will
    enhance the quality of life of all residents in the City of Miami. These improvements will alleviate current traffic and safety
    issues at multiple locations.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funds will be used to continue improving traffic safety in the city neighborhoods by performing the necessary design
    and construction of roadway improvements and providing traffic calming devices.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The project will provide residents with increased safety in their neighborhood. The residents' experience will include a
    reduction of speed, enhanced safety for pedestrians and bicyclists, and a better quality of life overall. No direct services
    as this is a roadway project.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     City of Miami residents and visitors.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The ultimate goal of this initiative is to implement roadway improvements that will enhance the quality of life of all
    residents in the City of Miami. These improvements will alleviate current traffic and safety issues at multiple locations. The
    outcome will be measured by: 1) Project completion; and 2) Reduction in the number of pedestrian/vehicular accidents
    caused by drivers exceeding speed limits.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The city will be subject to an audit. Standard contract penalties will be sufficient.
