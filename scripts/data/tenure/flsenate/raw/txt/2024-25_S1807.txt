4. Project/Program Description
    Miami-Dade County’s Department of Transportation and Public Works (DTPW) Vision Zero goal is to end traffic fatalities
   and serious injuries by 2040, while increasing safe, reliable, sustainable, and equitable mobility for all. A segment of SW
   152nd St. and a segment of 184 St. were identified in the 2021 Vision Zero Framework Plan as a high-injury vehicle crash
   location where residents do not feel safe walking, biking, or driving. This project seeks the construction of roadway safety
   improvements including installation of high-visibility crosswalks, upgrade of curb ramps, installation of retro-reflective
   borders on signal heads, upgrades to existing traffic signal countdown timers and pedestrian push buttons. These safety
   countermeasures are aimed to eliminate the number of fatalities and serious injuries taking place on this location near
   schools, businesses, and residential neighborhoods and seeks to improve the quality of life and economic vitality of these
   neighborhoods.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to engineer, design and construct the identified safety countermeasures. These safety countermeasures are
    aimed to eliminate the number of fatalities and serious injuries taking place at these locations near schools, etc. This
    project seeks the construction of safety improvements including installation of high-visibility crosswalks, upgrade of curb
    ramps, installation of retro-reflective borders on signal heads, upgrades to existing traffic signal countdown timers and
    pedestrian push buttons.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     All of the state appropriated funds would go towards the construction of the safety improvements. These improvements
    would save lives, eliminating fatalities and serious injuries taking place near schools, business, and residential
    neighborhoods. Long-term this will improve the quality of life and economic vitality of these neighborhoods.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The construction of this infrastructure project will provide the areas with a safer environment for bicyclists, pedestrians,
    drivers and local residents with high visibility crossings, walking areas, and secure access to transit. This project will
    improve mental and physical health and promote a sense of community, as well as create a safe network for all modes of
    transportation.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Local residents are the target population. This project funding request seeks to accomplish this by constructing safety
    countermeasures to eliminate the number of serious injuries taking place on this corridor located near schools,
    businesses, and residential neighborhoods. SW 152nd St. and SW 184th St. corridors have been identified as part of the
    Top 100 Vision Zero locations that need immediate safety infrastructure in place. Allocation of funds will expedite the
    construction of projects and address a major safety concern for Miami-Dade residents and visitors.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The main goal is to eliminate all fatalities along these corridors. Construction of this infrastructure project will provide the
    areas with a safer environment for bicyclist, pedestrians, and local residents with safer crossings and a walking area.
    Creating a safe transportation system can create a healthier lifestyle, promote the use of all modes of transportation.
    Implementing safe route to schools so that children who do not have vehicles can attend schools by walking or cycling.
    This results in a more active lifestyle which reduces the risk of other health complications including diabetes,
    cardiovascular disease, high blood pressure and contributes to improved mental health. Pre- and post-ped/bike counts,
    tracking of transit usage, and before/after implementation crash data analysis are ways we will measure the success of
    our countermeasures.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
