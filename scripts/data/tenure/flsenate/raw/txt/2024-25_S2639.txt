    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose of this request is to provide funding for FDLRS to provide professional learning/development statewide to
    include the technology, content and personnel to implement. To provide on-demand and fully accessible portal for all K-
    12 teachers in Florida to access high-quality professional development tools as well as an open, collaborative platform
    allowing for communication between teachers for professional development and best-practices. School districts in the
    state of Florida will be able to access professional development/learning courses online.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     These funds will be used to provide a technology platform and dedicated support team member that will work alongside
    the FDLRS team in the 2024-2025 school year to rollout this initiative statewide. Their job duties will include creating and
    executing a training and rollout plan, supporting content creation, organizing training sessions for school district staff,
    support with updating and maintaining the registration system, coaching professional development content creators on
    best practices, managing the timeline and completion of project tasks from start to finish.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     School district employees in the state of Florida will have equitable access to growth opportunities in the field of special
    education through professional development. FDLRS employees will be trained and supported through the creation,
    implementation and rollout phases in the 2024-25 school year. This project will provide direct, year-round professional
    development services to K-12 Florida teachers as well as a collaborative platform for teachers throughout the State to
    share in professional development and best-practices.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This project will serve all public-school employees taking courses through FDLRS. The target population is any State of
    Florida K-12 teacher. Upward of 185,000 (last official estimate posted by DOE) teachers have the possibility of being
    served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit to the overall quality of education and teacher preparedness is to provide for the first time in Florida
    an on-demand and fully accessible portal for professional development for Florida K-12 teachers via a state partner entity
    that is familiar to teachers and School Districts throughout Florida. This will be measured by course completion analytics,
    new development and delivery of professional learning and development courses & programs and overall teachers
    participating in professional development learning programs across the state of Florida.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Monetary fund's will be returned to the state.
