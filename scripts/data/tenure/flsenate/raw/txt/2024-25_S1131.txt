4. Project/Program Description
    The Women of Tomorrow Mentor & Scholarship Program is specifically designed to meet the needs of at-risk teenage girls
   in our state’s public high schools. The program has impacted more than 17,000 mentees in South Florida alone since its
   inception and continues to reach approximately 3,000 young women annually in more than 115 South Florida public
   schools with a network of 200+ professional women mentors. This year's funding would help launch an online channel of 10
   documentary-style videos and 100+ short-form videos to teach life skills, workforce development, academic resilience and
   how to stop negative behaviors. Available online, videos will broaden our reach from South Florida mentees to students
   statewide. By preparing high school students for college/vocational/technical school and ultimately the workforce, we are
   developing pathways out of poverty to ensure future economic success for both them and the state. Funding will also
   support phase 3 of our Alumnae Network expansion.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     We will launch a library/online channel of 10 educational videos and 100+ short videos that teach life skills, increase self-
    esteem, stop negative behaviors, and focus on academic resilience. These on-demand videos will allow us to broaden
    our reach from South Florida to the entire state. By preparing girls in grades 9-12 for higher education and ultimately the
    workforce, we are developing pathways out of poverty to ensure future economic success for both them and the state.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     A series of 10 educational videos on a variety of relevant topics will be hosted in a video library/channel that will allow
    girls in public high schools from grades 9-12 to watch on-demand. We will utilize social media to highlight the videos to
    increase engagement from this cohort.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Creation of a video library with curated content developed specifically to meet the interest and needs of at-risk young
    women in grades 6-12 as they navigate challenges, achieve academic success, prepare for college and the workforce,
    and discover career paths and opportunities.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Target population for this project is at-risk girls in grades 9-12 throughout the entire State of Florida.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Expected benefits include improved quality of education, improved physical and mental health, enriched cultural
    experiences, and protection from harm, physical dangers, diversion from criminal and juvenile justice system.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Unused funds would be returned to the State.
