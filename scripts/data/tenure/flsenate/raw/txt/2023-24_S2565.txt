4. Project/Program Description
    Training individuals to be an inspector for construction, engineering & inspection projects, which inspects and documents a
   project's progress. The Construction Inspector Trainee Program develops inspectors through classroom training,
   mentoring, and on-the-job experience. Those completing this program will have verified, highly desirable, and proficient
   skills as an Intern Construction Inspector and will be ready to transition into the construction industry.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To provide training for low to medium income residents in the Jacksonville area, give a second chance to ex-offenders,
    homeless and the underserved in our communities. In addition, the intent of the program is to provide self-worth and role
    models to the community and possible future employment opportunities, and more candidates to the construction
    industry.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     We will provide instructors that have many years of experience. Trainees will have on the job training. To start this
    program, we are looking at nine inspector trainees from the local community, enlisted in a 16-week program and each
    trainee will be paid $17.75 an hour for 16 weeks.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     An opportunity to widen their skill set or acquire a new one. Provide jobs and future opportunities to enter the
    construction industry.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Low to medium income and underserved communities. To start this program, we are looking at nine inspector trainees
    from the Jacksonville community, enlisted in a 16-week program and each trainee will be paid $17.75 an hour for 16
    weeks.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Provide self-worth and role models to the community and future employment opportunities. Testing will be done at the
    end of program and possible employment opportunities. To provide more candidates to the construction field.

     This program can't be measured as other programs, as we are looking to encourage everyone to try to grow
