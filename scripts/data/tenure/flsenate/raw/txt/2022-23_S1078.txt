12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Purchase of glass crusher machine will be used to produce granular materials from recycle glass.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Collection and delivery of recycle glass to the glass crusher facility for processing into usable materials will eliminate
    transportation costs to truck glass to landfills, which are located hundreds of miles from Key West. Greenhouse gas
    emissions will not be produced, when recycle glass is processed into usable fill materials using a regionally located glass
    crusher, and when fill materials are transported to local material yards from hundreds of miles away.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

    Glass recycle will be collected from residents and businesses, delivered to the glass crusher facility, converted into fill
    materials, and distributed to material yard for use in concrete, asphalt, and general fill material.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Residents and visitors to the City of Key West. 31,951 persons; based on 24,649 population (2010 US Census, Key
    West)
    and 7,302 average daily visitors to Key West.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Glass crusher will produce granular fill material, eliminate the transport of glass recycling to the materials recovery
    facility, approximately 150 miles from the transfer station, reduce transportation of fill materials to local material yards,
    and reduce greenhouse gas emissions. Less trucks use less fuel, wear less tires on the road, drip less oil, emit less
    greenhouse gas, which in turn improves nearshore water and air quality. Less large trucks entering, exiting and driving on
    the road that spans the Florida Keys will decrease vehicular accidents and injuries. Less trucks will create less congestion
    and ease traffic.
