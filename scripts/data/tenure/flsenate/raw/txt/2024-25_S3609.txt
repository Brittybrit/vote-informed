4. Project/Program Description
    The purpose of this project is to educate impoverished families in much needed topics related to parenting. In addition, the
   project aims to provide prevention and intervention strategies leading to healthy and productive lifestyles. Project will be
   conducted at all of our facilities located in East Little Havana and Hialeah. This is a one-year project that will provide weekly
   intervention services to those enrolled in the program. Intervention services will be conducted in person and/or virtually as
   needed. Services will be provided by licensed clinicians and Case Management staff. Services also include free childcare
   to families participating in the project. Mental Health is a current concern in our community, particularly amongst minority
   low-income families. Centro Mater is strongly committed to enhancing the lives of the families we serve and through the
   implementation of this project, we will be a step closer in attaining our vision.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose of this project is to provide educational and parenting prevention opportunities as well as intervention
    services to families in need. The goal of this project is designed to provide information, resources and services that will
    help empower individuals and families with new knowledge, beliefs and strategies to make good and healthy lifestyle
    choices. Project goals also include prevention-intervention programs which will be individualized for at risk families
    experiencing mild to moderate levels of distress. Any families needing long-term programs will be referred to by Social
    Services/Mental Health Professionals. Included in the project goals is to provide a specific component targeting fathers.
    This component with a focus to increase their knowledge and skills associated with their role. Lastly, in order to ensure
    families receive support and are able to take advantage of all available opportunities, Centro Mater will provide free
    childcare to all participating families.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Primary Activities: Short Term-Once a week sessions lasting 1 hour each for 12 weeks where the following topics will be
    covered: Prenatal Families, Proper nutrition and dangers of smoking, drinking and the use of legal and illegal substances
    while pregnant, ABC for parents, Parents & children with Special Needs & Health Challenges, Life altering disorders, Self-
    worth, Brain Development, Positive Discipline, Building empathy, anger management .
    Secondary Activities: These will be implemented only with at risk families presenting family dysfunction altering their
    home, work and school environment-intervention will be weekly for a period up to 20 sessions. The following sessions will
    be covered: Nurturing skills: These topics will be tailored-to Latino individuals concentrating on core competencies
    covering all the aspects of parenting in their home language. The subjects Alcohol, Anger and Abuse prevention will be
    integrated into the sessions.
    NEEDS ASSESSMENT
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     All families participating in this project will complete intake before entering the program and once enrolled, Social
    Services staff will ensure that all families complete a Family Assessment Questionnaire twice within the program year.
    With the data collected and in conjunction with families, the Social Service staff establishes a Family Partnership which
    identifies family goals as well as needs which are addressed with information, referrals and interventions services. Staff
    will provide ongoing monthly follow-up to support attainment of goals and address identified needs, as well as to assess
    the overall health and wellness of the family dynamics. Licensed professionals will provide special educational
    opportunities as well as small group sessions covering topics as previously noted. Services also include weekly
    psychosocial groups, available in person and virtually. In addition, Centro Mater will offer free childcare while family
    members are receiving services.

    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcome of this project is that families will attain knowledge on Mental Health Illnesses and Wellness as
    well as Family Planning. Centro Mater will use several methods to measure the outcomes for this project. Families
    attending the educational opportunities will receive a pre and post assessments to measure the knowledge gained.
    Established Family Partnerships, not only will address goals and needs, but strategies to achieve improved family
    relationships, healthy family dynamics and stability. The Social Services team will collect and utilize it to determine the
    number of families: with identified needs, provided resources, receiving services, and attaining set goals in order to
    measure project benefits and ensure attainment of determined outcome. Data will also be used to ensure efficacy of this
    project and families are receiving the services needed to attain positive outcomes as intended by this project.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Centro Mater has on-going internal controls to monitor contract compliance and address any deficiencies or areas of
    improvement. Monitoring controls include review of data monthly and meets with key staff involved with the project to
    identify areas meeting predetermined outcomes and any areas not meeting benchmarks. If there are any areas identified
    as needing improvement, Centro Mater team works together to develop and implement an improvement plan to address
    the shortcoming and ensure compliance.
