1. Project Title         Rapid DNA Technology

2. Senate Sponsor        Blaise Ingoglia

3. Date of Request       02/20/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    The funds will allow 11 county sheriff’s offices (Hernando, Baker, Santa Rosa, Martin, Manatee, Flagler, Charlotte,
   Nassau, Clay, Hardee, and Leon) to each purchase a Rapid DNA machine to install at their jail booking station. The use of
   a Rapid DNA machine will allow law enforcement to collect the DNA of a qualifying arrestee during the jail booking process
   and have the DNA sample cross-referenced with CODIS and results returned while an arrestee in still in custody
   (approximately 90 minutes).

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will allow 11 sheriff’s offices to purchase a Rapid DNA machine to install at their jail booking station. The use
    of Rapid DNA machines will significantly reduce the amount of time it takes law enforcement to generate a DNA profile
    from an qualifying arrestee and cross-reference it with CODIS. This will allow law enforcement to determine whether the
    arrestee’s DNA is a match to any unsolved crimes while the arrestee is still in custody.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The funds will assist law enforcement in determining whether a qualifying arrestee's DNA is a match to any unsolved
    crimes while that person is still in custody (within 90 minutes).
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The funds will allow law enforcement to determine whether a qualifying arrestee's DNA is a match to any unsolved
    crimes before that person is released back into the community. This will enhance public safety and reduce costs to both
    law enforcement agencies and the citizens they serve.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     All citizens.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

    Protect the general public from harm from criminals. The benefit can be measured based on the number of arrestees
    whose DNA is a match to an unsolved crime(s) and who are not released back into the community as a result.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Discontinuation of the program.
