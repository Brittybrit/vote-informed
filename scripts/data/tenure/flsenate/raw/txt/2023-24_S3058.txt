4. Project/Program Description
    During the disaster at Surfside 2022, significant deficiencies were identified with Miami-Dade Fire Rescue's (MDFR) mobile
   command post, a 1995 Blue Bird Bus. This mobile command post is often utilized and recently responded to the RedAir
   crash at Miami International Airport where it supported multiple agencies, such as MDFR, Miami-Dade Police Department
   (MDPD), and National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) for several days. Due to the age of the vehicle and its
   components, intermittent failures occurred as exhibited when the 12V electrical system that operates radios and
   environmental control systems malfunctioned. During large high-profile events, several agencies often respond and need to
   operate cohesively. This project aims to provide MDFR and supporting emergency-response agencies with a state of art
   mobile command post to be utilized during emergencies, and in turn, provide citizens with a more efficient and structured
   emergency response from first-responders.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project will provide a shared resource for various emergency response and command staff teams in the surrounding
    area. For instance, MDFR often provides command post and logistical support to local law enforcement who may be the
    leading authority in a given event. It will also supply a sturdy shelter, meeting area, and temperature-controlled
    environment for staff members to utilize during an emergency.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

    This project will better assist citizens of Miami-Dade County, and surrounding areas as needed, during an emergency by
    MDFR and other local first-responder agencies.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Assisting citizens by providing a more easily accessible, logical, and modern mobile command post for first responder
    command staff during an emergency. It will provide shelter from the elements, a meeting place for all staff members to
    gather and discuss useful courses of action, a workspace for command staff at incident site(s), and a clear hub for
    sharing all pertinent information for emergency response teams. This will, in turn, assist citizens by offering a safe and
    logical emergency response experience, as well as a unified command center for tackling crises.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Miami-Dade County, its outlying communities, and other surrounding areas, which is approximately 2.7 million residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     This project is the evolution of command-and-control infrastructure. It cannot be denied that every incident requires an
    Incident Commander (IC), and as the incident grows the IC needs a command staff to fulfill tasks as defined by the
    National Incident Management Systems Incident Command System. The support branches under the IC provide valuable
    assistance to the IC, but may only do so when presented with the same intelligence. This can only happen when branch
    supervisors are near the IC. During incidents, the mobile command post provides the shelter, proximity, and shared
    resources to accomplish the missions set forth by the IC. As the incident grows, space is needed to accommodate the
    additional command staff. The electronic resources needed to make timely and effective decisions are most easily made
    available in a temperature-controlled, state of the art mobile command vehicle. Although this request is for a state of art
    mobile command post, the mission it supports is timeless.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Nonpayment until deliverables are met.
