12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The goal is to reach women in under resourced communities by providing free pregnancy testing, limited ultrasounds,
    community referrals, online educational prenatal and parenting lessons. This lowers infant mortality and abortion rates.
    These free services allow moms and their babies a healthy start and provides women with the resources they lack to
    provide for their newborn infants. The Mobile Medical unit will go into local neighborhoods to give women acess to
    prenatal and pregnancy care.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Medical grade pregnancy tests to determine pregnancy; limited ultrasounds to determine viability of pregnancy and
    gestational age; community referrals to healthcare agencies, such as Healthy Start, WIC, Dept. Children & Families,
    clinics, shelters, Financial Assistance programs; online prenatal and parenting lessons (over 160 lessons available
    including pregnancy, parenting and life skills classes).
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Medical grade pregnancy tests to determine pregnancy; limited ultrasounds to determine viability of pregnancy and
    gestational age; community referrals to healthcare agencies, such as Healthy Start, WIC, Dept. Children & Families,
    clinics, shelters, Financial Assistance programs; online prenatal and parenting lessons (over 160 lessons available
    including pregnancy, parenting and life skills classes).
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Jobless persons, at risk youth, Economically disadvantaged persons, Homeless, Grade school students, High School
    students, University/College Students.
