                          Miami-Dade Police

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       12/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    MDPD Real-Time Crime Center (RTCC) is responsible for the coordination and distribution of real-time information to
   police officers and investigators based upon an aggregated search with immediate analysis of available data. The RTCC
   operates 24/7. The addition of a state-of-the-art cloud system will enhance operations allowing video information sharing
   with the Emergency Operations Center, Tactical Response Teams, and officers, expanding investigative capabilities
   exponentially.

     It creates a public safety ecosystem that combines video with other utilities like CAD data, gunfire detectors, real-time
    officer geolocator feeds, a registry map of all the public and private cameras in the region, a multi-media tips line for the
    public, and a digital evidence vault for investigators. It enables law enforcement and public safety agencies to operate
    more efficiently, improved operational intelligence, and with a proactive emphasis on officer, citizen, and community
    safety.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Upgraded cloud software creates a public safety ecosystem that creates a public safety ecosystem that combines video
    with other utilities like CAD data, gunfire detectors, real-time officer geolocator feeds, a registry map of all the public and
    private cameras in the region, a multi-media tips line for the public, and a digital evidence vault for investigators.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     If funding is not provided, the MDPD will be limited in its response and efforts to implement sustainable initiatives to
    reduce the crime plaguing our communities. Funding will allow for a comprehensive Countywide strategy to combat
    community violence, along with the underlying causes and profound aftermath, to provide long-term solutions and
    improved quality of life to Miami-Dade County residents.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The entire Miami-Dade County population of 2.8 million people
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Reduction in crime and quicker response time for dispatch of police services. Metrics will include statistics on incidents of
    violent crime, including but not limited to homicides by firearm, contact shootings and non-contact shootings.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
