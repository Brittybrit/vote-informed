12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The Read to Lead Program is an incentive based reading initiative developed in 2001 by the late Honorable Robert B.
    Ingram, PhD., member Miami Dade County School Board. Ever since its inception over 10,000 students from Miami-Dade
    County Public Schools have benefited from the reading emersion program.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Educational and tutorial services/excursions for approximately ninety elementary school students.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Awareness campaign for identified Miami Gardens Elementary School and tutorial support . And excursion opportunities
    for approximately ninety students.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     At-risk youth and grade school students (50-100 students).
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

    Improvement in grade report and teacher evaluation. Exposure to new content (or historical destinations) in the State of
    Florida.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Recoupment of funds and forfeiture of future grant opportunities.
