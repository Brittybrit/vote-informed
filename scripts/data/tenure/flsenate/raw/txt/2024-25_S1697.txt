3. Date of Request       12/01/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Miami-Dade Fire Rescue (MDFR) seeks to purchase one (1) new technical rescue telehandler. This project aims to
   replace Miami-Dade Fire Rescue's existing 18-year-old Technical Rescue telehandler. The current equipment is in constant
   need of repair, costing the department additional funds to maintain its operational use. A new telehandler will allow MDFR
   to respond to technical rescue calls at emergency scenes requiring the relocation of large-scale vehicles, equipment, and
   concrete structures. The goal of this project is to provide reliable equipment for technical rescues.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     This project aims to replace Miami-Dade Fire Rescue's (MDFR) existing 18-year-old Technical Rescue telehandler. The
    current equipment is in constant need of repair, costing the department additional funds to maintain its operational use. A
    new telehandler will allow MDFR to respond to technical rescue calls at emergency scenes requiring the relocation of
    large-scale vehicles, equipment, and concrete structures. The goal of this project is to provide reliable equipment for
    technical rescues.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     The purpose of this project is to provide reliable equipment for technical rescue. A functioning telehandler will be ready
    and available for use during emergencies, whereas the current equipment is frequently unavailable due to service-related
    needs. The proposed equipment has a greater carrying capacity of 10,000-15,000 pounds instead of the 8,000-pound
    maximum on the existing equipment.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Citizens of Miami-Dade County will benefit from this project as it will provide reliable equipment during structural collapse
    events where heavy concrete must be moved for search and rescue efforts as experienced during the Surfside Building
    Collapse in 2021. Emergency teams will be able to provide more thorough rescue efforts and save lives by locating
    people trapped in confined spaces.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     This project will serve all communities of Miami-Dade County and the region. Miami-Dade County has about 2.7 million
    residents.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     By replacing the existing telehandler with one that is more reliable and has a greater carrying capacity, first responders
    can perform technical search and rescue operations in a safer manner and with a clear mind. A telehandler performs
    tasks that would otherwise require a team of fire personnel. With the assistance of a new telehandler, first responders are
    able to streamline and reduce the manual labor which is costly, time-consuming, and minimizes the risk of accidents and
    injuries.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in nonpayment.
