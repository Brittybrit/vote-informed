    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     A Non-profit Community Organization assisting student athletics with scholarships for a higher education. To Support the
    Cheeseborough Invitational Track & Field Event held annually. Expenses for employee, officials, timekeeper, meals for
    volunteers and workers for the event, tee shirts, trophies, photographer, security and purchase new or repair equipment
    used in the events.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     As a Non-Profit, communicating with the community the availability of scholarship funds from donations and funds from
    CSE Inc. We want to reach families with and without current are future student athletics that may be struggling with
    limited resources. Make donations to 10-20 families during the holidays.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     As a Non-profit communicating to help purchase uniforms for student athletics, award scholarships, and funds to
    promote the Cheeseborough Invitational Track and Field Annual event involving local and surrounding counties schools.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Economically disadvantaged persons, at-risk-youth, grade school students, high school students, university/college
    students. Our target population outreach is expected to serve 400-800.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     1. Improve physical health - promoting physical fitness, running, exercise- by determining the physical ability of each
    athlete in their appropriate event.
    2. Enrich cultural experience - socializing with students from different cultures, teaching healthy honest competing skills
    and promoting enhance self- esteem in competition and socializing, meet and greet, teacher and coach one-on-one and
    encouragement training, and getting out into the community, noting that boarding one's education can offer greater
    opportunities in self, group, and social development.
    3. Improve quality of education- Providing scholarships for high education - this helps and benefits families with low
    income and our at risk students with some of the expenses of college tuition.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     The organization should be allowed to correct its shortfalls and given a deadline to correct the reasons they are failing to
    meet performance measures. If not corrected within the deadline requested by the state, then they should discontinue
    appropriating funds until all guidelines are administered correctly.
