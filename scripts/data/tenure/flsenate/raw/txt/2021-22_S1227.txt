4. Project/Program Description

   These units include a combination of home ownership and rentals and will provide critically needed affordable housing to
   the Upper Keys workforce, helping to replace housing stock lost in Hurricane Irma.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    Funds will be used to complete construction of 20 affordable housing units located on two sites in the upper Florida
    Keys.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     These units include a combination of home ownership and rentals and will provide critically needed affordable housing in
    the Upper Keys workforce, helping to replace the units lost as a result of Hurricane Irma.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The availability of 20 units of affordable housing, including a combination of ownership and rental opportunities with close
    proximity to places of employment.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Monroe County workforce, including economically disadvantaged persons/families. A minimum of 20 families will be
    served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The addition of 20 units of affordable housing will provide critically needed housing options for the local workforce,
    improving transportation conditions, increasing and improving economic activity, and enhancing economic self-sufficiency
    by providing housing options in proximity to places of employment. Rental and ownership units successfully occupied by
    Monroe County's workforce will be the measure of success of the project.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?
