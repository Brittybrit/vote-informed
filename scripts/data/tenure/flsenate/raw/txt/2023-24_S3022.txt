1. Project Title          Feeding South Florida Family Sustenance Inflation Mitigation Program

2. Senate Sponsor         Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request        03/03/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    With the cost of food, housing, and fuel continuing to rise, healthy food is out of reach for many working families in South
   Florida, especially Miami-Dade County. Feeding South Florida will work to provide immediate access to nutritious food,
   educate families about temporary benefits, and enroll individuals in Feeding South Florida workforce training programs to
   stabilize the family unit and help families move from a place of needing assistance to economic self-sufficiency.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

    The purpose of the funds are to invest in our community by mitigating or eliminating barriers to healthy eating and
    moving families from a place of needing assistance to a place of economic self-sufficiency.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Feeding South Florida will work to stabilize a family unit or household helping to build a solid foundation of support that
    includes distribution of healthy food and meals, assisting families with benefits, enrolling families and individuals in
    Feeding South Florida's workforce training programs to help families achieve economic self-sufficiency.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Feeding South Florida will provide direct distribution of food and other grocery items; application assistance to citizens
    who may be potentially eligible for benefits; as well as hands-on workforce development and soft skill training for all
    individuals interested in making themselves more marketable in the workforce and gaining employment with a living
    wage.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Feeding South Florida will focus its efforts on families who are low income and have a combined household income of
    less than 185% of the federal poverty line. The goal is to ensure government assistance is temporary and families work
    on becoming self-sufficient. The project estimates that 10,000 families will be served.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected benefit is more families and individuals will become economically self-sufficient. This will help students
    increase performance, behavior and attendance at school, as well as help working adults be more productive and reduce
    sick time and accidents in the workplace.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Feeding South Florida will work on a reimbursement basis. Once benchmarks or outcomes are met at regular intervals,
    Feeding South Florida will submit an invoice to the State for payment. If targets are not met, Feeding South Florida does
    not receive the funding.
