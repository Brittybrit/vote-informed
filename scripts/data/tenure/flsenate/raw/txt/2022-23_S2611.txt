12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will address flooding and right-of-way improvements including new asphalt, new drainage, new curb and
    gutter and broken sidewalk replacement.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Funds will be used to address flooding and right-of-way improvements including new asphalt, new drainage, new curb
    and gutter and broken sidewalk replacement.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The direct services provided to citizens by this project include more walkable neighborhoods and safer streets.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is all residents and visitors of the City of Miami. The amount of individuals who will be served will
    likely exceed 20,000 per day.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     1. Improve physical health: Comparing number of flooding complaints before and after project
    2. Enhance/preserve/improve environmental or fish and wildlife quality: Comparing number of cleaning sweeps by
    stormwater operations crews on the improved stormwater control structures (Pre & post construction) as well as physical
    observation of water being retained in structures or swales for longer periods before running off into adjacent waterways.
    3. Protect the general public from harm (environmental, criminal, etc.): Same as #2
    4. Improve transportation conditions: Comparing number of traffic accidents of affected roadways and from observation of
    better traffic flows due to reduced flooding in travel ways.
