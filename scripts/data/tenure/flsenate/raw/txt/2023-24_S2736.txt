4. Project/Program Description
    Mama Hattie's House will offer "A Place to Call Home" for girls transitioning from traumatic experiences, including young
   women aging out of foster care, girls reclaiming their lives from human trafficking and other forms of abuse; and girls
   transitioning from homelessness or exiting incarceration. The complex will support girls from all walks of life who are
   experiencing challenges that could prevent them from growing up to become healthy, productive women on track for life-
   long success.

   We are currently in a $20 million Capital Campaign to build Mama Hattie's House, which will be a five story, 34,000 square
   foot facility, located in the heart of Miami-Dade County's Overtown community, and tentatively slated to begin construction
   by year end 2023.

   The vision of Mama Hattie's House is that all girls have a safe and healthy "Place to Call Home": where they receive the
   support, tools and love necessary to achieve their full potential.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Mama Hattie's House will offer a residence for girls aging out of foster care, exiting detention, or transiting from
    homelessness, abuse and neglect. Programs under the same roof will provide physical, emotional, social, academic and
    career support with an emphasis on preparing young women to lead fulfilling and productive lives. Girls will also have
    access to an on-call medical and mental health services, and a host of on-site support services.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Residential co-living style space with single and double bedrooms with private bathrooms for girls. House parent quarters
    with full amenities. Common area spaces for dining, studying, social gatherings, music and artistic explorations. A
    commercial kitchen space providing healthy meals and snacks daily, and where girls can learn to prepare nutritious
    meals. Experienced and professional psychological and medical evaluation. Personal goal setting. Educational
    assessment, tutoring and academic enrichment. Home, family, and life management skills. Individual, group, and family
    counseling. Financial capacity-building. Career skills training and development. Family unification and strengthening.
    Substance abuse recovery referrals as needed.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Mama Hattie's House citizen's services. Affordable ground floor retail space. Co-working and business space for young
    female entrepreneurs. Shared work spaces for interns and volunteers. A warming kitchen, bathrooms, secure storage,
    and reception area. Retail space for tenants and partners who bring vital goods and services to the surrounding
    community.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population is Miami-Dade at-promise girls in the following categories: Girls ages 11 to 17 who are transiting
    from homelessness, abuse and neglect, at risk of sex trafficking and/or exiting detention; Girls 18 to 24 who are aging out
    of the foster care system; Girls ages 7 to 17 who are experiencing challenges with academics, behavior and school
    attendance.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Mama Hattie's House will offer a loving and nurturing environment for girls who need it, where they can feel safe,
    supported, and empowered to reach their full potential, without fear of abuse or trauma. Girls who successfully exit Mama
    Hattie's House will have developed the life and workforce skills necessary to live and work independently and become
    productive members of the community.
