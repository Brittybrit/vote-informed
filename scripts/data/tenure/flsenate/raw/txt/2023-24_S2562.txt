1. Project Title          Bruce Hall Renovation

2. Senate Sponsor        Ana Maria Rodriguez

3. Date of Request       03/03/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Renovation of 'Bruce Hall,' a 27,000 sq. ft. building in Key West owned by the Monroe County School District ('MCSD').
   Renovations will allow MCSD to relocate its Administration Building from 241 Trumbo Road, Key West, FL. Relocation of
   the Admin Building will free the Trumbo Property for development of affordable workforce housing for MCSD employees
   and other 'essential services personnel' as per Fla. Stat. 1001.43(12), at no cost to MCSD via public private partnership
   with SPGL, LLC.

     Renovation of 'Bruce Hall,' a 27,000 sq. ft. building in Key West owned by the Monroe County School District ('MCSD').
    Renovations will allow MCSD to relocate its Administration Building from 241 Trumbo Road, Key West, FL. Relocation of
    the Admin Building will free the Trumbo Property for development of affordable workforce housing for MCSD employees
    and other 'essential services personnel' as per Fla. Stat. 1001.43(12), at no cost to MCSD via public private partnership
    with SPGL, LLC.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     This project will allow the administration to move out of there current location so that it can be used to provide much
    needed affordable housing for local teachers.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     This project will allow the administration to move out of there current location so that it can be used to provide much
    needed affordable housing for local teachers.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    Economically disadvantaged persons, At-risk youth, Monroe County School District Employees, Preschool students,
    Grade school students, High school students. 101-200 individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Improve quality of education by complete development of affordable workforce housing at 241 Trumbo Road within 24
    months of completing Bruce Hall renovation. Documented increase in employee retention rates within 24 months of
    completing Trumbo Workforce Housing Development. Create specific immediate job opportunities with documented
    reduction in number of applicants that reject offers of employment with MCSD due to inability to locate affordable housing
    within 36 months of completing Trumbo Housing development. Enhance specific individual's economic self sufficiency by
    documented reduction in applications for state or federal housing assistance by MCSD employees within 36 months of
    completing Trumbo Housing development. Preservation of education program with documented reduction in total number
    of teaching and education support positions left vacant at the start of each school year within 36 months of completing
    Trumbo Housing Development.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Proportional loss of funding in next fiscal year until deliverables and performance measures have been complied with
