1. Project Title          Miami-Dade County Shovel-Ready Tree Canopy

2. Senate Sponsor         Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request        12/05/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    This project matches a one time disbursement of tree planting funds to enhance public land in Miami-Dade County for
   beautification, urban cooling and water management. Specifically, tree plantings will be in environmental preserves,
   schools and government facilities. Examples include Camp Matecumbe/Boystown, Tamiami Complex Addition, Fuch's
   hammock and many more. Demonstration projects can be seen at Bowman K-8 school. Funding will be used for an
   additional 6,000 trees and understory plants. If funded, staff also work with state elected officials to identify government
   facilities and schools to prioritize, leveraging their local expertise. This effort builds on county legislation and an MOU
   between the county and the school board to enhance tree canopy on county land.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The purpose of the fund request is rapid enhancement of tree canopy in the areas that are either in the most need or
    have specifically been set aside for forests. The tree plantings will enhance public land in Miami-Dade County for
    beautification, urban cooling and water management. Targeted planting areas are located where the trees have the
    highest success of survival.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     County staff identified planting 2,000 trees and small plants at Camp Matecumbe and 1,500 trees at a preserve next to
    the Everglades. Additional trees will be planted in government facilities and schools. The schools in northern Miami-Dade
    County are in particular high need of tree canopy. If funded, staff will work with state elected officials to identify
    government facilities and schools to prioritize, leveraging their local expertise.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Trees and forests have a huge number of benefits including cooling, beautification, sense of place, wildlife connections,
    absorption of stormwater, breaking up winds during hurricanes and many more. Increased tree canopy absorbs and
    mitigates air pollution and creates patches of high performing features of value in the urban landscape.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The target population are within the census places that have high poverty rates and low tree canopy per county
    assessments. This footprint includes much of the unincorporated parts of Miami-Dade County in the southern parts of the
    county and Florida City. Additional effort will be in the Environmentally Endangered Lands preserves within the Southern
    part of the county regardless of census place and any high priority recommendations provided by stakeholders such as
    state officials and the school board.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcome is a significant tree planting effort in environmental preserves, schools and government facilities.
    Examples include Camp Matecumbe, Fuch's hammock, and many more. Demonstration projects can be seen at Bowman
    K-12 school. The primary metric will be a minimum 6,000 trees and understory plants will be planted. Additional metrics
    include: trees plantings associated with stormwater features, plants grown from local growers, tree plantings at
    schools/education, acres of enhanced, and many more.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Funds will be returned if deliverables are not met.
