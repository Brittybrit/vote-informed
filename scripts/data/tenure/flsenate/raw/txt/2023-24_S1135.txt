4. Project/Program Description
    The City of Coral Gables is proposing to replace its existing sanitary sewer force main pipe system. Due to the age and
   substandard pipe material, the existing sewer pipe force main is vulnerable to breaks that cause wastewater spills and
   overflows that can become the source of groundwater and surface water pollution; and ultimately affect water quality and
   the natural environment. This project is necessary to prevent nutrient pollution and negative impacts to water quality in
   groundwater and surrounding surface water bodies. It is also important to the maintenance of overall public health.
   Additionally, this project will also improve the capacity and reliability of City’s sewer force main system, which serves
   residents throughout the municipality.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The funds will be used to fund improvements to prevent sanitary sewer spills and overflows from an aged sewer
    collection system. The City aims to replace and upgrade existing force main lines with new PVC pipes in addition to
    installing proper valves and features. Benefits will be realized by the community at large and by the surrounding
    environment.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Design and construction services will occur to replace portions of and maintain the existing force main.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     The sanitary sewer service will be more resilient, including reduced nutrient pollution and reduced negative impacts to
    water quality in groundwater and surrounding surface water bodies. Citizens will experience reduced risk of exposure to
    contaminated waters thereby improving overall public health.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

    The entire community of Coral Gables is expected to benefit as well as any individuals who live outside of the city but
    who are connected to the city's sanitary sewer system. Approximately 50,000+ individuals.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The environmental benefits of this project can be measured by monitoring pollution levels in surrounding water bodies
    (including Biscayne Bay) and in ground water. The City currently partners with FIU to monitor water quality in certain key
    areas and would be able to assist with this endeavor.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Withholding grant funds.
