4. Project/Program Description
    Miami-Dade Fire Rescue (MDFR) planned Eureka Fire-Rescue Station 71 will be located at 15450 SW 184 Street, Miami,
   FL 33187. Station 71 will be manufactured by Extreme Modular Buildings and will be a 3,580-square-foot of one (1) Engine
   Bay with five (5) personnel dormitories, bathrooms, a kitchen and day room. Additional supporting spaces to conduct all fire
   operations will also be included. At present time, Rescue 71 is operating from Modello Fire-Rescue Station 6 located at
   15890 SW 288 Street, Miami, FL 33033. Upon completion of the new Station 71, Rescue 71 will be relocated to it's
   permanent station.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     Station 71 would allow MDFR to immediately address the increased demands for fire-rescue services resulting from
    significant current and projected population and construction growth in South Miami-Dade County (SMDC). In addition to
    10% population growth in the last 10 years, MDFR identified 66 new developments in SMDC undergoing or approved
    through the County's CDMP, adding more than 17,846 residential units and nearly one million sq.ft. of commercial space
    to the area in the next five years.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Implementation of Temporary Station 71 at 15450 SW 184 Street (Eureka Drive), will allow MDFR to relocate Rescue 71,
    currently operating out of Station 6 at 15890 SW 288 Street, to its permanent location and enhance service to the
    intended community by reducing response times to emergency care.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Residents and visitors within the Station 71 service territory will benefit from reduced response times for Advanced Life
    Support (ALS) and fire emergency services. The rescue unit will enhance service delivery and improve response times to
    communities in the immediate area served by the station, as well as neighboring communities, which have been facing
    increases in service demands over the past five years, coinciding with population growth and economic development.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Approximately 118,000 residents reside within the proposed service area and surrounding communities. From October 1,
    2022 through September 30, 2023, MDFR responded to 5,029 incidents within the proposed service area. Eureka Fire-
    Rescue Station 71 is projected to respond to 1,608 calls within the new proposed territory.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Construction of Eureka Fire-Rescue Station 71 is expected to alleviate service demands currently handled by existing
    fire-rescue resources (units) and reduce response times to incidents occurring within the proposed service area. MDFR
    utilizes Computer Aided Dispatch (CAD) data to track response times, unit availability, and unit reliability, which will be the
    key performance measures analyzed to determine that the intended outcomes are being met. The construction of Eureka
    Fire-Rescue Station 71 and relocation of Rescue 71 is expected to have a positive impact on response times, as well as
    a positive impact on existing units responding to incidents in South Miami-Dade County, as those units availability and
    reliability will likely increase with the addition of the new fire-rescue station.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Failure to meet deliverables will result in non-payment
