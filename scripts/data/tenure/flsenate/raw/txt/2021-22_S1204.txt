4. Project/Program Description
    Our program is designed to create and formulate a long term work experience training program for individuals with autism
   and other developmental and intellectual disabilities in an educational setting. Our objective is to enhance transition
   planning and career development for these individuals.
   Some of the innovative features of our program include: *Customized employment opportunities, work experiences, and
   transition planning for each student, driven by the student's interests, support needs, strengths, and contributions rather
   than by generic job descriptions and available openings. Customized implies flexibility and creativity enabling support staff
   to modify parameters and desired outcomes as the student's interests and skills evolve.
   *Entrepreneurial options or self-employment as post school outcomes or career experience options for students, in addition
   to traditional wage employment.
   *Linking employment vendors to the school to
   promote collaborative funding of employment.

12. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     To create customized employment opportunities, work experiences, and transition planning for each student, driven by
    student’s interests, support needs, strengths, and contributions rather than by generic job descriptions and available
    openings.
    Customized implies flexibility and creativity enabling support staff to modify parameters and desired outcomes as the
    student’s interests and skills evolve.
    Create entrepreneurial options or self-employment as post school outcomes or career experience options for students, in
    addition to traditional wage employment.
    And last, but not least, link employment vendors to the school to promote collaborative funding of employment and
    transitional activities.

 To create a training program that focuses on the development of the general competencies required for work in
competitive employment.
To create a training program that focuses on the development of general competencies required to work in the child care
industry.
Procure contract work that would involve work for private businesses under contract for a price.
The work will be utilized as a tool for on-the-job training.
Activities may involve sorting, collating, assembling, and packaging products.
To create innovative small business models designed around the skills and interests of the students such as, a training
program that focuses on the necessary skills to work in the food service industry (In Good Tastes), a training program that
teaches the necessary skills to effectively manage, design, create, package, and sell candles for a bath and beauty
business (Candles & Such).
Minimum 30 hours per week for 11 months to teach competencies in math, reading, and life skills.
