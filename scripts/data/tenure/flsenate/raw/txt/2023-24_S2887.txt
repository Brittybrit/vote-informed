4. Project/Program Description
    KU will purchase cutting-edge virtual sim. training systems for 18 campuses to be used by Crim Justice, CyberSecurity,
   CSI, Forensics, Accounting, & IT students preparing for law enforcement careers. These allow instructors to put students in
   virtual law enforcement training scenarios to broaden traditional academic standards & practices allowing us to continue
   our track record of success. KU will make simulators available to law enforcement agencies for ongoing training purposes
   for sworn LEO.

    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     KU will purchase cutting-edge virtual sim. training systems for 18 campuses to be used by Crim Justice, CyberSecurity,
    CSI, Forensics, Accounting, & IT students preparing for law enforcement careers. These allow instructors to put students
    in virtual law enforcement training scenarios to broaden traditional academic standards & practices allowing us to
    continue our track record of success. KU will make simulators available to law enforcement agencies for ongoing training
    purposes for sworn LEO.
    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     Educating students in a virtual environment that employs law enforcement concepts of continuum, discretion, and critical
    thinking as well as understanding, applying, analyzing, and evaluating relevant knowledge.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     Enabling students in Keiser University's Criminal Justice, CyberSecurity, Crime Scene Investigation, Forensics,
    Accounting and IT programs, as well as current Law Enforcement Officers, to train with daily real-life scenarios faced in
    their future careers, working to alleviate the current Law Enforcement shortage.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     Keiser University students enrolled in Criminal Justice related fields such as Cybersecurity, Crime Scene Investigation,
    and Forensics. Our Accounting, Homeland Security, and IT students may use it as they may also be preparing for careers
    in law enforcement. Keiser University has approximately 20,000 students.

    Law Enforcement Officers and Agencies - Each campus will have an affiliation with local law enforcement training
    agencies for them to be able to demonstrate scenarios and teach courses using the simulators. The number using the
    simulator will vary by campus and is unable to be determined until all reciprocal affiliations are in place.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     Utilizing virtual simulation inside of classroom will increase the level of knowledge, experience, and familiarity with real
    life scenarios and experiences for students. Preparing students for hands on careers using industry leading technology
    and qualified faculty will provide our graduates with competitive advantages in the workplace. These student success
    outcomes will be measured by increased student retention rates, course completions, graduation rates, and potential
    successful job placement post graduation.
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Keiser University is committed to addressing Florida's law enforcement shortage through talent development and
    innovative training. In the event that performance measures are not met, we would ask for a 30-day performance
    improvement plan.
