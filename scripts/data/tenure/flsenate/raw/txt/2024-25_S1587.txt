4. Project/Program Description
    Funding to continue the Veterans Access Clinic (VAC) at Nova Southeastern University. The VAC provides outreach and
   direct patient care services to veterans and fmilies who are unable to access care through other outlets at no cost to the
   patient(s). Direct services include primary care, dental care, vision care, physical and speech therapy, psychological and
   behavioral health care
