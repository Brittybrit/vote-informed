1. Project Title          Palmetto Bay Stormwater Improvements-Sub-Basin #11

2. Senate Sponsor        Alexis Calatayud

3. Date of Request       02/06/2023

4. Project/Program Description
    Funding is being requested for the construction and construction support for stormwater improvements to sub-basin 11.
   Improvements include building additional catch basins, manholes, culverts & exfiltration to connect the catch basins within
   this sub-basin. Improvements will reduce pollutant load contribution from 3 main pollutants from the sub-basin into
   Biscayne Bay Aquifer, control flooding during and after major storm events and improve surface water quality.

14. Program Performance
    a. What specific purpose or goal will be achieved by the funds requested?

     The specific goal is the significant reduction or elimination of flooding and standing water due to storm or heavy rain
    events.

    b. What activities and services will be provided to meet the intended purpose of these funds?

     A contractor and construction management staff will be sought using a public Invitation to Bid (ITB). A kick-off meeting
    will be held to go over the project and a start date will be set. Permits will be applied for by the selected contractor,
    regular project updates will be provided as stated in the final contract with the funding agency.
    c. What direct services will be provided to citizens by the appropriation project?

     No direct services will be provided, but all Miami-Dade citizens who may drive through the Palmetto Bay Sub-Basin #11
    roadways will benefit from having a safe flood free roadway to travel.
    d. Who is the target population served by this project? How many individuals are expected to be served?

     The targeted population for this project are the residents of the approximately 48.37 acres of existing residential and
    commercial development with approximately 6,470 linear feet of roadway. The expected number of individuals to be
    served is 500-600 homes along the impacted roadways and over 2,500 of daily drivers.
    e. What is the expected benefit or outcome of this project? What is the methodology by which this outcome will
    be measured?

     The expected outcome of this project is to limit or eliminate standing waster after a heavy storm or rain event. This
    outcome will be measured by the decreased pollutants recorded, number of complaints and calls to the Public Services
    Department and Village Hall about flooding in this area. [Pollutant Reduction Analysis: Phosphorous (currently 28.75
    kg/yr to 2.11 kg/yr), Nitrogen (currently 226.51 kg/yr to 23.78 kg/yr), and Suspended Solids (currently 2,715.24 kg/yr to
    256.59 kg/yr).]
    f. What are the suggested penalties that the contracting agency may consider in addition to its standard penalties
    for failing to meet deliverables or performance measures provided for the contract?

     Withholding of funds is a fair penalty for failing to meet deliverables.
