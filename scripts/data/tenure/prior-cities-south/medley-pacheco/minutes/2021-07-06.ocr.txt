- Request by Mayor Martell for Discussion and Action on the Installation of a
Concrete Pre-Cast Wall Along the 600’s Lots of the Lakeside Retirement Park.

Mayor Martell began the discussion by bringing attention to the estimates/proposals previously
circulated for the proposed item. Mayor Martell further explained that the proposed concrete wall
would provide security to the residents located in the 600’s lots. Town Attorney Lillian Arango
stated that three (3) new proposals could be obtained for the work requested or the Town could
simply expand the previous work order issued for the construction of the concrete wall along
NW 107" Avenue that was previously solicited and approved. Town Attorney Arango also
mentioned that the Town Council can formally waive the procurement process if the need to
erect the wall was a safety and/or a wellbeing of the residents.

Mayor Martell translated the intent of the matter into the Spanish language for those in
attendance. Councilperson Ayala asked to table the item for future discussion since the proposed
wall shown to run along the Lakeside Parcel “B” that was recently approved to be cleaned and
converted into a passive park. Lourdes Rodriguez, resident of lot 729 of the Lakeside Retirement
Park spoke on the matte and asked the Council to inform the lakeside residents and ask them for
input before the matter is move forward for action. Vice-Mayor Pacheco referred to the Town of
Medley Public Informational Meeting of June 21, 2021 in which lakeside residents spoke in
favor of the Mayor’s proposal for the Lakeside Parcel “B”. Medley residents Loly Tuya and
Fortunana Castellanos also spoke on the matter and spoke about what took place at the
informational meeting of June 21, 2021. Residents Maria Aleman residing at NW South River
Drive also spoke about what took place during the June 21, 2021 informational meeting and
expressed agreement with the improvements performed by the Town during the last few years.
Alejandro Martinez resident of 8181 NW South River Drive Lot E-549 also spoke about what
transpired at the public informational meeting of June 21, 2021 and pleaded for the Town
Council to stop arguing and work together.

Councilperson Ayala joined the discussion and clarified to what in his opinion took place before
and during the informational meeting of June 21, 2021. In addition, Councilperson Ayala
expressed his disagreement with the Mayor’s proposal in entering into a long-term lease for the
Lakeside Parcel “B” to unknown investors as opposed to use the property for a public and
community benefit. Councilman Ayala further stated that he has been in contact with federal
sources with the idea to potentially develop the property in question for housing purposes.

Councilwoman Stefano explained that the Lakeside Parcel “B” is zoned R-1 which makes it
designated it for residential purposes and not commercial unless the zoning designation is
changed. Councilwoman Stefano further stated that when the use of the property was decided,
the Mayor’s idea of the senior care facility was approved but it has yet to take place. Resident
Alfonso Alfonso residing at the Lakeside Retirement Park Lot 657 also spoke on the matter and
reinstated what he said at the public informational meeting of June 21, 2021 which is to ask the
Town to prioritize the cleaning of the parcel “B” and then address the possibilities of
development. Mr. Alsonso further stated that the proposed concrete wall along the 600’s lots
should be discussed with the affected residents.

- Discussion and Action on the Town of Medley COVID-19 Return to Work
Policy.

Human Director Olga Quin addressed the Council and explained that last February the COVID-19
return-to-work policy was last amended. However, the state has since lifted the state of emergency
and many of the mandates previously imposed including but not limited to the face mask
mandates, temperature checks, social distancing protocols and testing requirements. Thus, the
Town’s current practices needed to be revisited for both employees and the public.

After a brief discussion, the following was decided to be effective July 7, 2021:

- Mask Mandate: Optional for both, Employees, and the Public;
- Temperature Checking: Continue Practice for Public and Employees;
- Capacity Limitation/Social Distancing: Flexible and Optional;
- Social Gatherings/Events: Flexible and Optional;
- Testing Requirements- Continue the Testing Requirement for Domestic and International
Travel.

Vice-Mayor Pacheco seconded the motion and it carried unanimously.

- Discussion and Action on Upcoming Town of Medley Events.

Town Clerk Martinez addressed the Council and explained that following the earlier scheduled
Council workshop where upcoming Town events were discussed, a memorandum had been
presented to include all directives and Council consensus noted for the upcoming Town events
from July to December 2021 for formal action as discussed. It was also announced that the next
Council workshop to further discuss Town events was, as requested, to be held on September 7,
2021.

Without further discussion, Mayor Martell made a motion to approve all upcoming Town events
as presented. Councilwoman Stefano seconded the motion, and it was carried unanimously.

- Discussion and Action on the Cancellation of the August 2, 2021 Regular
Council Meeting.

Town Clerk Martinez addressed the request following the Town’s practice to cancel the August
Regular Council meeting for the month of August.

Without further discussion, Mayor Martell made a motion to cancel the August regular council
meeting. Councilwoman Stefano seconded the motion, and it was carried unanimously.

The next item to be addressed was the Add-On Item a requested by Councilwoman Ayala. It was
discussed and acted upon as follows,

- Item 13(A)18 Request by Councilwoman Ayala for Discussion and Action on the
Request to Have Police Presence on all Regular and Public Meetings.

Councilwoman Ayala addressed the item and explained that police presence is necessary
especially when controversial items are discussed in public forums. Councilwoman Ayala further
explained that although language is put in place to address inappropriate behavior, derogatory
comments and racial slurs, the Mayor has at times failed to impose order, and she has also
noticed that order is maintained whenever police presence is present.

Without further discussion Councilwoman Ayala made a motion as follows,

“TO HAVE POLICE PRESENCE AT ALL PUBLIC MEETINGS, NOT
LIMITED TO REGULAR, SPECIAL AND INFORMATIONAL
MEETINGS, REQUIRING ALL APPLICABLE STAFF TO ATTEND
SAID MEETINGS.”

- Monthly Update by Town Staff on Current Town Projects and Related Finances.

Finance Director Roy Danziger approached the Council and referred to the DR-420 form that
had been previously circulated. Director Danziger further stated that the reporting form needed to
be completed and submitted to the Florida Department of Revenue and the Office of the Property
Appraiser by August 4, 2021. Thus, he reminded the Council of the budget workshop scheduled
for July 15, 2021 in which the tentative millage rate had to be set. He also stated that as the
DR-420 shows, the current millage rate is 4.800 mills and the rills back rate is 4.4029 and a
preview of what would be presented at the budget workshop, which is a proposed operating
millage rate of 3.900 mills. Councilperson Ayala mentioned how the Town’s proposed millage
rate was connected to the annexation efforts being pursued and how additional meetings and
discussions were being arranged on the matter.

Since there was no further business to consider Mayor Martell made a motion to adjourn the
meeting, Councilwoman Stefano seconded the motion, and the meeting was adjourned at
10:32PM on July 6, 2021.
