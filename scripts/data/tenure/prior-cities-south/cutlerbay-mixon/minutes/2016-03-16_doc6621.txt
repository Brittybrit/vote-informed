March 16, 2016 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT
 Gary Ratay and Janet Delgado of Kimley-Horn and Associates presented an
update on the Caribbean Boulevard Roadway Improvement Project. Mayor Bell
reported on Palmetto Bay road closures due to traffic on Old Cutler Road and
suggested addressing the issue with the County. Council Member Loyzelle
suggested pairing the Gap 2 project with the bridge project and requested
Miami-Dade County District 8 Chief of Policy & Planning Sean McCrackine relay
the message to the County. Miami-Dade Police Lieutenant Peter Zaharakos
reported on proactive enforcement efforts for robbery prevention. Council
Member Loyzelle suggested the installation of solar powered speed signs to
reduce speeding. Town Manager Rafael Casals announced a potential date of
May 25, 2016 for a Social Media & Public Records Workshop to satisfy the State
of Florida annual ethics training requirements for local elected officials. Town
Manager Rafael Casals reported on vetoes from Florida Governor Rick Scott’s
office in respect to the Town's Legislative Priorities. Town Manager Rafael
Casals announced the Miami-Dade Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO)
Multimodal Mobility Options for South Miami-Dade Advisory Committee Meeting
on March 17, 2016 and the ChamberSOUTH Transportation Committee General
Membership Breakfast on March 23, 2016.
 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT
 Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman reported on an amicus brief supporting the
Environmental Protection Agency's Clean Power Plan Rule, and requested
Council consent to sign on to the amicus brief. At the request of Council
Member Loyzelle, Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman provided clarification on the
amicus brief. A motion was made by Council Member Loyzelle, seconded by
Council Member Mixon to allow the Town of Cutler Bay to sign on to the amicus
brief. Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion passed 5-0.

 No report.

 A.   AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, COMPREHENSIVELY UPDATING AND REVISING
CHAPTER 3 “LAND DEVELOPMENT REGULATIONS” OF THE TOWN CODE OF
ORDINANCES; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS;
PROVIDING FOR CODIFICATION; PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Ordinance. Town Manager
Rafael Casals provided historical background on the item and introduced Mr.
Henry Iler of Iler Planning and Consulting to present the proposed changes to
obtain Green Certification Gold status. Building and Code Compliance Director,
Sandra Aronoff presented proposed code changes. Town Manager Rafael Casals
thanked Grants Coordinator Lakeesha Morris for her efforts on the application
process. After discussion, Mayor Bell opened the public hearing. There being no
one wishing to speak, Mayor Bell closed the public hearing. A motion was made
by Council Member Mixon, seconded by Council Member Coriat that this
Ordinance be approved on first reading. The motion carried by the following
vote:
Yes:   Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat
5 -
 12. ORDINANCES FOR SECOND READING (PUBLIC HEARING REQUIRED)
 A.   AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, AMENDING ORDINANCES 06-10, 09-06 AND 10-04
RELATING TO “FLOODPLAIN MANAGEMENT REGULATIONS”; PROVIDING FOR
SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE CODE; PROVIDING FOR
CONFLICTS; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Ordinance. Town Manager
Rafael Casals introduced Stormwater Utility Manager Yenier Vega to inform the
Council that the proposed changes may earn additional points to increase the
Town's rating as part of the Federal Emergency Management Agency’s (FEMA)
National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP). Council Member Mixon expressed
gratitude and praise toward Public Works Director Alfredo Quintero and
Stormwater Utility Manager Yenier Vega for their efforts. Mayor Bell opened the
public hearing. There being no one wishing to speak, Mayor Bell closed the
public hearing. A motion was made by Council Member Loyzelle, seconded by
Council Member Coriat, that this Ordinance be approved on second reading. The
motion carried by the following vote:
Yes:   Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat
