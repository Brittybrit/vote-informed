July 3, 2018Town Council Workshop Meeting Minutes

A. Discussion Regarding Miami-Dade County Public Schools

· Renewal of Interlocal Agreement

· Status of Joint Use Agreement

· School Resource Officer(s)

Mayor Bell provided information on the reasons for the workshop, and spoke on

the state mandate for schools to provide school resource officers.

Mayor Bell provided background information on the expiring interlocal

agreement with Miami-Dade County Public Schools, and the difficulties

renegotiating with the school district.

There was discussion on the different programs offered at Cutler Bay Middle

School and Cutler Bay High School, the amount of funding the schools receive as

part of the agreement, school grades, and the cost of adding school resource

officers.

Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman provided information on the state mandate for

school resource officers, a proposed Memo of Understanding sent to local

municipalities by the school district asking them to indemnify the school system,

and another proposed Memo of Understanding suggesting the school board pay

for school resource officers and the indemnification be mutual. He also

explained the Town is under no obligation to pay for school resource officers.

There was discussion on what other local municipalities are proposing to do to

provide school resource officers, associated costs to the Town regarding the

Interlocal Agreement with the schools, and options on the cost of adding full time

officers or strict overtime costs to provide school resource officers from the Town

police force.

Police Major Leo Ricelli provided information on the cost of adding five (5) full

time officers, and the overtime costs for current officers to provide coverage for

school resource officers

There was discussion on the possibility of a Joint Use Agreement with the schools

for use of the school basketball courts.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

There was discussion on reducing the funding of the middle school and high

school and which programs to fund, and the possibility of renewing the Interlocal

Agreement but for a shorter term.

Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman explained the potential consequences of not

renewing the Interlocal Agreement with the school district.

There was discussion about the school district's terms for renewal of the

Interlocal Agreement, and the Town's options going forward.

Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman provided information on a proposed Memo of

Understanding from the Town of Miami Lakes and the the City of Hialeah

Gardens regarding school resource officers, and suggested to wait and see what

they negotiate and bring it back to the Council.

There was Council consensus directing the manager not to renew the Interlocal

Agreement and remove it from the upcoming budget, to eliminate discussion on

a Joint Use Agreement to use the school basketball courts, and not appropriate

funds for school resource officers.

Mayor Bell announced the Cutler Bay - Whispering Pines Fourth of July Parade

and Celebration.

3. Adjournment

The meeting adjourned at 8:08 PM.
