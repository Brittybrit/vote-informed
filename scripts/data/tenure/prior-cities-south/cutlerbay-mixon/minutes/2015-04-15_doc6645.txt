 4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Mayor Bell brought forth the Local Channel 10 news article “Top 10 South Florida
Schools with Violent Incidents” from April 1, 2015 highlighting Cutler Bay Middle
School as the Number 3 most violent school in Miami-Dade County and
introduced Mr. Paul Pfeiffer, Principal of Cutler Bay Middle School to address the
issue before the Town Council. Principal Pfeiffer spoke on the measures that
have been and are being taken to improve and reduce the number of incidents
taking place at the school. Principal Pfieffer explained the active measures in
place to ensure staff remains on-course with the cultural change implemented.
The Town Council expressed concern of this issue and offered support to
solving the problem by working together through our educational compact.
Mayor Bell reported on Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) amendment to
change the population requirement for represnetation to be increased and
assured this item will remain under close-watch as the structure-change goes to
vote.
Council Member Coriat reported on his first attendance at Miami-Dade County
Days in Tallahassee.
Council Member Mixon also reported on attending Miami-Dade County Days in
Tallahassee.
Vice Mayor Sochin reported on various State legislative items and areas of
concern.
Council Member Loyzelle also reported on various State legislative items and
areas of concern. Council Member Loyzelle reported on the March 20, 2015 trip
to the South Dade Landfill and announced the upcoming trip to the Water and
Sewer Department. Council Member Loyzelle commended the Parks and
Recreation staff for the work they put into the Easter Egg Hunt and announced
the upcoming Bike-B-Que & Rodeo event.
Mayor Bell reported on a meeting with Ken Detzner, Florida Secreatary of State to
discuss the possibility of hosting state artifacts at Cutler Bay Town Hall. Mayor
Bell reported on her meeting with Miami-Dade County Mayor Carlos Gimenez in
Tallahassee. Mayor Bell also announced the Grand Opening of Sephora inside
JCPenny at Southland Mall Event on May 1, 2015 and Open Mic Night Event on
April 22, 2015. Mayor Bell reported on her meeting with Miami-Dade Public
Schools Superintendent Alberto Carvalho. Mayor Bell reported on her meeting
with Congresswoman Illeana Ros-Lehtinen. Mayor Bell reported on her
attendance at the Easter Egg Hunt and her participation in the Distracted Driving
Presentation. Mayor Bell announced upcoming events including Town of Cutler
Bay Employee Appreciation Day on April 17, 2015 and an Ethics Course
sponsored by Weiss Serota on April 24, 2015.
 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell opened Public Comments. Youth Council Committee Chair Victor
Mederos and Youth Council Committee Member Ashley Gray spoke on behalf of
the Youth Council Committee in favor of the name change of Linear Park to Blue
Heron Park. Council Member Coriat indicated that the Parks and Recreation
Advisory Committee also voted in favor of the name change recommendation by
the Youth Council Committee.

 9. ORDINANCES FOR FIRST READING (NO PUBLIC HEARING REQUIRED)
 AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE BORROWING OF FUNDS IN AN
AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED $13,000,000 FOR THE PURPOSE OF REFUNDING THE
TOWN’S PROMISSORY NOTES, SERIES 2010A, B, C, D AND E; PROVIDING FOR
AUTHORIZATION; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Ordinance. Town
Manager Rafael Casals introduced this item as a walk-on agenda item and
introduced Robert Daddario, the Town's Finance Director to provide a brief
overview on loan refinancing and consolidation with TD Bank. Robert Daddario
introduced our Financial Advisor Mr. Ed Stull, Managing Director of First South
West, who reiterated the benefits for the Town to refinance with TD Bank and
answered questions from the Town Council.
Mayor Bell opened the public hearing. There was no one wishing to speak.
Mayor Bell closed the public hearing.
A motion was made by Council Member Mixon, seconded by Council Member
Coriat, that this Ordinance be approved on first reading. The motion carried by
the following vote:
Yes:   Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat

 13. MAYOR AND COUNCIL COMMENTS
 The Town Council came to a general consensus for staff to urge TD Bank to drop
the pre-payment penalty as part of the loan refinancing.
 A.   ·   Discussion regarding Separation of State of FL
 A motion was made by Council Member Loyzelle, seconded by Council Member
Coriat that this item be moved to the front of the agenda. Mayor Bell called for
the vote and the motion carried 5-0. Vice Mayor Sochin introduced City of South
Miami Vice Mayor Walter Harris to speak regarding the separation of the State of
Florida. Vice Mayor Harris spoke in favor of the separation of the State of Florida
and requested support from the Town Council.
 14. OTHER BUSINESS
 Mayor Bell addressed concern associated with traffic issues. Town Manager
Casals acknowledged this concern with implementing a Town-wide Traffic
Calming analysis into the Transportation Master Plan. Council Member Mixon
suggested promoting instruction urging drivers not to stop in the middle of the
traffic circle and the importance of maintaining a steady flow of traffic.
