2. RESOLUTIONS - PUBLIC HEARING

A. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE FINAL MILLAGE RATE OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2017 THROUGH

SEPTEMBER 30, 2018 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTES SECTION 200.065; AND

PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the title of Resolution 2A and 2B. Mayor Bell

opened the Public Hearing for item 2A. There being no one wishing to speak,

Mayor Bell closed the Public Hearing. Mayor Bell opened the Public Hearing for

item 2B. There being no one wishing to speak, Mayor Bell closed the Public

Hearing.

Finance Director Robert Daddario read the TRIM Statement mandated by the

State of Florida into the record: The tentative millage rate of the Town of Cutler

Bay for FY 2017-18 of 2.5199 mills per $1,000 of assessed property value within the

Town of Cutler Bay is 13.42% greater than the roll-back rate of 2.2217 mills per

$1,000 of assessed property value. Ad valorem taxes are being increased

primarily as a result of expenditures for increased costs of contracted police

services, as well as to provide for various improvements to park and public

facilities.

Town Manager Rafael Casals informed the Council that the advertisement for the

Second Public Hearing was advertised on September 24, 2017 in the Miami

primarily as a result of expenditures for increased costs of contracted police

services, as well as to provide for various improvements to park and public

B. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE FINAL OPERATING AND CAPITAL OUTLAY BUDGET

FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2017 THROUGH SEPTEMBER

30, 2018 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTES SECTION 200.065; AUTHORIZING THE

TOWN MANAGER TO MAKE EXPENDITURES CONSISTENT THEREWITH AND

AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO MAKE CERTAIN BUDGET AMENDMENTS

WITHIN A DEPARTMENT PROVIDED THAT THE TOTAL OF THE APPROPRIATIONS

IS NOT CHANGED; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Manager Rafael Casals informed the Council of the changes made to the

budget based on the items discussed during the First Budget Hearing on

September 21. At the request of Vice Mayor Loyzelle, Finance Director Robert

Daddario provided clarification on the changes in the budget. A motion was

made by Council Member Mixon, seconded by Council Member Coriat, to

approve $3000 for a proposed Youth Council Committee trip. Town Manager

Rafael Casals provided clarification on $5,000 allocated for all committee

expenses for the fiscal year. After discussion, Council Member Mixon withdrew

her motion. There was discussion on budget changes from July 26 through

September 13, the grant writer's salary, and funds allocated for the Miami-Dade

County Public Schools Cambridge Program. There was also discussion on the

budget planned dip of $4.1 million at the proposed millage rate of 2.5199, the

difference in tax payments with a lower millage rate, cost for a special election

for a bond, the cost for the Town Center Plaza project plans, and an increase in

the Stormwater Budget.

After the discussion of both millage and budget was completed, a motion was

made by Council Member Mixon, seconded by Council Member Coriat to move

back to item 2A. Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion passed 5-0. Item

2A was completed.

Finance Director Robert Daddario clarified the final budget amount of

$51,751,537.00 based on an adopted millage rate of 2.3907.

A motion was made by Council Member Coriat, seconded by Council Member

Mixon, that this Resolution be adopted as amended.

Council Member Callahan expressed that he would prefer that the budget be

based upon less estimated costs. Mayor Bell spoke on the ceiling millage rate

for next fiscal year and the bond possibility. Vice Mayor Loyzelle spoke on the

possibility of a five year pro-forma for the future. Town Manager Rafael Casals

informed the Council that Town Staff is working on a five year pro-forma for the

future. Mayor Bell expressed gratitude to Town Staff for their budget efforts.

At 8:27PM, the motion carried by the following vote:

Yes: Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member Mary Ann

Mixon and Council Member Roger Coriat

4 -

No: Council Member Michael Callahan1 -
