2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Town's Strategic Master Plan Update (2014-19)

Sponsors: Town Manager's Office

Town Manager Rafael Casals stated that the 2014-2019 Strategic Master Plan

document is a culmination of approximately seven meetings that were held

throughout the community. Notifications for these meetings were posted on the

Town's website, as well as the Town's bulletin board, and twice in the Neighbors

section of the Miami Herald.

Town Manager Casals stated additional methods utilized to notify residents of

the strategic master plan meetings, including door-to-door distribution of flyers,

press releases, and emails to the Town's opt-in email list.

Consultant Merrett Steirheim stated that it has been a privilege to work on the

Strategic Master Plan with the citizens and Town staff. Mr. Steirheim suggested

interfacing the Strategic Master Plan with the Town's other master plans. He

suggested that before the end of the calendar year, the Public Works Department

should update their plans in terms of the dollars spent, the accuracy of the

forecasts, and the work remaining to be completed.

Town Manager Casals stated that there are nine strategic achievement areas that

have been identified in the plan. Goal 1 is resident friendly and responsive Town

government. Goal 2 is financial stability and sustainability. Goal 3 is growth

management. Goal 4 is economic and business development. Goal 5 is

community identity. Goal 6 is public safety. Goal 7 is parks and recreation. Goal

8 is codes and code enforcement. Goal 9 is public works.

Town Manager Casals explained the process utilized during the public outreach

meetings to prioriitize the needs as expressed by the citizens of the community.

Mayor MacDougall requested a projection for completion and distribution of the

strategic master plan document.

Mr. Steirheim stated that after the current meeting, the document would be put

into final form and a public meeting would be scheduled for adoption.

Mayor MacDougall suggested that Town Manager Casals share some highlights

from the strategic plan draft document.

The Council had a discussion regarding golf carts and restrictions imposed by

the County and State codes, which resulted in Mayor MacDougall suggesting that

a Council Member put forward a resolution regarding golf carts to bring to a later

Town Council meeting. Council Member Bell stated that she would like to do

this.

Council Member Loyzelle questioned if the findings on the circulator ridership

would be moved to the Transportation Master Plan. Town Manager Casals stated

that the information would be included in that report as well.

Mr. Steirheim suggested that the Town conduct a citizen survey that could be

used to provide benchmarks for the Town's accomplishments.

Council Member Loyzelle suggested that she, along with the Town Clerk, reach

out to the Town of Tequesta regarding the media relations outreach component

of the Strategic Master Plan. Council Member Mixon stated that she provided the

Town Clerk with the information she received during a workshop regarding

social media and municipalities.

Council Member Loyzelle stated that the Communities for a Lifetime Committee

is currently examining the senior needs survey to compile a list of priorities that

the Committee would like to address that can be incorporated into the Strategic

Master Plan.

Mr. Steirheim stated that the Town finances are in terrific shape. However, he

suggested that the Finance Department provide monthly reports on the webpage,

rather than the current practice of providing quarterly updates. The Human

Resources Department should update the administrative code, the policy and

procedure manual, update job descriptions, and implement a pay plan for the

Town's 33 employees. Mr. Steirheim suggested the implementation of an online

tracking system for grants.

Mr. Steirheim stated that the Town should continue to try to square off its

boundaries despite any political opposition. He also suggested that the Town

pursue sustainable landscape and beautification partnerships with homeowners'

associations and neighborhood associations.

Mr. Steirheim stated that there is a benefit of having a business committee with a

Council liaison, consisting of the three business organizations within the Town.

The recommendation is to have three individuals from each business

organization form a committee with one Council member designated as a liaison.

There were mixed feelings at the various public meetings regarding the sign

ordinance, as well as mixed feelings from the Town Council. One of the

suggested projects for the business committee would be to review the sign

ordinance and come back with recommendations that might be more amenable

to the business community. Mr. Steirheim also suggested that the Town retain a

professional to assist with the Town economic development plan.

Mr. Steirheim suggested the creation of a Cutler Bay special events committee

and charge said committee with recommending additional community events

beyond those currently in place. Council Member Bell stated that the Town had

an events committee in the past for which she served as liaison to the Town

Council. Attendance for the committee suffered causing an inability to conduct

committee meetings due to lack of quorum. The remaining members of the

events committee were appointed to the Parks and Recreation Committee.

Mr. Steirheim suggested the adoption of a license plate recognition system. He

also suggested the creation of a video for the proper navigation of traffic circles.

He also suggested that the Town consider increasing police staffing as

Mr. Steirheim suggested working with South Florida Water Management District

to discuss the feasibility of creating access to Biscayne Bay. He stated that this

endeavor was also addressed in the first Strategic Master Plan. He also

suggested the addition of basketball courts, lightning detection devices, and

outdoor exercise and weight training equipment.

Mr. Steirheim discussed the need to study the current land development

regulations and develop a more stringent code. In addition, he suggestion the

addition of another code enforcement officer, procurement of an electronic

permitting system, and authoriization for a space utilization study for the Town

Center building.

Mr. Steirheim suggested the development of a pedestrain-safe walkway to

Southland Mall. He also discussed a townwide storm drain cleaning program

operating on a two-year cycle. He also suggested addressing the flooding and

drainage issues in the Town's gated communities.

Mayor MacDougall expressed his pleasure with the progress the Town has made

over the last 8.5 years. He thanked Mr. Steirheim and staff for their expediency,

as well as their work and staff time.

Town Manager Casals stated that the next steps would be to include milestones

in the plan, and then bring the plan back for adoption on the October 2014 Town

Council agenda.

3. Adjournment

A motion was made by Council Member Loyzelle and received a second from

Vice Mayor Sochin to adjourn. The meeting adjourned at 5:55 p.m.
