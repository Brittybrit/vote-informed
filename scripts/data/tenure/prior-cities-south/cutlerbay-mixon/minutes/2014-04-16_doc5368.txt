April 16, 2014Town Council Meeting Minutes

1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, & PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

Mayor MacDougall called the meeting to order at 7:01 p.m.

Council Member Peggy Bell, Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member

Mary Ann Mixon, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin and Mayor Edward MacDougall

Present: 5 -

2. PROCLAMATIONS, AWARDS, AND PRESENTATIONS

A. Proclamation - Health South Anniversary

The Mayor and Council Members presented a proclamation to representatives

from Health South in celebration of their anniversary.

B. Proclamation - Texting While Driving Awareness

The Mayor and Council Members presented a proclamation to the members of

the Cutler Bay Academy of Advanced Studies - Centennial Campus in recognition

of texting while driving awareness.

C. Recognition of Senior Games Participants

Sponsors: Sue Ellen Loyzelle

The Mayor and Council Members presented certificates of thanks to the

participants of the Senior Games which were recently held in the Town of Cutler

Bay.

D. Presentation - Audit Results for Fiscal Year end September 30, 2013 by Alberni,

Caballero & Company, LLP, Certified Public Accountants

Andrew Fierman from the Town's Audit firm Alberni, Caballero & Company, LLC

presented the audit report for the year ending September 30, 2013 to the Town

Council.

3. APPROVAL OF MINUTES

A. · Regular Town Council Meeting, March 19, 2014

· Town Council Workshop, March 19, 2014

· Attorney-Client Session, March 19, 2014

A motion was made by Council Member Mixon, seconded by Vice Mayor Sochin,

that the Minutes be approved. The motion carried by an unanimous vote. (5-0)

Council Member Bell, Council Member Loyzelle, Council Member Mixon, Vice

Mayor Sochin and Mayor MacDougall

Yes: 5 -

4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS

A motion was made by Council Member Bell that she and Mayor MacDougall

attend a meeting with the Town Manager and Superintendent Moran. The motion

received a second from Council Member Mixon. After discussion, Mayor

MacDougall called for the vote and the motion passed 5-0.

Vice Mayor Sochin inquired regarding basketball courts.

Council Member Mixon provided information regarding planned Earth Day

events.

Council Member Loyzelle reported on the Unclaimed Property Town Hall meeting

that she hosted.

Mayor MacDougall reported on the proposed new process of expediting

permitting with electronic transfers to Miami-Dade County and inquired about

updated Charter Official evaluation forms.

5. PUBLIC COMMENTS

Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman advised the public that if anyone wishes to

speak on an item on the Consent Agenda, this would be the appropriate

opportunity.

Bill Meiklejohn spoke regarding the CBBA and also a sign for Rotary.

Vernon Jones spoke regarding Perrine Baseball.

Steve Zarzecki thanked the Town Council for hosting the Senior Games and

invited them to the Concerned Citizens of Cutler Bay upcoming meeting.

Tom Condon congratulated staff on the audit and spoke of comments made by a

Council Member at a previous meeting.

6. REPORTS

A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT

Town Manager Rafael Casals referred to the written monthly report provided to

the Town Council, invited Major Miller to introduce a new Police Officer, invited

Gary Ratay and Alinna Phillips of Kimley-Horn and Associates to report on the

progress of the Caribbean Boulevard roadway project and Joe Corradino of The

Corradino Group to provide an update on the Transportation Master Plan.

B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT

Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman mentioned the filing of a bid protest by Oracle

Elevator and reminded the Council about the settlement agreement that is on this

agenda for Citizen's Awareness.

C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT

Town Clerk Debra Eastman introduced Emily Cordoso from the Town's IT vendor

who has been assisting in the preparation of the various pages which will be

incorporated as a part of the new Town website. Ms. Cordoso provided a quick

review of the pages prepared to date. The Town Clerk reported on the items

which are left to accomplish for the new website, gave an update on the

implementation of the agenda management system and an update on the

candidates who have filed papers with the Town Clerk for the November election.

D. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS

Members of the Town Council reported on the meetings of various Town

Committees.
