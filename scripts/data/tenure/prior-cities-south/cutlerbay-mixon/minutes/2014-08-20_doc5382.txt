2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Fiscal Year 2014-15 Proposed Budget (2nd Council Budget Workshop)

Sponsors: Town Manager's Office

Town Manager Rafael Casals explained that this is the second budget workshop

that the Town Council has held this year and that he has already met individually

with all the members of the Town Council regarding the proposed budget.

Town Manager Casals and Town Attorney Mitch Bierman explained the decrease

in revenue from the franchise fees, the fee structure and the exemptions.

Town Manager Casals explained the planned dip of 3.48 million which will

include the loss of franchise fees and the principal payment on the Town Center

Building.

Council Member Bell suggested that the Town Council go to the roll back rate

this year. Council Member Loyzelle agreed and provided some line item cuts.

There was discussion regarding the snap back benefits for the police.

Council Member Bell indicated that the strategic plan meetings have identified a

concern from the voters for the Town to provide a new pool. She suggested that

$12,000 be placed in the budget toward the cost of looking at the possibility for a

new pool.

Finance Director Robert Daddario reminded the Town Council of the Budget

Hearing Dates established at September 9, 2014 and September 23, 2014 at 7 p.m.

3. Adjournment

The meeting adjourned at 4:05 p.m.
