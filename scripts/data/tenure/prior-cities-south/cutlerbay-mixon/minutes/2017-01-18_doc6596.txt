 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT
 The Town Attorney had no report.
 C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT
 Interim Town Clerk Debra Eastman thanked the Town Council for appointing her
as the Interim Town Clerk. She reported on the status of filling the vacant
position of Assistant to the Town Clerk and has been working with Municode to
update ordinances for 2016 and recodification. Codification of the Town's
ordinaces has been set semi-annually. She explained the use of the Ordbank and
other features. She reported on other practices and procedures within the Town
Clerk's office and the planned Ethics Training in February and the renewal of
lobbyist registrations for 2017. Vice Mayor Loyzelle suggested that the
Committees be invited to the Ethics Training. Mayor Bell suggested that a
workshop be held with all of the committee members to update them on ethics
and sunshine requirements.

 D. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS
 Council Member Coriat announced the next Education Advisory Committee on
February 2, 2017 and informed that the Parks and Recreation Committee meeting
in January was cancelled due to a lack of quorum. He announced Cutler Bay
Chili Day which will take place at Cutler Ridge Park on Jan. 21, 2017 and the Tour

Committee meeting and the discussion of their strategic master plan. Vice Mayor
Loyzelle spoke of a town survey. Town Manager Ralph Casals suggested that
the survey may be merged with other pending survey subjects for town services,
open space and an AARP Age-Friendly community. Mayor Bell suggested an
on-line survey might be considered.
Mayor Bell reported on the proposed trip to Tallahassee for the Youth Council
Committee and the estimated cost of $4,000. Council Member Coriat suggested
that the members may want to set up a "Go Fund Me" page to get the word out.

with the Cutler Bay Business Association.

committees. Mayor Bell explained that the policy will be on a case by case basis
as discussed in the Workshop. Town Attorney Mitch Bierman provided
clarification regarding liability for travel and he will provide information to the
Town Council on the policies from other municipalities.

 14. MAYOR AND COUNCIL COMMENTS
 A.   ·   Discussion Regarding Advisory Committee Unification of Bylaws
(Vice Mayor Loyzelle)
 Vice Mayor Loyzelle suggested that there be a Unified Bylaws Committee made
up of one person from each of the committees except the Youth Council
Committee and the Floodplain Master Plan Committee. After discussion Vice
Mayor Loyzelle made a motion to form an information gathering group to work
with Council Member Callahan to form a uniform set of bylaws. The motion
received a second from Council Member Callahan. Mayor Bell called for the vote
and the motion failed 3-2 with Mayor Bell, Council Member Coriat and Council
Member Mixon voting in opposition. After further discussion, Mayor Bell made a
motion to create a task force solely for the creation of universal bylaws with
Council Member Callahan serving as the liaison and that the committee will
sunset once a recommendation has been made to the Town Council. Council
Member Callahan gave a second to the motion. Mayor Bell called for the vote
and all were in favor.
