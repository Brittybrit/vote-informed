 8:00 PM   Council Chambers Tuesday, October 6, 2015
 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, MOMENT OF
SILENCE & INVOCATION
 Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 8:02PM. Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline
Wilson called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance.
Mayor Bell polled the Council to establish a voting procedure to select a Town
Clerk. The general consensus of the Council was for each member to submit a
ballot listing the selected candidate of choice to the Town Attorney for tabulation.
 Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat
 Present:   5 -
 2. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell opened Public Comments and there were none.
 3. RESOLUTIONS
 Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. The Town
Council submitted ballots to the Town Attorney for tabulation. Town Attorney
Mitchell Bierman announced Jacqueline Wilson as the selected candidate for the
Town Clerk position by majority vote of the Town Council.
 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, APPOINTING THE TOWN CLERK AND AUTHORIZING
THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN ENGAGEMENT LETTER ON THE BEHALF OF THE
TOWN; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE
 A motion was made by Council Member Mixon, seconded by Vice Mayor Sochin,
that this Resolution be adopted and enter in with Jacqueline Wilson. Council
Member Loyzelle spoke in opposition of the motion. Vice Mayor Sochin spoke in
favor of the motion. Council Member Mixon spoke in favor of the motion. Mayor
Bell spoke in favor of the motion. After discussion, the motion carried by the
following vote:
