1. Call to Order, Roll Call, & Pledge of Allegiance

Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 3:05PM. Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline

Wilson called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,

Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present 5 -

Fiscal Year 2015-16 Proposed Budget

(First Council Budget Workshop)

Town Manager Rafael Casals announced the upcoming Budget meetings

including the Second Budget Workshop on August 19, 2015, the First Budget

Hearing on September 10, 2015 and the Second Budget Hearing on September

24, 2015.

Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced Finance Director Robert Daddario to

provide a brief collective overview of the FIscal Year 2015-2016 Proposed Budget

based on the 2.5348 ceiling millage rate in accordance with the July 15, 2015

Regular Town Council Meeting.

Finance Director Robert Daddario and Town Manager Rafael Casals provided an

overview of the FIscal Year 2015-2016 Proposed Budget components and

explained the general fund budget for each department.

There was discussion regarding cost recovery.

Finance Director Robbert Daddario provided a breakdown of the Special Revenue

Fund and Special Revenue Projects and addressed the net change for the

proposed Fiscal Year 2015-2016 Budget versus the adopted Fiscal Year

2014-2015 Budget.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Council Member Loyzelle suggested incorporation of travel funds for committees

and funding to facilitate staff training to develop the succession plan for the

Town Manager. Council Member Loyzelle spoke in favor of focusing on public

relations for the Town and utilizing the Economic Growth Committee to assist

with the Town website and newsletter.

Vice Mayor Sochin thanked staff for the presentation and spoke in favor of
