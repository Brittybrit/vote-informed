October 30, 2014 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, & PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
 Town Clerk Debra Eastman called the roll with the following Council Members in
attendance: Council Member Bell, Council Member Loyzelle, Council Member
Mixon, Vice Mayor Sochin. Mayor MacDougall was in attendance and was seated
in the audience. Vice Mayor Sochin chaired the meeting.
 Council Member Peggy Bell, Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member
Mary Ann Mixon, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin and Mayor Edward MacDougall
 Present:   5 -
 2. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 There were no public comments at this time.
 3. QUASI-JUDICIAL HEARINGS (PUBLIC HEARING REQUIRED)
 A.   PUBLIC HEARING REGARDING ALLEGATION OF CHARTER VIOLATION BY
MAYOR.
 Town Attorneys Bierman and Friedman provided the Town Council with a memo
with suggested procedures for the public hearing. A motion was made by
Council Member Mixon and received a second from Council Member Bell to
follow the procedures as outlined by the Town Attorneys. Vice Mayor Sochin
called for the vote and the motion carried 4-0.
Council Member Loyzelle made a motion to enter the complaint and the
attachments, as provided in the agenda packet, into the record. The motion
received a second from Council Member Mixon. Vice Mayor Sochin called for the
vote and the motion carried 4-0. Town Clerk Debra Eastman gave the oath
anyone wishing to speak.
Complaintant Eduardo Wolmers explained the complaint and his belief that
Mayor MacDougall has violated the Citizens Bill of Rights in the Town of Cutler
Bay Charter. Mr. Wolmers presented five documents as evidence as follows:
Letter from Ed MacDougall, Mayor of Cutler Bay; settlement agreement from
Miami-Dade County Commission on Ethics and Public Trust in the case of
MacDougall v Castillo and Mayol (C13-02); Miami-Dade County Commission on
Ethics and Public Trust in the case of MacDougall v Castillo and Mayo (C13-02)
Public Report and Final Order; Election flyer entited "Are you Comfortable?"; and
Election flyer entitled "A letter from the Mayor of Cutler Bay".
Mayor Edward MacDougall read the letter that he authored and that is entitled
"From: Ed MacDougall, Mayor of Cutler Bay". He spoke of the Black's law
dictionary definition of "guilty" and that he believes the complaint has no merit.
Members of the Town Council asked questions of Mayor MacDougall. Mr.
Wolmers was given an opportunity for rebuttal.
Vice Mayor Sochin suggested that the Mayor be given an opportunity to pay a
fine and that the complaint be dropped. Mayor MacDougall was not in favor of
the suggestion.
Vice Mayor Sochin opened the public hearing. John Sykes, Steve Zarzecki, Stan
Hutton, Ralph Geronimo, Ron Derra, Richard Rebuth and Eugene Taccolini
spoke. There being no one else wishing to speak, Vice Mayor Sochin closed the
public hearing. The Town Council discussed the item.
Vice Mayor Sochin passed the gavel to Council Member Loyzelle.
A motion was made by Vice Mayor Sochin that Mayor MacDougall violated the

Town of Cutler Bay Town Charter Citizens’ Bill of Rights, specifically Section (A)
(14), Misuse of Authority and recommended a reprimand and a fine of $50. The
motion received a second from Council Member Bell. After further discussion,
Vice Mayor Sochin amended the motion to remove the fine of $50 and added to
impose a censure. The amendment received a second from Council Member
Bell.
Town Clerk Debra Eastman called the roll and the amended motion carried 4-0.
At the request of the complainant, Ed Wolmers, Vice Mayor Sochin further
amended the motion to include a violation of Section (A) (2) of the Citizens’ Bill of
Rights, Truth in Government. The amendment received a second from Council
Member Mixon.
Town Clerk Debra Eastman called the roll and the amendment carried 4-0.
