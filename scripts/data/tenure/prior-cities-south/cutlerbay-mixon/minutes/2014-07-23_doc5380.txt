Interim Community Development Director Kathryn Lyon provided the facts of the

application of Coco Bay, LLC and introduced Fernando Martinez and Mario

Aguilar who explained the project.

Council Member Peggy questioned the responsibility for maintenance of the

fence. Town Attorney Chad Friedman pointed out the perimeter wall fence and

suggested that there may need to be a provision to require the owners to

maintain the fence in a uniform way and the applicant agreed.

In response to a concern of visable likeness in the renderings of the homes

voiced by Mayor MacDougall, the applicant agreed to bring additional renderings
