 4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Council Member Coriat announced that he attended an event held by Dr. Feldman
recognizing two students for their achievements from every school in District 9.
Council Member Coriat also attended the January meeting of the Concerened
Citizens where they discussed the South Florida Water Management project and
also general ideas and theories about what can be done with the property.
Council Member Mixon encouraged the Town Council to participate in the
wetlands clean up at Black Point, along with students from Whigham Elementary
School on January 31.
Council Member Loyzelle apologized for her late arrival and encouraged the
members of the Town Council to attend the Transportation Summit on January
22. She reported on the ethics training on January 12 and encouraged
attendance at the grand opening of the Florida Technical College at Southland
Mall on January 28.
Mayor Bell reported that the Chili Day event was a huge success and that she
attended a Mayor's Roundtable and participated in the sea level rise discussion.
Mayor Bell has participated in a conference regarding legislation at the League of
Cities and they will be adding texting while driving to their legislative agenda.
Mayor Bell reported on her attendance at the Martin Luther King, Jr. Day parade
and an event where Congresswoman Frederica Wilson will give college
scholarships. Mayor Bell will be attending the Transportation Summit, has
toured the restoration project at Cutler Bay/Palmetto Bay, and annouced that on
January 22 at the Miami Dade County Library there will be a meeting of the Miami
Dade County Relations Board to discuss items of concern. Mayor Bell
announced the Shoes for Haiti project and requested that gently used shoes be
donated by using the box at the Town's reception desk.
 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Ed Wolmers asked about the signal light at the intersection of Caribbean Blvd
and Franjo Road. Town Manager Ralph Casals later in the meeting responded to
Mr. Wolmers that he will discuss this with Miami Dade County signalization unit
for a check of the light.
Steve Zarzecki renewed the annual invitation to the Town Council and staff to
attend the Concerned Citizens meetings on the first Tuesday of each month.
Amy Roda asked about any considered zoning changes. Town Attorney Mitch
Bierman addressed the process for rezoning, public hearings for site plan
approval and open workshops held by the Town Council.
Eduardo Varona asked for the opportunity to come before the Town Council with
a 25 minutes update on the work done to the wetlands property.
 6. REPORTS
 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT
 Town Manager Ralph Casals presented his report to the Town Council. Gary
Ratay and Alina Phillips presented an update to the Caribbean Boulevard
Roadway Improvement Project.
Town Manager Ralph Casals reported on the progress of sign replacements in
town.

 Town Attorney Mitch Bierman reported on the case of Realty Associates Funds v

 C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT
 Town Clerk Debra Eastman reported that in 2014 the Clerk's office performed
1,507 lien searches and collected fees in excess of $87,000. Town Clerk Debra

installation gala on Saturday, February 7 at Parrot Jungle Island. Town Clerk

 D. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS
 Council Member Loyzelle reported on the Fall Prevention Workshop conducted
by the Community for a Lifetime Advisory Committee and the Division of Elder
Affairs and thanked Mayor Bell for attending and gave notice that there were
brochures regarding this subject at the back of the room on the table. Council
Member Loyzelle announced that the next meeting of the Community for a
Lifetime Advisory Committee will be on February 4 at 6 p.m. Council Member
Loyzelle announced that the Founder's Day event will commemmorate the
Town's tenth anniversary and will be held on February 21, 2015 at Cutler Ridge
Park.
 ·   Discussion regarding Committees
 Mayor Bell spoke of the number of committees that have been appointed since
incorporation. She urged the members of the Town Council to be mindful of
using staff time to augment town committees and urged that each committee be
goal oriented and self sustaining. Mayor Bell suggested that each committee
provide a final report around June.
Mayor Bell suggested that due to her Mayoral duties a new liaison be appointed
to the Parks and Recreation Advisory Commitee. Council Member Roger Coriat
volunteered to serve as the liaison to the Parks and Recreation Advisory
Committee. Town Manager Casals stated that Town staff will prepare the
appointment for the next Town Council meeting.

 7A. RESOLUTIONS
 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, APPROVING THE INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN
MIAMI-DADE COUNTY AND TOWN OF CUTLER BAY FOR LOCAL POLICE
SERVICES; AUTHORIZING AND DIRECTING THE TOWN MANAGER TO EXECUTE
SAID AGREEMENT; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the title of the Resolution. Town Manager Ralph
Casals explained that this resolution will authorize the Town Manager to enter
into an interlocal agreement with Miami-Dade County for local police services.
Town Manager Casals introduced Miami Dade County Police Chief Hernan
Organvidez who answered questions from the Town Council about publishing
crime statistics and securing additional police officers in conjunction with future
Town budgets. Chief Organvidez agreed to assist the Town Manager in
establishment of a Police Explorer Post in Cutler Bay. A motion was made by
Council Member Mixon, seconded by Council Member Loyzelle, that this
Resolution be adopted. The motion carried by the following vote:
Yes:   Mayor Peggy Bell, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon, Council Member Sue Ellen
Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat
