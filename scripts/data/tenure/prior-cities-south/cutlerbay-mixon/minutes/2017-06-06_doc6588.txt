A. · Discussion on Louise Lockwood Memorial Butterfly Garden (6:30 p.m. to 7:00 p.m.)

Town Manager Ralph Casals explained that he revisited Whispering Pines Park

and is presenting some cost saving measures. Vice Mayor Loyzelle spoke in

favor of planting the butterfly garden and would like to have it completed for the

July 4 celebration. She requested a consensus at this workshop so that work

could begin and will bring the item back for a vote at the June 21, 2017 Town

Council meeting. There was discussion regarding the trees and other plants and

relocating the plaque to the new location. There was concern regarding the

consistent watering of the new plantings. Council Member Mixon suggested that

as an alternative

a certificate and scholarship be given to a student annually as an award for

service to the community.

Mayor Bell acknowledge two gentlemen in the audience and permitted them to

speak. Mr. Adam Skowronski explained the need for all of the planned

plantings. Mr. Skowronski spoke in favor of planning the garden and

encouraged watering of the plants. There was discussion of getting volunteers to

assist in the watering process.

Council Member Callahan suggested that the entire budget, including the

rededication plaque be set at $1,500. Mayor Bell recapped that the total budget

would be $1,500 to move the stone, add a small plaque to rededicate the garden,

and a few plants, starting with a smaller area, using the relocated benches and

see what the Town Manager can put together for $1,500. Mayor Bell asked for

consensus and there was agreement. Council Member Coriat referred to this as

B. · Fiscal Year 2017-18 Budget Visioning Workshop (7:00 p.m.)

The meeting reconvened at 7:28 p.m. Town Manager Rafael Casals opened the

discussion with an explanation of possible new legislation that may impact

revenue and provided the dates and times for future budget workshops for the

Town Council.

Council Member Mixon informed the Council of her re-appointment to the Florida

League of Cities Utilities, Natural Resources & Public Works Legislative Policy

Committee.

Town Manager Rafael Casals discussed major projects that will be carried over

into the 2017-18 projected budget from last fiscal year, including basketball

courts, hardening of Town Center building windows, and a new HVAC unit for the

Town Center building. There was discussion on the urban expansion area, the

upcoming annexation and basketball court workshop scheduled for July 31, 2017,

the Town Center Plaza project, remaining park impact fees, studies on purchase

of property cost, and possibly touring vacant properties.

Vice Mayor Loyzelle spoke on the need for a Public Information Officer for the

Town. There was discussion about adding a bus stop at the mall, funding for

advisory committees and the Deputy Clerk position.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Council Member Coriat suggested each Council member provide a Council

Report similar to the Town Manager and Mayor Report to streamline Council

Meetings. Vice Mayor Loyzelle spoke in opposition of Council Member reports.

Town Manager Rafael Casals will provide a template for Council Members.

3. Adjournment

The meeting adjourned at 9:30 p.m.
