A. · Canoe/Kayak Launch Project Presentation - Marlin Engineering, Inc.

Mr. Miguel Soria of Marlin Engineering gave a presentation and overview of the

proposed canoe/kayak launch which included minutes from meetings held by the

Town's Parks and Recreation Committee. Mayor Bell called for a recess at 7:24

p.m. to give the Town Council an opportunity to read the meeting minutes.

Mayor Bell resumed the meeting at 7:33 p.m.

Mr. Soria reviewed the preliminary plan and Council Members commented on

additional parking and number of current spaces, adjacent land, dog friendly

park, use for camping, school activities and educational programs, possible

funding of trees, the number of and cost of the small and large pavillions and the

possibility of adding bathrooms. They discussed lighting, potential for additional

seating, future plantings and the funding of the project.

Mayor Bell thanked Mr. Soria for the presentation.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion
