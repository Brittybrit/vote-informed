Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 4:08PM. Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson

called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman provided a presentation on the Sunshine Law

with an overview of basic requirements and case examples pertinent to the law.

Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman answered questions and stressed the

importance of proceeding with caution.

Town Attorney Alison Smith provided a presentation "Tackling the Social Media

Monster" explaining the pitfalls as well as the benefits of Social Media use and

the Public Records aspect. Town Attorney Alison Smith answered questions and

stressed the importance of proceeding with caution.

The meeting briefly recessed at 6:34PM and reconvened at 6:48PM.

Town Attorney Robert Meyers provided a presentation on State and County

Ethics Provisions. Town Attorney Robert Meyers answered questions and

stressed the importance of proceeding with caution.
