5:00 PM Council ChambersWednesday, September 30, 2015

1. Call to Order, Roll Call, & Pledge of Allegiance

Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 5:16PM. Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline

Wilson called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance. Vice Mayor

Sochin arrived at 5:36PM.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,

Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present 5 -

· Overview of Town's Business Planning Process

Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced Mr. Merrett Stierheim and Mr. Michael

Levinson to present an overview of the the Town Business Planning Process.

· Overview of Town's Florida Green Building Coalition (FGBC) Recertification

Process

Town Manager Rafael Casals referred to the Town as the first municipality in

Miami-Dade County to obtain the Florida Green Building Coalition Silver

Certification. Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced Planning and Zoning

Director Kathryn Lyon to discuss the Town Florida Green Building Coalition

(FGBC) Recertification Process and Mr. Henry Iler of Iler Planning for a brief

presentation on the Florida Green Building Coalition (FGBC) Recertification. At

the request of Council Member Coriat, Mr. Iler confirmed that the Town would

require thirty (30) more points beyond the fourty three (43) the Town has already

applied for to reach Gold and Platinum status.

There was discussion on Green Master Plan development.

Planning and Zoning Director Kathryn Lyon informed the Town Council an

upcoming Ordinance regarding peddlers and solicitors in the right of way on

major roadways in the Town.

· Code Compliance-Planning and Zoning Recommended Changes to Land

Development Regulations

Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced Building and Code Compliance Director

Sandra Aronoff to present a brief overview of the Top Ten Code Compliance

· Public Works and Stormwater Utility Recommended Changes to Town Code

Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced Public Works Director Alfredo Quintero

to brief the Council on potential changes to the Town Flood Management

Ordinance. Mayor Bell spoke in opposition of County service on the crossroad at

216 Street and Old Cutler Road.

· Charter Officials 2015 Annual Performance Reviews

Mayor Bell reported on 2015 Charter Official Annual Performance Reviews and

presented recommendations to the Town Council. After discussion, the Council

came to a general consensus for Town Manager Rafael Casals Employment

Agreement Salary Section 3.1. After further discussion, there was a consensus

to consult the Town Attorney to revise Section 6.4 language of the Town Manager

Employment Agreement.

· Discussion Regarding the Guidelines for Selecting Potential Candidates for the

Town Clerk Position

Town Manager Rafael Casals and Town Attorney Chad Friedman explained the

voting process for selecting finalists to interview for the Town Clerk position.

The Town Council submitted votes to Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson; the

meeting recessed at 8:44PM to tally the votes and reconvened at 8:52PM. Town

Attorney Chad Friedman advised a second round of voting was in order and

announced the finalists: Jacqueline Wilson, Elizabeth Sewell, Barbara Herrera,

Alan Eisenberg. Mayor Bell suggested a Special Town Council Meeting

immediately following finalist interviews on October 6, 2015 to select a Town

Clerk.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

There were no questions or comments.

3. Adjournment

A motion was made by Vice Mayor Sochin to adjourn. The motion received a

second from Council Member Coriat. Mayor Bell adjourned the meeting at

9:13PM.
