January 20, 2016 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Council Member Loyzelle reported on attendance at the Chili Cook Off and Golf
Cart Rodeo Event and thanked all involved for their efforts.
Vice Mayor Sochin spoke on efforts to remove golf cart access restriction to
Black Point Marina and reported on attendance at the Martin Luther King Jr.
Parade.
Mayor Bell echoed gratitude for all involved with golf cart outreach and
legislative efforts for golf cart access.
Council Member Coriat reported on attendance at the From Home to Home
Exhibit at the Alper JCC Childrens Museum and on attendance at the Symposium
for Human Trafficking at Southwood Middle School.
Council Member Mixon reported on attendance at the Human Trafficking
Discussion at Cutler Bay Senior High School.
Mayor Bell also spoke on Human Trafficking issue and is working with the Town
Manager to arrange a Human Trafficking Symposium at Town Hall to raise
awareness. Mayor Bell reported on the State of the Town Address also reported
on attendance at several events and meetings. Mayor Bell also reported on
attendance at the Miami-Dade Board of County Commissioners Meeting and
announced approval of the Town's funding request for the extension of the
Caribbean Boulevard Roadway Improvement Project Gap#2. Mayor Bell thanked
Commissioner Daniella Levine Cava for sponsoring this item, the Board of
County Commissioners for approval and Commissioner Dennis Moss for
support. Mayor Bell also reported on a meeting with newly appointed
Metropolitan Planning Organization Director Aileen Bouclé.
Council Member Loyzelle announced Miami-Dade Animal Services Blanket Drive
accepting donations through January 30, 2016 and Miami-Dade County Days in
Tallahassee on February 8-10, 2016.
 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell opened Public Comments. Mr. James Woodard thanked Mayor Bell
and Council Member Mixon for sponsoring a Resolution opposing bear hunting
and expressed support. Mr. Steve Zarzecki reported on a traffic accident that
occured on January 19, 2016 and expressed traffic concerns. Mr. Arthur "AJ"
Meyer spoke in favor of supporting the City of Homestead Resolution urging
Miami-Dade County to move forward with the relocation of the Dade County
Youth Fair to South Dade. Mr. Meyer also reported on recent appointment to the
Miami-Dade Expressway Authority. Mr. Calvin Gilles expressed concern with the
92nd Avenue and 216 Street intersection suggested implementation of a
roundabout. Ms. Rosa Gonzalez expressed traffic concerns associated with the
Caribbean Boulevard Roadway Improvement Project planned detour. Mr. Ricky
Fernandez expressed traffic concerns associated with the Caribbean Boulevard
Roadway Improvement Project planned detour. Mr. Henry Clifford expressed
enthusiasm with Old Cutler Road and Caribbean Boulevard roadway projects and
spoke on traffic calming and in support of the Resolution opposing bear hunting.
Mayor Bell closed Public Comments.

 ·   Robert Daddario, Finance Department- Quarterly Financial Report
 Finance Director Robert Daddario presented an overview of the Town's Quarterly

 C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson announced the State of the Town Address video
has been uploaded to the Town website. Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson also
announced several upcoming events and requested clarification from the Council
regarding a potential February Workshop to discuss various items. A motion
was made by Council Member Loyzelle, seconded by Council Member Mixon to
schedule a Town Council Workshop on February 24, 2016 at 6PM. Mayor Bell
called for the vote and the motion passed 4-1 with Vice Mayor Sochin in
opposition. Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson also reported on the vacant position
in the Town Clerk Office.
 D. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS
 Council Member Coriat reported on the progress of the Parks and Recreation
Committee and the installation of the Weatherbug Lightening Detection System
at Cutler Ridge Park, Franjo Park, Lakes by the Bay Park and Bel Air Park.
Council Member Coriat reported on current and upcoming park maintenance
projects. Council Member Coriat also announced the Tour de Parks Bike Ride
Event on January 23, 2016, the Wings Over the Bay Event on February 20, 2016
and the next Parks and Recreation Committee Meeting on February 1, 2016.
Council Member Mixon reported on the progress of the Floodplain Mitigation
Plan Committee and announced the next committee meeting on March 24, 2016.
Vice Mayor Sochin reported on the progress of the Education Advisory
Committee and a new homework initiative.
Council Member Loyzelle reported on the progress of the Communities for a
Lifetime Committee and announced the next committee meeting on February 3,
2016. Council Member Loyzelle also reported on a pending resignation which
will result in a vacancy on the committee. There was discussion regarding other
pending committee vacancies. Council Member Loyzelle announced the
Communities for a Lifetime Special Committee Meeting on February 6, 2016 to
complete their Strategic Plan.
