1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, & MOMENT OF

SILENCE

The meeting was called to order by Mayor Peggy Bell at 7:05 p.m.

Mayor Bell requested a moment of silence and prayers for those who have lost

loved ones, the Middle East troubles and the people of Hawaii.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member Mary Ann

Mixon, Council Member Michael P. Callahan and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present: 5 -

A. · Introduction of Florida State Representative House District 114, Javier E. Fernandez

Newly elected Florida State Representative, House District 114, Javier E.

Fernandez thanked the members of the Town Council for inviting him to the

meeting and for their support. Representative Fernandez indicated that he will

be preparing for the next legislative session and will be reaching out to the Town

Council. Mayor Peggy Bell indicated that "rail" is a priority to the people of

Cutler Bay.

Students from F C Martin Elementary School explained their save the manatee

project and provided the Town Council with a copy of the brochure they

produced. Mayor Bell thanked them and Vice Mayor Loyzelle for her

involvement.

2. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS, BOARD & COMMITTEE REPORTS

A. · Discussion Charter Revision Commission Proposals

Town Attorney Mitch Bierman reviewed the report of the Charter Revision

Commission and explained each of the recommendations made by the

Commission. The Town Council discussed each item on the report and

ultimately agreed to place four questions on the November General Election

ballot, November 6, 2018. The Town Council instructed the Town Attorney to

prepare a resolution for the Town Council to accomplish this and directed the

4. REPORTS

A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT

· Town Manager Monthly Report May 2018

Town Manager Ralph Casals announced that the Florida Planning and Zoning

Association recently presented the Town with the 2018 Sustainability Award for

our Green Master Plan. He thanked the Town's consultant, Mr. Iler and Director

Kathryn Lyon for their work on the plan.

Town Manager Ralph Casals reported that the potential tenant for the Penthouse

floor of the Town Center signed a letter of intent which was actively negotiated

and has now decided not to go forward. The Town Attorney is seeking

reimbursement for expenses incurred for plans.

Town Manager Ralph Casals informed the Town Council of the receipt of two

letters of interest in purchasing the Town Center Building and adjacent land.

The offers have been deemed unacceptable and no action will be taken.

Town Manager Ralph Casals informed the Town Council about his recent

outreach activity for the GO Bond. He informed the Town Council about

upcoming meetings being held in the area regarding the rapid transit project.

Town Manager Ralph Casals reported that he has a letter of intent for the Garcia

property. He is still working to achieve the survey, environmental study and two

appraisals. Mayor Bell thanked the Town Council for allowing her to be involved

in the process along with the Town Manager and Town Attorney.

· Robert Daddario, Finance Department-Quarterly Financial Report

Finance Director Robert Daddario provided an overview of the quarterly financial

report.
