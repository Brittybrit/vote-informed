July 16, 2014 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, & PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
 Mayor MacDougall called the meeting to order at 7 p.m. Town Clerk Debra
Eastman called the roll. Council Members Peggy Bell, Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Mary
Ann Mixon, and Mayor MacDougall were in attendance. Vice Mayor Sochin
arrived at 7:02 p.m.
Mayor MacDougall led the Pledge of Allegiance.
 Council Member Peggy Bell, Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member
Mary Ann Mixon, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin and Mayor Edward MacDougall
 Present:   5 -
 2. PROCLAMATIONS, AWARDS, AND PRESENTATIONS
 ·   Proclamation, Yamilla Carballo, Former Principal, Cutler Bay Academy of Advanced
Studies, Centennial Campus
 Mayor MacDougall and the Town Council presented a proclamation to Yamilla
Carballo commending her service and dedication to the students of the Town of
Cutler Bay as the Principal of the Cutler Bay Academy of Advanced Studies,
Centennial Campus and wished her well in her future endeavors. Ms. Carballo
thanked the Town for their support throughout her tenure.
Town Manager Rafael Casals, along with the Mayor and Town Council presented
an award recently received by Public Works Director Alfredo Quintero as the
Professional Public Works Manager of the year for the Old Cutler Road project.
 3. APPROVAL OF MINUTES
 ·   Regular Town Council Meeting, June 18, 2014
 A motion was made by Council Member Loyzelle, seconded by Council Member
Mixon, that the Minutes be approved. The motion carried by an unanimous vote.
(5-0)
 4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Council Members reported on ongoing various projects and events.
 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Town Attorney Mitch Bierman reminded that the items on the consent agenda
will be voted at once, and that if anyone from the public wished to speak
regarding those items they may wish to do so at this time.
Rosi Alvarez spoke regarding policy and procedures with regard to the Education
Advisory Board and suggested that the policies be revisited.
There was a discussion regarding the use of members of the Town staff as it
relates to the operation of Town committees.
Don Anthony spoke in support of the pet ordinance on the agenda for first
reading. Commissioner Michele Lazarow, Hallandale Beach spoke in favor of the
pet ordinance. Town of Bay Harbor Islands Council Member Kelly Reid also
spoke in favor of the pet ordinance.

 6. REPORTS
 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT
 Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced the new Parks and Recreation Director
Etienne Bejarano and he was welcomed to Cutler Bay. Mr. Casals reported on
various meetings held the previous month, the strategic master plan, the
transportation master plan, and that there will be a budget workshop on July 23.
Gary Ratay of Kimley Horn provided an update on the Caribbean Boulevard
project. Council Member Bell thanked the Town Manager for prudently moving a
budget workshop to July. Council Member Loyzelle asked for an update on the
PACE program and the Town Manager provided an update.
 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT
 Town Attorney Mitch Bierman provided an update on the review of information
by legal as part of the recodification of the Town ordinances.
 C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT
 Town Clerk Debra Eastman provided an update the status of candidates for
Mayor and Town Council, and update the status of the construction of the new
website, and reiterated the status of the recodification of the town code.
 D. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS
 Council Members provided updates to various committee meetings and projects.

WITHIN THE TOWN; PROVIDING FOR PROHIBITIONS; PROVIDING FOR

DATE.
 Sponsors:   Mayor Peggy Bell
 Council Member Bell made a motion to approve on first reading and to amend
section 2C, existing pet stores, to reflect the prohibition in that section be
effective immediately rather than for it to take effect in six months. The motion
received a second from Council Member Mixon. Council Member Loyzelle
expressed opposition. Vice Mayor Sochin expressed that he would like an
opportunity to study further.
