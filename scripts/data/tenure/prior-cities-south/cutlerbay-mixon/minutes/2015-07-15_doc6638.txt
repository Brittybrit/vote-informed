Mixon. Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion passed 4-1 with Vice Mayor
Sochin in opposition. Council Member Loyzelle reported on attendance at the
Underline Meeting on June 25, 2015. Council Member Loyzelle also reported on

Loyzelle announced Wetland Restoration Project Volunteer events on July 25,

announced upcoming conferences including the Florida Council on Aging

August 13-15, 2015. Vice Mayor Sochin reported on attendance at the Concerned
Citizens Meeting. Council Member Loyzelle announced Miami-Dade County
Budget Workshop on July 22, 2015. Mayor Bell announced the Miami-Dade
C.O.R.E. 2015 Hurricane

 6. REPORTS
 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT

and the Second Budget Workshop on August 19, 2015.
 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT

Assistant to Town Clerk position will be accepted until July 31, 2015 and
announced the Florida League of Cities Annual Conference August 13-15, 2015 in

 D. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS
 Council Member Coriat reported on the Parks and Recreation Committee and
announced the next Parks and Recreation Committee meeting on August 3, 2015.
Council Member Mixon reported on the progress of the Floodplain Mitigation
Plan Committee in efforts to earn points and lower insurance rates with recent
approval from the State of Florida and the Federal Emergency Management

 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, DETERMINING THE PROPOSED MILLAGE RATE, AND
THE DATE, TIME AND PLACE FOR THE FIRST AND SECOND PUBLIC BUDGET
HEARINGS AS REQUIRED BY LAW; DIRECTING THE TOWN CLERK AND TOWN
MANAGER TO FILE THIS RESOLUTION WITH THE PROPERTY APPRAISER OF
MIAMI-DADE COUNTY PURSUANT TO THE REQUIREMENTS OF FLORIDA
STATUTES AND THE RULES AND REGULATIONS OF THE DEPARTMENT OF
REVENUE OF THE STATE OF FLORIDA; AND PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman briefly explained that Florida Law requires the
Council to adopt a maximum millage rate in preparation for the adoption of the
budget in September. Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the
Resolution. A motion was made by Vice Mayor Sochin, seconded by Council
Member Mixon, that this Resolution be adopted. Mayor Bell suggested amending
to reflect initial ceiling of 2.49 instead of 2.5348. After discussion, the motion
carried by the following vote:
