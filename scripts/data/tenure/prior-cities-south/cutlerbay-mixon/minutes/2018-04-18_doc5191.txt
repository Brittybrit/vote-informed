A. AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, AMENDING THE TOWN’S COMPREHENSIVE PLAN FUTURE LAND

USE ELEMENT MIXED USE CATEGORY LAKES-BY-THE-BAY TO PERMIT

RESIDENTIAL USES AS A PART OF A MIXED USE PROJECT; PROVIDING FOR

TRANSMITTAL; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICTS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND

PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the title both ordinances 8A and 8B for first

reading. Applicant's Attorney Juan Mayol provided a power point presentation to

the Town Council explaining the elements of the proposal. Town of Cutler Bay

consultant Alex David of Calvin, Giordano, Inc. provided the details of the review

of the appllication and recommended approval. There was discussion regarding

traffic and traffic study methodology, lack of community facilities, density,

commercial uses and residential use.

Mayor Bell opened the Public Hearing. Ed Varona, Rizelda Ruiz, Antonia

Bucacci, Stephan Botbol, Paul Mathieson, spoke in opposition. Luis Hernandez

spoke in favor.

Attorney Juan Mayol rebutted in support of the project.

A motion was made by Council Member Callahan, seconded by Council Member

Mixon, that this Ordinance be denied. There was discusion regarding amending

the comprehensive plan to permit residential uses to be added and the impact

upon alerady high traffic volume. After discussion, Mayor Bell called for the vote

and the motion carried by the following vote:

Yes: Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member Mary Ann

Mixon, Council Member Michael Callahan and Council Member Roger Coriat
