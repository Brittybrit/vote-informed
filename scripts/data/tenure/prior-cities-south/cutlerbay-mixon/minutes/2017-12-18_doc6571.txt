1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE & MOMENT OF

SILENCE

Vice Mayor Sue Loyzelle called the meetinhg to order at 6:35 p.m. Town Clerk

Debra Eastman called he roll with the following in attendance: Council Members

Roger Coriat, Michael Callahan and Mary Ann Mixon and Vice Mayor Sue

Loyzelle. Vice Mayor Sue Loyzelle led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Vice Mayor Sue Loyzelle informed the Town Council about the annual Dr. Martin

Luther King parade and requested concensus for participation on January 13,

2018. After discussion with the Town Manager regarding requested resources

consensus was reached.

Mayor Peggy Bell arrived at 6:38 p.m. and Vice Mayor Sue Loyzelle turned the

gavel to Mayor Bell. Mayor Bell requested a moment of silence for the victims

and families of the train wreck tragedy in Washington state.

A. · Town Center Plaza Next Steps

Town Manager Ralph Casals explained that the Town Council adopted

resolutions regarding this project. Subsequently, the Town Council did not adopt

a proposed resolution to rescind their previous action. However, the Town

Council agreed to step back and review the project before taking further action.

As Town Manager, he is now seeking direction as to the will of the Town Council

as how to move forward. Mr. Casals introduced George Puig from Kimley Horn

and Associates and provided a hand out with a breakdown of the various

amenities and the estimated cost associated with each of them.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

The Town Council discussed the various amenities and concluded that a major

concern is the lack of public involvement and that at previous input meetings,

where the project was discussed, the ultimate cost may not have been available

or shared adequately. There was also concern that the public may have had

misleading information that the project was only a skate park and not a Town

Center Plaza with many different features including a skate spot. Mr. George

Puig from Kimley-Horn and Associates answered questions for the Town Council

and provided further information by discussing perhaps scaling down or

eliminating custom options.

There was discussion of bonding multiple projects and the process for doing so.

There was discussion of whether the Town Council or the public should select

the various amenities to eliminate or keep and how the amenities could be

phased in yearly perhaps eliminating the need for a bond. There was discussion

about apportioning the soft costs and overhead and the near impossibiity of

doing that accurately.

After discussion on how to move forward with informational meetings, it was

concluded that two additional meetings be held on Saturday, January 20, 2018

and Wednesday, January 24, 2018. If at that point a member of the Town Council

feels more input is warranted they may speak with the Town Manager.

The meeting adjourned at 9:40 p.m.
