First Budget Hearing

1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, & MOMENT OF SILENCE

Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 7:02 p.m. Town Clerk Debra Eastman

called the roll. Mayor Bell thanked everyone for their hard work and dedication

for all they have done in the past few days in the aftermath of Hurricane Irma.

She thanked the Town Manager Ralph Casals and staff and the Police and all

who contributed and provided their assistance during and after Hurricane Irma.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member Mary Ann

Mixon, Council Member Michael P. Callahan and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present: 5 -

2. PUBLIC COMMENTS

Mayor Bell opened public comments. There being no one wishing to speak,

public comments was closed.

3. RESOLUTIONS

A. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE TENTATIVE MILLAGE RATE OF THE TOWN OF

CUTLER BAY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2017 THROUGH

SEPTEMBER 30, 2018 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTES SECTION 200.065; AND

PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the title of the resolution. Finance Director

Robert Daddario read the TRIM statement. Town Manager Ralph Casals

explained the changes made to the budget since the last budget workshop held

by the Town Council.

The Town Council discussed the history of the Town's millage rate and what rate

is appropriate for this year.

Mayor Bell opened the public hearing. There being no one wishing to speak, the

public hearing was closed.

Council Members discussed proposed projects, expenses, possible bonding,

hiring of a public information officer, record retention consultant, holiday lighting

and the need for future workshops. A motion was made by Council Member

Mixon, seconded by Council Member Callahan, that this Resolution be adopted

setting the tentative millage at $2.5199 per $1,000.00 of assessed property value..

The motion carried by a vote of 4-1 with Council Member Coriat voting in

opposition.

B. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE TENTATIVE OPERATING AND CAPITAL OUTLAY

BUDGET FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2017 THROUGH

SEPTEMBER 30, 2018 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTES SECTION 200.065;

AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO MAKE EXPENDITURES CONSISTENT

THEREWITH AND AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO MAKE CERTAIN

BUDGET AMENDMENTS WITHIN A DEPARTMENT PROVIDED THAT THE TOTAL OF

THE APPROPRIATIONS IS NOT CHANGED; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE

DATE.

The Town Council discussed the history of the Town's millage rate and what rate

is appropriate for this year.

Mayor Bell opened the public hearing. There being no one wishing to speak, the

public hearing was closed.

Council Members discussed proposed projects, expenses, possible bonding,

hiring of a public information officer, record retention consultant, holiday lighting

and the need for future workshops A motion was made by Council Member

Mixon, seconded by Council Member Coriat, that this Resolution be adopted

setting the tentative budget at $52,042,577. The motion carried by an unanimous

vote. (5-0)

4. PUBLIC COMMENTS

Mayor Bell opened public comrnents. There being no one wishing to speak,

Mayor Bell closed public comments.

5. ADJOURNMENT

The meeting adjourned at 10:03 p.m.
