 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, & PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
 Mayor MacDougall called the meeting to order at 7 p.m. Mayor MacDougall
pointed out that this is his last Town Council Meeting and he thanked the
members of the audience for their service to the Town.
Town Clerk Debra Eastman called the roll with the following Council Members in
attendance, Council Member Peggy Bell, Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle,
Council Member Mary Ann Mixon, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin and Mayor Edward
P. MacDougall.
 2. PROCLAMATIONS, AWARDS, AND PRESENTATIONS
 A.   ·   Miami-Dade County Public Schools Vice-Chair, Dr. Lawrence S. Feldman,
Proclamation to Mayor MacDougall
 Dr. Lawrence Feldman presented Mayor MacDougall with a proclamation from
the Miami-Dade County School Board recognizing his years of service to the
Town and outstanding support of the schools.
 B.   ·   Proclamation to William R. Johnson
 Sponsors:   Mayor Peggy Bell
 Council Member Peggy Bell and Mayor MacDougall presented a proclamation to
Mr. William Johnson who serves tirelessly as a volunteer for many Town events.
Mr. Johnson expressed his gratitude and will continue to be of service.
 C.   ·   Presentation - Government Finance Officers Association (GFOA) Award
 Mayor MacDougall presented an award received from the Government Finance
Officers' Association for excellence in 2013 annual financial reporting to Town
Finance Director Robert Daddario and Town Manager Rafael Casals.
Town Manager Casals explained that Police Officer Leonardo Cabrera was
recognized by the Director of Miami Dade County Police last week with a life
saving award resulting from an incident that happened in Cutler Bay. The Mayor
and Town Council congratulated Officer Cabrera and his family and thanked him
for his outstanding service to the Town of Cutler Bay.
Mayor MacDougall recognized members of his family in the audience and Mr. Joe
Corradino, Vice Mayor of the Village of Pinecrest and Mr. Merrett Steirheim.

 There were no minutes.

Council Member Loyzelle announced the Amscot and the Cutler Bay Police are
hosting a bike helmet giveaway on October 18.

 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Darryl Boyette, President of the Cutler Bay Business Association expressed
support for an Economic Advisory Committee. Mr. Boyette presented a
document to the Town Council which outlined the proposed 5th Annual Taste of
the Bay event which he would like to host at Lakes by the Bay Park. Mr. Boyette
explained the proposed activities. Mayor MacDougall thanked Mr. Boyette for his
work. Council Member Loyzelle expressed concern about holding the event at
the proposed venue and the short timeline to hold the event in February, 2015,
especially if the Town Council should deny the request. Vice Mayor Sochin also
expressed concern, along with Town Manager Casals who explained that this
would be the first time the venue would be used and that there are no previous
experiences or policies. Mayor MacDougall recognized that this is only a
preliminary discussion and that Mr. Boyette will need to work out the details with
the Town Manager and Town Council.
David Feinberg thanked the Mayor and Council Members for all of their work and
accomplishments. He expressed concerns that candidates running for office in
Cutler Bay are hiring lobbyists to assist with their campaigns.
Ed Wolmers spoke in support of the comments made by Mr. Feinberg regarding
lobbyists.
Eugene Teccolini asked if the lobbyist issue could be voted on by the Town
Council tonight. Mayor MacDougall explained the issue would have to be an
ordinance previously prepared for the agenda. Town Attorney Chad Friedman
opined that he would look into how the ordinance could be crafted.

October 15, 2014 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
Council Member Loyzelle sponsored the item to be heard and requested that
Brian Trainor and then the public be allowed to comment.
Town Attorney Chad Friedman explained a provision of theTown Charter,
violations, that was added during the last Charter review revision. Town
Attorney Friedman stated that the Mayor is entitled to a public hearing or he
could choose to waive the hearing.
Vice Mayor Sochin opened the public hearing for comments.
Brianna Trainor stated that she is the daughter of Brian Trainor. She stated that
she does not appreciate the negative connotation in which her family has been
portrayed.
Brian Trainor stated that the issue started because his sidewalks were poorly
done. He stated that he was dumbfounded by the Mayor's response to his email
regarding the shoddy work completed on the sidewalks.
An unidentified speaker stated that he believes a lot of time was wasted tonight.
He stated that this process is making mountains out of mole hills.
Richard Hibbard stated that he is embarrassed for what is going on. He stated
that the incident occured over a year ago and this comes up when an election is
coming.
Ed Wolmers stated that he has worked with the Mayor over the years, but the
Mayor made a mistake and should face the penalty. He asked that the Mayor
consider just paying the fine and save the time and advertising fees.
TW Chamberlin agreed with the opinion of Mr. Wolmers and he thanked Mr.
Trainor for coming forward.
Peggy Schuler stated that she finds it sad, that after eight year of being Mayor his
tenure has to end in this way. She stated that it does not look good for the
Council and that she is scared to think of what will happen in the next four years.
She stated that she is disappointed in the Town Council. Mayor MacDougall
stated he is willing to waive the public hearing and pay a fine.
After discussion, a motion was made by Council Member Loyzelle that the Mayor
agrees to waive his right to a public hearing, admits guilt, and will pay a $50 fine.
The motion received a second from Vice Mayor Sochin. The motion carried by an
unanimous vote. (4-0.)
 B.   Discussion of resident complaint regarding alleged charter violation by Mayor
MacDougall
 Sponsors:   Mayor Peggy Bell
 Council Member Bell stated that the item was brought forward by resident Ed
Wolmers. Town Attorney Chad Friedman stated that this item would follow the
same prodedure as the previous item, 13A.
Vice Mayor Sochin opened the public hearing to allow Mr. Wolmers to speak.
Ed Wolmers stated that the complaint was based on a letter circulated to voters
of the Town. He stated that the Mayor is entitled to his opinion, however, he is
not allowed to put forward his opinions as if they were from the Council.
Mayor MacDougall stated that he has not broken any rules and only spoke his
mind as he is allowed to under the First Amendment.
Council Member Mixon made a motion to schedule a hearing to address the
complaint. The motion received a second from Council Member Loyzelle.
Mayor MacDougall stated that he would like a public hearing on the item.
Vice Mayor Sochin opened the public hearing for comments. There were none.
Vice Mayor Sochin called for the vote. The motion carried by an unanimous vote.
(4-0)
