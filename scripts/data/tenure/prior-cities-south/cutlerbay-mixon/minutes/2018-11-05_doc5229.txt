A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER BAY,

FLORIDA, APPROVING A LETTER OF INTENT BETWEEN THE TOWN AND GCF INVESTMENTS,

INC. RELATED TO A PROPERTY EXCHANGE OF PROPERTY OWNED BY GCF INVESTMENTS,

INC. GENERALLY LOCATED EAST OF OLD CUTLER ROAD, SOUTH OF S.W. 208TH STREET,

NORTH OF S.W. 212TH STREET, AND WEST OF S.W. 92ND PLACE AND THE TOWN’S EXISTING

TOWN HALL SITE AND/OR ADJACENT PARKING PARCEL; PROVIDING FOR AUTHORIZATION;

AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the title of the resolution. Town Attorney Chad

Friedman explained the resolution, the purpose and the specifics of the lettter of

intent and the opportunities and advantages to the Town. He explained that this

keeps the property off the market for 120 days and the ultimate decision will be

with the Town Council.

Town Manager Ralph Casals explained the current assets of the Town and the

current debt service.

The Town Council discussed land swap details, debt service, and the process.

Council Member Callahan asked about the cost of the process including the

financial advisor, attorneys, etc.

Vice Mayor Loyzelle made a motion to defer the resolution. There was no

second offered.

Council Member Callahan made a motion to cap the financial expense related to

the letter of intent to $10,000. The motion received a second from Council

Member Mixon. Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion was adopted 5-0.

There was further discussion regarding the cost of appraisals already done and

those proposed and how that would be paid.

Council Member Callahan made a motion to adopt the resolution with the

amendment of the financial cap. The motion received a second from Council

Member Mixon. Vice Mayor Loyzelle spoke in opposition. Mayor Bell called for

a roll call vote.

The motion carried by the following vote:

Yes: Mayor Peggy Bell, Mary Ann Mixon, Council Member Michael Callahan and Council

Member Roger Coriat
