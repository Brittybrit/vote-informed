6:00 PM Council ChambersWednesday, August 31, 2016

1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, MOMENT OF

SILENCE & INVOCATION

Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 6:08PM. Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson

called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance. Mayor Bell led a Moment

of Silence in honor and memory of Cutler Bay resident Louise Lockwood.

Members of Council shared memories of Ms. Lockwood, and Council Member

Loyzelle suggested the Town honor her memory at the next July 4, 2017 Event on

its Fiftieth (50) Anniversary.

A. · Fiscal Year 2016-17 Proposed Budget

(Third Council Budget Workshop)

Town Manager Rafael Casals announced the upcoming First Budget Hearing on

September 13, 2016 and the Second Budget Hearing on September 27, 2016.

Town Manager Rafael Casals also announced the Town as the recipient of the

Government Finance Officers Association (GFOA) Budget Book Award. Town

Manager Rafael Casals introduced Finance Director Robert Daddario to provide a

brief collective overview of the Fiscal Year 2016-2017 Proposed Budget based on

the 2.5329 ceiling millage rate in accordance with the July 20, 2015 Regular Town

Council Meeting. Town Manager Rafael Casals and Finance Director Robert

Daddario highlighted changes from the July 18, 2016 Second Town Council

Budget Workshop and explained the general fund budget for each department.

There was discussion regarding the development of the Community Center at

Town Hall. There was discussion regarding committee funds. There was

discussion regarding live video streaming capabilities for Town Council

Meetings. There was discussion on Park Impact Fees. There was discussion on

a Public Information Officer position as requested in the July 18, 2016 Second

Budget Workshop. Town Manager Rafael Casals agreed to explore locating

funding mid-year for a part-time Public Information Officer. There was

discussion on potential partnerships with neighboring municipalities and

additional funding for Town sponsored events that engage more diverse age

groups.

B. · Discussion- Charter Officials 2016 Annual Performance Reviews

There was discussion of a potential workshop for the Charter Officials 2016

Annual Performance Reviews. There was discussion on standardization of the

Charter Official Performance Evaluation forms to mirror the Clerk format.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Mayor Bell informed the Council of the Florida City Gas Town Hall Meeting on

September 22, 2016. Council Member Loyzelle congratulated Town Manager

Rafael Casals on his thirtieth (30) anniversary in municipal government. The

Council reached a general consensus to hold a Special Workshop at 6:30PM

prior to the September 13, 2016 Town Council Meeting to discuss Charter

Officials 2016 Annual Performance Evaluations.

3. Adjournment

Mayor Bell adjourned the meeting at 9:24PM.
