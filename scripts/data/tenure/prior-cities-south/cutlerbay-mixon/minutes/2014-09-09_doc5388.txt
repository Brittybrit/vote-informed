 3. RESOLUTIONS
 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE TENTATIVE MILLAGE RATE OF THE
TOWN OF CUTLER BAY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1,
2014 THROUGH SEPTEMBER 30, 2015 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTE
200.065 (TRIM BILL); PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Clerk Eastman read the resolution into the record.
Pursuant to State law, Finance Director Robert Daddario read the TRIM notice
into the record. The proposed millage rate of the Town of Cutler Bay for fiscal
year 2014-15 of 2.7202 per $1,000 of assessed property value within the Town of
Cutler Bay is 13.78 percent greater than the rollback rate of 2.3907 mills per
$1,000 of assessed property value. Ad valorem taxes are being increased
primarily as a result of expenditures required for the debt service on the Town's
existing loans and to provide for increased costs of contracted police services.
Town Manager Rafael Casals stated that the first order of business is to set the
new maximum rate.
Town Attorney Chad Friedman stated that if the Town Council sets the millage
rate, they would not be able to go above that rate. It would become the new cap.
Town Attorney Friedman stated that the millage needs to be voted on first, but a
discussion can take place regarding the budget prior to a formal vote on the
millage.
Council Member Bell made a motion to adopt the rollback rate of 2.3907. Council
Member Mixon seconded the motion.
Council Member Loyzelle suggested that the rollback rate not be adopted to
provide some wiggle room in the budget. Town Manager Casals stated that
projects have been identified in the budget that could be deferred that would
cover the cost of adopting the rollback rate.
Mayor MacDougall called for a vote on Item 3A. Town Clerk Eastman conducted
a roll call. The motion passed with all voting yes, with the exception of Council
Member Loyzelle.

 B.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE TENTATIVE OPERATING AND CAPITAL
OUTLAY BUDGET FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2014
THROUGH SEPTEMBER 30, 2015 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTE 200.065
(TRIM BILL); AND AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO MAKE
EXPENDITURES CONSISTENT THEREWITH AND AUTHORIZING THE TOWN
MANAGER TO MAKE CERTAIN BUDGET AMENDMENTS WITHIN A DEPARTMENT
PROVIDED THAT THE TOTAL OF THE APPROPRIATIONS IS NOT CHANGED; AND
PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Clerk Eastman read the resolution into the record.
Mayor MacDougall requested a motion and second for discussion.
Council Member Bell made a motion for discussion, which was seconded by
Council Member Mixon.
Mayor MacDougall invited the Council Members to ask questions on the budget.
Council Member Bell asked the Town Manager about the necessity for money in
the budget to complete a study regarding a community center for the public,
which has also been addressed during the strategic planning workshops. Town
Manager Casals stated that no funding is needed at the moment.
Council Member Bell asked the Town Manager if funding for a pool study to
replace the swimming pool was included in the tentative budget. Town Manager

Casals confirmed that funding is in the budget for that purpose.
Town Manager Casals stated that the four biggest changes in the budget were
the rollback rate, the addition of the $12,000 for the Parks pool master planning,
the removal of $12,000 from the green recertification plan, and then a few other
changes in categories that will be discussed department by department.
Mayor MacDougall expresed concern regarding the hardening of the Town
Center building. Town Manager Casals stated that the Town has applied for
grants for building hardening through the State Department of Community Affairs
during two cycles. The Town did not receive either grant. Town Manager Casals
stated that he would provide information to the Town Council, between first and
second reading of the budget, regarding a study that was conducted, which
includes a preliminary number for the cost of hardening the Town Center
building.
Council Member Mixon suggested that the Town Manager and Finance Director
provide quarterly reports that would identify the current status of revenue versus
expenses, which would prevent major surprises throughout the year, to be
presented during a public meeting.
Mayor MacDougall requested a motion to approve Item 3B.
Council Member Mixon made a motion to approve Item 3B, seconded by Council
Member Bell.
Town Attorney Friedman advised that a public hearing would have to be opened
on Item 3B.
Mayor MacDougall opened Item 3B for public discussion. With no one coming
forward, Mayor MacDougall closed the public hearing. Mayor MacDougall
requested a motion and second on Item 3B.
Council Member Mixon made a motion to approve Item 3B, seconded by Council
Member Bell.
Town Manager Casals reviewed the changes to the budget by department with
the Town Council.
Council Member Loyzelle requested that the Town Manager explore the
possibility of adding an additional officer in the Police Department. Town
Manager Casals stated that he would use the time between the first and second
hearing to explore the possibility and then report back to the Town Council on
his findings.
Council Member Loyzelle suggested that the funding allocated for the video
buildout of the Granicus software be redirected to funding an additional police
officer.
Council Member Mixon and Council Member Bell thanked the Town Manager and
staff for their work on the budget.
Council Member Loyzelle suggested that the changes to the budget be reflected
in a different color to better reflect the cost savings in the budget narrative.
