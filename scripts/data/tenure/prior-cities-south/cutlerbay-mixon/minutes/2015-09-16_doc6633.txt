 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, SUPPORTING THE TOWN OF SURFSIDE’S
RESOLUTION URGING THE STATE OF FLORIDA LEGISLATURE AND THE
FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION (“FDEP”) TO
ESTABLISH CHEMICAL TESTING STANDARDS PRIOR TO ISSUING A COASTAL
CONSTRUCTION CONTROL LINE (“CCCL”) PERMIT WHICH AUTHORIZES THE
TRANSFER AND PLACEMENT OF EXCAVATED SAND SEAWARD OF THE CCL
ONTO A PUBLIC BEACH; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. Council
Member Coriat requested a correction to the second sentence in the second
paragraph of the memo to read "I believe it would be appropriate for the Town of
Cutler Bay, a recognized advocate for environmental issues, to join in expressing
its support for this measure." Council Member Coriat provided background and
expressed support. A motion was made by Vice Mayor Sochin, seconded by
Council Member Loyzelle, that this Resolution be adopted as amended. Mayor
Bell called for the vote and the motion passed 5-0.
