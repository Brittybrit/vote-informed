4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS

The Town Council observed a moment of silence for Mr. David Seigel, former

Manager of Southland Mall. Council Members advised of various upcoming

events. Council Member Loyzelle informed that she will be participating in the

Martin Luther King, Jr. Day parade and will be making a donation from her

meeting allotment. Mayor MacDougall explained that he attended the Everglades

Coalition Conference and because there was no Council Meeting held in

December he paid for the expense. A motion was made by Council Member Bell

and received a second from Council Member Loyzelle to reimburse Mayor

MacDougall his expenses to attend the Everglades Coalition Conference. The

motion carried 4-0. Town Clerk Debra Eastman reported that the Town Council

Travel Policy Resolution 08-29 is not in line with the current practice of individual

5. PUBLIC COMMENTS

Jaime Reyes spoke about the Linear Park land and expressed support for a park.

Jane Walker spoke regarding methods of tolling and her work as an activist.

6. REPORTS

A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT

Town Manager Ralph Casals presented a written update to the Town Council,

introduced a new Police Officer, provided an update to the Old Cutler Road

project via Ramon Soria and an update to the Caribbean Boulevard project via

Gary Ratay.

B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT

Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman reported on the FPL case.

C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT

Town Clerk Debra Eastman confirmed the will of the Town Council to purchase a

table at the Miami Dade League of Cities Gala, updated the Town Council on

trainings being provided to staff and committees on public records and the

sunshine law, provided an update on the status of the Granicus project, the

Laserfiche project, use of the LED message board on Old Cutler Road and

preparation of an informational package for 2014 General Election candidates.

D. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS

Council Member Bell provided an update on the Parks and Recreation Committee

and the Youth Council Committee. Vice Mayor Sochin provided an update on the

Education Advisory Committee.
