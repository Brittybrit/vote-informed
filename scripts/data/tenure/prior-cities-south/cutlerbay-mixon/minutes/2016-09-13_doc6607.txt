6:30 PM Council ChambersTuesday, September 13, 2016

1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, MOMENT OF

SILENCE & INVOCATION

Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 6:31PM. Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson

called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance. Mayor Bell spoke on the

passing of Mitzi Gimenez, mother of Miami-Dade County Mayor Carlos Gimenez

and informed of a Moment of Silence to take place at the September 13, 2016

First Budget Hearing.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,

Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present 5 -

· Discussion- Charter Officials 2016 Annual Performance Reviews

Mayor Bell welcomed Town Attorney Alison Smith in attendance. Mayor Bell

provided a recommendation for Council consideration regarding Charter Official

Performance Evaluations. There was discussion on standardization of the

Charter Official Performance Evaluation forms in preparation for next year.

Council Member Loyzelle clarified merit increase amounts for the Town Manager

and Town Clerk relative to percentages for transparency. At the request of

Council Member Loyzelle, Town Manager Rafael Casals clarified increased salary

amounts were budgeted in the Fiscal Year 2016-17 Proposed Budget. Mayor Bell

reported on the Town Clerk's status with the Certified Municipal Clerk

designation plan.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

There were no Town Council Questions/Comments/Discussion.

3. Adjournment

Mayor Bell adjourned the meeting at 6:50PM.
