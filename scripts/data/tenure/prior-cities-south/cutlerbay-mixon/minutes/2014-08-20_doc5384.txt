August 20, 2014 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, & PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
 Council Member Bell arrived at 7:02 p.m.   Vice Mayor Sochin arrived at 7:08 p.m.
 Council Member Peggy Bell, Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member
Mary Ann Mixon, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin and Mayor Edward MacDougall
 Present:   5 -
 2. PROCLAMATIONS, AWARDS, AND PRESENTATIONS
 A.   Cutler Bay Police Officers of the Quarter Awards
 Police Major Julie Miller presented awards as the Police Officer of the Quarter to
Sergeant Frank Farines and Officers Zayas and Pena and explained their heroism
in saving the life of a 15 year old girl. The Mayor and Town Council thanked them
for their service to the community.
Town Manager Rafael Casals reported that Major Miller and Officer Debbie Fleites
responded to a baby drowning in a pool and were able to revive the child. The
Mayor and Town Council commended them for their outstanding service.
 B.   Relay for Life Presentation to Mayor MacDougall
 Liz Abello, Cheri Ball and Damian Morgan presented a thank you plaque to Mayor
MacDougall for the participation of the Town of Cutler Bay in the 2014 Relay for
Life event that was held in conjunction with the Village of Palmetto Bay and
Pinecrest.
 C.   Miami-Dade County Vice Chair Lynda Bell Presentation
 Miami-Dade County Vice Chair Lynda Bell spoke regarding the many projects and
efforts that have been accomplished over the past few years and how
collaborating with the Town of Cutler Bay has positively impacted the Town.
Mayor MacDougall and members of the Town Council and Town Manager Rafael
Casals thanked Ms. Bell for her assistance and the assistance of her staff.
 3. APPROVAL OF MINUTES
 A.   ·   Regular Town Council Meeting, July 16, 2014
·   Budget Workshop, July 23, 2014
·   Zoning Workshop, July 23, 2014
 A motion was made by Council Member Loyzelle, seconded by Council Member
Mixon, that the Minutes be approved as amended. The motion carried by an
unanimous vote. (5-0)
 4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Council Member Loyzelle reported on the AARP senior employment program,
some criteria of the program and the benefits to the employee and the employer.
She reported that the Town Manager has such an employee in his office who will
work 18 hours per week at no charge to the Town.
Council Member Loyzelle thanked the Town Manager and the Building
Department personnel for the strategic planning meetings being held and
especially the meetings conducted in Spanish held at Saga Bay Apartments and

Coral Bay Terrace. She explained that Vice Mayor Sochin also attended and
provided the pastry. Council Member Loyzelle explained that the top three items
discussed at the Saga Bay meeting were to have senior activities hosted by the
Town at the senior living facilties, holding a monthly dance, and coordinating

Cultural Arts Center is available and opined that the shows are wonderful.
Vice Mayor Sochin stated he would like to see added to the parks, especially
Lakes by the Bay, a facility for basketball, volleyball and racquetball. Vice Mayor
Sochin made a motion for the Town Manager to look at this possibility and to
consider both space and funds. The motion received a second from Council
Member Loyzelle. Council Member Bell pointed out as liaison to the Parks and
Recreation Committee she is aware that they are looking into the feasibility of
using existing facilities for a community center and that they have allocated
$500,000 in the budget for major improvements to the parks. Council Member
Loyzelle indicated her desire to make this idea part of the Strategic Master Plan.
Vice Mayor Sochin requested that the Town Manager attend future League of
Cities functions being attended by Council Members.
Council Member Bell thanked the Town Manager for holding two of the Strategic
Master Plan outreach meetings at the elderly resident buildings.
Council Member Bell acknowledged two employees of the Town that received
complimentary letters from the public and she thanked the residents for taking
time to recognize our exemplary town employees.
 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Madeline Volum a Guardian ad Litem stated that their mission is to promote the
program and to get more guardians involved. She requested the assistance of
the Town Council in promoting the program. Council Member Bell requested that
the Town post Guardian ad Litem information the Town's website.
 6. REPORTS
 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT
 Town Manager Rafael Casals reported the next public outreach meeting for the
Stategic Master Plan will be August 28, 2014 and also reported the dates for the
upcoming budget meetings. The Town Manager introduced Michelle Lopez of
the Corradino Group who will be working on updating the Transportation Master
Plan which will be funded by a $35,000 NPO grant.
Gary Ratay and Alina Phillips of Kimley Horn and Associates provided an update
on the Caribbean Blvd project.
Mr. Casals also complimented Major Julie Miller on the oversight of the police
department on the first day of school which went smoothly.
 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT
 Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman reported on FPL franchise fee issue and
informed that it is going to mediation in the next couple of weeks.
 C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT

Town Clerk Debra Eastman reported that at the end of qualifying there are two
candidates for the Office of Mayor - Peggy Bell and Art Nanni and two candidates
for Council Member Seat 1 - incumbent Mary Ann Mixon and Ed Wolmers and
that Roger Coriat who qualified for Council Seat 3 is considered elected as he is
unopposed.
The Town Clerk asked the wishes of the Town Council regarding their previous
practice not to have a meeting in December. The Town Charter, however, does
not allow for a meeting in November until the election results are certified by
Miami-Dade County. A motion was made by Council Member Bell and received a
second from Council Member Loyzelle to not hold a council meeting in
November, but rather to hold a meeting on December 10, 2014. The Mayor called
for the vote and the motion carried 5-0.
 D. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS
 Council Member Mixon reported on the July 17 meeting of the Floodplain
Mitigation Plan Advisory Committee. The meeting consisted of setting goals for
the mitigation plan. Projects will be prioritized and evaluated on a federial
criteria. The next meeting is expected to be in October.
Council Member Bell reported on the Parks and Recreation Committee meeting
where they reviewed the Parks Master Plan. They are discussing priorities and
like the idea of a community center within existing facilities. They are working
hard to produce a good advisory for the Town Council.
Vice Mayor Sochin reported on the Education Advisory Committee and that the
school principals were in attendance. Vice Mayor Sochin reported that there
were positive discussions and he was pleased with the progress and direction.
Council Member Loyzelle reported on the Communities for a Lifetime Committee
and that they had elections resulting in new Chairwoman Heather Fraser, Vice
Chair Jose Rodriguez and Secretary Gilda Chang.
Council Member Loyzelle and Council Member Bell both requested that the Town
Clerk send an email to all current Committee members reminding them that their
term on a committee runs concurrent with the Council member who appointed
them and it is dictated by ordinance. Mayor MacDougall accepted this as a
motion and it received a second from Vice Mayor Sochin. Mayor MacDougall
called for the vote and it carried 5-0.
