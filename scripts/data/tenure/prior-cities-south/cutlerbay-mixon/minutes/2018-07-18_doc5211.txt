1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, & MOMENT OF

SILENCE

Mayor Peggy Bell called the meeting to order at 7:05 p.m. Present were Council

Member Michael Callahan, Council Member Roger Coriat, Vice Mayor Sue

Loyzelle and Mayor Peggy Bell. Council Member Mary Ann Mixon was absent.

Mayor Bell called for a moment of silence for long-time resident Carol Paul in

honor of her participation in local government. Ms. Paul served as the Treasurer

of the Concerned Citizens organization and was known to be an outspoken

resident.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member Michael P.

Callahan and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present: 4 -

Council Member Mary Ann MixonAbsent: 1 -

2. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS, BOARD & COMMITTEE REPORTS

Mayor Bell called attention to the written reports of the Town Council. Council

Member Coriat called attention to a program where group partners with schools

and other agencies can identify safe ways to schools to prevent the use of cars.

He announced that there will be a back to school event and requested that the

Town Manager and staff use their resources to help promote this program. Vice

Mayor Loyzelle announced the next Community for a Lifetime meeting and

Mayor Bell announced he next Economic Growth Committee meeting.

Mayor Bell instructed that she will be conducting the meeting with the allowance

of a five minute limit for comments by members of the Town Council and a three

minute limit for public comments.

3. PUBLIC COMMENTS

Adam Skowronski thanked the Town Council for the presentation at the July 4

event and reported on the continued appreciation for the butterfly garden.

Tara Favaro requested assistance with the tiki hut at her home that is not

permitted.

4. REPORTS

A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT

· Town Manager Monthly Report July 2018

Town Manager Ralph Casals reported on upcoming meetings including Flood

Mitigation meeting. He reported on the status of the Miami Dade County Public

Works Department bridge project on Caribbean Boulevard. He will be attending

the Transportation Planning Organization meeting and meetings regarding

Miami-Dade transit.

· 2017 CAFR and Governance Letter

The Town Auditor, Mr. Fierman provided the auditors report and congratulated

the Town on receiving the GFOA Certificate of Achievement for the eleventh year

running.

· G.O. Bond Referendum Summary of Expenditures

Town Manager Ralph Casals provied a summary of the expeditures received to

date for the June 5, 2018 General Obligation Bond Referendum election.

B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT

The Town Attorney had nothing to report.

C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT

Town Clerk Debra Eastman reported on the upcoming qualifying period dates

and announced the names of the candidates who have opened accounts thus far.

5. CONSENT AGENDA

Mayor Bell confirmed that the Town Council did not wish to pull any items from

the consent agenda. A motion to adopt the consent agenda was made by Roger

Coriat and the motion received a second from Michael Callahan. Mayor Bell

called for the vote and all were in favor (4-0)

7. ORDINANCES FOR FIRST READING (NO PUBLIC HEARING REQUIRED)

A. AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, AMENDING CHAPTER 3 OF THE TOWN CODE, “LAND

DEVELOPMENT REGULATIONS,” BY CREATING A SECTION 3-18, “ENCLOSED

USES” TO PROVIDE REGULATIONS FOR SUCH USES; AMENDING SECTION 3-33,

“SITE PLAN APPLICATION” RELATING TO APPLICATIONS WITHIN THE TOWN

CENTER DISTRICT AND CONSTRUCTION OF SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENCES;

AMENDING SECTION 3-113.5, “REQUIRED SIGNAGE” RELATING TO ASSISTED

LIVING FACILITIES AND GROUP HOMES; AMENDING SECTION 3-158, “SHEDS”

RELATING TO REGULATION OF SUCH STRUCTURES; PROVIDING FOR

SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICT; PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE

CODE; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the title of the ordinance into the record. Town

Manager Ralph Casals explained the purpose of the annual housekeeping of the

LDRs. Town Planner Kathryn Lyon explained the proposed changes and the

Town Council discussed them. A motion was made by Council Member Coriat,

seconded by Vice Mayor Loyzelle, that this Ordinance be approved on first

reading. The motion carried by the following vote:

Yes: Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member Michael

Callahan and Council Member Roger Coriat

A. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, DETERMINING THE PROPOSED MILLAGE RATE, AND THE DATE,

TIME AND PLACE FOR THE FIRST AND SECOND PUBLIC BUDGET HEARINGS AS

REQUIRED BY LAW; DIRECTING THE TOWN CLERK AND TOWN MANAGER TO FILE

THIS RESOLUTION, IF REQUIRED, WITH THE MIAMI-DADE COUNTY PROPERTY

APPRAISER; AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO CHANGE BUDGET HEARING

DATES IF NEEDED; AND PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the title of the Resolution. Town Manager Ralph

Casals explained the budget setting process. A motion was made by Council

Member Coriat, seconded by Council Member Callahan, that this Resolution be

adopted. It was determined that the resolution should be amended to hold the

second budget hearing on Monday, September 17, 2018 at 7 p.m. Council

Member Coriat made an amendment to his motion which was accepted by

Council Member Callahan. Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion was

adopted as amended . The motion carried by a 4-0 vote with Council Member

Mixon absent.
