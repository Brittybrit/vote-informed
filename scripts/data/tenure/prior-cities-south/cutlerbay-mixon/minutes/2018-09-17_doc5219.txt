A. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE FINAL MILLAGE RATE OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2018 THROUGH

SEPTEMBER 30, 2019 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTES SECTION 200.065; AND

PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read both the millage resolution and the budget

resolution as they appear on the agenda into the record. Finance Director

Robert Daddario read the following TRIM statement: The tentative millage rate of

the Town of Cutler Bay for FY 2018-19 of 2.4739 mills per $1,000 of assessed

property value within the Town of Cutler Bay is 11.62% greater than the roll-back

rate of 2.2164 mills per $1,000 of assessed property value. Ad valorem taxes are

being increased primarily to help provide for operating expenditures required to

maintain current levels of resident services, as well as to provide for various

improvements to park and public facilties.

Town Manager Ralph Casals explained the five meeting journey up to this point,

explained the action items requested by the Town Council at the first budget

hearing meeting on September 4, 2018, explained the handout of millage cost

per department, cost of living increase, employee policy and procedures for

implementing merit increases and again recommended the distribution of town

phones to key employees for town use along with employing the services of

SMARSH to archive text messages. He explained by keeping the proposed

millage approximately $200,000 additional revenue would be generated and

would require a super majority vote of the Town Council.

Mayor Bell opened the public hearing. Mr. Leonard Coates spoke about the

millage rate and in opposition to an increase. Mr. Ignacio Gutierrez spoke about

a second home in Cutler Bay and spoke in opposition to a tax increase. There

being no other speakers, Mayor Bell closed the public hearing.

Town Council Members discussed the action items provided by the Town

Manager, the proposed and current millage rate, the Line2 service vs. SMARSH

archive of text messages, tax increase amount for the average home and the

artificial dip into reserves.

A motion was made by Vice Mayor Loyzelle to adopt the millage rate of 2.3907.

The motion received a second from Council Member Callahan. There was

further discussion regarding the millage rate, property values, additional

homestead exemption and the cost of doing business. Town Clerk Debra

Eastman called the roll and the motion failed with Council Member Coriat and

Callahan voting in favor along with Vice Mayor Loyzelle and Council Member

Mixon and Mayor Bell voting in opposition.

There was discussion regarding a new millage rate proposal that would generate

$100,000 in revenue. Vice Mayor Loyzelle made a motion to adopt a millage rate

of 2.4323 mills per $1,000 of assessed property value which is 9.74% greater than

the roll-back rate of 2.2164 mills. The motion received a second from Council

Member Mixon. Town Clerk Debra Eastman called the roll and the motion carried

by the following vote:

Yes: Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member Mary Ann

Mixon and Council Member Roger Coriat

B. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE FINAL OPERATING AND CAPITAL OUTLAY BUDGET

FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2018 THROUGH SEPTEMBER

30, 2019 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTES SECTION 200.065; AUTHORIZING THE

TOWN MANAGER TO MAKE EXPENDITURES CONSISTENT THEREWITH AND

AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO MAKE CERTAIN BUDGET AMENDMENTS

WITHIN A DEPARTMENT PROVIDED THAT THE TOTAL OF THE APPROPRIATIONS

IS NOT CHANGED; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Manager Ralph Casals explained that the changes from the first hearing of

September 4, 2018 were made to the budget before the Town Council. There

was a discusision regarding the text message archiving software proposal and

staff cell phones. Vice Mayor Loyzelle made a motion to reduce the line item

for cell phones in favor of staff having a second line on their personal cell phone.

The motion received a second from Council Member Coriat. Mayor Bell called

for the vote and the motion failed with Vice Mayor Loyzelle voting in favor and

the remainder of the Town Council voting in opposition (1-4). A motion was

made by Council Member Coriat to approve the total budget of $51,482,178. The

motion received a second by Council Member Mixon. The motion carried by the

following vote:

Yes: Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member Mary Ann

Mixon, Council Member Michael Callahan and Council Member Roger Coriat
