1. Call to Order, Roll Call, & Pledge of Allegiance

Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 3:03PM. Standing in as Acting Clerk on

behalf of Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson, Assistant to Town Manager

Jazmin Gonzales called the role. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Council Member Mixon arrived at approximately 3:10PM.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,

Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present 5 -

· Discussion regarding existing sign regulations

Town Manager Rafael Casals provided a brief introduction pertaining to the Sign

Code Ordinance. Council Member Loyzelle explained why she requested this

Town Council Workshop and clarified that the intent is to educate, not to change

the existing Sign Ordinance. Town Attorney Chad Friedman presented the

history of the Sign Code Ordinance, the amortization schedule and the

regulations currently in place. Town Attorney Chad Friedman clarified that the

Town Code does not regulate signs which cannot be seen from the right of way

and only affects those signs that can be seen from the right of way.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced Town Planning Consultant Joseph

Corradino, Corradino Group to assist with responses to Town Council

Questions. Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced Planning & Zoning Director

Kathryn Lyon to clarify the definitions of a pole sign and a monument sign as per

the Town’s adopted Land Development Regulations before the Council, and to

present the current standings with the amortization of commercial businesses

and to expand on the process and how the Sign Code Ordinance reads.

3. Adjournment

A motion was made by Vice Mayor Sochin to adjourn. The motion received a

second from Council Member Coriat. Mayor Bell adjourned the meeting at

4:27PM.

WE, THE TOWN COUNCIL OF CUTLER BAY, HEREBY RECOMMIT OURSELVES TO MAINTAIN

CIVILITY IN OUR PUBLIC AND POLITICAL DISCOURSE AND WE HEREBY PLEDGE OUR

COMMITMENT TO THE FOLLOWING PRINCIPLES:

• We will respect the right of all citizens in our community to hold different opinions;

• We will avoid rhetoric intended to humiliate or question the wisdom of those whose opinions are

different from ours;

• We will strive to understand differing perspectives;

• We will choose our words carefully;

• We will speak truthfully without accusation and we will avoid distortion; and

• We will speak out against violence, prejudice and incivility of their forms, whenever and wherever

they occur.

PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTES 286.0105, THE TOWN HEREBY ADVISES THE PUBLIC

THAT IF A PERSON DECIDES TO APPEAL ANY DECISION MADE BY THIS COUNCIL WITH

RESPECT TO ANY MATTER CONSIDERED AT ITS MEETING OR HEARING, HE OR SHE WILL

NEED A RECORD OF THE PROCEEDINGS, AND THAT FOR SUCH PURPOSE, THE AFFECTED

PERSON MAY NEED TO ENSURE THAT VERBATIM RECORD OF THE PROCEEDING IS MADE,

WHICH RECORD INCLUDES THE TESTIMONY AND EVIDENCE UPON WHICH THE APPEAL IS

TO BE BASED. THIS NOTICE DOES NOT CONSTITUTE CONSENT BY THE TOWN FOR THE

INTRODUCTION OR ADMISSION OF OTHERWISE INADMISSIBLE OR IRRELEVANT EVIDENCE,

NOR DOES IT AUTHORIZE CHALLENGES OR APPEALS NOT

OTHERWISE ALLOWED BY LAW.
