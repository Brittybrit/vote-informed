1. Call to Order, Roll Call, & Pledge of Allegiance

Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 6:18pm. Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline

WIlson called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,

Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present 5 -

· Discussion on the guidelines of selecting potential candidates for an interview for

the Town Clerk position

Town Manager Rafael Casals opened discussion of the selection process of

potential candidates for the Town Clerk position by reviewing the informational

package provided to the Town Council containing all applications received. The

Council expressed concern of number of applications received and indicating

potential deterrants which may have contributed to the limited response. After

discussion, the general consensus of the Council was to expand the application

pool through increasing the salary range to $70,000-$90,000 and adjust the

minimum requirements to reflect a Bachelor's Degree in Public Administration or

related field, and/or minimum of 5 years experience as a municpal clerk, 3years

progressive municipal government experience, with an agreement to secure

Certified Municipal Clerk designation. The Council also directed Town Manager

Rafael Casals to re-evaluate the existing pool of applicants according to the new

criteria and re-advertise the Town Clerk position over a 45 day period based on

the new minimum requirements and report back to the Town Council.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Mayor Bell reported on the Cutler Bay Middle School violence issue and provided

a status update reiterating that the figures from the Channel 10 media piece

released on May 4, 2015 were based upon 3 year old data, pronouncing Cutler

Bay Middle School as the most violent school in Miami-Dade County. Mayor Bell

stated she had spoken with Miami-Dade County Public Schools Superintendent,

Alberto Carvalho and requested he attend the Town of Cutler Bay May 20, 2015

Town Council Meeting to which he declined. Mayor Bell also briefed the Council

on the Open Letter she had written and published to Facebook, Twitter and news

media, addressing the Cutler Bay Middle School violence issue. Mayor Bell

reported that Superintendent Carvalho called Mayor Bell in response to her letter

to state that this issue is very important to him and that he would like to meet

iwth the Council, however, no date has been established yet. Mayor Bell

expressed her appreciation that this information has been uncovered and

recognized it as an opportunity to turn this school around.

Council Member Loyzelle stated that she spoke with Dr. Lawrence Feldman, Vice

Chair of the Miami-Dade County Public School Board in reference to the Cutler

Bay Middle School violence issue and that it was a productive conversation.

Council Member Loyzelle continued to say that she would like to help identify

solutions, and as a partner with the school, the Town should assist with helpful

ideas. Council Member Loyzelle suggested introducing a junior police explorers

program, reiterating that Cutler Bay Middle School is a criminal justice magnet

school and whether through a neighborhood resource officer or alternate

solution we should get this issue under control.

Council Member Mixon expressed concern with social media and its potential to

instigate fighting. Council Member Mixon indicated a need for more discipline

and stated that every child has the right to a safe and secure education.

Mayor Bell opened discussion regarding the Town's sign ordinance, requesting

Town staff provide a historical package for comprehensive review and requested

a workshop for thorough review of land devlopment regulations.

Council Member Loyzelle indicated she was under the impression that the

purpose of this workshop was to address the sign ordinance, and reminded the

Council of the 4-1 vote favoring her proposed sign workshop at the March 18,

2015 Town Council Meeting. Council Member Loyzelle reiterated this would be an

educational discussion, and she would like to put the issue to rest.

Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman interjected stating if the goal of the workshop is

to gain more education on the history and how the sign ordinance came to be,

that could be the first part, the second part could be the land development

regulations, advising the importance of understanding the sign ordinance in its

entire context.

After discussion, the general consensus of the Council was to combine the sign

ordinance regulations with the land development regulations to follow.
