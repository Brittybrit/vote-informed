2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Town Council Workshop: Transportation Master Plan Update (2010)

Sponsors: Town Manager's Office

Town Manager Rafael Casals thanked the Mayor and Town Council for the

opportunity to provide an update of the Transportation Master Plan. Town

Manager Casals introduced Joe Corradino of The Corradino Group as the

consultant selected by the Town to prepare the update to the Transportation

Master Plan.

Joe Corradino provided the Town Council with a detailed overview of the

process of updating the Transportation Master Plan and the transportation

issues identified throughout the process.

Council Member Bell questioned if the projects in the Transportation Master Plan

update are prioritized. Mr. Corradino stated that the project priorities are

arranged by year.

Council Member Bell suggested the addition of a traffic circle on Cutler Ridge

Drive and Franjo Road, if one is not already included in the updated plan. Town

Manager Casals stated that direct feedback was received regarding the

suggested intersection, and that it should be reviewed by Mr. Corradino's team.

Mr. Corradino stated that the project is included in the plan.

VIce Mayor Sochin questioned how the Town should handle future development

of multifamily projects. Mr. Corradino stated that traffic studies must be

conducted before a development is approved that shows that there is sufficient

roadway capacity to support the project. However, he stated that in his 20 years

of experience no study has ever returned results that concluded that the

proposed development would cause traffic issues.

Town Manager Casals stated that the updated plan would be revised per the

direction received from the Town Council to make the document an easier read.

Town Manager Casals stated that the revised document will most likely be

brought back before the Town Council in October for adoption. Once the plan is

approved and adopted, a formal presentation will be made before the MPO.

Town Manager Casals stated that he is available to meet with the Town Council

individually.

Town Manager Casals requested that the Town Council allow Mr. Corradino to

present his report regarding annexation.

Mr. Corradino stated that the Administration of Miami-Dade County has decided

to study annexation again. The County has selected a consultant that will

probably be under contract for their next Commission meeting. That consultant

will have a limited amount of time to go through an extensive, broad scope of

service that will make recommendations back to the County Commission for

dealing with annexation and incorporation. There has been no substantive

movement on the issue since the issue was addressed with the Town Council

last spring.

Mayor MacDougall stated that there are currently two study areas, Areas 1 and 2,

all the way down to Naranja, and all the way up to Southwest 120th Street, which

remove the areas that the Town probably did not want to take in anyway. Mayor

MacDougall questioned if the new study would be more likely to have a positive

financial outcome.

Mr. Corradino stated that the analysis would be fairly simple. The process would

involve simply removing the excluded areas from the original study and re-doing

the numbers. He stated that with the areas in question removed, the Town would

probably not have to invest the significant resources that had been anticipated.

Mayor MacDougall thanked Mr. Corradino for his professionalism.

Town Manager Casals stated that the consultant would continue to monitor the

incorporation/annexation process with Miami-Dade County and provide the Town

Council with any updates in the upcoming meetings.

3. Adjournment

A motion was made by Vice Mayor Sochin to adjourn, and was seconded by

Council Member Loyzelle, with all voting in favor.
