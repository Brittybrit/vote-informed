 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, MOMENT OF
SILENCE & INVOCATION
 Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 7:01PM. Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson
called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance. Mayor Bell led a Moment
of Silence in honor and in memory of Mitzi Gimenez, mother of Miami-Dade
County Mayor Carlos Gimenez.
 Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat
 Present:   5 -
 Mayor Bell welcomed Town Attorney Alison Smith in attendance. Pursuant to
State law, Finance Director Robert Daddario read the TRIM statement into the
record: The proposed millage rate of the Town of Cutler Bay for FY 2016-17 of
2.5329 mils per $1,000 of assessed property value within the Town of Cutler Bay
is 14.13% greater than the roll-back rate of 2.2194 mils per $1,000 of assessed
property value. Ad valorem taxes are being increased primarily as a result of
expenditures for increased costs of contracted police services, as well as to
provide for various improvements to park and public facilities.
Town Manager Rafael Casals provided a brief overview and timeline of the
budget process.
 2. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell opened Public Comments. Mr. David Fienberg spoke in favor of
funding for an additional traffic patrolman, in favor of authorized overtime for
Police services on Halloween night, and on the maintenance of the emergency
radio equipment at the Town Hall building. Mayor Bell closed Public Comments.

 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE TENTATIVE MILLAGE RATE OF THE
TOWN OF CUTLER BAY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1,
2016 THROUGH SEPTEMBER 30, 2017 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTE
200.065 (TRIM BILL); PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Manager Rafael Casals reported on increased property values and
provided clarification on the rollback millage rate versus the ceiling millage rate.
At the request of Council Member Coriat, Finance Director Robert Daddario
provided clarification on ad valorem revenue comparison between FY2015-16
and FY2016-17. At the request of Council Member Loyzelle, Town Manager
Rafael Casals provided clarification on the proposed budget plan dip of $4.3
million based on a ceiling millage rate of 2.5329. At the request of Council
Member Mixon, Finance Director Robert Daddario reported on unassigned
reserve funds as of September 30, 2015 at approximately $18.5 million. Town
Manager Rafael Casals provided a general overview of the Executive Summary
for Fiscal Year 2016-17 Proposed Budget. After discussion, Town Clerk
Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. A motion was made by Vice
Mayor Sochin, seconded by Council Member Mixon, that this Resolution be
adopted. Council Member Coriat proposed a friendly amendment to reflect a
2.3907 millage rate. After discussion, the motion remained at the 2.5329 millage
rate and failed by the following vote:
Yes: 2 - Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin and Council Member Mary Ann Mixon
No: 3 - Mayor Peggy Bell, Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, and Council
Member Roger Coriat
A motion was made by Council Member Coriat, seconded by Council Member
Mixon that this Resolution be adopted as amended with a millage rate of 2.3907.
After discussion, the motion carried at 8:12PM by the following vote:
Yes:   Mayor Peggy Bell, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon, Council Member Sue Ellen
Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat

 B.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE TENTATIVE OPERATING AND CAPITAL
OUTLAY BUDGET FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2016
THROUGH SEPTEMBER 30, 2017 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTE 200.065
(TRIM BILL); AND AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO MAKE
EXPENDITURES CONSISTENT THEREWITH AND AUTHORIZING THE TOWN
MANAGER TO MAKE CERTAIN BUDGET AMENDMENTS WITHIN A DEPARTMENT
PROVIDED THAT THE TOTAL OF THE APPROPRIATIONS IS NOT CHANGED; AND
PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. A motion was
made by Council Member Coriat, seconded by Council Member Mixon that this
Resolution be adopted. The motion carried by the following vote:
Yes: 4 - Mayor Peggy Bell, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon, Council Member
Sue Ellen Loyzelle, and Council Member Roger Coriat
No: 1 - Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin
Town Attorney Alison Smith cautioned the Council to establish a new reduced
budget figure based on the established millage rate ceiling of 2.3907 prior to
voting. Council Member Coriat withdrew the motion and Council Member Mixon
withdrew the second. After discussion, Town Manager Rafael Casals provided a
breakdown of the proposed cuts to the Town Center Capital Outlay Budget
totaling $1,229,850 million. Finance Director Robert Daddario reported a plan dip
of $3,461,737 with the adoption of the 2.3907 millage rate and the reduction of
$1,229,850 million from the Town Center Capital Outlay Budget. The Council
came to a general consensus on the Town Center Capital Outlay Budget cuts.
There was discussion on Park Impact Fees. Mayor Bell reported on a recent
meeting with Town Manager Rafael Casals and Congresswoman Ileana
Ros-Lehtinen regarding Zika funding. Mayor Bell also reported on Town efforts
discussed with Florida Governor Rick Scott advocating for Zika funding. Finance
Director Robert Daddario confirmed the total proposed budget figure for all funds
combined at $52,171,433. Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the
Resolution. Town Attorney Alison Smith advised the Council that a motion to
reconsider was in order. A motion was made by Council Member Coriat,
seconded by Council Member Mixon to reconsider the prior motion to approve
item 3B. The motion carried by the following vote:
Yes: 5 - Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann
Mixon, Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, and Council Member Roger Coriat
A motion was made by Council Member Coriat, seconded by Vice Mayor Sochin,
that this Resolution be adopted. At 10:07PM, the motion carried by the following
vote:
Yes:   Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat
5 -
