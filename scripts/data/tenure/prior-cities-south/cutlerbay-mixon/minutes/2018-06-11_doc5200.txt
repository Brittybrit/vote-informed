A. · Fiscal Year 2018-19 Budget Visioning Workshop

Mayor Bell spoke on the outcome of the bond referendum election. There was

discussion on the possibility of purchasing open land.

There was Council consensus to have the Town Manager bring back information

on the cost of acquiring vacant land and appraisal numbers on the land before

the June 20, 2018 Town Council Meeting, and if the information was not available

prior to that time, call a Special Call Meeting to discuss with the Council prior to

the July 9, 2018 Budget Workshop.

Town Manager Rafael Casals announced the budget workshop dates of July 9

and July 23, and the pending scheduling of the first and second budget hearing

dates. The Town Manager also provided information on the large expenditure

items on the Fiscal Yeah 2018-19 Budget.

There was discussion on the Franjo Park rebuilding logistics and requirements

on bringing the park up to current code. There was Council consensus directing

the Town Manager to make the rebuilding of Franjo Park a priority, and to come

back to the Council with different plans.

There was discussion on the artificial budget dip, the potential of purchasing
