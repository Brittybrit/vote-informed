There were no public comments.

6. REPORTS

A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT

Town Manager Rafael Casals presented a written Town Manager's report to the

Town Council. He invited Ramon Soria and Maria Porrata to provide an update

on the Old Cutler Road Improvements and the upcoming Grand Re-opening.

Gary Ratay and Alina Phillip of Kimley-Horn provided an update on the Caribbean

Boulevard road project. Town Manager Casals reported on the Relay for Life

team and also requested that the Town Council meet on March 19 at 5 p.m. in a

Town Council Workshop to discuss the Town Center building.

B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT

Town Attorney Mitch Bierman advised the Town Council on the Publix litigation

and a new legal matter regarding Citizens Awareness Foundation, Inc. v. Town of

Cutler Bay. Mr. Bierman requested that an Executive Session may need to be

called on Citizens Awareness v. Town of Cutler Bay, Case No. 14-003271 CA01. A

motion was made by Counci Member Loyzelle to hold an Executive Session. The

motion received a second from Council Member Mary Ann Mixon. Mayor

MacDougall called for the vote and the motion passed 5-0.

C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT

Town Clerk Debra Eastman reported on the status of the installation and use of

Granicus for website audio streaming and agenda management. She informed

the Council that there are presently two residents who have opened campaign

accounts for Mayor and for Seat 3. Town Clerk Eastman reported on the practice

of Miami-Dade County and other municipalities paying for the return postage for

absentee ballots. It was agreed via consensus to pay for return postage for

absentee ballots for the November 4, 2014 election.

D. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS

Council Members Bell and Mixon provided information on upcoming Committee

events and agendas.

13. MAYOR AND COUNCIL COMMENTS

Council Member Loyzelle reported that she will be holding a Town Hall style

meeting regarding the Bureau of Unclaimed Property records of unclaimed

property in order to inform residents of money due to them. Council Member

Loyzelle made a motion to use up to $1,000 in her budgeted travel funds to pay

for an advertisement for the Town Hall meeting. Vice Mayor Sochin provided a

second. Mayor MacDougall called for the vote and all were in favor.

After further discussion, Council Member Loyzelle made a motion to reconsider

the vote. Mayor MacDougall provided a second. Mayor MacDougall called for the

vote and the motion to reconsider failed 3-2 with Mayor MacDougall and Council

Member Loyzelle voting in favor. Council Member Mixon, Council Member Bell

and Vice Mayor Sochin voted in opposition of the reconsideration.
