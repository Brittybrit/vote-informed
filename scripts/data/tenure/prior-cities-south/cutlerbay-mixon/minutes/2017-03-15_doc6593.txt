 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, & MOMENT OF
SILENCE
 Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 7:10PM. Interim Town Clerk Debra
Eastman called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance. Vice Mayor
Loyzelle led a moment of silence in remembrance of Jamie Bell, son of Mayor
Peggy Bell and Marty Bell. Mayor Bell expressed gratitude to Town Staff and the
community for their support during this difficult time. Mayor Bell said a few
words in honor of her son Jamie Bell.
Mayor Bell requested to move forward item 5C on the Consent Agenda. A motion
was made by Council Member Coriat, seconded by Council Member Mixon to
move forward item 5C on the Consent Agenda. Mayor Bell called for the vote and
the motion passed 5-0. Town Manager Rafael Casals requested a motion to defer
item 10A on the agenda to no date certain. A motion was made by Council
Member Coriat, seconded by Council Member Mixon to defer item 10A on the
agenda to no date certain. Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion passed
5-0.

March 15, 2017 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 A. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Vice Mayor Loyzelle reported on attendance at the Palmetto Bay 15th Anniversary
Picnic Celebration and the presentation of a proclamation to the Village of
Palmetto Bay honoring their 15th Anniversary. Vice Mayor Loyzelle announced
the upcoming Relay for Life Event on March 18 and the upcoming Miami Dade
County League of Cities Annual Gala on May 13. A motion was made by Vice
Mayor Loyzelle, seconded by Council Member Mixon, to purchase a table for the
Miami Dade County League Of Cities Annual Gala. Mayor Bell called for the vote
and the motion passed 5-0. Vice Mayor Loyzelle reported on the Adult Eduication
Program at Cutler Bay Senior High School and the AARP Foundation Senior
Community Service Employment Program's Office Skills Training Program.
Council Member Callahan announced the upcoming South Florida Diving
Museum Interactive Workshop Event at the COAST Academy on March 24 and
the Annual Baynanza Cleanup Event on April 22.
Council Member Coriat announced the South Miami-Dade Cultural Arts Center
MOMIX Opus Cactus Event on March 19, and the Beer and Music Backyard
Festival on March 25. Council Member Coriat reported on the recent Education
Advisory Committee meeting and announced the upcoming meeting on April 6.
Education Advisory Committee Chair Ms. Marisa Portuondo provided a
recommendation on behalf of the Education Advisory Committee regarding
Senate Bill 78. Mayor Bell passed the gavel to Vice Mayor Loyzelle. A motion
was made by Mayor Bell, seconded by Council Member Coriat to add Senate Bill
78 to the legislative agenda. After discussion, Vice Mayor Loyzelle called for the
vote and the motion passed 5-0.
Council Member Mixon announced the upcoming Floodplain Mitigation Plan
Committee Meeting on March 23.
 ·   Mayor Bell Monthly Report February 7 - March 2 2017
 Mayor Bell announced the upcoming Youth Council Committee trip to
Tallahassee on April 30, the upcoming Town Council Workshop for the Town
Clerk selection process on March 29, and the upcoming Open Mic Night on April
24. Mayor Bell reported on the circulator bus and its new design.
 B. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS
 Council Member Callahan reported on attendance at the 2nd Annual Wings Over
the Bay Event. Council Member Callahan announced the upcoming Bite Nite
Food Truck Festival on March 17 and the Annual Easter Egg Hunt on April 1.
Vice Mayor Loyzelle announced Miami-Dade County Days on April 5-6, and
requested that the Interim Clerk poll the Community of a Lifetime Committee to
reschedule the April meeting to a later date. Vice Mayor Loyzelle spoke on the
Louise Lockwood Butterfly Garden and requested the discussion be added to the
Parks and Recreation Committee Meeting agenda for April.
 3. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell opened Public Comments. Mr. Adam Skowronski spoke in favor of
modifying residential shutter regulations allowing side doors and side windows
to remain shuttered. Mr. Adam Skowronski also spoke in opposition of peacocks
in the community. Ms. Michelle Styles, Branch Manager at the South Dade
Regional Library, spoke on programs and services available at the library. Mayor
Bell closed public comments.

March 15, 2017 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 4. REPORTS
 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT
 Town Manager Rafael Casals announced that the Town is the recipient of the
2017 Community Health Champion Award, and announced all Town police
officers have been outfitted with body cameras. Town Manager Casals also
announced several grants awarded to the Town, including the Florida
Department of Environmental Protection Matching Grant for $274,000.00 for a
drainage project along Southwest 100th Avenue, the Neat Streets Miami Matching
Grant for $12,500.00 to increase tree canopy along Southwest 87th Avenue, and
the Children's Trust Grant for $136,000.00 for a summer robotics camp for 40
students from Cutler Bay Middle School.
Vice Mayor Loyzelle announced Miami-Dade County District 8 Mom and Pop
Small Business Grant Program Workshop on March 16.
Town Manager Rafael Casals announced the Whispering Pines Park renovations
and equipment installation beginning in April. Town Manager Rafael Casals
reported on attendance at the recent Concerned Citizens of Cutler Bay Meeting,
and also announced the Town Council's participation at the Miami- Dade League
of Cities Legislative Action Days in Tallahassee on March 21.
Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced Gary Ratay of Kimley Horn and
Associates to present an update on the Caribbean Boulevard Gap 2 Project.
Public Information Officer Janet Delgado with Kimley Horn and Associates
provided an update on community outreach for the Caribbean Boulevard Gap 2
Project.
Town Manager Rafael Casals informed the Council a motion would be required
for approval to purchase tickets for spouses for the Miami-Dade County League
of Cities Annual Gala Event. A motion was made by Vice Mayor Loyzelle,
seconded by Council Member Mixon to allow the purchase of tickets for spouses
for the Miami-Dade County League of Cities Annual Gala Event. Mayor Bell
called for the vote, and the motion passed 5-0.
 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT
 There was no Town Attorney Report.
 C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT
 There was no Town Clerk Report.
Vice Mayor Loyzelle announced the election of Florida State Representative
Kionne L. McGhee as the Leader Designate of the Florida House of Democrats.
