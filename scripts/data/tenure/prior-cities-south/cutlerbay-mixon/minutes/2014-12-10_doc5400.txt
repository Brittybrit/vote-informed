 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, & PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
 Mayor Peggy Bell called the meeting to order at 7 p.m. Town Clerk Debra
Eastman called the roll.
Mayor Bell gave an opening statement thanking the voters for their confidence by
electing her Mayor and looking forward to the future. Re-elected Council Member
Mary Ann Mixon, Seat 1, thanked voters for their continuing faith in her. Newly
elected Council Member Coriat, who ran unopposed, expressed his excitement to
begin his service to the Town. Council Member Loyzelle and Vice Mayor Sochin
expressed their congratulations to the newly elected members.
 2. PROCLAMATIONS, AWARDS, AND PRESENTATIONS
 A.   ·   Cutler Bay Officer of the Quarter
 Police Commander Julie Miller explained the incident involving extraordinary
police work that led to the commendation of the following police personnel: Lt.
Frank Roig, Det. Jon Robert, Det. Danny Hurtado, Det. Ruben Moya, Det. Jorge
Baluja, Det. Raymond Haar, Det. Thomas Molina, Officer Elisa de Rojas and Sgt.
Ronald Rodriguez. Mayor Bell and members of the Town Council, along with
Town Manager Ralph Casals, praised the outstanding work of the police, thanked
them for their exemplary service and congratulated them on their award.

 4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Council Member Loyzelle announced that the 2015 Transportation Summit will
take place on January 22 and encouraged members of the Council to attend.
Council Member Loyzelle indicated that she will be unable to attend the Martin
Luther King, Jr. parade in West Perrine this year and asked that other members
of the Town Council participate.
Vice Mayor Sochin announced that he attended the Town Bus Party and thanked
the Town Manager for a terrific event.
Council Member Coriat stated for the record that he will be opting out of the
Town's health insurance as he has health insurance coverage at his job. This is
a savings to the Town of approximately $8,100 annually.
Mayor Bell announced that she met with Miami-Dade County Police Director JD
Paterson and Cutler Bay Police Commander Julie Miller where they honored the
officers of the month in a wonderful ceremony.
Mayor Bell asked if there was any objection to her including an invocation to the
monthly Town Council agenda using different churches and faiths. There was no
objection.
Mayor Bell reported on her recent attendance at the Veteran's Memorial at
Graceland Memorial Gardens.
 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Susan McCarthy spoke regarding an abandoned property adjacent to her home
and the surrounding problems. Police Commander Julie Miller and Code
Compliance DIrector Sandra Cuervo met with her outside to discuss the
situation.
