 3. RESOLUTIONS
 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE TENTATIVE MILLAGE RATE OF THE
TOWN OF CUTLER BAY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1,
2015 THROUGH SEPTEMBER 30, 2016 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTE
200.065 (TRIM BILL); PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. Pursuant
to State law, Finance Director Robert Daddario read the TRIM statement into the
record: The proposed millage rate of the Town of Cutler Bay for FY 2015-16 of
2.5348 mils per $1,000 of assessed property value within the Town of Cutler Bay
is 16.01% greater than the roll-back rate of 2.1849 mils per $1,000 of assessed
property value. Ad valorem taxes are being increased primarily as a result of
expenditures for increased costs of contracted police services, as well as to
provide for various improvements to park and public facilities.
Town Manager Rafael Casals provided a brief overview and timeline of the
budget process.

 B.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE TENTATIVE OPERATING AND CAPITAL
OUTLAY BUDGET FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2015
THROUGH SEPTEMBER 30, 2016 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTE 200.065
(TRIM BILL); AND AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO MAKE
EXPENDITURES CONSISTENT THEREWITH AND AUTHORIZING THE TOWN
MANAGER TO MAKE CERTAIN BUDGET AMENDMENTS WITHIN A DEPARTMENT
PROVIDED THAT THE TOTAL OF THE APPROPRIATIONS IS NOT CHANGED; AND
PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. Mayor Bell
opened the public hearing. There being no one wishing to speak, Mayor Bell
closed the public hearing. A motion was made by Vice Mayor Sochin, seconded
by Council Member Coriat, that this Resolution be adopted. At the request of
Town Attorney Chad Friedman, Finance Director Robert Daddario confirmed the
total proposed budget figure for all funds combined at $52,018,174.00 for the
record. Town Manager Rafael Casals announced the Second Budget Hearing on
September 24, 2015. At the request of Vice Mayor Sochin, Town Manager Rafael
Casals confirmed for the record that funds may be reallocated as needed without
altering the total budget figure. Council Member Loyzelle announced the
September 30, 2015 Town Council Workshop and requested a discussion be
added to the agenda to discuss policy development for committee funding.
Mayor Bell acknowledged the September 30, 2015 Town Council Workshop
agenda as lengthy, but opted to coordinate with Town Manager Rafael Casals to
accommodate her request, time-permitting. Mayor Bell reopened the public
hearing. There being no one wishing to speak, Mayor Bell closed the public
hearing. At 7:38PM the motion carried by the following vote:
Yes:   Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle
and Council Member Roger Coriat
4 -
 Absent:   Council Member Mary Ann Mixon 1 -
