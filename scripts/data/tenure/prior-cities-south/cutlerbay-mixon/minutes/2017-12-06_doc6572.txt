6:30 PM Council ChambersWednesday, December 6, 2017

Special Town Council Meeting

1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, & MOMENT OF SILENCE

Mayor Peggy Bell called the meeting to order at 6:50 p.m. Town Clerk Debra

Eastman called the roll with the following Town Council Members present:

Council Member Michael Callahan, Council Member Roger Coriat, Council

Member Mary Ann Mixon, Vice Mayor Sue Loyzelle and Mayor Peggy Bell. In

the absence of Town Manager Ralph Casals, Interim Town Manager Sandra

Aronoff was in attendance.

Mayor Bell led the pledge of allegiance and called for a moment of silence for

the victims and families involved with the California wildfires.

Mayor Bell welcomed the audience and asked that if they wish to speak they fill

out a speaker card. Further Mayor Bell provided the rules of procedure for the

meeting.

Council Member Coriat made a motion to allow the public to speak prior to the

presentation. The motion received a second from Council Member Callahan.

Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion passed 5-0.

Mayor Bell introduced Miami Dade County Commissioner Daniella Levine Cava

who was in attendance.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member Mary Ann

Mixon, Council Member Michael P. Callahan and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present: 5 -

2. PRESENTATION

A. · Presentation- Miami-Dade County Traffic Study S.W. 87 Avenue Bridge-

Frank P. Guyamier, Deputy Director, Engineering, and Darlene M. Fernandez, Assistant

Director, Traffic Services, Miami-Dade Department of Transportation and Public Works

Frank Guyamier, Deputy Director of Engineering and Darlene Fernandez,

Assistant Director Traffic Services, Miami Dade Department of Transportation and

Public Works were introduced by Interim Town Manager Sandra Aronoff. They

provided information and explained the results of the study on the Miami Dade

County Traffic Study regarding the S.W. 87 Avenue Bridge and answered

3. PUBLIC COMMENTS

Mayor Bell invited County Commissioner Daniella Levine Cava who spoke about

traffic and public transportation and the need for more jobs in South Dade.

Mayor Bell invited Palmetto Bay Mayor Eugene Flynn who spoke about their

circulator bus and the need for rail service. They spoke about the need for more

studies and an environmental assessment.

Mayor Bell opened the public comments and asked that comments be kept to a 2

minute limit.

Tom Condon, Kevin Woitke, William Bell, Barbara Condon, Melanie Nunez,

Sandra Wong, Teresa Schuster, Yunior Santana, Michelle Craven, Edward

Verona and Steve Zarzecki spoke in favor of opening the bridge at S.W. 87th

Avenue.

Palmetto Bay Mayor Flynn spoke about the role of the County and their failure to

provide for transportation needs.

Palmetto Bay Council Member David Singer encouraged working together to

expedite the SMART plan.

Joe Bier spoke in concurrence with the need for alternatives to assist with traffic

problems.

Deputy Town Clerk Mauricio Melinu read into the record emails received on

behalf of the Mayor and Town Council from Barbara Ruiz, Felipe Balthazar,

Francisco Amador, Paul Vrooman, Gary Apostolakos, Geraldine Carrillo, Jeanee

McJilton, Mariajose Amador, Mike Schuler, Nikki Watarz, Sandra Arthur, Sharon

Johnson, Bill Kestel and Susan Lewis.

Loretta Gregory, Steve Turner, Fei Tang, Nils Linfors, Debby Hutchins, Gilbert

Viera, Beatriz Herrmann and Kathryn Linfors spoke in opposition of opening the

bridge at S.W. 87th Avenue.

Leslie Lamkin asked that the opening of the bridge be tabled until the master

plan is complete.

There being no further public comment, Mayor Bell thanked everyone for their

comments.

4. RESOLUTIONS

A. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, SUPPORTING THE PROPOSED CONSTRUCTION OF A BRIDGE ON

S.W. 87 AVENUE AND URGING MIAMI-DADE COUNTY COMMISSIONER DANIELLA

LEVINE CAVA TO SUPPORT CONSTRUCTION OF THE BRIDGE ALSO; AND

PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the title of the resolution. After discussion by

the Town Council a motion was made by Council Member Coriat, seconded by

Council Member Callahan, that this Resolution be adopted . The motion carried

by an unanimous vote. (5-0)

5. ADJOURNMENT

The meeting adjourned at 9:57 p.m.
