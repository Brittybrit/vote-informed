May 21, 2014 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, & PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
 Council Member Mixon made a motion to allow Vice Mayor Sochin to participate
in the Town Council meeting via telephone. The motion received a second from
Council Member Bell. Mayor MacDougall called for the vote and all were in favor.

Mary Ann Mixon, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin and Mayor Edward MacDougall
 Present:   5 -
 2. PROCLAMATIONS, AWARDS, AND PRESENTATIONS
 A.   Palmetto Raiders All-American Scholars
 The Mayor and Town Council presented 100 percent scholars Daniel Henderson
and Lance Holden, members of the Palmetto Raiders, with a certificate of
achievement for their outstanding scholastic accomplishment.
 B.   Miami-Dade Fire Rescue 2013 Annual Report
 Miami-Dade County Fire Chief Dave Downey presented the annual Fire
Department Report to the Mayor and Town Council. Members of the Town
Council thanked Chief Downey for his service and the recent open house.

 ·   Regular Town Council Meeting, April 16, 2014
 A motion was made by Council Member Loyzelle, seconded by Vice Mayor
Sochin, that the minutes be approved. The motion carried by an unanimous vote.

 Council Member Bell, Council Member Loyzelle, Council Member Mixon, Vice

 Yes:   5 -
 4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Marilyn Horn presented thank you notes from school students to the Town
Manager, Mayor and Town Council for their assistance and presentations at the
Earth Day event.
Council Member Bell acknowledged the Police bike patrol in her neighborhood.
Council Member Bell explained the request to "Zillow" to add the Cutler Bay High
School to their website as a feature of the Town of Cutler Bay. Council Member
Bell announced that there will be a barbeque event on June 20 as a employee
appreciation and particularly emphasized the Parks and Recreation employees
for going above and beyond the call of duty.
Council Member Loyzelle thanked staff for the Earth Day events recently and
reported on her attendance at the enviromental fair.
Council Member Bell thanked Mayor MacDougall and Superintendent Carvalho
regarding their assistance with changes at Cutler Bay Academy, Centennial
campus regarding the addition of water sports and the removal of the 8th grade
from the high school.
Mayor MacDougall reported on recent meetings of the South Dade Mayors and
their impact upon the County denial of a soccer stadium at the Port of Miami
location.
Vice Mayor Sochin reported on his attendance at career day in the Village of
Pametto Bay.

Mayor MacDougall reported on attendance at the opening of a new local

 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Jonathan Osorio spoke regarding the tennis courts and the use of some courts
for pickle ball. Mayor MacDougall provided a history of the tennis courts and
surrounding amenities and reminded that the courts are for all to use.
 6. REPORTS
 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT
 Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced town lobbyist Fausto Gomez who
provided an end of legislative session report to the Town Council. The Mayor
and Town Council Members thanked him for the report.
Gary Ratay and Alina Phillip of Kimley Horn and Associates provided an update
to the progress of the Caribbean Boulevard project.
The Town Manager reported on the progress of securing funds to further
improve areas of Caribbean Boulevard.
The Town Manager reported to the Town Council that the Town is eligible to
receive a $13,261 Byrne Grant. He advised the Town Council that the Town
received funding last year for burglary suppression. This year NRU officers have
been trained to provide security surveys and would like to use the grant to
enhance their crime prevention program. He stated that the Town is currently
seeking public comment regarding this project. The period of public comment
has been advertised using fliers and has been posted on the Town’s website.
 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT
 Town Attorney Mitch Bierman reported that they were recently successful when
challenged by a former employee who applied for unemployment compensation.
 C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT
 Town Clerk Debra Eastman reported on her participation at the Cutler Bay
Concerned Citizen's Meeting regarding Town Elections, the status of the new
website pages and the automated agenda software Granicus debugging. She
reported on the iLegislate app that is available to the Council members.
 D. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS
 Members of the Town Council reported on meetings of various Town
Committees.
