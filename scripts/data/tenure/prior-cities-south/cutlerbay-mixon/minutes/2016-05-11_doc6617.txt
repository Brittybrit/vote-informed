1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, MOMENT OF

SILENCE & INVOCATION

Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 6:34PM. Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson

called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance. Council Member Loyzelle

arrived at 6:37PM.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,

Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present 5 -

Applicant: Village of Old Cutler Corp.

Application No.: SP-2015-001

Location: West of SW 82 Ave (between SW 188 St and SW 189 St)

Zoning District: NC-1 (Neighborhood Center 1)

FLUM

Designation: Mixed Use

Folio No.: 36-6009-003-0310 & 36-6009-003-0290

Legal: Tenalla Ocean Farms Additions PB 12-40 Lots 1-2-3 BLK 14 and all of

lots 4 & 5 Lots 6 & 7 Less S 10 ft & all Lots 8 thru 13 Blk 14

Property Size: 3.64 Acres

CVS Pharmacy and three (3) story mixed use residential building.

Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced Planning and Zoning Director Kathryn

Lyon to provide a brief overview of the project, with consultant support from

Alex David with the Corradino Group, and Robin Pelensky with Iler Planning.

Planning and Zoning Director Kathryn Lyon introduced Mr. Graham Penn of

Bercow Radell & Fernandez for a brief presentation on behalf of the applicant.
