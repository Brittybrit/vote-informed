September 24, 2015 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE FINAL MILLAGE RATE OF THE TOWN
OF CUTLER BAY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2015
THROUGH SEPTEMBER 30, 2016 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTE 200.065
(TRIM BILL); PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Finance Director Robert Daddario read the TRIM statement mandated by the
State of Florida into the record: The tentative millage rate of the Town of Cutler
Bay for FY 2015-16 of 2.3907 mils per $1,000 of assessed property value within
the Town of Cutler Bay is 9.42% greater than the roll-back rate of 2.1849 mils per
$1,000 of assessed property value. Ad valorem taxes are being increased
primarily as a result of expenditures for increased costs of contracted police
services, as well as to provide for various improvements to park and public
facilities.
Town Manager Rafael Casals provided a brief overview and timeline of the
budget procedure.
Mayor Bell opened the public hearing. Ms. Tanya Rovin spoke in opposition of
an increase in property tax. Town Manager Rafael Casals directed Ms. Rovin to
meet with Public Works Director Alfredo Quintero to review her flood insurance
plan and ensure she is receiving the discounted rate received for residing within
the Town.
Mr. Sergio Lopez thanked the Town for their efforts and the development of Old
Cutler Road and Caribbean Boulevard, and expressed concern with the millage
rate. Mayor Bell provided clarification for the proposed millage rate. Mr. Lopez
suggested a budget summary sheet provided for reference at budget meetings in
addition to the Town's current outreach methods and requested the gross
percentage increase in revenues expended for 2014 to 2015. Town Manager
Rafael Casals confirmed the increase in ad valorem revenues from 2014 to 2015
at $383,022 as a result of increased property values. Mayor Bell closed the public
hearing. A motion was made by Council Member Loyzelle, seconded by Vice
Mayor Sochin, that this Resolution be adopted. At 7:39PM, the motion carried by
the following vote:
Yes:   Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat
5 -
 B.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE FINAL OPERATING AND CAPITAL
OUTLAY BUDGET FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2015
THROUGH SEPTEMBER 30, 2016 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTE 200.065
(TRIM BILL); AND AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO MAKE
EXPENDITURES CONSISTENT THEREWITH AND AUTHORIZING THE TOWN
MANAGER TO MAKE CERTAIN BUDGET AMENDMENTS WITHIN A DEPARTMENT
PROVIDED THAT THE TOTAL OF THE APPROPRIATIONS IS NOT CHANGED; AND
PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Mayor Bell opened the public hearing. There being no one wishing to speak,
Mayor Bell closed the public hearing. A motion was made by Council Member
Mixon, seconded by Council Member Coriat, that this Resolution be adopted.
Council Member Loyzelle explained that the roll back rate was explored, but did
not seem feasible. At 7:45PM, the motion carried by the following vote:
Yes:   Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat
