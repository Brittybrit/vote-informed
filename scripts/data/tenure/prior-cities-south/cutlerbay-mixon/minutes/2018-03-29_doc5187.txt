A. · Discussion Regarding General Obligation Bond Referendum

· Projects

· Key Dates

Town Manager Ralph Casals introduced the bond team of Town Attorneys Chad

Friedman and Jeff DeCarlo, Financial Advisor Ed Stull and Town Finance

Director Robert Daddario. Manager Casals requested that the Town Council

provide direction to him via consensus as to go forward with two appraisals, a

survey and an environmental study on the proposed property for purchase.

There was discussion on the cost of an appraisal, the length of time it will take to

obtain it and the expiration date of the appraisal documents. Town Manager

Ralph Casals provided the Town Council with an internal workbook document for

discussion purposes outlining the timeline and deadlines, outlining the elements

of the bond question and sought Council input on the items important to

providing informational materials to voters regarding the purpose of the bond.

There was discussion of the legal deadlines and the additional meetings and

informational forums and mailings that may be used to provide information to the

voters. There was discussion regarding whether the bond funds would be used

to fix currently owned parks or whether the funds should be used only for newly

acquired property. There was a discussion of the proposed goals and acquiring

open land for public facilities and what it would include. There was discussion

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Council Member Coriat spoke about being in favor of the GOB and looks forward

to working to promote the passage of the bond. He stated that although he voted

in favor of setting time limits for debate at the last Town Council meeting, that at

the Special Call meeting on Monday, he intends to make a motion to amend the

rules that the Town Council adopted last week and to abide by Roberts Rules

allowing ten minutes for debate and rebuttal unless waived by majority vote of

Council.
