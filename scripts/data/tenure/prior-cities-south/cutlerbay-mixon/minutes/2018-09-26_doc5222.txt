10. RESOLUTIONS

A. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, DESIGNATING LINCOLN CITY PARK AS A DOG PARK; PROVIDING

FOR IMPLEMENTATION; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Mayor Bell amended the proposed Resolution to direct the Town Manager to

explore the feasibility of converting Lincoln City Park into a dog park.

There was discussion on the parks master plan, and the possibility of the land

being pine rockland.

Mayor Bell opened Public Comments. Marty Bell spoke in favor of the

Resolution.

There being no one else wishing to speak, Mayor Bell closed Public Comments.

There was discussion on the cost of a feasibility study and time, the possibility to

look at the current site and other parks in Town for a dog park, and the possibility

of extending the feasibility study to all parks, resident concerns on maintaining

the park as a rockland hammock and the size of the park, the possibility of

making it a passive park, and discussion in the Parks and Recreation Advisory

Committee on the need for a dog park but not at this location.

A motion was made by Mayor Bell, seconded by Council Member Mixon, to

approve the Resolution with the amendment directing the Town Manager to look

into the feasibility of Lincoln City Park becoming a future dog park.

After further discussion, Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion passed 3-2,

with Council Member Coriat and Vice Mayor Loyzelle voting in opposition.
