 4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Vice Mayor Sochin reported on his attendance at the Military Meet and Greet
event in Homestead honoring Military Veterans.
Council Member Loyzelle announced that the Town of Cutler Bay has been
selected to receive the Florida Council on Aging's Quality Senior Living Award on
August 11, 2015 at the Annual Conference on Aging. Council Member Loyzelle
also reported on her attendance at the National Association of Community and
Restorative Justice Conference on June 1-3, 2015. Council Member Loyzelle also
reported that the South Police Station and the South Dade Government Center
have been established as Safe Haven locations in Cutler Bay.
Mayor Bell announced Governor Scott has signed the Transportation bill to
include the use of golf carts on County Roads. Mayor Bell thanked State Senator
Dwight Bullard, State Senator Gwen Margolis, State Representative Erik Fresen
and State Representative Kionne McGhee for their assistance and support.
Mayor Bell introduced Sean McCrackine, Chief of Policy and Planning for
Commissioner Levine Cava to report on the South Dade Solutions Summit
hosted by Miami-Dade County Commissioners Daniella Levine Cava and Dennis
Moss. Mayor Bell provided an update on the Cutler Bay Middle School Violence
issue and announced a Town Council Workshop on July 13, 2015 with Mr.
Alberto Carvalho, Superintendent of Miami-Dade Public Schools and Cutler Bay
Middle School Principal to present the Cutler Bay Middle School Action Plan.
Mayor Bell also requested the Education Advisory Committee receive an
invitation to this workshop.

 13.   MAYOR AND COUNCIL COMMENTS
 Council Member Loyzelle reported on her attendance at the Village of Palmetto
Bay's Council Meeting and concerns associated with the PACE Program. Mayor
Bell acknowledged the concerns associated with the PACE Program and the
additional workload Town Staff has incurred, and also acknowledged the
sentiments have been conveyed to the PACE Board of Directors.

called for the vote and the motion carried 4-0.

 WE, THE TOWN COUNCIL OF CUTLER BAY, HEREBY RECOMMIT OURSELVES TO MAINTAIN
CIVILITY IN OUR PUBLIC AND POLITICAL DISCOURSE AND WE HEREBY PLEDGE OUR
COMMITMENT TO THE FOLLOWING PRINCIPLES:
• We will respect the right of all citizens in our community to hold different opinions;
• We will avoid rhetoric intended to humiliate or question the wisdom of those whose opinions are
different from ours;
• We will strive to understand differing perspectives;
• We will choose our words carefully;
• We will speak truthfully without accusation and we will avoid distortion; and
• We will speak out against violence, prejudice and incivility of their forms, whenever and wherever
they occur.
PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTES 286.0105, THE TOWN HEREBY ADVISES THE PUBLIC
THAT IF A PERSON DECIDES TO APPEAL ANY DECISION MADE BY THIS COUNCIL WITH
RESPECT TO ANY MATTER CONSIDERED AT ITS MEETING OR HEARING, HE OR SHE WILL
NEED A RECORD OF THE PROCEEDINGS, AND THAT FOR SUCH PURPOSE, THE AFFECTED
PERSON MAY NEED TO ENSURE THAT VERBATIM RECORD OF THE PROCEEDING IS MADE,
WHICH RECORD INCLUDES THE TESTIMONY AND EVIDENCE UPON WHICH THE APPEAL IS
TO BE BASED. THIS NOTICE DOES NOT CONSTITUTE CONSENT BY THE TOWN FOR THE
INTRODUCTION OR ADMISSION OF OTHERWISE INADMISSIBLE OR IRRELEVANT EVIDENCE,
NOR DOES IT AUTHORIZE CHALLENGES OR APPEALS NOT OTHERWISE ALLOWED BY LAW.
