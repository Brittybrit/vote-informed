 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, MOMENT OF
SILENCE & INVOCATION

called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance. Mayor Bell led a Moment

Carol Cohen, sister-in-law of Vice Mayor Sochin.

 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT
 Town Manager Rafael Casals announced the appointment of Jazmin Gonzalez as
Acting Administrative Services Director. Town Manager Rafael Casals
introduced Police Major Julie Miller to report on the Grinch Busters detail to
reduce holiday related crime. Police Major Julie Miller also reported on the Black
and Blue Run and thanked everyone for their efforts and support. Town Manager
Rafael Casals reported on the distribution of turkeys received from Miami-Dade
County League of Cities. Town Manager Rafael Casals also reported on staff
attendance at Public Records Training, grant funding received for bike pedestrian
facilities improvements and on receipt of the 2015 Best Practices Government
Finalists List Award. Gary Ratay and Alina Philipp of Kimley Horn and Associates
presented an update on the Caribbean Boulevard Roadway Improvement Project.
Town Manager Rafael Casals reported on the recent Green Corridor PACE
Meeting and announced the transition of Executive Director and District
Secretary duties to a newly selected firm via RFP process.
 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT

 C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT

and announced upcoming requests from Town Council Liaisons to establish
2016 committee meeting dates.

 Council Member Coriat reported on the Parks and Recreation Committee and
announced several upcoming events. Council Member Coriat also announced
the next Parks and Recreation Committee Meeting on January 4, 2016.
Council Member Mixon announced the next Floodplain Mitigation Plan Advisory
Committee Meeting on December 10, 2016.
Vice Mayor Sochin reported on the Education Advisory Commitee Meeting at the
South Dade Cultural Arts Center along with members from the Youth Council
Committee. Vice Mayor Sochin also thanked Building and Code Compliance
Director, Sandra Aronoff and staff for assistance with a recent permit.
Council Member Loyzelle reported on the progress of the Communities for a
Lifetime Committee and announced the next committee meeting on December 9,
2015.

November 19, 2015. Mayor Bell also reported on the progress of the Youth
Council Committee and announced the next Youth Council Committee Meeting
on November 23, 2015. Mayor Bell also announced that the Green Corridor PACE
Board will try to meet on a quarterly basis.

November 18, 2015 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, ACCEPTING URBAN AND COMMUNITY FORESTRY
GRANT AWARD IN THE AMOUNT OF $7,500; AUTHORIZING THE TOWN
MANAGER TO ENTER INTO A MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT WITH THE
FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE AND CONSUMER SERVICES, AND
PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 This Resolution was adopted on Consent.
 B.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND THE TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, IN SUPPORT OF THE UNITED STATES
ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY’S (THE “EPA”) CLEAN POWER PLAN
RULE FOR PURPOSES OF SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCING GREENHOUSE GAS
EMISSIONS THAT THREATEN THE SOUTH FLORIDA REGION THROUGH
CLIMATE CHANGE AND SEA LEVEL RISE AND OPPOSING ANY REQUESTS THAT
THE CLEAN POWER PLAN RULE BE STAYED DURING PERIODS OF LEGAL
CHALLENGE OR LITIGATION; PROVIDING FOR TRANSMITTAL AND PROVIDING
FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 This Resolution was adopted on Consent.
 C.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND THE TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, IN SUPPORT OF THE MIAMI TIGER BEETLE AND
ENCOURAGING THE FLORIDA FISH AND WILDLIFE CONSERVATION
COMMISSION TO DESIGNATE THE MIAMI TIGER BEETLE AS A THREATENED
SPECIES; PROVIDING FOR TRANSMITTAL; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE
DATE.
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. Council Member
Mixon expressed support and made a motion that this Resolution be adopted.
The motion received a second from Council Member Coriat. Vice Mayor Sochin
spoke in opposition. After discussion, Mayor Bell called for the vote and the
motion passed 4-1.
 D.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, AMENDING RESOLUTION 14-66, WHICH ADOPTED THE
BUDGET FOR THE 2014/2015 FISCAL YEAR BY REVISING THE 2014/2015
OPERATING AND CAPITAL BUDGET AS OUTLINED IN EXHIBIT “A” ATTACHED
HERETO; AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO DO ALL THINGS NECESSARY
TO CARRY OUT THE PROVISIONS OF THIS RESOLUTION; AND PROVIDING FOR
AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 This Resolution was adopted on Consent.

November 18, 2015 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, APPROVING THE AWARD OF GRANT FUNDING FROM
THE CUTLER BAY COMMUNITY FUND TO CUTLER BAY HIGH SCHOOL IN THE
AMOUNT OF $2,500 IN SUPPORT OF THE SCHOOL’S COAST PROGRAM AND TO
CUTLER BAY MIDDLE SCHOOL IN THE AMOUNT OF $5,000 TO SUPPORT THE
SCHOOL’S POSITIVE BEHAVIOR SYSTEM; PROVIDING FOR AUTHORIZATION;
AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. Town Manager
Rafael Casals presented the item awarding funds from the Cutler Bay Community
Fund and welcomed Principal Paul Pfeiffer of the Cutler Bay Middle School and
Principal Lucas De La Torre of Cutler Bay High School. After discussion on
funds allocation, a motion was made by Council Member Loyzelle, seconded by
Council Member Mixon to approve the additional $500 for the High School,
increasing to $3000 and the funds for the Middle School remain at $5000. Mayor
Bell called for a vote and the motion passed 5-0.
Council Member Loyzelle announced the 24 hour online donation event Give
Miami Day on November 19, 2015.
Council Member Loyzelle suggested designation of a specific event to help raise
money for the Cutler Bay Community Fund. Mayor Bell suggested a workshop to
address various items. A motion was made by Council Member Loyzelle,
seconded by Council Member Mixon to schedule a Town Council Workshop in
February at the January 20, 2016 Regular Town Council Meeting to discuss Town
Special Events and other items. Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion
passed 4-1 with Vice Mayor Sochin in opposition. After discussion, Mayor Bell
requested the Town Clerk compile requests for the Council to determine whether
or not to schedule a workshop at the January 20, 2016 Regular Town Council
Meeting.
 B.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF

made by Council Member Mixon, seconded by Vice Mayor Sochin, that this

 A.   AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, AMENDING CHAPTER 30 “TRAFFIC AND MOTOR
VEHICLES” REGARDING GOLF CART USE ON STREETS WITHIN THE TOWN;
DELETING EXHIBIT “A” TO ORDINANCE 10-03; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY;
PROVIDING FOR CONFLICT, AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Ordinance. Mayor Bell opened
the public hearing. There being no one wishing to speak, Mayor Bell closed the
public hearing. There was discussion on golf cart access at the County level,
and consistent speed limits. After discussion, a motion was made by Council
Member Loyzelle, seconded by Council Member Mixon, that this Ordinance be
approved on second reading. The motion carried by the following vote:
Yes:   Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
