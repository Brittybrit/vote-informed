A. · Visioning For Next Two Years - Acquisition of Property

Mayor Bell explained that this is a visioning session and she is asking for the

opinion and direction of the Town Council as to whether they have any interest in

acquiring land. The Town Manager identified parcels within Cutler Bay, whether

or not they are for sale, and provided a map to help facilitate the Town Council in

identifying parcels of vacant land. Mayor Bell suggested that it will be beneficial

if each Council Member to talk individually with the Town Manager and to identify

parcels of interest.

After discussion, there was concensus that the Town Council Members

individually will provide the Town Manager with a list of up to five parcels of

interest. By consensus, the Town Council agreed that the Mayor, Town Manager

and Town Attorney may go forward with parcels identified to the Town Manager,

negotiate a price and bring the information back to the Town Council.

Council Member Roger Coriat announced that he spoke with Commissioner

Daniella Levine Cava regarding the potential Youth Council Committee trip to

Tallahassee. Ms. Levine Cava would like to meet with the youth prior to the trip

and after the trip. She will be donating $250 to help defray the expenses of the

trip.

Vice Mayor Loyzelle inquired about a trip policy for Town Committees. Town

Attorney Chad Friedman explained the release form and liability. Vice Mayor

Loyzelle asked about background checks for the chaperones and the Town

Manager will take care of that.

2. Adjournment

There being no further discussion, the meeting adjourned at 8 p.m.
