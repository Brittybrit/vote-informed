A. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE PROPOSED MILLAGE RATE OF THE TOWN OF

CUTLER BAY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2018 THROUGH

SEPTEMBER 30, 2019 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTES SECTION 200.065; AND

PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the resolution. Town Finance Director Robert

Daddario read the TRIM statement as follows: The proposed millage rate of the

Town of Cutler Bay for FY 2018-19 of 2.4739 mils per $1,000 of assessed property

value within the Town of Cutler Bay is 11.62% greater than the roll-back rate of

2.2164 mils per $1,000 of assessed property value.

Ad valorem taxes are being increased primarily to help provide for operating

expenditures required to maintain current levels of resident services, as well to

provide for various improvements to park and public facilities.

Town Manager Ralph Casals reviewed the "millage cost by department"

document. There was discussion regarding the cost of the Town Center, number

of employees including seasonal staff, contracted services, and the cost of

rebuilding Franjo Park.

Council Member Coriat made a motion to adopt a millage rate of 2.4739 per

thousand dollars of assessed property value. The motion received a second from

Mayor Bell. Town Attorney Mitch Bierman explained that the motion requires a

2/3 vote of the governing body. Finance Director Robert Daddario read the TRIM

statement again as above.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman called the roll and the motion failed in a tie vote with

Council Member Coriat and Mayor Bell voting in favor and Vice Mayor Loyzelle

and Council Member Callahan voting in opposition and Council Member Mixon

absent.

Vice Mayor Loyzelle made a motion to adopt a millage rate of 2.3907 per

thousand dollars of assessed value. The motion received a second from Council

Member Callahan. Finance Director Robert Daddario read the TRIM statement

that the fixed rate of 2.3907 is 7.86% greater than the roll back rate. Council

Member Coriat and Mayor Bell spoke in opposition to the lower millage rate.

Council Member Callahan and Vice Mayor Loyzelle spoke in favor of the lower

millage rate.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman called the roll and the motion failed with Council

Member Callahan and Vice Mayor Loyzelle voting in favor and Council Member

Coriat and Mayor Bell voting in opposition. Council Member Mixon was absent.

Council Member Coriat reintroduced the original motion to adopt a millage of

2.4739 per thousand dollars of assessed value. Council Member Michael

Callahan provided the second to the motion. There was discussion about

continuing to review the budget including merit increases and benefits for staff.

Mayor Bell requested public comments and there were none. Town Clerk Debra

Eastman called the roll and the motion carried (4-0) with Council Member Mixon

absent.

Yes: Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member Michael

Callahan and Council Member Roger Coriat

B. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE TENTATIVE OPERATING AND CAPITAL OUTLAY

BUDGET FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2018 THROUGH

SEPTEMBER 30, 2019 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTES SECTION 200.065;

AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO MAKE EXPENDITURES CONSISTENT

THEREWITH AND AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO MAKE CERTAIN

BUDGET AMENDMENTS WITHIN A DEPARTMENT PROVIDED THAT THE TOTAL OF

THE APPROPRIATIONS IS NOT CHANGED; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE

DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the title of the resolution. Vice Mayor Loyzelle

discussed the cost of the issuance of cell phones and archiving services vs. a

product called Line 2. Town Attorney Mitch Bierman explained the archiving of

public records. Mayor Bell opened the public hearing on both items. There

were no comments.

The Town Council agreed to review the budget summary pages at this time.

Council Member Coriat made a motion to eliminate the window hardening, lobby

improvements, security cameras and restroom upgrades from the budget.

Council Member Callahan provided a second to the motion. Mayor Bell called

for the vote and all were in favor.

Council Member Coriat made a motion to approve the budget resolution as

amended by the previous motion and vote for a total budget of $51,581,678.00.

The motion received a second from Vice Mayor Loyzelle. Town Clerk Debra

Eastman called the roll and the motion carried (4-0) with Council Member Mixon

absent.

The Town Council discussed action items which they requested that the Town

Manager address prior to the second budget hearing, including merit increases

and position reassignment and the cost of living increases.

Yes: Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member Michael

Callahan and Council Member Roger Coriat
