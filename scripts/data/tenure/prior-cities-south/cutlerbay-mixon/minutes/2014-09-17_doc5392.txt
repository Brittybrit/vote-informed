 2. PROCLAMATIONS, AWARDS, AND PRESENTATIONS
 A.   ·   Report by Miami-Dade Transit Department regarding Cutler Bay Circulator Bus
Annual Report
 Jerry Bryant, Chief of Service, Planning and Scheduling for Miami-Dade County
Transit presented the annual report regarding the Cutler Bay Circulator Bus to
the Town Council. He reported that the bus is equipped with WiFi and will soon
be included in the bus tracker information service. In addition, he addressed
future plans for access to Southland Mall and Wal-Mart.
 B.   ·   Report by Principal Paul Pfeiffer of Cutler Bay Academy of Advanced Studies,
Cutler Ridge Campus
 Principal Paul Pfeiffer, new principal of Cutler Bay Academy of Advanced
Studies, Cutler Ridge Campus, presented a report to the Town Council. Principal
Pfeiffer is a resident of Cutler Bay and lives one mile from the school. He
reported that since his arrival in mid-December and after implementing a few
operational and instructional changes, the school has moved up 89 points in its
accountability report. His vision is to double that move during the current school
year. Principal Pfeiffer further outlined his goals and objectives for the upcoming
year.

 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT
 Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman requested that the Town Council hold an
executive session, in accordance with Florida Statutes 286.01(8), to obtain advice
regarding the Realty Associates Fund 9 LP versus Town of Cutler Bay MGCF
Investment, Inc, to discuss settlement negotiations or strategy relating to
litigation expenditures. This consists of two cases, the direct challenge and the
appeal. The appeal is set for oral argument next week. There will not be a
mediation prior to that. However, the mediation will occur prior to a decision in
that case, which may result in resolving it without having a formal decision. Mr.
Bierman would like to have the executive session scheduled at the beginning of
October.
Mr. Bierman stated there is a motion to dismiss in the case of Coulson versus the
Town of Cutler Bay. The hearing is set for October 3, 2014, and he will report
back to the Council on the results.

 Art Nanni stated that he has heard positive comments regarding the
improvements to the Town roadways. Mr. Nanni asked the Town Council the
question about the Cutler Bay Watchdog website again because he only heard
responses from the Mayor and Council Member Loyzelle. Mayor MacDougall
stated that since no one responded when asked if they knew anything, then that
is a no.
Ed Wolmers asked what the lifespan is for the Town circulator buses and if there
is a long-term plan for replacement costs. He stated that the traffic on Franjo
Road being diverted on Holiday Road is a safety issue. He stated that there have
already been two head-on accidents.
Town Manager Casals reported that the bus was purchased under a federal
stimulus bus and the required lifespan of the bus is a minimum of 11 years. Mr.
Casals stated that routine maintenance and safety checks are conducted by
Miami-Dade Transit.

 13. MAYOR AND COUNCIL COMMENTS
 ·   Continuation of Discussion from August 20, 2014 Town Council Meeting regarding
Parks Master Plan
 Sponsors:   Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin
 Vice Mayor Sochin stated that he believes the Town's parks are underused
because there are no activities for small groups to do there. He stated that there
are no volleyball courts in any of the Town parks. He stated that arrangements
are being made with the schools to take advantage of some of their facilities, but
that will be somewhat limited. He stated that he would like to see some effort
made to get started on projects to better utilize the parks with funding that the
Town Manager has stated is available in the budget.
Town Manager Rafael Casals reported that he will be meeting with School Board
representatives on Monday to determine how the process can be expedited to
use the school facilities.
 14. OTHER BUSINESS
 Town Manager's Agreement Amendment - Mayor MacDougall
 A motion was made by Vice Mayor Sochin to amend the Town Manager's
employment agreement to increase the annual vacation days to 23 days from the
current amount. The motion received a second from Council Member Mixon.
After discussion, Mayor MacDougall called for the vote. The motion carried by an
unanimous vote. (5-0)
 Green Master Plan - Mayor MacDougall
 Mayor MacDougall reported on ten homes being built in Saga Bay with green
elements, using all the latest technology. He stated that the Town of Cutler Bay
has taken a leadership role with the programs they have instituted, such as the
PACE program and the planting of trees within the Town.
Town Manager Casals reported on the items that the Town has completed
regarding its green initiative. The document that would encompass all of this
information is called the Green Master Plan. Mr. Casals stated that such a
document was recently completed by the City of Doral.
 15. ADJOURNMENT
 A motion was made by Vice Mayor Sochin to adjourn. The motion received a
