 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, MOMENT OF
SILENCE & INVOCATION
 Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 7:09PM. Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson
called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance.
 Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat

item 9A, Rezoning item 9B, and Amending Future Land Use Map item 11B
requested deferral.

 Mayor Bell acknowledged Florida State Representative Jose Javier Rodriguez in
attendance and invited him to speak.

Foundation unable to attend the meeting.
 B.   ·   Presentation- Palmetto Raiders Football Program- Clarence Moss, Commissioner
 Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced Palmetto Raiders Football Program
Commissioner Clarence Moss to report on recent awards and accomplishments.
 C.   ·   Certificate of Appreciation- Cutler Bay Solar Solutions
 Mayor Bell and members of the Town Council presented a Certificate of
Appreciation to Mr. Raul Vergara of Cutler Bay Solar Solutions for the installation
of a solar-powered informational kiosk in front of Town Hall.

opposition of Florida Power and Light installation of concrete poles.
Council Member Mixon reported on attendance at the Everglades Foundation
Seminar.
Council Member Coriat reported on participation at the Vineland K-8 Center
Career Day Event as a Council Member.
 A.   ·   Mayor Bell Monthly Report April 15-May 12 2016
 Mayor Bell reported on attendance at Open Mic Night and the Informational Town
Hall Meeting on Turkey Point Cooling Canals.

 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell opened Public Comments. Ms. Susan Nussbaum spoke in opposition
of agenda item deferrals. Ms. Nussbaum spoke in opposition of density and
rezoning item 9A. Ms. Nussbaum also spoke in opposition of building permits
issued without prior communication with the Home Owners Association. At the
request of Mayor Bell, Town Manager Rafael Casals provided clarification on
building permits and issuance procedure. Mr. David Bader spoke in opposition
of development. Mr. Marco Fernandez spoke in opposition of development. Ms.
Fabiana Estrada spoke in opposition of rezoning and development. Mayor Bell
closed Public Comments.
 6. REPORTS
 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT
 Town Manager Rafael Casals reported on Distracted Driving initiative efforts.
Town Manager Rafael Casals also reported on notices sent out pertinent to a
Miami-Dade County property zoning application. Town Manager Rafael Casals
provided a progress report on vacant property research in accordance with Town
Resolution 16-25 adopted at the April 20, 2016 Regular Town Council Meeting.
Town Manager Rafael Casals announced the Tri-City Hurricane Preparedness
Workshop on June 4, 2016 and multiple Public Outreach Meetings in preparation
for the Town Center Plaza Conceptual Plans Design project and the Town
Aquatics Facilities project. Town Manager Rafael Casals also announced the
Cutler Ridge After School Care Program Dance on June 1, 2016. Town Manager
Rafael Casals reported on the completion of a recent audit and thanked Town
Staff for their efforts. Gary Ratay of Kimley-Horn and Associates presented an
update on the Caribbean Boulevard Roadway Improvement Project and reported
on coordination efforts to pair the bridge and Gap 2 projects together.
 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT
 At the request of Vice Mayor Sochin, Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman provided
an update on the Publix litigation case.
