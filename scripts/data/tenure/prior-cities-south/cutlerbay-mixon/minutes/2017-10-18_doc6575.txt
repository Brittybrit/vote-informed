5:30 PM Council ChambersWednesday, October 18, 2017

1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE & MOMENT OF

SILENCE

Mayor Peggy Bell called the meeting to order at 5:45 p.m. Town Clerk Debra

Eastman called the roll and Council Members Callahan, Coriat and Mixon and

Vice Mayor Loyzelle and Mayor Bell were in attendance.

Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance.

A. 2018 STATE LEGISLATIVE PRIORITIES

Town Manager Ralph Casals introduced Town Lobbyist Fausto Gomez. Mr.

Gomez complimented the Town Manager for the 2018 Legislative Priorities

Booklet and his on-going excellent work and communications.

The Priorities Booklet contained details and support for the following priorities:

1. Canal Bank Erosion Project

2. Cutler Bay Wetland Pavilion

3. Business Incubator

4. Economic Development Plan

5. Active Adults Services

6. Drainage Project - Cutler Ridge Sec 3

7. Drainage Project - Saga Bay 1.7

Mr. Gomez explained the current funding challenges and climiate at the State's

capital and the expected financial impact of Hurricane Irma in September.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Members of the Town Council thanked Mr. Gomez for his work. Town Manager

Casals reminded that members of the Town Council will be visiting Tallahassee

to meet with Senators and Representatives regarding legislative issues in either

November or December.

3. Adjournment

The meeting adjourned at 7:43 p.m.
