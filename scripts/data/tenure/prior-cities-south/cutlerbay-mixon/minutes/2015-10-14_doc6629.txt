1. Call to Order, Roll Call, & Pledge of Allegiance

Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 6:03PM. Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson

called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Mayor Bell opened Town Council Comments while waiting for Town Lobbyist

Fausto Gomez to arrive.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,

Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present 5 -

2016 STATE LEGISLATIVE ITEMS SUBMITTAL

Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced Town Lobbyist Fausto Gomez to

present the 2016 State Legislative Items. Mayor Bell questioned if the number of

legislative priorities should be reduced. Council Member Loyzelle expressed

concern about Senior Service Programs. Vice Mayor Sochin praised the

progress with the Golf Carts initiative and spoke in favor of escalating to the next

level granting access to Black Point Marina. Town Manager Rafael Casals

suggested the items currently listed remain on the list of priorities in the event

they become an item of interest with the State Legislature and reiterated that

other items of importance may also be added as the Council sees fit.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Council Member Coriat congratulated Jacqueline Wilson on appointment as

Town Clerk and suggested a tier method for voting in the future. Council

Member Loyzelle reported on attendance at the South Florida Executive

Magazine Reception hosted by Superintendent of Miami-Dade County Public

Schools, Alberto Carvalho on October 13, 2015 and at Communities for a Lifetime

Committee Meeting featuring an Age Friendly Communities presentation by

AARP on October 7, 2015.

Council Member Loyzelle announced Miami-Dade County Days February 9-10,

2016 and announced the Kick-off Reception on October 27, 2015.

Mayor Bell suggested arriving for Miami-Dade County Days earlier to allow more

time to address Legislative items accordingly.
