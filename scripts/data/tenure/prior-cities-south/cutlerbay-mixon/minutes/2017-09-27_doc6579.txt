2. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS, BOARD & COMMITTEE REPORTS

Mayor Bell spoke on the hurricane damage sustained in Puerto Rico and the

Carribean Islands, and informed the public that the Town Manager will look into

ways to assist those in need. Vice Mayor Loyzelle offered to provide information

to the Town Manager regarding United Way and Red Cross.

Town Manager Rafael Casals announced that the Town's internet service

provider is down, and live streaming of the Council Meeting online will not be

available. Town Clerk Debra Eastman explained that a recording of the meeting

will be made available online as soon as possible.

Mayor Bell led a moment of silence.

A motion was made by Council Member Coriat, seconded by Council Member

Mixon, to move forward the Consent Agenda prior to Town Council

Announcements. Town Attorney Chad Friedman advised the Council that Public

Comments should be heard first. An amended motion was made by Council

Member Coriat, seconded by Council Member Mixon, to move forward the

Consent Agenda to immediately follow Public Comments. Mayor Bell called for

the vote and the motion passed 5-0.

· Town Council Message- Hurricane Irma Recovery

Town Manager Rafael Casals expressed gratitude to Town Staff for their efforts

regarding Hurricane Irma Recovery, and announced damage to Franjo Park and

efforts for reimbursement through FEMA. Council Member Callahan requested

an email blast go out to residents explaining damage as the reason for the

closure of Franjo Park. At the request of Vice Mayor Loyzelle, Town Manager

3. PUBLIC COMMENTS

Mayor Bell opened Public Comments. There being no one wishing to speak,

Mayor Bell closed Public Comments.

Council Member Callahan expressed concerns regarding issues with the

availability of prerequisites for students in order to be able to transfer from the

Middle School Cutler Bay Ocean Academy of Science and Technology (COAST)

program to the High School COAST program. Mayor Bell requested the Town

Manager schedule a meeting with the Miami-Dade County Public School District

representatives as soon as possible. Town Attorney Chad Friedman spoke on

attempts to negotiate and open up the current Interlocal Agreement with the

school district. There was discussion on alternatives available to satisfy

prerequisites. Town Manager Rafael Casals suggested a Town Council

Workshop be held with only the issues pertaining to the school district on the

agenda.

B. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, APPROVING DISCOUNTED FENCE PERMIT FEES DUE TO

DAMAGE CAUSED BY HURRICANE IRMA; PROVIDING FOR AUTHORIZATION; AND

PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the title of the Resolution. Town Manager

Casals provided background information on the item. There was discussion on

the possibility of the Town covering the remaining 35 percent of costs, how to

notify residents of the discount, and expanding the discount to include all

hurricane damages.

A motion was made by Council Member Coriat, seconded by Council Member

Mixon, that this Resolution be adopted with amendments to include 30 percent

discount on all hurricane related repairs and that it be retroactive to September

18, 2017. Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion passed 5-0.

C. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO ISSUE A PURCHASE

ORDER TO KIMLEY-HORN AND ASSOCIATES, INC. FOR THE FINAL TOWN CENTER

PLAZA CONSTRUCTION PLANS AND DESIGN SERVICES; AND PROVIDING FOR AN

EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the title of the Resolution. Town Manager

Rafael Casals provided background information on the item.

A motion was made by Council Member Mixon, seconded by Council Member

Coriat, that this Resolution be adopted.

Council Member Callahan requested the item be deferred until after a Council

workshop to discuss financing of the project. Council Member Coriat spoke in

favor of deferral. Council Member Mixon spoke in opposition of deferral. Mayor

Bell spoke in favor of the item and in opposition of deferral. There was

discussion on the use of park impact fees. Council Member Mixon spoke on

potential revenue from street league skate competitions. Town Attorney Chad

Friedman advised the Council on the use of park impact fees, the consideration

of the area for the project as a park space in order to use park impact fees, and

the potential to replenish park impact fees with bond funding. There was

discussion on phasing in construction of plaza. VM Loyzelle expressed concern

with potential liabilities concerning the project and cost of the overall project.

Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion passed 4-1, with Vice Mayor

Loyzelle in opposition.
