 7:00 PM   Council Chambers Wednesday, February 18, 2015
 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, INVOCATION & PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
 Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 7:02pm. Town Clerk Debra Eastman
called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance.
 Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat
 Present:   5 -
 Invocation by Pastor Tania of Community Connection
 Reverend Tania Brito, Pastor at Community Connexion provided the invocation.
 2. PROCLAMATIONS, AWARDS, AND PRESENTATIONS
Daniella Levine Cava, Miami-Dade County Commissioner District 8
 Commissioner Levine-Cava shared her report card on her three month
anniversary and reviewed her agenda including collection of used cell phones for
domestic violence victims, used childrens books for Kiwanis Club and used
adults books for inmates at Miami Dade detention facility, and a new stray cats
program. Commissioner Levine-Cava confirmed she has already passed
legislation to support a state-wide fracking ban. Council Member Loyzelle
thanked Commissioner Levine-Cava for her efforts concerning Mom and Pop
grants and volunteered to review these important grants. Commissioner
Levine-Cava also reported on Metropolitan Planning Organization, structural
changes and reminder of monthly MPO meetings.

 Police Officer of the Quarter Awards - Officer Johnnie Crawford and Officer
Anthony Zayas
 Town of Cutler Bay Police Commander Julie Miller presented the Officer of the
Quarter Award to Anthony Zayas who also received this award in October, and
whom continues to distinguish himself with impressive quarterly statistics.
Commander Miller also extended apologies on behalf of Officer Johnnie
Crawford who was unable to attend the meeting.
 Town Clerk Debra Eastman Proclamation
 Mayor Bell presented Town Clerk Debra Eastman with a Proclamation in honor of
her years of service as a Town Clerk and her retirement on March 6, 2015.
 3. APPROVAL OF MINUTES
 A.   ·   January 7 2015 Executive Session Minutes
·   January 7 2015 Town Council Workshop Minutes
·   January 21 2015 Regular Town Council Minutes
 Council Member Loyzelle requested a correction to the January 7, 2015 Executive
Session Minutes to reflect that she was not in attendance.
Vice Mayor Sochin made a motion to approve the minutes as amended. The
motion received a second from Council Member Mixon. Mayor Bell called for the
vote and the motion passed 5-0.
 4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Mayor Bell reported on her recent trip to Tallahassee to meet with State
Legislators in order to raise awareness to 2015 adopted legislative priorities.
Mayor Bell attended the Homestead State of the City event. Mayor Bell
accompanied Commissioner Levine-Cava on a tour of Cutler Bay, including the
World Wetlands event at Blackpoint Marina. She attended the Miami Dade
League of Cities event as is now a member of the Board of Directors. Mayor Bell
attended the PACE board meeting. Mayor Bell also reported on the progress of
the schools renaming committee. Mayor Bell will be working with the Town
Manager on arranging tours of landfill and water treatment plant.
Town Attorney Mitch Bierman recognized Frank Balzebre, of Miami-Dade County
Mayor Carlos Gimenez's office who was in attendance.
Vice Mayor Sochin reported on his participation in the Real Men Read event at
the middle school, talking to children about reading.
Council Member Loyzelle announced Founders Day on Saturday, February 21,
2015 from 11-3pm. She also mentioned an upcoming trip on March 11-12 2015, to
Tallahassee following up on items not previously represented during the Mayors
visit. Council Member Loyzelle reported on her attendance with Mayor Bell at the
Congressional swearing in. Council Member Loyzelle also reported on the
opening of the Marilyn Hope Center which is already at capacity. She will be
working with the Town Manager to organize a Welcome Wagon event.
Council Member Mixon attended the World Wetlands event which was a
successful event.

 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell opened Public Comments and there were none.
 6. REPORTS
 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT
 Town Manager Ralph Casals presented his report to the Town Council. Gary
Ratay and Alina Phillips of Kimley Horn and Associates presented an update on
the Caribbean Boulevard Roadway Improvement Project. Town Manager Ralph
Casals announced the Relay for Life Event on February 28-29 at Evelyn Greer
Park as well as a briefing on the Shoes for Haiti Drive. Town Manager Ralph
Casals requested direction whether to move forward with the foreclosure and
demolition of an abandoned property. There was no objection.
 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT
 Town Attorney Mitch Bierman reported that the case of Realty Associates Fund
IX v Publix and the Town of Cutler Bay has not been resolved and has been
scheduled for trial. Mr. Bierman expressed gratitude and well wishes for Town
Clerk Debra Eastman in her retirement.
 C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT
 Town Clerk Debra Eastman presented her final report with progress on projected
goals and accomplishments achieved during her term as Town Clerk for the
Town of Cutler Bay and thanked the Town Council for their support.
 ·   Memo- Town Clerk sick leave
 Council Member Mixon made a motion to approve payment of accrued sick leave
to the Town Clerk upon separation. The motion received a second from Council
Member Loyzelle. Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion passed 5-0.
 D. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS
 Vice Mayor Sochin reported on the progress of the Education Advisory
Committee and thanked Town Manager Ralph Casals for attending Committee
Meetings. Vice Mayor Sochin informed that the Neighborhood Resource Unit of
Cutler Bay Police Department has introduced a new program bringing police
closer to the Town of Cutler Bay.
Council Member Loyzelle reported that the Communities for a Lifetime Advisory
Committee is finishing their strategic plan and will have a booth at the Founder's
Day event.
Mayor Bell reported on Parks and Recreation Committee. Mayor Bell also
reported on Youth Council Committee and their town-wide No Place for Hate
initiative.

 12. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell opened Public Comments and there were none.
 13. MAYOR AND COUNCIL COMMENTS
 Council Member Mixon thanked Town Clerk Debra Eastman for her service and
for setting the bar so high with what to look for in the next Town Clerk. Council
Member Loyzelle also thanked Town Clerk Debra Eastman for her service as well
as welcomed Jackie Wilson to the Interim Clerk position. Vice Mayor Sochin
wished Debra Eastman good luck in her retirement.
Mayor Bell praised the team visit to Tallahassee and was pleased to report back
that they were welcomed and invited to return. Mayor Bell reported on
Representative Richardson's pilot program banning plastic bags, she will report
back more information as it becomes available. There was also a discussion
regarding the possibility of the state eliminating the communication service tax
and the ad valorem tax.
