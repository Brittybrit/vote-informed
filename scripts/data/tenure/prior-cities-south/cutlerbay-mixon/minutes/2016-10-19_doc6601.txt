 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell opened Public Comments. Ms. Lucia Castillas spoke on Chanterelle
Community Home Owners Association issues. Town Manager Rafael Casals
clarified Chanterelle Community as a private gated community with private roads.
Mr. Raul Vergara spoke in favor of Mayor Bell’s sponsorship of item 7E on the
Consent Agenda and spoke on solar industry regulations. Mr. Raul Vergara also
spoke on issues with the Town's solar kiosk and stated he will correct them. Ms.
Laura Reynolds spoke in favor 7E on the Consent Agenda. Ms. Laura Reynolds
also spoke on state guidelines on the Coastal High Hazard Areas and inquired if
Town staff contacted Miami-Dade County. Mr. Eduardo Verona spoke on Coastal
High Hazard Area and requested clarification on Ordinances for First Reading
item 11B. Mayor Bell closed Public Comments.
A motion was made by Council Member Coriat, seconded by Council Member
Mixon, to pull item 7E from the Consent Agenda. Mayor Bell called for the vote
and the motion passed 5-0.
 6. REPORTS
 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT

recent Hurricane Matthew, and the Miami-Dade County Mosquito Abatement
Program. Town Manager Rafael Casals also reported on the Caribbean
Boulevard Roadway Improvement Gap 2 project and the audit process for the
Town IT Department. At the request of Council Member Loyzelle, Town Manager
Rafael Casals provided clarification on the Red Light Camera Program end date.
Building & Code Compliance Director Sandra Aronoff provided further

Council Member Loyzelle reported on a meeting with the Enclave Home Owners

provided a report on public outreach efforts regarding crime.
 ·   2015-16 Miami-Dade Transit Circulator Annual Report

Transit Department to present the Annual Transit Circulator Report for 2015-2016

There was discussion on the Town Circulator Bus.
 ·   Robert Daddario, Finance Department- Quarterly Financial Report
 Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced Finance Director Robert Daddario to
present an overview of the Town's Quarterly Financial Report and the results

Social Security percentage allotment.
