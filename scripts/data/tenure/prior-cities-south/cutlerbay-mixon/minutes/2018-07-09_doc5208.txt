July 9, 2018Town Council Workshop Meeting Minutes

A. · Fiscal Year 2018-19 Proposed Budget

Town Manager Rafael Casals spoke about the increase in the Town's property

values of 6.18 percent, and spoke on the proposed Fiscal Year 2018-19 Budget

based on a max ceiling millage rate of 2.4739, compared to the existing 2.3907

millage rate for the adopted Fiscal Year 2017-18 Budget.

There was discussion on the impact of the potential addition to the homestead

exemption, the possibility of purchasing open land in the future, the fund

balance reserve, the Town Center Building operating budget, and general fund

revenues.

Town Attorney Chad Friedman spoke about the franchise fee agreement with

Florida Power and Light, and the Town options to negotiate after the Miami-Dade

County agreement expires in 2020.

There was discussion on individual department budgets, a potential accounting

software upgrade to allow certain online payments, Town holiday lighting, Town

Police budget, the potential of increasing revenue for the Town golf tournament,

and possibility of retrofitting the Town Center Building for LED lighting.

Town Manager Rafael Casals spoke about Town occupied space in the Town

Center Building and proposals the Town has received for the purchase of the

Town Center Building.

There was discussion on the possibility of selling the Town Center Building and

leasing back space, the potential of purchasing land and constructing a facility

for Town operations, and the possibility of putting out a request for interest for

the Town Center Building.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Mayor Bell spoke about Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA)

funding.

Vice Mayor Loyzelle announced the Communities for a Lifetime Committee

meeting on July 11.
