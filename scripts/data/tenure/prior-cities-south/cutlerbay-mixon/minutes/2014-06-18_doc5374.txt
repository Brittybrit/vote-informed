June 18, 2014 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, & PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
 Council Member Peggy Bell, Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member
Mary Ann Mixon, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin and Mayor Edward MacDougall
 Present:   5 -
 2. PROCLAMATIONS, AWARDS, AND PRESENTATIONS
 A.   ·   Alan Ricke, Town of Cutler Bay Parks and Recreation Director
 Mayor MacDougall invited retiring Parks and Recreation Director Alan Ricke to
speak regarding his career and Parks and Recreation. Mr. Ricke provided a brief
synopsis. Council Members thanked Mr. Ricke for his service and wished him
well on his retirement, as did Town Manager Rafael Casals and Town Attorney
Mitch Bierman. The Mayor and Town Council presented Mr. Ricke with a
proclamation.
A motion was made by Council Member Mixon to allow Vice Mayor Sochin to
participate in the meeting via telephone. The motion received a second from
Council Member Bell. Mayor MacDougall called for the motion and all were in
favor.
 3. APPROVAL OF MINUTES
 A.   ·   Town Council Workshop, May 19, 2014
·   Regular Town Council Meeting, May 21, 2014
 A motion was made by Council Member Loyzelle, seconded by Council Member
Mixon, that the Minutes be approved as amended. The motion carried by an
unanimous vote. (5-0)
 4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Members of the Town Council provided information on upcoming events.
 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Sandra Wong reported her understanding that there will be a new school
Principal at the High School and expressed her concern that there will be a
suitable replacement.
Victor Mederos spoke in support of Ms. Wong and echoed her concerns.
Janelle Melchoir added that she has been approached by many concerned
parents and residents with regard to a new principal.
Mayor MacDougall and members of the Town Council assured the concerned
citizens that the Town will continue its committment to work closely with the
schools.
 6. REPORTS
 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT

conjunction with the Master Plan Update, awards that were received for the Old
Cutler Road improvement project and the receipt of a grant for after school and
summer programs. Gary Ratay from Kimley Horn and Associates, Inc. provided
