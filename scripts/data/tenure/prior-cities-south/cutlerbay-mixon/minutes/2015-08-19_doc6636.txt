3:00 PM Council ChambersWednesday, August 19, 2015

1. Call to Order, Roll Call, & Pledge of Allegiance

Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 3:01PM. Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline

Wilson called the roll. Council Member Loyzelle arrived at 3:18PM. Mayor Bell

led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Fiscal Year 2015-16 Proposed Budget

(Second Council Budget Workshop)

Town Manager Rafael Casals briefed the Town Council on the Fiscal Year

2015-2016 Proposed Budget and the Town Hall hardening project. Town

Manager Casals confirmed that the 2015-2016 Proposed Budget has been

comprised of the 2.3907 millage rate which is the existing millage rate, translating

to a $284,907 reduction in the budget.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Council Member Mixon thanked Town staff for their efforts. Vice Mayor Sochin

reiterated that the proposed budget at the existing millage rate still includes two

additional police officers and thanked Town staff for their efforts. Mayor Bell

also echoed gratitude for Town staff efforts. At the request of Council Member

Mixon, Town Manager Rafael Casals and Finance Director Robert Daddario

reported over $21.3M in reserves as of September 30, 2014, which translates to

over one year’s worth of operating expense funds in reserves. As requested by

Council Member Loyzelle, Town Manager Rafael Casals confirmed the rollback

rate would be 2.18949 and in terms of financial impact, the current proposed

budget would need to be reduced by $406,896 in order to not reflect any increase

to Town residents. Town Manager Rafael Casals announced the First Budget

Hearing on September 10, 2015 and the Second Budget Hearing on September

24, 2015.

3. Adjournment

A motion was made by Council Member Mixon to adjourn. The motion received a

second from Council Member Coriat. The meeting adjourned at 3:37PM.
