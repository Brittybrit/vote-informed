May 19, 2014Town Council Workshop Meeting Minutes

1. Call to Order, Roll Call, & Pledge of Allegiance

The Town Council Workshop was called to order at 3 p.m. on Wednesday, May

19, 2014 at the Town Council Chambers, 10720 Caribbean Boulevard, Cutler Bay,

Florida. Mayor MacDougall called the meeting to order at 3 p.m. Town Clerk

Debra Eastman called the roll. In attendance was Council Member Bell, Council

Member Mixon and Mayor MacDougall. Vice Mayor Sochin was in attendance via

telephone. Council Member Loyzelle was absent.

2. Discussion

· Annual Evaluations of Charter Officials (Town Attorney, Town Manager,

Town Clerk)

· Discussion reference Town Clerk

There was a discussion regarding the annual performance evaluation of Charter

Officials. It was decided to use the same performance evaluation forms used the

previous year. Because the ranking on the Town Clerk form is based upon a

1-3 ranking and the ranking on the Town Manager and Town Attorney form is

based upon a 1-5, the Town Clerk was requested to change the Town Clerk form

to reflect the same 1-5 ranking. The Town Clerk was requested to distribute the

forms to the Mayor and members of the Town Council.

The members of the Town Council will return their completed forms to either the

Mayor or the Town Clerk by Friday, June 6, 2014. This will allow time for the

Mayor to compile the evaluations into one evaluation form for each Charter

Official.

There was a discussion regarding a proposed employment agreement with the

Town Clerk. The agreement was reviewed. Mayor MacDougall requested and it

was consensus, that the agreement be reviewed for legal sufficiency and that an

item be prepared as a walk on item for the May 21, 2014 Town Council meeting.

3. Adjournment

The meeting adjourned at 4 p.m.
