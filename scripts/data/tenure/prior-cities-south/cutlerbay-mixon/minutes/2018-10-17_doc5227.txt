area to french drains with flat landscaping, out buildings and maintenance of lot

BAY, FLORIDA, AMENDING SECTION 3-58, “NC, NEIGHBORHOOD CENTER

DISTRICT” RELATING TO PERMITTED USES FOR PERSONAL SERVICES;

CENTER DISTRICT” RELATING TO PERMITTED USES FOR PERSONAL SERVICES;

PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING FOR CONFLICT; PROVIDING FOR

INCLUSION IN THE CODE; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.....

this Ordinance be adopted. The motion carried by the following vote:

B. AN ORDINANCE OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA GRANTING TO FLORIDA

POWER & LIGHT COMPANY, ITS SUCCESSORS AND ASSIGNS, A NON-EXCLUSIVE

ELECTRIC FRANCHISE, IMPOSING PROVISIONS AND CONDITIONS RELATING

THERETO; PROVIDING FOR MONTHLY PAYMENT OF A FRANCHISE FEE TO THE

TOWN; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE

DATE.

It now being 1 a.m. on Thursday, Oct. 18, 2018 Mayor Bell called for a 5 minute

break. A motion was made to move up the hearing of the ordinance. The

motion received a second from Council Member Mixon. Mayor Bell called for the

vote and it was adopted 5-0.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the title of the ordinance. Town Manager Ralph

Casals explained the agreement and indicated some scrivenor error changes.

Mr. Baldwin English of FPL had no further comments. Mayor Bell opened the

public hearing. There being no one wishing to speak, she closed the public

hearing.

Mr. English explained to the Town Council that he is working with public works

to see where lights can be placed on 87th Ave.

A motion was made by Council Member Coriat, seconded by Council Member

Mixon, that this Ordinance be adopted as amended. The motion carried by the

following vote:

Yes: Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Mary Ann Mixon, Council

Member Michael Callahan and Council Member Roger Coriat
