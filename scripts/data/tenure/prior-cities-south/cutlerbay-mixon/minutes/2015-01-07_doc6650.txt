1. Call to Order, Roll Call, & Pledge of Allegiance

Mayor Peggy Bell called the workshop to order at 5:17 p.m.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,

and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present 4 -

Council Member Sue Ellen LoyzelleAbsent 1 -

2015 STATE LEGISLATIVE ITEMS SUBMITTAL

Town of Cutler Bay Lobbyist Fausto Gomez and his associate Manny Reyes was

in attendance via telephone.

Town Manager Ralph Casals explained the reason for the meeting was to discuss

the Town Council's 2015 legislative priorities and he provided an overview of the

legislative items contained in the agenda materials.

Lobbyist Fausto Gomez provided an update of some of the bills that are already

filed in Tallahassee such as texting while driving and red light cameras.

2. Town Council Discussion

Members of the Town Council discussed items of interest to the Town of Cutler

Bay, which included the use of golf carts, texting while driving, red light cameras,

and fracking.

There was discussion regarding Miami Dade County Days which is held in April

of each year and is when the Town Council visits Tallahassee. Mayor Bell

informed the Council that she will be going to Tallahassee in advance of that to

meet with Committees and encouraged the members of the Town Council to

attend. Town Manager Casals indicated that he will work with Mr. Gomez to set

the appropriate arrangements and meetings.

Council Member Mixon asked about whether Council Member Coriat should

receive payment for deciding not to be enrolled in the Town's health insurance

plan. Town Attorney Mitch Bierman offered an explanation of the current benefit

provided to the Vice Mayor due to his current health care provider. Council

Member Coriat explained that he is not interested in receiving the Town's health

insurance benefit.
