 9. QUASI-JUDICIAL HEARINGS (PUBLIC HEARING REQUIRED)
 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, APPROVING A CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATION TO
PERMIT THE CONVERSION OF 58,428 SQUARE FEET OF OFFICE SPACE IN AN
EXISTING 4 STORY BUILDING LOCATED AT 10750 CARIBBEAN BOULEVARD
FROM BUSINESS USE TO A CHARTER SCHOOL, AS LEGALLY DESCRIBED IN
EXHIBIT “A”, CONSISTING OF APPROXIMATELY 2.16 NET ACRES; AND
PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman read the quasi-judicial statement and asked if
anyone wished to speak on this issue that they be given an oath from the Interim
Town Clerk. Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson provided the oath and read
the title of the Resolution. Council Member Coriat disclosed a meeting on July
13, 2015 with representatives of Bercow Radell and Fernandez. The staff report
was presented by Kathryn Lyon, Planning and Zoning Director along with
support from Joseph Corradino of The Corradino Group. In attendance
representing the applicant Pinnacle Investment Properties, was Mr. Tom
Robertson of Bercow Radell and Fernandez. Mr. Robertson introduced Mr. Tom

James Socash to speak on the landscape plan, Mr. Christopher Heggen to speak
on the transportation plan, and Ms. Christine De Zayas Jurado to speak on the

Mr. Robert Cambo expressed concern with the restriction on cueing. After
further discussion, a motion was made by Mayor Bell to approve the application
subject to all conditions in the application with amendments removing the
double stacking restriction, removing the requirement of a school resource
officer but requiring a security guard with comparable credentials to that of a
Miami-Dade County Public School Security Guard and requiring reimbursement
of Town expenses for police services.
