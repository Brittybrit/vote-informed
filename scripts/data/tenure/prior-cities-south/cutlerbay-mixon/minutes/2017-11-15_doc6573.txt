1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, & MOMENT OF

SILENCE

Mayor Peggy Bell called the meeting to order at 7:06 p.m. Town Clerk Debra

Eastman called the roll with Council Members Roger Coriat, Michael Callahan,

Mary Ann Mixon and Mayor Peggy Bell in attendance.

Cub Scout Pack 357 with Leader Jim Bryan presented the colors, led the Pledge

of Allegiance and recited the Scout Oath.

Mayor Bell called for a moment of silence for those involved in recent church

shootings and all of the victims and families of gun violence.

Council Member Coriat made a motion to pull items 5I, 5J and 5L to be taken up

by the Town Council following completion of public comments. Council Member

Mixon provided a second. Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion passed

5-0.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member Mary Ann

Mixon, Council Member Michael P. Callahan and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present: 5 -

2. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS, BOARD & COMMITTEE REPORTS

· Town Council Monthly Reports November 2017

Robert Duncan, Chair of the Education Advisory Committee reported to the Town

Council on behalf of the EAC that the committee voted in support of the

legislative priorities as set by Miami Dade County Schools and the EAC voted in

agreement and also requested that the Town Council support the legislative

priorities as well. Mayor Bell thanked Mr. Duncan for his report.

Mayor Bell reported that she, Town Lobbyist Fausto Gomez, Vice Mayor Loyzelle,

Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman and Town Manager Ralph Casals met in

Tallahassee to endorse the Town's legislative priorities and met with State

Legislators.

A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT

· Town Manager Monthly Report November 2017

· 2017 Local Mitigation Strategy Annual Report

· 2017 Flood Mitigation Projects Annual Report

Public Works Director Alfredo Quintero provided a report regarding the

certified flood plain managers on staff.

secure appraisals and work out the language of the contract.

10. RESOLUTIONS

A. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, REDUCING TOWN PERMIT FEES FOR SOLAR PANELS; PROVIDING

FOR AUTHORZATION; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the title of the resolution. Mayor Peggy Bell

explained the solar cooperative and its association with PACE and that the

B. A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF CUTLER

BAY, FLORIDA, AMENDING RESOLUTION 06-05; ADOPTING RULES OF

PROCEDURE RELATED TO DEBATE; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

Town Clerk Debra Eastman read the tilte of the resolution. Mayor Bell explained

why she was bringing this item to the Town Council and stated it is important that

adopted items have finality. A motion to adopt the resolution was made by

Council Member Mixon and it received a second from Council Member Coriat.

Vice Mayor Sue Loyzelle spoke in opposition to the resolution. Council Member

Callahan asked about a motion to reconsider being on the prevailing side and

spoke in opposition. Town Manager Ralph Casals reminded the Town Council

that the last action they adopted was for the Town Manager to issue a purchase

order. After further discussion, Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion

failed 2-3, with Mayor Bell and Council Member Mixon voting in favor and

Council Members Callahan and Coriat and Vice Mayor Loyzelle voting in

opposition.
