1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE & MOMENT OF

SILENCE

Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 6:45PM. Mayor Bell explained the

format and use of a workshop for discussion purposes. Deputy Town Clerk

Mauricio Melinu called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance. Vice

Mayor Loyzelle arrived at 7:03PM.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member Mary Ann

Mixon, Council Member Michael P. Callahan, and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present 5 -

A. · Discussion Regarding Possible Acquisition of Vacant Property

Town Manager Rafael Casals provided a brief overview and timeline regarding

the research on vacant property, and provided a report on the status of the top

four properties chosen by the Council. Mayor Bell provided background

information on property number three (3).

Town Attorney Chad Friedman informed the Council that no formal offer was

ever made on property number one (1), and that the proposed price was in the 7

million dollar range. There was discussion on the property owner decision not to

sell for proposed prices.

Mayor Bell provided information on discussions with the property owners of

property number three. There was discussion on the public right of way land

located within property for sale, property owner willingness to sell half of the

property, potential property appraisal price ranges, and acquring a formal

appraisal on the property.

Council Member Mixon suggested arriving on an acceptable offer prior to go

ahead with a formal appraisal, with a formal contract and a formal offer subject

to appraisal. Council Member Coriat agreed with Council Member Mixon, and

expressed support in exploring the potential to purchase the land.

There was discussion on the Council about ideas for open spaces, inquiry with

property owners on potential of acquiring vacant land, and the possibility of

partnerships with land owners.

Town Manager Rafael Casals spoke about reaching out to the owners of property

number two, and a potential development plan. There was discussion on how

Mayor Bell spoke briefly on property number four. Council Member Coriat spoke

on a property east of Lakes by the Bay Park. Town Manager Rafael Casals

Mayor Bell requested Council consensus to move forward with a contract offer

Town Manager Rafael Casals will speak with the County in regards to having to

open up Southwest 200th Street. There was discussion about the deciding

factors to determine whether to move forward with a purchase. Town Attorney

Chad Friedman explained the process for acquriing an appraisal and keeping it

confidential under Public Record Law. At the request of Council Member Mixon,

Town Manager Rafael Casals spoke on the different ways to purchase land

through a general obligation bond or a general loan. The Council came to a

consensus to have the Town Manager move forward with a formal contract and

appraisals for property number three. Town Manager Rafael Casals assured the

Council that the Town staff will reach out to other vacant land owners to gauge

willingness to sell.

Council Member Coriat spoke about vacant property located on Southwest 216th

and Old Cutler Road, the incorporation concept with Lincoln City Park, and about

the Old Cutler and 184th nine acre property.

Council Member Mixon spoke about land near Whispering Pines Lake as a

possible pocket park, vacant property backing up to Cutler Ridge Park possibly as

an addition to Cutler Ridge Park, and a parcel located off Caribbean Boulevard

and the Turnpike.

Council Member Callahan spoke on polling the Parks and Recreation Committee

on vacant land, properties two and four as a site for an aquatics center, and

spoke in agreement with Council Member Mixon's and Council Member Coriat's

visions for vacant land.

Mayor Bell suggested a dog park on the 216th and Old Cutler Road property and

spoke in agreement with Council Member Mixon's vision for the land on

Caribbean Boulevard and the turnpike.

Vice Mayor Loyzelle spoke in agreement with Council Member Callahan's vision

for properties two and four, and also spoke on a property on Southwest 211th

Street for a possible assisted living facility or nursing home. Town Manager

Rafael Casals spoke about development ideas ranging from restaurants to a

large assisted living facility at the property on Southwest 211th Street.

Vice Mayor Loyzelle spoke in agreement with Council Member Mixon's vision on

the property backing into Cutler Ridge Park.

Town Manager Rafael Casals provided an update on the tenants of Sandbar,

formerly known as Cutler Bay Sports Bar.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Mayor Bell expressed gratitude to Police Major Leo Ricelli for his attendance at

meetings. Town Manager Rafael Casals spoke on a potential Police Community

Forum in September 2017.

There was Council consensus on developing a maintenance plan using

volunteers for the Louise Lockwood Memorial Butterfly Garden.

Mayor Bell expressed gratitude to Vice Mayor Loyzelle and Council Member

Mixon for accepting a Youth Council Committee grant for a health fair at the

Florida League of Cities event in Orlando.
