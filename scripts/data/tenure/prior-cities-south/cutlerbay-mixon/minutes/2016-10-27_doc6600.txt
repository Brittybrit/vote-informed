2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Members of the Town Council discussed the application and asked questions of

Mr. Juan Mayol of Holland & Knight LLP and Mr. Victor Dover with Dover, Kohl &

Partners with support from Ms. Elisa Solorzano of David Plummer & Associates.

Mayor Bell announced Town participation in a contest urging residents to vote

NO on Amendment 1. Council Member Coriat reported on participation in

restoration workdays at the wetlands site along Old Cutler Road. Mayor Bell

spoke on Town efforts with South Florida Water Management for Phase 2 to

restore the waterflow of the property. Council Member Loyzelle announced the

upcoming Trunk-or-Treat Halloween Event on October 28, 2016.
