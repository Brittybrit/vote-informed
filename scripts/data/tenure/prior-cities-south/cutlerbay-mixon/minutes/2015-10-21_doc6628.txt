 E.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, URGING THE FLORIDA LEGISLATURE TO REMOVE
BARRIERS TO CUSTOMER-SITED SOLAR POWER AND EXPRESSING SUPPORT
FOR THE FLORIDIANS FOR SOLAR CHOICE BALLOT PETITION; AND PROVIDING
FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Mayor Bell opened the item for the public to allow Mr. Knight to answer Council
questions regarding FPLs position on this issue. Council Member Mixon spoke
in support of the Resolution. Council Member Coriat also spoke in support of the
Resolution. Mayor Bell spoke in opposition of Mr. Knight's proposed changes to
the Resolution. After discussion, a motion was made by Council Member Mixon,
seconded by Council Member Coriat, that this Resolution be adopted. After
further discussion, Mayor Bell called for the vote and the motion carried 4-1 with
Vice Mayor Sochin in opposition.

PLAN, TITLED “MIAMI-DADE CONNECTION 2.0 RECOMMENDATION,”
ESTABLISHING AN EFFICIENT AND FUNCTIONAL TRANSPORTATION SYSTEM
FOR THE RESIDENTS OF MIAMI-DADE COUNTY; REQUESTING THE MIAMI-DADE
COUNTY COMMISSION TO IMPLEMENT THE WILL OF ITS RESIDENTS IN THIS
CURRENT BUDGET SESSION BY ADOPTING THE PROPOSED
TRANSPORTATION PLAN AND IDENTIFYING A SOURCE OF FUNDING FOR THIS
INITIATIVE; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. Mayor Bell spoke
in favor of the Resolution. Vice Mayor spoke in opposition. Town Attorney

 12.   ORDINANCES FOR SECOND READING (PUBLIC HEARING REQUIRED)
 A.   AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, REGARDING BUDGETING AND RESERVES; AMENDING
ORDINANCE 13-06 TO INCREASE THE MINIMUM RESERVE PERCENTAGE FOR
THE RESERVE FUND BALANCE OF THE TOWN’S ANNUAL GENERAL FUND
BUDGET; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; PROVIDING FOR INCLUSION IN THE
CODE; REPEALING CONFLICTING ORDINANCES; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Ordinance. Mayor Bell spoke
in support of this item. Town Manager Rafael Casals presented the item to the
Council with 17% proposed fund balance and reserves. Mayor Bell opened for
public hearing. There being no one wishing to speak, Mayor Bell closed the
public hearing. A motion was made by Vice Mayor Sochin, seconded by Council
Member Loyzelle, that this Ordinance be approved on second reading. The
motion carried by the following vote:
Yes:   Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat

 14. OTHER BUSINESS
 Council Member Coriat discussed Public Comments for acknowledgement. At
the request of Council Member Mixon Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman provided
clarification on the procedure for a Public Hearing. Town Attorney Mitchell
Bierman also provided clarification to Charter provisions regarding the Right to
be Heard and the Right to a Public Hearing. After discussion, Council Member
Mixon requested to poll the Council for position on moving forward with a public
hearing. Mayor Bell recused herself and Vice Mayor Sochin received the gavel.
Council Member Mixon expressed opinion of no merit to move forward with a
public hearing, by general consensus, the Council concurred.
