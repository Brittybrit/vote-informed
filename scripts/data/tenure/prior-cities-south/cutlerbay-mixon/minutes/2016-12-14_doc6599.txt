2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

There was discussion regarding the new House rules for appropriation requests.

At the request of Vice Mayor Loyzelle, Town Manager Rafael Casals provided

clarification on the efforts of Town staff to provide information now required

under the new House rules for appropriation requests. There was discussion on

Florida Recreation Development Assistance Program (FRDAP) grants to

purchase land for park space and funding for climate change and sea level rise.

There was discussion on General Obligation Bonds (GOB). The Council came to

a general consensus to place the Business Incubator item on hold pending a

discussion on grants and local funding. There was discussion on funding for

Active Adult Services.

Town Manager Rafael Casals suggested the Saga Bay 1.2, Cutler Bay Wetland

Pavilion, Economic Development Plan, and the Senior Services Program items

remain on the list of priorities, and reiterated that other items of importance may

also be added as the Council sees fit.

Town Lobbyist Fausto Gomez suggested adding a transportation initiative for

next year. Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman spoke on the possible extension of

Metrorail by Miami-Dade County and the Town being in a position to have a

station. Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman also spoke on the possibility of funds

for intermodal transportation.

Town Lobbyist Fausto Gomez suggested the Council visit Tallahassee during

committee meeting weeks to meet with State Representatives, rather than during

Miami-Dade County Days. The Council came to a consensus to visit Tallahassee

the week of February 21 through 24, 2017.
