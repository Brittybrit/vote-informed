July 23, 2014Town Council Workshop Meeting Minutes

1. Call to Order, Roll Call, & Pledge of Allegiance

Mayor MacDougall called the meeting to order at 3:02 p.m. Town Clerk Debra

Eastman called the roll. Present were Council Members Peggy Bell, Mary Ann

Mixon, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin and Mayor Ed MacDougall. Council Member

Sue Ellen Loyzelle arrived at 3:07 p.m.

Mayor MacDougall led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Council Member Peggy Bell, Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council Member

Mary Ann Mixon, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, and Mayor Edward MacDougall

Present 5 -

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Fiscal Year 2014-15 Proposed Budget

Sponsors: Town Manager's Office

Town Manager Rafael Casals explained that the newly proposed budget is based

upon the millage rate of 2.5702, which is the 2013-2014 millage rate. He provided

explanations to the items highlighted in the Executive Summary.

Finance Director Robert Daddario provided various explanations to the Town

Council. Council Member Loyzelle requested, and it was agreed via consensus

of the Council, that an additional two columns be added to the spreadsheets to

show the 2014-2015 proposed dollar amounts and another column with the net

plus or minus.

There was discussion regarding the cost of contractually reinstated police

benefits that had previously been eliminated and the impact the reinstatement

will have on the budget. There was discussion regarding the cost of adding an

additional police officer to the force.

There was also discussion regarding the park impact fees.

3. Adjournment

The meeting adjourned at 5:16 p.m.
