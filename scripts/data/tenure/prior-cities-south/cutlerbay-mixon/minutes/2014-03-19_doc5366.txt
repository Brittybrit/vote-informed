2. PROCLAMATIONS, AWARDS, AND PRESENTATIONS

A. Certificate of Recognition - Eagle Scout Project

Sponsors: Peggy Bell

Scout Peter Rodriguez, an aspiring Eagle Scout, recently produced 900 pocket

flags to be given to US servicemen. He was presented with a Certificate of

Recognition by the Mayor and Town Council.

B. Certificate of Appreciation - Chipotle Mexican Grill

The Mayor and Town Council thanked Mr. Italo Scaldasseri of Chipotle Mexican

Grill for his donation of food to the Relay for Life event and presented him with a

Certificate of Appreciation.

The proclamation was deferred to a future meeting.

A. · Local Planning Agency, February 19, 2014

· Regular Town Council Meeting, February, 2014

A motion was made by Council Member Loyzelle, seconded by Council Member

Mixon, that the Minutes be approved. The motion carried by an unanimous vote.

(5-0)

4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS

Council Member Loyzelle reported on upcoming meetings. Vice Mayor Sochin

reported on the spill of hydraulic fluid on newly paved roads and the under-use

of Lakes by the Bay Park and state of the wifi connectivity in the parks. Vice

Mayor Sochin, Council Member Loyzelle and Council Member Bell discussed the

preparation and distribution of newletters created by members of the Town

Council. Mayor MacDougall gave a report on his recent trip to Tallahassee and

legislation regarding flood insurance, tolls on Miami-Dade Expressways and red

light cameras.

5. PUBLIC COMMENTS

Tom Condon spoke in opposition to having newsletters from individual members

of the Town Council sent out by the Town Clerk.

AJ Meyer congratulated Peter Rodriguez for the flag project and commended

Vice Mayor Sochin for his review of Town parks.

A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT

Town Manager Rafael Casals provided a written report to the Town Council and

highlighted that the circulator bus ridership is currently at record high numbers.

He reported on the successful grand re-opening event for Old Cutler Road event.

Gary Ratay of Kimley Horn and Associates provided an update on the Caribbean

Boulevard project. Town Manager Casals reported on the recent training of

members of the police force regarding anti-bullying.

B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT

Town Attorney Chad Friedman reported on the Publix litigation.

C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT

Town Clerk Debra Eastman reminded the Mayor and Town Council of the

upcoming Miami-Dade Days in Tallahassee, reported on the continuing website

project and the completion of the Ordinance Register that is required by Town

Charter and that is on file in the Town Clerk's office.

D. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS

Council Member Mixon reported on the Floodplain Mitigation Plan Committee

meetings. Council Member Bell reported on the recent meeting of the Youth

Council. Vice Mayor Sochin reported on the recent meeting of the Education

Advisory Committee. Council Member Loyzelle reported on the recent

Communities for a Lifetime Committee meeting and projects.

Mayor MacDougall annouced that Miami-Dade County has agreed to change the

name of Eureka Drive/Burger King Way, from US 1 to Old Cutler Road, to John F.

Cosgrove Way after the Town of Cutler Bay's first Mayor.

Vice Mayor Sochin made a motion to require that all correspondence, from the

members of the Town Council to the Town, be done on their own time and on

their own computers. The motion received a second from Mayor MacDougall.

After discussion by the Town Council, Mayor MacDougall called for the vote. The

motion failed 2-3 with Council Members Loyzelle, Mixon and Bell voting in

opposition.
