 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE FINAL MILLAGE RATE OF THE TOWN
OF CUTLER BAY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2014
THROUGH SEPTEMBER 30, 2015 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTE 200.065
(TRIM BILL); PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Sponsors:   Town Manager's Office
 Town Clerk Debra Eastman read both the millage and the budget resolution titles
into the record. Town of Cutler Bay Finance Director Robert Daddario read a
statement into the record as follows: The tentative millage rate of the Town of
Cutler Bay for FY 2014-15 of 2.30907 mills per $1,000.00 of assessed property
value within the Town of Cutler Bay is zero percent greater than the roll-back rate
of 2.3907 mills per $1,000.00 of assessed property value. As the tentative millage
rate is equal to the roll-back millage rate, this represents no increase in ad
valorem revenues for FY 2014-15.
Council Member Bell thanked the Town Manager and Finance Director for finding
approximatey $300,000 in the budget to lower the tax rate. Mayor MacDougall
requested that the Town Manger provide an update to the Green Master Plan.
After discussion, it was decided that in addition to providing a written financial
status report to the Town Council, the Town Manager will quarterly include a
verbal financial report to the Town Council. Council Member Loyzelle spoke
about increasing the number of police officers and asked about putting off some
other budgeted projects to make room for funding the police. Council Member
Loyzelle made a motion to remove $70,000 from the Clerks budget and $30,000
from tree trimming in order to provide for more police. The motion received a
second from Council Member Bell. Council Member Bell suggested that the
Town Manager and Town Council review the budget at mid-year to see if there is
any unencumbered funds that could be used to add police. Mayor MacDougall
called for a vote on the motion and the motion failed 1-4 with Council Member
Loyzelle voting in favor.
A motion to adopt the final millage rate of 2.3907 mills per $1,000.00 of assessed
property value which is the roll-back rate of 2.3907 mills per $1,000.00 of
assessed property value was made by Council Member Peggy Bell. The motion
received a second from Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin. Town Clerk Debra Eastman
called the roll and the and the motion carried 5-0. The resolution was adopted at
7:26 p.m.
The motion carried by the following vote:
Yes:   Council Member Peggy Bell, Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, Council
Member Mary Ann Mixon, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin and Mayor Edward
MacDougall
