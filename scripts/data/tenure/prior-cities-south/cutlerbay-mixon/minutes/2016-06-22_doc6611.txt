1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, MOMENT OF

SILENCE & INVOCATION

Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 6:07PM. Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson

called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Fiscal Year 2016-17 Budget Visioning Workshop

Town Manager Rafael Casals provided a brief overview and timeline of the

budget process in comparison to prior years and expressed gratitude for the

opportunity to start the process earlier with the Town's first Budget Visioning

Workshop to brainstorm and discuss plans, priorities and ideas for the upcoming

Fiscal Year 2016-2017. Town Manager Rafael Casals announced a Second

Budget Workshop on July 18, 2016. Town Manager Rafael Casals reported on

increased property values and the Towns success rate with increased property

values consecutively in years prior. Town Manager Rafael Casals highlighted

additional projects budgeted for the Fiscal Year 2016-2017 Proposed Budget and

projects carried over from the Fiscal Year 2015-2016 Budget. Town Manager

Rafael Casals provided an overview of the Fiscal Year 2015-2016 Proposed

Budget components and explained the general fund budget for each department.

There was further discussion on events, property maintenance, enhanced bus

circulator efforts and other plans, priorities and ideas for the upcoming Fiscal

Year 2016-2017.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Mayor Bell spoke in favor of a Public Information Office for the Town and

enhanced marketing efforts. Council Member Loyzelle spoke in favor of a Public

Information Office for the Town and enhanced marketing efforts and the Code

Red alert system. Vice Mayor Sochin echoed support of a Public Information

Office and enhanced marketing efforts. Council Member Coriat spoke in favor of

investment in Town parks and events. Mayor Bell spoke in favor of funds

allocation for a Youth Council Committee trip to Tallahassee. Council Member

Loyzelle suggested fund-raising and partnering with other municipalities to

obtain a group rate. Council Member Loyzelle spoke in favor of funding to
