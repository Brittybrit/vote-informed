7:00 PM Council ChambersMonday, July 13, 2015

1. Call to Order, Roll Call, & Pledge of Allegiance

Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 7:06pm. Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline

Wilson called the role. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,

Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle, and Council Member Roger Coriat

Present 5 -

· Presentation by Alberto M. Carvalho, Superintendent of Miami-Dade County Public

Schools

Mayor Bell welcomed Superintendent of Miami-Dade County Public Schools,

Alberto M. Carvalho, School Representative Dr. Lawrence Feldman, Assistant

Superintendent of Intergovernmental Affairs Ms. Iraida Mendez-Cartaya and

South Region Superintendent Ms. Barbara Mendizabal in attendance and

expressed gratitude. Mayor Bell announced that this meeting will provide an

opportunity to hear the action plan for Cutler Bay Middle School and permit the

Council to plan a follow-up workshop to discuss the action plan. Mayor Bell

requested that questions following the presentation be related to the

presentation and not past events. Mayor Bell introduced Superintendent of

Miami-Dade County Public Schools, Alberto M. Carvalho to present the Cutler

Bay Middle School Action Plan before the Town Council. Mr. Carvalho stated

that Cutler Bay Middle is not the most violent school in the State of FL, it is not

the most violent school in Miami-Dade County. Mr. Carvalho explained that the

data collection systems alignment reporting the statistics was flawed, but has

been corrected. Mr. Carvalho reported that culture issues beyond the school

impact and contribute to the issue when the values taught in school are not

reiterated outside the school and are not being taught to today’s youth,

expressing the importance of a connection between school and community for

improvement. Mr. Carvalho introduced South Region Superintendent Ms.

Barbara Mendizabal to speak on behalf of Principal Paul Pfeiffer who was unable

to attend the meeting due to a family emergency. Ms. Mendizabal provided an

overview of the measures taken for transformation of Cutler Bay Middle School

and Principal Pfieffer's action plan for implementation focusing on creating a

safe environment to promote learning. Mr. Carvalho introduced Mr. Jaime

Torrens, Chief Facilities Officer to present developments with regard to the

general obligation bond investment for schools in Cutler Bay, particularly Cutler

Bay Middle School and the transformation taking place in hopes of making this

school an asset to the community. Mr. Torrens introduced Deputy

Superintendent Ms. Valtena Brown to present the goals for Cutler Bay Middle

School and the steps taken to reach those goals as the transition moves forward.

Mr. Carvalho announced they are going to fund a music program in the Middle

School. Mr. Carvalho acknowledged commitment to the investments, but the

vigilance will not mend itself. Mr. Carvalho suggests the school and community

declare this school a top priority and challenges the Town to address the parents

directly to orient children in the right direction with the appropriate plans in place

to help this school get back on track.

2. Town Council Questions/ Comments/ Discussion

Mayor Bell opened for Town Council Questions and Comments. Council Member

Mixon spoke in strong favor of the return of the music program and thanked the

Superintendent for bringing it back. Mayor Bell announced Commissioner

Daniella Levine-Cava in attendance. Council Member Coriat stressed the

importance of dealing with the impact of bullying and the severe effects it has on

children. Vice Mayor Sochin suggested enforcement of uniforms. Mayor Bell

acknowledged a correlation between behavior of students and how they dress.

Mr. Carvalho stated the responsibility of the parent is being replaced by the

placement of a principal and the principal has a right to demand the role of the

parent be restored to teach their children to conduct themselves in an

appropriate manner. Council Member Loyzelle thanked Mr. Carvalho for his

attendance and for her invitation received from Principal Pfeiffer to attend the

National Association on Community and Restorative Justice Conference June

1-3, 2015. Council Member Loyzelle reported on her attendance at the

Conference and announced that she would like to donate books presented to her

as a doorprize at the Conference to the Superintendent. Town Manager Rafael

Casals thanked the Superintendent and members of the School Board for their

attendance. Mayor Bell acknowledged members of the Education Advisory

Committee, also in attendance. Mayor Bell introduced Commissioner Daniella

Levine-Cava who expressed support of addressing violence in a broader context,

and thanked the Mayor, Town Council and Superintendent for tonight's meeting.

Commissioner Levine-Cava announced the Miami-Dade C.O.R.E. 2015 Hurricane

Preparedness Engagement Session on July 21, 2015, the County Budget Meeting

on July 22, 2015, and the Homes for All Pets Workshop event on July 25, 2015.

Mayor Bell introduced Dr. Feldman to express his appreciation for this meeting

and the current staff in place. Council Member Loyzelle presented Mr. Carvalho

with her donation of books obtained at the National Association on Community

and Restorative Justice Conference. Mayor Bell thanked everyone for attending

and requested a brief recess at 8:22PM prior to scheduling the follow-up

workshop to discuss the action plan. The meeting reconvened at 8:39PM and the

Mayor requested Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson to poll the Council in

efforts to schedule the follow-up workshop to discuss the action plan.

3. Adjournment

A motion was made by Council Member Mixon to adjourn. The motion received a

second from Vice Mayor Sochin. The meeting adjourned at 8:40PM.
