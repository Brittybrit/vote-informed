February 17, 2016 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Vice Mayor Sochin reported on Council attendance and participation in
Miami-Dade County Days in Tallahassee.
Council Member Loyzelle announced the Property Appraiser Tax Exemption
Filing Period. Council Member Loyzelle reported on attendance at the Florida
Senate Children, Families and Elder Affairs Committee Meeting featuring a
Communities for a Lifetime presentation by Florida Department of Elder Affiars
Secretary Samuel Verghese and shared a brief video of the presentation
highlighting the Town of Cutler Bay Communities for a Lifetime Committee.
Council Member Loyzelle also announced several upcoming events.
Mayor Bell reported on the progress of the Miami-Dade League of Cities
Transportation Committee and the South Dade Mayor's Coalition and mass
transit initiatives. Mayor Bell reported on attendance at several meetings and
events and announced a Human Trafficking Symposium on March 14, 2016.
Council Member Coriat reported on attendance at the El Dorado Grand Opening
along with Council Member Mixon, Vice Mayor Sochin and Town Manager Rafael
Casals.
 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell opened Public Comments. Mr. Tom Condon requested procedural
clarification regarding the March 16, 2016 Regular Town Council Meeting and
requested the Cutler Properties site plan application item be moved to the front
of the agenda. Mayor Bell closed Public Comments.
 6. REPORTS
 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT
 Gary Ratay of Kimley Horn and Associates presented an update on the Caribbean
Boulevard Roadway Improvement Project and introduced new Public Information
Officer Janet Delgado. Police Major Julie Miller reported on enforcement efforts
and successes associated with a recent rise in robberies and clarified the
difference between a robbery and a burglary. Town Manager Rafael Casals
announced several upcoming events and provided a status update on the
installation of a new traffic signal at SW 216 Street. Town Manager Rafael Casals
also reported on revenue from the Annual Chili Cook-Off Event and received
Coucil approval by general consensus to donate the funds to the Cutler Bay
Community Fund.
 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT
 Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman announced item 8A Fee Schedule Resolution on
the agenda should be read and voted on after the adoption of item 12A Ordinance
for Second Reading. Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman also reported on a
contested Property Assessment case victory.
 C. TOWN CLERK'S REPORT
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson announced upcoming events and deadlines.
Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson also provided a status update on the vacant
position in the Town Clerk's Office.

 D. BOARD AND COMMITTEE REPORTS
 Council Member Loyzelle announced the next Communities for a Lifetime
Committee Meeting on March 2, 2016 and elaborated on the upcoming East Ridge
Grand Opening Event on February 19, 2016. Council Member Loyzelle also
announced the upcoming Communities for a Lifetime Workshop on March 22,
2016.
Vice Mayor Sochin reported on the progress of the Education Advisory
Committee and initiatives involving homework and recess. There was
discussion on recess and recess legislation.
Council Member Mixon announced the next Floodplain Mitigation Plan
Committee Meeting on March 8, 2016 and announced World Wetlands Day
Cleanup Event on February 20, 2016. Council Member Mixon also reported on
the Governor-Issued Immediate Action to relieve flooding in the Everglades.
Council Member Coriat reported on the the Tour de Parks Event on January 23,
2016 and thanked everyone for their efforts and participation. Council Member
Coriat reported on the progress of the Parks and Recreation Committee and
announced various upcoming events. Council Member Coriat also announced
the next Parks and Recreation Committee Meeting on March 7, 2016.
Mayor Bell reported on the status of the Economic Growth Committee and an
upcoming request for extension. Mayor Bell also reported on the progress of the
Youth Council Committee. Mayor Bell thanked Town staff for their efforts with
Town events. Mayor Bell announced an upcoming Sea-Level Rise Symposium
and a Green Corridor PACE Meeting on March 11, 2016.
Town Manager Rafael Casals announced thirty-three (33) applicants responded
to the vacant position in the Town Clerk's Office.

Mixon, to adopt the Consent Agenda. Mayor Bell called for the vote and the
motion passed 5-0.
