CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, APPROVING AN AGREEMENT WITH ACOSTA
TRACTORS INC. FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF SW 212 STREET DRAINAGE
IMPROVEMENTS; PROVIDING FOR AUTHORIZATION OF THE TOWN MANAGER
TO EXECUTE THIS AGREEMENT; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Interim Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. Town
Attorney Mitchell Bierman informed the Town Council that this item is currently
under bid protest. South Eastern Engineering Contractors filed a bid protest.
South Eastern Engineering Contractors' bid was submitted without State of
Florida credential documentation required in the RFP, when it was further
investigated as to whether they actually held the credential at the time of the
proposal submittal, it was determined that they did not. Town Manager Rafael
Casals found them to be nonresponsive. South Eastern Engineering Contractors
were not in attendance to respond to this item. Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman
recommended approving the Town Manager's recommendation. A motion was
made by Council Member Mixon, seconded by Vice Mayor Sochin, that this
Resolution be adopted.
