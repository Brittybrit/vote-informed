 4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Council Member Loyzelle requested Council approval to host an Alliance for
Aging Community Conversations Workshop on June 27, 2016. The Council came
to a general consensus to approve the workshop. Council Member Loyzelle
reported on Circulator Bus efforts. Council Member Loyzelle also reported on a
recently adopted Miami-Dade County Resolution allowing circulators on the
busway and expanded on Town efforts associated with bus circulators and
routes. Town Manager Rafael Casals provided additional information on efforts
associated with bus routes.
Council Member Coriat announced the Lincoln City Park Community Input
Meeting on July 9, 2016 and "The Big Sting: Mosquitos in South Florida"
Educational and Community Outreach Workshop on July 14, 2016.
Council Member Mixon announced a Florida Fish and Wildlife Commission
discussion on bear hunting on June 22, 2016, and spoke in opposition of bear
hunting.
Mayor Bell reported on attendance at a recent South Dade Mayors Coalition
Meeting. Mayor Bell announced the South Dade Solutions Summit Event on July
15, 2016, and the South A Municipal Advisory Committee Meeting on June 28,
2016. Mayor Bell reported on the Tri-City Hurricane Preparedness Workshop and
thanked Town staff for their efforts. Mayor Bell also reported on a Cutler Bay
Senior High School yearbook ad and graduation medallions provided by the
Town in honor of the Cutler Bay Senior High School Inaugural Class and
Commencement Ceremony.
 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell reviewed the procedure for Public Comments. Mayor Bell opened
Public Comments. Ms. Jean McJilton spoke on traffic study item 7C and
expressed concern. Ms. Jean McJilton spoke in opposition of item deferrals. Mr.
Marco Fernandez spoke in opposition of the 87th Avenue and Old Cutler Road
project. Mr. Stephen Zarzecki spoke in opposition of traffic study item 7C and
the traffic study methodology. Ms. Elizabeth Ott spoke in opposition of
Quasi-Judicial items 9A through 9G.
There was discussion among the Council on the deferral of items.
Mayor Bell closed Public Comments.

 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT
 Town Manager Rafael Casals reported on the Annual 4th of July Spectacular at
Black Point Park and Marina event and efforts with Miami-Dade County to allow
golf cart access on the day of the event. Town Manager Rafael Casals provided a
progress report on vacant property research in accordance with Town Resolution
16-25 adopted at the April 20, 2016 Regular Town Council Meeting. Town
Manager Rafael Casals announced an awarded grant from Miami-Dade
Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) for the Complete Streets Study
Corridor Analysis. Town Manager Rafael Casals provided an update on the
Florida Department of Transportation’s (FDOT) Transportation Alternative
Program grant application for the Manta Drive Roadway Improvement Project.
Town Manager Rafael Casals announced the Department of Juvenile Justice
(DJJ) selection of the Town to present the Afterschool Program before the
Department of Juvenile Justice (DJJ) State Advisory Group (SAG). Town
Manager Rafael Casals provided an update on Florida Green Building Coalition
(FGBC) Certification, street light testing on Caribbean Boulevard, and on
negotiations with Miami-Dade County to pair the Gap 2 Project with the Bridge
project. At the request of Council Member Coriat, Town Manager Rafael Casals
provided additional information on vacant property research.
 ·   Robert Daddario, Finance Department- Quarterly Financial Report
 Finance Director Robert Daddario presented an overview of the Town's Quarterly
Financial Report and the results from the year and second quarter ended March
31, 2016. At the request of Council Member Loyzelle, Finance Director Robert
Daddario provided clarification on the Quarterly Financial Report.
 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT
 Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman informed the Council of a Miami-Dade County
Ordinance passed on first reading regarding requirements on affordable housing
