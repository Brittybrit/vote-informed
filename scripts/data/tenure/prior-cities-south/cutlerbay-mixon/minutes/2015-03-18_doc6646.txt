 4. TOWN COUNCIL ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Council Member Loyzelle reported on her recent trip with the Town Manager to
Tallahassee to meet with State Legislators to discuss legislative priorities not
previously represented during the Mayors visit. Council Member Loyzelle also
met with the new Director of Communities for a Lifetime Ms. Cory Burke
Livingston to discuss senior needs within the Town and shared the Communities
for a Lifetime Committees Strategic Plan. Council Member Loyzelle will return to
Tallahassee April 8-9 2015 with the Town Council and Town Manager for
Miami-Dade Days. Council Member Loyzelle reported on the Miami ePrix event
with a brief news clip and slideshow presentation. Council Member Loyzelle
announced her attendance with Mayor Bell to Movie Night which was
rescheduled for Friday, April 17, 2015 due to inclement weather. Council Member
Loyzelle mentioned the upcoming Easter Egg Hunt scheduled for Saturday, April
4, 2015. Council Member Loyzelle also mentioned pictures from our Founders
Day Event featured in El Latino Magazine. Council Member Loyzelle reported on
her attendance with Mayor Bell and Vice Mayor Sochin at the East Ridge
Luncheon and their new development open to the Community for events.
Vice Mayor Sochin reported on his participation in a seven mile bike ride event
with Miami-Dade County Mayor Carlos Gimenez, Palmetto Bay Mayor Eugene
Flinn and District 8 Miami-Dade County Commissioner Daniella Levine-Cava.
Mayor Bell attended opening day at Franjo Park where she threw the opening
pitch. Mayor Bell announced her attendance with Vice Mayor Sochin to the Grand
Opening of Commissioner Levine-Cava's Government Center. Mayor Bell
reported on the Town Council's attendance to our Founders Day event and
commended staff for a successful event. Mayor Bell mentioned her attendance
with Town Attorney Mitch Bierman and Council Member Loyzelle to the State of
the County Event. Mayor Bell attended Commissioner Levine-Cava's District 8
Mayors Meeting to brief on Town issues and legislative items. Mayor Bell also
attended the Relay for Life event along with Vice Mayor Sochin and Council
Member Coriat and recognized Town Manager, staff and interns for their efforts.
Mayor Bell also reported on her participation with Mr. Gus Pego, Florida
Department of Transportation District Six Secretary and Ms. Ysela Llort,
Miami-Dade County Transit Director in Vitual Town Hall Transportation Meeting
hosted at Cutler Bay Town Hall, arranged by State Representative Kionne
McGhee and Congressman Carlos Curbelo who attended virtually along with
Homestead Mayor Jeff Porter and Florida City Mayor Otis Wallace. Mayor Bell
also attended a ribbon cutting event for Saint Richards Holy Rosary Carnival
fundraiser. Mayor Bell reported on her meeting with Town Manager Rafael Casals
held with Miami-Dade School personnel to determine name changes for the High
School and Middle School and discuss issues of concern; Town Manager Rafael
Casals confirmed both names were passed by the School Board for Cutler Bay
Middle School and Cutler Bay High School. Mayor Bell attended with Vice Mayor
Sochin the CBBA luncheon where she shared her report card on her first 120
days in office as Mayor. Mayor Bell also announced the Tour of the South-Dade
Landfill Facility on Friday March 20, 2015.

 5. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell opened Public Comments.
Mr. Drew Peterson voiced his concerns associated with the sign ordinance
passed in 2008.
Mr. Tom Condon presented his concern associated with the Town Center Plaza
item on the agenda, that the location should be more centrally located.
Mr. Steve Zarzecki thanked Town staff for their patience with his questions and

Comprehensive Plan.
 6. REPORTS
 A. TOWN MANAGER’S REPORT
 Town Manager Rafael Casals introduced Robert Daddario, FInance Department to
present the Town's Quarterly Financial Report. Gary Ratay and Alina Phillips of
Kimley Horn and Associates presented an update on the Caribbean Boulevard
Roadway Improvement Project. Gary Ratay also announced that Alina Phillips
will be moving on to pursue another career opportunity. Town Manager Rafael
Casals thanked his staff as well as Town Attorney Mitch Bierman for materials
prepared for the Tallahassee visits to discuss Legislative Priorities. Town
Manager Rafael Casals thanked the Parks and Recreation Department for
coordination of trip to the Cleaveland Orchestra with senior citizens in the
community on March 3, 2015. Town Manager Rafael Casals also reminded the
Town Council that we will continue to accept applications for the vacant Town
Clerk position until March 20, 2015. Town Manager Rafael Casals also mentioned
upcoming events including Open Mic Night Town Hall Meeting, Landfill Facility
Tour and Annual Employee Appreciation Picnic. Town Manager Rafael Casals
concluded his report with a status update on Marlin Road.
 ·   Robert Daddario, Finance Department- Quarterly Financial Report
 Robert Daddario, Finance Department presented an overview of the Town's
Quarterly Financial Report for the first quarter, explaining revenue and
expenditure results and confirming the first quarter results are consistent with
first quarter in prior years. Mr. Daddario closed with remarks that we are about to
begin our auditing period and announced unaudited figures ending fiscal year
2014 which ended on September 30, 2014 with an approximate $1.8 million
surplus, putting our reserves a little bit over $21 million.
 B. TOWN ATTORNEY’S REPORT
 ·   Update on Coulson v Town of Cutler Bay
 Town Attorney Mitch Bierman requested an Executive Session on the case of
Coulson v. Town of Cutler Bay. Town Attorney Mitch Bierman provided a status
update on the Realty Associates Publix case. Town Attorney Mitch Bierman
concluded his report with an update on the Red Light Camera Federal Class
Action Lawsuit.
