 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, SELECTING SUPERIOR PARK SYSTEMS, INC. FOR THE
INSTALLATION OF PLAYGROUND EQUIPMENT AND SHADE STRUCTURES AT
VARIOUS TOWN OWNED PARKS; PROVIDING FOR WAIVER OF COMPETITIVE
BIDDING; PROVIDING FOR AUTHORIZATION; AND PROVIDING FOR AN
EFFECTIVE DATE. (*previously deferred per July 20, 2016 Regular Town Council
Meeting)
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. A motion was
made by Council Member Coriat, seconded by Vice Mayor Sochin, that this
Resolution be adopted. Council Member Coriat provided clarification on the
detailed report for item 8A. Town Manager Rafael Casals provided a staff report
on the item and provided a breakdown of associated funds. After discussion,
Mayor Bell suggested funds be allocated to Lakes by the Bay Park for a
vitacourse. Council Member Coriat requested a reduction of additional
equipment. At the request of Town Manager Rafael Casals, Parks and Recreation
Director Etienne Bejarano provided clarification on the utilization of park impact
fees. Finance Director Robert Daddario provided clarification on the costs
associated with the project. After further discussion, an amended motion was
made by Council Member Coriat, seconded by Council Member Mixon to exclude
Saga Lake Park from the proposal and adopt the Resolution as amended. Mayor
Bell called for the vote, and the motion passed 4-1 with Council Member Loyzelle
in opposition.

 B.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, SELECTING DOMINICA RECREATION PRODUCTS, INC.
FOR THE INSTALLATION OF OUTDOOR FITNESS EQUIPMENT AND SHADE
STRUCTURES AT VARIOUS TOWN OWNED PARKS; PROVIDING FOR WAIVER
OF COMPETITIVE BIDDING; PROVIDING FOR AUTHORIZATION; AND PROVIDING
FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE. (*previously deferred per July 20, 2016 Regular Town
Council Meeting)
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. Town Manager
Rafael Casals presented the staff report on the item and expressed support for
the Parks and Recreation Committee recommendation. There was discussion on
the allocation of exercise equipment at the parks. After discussion, the Council
came to a general consensus to remove Bel Aire Park from the proposal and
convert Saga Lake Park and Blue Heron Park to a circular layout. A motion was
made by Council Member Mixon, seconded by Vice Mayor Sochin, that this
Resolution be adopted as amended. Mayor Bell called for the vote, and the
motion passed 4-1 with Council Member Loyzelle in opposition.
