 1. CALL TO ORDER, ROLL CALL, PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE, MOMENT OF
SILENCE & INVOCATION
 Mayor Bell called the meeting to order at 7:00PM. Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson
called the roll. Mayor Bell led the Pledge of Allegiance.
 Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat
 Present:   5 -
 2. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell opened Public Comments and there were none.
 3. RESOLUTIONS
 Finance Director Robert Daddario read the TRIM statement mandated by the
State of Florida into the record: The tentative millage rate of the Town of Cutler
Bay for FY 2016-17 of 2.3907 mils per $1,000 of assessed property value within
the Town of Cutler Bay is 7.72% greater than the roll-back rate of 2.2194 mils per
$1,000 of assessed property value. Ad valorem taxes are being increased
primarily as a result of expenditures for increased costs of contracted police
services, as well as to provide for various improvements to park and public
facilities.
Town Attorney Mitchell Bierman provided a brief overview of the procedure to
remind the Council to adopt the millage rate prior to the final budget.

September 27, 2016 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 A.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE FINAL MILLAGE RATE OF THE TOWN
OF CUTLER BAY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2016
THROUGH SEPTEMBER 30, 2017 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTE 200.065
(TRIM BILL); PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. Town Manager
Rafael Casals provided a brief overview and timeline of the budget procedure. A
motion was made by Council Member Coriat, seconded by Council Member
Mixon, that this Resolution be adopted at the 2.3907 millage rate. At the request
of Council Member Loyzelle, Town Manager Rafael Casals provided clarification
on changes from the First Budget Hearing, the Second Budget Hearing, and the
revised plan dip. At 7:18PM, the motion carried by the following vote:
Yes:   Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat
5 -
 B.   A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND TOWN COUNCIL OF THE TOWN OF
CUTLER BAY, FLORIDA, ADOPTING THE FINAL OPERATING AND CAPITAL
OUTLAY BUDGET FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2016
THROUGH SEPTEMBER 30, 2017 PURSUANT TO FLORIDA STATUTE 200.065
(TRIM BILL); AND AUTHORIZING THE TOWN MANAGER TO MAKE
EXPENDITURES CONSISTENT THEREWITH AND AUTHORIZING THE TOWN
MANAGER TO MAKE CERTAIN BUDGET AMENDMENTS WITHIN A DEPARTMENT
PROVIDED THAT THE TOTAL OF THE APPROPRIATIONS IS NOT CHANGED; AND
PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
 Town Clerk Jacqueline Wilson read the title of the Resolution. Town Manager
Rafael Casals provided an overview of the budget and highlighted changes in the
general budget fund summaries. Finance Director Robert Daddario clarified the
final budget amount of $52,171,433.00. A motion was made by Council Member
Loyzelle, seconded by Council Member Mixon, that this Resolution be adopted.
Council Member Loyzelle requested clarification on the remaining funds in the
line item for park improvements. There was discussion on park impact fees.
There was discussion on the process of allocating funds towards the Town
Center Plaza. At 7:48PM, the motion carried by the following vote:
Yes:   Mayor Peggy Bell, Vice Mayor Ernest Sochin, Council Member Mary Ann Mixon,
Council Member Sue Ellen Loyzelle and Council Member Roger Coriat
5 -
 4. PUBLIC COMMENTS
 Mayor Bell opened Public Comments and there were none.

September 27, 2016 Town Council   Meeting Minutes
 5. MAYOR AND COUNCIL COMMENTS
 Council Member Mixon announced participation in the upcoming Florida County
League of Cities Energy, Environment and Natural Resources Committee Meeting
on September 30, 2016 and invited potential business requests from Council to
present to the committee. Mayor Bell announced the Traffic Calming Master Plan
Public Involvement Meeting on September 28, 2016 and the upcoming Burlington
Grand Opening on September 30, 2016. Town Manager Rafael Casals thanked
Town staff for their diligence in the budget process and announced the launch of
the Town Mobile Application (app). At the request of Council Member Loyzelle,
Town Manager Rafael Casals reported on Miami-Dade County Main Library's
recent request to participate as a panelist at the Alliance for Aging Workshop on
September 28, 2016 to discuss circulator bus and adult programs efforts. There
was discussion on the new format for Town Press Releases.
 6. OTHER BUSINESS
 There was no other business.
 7. ADJOURNMENT
 A motion was made by Council Member Mixon to adjourn. The motion received a
second from Vice Mayor Sochin. The meeting adjourned at 7:56PM.
