Conference Session: “Attendance Areas for 2024-2025”
Ms. Mari Tere Rojas, School Board Chair, welcomed everyone to the April 17,
2024, Conference Session on Attendance Boundaries for the 2024-2025 school
year. The Conference Session was called to order at 10:00 a.m. and
Superintendent Dr. Jose L. Dotres led those in attendance with The Pledge of
Allegiance to the Flag of the United States of America. The Board then held a
Moment of Silent Meditation.
The Chair announced that this Conference Session will now be turned over to the
Superintendent Dr. Jose L. Dotres to give opening remarks. However, before
turning the presentation over to the Superintendent, she wanted to give a special
thank you the Chair and Vice Chair of the Attendance Boundary Committee, Mr.
Alfred Billings and Ms. Tamia Spells for their dedication and commitment to this
process. The Chair took the opportunity to also thank everyone who made this
possible from district personnel to region personnel, school site administrators, and
the amazing parents who have been very involved and engaged in this process
along with Mr. Eddy Barea, and Ms. Cynthia Gracia who represented the Chair
throughout these meetings.

remarks which he stated that this is the 43
rd 
School Board meeting to review and
act on attendance boundary recommendations that were presented to parents,
students, interested community members, and organizations throughout the
district’s Attendance Boundary Committee (ABC). As always, the input of the
community is the number one factor of how we approach this process for boundary
recommendations. This morning’s Attendance Zone Recommendations are being
presented as an Initial Reading to the School Board. The Final Reading on the
Attendance Zone Recommendations will be held on June 18, 2024, at which time
the School Board will vote on these recommendations.

Project ADAM (Automated Defibrillators in Adam's Memory) began in 1999 in
Wisconsin after the death of Adam Lemel, who was just 17 years old when he
collapsed and died while playing basketball. Adam suffered a sudden cardiac
arrest (SCA). If cardiac arrest had been identified and treated quickly with CPR
and the use of an AED, his life could have been saved. Project ADAM was
established by Adam's parents in collaboration with Children's Wisconsin.
Over two hundred lives have been saved by Project ADAM since its beginnings.
SCA occurs when the heart stops beating, leading to loss of circulation and loss of
consciousness, with no blood flow to the brain and other vital organs. The highest
chance of survival was within the first 4 minutes and declined by 10% for every
additional minute that circulation was not restored. These events commonly come
without warning, making the mission of Project ADAM to be prepared to respond
that much more valuable.
A study was conducted in over 2,000 schools throughout the country. A comparison
was done of the survival rate of SCA in schools with a cardiac emergency response
team versus the survival rate at schools without a cardiac response team. In
schools without a cardiac emergency team, the survival rate was 24% compared
to an 85% survival rate in schools that had a Project ADAM-like program.
Nicklaus Children’s Hospital Heart Institute believes in the valuable mission of
Project Adam and is steadfast in providing schools and community organizations
the education and tools to develop and sustain their SCA response programs. It
was their hope to one day have every school in South Florida prepared to respond
during these critical moments to help save a life from sudden cardiac death.
In Miami-Dade County, there are more than three hundred certified “Heart Safe
Schools” and 17 Recreational Park Teams that can successfully activate a sudden
cardiac arrest program and an effective emergency response to promote a safe
environment for students, visitors, and staff.
Today we celebrate the staff of every M-DCPS that has received training provided
by Nicklaus Children’s Hospital Heart Institute clinicians in life-saving methods,
including response plan development, performing Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation
(CPR), and the use of automated external defibrillators.
