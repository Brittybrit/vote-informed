 Miami-Dade County Public Schools Parent Academy Re-Engaging
Families
♦♦♦♦♦
The Superintendent, Dr. Dotres presented this item. Dr. Dotres, introduced
staff and then stated, one of his top priorities was to re-engage families as
the district recovered from the impact of the pandemic. The crucial bond
between parents and education fosters a sense of partnership and
engagement that is critical for student success. The district’s collective
goal was to “Connect and Inspire”, to empower parents and caregivers and
give them the tools that would help them make important decisions
regarding their children’s education and how to best navigate Miami- Dade
County Public Schools. To achieve this successfully, the rebranding
and relaunching of the M-DCPS Parent Academy was essential. Already
recognized by many in our community, the M-DCPS Parent Academy
embarked on a mission to re-envision itself and develop new topics and a
series of webinars designed to meet the needs of parents and caregivers.
Staff ensured that all workshop content was available in three languages:
English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole. Weekly Webinars were offered in the
morning and evening hours, providing flexibility and options for stay-at-
home parents, and working parents alike. Webinar topics for 2022-2023
focused on vital topics such as Magnet/Choice Programs, Financial
Literacy in support of the Future Bound Miami Initiative, a Grandparenting
Series, a Police and Family series, and a tutorial series on the state’s new
reading and mathematics FAST assessments. Additionally, schools started
hosting “watch parties” facilitated by Parent Academy staff, encouraging
parents and caregivers to visit their children’s schools, and making
connections with other caregivers, administrators, teachers, and staff.
