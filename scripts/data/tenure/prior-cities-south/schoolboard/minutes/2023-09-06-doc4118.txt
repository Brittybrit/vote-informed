Dedicated to serving his fellow Floridians, Representative Perez was first
elected to the Florida House of Representatives in 2017 to fill a vacant seat.
During his time in office, he has been instrumental in championing key
legislation pertaining to education, child welfare, healthcare, family stability,
and election integrity.
During his first legislative session in 2018, Representative Perez sponsored
and successfully passed House Bill 1435 on child welfare, more specifically
the bill established family finding programs to assist efforts in finding family
members or close adults for children removed from their homes; authorized
the establishment of a kinship navigator program to assist relatives and non-
relatives with services and resources; and clarified changes to daycare or
early childhood education requirements for children from birth to school age in
foster care. Because of his child welfare efforts, Representative Perez was
honored with the Children’s Home Society of Florida 2018 Legislator of the
Year.
The Chair also mentioned that Representative Perez has served as the former
Florida House of Representative Chair of the Judiciary Committee, Pandemic
and Public Emergencies Committee, and Public Integrity and Elections
Committee. He currently serves as the Chairman of the Rules Committee and
is also a member of the Appropriations Committee which oversees the budget
for the State of Florida. Representative Perez has been a long-standing
supporter of K-12 schools, including fighting for funding needed to educate our
future leaders. K-12 will always be a priority for Florida. This means advocating
for better technology and public-school teachers. Representative Perez also
has a passion for Florida’s college and university systems, specifically a
passion for higher education and workforce training for students with
disabilities. He has continued to advocate for these programs as a means to
fill workforce gaps while investing in those individuals that will become the
fabric of tomorrow in our state. He has championed education funding and this
past legislative session $252.8 million was allocated for classroom teachers
and other instructional personnel salary increases. The Florida Education
Finance Program (FEFP) funding formula received an increase of $404.67
(4.91%) for a total of $8,648.1, per student.
As a result of his steadfast commitment and unwavering leadership as a
House Representative, Daniel Perez was appointed Speaker of the Florida
House and will gavel in from 2024 through 2026. As Speaker, he seeks to
tackle critical issues pertaining to education, healthcare, infrastructure,
affordable housing, and property insurance, while staying true to being the
voice of his constituents in Tallahassee.
Representative Perez received a bachelor’s degree at Florida State University
in 2009 and a Juris Doctorate Degree at Loyola University New Orleans
College of Law in 2012. He is a devoted family man married to Stephanie Ann
Perez and his children Camila Lucia and Matias Daniel.
The Chair stated that “Education is the Bedrock of Our Country,” and it is part

School Board of Miami-Dade County, Florida, proudly honors and recognizes
Florida State Representative and future House Speaker Daniel “Danny” Perez
