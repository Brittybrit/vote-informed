11:00 A.M. - PRESENTATION OF RESOLUTIONS
The Chair called the meeting to order at 11:08 a.m., and Ms. Baez-Geller
led those in attendance in The Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag of the
United States of America. The Chair requested that all continued to
stand silently for a moment of reflection and respect. She said that words
cannot express the tremendous grief our brothers and sisters are
experiencing over in Haiti following the earthquake on Saturday. As a
community and a nation, all are stunned and mourn the tragic loss of
lives and injuries; and deepest sympathy goes out to the victims, their
families, and friends. She extended words of encouragement to the
survivors, hoping that the interest of the community helps move toward
them and may the solidarity of all alleviate the consequences of the
tragedy. The Board then held a moment of silent meditation.
The Chair informed the public that pursuant to Board policies, proposed
Board member resolutions would be presented at the 11:00 a.m.
session; and, upon vote being taken, the Board would recess to present
the approved resolutions. The Board would then reconvene its Regular
public meeting, including other hearings, in accordance with the policies
of the Board, at 1:00 p.m., or soon thereafter.
The Chair invited anyone from the audience who had not already signed
up and wished to address a resolution, endorsement, or proclamation to
proceed to the lobby counter outside the auditorium for assistance from
staff. She advised that the Board must approve additional speakers by
a majority vote.

With great pleasure, Dr. Navarro presented this proclamation. She said
that Mr. Andres Asion has been at the forefront of the South Florida Real
Estate Market for more than 20 years, specializing in all aspects of high-
rise development sales. While real estate is his passion, giving back to
the community is where he finds his greatest reward.
The Miami Beach born Real-estate broker learned the importance of
community service at an early age. As a child, Andres would accompany
his Cuban-born father to charity events. An air conditioning mechanic by
trade, Julian Asion taught his children to serve the community through
sweat equity. The elder Asion would make giant pans of paella, and
young Andres helped by exchanging plates for fundraising tickets.
His father’s example remains an essential component of Andre’s
philanthropic efforts. In 2016, he founded the Andres Asion Foundation,
aimed at assisting less fortunate children and families through
contributions in which 100% of all funds benefit the organizations being
supported.
Shortly after the pandemic hit, the Foundation served as a resource to
alleviate economic burdens among fellow Miami Dade County residents,
through grocery giveaways.
What began as a routine morning bike ride turned into an opportunity to
help the community. Andres made a video showing a line of cars
stretched out for blocks in South Beach for a food distribution event and
shared it to his social media. A follower was taken aback by the need
they saw and offered Andres $500 to rally additional help.
Asion quickly channeled his do-good energy and sprang into action, he
connected with Presidente Supermarket which agreed to host a grocery-
giveaway and to match donations.
Andres began surprising shoppers at the supermarket’s checkout,
covering bills totaling $4,000. Since then, the Foundation has raised
more than $120,000 for grocery store giveaways. Families from Miami
Southridge High School in District 7 were beneficiaries of Andres’
generosity.
Through the Foundation, he is also actively involved with the Overtown
Youth Center, St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital and Amigos for
Kids. Mr. Asion is to be commended for his commitment to servant
leadership.
