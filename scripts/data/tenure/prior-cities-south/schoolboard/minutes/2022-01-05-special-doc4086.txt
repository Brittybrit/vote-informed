SP-1
Ms. Perla Tabares Hantman, Chair, recommended that The School
Board of Miami-Dade County, Florida, establish parameters and critical
elements that will govern the identification of qualified candidates for
the position of Superintendent of Schools, as follows:
1. Adopt the minimum qualifications for the position of Superintendent
of Schools, to include:
a. Demonstrated experience as a classroom teacher, a school-site
Principal, and school districtwide experience.
b. At a minimum a Master’s degree or higher; Doctoral degree
preferred.
c. An understanding of our diverse community and knowledgeable
of the needs of our students, including our English Language
Learners, Exceptional Education Students, low performing
students, our most gifted and talented students, and our
students who come from economically deprived neighborhoods.
d. At least three (3) years of Highly Effective/Effective public school
classroom teaching experience.
e. At least seven (7) years of progressively responsible public
school administrative experience.
f. Knowledge in administration, supervision, curriculum, business
affair, personnel management, and program evaluation.
g. Oral and written communication skills.
h. Ability to work with diverse group of people.
i. Knowledge of and experience with industry standard computer
applications.
2. Identify the process and tentative timeline for the selection and
appointment of the Superintendent of Schools. The process
includes a letter of intent/interest and a resume to be submitted to
the School Board Attorney within seven (7) days from today, for the
Board’s review and consideration.
3. Authorize the Chair to schedule additional special Board meetings
to further discuss the selection and appointment of the
Superintendent.
4. Direct the School Board Attorney to develop a website with all the
information regarding the selection and appointment of the
Superintendent, to be updated periodically, where stakeholders can
provide their thoughts and ideas about the type of individual the

Chair Hantman introduced this item and explained the reasons it was
brough forth for consideration. On December 9, 2021, the
Superintendent of Schools, Mr. Alberto Carvalho, informed the School
Board of his decision to resign from his position of Superintendent of
Miami-Dade County Public Schools. As a result of the Superintendent’s

govern the identification of qualified candidates for the position of
Superintendent of Schools. As such, this item sought to have the Board
discuss and set forth parameters on critical elements.
At this time, the Chair brought to the Board’s attention the Succession
Management Plan, as submitted by Superintendent Carvalho in June
of 2021 in response to Agenda Item H-24, previously approved by the
Board on April 24, 2021. Page 2 of 3 provided some suggestions as to
where to begin the process. She noted these were just the
Superintendent’s suggestions and stressed that ultimately it was the
Board who would decide the course of action. The key issues were:
1) The hiring process—whether it would be handled internally or
externally; she expressed a personal preference of handling it
internally.
2) The critical attributes for the position of Superintendent. The last job
description they had for the position of Superintendent dated back
to 1990. They also had a list of specification of this position from
2004 when the Board hired a consultant firm. She had also obtained
sample job descriptions from school districts across the country.
3) The application process. In 2001 applications were instructed to
submit applications to the Board Attorney’s Office.
4) The timeline for the process. The deadline for application. The
Superintendent’s last day would be February 3
