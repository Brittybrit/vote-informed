Ms. Rojas recognized Dr. Delia C. Garcia who was formerly Dean of the
College of Education at Florida International University (FIU) and Associate
Professor in the Department of Leadership and Professional Studies. She
served FIU in faculty and administrative capacities for 41 years before retiring
in 2020. Prior to her appointment as Dean in 2010, she served as Interim
Dean and held positions of Chairperson of the Departments of Educational
Leadership and Policy Studies and Leadership and Professional Studies at
FlU.
Dr. Garcia has been a leader in the area of community engagement for the
past 40 years always committed to forging university-school partnerships that
address challenges facing our communities and that focus on problem-
solving. She secured federal, state and foundation grants totaling more than
$16 million, addressing the preparation of teachers in urban schools and
family literacy efforts, Throughout the years, her teacher education projects
generated tuition scholarships for more than 400 teachers in the Miami-Dade
County Public Schools (M-DCPS), providing a means for the completion of
graduate degrees by many educators. Additionally, she led efforts in providing
professional development training to over 1,000 teachers in the district in the
area of TESOL following the State mandate established through the LULAC
Consent decree requiring teachers to be ESOL endorsed. In this effort she
collaborated with M-DCPS in the provision of in-service initiatives.
Through Dr. Garcia's work in the area of family literacy, a model of family
education for linguistically and culturally diverse populations was created. The
Families Learning at School and Home (FLASH) program increased the
participation of linguistically and culturally diverse families in the educational
process of their children by improving their Englishiiteracy skills thus
enhancing their abilities fo work with their children in instructional activities
and assisting them to navigate the educational system in the United States.
The program was recognized by the United States (U.S.) Depariment of
Education as an Academic Excallence project and its model was replicated
nationwide. The program served over 35 local schools in M-DCPS and
reached over 3,000 families in the South Florida community. The project held
additional school sites in the Broward and Monroe County school districts.

During her tenure at FIU, Dr. Garcia secured a five-year $2.8 million grant &-6 cow

Dr. Garcia's scholarly publications address the areas of family involvement,

President of the Bilingual Association of Florida (BAF) for five years, the state
affiliate of the National Association for Bilingual Education (NABE). She
chaired state and district advisory committees focusing on issues of narrowing
the achievement gap in our schools as well as the preparation of adult
educators in the state.
