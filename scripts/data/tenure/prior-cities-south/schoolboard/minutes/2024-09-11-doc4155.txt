Dr. Bendross-Mindingall presented that Girl Power Rocks was a nonprofit organization
aiming to empower at-promise girls and their families. The organization was founded
in 2000 by the World Literacy Crusade (WLC) of Florida, as an international, nonprofit,
community literacy program. Girl Power Rocks has formed partnerships across
Miami-Dade County to provide girls with safe spaces and evidence-based programs
and services. Every year, Girl Power Rocks invites hundreds of at-promise girls and
their families to embark on a nurturing and life-changing journey of empowerment,
academic excellence, and personal growth.
Girl Power Rocks has helped thousands of at-promise girls make measurable
improvements (85% or more) in their academic performance, decision-making,
connections to school and family, conflict resolution, personal growth, and college and
career readiness. As program participants are often transitioning from traumatic
experiences, Girl Power Rocks is steadfast in its commitment to providing safe spaces
where girls can connect in positive ways, and receive emotional and mental health
support and academic tutoring, while learning how to make healthy choices. In this
environment, girls gain positive self-esteem, a sense of purpose, and protection
against psychological distress, substance abuse, delinquency, academic failure, and
school abandonment.
Dr. Bendross-Mindingall also mentioned that Girl Power takes the needs and desires
of every girl seriously, providing programs and activities that create healthy, smart,
strong, and active girls. Girl Power's programs are developed to empower young girls
to get more out of life. Girl Power encourages young girls to build confidence,
competence, and pride within themselves. It also provides workshops and materials
that inform girls about the risks and consequences associated with substance abuse,
low self-esteem, lack of education, and more.
On behalf of the Board of Directors at Girl Power Rocks Inc. Organization, Ms. Iesha
Mannings, Vice Chair thanked M-DCPS for honoring their organization.
Dr. Bendross-Mindingall presented a resolution to the organization for the recognition
of the outstanding contributions and support that Girl Power Rocks Inc. provides to
girls in Miami-Dade County. We express our deepest gratitude for their work and
dedication on behalf of girls who are part of Miami-Dade County Public Schools.
Members of the Board, the Chair, the student advisor and the Superintendent thanked
Dr. Bendross-Mindingall for bringing this item forward to honor Girl Power Rocks Inc.

Ms. Diana Weinbaum stated. “that it was with immense pride that we stand before you
today to celebrate a remarkable partnership between Cognia and the Miami-Dade
County Public Schools a relationship that began in 1914 when Miami Senior High
School first received its accreditation through one of our accrediting bodies, SACS
CASI. One hundred years later, your entire school district was awarded Cognia’s
System Accreditation by not only SACS CASI, but also Cognia’s other accrediting
bodies, NCA CASI and NWAC. With this approach, your entire system is reviewed
once per accreditation cycle. Your whole team receives unified themes across
schools, resulting in coordinated horizontal and vertical progress for all levels of
schools and the district office. Accreditation from Cognia is a prestigious recognition,
valued not only across this country but around the world. With 129 years of experience
evaluating educational systems in 95 countries, Cognia holds its partnerships to the
highest standards, focusing on productive learning environments, equitable resource
allocation, and effective leadership. The journey to Cognia accreditation is no simple
task. It’s not a checklist or a one-time event. It is an ongoing pursuit of continuous
improvement. Miami-Dade County Public Schools embarked on this challenging
journey almost two years ago, engaging in a meticulous external audit of its systems
and practices. Special recognition goes to the incredible leadership and hard work of
your Transformation Office who, along with the entire district team, showed
tremendous dedication to making this accomplishment possible. Through the
Accreditation Review process, the evaluation team reviewed your diagnostics and
evidence for 30 Cognia Performance Standards. Each standard is evaluated using a
rubric rating, and your district received the highest rating on 28 Performance
Standards. The district also received an Index of Education Quality score of 395 out
of 400 possible points. This achievement underscores the student-first approach that
is at the heart of everything this district does, as well as the outstanding collaborative
leadership of the board and district leadership teams. Throughout the evaluation
process, Cognia identified numerous strengths, particularly in the areas of culture,
leadership, engagement, and growth in learning. The dedication to fostering an
environment where every student can realize their fullest potential is evident, as you
cultivate a system-wide culture founded on respect for all stakeholders. By
empowering your community to support the academic and non-academic needs of
the learners, you have created a powerful process to ensure the systems’ values and
vision for the future are supported and communicated effectively. Cognia’s evaluation
team also commended you for the sophisticated and impactful use of data to address
needed improvements and provide support for your areas of success. As you embrace
your district's continuous improvement needs, your leaders have cultivated innovative
practices to hire and retain the best teachers and school leaders to ensure that you
can provide the very best for every student who comes through your door. As proud
representatives of Cognia, the largest non-profit education improvement organization
in the world, it is our distinct honor to present you with your System-wide Accreditation,

efforts of our custodial teams who play such a crucial role in our schools. The Clean
Sweep Awards were created to shine a spotlight on the hard work and dedication of
those who often work behind the scenes to ensure our schools are clean, safe, and
welcoming for students and staff alike. Our custodians are the unsung heroes of the
school system. Every day, they take on the enormous task of maintaining the
cleanliness of our facilities, from classrooms and hallways to cafeterias and
playgrounds. Their efforts directly contribute to creating an environment where
learning can thrive and where students and staff feel safe and supported. We know
that a clean and well-maintained school environment isn’t just about appearances—
it’s about health, safety, and creating the right conditions for learning. When our
schools are clean, students can focus better, teachers can teach more effectively, and
everyone in the school community benefits. To achieve a Clean Sweep Award,
schools must pass a rigorous sanitation audit. This audit requires that at least 95% of
the school’s facilities meet or exceed our stringent cleanliness standards. Many
schools go beyond this threshold, with some achieving scores as high as 100%.

Mr. Roberto Alonso announced that this Board item is the Ethical and responsible use
of the artificial intelligence (AI) for high school students. He recognized that in an
increasingly Artificial Intelligence (AI)-driven world, educating students about Artificial
Intelligence is not merely an option, but a necessity. At its regular meeting of March
13, 2024, the School Board approved Item H-3, “Artificial Intelligence,” proffered by
Vice Chair Monica Colucci, which directed the Superintendent to, among other things,
review School Board policies and procedures related to the use and implementation
of artificial intelligence (AI) and, if necessary, initiate rulemaking proceedings to
amend existing, applicable policies governing the use of AI. This H-3 item requiring a
comprehensive review of existing policies and programs was an important step in
recognizing this growing area of technology and ensuring that school personnel and
students are informed about the safe, ethical, and responsible use of AI in schools.
AI's pervasive influence spans across all sectors, necessitating a comprehensive
understanding of its capabilities and implications for students to navigate this modern
landscape effectively.
The future workforce will undoubtedly be impacted by AI, with many jobs requiring
direct AI expertise or proficiency in utilizing AI-powered tools. Early exposure to AI
concepts and applications equips students with the skills necessary to thrive in this
evolving job market. Additionally, studying AI fosters computational thinking, logical
reasoning, and problem-solving skills – transferable assets applicable across
numerous disciplines. The potential for AI to inspire innovation and fuel creative
solutions underscores its value in preparing students for the challenges and
opportunities of the future.
However, AI education must also encompass the ethical and responsible use of this
technology. Understanding the potential for bias and unfairness in AI systems is
crucial to ensure its equitable application. Safeguarding privacy and security in the
age of AI-driven data collection and analysis is paramount. Students must be
empowered to demand transparency and accountability from AI developers to foster
trust and ensure responsible AI deployment. Furthermore, considering the far-
reaching social and environmental impacts of AI, it is imperative to guide its
development and utilization towards beneficial outcomes for society and the planet.
Even within their current role as students, AI offers transformative potential. AI-
powered tools can personalize learning experiences, catering to individual needs and
optimizing educational outcomes. Research, data analysis, and creative projects can
be enhanced through AI assistance, empowering students to explore complex ideas
and generate innovative solutions. Cultivating critical digital literacy skills enables
students to evaluate information critically and make informed decisions about
technology use in an AI-saturated environment.
The long-term benefits of AI education are undeniable. A solid foundation in AI opens
doors to a plethora of career opportunities across diverse sectors. The ability to adapt
and thrive in a rapidly changing world where AI will continue to play a central role is
invaluable. Ultimately, AI education empowers students to become active participants
in shaping the future of technology and its societal impact, fostering informed decision-
making and advocacy for responsible AI use. The rapid advancement of AI
technologies presents both immense opportunities and potential risks for our
students. AI is already transforming various aspects of our lives; from the way we work
to the way we interact with the world around us. It is imperative that our students are
prepared to navigate this AI-driven landscape ethically and responsibly.
The Hour of Code is a global event where millions of students engage in one hour of
coding, with or without AI and learning how AI works. The Hour of Code is a free
introduction to computer science through fun activities and videos for learners of all
skill levels. Code.org is an internationally recognized coordinator and curriculum
=== PAGE 27 ===
Minutes of the September 11, 2024, Regular School Board meeting
27
provider for computer science and has hosted Hour of Code annually over the last 12
years. First nationally, and now reaching international scale, The Hour of Code is
incorporated in countries accounting for over 45 languages, 100 million students, over
1 billion hours of computer science instruction, and to more than 50 million female
students, accounting for 50 percent of students engaging in coding related activities.
This year’s Creativity with AI Hour of Code will be hosted during the week of December
9-15 and is grounded on the theme of “Creativity with AI.” This year's celebration of
both coding and AI is supported by over 400 partners, 20,000 educators, and 58,000
volunteers.
Integrating AI education into our curriculum through initiatives like The Hour of Code
not only equips students with essential 21st-century skills but also empowers them to
become informed and responsible digital citizens. By fostering a deep understanding
of AI ethics, we can ensure that our students are prepared to harness the power of AI
for good while mitigating its potential negative impacts.

importance of early detection of breast cancer. In 2024, approximately 310,720
women and 2,800 men will be diagnosed with invasive breast cancer.
This information is provided by Breastcancer.org. Donate to support free resources
and programming for people affected by breast cancer in the U.S. along with 51,400
new cases of non-invasive breast cancer.

common cancer diagnosed in women, other than skin cancer. Currently, one out of
eight women in the United States has the chance of developing breast cancer.
Mammograms, regular visits to the doctor, and self-examinations are critical to early
detection, treatment, and improved survival rates. There is a 98 percent chance of
survival if breast cancer is detected early.

Cancer Society’s rallying cry to raise awareness and dollars to fight breast cancer.
Through the leadership of Miami-Dade County, MSABC walkers and supporters can
be proud that their efforts are helping more than an estimated 4 million breast cancer
survivors celebrate another birthday this year.
It is important to raise awareness in our community of breast cancer prevention and
to recognize October as National Breast Cancer Awareness Month throughout Miami-
Dade County Public Schools. To help raise awareness about breast cancer outreach
efforts will be made throughout the District for teachers and support staff.
