infrastructure and implement safety measures near the schools which

measures, and improve signage to increase pedestrian safety.
Furthermore, Walk Safe conducts research to assess the effectiveness of

Dr. Mickey Witte gave an overview of the program’s history dating back to
2002 when the program was discovered. She also thanked Ms. Rojas,
and the Board for the recognition. She explained that they are all about
injury prevention and evidence based. Dr. Witte stated that the director of
the program Dr. Gillian Hotz was tested with decreasing the number of
kids that were ending up in their trauma center at the University of Miami
where we work. They received many children from Miami-Dade County
with head injuries, and sometimes fatal injuries due to being hit by cars
while walking and biking. Dr. Witte pointed out that Dr. Hotz was tasked
to come up with a solution, and that's how the Walk Safe Program was
born. She uttered that thanks to the School Board and their support of the
program where they have the train, the trainer model. She highlighted that
since the School Board has mandated it, they have been able to decrease
the number of kids getting hit by cars in Miami-Dade Count, and while
walking and biking has been decreased by 78 to 80 percent. Dr. Witte
provided some highlights of what is current in this field of evidence-based
injury prevention research. She thanked the Chair and the Board again for
their partnership and advised they will continue to be present in Miami-
Dade County Schools.
