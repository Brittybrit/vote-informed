THE SCHOOL BOARD OF MIAMI-DADE COUNTY, FLORIDA
Miami, Florida
Minutes of the November 14, 2023, School Board Organization Meeting
This being the second Tuesday following the general election in November
2023, the following members of The School Board of Miami-Dade County,
Florida, met for the Organizational Meeting at 10:00 a.m. in the School
Board Auditorium of the School Board Administration Building, 1450
Northeast Second Avenue, Miami, Florida, 33132.
PRESENT AT 10:00 A.M.:
Mr. Roberto Alonso, Member, District 4

Dr. Jose L. Dotres, Superintendent of Schools
Mr. Walter J. Harvey, General Counsel
This being the Organization Meeting at which the Superintendent presides,
Dr. Jose L. Dotres assumed the Chair and called the meeting to order at
10:04 a.m. The Superintendent led those in attendance in The Pledge of
Allegiance to the Flag of the United States of America. The Board then held
a Moment of Silence.
Superintendent Dr. Dotres said that the primary purpose of today’s
Organization Meeting was to elect a School Board Chair and Vice Chair, as
required by Section 1001.371 of the Florida Statutes, which states in part:
“In November of each year, the district school board shall organize
by electing a Chair. In an election year, that date shall coincide with
the requirements of s. 100.041(3)(a). It may elect a Vice Chair, and
the district school superintendent shall act ex officio as the
secretary.”
He then relinquished the floor to General Counsel Walter J. Harvey, who
provided the procedures for the Board to follow in the implementation of
these elections.
Following instructions from General Counsel Walter J. Harvey,
Superintendent Dotres opened the forum to receive nominations for Chair
of The School Board of Miami-Dade County, Florida.
Mr. Daniel Espino nominated Ms. Maria Teresa “Mari Tere” Rojas to serve
as Chair.
Mr. Roberto Alonso also nominated Ms. Maria Teresa “Mari Tere” Rojas to
serve as Chair.

Mr. Daniel Espino nominated Ms. Monica Colucci to serve as Vice Chair of
the School Board. Mr. Espino requested a Point of personal privilege. He
stated that next week he celebrates a year of joining this Board and it's

education in more ways than I could have possibly, possibly imagined. Not
only in terms of what we do as for our students, what we do in this building,
how this Board dynamic works and despite the ups and the downs, the
twists and the turns, it's been my privilege. It's only been my privilege to be
your Vice chair. But today I'm going to nominate Monica Colucci to be vice
chair as we continue the twists and turns of the legislative changes that
teachers have to navigate, and I can think of nobody better with the
classroom experience that she's got to help charter that course for all of us.

SP-1

School Board of Miami-Dade County, Florida, authorize its officers, on
behalf of the Board, to sign and file all necessary formal documents and
affidavits as required by law.
