THE SCHOOL BOARD OF MIAMI-DADE COUNTY, FLORIDA
Miami, Florida
Minutes of the November 17, 2020 School Board Organization Meeting
This being the second Tuesday following the general election in November
2020, the following members of The School Board of Miami-Dade County,
Florida, met in Special Session at 10:00 a.m. in the Board Room of the
School Board Administration Building, 1450 Northeast Second Avenue,
Miami, Florida, 33132.
PRESENT: Ms. Lucia Baez-Geller, Member, District 3
Dr. Dorothy Bendross-Mindingall, Member, District 2
Ms. Christi Fraga, Member, District 5
Dr. Steve Gallon III, Member, District 1
Ms. Perla Tabares Hantman, Member, District 4
Dr. Lubby Navarro, Member, District 7
Dr. Marta Pérez, Member, District 8
Ms. Maria Teresa “Mari Tere” Rojas, Member, District 6
Ms. Luisa Santos, Member, District 9
Mr. Alberto M. Carvalho, Superintendent of Schools
Mr. Walter J. Harvey, School Board Attorney
Ms. Maria Martinez, Student Advisor
Prior to the Board’s annual Organization Meeting, an installation ceremony
was convened for the newly elected and re-elected School Board members.
Following a presentation of colors by members of the Miami-Dade School
Police Honor Guard, and the Pledge of Allegiance, led by Student Advisor
Maria Martinez, a video of the National Anthem was played. The Board and
its audience then held a moment of silent meditation.
During the installation ceremony, a gathering of family and friends of School
Board members witnessed the swearing in of:
Dr. Steve Gallon III, Member, District 1, by Zoey V. Gallon;
Lucia Baez-Geller, Member, District 3, by Bertha Moro;
Luisa Santos, Member, District 9, by Dr. Eduardo J. Padron;
Christi Fraga, Member, District 5, by Dr. Mark B. Rosenberg;
Additionally, the following School Board members who are continuing their
service were recognized: Dr. Dorothy Bendross-Mindingall, District 2; Ms.
Perla Tabares Hantman, District 4; Dr. Lubby Navarro, District 7; Dr. Marta
Pérez, District 8; and Ms. Maria Teresa Rojas, District 6. Former School
Board Members Dr. Lawrence S. Feldman and Dr. Martin Karp were also
recognized, as well as Superintendent Alberto M. Carvalho.
Elected Officials in attendance today were asked to stand and be
recognized.
Following the ceremony, newly elected and current School Board members
were asked to take their seats on the dais.

At 12:29 p.m., the Organization Meeting was convened.
This being the Organization Meeting at which the Superintendent presides,
Mr. Alberto M. Carvalho assumed the chair and called the meeting to order.
Superintendent Carvalho extended his congratulations to the sworn in
members and welcomed the Honorable School Board Members.
Continuing, he said that the primary purpose of today’s Organization
Meeting was to elect a School Board Chair and Vice Chair, as required by
Section 1001.371 of the Florida Statutes, which states in part:
“In November of each year, the district school board shall organize
by electing a chair. In an election year, that date shall coincide with
the requirements of FS. 100.041(3)(a). It may elect a vice chair, and
the district school superintendent shall act ex officio as the
secretary.”
At this time, a motion was entertained to allow School Board Member Dr.
Steve Gallon III to participate in these proceedings remotely, given that his
physical absence presented an extraordinary situation; Dr. Navarro moved,
seconded by Ms. Rojas, and upon vote being taken the same carried 8-0.
[It is noted for the record that Dr. Gallon was away from the dais when this
vote was taken.]
The Chair then relinquished the floor to School Board Attorney Walter J.
Harvey, who provided the procedures for the Board to follow in the
implementation of these elections.
Following instructions from Board Attorney Harvey, Superintendent
Carvalho opened the forum to receive nominations for Chair of The School
Board of Miami-Dade County, Florida.
Ms. Rojas nominated Ms. Perla Tabares Hantman to serve as Chair.
Dr. Navarro nominated Ms. Perla Tabares Hantman to serve as Chair.
Dr. Pérez nominated Dr. Dorothy Bendross-Mindingall to serve as Chair;
however, Dr. Bendross-Mindingall graciously declined the nomination.
Ms. Fraga nominated Ms. Perla Tabares Hantman as Chair.
Dr. Pérez nominated Dr. Lubby Navarro as Chair; however, Dr. Navarro
graciously declined the nomination.
Dr. Gallon nominated Ms. Perla Tabares Hantman as Chair.
Hearing no further nominations, the forum for nominations of Chair was
closed and Superintendent Carvalho called for votes on the nomination of
Chair, with the following results:
Board Members Nominees for Chair
Dr. Pérez voted for: Dr. Bendross Mindingall
Ms. Baez-Geller voted for: Ms. Hantman
Ms. Rojas voted for: Ms. Hantman
Dr. Bendross-Mindingall voted for: Ms. Hantman
Dr. Navarro voted for: Ms. Hantman
Ms. Santos voted for: Ms. Hantman
