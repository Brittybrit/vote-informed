Ms. Lucia Baez-Geller introduced Mr. Jonathan Babika from the Little Lighthouse
Foundation (LLF). She spoke on that the LLF was founded in 2010 by Robert Sena,
along with co-founders Aaron Resnick and Charlie Venturi, The Little Lighthouse
Foundation (LLF) is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that assists
underserved children and their families throughout South Florida. LLF’s goals are to
provide impactful experiences to the youth and to make volunteer opportunities
accessible for those who are ready and willing to give their time. LLF first began with
the Adopt-a-Meal program at Ronald McDonald House Charities (RMHC) where
volunteers serve meals to the families of children receiving treatment at nearby
hospitals. Since then, LLF has grown in strides to launch over 20 proprietary
programs with 33 partner facilities, including, but not limited to, homeless shelters,
hospitals, and youth centers. LLF’s programs include Adopt-a-Meal, Activity Hour,
Story Hour and Move & Groove at Chapman Partnership, Craft Night and Beauty All
Around at Lotus House, and Adopt-a-Floor at Nicklaus Children’s Hospital, among
many more. The organization also holds annual Specialty Programs which serve
hundreds of children and families at a time and bring together hundreds of volunteers.
These include LLF’s Back to School, Children’s Halloween Party, and Holiday Toy
Distribution. This year, under the leadership of Executive Director Jonathan Babicka,
The Little Lighthouse Foundation will celebrate its “Birthday of Service” to benefit
Miami-Dade County Public Schools (M-DCPS), in collaboration with student and
parent volunteers from all Miami Beach Feeder Pattern schools. As one of South
Florida’s top volunteer organizations, LLF aims to continue to expand its reach,
provide volunteer opportunities, and give back to our M-DCPS schools. Today, the
School Board of Miami-Dade County recognizes the countless contributions of The
Little Lighthouse Foundation and appreciates the continued partnership that allows
us to bring more opportunities and resources to our students and families throughout
South Florida.
Mr. Jonathan Babika thanked the Board, Board Chair, Superintendent of Schools and
the audience. He spoke about the events the Little Lighthouse Foundation has done
in the past such as the Halloween party and Holiday Toy distribution where they
distributed 19,000 toys to over 6,000 underserved children.

Miami Dade County Public Schools is at our heart, a community service organization
and as such, we use our resources to work with others to make sure that the quality
of life in Miami Dade is raised. As a result, we have partnered once again with Jackson
Health Systems, the American Heart Association and the American Red Cross and

provide this, we've worked together with our partners to staff each of these locations.

they are our school police, officers, they are municipal firefighters and others that are
certified CPR instructors and are giving up their time to this important day. We have
the capacity across these 10 sites to work with over 3000 Miami-Dade County
residents and we have currently already had registration for over 1000. This is truly a
collaborative effort and so, I would just like to take a moment and thank those of our
staff who really have stepped up. Ms. Brenda Wilder from Comprehensive Health
Services, Cheryl Palmer and Bobby Williams from Life Skills. Our Communications
Office has been fantastic, particularly Carlos Sarmiento and our Region Directors and
of course the 10 principal of the schools that will be opening their doors will be having

www.CPRDaymiami.org to register. We are looking forward to it. We know that 9
times out of 10 a cardiac event does not happen when you're at your doctor's office
or you’re at the hospital, often times it happens at home, it happens at a restaurant
and usually it's going to be one of your loved ones who's affected. Being able to
understand the steps to take to administer lifesaving CPR until help can arrive is very
