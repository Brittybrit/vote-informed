Superintendent Carvalho thanked the Board for coming together for this
Special Board Meeting, which was attempted to be scheduled a couple
of times and postponed for different reasons. Over the past 12 years,
the school system has been periodically reorganized on the basis of
seeking increased efficiencies, streamlining the organization in light of
shifting conditions, and realign the Districts momentum, priorities and
performance a subsequent to the changing conditions of 2020 and
taking into account the forecast of changing trends, and the retirement
and/or departure of certain key individuals within this organization.
Continuing, he said this reorganization attempts to create efficiencies,
protect the long-term fiscal viability of the District, focused on the
singular objective of student achievement, while striving to implement
best practices in terms of realigning key personnel and streamlining
business practices in response to the evolving nature of this work, as
well as fiscal and social conditions. The bulk of this reorganization
focused on the realignment and augmentation of skill set in the area of
data center management and ITS, as a result of finding in previous
audits, interna/external reviews, and in-depth research regarding best
practices in the management of data systems considering the
vulnerability private and public sectors face, specific to data safety and
cyber security. This reorganization also provided for the implementation
of succession management, incorporates a workforce planning model
in ITS, and enhances the Districts technology infrastructure in a
deliberate way. It also consolidates and streamlines functions of offices;
fewer people doing larger and deeper levels of work across the system.
He noted the importance of this in anticipation of fiscal constraints. This
item reflects a savings of $687K. Concluding, he recognized Mr. Dotres
and his staff for their work on this item.
