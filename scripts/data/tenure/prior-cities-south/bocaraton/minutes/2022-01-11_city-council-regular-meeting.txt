 10.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

          A.       Resolution No. 2-2022
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the Three Thousand
                  South Condominium located at 3000 South Ocean Boulevard, a Coastal Construction Control
                  Line (CCCL) variance, from Section 28-1556(3), Code of Ordinances, which variance is required
                  for the construction of a new seawall, and replacement of a pool, pool deck with associated
                  fence, pool equipment and outdoor shower, and beach stairs, (where no structure is permitted
                  seaward of the CCCL without a variance); providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                  (SC-21-03)

        The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Council
        Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to
        speak.

         John Lindgren, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Lindgren began by reviewing
         components of the applicant's request. Aerial photographs were shown of the subject site in relation to
         the CCCL. Pictures of the deteriorating condition of the existing seawall were presented. Mr. Lindgren
         then reviewed the proposed site plan and outlined the justification for rebuilding the seawall in the new
         location. It was noted that the existing pool was in a similar state of disrepair and would also be rebuilt.
         The plan to replace the existing vegetation was summarized. It was emphasized that the existing
         condominium was built prior to establishment of the CCCL and the application met all criteria for granting
         a variance. The Environmental Advisory Board recommended approval with conditions regarding the
         protection of sea turtle nesting activity. The Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval. The
         required notices had been mailed and property signage posted in accordance with the applicable
         statutes. Staff recommends approval. Upon concluding, Mr. Lindgren responded to members' questions.
         Greg Polidora, Senior Environmental Officer, provided additional information about the required sea
         turtle nesting plan.

        The public hearing was opened. Ele Zachariades, on behalf of the applicant, thanked staff for their
        presentation and advised Council that all parties would meet prior to commencement of the building to
        review the sea turtle nesting plans. Ms. Zachariades reiterated that the original construction of the
        seawall was integrated with the structure of the parking garage, necessitating the applicant's request to
        build a new seawall in front of the existing structure. Dr. Don Richardson, environmental consultant for
        the project, was sworn in and provided additional information regarding the vegetation and impact to sea
        life. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                          Member Monica Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 2-2022.

        Members made final comments and shared their position.

                          Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine
                          O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                          Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

 19.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

 Council Member Drucker inquired about the process for Council to add members to the Boca Raton Housing
 Authority (BRHA). The City Attorney provided information. Mayor Singer noted that the topic of a joint meeting
 with the BRHA had been proposed at a prior Council meeting, and Mr. Ahnell shared that the executive director
 and chair of the BRHA were scheduled to give a presentation at an upcoming Workshop. Discussion ensued.
 There was consensus from Members to gather more information from the scheduled presentation and a
 possible joint meeting prior to deciding whether or not to increase the membership.
