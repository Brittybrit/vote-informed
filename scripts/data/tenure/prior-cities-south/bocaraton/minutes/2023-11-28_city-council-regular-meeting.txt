11.    REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A.     Resolution No. 118-2023
                A resolution of the City of Boca Raton amending the "Schedule of Minimum Civil Penalties for
                Violations Enforced Pursuant to Section 9-60, Code of Ordinances" (Resolution No. 163-2018,
                as amended by Resolution No. 68-2022) to provide for a civil penalty for violations of Section
                9-110 (prohibition of the sale or distribution of polystyrene foam products on City property) and
                Section 9-111 (prohibition of balloons and confetti in outdoor areas on City property); and
                update a cross reference to the Code of Ordinances; providing for severability ; providing for
                repealer; providing an effective date

         Jim Bell, Long Range Project Manager, gave the presentation. He briefly reviewed the resolution and
         noted that the prohibitions are already in City Code but that ticketing fines had not yet been adopted .
         Mr. Bell advised that staff recommends approval or the ordinance. Upon concluding, he responded
         to Members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed.

                          Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member
                          Fran Nachlas, to approve Resolution No. 118-2023.

          Deputy Mayor Mayotte made final comments in support of the resolution.

                          Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte,
                          Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council Member
                          Marc Wigder voting yes.

13.     PUBLIC REQUESTS:

Robert DuKate highlighted pedestrian and bicyclist safety regarding the proposed railroad crossing at Jeffery
Street. Mr. DuKate stated that he believed the information submitted on the application to the FOOT was
outdated and encouraged Council Members to take public safety into account.

Harold Chaffee also expressed his concerns regarding the proposed extension of Jeffrey Street and cautioned
Members to consider public safety impacts.

Tony Bazant, retired City of Boca Raton employee, commented on the General Employee pension and the
desire for retirees to have a cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) .

Cil Ciliberti commented on opening Jeffrey Street and encouraged transparency between the City and the
Greater Boca Raton Beach and Park District in the development of the North Park Project.

Jonathan Ounjian expressed his opposition to the new time limit for public comment and provided a copy of
the State Statute regarding affordable housing. Mr. Ounjian then requested that the City Attorney provide
information regarding her legal interpretation of Senate Bill 102.

Lily Perez addressed comments made by her neighbor at a previous Council meeting about her feeding of
pigeons.

 At the request of Mayor Singer, Acting City Manager Brown provided additional information in response to
 comments made.

 Council Member Nachlas stated that she looked forward to the results of the research and shared her support

 branding for the City's 100th anniversary and beyond.
