4.   FY 2026-27 BUDGET DISCUSSION:

     City Manager Mark Sohaney and Deputy City Manager /CFO Jim Zervis explained the purpose of the
     Special Workshop and advised that no decisions would be required today as the budget figures were
     not final, that estimated tax revenue would be received from the County in July, and to keep in mind
     the impact of the proposed November ballot question regarding property tax reform . Michael
     Middleman, Deputy Director for the Office of Management & Budget (0MB) , began the presentation of
     the preliminary FY 2026-2027 Budget. Mr. Middleman noted that the proposed budget maintains the
     current millage rate , and he outlined changes to General Fund revenues, expenditures, staffing, union
     contract impacts, and the projected decline in Citywide revenues. The Capital Improvements Program
     (CIP) was also reviewed , including major infrastructure projects such as wastewater upgrades, railroad
     crossing improvements, municipal facility projects, and Memorial Park enhancements, prompting
     extensive discussion about funding sources. 0MB Director Sharon McGuire and Public Works and
     Engineering Director Zach Bihr provided additional information and clarified that budgeted amounts
     represented spending authority rather than approved or guaranteed expenditures. Additional
     discussion focused on proposed changes to the fire assessment methodology. Staff responded to
     Members' questions about departmental reorganizations , staffing reductions , fire assessment history,
     non-profit funding, Al implementation , and the possibility of creating an independent internal audit
     function. The Workshop concluded with discussion about projected tax revenues , union negotiations,
     grant funding , and future budget conversations. Staff confirmed that further information would be
     provided as the budget process continued.

5.   PUBLIC COMMENT:

     Andrea Levine O'Rourke advised that she understood the difficult decisions to be made and asked that
     funding for public art be kept in the budget.

     Joanna Marie Kaye, Executive Director of the Festival of the Arts, requested advance notice of the
     Council 's decision to support the following year's festival in order to begin planning .

     Brianna Hagquist requested information on how funding is structured between the City and the Greater
     Boca Raton Beach and Park District.
        Mike Liebelson commented on the City's population growth compared to the amount of taxes collected.
        He suggested that additional information be provided for City expenditures and CIPs be planned for
        multiple years.

       John Anderson commented on a number of topics, including the CRA, the Beach and Park District, the
       need for more boat ramps , and The Boca Raton Hotel.

       Jonathon Ounjian encouraged the supporters of Save Boca to look forward and suggested that the City
       focus on revenue growth and not budget cuts.

        Martin Bell spoke about the timing of the City's consideration to purchase property , the need for a new
        City Hall, and supported the consideration of new construction assessments.

       Council Member Jonathan Pearlman cautioned against depleting the City's reserve funds , advocated
       for the protection of taxpayers, and encouraged staff to be mindful of their expenditures.

 6.     ADJOURNMENT:

        The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 3:30 pm.
