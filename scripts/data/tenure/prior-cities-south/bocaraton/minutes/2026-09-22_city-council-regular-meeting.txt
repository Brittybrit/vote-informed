                   An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton , Florida, creating a Public Affairs Department;
                   amending Chapter 2, "Administration," Article IV, "Departments"; Division 1, Section 2-288,
                   "Departments Established ," Code of Ordinances, to include the Public Affairs Department;
                   creating Chapter 2, Article IV, Division 10, "Public Affairs Department"; providing for the
                   composition of the department and the powers , duties, and responsibilities of the Public
                   Affairs Director; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification ;
                   providing an effective date

                   City Manager Mark Sohaney gave a brief overview of City division and department changes
                   over the last year and advised that the Department of Public Affairs was created in order to

                   Anne Marie Connolly then gave a brief PowerPoint presentation . She outlined the

                   herself available for questions.
