3.        PUBLIC HEARING

                  Resolution No. 121-2021
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton equalizing the assessments; levying the same as
                  assessments against benefited properties within the Downtown Special Assessment District, City
                  of Boca Raton, Florida; directing the City Clerk to record assessments in the Improvement Lien
                   Book; establishing priority of lien and payment of principal and interest; providing for severability;
                   providing for repealer; providing an effective date

           Stephen Timberlake, Special Projects Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He provided a brief
           history of the Downtown Special Assessment, and the requirement to fund the debt service and
           administrative costs for the special assessment revenue bonds. Mr. Timberlake noted that the purpose of
           the meeting was to hear and consider any objections to the special assessment. The method of
           calculating the assessment rate was explained and various charts and graphs were presented. Two
           objection letters had been received, and the concerns had been addressed directly with the property
           owners. Upon concluding, Mr. Timberlake made himself available for questions.

           The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, was closed.

                           Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member
                           Andy Thomson, to approve Resolution No. 121-2021. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                           Scott Singer, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                           Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

4.        ADJOURNMENT

The meeting was adjourned without objection at 1:55 PM.
