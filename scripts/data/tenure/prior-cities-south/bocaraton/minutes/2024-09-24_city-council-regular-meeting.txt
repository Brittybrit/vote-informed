         A.      Ordinance No. 5701
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Code of Ordinances,
                 pertaining to carwashes, to: (i) create Article XV, "Supplementary District Regulations," Division
                 1, Section 28-1319, "Carwashes," to reorganize existing carwash regulations and add new
                 regulations for carwash operations; (ii) amend Article XI , "Business and Commercial Districts,"
                 Division 3, "R-B-1 Motel-Business District," Section 28-743, "Conditional Uses" to allow
                 "carwash" as a conditional use in the R-B-1 Motel-Business District, subject to certain
                 requirements and limitations; (iii) amend the B-4, LIRP and M-1 Zoning Districts to update
                 terminology, replacing "Car Laundries" and "Auto Laundries" with "Carwashes"; and (iv) amend
                 Article XVI, "Off-Street Parking and Loading," Section 28-1655, "Required Off-Street Parking"
                 to add parking requirements for carwashes; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                 providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-23-11)

         Senior Planner Heather Hansen gave the PowerPoint presentation. She provided an overview of the
         proposed text amendments, a history of the property, and details of the new regulations. She advised
         that staff recommended approval and upon concluding, made herself available for questions.

         The public comment period was opened. Jonathan Ounjian advised that he supported the proposed
         text amendment and urged Council to approve the ordinance. Attorney Ele Zachariades, on behalf of
         her client, advised that she would have a presentation available for the second hearing of this
         ordinance. Seeing no one else come forward, the public comment period was closed.

         The Mayor advised that the next hearing for this ordinance would be on October 22, 2024.

         C.       Ordinance No. 5705
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 2, "Administration," Article 111,
                 "Boards, Committees, Commissions and Special Magistrates," Division 3, "Special
                 Magistrates," Code of Ordinances, to delegate authority to the Special Magistrate to consider
                 and act upon applications for code compliance lien reductions and to establish procedures for
                 lien reduction requests; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective
                 date (AM-24-09)

         Deputy Director of Development Services Erin Sita gave the PowerPoint presentation. She gave an
         overview of the Code Compliance process, the proposed amendments, and advised that staff
         recommended approval. Upon concluding, she made herself available for questions. Discussion
         ensued . Development Services Director Brandon Schaad answered Members' question and provided
         additional information.

         The public comment period was opened. Jonathan Ounjian spoke against the handling of the Dan burg
         project. Chris DeSilva addressed random topics. Seeing no one else come forward, the public
         comment period was closed.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                         Yvette Drucker, to approve Ordinance No. 5705.

         Extended discussion ensued. Council Member Thomson advised that the proposed changes to
         procedure were in line with those of neighboring municipalities. Mayor Singer and Deputy Mayor
         Drucker agreed that appeals should be handled at the Special Magistrate level but that the current
         process could be revised . Council Member Wigder shared his opposition to applications returning to
         the original magistrate for appeal hearing, and Council Member Nachlas expressed concerns with the
         process and advised that she would support postponing the decision to a future date. Mr. Schaad and
         Ms. Sita provided additional information and clarification. There was consensus to postpone the public
         hearing to November 19, 2024.
