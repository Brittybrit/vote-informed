 11.:.   QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

           A.         Resolution No. 1-2020
                     A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a site plan to authorize the construction of an
                     approximately 22,500 square foot restaurant building, together with a technical deviation from the
                     2002 Palm Beach County Unified Land Development Code Table 7.2-1 to reduce the number of
                     required parking spaces, and a technical deviation from the 2002 Palm Beach County Unified Land
                     Development Code Section 7.2.D.6 to reduce the required length of a loading space, for the Plaza
                     located at 5355 Town Center Road; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-18-09)

         The City Attorney reviewed the Quasi-Judicial procedures. Council Members made their ex parte
         disclosures. The Deputy City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

         Tamashbeen Rahman, Chief Planner, gave a brief PowerPoint presentation. The location of the project
         was identified, and elements of the site design were provided. Landscape buffers and traffic considerations
         were discussed. Technical deviations related to parking spaces and loading zone dimensions were
         reviewed. The Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval with conditions. Development Services
         staff recommended approval with conditions included in the resolution. Upon concluding, Ms. Rahman
         made herself available for members' questions.

        The public hearing was opened. Michael Marshall, on behalf of the applicant, asked for clarification of the
        proposed conditions regarding reservation of parking spaces. Development Services Director Brandon
        Schaad and City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser provided additional information. Mr. Marshall responded to
        statements made by staff. Discussion ensued.

The meeting was recessed at approximately 6:25 pm and reconvened at 6:28 pm.

        Mr. Marshall stated he had resolved the question regarding parking. He highlighted several components of
        the project, including pedestrian connections and the addition of new sidewalks, and asked Council to
        support the resolution. Mr. Marshall then responded to Council members' questions. Glenn Gromann spoke
        in support of the project. There was no cross-examination or rebuttal. Seeing no one else come forward,
        the public hearing was closed.
