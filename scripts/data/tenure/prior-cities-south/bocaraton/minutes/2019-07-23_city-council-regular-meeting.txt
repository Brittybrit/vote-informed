 17.     QUASI-JUDICIAL PUBLIC HEARINGSNARIANCES & APPEALS:

                   Resolution No. 78-2019
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering a Coastal Construction
                  Control Line (CCCL) variance, from Section 28-1556(3), Code of Ordinances, which variance is
                  required for the construction of a four-story, approximately 48 feet tall, 8,666 square foot single
                  family residence seaward of the CCCL (where no structure is permitted seaward of the CCCL
                  without a variance), for a residential (R-3-F) zoned property located at 2500 North Ocean
                  Boulevard; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-12-06)

         The City Attorney read the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the proceedings. Members made
         ex-parte disclosures. The Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation. City Code provisions
         and variance criteria were reviewed. Elevations of the proposed building and site plan were presented. A
         summary of environmental issues was discussed, and analysis of the applicant's response to variance
         criteria was provided. Mr. Schaad noted that the project is inconsistent with the City's Comprehensive Plan.
         Staff recommends denial of the request for variance. The Environmental Advisory Board (EAB)
         recommended denial. Mr. Schaad then reviewed staff's responses to the applicant's presentation at the
         April 10, 2019 EAB meeting. Upon concluding, he made himself available for members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Neil Schiller made a PowerPoint presentation on behalf of the applicant.
         Mr. Schiller requested that three members recuse themselves from voting on the agenda item. Mayor
         Singer, Council Members O'Rourke and Mayotte responded to Mr. Schiller's comments and declined to
         recuse themselves. Mr. Schiller then asked that the portion of Mr. Schaad's presentation regarding sea
         turtles be stricken from the record. Jane Herndon, former Deputy Director of the Florida Department of

Regular Minutes                                          8                                                July 23, 2019
         Environmental Protection, testified as to the department's permitting process. Bob Tuthill, architect of the
         project, reviewed the design of the proposed building, as well as changes that had been made to the original
         plans. Don Richardson, owner of Ecological Consultants, commented on the position of the house in
         respect to the vegetation, dunes and sea turtle nesting habitat. Mr. Richardson also addressed lighting,
         window coverings and reflectivity. Mr. Schiller commented on statements made by staff and consultants.
         Variance criteria were reviewed. Mike Jenkins from ATM consulting, consulting counsel Nancy Stroud, and
         Brandon Schaad responded to statements made by the applicant. Michael Laszlo, Helen Kaye, Gil Vizner,
         Lisa Salvati, Tom Slazinski, Bruce Diller Verstandig, Jessica Gray and David Sergi spoke in opposition to
         the resolution. Richard DeWitt, on behalf of the applicant, cross examined Mr. Jenkins regarding his report.
         Mr. Schiller offered his rebuttal to comments made by the public. Seeing no one else coming forward to
         speak, the public hearing was closed.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 78-2019 to deny granting the variance.

          Members made final comments.

                  Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member
                  Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting
                  yes.
