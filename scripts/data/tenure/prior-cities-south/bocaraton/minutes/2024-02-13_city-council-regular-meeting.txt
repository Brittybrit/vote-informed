          A.       Ordinance No. 5680

                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the City' s Comprehensive Plan by amending
                  Future Land Use Element Policies LU. 1. 1. 5, LU. 1. 1. 6 and LU. 1. 1. 10, and amending housing
                  element objective HO. 1. 5. 0 and policy HO. 1. 5. 3 to establish a framework for the affordable
                  housing provisions of Subsections 166. 04151( 6) and ( 7), Florida Statutes; providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date ( SC- 23- 14)

          Mayor Singer noted that the ordinance had been transmitted to the State in December and that it had
          been re -introduced on January 8, 2024. The Mayor then advised that this was the second of two public
          hearings, and that four votes would be required for adoption.


          Development Services Director Brandon Schaad gave the PowerPoint presentation. He provided a
          background of the proposed amendments to the Comprehensive Plan and the revisions made since
          the transmittal. He advised that both the Planning and Zoning Board and staff recommended approval.
          Upon concluding, he made himself available for questions.

          The public hearing was opened. Jonathan Ounjian stated that he agreed with the Treasure Coast
          Regional Planning' s opinion and shared that he was against the proposed restrictions imposed by this
          ordinance. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

          B.      Ordinance       No. 5684

                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton establishing procedures and regulations pursuant to
                  Section 166. 04151( 6), Florida Statutes, for development of affordable housing ( referred to as
                   Commercial Industrial Multifamily Development' or " CIMD"), including workforce housing;
                  establishing eligibility requirements, development standards, and approval procedures
                  applicable to such development, ( including reduced parking requirements, where applicable);
                  adding CIMD as a permitted use in the following zoning districts ( where the Future Land Use
                  Designation is Planned Mobility): R- B- 1 ( Motel Business), B- 2 ( Community Business), MC
                   Medical Center), POI ( Professional, Office, and Institutional), City CG ( Commercial General
                  City),     CHO ( Commercial            High   Office),   LIRP (    Light    Industrial   Research    Park),    M- 3
                   Manufacturing Industrial) and W-1 ( Warehouse District); providing for severability; providing
                  for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date ( AM-23- 10)

         Development Services Director Brandon Schaad gave the PowerPoint presentation. He provided an
         overview of the proposed ordinance and noted that it had been revised to remove redundant or
         duplicative language. Mr. Schaad then outlined the changes proposed by Council Member Nachlas
         and those recommended by Council Member Wigder. He advised that staff recommended approval
         with the revisions as proposed by staff and Council Member Nachlas on January 23, 2024, and that
         the Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval of the ordinance prior to the January 23rd
         revisions. Upon concluding, he responded to Members' questions. Mayor Singer shared his position
         on the proposed changes and stated that he was not inclined to support the revisions proposed by
          Council Member Nachlas. Discussion ensued. At the request of Ms. Nachlas, City Attorney Diana
          Grub Frieser provided information regarding the Bert Harris Private Property Rights Protection Act.

          The public hearing was opened. Ele Zachariades spoke in favor of the ordinance and the proposed
          changes and responded to Members' questions. Malcolm Butters shared that he was not in favor of
          this ordinance as presented and would like the proposed retail requirement removed. Juan Caycedo
          spoke in favor of passing the ordinance but without the retail condition. Lon Tabatchnik spoke against
          the ordinance as proposed, supporting the original version of the ordinance, and suggested that
          restaurant/ retail condition       should   not be included.     David    Abers    spoke   in favor of the ordinance   and
          urged Council to support it. Jonathan Ounjian spoke in favor of the ordinance as proposed but
          expressed concerns regarding the parking. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was
          closed. Discussion           ensued.


                               Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council Member
                               Yvette Drucker, to approve Ordinance No. 5684 with the proposed revisions dated
                               February 8, 2024.

 The meeting was recessed at 7: 26 PM and resumed at 7:41 PM.

          Council Member Nachlas proposed that her amendment of a retail/ restaurant condition for accessory
          use be reduced to 2, 000 square feet from 5, 000, and that a waiver of the parking requirements still be
          included. Ms. Frieser clarified the amendment and read proposed language into the record on page
          9, subsection ( 2)( b). Mr. Schaad confirmed that the retail or restaurant use did not need to be located
          within the residential building, as long as it was on -site on the ground floor. At the request of Ms.
          Nachlas,    Lon Tabatchnik         shared   that he was in favor of the revised            amendment.   Council   Member
          Wigder expressed his support of the revision, including his amendment on lines 15- 17 from page 9.
          Ms. Frieser      clarified    the amendment.
