                 medical office, and retail use; superseding Resolution No. 163-2009; for property located at 5099
                 North Federal Highway: providing for severability: providing for repealer: providing an effective
                 date (UC-16-07)
                         Ordinance No. 5375


Regular Meeting Minutes                                4                                                January 24, 2017
         Tamash Rahman, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The petitioner's request was
         reviewed. The location and zoning of the property were provided, as was background information on the
         various legislation that amended the permitted uses on the site since 1981. Ms. Rahman noted that the
         Planning and Zoning Board unanimously recommended approval of the petition. Staff recommends
         approval as well. Ms. Rahman made herself available for questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Ele Zachariades, Dunay Miskel on behalf of the petitioner, made brief
         comments in support of the approval of the ordinance and responded to questions from Council
         members. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Michael
                  Mu/laugh, to approve Ordinance No. 5375. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie,
                  Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott
                  Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes.
