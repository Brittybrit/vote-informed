12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A        Ordinance No. 5450
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 23 "Planning and Development,"
                  Article IV, Division 5, Section 23-190, "Driveways and Off-Street Parking Facilities other than
                  Single-Family and Duplex," Code of Ordinances, amending provisions relating to required vehicle
                  reservoirs for gated developments: clarifying that Section 23-190 does not apply to single-family
                  and duplex residential plots; providing for severability: providing for repealer: providing for
                  codification; providing an effective date (AM-17-13)
                           Ordinance No. 5450

         Andrew Capra, GIS Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Background information was provided. A
         handout showing suggested changes to the proposed amended language was distributed. Staff
         recommends approval of the amendment. Mr. Capra noted that the Planning and Zoning Board
         recommended approval with a minor change. In response to a question regarding communities with
         more than one entry, Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, provided a handout containing
         additional language that addressed that circumstance and explained same. Maria Tejera, Traffic
         Engineer, responded to questions as well.

         The public hearing was opened. JoAnn Landon inquired about gated communities with two-lane entries.
         Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

         For clarity, the City Attorney suggested adding additional language to the table outlining the requirements
         for reservoir length.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers,
                  to approve Ordinance No. 5450 as amended. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie,
                  Deputy Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers voting yes.

                  Ordinance No. 5451
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 25, "Streets and Sidewalks," Article II,
                  "Vacation and Abandonment of Streets and Easements," Code of Ordinances, amending and
                   updating procedures for the vacation and abandonment of rights-of-way and special purpose
                  easements, by deleting, revising and/or restating portions of Sections 25-31 - 25-40, and by
                  creating Sections 25-41 and 25-42, Code of Ordinances: providing procedures for the vacation
                  and abandonment of required rights-of-way and special purpose easements in conjunction with a
                  site plan approval; providing procedures for the administrative abandonment of non-required
                   rights-of-way and special purpose easements: and providing procedures for the vacation and
                  abandonment of rights-of-way and special purpose easements pursuant to a replat: providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer: providing for codification: providing an effective date (AM-16-
                  Ql)
                           Ordinance No. 5451

         Lynn Bodor, Property Specialist Appraiser, gave the PowerPoint presentation, noting that this was a
         streamlining effort. Background information was provided, including the meaning of paper easements.
         The changes to the methods of abandonment were summarized. Ms. Bodor provided details of a number
         of past abandonments that would have been done administratively had this code change been in place.
         She then responded questions.

         The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Deputy Mayor Scott Singer,
                  to approve Ordinance No. 5451.

             Members made final comments.

                  Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member
                  Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers
                  voting yes.

                            Resolution No. 60-2018

         The Mayor commented on the pre-emption of home rule.

         come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                        Member Monica Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 60-2018.
