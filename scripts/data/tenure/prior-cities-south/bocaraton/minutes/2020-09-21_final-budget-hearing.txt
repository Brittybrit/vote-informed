 3.     REBUDGETING OF FY 2019 - 2020 BUDGET APPROPRIATIONS

City Manager Leif Ahnell gave a PowerPoint presentation on the 2020-2021 budget. Mr. Ahnell reviewed the
mission, vision, and guiding principles of the City and discussed the priorities under each . Significant challenges
and opportunities were outlined . Mr. Ahnell advised that the millage rate would be marginally lower than that of the
previous year and noted that the City's assessed property values continued to rise . Changes to the general fund
operating costs from the previous year were highlighted. Mr. Ahnell then reviewed the tax bill flyer and emphasized
the comparison to other cities, noting that the City's property tax rate continues to be below that of surrounding
municipalities. The fire assessment fee and water and sewer rates remain unchanged, and sanitation rates would
go up slightly due to keeping those services in-house. Sharon McGuire, Office of Management and Budget Director,
reviewed the operating re-budgets. Ms. McGuire explained that these were due to projects across multiple City
departments that had not reached completion within the previous fiscal year. Upon concluding, Mr. Ahnell and Ms.
McGuire responded to members' questions.
