1b      REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A.       Ordinance No. 5469
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton creating Article X, Chapter 17, Code of Ordinances;
                  establishing fertilizer use regulations pursuant to the requirements of Florida Statutes; providing
                  for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date
                          Ordinance No. 5469

         Mo Morel, City Civil Engineer, gave a PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Morel discussed impacts of excessive
         nutrients on bodies of water and the resulting requirement of local governments to adopt a fertilizer
         ordinance. The provisions of the ordinance were outlined, including regulating the proper use of fertilizer,
         training and licensing requirements, and establishing fertilizer free zones. Mr. Morel responded to
         questions and comments by members.

         The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed.

                           Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                           Jeremy Rodgers, to approve Ordinance No. 5469. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                           Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                           Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
