I.      Welcome and Introductions - Mayor Scott Singer and Chairman Bob Rollins

        Mayor Scott Singer called the meeting to order at 5:30 pm . The participants introduced themselves.
II.     Discussion Topics

        A.      Ocean Breeze Golf Course Update

        Commissioner Robert Rollins, Chairman of the Greater Boca Raton Beach & Park District (District)
        reviewed the District's interview process for consultants/architects being considered for the Ocean Breeze
        Golf Course. Details were provided. After narrowing the proposers to a short list of five consultants, Nick
        Price/Tom Fazio II was selected. He noted that the next step was to draft a consulting agreement and
        then reviewed the anticipated design and permit timeline. Discussion followed; members of both bodies
        offered comments. Chairman Rollins confirmed that several public input meetings would be scheduled
        prior to finalizing the golf course design.

Ill.    Public Comment

        The floor was opened for public comment. Kevin Wrenne inquired whether any of the proceeds from the
        sale of the Municipal Golf Course West would go towards the development of the Ocean Breeze golf
        course property. Joel Buoy, on behalf of the Boca Golf Association, thanked the District for their work in
        choosing the golf course architect and for listening to the Association's input. Robert DuKate and Harold
        Chafee offered their thanks for the District's acquisition of the golf course while expressing concerns with
        the effects a hotel would have on the Boca Teeca neighborhood, increased traffic, and the profitability of
        the course. Michelle Grau asked about the construction costs and costs of RFP process. Chairman
        Rollins provided information. David Sergi commented on the profitability of the Red Reef Golf Course.
        Both Chairman Rollins and City Manager Leif Ahnell provided responses. other members commented.
        Barry Tetrault asked whether the City Council would agree to help fund the construction of the course.
        Jessica Gray asked about the possibility of acquiring the properties located at 2500 and 2600 N. Ocean
         Boulevard. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public comment period was closed.

         Chairman Rollins provided further information on the financing of acquisition and development of the golf
         course. City Manager Ahnell, Commissioner Rollins and City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser provided
         additional information on the 2500 and 2600 N. Ocean Boulevard properties. Discussion ensued on a
         number of topics including , but not limited to, financing , current and future projects, a master interlocal
         agreement, and the need for more regular joint meetings. Chairman Rollins thanked the members of
         Boca Golf Association and residents of Boca Teeca for their support and input.
 IV.     Adjournment

         Mayor Singer thanked everyone for their input. The meeting was adjourned without objection at
         approximately 6:42 pm.
