                       Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

       B.      City Manager Executive Performance Review

       Mr. Brown advised that staff had helped make his first five months effective, provided highlights, and
       noted items that could be improved on.

       Council Member Wigder advised that he was pleased with Mr. Brown's performance and appreciated
       all the hard work he had put in.

       Council Member Nachlas thanked Mr. Brown for his hard work and stated her continued support.

       Council Member Thomson thanked Mr. Brown and advised that he appreciates his dedication and
       hard work.

       Deputy Mayor Drucker highlighted Mr. Browns efforts and asked that he continue with the improved
       communication.

       Mayor Singer agreed with Mr. Brown that he could benefit by delegating more and encouraged him to
       continue on the current path.

18.   CITY ATTORNEY REPORTS:

       A.      City Attorney Executive Performance Review

       Council Member Nachlas thanked Ms. Frieser for her hard work and years of service.

       Council Member Thomson thanked Ms. Frieser for her continued efforts and advised that he would
       expound more at the next Council meeting.

       Deputy Mayor Drucker thanked Ms. Frieser for her service and stated that she looked forward to
       communication improvements.

       Mayor Singer advised that communication had improved, however, he believed the mitigation of harm
       to the City could be enhanced, and that Mr. Frieser's impending retirement and subsequent transition
       to a new City Attorney should be considered. He concluded by thanking Ms. Frieser for her service.

       Council Member Wigder advised there was a need for extended discussion regarding the transition to
       a new City Attorney, whether it be an internal or external hire.

       Ms. Frieser advised that she appreciated both the compliments and constructive comments and was
       very proud of her 25 years of service to the City. She advised that she would work with the Mayor and
       Council on a smooth transition when the time comes.
