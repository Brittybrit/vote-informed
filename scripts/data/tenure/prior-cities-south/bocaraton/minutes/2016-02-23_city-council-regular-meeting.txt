17.    QUASI-JUDICIAL PUBLIC HEARINGSNARIANCES & APPEALS:

                Resolution No. 20-2016
                A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the appeal by Taxi USA of
                Palm Beach, LLC d/b/a Taxi Taxi of the decision of the Hearing Officer denying an amendment to
                an existing Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity for an additional 30 taxicabs;
                providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                         Resolution No. 20-2016
                         Taxi USA Appeal Documents Part I
                         Taxi USA Appeal Documents Part II

       The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
       made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

       Jenny Wright, Senior Code Compliance Officer, gave the PowerPoint presentation, beginning with a brief
       review of process to obtain a Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity. A history of the Taxi Taxi
       application for an additional 30 taxicabs and the subsequent denial was provided. In conclusion, Ms.
       Wright reviewed the actions available to the Council as they consider this appeal. She then made herself
       available for questions.

       The public hearing was opened. Appellant Michael Solomon, Taxi Taxi, presented his case for granting
       the appeal thereby allowing his company to add 30 taxicabs that could not only drop off but also pick up
       passengers in Boca Raton. He addressed the population growth that could support more taxicabs, as
       well as the increase in illegal operators, and transportation network companies such as Uber and Lyft.




             Regular Minutes                             4                              February 23, 2016
        Mr. Solomon then countered a number of points made by the Hearing Officer in his Final Order. In
        conclusion, he requested that Council reverse the decision of the Hearing Officer.

        Neil Schiller, presenting on behalf of Yellow Cab, gave a PowerPoint presentation challenging the
        application of Taxi Taxi and supporting the decision of the Hearing Officer. Mr. Schiller argued that Taxi
        Taxi did not prove the need for additional taxi service in Boca Raton. Upon concluding, he responded to
        questions from Council members.

        Brock Rosayn, Metro Taxi, gave a presentation in support of the decision of the hearing officer. He
        maintained that there is not a need for additional taxi service in Boca Raton. James Parks, A1A
        Limousine Service, read into the record a letter from the company's president in support of the addition of
        30 taxicabs. Jerry Goldman, Personal Limousine Service, spoke in support of the additional taxicabs
        requested by Taxi Taxi. Ray Garcia, Prestige Limousine, requested that Council reverse the decision of
        the Hearing Officer. No one else came forward to speak. There was no cross examination. The
        appellant, Michael Solomon, offered comments in rebuttal and in support of the appeal. He then
        responded to questions from Council members. The public hearing was then closed.

                      Motion was made by Council Member Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Jeremy
                      Rodgers, to approve Resolution No. 20-2016 affirming the decision of the Hearing Officer.

        Members made comments, asked questions, and offered their positions. The Mayor provided information
        on what may be happening at the state level regarding vehicles for hire.

                      Motion carried 3 to 2; Deputy Mayor Robert S. Weinroth, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers,
                      Council Member Scott Singer voting yes; Mayor Susan Haynie, Council Member Michael
                      Mu/laugh voting no.
