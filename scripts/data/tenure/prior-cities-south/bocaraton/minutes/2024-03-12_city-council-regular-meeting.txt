           A.     Boca Raton Housing Authority

          John Scannell and Summer Faerman interviewed and responded to Member's questions.

         that the board was not going in the direction she thought was productive. Seeing no one else come

11 .    REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:


         A.      Ordinance No. 5689
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton providing for the vacation and abandonment of a
                 utility easement, approving and accepting a replacement utility easement deed, for the
                 property generally located at 215 SE Spanish Trail, as more specifically described herein;
                 providing conditions for vacation and abandonment; providing for severability; providing for
                 repealer; providing an effective date (EA-23-05)

         Development Services Deputy Director Erin Sita gave the PowerPoint presentation and provided a
         brief overview of the relocation of the utility easement. She advised that staff recommended
         approval and, upon concluding, responded to Members' questions.

         The public comment period was opened. David Milledge spoke on behalf of the applicant. He stated
         that the relocation of the sewer easement was completed, already benefitting drainage in the area
         and they are waiting for approval of this ordinance to get the final inspection. Seeing no one else
         come forward, public comment period was closed.

         Mayor Singer added that he had spoken with City Manager George Brown regarding staff approval
         for these types of easements, and Mr. Brown advised that staff was looking into streamlining
         processes so items such as this could be approved administratively.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                          Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5689. Motion carried unanimously;
                          Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte, Council Member Yvette
                          Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

 19.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

 Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte gave a PowerPoint presentation regarding items remaining on her to-do list.
 Ms. Mayotte encouraged innovation and productivity and provided a number of ideas to promote creativity
 in City projects.

 Council Member Fran Nachlas congratulated Ms. Drucker and the City on receiving Safe Streets awards
 and asked for an update on the Palmetto Park Road Bridge construction. Ms. Nachlas then proposed
 postponement of an upcoming agenda item, Ordinance No. 5683, scheduled for the next Council meeting.
 Discussion ensued. Consensus was made to postpone the public hearing for the ordinance until further
 data could be provided for consideration. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser advised that the motion was not
 required to be made for a date certain. Mr. Brown provided additional information.

                           Motion was made by Council Member Fran Nachlas, seconded by Council Member
                           Marc Wigder, to postpone Ordinance No. 5683 from the March 26, 2024, meeting
                           to a future date. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.
