          Q.      Resolution No. 91-2019
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton encouraging the Florida Public Service Commission ("PSC")
                  to set meaningful energy efficiency goals, to meet those goals through programs targeted to
                  residential and commercial customers with a focus on low to moderate income households;
                  providing for transmittal of the Resolution to the Chair and Clerk of the PSC and filing in relevant
                  PSC dockets; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

        Council Member Mayotte, who requested the resolution, gave a PowerPoint presentation and asked for
        members' support.

        The public hearing was opened. Sophia Eccleston and Dr. Steve Sim, on behalf of FPL, expressed their
        concerns with language in the resolution. Dr. Sim and Ms. Eccleston then responded to members'
        questions. Discussion ensued. The City Attorney provided additional information and addressed the legality
        of the resolution language. George Cavros commented in support of the resolution. Alan Feinberg voiced
        his support of FPL. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

        Members commented on remarks made during public comment. Deputy Mayor Rodgers handed out the
        resolution passed by the County Commission. Discussion ensued. Members proposed additional staff
        analysis; the City Manager provided additional information. Further discussion ensued.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council member
                          Andrea Levine O'Rourke motion to approve resolution as amended.

                          Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andy
                          Thomson, to table Resolution No. 91-2019.

         Members made final comments

                          Motion to table carried on a 3 to 2 vote; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                          Rodgers, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes. Council Member Monica Mayotte
                          and Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke voting no.

           6-      Ordinance No. 5494
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Article Ill, Chapter 12, Section 12-87, Code of
                  Ordinances, of the General Employees' Pension Plan allowing elected General Employee Trustees
                  to participate in the Deferred Retirement Option Plan while serving as trustee; providing for

                  O'Rourke, to approve Ordinance No. 5494. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,
                  Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

           12-     Ordinance No. 5495
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton authorizing an eighth amendment to the Agreement for
                  Purchase and Sale dated November 27, 2017, by and between the City of Boca Raton and G. L.
                  Acquisitions Corporation for the purchase and sale of the City of Boca Raton Municipal Golf Course
                  West, as authorized by Ordinance No. 541 O; authorizing an increase of the purchase price, an
                  increase of the total deposit, an eighth extension of the inspection period, a modification of the
                  closing date, potential additional modifications of the closing date and the purchase price, and an
                  increase of the maximum amount of the liquidated damages; providing for severability; providing
                  for repealer; providing an effective date

          Deputy City Manager George Brown provided a brief description of the ordinance. The current contract was
          reviewed, and modifications of the sale were explained. Effects of the amendment were discussed.
          The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed. Members commented.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Monica
                  Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5495. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy
                  Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
