11.   QUASI-JUDICIAL A ND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

       A.      Mutual of America Residential - 1150 NW Broken Sound Parkway

               1.       Resolution No. 14-2025
                        A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the approximately 11.68-acre
                        property generally located at 1150 Northwest Broken Sound Boulevard, an
                        amendment to an approved site plan (Planning and Zoning Board Resolution No.
                        2024-001) for a commercial industrial multifamily development to authorize the
                        construction of a new seven (?)-story, 287-unit, approximately 355,055 square foot
                        multi-family residential building, including 10 percent affordable housing and 5
                        percent workforce housing units, and 2,000 square feet of retail space

              The City Attorney read the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing.
              Members made their ex-parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who
              wished to speak.

               Planner Jacob German gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began by giving a background
               of the property, the proposed amendments, site plans, and elevations. The project complies
               with all applicable Code requirements. Mr. German advised that the Planning and Zoning
               Board and staff recommend approval. Upon concluding, he made himself available for
               questions.

               David Milledge, attorney for the applicant, answered Members' questions. Development
               Services Director Brandon Schaad added clarification and answered Members' questions.
               Bonnie Miskel stated that there was no issue and agreed to the revisions made.

               The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council Member
                        Fran Nachlas, to approve Resolution No. 14-2025 with the declaration of covenants
                        and restrictions as revised February 10, 2025. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                        Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council
                        Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

       B.      Office Depot Mixed Use - 6600 North Military Trail

               1.       Resolution No. 18-2025
                        A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the approximately 28.89-acre
                        property generally located at 6600 North Military Trail, an amendment to an approved
                        site plan (Planning and Zoning Board Resolution No. 08-20) for a Commercial
                        Industrial Multifamily Development (CIMO), construction of which will be in four
                        phases, to include: in Phase I, the demolition of 245,983 square feet of office; in
                        Phase II, the construction of 42,840 square feet of commercial retail and restaurant
                        use; in Phase 111, the construction of a 36,396 square foot fitness and wellness
                        facility; and Phase IV, the construction of an eight (8)-story, 500-unit multi-family
                        residential building, inclusive of 10% affordable housing units (with all non-residential
                        uses expressly conditioned upon the timely completion of Phase IV), and related
                        site improvements including the installation of walkways and landscaping; together
                        with a technical deviation from Section 28-1655, Code of Ordinances, to temporarily
                        reduce the number of required parking spaces from 1,166 spaces to 1,160 spaces

                         (a reduction of 6 spaces during Phases I, II and Ill); considering the abandonment of
                         multiple portions [two (2) 20-foot and one (1) 32-foot] of a utility easement; providing
                         for repealer; providing an effective date. (SPA-06-08/CIMD)

              The City Attorney confirmed the quasi-judicial procedures were still in effect which would
              govern the public hearing. Members made their ex-parte disclosures. The City Clerk
              administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

              Planner Heather Waldstein gave the PowerPoint presentation. She gave a brief history of the
              project, technical deviations, phased site plan, multimodal plans, and site plan elevations.

              available for questions. Discussion ensued. Ms. Waldstein provided clarity and answered
              Members' questions.

              Milledge, provided clarification by reading into the record revisions to proposed conditions.

              and urged Council's approval. David Milledge made final comments and thanked the Council
              for their consideration. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

                        Fran Nachlas, Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting

12.   REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

       A.      BRIC FLUM Amendment - 5000 T-Rex Avenue

               1.        Ordinance No. 5719
                        An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the Future Land Use Map of the
                        Comprehensive Plan to redesignate the approximately 124.21-acre Boca Raton
                        Innovation Campus (BRIC) property, generally located at 5000 T-Rex Avenue, from
                        Planned Mobility (PM) to Enhanced Mobility (EM); providing for revisions to the Future
                        Land Use Map; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-24-05)

               The Mayor advised that this was the second of two public hearings and that four votes would
               be required for approval.

               Senior Planner Peter Begovich gave the PowerPoint presentation. He provided background,
               reviewed the FLUM amendment and gave staff analysis. He advised that the Planning and
               Zoning Board and staff recommend approval. Upon concluding, he made himself available
               for questions.

               The public comment period was opened. Paul Polcyn spoke against the project and advised
               that his community HOA had been neglected by the potential adjacent development. Richard
               DeWitt, attorney for property owners at Telecom 1 & 2, cited concerns with spot zoning and
               requested this item be tabled for revision or denied. Leslie Del Monte expressed
               apprehension regarding spot zoning for the proposed property and urged inclusion of the
               surrounding properties. Bonnie Miske! clarified information shared during public comments.
               Jonathan Ounjian spoke in favor of the amendment and future development. Seeing no one
               else come forward, the public hearing was closed.
