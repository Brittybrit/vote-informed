            The Special Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was called to order
                                        by Mayor Susan Haynie at 2:30 PM.

1.     PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG

2.     ROLL CALL

         Mayor Susan Haynie
         Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers
         Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke
         Council Member Scott Singer
         Council Member Robert S. Weinroth

3.     PUBLIC HEARING

        A        Resolution No. 115-2017

                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton equalizing the assessments; levying the same as
                 assessments against benefited properties within the Downtown Special Assessment District, City
                 of Boca Raton, Florida; directing the City Clerk to record assessments in the Improvement Lien
                 Book; establishing priority of lien and payment of principal and interest; providing for severability;
                 providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                         Resolution No. 115-2017

       Stephen Timberlake, Special Projects Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He provided a brief
       history of the special assessment and noted that the purpose of the meeting was to hear and consider
       any and all objections to the special assessment, which is required to fund the debt service and
       administrative costs for the special assessment revenue bonds. The method of calculating the
       assessment rate was explained and various charts and graphs were presented. Upon concluding, Mr.
       Timberlake made himself available for questions.

       The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Council Member
                          Scott Singer, to approve Resolution No. 115-2017. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                          Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Andrea Levine
                          O'Rourke, Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes.

4.     ADJOURNMENT

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 2:35 PM.
