            The Special Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was held in the
                                     Auditorium at 6500 Congress Avenue.


1.     CALL TO ORDER:

The meeting was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at 1:30 PM.

2.     PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

3.     ROLL CALL:

      Mayor Scott Singer
      Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker
      Council Member Fran Nachlas
      Council Member Andy Thomson
      Council Member Marc Wigder (attended remotely)


4.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARING:


       A.       Resolution No. 146-2024
               A resolution of the City of Boca Raton approving an agreement with CBRE, Inc. for the delivery
               of professional services associated with the management and evaluation of public private
               partnership (P3) proposals for qualifying projects, including unsolicited proposals, the
               solicitation and assessment of competitive proposals, the negotiation of a Master Development
               Agreement, and the evaluation of long-term operation and maintenance options for the
               Governmental Master Plan Project; authorizing a waiver of procurement procedures; providing
               for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

        The Mayor announced the purpose for the meeting, noting the item was pulled from the December
        10th Regular Meeting Agenda due to questions being raised, and advised the question had since
        been addressed. Deputy City Manager Andy Lukasik made himself available to respond to other
        questions.

        The public comment period was opened and , seeing no one come forward, closed .

        Council Members shared their thoughts and concerns on the agreement with CBRE and
        reimbursement schedule.

                       Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                       Fran Nachlas, to approve Resolution No. 146-2024. Motion carried unanimously;
                       Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas,
                       Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.
 5.      ADJOURNMENT:

 The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 1:41 PM.
