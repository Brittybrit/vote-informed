 12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A.       Ordinance No. 5558
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Code of Ordinances, to
                  provide for athletic training facility uses and define such uses; amending Article XII, "Industrial
                  Districts," to provide for an "Athletic Training Facility" as a permitted use in the M-1 Light Industrial
                  and M-3 Manufacturing Industrial Districts; amending Article XV, Division 1, "Supplementary District
                  Regulations," Section 28-1316, "Athletic Training Facilities," to establish regulations for such uses
                  and establish a 15,000 square footage limit; amending Article XVI, Section 28-1655, "Required Off­
                  Street Parking," to provide parking standards for athletic training facilities; amending Article XII,
                  Division 6, "M-1 Light Industrial District," Section 28-1097, "Permitted Uses," to provide consistency
                  in terminology and formatting with respect to athletic training facilities; providing for severability;
                  providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-20-01)

         Jim Bell, Long Range Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Bell noted that this was the second
         public hearing for this item. He began with a brief overview of the ordinance and discussed the clarifications
         and changes being proposed by the amendment. He explained that the amendment addressed problems
         in the City Code related to athletic training facilities. Mr. Bell stated the ordinance would make zoning
         districts M-1, M-2, and M-3 consistent and also provide needed definitions, specific regulations, and parking
         requirements. He affirmed that the proposed regulations and limitations would help to address potential
         future loss of industrial space in the City. Staff recommends approval of the ordinance. Upon concluding,
         he made himself available for members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one indicate their desire to speak, closed.

                                   Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council
                                   Member Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5558. Motion carried 4 to O;
                                   Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                                   Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

           B.      Ordinance No. 5566
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 22, "Marine Structures," Article Ill,
                  "Docks and Other Mooring Facilities," Code of Ordinances, by creating Section 22-69, "Marine
                  Structures in the Hillsboro and C-15 Canals," to provide that certain size requirements for dolphins,
                  finger piers and boat lifts in the Hillsboro and C-15 Canals shall not be applicable so long as a valid
                  permit for such improvements has been issued by the South Florida Water Management District,
                  and by amending Section 22-58, "Docks," to eliminate a duplicative provision; providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-20-
                  08)

         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Schaad began
         by explaining that this item was created to streamline the code. He explained that the City defers to South
         Florida Water Management District (SFWMD) requirements for size and location requirements of docks,
         dolphin pilings, finger piers, and boatlifts. A map was shown that displayed the jurisdiction of the Hillsboro
         and C-15 canals within the City. Mr. Schaad noted the frequency of variance requests due to the shallow
         depth of the Hillsboro Canal near its shore and C-15 canal limitations. He reassured members that all
         marine structures would remain subject to building permit requirements. The Marine Advisory Board
         recommended approval 6-1 and the Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval 7-0. Staff
         recommends approval. Upon concluding, he made himself available for members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one indicate their desire to speak, closed.

                                   Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council
                                   Member Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5566. Motion carried 4 to O;
                                   Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                                   Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

           C.     Ordinance No. 5567
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton authorizing a Thirteenth Amendment to the Agreement for
                  Purchase and Sale dated November 27, 2017, by and between the City of Boca Raton and G.L.
                  Acquisitions Corporation for the purchase and sale of the City of Boca Raton Municipal Golf Course
                  West, as authorized by Ordinance No. 541 O; authorizing a twelfth extension of the inspection
                  period; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

         George Brown, Deputy City Manager, made the presentation. Mr. Brown explained that G.L. Homes had
         asked to extend the inspection period to allow more time to resolve issues with the County regarding a
         public safety communication tower on the property. Upon concluding, he made himself available for
         members' questions. Council Member Mayotte asked if the situation was nearing a resolution, to which Mr.
         Brown advised G.L. Homes was working on options to keep the project moving forward.

         The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one indicate their desire to speak, closed.

                                   Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council
                                   Member Andy Thomson, to approve Ordinance No. 5567. Motion carried 4 to O;
                                   Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                                   Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
