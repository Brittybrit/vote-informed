          B.       Resolution No. 169-2021
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering accepting and approving the final plat
                  known as Boca Haven (a replat of Lots 4 and 5 and a portion of Lot 3, Block 1, Bible
                  Conference Estates) for an approximately 1.5 acre property generally located west of
                  Northwest 6th Avenue and north and south of Northwest 6th Street Extended, authorizing the
                  Mayor and City Clerk to execute the Plat; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                  (SUB-19-02)

        The Mayor confirmed that the public hearing would be conducted pursuant to the same quasi-judicial
        procedures as previously stated. Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk
        administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

        Tori Boone, Land Development Coordinator, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began by
        displaying an aerial photo of the subject property and reviewed components of the applicant's
        request. A table outlining the lot area and density was shown. The tentative plat was approved by
        both the Planning and Zoning Board and City Council in 2020. Ms. Boone advised that the final plat
        was consistent with the tentative plat and met all other platting requirements. Development Services
        staff recommended approval. Upon concluding, she made herself available for members' questions.

        The public hearing was opened. Lynn Yurosco, Mark Rosenthal, Eugene Lehenbauer, Candace
        Lehenbauer, and Veronica Risko expressed their opposition to the proposed resolution, citing
        concerns regarding the preservation of the lake, local wildlife and the requested setbacks. Mayor
        Singer inquired about the lake and the area being referred to as wetlands. Brandon Schaad,
        Development Services Director, noted that there was a notation on the plat that specifically prohibited
        filling in the lake. Steve Utrecht, speaking on behalf of his client who owned a nearby property, stated
        that he had no objection to the project as long as the contours of the lake were not changed. Dr.
        Steven Eigles, petitioner, addressed concerns raised by previous speakers and made himself
        available for members' questions. Glenn Gromann expressed his support for the project and
        complimented Ms. Boone on her presentation. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing
        was closed. Dr. Eigles reiterated that there was no intent to alter the lake in any way and advised that
        the development of the property would begin as quickly as possible upon approval. Seeing no one
        else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

         Mr. Schaad responded to members' questions regarding environmentally sensitive lands, mailed and
         published notices, and setback requirements. Discussion ensued.

          C.      Resolution No. 170-2021
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a conditional use approval pursuant to
                  Section 28-340(1), Code of Ordinances, to authorize a place of worship (Yagdil Torah
                  Mikvah) in the R-1-D zoning district for the property located at 460 Northwest Spanish River
                  Boulevard; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (CA-20-04)

        The Mayor confirmed that the quasi-judicial procedures reviewed earlier remained in effect. Members
        made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

        Heather Hansen, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Ms. Hansen began with a
        description of the subject property and a review of the applicant's request. She explained the differences
        between a Mikvah and traditional synagogues or churches that led to the two technical deviations and
        two variance requests. A site plan of the current subject property was shown alongside the proposed
        alterations to the existing home. A rendering of the elevation was displayed. Ms. Hansen noted the
        Planning and Zoning Board requested the parking spaces be reduced in size to allow a larger landscape
        buffer along Spanish River Boulevard. The applicant revised the site and landscape plans as requested.
        The Board voted 4-2 to approve the conditional use, the site plan and variances. Objections from the
        public were relayed. Staff recommends approval of the application with conditions. Upon concluding,
        she made herself available for members' questions. Ms. Hansen provided additional information
        regarding hours of use, parking, and zoning.

        The public hearing was opened. Kristin Nowicki, Senior Project Planner for WGI, presented on behalf of
        the applicant. She began by providing additional information about the use of the Mikvah, noting that use
        will be by appointment only. Ms. Nowicki noted the compatibility with surrounding building uses and
        highlighted the proposed modifications to the existing structure. It was pointed out that the project is
        expected to result in fewer traffic trips than a single-family home. Rabbi Light provided the history of
        Mikvah use and asked the Council for their support. The following expressed their approval of the
        project: Diana Barr, Julie Weiss, Deon Nathan, Judith Nathan, Leela Mahn, Adina Shayowitz, Charissa
        Hurd, Valeria Palmer, Abraham Meth, Shraga Greenbaum, Shraga Greenbaum, Chaim Morgan, Annette
        Ben-Habib, Deidre Zussman, Simona Roth, Nechama Feder, Deanna Casper, Yaffa Stransfeld,
        Tzipporah Nechama Zwiren, Batsheva Wasser, Aliza Sloan, Nechama Turk, Ayelet Rabinowitz,
        Suzanne Zigun, Kerrie Greenwald, Abby Brody, Zvi Bach, and Luly Horovitz, citing the private nature of
        the Mikvah's use and the fact that most individuals who will use the facility would be travelling there on
        foot. The following individuals expressed their opposition to the proposed Mikvah, citing traffic and crowd
        concerns and use of the property: Phil Liberty, Ekaterini Alexopoulus, Steve Duke, Dana Lynch, Keith
        Gamarra, and Charlie Helton. Rabbi Light, on behalf of the applicant, made final statements. He relayed
        his distress at certain comments made during the public hearing, and affirmed that, as Rabbi for the
        affected community, he encouraged a broader discussion of acceptance between those of different
        faiths. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Andy
                          Thomson, to approve Resolution No. 170-2021.

         Council Members thanked all those who spoke during the public hearing, addressed the concerns of
         residents opposing the project, and shared their reasons for supporting the application. The Mayor
         and Council Members also expressed their deep disappointment with remarks directed at members
         of the Orthodox community and encouraged residents to be tolerant and accepting of others.

                          Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine
                          O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte,
                          Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

          A        Ordinance No. 5604
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering an amendment to the City's
                  Comprehensive Plan to create a property rights element; providing for severability; providing
                  for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-21-06)

         Mayor Singer noted that this was a transmittal hearing for the ordinance and the first of two public
         hearings.

         Tamash Rahman, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began with a brief
         description of the Comprehensive Plan and the elements included within it. Ms. Rahman explained
         that House Bill 59 requires all local governments to update their Comprehensive Plan to incorporate
         a property rights element that ensures private property rights are considered in local decision­
         making. The proposed amendment meets the Florida Statutory requirement. Both the Planning and
         Zoning Board and staff recommended approval. Upon concluding, she made herself available for
         members' questions.

        The public hearing was opened. Charlie Helton commented on the ordinance and the effect on his
         personal property rights. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                          Andrea Levine O'Rourke, to transmit Ordinance No. 5604. Motion carried
                          unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council
                          Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy
                          Thomson voting yes.

          B.       Ordinance No. 5605
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 19, "Building Regulations," Article
                  VI, Division 3, "Permitting and Construction Review Board," Section 19-216, "Membership,"
                  Code of Ordinances, to require at least one member of the board hold a state license or
                  certification as a professional engineer with experience in electrical engineering; providing
                  for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date
                  (AM-21-11)

           Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, briefly reviewed the ordinance. He explained
           that the recertification ordinance adopted earlier in the year requires submittal of reports from both
           a structural and an electrical engineer. Mr. Schaad noted that the Permitting and Construction
           Review Board is designated to review appeals of the recertification program, and the makeup of
           the board currently requires an architect and a structural engineer, with the remaining seats to be
           filled by any of a number of professions. The proposed ordinance would require that an electrical
           engineer fill one of the remaining seats. Staff recommends approval. Upon conclusion, he made
           himself available for members' questions.

           The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                          Andrea Levine O'Rourke, to approve Ordinance No. 5605. Motion carried
                          unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council
                          Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy
                          Thomson voting yes.
