 17.      QUASI-JUDICIAL PUBLIC HEARINGS/VARIANCES & APPEALS:

           A.      Resolution No. 134-2019
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering a Coastal Construction
                  Control Line (CCCL) variance, from Section 28-1556(3), Code of Ordinances, which variance is
                  required for the alteration of an existing outdoor deck and pool area seaward of the CCCL (where
                  no structure is permitted seaward of the CCCL without a variance}, for a multifamily residential (R­
                  S-A) zoned property located at 1400-1500 South Ocean Boulevard; providing for repealer; providing
                  an effective date (SC-19-01)

         The City Attorney read the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the hearing. Members made their
         ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

         Ingrid Allen, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The applicant's request for variance was
         reviewed. Aerial photos of the property, existing elevations and proposed changes were displayed. Ms.
         Allen noted all variance criteria had been met. The Environmental Advisory Board and Planning and Zoning
         Board recommend approval. Development Services staff recommends approval. Upon concluding, Ms.
         Allen made herself available for questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Michael Fichera, on behalf of the applicant, gave a brief presentation. He
         noted that the proposed renovations are restricted to the existing hardscape and roof replacement of the
         underground parking garage. Upon concluding, Mr. Fichera responded to members' questions. There was
         no cross-examination or rebuttal. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

         Members made final comments.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                  Rodgers to approve Resolution No. 134-2019. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,
                  Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes

 20.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member O'Rourke commented on the Flamingo Comedy Festival and the previous days' presentation on
the Wildflower Park.

Council Member Mayotte encouraged residents to conserve water as the dry season begins and asked the City
Manager for an update on Camino Real bike lanes.

Mayor Singer began a discussion regarding proposed improvements to the North Dixie Highway corridor. Council
members offered their support and gave direction to the City Manager to draft an agreement with interested parties.

Council Member Thomson provided a handout outlining his suggestions for a revised interlocal agreement (ILA)
with the Greater Boca Raton Beach & Park District (District) regarding development of the proposed Ocean Breeze
Golf Course. Members offered their general support of his proposed terms. The City Attorney provided additional
information. Discussion ensued. There was consensus by Members to have the City Attorney draft the proposed
ILA and forward it to the District for their review.
