12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

                   Ordinance No. 5367
                   An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering a temporary moratorium on the operation of
                   Medical Marijuana Treatment Centers and Medical Marijuana Dispensing Organizations within
                   the City for a period of one (1) year; providing for severability; providing an effective date (SC-14-
                   04R2)
                            Ordinance No. 5367

        Tamash Rahman, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation, beginning with background
        information on the Compassionate Medical Cannabis Act of 2014 and previous City ordinances related to
        moratoriums on medical marijuana dispensing organizations. Ms. Rahman briefly reviewed Amendment
        2 that would legalize medical marijuana and is on the November 8 ballot. Justifications for the
        moratorium were provided. It was noted that, if Amendment 2 does not pass, this moratorium will
        terminate. Staff recommends approval. Upon concluding, Ms. Rahman responded to several questions
        from Council members.

        The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed.

                   Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Michael
                   Mu/laugh, to approve Ordinance No. 5367.

         Members made final comments.

                   Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council
                   Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth
                   voting yes.

                   Ordinance No. 5368
                   An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton relating to the future land use element of the City's
                   Comprehensive Plan; amending Policy LU.1.1.5 to increase the maximum intensity (floor area
                   ratio) in the commercial, general industrial, light industrial, and manufacturing future land use
                   designations for a self-storage facility (interior storage unit access) where permitted in Chapter 28
                   "Zoning", Code of Ordinances; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an
                   effective date (SC-16-03)
                            Ordinance No. 5368

                   Ordinance No. 5369
                   An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28 "Zoning," Code of Ordinances, to
                   incorporate new provisions relating to self-storage facilities (interior storage unit access);
                   amending Section 28-307(1) relating to building intensity; amending the C-1 District regulations,
                   Sections 28-872 and 28-873; amending the W-1 District regulations, Sections 28-1017 and 28-
                   1019; amending the IG/SI District regulations. Sections 28-1042 and 28-1043; amending the M-1
                   District regulations, Sections 28-1072 and 28-1073; amending the M-2 District regulations,
                   Sections 28-1097 and 28-1098; amending the M-3 District regulations, Sections 28-1123 and 28-
                    1124; amending Article XV, Supplementary District Regulations, Division 1, by creating a new
                   Section 28-1310 entitled "Self-Storage Facility (interior storage unit access)" and establishing
                   development standards for such use; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing
                   for codification; providing an effective date (AM-15-03)
                            Ordinance No. 5369

        Mayor Haynie noted that this is a transmittal hearing for Ordinance 5368 and the first of two public
        hearings on both Ordinance 5368 and Ordinance 5369.




 Regular Minutes                                       4                                                November 8, 2016
       Ingrid Allen, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation on both ordinances contemporaneously..
       The intent of the ordinances was briefly reviewed and background information was provided. It was noted
       that the amendments to the Comprehensive Plan and the Code of Ordinances, which would increase the
       floor area ratio (FAR) for interior self-storage facilities in certain zoning districts, were triggered by the
       applicant's site-specific project. Details of the amendments were provided. Ms. Allen then explained
       staff's analysis and the recommended denial of the petitions. The Planning and Zoning Board
       recommended approval of the ordinances with several amendments to Ordinance 5369. Upon
       concluding, Ms. Allen responded to a number of questions centered on the ramifications of the code
       amendments. The City Attorney provided clarification. Deputy City Manager George Brown addressed a
       number of questions and suggested several changes to the ordinances that would help clarify some of
       the questions posed. Further discussion ensued.

       The public hearing was opened. Charles Siemon, Gray Robinson, on behalf of the applicant, gave a
       PowerPoint presentation in support of the ordinances. He addressed some of the comments made during
       the discussion of the staff presentation and then asked that Council vote to transmit the Comprehensive
       Plan ordinance. Upon concluding, Mr. Siemon responded to questions from Council. Deputy City
       Manager George Brown also responded to questions, providing information on mezzanines and other
       topics.

       Chris Baczewski commented on both ordinances, contending that their passage would result in over­
       development of self-storage in Boca Raton and have other unintended consequences. Tom Head
       addressed some of Mr. Baczewski's comments. Charles Siemon made final comments and responded to
       questions about roll-up doors. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

       The City Attorney reviewed the suggested language to be added to both ordinances which would specify
       that the maximum FAR of 1.0 would only apply if the self-storage use were the only use on the property.
       In Ordinance 5368, said language would be inserted in the paragraphs related to the increase in intensity
       for self-storage facilities in various zoning districts.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, seconded by Council Member Robert S.
                  Weinroth, to transmit Ordinance No. 5368, as amended, to the appropriate agency.

          Members made final comments.

                  Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council
                  Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth
                  voting yes.

       Members then discussed the Ordinance 5369, the proposed Code amendments, noting that no vote
       would be taken and further discussion would serve to inform staff. It was suggested that the amendment
       be revised to limit it to a conversion of existing buildings. Further concerns were expressed.
