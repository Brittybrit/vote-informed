         Mayor Haynie noted that the public hearings on Items A., B., and C. would be conducted
         contemporaneously. A separate vote would be taken on each ordinance and resolution. The City
         Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
         made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to
         speak.

         Tamash Rahman, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation beginning with a review of
         the components of the petitioner's request. The location, future land use and zoning of the
         property were provided. Ms. Rahman provided details of staff's analysis of the project for
         consistency, compatibility and concurrency with the Comprehensive Plan, with particular
         emphasis on the property's location in a coastal high hazard area and flood zone. She then
         reviewed the rezoning, including the traffic analysis. The site plan was then described, including
         renderings and building elevations. The requested technical deviation for a reduction in reservoir
         length was supported by the City's Traffic Engineer. The Planning and Zoning Board
         recommended approval with recommendations, which, it was noted, were included in the site
         plan by the developer. Staff recommended approval of the all petitions. Upon concluding, Ms.
         Rahman responded to a number of questions from Council members. In response to a question,
         Maria Tejara, City Traffic Engineer, emphasized that there was no objection to the reservoir
         length as the number of units does not justify the reservoir length required by City Code.
         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, provided information on the condition
         regarding hurricane evacuation.

         The public hearing was opened. Bonnie Miske!, on behalf of the petitioner, gave a PowerPoint
         presentation in support of the project. The application was reviewed. The differences in the
         original and revised proposals were pointed out, emphasizing the amount of green space. Jorge
         Garcia, project architect, narrated a video illustrating the project. Both Ms. Miskel and Mr. Garcia
         noted that they had worked with the neighbors to improve the project. Members asked a number
         of questions regarding landscaping, public access areas, setbacks, and public art. John
         Donaldson, JMD Engineering, responded to questions about hurricane evacuation times and trip
         generation analysis. Mark Simmons, Riviera Civic Association, noted the Association's support of
         the project. Carrie McGurk, vice president of the Marbella Condominium board, provided
         information on the relationship with the Ocean Palm developers. She requested that a decision
         be postponed until Marbella residents have returned and have an opportunity to voice their
         concerns. She expressed a number of her own concerns. Glenn Gromann offered support of the
         project, addressed the previous speaker's comments, and advocated approval of the project. No
         one else came forward to speak.

         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, recapped the presentation he made at the first
         public hearing on the ordinance. He noted the Planning and Zoning Board recommendation to
         deny passage of the ordinance. Staff recommends approval. Mr. Schaad then responded to a
         number of questions from Council members.

         The public hearing was opened. Glenn Gromann offered his opinion regarding medical marijuana
         and problems with dispensaries. He stated his support of passage of the ordinance. Eric Sevell
         shared comments in response to an article he posted. He stated his opposition to the ordinance,

                  Resolution No. 130-2017
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton concluding the selection
                   procedures specified in Section 3 of Ordinance No. 5406 in connection with the potential
                   sale of the City's Municipal Golf Course West; selecting a purchaser of the City's
                   Municipal Golf Course West and determining that selection to be in the best interests of
                  the City; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for an effective date
                           Memo
                           Resolution No. 130-2017

         Deputy City Manager George Brown gave the PowerPoint presentation on the potential sale of
         the City's western golf course. He noted that should a purchaser be selected and Resolution No.
         130-2017 be adopted, then Ordinance No. 5410 authorizing the sale would be introduced.
         Background information on the process to date was reviewed, noting that all steps to date had
         been completed by the two finalists, GL Acquisitions Corporation (GL Homes) and Lennar
         Homes, LLC (Lennar). The procedures to be followed at the public hearing were also reviewed.
         Details of both offers to purchase were provided, including purchase price, deposits and refunds,
         remedies, title, inspection, insurance, environmental remediation, development approvals,
         assignment, current golf course operations and eminent domain.             Each offer was then
         summarized. Finally, the fiscal impact of each offer was discussed. Upon concluding, Mr. Brown
         responded to questions. The City Attorney also provided responses.

         The public hearing was opened. As determined by a random drawing, GL Homes was the initial
         presenter. Larry Portnoy, Vice President, gave a PowerPoint presentation. He began with the
         justification for the decrease in purchase price and then provided a detailed comparison of the GL
         Homes and Lennar offers, noting the large number of contingencies in the Lennar contract. In
         closing, he contended that his company's offer was superior. Upon concluding, Mr. Portnoy
         responded to Council members' questions. Bruce Grundt, Vice President with Lennar, gave a
         PowerPoint presentation in support of Lennar's offer. He noted that Lennar is a local company
         and had worked with City in the past on the land for the Downtown Library. He then reviewed
         details of Lennar's offer. Upon concluding, he responded to Council members' questions.
         Discussion of several issues ensued.

         As determined by a random drawing, Lennar was the first to offer a rebuttal. Bruce Grundt
         compared the price offers and contended that the City would be better off working with Lennar. In
         rebuttal for GL Homes, Senior Vice President Alan Fant explained the reasons for the reduced
         price offer, comparing the two offers with regard to risk and contingencies and making the case
         that the GL offer is superior. He proffered that GL would increase their offer to a $65 million
         minimum with the terms and conditions of the Lennar contract. Larry Portnoy responded to some
         of Mr. Grundt's comments during his rebuttal. Seeing no one else came forward to speak, the
         public hearing was closed.

         Council members began discussion and deliberation.

At this time, Mayor Haynie noted that it was nearing 11 pm and a motion was needed to continue the
meeting beyond that time.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Council Member
                  Scott Singer, to extend the meeting beyond 11 :00 pm. Motion carried unanimously on a
                  voice vote.

         Members continued with comments, stating their positions and posing a number of questions to
         the finalists and staff. The City Attorney provided an explanation of the contract process thus far
         and suggested to continue with the next step during which the finalists can adjust their offers. Mr.
         Brown and Ms. Frieser responded to further questions. Members continued to comment. The
         finalists were asked to present their best offer after a recess.
