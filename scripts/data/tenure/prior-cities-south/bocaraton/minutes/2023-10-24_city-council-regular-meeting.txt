          G.      Ordinance No. 5675
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the Boca Raton Downtown Development of
                 Regional Impact (DORI) Development Order (Ordinance No. 4035, as amended) to expand the
                 authority of the Executive Director to issue Administrative Individual Development Approvals
                 (IDAs) from 5,000 square feet (of building construction I change of use, or a conversion or transfer
                 of office equivalent development), to a threshold of: 5,000 square feet or, for properties with
                 existing building square footage or office equivalent development greater than 250,000 square
                 feet, two percent of the existing square footage or office equivalent development; providing for
                 severability; providing for repealer, providing an effective date (AM-23-14)

          Development Services Director Brandon Schaad gave the PowerPoint presentation . Mr. Schaad began
          with the background of the ordinance and explained related ordinances. He advised that the Planning
          and Zoning Board and staff recommended approval. Upon concluding he made himself available for
          questions. Discussion ensued. Mr. Schaad provided additional insight and responded to Members'
          questions.

           The public comment period was opened. Jonathan Ounjian voiced his concern over giving staff
           authority to make additional administrative approval. Seeing no one else come forward , the public
           comment period, was closed.

                          Wigder, to approve Ordinance No. 5675.

17.      CITY MANAGER RECOMMENDATIONS AND REPORTS:

Deputy City Manager George Brown asked for approval from Council Members for the scheduling of the
ordinance related to SB-102 (Live, Local, Act). Discussion ensued. Mr. Brown reviewed the schedule and
responded to Members' questions. There was consensus from Members for staff to proceed with the schedule
as outlined. Council Member Wigder asked about an update on The Center for the Arts and Innovation (TCAI).
Discussion ensued and Mr. Brown advised that additional information would be provided at a future date.
Members inquired about an update from TCAl's Andrea Virgin. Mr. Wigder then inquired about the Affordable
Housing Advisory committee and the acceptance of SHIP funds. Mr. Brown stated that staff is preparing the
necessary documents to renew the agreement. Council Member Drucker asked about the hiring process for the
newly implemented Director of Transportation & Mobility position. Mr. Ahnell had advised that the position had
been posted that day.

 18.     CITY ATTORNEY REPORTS:

The City Attorney had no report.

 19.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Wigder highlighted the "Walk Against Hate" at the Spanish River Library and shared that he
was very pleased with the attendance. He continued by asking if the City could put out an RFP to add a cafe at
the library. Mr. Wigder noted the extra police presence at the local schools related to tensions regarding the war
in Israel.

Council Member Yvette Drucker highlighted local events regarding mobility and transportation. She advised that
she had attended the FAU Homecoming event, and thanked staff for providing promotional material for residents
that emphasized Mobility Week. She then shared that she attended a Latin Shabot mass and thanked Police
Services for their continued patrols at local synagogues.

Deputy Mayor Mayotte shared slides that showed examples of tactical urbanism and public safety in Aspen,
Colorado. She commented on the recent recycling conference she attended and highlighted best recycling
practices.

Council Member Nachlas mentioned that the upcoming TimberTech Golf Tournament would be recycling all the
waste generated from the event. She highlighted events for Mobility Week and concluded by stating that she
continues to stand with Israel.

 Mayor Scott Singer reiterated his support of Israel, highlighted upcoming City events, and thanked the City's first
 responders.
