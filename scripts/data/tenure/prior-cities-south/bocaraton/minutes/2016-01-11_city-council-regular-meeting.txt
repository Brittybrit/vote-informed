            A.           Resolution No. 12- 2016
                        A resolution of the City of Boca Raton requesting the Greater Boca Raton Beach and Park District
                        consider acquisition of oceanfront properties in the City for preservation and public use; providing
                        for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

            Mayor Haynie           requested      this resolution   be drafted      in light of the recent       public response       to the variance
            hearing for 2500 N. Ocean Boulevard. She noted that there are three buildable oceanfront properties
            which she felt could be acquired by the Beach and Park District, thus preventing any future development.
            Discussion ensued.              Council    Member Singer addressed               his reluctant vote on the lot width variance for
            2500 N. Ocean Boulevard, the Petruzzelli lot and the legal standing of a potential variance request, and
            recognition of a landowner' s right to develop a lot. He requested to amend the resolution in Section 1,
            line 11 to specify undeveloped oceanfront properties in the City of Boca Raton and not enumerate the
            addresses.   Mayor Haynie accepted the friendly amendment.       Discussion ensued.     The City Attorney
            read into the record language generally consistent with the following in Section 1: on line 11, strike the
            word "     certain";   strike   the    remainder    of the   Section;     add,    at the     end   of the    Section,    the    parenthetical
            phrase " this request has no impact on the development potential, or lack thereof, of any property."

                               Motion was made by Mayor Susan Haynie, seconded by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers,
                               to approve         Resolution   12- 2016 as amended.              Motion    carried      4- 0; Mayor    Susan       Haynie,
                               Deputy Mayor Robert S. Weinroth, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott
                               Singer voting yes.
