4.     ORGANIZATIONAL PROCEEDINGS:

        A.     Selection of Deputy Mayor

                       Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Deputy Mayor Yvette
                       Drucker, to appoint Fran Nachlas as Deputy Mayor. Motion carried unanimously on a
                       voice vote.

        B.     Selection of CRA Chair and Vice Chair

                       Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, seconded by Council Member
                       Yvette Drucker, to appoint Council Member Marc Wigder as Chair and Council Member
                       Andy Thomson as Vice Chair of the CRA. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

        C.     Selection of Affordable Housing Advisory Committee Representative (Ordinance No. 5691)

                       Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, seconded by Council Member Marc
                       Wigder, to appoint Andy Thomson to the Affordable Housing Advisory Committee.
                       Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

5.     OTHER BUSINESS:

        A.     Selection of TPA (Transportation Planning Agency) Representatives and Two Alternates

                       Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Mayor Scott
                       Singer, to reappoint Council Member Yvette Drucker and Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas
                       as the TPA (Transportation Planning Agency) Representatives and Council Member
                       Marc Wigder and Council Member Andy Thomson as Two Alternates. Motion carried
                       unanimously on a voice vote.
