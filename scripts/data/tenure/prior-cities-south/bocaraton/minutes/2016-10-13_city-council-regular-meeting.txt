17.    QUASI-JUDICIAL PUBLIC HEARINGS/VARIANCES & APPEALS:

                   Resolution No. 116-2016
                   A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the appeal of the decision
                   of the Community Appearance Board denying Orangetheory Fitness' requested two (2) flat wall
                   signs (Permit Numbers 16-4802 and 16-4803) to be located on the North and West elevation of a
                   commercial building located at 10 East Palmetto Park Road; providing for severability; providing
                   for repealer; providing an effective date
                            Resolution No. 116-2016

        The Senior Assistant City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public
        hearing. Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to all those who
        wished to speak.

        John Harbilas, Zoning Officer, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The locations of the wall signs on the
        property were provided. Mr. Harbilas provided background information on the Community Appearance
        Board (CAB) consideration of both permits and the reasons for the denials. Staff finds that both signs are
        acceptable and recommends granting the appeals, reversing the decisions of the CAB. Mr. Harbilas then
        responded to questions from Council members.




 Regular Minutes                                        5                                               October 13, 2016
       The public hearing was opened. Angela Ryan, Bergen Sign Company on behalf of Orangetheory Fitness,
       addressed the sign and wall colors. She stated the position of the appellant with regard to the colors,
       pointing out that other nearby companies have multi-colored signs and that the color of the building is so
       pale as to be neutral. Mark Jacobsen, CAB chairman, stated that the Board only considers aesthetics and
       explained the board's reasons for denial of both permits. He responded to questions of members. Glenn
       Gromann expressed his support of the position of CAB and suggested that the building should be painted
       a neutral color. Alex Lashnikov with Orangetheory addressed the history of the permit hearings and
       resulting CAB decisions, explaining that Orangetheory is working to standardize their company brand as
       reflected in their signs. He advocated allowing the use of their trademark sign. During rebuttal, Mark
       Jacobsen stated that the Board is willing to work with the company. Ms. Ryan passed her phone to
       Council members to illustrate the paleness of the pink building color. The public hearing was then closed.

       Members commented and expressed their positions. In response to a question, City Manager Leif Ahnell
       stated that Code Enforcement could look into any possible violation related to the lack of maintenance of
       the building. The paleness of the building color was discussed. Mayor Haynie agreed with Council
       Member Weinroth's position that the Council and CAB need to have a joint workshop to discuss the
       evolving sign code and requirements.

                       Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                       Michael Mu/laugh, to approve Resolution No. 116-2016 granting the appeal and reversing
                       the decision of CAB. Motion carried 4 to 1; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Michael
                       Mu/laugh, Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes;
                       Council Member Jeremy Rodgers voting no.       ·

       B.         Resolution No. 117-2016
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the appeal of the decision
                  of the Community Appearance Board denying Garden Cemetery's requested freestanding sign
                  (Permit No. 16-5088) in the entrance median for a property located at 4103 N Military Trail;
                   providing for severability; providing for repealer: providing an effective date
                           Resolution No. 117-2016

       Mayor Haynie confirmed that the same quasi-judicial procedures would govern this public hearing.
       Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to all those who wished
       to speak.

       John Harbilas, Zoning Officer, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The location of the sign on the site was
       identified and the sign was described. It was noted that the sign met Code. The site was further
       described and the history of the Community Appearance Board (CAB) review was provided. Staff, having
       reviewed the sign, recommended that the appeal be granted and the decision of CAB be reversed. Mr.
       Harbilas then responded to questions. It was noted that CAB took issue with the placement of the sign.

       The public hearing was opened. Sherry Dillard, Art Sign Company, made the case for the placement of
       the sign as necessary to identify the property. Ryan Leeds, on behalf of the property owner, explained
       the company's need for the sign, noting that currently many people cannot locate the property. Mark
       Jacobsen, CAB chairman, explained the Board's position on the placement of the sign and the lack of
       landscaping due to the size of the median. He also shared the suggestions offered by CAB for improving
       the property's signage in other ways. At Mayor Haynie's request, Mr. Leeds addressed the landscaping
       issue, the size of the sign and the median, as well as the appearance of the sign. Ms. Dillard stated that
       the sign footer could be put below grade in order to accommodate the landscaping CAB requested.
       Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

       Members offered comments. Mayor Haynie suggested a condition to require landscaping at the base of
       the sign.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Council Member Scott
                  Singer, to approve Resolution No. 117-2016 granting the appeal subject to the applicant providing
                  landscaping at the base of the sign. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy
                  Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott Singer,
                  Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes.
