 ll..:.   QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

          A       Resolution No. 17-2020
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering an amendment to a conditional use approval
                  (Resolution No. 44-12) to authorize the expansion of an existing conditional use (Hospice by the
                  Sea) in a single family residential (R-1-D) zoning district, for the property located at 1531 West
                  Palmetto Park Road; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (CA-00-04R3)

           The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Council
           Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to
           speak.

           Susan Lesser, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began by providing a brief
           summary of the application. The location of the property and proposed site plan were reviewed. Details
           of the proposed cafeteria addition were discussed, as well as parking and traffic considerations. Proposed
           changes to building elevations were presented. The Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval.
           Staff recommends approval. Upon concluding, Ms. Lesser made herself available for questions.

           The public hearing was opened. Bradley Miller, on behalf of the applicant, made himself available for
           members' questions. There was no rebuttal or cross-examination. Seeing no one else come forward, the
           public hearing was closed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member Andrea
                  Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 17-2020. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott
                  Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea
                  Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

 12.      REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A       Ordinance No. 5513
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28 "Zoning," Code of Ordinances,
                  regarding pharmacies and medical marijuana treatment center dispensing facilities; removing
                  medical marijuana treatment center dispensing facilities as prohibited uses within the City and
                  amending the following zoning districts to allow pharmacies and medical marijuana treatment
                  center dispensing facilities as permitted uses or conditional uses, as applicable: Local Business (B-
                  1) District, Community Business (B-2) District, Central Business (B-3) District, General Business
                  (B-4) District, Commercial (C-1) District, City General Commercial (CG) District, City Commercial
                  High Office (CHO) District, Light Industrial Research Park (LIRP) District, Manufacturing Industrial
                  (M-3) District, Medical Center (MC) District, Motel-Business (R-B-1) District, Village Center (VC)
                  District, Professional, Office and Institutional (POI) District, and Neighborhood Convenience
                  Business (NCBD) District; authorizing pharmacies and medical marijuana treatment center
                  dispensing facilities as conditional uses in commercial nodes of residential planned unit
                  developments; providing regulations for pharmacies, medical marijuana treatment center
                  dispensing facilities and businesses with marijuana-derived products; amending Article I, Section
                  28-2, "Definitions," by amending the definitions of "medical marijuana treatment center dispensing
                  facility" and "neighborhood serving retail", and creating a definition for the term "pharmacies";
                  providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective
                  date (AM-19-08)


Regular Minutes                                           4                                          February 11,2020
          Tamash Rahman, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation, and noted that this was the second
          of two public hearings. Ms. Rahman began by reviewing the changes made to the ordinance since the
          first public hearing. A summary of the proposed regulations was provided. Upon concluding, Ms. Rahman
          and Development Services Director Brandon Schaad responded to members' questions.

          The public hearing was opened. Robert Eisen, on behalf of Investments Limited, relayed his concerns
          regarding the square footage requirement. Shari Kaplan Stellino, on behalf of Cannectd Wellness, shared
          her son's experience with medical marijuana and her desire to see the ordinance passed. Eric Sevell,
          Seth Penalver, Jason Iberra, Cristina Escalante, Charles Felix, Marko Andrioff, and Emily Hartoa spoke
          in support of the ordinance. Glenn Gromann, Alan Feinberg, and Terri Murru expressed their opposition
          to the proposed ordinance. Seeing no one else come forward, the public comment period was closed.

          The City Attorney reviewed revisions made to the ordinance, noting that a number of changes involved
          cleanup to the language, while others were more substantive. Mr. Schaad responded to a remark made
          during the public comment period regarding the LIRP zoning district. An amendment to the ordinance
          was suggested by Council Member Thomson, to reduce the minimum square footage requirement from
          5,000 to 2,500. Council Member Mayotte agreed, and proposed a limit of allowable distances between
          dispensaries and existing pharmacies. Staff displayed maps illustrating a restriction of 4,000-foot
          proximity to existing pharmacies. Discussion ensued. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser read proposed
          language into the record regarding distance separation. Deputy Mayor Rodgers expressed concerns
          regarding the proposed distance amendment and suggested language that would postpone the effective
          date of the ordinance, as well as require conditional use approval. Members expressed doubts that an
          additional delay would yield action by the State legislature.

           The City Attorney, noting the motions applied to the 2/11/2020 revision of the ordinance, identified the
           pages and sections that would amend the minimum gross floor area to 2,500 square feet and add
           language setting a distance separation of 4,000 feet from existing pharmacies.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member Monica
                  Mayotte, to amend Ordinance No. 5513 to lower the minimum required square footage from 5,000
                  to 2,500. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member Monica
                  Mayotte, to amend Ordinance No. 5513 establishing a distance separation of 4,000 feet from
                  existing pharmacies.

           Ms. Frieser provided additional clarification regarding the distance separation maps developed by staff.
           Members commented.

                  Motion carried on a 3 to 2 vote; Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                  Member Andy Thomson voting yes. Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers and Council Member Andrea
                  Levine O'Rourke voting no.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke, to amend Ordinance No. 5513 to postpone the effective date to June 1, 2021.

           The City Attorney read the new language into the record. Further discussion ensued.

                  Motion failed on a 2 to 3 vote; Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers and Council Member Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke voting yes, Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy
                  Thomson voting no.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member Monica
                  Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5513 as amended.

           Members offered final comments and stated their positions.

                  Motion carried on a 3 to 2 vote; Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                  Member Andy Thomson voting yes. Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers and Member Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke voting no.

          B.      Ordinance No. 5516
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton establishing the Downtown Advisory Board; providing for
                  the membership, appointment, term, quorum, officers, powers and duties; providing for severability;
                  providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date

          Ruby Childers, Downtown Manager, briefly reviewed the ordinance and responded to members'
          questions.

          Mayor Singer and Council Member O'Rourke inquired about the intended goals of the proposed board.
          Members expressed concerns regarding board member requirements. Mayor Singer suggested the
          ordinance be refined and reconsidered at a later date.

          The public hearing was opened. Derek VanderPloeg commented in support of the proposed ordinance,
          citing the need to address goals not yet been met by the Downtown Advisory Committee. Robert Eisen
          suggested the creation of a task force to identify the duties and goals of the proposed board. Seeing no
          one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

          Council Member O'Rourke inquired about board member qualifications. Ms. Childers responded, and
          discussion ensued. The City Attorney suggested the scope of the board be determined prior to its
          creation.

                  Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke,
                  to postpone consideration of Ordinance No. 5516 to the regular meeting of April 14, 2020. Motion
                  carried unanimously on a voice vote.

                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton authorizing the Mayor and City Clerk to execute an easement
                  deed to Florida Power & Light over certain lands at the City-owned parking lot located at 190
                  Northeast 2nd Street; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

           advised members that a condition to the approval of the easement could be added, which stated that
