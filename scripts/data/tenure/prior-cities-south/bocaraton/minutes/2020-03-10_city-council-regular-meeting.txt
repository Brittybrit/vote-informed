 12.         REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

                       Ordinance No. 5520
                      An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the Boca Raton Downtown Development of
                      Regional Impact (DORI) Development Order regarding the requirements and limitations set forth in
                      Ordinance No. 5513 (regulating retail pharmacies and medical marijuana treatment center
                      dispensing facilities) in the DORI Development Order; providing for severability; providing for
                      repealer; providing an effective date (SC-20-02)

             Ruby Childers, Downtown Manager, briefly reviewed the ordinance. She noted the proposed amendments
             were made in response to the approval of Ordinance No. 5513 on February 11, 2020. The Planning and
             Zoning Board and Community Redevelopment Agency recommended approval. Staff recommends
             approval. Upon concluding, Ms. Childers responded to members' questions.

                      Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member Monica
                      Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5520.

             Members offered final comments and stated their positions.

                      Motion carried on a 3 to 2 vote: Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                      Member Andy Thomson voting yes. Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers and Council Member Andrea
                      Levine O'Rourke voting no.

 19.        CITY ATTORNEY REPORTS:

The City Attorney requested authorization to schedule an executive session to receive advice in the matter referred
to as the Second Billboard Case, Case No. 50-2019-CA-013507.

                     Motion was made by Council Member Thomson, seconded by Council Member Mayotte, to
                     authorize the executive sessions as requested. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

 20.        MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Mayotte asked City Manager Ahnell for an update regarding the tower at Gumbo Limbo. Mr. Ahnell
advised that the cost and complexity of making the observation tower ADA compliant had grown significantly, and
the City was waiting on direction from the Beach and Park District. Ms. Mayotte also provided an update from the
County's Water Resources Taskforce, and their proposed bond referendum for environmental projects throughout
the County. Finally, she reminded citizens of the 2020 Census dates and deadlines.

Council Member O'Rourke noted the cancellation of the March 19th FAU Media Day for the Eco-Turtle project.

Mayor Singer noted that the online 2020 Census form will be available to complete at both City libraries.

 21.       ADJOURNMENT:
