          Mayor Haynie noted that the public hearings on items A, B and C would be conducted
         contemporaneously, as the petitions are related and a separate vote would be taken on each petition.
         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
         made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to all those who wished to speak.

         Tamash Rahman, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Ms. Rahman reviewed the
         applicant's request as well as the appeal of the Planning & Zoning Board's decision. The location and
         size of the property and background information were provided. Ms. Rahman noted that the applicant's
         proposal does not impact traffic and the open space requirement is still met. Discussion then turned to
         the appeal. Staff recommends approval of the amendments to the future land use and the master plan;
         staff also supports granting the appeal. Ms. Rahman then responded to questions from Council members.

         The public hearing was opened. Mitch Kirschner, Gray Robinson on behalf of the petitioner, principally
         addressed the appeal of the Planning & Zoning Board decision on the site plan. Bradley Miller, Miller
         Land Planning and agent for the Vistazo of Boca Raton Community Association, gave a PowerPoint
         presentation reviewing the request, identifying the location of the proposed new parking spaces, providing
         justification for the request and addressing the appeal. Upon concluding, he responded to questions from
         Council members.

         Roger Paris, president of the Vistazo Community Association, addressed the problem of parking in his
         neighborhood and addressed some residents' view of the necessity for new parking spaces. Les
         Auerbach, Vistazo Community Association board member, added his comments regarding the parking

          Mayor Haynie noted that the public hearings on items D, E and F would be conducted
         contemporaneously, as the petitions are related and a separate vote would be taken on each petition.
         The Mayor also stated that the quasi-judicial procedures remain in effect. Members made their ex parte
         disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to all those who wished to speak.

         Tamash Rahman, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The petitioner's request for an
         amended land use and rezoning were reviewed, and the location, zoning, and history of the project were
         provided. Staff reviewed the future land use map amendment for consistency, compatibility and
         concurrency of the project with the Comprehensive Plan and found that the project to be compliant. Ms.
         Rahman then turned to an analysis of the rezoning, which staff also finds to be consistent with the
         Comprehensive Plan. The traffic analysis for the proposed project was reviewed. Details of the site plan
         and the project were provided. It was noted that the Planning & Zoning Board recommended approval of
         the future land use map amendment and the rezoning. Staff also recommends approval of both petitions.

         Nora Fosman, Senior Environmental Officer, explained the purpose of a payment in lieu of an
         environmental set-aside. She reviewed the conservation areas on the property. It was noted that the
         applicant has met the requirements consideration of a payment in lieu of a set aside. The method of
         calculation of the payment was reviewed. Ms Fosman noted that the Environmental Advisory Board
         unanimously recommended approval of the payment in lieu. Upon concluding, both Ms. Fosman and Ms.
         Rahman responded to questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Michael Marshall, Gray Robinson on behalf of the applicant, gave a
         PowerPoint presentation addressing the future land use map amendment and the rezoning. He began by
         addressing the history of the property, noting that the new owner has provided a much needed upgrade to
         the development. Details of further planned improvements that necessitated the requested changes were
         reviewed, noting that the proposal exceeds minimum requirements of the Code. Mr. Marshall explained
         the technical deviation with respect to the driveway reservoir. Upon concluding, he responded to
         questions from Council members. John Donaldson, traffic engineer, addressed the trip generation
                                                                                 nd
         analysis for the project as it relates to an increase in traffic on NW 2 Avenue. Council Member Rodgers
         asked that the open space in the rezoning ordinance be limited to 40% to which the applicant agreed.

         Ashley Harden, representing the Board of Directors of the Royal Poinciana community, stated the Board's
         support of the petitions. Lenore Wachtel voiced opposition to increasing density and traffic in the City.
                                                                         nd
         Sally Friedman voiced concern about increased traffic on NW 2 Avenue, a constrained roadway. Irina
                                                             nd
         Borisova voiced concern with the traffic on NW 2 Avenue and opposed increased building in the area.
                                                                                                       nd
         Harold Chafee also commented on congestion in the north end of the City and traffic on NW 2 Avenue.
                                                                  nd
         Robert DuKate commented on the bad traffic on NW 2 Avenue and voiced opposition to increased
         rental units in the City and the resulting traffic.

         There was no cross examination. Mr. Marshall offered rebuttal regarding traffic and the manner in which
         the analysis was done. He emphasized that the proposed improvement to the existing development
         would benefit the neighborhood. Mr. Donaldson then addressed traffic and noted that actual data

         at the train crossing, before responding to further questions. Mr. Marshall addressed a question about
         the demand for rental units. The public hearing was then closed.

                  Resolution No. 9-2017
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the appeal by Yamato
                   Investments LLC of the decision of the Community Appearance Board denying approval of two
                  freestanding signs, the eastern sign with tenant sign copy for three (3) tenants and the western
                  sign with tenant sign copy for four (4) tenants; to be located at 331 and 351 East Yamato Road;
                  providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                           Resolution No. 9-2017

         Mayor Haynie noted that the same qj will govern this hearing. Members made their ex parte disclosures.
         The City Clerk administered the oath to all those who wished to speak.

         John Harbilus, Zoning Officer, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began with the history of the
         various Community Appearance Board (CAB) and City Council reviews of the signs at the site.
         Renderings of the two signs were provided. Staff recommends granting the appeals and reversing the
         CAB decisions. Upon concluding, Mr. Harbilus responded to questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Doug Mummaw, on behalf of the appellant, gave a PowerPoint
         presentation in support of granting the appeals. Upon concluding, he responded to questions. Members
         commented and voiced their opinions.

         Mark Jacobson, CAB chair, addressed the Board's decisions. He noted that the denials were concerning
         the color in the sign panels. Barry Pokrifcsak, sign designer, commented on signs representing company
         brands with color. He then responded to questions. Lenore Wachtel advocated updating the sign code.
         Mr. Jacobson addressed the Learning Experience signs, offered support for having a sign professional on
         the Community Appearance, and responded to questions.

         There was no cross examination. Mr. Mummaw made rebuttal comments regarding establishing a master
         sign criteria. Members commented.

         Council Member Weinroth proposed approval of sign with condition that no panel has more than 3 colors.
         The City Attorney read language that addressed the number of colors. Members commented on the
         allowable number of colors on sign panels. The City Attorney provided clarification. Deputy Mayor
         Mullaugh suggested that the staff recommendation be followed. The City Attorney addressed a second
         option for an amendment which would be to approve with the Sound Advice and Dunkin Donuts panels
         only; other panels would have to be approved by CAB.

                       Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Council Member
                       Scott Singer, to approve Resolution No. 9-2017 granting the appeal and reversing the
                       decision of the Community Appearance with conditions.

         Members offered comments and stated their positions.

                  Motion carried on a 3 to 2 vote; Mayor Susan Haynie, Council Member Scott Singer, Council
                  Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes. Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member
                  Jeremy Rodgers voting no.
