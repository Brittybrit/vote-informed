                                    MINUTES OF THE SPECIAL MEETING
                                              CITY COUNCIL
                                      CITY OF BOCA RATON, FLORIDA
                                           Monday, March 7, 2018
                                                11: 30 AM

              The Special Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was called to order
                                         by Mayor Susan Haynie at 11 :31 AM.

 1.     PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

 2.     ROLL CALL:

          Mayor Susan Haynie
          Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers
          Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke
          Council Member Scott Singer
          Council Member Robert S. Weinroth

 3.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARING:

         A.        Resolution No. 42-2018
                   A resolution of the City of Boca Raton authorizing the Mayor and City Clerk to execute the 2017 -
                   2020 agreement between the City and the Fraternal Order Police Lodge #35, Inc.; providing for
                   severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                            Resolution No. 42-2018

        Linda Davidson, Financial Services Director, briefly reviewed the resolution, noting that the agreement
        had been ratified by the FOP. Upon conclusion, she made herself available for questions.

        The public hearing was opened. John Cagno, President of FOP Lodge #35, urged support of the
        agreement. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                   Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Council Member Andrea
                   Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 42-2018.

        Members made final comments, offered their support and thanked the police for their service.

                   Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council
                   Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S.
                   Weinroth voting yes.

 4.     PUBLIC REQUESTS:

 There were no public requests.

 5.     ADJOURNMENT:

 The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 11 :38 am.
