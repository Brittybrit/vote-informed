        A.      Ordinance No. 5661
                An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Article X, "Multifamily
                Residential Districts," Code of Ordinances, to: (i) specify that the maximum density set forth in
                the R-3, R-3-A, R-3-B, R-3-C, R-4, R-5 and R-5-A multifamily zoning districts is applicable to
                single-family residences and duplexes in such districts (where permitted), and (ii) affirm the
                minimum plot width, minimum plot depth , and minimum plot area for single-family residences
                in the R-4 district; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification;
                providing an effective date (AM-22-09)

        The Mayor noted that this was the second of two public hearings.

        Tori Boone, Zoning Manager, gave the presentation. She began with a brief overview of the text
        amendment proposing the same density for single family vs multi-family development. She advised
        that the Planning & Zoning Board and staff recommended approval. Upon concluding, she made
        herself available for questions.

        C.        Ordinance No. 5667
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton relating to the General Employees' Pension Plan ;
                  providing for a one-time lump sum payment paid for certain retired or disabled general
                  employees; providing for repealer; providing for an effective date

        Linda Davidson, Financial Services Director, gave the presentation. She gave a brief overview and
        advised that the City recommended certain changes in a one-time lump sum of their annual
        retirement benefit. Upon concluding, she made herself available for questions.

        The public comment period was opened. Steve Griffith spoke about the benefits of working for a
        municipality but expressed concerns with the Cost-of-Living Adjustment (COLA) process and
        explained that he was would rather receive the COLA as a raise than a one-time lump sum payment.
        Tim Tritle also spoke in favor of receiving a percentage COLA for the General Employees' Pension.
        Mark Griffith advised that he was in favor of a percentage as a raise instead of the lump sum
        payments. Seeing no one else come forward, the public comment period was closed.

        Further discussion ensued. Mr. Ahnell answered questions raised during the public comment period
        and offered additional insight.

                           Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                           Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5667. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                           Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                           Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

19.    MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte highlighted National Clean Energy week aimed at reducing fossil fuels. She
continued by thanking those who have embraced and installed solar energy systems. Ms. Mayotte concluded
by mentioning the upcoming Timbertech Tournament to be held at the newly renovated Old Course at Broken
Sound Club and was looking forward to attending.

Council Member Yvette Drucker noted that it was Hispanic Heritage month. She gave a report on the TPA
meeting she attended and advised that the public could go online to comment about the Vision 20/50 Long
Reach Transit Plan. Ms. Drucker concluded by recognizing the Florida League of Cities' promotion for Mobility
Week.

Council Member Fran Nachlas thanked staff for working hard on the City's budget, especially for extending
programs without raising taxes. She then highlighted upcoming City events.

Council Member Marc Wigder mentioned the fantastic fiscal position that City Manager Ahnell has led the City
through during his tenure. He continued by discussing the solar initiative and recommended that the City

Mayor Scott Singer praised staffs work regarding staying fisca lly responsible. He continued by discussing a

meet the size requirement. He asked if staff could work to amend the zoning to allow for smaller facilities.
