       A.       First Budget Amendment FY 2024-2025

                1.      Ordinance No. 5724
                        An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the Fiscal Year 2024-2025 Budget
                        through the First Budget Amendment; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                        providing an effective date

       Jim Zervis gave the presentation and briefly explained the proposed amendments. Upon concluding,
       he advised that staff recommended approval and made himself available for questions. Discussion
       ensued and Mr. Zervis provided additional information .

       The public hearing was opened . Anastasia Colas spoke about the importance of vetting proposed new
       software. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

                            Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                            Yvette Drucker, to approve Ordinance No. 5724. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                            Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council
                            Member Andy Thomson , Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes .

        B.      Private Transportation Facilities (Vertiport) Text Amendment


                1.      Ordinance No. 5725
                        An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the City's Comprehensive Plan ,
                        Future Land Use Element, Policy LU .1.1.10, to add private transportation facilities to
                        the list of major semi-public uses, as principal permitted uses in the Institutional (Pl)
                        Future Land Use category; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing
                        an effective date (SC-24-09)


  Regular Meeting Minutes                                 4                                        February 25, 2025
       Jim Bell , Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation . He shared the purpose of the Text
       Amendment and provided background on the growing use of private transportation facilities , or
       vertiports. Mr. Bell shared points from the discussion of the Planning and Zoning Board and advised
       that both the Board and City staff recommend approval. Upon concluding , he made himself available
       for questions. Mr. Bell and the Development Services Director Brandon Schaad answered Members'
       questions and provided additional details. It was noted that this was a transmittal hearing and upon
       approval from the State, it would be brought back before the Council for a final vote.

      The public comment period was opened and , seeing no one come forward, closed.

                            Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                            Fran Nachlas, to transmit Ordinance No. 5725. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                            Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council
                            Member Andy Thomson , Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

       C.       Chapter 7 Fire Prevention and Protection Text Amendment


                1.      Ordinance No. 5726
                        An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton repealing Chapter 7, Code of Ordinances, in
                        its entirety; adopting a new Chapter 7, Code of Ordinances; adopting the Florida Fire
                        Prevention Code; providing for local amendments to the Florida Fire Prevention Code;
                        providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an
                        effective date

                Assistant Chief Jay Sumner gave the PowerPoint presentation. He provided the reasons for
                changes to the Code, a summary of the language revisions , and the requirement to have all
                inspection reports be uploaded to an internet-based reporting system . Assistant Chief Sumner
                advised that staff recommend approval and , upon concluding , made himself available for
                questions.

       The public comment period was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed .

                            Motion was made by Council Member Fran Nachlas, seconded by Council Member
                            Andy Thomson, to approve Ordinance No. 5726. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                            Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nach/as, Council
                            Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.
