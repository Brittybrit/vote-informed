12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A.       Ordinance No. 5476
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Code of Ordinances,
                  authorizing Planned Mobility Development in the City CG Commercial General, City CHO
                  Commercial High Office, R-B-1 Motel-Business and POI Professional, Office and Institutional
                  zoning districts: providing applicable development regulations for Planned Mobility Development:
                  amending Section 28-307(1 ), to specify a maximum floor area ratio of 0.50 for Planned Mobility
                  Developments in certain districts: providing for severability: providing for repealer: providing for
                  codification: providing an effective date (AM-18-08)
                           Ordinance No. 5476

         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The southwest
         planning area that would be affected by the implementation of the planned mobility regulations was
         identified. Comprehensive Plan objectives and policies that are applicable to this area were reviewed.
         Background information on staffs work to date related to the southwest planned mobility area was
         presented. Mr. Schaad discussed the small area plan, noting that the proposed ordinance reflects many
         of the concepts identified during the planning process for the plan. The PMD regulations were reviewed
         and explained. Topics included, but were not limited to, street types and designs, building setbacks and
         height, building step-backs, glazing requirements, valet and street parking, surface parking lots, open
         space, and transit. The Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval. Staff recommended
         approval as well. Mr. Schaad noted that the Planning and Zoning Board asked that staff provide
         information on a funding mechanism for infrastructure improvements.

         The public hearing was opened. Michael Marshall, speaking on behalf of Cypress Realty, addressed the
         ordinance's consistency, or lack thereof, with the Comprehensive Plan. He stated his belief that the
         southwest area needs to have a mixed use in order to support a walkable environment. Gene Folden
         commented on the width of type A roadways and asked that roads not be narrowed and that parking
         spaces not be made smaller. Seeing no one else came forward, the public hearing was closed.

         Mr. Schaad responded to previous comments and emphasized that the ordinance is intended to promote
         a pedestrian friendly area. He then responded to members' questions. The City Attorney responded to
         comments made by Mr. Marshall, disagreeing with his assertions with regard to the Comprehensive Plan.
         She provided additional information. Members asked further questions of Mr. Schaad.

                   Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member
                   Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5476.
