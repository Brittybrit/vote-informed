 .11.:.   QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

          A.      Ordinance No. 5519
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering a long-term lease agreement with Madison
                  Communications of Georgia, Inc., pursuant to Chapter 13, Article Ill, "Sale of City Property," Code
                  of Ordinances, for a portion of the City-owned property located at 1700 Southwest 18th Street known
                  as Alamanda Park (generally in the southeast of the parcel}, for the purpose of construction,
                  maintenance, and operation of a communications tower and associated facilities, pursuant to terms
                  and conditions of the agreement, authorizing a non-exclusive easement for access and utility
                  purposes, as described in the Agreement; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-
                  18-01)

          B.      Resolution No. 27-2020
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a wireless communication facilities site plan
                  approval for a 140 foot tall stealth communication tower and compound area at a City-owned
                  property, Alamanda Park, located at 1700 Southwest 18th Street; considering one (1) modification
                  of development parameters, pursuant to Section 28-1628(17), Code of Ordinances, to authorize a
                  telecommunication tower taller than 100 feet in a residential zoning district; providing for repealer;
                  providing an effective date (WCF-18-01/SPA-18-01)

           The Mayor advised the public hearings on items 11 A. and B. would be conducted contemporaneously
           as the items are related, and a separate vote would be taken on the Ordinance and Resolution. The City
           Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Council Members
           made their ex parte disclosures. The Deputy City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to
           speak.

           Tamash Rahman, Chief Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Ms. Rahman began with a review
           of the applicant's request. An aerial view of the property was presented and existing use of the site was
           described. Terms of the proposed lease were summarized including, but not limited to, yearly rent
           payments, construction costs and the option for the City to utilize the tower at no expense. Photos that
           displayed renderings of the proposed tower were displayed. The Community Appearance Board
           recommended denial by a vote of 3 to 2. The applicants request for additional tower height was discussed.
           Both the Planning and Zoning Board and Development Services Staff recommended approval with no
           conditions. Upon concluding, Ms. Rahman responded to members' questions.

           The public hearing was opened. Michael Hahn, on behalf of the applicant, gave a PowerPoint
           presentation. He began by outlining the wireless coverage area affected by the proposed tower and
           reviewed the applicant's justification for additional tower height. Mr. Hahn displayed photographs
           comparing the proposed tower to existing tower designs. Forecasted revenue and tenant projections were
           provided. Upon concluding, he made himself available for questions. Council Member Thomson
           advocated the addition of an American flag to the tower. Mayor Singer requested clarification regarding
           the potential improvement to wireless coverage and the proposed tower height. Thomas Ward shared his
           support for the project and questioned the cost for small business to rent space on the tower. Frederick
           Pfister inquired if the proposed tower would have lighting on the top for air traffic. Seeing no one else
           come forward, the public hearing was closed. There was no cross examination or rebuttal.

           Members made final comments. There was not consensus among members to add a flag to the tower,
           owing to the amount of maintenance the flag would require, as well as the desire to maintain minimal
           visual impact.
