11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

                  Resolution No. 52-2017
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering an amended and restated master plan/site
                  plan for the Fairway Commons PMD, Resolution No. 13-2015, as amended, to i) amend the
                  approved Planned Mobility Matrix to delete the requirement for an internal shuttle circulator and
                  adjust matrix points allocated to Pedestrian/Bicycle Paths and Physical Character-Amenities to
                  reflect current Matrix scoring methodology, ii) modify the previously approved Shared Parking
                  Analysis to allow additional fast food restaurant floor area and bar area in exchange for a
                  comparable decrease in retail and restaurant floor area and allow for future reallocations of
                  retail/restaurant floor area, and iii) amend conditions related to an updated Overall Master
                  Plan/Site Plan (Sheet C-02), Planned Mobility Matrix Evaluation, Shared Parking Analysis, and
                  modifications to the Master Plan/Site Plan and Shared Parking Analysis to allow for future
Regular Minutes                                        3                                                   May 9, 2017
                  reallocations of retail/restaurant floor area for the Fairway Commons PMD located at 5500-5580
                  North Military Trail; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (PMD-14-01 R2/SPA-14-
                  01 R2)
                           Resolution No. 52-2017            Drawing

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
         made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to all those who wished to speak.

         Jim Bell, Development Services Acting Deputy Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Background
         information on the project was provided. The location of the site was identified and the requested
         modifications were reviewed. Mr. Bell detailed the proposed changes to the Planned Mobility Matrix,
         which would reduce the matrix points, and then reviewed the shared parking analysis. The proposed
         amendments to the Master Plan/Site Plan were listed and the changes to the text of the conditions were
         provided. The Planning & Zoning Board unanimously approved the requested modifications. Staff also
         recommended approval of the petition. Upon concluding, Mr. Bell responded to questions from Council
         members.

         The public hearing was opened. Michael Marshall, GrayRobinson on behalf of the petitioner, discussed
         the timing of the initial approval of this project and the changes to the matrix. He also explained the
         changes that occurred as the project was leased, addressed previous comments and answered further
         questions. Brian Schmier, developer of Fairway Commons, provided further information in response to
         several previous questions. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Scott Singer, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers,
                  to approve Resolution No. 52-2017. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy
                  Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Scott
                  Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes.
