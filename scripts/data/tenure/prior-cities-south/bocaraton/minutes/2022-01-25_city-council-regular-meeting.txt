10.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

         A.      Resolution No. 1-2022
                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a conditional use approval pursuant to Section
                 28-1238(8), Code of Ordinances, to authorize the development of a passive park (Ocean Strand
                 Park) on the portion of an approximately 14.8-acre property located at 2300 North Ocean
                 Boulevard that is west of North Ocean Boulevard; providing for repealer; providing an effective
                 date (CA-21-02)

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing.
         Members made their ex parte disclosures. The Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to
         speak.

         John Lindgren, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began with a review of the
         applicant's request and displayed aerial photographs of the subject location. The site plan of the
         property was shown, and proposed park features were identified. Mr. Lindgren explained the aspects
         of the archeological site on the land and presented a photograph that outlined the principal
         archaeological area that would be avoided. Staff analysis showed that the existing infrastructure and
         proposed amenities were appropriate to the site, that the proposed use is compatible with adjacent
         uses, and that archaeological concerns had been addressed. The required notices and posting on the
         property had been completed. The Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval of the site plan
         and conditional use. Staff recommended approval. Upon concluding, Mr. Lindgren responded to
         members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Carol Perez, landscape architect from AGT Land, gave the PowerPoint
         presentation. She began with a description of the current state of the subject property. Details of the
         proposed features of the park were discussed. A site plan identifying signage and park elements was
         displayed. Upon concluding, she provided additional information regarding bike racks and recycle bins.
         Mayor Singer inquired about the prospect of vehicle parking. Erin Wright, newly elected Chair of the
         Greater Boca Raton Beach and Park District (BPD), told members that she was looking forward to
         developing the relationship between the City and the BPD. Ms. Wright provided a brief history of the
         land and asked for the City's approval. She explained the reasons for not including parking on the site,
         including, but not limited to, parking meter installation, lack of staffing and environmental concerns.
         Upon concluding, she made herself available for questions. Sean Swartz, on behalf of the applicant,
         noted that it was only the conditional use request that was before Council for their consideration, and
         not the site plan. Gabriel Banfi, a resident of a neighboring condominium, expressed his support for the
         park as proposed. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

11.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A       Ordinance No. 5607
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the Boca Raton Downtown Development of
                 Regional Impact (DORI) Development Order to add provisions allowing for and regulations
                 governing space efficient mechanical and automated parking systems; providing for severability;
                 providing for repealer; providing an effective date (AM-20-12)

        Mayor Singer briefly summarized the prior day's CRA discussion regarding the continuation of this public
        hearing to a future meeting date. The City Manager and the City Attorney provided clarification regarding
        the staff presentation and future meeting dates.

                      Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member
                      Monica Mayotte, to continue the public hearing on Ordinance No 5607 to February 8, 2022.

        Deputy City Manager O'Rourke asked if continuing the matter to the next meeting would provide enough
        time for staff to conduct the research requested. City Manager Leif Ahnell advised that the narrow
        timeframe could limit the amount of information staff would be able to collect. Discussion ensued.
        Members examined the need for clearer communication and clarification of expectations between
        Council and staff. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser asked if the direction of the body was for staff to give
        input on the ordinance as it was written, or if staff was to conduct a discretionary review. There was
        consensus from members to have staff give a thorough appraisal of the ordinance and come forward
        with any concerns that might exist. Discussion continued regarding the timeframe of the next hearing
        and the role of staff input on Council Member initiated legislation.

                      Motion carried 4 votes to 1; Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                      Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes; Deputy Mayor
                      Andrea Levine O'Rourke voting no.

        Members made final comments.
