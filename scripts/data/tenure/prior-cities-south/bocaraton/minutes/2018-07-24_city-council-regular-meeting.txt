                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering an amendment to the
                  Master Plan (Resolution No. 37-88. as amended by Resolution No. 164-2005) for Hillsboro El Rio
                  Park. located at 400 Southwest 18th Street to add and remove certain park features and
                  amenities: providing for repealer: providing an effective date (SC-18-04)
                           Resolution No. 88-2018
                           Drawings

        The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
        made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

        Ingrid Allen, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Background information on the park, as
        well as the location, land use and zoning of the site were provided. Ms. Allen reviewed the proposed
        master plan noting elements being removed, such as a fishing pier and boardwalk, and new items being
        added, including sports courts and a non-motorized boat launch. Ms. Allen stated the Parks and
        Recreation Board unanimously approved the proposed master plan; Staff recommends approval as well.
        Upon concluding, she responded to questions about a submerged barge in the canal. Ms. Allen advised
        that the barge was within the jurisdiction of the Lake Worth Drainage District.

        The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Monica
                  Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 88-2018. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,

                  Resolution No. 109-2018
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the application of Blue
                   Martini Boca LLC for a settlement and reduction of two (2) code enforcement liens imposed
                   against property located at 6000 Glades Rd #C1380; providing for severability; providing for
                   repealer; providing an effective date
                           Resolution No. 109-2018

        Doug Sheedy, Code Compliance Manager, gave a brief presentation, providing background information
        on the code violations and circumstances to date, noting that Staff does not support the lien reduction.
        He then made himself available for questions.

        The public hearing was opened. Scott Topolski, attorney on behalf of Blue Martini, provided details of the
        nine alleged violations and liens imposed by the Special Magistrate, noting that Blue Martini has an
        appeal pending on this matter before the Palm Beach County Circuit Court and making his case for the
        reduction. Mr. Topolski then responded to Council members' questions, as did Staff.

        Bob Ziegler, a resident of Via Verde, reviewed the violations and the effect of the loud noise on nearby
        neighborhoods over a number of years. He thanked the City Council and City Staff for their assistance
        throughout this issue. In conclusion, he requested that the fines not be reduced. Seeing no one else
        come forward to speak, the public hearing closed.

                   Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, to adopt
                   Resolution No. 109-2018 to deny the lien reduction request.

        Members made final comments.

                   Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council
                   Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke voting yes.
