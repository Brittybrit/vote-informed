11.    QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

               Resolution No. 31-2016
               A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering an amendment to conditional use approval
               Resolution No. 123-2011 {as amended by Resolution No. 65-2013) (which authorized a school in
               a R-1-A Zoning District) to allow the construction of a 6,981 square foot addition to an existing
               9,311 square foot building, a 7,003 square foot addition to an existing 3,721 square foot building,
               a new 2,350 square foot building and a new 486 square foot clock tower, and parking lot
               modifications to provide twenty-five {25) additional parking spaces {for a total number of parking
               spaces of 688) and to allow three {3) non-residential improvements in a residential district
               exceeding 25 feet in building height: {1) a 29 foot 5 inch building addition; {2) a 28 foot building;
               and (3) a new 35 foot 8 inch clock tower; for St. Andrew's School located at 3900 Jog Road;
               providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date {UC-07-01 R3/CA)
                        Resolution No. 31-2016
                        Plans

       Elizabeth Anderson, Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation, beginning with a review of the petition
       and the location of the site. The improvements, including the Entrepreneurial Center, the gateway
       building, the Learning Lab and the clock tower, were described. Ms. Anderson reviewed the additional
       parking and noted the lack of any traffic impacts. The conditional uses required are for height. PZB
       approved unanimously and staff recommends approval.

       The public hearing was opened. Mary Jo Finnocchiaro, on behalf of St. Andrew's School, briefly
       addressed the School's commitment to excellence in education and the need for these new spaces in that
       endeavor. She expressed thanks for the Council's consideration. Seeing no one else come forward to
       speak, the public hearing was closed.

               Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, seconded by Council Member Jeremy
               Rodgers, to approve Resolution No. 31-2016.

       Members made final comments.

               Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council
               Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth
               voting yes.
