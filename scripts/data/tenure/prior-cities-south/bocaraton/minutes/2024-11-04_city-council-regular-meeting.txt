           C.      Ordinance No. 5715
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the City's Comprehensive Plan by amending
                  Future Housing Element Policy HO.1.5.3 to create the potential to authorize additional density
                  and intensity for a commercial industrial multifamily development (CIMO) that is integrated with
                  the Boca Raton Tri-Rail Station as a bonus and incentive to encourage such development;
                  providing for severability ; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-24-07)

           Mayor Singer noted that this would be the first of two public hearings, and that three votes would be
           required to transmit Ordinance No. 5715.

           Chief Planner Tamash Rahman gave the PowerPoint presentation . She provided a background of
           the code text amendments, and the proposed bonuses and incentives to encourage development of
           the Tri-Rail station property . She advised that the Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval
           and Development Services staff recommended transmittal. Upon concluding , she responded to
           Members' questions and discussion ensued .

           The public hearing was opened . David Milledge gave a PowerPoint presentation on behalf of his
           client and expressed support for the ordinance. Seeing no one else come forward , the public hearing
           was closed .

 13.      PUBLIC REQUESTS:

 Representative Marie Woodson expressed her desire that City employees who are members of SEIU receive
 bonuses equal to those of non-union employees.

 John Clubb spoke on the topic of sexual abuse of children , shared that he was an advocate for ending the
 statute of limitations for prosecuting offenders, and requested the Council's support in his endeavors.

 Alphonso Mayfield, Political Coordinator for the SEIU , Charles Ridley , Matthew Chapnick, Linda Joseph,
 Angela McDonald, Sheila Gaines, Michael Clarke, Afifa Khaliq , and Charmaine Postal all spoke in favor of
 bonuses and re-negotiating a contract for the union employees. George Brown gave a brief history of the most
 recent contract and urged the union employees and representatives to look ahead to upcoming negotiations.

 Discussion ensued. Council Member Thomson advised that he was in favor of staff looking into a one-time
 bonus for the union employees. Deputy Mayor Drucker noted that the current City Manager and Acting City
 Attorney were not part of contract negotiations for the current SEIU contract, and asked Mr. Brown if he could
 research the possibility of providing the requested bonus to Union employees . Council Member Wigder noted
 the recent appointments of the new City Manager and City Attorney , assured union members that their
 upcoming contract would be negotiated in good faith and asked them to give the City Manager and Acting City
 Attorney time to look into their request. Council Member Nachlas echoed comments made by Mr. Wigder,
 shared her appreciation for the Union employees and urged them to work closely with their representatives
 when negotiating. Mayor Singer also thanked the Union employees for their service, highlighted the City
 Manager's addition of the day after Thanksgiving as a City holiday , and noted Mr. Brown's respect for the
 dedication of hard-working City employees.
