16.     RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:

         A.       Resolution No. 1-2019
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering the application of Gaddis Properties LLC for a
                  settlement and reduction of one code enforcement lien imposed against property located at 200
                  East Boca Raton Road; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective
                  date
                           Resolution No. 1-2019

         Doug Sheedy, Code Compliance Manager, gave a brief presentation. He explained the request of the
         owner for a reduction of the lien and noted that staff does not support a reduction of the lien.

         Hans Huang, chief legal officer for Kaufman Lynn Construction, tenant of the property, noted that he was
         appearing on behalf of a number of entities involved in this matter. He asked that the City assist in
         getting the Gaddis property converted into a parking lot. Michael Doyle, Kaufman Lynn project manager
         for Tower 155, noted the company's cooperation at every step of the process for using the property as a
         parking lot and addressed the lengthy time for a hearing on their request. He stated his objection to the
         imposition of back-dated fines and stressed the fact that the site had been restored quickly. Mr. Doyle
         asked for a reduction based on this timing. Mr. Huang provided a handout of an email exchange and
         further information on the lengthy process. Derek Vander Ploeg provided background information on the
         lease of the property for use by Tower 155 as a parking lot. Seeing no one else come forward to speak,
         the public hearing was closed.

         In response to questions, Doug Sheedy gave further details of the timeframe for imposition of the fines.
         Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided additional information on the timing. Mr.
         Huang responded to a previous comment on scheduling of a hearing regarding the lot, and answered
         further questions about timing, as did Mr. Schaad. Members began a discussion of the request. Deputy
         Mayor Rodgers suggested a reduction, perhaps to $20,000. Council Member Thomson suggested a
         reduction of the fine to $10,000. Mayor Singer suggested a fine of $49,000. Further discussion ensued.
         Members agreed that the lien should be reduced by $21,220 to $40,000 to be paid within 15 days.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andrea
                  Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 1-2019 reducing the lien as stated. Motion carried
                  unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica
                  Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

17.     QUASI-JUDICIAL PUBLIC HEARINGS/VARIANCES & APPEALS:

         A.       Resolution No. 21-2019
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the appeal of the decision
                  of the Community Appearance Board denying Modernizing Medicine lnc.'s requested wall sign
                  (Permit No. 18-9195) located at 4850 Network Way; providing for repealer; providing an effective
                  date
                           Resolution No. 21-2019

         Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Attorney confirmed that quasi-judicial procedures
         remained in effect. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.




Regular Minutes                                       5                                            February 12, 2019
         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The location of the
         property was identified and information on the signage and location of same was provided. Mr. Schaad
         discussed the Community Appearance Board (CAB) review and denial of the sign permit and the
         appellant's argument for granting the appeal. He then presented staff's analysis that addressed
         compliance of the sign with the Code, historical precedent, and CAB's authority. Staff recommended
         reversing the decision of CAB and approving the sign permit as submitted. Upon concluding, he
         responded to questions.

         Jennifer Ronneburger and Kathleen Bocek, Senior Vice President of Modernizing Medicine, noted that
         the petitioner agreed with the staff presentation. Mark Jacobsen, CAB chair, offered in detail CAB's
         rationale for the denial of this sign which was to be placed on a building that is architecturally significant.
         He then responded to questions from members. Doug Mummaw provided a handout and commented on
         the significance of the building on which the sign is proposed to be placed. He suggested that the sign
         should be iconic and detached from the building. Danielle Vennett, BRIC General Manager, addressed
         the concerns expressed about the building. She commented on the importance of Modernizing Medicine
         and its importance to the City. She asked that all this be taken into consideration.

         During cross examination, Mark Jacobsen questioned Doug Mummaw, suggesting that a sign could be
         free standing. Ms. Vennett also questioned Mr. Mummaw on his suggestion regarding a free standing
         sign. Mr. Mummaw responded. Jennifer Ronneburger offered rebuttal on behalf of the petitioner. She
         addressed the attachment of the sign to the building as well as other points made by Mr. Jacobsen.
         Kathleen Bocek addressed the historic value of the building and the need for a business sign, noting that
         Modernizing Medicine wants to be a good corporate citizen. She asked that the Council allow them to put
         up the sign. Brandon Schaad responded to Council members' questions. Members commented on the
         petition and offered their opinions.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andy
                  Thomson, to approve Resolution No. 21-2019 to reverse the decision of the Community
                  Appearance Board granting the appeal. Motion carried on a 4 to 1 vote; Mayor Scott Singer,
                  Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy
                  Thomson voting yes; Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke voting no.
