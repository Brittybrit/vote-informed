 12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

           A       Ordinance No. 5540
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the Code of Ordinances pertaining to mobile
                  food dispensing vehicles; deleting Chapter 16, Article Ill, Section 16-58, "Operating Food-Service
                  Vehicles Near Schools," Code of Ordinances, to delete a prohibition on operating a mobile food
                  dispensing vehicle near a school; amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Code of Ordinances, to provide
                  a definition and specify zoning districts where mobile food dispensing vehicles shall be an allowed
                  accessory use; to create Article XV, Division 1, Section 28-1315, "Mobile Food Dispensing
                  Vehicles," to set forth regulations; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for
                  codification; providing an effective date (AM-20-06)

         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation, noting that this was
         the second of two public hearings. A brief summary of the ordinance was provided. The Planning and
         Zoning Board unanimously recommended against adoption of the ordinance, primarily due to concerns
         regarding food-service vehicle owners operating in residential neighborhoods. Development Services staff
         recommends approval and advises the concerns of the Planning and Zoning Board be addressed in a
         separate follow-up ordinance. Upon concluding, he responded to members' questions. The City Attorney
         provided additional information.

         The public hearing was opened, and, seeing no one indicate their desire to speak, closed .

                       Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member
                       Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5540.

         Members expressed concerns regarding the proposed ordinance. At the Mayor's request, City Attorney
         Diana Grub Frieser expounded on recent State statute and advised members that they may consider
         amending the parameters in a future ordinance. Discussion ensued . The City Manager provided additional
         information. Ms. Frieser noted that the proposed ordinance expands the permissions currently available.
         Members made final comments.

                       Motion carried 4 to O; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council
                       Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
