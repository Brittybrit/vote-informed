                  Citizens' Pedestrian and Bikeway Advisory Board

         Terry Stern and Holli Sutton interviewed.

                  Council Member Monica Mayotte nominated Holli Sutton. Mayor Scott Singer nominated Terry
                  Stern. In the voting, Ms. Sutton received two votes, Ms. Stern received three votes. Therefore,
                  Ms. Stern was appointed to the Citizens' Pedestrian and Bikeway Advisory Board.

         D.       Community Advisory Panel

         Linda Weiss Rose, Michael Bell and Cristina Escalante interviewed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                  Rodgers, to reappoint Linda Weiss Rose, Jay Van Vechten and Cristina Escalante to the
                  Community Advisory Board. Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke nominated Michael Bell.
                  Ms. Weiss Rose received four votes, Mr. Van Vechten received five votes, Ms. Escalante
                  received four votes and Mr. Bell received two. Therefore, Ms. Weiss Rose, Mr. Van Vechten and
                  Ms. Escalante were reappointed to the Community Advisory Panel.

                  General Employees' Pension Board

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member Monica
                  Mayotte to reappoint the incumbents to the General Employees' Pension Board. Motion carried
                  unanimously on a voice vote.

         E.       Marine Advisory Board

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member Andrea
                  Levine O'Rourke, to reappoint Garry O'Donnell to the Marine Advisory Board. Motion carried
                  unanimously on a voice vote.

                  Planning and Zoning Board

         Thomas Zeichman and Jean DaRocha interviewed. Derek Vander Ploeg and Mark Jacobsen withdrew
         their applications due to potential voting conflicts and asked that consideration be given for professionals
         to serve on the Planning and Zoning Board. The City Attorney explained how ethics rules concerning
         voting conflicts affect the Planning and Zoning Board and Community Appearance Board differently.
         Discussion followed. Council members agreed to vote on the incumbent and re-advertise the open
         position for the following Council Meeting.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member
                  Monica Mayotte, to reappoint Thomas Zeichman to the Planning and Zoning Board. Motion
                  carried unanimously on a voice vote .

16.     RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:

Mayor Singer reviewed the previous day's discussion about upcoming canal clearing by the Lake Worth Drainage
District (LWDD). The City Attorney provided copies of the 2003 interlocal agreement that addressed notification
prior to the commencement of canal clearings. She agreed that a meeting with LWDD officials should be held to
discuss future action and advised a resolution for dispute resolution could be adopted.

The City Attorney provided information about squatters on the golf course site and cooperation with the District.
She also requested authorization to schedule three executive sessions to receive advice in regards to pending
litigation in the following cases: Case No. 9: 18-cv-80771-RLR, known as the Conversion Therapy case; Case No.
50-2018-CA-012368 XXXX-MB, known as Hidden Valley/ Boca Royale case; and Case No. 50-2018-CA-013466
XXXX-MB, known as the Midtown-Bert Harris case.

                   Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member Andrea
                   Levine O'Rourke, to authorize the executive sessions as requested. Motion carried unanimously;
                   Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                   Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

20.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member O'Rourke commented on the successful call to artists for the beach tunnel mural project at
Spanish River Park.

Council Member Thomson commended the Police Department's response to the recent lockdown at Boca Raton
Community High School.

Council Member Mayotte discussed the recent student leadership conference held at Boca Raton High School,
the launch of the Coastal Connection program at Gumbo Limbo's Sea Turtle Day, and the successful Boating and
Beach Bash. She emphasized the importance of home rule and encouraged citizens to get involved.

Deputy Mayor Rodgers concurred with Ms. Mayotte's comments on importance of home rule and provided
information regarding the upcoming application deadline for the FAU Tech Runway competition.

Mayor Singer highlighted upcoming Downtown events and passed along recent compliments he had received
about City programs and services.

21.     ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 7: 11 PM.
