                              MINUTES OF THE FINAL BUDGET HEARING
                                          CITY COUNCIL
                                  CITY OF BOCA RATON , FLORIDA
                                  TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 22 , 2026
                                             5:15 PM

The Final Budget Hearing of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton , Florida was held in the Auditorium at
      6500 Congress Avenue. The meeting was called to order by Mayor Andy Thomson at 5:15 PM .

1.      PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

2.     ROLL CALL:

       Mayor Andy Thomson
       Deputy Mayor Michelle Grau
       Council Member Yvette Drucker
       Council Member Jon Pearlman - Attended Remotely
       Council Member Stacy L. Sipple

3.     REBUDGETING OF FY 2025 - 2026 BUDGET APPROPRIATIONS:

       Deputy Mayor Michelle Grau recused herself from the discussions of grant funds for three non-profit
       applicants, citing a conflict of interest regarding her husband's accounting firm , Grau and Associates .
       Members reached consensus on the amounts for Aid to Victims of Domestic Abuse , Mae Volen Senior
       Center, and Vets Helping Heroes.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                        Stacy L. Sipple, to accept the appropriation of funds for the first group of non-profits.
                        Motion passed 4 to O on a voice vote.

        Members, including Deputy Mayor Grau , then discussed allocations to the remaining non-profit
        applicants and there was consensus to approve the final adjusted amounts.

                       Motion was made by Council Member Stacy L. Sipple, seconded by Council Member
                       Yvette Drucker, to accept the appropriation of funds for the second group of non-profits.
                       Motion passed unanimously on a voice vote.

       Michael Middleman , Deputy Director of the Office of Management & Budget, presented the proposed
       expenditure and revenue amendments to the FY 2026-27 Budget, as detailed in Exhibit 1. The
       presentation also addressed operating re-budgets in Exhibit 2 and Capital Improvement Program (CIP)
       re-budgets in Exhibit 3. The updated General Fund Balance Sheet, including the Fund Balance
       Reserve, Retirement Sustainability Reserve, and Hurricane/Disaster Emergency Reserve, was
       presented as Exhibit 4. Upon concluding , he made himself available for Members' questions.

 4.    PUBLIC HEARING TO FINALIZE THE BUDGET AND ADOPT MILLAGE RATES:

        A       Ordinance No. 5798
                An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton adopting final millage rates and levying ad valorem
                taxes for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2026 and end ing September 30, 2027; stating
                the percentage by which the millage to be levied exceeds the "rolled-back rate"; directing the
                City Manager or the Office of Management and Budget Director to adjust the adopted millage
                rates in the event of changes in the assessment roll and taxable value; providing for
                severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
              B.        Ordinance No. 5799
                        An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton adopting a final budget and appropriating funds for
                        the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2026 , and ending September 30, 2027; providing for
                        severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

                        The public hearing was opened . Earl Starkoff questioned if the re-budgets would affect the
                        tax rates for next year and Mr. Zervis advised that the re-budgeted items would not have any
                        impact on taxes. Seeing no one else come forward , the public hearing was closed .

                        Mr. Zervis read into the record the proposed operating millage rate of 3.6476 , which is 4.39%
                        greater than the rolled-back rate of 3.4941 . The voter approved debt service millage rate is
                        0.0163 and the total proposed millage rate is 3.6639.

                                  Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                                  Stacy L. Sipple, to approve Ordinance No. 5798. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                                  Andy Thomson, Deputy Mayor Michelle Grau, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                                  Council Member Jon Pearlman, Council Member Stacy L. Sipple, voting yes.

                                  Motion was made by Council Member Stacy L. Sipple, seconded by Council Member
                                  Yvette Drucker, to approve Ordinance No. 5799 with Exhibits 1, 2, and 3. Motion
                                  carried unanimously; Mayor Andy Thomson, Deputy Mayor Michelle Grau, Council
                                  Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Jon Pearlman, Council Member Stacy L.
                                  Sipple voting yes.

  5.        RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:

             A.        Resolution No.117-2026
                       A resolution of the City of Boca Raton adopting the FY 2026/27-2031/32 Capital
                       Improvements Program; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an
                       effective date

                        Mr. Zervis advised that the Capital Improvements Program (CIP) had been provided to Council
                        and made available to the public, along with Exhibits 1,2, and 3.

                        The public hearing was opened and , seeing no one come forward , closed .

                                  Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                                  Stacy L. Sipple, to approve Resolution No. 117-2026. Motion carried unanimously;
                                  Mayor Andy Thomson, Deputy Mayor Michelle Grau, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                                  Council Member Jon Pearlman, Council Member Stacy L. Sipple voting yes.

  6.        ADJOURNMENT:

           The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 6:05 PM .
