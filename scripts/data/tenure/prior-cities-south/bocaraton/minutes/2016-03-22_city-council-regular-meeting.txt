12.    REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

        A.     Ordinance No. 5343
               An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Sections 16-32 and 16-33, Code of
               Ordinances, to prohibit commercial loading and unloading from public rights-of-way within the
               Community Redevelopment Area; including a reference to the definition of "collection services"
               and correcting a cross-reference; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for
               codification; providing an effective date
                        Ordinance No. 5343

       Ruby Childers, Downtown Manager, gave the presentation. She noted the revisions that were made to
       the ordinance in response to Council's direction at the March 8 Council meeting. Streets on which
       loading and unloading would be prohibited were identified. Upon concluding, Ms. Childers made herself
       available for questions.

       The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed. The City Attorney
       noted that there was a scrivener's error, on page 3, line 11 of the ordinance, a missing comma after
       'Road.' Deputy Mayor Weinroth asked if a Chamber of Commerce representative wanted to comment;
       they did not.

                Motion was made by Council Member Michael Mu/laugh, seconded by Council Member Jeremy
                Rodgers, to approve Ordinance No. 5343 as amended.

       Members made final comments.

                Motion passed 4 to 1; Mayor Susan Haynie, Council Member Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member
                Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott Singer voting yes; Deputy Mayor Robert S. Weinroth
                voting no.

               Ordinance No. 5344
               An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton relating to the City's canvassing board; amending Section
               6-11, Code of Ordinances, to designate the City Clerk as chairperson; providing for severability;
               providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date
                       Ordinance No. 5344

       Susan Saxton, City Clerk, briefly explained the nature of the ordinance and then made herself available
