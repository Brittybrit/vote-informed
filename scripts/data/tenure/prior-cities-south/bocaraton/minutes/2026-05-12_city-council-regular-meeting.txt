 11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A.       Resolution No. 45-2026 Holiday Inn & Suites CIMO
                   A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering , for the approximately 5.02-acre property
                   generally located at 701 NW 53rd Street, an amendment to an approved site plan (Planning
                   and Zoning Board Resolution No. 88-06) to authorize a Commercial Industrial Multifamily
                   Development (CIMO), which includes the following: (A) the conversion of an existing
                   approximately 83,697 square foot hotel building into a 125-unit residential building, including
                   10% affordable and 5% workforce units; (8) the conversion of an existing amenities building
                   into a 5,545 square foot restaurant with outdoor dining; and (C) the conversion of internal
                   ground floor space to create three (3) retail units totaling 4,501 square feet, together with both
                   on-site and off-site improvements ; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SPA-87-
                   25R1/CIMD)

           The City Attorney read the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing. Members
           made their ex-parte disclosures, and the City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to
           speak.

           Senior Planner Eoin Devlin gave the PowerPoint presentation . He reviewed the location of the
           proposed project, components of the applicant's request, and the history of the property. The site
           plan and elevations were shown. Mr. Devlin advised that both the Community Appearance Board and
           Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval , and staff recommended approval subject to
           conditions. Upon concluding, he made himself available for questions.



Regular Meeting Minutes                                    4                                              May 15, 2026
          The public hearing was opened. Attorney for the applicant David Milledge gave a PowerPoint
          presentation . He reviewed the applicant's request and noted improvements to the property which
          would also benefit the public use along the El Rio Trail. He advised that the proposed project met or
          exceeded all zoning requirements and traffic to the site would be reduced . Upon concluding, he
          responded to Members' questions. Richard Warner, Mike Liebelson , and Meredith Madsen spoke in
          favor of the project. Seeing no one else come forward , the public hearing was closed .

                          Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                          Stacy Sipple, to approve Resolution No. 45-2026.

          The City Attorney asked the applicant to confirm the proffer of 5 parking spaces for public use.
          Discussion ensued and Brandon Schaad read the amended language. Further discussion ensued.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                          Stacy Sipple, to approve Resolution No. 45-2026 as amended with the language read
                          by Mr. Schaad. Motion passed 4 votes to 1; Mayor Andy Thomson, Deputy Mayor
                          Michelle Grau, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Stacy Sipple voting
                          yes; Council Member Jon Pearlman voting no.
