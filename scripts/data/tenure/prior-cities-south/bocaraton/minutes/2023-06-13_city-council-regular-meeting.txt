        The Mayor noted that the public hearings on items 10.A., 10.A.1., and 10.A.2. would be conducted
        contemporaneously as the items were related, and pursuant to quasi-judicial procedures as required
        for items 10.A. 1. and 1O.A.2. He noted that a separate vote would be taken on each item and should
        item 10.A. not be adopted, items 10.A.1. and 10.A.2. would not be considered. The City Attorney
        reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Council Members made
        their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

        Chief Planner Tamashbeen Rahman gave the PowerPoint presentation. She provided a brief history
        of the project and outlined components of the requested amendment. The requested modifications,
        technical deviations, and driveway variance were reviewed. Renderings of the proposed building and
        traffic impacts were also discussed. The Planning & Zoning Board recommended approval of the
        application and staff recommended approval with a proposed recommendation of approval regarding
        the hours of operation of pool amenity areas. Upon concluding, Ms. Rahman made herself available
        for Members' questions. Discussion ensued and Traffic Engineer Naresh Machavarapu provided
        additional information regarding traffic and mobility concerns.

        The public hearing was opened. Attorney Bonnie Miskel, on behalf of the applicant, gave a
        presentation. Ms. Miskel reviewed the project history and the requested modifications and variance.
        She reviewed modifications to the original site plan and explained the proposed circulation plan
        through the property. Upon concluding, Ms. Miskel responded to Members' questions. Discussion
        ensued . On behalf of the applicant, Juan Pablo Alfonso and architect Roger Grave de Peralta provided
        additional information. Development Services Director Brandon Schaad added clarification regarding
        drainage requirements. Keith Nelson, of the Riviera Civic Association (RCA), spoke against the pool
        bar and asked for a stipulation that the bar not be staffed. Kevin Meaney stated that he was in favor
        of the proposed changes. Cynthia Wohl inquired how the berm would retain or defuse a flooding
        situation and asked if the residents could be advised to be considerate of the single-family homes
        along Wavecrest Way. Glenn Gromann spoke in favor of the project and encouraged Members'
        approval. Katy McDougal advised that members of the RCA were not against the proposed pool but
        were opposed to the commercial operation of the bar area. Seeing no one else come forward, the
        public hearing was closed.

        There was no cross examination. Ms. Miskel offered final comments on behalf of the applicant and
        responded to additional questions from Council Members. Further discussion ensued regarding the
        use of the proposed pool area.

                       Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                       Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5650.

        City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser and Mr. Schaad provided additional clarification. At the Mayor's
        request, Ms. Frieser read into the record a proposed condition which prohibited amplified sound in the
        outside areas between 8pm and 8am. Ms. Frieser and Mr. Schaad then confirmed that the restricted
        hours for the pool amenities area would replace condition number 10 and the condition regarding
        amplified noise would become condition number 11. Ms. Frieser noted that the conditions would apply
        to both Ordinance No. 5650 and Resolution No. 54-2023.

                       Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                       Fran Nachlas, to amend the motion on the floor to include conditions 10 and 11. Motion
                       carried unanimously on a voice vote.

                       Motion to approve Ordinance No. 5650 as amended carried unanimously: Mayor Scott
                       Singer, Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                       Member Fran Nachlas, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

19.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

 application for that Community to be added to the National Register of Historic Places. Ms. Mayotte requested
 that a resolution be drafted expressing support for the designation. There was consensus from Members to
 have staff prepare the resolution for the next Council Meeting.

 Council Member Nachlas requested that the consideration of Ordinance No. 5652 be postponed until after
 the August 21 st Joint Meeting with the Beach and Park District. City Manager Leif Ahnell provided additional
 information. Council Members weighed in and discussion ensued. Members agreed to postpone the public
 hearing of the ordinance until after the Joint Meeting.

 Council Member Wigder gave a brief presentation on the Institute for Elected Officials he attended that was
 hosted by the Florida League of Cities. Mr. Wigder shared some of the topics that were covered and
 expressed his gratitude for the opportunity to attend.

 everyone a safe summer.
