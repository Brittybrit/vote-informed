                           MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING
                                        CITY COUNCIL
                                CITY OF BOCA RATON, FLORIDA
                                         APRIL 1, 2024
                                           10:30 AM

The Organizational Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was held in the Auditorium
     at 6500 Congress Avenue. The meeting was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at 10:30 AM.·

1.     CALL TO ORDER:

2.     PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

3.     ROLL CALL:
         Mayor Scott Singer
         Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte
         Council Member Yvette Drucker
         Council Member Fran Nachlas
         Council Member Marc Wigder

4.     CEREMONIAL PROCEEDINGS:

        A.     Recognition of and comments by outgoing Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte

        The City Manager, City Attorney, Mayor, and Council Members each commented on Deputy Mayor
        Mayotte's tenure on Council, thanking her for her support, her passion for the environment and
        sustainability, and dedication to public service.

        Ms. Mayotte expressed her gratitude to her family, friends, colleagues, staff, and the community for
        their support and urged her colleagues to be bold and take risks to make great things happen in Boca
        Raton. She then highlighted various pinnacles throughout her tenure.

        The Mayor and Council Members presented a plaque to Ms. Mayotte in recognition of her service.

        B.     Administration of Oath of Office for re-elected Council Member Yvette Drucker and newly
               elected Council Member Andy Thomson

        Selma Drucker administered the oath of office to Council Member-elect Yvette Drucker.

        David Cassidy, Lead Pastor of Spanish River Church, administered the oath of office to Council
        Member-elect Andy Thomson.

         C.    Comments by re-elected Council Member Yvette Drucker and newly elected Council Member
               Andy Thomson

        Council Member Yvette Drucker thanked the community for re-electing her and shared her excitement
        about the new term to come and the opportunity to continue to serve the City. She then welcomed Mr.
        Thomson back to the Council and thanked everyone for their support, including her campaign team,
        friends, colleagues, and family.

        Council Member Thomson commented on his past service on City Council and expressed his
        thankfulness to be able to serve again. He thanked his family, campaign team, and his colleagues for
        their support.
         D.       Comments by Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Fran Nachlas, and Council Member Marc
                  Wigder

         The Mayor and Council Members welcomed Council Members Drucker and Thomson back to the dais.

5.      ORGANIZATIONAL PROCEEDINGS:

         A.       Selection of Deputy Mayor

                            Motion was made by Council Member Fran Nachlas, seconded by Council Member
                            Marc Wigder, to appoint Yvette Drucker as Deputy Mayor. Motion carried unanimously
                            on a voice vote.

          B.      Selection of CRA Chair and Vice Chair

                            Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Deputy Mayor Yvette
                            Drucker, to appoint Fran Nachlas as CRA Chair and Andy Thomson as Vice Chair.
                            Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

          C.      Selection of Auditor Selection Committee Chair (Ordinance No. 5690)

                            Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                            Andy Thomson, to appoint Marc Wigder as Auditor Selection Committee Chair. Motion
                            carried unanimously on a voice vote.

          D.       Selection of Affordable Housing Advisory Committee Representative (Ordinance No. 5691)

                            Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                            Fran Nachlas, to appoint Marc Wigder as the Affordable Housing Advisory Committee
                            Representative. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

6.       OTHER BUSINESS:

          A.       Selection of TPA (Transportation Planning Agency) Representatives and Alternates

                             Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                             Marc Wigder, to designate Yvette Drucker as the first representative and Fran Nachlas
                             as the second representative to the TPA. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

                             Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker,
                             to designate Scott Singer, Andy Thomson, and Marc Wigder as the TPA Alternates at
                             large. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

           B.      Selection of Palm Beach County League of Cities Voting Delegate and Alternate

                             Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                             Fran Nachlas, to appoint Yvette Drucker as the PBC League of Cities Voting Delegate.
                             Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

                             Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Marc Wigder,
                             to appoint Andy Thomson as the PBC League of Cities Voting Alternate. Motion carried
                             unanimously on a voice vote.
