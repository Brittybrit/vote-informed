11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         Mayor Haynie noted that the public hearings on Items A., B., C., D., and E. would be conducted
         contemporaneously and pursuant to quasi-judicial procedures. A separate vote would taken on each
         ordinance and resolution. Ordinance No. 5428 would require four votes for passage. The City Attorney
         reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures. Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk
         administered the oath to those who wished to speak..

         Elizabeth Eassa, Development Services Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The changes to the
         project since it was first presented to City Council were reviewed. Topics included, but were not limited
         to, the site plan, trip generation, and the modified condition related to a voluntary contribution to the
         School District as detailed in the school capacity availability determination (SCAD) letter. Ms. Eassa
         stated staff's recommendation for approval, subject to the conditions in the ordinances and resolutions.

         The public hearing was opened. Heather Allen, on behalf of the applicant, summarized the revised
         project and provided a history of the property. She addressed the project's consistency with the City's
         Comprehensive Plan, the rezoning, and the project's trip generation. The revised site plan was then
         reviewed, noting the decrease in number of units and the number of parking spaces along the access
         road. The technical deviation regarding on-street circulation was addressed and the latest school
         concurrency letter was reviewed. In conclusion, Ms. Allen advocated approval of the project and then
         responded to questions. She noted that her client agreed that the condition that the voluntary contribution
         to the School District be held by the City was acceptable.

         Susan Rentler expressed concern with the amount of traffic that the project will generate and noted her
         opposition to the rezoning and the height of the buildings. Maria Tejara, City Traffic Engineer, addressed
         the possibility of channeling to reduce some of the traffic concerns. Mike Rentler expressed his concerns
         with traffic, the lack of parking, and the height of the townhomes among other issues. He stated his
         opposition to the project. Glenn Gromann addressed some of the concerns expressed previously as well
         as school concurrency. He advocated approval. Charlie Helton offered support of the project. Joanne
         Wise, after being sworn in, advocated that the land remain vacant or that the number of units be
         decreased. She expressed opposition to the project. There was no cross examination. In rebuttal, Ms.
         Allen responded to some of the previous comments, including traffic through the neighborhood. Seeing
         no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.
