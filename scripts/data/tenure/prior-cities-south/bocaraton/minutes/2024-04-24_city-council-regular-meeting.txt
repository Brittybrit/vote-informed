17.       CITY MANAGER RECOMMENDATIONS AND REPORTS:

Mr. Brown reminded the Council Members that Boca Bash will be Saturday and that Wildflower and Silver
Palm Parks will be closed to the public. He also noted that the City will be sponsoring the Chamber of
Commerce's breakfast for the month of May at the Broken Sound Club.

18.       CITY ATTORNEY REPORTS:

The City Attorney had no report.

 19.      MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Wigder shared a photo from his recent attendance at the Florida League of Cities Institute
for Elected Municipal Officials. He then commented on the first meeting of the City's Auditor Selection
Committee, congratulated the Financial Services team on the recent Department Head transition, and
reported on a community meeting of the Boca Raton Housing Authority.

Council Member Nachlas reported on the rehiring of Transportation Analyst Pia Hansson Nunoo and the
quest for achieving silver status from the League of American Bicyclists. She then commented favorably
on a recent Tri-Rail tour and events that she had attended City-w ide.

Mayor Singer commented on the Boca Bash, noted that it was not a City-sponsored event and cautioned
all to boat safely. He then highlighted the reopening of the James A. Rutherford Park and the upcoming
Shred event.

 20.      ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting adjourned without objection at approximately 3:43 PM.
