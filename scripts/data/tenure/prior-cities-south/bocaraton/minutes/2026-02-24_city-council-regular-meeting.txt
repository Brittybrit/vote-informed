        The Mayor noted that the public hearings on 11 .A and B would be conducted contemporaneously as
        the items were interrelated. He noted that a separate vote would be taken on each item, and should
        item 11 .A not be approved , then no vote would be taken on the remaining item. The City Attorney read
        the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing. Members made their ex-parte
        disclosures, and the City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

         Senior Planner Eoin Devlin gave the PowerPoint presentation . He provided a history of the proposed
         areas affected , reviewed the applicants request, and highlighted the site plan . He advised that the
         application would not lead to an increase in onsite activities or an increase in traffic. Mr. Devlin noted
         that the application was for a relocation of an existing use already on the property, consistent with the
         Comprehensive Plan and complied with all applicable codes. He further advised that the Planning and
         Zoning Board , Community Appearance Board , and staff recommended approval. Upon concluding ,
         he made himself available for Members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened . On behalf of the applicant, attorney Bonnie Miske! responded to
         Members' questions and reviewed the site plans and project renderings. Stephanie Rosenweig and
         Joseph Pujalski stated their opposition to the project. Bonnie Miske! clarified comments made during
         the public hearing. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

         A.       Ordinance No. 5757
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 2, "Administration ," Article II , "City
                  Council ," Section 2-27, "Rules of Procedure," of the City Code of Ordinances; updating and
                  modernizing City Council meeting procedures; providing for severability; providing for
                  repealer; providing for codification ; providing an effective date.

          City Manager Mark Sohaney gave a brief presentation detailing the proposed changes and updates
          to the Rules of Procedure. City Attorney Joshua Koehler advised that there had been no significant
          update to the rules since 1966 and they should reflect how the Council operated today. He further
          advised that, going forward , the Roberts Rules of Order to be used would be the latest edition .

          The public hearing was opened. Pam Paschke and Richard Warner asked various questions related
          to the changes. Joseph Pujalski questioned the City's policy concern ing various topics. Seeing no
          one else come forward , the public hearing was closed . Discussion ensued, and Mayor Singer and
          Mr. Koehler responded to questions and provided additional information.

         B.      Ordinance No. 5767
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Code of Ordinances,
                 amending Article I, Division 1, Section 28-2 , "Definitions," to create a definition of
                 "Freestanding Emergency Facility" and amending the definition of "Outpatient Surgery Center"
                 to exclude emergency room procedures from said definition, amending Article XI, "Business
                 and Commercial Districts," to provide that Freestanding Emergency Facilities shall be
                 Conditional Uses in the B-4, MC, City CG and City CHO zoning districts, and amending Article
                 XV, "Supplementary District Regulations," Division 1, to create a new Section 28-1320, Code
                 of Ordinances, to set forth eligibility criteria for properties to include a Freestanding
                 Emergency Facility ; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification ;
                 providing an effective date (AM-25-11)

          Public Works and Engineering Director Brandon Schaad gave the PowerPoint presentation, noting
          that this was the continuation of the 2nd of two public hearings on this item. He began with a
          background of the project, summary of the text amendment, and showed a map of areas eligible for
          free-standing emergency facilities. He described additional research done of neighboring cities that
          permit FSEF's and noted that Palm Beach County is considering a similar ordinance. The Planning
          and Zoning Board and staff recommended approval as presented . Upon concluding, Mr. Schaad
          responded to Members' questions and discussion ensued .

          The public hearing was opened. John Donaldson advised that he performed traffic studies on this
          use in other areas and was concerned with the data calculations. Dr. Evan Goldstein provided
          highlights of his experience in emergency medicine . Discussion ensued and Dr. Goldstein provided
          additional information in response to Members' questions. Josh Brodie shared his thoughts about
          the policy and procedural events that led to the item's postponement. Jeffrey Barker, Attorney for a
          prior applicant, stated his issues with parking and traffic ingress and egress. Gila Brand spoke in
          favor of the project and relayed concerns about the delay of the ordinance. Jonathan Ounjian spoke
          in favor of FSEF's in the LIRP district. Richard Warner shared his disappointment with the process of
          getting this project approved . Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed .
          Extended Discussion ensued .


 The meeting recessed at 8:23 P.M. and resumed at 8:39 P.M.


          There was consensus to postpone the item for discussion at the next Council Workshop Meeting on
          March 23, 2026, and the public hearing for the Regular Meeting on March 24, 2026.

                          Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, seconded by Council Member
                          Yvette Drucker to postpone Ordinance No. 5767 to the next regular Council Meeting
                          on March 24, 2026. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

         H.      Ordinance No. 5777
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 6, "Elections," Section 6-16, "Proof
                 of Residency and Elector Status, " Code of Ordinances, to increase the number of required
                 documents supporting a candidate's residency affidavit for the offices of Mayor and City
                 Council Member from two (2) to three (3); to add to the list of acceptable documents; and to
                 require submission of a current voter information card confirming elector status at the time of
                 qualifying; and to provide for the use of voting history as evidence of nonresidency; providing
                 for severability ; providing for repealer; providing for codification ; providing an effective date

         City Manager Mark Sohaney gave a brief history of the proposed amendment. City Attorney Joshua
         Koehler detailed the proposed changes and advised that the existing residency requirements were
         being strengthened . Discussion ensued .

         The public hearing was opened . Pam Paschke questioned proof of residency for renters. Richard
         Warner stated that he agreed with the proposed changes. Joe Majhess suggested two years of
         residency and expressed his support of the ordinance. Seeing no one else come forward, the public
         hearing was closed . Extended discussion ensued.

                          Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Yvette
                          Drucker, to approve Ordinance No. 5777. Motion carried unanimously; Council
                          Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc
                          Wigder, Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas voting yes.
