11.    QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

        A       Boca Raton Christian School

                1)       Resolution No. 55-2025
                        A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering , for the Boca Raton Christian School ,
                        an approximately 19.84-acre property generally located at 470 Northwest 4th Avenue:
                        (i) an amended and restated conditional use approval consolidating City Council
                        Resolution Nos. 309-77 , 19-79, 97-80 , 52-93 , 82-95 and 93-2008 ; and (ii) a conditional
                        use amendment to increase the student enrollment from 800 students to 950 students
                        in the single-family residential (R-1-D) and multifamily residential (R-3 and R-4) zoning
                        districts; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (CA-24-04)

                2)       Resolution No. 56-2025
                        A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering , for the Boca Raton Christian School ,
                        an approximately 19. 84-acre property generally located at 470 Northwest 4th Avenue,
                        five (5) technical deviations from Section 23-190(d) , Code of Ordinances, as follows: (i)
                        for an existing driveway on Northwest 4th Avenue, to reduce the required driveway
                        reservoir distance from 100 feet to 25 feet, (ii) for an existing driveway on Crawford
                        Boulevard , to reduce in the required driveway reservoir distance from 100 feet to 15
                        feet, eliminate a required four (4) foot wide median separator, and to reduce the required
                        number of egress lanes from two (2) to one (1 ), and (iii) for an existing driveway on
                        Northwest 7th Street, to reduce the required driveway reservoir distance from 100 feet
                        to 40 feet; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-25-04)

        The Mayor advised that the public hearings would be conducted contemporaneously as the items
        were interrelated . He noted that a separate vote would be taken on each item and should item 11.A.1.
        not be approved , no vote would be taken on the remaining item. The City Attorney read the quasi­
        judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing . Members made their ex-pa rte disclosures.
        The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

        Jacob German , Planner II , gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began with a review of the
        applicant's request, including the project location , history of the school's 2008 master plan , and
        approved site plan . Mr. German advised that staff supported the requested technical deviations, and
        he shared a drawing of the property's queuing diagram. Staff analysis found the proposed project
        was consistent and compatible with the City's Comprehensive Plan . The Planning and Zoning Board
        and Staff recommended approval. Upon concluding , Mr. German responded to Council Members'
        questions and Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided additional information.

        The public hearing was opened . Bonnie Miske!, representing the applicant, gave a brief PowerPoint
        presentation which reviewed the proposed conditional use and technical deviations. Pastor Cameron
        Free , speaking on behalf of the school , provided additional information on the history of the school
        and asked for Council's support. Project architect Juliet Schiff and traffic engineer John Donaldson
        provided additional information. Ms. Miske! concluded by reviewing the criteria for the technical
        deviation of driveways and made herself and her colleagues available for questions. Seeing no one
        else come forward , the public hearing was closed . There was no cross examination or summation .

13.    REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS/SETTLEMENTS:

        A.      Lien Reduction - 1489 W Palmetto Park Road

                1)       Resolution No. 53-2025
                        A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering the application of 1489 Mortgagee,
                        LLC , for a settlement and reduction of one (1) code enforcement lien imposed against
                        property located at 1489 West Palmetto Park Road ; providing for severability ; providing
                        for repealer; providing an effective date

        Development Services Deputy Director Erin Sita gave the PowerPoint presentation and provided a
        case summary of the code violations that led to the lien. She advised that staff recommended a 75%
        reduction of the total lien and , upon concluding , responded to Members' questions.

        The public hearing was opened . Elle Zachariades , attorney for the owner, gave a brief PowerPoint
        presentation, provided additional information about the property , and noted that the lien had been
        applied under the building 's previous owners. Anuj Grover, on behalf of Grover Corlew, described the
        timeline for the purchase of the property. Ms. Zachariades concluded by requesting a 100% reduction
        of the fine .

                            Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                            Fran Nachlas, to approve Resolution No. 53-2025 and impose the reduced fine of
                            $30,000.

       Discussion ensued and Council Members weighed in .

                            Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder to further reduce the fine to $7,500.

                            Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson to reduce the fine to $0.

        The discussion continued and Ms. Zachariades, Mr. Grover, Mr. Schaad and City Attorney Joshua
        Koehler provided additional information.

                            Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council Member
                            Andy Thomson, to amend the motion on the floor and approve Resolution No. 53-2025
                            with a reduction of 100% of the lien. Motion to amend passed unanimously on a voice
                            vote.

       Mr. Brown clarified that Section 1 of the resolution would now reflect that the total amount of the lien
       due was zero.

                            Motion to approve Resolution No. 53-2025 as amended carried unanimously; Mayor
                            Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                            Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.
