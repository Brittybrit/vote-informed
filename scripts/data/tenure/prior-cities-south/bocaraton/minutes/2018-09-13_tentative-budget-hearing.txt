       PRESENTATION ON TENTATIVE               BUDGET,      PROPOSED       MILLAGE     RATES,     AND    CAPITAL
       IMPROVEMENTS PROGRAM:

City Manager Leif Ahnell gave PowerPoint presentations on the proposed budget and millage rate as well as the
proposed Capital Improvements Program.· The City's guiding principles - Financially Sound City, World Class
Municipal Services, Strong Partnership with Community, and Vibrant and Sustainable City - were reviewed, noting
the policy priorities associated with these principles. Changes to the general fund operating costs for the 2018-19
fiscal year were highlighted, as were new personnel and programs. Various charts and tables were presented
illustrating year-over-year millage rates and assessed property valuations; allocation of operating expenses; and
comparison of Boca Raton to other cities' fire assessment fee, property tax rates, and water/sewer rates. The
presentation was then turned over to Sharon McGuire, Office of Management and Budget Director, who reviewed
the 2018-19 portion of the six-year Capital Improvements Program. Highlights of various infrastructure, vehicles,
technology updates and renovations requested by City departments were provided. Upon concluding, Ms.
McGuire responded to several questions, as did the City Manager.
