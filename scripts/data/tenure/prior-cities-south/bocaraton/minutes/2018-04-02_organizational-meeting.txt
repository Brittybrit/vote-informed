                                          10:30 AM

     The Organizational Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was called to order ·
                                    by Mayor Susan Haynie at 10:31 AM.

1.   CALL TO ORDER:

2.   PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

3.   ROLL CALL:

       Mayor Susan Haynie
       Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers
       Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke
       Council Member Scott Singer
       Council Member Robert S. Weinroth

4.   CEREMONIAL PROCEEDINGS:

      A.     Recognition of and comments by outgoing Council Member Robert S. Weinroth

      The Mayor and Council members each commented on Council Member Weinroth's tenure on Council,
      thanking him for his support and dedication to public service. Council Member Weinroth commented on
      his time in office, thanking city staff, first responders and his wife. Mayor Haynie then presented Council
      Member Weinroth with a plaque in recognition of his service.

      B.     Administration of Oaths of Office for newly-elected Council Members

      Commissioner Steven Abrams commented on Council Member Weinroth's tenure and thanked him for his
      service. He then administered the oath of office to Council Member-elect Jeremy Rodgers. After being
      sworn in, Council Member Rodgers made brief comments in which he thanked Commissioner Abrams,
      his family, City staff and his supporters.

      Council Member Andrea L. O'Rourke administered the oath of office to Council Member-elect Monica
      Mayotte. After being sworn in, Council Member Mayotte offered her comments, thanking her family,
      friends and supporters.

5.   ORGANIZATIONAL PROCEEDINGS:

      A.     Selection of Deputy Mayor

              Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Rodgers, seconded by Mayor Haynie, to appoint Scott Singer
              as Deputy Mayor.

              Motion to amend the nomination and reappoint Jeremy Rodgers as Deputy Mayor was made by
              Council Member O'Rourke and seconded by Council Member Mayotte.

      Deputy Mayor Rodgers stated that, while he was thankful for the vote of confidence, he was happy to
      serve as a Council member and did not wish to be considered for the Deputy Mayor position. Discussion
      ensued. The City Attorney advised of the voting procedure.

              Motion to amend and reappoint Jeremy Rodgers as Deputy Mayor failed on a 2 to 3 vote; Council
              Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Monica Mayotte voting yes; Mayor Susan
              Haynie, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott Singer voting no.
                   Motion to appoint Scott Singer as Deputy Mayor carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie,
                   Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member
                   Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte voting yes.

         B.       Selection of CRA Chairman and Vice Chairman

                   Motion was made by Council Member Rodgers, seconded by Deputy Mayor Singer, to appoint
                   Andrea O'Rourke as CRA Chairman. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy
                   Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Scott
                   Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte voting yes.

                   Motion was made by Council Member Rodgers, seconded by Deputy Mayor Singer, to appoint
                   Monica Mayotte as CRA Vice Chairman. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie,
                   Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member
                   Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte voting yes.

6.      OTHER BUSINESS:

         A.       Selection of TPA (Transportation Planning Agency) Representatives (2)

                   Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Singer, seconded by Council Member O'Rourke, to designate
                   Mayor Haynie and Council Member Rodgers as the representatives to the TPA and Deputy
                   Mayor Singer and Council Member O'Rourke as the alternate representatives. Motion carried
                   unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Andrea
                   Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte voting yes.

         B.       Selection of Palm Beach County League of Cities Voting Delegate & Alternate

                   Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Singer, seconded by Council Member O'Rourke, to designate
                   Council Member Mayotte as the voting delegate to the Palm Beach County League of Cities and
                   Council Member Rodgers as the alternate delegate. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan
                   Haynie, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council
                   Member Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte voting yes.

         C.       Selection of IPARC (lnterlocal Plan Amendment Review Committee) Representative

                   Motion was made by Council Member O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member Rodgers, to
                   designate Council Member Rodgers as the /PARC representative. Motion carried unanimously;
                   Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke,
                   Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte voting yes.

7.      ADJOURNMENT:

Deputy Mayor Singer welcomed Council Member Mayotte and congratulated Council Member Rodgers. Mayor
Haynie thanked everyone and made final comments.

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 11:10 am.
