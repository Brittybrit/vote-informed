                   Resolution No. 76-2016
                   A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the appeal by M.E. Signs,
                    Inc., on behalf of Investments Limited, of the decision of the Community Appearance Board
                   denying approval of, alteration and relocation of an existing freestanding sign with new tenant
                   copy (to be located at 351 East Yamato Road), and denying approval of sign copy for four (4)
                   tenants on a new freestanding sign (to be located at 331 East Yamato Road); providing for
                   severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                            Resolution No. 76-2016

           Mayor Haynie noted that the agenda had been reordered to hear item 17. C. first. She also stated that
           the same quasi-judicial procedures explained earlier in the meeting would continue to apply. Members
           noted their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to all those who wished to speak.

          John Harbilas, Zoning Officer, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He reviewed the site plan and the
          location of the two signs in question. Descriptions were provided. Staff does not support the existing sign
          at 351 Yamato Road and recommends that Council affirm the Community Appearance Board (CAB)

      Regular Minutes                                       9                                      September 13, 2016
     denial of permit# 16-2481. With regard to the new sign at 331 Yamato Road, staff recommends that
     Council grant the appeal and reverse the CAB decision. Upon concluding, he responded to questions
     from Council.

     The public hearing was opened. Doug Mummaw, on behalf of the petitioner, gave a PowerPoint
     presentation in support of the appeal. He began with a discussion of architectural signage, way finding,
     sign color, branding and other related topics. The two signs were described; the objections of the CAB
     were listed. In conclusion, Mr. Mummaw requested that both signs be approved and then responded to
     questions. Juan Caycedo, CAB chairman, addressed the subjectivity of signage and the way in which
     CAB has evolved in its consideration of signage. He then addressed the signs in question, expressing his
     opinion, and that of the CAB, that both signs should be of the same design. Mr. Caycedo asked that
     Council carefully consider all sign variances and, in this case, not grant the appeal. He reiterated his
     comments about sign pollution and then responded to Council members' questions.

     During rebuttal, Mr. Mummaw responded to Mr. Caycedo's comments and continued to explain his
     position with respect to signage. Mr. Caycedo made final comments noting that signs on building may be
     colorful but monument signs should have monochromatic letters with colorful logos. Seeing no one else
     come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

     Members made final comments, expressing opinions on the subject of signage and these appeals. The
     Mayor noted that the matters could be remanded to CAB or the matter could be postponed.

                  Motion was made by Mayor Susan Haynie, seconded by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, to
                  adopt Resolution No. 76-2016 to remand both matters to the Community Appearance Board.
                  Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council
                  Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth
                  voting yes.

              Resolution No. 35-2016
              A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a variance from the provisions of Section 24-
              96(b)(1 ), Code of Ordinances, to allow one free-standing sign with eleven (11) tenant names on
              two sign faces (where only four (4) tenant names are allowed on two sign faces): and a second
              free-standing sign with six (6) tenant names on each sign face (where only four (4) tenant names
              are allowed on each sign face), for a commercial property located at 1944 NE Fifth Avenue:
              providing for severability: providing for repealer: providing an effective date (SCV-15-02)
                        Resolution No. 35-2016

     Again, Mayor Haynie reminded everyone noted that quasi-judicial procedures continued to apply..
     Members made their ex parte disclosers. The City Clerk administered the oath to all those who wished to

     names on the sign, not the overall size of the monument. Numerous examples of signs were provided.
     Mr. Mummaw detailed the hardships asserted by his client. Upon concluding, he responded to questions
     from Council members. Discussion ensued. Topics included, but were not limited to, allowing more

     the center, and architectural integration of signs. Mr. Mummaw provided a depiction of a compromise sign
     with fewer panels. Juan Caycedo, chairman of CAB, commented on the proposals and the discussion at
     CAB. He noted that the variance was only for the number of tenants on a sign, not color or design. He
     reiterated his views on visual contamination and asserted that approving this variance could change the
     character of the City. He then responded to members' questions. It was suggested that this matter be
