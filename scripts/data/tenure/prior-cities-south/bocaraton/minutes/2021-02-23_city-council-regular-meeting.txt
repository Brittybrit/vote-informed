 20.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Mayotte remarked on the rising level of noise pollution attributable to modified vehicles travelling
throughout the City.

Deputy Mayor O'Rourke commented on pedestrian walkability and traffic within the City; she expressed her intention
to have the topics be included in the upcoming Strategic Planning workshop. She reiterated her desire to see the
Boca Raton Arts and Innovation Center project move forward expeditiously. Ms. O'Rourke also noted that the next
CRA and Council meetings would be at the end of March.

Council Member Drucker echoed the comments of members regarding traffic concerns and noted an upcoming joint
meeting with the Beach and Park District. She encouraged everyone to continue to wear their masks and follow
CDC guidelines, and stressed the importance of voting in the upcoming election.

Mayor Singer acknowledged the increased patrols by Police Services to address traffic concerns and thanked City
staff for their work. He concluded by urging residents to vote in the upcoming election.
