 3.     PRESENTATION ON TENTATIVE BUDGET, PROPOSED MILLAGE RATES, AND CAPITAL
        IMPROVEMENTS PROGRAM:

        Michael Middleman, Deputy Director of the Office of Management and Budget (0MB), gave the
        presentation of the proposed FY 2026-27 budget and millage rate . Mr. Middleman noted the total
        millage rate of 3.6639 mills, slightly below the FY 2025-26 rate, and a 6.63% increase in assessed
        property values. The general fund budget totals were reviewed, as well as citywide revenues and
        expenditures. Upon concluding, Mr. Middleman, 0MB Director Sharon McGuire, and City Financial
        Officer and Deputy City Manager James Zervis responded to Members' questions and provided
        additional information .

        Mr. Middleman then presented the proposed FY 2026-27 through FY 2031-32 Capital Improvements
        Program (CIP) . He explained that the six-year CIP projects were selected based on Citywide needs
        and committee approval and funded through sources including ad-valorem taxes, utility revenues,
        grants, bond proceeds, and other funding. Major FY 2026-27 projects include wastewater and water
        treatment improvements, the City Services Building, Memorial Park enhancements, fleet facilities, roof
        replacements, and Police Services Facilities. Mr. Zervis provided additional information .

           B.      Resolution No. 105-2026
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton adopting an amended and restated Boca Raton
                  Municipal Facilities and Services User Fee Schedule; providing for severability; providing for
                  repealer; providing an effective date

                   Jean-Michel Neault, Internal Audit & Compliance Supervisor, presented the proposed revisions
                   to the City's Municipal Facilities and Services User Fee Schedule. The recommended changes
                   included fee adjustments across mu ltiple departments, with increases generally intended to
                   better reflect service costs while maintaining competitive pricing. An increase to water, sewer,
                   and stormwater rates was based on the June 2026 Consumer Price Index, as required by the
                   City Code. The Financial Advisory Board recommended approval. Upon concluding, City
                   Manager Mark Sohaney and Deputy City Manager Andy Lukasik responded to Members'
                   questions and provided additional information .

                   The public hearing was opened . Albert Cacace , Mike Liebelson , and Steve Griffith commented
                   about the cost of resident vs. non-resident user fees. Seeing no one else come forward , the
                   public hearing was closed .

                            Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                            Stacy L. Sipple, to approve Resolution No. 105-2026. Motion carried 4 votes to 0;
                            Mayor Andy Thomson, Deputy Mayor Michelle Grau, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                            Council Member Stacy L. Sipple voting yes.
