           The Deputy Mayor noted that the public hearings would be conducted contemporaneously as the
           items were interrelated. She noted that a separate vote would be taken on each item and should
           items A.1 and B.1 not be approved, no vote would be taken on the remaining items. The City Attorney
           read the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing. Members made their ex-
           parte disclosures, and the City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

           Senior Planner Jim Bell gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began with a history of the property,
           and reviewed the application requests, zoning change, and future land use changes. He advised that
           the Community Appearance Board approved the application with recommendations, the Planning
           and Zoning Board approved, and staff recommend approval with one condition. Upon concluding, he
           responded to Members' questions.

           Attorney for the applicant Michael Marshall advised that he had a presentation but it was covered by
           staff's material and he offered to respond to any questions.

           The public hearing was opened. Jonathan Pearlman questioned the parking and proposed uses.
           Mike Liebelson expressed concerns about ex-parte procedures. Vivian Lesher relayed her worry that
           the construction would disturb any wildlife that was on the property. Seeing no one else come forward,
           the public hearing was closed.

           Discussion ensued and Development Services Director Brandon Schaad responded to Member's
           questions.
