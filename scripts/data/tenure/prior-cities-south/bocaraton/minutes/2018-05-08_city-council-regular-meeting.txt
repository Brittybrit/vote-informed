12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A.       Ordinance No. 5453
                  An Ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Article V, "Qualifications and Elections,"
                  Section 5.03, "Qualifications," of the Charter of the City of Boca Raton to modify the requirements
                  to qualify as a candidate for City Council, including the office of Mayor, by: (i) extending the
                  length of the residency requirement, establishing certain requirements for residency, and
                  requiring proof of such residency (to be prescribed by Ordinance), and (ii) eliminating the $25
                  qualifying fee and instead requiring the verified signatures of a specified number of registered
                  voters of the City, and providing for procedures for collection, submission, and verification of
                  such signatures (to be prescribed by Ordinance); providing for a special election regarding the
                  proposed Charter amendments to be held on August 28, 2018 (the date of the primary election);
                  amending Chapter 6, "Elections," of the Code of Ordinances to create (subject to approval of
                  one, or both, of the above Charter amendments) a new Section 6-16, "Proof of Residency,"
                  prescribing procedures for establishing residency, and a new Section 6-17, "Filing of Petition,"
                  prescribing procedures for the collection, submission, and verification of signatures; providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing for revisions to the City Charter and codification in
                  the City Code, respectively; providing an effective date
                            Ordinance No. 5453

         The City Manager briefly explained the substance of the ordinance. Members commented and asked
         questions. Information on the form of petitions was discussed. Mayor Singer suggested that the proposed


Regular Minutes                                         3                                                  May 8, 2018
         ballot questions should be on the November ballot rather than the August ballot in order to avoid voter
         confusion. Discussion ensued.

         The public hearing was opened. Tamara McKee voiced her concerns and asked that the ordinance not
         be adopted. Jason Pellish reported on the results of a Boca Watch website poll on possible changes to
         residency requirements for candidates running for City Council. Kathy Cottrell offered support of the
         increased residency requirements. David Sergi expressed his concerns with the proposal to gather 200
         signatures rather than pay a fee. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was
         closed.

         Members commented further. The City Attorney provided information on the effect of possible changes to
         the title should consideration of the ordinance be postponed to a date certain.

                  Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, to
                  postpone Ordinance No. 5453 to the Council meeting of July 24, 2018.

         Members made final comments.

                  Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                  Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers voting yes.

14.    PUBLIC REQUESTS:

Stephen Walsh noted his appearance at a prior meeting and asked if there was a response to his question
regarding the height of single family homes. The City Manager related that staff would contact him.

JoAnn Landon expressed concern for the possible loss of trees along the Palmetto Park Road shared use
pathway.

Jason Pellish asked about the optimal population for the City and expressed his dislike of the term "world class"
when describing the City. Mayor Singer offered comments on the world class nature of Boca Raton. Council
Member Rodgers commented on a population cap that the City tried to impose in the past. The City Attorney
commented on the lawsuit regarding the cap, which the City lost.

Christina Escalante commented on the City's ban on medical marijuana. The City Manager noted that the ban will
be reconsidered in October. Council Member O'Rourke addressed the considerations the Council made when
debating the ban.

        MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Mayotte announced the upcoming joint meeting with Beach & Park District. Council Member
O'Rourke announced the upcoming workshop to receive public input on the Midtown small area plan.

The City Attorney gave a brief presentation on the petition signature requirements for qualifying in a number of
surrounding cities. Members commented. Council Member O'Rourke then initiated a discussion of the possibility
of appointing an interim Council member, noting the short timeframe for receiving applications and making an
appointment prior to the August election. She suggested bypassing an appointment. Members weighed in,
offering their opinions. In general, members were in agreement with not making an appointment.

Deputy Mayor Rodgers shared information he received at the Transportation Planning Agency meeting regarding
Brightline and the timing of quiet zones. The City Manager shared that he had signed the applications for quiet
zones in Boca Raton. Mr. Rodgers asked that the public pay attention to the lights and gates at railroad
crossings.

Mayor Singer announced the upcoming Strategic Planning Follow-up Session and the Business of Sports
Luncheon presented by the City's Economic Development Division. Council Member O'Rourke thanked the
Economic Development staff.
