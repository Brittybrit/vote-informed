I.     Welcome - Mayor Scott Singer and Chair Susan Vogelgesang

       Mayor Scott Singer called the meeting to order at 5:34 pm. The participants introduced themselves.

II.    Discussion Topic

       A.      Boca National Golf Course Update

       Commissioner Robert Rollins and Arthur Koski of the Greater Boca Raton Beach & Park District (District)
       provided a summary of the project thus far and stated, with Council's approval, they were ready to
       proceed to the RFP. Council Members asked several questions regarding the timeline and clarification as
       to the cost. Lengthy discussion ensued. District Commissioners stated that, at this time, they were only
       seeking approval for the Phase I design concept. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser requested a list of the
       Phase I details in order to prepare a resolution. Further discussion ensued. District Chair Susan
       Vogelgesang, course designers Tom Fazio and Nick Price and traffic engineer John Donaldson
       responded to Members' questions.

Ill.   Public Comment

       The floor was opened for public comment. Anthony Ciliberti, Andrea Steckloff, Dorothy Provenzano,
       Charles Possenriede, Andrew Dickson, Richard Close, Tom Vassel, Tom Lambert, Rob Berthiaume
       Robert Dukate, Lenore Wachtelk and Mr. I. Rubinov expressed concerns regarding funding of the golf
       course. Gil Linder asked several questions regarding the design of the course; Mr. Rollins and Mr. Fazio
       responded. Harold Chaffee shared his view of the project. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the
       public comment period was closed.

       Merv Timberlake, financial consultant for the Beach and Park District, commented on the District's ability
       to fund the course. Following additional discussion, the District Commissioners advised they would review
       funding and design options at their next meeting and present the findings at the June 10th City Council
       Workshop. Mayor Singer and Chair Vogelgesang thanked everyone for their participation.

IV.    Adjournment

       The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 8:29 pm.
