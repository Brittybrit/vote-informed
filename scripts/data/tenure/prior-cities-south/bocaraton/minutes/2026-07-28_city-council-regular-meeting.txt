 9.      RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:


          A.       Resolution No. 86-2026
                   A resolution of the City of Boca Raton, Florida, ranking the firms submitting statements of
                   qualifications in response to Request for Qualifications No. 2026-035-IG , Downtown Civic
                   Area/Memorial Park Master Plan ; directing the City Manager to commence negotiations with
                   the highest-ranked firm for a professional services agreement; authorizing sequential
                   negotiations with the second- and third-ranked firms if the City is unable to negotiate a
                   satisfactory agreement with a higher-ranked firm; requiring that any negotiated agreement be
                   presented to the City Council for consideration and approval; providing for severability;
                   providing for repealer; providing an effective date

           Deputy City Manager Andy Lukasik gave a brief introduction of the process to date and advised how
           the presentations would proceed .

           Jeffrey Huber of Brooks Scarpa Huber introduced speakers for their team, including Phillip Dugdale,
           Brad Davis, Alexandra Witthaus, and lrvans Augustin . An overview was provided of the company's
           background, recent projects, and accomplishments. The team outlined its vision for the Downtown
           Civic Area and described their proposed approach to public engagement to ensure the project reflects
           community priorities. Upon concluding, team members responded to Council Members' questions.

The meeting was recessed at 7: 19 pm and resumed at 7:26 pm.

           Nilsa Zacarias of ChenMoore and Associates introduced speakers for their team, including Lance
           Lilly, Rick Conzalez, Aleli de la Vega, Sara Colon, Santiago Cleves, and Susan Dombrowski. They
           provided an overview of the company 's history, recent projects, and accomplishments. The team
           outlined its vision for the Downtown Civic Area and described their proposed approach to public
           engagement to ensure the project reflects community priorities. Upon concluding, team members
           responded to Council Members' questions.

The meeting was recessed at 8:09 and resumed at 8:15 pm.

           Edward Ng introduced speakers for the Corradino Group, including Matt Wojciechowski, Renee
           Kissane, Erick Valle, Estela Valle, Corey Lafferty, Barry Miller, Adriana Savino-Miller, and Brian
           Harrison. They provided an overview of the company's history, recent projects, and accomplishments.
           The team outlined its vision for the Downtown Civic Area and described their proposed approach to
           public engagement to ensure the project reflects community priorities. Upon concluding , team
           members responded to Council Members' questions.

The meeting was recessed at 9:04 pm and resumed at 9:27 pm.

           Further discussion ensued regarding the final decision. In the voting , ChenMoore and Associates
           was ranked first, Corradino Group ranked second , and Brooks Scarpa Huber ranked third .

Re: Supplemental Disclosure Regarding Resolution 86-2026, Item 9A

In the interest of maintaining the highest standards of ethics and transparency, I have voluntarily
chosen to make a disclosure regarding Resolution 86-2026, Item 9A, from the July 28, 2026 City
Council meeting.

At that meeting, I voted on Resolution 86-2026, Item 9A, which ranked three firms for the
Downtown Civic Area/Memorial Park Master Plan. Chen Moore & Associates was ranked first
by a 5-0 vote.

Only after the meeting did I learn, for the first time, that Chen Moore & Associates is a client of
Grau & Associates, where my husband serves as partner and I am an outside employee. For
approximately five years, the firm has provided Chen Moore & Associates with overhead audit
rate services for a flat fee of approximately $19,000 annually. I have never performed,
supervised, or otherwise participated in that engagement and was entirely unaware of the client
relationship when I cast my vote.

Upon learning of the relationship, I promptly contacted the City Attorney and the Palm Beach
County Commission on Ethics for guidance and to ensure the public record is complete. I am
disclosing the relationship and the circumstances under which I became aware of it.

It is my understanding that the ranking itself created only a contingent opportunity to pursue a
future contract, and that any actual financial benefit would arise only ifthe City Council later
approves, and the parties execute, a binding agreement.

Nevertheless, in the interest of full transparency, good faith, and adherence to the highest ethical
standards, I am disclosing this matter for the record. I will abstain from voting and will not
participate in any further matter concerning the negotiation, consideration, or approval of a
potential agreement with Chen Moore & Associates.
