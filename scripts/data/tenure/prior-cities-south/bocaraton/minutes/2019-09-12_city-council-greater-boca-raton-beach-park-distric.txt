I.     Welcome - Mayor Scott Singer and Chair Susan Vogelgesang

       Mayor Scott Singer called the meeting to order at 5:00 pm. The participants introduced themselves.

II.    Discussion Topic

       A.      Golf Course and Funding Options

       Chair Susan Vogelgesang of the Greater Boca Raton Beach & Park District (District) began by asking for
       approval of the Price-Fazio design concept for the Ocean Breeze Golf Course. Mayor Scott Singer inquired
       if the District had examined alternative design options provided by the Council. Chair Vogelgesang stated
       that there had been changes made to the design in order to lower costs and invited Wayne Branthwaite,
       on behalf of Price-Fazio, to review modifications that had been made to the course design since the May
       28, 2019 Joint Meeting. Design changes and related costs were discussed, including components of the
       project that would be deferred to a later date, such as the clubhouse, tunnel and halfway house. Council
       Members asked a number of questions regarding elements of the design and the estimated expense.
       Lengthy discussion ensued.

Ill.   Public Comment

       The floor was opened for public comment. Rick Heard, Richard Laman, Sue Daddino, Andrea Stekloff,
       Bruce DillerVerstandig, Lynn Glover, and Robert Dukate expressed concerns regarding funding and design
       of the golf course. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public comment period was closed.

       Further discussion ensued. Council Members reiterated their position that the cost of the Price-Fazio golf
       course design was too high. District Members agreed to take at a vote at their September 16th meeting as
       to whether the District should build the golf course on their own.

       Council Members and Commissioners made final comments. There was consensus to schedule the next
       joint meeting on Tuesday, November 12th at 5:30 pm.

 IV.   Adjournment

       The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 8:04 pm.
