                 Resolution No. 135-2016
                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a Planned Mobility Development (PMD) Master
                  Plan and Site Plan to allow for a single phase development of a 180-unit multifamily residential
                 development for the 5500 Broken Sound PMD generally located at 5500 Northwest Broken
                  Sound Boulevard ; considering a modification of development parameters related to el iminating a
                  planted buffer for the sidewalk along NW 56th Street; considering a waiver of plat requirements:
                  providing for severability: providing for repealer: providing an effective date (PMD-15-04ISPA-14-
                  03R1)
                          Resolution No. 135-2016
                          Drawings

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing . Members
         made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk ad ministered the oath to all those who wished to speak.


         Elizabeth Eassa, Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation beginning with a review of the planned
         mobility development (PMD) project, including the zoning, land use, acreage and location. Details of the
         master plan/site plan were provided. Topics reviewed included, but were not limited to, number of units,
         site access, parking, building elevations, traffic analysis and compliance with PMD requirements. The
         Community Appearance Board reviewed the project and recommended several landscape
         enhancements. The modification of a development parameter related to the relocation of a required
         planted buffer adjacent to sidewalks was supported by staff. The Planning & Zoning Board did not support
         the project due to the applicant's request related to a reduced degree of participation in the City's transit
         program. However, the developer has eliminated that request and, as a result, staff recommends
         approval of the project. Upon concluding, Ms. Eassa responded to questions from Council members.

                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton supporting reasonable efforts to maintain the Ocean
                 Breeze property located at 5800-5801 NW 2nd Avenue as an operational golf course; providing
                 for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

        Council Member Rodgers addressed this resolution, explaining his reasons for bring it forward.

        The public hearing was opened. Lenore Wachtel offered support of the resolution and also advocated
        accepting the GL Homes offer to purchase the western golf course. In the interests of clarity, Mr.
        Rodgers read the entirety of the resolution and then offered further comments in support of the resolution.
        Robert DuKate advocated passage of the resolution. Al Zucaro com mented on the language of the
        resolutions. Paul Preste commented on the Lennar and GL Homes golf course proposals. Seeing no one
        else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

        Council Member Weinroth offered an amendment to Section 1 of the resolution to read "The City Council
        expresses its support for maintaining the Ocean Breeze property as an operational golf course."

                  Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Michael
                  Mu/laugh, to amend Resolution No. 147-2016 as stated.

        Discussion ensued. Mr. Rodgers explained his rational for the wording he requested. The City Attorney
        commented on the language, noting that the resolution is non-bind ing. She suggested modifying Council
        Member Weinroth's language, changing 'support for maintaining' in Section 1 to 'support for the
        maintenance of' and to modify the title on line 3 to read 'supporting the maintenance of ... ' Both Council
        Member Weinroth and Deputy Mayor Mullaugh accepted those changes to their amendment.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Michael
                  Mu/laugh, to approve Resolution No. 147-2016 as amended. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                  Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council

                 Resolution No. 144-2016
                 A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the appeal of the decision
                 of the Community Appearance Board denying the Mae Volen Senior Center's requested entrance
                 frees tanding sign (Permit Number 16-4386) and a driveway directional sign (Permit Number 16-
                  4387) for a property located at 1515 West Pal metto Park Road: providing for severability:
                  providing for repealer: providing an effective date
                          Resolution No. 144-2016

        The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
        made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to all those who wished to speak.

        John Harbilas, Zoning Officer, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The subject property and sign locations
        were identified, and the previously approved signs were described. The signs that are the subject of the
        appeal were then described. The Community Appearance Board (CAB) decisions for denial of the
        permits due to the size of the logo and the nature of the tag line were reviewed . Mr. Harbilas noted that
        staff recommends granting the appeals and reversing the decisions of the CAB. Upon concluding, Mr.
        Harbilas responded to questions from Council members.

        The public hearing was opened. Dave Freudenburg, on behalf of the appellant, addressed sign tag lines
        and provided a handout illustrating another sign with a tag lines. He advocated granting the appeal,
        addressed the lack of Hospice signage, and responded to questions. Elizabeth Lugo, Volen Center
        director, addressed the reason that Hospice did not choose to participate in the proposed signage and
        then responded to members' concerns and questions. Deputy City Manager George Brown responded to
        a question about the allowance of any future Hospice sign. Discussion ensued.

        Jessica Dornblaser, CAB member, gave a PowerPoint presentation explaining why the Board denied both
        sign permits . She provided background, addressed the Reed v Gilbert Supreme Court ruling on signs,
        reviewed CAB standards and the Board's position on effective taglines. Upon concluding, she responded
        to questions. Mark Jacobsen, CAB chairman, offered comments regarding the Board's position on
        allowing taglines. Members asked further questions of both Ms . Dornblaser and Mr. Jacobsen. B ill
        Reicherter, Priority 1 Signs, expressed his position that the CAB decisions with respect to these signs are
        subjective. Glenn Gromann commented on taglines on signs and advocated granting the appeal.

        There was no cross examination . Dave Freudenberg made brief final comments, again requesting that
        Council grant the appeal. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed .

        The City Attorney commented on the rules and regulations of the CAB, noting that their decisions are not
        random . The City Manager noted that a sign code rewrite is in the works in light of the Reed decision.
        The amended Code should be brought to Council next spring. Discussion ensued. Information was
        provided about the size of logos and other topics. Comments followed about the content of taglines.
        Members addressed the appeal, thanked the CAB, and further expressed their opinions.

        Council Member Weinroth moved that, on the monument sign, the butterfly logo be reduced to 1½ times
        the size of the largest letter and the tag line be retained; on the directional sign, the logo be reduced to
        1½ the size of the largest letter and the tag line be removed. The City Attorney stated that these
        conditions were clear and would be included in the resolution.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                          Michael Mu/laugh, to approve Resolution No. 144-2016 reversing the decisions of the CAB
                          with the conditions as stated.

         Members made final comments and stated positions.

                          Motion carried on a 4-1 vote; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh,
                          Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes; Council
                          Member Jeremy Rodgers voting no.
