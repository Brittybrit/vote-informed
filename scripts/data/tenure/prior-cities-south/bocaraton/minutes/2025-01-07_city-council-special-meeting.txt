4.     OTHER BUSINESS:

        A.     Government Campus Master Plan
               The purposes of the meeting are to: provide an opportunity for the City Council, potential
               responders to the Downtown campus opportunity, and members of the public to have a
               public discussion before the January 9, 2025 response deadline (" Deadline") and give an
               opportunity for interested parties to ask questions and have a dialogue; and allow City
               Council members to confirm their expectations and provide direction for how the City will
               proceed.

Mayor Singer welcomed everyone and provided an overview of the meeting . A chart was shown with a
timeline which described the process for receiving proposals, evaluation , ranking, and public discussion
opportunities. Members discussed how rapidly the process should proceed . Council Member Wigder gave a
presentation on the needs of the Police Headquarters, City Hall , Community Center, and other public facilities
in addition to the proposed retail component. Extended discussion ensued .

The public comment period was opened . Lorraine Blank cautioned about growing the area beyond its
capacity , shared concerns about having a hotel within a few blocks of her home, and the effect on safety and
quality of life. Alfred Bonner relayed concerns about the aggressive timeline and encouraged getting more
feedback from residents . Jorge Garcia urged staff to take the time to lay out the needs and wants and utilize
a schedule. Lauren Bishop inquired about the conservation of the athletic fields . David Kaye shared his
opposition to any allowance of City funds to build City offices, suggested the developers rebuild the new
Police Station first, asked that the Downtown library be preserved , and that consideration of pedestrians and
cyclists be prioritized . Mark Sneckenburg noted the existing traffic congestion in the Downtown and expressed
concerns that it would worsen with the proposed development. Cyndi Bloom suggested a schematic or scale
model be created for the public to view the proposed development. Seeing no one else come forward , the
public comment period was closed .

Discussion ensued . The Mayor and City Manager George Brown responded to questions and concerns raised
during the public comment period . Further discussion ensued , and CBRE Executive Vice-President Michael
McShea answered Members' questions and provided additional information . Council Members stressed their
commitment to transparency and to receiving input from the residents . City Attorney Joshua Koehler
confirmed that public discussions were permitted , and that after January 9th , private discussions were
prohibited . There was consensus to have staff implement the timeline, finalize the space needs for City Hall ,
continue land-use planning updates, begin work on an interim agreement and start planning the long-term
needs for a partnership agreement. There was consensus to approve the timeline for review and public
comments . Mr. McShea confirmed that financial plans would be expected from the proposers and that CBRE
would create a dashboard to make all of the pertinent information available.

5.    ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 7:40 PM .
