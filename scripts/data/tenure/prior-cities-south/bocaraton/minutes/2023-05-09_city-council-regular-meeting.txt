 19.    MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

         A.      City Manager Executive Performance Review

         Mayor Singer highlighted the need for proactive communication and internal collaboration. He noted
         staffing level issues and the volume of public inquiries and concluded by stating that he was
         appreciative of the work that was being done.

         Council Member Marc Wigder stated that he appreciated Mr. Ahnell answering his questions and the
         time the City Manager had spent advising him on various issues and providing historical context.

         Council Member Yvette Drucker thanked Mr. Ahnell for responding to her requests. She advised that
         she desired to have better communication with administrative staff and noted the need for additional
         staffing on the third floor. Ms. Drucker also noted the time required to settle expense reports.

         Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte agreed with prior comments made and stated that she appreciated Mr.
         Ahnell's dedication and financial acumen . She highlighted that communications could be enhanced .
         Ms. Mayotte suggested additional ideas for improvement such as collaboration with community
         partners and better implementation of complicated projects. She further stated that the politics of
         issues should be left to the Council Members and not the staff.

         Council Member Fran Nachlas complimented her interactions with Mr. Ahnell and agreed with other
         comments made. She highlighted the need for proactive communication and staffing levels on the
         third floor.

         Mayor Singer made final comments.

         B.      City Attorney Executive Performance Review

         Mayor Singer reiterated the importance of communications and stated that he would like to be aware
         of high profile matters.

         Deputy Mayor Mayotte agreed that communication could be improved and looked forward to working
         together in a more proactive way. She mentioned that the legal review needed to be involved earlier
         in the development process. She concluded by stating we need to be good stewards of taxpayer funds.

         Council Member Nachlas thanked the City Attorney for taking the time to work with her and agreed
         that legal should be involved in City processes earlier.

         Council Member Wigder thanked Ms. Frieser for all she had done and stated that there was always
         room for improvement, noting areas of promptness, collaboration, and earlier engagement.

         Council Member Drucker thanked Ms. Frieser for the help she provided and how she accommodated
         Ms. Drucker's schedule.

         Mayor Singer made final comments.

Council Member Wigder began his report by noting that the City had completed an RFP for an outside
consulting firm to find a new City Manager. Mr. Wigder complimented Deputy City Manager George Brown,
noting his more than 40 years of service, and stated that he should be appointed as the next City Manager.
Mr. Wigder then made a motion to promote Deputy City Manager George Brown as the next City Manager,
seconded by Ms. Mayotte. Discussion ensued. Members spoke favorably of Mr. Brown and discussed the
timing and transition process. The City Attorney requested clarification and advised that an agreement could
be drafted. Discussion ensued regarding the transition of other executive level positions. Additional comments
were made for the succession of Mr. Brown to City Manager. Mr. Brown stated that he agrees with accepting
the position only when Mr. Ahnell was ready to retire. Ms. Frieser advised that the motion should be withdrawn ,
and she would provide additional information at the next meeting. It was not withdrawn; Mayor Singer made
a motion to lay the current motion on the table, seconded by Council Member Drucker. The motion carried
unanimously on a voice vote. Mr. Wigder continued with his report and advised that he is happy that there will
be continuity and urged that he was looking forward to Strategic Planning .

Council Member Drucker shared that she was also looking forward to the Strategic Planning Session .

Deputy Mayor Mayotte reiterated comments made, noted this would be her last Strategic Planning session
and asked the City Manager for a copy of the PMD matrix.

Council Member Nachlas expressed her eagerness for the upcoming Strategic Planning. Ms. Nachlas
mentioned while she was bike riding, she witnessed a cyclist who had been struck by a car and highlighted
the need for Vision Zero and the safety for our pedestrians and cyclists. She continued by congratulating the
FAU Sandy Owls women's beach volleyball team on reaching the NCAA Championships and concluded by
highlighting National Nurses' Week.

Mayor Singer also congratulated FAU women's volleyball team. He then noted that Deputy City Manager
George Brown, Council Member Yvette Drucker, and he had been awarded the Home Rule Hero award for
their efforts during the 2023 Legislative Session. The Mayor turned the discussion to the upcoming Strategic
Planning Sessions, noting the goals and priorities focused on in the past. He expressed his desire to keep the
discussions focused on high level strategies rather. Council Member Drucker commented on Vision Zero, the
importance of creating an action plan, and the need for staff specifically dedicated to mobility and complete
streets. Members reviewed topics for the upcoming Planning Sessions.
