 12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A.       Ordinance No. 5590
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 24, "Signs," Article I, Section 24-6,
                  "Unlawful Signs, Sign Structures," and Article Ill, Division 2, "Sign Regulation By Zoning District,"
                  Section 24-81, Code of Ordinances, to allow painted wall signs in commercial, industrial and similar
                  zoning districts, where the painted wall sign is internally-focused and not visible from grade from
                  adjacent properties and rights-of-way (and therefore no sign permit is required); providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-21-
                  08)

         Brandon Schaad, Director of Development Services, gave the presentation. He began with a brief
         background of the Sign Code and proceeded to review the proposed amendment. Mr. Schaad noted that
         since the introduction of the ordinance, clarifying language had been added. The Planning and Zoning
         Board recommended approval. Upon concluding, Mr. Schaad made himself available for members'
         questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Michael Goodwin provided a handout and spoke in support of the
         ordinance. Robert Eisen, representing Investments Limited, also expressed approval of the ordinance. Glen
         Weldon shared his support and suggested that the parameters outlined in the proposed ordinance be
         expanded. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                          Member Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5590 as revised.

         Council Member Mayotte stated that she felt the proposed ordinance was too restrictive and questioned if
         the Mizner Amphitheater stage door mural conflicted with the ordinance. Deputy Mayor O'Rourke remarked
         that more art in public places should be permitted, and suggested changes be made to expand the
         parameters of what would be allowed. Council Member Drucker conveyed her approval and noted that
         other cities she had visited incorporated a lot of art into their public spaces. Council Member Thomson
         inquired how staff had arrived at the proposed standards. Mr. Schaad provided additional information on
         the maximum size of the mural allowed. Discussion ensued. Ms. O'Rourke suggested amending the
         maximum allowable size. Members inquired as to the regulation of content. City Attorney Diana Grub
         Frieser provided additional information. Ms. Frieser then clarified where in the ordinance the language could
         be modified to raise the allowable height from 12 to 15 feet.

                          Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                          Member Andy Thomson, to amend Ordinance No. 5590 as revised, to raise the height limit
                          from 12 feet to 15 feet. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

          B.       Ordinance No. 5591
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 2, Article Ill, Boards, Committees,
                  Commissions and Special Magistrates, Code of Ordinances, for the purpose of abolishing the Art
                  in Public Places Advisory Board; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for
                  codification; providing an effective date

        Ruby Childers, Downtown Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She outlined the history of the
        Board and the art projects undertaken. Staff recommends approval of the ordinance. Upon concluding, Ms.
        Childers responded to members' questions. Both Mayor Singer and Ms. Childers confirmed that some of
        the board members had relayed the frustration they felt due to the the constraints related to being under
        the Sunshine Law. Members expressed their desire to see the momentum of public art installations to
        continue. Discussion ensued.

        The public hearing was opened. Glen Weldon commented favorably on the Community Appearance Board
        and suggested that board already provided the mechanism to review proposed public art. Cristina Escalante
        expressed disappointment that the board would be abolished and suggested language to change the
        ordinance. Seeing no one else indicate their desire to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member
                          Yvette Drucker, to approve Ordinance No. 5591.

        Deputy Mayor O'Rourke advised that she would be requesting additional funding for art at the upcoming
        Budget Hearing,

                          Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke,
                          Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy
                          Thomson voting yes.
