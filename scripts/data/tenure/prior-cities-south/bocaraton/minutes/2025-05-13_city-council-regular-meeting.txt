10.    RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:


            A.       Government Campus Redevelopment Partnership Opportunity
                     1.     Reports from Staff and CBRE

                     City Manager George Brown advised that the City received updated site plans and renderings
                     from Terra & Frisbie and that they would be made available on the City's website. Deputy City
                     Manager Andy Lukasik provided additional updates about upcoming milestone events,
                     financial models, and City Hall interim options before and during the construction . Discussion
                     ensued .

                    2.     Public Comment

                    Steve Zurn Tobel expressed concerns about over development and the future vision of the City.
                    Yvonne Zurn Tobel asked why the Government Campus was being mixed with retail ,
                    commercial , and residential. Michael Welch shared that he felt traffic was out of hand and the
                    character of the City was changed. Steve Griffith asked about an alternative site to build City
                    Hall in order to keep the green space downtown. City Manager George Brown advised that the
                    City is trying to create a place where people would want to socialize and enjoy the space.
                    Mayor Singer advised that the Community Center and City Hall are well past their useful life
                    and building a new campus would create a new vibrant City square.

11.    QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

      A.            Boca Technology Center

                    1. Resolution No. 63-2025
                       A resolution of the City of Boca Raton making findings of fact and conclusions of law
                       pertaining to the Boca Raton Technology Center Development of Regional Impact (ORI) ;
                       considering an amendment to the ORI development order to reduce the total acreage of the
                       ORI from approximately 224.44 acres to approximately 217.84 acres (a 6.6 acre reduction)
                       by removing Tri-Rail Station property located at 680 Yamato Road ; modifying Exhibit "2"
                       (Master Plan Map H-1) of the ORI; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-
                       24-10)

        The City Attorney read the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing. Members
        made their ex-parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

        Peter Begovich , Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation . He began with the description of
        the applicant's request and location of subject property . He continued with the Tri-Rail history , CIMO
        history , Master Plan , and staff findings . He advised that the Planning and Zoning Board and staff
        recommend approval. Upon concluding , he made himself available for questions.
