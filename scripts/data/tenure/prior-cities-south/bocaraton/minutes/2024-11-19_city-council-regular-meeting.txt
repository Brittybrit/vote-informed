         The Mayor noted that hearings on items A. , B., B.1., and B.2. would be conducted contemporaneously
         as the petitions were related , with Items B.1. and B.2. pursuant to quasi-judicial procedures. A
         separate vote would be taken on each item , and should items A. and B. not be approved, items B.1.
         and B.2. would not be considered . The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would
         govern the public hearings. Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered
         the oath to all those who wished to speak.

         Peter Begovich , Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation . He began with a brief history of
         the proposed amendment in Ordinance No. 5702. The existing Right-of-Way (ROW) and properties
         adjacent to it were displayed , as well as the projected widening of NW 5th Avenue. Mr. Begovich
         advised that both the Planning and Zoning Board and Development Services staff recommend
         approval , that Council voted 5-0 to transmit the amendment to the State for review and it had been
         returned with no comments. Continuing with the remaining agenda items for this topic, he then
         reviewed the applicant's related request and the consistency , compatibility , and concurrency of the
         proposed project. Mr. Begovich discussed the proposed rezoning of the site, and shared the site plan ,
         traffic study results , and landscaping plans. The applicant's request for a technical deviation had been
         evaluated and staff had no comments. Mr. Begovich noted the applicant's concerns with two
         cond itions of the site plan resolution , Condition #3 and #7a i. The Planning and Zoning Board and staff
         recommended approval of the project. Upon concluding , he responded to Members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened . Bonnie Miskal, attorney for the applicant, gave a PowerPoint
         presentation . She provided a history of the site and reviewed the previous applications compared to
         the current proposal. Ms. Miskel discussed the components of the application , which included the
         FLUM and rezoning , site plan approval, technical deviation of vehicle reservoir length , abandonment
         of sewer/water and lake maintenance easements. She advised that the applicant and staff had met
         with neighbors who were concerned with additional traffic that would result from the proposed project.
         Ms. Miskel shared that her client offered to increase the height of trees that would provide a buffer to
         the adjacent neighborhood , from 16 to 20 feet. She advised that her client's only remaining concern
         was the condition requiring them to underground the current overhead utility lines. Discussion ensued
         and Ms. Miskal responded to Members' questions. Manuel Martinez, on behalf of the applicant,
         advised that there were ADA accommodations in the amenity areas, but not within the individual
         residential units. Maryanne Sarner spoke in opposition to the proposed project, citing concerns with
         additional traffic. Neal Tempel shared his objections to the project, noting the increased traffic from
         the 1-95 interchange and future expansion of NW 5th Avenue. Kathleen MacDonald agreed that the 1-
         95 exit on Spanish River had added a lot of traffic on NW 5th Avenue and cautioned against adding
         more density without the necessary infrastructure. There was no cross examination or rebuttal, and
         Ms. Miskel concluded her presentation with an appeal to Members for modification of the condition
         requiring undergrounding of power lines. Members made additional ex parte disclosures, and the
         public hearing was closed .

          Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided clarification of the condition in question. He
          advised that if the undergrounding was not feasible , the applicant would need to submit proof that they
          had attempted to fulfill the condition. Naresh Machavarapu , Traffic Engineer, provided the results of a
          traffic study , planned improved signalization and the timeline of widening NW 5th Avenue. Discussion
          ensued and Members weighed in.

                          Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                          Marc Wigder, to approve Ordinance No. 5702. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                          Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council
                          Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

The meeting was recessed at 7:39 pm and resumed at 7:54 pm.

         Development Services Director Brandon Schaad confirmed that he and Ms. Miske! came to an agreement
         regarding Condition #3, that the requ irement to underground overhead power lines would be kept in the
         resolution but that staff would work with the applicant if the condition was not achievable.
