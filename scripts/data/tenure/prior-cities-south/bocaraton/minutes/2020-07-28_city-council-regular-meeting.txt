           B.     Ordinance No. 5524
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28 "Zoning," Code of Ordinances, to
                  provide for large-scale indoor commercial recreation uses and define such uses; amending Article
                  XII, Division 8, "M-3 Manufacturing Industrial District," to add large-scale indoor commercial
                  recreation as a conditional use, and establish a 50-foot height limit; amending Article XV, Division
                  1, "Supplementary District Regulations," to establish regulations for such uses; amending Article
                  XVI, Section 28-1655, "Required Off-Street Parking," to provide parking standards; providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-19-
                  06)

         Mayor Singer noted that this was the first of two public hearings and no vote would be taken.

         Heather Hansen, Senior Planner, gave a brief PowerPoint presentation. She began with a review of the
         applicant's request and summarized elements of the text amendment. Components of the application
         included, but were not limited to, an allowance for large-scale indoor commercial recreation, a height
         limitation, and required off-street parking for the conditional use. Both the Planning and Zoning Board and
         Development Services staff recommend approval. The proposed amendment is consistent with the City's
         Comprehensive Plan. Upon concluding, Ms. Hansen made herself available for questions

         The public hearing was opened. Bonnie Miskel, on behalf of the applicant, gave a PowerPoint presentation.
         Ms. Miskel provided background information of the project that led to the proposed new use category. She
         commented on the residential neighborhood to the north of the subject area and described steps taken to
         alleviate the residents' concerns. Upon concluding, she made herself available to answer members'
         questions. Glenn Gromann and Malcolm Butters expressed their support of the proposed project. Seeing
         no one else indicate their desire to speak, the public hearing was closed. Council Member O'Rourke
         expressed her support. Mayor Singer noted the second public hearing on this item would be on August 25,
         2020.

 17.     QUASI-JUDICIAL PUBLIC HEARINGS/VARIANCES & APPEALS:

          A.       Resolution No. 100-2020
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the appeal of the decision of
                  the Community Appearance Board denying Gift of Life Marrow Registry, lnc.'s requested wall sign
                  (Permit No. 20-1916) located at 5901 Broken Sound Parkway Northwest; providing for repealer;
                  providing an effective date

         The City Attorney noted that quasi-judicial procedures still applied. The City Clerk administered the oath to
         those who wished to speak. Members made their ex-parte disclosures

         Austin Budazewski, Zoning Officer, gave the PowerPoint presentation. An aerial view of the property was
         provided, as well as a description and illustration of the proposed sign. The decision of the Community
         Appearance Board's (CAB) denial was reviewed. Staff analysis concluded that while the CAB may restrict
         design aspects of a sign, they may not deny placement of a sign where the Code otherwise allows it. Staff
         recommends that the CAB decision be reversed, with conditions that the location and size of the sign be
         altered. The applicant agrees to the changes. Upon concluding, Mr. Budazewski made himself available for
         members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Michael Buttress, on behalf of the appellant, stated he had nothing further
         to add and thanked Mr. Budaszewski for his presentation. Jay Feinberg, also on behalf of the appellant,
         provided additional information and responded to members' questions. Glenn Gromann expressed his
         support. Seeing no one else indicate their desire to speak, the public hearing was closed. There was no
         cross-examination or rebuttal.

                       Motion was made by Council Member Thomson, seconded by Council Member Mayotte, to
                       approve Resolution No. 100-2020 reversing the decision of the Community Appearance Board
                       and granting the appeal with the recommended conditions. Motion carried unanimously;
                       Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine
                       O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
