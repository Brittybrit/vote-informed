 1.     PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG

 2.     ROLL CALL

         Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker
         Council Member Fran Nachlas

The Mayor announced the purpose of the special meeting.

 3.     PUBLIC HEARING

         A.     Resolution No. 85-2024

                A resolution of the City of Boca Raton equalizing the assessments; levying the same as
                assessments against benefited properties within the Downtown Special Assessment District,
                City of Boca Raton, Florida; directing the City Clerk to record assessments in the Improvement
                Lien Book; establishing priority of lien and payment of principal and interest; providing for
                severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

          Stephen Timberlake, Special Projects Manager, gave the presentation. He provided a brief history of
          the Downtown Special Assessment, the requirement to fund the debt service, and administrative
          costs for the special assessment revenue bonds. Mr. Timberlake noted that the purpose of the
          meeting was to hear and consider any objections to the special assessment. The method of
          calculating the assessment rate was explained and various charts and graphs were presented. No
          objection letters were received. Upon concluding, Mr. Timberlake made himself available for
          questions. In response to Council Member Wigder's question, Mr. Timberlake advised that the special
          assessment bonds would be paid off in 2030.

          The public hearing was opened. Eddie Weinstein had no objection to the assessment but noted that
          the biggest problem was parking ; he commented on developers building multimillion dollar
          condominiums with only 1.75 parking spaces per unit, parking meters, clarification on the benefit of
          the assessment and planting additional shade trees. Jonathan Ounjian commented on the
          assessment report and stated he felt some numbers were inaccurate. City Manager George Brown
          provided additional information on the initial downtown assessment, shade trees, parking meters,
          and crosswalks. The reasons for the downtown assessment were also elaborated.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Fran Nachlas, seconded by Council Member
                        Marc Wigder, to approve Resolution No. 85-2024. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                        Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council
                        Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.
