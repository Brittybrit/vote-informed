                            MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING
                                         CITY COUNCIL
                                 CITY OF BOCA RATON, FLORIDA
                                   THURSDAY, MARCH 31, 2022
                                            10:30 AM

The Organizational Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was held in the Auditorium at
         6500 Congress Avenue. The meeting was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at 10:30 AM.

 1.     CALL TO ORDER:

 2.     PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

 3.     ROLL CALL:

          Mayor Scott Singer
          Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke
          Council Member Yvette Drucker
          Council Member Monica Mayotte
          Council Member Andy Thomson

 4.     ORGANIZATIONAL PROCEEDINGS:

         A      Selection of Deputy Mayor

                        Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Mayor Scott
                        Singer, to reappoint Andrea Levine O'Rourke as Deputy Mayor. Motion carried
                        unanimously on a voice vote.

                Selection of CRA Chairman and Vice Chairman

                        Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                        Member Yvette Drucker, to reappoint Monica Mayotte as CRA Chair. Motion carried
                        unanimously on a voice vote.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                        Andrea Levine O'Rourke, to reappoint Andy Thomson as CRA Vice Chair. Motion
                        carried unanimously on a voice vote.

 5.     OTHER BUSINESS:

         A      Selection of TPA (Transportation Planning Agency) Representatives and Alternates

                        Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                        Member Monica Mayotte, to designate Yvette Drucker and Andy Thomson as the
                        representatives to the TPA. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

                        Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Monica
                        Mayotte, to designate Scott Singer, Andrea Levine O'Rourke, and Monica Mayotte as
                        the TPA Alternates at large. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

         B.     Selection of Palm Beach County League of Cities Voting Delegate & Alternate

                        Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine
                        O'Rourke, to appoint Monica Mayotte as the PBC League of Cities Voting Delegate.
                        Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.
                            Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                            Member Monica Mayotte, to appoint Yvette Drucker as the PBC League of Cities Voting
                            Alternate. Motion carried on a voice vote.

          C.      Selection of IPARC (lnterlocal Plan Amendment Review Committee) Representative

                            Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                            to appoint Andrea Levine O'Rourke as the /PARC Representative. Motion carried
                            unanimously on a voice vote.

 6.      ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 10:35 am.
