I.     Welcome - Mayor Scott Singer and Chair Erin Wright

The meeting was called to order at 6:35 pm. Mayor Scott Singer and Chair Erin Wright made opening
comments and members introduced themselves.

II.     Discussion Topics

Chair Wright asked to move item I1.E., Patch Reef Park Playground Renovations, to be considered first. There
was consensus from all Members to do so.

        E.     Patch Reef Park Playground Renovations

        Chair Erin Wright asked that the permitting process for this renovation be expedited and that all fees
        be consolidated for payment at the end of the project. Acting City Manager George Brown advised that
        City staff would accelerate the playground renovation and confirmed that the fees could be payable at
        the end . Commissioner Rollins provided additional information regarding the urgency of the request,
        citing health issues of a local child with special needs.

        A.      Parks and Recreation Master Planning

        Executive Director Briann Harms noted that she had discussed this topic with Mr. Brown and expressed
        the desire of the Greater Boca Raton Beach and Park District (District) to collaborate with the City on
        where to put recreation facilities so as to make the best use of their resources. Mr. Brown confirmed
        that he had spoken with Ms. Harms on this subject and agreed that staff of both entities could work
        together to present a plan to the bodies that would meet current and future recreation needs. Members
        weighed in and Mr. Brown stated that staff would work on creating a timeframe for the plan.

        B.      North Park Project (Boca Teeca) Update

        Ms. Wright showed renderings of the conceptual design for the Boca Teeca golf course property, now
        known as the North Park Project, and reviewed components of the proposal for the southeast and
        northeast quadrants of the property. Mayor Scott Singer noted that the plans to connect the east and
        west portions of Jeffery Street were still in process. Liudmila Fuentes, representing consultant Miller
        Legg, advised that she was aware of the plans for Jeffery Street and was consulting with City staff. Ms.
        Fuentes then responded to questions and provided additional details regarding the drawings shown.
        Council Member Marc Wigder inquired about the western portion of the property. Ms. Wright and District
        Counsel Jacob Horowitz outlined the limits of development on land subject to the bonds, and Ms. Harms
        stated that 2026 was the earliest the bonds could be paid off without a penalty. Mr. Wigder then asked
         if there had been consideration given to the City taking over development of the western portion of the
         property, and Ms. Wright advised that District Commissioners had not had conversations on that topic.
         Discussion ensued between Council and Commissioners regarding elements of the proposed design ,
         including bike trails, the possibility of a Jeffery Street tunnel, and improved mobility and walkability.
         There was consensus from Council Members to approve the conceptual plan presented.

         C.        CRA

         Chair Wright read a statement into the record regarding the District's position on the proposed extension
         of the CRA and their request for an exemption from the annual tax increment financing (TIF) payments.
         Mr. Brown then summarized the request for District residents to use the downtown Community Center
         and advised that an analysis of the impact to City residents had not yet been performed , but that staff
         could collect the data. Discussion ensued regarding the extension of the TIF, City resident rates
         compared to those in the district, and the 1986 interlocal agreement between the two bodies.

         D.        Children's Museum Relocation Options

         Mr. Brown opened the discussion by explaining that the City was exploring the feasibility and relocation
         options for the Children's Museum in order to incorporate its current location as part of the future
         government campus. He shared that City staff had discussed with District staff the possibility of
         relocating the building to Sugar Sand Park, and should that occur, the District's desire for the City to
         handle operations of the Museum. Conversation followed on a number of topics, including the timeline
         of the proposed project, the impact on the environment, and other City recreational amenities that might
         be affected .

Ill.     Public Comment

         The floor was opened for public comment. Robert Dukate, on behalf of the Boca Raton Bicycle Club,
         read a resolution prepared by the Bicycle Club into the record regarding bicycle safety. Raul Travieso
         thanked Council and District Commissioners for their support of the pickleball playing community and
         complimented the steps taken to incorporate the sport into City parks. Cil Ciliberti asked that City plans
         for development of the Golf and Racquet Club be shared with the District.
         Angelo Sands commented on off-course golf services and expressed his opposition to private groups
         being involved in the development of the North Park project. Garth Duff-Gray , representing the Boca
         Raton Junior Rugby Club, thanked the City and District for their support. Harold Chaffee voiced his
         support for a recreation master plan, a needs assessment, the connectivity of City parks, and expressed
         concern regarding the safety for pedestrians on the Clint Moore bridge. Veronica Quintero lobbied for
         more open space for the game of ultimate frisbee. Betsy Caceres highlighted Hispan ic heritage month
         and requested spaces for programming for the Hispanic community. Peter Sachs spoke in support of
         his client, one of the applicants to develop a sports/racquet facility on the north portion of the North
         Park project. John Holloway, President and CEO of Coastal Stewards, shared that the organization
         was moving forward with the Gumbo Limbo gift shop, the sea turtle educational permit, and the turtle
          rehabilitation permit. Mary Sol Gonzalez expressed her support for saving the Children's Museum.
          Jamie Godino requested performance space for the theater that he represented.


Council Members and District Commissioners made final comments and thanked all those in attendance for
their participation. There was consensus to schedule the next Joint Meeting in six months.
