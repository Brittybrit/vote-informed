 20.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Mayotte read a letter she had prepared that was addressed to Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers,
citing his recent public comments and social media posts regarding Covid-1_9 related restric~ions. She shared her
concern that his remarks could be misinterpreted by some to be representative of the Council as a whole.
                                                       3                                            June 9, 2020

Deputy Mayor Rodgers thanked Ms. Mayotte for her remarks and acknowledged their differing views on the current
state of emergency. He asserted that he had been speaking solely on behalf of himself and his constituents in the
instances cited and would continue to do so. Council Member O'Rourke expressed her agreement with Ms. Mayotte
and shared her apprehension that Mr. Rodgers' position about Covid-19 issues could be mistakenly attributed to
the Council. A brief discussion ensued.

Council Member O'Rourke responded to communication she received from residents asking the City to require the
wearing of facial coverings in public. She noted the impracticality of enforcing such a mandate and asked residents
to be mindful of others. She also commented positively on the March for Unity.

Council Member Thomson thanked his fellow Council members for working together and relayed a conversation he
had with neighboring elected officials about the participation rate of the 2020 Census. Mr. Thomson suggested
additional strategies to encourage residents to complete the Census. Members expressed their support.

Mayor Scott Singer reiterated the importance of completing the Census, stressing the impact of the results on the
amount of Federal funding received. He remarked positively regarding the peaceful protests within the City and the
previous weekend's March for Unity.
