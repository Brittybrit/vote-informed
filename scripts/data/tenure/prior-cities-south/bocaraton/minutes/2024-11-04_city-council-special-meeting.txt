 5.     CITY MANAGER REPORTS:

         A.       TCA&I - Follow Up to 10/21/24 Meetings

                  Mayor Singer announced that the purpose of the Special Meeting was to have a follow up
                  discussion to the October meetings . The public comment period was opened and , seeing
                  no one come forward to speak, closed . Mayor Singer noted that City Manager George
                  Brown provided a detailed report at the preceding CRA meeting . Mr. Brown advised that,
                  based upon the decision of the CRA, the Council would have the same suspension of any
                  action until January 7, 2025 , and a report would be provided at the January 13th CRA and
                  Council Workshop meetings. Mayor Singer clarified that the City was not waiving any rights
                  to act before then , but that he did not anticipate any action prior to receiving more
                  information from TCA&I , including figures regarding their fundra ising efforts.
