        A          Resolution No. 125-2017
                   A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a Conditional Use approval pursuant to
                   Section 28-1098(9), Code of Ordinances, to allow a family resource center (career training and
                   counseling facility), in the General Industrial (M-2) zoning district for the property located at 1595
                    NW 1st Court; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (CA-17-02)
                            Resolution No. 125-2017
                            Drawings

        The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
        made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

        Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He reviewed the
        request and identified the location and zoning of the property and provided background information,
        including that a building permit for the facility had been issued by the City in error. Mr. Schaad then
        reviewed the reasons for the determination that the requested use would adversely affect the surrounding
        area. The applicant's justifications for the requested use of the building as a career training and
        counseling facility were listed. Mr. Schaad then detailed the numerous problems currently created by the
        existing facility, most notably parking, that would be exacerbated by approval of the application. The
        traffic analysis was provided and the actions of the Planning and Zoning Board were reviewed. Staff
        recommended denial of the request. Mr. Schaad noted that if Council decided to approve the conditional
        use, there were conditions in the resolution addressing the issues identified by staff. Upon concluding, he
        responded to Council members' questions, as did Transportation Planning Engineer Quan Yuan.
        Discussion of a number of issues ensued.

        The public hearing was opened. Christina Bilenki, Dunay Miske! Backman, on behalf of Boca Helping
        Hands, gave a PowerPoint presentation. Details of the services and programs of Boca Helping Hands
        were provided. It was noted that Boca Helping Hands has opened locations in West Boca Raton and
        Boynton Beach to serve residents in those areas. Information was provided on the job training programs
        offered at the main facility. It was noted that the building in question would provide an environment more
        conducive to learning for clients participating in the job training programs. Ms. Bilenki then reviewed the
        site plan, noting that the recommendations of the Planning and Zoning Board had been incorporated in
        same. She then responded to a number of assertions made in the City staff presentation, including the
        parking issue. In conclusion, she noted that Boca Helping Hands is working to continue to address these
        issues. She then introduced Gary Peters, Board President, who talked about the work of Boca Helping
        Hands and the numerous people who volunteer there. He requested approval of the application, and
        then responded to Council members' questions. Jack Webb, representing the family charitable trust that
        donated the building in question, offered support of the Boca Helping Hands and the job training
        programs that would take place in the facility. Bill Trinka commented on the conditional use approval
        initially granted to Boca Helping Hands. He presented a number of pictures illustrating the problems he
        asserts are caused by Boca Helping Hands that impact his neighboring property, including parking,
        loitering, and garbage. Susan Whiting supported the need for training people and also the need for
        parking safety. She asked that the outstanding issues be addressed before granting this request. Dr.
        Charles Tate offered support of Boca Helping Hands and the irreplaceable service it provides. He noted
        that the problems detailed are only getting worse because so many people need these services.

        Ms. Bilenki addressed the ways in which the problems detailed by the neighbors were being handled.
        Council Member O'Rourke asked for specifics, to which Ms. Bilenki responded. In response to Council
        Member Weinroth, Mr. Peters acknowledged that a condition to have personnel police the area was
        acceptable, and then responded to further questions. Deputy Mayor Rodgers asked that a condition be
        included to place garbage receptacles outside. Council Member Singer asked a number of questions
        about parking and other topics, to which Mr. Peters and Ms. Bilenki responded.          During cross
        examination, Bill Trinka refuted a number of the previous comments. Seeing no one else come forward,
        the public hearing was closed.

                    Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                    Rodgers, to approve Resolution No. 125-2017.
