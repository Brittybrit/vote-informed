I.     Welcome - Mayor Scott Singer and Chair Susan Vogelgesang

       Mayor Scott Singer called the meeting to order at 5:30 pm.

II.    Discussion Topic

       A.      Ocean Breeze Golf Course

       Mayor Scott Singer began by briefly reviewing the outcome of the prior Joint Meeting. Chair Susan
       Vogelgesang of the Greater Boca Raton Beach & Park District (District) invited Commissioners to discuss
       the options for golf course operation. Wayne Branthwaite, on behalf of Price-Fazio, reviewed the reduced
       scope of work within the proposed project. Lengthy discussion ensued regarding the scale of the project
       and whether or not the City would issue an RFP for alternate course designs.

Ill.   Public Comment

       The floor was opened for public comment. Greg Galanis, Bruce Diller Verstandig, Rick Heard, Jim
       Sumislaski, Angelo Sands, Richard Close, Robert DuKate, Cil Gilberti, and Harold Chaffee commented on
       the design and cost of the proposed course, and the desire to see the golf course built quickly. Mr.
       Branthwaite offered to meet with City staff to provide additional background on the Price-Fazio design.
       Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public comment period was closed.

       Further discussion ensued. Topics included, but were not limited to, the extent of the District's approval of
       the City's course design, responsibility for operating expenses, and the prospect of shared profits and
       losses. City Manager Leif Ahnell advised staff will create an updated interlocal agreement before the end
       of the year. Council Members and Commissioners made final comments. There was consensus to schedule
       the next joint meeting on Monday, January 13 at 6:00 pm.

IV.    Adjournment

       The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 8: 18 pm.
