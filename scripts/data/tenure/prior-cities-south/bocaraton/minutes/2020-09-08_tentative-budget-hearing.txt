               The Tentative Budget Hearing of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida
                             was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at 6:00 PM.

Mayor Singer welcomed all those listening to the Tentative Budget Hearing prior to reading a brief statement
explaining that the meeting would be conducted as a Virtual Meeting using Communications Media Technology.
He then listed the members of the City Council appearing remotely, noting that Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers was
absent due to military service. He also noted that City Manager Leif Ahnell and City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser
were also attending remotely.

 3.     PRESENTATION ON TENTATIVE BUDGET, PROPOSED MILLAGE RATES, AND CAPITAL
        IMPROVEMENTS PROGRAM:

City Manager Leif Ahnell gave PowerPoint presentations on the proposed budget and millage rate. He began by
observing that, although the annual Goal Setting sessions were not held due to the Coronavirus pandemic, staff
was continuing to work on 2020 objectives and new goals had been added. Mr. Ahnell reviewed the City's guiding
principles - Financially Sound City, World Class Municipal Services, Strong Partnership with Community, and
Vibrant and Sustainable City - and noted policy priorities associated with these principles. Tables and charts were
presented illustrating millage rates and assessed property valuations, and allocation of operating expenses. Mr.
Ahnell reviewed the City flyer that explains residents' property tax bill. He highlighted comparisons of Boca Raton
to other cities' fire assessment fee, property tax rates, and water and sewer rates. No increase in the fire assessment
fee was proposed, and water and sewer rates will remain unchanged. A small increase in sanitation rates was
proposed to account for additional staff and capital equipment. The presentation was then turned over to Sharon
McGuire, Office of Management and Budget Director, who reviewed the 2020-21 portion of the six-year Capital
Improvements Program. Ms. McGuire highlighted requests of City departments for various infrastructure projects,
vehicles and equipment, technology updates, and renovations. Upon concluding, Ms. McGuire responded to
questions, as did the City Manager.

 4.      PUBLIC HEARING ON TENTATIVE BUDGET AND PROPOSED MILLAGE RATES:

          A.    Resolution No.127-2020
                A resolution of the City of Boca Raton adopting proposed millage rates necessary to fund a tentative
                budget for the 2020-2021 fiscal year; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an
                effective date

          B.    Resolution No. 128-2020
                A resolution of the City of Boca Raton adopting a tentative budget for the 2020-2021 fiscal year;
                providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

        The City Clerk read Resolution No. 127-2020 in full and the title of Resolution No. 128-2020.

         C.      Discussion, public comments and explanation of "percentage increase in the proposed millage
                 rate over the rolled-back rate necessary to fund the budget, if any."
          Council members had no questions regarding the proposed millage rate or the tentative budget.

           D.       Open public hearing on tentative budget and proposed millage rates.

          Mayor Singer opened the public hearing, noting that submissions had been received by representatives
          of the non-profit organizations and it would not be necessary for them to make additional presentations.
          The City Clerk read a question from Gil Garmin asking if the City's cultural budget had been reduced. Mr.
          Ahnell responded. No one else indicated their desire to speak.

           E.       City Council "shall amend the tentative budget as it sees fit."

              The City Manager reminded Council members to submit their proposed budget modifications prior to the
              date of the final budget hearing, September 22. No amendments were made to the tentative budget.

           F.       Re-computation of proposed millage rates.

          There was no re-computation of the millage rate.

           G.       Public announcement of percent, if any, by which recomputed proposed millage rates exceed
                    "rolled-back rate", characterized as "percentage decrease in property taxes tentatively adopted."

          No announcement was necessary.

           H.       Adopt Resolution No. 127-2020 incorporating recomputed proposed millage rates.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor Andrea
                         Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 127-2020. Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor
                         Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson
                         voting yes.

           I.       Adopt Resolution No. 128-2020 incorporating amended tentative budget.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member Andy
                        Thomson, to approve Resolution No. 128-2020. Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea
                        Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting
                        yes.

           J.       Close public hearing.

         The public hearing was closed.
