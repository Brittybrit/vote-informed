                               MINUTES OF THE SPECIAL MEETING
                                         CITY COUNCIL
                                 CITY OF BOCA RATON, FLORIDA
                                 MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 23, 2024
                                            1:30 PM*

               *The Special Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida
                        was held in the Auditorium at 6500 Congress Avenue.

1.     CALL TO ORDER:

The meeting was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at approximately 4:01 PM.

2.     PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

3.     ROLL CALL:
        Mayor Scott Singer
        Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker
        Council Member Fran Nachlas
        Council Member Andy Thomson
        Council Member Marc Wigder

4.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARING:

 Mayor Singer announced the purpose of the special meeting and noted that the public hearing on the
 Community Redevelopment Agency (CRA) resolution for this matter had been postponed to the October 21,
 2024, CRA meeting. He suggested postponing the public hearing on the City Council resolution to the same
 date.

        A.    Resolution No. 97-2024
              A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering the approval of the "Landlord Plans"
              submitted by The Center for Arts and Innovation, Inc. in connection with the development of a
              performing arts center and the redevelopment of the Amphitheater, as required by the Pre­
              Construction and Development Agreement entered into between the Boca Raton Community
              Redevelopment Agency, the City of Boca Raton, and The Center for Arts and Innovation, Inc.
              on October 12, 2022; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective
              date

         The Mayor encouraged Council Members wishing to comment on this item to wait until the October
         21, 2024, meeting date. The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak,
         closed. The Mayor then suggested a motion to adjourn this matter to a special meeting to be held on
         October 21, 2024, at the conclusion of the CRA meeting.

                               Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council
                               Member Nachlas, to postpone Resolution No. 97-2024 to a special meeting to
                               be held on October 21, 2024, immediately following the CRA meeting. Motion
                               carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker,
                               Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council Member Andy Thomson, Council
                               Member Marc Wigder voting yes.
5.     ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 4:03 PM.
