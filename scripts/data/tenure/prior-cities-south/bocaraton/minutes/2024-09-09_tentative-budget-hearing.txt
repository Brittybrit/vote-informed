3.    PRESENTATION ON TENTATIVE BUDGET, PROPOSED MILLAGE RATES, AND CAPITAL
      IMPROVEMENTS PROGRAM:                                  •

      Budget Director Sharon McGuire gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began by reviewing the
      Mission, Vision and Values, and Strategic Priorities. Ms. McGuire explained the proposed millage rate
      of 3.6782 mils, which was slightly lower than the FY 2023-24 rate. She further explained the General
      Fund Operating Costs, new additions to the Fiscal Year 2024-25 Budget, and described the Property
      Tax Bill by Taxing Authority breakdown . Also discussed were the tax rates and Fire Assessment fees
      of surrounding cities and how Boca Raton is the lowest of the cities compared. Ms. McGuire concluded
      her presentation regarding millage rates and made herself available for Members' questions.

      Ms. McGuire continued by briefly reviewing the Capital Improvements Program for Fiscal Year 2024/25-
      2029/30. Upon concluding, she made herself available for questions.

 5.      RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS

           A.      Resolution No. 89-2024

                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton imposing Fire Services Assessments against Assessed
                  Property (residential, commercial, and industrial) located within the City for the fiscal year
                  beginning October 1, 2024; approving the fiscal year 2024-2025 rate of assessment; approving
                  the fiscal year 2024-2025 Assessment Roll (based on the rate of assessment); providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

           Mayor Singer reminded everyone that the final millage rate and final budget would be determined at
           the Final Budget Hearing on Monday, September 23, 2024, at 6PM .

           City Manager Brown noted that fire services assessments were not increasing over last year and
           would remain at $155.00 for single family residences and no changes according to the schedule for
           commercial properties.

           The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, was closed .

          B.      Resolution No. 90-2024

                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton adopting an amended and restated Boca Raton
                  Municipal Facilities and Services User Fee Schedule; providing for severability; providing for
                  repealer; providing an effective date

           Compliance Auditor Jean-Michel Neault gave the presentation. He began with an explanation of the
           purpose of user fees and gave a brief review of the proposed changes to the User Fee Schedule.
           Upon concluding, he responded to Members' questions. Council Member Wigder voiced his concerns
           with the increase in golf fees, especially since the Golf and Racquet Club was under construction. He
           then asked if consideration would be given to not increasing the fees. Discussion ensued and Mr.
           Brown provided additional information.

           The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, was closed.

           Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member Fran Nachlas,
           to approve Resolution No. 90-2024.

           Under discussion, Council Member Wigder asked that they consider not increasing golf fees or the
           frequent player card annual fee at both the Golf and Racquet Club and Red Reef Golf Courses.

                           Motion was made by Council Member Wigder to amend Resolution No. 90-2024 to
                           eliminate the increase to the daily fees, resident and non-resident fees at both golf
                           courses and the frequent player card cost at the Golf and Racquet Club.

           Discussion ensued . Mr. Brown recommended Council adopt the User Fee Schedule as presented ,
           and advised staff would then gather the information on the proposed increase for Council, and the
           schedule could be amended at any time thereafter. Council Member Wigder withdrew his motion to
           amend.

                           On the original motion to approve Resolution No. 90-2024 as presented, motion carried
                           4 votes to 1; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran
                           Nachlas, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes, Council Member Marc Wigder
                           voting no.

 6       ADJOURNMENT

 The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 6:41 PM.
