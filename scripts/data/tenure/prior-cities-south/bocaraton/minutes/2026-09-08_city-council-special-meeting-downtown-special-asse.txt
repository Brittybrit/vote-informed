3.     PUBLIC HEARING:

       A       Resolution No. 99-2026
               A resolution of the City of Boca Raton equalizing the assessments; levying the same as
               assessments against benefited properties within the Downtown Special Assessment District,
               City of Boca Raton , Florida; directing the City Clerk to record assessments in the improvement
               lien book; establishing priority of lien and payment of principal and interest; providing for
               severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

      Special Projects Manager Stephen Timberlake gave the presentation . Mr. Timberlake began by noting
      that the assessment funds debt service and administrative costs for special assessment bonds
      supporting infrastructure projects that benefit downtown property owners. He advised that the purpose
      of the special meeting was to hear comments and concerns regarding the assessments and consider
      adjustments and equalization . Mr. Timberlake stated that the total assessment amount would not
      change, only the pro-rata share assigned to individual parcels. The 2026 assessment rate is 0.000100,
      a decrease from 2025, due primarily to an increase in downtown property values. Upon concluding, Mr.
      Timberlake made himself available for Member's questions.

      The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward , was closed .

                        Motion was made by Council Member Stacy L. Sipple, seconded by Council Member
                        Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 99-2026. Motion carried unanimously on a
                        voice vote; Mayor Andy Thomson, Deputy Mayor Michelle Grau, Council Member
                        Yvette Drucker, Council Member Stacy L. Sipple voting yes.

 4.    ADJOURNMENT

       The meeting adjourned without objection at approximately 10:23 AM .
