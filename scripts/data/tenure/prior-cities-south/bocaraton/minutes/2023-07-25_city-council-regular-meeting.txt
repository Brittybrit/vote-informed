 11 .   REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A.       Ordinance No. 5653
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton establishing procedures and regulations to implement
                 Section 166.04151 (7), Florida Statutes, as created by Chapter 2023-17, Laws of Florida (Live
                 Local Act of 2023) for development of statutorily authorized mixed-use residential development
                 (including affordable housing); noting the business and commercial, industrial, and
                 miscellaneous zoning districts in which the mixed-use directive is applicable; providing land
                 development regulations (including those generally applicable in multifamily zoning districts
                 and providing an administrative approval process); noting the statutorily mandated maximums
                 for density and height; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for
                 codification; providing an effective date (AM-23-07)

          Acting City Manager George Brown gave the presentation. He began with a brief review of the Live
          Local Act and the proposed Ordinance No. 5653, noting that the intent of the ordinance was to comply
          with the Live Local Act by updating the Zoning and Development processes. He further advised that
          65 percent of the total square footage must be residential. The Planning and Zoning Board and staff
          recommended approval as revised. Upon concluding, Mr. Brown made himself available for
          Members' questions. Discussion ensued and Mr. Brown responded to Members' comments and
          questions. Council Member Marc Wigder proposed an amendment that the Floor Area Ratio (FAR.)
          be proportional to the new required density. Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte agreed with Mr. Wigder's
          proposed amendment and asked if the City had any FAR. limits. Council Member Yvette Drucker
          shared her concern that the City was preempting the State's Live Local Act. City Attorney Diana Grub
          Frieser provided additional information and answered Members' questions.

          The public hearing was opened . Jonathan Ounjian spoke in favor of affordable housing and advised
          that the density in the downtown should also apply to the new Live Local Act, and this proposed
          ordinance. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

          Further discussion ensued. Ms. Frieser clarified comments made and provided additional information
          regarding the FAR. , density, and confirmed that the proposed ordinance was in accordance with the
          Live Local Act. Members proposed that staff investigate what actions other cities were taking and
          conduct additional research regarding the nonresidential uses and the location of those uses, the
          distribution percentages, the FAR. requirement as a limitation, and options to incentivize for bonus
          affordable units beyond the requirement.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council Member
                         Yvette Drucker, to postpone Ordinance No. 5653. Motion carried unanimously on a
                         voice vote.

 The meeting was recessed at 8:27PM and resumed at approximately 8:37PM.

                       codification; providing an effective date (AM-22-01)

          Mayor Singer noted that the hearings on Ordinance Nos. 5654 and 5655 would be held
          contemporaneously and that this was a transmittal hearing. He noted that this would be the first of
          two public hearings, and that three votes would be required to transmit Ordinance No. 5654.

          Development Services Director Brandon Schaad gave the presentation. He began with the
          background of the subject property and the proposed ordinances. Mr. Schaad discussed the revisions
          that had been made since the agenda publication, which included geographic eligibility, building
          massing model, and clarified design standards. He continued by discussing a potential closure of the
          Yamato Tri-Rail Station, status of existing buildings, and code enforcement where violations impact
          compliance of the Enhanced Mobility District (EMO). He further advised that there were two areas of
          disagreement between the City and the applicant: platting requirements and the issuance of a
          certificate of occupancy for a building without completing all construction in an approved development
          sequence. Upon concluding, Mr. Schaad made himself available for questions. Discussion ensued.
          Mayor Singer asked if the disagreements could be worked out after the ordinance is approved.

          The public hearing was opened. Attorney Bonnie Miske! spoke on behalf of the applicant. Ms. Miskel
          presented her client's concerns regarding the platting, certificate of occupancy (CO), and permitting
          requirement issues. Upon concluding , she made herself available for questions. Jonathan Ounjian
          spoke in favor of equal zoning for all districts in the City. Seeing no one else come forward, the public
          hearing was closed.

          Further discussion ensued. Mr. Schaad clarified comments made and offered additional information
          relating to the platting and the CO process. Ms. Frieser provided additional information. Mr. Wigder
          agreed with the platting requirements as proposed by staff and Ms. Miskel agreed that the CO
          requirements were a non-issue but that additional platting requirements are not necessary. Ms.
          Nachlas advised that her concern was ensuring the applicant delivers as promised.

                                   Member Yvette Drucker, to transmit Ordinance No. 5654 as revised.
