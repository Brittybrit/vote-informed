           A.      Resolution No. 146-2020
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering tentative plat approval for Boca Haven (a replat
                  of Lots 4 and 5 and a portion of Lot 3, Block 1, Bible Conference Estates) for an approximately 1.5
                  acre property generally located west of Northwest 6th Avenue and north and south of Northwest
                  6th Street extended; subject to conditions; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SUB-
                  19-02)

         The City Attorney read the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing. Members made
         their ex-parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

         Tori Golden, Land Development Coordinator, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The location of the
         proposed development was displayed, and the applicant's request was reviewed. Dimensions of the three
         subject lots were outlined. The Planning and Zoning Board and Development Services staff recommend
         approval. Upon concluding, Ms. Golden made herself available for members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Noel Barnett, on behalf of the applicant, thanked City staff for the
         presentation and made himself available for members' questions. Steven Utrecht expressed his support for
         the project based on an amendment clarifying that there would be no filling of the lake to enlarge a lot. John
         Holland gave a PowerPoint presentation and shared his apprehension regarding the proposed
         development of the subject lot and highlighted potential impacts to his adjacent property. Kenneth Green
         referred to a letter he submitted to Council Members regarding his concerns about the proposed project.
         Noel Barnett responded to Mr. Holland's concerns regarding construction on the lot and potential drainage
         issues. Stephen Eigles, owner of the subject property, confirmed that he had no plans to fill the lake, as
         well as his desire to preserve existing landscaping. Seeing no one else indicate their desire to speak, the
         public hearing was closed.

         Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided additional information regarding the conditions
         of approval. The City Attorney asked for clarification of the language to be added to the resolution. Mr.
         Schaad and Tori Golden read language into the record regarding the proposed conditions.

                       Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member Andy
                       Thomson, to approve Resolution No. 146-2020 as amended.

         Members made final comments.

                       Motion passed 4 to O; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council
                       Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

 11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

          A.       Resolution No. 137-2020
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a Planned Mobility Development (PMD) Master
                  Plan to authorize development of a 277-unit multifamily residential project and modify one (1)
                  development parameter, pursuant to Sections 28-1720(1) and 28-1843, Code of Ordinances, to
                  eliminate a required sidewalk in a specific location, for the property generally located at 6401, 6405,
                  6409, 6413 and 6421 Congress Avenue; considering a waiver of a plat requirement; providing for
                  repealer; providing an effective date (PMD-18-01)

         The Mayor confirmed the same quasi-judicial procedures previously read by the City Attorney would apply.
         Members made their ex parte disclosures. The Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.
Regular Minutes                                           5                                               October 14, 2020
        Tamashbeen Rahman, Chief Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began by noting that a site
        plan approval was not included in this proposal and would require a separate application, and if approved,
        the project would meet the residential unit capacity for the northwest planning area. Ms. Rahman provided
        an overview of the existing property, as well as a history of the location site plan approvals, noting that the
        proposed plan met all building setback requirements and requested no technical deviations. Ms. Rahman
        pointed out that there is no traffic signal at the existing driveway, but if warranted, installation would be
        funded by the property owner. Traffic flow and parking details were outlined, and the requirement for the
        applicant to install additional or enlarged sidewalks was discussed. A modification to development
        parameters that was supported by staff involved a decision to not install sidewalks in one particular area in
        order to preserve existing trees. Ms. Rahman explained the applicant's request for a waiver of platting
        requirements and variations from engineering design standards manual regarding parking space size. Both
        were supported by staff. The Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval. Ms. Rahman noted the
        rescheduling and re-advertisement of the item from the originally scheduled date. She advised the proposed
        project meets the intent of PMD and is consistent with the City's comprehensive plan. Staff recommends
        approval with the accompanying conditions included in the resolution. Upon concluding, Ms. Rahman
        responded to members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Ele Zachariades, on behalf of the applicant, gave the presentation. Ms.
         Zachariades reviewed the deviations requested and noted the project met the City's planned mobility
         parameters. The existing site plan was shown and compared to the proposed master plan, highlighting the
         existing and proposed sidewalk and noted the improvements being made on 65th street by the applicant.
         Ingress and egress from the property were reviewed. Pedestrian connectivity and variations or surface
         parking spaces were discussed. An elevation of the proposed building was shown. Upon concluding, she
         made herself available for questions. Ms. Zachariades confirmed there would be 25 electric car charging
         spaces throughout the property. John Donaldson, traffic engineer for the project, provided additional
         information. Jonathan Ounjian expressed his opposition to the project, citing his concerns with the project's
         density and resulting traffic. David Sergii conveyed his displeasure with a comment made by the
         representative of the applicant. Judy Farmer shared her concerns about project construction and how it
         would affect nearby businesses.

         During cross-examination, Mr. Ounjian asked for clarification about the determination of distance for
         intended uses. Ms. Zachariades responded. Mr. Schaad provided clarification regarding how the PMD
         matrix is utilized for evaluations and confirmed that staff has consistently applied the rules. He also noted
         that, per code, on-site notice was not required to be posted for this project. Ms. Zachariades made final
         comments. Seeing no one else indicate their desire to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                      Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member Andy
                      Thomson, to approve Resolution No. 137-2020.

         Members made final comments. Mr. Schaad and Maria Tejera, City Traffic Engineer, responded to
         questions.

                      Motion passed 4 to O; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council
                      Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

 16.     RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:

           A.      Resolution 163-2020
                   A resolution of the City of Boca Raton authorizing the acceptance of a donation of property located
                   at 17751 Boca Club Boulevard (consisting of approximately 167.17 acres and known as the Boca
                   Golf & Tennis Country Club) pursuant to the terms and conditions of a donation agreement
                   (including a joint defense agreement); authorizing, after donation, a lease of the property to the
                   donor for up to one year (to provide a transition period); authorizing a waiver of procurement for
                   an assumption of a lease of electric golf carts; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                   providing an effective date

         Deputy Mayor Michael Woika gave the PowerPoint presentation. The location and amenities of the property
         were reviewed. The history of the course and current ownership were discussed. The donation agreement,
         joint defense agreement, and ground lease were reviewed, and points of each were highlighted. The City
         Clerk read a letter into the record from the owners of the Boca Raton Hotel and Club. Mr. Woika noted that
         during the 11-month transition period, City staff would meet with neighbors to address their concerns. He
         advised there would be no debt associated with taking over the golf course and that staff would assess the
         potential fees and rates for play. Upon concluding, Mr. Woika made himself available for questions.

         The public comment period was opened. Ron Klein, Ann Averso, Frank Reiss, Herb Levy, Peter Pinter,
         Robert Trinchetto, Kenneth Denslow, expressed concerns about how the golf course donation would affect
         the security, traffic and property values of existing country club members and asked for a postponement of
         the vote. The Mayor read a letter into the record from Greg Galanis that stated his general support of the
         course donation and encouraged recreational development of the Ocean Breeze property. Jordan Serlin
         and Fred Weinberg shared concerns regarding jurisdiction issues for law enforcement and fire rescue
         responses. Craig Ehrnst expressed his support for the golf course donation. Shari and Sally Friedman,


Regular Minutes                                          7                                              October 14, 2020
         Jared Hershkowitz, Marilyn Soto, Kenneth Landau and Jeffry Chase conveyed their concerns about the
         course donation and possible affects to the surrounding residential neighborhood. The City Clerk read a
         comment submitted by Howard Markovitz asking for additional information about the donation.

                       Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member Monica
                       Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 163-2020.

         Deputy City Manager Mike Woika responded to several of the comments made by the public. He stated
         there were no known environmental concerns and that some needed improvements had been identified.
         Fees would be assessed and determined at a later time. Mr. Woika noted that it was not the City's intention
         to operate the western golf course at the same time as the new course. City Manager Leif Ahnell addressed
         several additional questions, including, but not limited to, traffic concerns, golf course, pool, and tennis court
         maintenance, and security. Mr. Ahnell also advised that the current owners of the golf course would not be
         receiving tax breaks from the City in exchange for the donation. Discussion ensued. Members expressed
         their support and shared their commitment to the recreational development of the Ocean Breeze property.

                       Motion passed on a 4 to O vote; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke,
                       Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

           B.     City Council Seat C Temporarily Unoccupied Position

         City Manager Leif Ahnell asked the City Clerk to read a letter from Council Member Jeremy Rodgers into
         the record. He then asked the Council for direction and advised the City Attorney could provide additional
         information. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser advised that, with respect to performing military service,
         elected officials may take a leave of absence which creates a 'temporarily unoccupied position'. Ms. Frieser
         explained that the remaining members of the body may elect an individual to fill the seat until either the
         elected official returns, or their term of office concludes. Discussion ensued. Additional information was
         provided by the City Attorney. Members thanked Council Member Rodgers for his service and Amanda
         Rodgers for her willingness to serve. Council Member Mayotte and Deputy Mayor O'Rourke suggested
         applicants be interviewed in order to fill the vacancy. There was consensus to direct staff to proceed with
         advertising of the temporary vacancy, and for applications to be due by 5:00 pm on October 22, 2020.

  Mayor Singer noted the time and asked for a motion to extend the meeting.
