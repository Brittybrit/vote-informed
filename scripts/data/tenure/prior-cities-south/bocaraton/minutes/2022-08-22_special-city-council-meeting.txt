 4.       REGULAR PUBLIC HEARING:


           A.        Ordinance No. 5616
                     An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton authorizing a transaction between the City, the Boca Raton
                     Community Redevelopment Agency( CRA), and the Center for Arts & Innovation Inc. ( formerly the
                     Boca Raton Arts District Exploratory Corporation ( BRADEC)), in connection with the development

                     of a performing arts center at Mizner Park and the redevelopment of the Mizner Park Amphitheater,
                     pursuant to Section 163. 380, Florida Statutes, and Chapter 13, Article III, " Sale of City Property,"
                     Code of Ordinances; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                      The proposed transaction affects parcels of real property owned by the City of Boca Raton and by
                     the Boca Raton Community Redevelopment Agency. The proposed sale of City- owned property
                     is being considered pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 13, Article III, City Code of Ordinances.
                     Pursuant to the requirements of Section 163. 380, Florida Statutes, notice of the potential
                     disposition of the property owned by the Community Redevelopment Agency was published March
                     30, 2021.   Because the disposition of the Agency' s property would be for less than fair market
                     value, the authorization of the City Council is required to complete the transaction.)

         The Mayor announced          that, based on the previous discussion at the Special Meeting of the CRA, this item
         will be postponed until a date certain of September 28, 2022. Council members agreed.

                               Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member Andy
                               Thomson, to postpone the public hearing on Ordinance No. 5616 to September 28, 2022,
                               following the conclusion of the Special CRA Meeting set for that date. Motion carried
                               unanimously on a voice vote.
