                           MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING
                                        CITY COUNCIL
                                CITY OF BOCA RATON, FLORIDA
                                   Wednesday, March 31, 2021
                                       HELD VIRTUALLY
                                           10:30 AM


         The Regular Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was called to order
                                     by Mayor Scott Singer at 10:31 AM.

Mayor Singer welcomed all those listening to the Regular Council Meeting prior to reading a brief statement
explaining that the meeting would be conducted as a Virtual Meeting using Communications Media Technology.
He then listed the members of the City Council appearing remotely, He advised that City Manager Leif Ahnell and
City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser were also attending remotely.

1.     CALL TO ORDER:

2      PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

3.     ROLL CALL:

         Mayor Scott Singer
         Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke
         Council Member Yvette Drucker
         Council Member Monica Mayotte
         Council Member Andy Thomson

4.     CEREMONIAL PROCEEDINGS:

               Recognition of outgoing Council Member Jeremy Rodgers

       The Mayor and Council members each commented on Council Member Rodgers' tenure on Council,
       thanking him for his support and dedication to public service. A farewell video created by Mr. Rodgers
       was shown. Mayor Singer then presented a plaque for Mr. Rodgers in recognition of his service.

        8.     Comments by newly elected Council Members Monica Mayotte and Yvette Drucker

        Mayor Singer congratulated both Ms. Mayotte and Ms. Drucker on their elections and invited them to
        speak. Ms. Mayotte began by thanking the community, her family, and colleagues for their support and
        commented on future issues and goals. She also thanked retiring City Clerk Susan Saxton for her
        dedication to the City and wished her all the best. Ms. Drucker also thanked all her supporters, family,
        and City staff for their help and encouragement and stated that she looked forward to working for a better
        Boca Raton.

5.     ORGANIZATIONAL PROCEEDINGS:

        A.     Selection of Deputy Mayor

        Council Member Andy Thomson nominated Monica Mayotte for Deputy Mayor. Motion died for lack of a
        second.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Mayotte, seconded by Council Member Drucker to
                        reappoint Andrea Levine O'Rourke as Deputy Mayor. Motion carried on a voice vote.
                 Selection of CRA Chairman and Vice Chairman

                            Motion was made by Deputy Mayor O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member Drucker, to
                            reappoint Monica Mayotte as CRA Chairman. Motion carried on a voice vote.

                            Motion was made by Deputy Mayor O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member Mayotte, to
                            reappoint Andy Thomson as CRA Vice Chairman. Motion carried on a voice vote.

6.      OTHER BUSINESS:

         A.      Selection of TPA (Transportation Planning Agency) Representatives and Alternates

         Mayor Singer commented that he was willing to step down from serving as the TPA representative in
         order to allow someone else the opportunity. Discussion ensued on how many representatives and
         alternates to nominate. Mayor Singer commented that Palm Beach County and other cities have multiple
         alternates, and he encouraged Members to choose two or three alternates to serve at large. Council
         Member Thomson noted that he would like to stay on this committee.

                            Motion was made by Council Member Mayotte, seconded by Deputy Mayor O'Rourke, to
                            designate Andy Thomson and Yvette Drucker as TPA Representatives. Motion carried on
                            a voice vote.

                            Motion was made by Mayor Singer, seconded by Council Member Mayotte, to designate
                            Scott Singer, Andrea Levine O'Rourke, and Monica Mayotte as the TPA Alternates at
                            large. Motion carried on a voice vote.

         B.      Selection of Palm Beach County League of Cities Voting Delegate & Alternate

                            Motion was made by Deputy Mayor O'Rourke, seconded by Mayor Singer, to appoint
                            Monica Mayotte as the PBC League of Cities Voting Delegate. Motion carried on a voice
                            vote.

                            Motion was made by Council Member Mayotte, seconded by Deputy Mayor O'Rourke, to
                            appoint Andy Thomson as the PBC League of Cities Voting Alternate. Motion carried on
                            a voice vote.

         C.      Selection of IPARC (lnterlocal Plan Amendment Review Committee) Representative

         Deputy Mayor O'Rourke commented that there are periodic meetings held virtually and noted she was
         willing to step down from the committee if Mayor Singer or another Council member wished to serve.

                            Motion was made by Mayor Singer, seconded by Council Member Mayotte, to appoint
                            Andrea Levine O'Rourke as the /PARC Representative. Motion carried on a voice vote

7.      ADJOURNMENT

Mayor Singer congratulated City Clerk Susan Saxton on her retirement and invited members to comment. Deputy
Mayor O'Rourke congratulated Ms. Saxton on her successful tenure as City Clerk and wished her well in her
retirement. Mayor Singer added that it was an honor and pleasure to work with Ms. Saxton and that he would
miss her leadership in this role. Ms. Saxton thanked everyone for their kind words.

Deputy Mayor O'Rourke congratulated Ms. Mayotte and Ms. Drucker on their successful elections.
