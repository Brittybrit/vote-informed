                          MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING
                                       CITY COUNCIL
                               CITY OF BOCA RATON, FLORIDA
                                Wednesday, September 12, 2018
                                          1:00 PM

      The Organizational Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was called to order
                                       by Mayor Scott Singer at 1:06 PM.

1.     CALL TO ORDER:

2.     PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

3.     ROLL CALL:

         Mayor Scott Singer
         Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers
         Council Member Monica Mayotte
         Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke

4.     CEREMONIAL PROCEEDINGS:

       A.     Administration of Oaths of Office for newly-elected Council Members

       Commissioner Steven Abrams made brief comments. He then administered the oath of office to Mayor­
       elect Scott Singer.

       Pastor Tommy Keidis also made brief remarks and then administered the oath of office to Council
       Member-elect Andy Thomson.

       After being sworn in, Mayor Singer thanked his fellow Council members, Commissioner Abrams, his
       family, City staff and his supporters. Council Member Andy Thomson thanked his family, friends and
       supporters.

5.     ORGANIZATIONAL PROCEEDINGS:

       A.     Selection of Deputy Mayor

       Council Member O'Rourke suggested that no change be made.

               Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member
               Andy Thomson, to reappoint Jeremy Rodgers as Deputy Mayor. Mayor carried unanimously on a
               voice vote.

Council Member Mayotte stated that, due to scheduling conflicts, she was unable to continue as an alternate
representative to the Transportation Planning Agency (TPA) and requested that another member be appointed to
fill that position. Mayor Singer noted that only Council Member Thomson had no committee assignments.
Council Member O'Rourke offered congratulations to her fellow Council members and noted that she looks
forward to working together.

       Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Monica Mayotte, to appoint
       Council Member Andy Thomson as the alternate representative to the TPA. Motion carried unanimously
       on a voice vote.

6.     OTHER BUSINESS:

There was no other business.
7.      ADJOURNMENT:

Council Member Mayotte congratulated Mayor Singer and welcomed Council Member Thomson to the dais. She
noted that importance of good discussion and coming to consensus working together. Deputy Mayor Rodgers
echoed those comments. Mayor Singer gave final words of thanks.

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 1:32 pm.
