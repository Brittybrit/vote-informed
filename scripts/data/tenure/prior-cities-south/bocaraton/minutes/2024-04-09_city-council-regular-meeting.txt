 10.   QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:
         A.       Resolution No. 24-2024
                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a plat entitled "Lot 8 of Lago Mar Shores"
                 (a replat of a parcel of land lying in Section 16, Township 47 South, Range 43 East) for the
                 approximately 0.17 acre property generally located at 980 Lago Mar Lane; authorizing the
                 Mayor and City Clerk to execute the plat; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                 (SUB-23-02)

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing.
         Members made their ex-parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who
         wished to speak.

         Ketcia Fleurilus, Land Development Coordinator, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She gave a
         brief history of the property and provided the proposed replat details and revisions. Ms. Fleurilus
         advised that the Planning and Zoning Board and staff recommended approval. She then read
         revised language regarding the pedestrian access easement. Upon concluding, she made herself
         available for questions and Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided additional
         information.

         The public hearing was opened. Attorney for the applicant Sarah Thompson, thanked staff, made
         herself available for questions, and noted that the applicant agreed with the additional revisions.
         Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council
                         Member Fran Nachlas, to approve Resolution 24-2024 as revised. Motion carried
                         unanimously. Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member
                         Fran Nachlas, Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder
                         voting yes.

15.     RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:

         A.      Resolution No. 29-2024
                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton authorizing the City Manager to execute a contract
                 with Circuit Transit Inc. for the purpose of providing Downtown, On Demand , Mobility
                 Services; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

         Municipal Services Director Zach Bihr gave the PowerPoint presentation. He reviewed the
         purpose and scope of services for the request for proposals. The areas of operation, fleet vehicle
         description, service mobile app and safety considerations were reviewed. Upon concluding, Mr.
         Bihr made himself available for questions. Discussion ensued, with Members sharing their
         comments and excitement for the new transportation service. City Manager George Brown and
         Mr. Bihr provided additional information and responded to Members' questions.

         The public comment period was opened. Glen Monroe and Luke Lacey of Boy Scout Troop 333
         both commented favorably on the circulator.

                         Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                         Fran Nachlas, to approve Resolution No. 29-2024. Motion carried unanimously.
                         Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas,
                         Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.
