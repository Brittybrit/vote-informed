                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering the reimbursement of the legal defense costs

                  severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

           Deputy Mayor O'Rourke withdrew the resolution from consideration and asked the City Attorney to
          provide additional information. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser explained that the proposed resolution
          concerned campaign endorsements by Council Members and suggested that an opinion clarifying the
          issue be requested from the Commission on Ethics. Ms. Frieser requested authorization from members
          to seek the opinion.

                           Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member
                           Yvette Drucker, to authorize the inquiry for opinion as requested. Motion carried
                           unanimously on a voice vote.

19.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

 Council Member Mayotte advised that she would not be available to attend the upcoming Environmental
 Advisory Board meeting and asked if another member could attend in her place. Council Member Drucker and
 Mayor Singer also noted scheduling conflicts and a brief discussion ensued. Ms. Mayotte then noted that the
 1-95 entrance ramp meters had been turned off while the Florida Department of Transportation explored
 options to improve their operation. She concluded her comments by highlighting the upcoming Food Truck
 Friday event.

 Council Member Drucker began by thanking staff for once again providing police reports to Council Members
 and confirmed with the City Manager that staff would contact the Boca Raton Historical Society regarding
 Certified Local Government (CLG) status. She ended her report by noting that schools were back in session
 and highlighted the importance of observing posted speed limits in school zones.

 Council Member Thomson echoed Ms. Drucker's comments regarding school zones and remarked on the
 change of traffic pattern at Spanish River High School. Mr. Thomson then read a letter from the principal of
 Spanish River High School commending Officer Brian Miller on his involvement at the school and dedication
 to keeping students safe at the crosswalks.

 Mayor Singer stated that the letter was an example of the many positive comments received regarding City
 staff and first responders and encouraged all to drive safely.

 20.     ADJOURNMENT:
