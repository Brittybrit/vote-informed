15.    RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:

         A       Resolution No. 119-2022
                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton providing legislative direction regarding whether (or not)
                 the City Council desires to consider an amendment to the zoning code to introduce Assisted
                 Living Facilities (ALFs) as an allowable use in the City's single-family zoning districts, and, if the
                 Council desires to consider such an amendment, directing the processing of such an amendment;
                 providing an effective date

        City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser provided a handout and gave a PowerPoint presentation. She began
        with an overview of the resolution which included, but was not limited to, background information on the
        original text amendment sought to the City's zoning code, the purpose of the proposed amendment,
        revised language in the final amendment request in February 2022, action taken by City staff and
        resulting legal action taken by the property owner. Ms. Frieser summarized the lawsuit brought by the
        property owner and reviewed the focus and purpose of any action to be taken by City Council. She
        advised the resolution was specifically created in response to the lawsuit and the direction sought from
        Council was whether or not to process the proposed ALF text amendment. Ms. Frieser stressed that the
        resolution was not quasi-judicial and did not place limitations on any future council consideration of an
        ALF amendment. Concern regarding the binary nature of the resolution was addressed. A clean revised
        version of Section 1 of the resolution was reviewed for clarification, and it was noted that the exhibit
        should be titled 'B' not 'A'. Notifications of the public hearing had been sent to the property owner of the
         proposed ALF and some residents of the surrounding neighborhood. The representatives of the property
        owner confirmed they were aware and agreed with the City to extend the response on the litigation
         knowing this matter was being discussed. Upon concluding, the City Attorney advised that she, City staff
         and outside counsel were available to respond to questions.

       At the request of Council Member Mayotte, Development Services Director Brandon Schaad displayed a
       map of properties within the City that would be eligible for ALF development under the criteria of the text
       amendment. Council Member Mayotte clarified the purpose of her request for the map and Mr. Schaad
       described the potential locations in further detail.

       The Mayor opened the public hearing. Barbara Caccaval Hodges spoke in opposition of the resolution.
       Ms. Frieser reiterated that the vote on the resolution would only be to provide direction to staff whether
       or not to process an application for a particular ALF. Ms. Hodges clarified that she was opposed to staff
       processing the ALF application. Jay Whelchel, applicant of the proposed ALF, spoke in support of the
       resolution and commented on the need for assisted living facilities and senior housing in the City. Holli
       Sutton, William Miller, Brian Stenberg, Jack McWalter, Sheila Mahon, Bill Snow, Laura Sweeney,
       Christine Lyons, Cynthia Briggs, Kathlean Gilio, Shannon Otto, John Gilio, Janna Evans, Pat Sipple,
       Glenn Gromann, Sandra Neklewicz, Lara Kittleson, and Ona Zerra expressed their opposition to the
       processing of the ALF application. Christine Hartofilis and Joanne Jackson spoke in support of
       processing. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

       Ms. Frieser commented that an appropriate motion was to either process or not process an amendment
       to the ALF text.

                       Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                       Andrea Levine O'Rourke, to direct staff to not process the text amendment.

       Ms. Frieser read provisions to the resolution into the record and Council Members offered their final
       thoughts.
                       Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine
                       O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                       Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
