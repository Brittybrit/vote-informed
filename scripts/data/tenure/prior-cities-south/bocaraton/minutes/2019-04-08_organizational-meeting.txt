     The Organizational Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was called to order
                                     by Mayor Scott Singer at 1: 18 PM.

1.   CALL TO ORDER:

2.   PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

3.   ROLL CALL:

       Mayor Scott Singer
       Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers
       Council Member Monica Mayotte
       Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke
       Council Member Andy Thomson

4.   ORGANIZATIONAL PROCEEDINGS:

      A.     Selection of Deputy Mayor

              Motion was made by Council Member Mayotte, to appoint Andrea Levine O'Rourke as Deputy
              Mayor. Motion failed for lack of second.

              Motion was made by Council Member O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member Thomson to
              reappoint Jeremy Rodgers as Deputy Mayor. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

      B.     Selection of CRA Chairman and Vice Chairman

              Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Thomson, to
              reappoint Andrea O'Rourke as CRA Chairman and Monica Mayotte as CRA Vice Chairman.
              Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

5.   OTHER BUSINESS:

      A.     Selection of TPA (Transportation Planning Agency) Representatives (2)

              Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Rodgers, seconded by Council Member O'Rourke, to
              designate Mayor Singer and Council Member Thomson as the representatives to the TPA and
              Deputy Mayor Rodgers and Council Member Mayotte as the alternate representatives. Motion
              carried unanimously on a voice vote.

      B.     Selection of Palm Beach County League of Cities Voting Delegate & Alternate

              Motion was made by Mayor Singer, seconded by Deputy Mayor Rodgers, to designate Council
              Member Mayotte as the voting delegate to the Palm Beach County League of Cities and Council
              Member O'Rourke as the alternate delegate. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

      C.     Selection of IPARC {lnterlocal Plan Amendment Review Committee) Representative

              Motion was made by Mayor Singer, seconded by Council Member Mayotte, to designate Council
              Member O'Rourke as the /PARC representative. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.
6.     ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 1:23 PM.
