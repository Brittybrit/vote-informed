                  Ordinance No. 5485
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Article IV of Chapter 12, Section 12-134, Code
                  of Ordinances, of the Police and Firefighters' Retirement System, regarding eligibility to serve on
                  the Board of Trustees; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification;
                  providing an effective date The public hearings on Ordinance No. 5486 and Resolution No. 60-
                  2019 will be conducted contemporaneously as the items are related.

          Linda Davidson, Financial Services Director, gave a brief explanation of the ordinance, noting that the
          amendment does not affect the pension plan. Ms. Davidson then made herself available for questions.

          The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member Andy
                  Thomson, to adopt Ordinance No. 5485. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy
                  Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
