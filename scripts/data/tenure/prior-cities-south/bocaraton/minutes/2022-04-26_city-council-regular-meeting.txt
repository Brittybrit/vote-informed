 10.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

          A.       Ordinance No. 5611
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the Boca Surgery Center located at 501
                  Glades Road, an amendment to Exhibit "A" (setting forth the site plan) of a universal conditional
                  (UC) approval (Ordinance No. 3564, as amended by Ordinance No. 3792) to authorize the
                  construction of an approximately 11,740 square foot medical office building and other site
                  improvements, including additions and modifications of surface parking area; providing for
                  repealer; providing an effective date (UC-86-01 R4/SC)

                  1.    Resolution No. 33-2022
                        A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for Boca Surgery Center located at
                        501 Glades Road, an amended and restated site plan (Resolution No. 91-05), to authorize
                        construction of an approximately 11,740 square foot medical office building and other site
                        improvements, including additions and modifications of surface parking area and
                        sidewalks; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (UC-86-01 R4/SPA)

           The Mayor noted that the public hearings on items 10.A. and B. would be conducted
           contemporaneously as the items were related, and a separate vote would be taken on each item.
           Ordinance No. 5611 would require four votes for adoption. Should item 10.A. not be adopted, item
           10.A.1. would not be considered. The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures which
           would govern the public hearing. Members made their ex parte disclosures, and the City Clerk
           administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

           Tamash Rahman, Chief Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began with a brief
           description of the applicant's request and provided background information on the property. The
           proposed site plan was displayed, and renderings of the building elevations were shown. Ms. Rahman
           then reviewed the results of the site plan traffic analysis. The Planning and Zoning Board
           recommended approval with several suggestions. Ms. Rahman noted that the required public notices
           and mailings had been executed. Staff recommended approval with the conditions included in the
           ordinance and resolution. Upon concluding, she responded to Members' questions.

           The public hearing was opened. George Gentile, President of 2GHO Inc., landscape architect and
           planner for the project, made the presentation. He began with drawings of the previously approved
           site plan compared to the proposed site plan. Components of the architecture were discussed, and
           renderings of the building elevations were shown. Mr. Gentile noted that less square footage was
           being requested than was originally approved, and that the applicant would be adding a new 8-foot
           public sidewalk and landscaping improvements. Upon concluding, he made himself available for
           questions. Glenn Gromann commented on the lack of prior maintenance of the existing building and
           landscaping. Harvey Plosker, current Medical Director of the property, spoke in support of the project
           but expressed concerns about the site plan, landscaping, and parking during construction. Dr. Plosker
           also inquired about the construction of new and improved monument sign, separate construction
           debris area, and whether there would be a construction time limit. There was no cross examination or
           rebuttal. Mr. Gentile made closing comments and addressed concerns raised during the public
           hearing. Seeing no one else come forward, the public comment was closed.

          B.       Resolution No. 31-2022
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the approximately 10.4-acre Temple Beth
                  El property generally located at 333 Southwest 4th Avenue: (i) an amended and restated
                  conditional use approval consolidating conditional use Resolution Nos. 192-1978, 82-1979, 175-
                  1979, 1-1984, 13-1992, 117-1992, 16-1996, 209-1996, 92-1998, 67-2013, and 059-2017, and
                  (ii) an amendment to the approved Conditional Use Approval (Resolution No. 192-1978, as
                  amended) to authorize the construction of an approximately 22,485-square foot, two (2)-story
                  mausoleum; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (CA-16-04R1)

         The City Attorney confirmed that the same quasi-judicial procedures would govern the public hearing.
         Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished
         to speak.

          Eoin Devlin, GIS Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began with a description of the
          location of the property and a review of the applicant's request. The history of the site was discussed,
          and components of the site plan amendment were outlined. Mr. Devlin then summarized the results of
          the parking study. The Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval with conditions. The
          required public notices and mailings were noted. Staff recommended approval with the attached
          conditions. Upon concluding, Mr. Devlin made himself available for questions.

          The public hearing was opened. Steve Kaufman, Executive Director of Temple Beth El, gave a
          PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Kaufman began with an aerial photograph that displayed the subject
          property and its surrounding neighbors. After providing a brief history of the Temple Beth El synagogue
          and mausoleum, a drawing of the proposed site plan was shown, and renderings of the proposed
          addition were displayed. Upon concluding, he made himself available for questions. Seeing no one else
          come forward, the public hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Council member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                          Andy Thomson, to approve Resolution No. 31-2022. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                          Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                          Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

          A        Ordinance No. 5610
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering a non-substantive revision to the City's
                  Comprehensive Plan to remove unnecessary (and erroneous) double-asterisks in Policy
                  LU.1.1.6. of the Future Land Use Element regarding density/intensity limits for certain land uses
                  in the land use categories; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an
                  effective date (SC-22-01)




Regular Minutes                                          4                                               April 26, 2022
         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He briefly
         reviewed the background of the Comprehensive Plan and highlighted the future land use categories
         that would be affected by the proposed clarification. The Planning and Zoning Board and staff
         recommend approval. Upon concluding, he made himself available for questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Joanne Snow spoke in support of the ordinance and shared misgivings
         regarding a proposed Assisted Living Facility (ALF}. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser noted that the
         proposed ordinance was not site-specific. Robert Sweetapple, attorney on behalf of Religious Science
         Unlimited, gave a PowerPoint presentation in opposition of the ordinance. Mr. Sweetapple outlined
         concerns with the legal advertising of the ordinance's public hearing and the processing of his client's
         application for an ALF. The City Attorney addressed the comments regarding the public notice and
         advised that litigation had already been filed by Mr. Sweetapple on behalf of his client. Bill Snow, Patrick
         Sipple, Janna Evans, Peter Boylan, Shane Kittelson, Lara Kittelson, Jennifer Gilson, Christine Lyons,
         Gene Hodges, and Lani Gospovic commented in support of the proposed ordinance and against the
         proposed ALF.

                         Motion was made by Deputy Mayor O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member Yvette
                         Drucker, to approve Ordinance No. 5610.

         Council Member Thomson commented on the concerns that had been raised regarding the public
         notice. Discussion ensued with the City Attorney providing additional information. Council Member
         Thomson spoke in support of re-noticing with revised language to highlight the transmittal of the
         ordinance. Council Members weighed in. There was consensus to re-notice the ordinance for a future
         meeting.

                         Deputy Mayor O'Rourke withdrew her motion.
