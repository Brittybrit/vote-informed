 12.       REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

            A       Resolution No. 129-2019
                   A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering an Interim Campus Development Agreement
                   between the Florida Atlantic University Board of Trustees and the City of Boca Raton for the period
                   ending no later than December 31, 2024; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing
                   an effective date

           Mayor Singer noted that this was the first of two public hearings and no vote would be taken. Deputy City
           Manager George Brown gave a PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Brown explained that the original Campus
           Development Agreement (CDA) expires on November 24, 2019. As the Florida Atlantic University (FAU)
           new Campus Master Plan is not yet finalized, the proposed Interim CDA was prepared in order to offset
           impacts until the Plan's completion. Minor adjustments to the Plan present no added impact to the City, and
           no additional development is authorized beyond the 2009 CDA. Upon concluding, Mr. Brown responded to
           questions.

           The public hearing was opened. Constance Scott, on behalf of FAU, expressed gratitude for the working
           relationship with the City of Boca Raton and Mr. Brown, in particular. Seeing no one else come forward, the
           public hearing was closed. Mayor Singer noted the second public hearing on this item would be on
           November 13, 2019.
