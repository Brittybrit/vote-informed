3.     REBUDGETING OF FY 2017 - 2018 BUDGET APPROPRIATIONS

Mayor Singer reviewed the purpose of the meeting, noting that rebudgeting would be considered during the public
hearing on the final budget.

4.     PUBLIC HEARING TO FINALIZE THE BUDGET AND ADOPT MILLAGE RATES

               Ordinance No. 5467
               An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton adopting final millage rates and levying ad valorem taxes
               for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2018 and ending September 30, 2019; stating the
               percentage by which the millage to be levied exceeds the "rolled-back rate"; directing the City
               Manager or the Office of Management and Budget Director to adiust the adopted millage rates in
               the event of changes in the assessment roll and taxable value; providing for severability;
               providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                       Ordinance No. 5467

               Ordinance No. 5468
               An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton adopting a final budget and appropriating funds for the
               fiscal year beginning October 1, 2018 and ending September 30, 2019; providing for severability;
               providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                        Ordinance No. 5468

       The City Clerk read Ordinance No. 5467 in full and the title of Ordinance No. 5468.

       City Manager Leif Ahnell gave a PowerPoint presentation on the 2018-19 budget. He provided an
       overview of change in millage rates and assessed property valuations and the identified the increase in
       general operating expenses and new personnel and programs proposed for the coming year. Various
       charts and graphs were presented illustrating favorable comparisons of Boca Raton to other city's fire
       assessment fees, property tax rates and water/sewer rates. The breakdown of the general fund program
       budget and the tax rate history were also discussed. Sharon McGuire, Office of Management and Budget
       Director, then reviewed the operating and capital improvement rebudgeted items, listed in Exhibit 1, which
       did not happen this year and will be carried over to the 2018-19 fiscal year budget. Mr. Ahnell provided
       additional information.

        C.     Open public hearing on final budget and final millage rates

       Mayor Singer opened the public hearing. David Sergei commented on parking for special events and
       funding to incentivize ridesharing. Council Members O'Rourke and Mayotte offered comments.
           D.       Discussion and explanation of percentage increase in millage over the rolled-back rate
                    necessary to fund the budget

           E.       Public comment

          There was no further public comment.

           F.       Close public comment

          The public comment period was closed.

          G.        City Council shall amend the adopted tentative budget as it sees fit

          Mayor Singer commented on the Non-Profit Funding Requests process. Members discussed changes to
          the funding list that each member had proposed. Individual asks were reviewed and changes were
          proposed. Working from the Mayor's list, changes were agreed to, resulting in an increase of $8,500 to
          the total amount of non-profit funding amount proposed by the Mayor.

                     Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andrea
                     Levine O'Rourke, to amend the budget to include the FY2019 Non-Profit Organization Funding
                     Requests as stated. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

          Council Member ORourke asked for an amount to be dedicated to the Art in Public Places, which would
          fund special events and continue the enthusiasm for this initiative. Discussion ensued. City Manager Leif
          Ahnell provided additional information.

                     Motion was made by Council Meimber Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member
                     Monica Mayotte, to amend the budget to include $25,000 in funding to continue the Art in Public
                     Places initiative (Exhibit 2). Motion carried on a voice vote.

          In response to Council Member Mayotte's questions, the City Manager provided information about
          funding for the Beach and Park District, the FAU Research Park and the Tech Runway. Council members
          briefly discussed funding for needed upgrades to Town Hall that will be requested by the Historical
          Society.

          H.        Recomputation of proposed millage rates

          No recomputation of the millage rates was necessary.

          I.        Adopt Ordinance No. 5467 incorporating recomputed final millage rates

                    Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                    Rodgers, to approve Ordinance No. 5467. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,
                    Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea
                    Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

          J.       Adopt Ordinance No. 5468 incorporating amended final budget

                     Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                     Jeremy Rodgers, to approve Ordinance No. 5468 including Exhibit 1, revised Exhibit 2, and the
                     FY2019 Non-Profit Organization Funding Requests as amended. Motion carried unanimously;
                     Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                     Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

          K.       Close public hearing
