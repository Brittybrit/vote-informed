          B.       Resolution No. 88-2026
                   A resolution of the City of Boca Raton, pursuant to Chapter 166, Florida Statutes, determining
                   whether the City or any dependent Special District within its boundaries holds fee simple title
                   to any real property that is appropriate for use as affordable housing; providing for severability;
                   providing for repealer; providing an effective date

                   Development Services Director Brandon Schaad gave a brief presentation and advised that
                   the CRA and City had no City-owned properties suitable for affordable housing. Upon
                   concluding, he made himself available for questions.

                   The public comment period was opened. Pam Paschke questioned which law enforcement
                   and fire rescue agencies respond to the Boca Raton Golf and Country Club. Mayor Thomson
                   responded that it was Palm Beach County's Fire & Police Services. Seeing no one else come
                   forward, the public hearing was closed.

                           Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                           Michelle Grau, to approve Resolution No. 88-2026. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                           Andy Thomson, Deputy Mayor Michelle Grau, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                           Council Member Jon Pearlman, Council Member Stacy L. Sipple voting yes.
