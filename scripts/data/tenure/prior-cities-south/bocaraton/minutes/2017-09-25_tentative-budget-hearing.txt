3.   PRESENTATION ON TENTATIVE BUDGET, PROPOSED MILLAGE RATES, CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS
     PROGRAM, AND MUNICIPAL FACILITIES AND SERVICES USER FEE SCHEDULE:

     A.     Proposed User Fee Schedule
                    Proposed User Fee Schedule

     City Manager Leif Ahnell gave a PowerPoint presentation reviewing the proposed budget for the coming
     fiscal year. The goals and priorities set by Council during the May 2017 Strategic Planning Sessions
     were listed. Mr. Ahnell proposed a millage rate of 3.6788 for the 2017-18 fiscal year, which is a slight
     decrease from last year. He then reviewed proposed changes in various operating costs, as well as
     detailing new personnel and a new program for canal maintenance and refuse collection. There followed
     a number of charts and graphs illustrating a resident's tax bil;, assessed property values in Boca Raton;
     tax rate history; and favorable comparisons of Boca Raton to neighboring cities' tax rates, fire
     assessment, and water/sewer rates. The presentation was then turned over to Sharon McGuire, Office of
     Management and Budget Director, who reviewed the 2017/18-2022/23 Capital Improvement Program
     (CIP). Ms. McGuire noted the sources of revenue that will fund capital improvements and then reviewed,
     by department, the projects, vehicles and equipment included in the CIP for 2017-18 fiscal year. Upon
     concluding, Ms. McGuire and Mr. Ahnell responded to questions from Council members.

     Jean-Michel Neault, Compliance Auditor, gave a PowerPoint presentation on the changes to the User
     Fee Schedule to become effective October 1, 2017. He reviewed the changes proposed for the coming
     year, and then responded to questions from Council members.

          C.      Discussion, public comments and explanation of "percentage increase in the proposed
                  millage rate over the rolled-back rate necessary to fund the budget, if any."

          Council members had no questions regarding the proposed millage or the tentative budget.

          D.      Open public hearing on tentative budget and proposed millage rates.
                         Presentations by non-profit organizations regarding funding requests.

         The public hearing was opened. Monica Mayotte and Sandi Schwartz asked that the position of
         Sustainability Officer be included in the upcoming budget. Charlie Helton advocated removal of the
         parking meters in the downtown area and along Spanish River Boulevard. Bob Trinka commented on the
         electric car charging stations and the pedestrian stop signs at intersections along St. Andrews Boulevard.
         Mr. Ahnell responded to his comments. Raul Travieso requested that additional pickle ball courts be
         constructed in City parks.

         At this time, representatives of 30 non-profit organizations addressed Council and requested funding for
         the coming year.

          E.      City Council "shall amend the tentative budget as it sees fit."

         No amendments were made to the tentative budget.

          F.      Recomputation of proposed millage rates.

         There was no recomputation of the proposed millage rate.

          G.      Public announcement of percent, if any, by which recomputed proposed millage rates
                  exceed "rolled-back rate", characterized as "percentage decrease in property taxes
                  tentatively adopted."

         No announcement was necessary, as there was no recomputation.

          H.      Adopt Resolution No. 107-2017 incorporating recomputed proposed millage rates.

                   Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                   Rodgers, to adopt Resolution No. 107-2017. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie,
                   Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member
                   Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes.

          I.      Adopt Resolution No. 108-2017 incorporating amended tentative budget.

                   Motion was made by Council Member Scott Singer, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers,
                   to approve Adopt Resolution No. 108-2017.

         Council Member O'Rourke advocated for a sustainability officer.

                   Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council
                   Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S.
                   Weinroth voting yes.

          J.      Close public hearing.

         The public hearing was closed.

5.       RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS

          A.      Resolution No. 109-2017

                   A resolution of the City of Boca Raton imposing Fire Services Assessments against Assessed
                   Property located within the City for the Fiscal Year beginning October 1, 2017; approving the
                   Fiscal Year 2017-2018 Rate of Assessment; approving the Fiscal Year 2017-2018 Assessment
                   Roll; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                            Resolution No. 109-2017

         Linda Davidson, Financial Services Director, gave a brief PowerPoint presentation on the proposed
         increase in the fire services assessment. She noted that one letter was received objecting to the
         increase. Upon concluding, she made herself available for questions. Council Member Weinroth
         suggested that the residential fee increase be cut in half.

         The public hearing was opened. Charlie Helton voiced opposition to the fire services assessment fee and
         advocated that it be eliminated. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was
         closed. In response to Council Member Weinroth's comments, Mr. Ahnell addressed the method of
         calculating the fee and provided other information. Discussion ensued; members shared their positions.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                         Member Scott Singer, to approve Resolution No. 109-2017. Motion carried on a 3 to 2 vote;
                         Mayor Susan Haynie, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Scott
                         Singer voting yes; Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth
                         voting no.

Mr. Ahnell announced that the Final Budget Hearing would be held on October 2, 2017. Members discussed the
nonprofit funding.  Council Member Weinroth addressed the hurricane related damages to the Tri-County
Humane Society, stating that any grant for same should be treated as a separate item. Members provided final
comments.

Council Member O'Rourke commented on the recent passing of former Boca Raton Mayor Carol Hanson.

Council Member Weinroth reported that the northbound entrance ramp of the new Spanish River Boulevard 1-95
interchange would be opening at 5:00 am on Tuesday morning.

6.      ADJOURNMENT

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 8:55 PM.
