15.    RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:

A.     Resolution No. 14-2023
       A resolution of the City of Boca Raton authorizing an offer of judgment in the case styled Natural Lands,
       LLC v. City of Boca Raton; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

       City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser provided a brief overview of the resolution which would authorize an
       offer of judgment in the case also known as 2500 North Ocean. The case had been discussed in an
       Executive Session and legal counsel had recommended an offer of judgement. Upon concluding, Ms.
       Frieser made herself available for questions.

       The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                        Andrea Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 14-2023. Motion carried
                        unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council
                        Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Fran
                        Nachlas voting yes.

B.     Resolution 15-2023
       A resolution of the City of Boca Raton authorizing offers of judgment in the litigation styled Robert W. Otto
       and Julie H. Hamilton v. City of Boca Raton, Florida, and County of Palm Beach , Florida; providing for
       severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

        City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser gave a brief overview of the resolution authorizing an offer of judgment
        in the case also referred to the Conversion Therapy case. As in the previous case, both in-house and
        outside counsel recommended an offer of judgment in connection with the case. Upon concluding, Ms.
        Frieser made herself available for questions.

        The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                        Fran Nachlas, to approve Resolution No. 14-2023. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                        Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                        Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Fran Nachlas voting yes.

 19.    MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Nachlas commented favorably on the Martin Luther King, Jr. Day celebration events and
congratulated her daughter and son-in-law on the birth of their daughter, Harper.

 Deputy Mayor O'Rourke offered her congratulations to Ms. Nachlas and her family.

Council Member Drucker reminded everyone of the Divergent Diamond Interchange (DOI) construction and road
closings at Glades Rd. and 1-95. She also commented on a celebration of the Lunar New Year with an event
partnered by Brightline Trains and Kapow Noodle Bar and stated that she hoped to see more of these
partnerships in the Downtown.

Council Member Mayotte noted the Brightline and Tri-Rail safety presentations at the last Workshop Meeting
and noted how important it is for the public to be educated on train safety. She encouraged everyone to please
pay attention when they are around the train tracks.

Mayor Singer announced the Brandt Drive bridge scheduled reopening, the DOI opening at Glades Rd. and 1-
95, and a lane changeover at the Palmetto Park Road/ El Rio bridge. He then highlighted the upcoming Boca
Raton First Responder Expo at the Town Center Mall and the Boca Raton Free Shred Event at 6500 North
Congress Avenue.

 20.    ADJOURNMENT:
