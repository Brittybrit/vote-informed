                                  MINUTES OF THE SPECIAL MEETING
                                            CITY COUNCIL
                                    CITY OF BOCA RATON , FLORIDA
                                     MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 8, 2025
                                               1:30 PM*


*The Special Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton , Florida was held in the Auditorium at 6500
    Congress Avenue. The meeting was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at approximately 2:04 PM .

 1.     PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG

 2.     ROLL CALL
        Mayor Scott Singer
        Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas
        Council Member Yvette Drucker
        Council Member Andy Thomson
        Council Member Marc Wigder

 3.     PUBLIC HEARING

          A.     Resolution No. 129-2025
                A resolution of the City of Boca Raton equalizing the assessments; levying the same as
                assessments against benefited properties within the Downtown Special Assessment District,
                City of Boca Raton, Florida; directing the City Clerk to record assessments in the improvement
                lien book; establishing priority of lien and payment of principal and interest; providing for
                severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

          Special Projects Manager Stephen Timberlake gave the presentation . He provided a brief history of
          the Downtown Special Assessment, the requirement to fund the debt service, and a map of the area
          affected by the assessment. Mr. Timberlake noted that the purpose of the meeting was to hear and
          consider any objections to the special assessment. The method of calculating the assessment rate
          was explained and a history of the assessment rate was displayed . Mr. Timberlake noted that no
          objection letters had been received , and he made himself available for questions.

          The public hearing was opened and , seeing no one come forward , closed .

                        Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Deputy Mayor Fran
                        Nachlas, to approve Resolution No. 129-2025. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                        Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                        Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

 4.     ADJOURNMENT

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 2:09 PM .
