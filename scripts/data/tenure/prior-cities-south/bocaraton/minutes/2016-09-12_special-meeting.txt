       Mayor Susan Haynie

       Council Member Jeremy Rodgers

       Council Member Robert S. Weinroth

3.     PUBLIC HEARING

        A.        Resolution No. 87-2016
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton equalizing the assessments; levying the same as
                  assessments against benefited properties within the Downtown Special Assessment District, City
                  of Boca Raton, Florida; directing the City Clerk to record assessments in the Improvement Lien
                  Book; establishing priority of lien and payment of principal and interest; providing for severability;
                  providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                           Resolution No. 87-2016

       Stephen Timberlake, Special Projects Manager, gave a brief PowerPoint presentation on the proposed
       2017 Downtown Special Assessment. He explained the method of determining the assessment rate and
       noted that the 2017 rate decreased due to an increase in property values. Mr. Timberlake stated that no
       objection letters had been received this year, and then made himself available for questions.

       The public hearing was opened. Lenore Wachtel asked when the bonds would be paid off and also
       commented on commercial properties. Mr. Timberlake provided a response. Seeing no one else come
       forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, seconded by Council Member Robert S.
                  Weinroth, to approve Resolution No. 87-2016. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie,
                  Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott
                  Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes.

4.     ADJOURNMENT

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 2:14 pm.
