         A.      Resolution No. 80-2024
                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the approximately 2-acre property
                 generally located at 6403 West Rogers Circle, a conditional use pursuant to Section 28-
                 1124(b), to authorize the storage, service, repair and cleaning of motor vehicles in an existing
                 building in the Manufacturing Industrial (M-3) Zoning District; providing for repealer; providing
                 an effective date (CA-23-02)

                 1.        Resolution No. 81-2024
                          A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the approximately 2 acre
                          property generally located at 6403 West Rogers Circle, an amendment to the approved
                          site plan (Resolution No. 87-32) to authorize modifications to the existing building
                          fa9ade and interior floor plan , and additional site modifications including parking lot and
                          landscaping improvements, to accommodate a motor vehicle showroom and the
                          storage, service, repair and cleaning of motor vehicles, together with three (3) technical
                          deviations from (i) Section 23-190(d), Code of Ordinances, to eliminate an additional
                          required 12 foot wide egress lane, (ii) Section 23-190(d}, Code of Ordinances, to
                          eliminate a required four (4) foot driveway median, and (iii) Section 28-1655(1), Code
                          of Ordinances, to reduce the required number of vehicle parking spaces from 77
                          spaces to 56 spaces; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SPA-88-07R1 )

         The Mayor noted that the public hearings on items 1O.A. and A.1. would be conducted
         contemporaneously as the items were related. He noted that a separate vote would be taken on each
         item and should item 1O.A. not be approved , no vote would be taken on the remaining item. The
         Deputy City Attorney read the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing.
         Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished
         to speak.

         Senior Planner Eoin Devlin gave a PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Devlin noted the concerns from the
         previous application and highlighted the recent revisions. He advised that the Planning and Zoning
         Board and the Development Services Department recommended approval. Upon concluding he
         made himself available for questions. Discussion ensued with Mr. Devlin answering Members'
         questions.

         David Milledge, attorney for the applicant, gave a brief PowerPoint presentation. He noted the
         changes from the previous meeting which included site plan changes and technical deviations, and
         upon concluding he answered Members' questions.

         The public comment period was opened. Thomas Laird , Charles Rhein , Louis Rosner, Zach
         Taminosian, Jim Falzone, Dale Gregory, Eric Poklyak, and Jim Smirglio all spoke against the project
         and urged Council to deny the application. Jonathan Ounjian spoke in favor of the project and urged
         Council to approve the application. Seeing no one else come forward, the public comment period
         was closed.

          David Milledge provided responses to commenters concerns.

                            Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                            Marc Wigder, to approve Resolution No. 80-2024.
