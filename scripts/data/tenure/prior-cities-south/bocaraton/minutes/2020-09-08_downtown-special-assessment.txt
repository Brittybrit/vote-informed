 3.     PUBLIC HEARING

          A.     Resolution No. 133-2020

                A resolution of the City of Boca Raton equalizing the assessments; levying the same as
                assessments against benefited properties within the Downtown Special Assessment District, City
                of Boca Raton, Florida; directing the City Clerk to record assessments in the Improvement Lien
                Book; establishing priority of lien and payment of principal and interest; providing for severability;
                providing for repealer; providing an effective date

       Stephen Timberlake, Special Projects Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He provided a brief
       history of the special assessment and noted that the purpose of the meeting was to hear and consider any
       and all objections to the special assessment, which is required to fund the debt service and administrative
       costs for the special assessment revenue bonds. The method of calculating the assessment rate was
       explained and various charts and graphs were presented. He noted that no objection letters were received,
       and one inquiry received regarding how the special assessment worked. Upon concluding, Mr. Timberlake
       made himself available for questions.

       The public hearing was opened. Joseph Graubart commented on the original municipal bond and whether
       it had been reissued. Seeing no one else indicate desire to speak, the public hearing was closed. Linda
       Davidson, Financial Services Director, provided additional information.

                      Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member Monica
                      Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 133-2020; Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Monica
                      Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson, and Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke voting
                      yes.
