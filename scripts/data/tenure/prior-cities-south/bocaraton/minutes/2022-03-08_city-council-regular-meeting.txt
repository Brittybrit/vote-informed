          C.       Resolution No. 24-2022
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton authorizing the City Manager and City Clerk to execute a
                  revocable license with 200 East Palmetto Condominium Association for the use of parking
                  spaces; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

         Mr. Ahnell briefly reviewed the resolution. He noted that the proposed agreement was a renewal of the
         original revocable license for three parking spaces and that the agreement included payment of the
         daily parking meter rate.

         The public hearing was opened. Karen Mazurek, property manager for the 200 East Palmetto
         Condominium, explained that the building does not have sufficient parking for vendors in the existing
         garage. She noted that the parking spaces were used via their valet service for vendors or repair
         services only. Upon concluding, she responded to members' questions. Seeing no one else come
         forward, the public hearing was closed. Deputy Mayor O'Rourke commented on the availability of
         parking in the Downtown and stressed the importance of making sure ample parking was provided in
         new development projects.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member
                          Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 24-2022. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                          Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                          Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

 10.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A       Resolution No. 19-2022
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering accepting and approving the final plat known
                  as Yamato Villas (a replat of all, or portions of, Lots 1 through 10 of Block 3 of the University
                  Heights Plat) for an approximately 1.7 acre property generally located at 300, 316, 382 and 398
                  West Yamato Road and 5001, 5033 and 5067 Northwest 3rd Avenue, authorizing the Mayor
                  and City Clerk to execute the plat; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (UC-16-
                  03/SUB)

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing.
         Members made their ex-parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished
         to speak.

         Tori Boone, Land Development Coordinator, gave the presentation. She began by reviewing the
         application. Background of the property's rezoning and master plan approval was discussed. Ms.
         Boone noted that the proposed final plat was consistent with the previously approved tentative plat,
         and that limited site work had been permitted to begin in February under an early work authorization.
         Staff recommended approval. Upon concluding, Ms. Boone made herself available for questions.

         Jerome Rich, applicant, thanked City staff for their work and made himself available for members'
         questions.

                          Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                          Member Monica Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 19-2022. Motion carried
                          unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council
                          Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy
                          Thomson voting yes.
