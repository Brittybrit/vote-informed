     The Final Budget Hearing of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was held in the Auditorium
          at 6500 Congress Avenue. The meeting was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at 6:00 PM.

1.       PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

2.       ROLL CALL:

         Mayor Scott Singer
         Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker
         Council Member Fran Nachlas

         Council Member Marc Wigder

3.       REBUDGETING OF FY 2024 - 2025 BUDGET APPROPRIATIONS:

Mayor Singer made opening remarks and discussed the purpose for the meeting.

Office of Management and Budget (0MB) Director Sharon McGuire, along with Financial Services Director
James Zervis, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Ms. McGuire began with a brief overview of the Mission,
Vision, and Values of the City. She advised that the proposed millage rate was 3.6782 mills per $1,000, which
was slightly lower than that for Fiscal Year 2023-2024. She continued with a review of the General Operating
Fund costs, new programs, projects and positions, and their alignment with Strategic Priorities. Ms. McGuire
then reviewed Exhibits 1 through 4 which included amendments to the budget proposing elimination of three
vacant positions, operating and CIP rebudgets, and Council requests. Upon concluding, she made herself
available for questions. Council Member Nachlas provided additional information on the two non-profit
organizations she requested be added to the list.

4.        PUBLIC HEARING TO FINALIZE THE BUDGET AND ADOPT MILLAGE RATES:

           A.     Ordinance No. 5704
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton adopting final millage rates and levying ad valorem
                 taxes for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2024 and ending September 30, 2025; stating
                 the percentage by which the millage to be levied exceeds the "rolled-back rate"; directing the
                 City Manager or the Office of Management and Budget Director to adjust the adopted millage
                 rates in the event of changes in the assessment roll and taxable value; providing for
                 severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

           B.     Ordinance No. 5703
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton adopting a final budget and appropriating funds for the
                 fiscal year beginning October 1, 2024 and ending September 30, 2025; providing for
                 severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

            The City Clerk read Ordinance No. 5704 in full and the title of Ordinance No. 5703.

           C.     Open public hearing on final budget and final millage rates:

           The public hearing was opened.

           D.     Discussion and explanation of percentage increase in millage over the rolled-back rate
                  necessary to fund the budget

           City Manager George Brown explained the millage rate.
           E.       Public comment

           There were no public comments.

           F.       Close public comment

           The public comment period was closed.

           G.       City Council shall amend the adopted tentative budget as it sees fit

            Mayor Singer suggested that, due to the elimination of three vacant employee positions, the savings
            of that amount be subtracted from the millage rate. Financial Services Director James Zervis
            explained the reduction to the General Fund and that adjusting the millage rate accordingly would
            result in the overall millage rate being reduced from 3.6782 to 3.6734 and the percentage over the
            roll back rate would be reduced from 6.92% to 6. 78%. In effect, the savings from the vacant positions
            would reduce the property tax roll by that same amount resulting in a savings for residents.

            Mayor Singer reviewed the Non-Profit Funding request process. Members discussed the proposed
            list and agreed to changes. Council Member Nachlas advocated for adding two non-profit
            organizations that had not submitted their applications. After further discussion, Members felt that
            those organizations should not be included this year. Mr. Zervis then summarized the amounts line
            by line and advised a net increase of $21,000 over the prior year. Council Members thanked the 0MB
            and Financial Services staff for their hard work.

           H.       Re-computation of proposed millage rates

            Mr. Zervis read the changes to the sections of Ordinance No. 5704 based on the adjustment to the
            millage rates as discussed.

           I.       Adopt Ordinance No. 5704 incorporating recomputed final millage rates

                                    Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council
                                    Member Fran Nachlas, to adopt Ordinance No. 5704 as amended,
                                    incorporating the recomputed final millage rates as read. Motion carried
                                    unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council
                                    Member Fran Nachlas, Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member
                                    Marc Wigder voting yes.

           J.       Adopt Ordinance No. 5703 incorporating amended final budget

                                     Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council
                                     Member Andy Thomson, to adopt Ordinance No. 5703 as amended,
                                     incorporating the changes made to Exhibits 1, 2, 3, and the additional funding
                                     of non-profit organizations as stated. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott
                                     Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council
                                     Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

            K.       Close public hearing

           The public hearing was closed.
