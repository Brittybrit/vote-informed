           A.      Ordinance No. 5768
                    An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton, Florida, amending Chapter 28, Zoning , Article XV,
                    Supplementary District Regulations, Division 1, Generally, Code of Ordinances, by creating
                    Section 28-1320, Public and Recreational Use Limitation for City-Owned Public Parks, to
                    limit such parks to public and recreational uses, including accessory uses related thereto;
                    prohibiting the development of other uses thereon ; providing for the continued use and
                    placement of easements, utilities, and infrastructure that do not substantially interfere with
                    such public and recreational uses; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing
                    for codification; providing an effective date

            Development Services Director Brandon Schaad gave a description of the proposed Ordinance and
            reviewed the proposed protections, limitations, and allowable uses within City parks. He advised that
            staff recommended approval and made himself available for questions. Council Member Marc Wigder
            gave a PowerPoint presentation, refuted misinformation , and advised that he supports approval of
            this ordinance.

           The public hearing was opened. Jonathan Pearlman spoke against the proposed Government
           Campus. Richard Warner relayed his disappointment with the wording of the ordinance. Martin Bell
           proposed alternative plans to the BACE Building and Police Station sites should the referendum be
           approved. Pam Paschke praised Mr. Pearlman's initial efforts to preserve City park land . Jonathan
           Ounjian spoke against the Save Boca group. Mike Liebelson was concerned with amending the City
           Charter regarding City owned land. Judy Morrow spoke about issues relating to tennis. Lyudmila
           Pearlman had concerns regarding City parks and land protections. Seeing no one else come forward ,
           the public hearing was closed .

           Further discussion ensued . Mr. Wigder advised that he had been working on this project to preserve
           City parks for several years. Council Member Thomson shared that this ordinance was meaningful
           and he would support it. Deputy Mayor Nachlas also expressed her support of the ordinance. Mayor
           Singer advised that he respected healthy discussion and that he believed misinformation by a certain
           group had been harmful to this project.

                           Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Deputy Mayor Fran
                           Nachlas, to approve Ordinance No. 5768. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott
                           Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member
                           Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

           B.      Ordinance No. 5770
                   An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering the adoption of amendments to the Boca
                   Raton Comprehensive Plan and its goals, objectives, and policies in accordance with a
                   requirement in Chapter 163, Florida Statutes, to conduct an evaluation and appraisal of the
                   Comprehensive Plan ; amending the Community Profile, Future Land Use Element,
                   Transportation Element, Housing Element, Infrastructure Element, Coastal Management
                   Element, Conservation Element, Recreation and Open Space Element, Intergovernmental
                   Coordination Element, Historic Preservation Element, Capital Improvements Element and the
                   map series; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-
                   25-01)

           Development Services Long Range Project Manager Jim Bell gave the PowerPoint presentation . He
           gave a brief history of the Comprehensive Plan (Comp Plan) and its future implementations as
           required by the State. He advised that the Planning and Zoning Board had approved the ordinance
           by a vote of 6-0, and staff recommended transmitting the ordinance to the Florida Department of
           Commerce for approval of the proposed amendments. Upon concluding , he made himself available
           for questions.

           C.      Ordinance No. 5771
                    An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, Zoning, and Chapter 23,
                    Planning and Development, Code of Ordinances, to create the Downtown Zoning District,
                    replacing land development regulations and review processes set forth in Ordinance No.
                    4035, as amended (the Downtown Development of Regional Impact (DORI) Development
                    Order) for areas rezoned to the         Downtown Zoning District, and setting forth related
                    regulations for future development in Downtown Boca Raton ; establishing              specific
                    requirements for the redevelopment of the Government Center area, including regulations
                    related to uses, parking , landscaping , open space and architectural standards; providing for
                    severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-25-20)

           Development Services Deputy Director Erin Sita gave the PowerPoint presentation. She gave a brief
           summary of the Downtown Zoning district and reviewed the proposed amendments. She advised that
           the amendment was consistent with the Comp Plan and that both the Planning and Zoning Board
           and staff recommend approval. Upon concluding , she made herself available for questions.
           Discussion ensued .

           The public hearing was opened. Donna Bloomberg was concerned with parking. Jonathan Ounjian
           spoke about the proposed land use and was in favor of ground floor retail on every building in the
           Downtown . Meredith Madsen suggested City-wide parking passes for residents. Richard Warner
           spoke against the ordinance. Mike Liebelson was concerned with height variances. Brandon Schaad
           clarified misinformation and provided additional details. Jon Pearlman spoke against the proposed
           amendments to ordinances in the Downtown. Pam Paschke was concerned with the height of the
           proposed buildings in the Government Campus. Discussion ensued and Mr. Schaad clarified
           comments made. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed. Further
           discussion ensued; the Mayor advised that this was the first of two public hearings and no vote would
           be taken tonight.


 The meeting recessed at 8:06 PM and resumed at 8: 19 PM.
