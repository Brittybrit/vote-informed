15.       RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:

           A.      Resolution No. 57-2024
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering the application of Edilane Piedra, for a
                  settlement and reduction of one (1) code enforcement lien imposed against the properties
                  located at 4720 Northwest 2nd Avenue, Units 0104 and 0105; providing for severability;
                  providing for repealer; providing an effective date

         Deputy Development Services Director Erin Sita gave a brief overview of the code violations and the
         proposed lien reduction from $152,115 to $76,115. Ms. Sita advised that staff recommended approval
         and made herself available for questions. Discussion ensued.

         The public comment period was opened. Sergio Piedra, the applicant, provided details on why he was
         requesting the lien be further reduced from $76,115 to $50,115. Jonathan Ounjian spoke in favor of the
         lien reduction and gave an example of another property that was not in compliance. Seeing no one else
         come forward, the public comment period was closed.

         Further discussion ensued and Ms. Sita responded to Members' questions and provided additional
         details.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                          Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 57-2024 as amended, lowering the fine from
                          $152, 115 to $50, 115, to be payable within 30 days. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                          Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council
                          Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

         Council Member Andy Thomson suggested the City's Special Magistrates be authorized to adjust fines
         at their hearings and City Manager Brown advised that City staff was currently considering this
         suggestion.

The meeting was recessed at 7:33pm and resumed at 7:42pm.
