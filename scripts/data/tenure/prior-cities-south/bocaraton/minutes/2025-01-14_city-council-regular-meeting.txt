 The Mayor announced that the public hearings on items 10.A, 10.A.1 , and 10.A.2 were interrelated , would be
 heard together and voted upon separately. If item 10.A was not approved , item 10.A. 1 would not be voted on ,
 and if item 10.A. 1 was not approved , there would be no vote for item 10.A.2. He then noted that this would be
 the second public hearing for Ordinance No. 5715 . The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures
 that would govern the public hearings. Council Members disclosed their ex-parte communications . The Clerk
 gave the oath to those who would be speaking .

 Tamash Rahman, Chief Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation . She began with background information
 of Ordinance No. 5175 and the subsequent request to amend the Comprehensive Plan and Zoning Code Text
 Amendment. Components of the request were highlighted. Analysis of the project was reviewed and both staff
 and the Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval. Ms. Rahman proceeded to review the request
 for a new 8-story mixed-use CIMO. The proposed site plan was shown , and requests for technical deviations
 were discussed. Renderings of the proposed buildings were shown and Ms. Rahman noted that the Planning
 and Zoning Board and staff recommended approval. Upon concluding , Ms. Rahman made herself available
 for questions.

 The public hearing was opened . David Milledge, on behalf of the applicant, gave a PowerPoint presentation.
 He reviewed components of the request and displayed aerial photos of the current subject area and an overlay
 of the proposed changes to the property. Maps of the pedestrian and traffic circulation and renderings of the
 building elevations were shown . The requested water easement abandonment and technical deviations were
 discussed . Upon concluding , Mr. Milledge responded to Council Members' questions about continuity of
 seNice to the Tri-Rail station . Mr. Schaad , Development SeNices Director, provided additional information
 regarding the sidewalk addition along Yamato Road. Deputy Mayor Drucker and Council Member Nachlas
 expressed their support for the Transit Oriented Development. Jonathan Ounjian expressed concerns about
 the parking requirements , traffic impacts, and that the proposed density would not be extended to nearby
 properties. Lillia Perez explained her opposition to the project and shared that she felt the City was being
 overdeveloped . Mark Sneckenburg spoke about current traffic and parking issues in the subject area and his
 concern that the development would worsen those matters. Seeing no one else come forward, the public
 hearing was closed . There was no cross examination or summation .
