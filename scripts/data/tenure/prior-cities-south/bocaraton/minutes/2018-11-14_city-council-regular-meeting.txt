                         agreement with the Mizner Park Cultural Arts Association, Inc. for the purpose of

         City Manager Leif Ahnell reviewed the budget amendment ordinance and the resolution requesting a

         request of Mayor Singer, Mizner Park Cultural Arts Association (MPCAA) Board Member Peg Anderson

                   approve Ordinance No. 5472. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor
                   Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke,
                   Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

         Members made final comments regarding the MPCAA Board and expressed a desire for more open
         communication. The City Attorney suggested that a letter could be written to the Board outlining the
         Councils' concerns.

                   Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andy
                   Thomson, to approve Resolution No.157-2018. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,

17.      QUASI-JUDICIAL PUBLIC HEARINGS/VARIANCES & APPEALS:

                  Resolution No. 150-2018
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a variance from Section 24 -78(a), Code of
                  Ordinances, to allow a 60 square foot flat sign in lieu of the maximum allowed area of a flat sign
                  of 32 square feet for the Islamic Center of Boca Raton, located at 3480 Northwest 5th Avenue:
                  providing for repealer: providing an effective date (SCV-17-01}
                           Resolution No. 150-2018

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the hearing. Members made
         their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.
         Tori Golden, Land Development Coordinator, gave a PowerPoint presentation . She provided background
         information, noting that the sign had been installed without a permit. A description of the sign, its
         dimensions, and location on the property were provided. The Community Appearance Board (CAB)
         denied the application. Staff recommended approval of the variance. Ms. Golden then made herself
         available for questions.
Regular Minutes                                         5                                             November 14, 2018
         The public hearing was opened. Rim Maclaren presented on behalf of the petitioner and provided the
         history of how the sign came to be installed without a permit. Mark Jacobsen, speaking on behalf of the
         CAB, reviewed the reasons why the board denied the variance. Mr. Jacobsen made himself available for
         questions. There was no cross examination or rebuttal. Mr. Maclaren made final comments, addressed
         the reasons his client was asking for the variance, and requested approval. The public hearing was
         closed.

         Members made final comments. There was discussion of the necessity of the applicant going back to
         CAB for approval of the permit. Ms. Frieser noted that in Section 2 of the resolution, the second condition
         would be removed so that it would not be required for the applicant to get CAB approval of the permit.

                    Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Monica
                    Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 150-2018 as amended. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                    Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member
                    Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes .
