3.     REBUDGETING OF FY 2016- 2017 BUDGET APPROPRIATIONS

Mayor Haynie highlighted the purpose of the meeting.

City Manager Leif Ahnell gave a PowerPoint presentation reviewing the 2017-2018 budget. The goals and policy
agenda priorities set by Council were enumerated. There followed a number of charts and graphs providing
information on the millage rate and property tax comparison, proposed increase and decrease in general fund
operating costs, new personnel, a typical tax bill, assessed valuations, tax rate history and favorable comparisons
of Boca Raton to other cities with regard to tax rate, fire assessment fee, and water and sewer rates. Mr. Ahnell
thanked staff for their work on the budget and made himself available for questions.

Sharon McGuire, Office of Management and Budget Director, referring to Exhibits 1 and 2 distributed to Council,
reviewed the amendments to the proposed budget and rebudgets of dollars not spent in the current year that will
be rolled into the fiscal year 2017-2018 budget. She then made herself available for questions. Mickey Gomez,
Recreation Services Director, provided additional information regarding the changes to the Beach and Park
District fund. The City Manager also responded to several questions from Council members.

                        Ordinance No. 5409

        The City Clerk read Ordinance No. 5408 in full and the title of Ordinance No. 5409.

        C.     Open public hearing on final budget and final millage rates

       Mayor Haynie opened the public hearing. Ellyn Okrent, CEO of Florence Fuller Child Development and
       the Children's Museum, requested additional funds to help cover roof damage on the Rickards Building
       due to Hurricane Irma. She responded to questions about the insurance implications and thanked the City
       for their support.
         Kari Oeltjen, Chief Development Officer of Habitat for Humanity, requested non-profit funding that will
         allow Habitat to continue to enhance their sustainability program.

         Dick Schmidt, Chairman of the Boca Raton Golf Charities, updated Council on the funding of the Boca
         Raton Regional Hospital Championship Golf Tournament, formerly the Allianz. Since speaking at the
         September 25 Workshop Meeting, Mr. Schmidt said that $500,000 had been secured from another
         source, so the request of the City was $656,500 for a one year commitment. He then provided further
         information on the benefits of a title sponsorship package. Edward Carbone, Tournament Director,
         echoed Mr. Schmidt's comments. They both responded to questions from Council members. Discussion
         ensued. City Manager Leif Ahnell provided additional information regarding advertising spots.

         Lisa McDulin, Director for Advancement at Place of Hope, requested non-profit funding for their
         programs.

         Sarah Pearson, Executive Vice President of the Boca Raton Chamber of Commerce, referencing
         previous comments, noted that the Chamber has supported the golf tournament in the past and supports
         keeping the event in Boca Raton.

         D.      Discussion and explanation of percentage increase in millage over the rolled-back rate
                 necessary to fund the budget

         E.      Public comment

        There was no further public comment.

         F.      Close public comment

         Seeing no one come forward to speak, the public comment period was closed.

         G.      City Council shall amend the adopted tentative budget as it sees fit

        Mayor Haynie suggested beginning with the non-profit organizations. Working from her allocations, she
        requested that all funding levels be maintained from last year with the additional appropriations for
        D.I.S.C., Inc. and Habitat for Humanity of South Palm Beach County. Council members each reviewed
        their proposed changes and discussed further changes. Several changes were made to the Mayor's
        allocations, which she reviewed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                  Rodgers, to amend the budget to include funding of non-profit organizations as stated by the
                  Mayor. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers,
                  Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert
                  S. Weinroth voting yes.

        Referring to Exhibit 3, the Mayor and Council members reviewed their requests for other funding
        changes. Council Member O'Rourke requested funding for a full time Resiliency/Sustainability Officer.
        Members discussed and offered their opinions. In response to questions, Mr. Ahnell provided information
        on contractors for canal cleaning, the overlap of an Innovation Officer and a Sustainability Officer, and the
        cost of a full time position. Members made final comments and, in general, agreed to inclusion of the
        Sustainability Officer position.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member
                  Robert S. Weinroth, to amend the budget to include approximately $120,000 to fund a full time
                  Resiliency/Sustainability Officer position.

        Deputy Mayor Rodgers inquired about the possibility of funding the position with a grant. The City
        Manager noted that this could be researched.

                  Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

        Council Member Weinroth requested $100,000 to assist in reconstruction of Tri-County Animal Rescue
        building destroyed by Hurricane Irma; Mayor Haynie requested $104,500. Discussion ensued.

                  Motion was made by Mayor Susan Haynie, seconded by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, to
                  amend the budget to include $104,500 for reconstruction of the Tri-County Animal Rescue
                  building.

        Responding to Council Member Singer's question, Sharon DiPietro, Tri County Animal Rescue, shared
        information regarding insurance coverage on the destroyed building.

                  Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

        Council Member Weinroth requested $180,000 in funding for reconstruction of the Children's Museum
        cottage damaged by Hurricane Irma and noted that it is a City building. Discussion ensued on the amount
        of funding and the possibility of FEMA reimbursement.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Robert S.
                  Weinroth, to amend the budget to include $100,000 in funding for the Children's Museum for
                  building renovation. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

        Council Member Weinroth requested an additional $50,000 in funding for the Festival of the Arts. Mr.
        Weinroth noted the importance of the Festival to the City. Discussion ensued. Charles Siemon, Festival
        of the Arts Chair, responded to questions about the increase in Festival expenses.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                  Rodgers, to amend the budget to include an additional $50,000 in funding for the Festival of the
                  Arts. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

        Council Member Weinroth requested one-time economic development funding of $656,000 for the Boca
        Raton Regional Hospital Championship Golf Tournament (f/k/a the Allianz Championship) to ensure that
        this event remained in Boca Raton. Mayor Haynie suggested $500,000 in light of the promise of funding
        from the Greater Boca Raton Beach and Park District and in the hope that the County would participate in
        funding as well. Discussion ensued. It was suggested to seek other funding partners and to leverage this
        contribution as best as possible. The City Manager noted that this funding will come back to the Council
        in a contract for approval.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                  Rodgers, to amend the budget to include one-time appropriation of $500,000 from the Economic
                  Development Fund for the Boca Raton Regional Hospital Championship Golf Tournament. Motion
                  carried unanimously on a voice vote.

        Council Member Weinroth requested funding of $128,000 for a temporary public restroom trailer at the
        Boca Raton Dog Park. Mickey Gomez, Recreation Services Director, provided information on the
        temporary restrooms. Dan Grippo, Municipal Services Director, provided information on the cost of a
        permanent facility. Discussion ensued. The City Manger provided information on some issues to
        overcome at the site in order to have a permanent facility. Members made further comments. It was
        suggested to ask the Greater Boca Raton Beach and Park District (BPD) to share in the funding.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Mayor Susan Haynie, to
                  amend the budget to include a one year appropriation of $64,000 for a temporary public restroom
                  trailer at the Boca Raton Dog Park contingent upon matching funds from the BPD. Motion carried
                  on a 4-1 vote; Mayor Susan Haynie, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member
                  Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes; Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers
                  voting no.

        Mayor Haynie requested funds to cover Boca Raton's signal control boxes in decorative, landscape
        patterned wraps. The wraps would improve the look of the signal boxes which would align with the efforts
        to incorporate art in public places. Mr. Ahnell provided estimates on the number of signal boxes and the
        cost to wrap each. Discussion ensued. In general, it was decided to begin with funding of $75,000.
