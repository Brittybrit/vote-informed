 6.     PROCLAMATIONS/PRESENTATIONS:

         A.     VTUSA/Brightline Presentation and Discussion

        Jose Gonzalez and Brian Kronberg gave a presentation on behalf of Brightline/ Virgin Trains USA, LLC
        (VTUSA). Mr. Gonzalez noted that representatives of VTUSA had met with a number of resident groups,
        including the Junior League of Boca Raton, residents of Library Commons and Friends of the Library. An
        aerial photo of the property outlined the proposed train station and garage. Topics covered included, but
        were not limited to, the design and function of the parking garage, relocation of the Community Garden and
        an elevated pedestrian bridge. Upon conclusion, they responded to a number of questions. Discussion
        ensued.

        The floor was opened for public comment. Jackie Reeves, on behalf of the Greater Boca Raton Chamber
        of Commerce, read a letter in support of the proposed train station. Jo-Ann Landon spoke about the need
        for trees and landscaping, and potential future complications regarding the library as a polling location
        during elections. Susan Reilly, Manjunath Pendakur, Cyndi Bloom, and Krista Maki shared their thoughts
        on behalf of the Downtown Library and its patrons. Charles Bennardini, Bill Gellen, John Poletto, Terri
        Waitzer and Carol Wishnov, residents of Library Commons, related their concerns with the garage's
        proximity to their neighborhood. Alexandra Rosenzweig shared her environmental concerns regarding the
        train and security issues of the proposed garage. Bruce Diller Verstandig remarked on the train station's
        effect on local traffic and the cost to taxpayers. Seeing no one else come forward, the public comment
        period was clm~ed. Mr. Gonzalez and Mr. Kronberg responded to comments and questions made by
        previous speakers. Further discussion ensued. There was consensus among members to direct the City
        Manager to continue working with Brightline/ VTUSA.

          A.       Ordinance No. 5503
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering a universal conditional (UC) rezoning, pursuant
                  to Article VI, Chapter 23, Code of Ordinances, of property consisting of: (i) approximately 0.9152
                  acres, generally located at 7021 and 7101 North Federal Highway, from Motel-Business (R-B-1) to
                  Commercial (C-1), and (ii) approximately 1.9430 acres, generally located at 7021 and 7101 North
                  Federal Highway, from General Business (B-4) to Commercial (C-1 ), subject to conditions;
                  providing for the appropriate revision of the Zoning District Map; providing for repealer; providing
                  an effective date (UC-17-02/ZC)

          B.      Resolution No. 108-2019
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a conditional use approval pursuant to Sections
                  28-873 and 28-1310, Code of Ordinances, to allow a self-storage facility (interior storage unit
                  access) for the properties located at 7021 and 7101 North Federal Highway; providing for repealer;
                  providing an effective date (UC-17-02/CA)

          C.       Resolution No. 109-2019
                  A resolution of the City Boca Raton considering a site plan, pursuant to a universal conditional (UC)
                  rezoning request, to authorize the construction of a 6,634 square foot multi-tenant retail building
                  and a 121,019 square foot self-storage facility (interior storage unit access) and office, together
                  with four (4) technical deviations from Section 23-190, Code of Ordinances, to eliminate a required
                  driveway egress lane and eliminate a required driveway median separator for a driveway on North
                  Dixie Highway, to eliminate a required median separator for a driveway on North Federal Highway,
                  and reduce a required driveway reservoir distance on North Federal Highway, on an approximately
                  3.546 acre vacant lot generally located at 7021 and 7101 North Federal Highway; providing for
                  repealer; providing an effective date (UC-17-02/SPA)

         Mayor Singer noted that the public hearings on items A, B, and C would be conducted contemporaneously
         as the petitions were related, and a separate vote would be taken on the ordinance and each resolution.
         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing. Council
         Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to
         speak.

         Osney Jean, Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Components of the petitioners request were
         reviewed, along with aerial photos of the property outlining the proposed site plan and elevations. Traffic
         considerations and technical deviations from City code were discussed. The proposal is consistent with
         Comprehensive Plan. Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval. Staff recommends approval.
         Upon concluding, Mr. Jean made himself available for questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Bonnie Miskel, on behalf of the applicant, gave a brief PowerPoint
         presentation and made herself available for questions. There was no cross-examination or rebuttal. Seeing
         no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                  Rodgers, to approve Ordinance No. 5503. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy
                  Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Article VI, "Historic
                  Preservation," Sections 28-237 and 28-239, Code of Ordinances, authorizing the ordinary
                  maintenance, repair or replacement of certain exterior features in a historic district or site that do
                  not involve a change in the design, material, color or outer appearance thereof, without review and
                  approval by the Historic Preservation Board; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                  providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-19-05)

         Ingrid Allen, Senior Planner, made the PowerPoint presentation. The background of the Code section was
         reviewed, and elements of the amendment were discussed. Ms. Allen noted that the proposed text
         amendment is consistent with the City's Comprehensive Plan. Planning and Zoning Board recommended
         approval. Staff recommended approval. On conclusion, Ms. Allen made herself available for members'
         questions.

         The public hearing was opened, and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member
                  Andy Thomson, to approve Ordinance No. 5500. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,
                  Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
