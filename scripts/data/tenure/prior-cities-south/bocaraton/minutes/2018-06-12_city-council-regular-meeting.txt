17.     QUASI-JUDICIAL PUBLIC HEARINGSNARIANCES & APPEALS:

                  Resolution No. 75-2018
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering a variance from Section 28-
                   1556(3), Code of Ordinances, to authorize a handicap access ramp approximately 10.6 feet
                  seaward of the Coastal Construction Control Line (CCCL) (where no structure is permitted
                  seaward of the CCCL), for a multifamily residential (R-5-A) zoned property located at 750 South
                  Ocean Boulevard; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-17-10)
                           Resolution No. 75-2018

        The City Attorney noted that the quasi judicial rules continued to apply. Members made their ex parte
        disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

         Ingrid Allen, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The City Code provision regarding the
         Coastal Construction Control Line (CCCL) was highlighted and the applicant's request reviewed. It was
         noted that the handicap ramp was built without a permit. A map of the property was provided, as were the
         size, zoning and land use. The location of both the CCCL and the handicap ramp on the property were
         identified. Aerial shots of the property before and after construction of the new ramp were shown. The
         staff analysis concluded that the new ramp does not impact the beach dune of to sea turtles. The
         Planning and Zoning Board unanimously approved the variance. Staff recommended approval as well.
         Upon concluding, Ms. Allen responded to members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Charley Putman, on behalf of the applicant, reviewed the request and
         referred to an aerial photo to identify the CCCL and the old and new access ramps. He noted that a
         grade change necessitated the ramp. He invited Michael Hawkins, the contractor, to explain how the
         permitted installation occurred prior to getting the variance. Mr. Putman then discussed the justification
         for approval of the ramp, which is required for ADA compliance. It was noted that the ramp empties on to
         a previously disturbed area. He distributed a letter from the manager of the Sabal Ridge Condominium
         regarding the importance of the ramp. Upon concluding, Mr. Putman responded to questions. Seeing no
         one else came forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                        Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andrea
                        Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 75-2018.

                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a variance from Section 24-96(c)(1 ). Code of
