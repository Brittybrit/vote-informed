11.       QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

           A.      Ordinance No. 5353
                   An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the Future Land Use Map of the
                   Comprehensive Plan for certain property consisting of 1.1337 acres, located on the northern
                   portion of 551 East Palmetto Park Road, as more specifically described herein, from Residential
                   Low (RL) to Commercial (C); providing for revisions to the Future Land Use Map; providing for
                   severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-14-03)
                           Ordinance No. 5353

                   Ordinance No. 5354
                   An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton rezoning a parcel of land consisting of 1.1337 acres
                   located on the northern portion of 551 East Palmetto Park Road, as more specifically described
                   herein, from Single Family Residential (R-1-B) zoning district to Local Business (B-1) zoning
                   district; providing for revisions to the Zoning District Map: providing for severability: providing for
                   repealer: providing an effective date (ZC-14-01)
                             Ordinance No. 5354

          Mayor Haynie noted that the public hearings on items A and B would be conducted contemporaneously
          as the petitions are related and pursuant to quasi-judicial procedures as required for Ordinance 5354. A
          separate vote would be taken on each ordinance. The Mayor also stated that Ordinance 5353 required
          four votes for \passage. The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures. Members made their
          ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk read the administered the oath to all those who wished to speak.

          Ingrid Allen, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation, identifying the location, zoning and future
          land use designation of the property. Ms. Allen reviewed the past priorities of the City Council with regard
          to the acquisition and development of the Wildflower property. The analysis of the flum amendment and
          the rezoning were reviewed. Several tables illustrating the trip generation associated with the proposed
          changes were presented and explained. Ms. Allen noted that staff recommends approval of both petitions
          as does the PZB. She then responded to questions from Council members.

          The public hearing was opened. Andrea O'Rourke commented on place-making. She was joined by
          Margaret Fitzsimmons and Jose Berrera who pres.ented an alternative plan for the Wildflower property.
          Bobra Bush, Peg Anderson, Gwen Tucker, Bob Tucker, Jackie Reeves, Jacqueline Dheere, Lee Babey,
          Tom Zeichman, Rick Howard, Phillip Kittell, Troy Mclellan, Jon Carter expressed support of a restaurant
          on the Wildflower property. Emily Gentile, representing the Beach Condo Association, expressed support
          of the Hillstone restaurant. Jerry Fedele, Chamber of Commerce Board Chair, expressed support of a
          restaurant and noted that walking paths and other amenities are not mutually exclusive with a restaurant.
          Jim Wood gave a brief PowerPoint presentation commenting on the revenue potential of the Wildflower
          property and expressed his lack of support for the ordinances. James Hendrey commented on the
          initiative petition referendum and aspects of the proposed restaurant lease. Lenore Wachtel expressed
          support of a park on the Wildflower property. Helene Eichler expressed lack of support for a restaurant
          and urged residents to vote on the referendum, as did Joe Pedalino. Mitch Kirschner commented on the
          initiative petition referendum and the Wildflower. Kim Heisey expressed support of open natural areas
          and parks. Eve Reed commented on the proposed lease, her lack of support of a restaurant, and public
          art. Glenn Gromann expressed support for both ordinances. George O'Rourke addressed a 2014 traffic
          study and advocated tabling the ordinances, as did Al Zucaro. Seeing no one else come forward to
          speak, the public hearing was closed. Under cross examination, George O'Rourke asked questions of
          Ingrid Allen regarding the traffic analysis.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member
                         Robert S. Weinroth, to approve Ordinance No. 5353.

          Members offered numerous comments addressing many of the statements made during the public
          hearing. Maria Tejara, City Traffic Engineer, responded to questions about the traffic analysis and
          provided information on her update to the original analysis.
