 12.       REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

            A       Ordinance No. 5572 (Second of two public hearings.)
                    An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28 "Zoning," Article XI, Division 3, "R­
                    B-1 Motel-Business District," Section 28-742, "Permitted Uses," Code of Ordinances, to authorize
                    business, trade, technical, or secretarial schools as permitted uses in the Motel-Business (R-B-1)
                    zoning district; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing
                    an effective date (AM-20-09)

           Jim Bell, Long Range Project Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation, and noted that this was the
           second of two public hearings. He began by reviewing the 2008 ordinance which inadvertently deleted
           educational uses from the R-B-1 zoning district and noted that this amendment was a housekeeping
           measure. Mr. Bell noted that staff had received a request from a vocational school within the City limits to
           reinstate and authorize certain educational uses. The Planning and Zoning Board and recommended
           approval. Staff recommended approval. Upon concluding, he made himself available for members'
           questions.

           The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one indicate their desire to speak, closed.

                             Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member
                             Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5572. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott
                             Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                             Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

 20.    MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Mayotte commented on the Boca Bash and expressed concern about the number of resources
being utilized to ensure the safety of those attending this non-permitted event. She then highlighted three sets of
legislative bills that, if signed by the Governor, would preempt local government action regarding renewable energy.
In conclusion, Ms. Mayotte noted an upcoming virtual meeting of the TPA and confirmed that one of the members
would be attending.

Council Member Drucker cautioned drivers to be aware of pedestrians and cyclists sharing the road and encouraged
all to follow the rules of the road, particularly in school zones. Ms. Drucker then expressed her commitment to the
topic of mental health, noting that May is Mental Health Awareness Month. She expressed her desire for some form
of community engagement and outreach regarding this topic. As a final point, Ms. Drucker stated that she had
received her second dose of the COVI D-19 vaccine.

Deputy Mayor O'Rourke began by thanking Councill Member Mayotte for her update on the legislative bills and
expressed her support for Ms. Mayotte's position. Ms. O'Rourke also shared her concerns about the Boca Bash
and inquired if there was an option for the City to annex Lake Boca and obtain jurisdiction over the area. She shared
information about a new YMCA meeting place for teenagers recently opened in Town Center Mall and noted the
groundbreaking for Boca Ice. Ms. O'Rourke noted the updated CDC guidelines about wearing masks outside. In
conclusion, she asked the City Manager what the next steps would be in regard to the recreational needs
assessment. City Manager Leif Ahnell advised that staff would begin to form a master plan and would report back
to Council in August.

Mayor Singer commented further on the Boca Bash, noting that he had contacted all participating agencies and
thanked them for their assistance. He agreed that a request for additional authority over Lake Boca should be added
to the City's priorities for the fall legislative session. Mayor Singer then addressed Ms. Drucker's comments
regarding mental health, noting that the City had partnered with non-profit centers to provide mental health
counseling at local high schools. He also highlighted the mental health outreach provided by Boca Raton Police
Department. He shared that he had also received his second vaccine and urged residents to continue to follow
CDC guidelines regarding Covid-19.
