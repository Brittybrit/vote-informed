                           MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING
                                        CITY COUNCIL
                                CITY OF BOCA RATON , FLORIDA
                                    FRIDAY, MARCH 31, 2023
                                           10:30 AM

The Organizational Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was held in the Auditorium
     at 6500 Congress Avenue. The meeting was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at 10:30 AM.

1.     CALL TO ORDER:

2.     PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:


3.     ROLL CALL:
         Mayor Scott Singer
         Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke
         Council Member Yvette Drucker
         Council Member Monica Mayotte
         Council Member Fran Nachlas

4.     CEREMONIAL PROCEEDINGS:
        A.     Recognition of and comments by outgoing Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke

       The Mayor and Council Members each commented on Deputy Mayor O'Rourke's tenure on Council,
       thanking her for her support and dedication to public service.

        Ms. O'Rourke expressed her gratitude to the community, her family, and colleagues for their support
        and commented on future issues and goals.

        The Mayor and Council Members presented a plaque to Ms. O'Rourke in recognition of her service.

         B.    Administration of Oath of Office for re-elected Mayor Scott Singer, newly elected Council
               Member Fran Nachlas and newly elected Council Member Marc Wigder

        City Clerk Mary Siddons administered the oath of office to Mayor-elect Scott Singer.

         Dr. Nachlas administered the oath of office to Council Member-elect Fran Nachlas.

         Mrs. Wigder administered the oath of office to Council Member-elect Marc Wigder.

         C.     Comments by Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Fran Nachlas, and Council Member
                Marc Wigder

        Mayor Singer welcomed Mr. Wigder to the dais and commented on the attributes and assets of the
        City. He noted he was looking forward to the journey ahead and thanked his colleagues, city staff,
        community leaders, family, and friends.

        Council Member Fran Nachlas shared that she was grateful to be able to serve the City and was
        excited to begin her first term. She thanked everyone for their support including her family, campaign
        team, colleagues, and residents.

         Council Member Wigder thanked Ms. O'Rourke for her contributions and service to the City and, in
         recognition of her service in Seat B, he personally presented her with a Queen B award. He then
         commented that he was looking forward to serving the City and thanked his family, friends, campaign
         and business teams, and his new colleagues. Mr. Wigder also discussed several of his priorities, which
         included maintaining fiscal discipline, City management succession and civic center planning,
         continuing to improve downtown connectivity and vibrancy and creating opportunities for local housing
         affordability and sustainable growth. He gave a final shout out to the F AU Owls.

         Mayor Singer thanked his campaign team, his late parents, extended family, and residents who
         supported him.

5.      ORGANIZATIONAL PROCEEDINGS:

          A.      Selection of Deputy Mayor


                           Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council Member
                           Fran Nachlas, to appoint Monica Mayotte as Deputy Mayor. Motion carried
                           unanimously on a voice vote.

          B.      Selection of CRA Chairman and Vice Chairman

                           Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member
                           Fran Nachlas, to appoint Marc Wigder as CRA Chair. Motion carried unanimously on
                           a voice vote.
                           Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                           Monica Mayotte, to appoint Fran Nachlas as CRA Vice Chair. Motion carried
                           unanimously on a voice vote.

6.       OTHER BUSINESS:
          A.      Selection of TPA (Transportation Planning Agency) Representatives and Alternates

                            Motion was made by Council Member Nachlas, seconded by Mayor Scott Singer, to
                            designate Yvette Drucker as the first representative to the TPA. Motion carried
                            unanimously on a voice vote.

                            Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                            to designate Fran Nachlas as the second representative to the TPA. Motion carried
                            unanimously on a voice vote.

                            Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Marc Wigder,
                            to designate Scott Singer, Marc Wigder, and Monica Mayotte as the TPA Alternates at
                            large. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

          B.       Selection of Palm Beach County League of Cities Voting Delegate and Alternate

                            Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member
                            Fran Nachlas, to appoint Yvette Drucker as the PBC League of Cities Voting Delegate.
                            Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

                            Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte,
                            to appoint Marc Wigder as the PBC League of Cities Voting Alternate. Motion carried
                            on a voice vote.

           C.      Selection of IPARC (lnterlocal Plan Amendment Review Committee) Representative
                   and Alternate


                            Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte,
                            to appoint Yvette Drucker as the /PARC Representative. Motion carried unanimously
                            on a voice vote.
