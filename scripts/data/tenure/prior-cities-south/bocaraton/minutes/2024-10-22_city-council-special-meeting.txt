                                  MINUTES OF THE SPECIAL MEETING
                                            CITY COUNCIL
                                    CITY OF BOCA RATON , FLORIDA
                                     TUESDAY, OCTOBER 22, 2024
                                               2:00 PM


The Regular Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton , Florida was held in the Auditorium at 6500
          Congress Avenue. The meeting was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at 2:00 PM .

1.     CALL TO ORDER:

2.     PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

3.     ROLL CALL:

       Mayor Scott Singer
       Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker
       Council Member Fran Nachlas
       Council Member Andy Thomson
       Council Member Marc Wigder

4.      CITY ATTORNEY APPLICANT INTERVIEWS:

       Mayor Singer advised that the purpose for today's meeting was to conduct interviews for the City
       Attorney position and thanked outgoing City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser for her years of service. City
       Manager George Brown gave a brief explanation of the process and introduced Jim Dineen, Recruiter
       who facilitated acquiring the candidates. Mr. Dineen explained the search, qualifications required, and
       the interview process. Applicants were brought in one at a time in alphabetical order and were all asked
       the same ten questions.

       Clayton F. Knowles was interviewed first. He offered a brief background of his education and
       experience.

       Joshua P. Koehler the current Deputy City Attorney of Boca Raton provided his education and
       experience and then began the interview process.


The meeting was recessed at 3:47 pm and resumed at 4:09 pm.


       Ronald Tomasko was interviewed and gave a brief background of his education and career and was the
       last to be interviewed .

        Upon concluding there was discussion amongst the members. Council Member Andy Thomson
        suggested reopening the posting in light of one applicant withdrawing from consideration . After
        extended discussion , consensus was had to select Joshua P. Koehler as the new City Attorney . Mayor
        Singer was nominated to negotiate the contract.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Fran Nachlas, seconded by Council Member
                        Marc Wigder, to enter negotiations of drafting an employment agreement with Joshua
                        P. Koehler for City Attorney. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Deputy Mayor Yvette
                        Drucker, nominating Mayor Singer to negotiate the contract on behalf of the Council.
                        Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

        Discussion ensued regarding terms of the contract.
5.       CITY MANAGER REPORTS:

Mr. Brown advised that Ms. Frieser would be leaving the City after 25 years of service on October 31st , and it
would be appropriate to appoint Mr. Koehler as acting City Attorney until the contract is approved .

                            Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                            Marc Wigder, to appoint Joshua P. Koehler as the acting City Attorney. Motion carried
                            unanimously on a voice vote.

Mr. Koehler was brought back into the auditorium and was advised that he was selected as the new City
Attorney .

6.       ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting was adjourned at 5:11 pm.
