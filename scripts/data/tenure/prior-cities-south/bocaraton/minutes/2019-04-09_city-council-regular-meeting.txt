            !!:,     Ordinance No. 5483
                     An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28 "Zoning," Article XII "Industrial
                      Districts," Division 2, "Light Industrial and Research Park (LIRP) Zoning District," Section 28-981,
                     "Conditional Commercial Nodes Uses Allowed," to authorize car and truck rentals and package
                     stores selling alcoholic beverages (subject to certain restrictions and requirements), as part of a
                     conditional commercial node master plan approval in the LIRP zoning district, by amending the
                      list of allowable and prohibited uses; amending Section 28-979, "Prohibited Uses," to affirm that a
                      package store selling alcoholic beverages is an allowable use in a Conditional Commercial Node:
                      providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification: providing an effective
                     date (AM-18-11)

            Mayor Singer stated that this was the first of two public hearings. Jim Bell, Development Services Long
            Range Project Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The objective of the text amendment was
            presented, as well as the history of the current code. Mr. Bell reviewed the locations of the LIRP
            commercial nodes and benefits of the inclusion of the vehicle rental use in the zoning district. The
            features of the text amendment allowing package stores to sell alcoholic beverages were then presented.
            Mr. Bell noted that the proposed uses were consistent with the zoning and the proposed changes would
            fall within the limits of City's Comprehensive Plan. The Planning and Zoning Board recommendations and
            approval were discussed. Staff recommended approval of the proposed text amendment following the
            second hearing. Mr. Bell then made himself available for questions. Members asked questions regarding
            vehicle rental businesses, types of package stores affected by the text amendment and the parking and
            storage of rental vehicles. Mr. Bell and Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided
            additional information.

            The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed. Mayor Singer noted that the
            second public hearing would be on April 23.

20.        MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

                     Boca Raton Historical Society Renovation of Town Hall

            Council Member Mayotte spoke on behalf of the need to support and preserve Town Hall, home to the
            Boca Raton Historical Society and Museum. She shared the results of recent fundraising efforts of the
            Historical Society and noted the planned new exhibits. Mary Csar, Executive Director of the Historical
            Society, gave a brief presentation on the repairs needed at Town Hall. Derek Vander Ploeg, the project
            architect, provided additional information. Ms. Csar and Mr. Vander Ploeg responded to questions. Hank
            Jahari, president of the Historical Society, asked the Council for their support. Board Member Denise
            Allman spoke about the need to preserve the history of the building. Members commented and expressed
            support for a contract with the Society. The City Manager noted that a contract and budget amendment
            would be brought before Council for approval.

Deputy Mayor Rodgers reported on his attendance at the recent meeting of the Business Development Board of
Palm Beach County and commented on the City's availability of Class A office space.

Council Member O'Rourke commented on available office space in the Downtown and the City's upcoming
Strategic Planning Workshop.

Mayor Singer provided details about the upcoming Strategic Planning Workshop. He reported on his attendance
at the Florida League of Mayors meeting and commented on staff efforts to meet ADA website requirements.
