 11.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A       Ordinance No. 5607
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the Boca Raton Downtown Development of
                  Regional Impact (DORI) Development Order to add provisions allowing for and regulations
                  governing space efficient mechanical and automated parking systems; providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (AM-20-12)

        Deputy City Manager George Brown made the presentation. He noted that the CRA had discussed the
        proposed ordinance at length, and recommended approval once several amendments had been made.
        Mr. Brown briefly reviewed the revisions which included, but not limited to, language clarification and
        formatting, the requirement of a full-time valet or attendant, and OSHA requirements. Upon concluding,
        he made himself available for Members' questions.

        The public hearing was opened. Joe Graubart asked a number of questions, including whether a
        maintenance log would be required, if there would be annual inspections, and if 24/7 attendants would
        be required. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed. Mr. Brown advised that
        the proposed ordinance required a maintenance plan and inspections of the systems. He also noted
        that an attendant would be required at all times unless the applicant could demonstrate that the garage
        could safely operate without them. Deputy Mayor O'Rourke clarified the point that IDA approval would
        be required for each automated parking system application received.

  The meeting was recessed at 6:19 pm to address technical issues and resumed at 6:23 pm.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member
                         Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5607 as amended.

         Council Member Drucker and Mayor Singer thanked staff and the private sector for their collaboration
         on the ordinance. Deputy Mayor O'Rourke commented that she would be voting no, not because she
         did not support the subject, but because she desired a more thorough process for staff analysis and
         recommendation. Council Member Mayotte thanked staff and other parties involved for their work on
         the ordinance.

                         Motion carried 4 votes to 1; Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                         Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes; Deputy
                         Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke voting no.
