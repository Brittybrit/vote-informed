11.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A.       Ordinance No. 5690
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton creating an Auditor Selection Committee pursuant to
                 Section 218.391 , Florida Statutes, for the purpose of selecting an auditor to conduct the annual
                 financial audit required by Section 218.39, Florida Statutes; providing for the membership,
                 appointment, term, quorum, powers, and duties of the committee; providing for severability;
                 providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date

         Linda Davidson , Assistant City Manager, reviewed the ordinance. She explained the purpose of
         establishing an Auditor Selection Committee, the composition of the membership, and function. Upon
         concluding, Ms. Davidson responded to Council Members' questions.

         The public comment period was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed.

                           Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council Member
                           Yvette Drucker to approve Ordinance No. 5690. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                           Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                           Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

          B.      Ordinance No. 5691
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 23, "Planning and Development,"
                  Article IV, "Development Regulations," Division 2, "Housing," Section 23-110, "The Affordable
                  Housing Advisory Committee (AHAC)," Code of Ordinances, to conform to Chapter 420,
                  Florida Statutes, pertaining to the State Housing Initiatives Partnership Program; providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date

          Teresa McClurg, Community Improvement Administrator, gave the presentation. She began with a
          review of the ordinance, including the background information, the purpose of the Affordable Housing
          Advisory Committee (AHAC) and a summary of the proposed amendments. Staff recommended
          approval to authorize the amendments and advised that the appointment of a City elected official to
          the AHAC be made at the City Council Organizational Meeting on April 1, 2024. It was also
          recommended that remaining members be appointed in the same manner as other board
          appointments. Upon concluding, Ms. McClurg responded to Council Members' questions.

19.      MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Mayor Singer suggested to save the bulk of comments for outgoing Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte for next
Monday's Organizational Meeting.

Deputy Mayor Mayotte gave a PowerPoint presentation providing her final thoughts and suggestions as
Deputy Mayor and encouraged her fellow Council Members and staff to be more productive by working smarter
and making changes for the better.

Council Member Drucker expressed her disappointment with the delay of the circulator RFP and hoped the
vendor contract would come before them at the first April meeting. She then commented positively on Ms.
Mayotte's tenure and wished her well.

Council Member Nachlas congratulated Ms. Drucker on her reelection, as well as Mr. Thomson's election. She
also echoed Ms. Drucker's comments and thanked Ms. Mayotte for her service to the City.

Council Member Wigder also remarked favorably on Ms. Mayotte's tenure and shared his appreciation for her
service to the City.

Mayor Singer highlighted the Organizational Meeting on April 1st and the State of the City on April 1oth at the
Mizner Park Amphitheater. The Mayor highlighted Ms. Mayotte's advocacy of environmentalism and
sustainability and thanked her for her friendship.

 20.     ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 6:44 PM.
