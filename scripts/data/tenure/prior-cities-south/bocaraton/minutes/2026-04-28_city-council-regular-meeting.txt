14.     PUBLIC REQUESTS:

         Earl Starkoff offered thoughts on the Downtown Civic Task Force and the recruiting process.

         Glenn Gromann expressed concern regarding the homeless population at City parks.

         Brianna Hagquist spoke about local wildlife and relayed her support for preservation of City parks.

         Christina Broeker expressed concern about an incident involving a homeless individual and
         commented favorably on additional girls' softball fields .

         Patty Dervishi raised concerns about the safety issues involving the shooting of iguanas.

         Robert Dukate spoke on zoning issues throughout the City.

         Judy Morrow spoke in support of tennis amenities within the City and commented on property taxes.

         Alexandra Avelo expressed concern about non-residents using local parks and services.

         Angela McDonald commented on recent events in Pearl City and invited Council Members to attend
         meetings in that community.

         Angela Maisey spoke about responsible growth in the Downtown, the Treasure Coast Regional
         Planning Council, development of North Park, and the proposed referendum question.

 17.    CITY MANAGER RECOMMENDATIONS AND REPORTS:

         City Manager Mark Sohaney summarized key updates from the previous day's workshop, including
         plans to pursue an RFP for a Community Master Plan consultant and ongoing discussions about the
         Downtown Civic Engagement Task Force. He recognized the recent Boca Bash and thanked agencies
         for their focus on safety at that event, provided updates on Police Chief recruitment and his efforts to
         gather information about homelessness in the City. Mr. Sohaney then highlighted upcoming events
         such as the Memorial Day ceremony and the installation of a new Memorial Park plaque and
         concluded by noting the City's blue flag beach designation, a new City website chatbot feature , and
         the upcoming Chamber breakfast.

         Council Member Drucker requested staff presentations be made available for Members' review prior
         to the Council Meetings.

         Mayor Thomson agreed with Ms. Drucker's comments and asked that the decision on the Task Force
         be addressed at the next Workshop. Council Member Sipple noted that the services of the Treasure
         Coast Regional Planning Agency were not offered to municipalities for free , but at cost.

 18.    CITY ATTORNEY REPORTS:

         The City Attorney informed the public where to access the presentations from the previous day's
         Workshop.

 19.    MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

         Council Member Drucker commented on federal , state, and local safety concerns about e-bikes and
         deliberation of the State legislature regarding elimination of property taxes.

         Deputy Mayor Grau spoke about the Downtown Civic Engagement Task Force, questioned whether it
         was the best approach , and suggested that the public outreach be handled by a consultant. Discussion
         ensued and Members weighed in .

         Council Member Jon Pearlman reiterated that he wished to withdraw his approval of the Downtown
         Civic Engagement Task Force and requested information regarding the repeal of the resolution; the
         City Attorney provided clarification.

         Mayor Thomson highlighted upcoming City events.

 20.    ADJOURNMENT:

         The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 7:04 pm.
