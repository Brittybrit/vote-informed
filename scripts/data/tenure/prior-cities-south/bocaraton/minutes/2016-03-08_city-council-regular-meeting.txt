12.    REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

        A.     Ordinance No. 5343
               An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Sections 16-32 and 16-33, Code of
               Ordinances, to prohibit commercial loading and unloading from public rights-of-way within the
               Community Redevelopment Area; including a reference to the definition of "collection services"
               and correcting a cross-reference; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for
               codification; providing an effective date
                        Ordinance No. 5343

       Ruby Childers gave a brief presentation. Background information on the hours for loading and unloading
       downtown was provided. Ms. Childers noted the increase in commercial activity at all hours that has
       become a detriment to traffic. She then reviewed the Code revisions as proposed in the ordinance. Upon
       concluding, Ms. Childers responded to questions from Council members. Concerns were expressed
       regarding buildings that may not have adequate parking and room for loading and unloading; discussion
       followed. It was suggested that the ordinance be continued.

       The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed.

                     Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Council Member Scott
                     Singer, to continue Ordinance No. 5343 to the March 22, 2016 City Council meeting.

       Members made final comments.

                      Motion carried 4-0; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Robert S. Weinroth, Council
                      Member Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Scott Singer voting yes.

20.    MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Mullaugh reported on a recent meeting of a board of which he serves.

Council Member Singer asked about the research of buildings in compliance with Ordinance 4035, noting that a
report would be coming forward on April 11. He then revisited his suggestion to keep the same goals for the
coming fiscal year. Members weighed in. He reviewed the focus of committees on which he serves and reported
on a bill regarding overnight mooring. In conclusion, he highlighted upcoming events downtown.

Deputy Mayor Weinroth noted dates of upcoming meetings of boards on which he sits as well as topics that will
be addressed in the future. He also reminded the public of an upcoming workshop on traffic at Palmetto Park
Road and 5th Avenue. In conclusion, he highlighted early voting and upcoming events.

Mayor Haynie highlighted the upcoming Boating and Beach Bash. She congratulated the Boca High NJROTC on
winning the Florida State Championship. She also noted that the boards on which she serves all have publicly
noticed meetings and all proceedings are transparent.
