12.    REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

                Ordinance No. 5367
                An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering a temporary moratorium on the operation of
                Medical Marijuana Treatment Centers and Medical Marijuana Dispensing Organizations within
                the City for a period of one (1) year; providing for severability; providing an effective date (SC-14-
                04R2)
                         Ordinance No. 5367

       Mayor Haynie noted that this was the first of two public hearings on this ordinance and no vote would be
       taken. Tamashbeen Rahman, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Background information
       on the Compassionate Medical Cannabis Act of 2014, Ordinances 5285 and 5323, and House Bill 307
       was provided. The justifications for imposing a moratorium were reviewed. It was noted that if
       Amendment 2 on the November ballot does not pass, the moratorium would terminate. The Planning &
       Zoning Board recommended approval, as does staff. Upon concluding, Ms. Rahman responded to
       questions from Council members.

       The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed.                Mayor Haynie
       announced that the second public hearing would be held on November 8.

       li,      Ordinance No. 5370
                An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton providing for the vacation and abandonment of a portion
                of a water easement located at 2140 North Federal Highway, as more specifically described
                herein; providing conditions for vacation and abandonment; approving and accepting a water
                easement deed over certain lands located at 2140 North Federal Highway; providing for
                severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (EA-16-05)
                        Ordinance No. 5370

       Lynn Bodor, Property SpecialisUAppraiser, gave a brief presentation describing the requested vacation
       and abandonment and the need for same. She then made herself available for questions.

       The public hearing was opened. Craig McDonald, on behalf of the petitioner, advocated passage of the
       ordinance and responded to questions from Council members. Rosangela DeMello, McDonald's
       Corporation, responded to a question regarding the timing of construction. Seeing no one else come
       forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                     Motion was made by Council Member Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Robert S.
                     Weinroth, to approve Ordinance No.5370. Motion carried 4 to O; Mayor Susan Haynie,
                     Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S.
                     Weinroth voting yes.
