 11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

           A.      Resolution No. 141-2025
                   A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the approximately 0.51-acre property
                   generally located at 104 NW 20th Street, a conditional use approval to authorize
                   approximately 10,560 square feet of retail use in an existing building in the M-2 general
                   industrial zoning district; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (CA-25-02)

         The City Attorney read the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing. Members
         made their ex-parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

         Senior Planner Heather Hansen gave the PowerPoint presentation . She provided a history of the
         location , previously approved uses, the proposed site improvements, parking space increase, and
         landscaping improvements . She advised that Planning and Zoning Board and staff recommend
         approval, subject to the conditions set forth. Upon concluding , she made herself available for questions.

         The public comment hearing was opened and , seeing no one come forward , was closed.

                           Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council Member
                           Andy Thomson, to approve Resolution No. 141-2025. Motion carried unanimously;
                           Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                           Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

 12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:


          A.       Ordinance No.5750
                   An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton relating to the General Employees' Pension Plan ;
                   providing for a one-time lump sum payment paid for certain retired or disabled general
                   employees; providing for repealer; providing for an effective date

          Deputy City Manager/ CFO Jim Zervis gave the presentation . He gave an overview of the proposed
          ordinance, noting the higher cost of living for retirees , and detailed the proposed amounts.

          The public comment hearing was opened and , seeing no one come forward , was closed .

                           Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                           Marc Wigder, to approve Ordinance No.5750. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                           Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                           Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

          B.       Resolution No. 147-2025
                   A resolution of the City of Boca Raton authorizing the City Manager to execute a
                   telecommunications Rights-of-Way Use Agreement with Telxius Cable, USA, Inc. for the
                   purpose of use of certain public rights-of-way for telecommunications purposes; providing for
                   severability; providing for repealer; providing for an effective date

          Public Works and Engineering Director Zach Bihr gave the presentation . He gave background
          information of the proposed resolution and advised that the infrastructure is already in place. Upon
          concluding , he made himself available for questions.

          The public comment hearing was opened . Jonathan Pearlman shared concerns with the proposed
          Right-of-Way and potential ecological impacts. Jonathan Ounjian stated that the timeline of the funds
          should be expedited . Seeing no one else come forward , the public hearing was closed.
