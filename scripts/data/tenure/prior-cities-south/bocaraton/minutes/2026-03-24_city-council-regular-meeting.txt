                    The Mayor noted that the public hearings on 11.A.1 through 11 .A.4 would be conducted
                    contemporaneously as the items were interrelated. He noted that a separate vote would be
                    taken on each item, and should item 11 .A not be approved, then no vote would be taken on
                    the remaining items. The City Attorney read the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern
                    the public hearing . Members made their ex-parte disclosures, and the City Clerk administered
                    the oath to those who wished to speak.

                    Development SeNices Director Brandon Schaad and Senior Planner Eoin Devlin gave the
                    PowerPoint presentation . Mr. Devlin highlighted the location , history, proposed FLUM
                    amendment, zoning and variance requests, and site plans were shown . The Planning and
                    Zoning Board and staff recommend approval. Upon concluding , he made himself available for
                    questions . Discussion ensued and Mr. Schaad provided additional details.

                    The public hearing was opened. Attorney for the applicant Bonnie Miskel gave a PowerPoint
                    presentation . She began with a history of the property and the affected areas of proposed
                    construction. Jim Tinson of Hart Howerton Architects gave additional details regarding the site
                    plan and residences . Michael Gehart, Landscape Architect, provided information relating to
                    the proposed landscaping. Marjie Alloy , Judith Kaye, Stephanie Rosenzwieg , Mike Liebelson ,
                    Richard Warner, Pam Paschke, Joseph Puljalski, Michael Schneider, expressed concerns
                    regarding landscaping , public access, and construction vehicles and access. Attorney for the
                    applicant Bonnie Miskel answered questions and provided additional details in response to
                    public comments. Seeing no one else come forward , the public hearing was closed .

                    Discussion ensued .

 The meeting was recessed at 8:45 PM and resumed at 9:02 PM .

           B.       Golf Envy

                    1.     Resolution No. 19-2026
                           A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering , for the approximately 4.36-acre
                           property generally located at 6501 and 6531 Park of Commerce Boulevard , conditional
                           use approval , pursuant to Section 28-978 , Code of Ordinances , to authorize a 3,263
                           square foot athletic training facility in an existing 19,862 square foot warehouse and
                           office building , in the Light Industrial Research Park (LIRP) zoning district; providing
                           for repealer; providing an effective date (CA-25-05)

         The City Attorney confirmed that the quasi-judicial procedures which governed the public hearing were
         still in effect. Members made their ex-parte disclosures, and the City Clerk administered the oath to
         those who wished to speak.

         Senior Planner Eoin Devlin gave the PowerPoint presentation . He provided a history of the property,
         outlined the applicant's request , and advised that both Planning and Zoning Board and staff
         recommended approval. Upon concluding , he made himself available for questions.

         The public hearing was opened . Attorney for the applicant Christina Bilenki advised that Mr. Devlin
         covered all points and she made herself available for questions . Pam Paschke inquired if Ms. Bilenki
         was a lawyer, planner, or lobbyist. Ms. Bilenki responded that she was a lawyer. Seeing no one else
         come forward , the public hearing was closed .

                           Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                           Fran Nachlas, to approve Resolution No. 19-2026.

         Mayor Singer suggested this type of use be handled solely by staff as this should be administrative.

                           Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council
                           Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc
                           Wigder voting yes.
