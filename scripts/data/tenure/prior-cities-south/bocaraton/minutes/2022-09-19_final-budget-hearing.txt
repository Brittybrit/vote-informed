3.       REBUDGETING OF FY 2022 - 2023 BUDGET APPROPRIATIONS

Sharon McGuire, Office of Management and Budget (0MB) Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation on the
2022-2023 Budget. She began with an overview of the Mission, Vision, and Guiding Principles of the City. The
proposed millage rate was 3.6784 mills per $1 ,000 which is slightly lower than that for Fiscal Year 2021-2022.
The general operating expenses were outlined and new personnel and programs for the coming year were
reviewed. Ms. McGuire emphasized the comparison to nearby cities, noting that the City's property tax rate
continues to be below that of surrounding municipalities. Upon concluding, she made herself available for
Members' questions.

4.       PUBLIC HEARING TO FINALIZE THE BUDGET AND ADOPT MILLAGE RATES

                   percentage by which the millage to be levied exceeds the "rolled-back rate"; directing the City

           8.        Ordinance No. 5631
                    An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton adopting a final budget and appropriating funds for the
                    fiscal year beginning October 1, 2022 and ending September 30, 2023; providing for severability;
                    providing for repealer; providing an effective date

           The City Clerk read Ordinance No. 5630 in full and the title of Ordinance No. 5631 .

           Sharon McGuire, 0MB Director, identified the rebudgeted items that would be carried over to the 2022-
           23 fiscal year budget. Ms. McGuire reviewed Exhibits 2 and 3 and outlined the funds affected, including

          G.       City Council shall amend the adopted tentative budget as it sees fit

         City Manager Leif Ahnell reviewed the requests for additional funding and amendments to the Fiscal Year
         2022-2023 Budget. Topics discussed included the City Manager succession plan, rewriting of the Land
         Development Code, Art in Public Places, hiring a consultant to streamline Development Services
         Processes, and Expansion of the City's Recycling Program. The last item discussed was the Government
         Campus Master Plan. Ms. Drucker urged staff to begin the process of approving the funds to hire a
         consultant. Discussion ensued and Mr. Ahnell provided additional information. Mayor Singer agreed that
         the Government Master Plan should be a priority. Mayor Singer proposed a new position to review policies
         and provide research directly to the Council Members and suggested a proposed budget for this position.
         Discussion ensued. Mayor Singer then opened the discussion for the Non-Profit funding requests. Mr.
         Ahnell reviewed the list of the Non-Profit Organizations and provided background on the process.
         Discussion ensued and Members weighed in. Mr. Ahnell then opened the discussion of the Non-Profit
         Funding requests. Members considered changes to the funding list that each member had proposed.
         Working from the Mayor's list, individual requests and modifications were reviewed, and amendments
         were made.

          H.       Re-computation of proposed millage rates

5.      RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS

         A.       Resolution No. 93-2022
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton adopting the FY 2022/23-2027/28 Capital Improvements
                  Program for the City of Boca Raton; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an
                  effective date

         Sharon McGuire, 0MB Director, advised that a presentation had been made at the Tentative Budget
         Hearing and no changes had been made. She then made herself available for Members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed.

                                Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Deputy Mayor Andrea
                                Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 93-2022 including Exhibits 1 and 3. Motion
                                carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                                Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

          B.       Resolution No. 105-2022
                   A resolution of the City of Boca Raton adopting an amended and restated Boca Raton Municipal
                   Facilities and Services User Fee Schedule; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                   providing an effective date

           Compliance Auditor Jean-Michel Neault gave the Power Point presentation. He provided an explanation
           of User Fees and a breakdown of the proposed changes by department. Upon concluding, he made
           himself available for questions. The Mayor inquired if any feedback had been received on the proposed
           user fees, to which the City Manager responded that he was not aware of any public comment.

           The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward , closed.

                                Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                                Andrea Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 105-2022.

           Mayor Singer noted that the current annual fee for boat launching was low compared to the daily costs
           but expressed concern about the proposed increase in the annual fee. Deputy Mayor O'Rourke advised
           that she had noticed the increase as well but had not received any comments from the public.

                                Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                                Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

6.       ADJOURNMENT

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 7:42 PM.
