3.      PRESENTATION ON TENTATIVE BUDGET, PROPOSED MILLAGE RATES, AND CAPITAL
        IMPROVEMENTS PROGRAM:

       Budget Director Sharon McGuire gave the PowerPoint presentation . She began by reviewing the Mission,
       Vision and Guiding Principles, and Strategic Priorities. Ms. McGuire explained that the proposed millage
       rate of 3.6783 mils, which was slightly lower than the FY 2022-23 rate. She further explained the General
       Fund Operating Costs, new additions to the Fiscal Year 2023-24 Budget, and described the Property Tax
       Bill by Taxing Authority breakdown. Also discussed were the tax rates and Fire Assessment fees of
       surrounding cities and how Boca Raton is the lowest of the cities compared . Ms. McGuire then concluded
       her presentation regarding millage rates and made herself available for Members' questions. City Manager
       Leif Ahnell explained the budget process to Council Members and responded to questions regarding
       additional staff. Discussion ensued regarding staffing, as well as the Beautification Fund, electric vehicles
       and charging stations for same.

        Ms. McGuire continued by briefly reviewing the Capital Improvements Program for Fiscal Year 2023/24 -
        2028/29 . Upon concluding, she made herself available for questions. Discussion ensued and there was
        consensus among members to make the RFP for last mile transit a priority. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser
        responded to a question regarding piggybacking on vendor contracts with other municipalities. Discussion
        ensued on moving the Final Budget meeting date and/or time or beginning the Tuesday meetings earlier.
        There was consensus to add continued budget discussion to the next Workshop agenda.

         B.      Resolution No. 88-2023
                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton adopting an amended and restated Boca Raton Municipal
                 Facilities and Services User Fee Schedule; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                 providing an effective date

         Compliance Auditor Jean-Michel Neault gave the presentation . He began with an explanation of the
         purpose of user fees and gave a brief review of the proposed changes to the User Fee Schedule. Upon
         concluding, he made himself available for questions. Discussion ensued with consensus to direct staff to
         research user fees paid between residents and non-residents. City Manager Leif Ahnell responded to
         questions regarding the cut back in camps, confirmed that there was no increase to boat permits, and
         advised that staff would research the costs associated with beach events.

         The public hearing was opened. Josie Machovec expressed her concerns with the beach and park pass
         increase adding there was no option to pro rate the cost. David Abers commented on the increase in golf
         fees. Mr. Ahnell responded to comments made regarding the beach permit increase and golf rates.

                           Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member Fran
                           Nachlas, to approve Resolution No. 88-2023. Motion carried unanimously. Mayor Scott
                           Singer, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Marc Wigder, Council Member
                           Fran Nachlas, Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte voting yes.
