15.     RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:


         A.        Resolution No. 53- 2023
                   A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering the reimbursement of the legal defense costs
                   incurred by former Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O' Rourke in connection with an election
                   complaint that was determined to be legally insufficient by the Florida Elections Commission;
                   providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

         The City Attorney gave a brief overview of the proposed reimbursement and advised that the Florida
         Elections Commission found the complaint legally insufficient. Ms. Frieser noted that Florida law
         provides that public officials are entitled to legal representation at the public' s expense in order to
         defend themselves with respect to a matter on which they prevail as long as it arises out of or in
         connection with the performance of their official duties and serves the public' s purpose. Upon
         concluding, she made herself available for Members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Andrea Levine 0' Rourke explained that she felt the need to defend
         her reputation    and stated that this matter had been a frivolous attempt to smear her character. Glenn
         Gromann spoke in favor of the resolution and respectfully requested the Council grant the request.
         Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

                            Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                            Monica Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 53- 2023. Motion carried unanimously;
                            Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                            Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

 18.     CITY ATTORNEY           REPORTS:


          A.        Succession     Planninq   Related   to the Position    of Citv Manaqer


          The City Attorney gave a brief overview of the steps to be followed for the succession of George Brown
          to the position of City Manager.

          Council Member Marc Wigder stated that he did not agree with the steps set forth in the City Attorney' s
          memorandum and advised that he would make a motion to pull the tabled motion from the previous
          meeting. Mr. Wigder explained that he believed the Mayor should be authorized to handle
          negotiations, as discussed in the previous meeting, expressed concern regarding delegation to the
          City Attorney and Human Resources Director, and noted that the Charter allows for special counsel
          to be hired to assist with the matter. Mayor Singer expressed concerns about a delegation of authority
          allowing him to handle negotiations as there may be Sunshine Law ramifications. The City Attorney
          confirmed that the Sunshine Law might be implicated and noted that her memorandum                     did not include
          delegation to the City Attorney or the Human Resources Director. Ms. Frieser provided information
          regarding the potential Sunshine Law impact( when there is a delegation to one member of an elected
          body, negotiations resulting from that delegation must occur within a noticed forum). She also


RegularAgenda                                                    6                                                    May 23, 2023
        explained that the Human Resources Department would conduct a salary compensation survey. Mr.
        Wigder advised that, as the HR Director works for the current City Manager, he believed that presented
        a conflict and he reiterated that the City Council, by Charter, hires the City Manager. Discussion
        ensued. The Mayor indicated that all Members were free to talk to Mr. Brown regarding terms of the
        contract. Deputy City Manager George Brown advised that salary data is easily obtained and that he
        felt HR was capable of compiling the information, that he was comfortable working with the City
        Attorney on the terms of the agreement and did not believe outside counsel should be retained, and
        that the City Council would make the final decision. Mayor Singer reviewed the points on which
         consensus appeared to be reached after the discussion.


         Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte, to
         proceed with negotiations to hire George Brown as the next City Manager, HR will compile the data
         and circulate it, Council Members would have discussions as necessary, and the City Attorney and
         Mr. Brown would negotiate the terms of the contract form to be brought to Members for review, advice,
         consent, and approval when ready. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.
