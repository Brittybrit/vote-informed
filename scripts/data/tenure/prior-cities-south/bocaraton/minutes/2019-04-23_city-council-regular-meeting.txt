11:,        REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

                      Ordinance No. 5481
                      An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28 "Zoning," Article XV,
                      "Supplementary District Regulations," Division 1, Section 28-1302, "Car Agencies," Code of
                      Ordinances, to delete the requirement that a car agency be enclosed by a six foot high masonry
                      wall: providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an
                      effective date (AM-18-07)



        Regular Minutes                                       3                                         April 23, 2019
           Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation, noting that this
           public hearing was continued from the March 26, 2019 City Council Meeting. Questions raised at the prior
           hearing were reviewed and responses provided. Topics included, but were not limited to, setbacks,
           impact on residential zoning and anticipated locations of future car agencies. Staff recommended
           approval of the ordinance as originally presented. Mr. Schaad presented further amendment options for
           Council consideration. He then made himself available for questions.

           The public hearing was opened. Doug Mummaw, on behalf of Investments Limited, made brief comments
           in support of the amendment as originally presented. Les Wilson from Boca First expressed support of
           the ordinance. Cristina Escalante came forward to inquire about medical marijuana dispensaries. Mayor
           Singer clarified the public hearing was regarding this ordinance only, and her question would be
           addressed during the public requests. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was
           closed.

                        Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andy
                        Thomson, to approve Ordinance No. 5481.

           Members made final comments.

                        Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council
                        Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy
                        Thomson voting yes.

           fl       Ordinance No. 5483
                    An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28 "Zoning," Article XII "Industrial
                    Districts," Division 2, "Light Industrial and Research Park (LIRP) Zoning District," Section 28-981,
                    "Conditional Commercial Nodes Uses Allowed," to authorize car and truck rentals and package
                    stores selling alcoholic beverages (subject to certain restrictions and requirements), as part of a
                    conditional commercial node master plan approval in the LIRP zoning district, by amending the
                    list of allowable and prohibited uses: amending Section 28-979, "Prohibited Uses," to affirm that a
                    package store selling alcoholic beverages is an allowable use in a Conditional Commercial Node:
                    providing for severability: providing for repealer: providing for codification: providing an effective
                    date (AM-18-11)

           Jim Bell, Development Services Long Range Project Manager, gave a brief PowerPoint presentation,
           noting that this was the second of two public hearing. The purpose of the amendment was discussed, and
           it was noted that the changes were consistent with the Comprehensive Plan. The Planning and Zoning
           Board recommended approval with an amendment to remove truck and non-habitable trailer rentals. Staff
           recommended approval as modified. Upon concluding, Mr. Bell then responded to Council members'
           questions.

          The public hearing was opened. Martin Heise, owner of affected property, expressed support of the
          amendment. He questioned parking and site plan review once the Code amendment was approved. Mr.
          Bell responded. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed. Mayor Singer asked
          Mr. Bell if an amendment could be added to address Mr. Heise's concern. Mr. Bell and Mr. Schaad
          advised they had language prepared. In order to allow the City Attorney time to review the language with
          staff, it was decided, without objection, to postpone a vote to later in the meeting and move on to Public
          Requests.
