                                   MINUTES OF THE FINAL BUDGET HEARING
                                               CITY COUNCIL
                                       CITY OF BOCA RATON, FLORIDA
                                      WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 27, 2023
                                                  5:05 PM

                      The Final Budget Hearing of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida
                                  was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at 5:05 PM.

 1.       PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG

 2.       ROLL CALL

             Mayor Scott Singer
             Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte
             Council Member Yvette Drucker

 3.       REBUDGETING OF FY 2023 - 2024 BUDGET APPROPRIATIONS

          Mayor Singer made opening remarks and discussed the purpose for the meeting.

          Office of Management and Budget (0MB) Director Sharon McGuire gave the PowerPoint presentation.
          Ms. McGuire began with a brief oveNiew of the Mission, Vison , and Guiding Principles of the City. She
          advised that the proposed millage rate was 3.6580 mills per $1 ,000, which is slightly lower than that for
          Fiscal Year 2022-2023. She continued by describing the strategic priorities and gave an oveNiew of
          the General Operating Fund costs. She then reviewed the Fire Assessment Fees, taxes and utility rates
          and noted that Boca Raton is among the lowest taxed cities in Florida. Upon concluding, she made
          herself available for questions.

          Ms. McGuire then continued by reviewing Exhibits 1-3, which were the Members' requests for
          amendments to the CRA budget, operating re-budgets, and Capital Improvement Program re-budgets.
          Upon concluding, Ms. McGuire and City Manager Leif Ahnell responded to Members' questions.

 4.       PUBLIC HEARING TO FINALIZE THE BUDGET AND ADOPT MILLAGE RATES


            A.        Ordinance No. 5664
                     An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton adopting final millage rates and levying ad valorem
                     taxes for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2023 and ending September 30, 2024; stating
                     the percentage by which the millage to be levied exceeds the "rolled-back rate"; directing the
                     City Manager or the Office of Management and Budget Director to adjust the adopted millage
                     rates in the event of changes in the assessment roll and taxable value; providing for
                     severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

            B.        Ordinance No. 5665
                     An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton adopting a final budget and appropriating funds for
                     the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2023 and ending September 30, 2024; providing for
                     severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

            The City Clerk read Ordinance No. 5664 in full and the title of Ordinance No. 5665.

            The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed.

           Council Member Nachlas requested funds to enhance the bike lane at the Don Estridge School

           to improved safety features for bike lanes. Council Member Wigder then introduced his proposal to
           add funds for City Hall refurbishment. Mayor Singer referred to Exhibit 4 and highlighted the items for
           TOD consultant, air quality improvements for City Hall, and the proposed COLA increase. Discussion
           ensued on additional line items in Exhibit 4 and there was consensus from Members to not include
           the suggested COLA increase.

                               Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council Member
                               Yvette Drucker, to amend Ordinance No. 5665 to include the line items discussed in
                               Exhibit 4. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

           Mayor Singer reviewed the Non-Profit Funding request process. Members discussed their proposed
           changes to the list and, working from the Mayor's list, agreed to changes resulting in an increase of
           $52,000.00 to the total amount of non-profit fund ing.

                               Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                               Monica Mayotte, to amend Ordinance No. 5665 to include additional funding of non­
                               profit organizations as stated by the Mayor. Motion carried unanimously on a voice
                               vote.

           Council Member Wigder then disclosed that he would recuse himself from the discussion and
           voting for the funding requests from the Gumbo Limbo Coastal Stewards and the FAU Research
           Park due to a conflict of interest for those items.

                               Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Yvette
                               Drucker, to amend Ordinance No. 5665 by removing funding consideration of
                               $150,000.00 for the FAU Research Park. Motion carried 4 votes to 0.

                                                                                    WHO MUST FILE FORM 88
   This form is for use by any person servi'ng at the county, city, or other local level of government on an appointed or elected board, council,
   commission, authority, or committee. It applies to members of advisory and non-advisory bodies who are presented with a voting conflict of
   interest under Section 112.3143, Florida Statutes.

   Your responsibilities under the law when faced with voting on a measure in which you have a conflict of interest will vary greatly depending
   on whether you hold an elective or appointive position. For this reason, please pay close attention to the instructions on this form before
   completing and filing the form.




                    INSTRUCTIONS FOR COMPLIANCE WITH SECTION 112.3143, FLORIDA STATUTES
   A person holding elective or appointive county, municipal, or other local public office MUST ABSTAIN from voting on a measure which
   would inure to his or her special private gain or loss. Each elected or appointed local officer also MUST ABSTAIN from knowingly voting on
   a measure which would inure to the special gain or loss of a principal (other than a government agency) by whom he or she is retained
                                                                                                  a
   (including the parent, subsidiary, or sibling organization' of prin.cipal by which he or she is retained); to the special private gain or loss of a
   relative; or to the special private gain or loss of a business associate. Commissioners of community redevelopment agencies (CRAs) under
   Sec. 163.356 or 163.357, F.S., and officers ,of independent special tax districts elected on a one-acre, one-vote basis are not prohibited
   from voting in that capacity.
   For purposes of this law, a "relative" includes only the officer's father, mother; son, daughter, husband, wife, brother, sister, father-in-law,
   mother-in-law, son-in-law, and daughter-in-law. A "business associate" means any person or entity engaged in or carrying on a business
   enterprise with the officer as a partner, joint venturer, coowner of property, or corporate shareholder (where the shares of the corporation
   are not listed on any national or regional stock exchange).


   ELECTED OFFICERS:
   In addition to abstaining from voting in the situations described above, you must disclose the conflict:
             PRIOR TO THE VOTE BEING TAKEN by publicly stating to the assembly the nature of your interest in the measure on wh ich you are
             abstaining from voting; and
             WITHIN 15 DAYS AFTER THE VOTE OCCURS by completing and filing this form with the person responsible for recording the
             minutes of the meeting, who should incorporate the form in the minutes.


   APPOINTED OFFICERS:
   Although you must abstain from voting in the situations described above, you are not prohibited by Section 112.3143 from otherwise
   participating in these matters. However, you must disclose the nature of the conflict before making any attempt to influence the decision,
   whether orally or in writing and whether made by you or at your direction.
