 11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

          A.       Resolution No. 150-2020
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a conditional use approval pursuant to Section
                  28-1396, Code of Ordinances, to authorize construction of a convenience store in excess of 750
                  square feet and accessory to a service station in the R-B-1 zoning district for the property generally
                  located at 5899 North Federal Highway; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (CA-18-
                  01)

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing. The City
         Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak. Members made their ex parte disclosures.

         John Lindgren, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began by providing a description of
         the property location. Mr. Lindgren reviewed components of the application and noted that it complied with
         conditional use approval criteria and was consistent with the Comprehensive Plan. The Planning and
         Zoning Board and Development Services staff recommended approval. Upon concluding, Mr. Lindgren
         responded to members' questions regarding generator installation and buffers between the subject property
         and adjacent residential homes. Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided additional
         information regarding conditional use approval requirements.

         The public hearing was opened. Ele Zachariades, on behalf of the applicant, thanked staff for their
         presentation. She confirmed that proposed plans included a generator that would power the whole site and
         that a wall and additional landscaping would be added to the west side of the property. Upon concluding,
         she made herself available for members' questions. There was no cross-examination or rebuttal. Seeing
         no one else indicate their desire to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member
                          Monica Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 150-2020.

 16.     RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:

           A.     Resolution No. 177-2020
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering the request of the Greater Boca Raton Beach
                  and Park District for an exemption from the annual tax increment payment due to the Boca Raton
                  Community Redevelopment Agency pursuant to Section 163.387(2)(d), Florida Statutes and City
                  Resolution No. 9-2020; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

         Michael Woika, Deputy City Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Woika began by providing
         background and history of the Tax Increment Program. He reviewed Resolution No. 9-2020 and outlined
         the procedure for a special district to request exemption from the tax increment payments. Estimated
         payments of the Greater Boca Raton Beach and Park District (BPD) for the next five years were displayed.
         Mr. Woika reviewed the application factors for exemption including, but not limited to, fiscal and operational
         impact of the exemption to both the CRA and BPD, the benefit to the specific purpose for which the BPD
         was created, and the benefit of the BPD's activities to the City's area of operation. City staff recommended
         denial of the application. Upon concluding, he made himself available for members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Jacob Horowitz, attorney, spoke on behalf of the BPD. He began with a
         review of the debt payment history. Mr. Horowitz suggested that, as past payments had been applied to the
         debt service at Mizner Park and that debt was satisfied, the funds might be better redirected through the
         BPD to other projects within the community. Briann Harms, Executive Director of the BPD, reiterated points
         made by Mr. Horowitz and, citing the importance of the City's parks and beaches, asked members for their
         support. Seeing no one else indicate their desire to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Mayor Scott Singer,
                          to approve Resolution No. 177-2020 to deny the exemption.

         Members commented and requested clarification from City staff. City Manager Leif Ahnell provided
         additional information and advised that, if the request were granted, there would be a significant shortfall in
         CRA revenues. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser noted that the criteria for exemption comes from State
         statute. Discussion ensued. Mr. Ahnell further clarified that the only individuals paying into the CRA and the
         Downtown are Downtown property owners. Mr. Horowitz and Ms. Harms responded to points made. Linda
         Davidson, Financial Services Director, confirmed the information relayed by the City Manager. Members
         expressed their positions.

                          Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke,
                          Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member
                          Andy Thomson voting yes.
