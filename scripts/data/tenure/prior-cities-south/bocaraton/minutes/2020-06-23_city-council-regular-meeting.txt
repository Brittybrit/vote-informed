 11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

           A.     Resolution No. 80-2020
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering approval of a use and development in the Public
                  Land (PL) zoning district pursuant to Section 28-1238(b)(1), Code of Ordinances, to authorize the
                  development of Wildflower Park, and the redevelopment of Silver Palm Park, for properties
                  generally located at 551 and 600 East Palmetto Park Road; providing for repealer; providing an
                  effective date (CA-20-01)

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. The City
         Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak. Council Members made their ex parte
         disclosures . At the Mayor's request, the City Attorney provided clarification regarding ex parte disclosures
         where the applicant is the City.

         John Lindgren, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation . He began with a review of the locations
         and condition of each site. Pictures that showed multiple vantage points of both parks were displayed. Mr.
         Lindgren provided a brief description of the Conditional Use Approval and noted that the site plan was
         approved by the Parks and Recreation Board in March . Drawings of the approved site plan were shown,
         as well as the revised site plan that reflected revisions made to accommodate the approved budget. Upon
         concluding, Mr. Lindgren responded to members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened . Kona Gray, consultant with EDSA, provided additional information . He
         answered questions about the alterations made to the plan in order to reduce costs. Jennifer Bistyga,
         Coastal Program Manager, informed Council members of her attempts to contact the owner of the adjacent
         property. Discussion ensued. Ms. Bistyga, Mr. Gray, and City Manager Leif Ahnell responded to questions
         and provided information about the project timeline.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member Andrea
                  Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 80-2020. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote;
                  Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                  Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
