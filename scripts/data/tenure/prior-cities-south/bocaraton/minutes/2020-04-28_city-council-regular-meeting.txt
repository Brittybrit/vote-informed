             A.          Ordinance No. 5521
                         An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the application process of taxicab and limousine
                         applicants to remove the referral of the application to the Financial Advisory Board; providing for

                     Jeremy Rodgers, to approve Ordinance No. 5521. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,
                     Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine
                     O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

            PUBLIC REQUESTS:

Sarah Pearson, on behalf of the Boca Raton Chamber of Commerce, commented on a letter sent to Council
members by CEO Troy Mclellan outlining a suggested financial assistance program for local small businesses.

Joe Mahjess commented on closures due to the Covid-19 pandemic and the impact on residents and businesses.

Mikhail Guseynov submitted a question regarding the reopening of beaches and community pools. City Manager
Ahnell provided information on the County order lifting restrictions on community pools.

Rachel Eade stressed that appropriate preparations should be made by the City in anticipation of the repeal of State
and County emergency orders.

The City Clerk read the following comment into the record:
Kris Mortenson expressed his concerns regarding the shallowness of the inlet and how this might affect boating
now that restrictions were being lifted. The City Manager advised that the dredge was currently being repaired and
would be put back into service once operational.

Rene Stern requested that her business be identified as essential. It was noted that stricter guidelines concerning
the designation of essential businesses was established under County guidelines.

  16.       RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:

             A.          Resolution No. 60-2020
                         A resolution of the City of Boca Raton regarding the intent of the City Council to transfer title of a
                         parcel of real property, owned by the City and located near the Don Estridge Middle School, to the
                         School Board of Palm Beach County to be used for the construction and operation of a new
                         elementary school; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

             Deputy City Manager Mike Woika gave a brief PowerPoint presentation, beginning with the background
            of the school reconstruction project. He explained that the School Board had determined they would need
            a larger site than what was included in the current lease. Maps were displayed showing the parcels
            involved, as well as options that could reduce the size of the property transfer. Upon concluding, Mr. Woika
            responded to members' questions.

            The public comment period was opened and, seeing no one indicate their desire to speak, closed.

                     Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                     Rodgers, to approve Resolution No. 60-2020. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,
                     Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine
                     O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

       Regular Minutes                                           4                                        April 28, 2020
Council Member O'Rourke commented on the financial assistance program proposed by the Chamber of
Commerce. The City Manager provided information. Members weighed in. Topics included, but were not limited to,
the need to retain existing businesses, criteria for applicants, and the possibility of additional funding from federal
programs. There was consensus among Council members to have staff research the issue and report back at the
next Special Workshop Meeting scheduled for the following Tuesday. Council Member O'Rourke concluded her
remarks by highlighting local charities and encouraged residents to get involved.

Deputy Mayor Rodgers inquired about communication received by the public. The City Clerk advised email and
voicemail related to the Council meetings had been made available to Council members prior to the meeting. Mr.
Rodgers thanked City staff for their work and urged County officials to hold a public meeting. He also referred to a
letter he is composing to the federal government regarding the tax code.

Council Member Mayotte commented on the need for additional Covid-19 testing sites in south Palm Beach County.

Mayor Singer assured residents that City officials and staff were advocating on their behalf and reminded residents
to enjoy the parks safely and responsibly.
