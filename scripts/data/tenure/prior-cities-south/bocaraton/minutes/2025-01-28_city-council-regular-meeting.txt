11.   REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

       A.      Ordinance No. 5723
               An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 23, "Planning and Development,"
               Article IV, Division 4, "Sidewalks and Bikeways," Section 23-162, "Sidewalk Policy," Code of
               Ordinances, to specify that the installation or widening of sidewalks shall not be required upon
               development or redevelopment of any property on the east side of State Road A 1 A, north of
               Palmetto Park Road; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for
               codification; providing an effective date (AM-24-03)

      Mariella Overdorff, Planning Intern, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She provided an overview of
      the proposed text amendment changes and assurances of the protection of environmentally sensitive
      lands on the east side of State Road A 1 A. She advised that the Planning and Zoning Board and staff
      recommend approval. Upon concluding, she made herself available for questions. Development
      Services Director Brandon Schaad answered a question from Mayor Singer.

      The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member
                        Fran Nachlas, to approve Ordinance No. 5723. Motion carried unanimously. Mayor
                        Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council
                        Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.
