12.    REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

        A       Ordinance No. 5581
                An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the Boca Raton Downtown Development of
                Regional Impact (DORI) Development Order to reflect established planning principles,
                guidelines, goals and requirements included within the DORI and the Amended Downtown
                Plan as specific criteria to be reviewed in connection with applications for individual
                development approval; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective
                date (AM-21-04)

        Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began by
        providing the background of Ordinance No. 4035. He noted that the proposed ordinance would
        incorporate objectives such as urban design, walkability and connectivity into the DORI Development
        Order, and provide ease of reference for staff and property owners. The Planning and Zoning Board
        recommended approval. Upon concluding, he made himself available for members' questions.
        Deputy Mayor O'Rourke advised that she proposed the ordinance to further enhance the walkability
        of the Downtown area and to make the process more concise for applicants and staff.

        The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed.

                        Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                        Member Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5581. Motion carried
                        unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council
                        Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy
                        Thomson voting yes.

20.    MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Drucker expressed her desire to see the Education Task Force reinstated, urged residents
to continue to be vigilant regarding Covid-19, and reminded all to be mindful of school zones while driving.

Council Member Mayotte reviewed the resolutions that she would be voting on at the upcoming Florida
League of Cities conference; members expressed unanimous support of the resolutions. She then
commented on the increase in COVID-19 cases and encouraged everyone to get vaccinated.

Deputy Mayor O'Rourke urged all who are eligible to get the Covid-19 vaccine. She then asked Council
Member Drucker what the assigned task would be for the Education Task Force. Ms. Drucker responded,
and brief discussion ensued. Ms. O'Rourke then commented on the Brightline train station site plan and
inquired about the walkability of the project. Mr. Ahnell responded. She then addressed the proposed building
recertification ordinance and thanked everyone who came forward to speak on the issue. She encouraged
residents to email Council members with specific ideas or suggestions on the topic.

Mayor Singer commented on the suggestion to reinstate the Education Task Force and proposed surveying
current City board members for feedback. Council Members weighed in. He then commented on the Covid-
19 vaccination rate. Mayor Singer concluded by highlighted the Back-to-School Tax Holiday and encouraged
all to be prepared for hurricane season.
