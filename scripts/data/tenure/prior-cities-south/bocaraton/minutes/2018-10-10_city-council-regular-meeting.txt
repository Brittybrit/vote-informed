                  located at 945-965 Lago Mar Lane, to eliminate a condition requiring a six (6) foot solid wall on

                  (PUD-05-03R1)
                          Resolution No. 137-2018
                          Drawings

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
         made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

        Jim Bell, Acting Deputy Director of Development Services, gave the PowerPoint presentation beginning

17.    QUASI-JUDICIAL PUBLIC HEARINGSNARIANCES & APPEALS:

        A         Resolution No. 140-2018
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the appeal by the Carlton
                  Condominium Association of the decision of the Community Appearance Board approving, with
                  conditions, the exterior painting of the building located at 901 East Camino Real; providing for
                   repealer; providing an effective date
                           Resolution No. 140-2018

        The City Attorney confirmed that quasi-judicial procedures remained in effect. Members made their ex
        parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

        Jim Bell, Acting Deputy Director of Development Services, gave a PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Bell
        briefly reviewed the appeal, showing pictures and examples of proposed paint colors. He presented the
        appellant's argument and advised that the Community Appearance Board (CAB) was operating within its
        purview. Mr. Bell reviewed the options before Council and stated that staff recommended the CAB's
        decision be upheld and the appeal denied. Upon concluding, he made himself available for questions.

        The public hearing was opened. Ellyn Bogdanoff, on behalf of the appellant, provided pictures and paint
        color chips for the Council to review. Upon concluding, she responded to questions. Mark Jacobson,
        CAB chair, provided information on the Board's decision and responded to questions from members,
        clarifying the location of the banding on the building. Nicky Siegel, resident of the Carlton, commented on
        the color choices. There was no cross examination. Ms. Bogdanoff made comments in rebuttal. Seeing
        no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                       Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Monica
                       Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 140-2018 to grant the appeal.

        Members asked several questions of staff and made final comments.

                       Motion carried on a 4 to 1 vote; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council
                       Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes; Council Member
                       Andrea Levine O'Rourke voting no.

          B.      Resolution No. 141-2018
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the appeal of the
                  decision of the Community Appearance Board denying VCA Property Acquisition
                  Corporation's requested freestanding sign (Permit No. 18-4267) located at 180 Northwest
                  Spanish River Boulevard; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                           Resolution 141-2018

          The City Attorney confirmed that quasi-judicial procedures remained in effect. Members made their ex
          parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

          Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave a brief PowerPoint presentation. He reviewed
          the zoning and uses of the property, presenting pictures of the proposed sign and the reasoning behind
          the denial of the Community Appearance Board (CAB). Staff recommended denial of the request. Mr
          Schaad then made himself available for questions.

          The public hearing was opened. Ana Maria Manresa presented on behalf of the appellant. The proposed
          sign dimensions and neighboring business' signs were discussed. Lisa Gillespie, also on behalf of the
          appellant, cited heightened traffic as the reason for needing the sign. Mark Jacobson, CAB chair,
          offered information regarding the Board's decision and responded to members questions. Ms. Manresa
          and Ms. Gillespie offered rebuttal and advised that the appellant would be willing to forego the building
          wall sign in favor of a larger monument sign.

                   Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andrea
                   Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 141-2018 affirming the decision of the Community
                   Appearance Board. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                   Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council
                   Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
