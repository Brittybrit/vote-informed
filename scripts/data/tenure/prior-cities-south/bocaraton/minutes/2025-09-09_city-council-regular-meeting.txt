           H.      Ordinance No. 5749
                   An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton authorizing , pursuant to chapter 13, Article Ill , "Sale
                   of City Property," Code of Ordinances, the sale of an easement for telecommunications
                   purposes over approximately 0.493 acres of City-owned land within Spanish River Park
                   (located at 3001 North Ocean Blvd) ; authorizing the execution of a purchase and sale
                   agreement; authorizing the execution of an easement deed pursuant to such purchase and
                   sale agreement; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

            Deputy Director of Development Services Erin Sita gave the PowerPoint presentation. She gave a
            history of the location , intended use of the easement, and reviewed the purchase and sale agreement.
            She advised that the Planning and Zoning Board and staff recommend approval. Upon concluding ,
            she answered Members' questions .

           The public comment period was opened. William Swaim wanted assurances that this cable would
           not cross his property. Jonathan Pearlman advised that the City does not need the money from this
           easement sale/lease, the site is environmentally sensitive, and the City should not rush into this sale/
           lease. Seeing no one else come forward , the public comment period was closed .

           Discussion ensued with City Attorney Josh Koehler advising that this use is specifically permitted by
           City Code. Mr. Schaad and Ms. Sita provided additional information and answered Members'
           questions. Guillermo Canete, on behalf of the applicant, advised that there is no new construction
           and they are utilizing existing infrastructure and conduits.

           Council Member Thomson questioned whether a fiber optic cable constitutes infrastructure and still
           had concerns. Further discussion ensued . City Manager Brown and Mr. Koehler provided additional
           details regarding this type of telecommunications easement and permitted uses.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                          Marc Wigder, to approve Ordinance No.5749. Motion passed four votes to one (4 to
                          1); Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                          Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes, Council Member Andy Thomson voting no.
