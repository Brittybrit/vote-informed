The Mayor announced the purpose of the special meeting.

 4.        REGULAR PUBLIC HEARING:

            A.     Ordinance No. 5616
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton authorizing a transaction between the City, the Boca
                  Raton Community Redevelopment Agency (CRA), and the Center for Arts & Innovation Inc.
                  (formerly the Boca Raton Arts District Exploratory Corporation (BRADEC)), in connection with
                  the development of a performing arts center at Mizner Park and the redevelopment of the Mizner
                  Park Amphitheater, pursuant to Section 163.380, Florida Statutes, and Chapter 13, Article Ill,
                  "Sale of City Property," Code of Ordinances; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                  providing an effective date (The proposed transaction affects parcels of real property owned by
                  the City of Boca Raton and by the Boca Raton Community Redevelopment Agency. The
                  proposed sale of City-owned property is being considered pursuant to the requirements of
                  Chapter 13, Article Ill, City Code of Ordinances. Pursuant to the requirements of Section
                  163.380, Florida Statutes, notice of the potential disposition of the property owned by the
                  Community Redevelopment Agency was published March 30, 2021 . Because the disposition of
                  the Agency's property would be for less than fair market value, the authorization of the City
                  Council is required to complete the transaction.)

          George Brown, Deputy City Manager, gave the presentation. He stated that the presentation previously
          made to the CRA, and the public hearing conducted by the CRA on August 22, 2022, were part of the
          record. Staff recommended adoption of the ordinance with the changes to the transaction documents as
          previously discussed and enumerated in the memoranda of September 20th and October 5 th _ Mr. Brown
          advised the amendment would include language proposed by Council Member Mayotte to the CRA
          resolution on the same subject, as well as parallel language from an amendment to the CRA resolution
          proposed by Mayor Singer and Council Member Thomson. Upon concluding, Mr. Brown made himself
          available for questions.

           The public hearing was opened . Michael Manko spoke in opposition of the ordinance, preferring the
           amphitheater to be kept as an open-air venue and asked that an alternate location be chosen for the
           performing arts center. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed . Mayor
           Singer suggested the motion to adopt would include all changes made to the previously approved CRA
           resolution.
                          Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member
                          Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5616 as amended. Motion carried on a 4 to 1
                          vote; Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member
                          Andy Thomson voting yes, Mayor Singer voting no.

 5.      PUBLIC REQUESTS:

There were no public requests.

 6.     ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 4: 13 PM.
