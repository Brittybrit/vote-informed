 10.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A.     Resolution No. 01-2023
                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for an approximately 8.5 acre property generally
                 located at 1751 North Military Trail and 21046 Commercial Trail, an amendment to the approved
                 Planning and Zoning Board Site Plan (Board Resolution No. 2021-014) to authorize the
                 construction of an approximately 121,030 square foot five-(5) story building consisting of
                 approximately 89,500 square feet of medical office, 23,817 square feet of general office, and 7,813
                 square feet of restaurant and a six-(6) story parking structure; removing the approximately 1.7 acre
                 portion of the adjacent Lake Worth Drainage District L-46 Canal from the approved site plan;
                 authorizing an alternative landscape plan and four (4) technical deviations: (i) To allow required
                 parking to be provided off-site on the aforementioned 1.7 acre L-46 Canal Property, (ii) Reduce the
                 required width of the existing southern driveway on Commercial Trail (from 24 feet to 23 feet); (iii)
                 Reduce the required reservoir distance of the existing northern driveway along Commercial Trail
                 (from 50 feet to 30 feet); and (iv) Reduce the required turning radii of the existing northern driveway
                 along Commercial Trail (from 30 feet to 25 feet); providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                 (SPA-20-13R1)

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing . Members
         made their ex-parte disclosures. Assistant City Clerk Phillip W. Daly administered the oath to those who
         wished to speak.

         Long Range Project Manager Jim Bell gave the presentation. Mr. Bell started with a brief overview of the
         project and a history of the property. Slides shown were site plans, proposed amendments, mobility
         improvements and architectural renderings . The traffic and parking study was discussed, including the
         technical deviations for offsite parking, and altering the driveways entering the property. The Plan ning &
         Zoning Board and staff recommended approval. Upon concluding, Mr. Bell made himself available for
         questions. Discussion ensued. Mr. Bell responded to Members' questions regarding the covered walkway
         and the number of parking spaces.

         The public hearing was opened. Attorney for the applicant, Bonnie Miskal, advised Mr. Bell had thoroughly
         covered her presentation and made herself available for questions. Mayor Singer asked for addit ional site
         renderings which were displayed. Ms. Miska! reviewed the renderings and spoke regarding some of the
         features of the building, including a rooftop terrace, the overall architecture, and landscape designs. Upon
         concluding , Ms. Miska! responded to Members' questions. Glenn Gromann spoke in favor of the project
         and commented on the need for Class A office space. Jonathan Ounjian advised that the Zoning should
         be consistent throughout the City. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

          In summation, project architect Juan Caycedo responded to a question concerning the landscape design.
         Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided additional information on comments made by
         Mr. Caycedo. Council Member O'Rourke asked Mr. Schaad to clarify a question Mr. Ounjian brought
         forward.

                         Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                         Member Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 01-2023.

          Council Members commented on the beauty of the building design .

                         Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke,
                         Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Fran
                         Nachlas voting yes.

          D.     Ordinance No. 5642
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton relating to the procedures for approval of site plans,
                 conditional uses, and conditional future land use map amendments and cond itional rezonings;
                 amending Chapter 23, "Planning and Development," Article VI, "Conditional Land Use
                 Amendments and Rezoning," Code of Ordinances, to (i) authorize amendments to approvals
                 pursuant to the conditional future land use map amendment and rezoning approval procedures
                 without reference to said procedures where the original approval has been fully implemented
                 (except in cases which increase residential density), and (ii) clarify language and procedures, and
                 delete obsolete provisions; amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Article II , Division 2, "Site Plans," Code
                 of Ordinances, to (i) authorize the City Manager to take administrative action on certain minor site
                 plan amendments, as defined, and (ii) clarify language and procedures, and delete obsolete
                 provisions; amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Article II, Division 4, "Conditional Use Approval," Code
                 of Ordinances, to (i) allow an amendment to a site plan associated with a conditional use to be
                 amended without requiring an amendment to the conditional use where the site plan amendment
                 will not result in a change of use (except to a permitted use in the zoning district, or a conditional
                 use already approved by the City Council), an increase in density or intensity, a reduction in open
                 space or previously required landscaping or a violation of any condition of the cond itional use
                 approval, (ii) increase the time available for obtaining a building permit prior to expiration of a
                 conditional use approval (from 18 to 24 months), and (iii) clarify language and procedures, and
                 delete obsolete provisions; creating Chapter 28, "Zoning," Article I, Section 28-9, "Standard
                 Application Processing Procedures," and Section 28-10, "Appeals," to provide a uniform method
                 for the processing of applications for development approval and appeals of decisions in connection
                 with such applications; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an codification;
                 providing an effective date (AM-21-02)

         Director of Development Services Brandon Schaad gave the presentation. Mr. Schaad began with a
         background of the proposed changes, including the streamlining process. He reviewed the code rewrite,
         deletions, and updates to the code language. Mr. Schaad advised this is the most significant streamlining,
         major modernization, and the most significant progress in the ongoing land development code rewrite to
         date. This would also free up staff time to allow for more impactful and longer-term project development.
         Staff and the Planning & Zoning Board recommended approval, Upon concluding, Mr. Schaad made
         himself available for questions. Council Member Mayotte thanked staff for their effort and inquired if the
         proposed size of 50,000 square feet could be adjusted in the future if necessary. Ms. O'Rourke also
         thanked staff and asked about the 50,000 s.f. limitations. Discussion ensued with Mr. Schaad answering
         Members' questions. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser provided additional insight. Council Member
         Drucker advised she was not comfortable with the 50,000 s.f. threshold and would not support approval
         of the Ordinance. Council Member Nachlas was also concerned over the 50,000 s.f. threshold and
         concurred with Ms. Drucker that 25,000 s.f. would be more manageable. Ms. Frieser clarified comments
         made by Council Members and reviewed the revisions and deletions of the proposal. Discussion ensued.
         Mr. Ahnell, Ms. Frieser, and Mr. Schaad answered questions related to the Council Members questions.
         Ms. Frieser provided clarification and reviewed the technical changes to the code rewrite.

         The public hearing was opened. Glenn Gromann complemented staff for the proposal and advised there
         are not many projects that would require Council approval in this size range, and he recommended staff
         approve the project. Jonathan Ounjian voiced his concern over the size of the proposal and the ability to
         add 50,000 s.f. to a property without Council's approval. Seeing no one else come forward, the public
         hearing was closed.

         Mayor Singer, Ms. Frieser, and Mr. Schaad provided additional information in response to remarks made
         during public comment.

 The meeting was recessed at approximately 8: 15 pm and reconvened at approximately 8:42 pm.
