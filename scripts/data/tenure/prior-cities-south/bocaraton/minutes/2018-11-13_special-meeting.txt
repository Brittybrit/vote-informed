3.     OTHER BUSINESS/SETTLEMENTS:

               Resolution No. 151-2018
               A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a proposed agreement to settle litigation styled
               Group P6-D, LLC v. City of Boca Raton and Boca Raton Community Redevelopment Agency
               (including the joinder of the property owner in the settlement); authorizing, if approved, the City
               Manager to execute the Settlement Agreement; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                        Resolution No. 151-2018

       City Attorney Diane Grub Frieser provided a brief overview of the resolution.

       The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed.

               Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andy
               Thomson, to approve Resolution No. 151-2018. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,
               Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea
               Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

4.     ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 3:12pm.
