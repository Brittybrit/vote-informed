12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A.       Ordinance No. 5785
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton , Florida, amending Chapter 28, "Zoning ," Article I,
                  Section 28-9 , "Standard Application Processing Procedures for Development Approvals ," to
                  establish lobbyist certification and disclosure requirements for development applications;
                  providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification ; providing an
                  effective date

          Development Services Director Brandon Schaad gave the presentation and highlighted the County
          Code that requires lobbyists to register with Palm Beach County. He explained that the proposed
          ordinance would reinforce this requirement within the City. Upon conclud ing , he responded to
          questions and provided additional information

          The public hearing was opened. Joe Graubart spoke in favor of the ordinance. Jonathan Ounjian
          stated he was concerned with development in the City but also supported the ordinance. Seeing no
          one else come forward , the public hearing was closed.
