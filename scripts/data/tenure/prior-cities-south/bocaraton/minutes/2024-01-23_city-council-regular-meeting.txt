11.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A        Ordinance No. 5680
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the City's Comprehensive Plan by amending
                 Future Land Use Element Policies LU.1.1 .5, LU .1.1.6 and LU .1.1.10, and amending housing
                 element objective HO.1 .5.0 and policy HO.1 .5.3 to establish a framework for the affordable
                 housing provisions of Subsections 166.04151(6) and (7), Florida Statutes; providing for
                 severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-23-14)

         Mayor Singer noted that the ordinance had been transmitted to the State in December of 2023 and
         that it had been re-introduced in January. The Mayor then advised that this was the first of two public
         hearings, and no vote would be taken.

         Development Services Director Brandon Schaad gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began by
         giving a brief background of the proposed amendments to the code. Mr. Schaad reviewed the process
         of transmittal, noted that the ordinance had been re-introduced due to a correction in the title and that
         language regarding the maximum Floor Area Ration had been revised for consistency. He advised
         that both the Planning and Zoning Board and staff recommended approval. Mr. Schaad concluded by
         sharing a staff email sent to Jonathan Ounjian that addressed his question regarding allowable
         density. He then responded to questions from Deputy Mayor Mayotte and provided clarification.

         The public hearing was opened. Jonathan Ounjian shared a PowerPoint and spoke against the
         allowable density on his proposed project. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public
         hearing was closed. Mayor Singer advised that the second public hearing for this item would take
         place on February 13, 2024.

          B.      Ordinance No. 5682
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Code of Ordinances,
                  to provide for fleet vehicle parking as a main (as opposed to accessory) use; amending Article
                  I, Section 28-2, "Definitions," to add a definition of "Fleet Vehicle Parking"; amending Article
                  XVI , "Off-Street Parking and Loading," Section 28-1646, "Definitions," to delete the definition
                  "Fleet Vehicles"; amending Article XII , "Industrial Districts," Division 8, "M-3 Manufacturing
                  Industrial District," Section 28-1123, "Permitted Uses," to add "Fleet Vehicle Parking" as a
                  permitted use; amending Article XV, Division 1, "Supplementary District Regulations," by
                  creating Section 28-1317, "Fleet Vehicle Parking," to establish regulations for such use;
                  amending Article XVI , "Off-Street Parking and Loading ," Section 28-1655 , "Required Off-Street
                  Parking ," to clarify the number of required fleet vehicle parking spaces; providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification ; providing an effective date (AM-
                  22-05)

          Jim Bell, Long Range Project Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He gave an overview of
          the revised ordinance with the Planning and Zoning Board proposed amendments to fleet vehicle
          parking , including the proposed restriction of pick up and drop off times, which staff does not support.
          He advised that both the Planning and Zoning Board and staff recommended approval with
          modifications. Upon concluding, he made himself available for questions. Discussion ensued . Mr. Bell

          C.      Ordinance No. 5684
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton establishing procedures and regulations pursuant to
                 Section 166.04151 (6), Florida Statutes, for development of affordable housing (referred to as
                 "Commercial Industrial Multifamily Development" or "CIMO"), including workforce housing;
                 establishing eligibility requirements, development standards, and approval procedures
                 applicable to such development, (including reduced parking requirements, where applicable);
                 adding CIMO as a permitted use in the following zoning districts (where the Future Land Use
                 Designation is Planned Mobility): R-B-1 (Motel Business), B-2 (Community Business), MC
                 (Medical Center), POI (Professional, Office, and Institutional), City CG (Commercial General
                 City), CHO (Commercial High Office), LIRP (Light Industrial Research Park), M-3
                 (Manufacturing Industrial) and W-1 (Warehouse District); providing for severability; providing
                 for repealer; providing for codification ; providing an effective date (AM-23-10)

          Mr. Brown advised that two copies of the ordinance had been provided; one that reflected changes
          made by staff and the other by Council Member Nachlas. Development Services Director Brandon
          Schaad gave the PowerPoint presentation . He began by giving a board overview of the proposed
          revisions and highlights from previous meetings. Mr. Schaad advised that both the Planning and
          Zoning Board and staff recommended approval. Upon concluding, he made himself available for
          questions. At Mayor Singer's request, Mr. Schaad provided additional information on the changes to
          the ordinance by staff, noting that it consisted of removal of repetitive wording and language cleanup
          that were not substantive. In response to a question by Mr. Wigder, he advised that the latest changes
          proposed by Ms. Nachlas had not been reviewed by the Planning and Zoning Board.

          The public hearing was opened. Jonathan Ounjian spoke in favor of the ordinance and asked that the
          City ensure that plans approved for retail and commercial actually get completed by the developers.
          Lon Tabatchnick shared his support for the ordinance and asked that developers be given credit for
          incorporating additional amenities in their properties. Ele Zachariades thanked staff for their work on

        staff for their work on this ordinance. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing, was closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Fran Nachlas to approve Ordinance No. 5684

         advising that additional restaurant or retail uses, rather than office use, would provide a better
         experience to residents of the planned developments, result in fewer car trips on local roads, and add
         to pedestrian friendliness.

                          Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Fran Nachlas
                          to approve Ordinance No. 5684 as revised by staff and with proposed revisions by Ms.
                          Nachlas.

         Extended discussion ensued. Ms. Zachariades commented and advised that she had suggested the
         amendments proposed by Ms. Nachlas. Ms. Drucker shared that she would need more data on the
         potential impact of the revisions in order to consider them and Mr. Wigder and Ms. Mayotte indicated
         their support for certain sections of the proposed changes.
