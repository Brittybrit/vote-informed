10.   QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

       A.      Resolution No. 140-2024
               A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the approximately 4.32-acre property
               generally located at 1081 Holland Drive, Conditional Use Approval, pursuant to Section 28-
               1198, Code of Ordinances to authorize, within the existing approximately 76,000 square foot
               industrial building, conversion of 28,365 square feet, and the addition of a 1,370 square foot
               mezzanine, for a large-scale indoor commercial recreation use (a padel facility) in the
               Manufacturing Industrial (M-3) zoning district; providing for repealer; providing an effective
               date (CA-24-01)

               1.       Resolution No. 141-2024
                        A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the approximately 4.32-acre
                        property generally located at 1081 Holland Drive, a site plan approval to authorize,
                        within the existing approximately 76,000 square foot industrial building, conversion of
                        28,365 square feet, and the addition of a 1,370 square foot mezzanine, for a large­
                        scale indoor commercial recreation use (a padel facility) and other site improvements,
                        including installation of a sidewalk, together with a technical deviation from Section 28-
                        1655, Code of Ordinances, to reduce the number of off-street parking spaces from 173
                        spaces to 153 spaces; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SPA-24-06)

      The Mayor noted that the public hearings on items 10.A. and A.1. would be conducted
      contemporaneously as the items were related. He noted that a separate vote would be taken on each
      item and should item 10.A. not be approved, no vote would be taken on 10.A.1. The City Attorney read
      the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing. Members made their ex parte
      disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

      Heather Hansen, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Ms. Hansen provided a brief
      background of the proposed Conditional Use and Site Plan Approvals. She advised that the Planning
      and Zoning Board and Staff recommend approval. Upon concluding, she made herself available for
      questions.

       The public hearing was opened. Javier E. Fernandez, attorney for the applicant, responded to
       Members' questions. Ms. Hansen noted that the square footage of the space should be 28,356 Square
       feet, and not 28,365 and advised that it would be corrected in the final resolution. Seeing no one else
       come forward, the public hearing was closed.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Deputy Mayor Yvette
                        Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 140-2024. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                        Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council
                        Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Fran Nachlas, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                        Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 141-2024. Motion carried unanimously;
                        Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas,
                        Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

       B.      Ordinance No. 5719
               An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the Future Land Use Map of the
               Comprehensive Plan to redesignate the approximately 124.21-acre Boca Raton Innovation
               Campus (BRIC) property, generally located at 5000 T-Rex Avenue, from Planned Mobility
               (PM) to Enhanced Mobility (EM); providing for revisions to the Future Land Use Map; providing
               for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-24-05)

        Mayor Singer noted that this was a transmittal hearing and the first of two public hearings, and three
        votes would be required to transmit.

        Peter Begovich, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He discussed the background of
        the property, zoning, and proposed amendments and land use changes to the Future Land Use Map
        (FLUM). He advised that Planning and Zoning and Staff recommended approval. Upon concluding,
        he made himself available for questions.

       The public comment period was opened. Jonathan Ounjian commented about the TR Danburg
       project, the number of residential units that would be approved with this project and stated that the
       Zoning should be fair for all zones.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Fran Nachlas, seconded by Council Member
                        Marc Wigder, to approve Ordinance No. 5719 for transmittal. Motion carried
                        unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member
                        Fran Nachlas, Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting
                        yes.

       C.      Ordinance No. 5720
               An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 19, "Building Regulations," Articles
               I, Ill, VI and VII, Code of Ordinances, and Chapter 21, "Flood Damage Prevention and
               Floodplain Management," Article I, Code of Ordinances, to: update the date of the Flood
               Insurance Study and Flood Insurance Rate Maps, provide a definition of the term "Crown of
               Road," establish distinctions in required finish floor elevations between new construction and
               additions to existing buildings and structures in non-special flood hazard areas, adopt
               provisions and definitions regarding newly established floodways (including sections of the El
               Rio Canal}, reduce the substantial improvement threshold (from 50% to 49%) and reduce the
               period for tracking cumulative cost of improvements (from three (3) years to one (1) year);
               remove obsolete provisions relating to flood-resistant design requirements contained in
               Section 19-239 and Section 19-240, Code of Ordinances; providing for severability; providing
               for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-24-11)
