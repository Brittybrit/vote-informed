11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A.       Resolution No. 1-2016
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering an amendment to a Conditional Use approval
                  (Resolution No. 23-2014) extending the closing hours of operation of the tasting room of a
                  previously approved microbrewery, Barrel of Monks Microbrewery, located in the Penn-Florida
                  Commerce Center at 1141 South Rogers Circle; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                  providing an effective date (CA-13-04R1)
                           Resolution No. 1-2016

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
         made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to all those who wished to speak.

         Jim Bell, Planning and Zoning Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The location of the
         microbrewery was provided and the previous conditional use approval was reviewed . The requested
         changes to the hours of operation were then detailed, and it was noted that no adverse impacts were
         identified by staff. After discussing a concern related to trash, the Planning & Zoning Board recommended
         approval. Staff also recommended approval of the petition.

         The public hearing was opened. Mitch Kirschner, GrayRobinson, gave a brief PowerPoint, explaining the
         need for the extended hours and requesting approval. He also addressed the concern regarding
         adequate trash receptacles . Dr. William McFee, a proprietor of the brewery, addressed the request for
         extended hours for tours and tastings and addressed security and trash concerns . He also noted that
         Barrel of Monks supports various charities. Dr. McFee then responded to questions from Council
         members. George Brown, Deputy City Manager, addressed a question regarding the hours of operation
         for tours of the brewery and for the tasting room. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public
         hearing was closed.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Council Member Jeremy
                  Rodgers, to approve Resolution No. 1-2016. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie,
                  Deputy Mayor Robert S. Weinroth, Council Member Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Jeremy
                  Rodgers, Council Member Scott Singer voting yes.

12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A.       Ordinance No. 5341
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering an amendment to Chapter 10 "Nuisances",
                  Article II "Lot Clearing", Section 10-26 "Definitions", Code of Ordinances, reducing the maximum
                  height of "untended vegetation" on properties within the City from 12 inches to 8 inches; providing
                  for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date
                            Ordinance No. 5341

         Deputy City Manager George Brown provided a brief history of the changes in the allowable height of
         untended vegetation in the City. It was noted that the genesis of this ordinance was the number of vacant
         properties that are not being maintained . He stated that staff recommends approval of the proposed
         change and then responded to questions from Council members.

         The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Scott
                  Singer, to approve Ordinance No. 5341. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie,
                  Deputy Mayor Robert S. Weinroth, Council Member Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Jeremy
                  Rodgers, Council Member Scott Singer voting yes.
