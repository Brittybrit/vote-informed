         A.     Ordinance No. 5653
                An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton establishing procedures and regulations to implement
                Section 166.04151(7), Florida Statutes, as created by Chapter 2023-17, Laws of Florida (Live
                Local Act of 2023) for development of statutorily authorized mixed-use residential development
                (including affordable housing); noting the business and commercial, industrial, and
                miscellaneous zoning districts in which the mixed-use directive is applicable; providing land
                development regulations (including those generally applicable in multifamily zoning districts
                and providing an administrative approval process); noting the statutorily mandated maximums
                for density and height; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for
                codification; providing an effective date (AM-23-07) The public hearings on Items B. and B.1
                will be conducted contemporaneously, as the petitions are related. This is the second of two
                required public hearings on the ordinances in accordance with Chapter 163 and Chapter 166,
                Florida Statutes, respectively.

         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation . Mr. Schaad
         gave a brief overview of the Live Local Act (SB 102), the draft ordinance, the proposed changes to the
         Floor Area Ration (FAR), setbacks, and potential parking reduction. He continued by advising that
         both Planning & Zoning and staff recommended approval. Upon concluding, he made himself available
         for Members' questions. Discussion ensued and Mr. Schaad responded to questions and provided
         additional information.

         The public comment period was opened . Jonathan Ounjian spoke about the density requirements and
         questioned if the City is following SB 102 as it was intended. Seeing no one else come forward. the
         public comment period was closed.

         Discussion ensued .
