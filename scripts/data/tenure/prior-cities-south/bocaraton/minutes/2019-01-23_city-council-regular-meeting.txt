11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

                  Resolution No. 8-2019
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering an amendment to the conditional use approval
                  for the Jog Road Center, approved by Resolution No. 50-92 to increase the maximum restaurant
                   square footage from 3,000 square feet to approximately 5,160 square feet, to authorize
                  approximately 4,230 square feet of professional office space and approximately 3,000 square feet
                  of personal services space, and to decrease the maximum retail square footage from 20,304
                   square feet to approximately 11,710 square feet; for the Jog Road Center located at 2901 and
                  2911 Clint Moore Road; providing for repealer, providing an effective date {CA-91-13R1)
                           Resolution No. 8-2019

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
         made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.
         Tamashbeen Rahman, Senior Planner, gave a PowerPoint presentation beginning with a review of the
         petition and the location and zoning of the property. Background information was provided and the site
         plan was reviewed. Parking and traffic impacts were discussed. The Planning & Zoning Board
         recommended approval. Staff recommended approval as well. Upon concluding, Ms. Rahman responded
         to questions.

         speak, the public hearing was closed.

                       Monica Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 8-2019. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor

12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

                  Ordinance No. 5477
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the Boca Raton Downtown Development of
                  Regional Impact {DORI} Development Order to authorize the Executive Director to issue
                  administrative Individual Development Approvals for 5,000 square feet or less of development or
                  office equivalent development, as applicable, for: a new building or addition to an existing
                  building; a change of use; a conversion of uses within a subarea; or a transfer of uses between
                  subareas; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date {AM-18-09)
                           Ordinance No. 5477

         Tamashbeen Rahman, Senior Planner, again gave a PowerPoint presentation. Background information
         was reviewed and an analysis of the proposal was provided. The Planning & Zoning Board recommended
         approval, as did staff. Upon concluding, she made herself available for questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Robert Eisen thanked staff for bringing this ordinance forward. Seeing
         no one come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                       Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                       Member Andy Thomson, to approve Ordinance No. 5477 as revised.

         Members made final comments.

                       Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                       Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

20.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Mayotte announced the dates for the Oasis Championship and commented on the mural art at
Red Reef Park. She then commented on a resident's email regarding the Lake Worth Drainage District. The City
Manager provided information. Mayor Singer weighed in with regard to the Lake Worth Drainage District.

Council Member O'Rourke provided information on the art murals and tunnels at Spanish River Park that will be
painted. She then highlighted upcoming events Downtown

Mayor Singer commented on the retirement of Deputy City Clerk Diane King and highlighted the Wings of
Freedom program. He also announced an upcoming town hall that he will host.
                                                                                                               th
Mayor Singer then gave a presentation on his attendance at the Mayors' Institute on City Design at which the 20
Street and University district area was chosen for review by other mayors and professionals who shared their
input based on their experiences. Recommendations were made to enhance the public realm by creating a
walkable, attractive street. There were also recommendations for the FAU gateway and the private parcels along
the corridor. Members weighed in. Discussion followed with regard to next steps. Brandon Schaad, Development
Services Director, responded to questions and provided information. The City Manager noted that timing of
moving ahead may be affected by FAU's master planning. Discussion continued. In general, members seemed to
