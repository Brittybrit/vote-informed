12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A.       Ordinance No. 5461
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the City's Code of Ordinances to establish two
                  new zoning districts within Article XI, Business and Commercial Districts, Chapter 28, Zoning,
                  Code of Ordinances, by creating a new Division 11, "City CG Commercial General," and Division
                  12, "City CHO Commercial High Office," which districts incorporate Palm Beach County's
                  regulations (in effect on April 23. 2002) for the CG General Commercial district and CHO
                  Commercial High Office district, respectively. except as specified, and expressly providing a list of
                  permitted and conditional uses for the new districts; amending Section 28-301 (c), to include the
                  new districts; amending Section 28-307(1 ), to specify the building intensity; providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-16-
                  11)
                           Ordinance No. 5461

                  Ordinance No. 5462
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering rezoning property annexed into the City in
                  2003 (generally located south of Glades Road. east of St. Andrews Boulevard, north of the Lake
                  Worth Drainage District L-46 and L-47 Canals, and west of Interstate 95) consisting of (i) 215.02
                  acres (more or less) of property to be rezoned from CG General Commercial District pursuant to
                  the Palm Beach County Code to "City CG Commercial General." (a new City zoning district being
                  considered contemporaneously herewith), and (ii) 26.90 acres (more or less) of property to be
                  rezoned from CHO Commercial High Office District (formerly Palm Beach County CS Specialized
                  Commercial District) pursuant to the Palm Beach County Code to "City CHO Commercial High
                  Office" (a new City zoning district being considered contemporaneously herewith); providing for
                  revision of the Zoning District Map; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an
                  effective date (ZC-16-01)
                           Ordinance No. 5462

         Mayor Singer noted that the public hearings on items A and B would be conducted contemporaneously as
         the ordinances are related. A separate vote would be taken on each ordinance.

         Elizabeth Eassa, Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation, noting that the ordinances are not part of
         the Midtown Small Area Plan. Provisions of the ordinances were discussed, including creation of the CG
         and CHO zoning districts and corresponding regulations. Permitted and conditional uses were reviewed,
         as well as regulations for mall parking, which include a three-step process for calculating the parking rate.
         Building height and additional changes were covered. Staff recommended approval.

         The public hearing was opened. Glenn Gromann, representing property owners in the affected area,
         relayed concerns with components of the proposed ordinances, including parking, the County ULDC and
         the original annexation agreements. Mayor Singer asked staff to clarify points mentioned by Mr.
         Gromann. Deputy City Manager George Brown provided information regarding zoning designations,
         specifically CGSE (Commercial General Special Exception). Bonnie Miskell commented on behalf of the
         Simon Group, thanking staff for their work and requesting two changes regarding parking. Michael
         Marshall, Gunster Law Firm, suggested additional clarification to language in the ordinances. Mr. Brown
         and City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser provided information. Discussion ensued. Seeing no one else come
         forward, the public hearing was closed.

The meeting was recessed at approximately 7:38 pm and reconvened at approximately 7:49 pm.

         The City Attorney read new language into the record, identifying pages and line numbers in Ordinance
         No. 5461, which addressed the concerns expressed related to parking. She then addressed previous
         comments regarding the annexation agreements.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andy
                  Thomson, to approve Ordinance No. 5461 as revised and amended. Motion carried unanimously;
                  Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                  Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
