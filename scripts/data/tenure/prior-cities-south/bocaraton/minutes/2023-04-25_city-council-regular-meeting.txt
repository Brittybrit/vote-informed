         A       Ordinance No. 5644
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning, " Article XVI, "Off-Street
                 Parking and Loading," Code of Ordinances, to expand the uses required to provide electric
                 vehicle parking spaces (from multifamily residential, commercial and industrial uses, to
                 multifamily residential and all non-residential uses) and increase the number of electric vehicle
                 parking spaces required for such uses (from 2% to 5% of the total required off-street parking
                 spaces), decrease the development threshold at which the requirements for electric vehicle
                 parking spaces are imposed (from 50 total parking spaces to 20 total parking spaces), provide
                 definitions for "EV-capable," "EV-ready" and "EV-installed" parking spaces, and add
                 requirements for such spaces; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for
                 codification; providing an effective date (AM-22-07)

          Sustainability Manager Lindsay Nieratka gave a presentation. She provided a summary of the
          amendments to the Code of Ordinances. Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided
          additional insight to the requirements for modifying a property or change of use and bringing the EV
          requirements up to code. He continued by providing examples of recently constructed properties
          which are compliant with the proposed ordinance. Discussion ensued. Council Member Marc Wigder
          raised concerns over the change of use for a property and the types of electrical services that may
          be required for various types of vehicles and the related costs. City Manager Leif Ahnell and City
          Attorney Diana Grub Frieser provided additional information. Council Member Fran Nachlas
          discussed the costs involved with the retro fitting of properties and asked for language to be added
          to clarify that a request for change of use, without additional parking requirements, would not trigger
          Ordinance No. 5644 on existing buildings. Council Member Yvette Drucker agreed with the new EV
          construction but had concerns over the retrofitting for EV spaces. Further discussion ensued .

          The public hearing was opened. Jonathan Ounjian presented slides and spoke against the EV
          parking requirements. Seeing no one else come forward , the public hearing was closed.

          Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte spoke in favor of EV parking requirements. Discussion ensued.
          Brandon Schaad and Lindsay Nieratka responded to Members' comments and provided additional
          information.
