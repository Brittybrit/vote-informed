         The Mayor noted the public hearings on items 11. A., B., and C. would be conducted contemporaneously
         as the items are related, and a separate vote would be taken on each item. He noted that this was the
         second of two public hearings for Ordinance No. 5524. The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial
         procedures which would govern the public hearing. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who
         wished to speak. Members made their ex-parte disclosures.

         Heather Hansen, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. A summary of the proposed text
         amendment was reviewed, and potential impacts were discussed. Ms. Hansen noted that the proposed
         amendment is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan, and approval was recommended by both the
         Planning & Zoning Board and Development Services staff. A brief history of the Peninsula Corporate Center
         ORI was provided and the amendment was summarized. The site plan was displayed, and components of
         the plan were outlined. Ms. Hansen discussed traffic considerations, including, but not limited to, the total
         number of parking spaces, a nonconcurrent parking agreement with a neighboring property and nearby
         HOA/resident concerns regarding weekend and evening traffic. Proposed revisions to a condition for
         approval were displayed. Development Services staff recommended approval of the amendment and the
         revised site plan conditions. Upon concluding, Ms. Hansen made herself available for members' questions.
         Mayor Singer asked for a review of the proposed revisions to conditions. Ms. Hansen pointed out the
         proposed limitation to the number of special events per year. Ms. Miske! confirmed that the parking
         agreement would still be in effect if the property immediately to the west of the proposed development was
         sold. The City Attorney and Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided additional
         information.

         The public hearing was opened. Bonnie Miskel, on behalf of the applicant, gave a PowerPoint presentation.
         She highlighted the agreement with a neighboring property to provide additional parking if needed. A history
         of the subject property was discussed. Components of the ORI amendment and conditional use approval
         were reviewed. A prior existing site plan approval was displayed and contrasted to the proposed site plan.
         Elements of the facility's interior were shown. Components of the M-3 zoning designation were discussed,
         including, but not limited to, building height, parking spaces and setbacks. Illustrations of the proposed
         building, floorplan layout and landscape plans were also presented. Conditional use criteria were reviewed.
         Ms. Miske! then addressed resident concerns regarding traffic issues, and noted actions taken by the
         applicant to address parking concerns. Ms. Miske! highlighted the most recent proposed condition regarding
         limiting the number of special events and the length of each event and requested that it be changed to 3-
         day events as opposed to one day per event. Upon concluding, she responded to members' questions.
         Christopher Heggen provided additional information on behalf of the applicant regarding parking.

         The public comment period was opened. Teresa Noland expressed concerns with the proposed use of the
         property, especially during special events. Jessica Gray also shared her misgivings regarding spillover of
         traffic into the Central Park neighborhood and asked specifically for the installation of speed bumps and
         extra security during large events. Meredith Westheimer voiced her uneasiness with the potential increase
         in traffic. Leslie Turchon also expressed concerns. Seeing no one else indicate their desire to speak, the
         public hearing was closed. There was no cross examination or rebuttal. Heather Hansen commented on
         conditions of the Planning and Zoning Board regarding traffic calming devices and signage. Development
