 11.    REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A.     Ordinance No. 5645
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Article XV, Division 2,
                 "Prohibited uses in all districts," by deleting Section 28-1329, "Bulk Storage of Flammable
                 Liquids," Code of Ordinances in its entirety; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                 providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-22-04)

          Jim Bell, Long Range Project Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began by describing
          the purpose of the text amendment and provided background information. Mr. Bell described the
          origins and current requirements of this section of the Code. Staffs analysis of the Code section
          found that it was redundant and unnecessary as existing Fire and Environmental Protection
          regulations better addressed requirements. He advised that public notice obligations had been met
          and that the Planning and Zoning Board and staff both recommended approval. Upon concluding, he
          responded to Members' questions.

          The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward , closed.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                         Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5645. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                         Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                         Member Fran Nachlas, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

                 an effective date

 19.    MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

 Council Member Wigder gave a brief PowerPoint presentation. He began by noting the growth of the City and
 the diversity of local businesses. Mr. Wigder cited a travel guide's list of top activities and hotels within the
 City. He remarked on the hybridization of the workforce that had evolved over the past few years and
 emphasized a number of benefits that Boca Raton offered to workers, including the quality of life, local office
 options that facilitated collaborations and many local options for socializing. Tables were shown that displayed
 migration and relocation trends, highlighting the much larger number of those moving to Boca Raton as
 opposed to those leaving. Mr. Wigder reiterated the tremendous opportunities the City provides for both
 corporate and individual entities and the need for us to communicate our City's strengths. He encouraged
 residents to get involved and tell their Boca Story and concluded by reminding all that the celebration for the
 FAU Men's Basketball Team had been moved to April 19th.

 Council Member Drucker remarked on her recent visit to Tallahassee in support of Home Rule and advised
 that she would give a comprehensive update when the current legislative session concluded.

 Deputy Mayor Mayotte advised that filmmaker Rory Fielding, producer of the film Troubled Waters, had
 another movie on the subject of how plastics affect human health. Ms. Mayotte also highlighted the Great
 American Clean Up taking place at South Beach Park.

  Council Member Nachlas welcomed Council Member Wigder and thanked him for his presentation. Ms.
  Nachlas inquired about an update on reports by the advisory boards and noted that there was a bust of Ben
  Ferencz by artist Yaacov Heller available for viewing in the Royal Palm Shopping Plaza.
