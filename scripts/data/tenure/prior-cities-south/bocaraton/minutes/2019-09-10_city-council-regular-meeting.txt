          6,.     Ordinance No. 5497
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering a corrective universal conditional (UC)
                 rezoning, pursuant to Article VI, Chapter 23, Code of Ordinances, to affirm that Ordinance No. 5287
                 (UC-13-05) incorporated the approval, and conditions as appropriate, set forth in Ordinance No.
                 3798 (UC-89-05), and that the conditions imposed by Ordinance No. 3798 were repealed by
                 Ordinance No. 5287, for the property located at 331-351 East Yamato Road; providing for repealer;
                 providing an effective date (UC-13-05R1/ZC)

        The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the hearing. Members made
        their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

        Jim Bell, Development Services Long Range Planning Director, gave a PowerPoint presentation. Details
        of the request for corrective rezoning were reviewed, including the rezoning, conditional use approval and
        site plan amendment approval. The current and proposed site plan were displayed. Results of the traffic
        study were provided, and staff analysis of the proposed changes was discussed. The Planning and Zoning
        Board recommended approval. Staff recommended approval. Upon conclusion, Mr. Bell responded to
        members' questions.

        The public hearing was opened. Doug Mummaw, on behalf of the applicant, gave a brief PowerPoint
        presentation. The existing site plan was reviewed along with photographs of the existing storefronts.
        Requested zoning changes were discussed, as well as the applicants proposed enhancements to
        landscaping. Mr. Mummaw responded to members' questions and comments. Seeing no one else come
        forward, the public hearing was closed.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke, to approve Ordinance No. 5497. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,
                  Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

      restructuring the fee assessment. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

 17.      QUASI-JUDICIAL PUBLIC HEARINGS/VARIANCES & APPEALS:

           6-      Resolution No. 107-2019
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the appeal of the decision of
                  the Community Appearance Board denying Bauman Medical Group's requested wall sign (Permit
                  No. 19-4545) located at 1450 South Dixie Highway; providing for repealer; providing an effective
                  date

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the hearing. Members made
         their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

         Wanda Sanders, Zoning Officer, gave a PowerPoint presentation. Background information of the appeal
         was provided, including photographs of the proposed signs. The Community Appearance Board (CAB)
         decision was reviewed, and the appellants argument was presented. Analysis of CAB design criteria was
         discussed. She then made herself available for questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Matthew Boone presented on behalf of the applicant. Photos of the building
         with renderings of the proposed sign were provided. Landscaping considerations were mentioned. Dr. Alan
         Bauman addressed the council and asked that the appeal be granted. Glenn Gromann commented in
         support of the applicant. There was no cross-examination or rebuttal. The public hearing was closed.

         Members made additional comments. Discussion ensued. Development Services Director Brandon Schaad
         responded to members' questions and provided additional information.

                   Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member
                   Monica Mayotte, to reverse the decision of the Community Appearance Board and approve
                   Resolution No. 107-2019 to grant the appeal with modifications. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                   Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member
                   Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
