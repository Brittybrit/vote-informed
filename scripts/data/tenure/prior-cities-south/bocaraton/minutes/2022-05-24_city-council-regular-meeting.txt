  11.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

           A.      Ordinance No. 5614
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering revisions to Policy LU.1.1.6. of the Future
                  Land Use Element of the City's Comprehensive Plan to: (i) remove double asterisks regarding
                  density limits for convalescent homes, nursing homes, assisted living facilities, hospitals, and
                  similar uses (which double asterisks were added in July 2020 during the City's statutorily­
                  required evaluation and appraisal process); (ii) remove single asterisks regarding density limits
                  for hotels; and for both matters, retaining the purpose of the asterisks in a note; providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-22-05)

          Mayor Singer noted that this was a transmittal hearing and the first of two public hearings. Three votes
          would be required to transmit.

          Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He stated that
          the proposed ordinance was a new version of Ordinance No. 5610 which had been introduced at a
          prior meeting. Mr. Schaad explained that it was a clarification of the Future Land Use Element of the
          City's Comprehensive Plan and briefly reviewed the background. He noted that prior amendments to
          the Comprehensive Plan and the Future Land Use Element had been adopted in 2020, and that the
          current revisions were being proposed to provide clarification and avoid confusion for future land use
          designations. The Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval on a 5 to 2 vote and staff
          recommended approval. Upon concluding, Mr. Schaad made himself available for Members' questions.

          The public hearing was opened. Joan Snow, Bill Snow, Gene Hodges, Nicole Droz, Kathy Gilio, Stacy
          Sipple, Christine Lyons, and Christine Allen spoke in favor of Ordinance 5614 and voiced concerns
          regarding an assisted living facility (ALF) proposed for SW 12 Avenue. Gene Hodges, Barbara
          Caccavale-Hodges, Ruth Ronin, Brian Clinton, Ken Deeken, Holli Sutton, Shane Kittleson, and Lara
          Kittelson expressed their support of Ordinance No. 5614. Seeing no one else come forward, the public
          hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                          Andrea Levine O'Rourke, to transmit Ordinance No. 5614.

          Council Member Thomson expressed his support for the ordinance. Council Member Mayotte stated
          that she felt the vote should be postponed until the outcome of related pending litigation was decided.

                          Motion carried four votes to one; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine
                          O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes;
                          Council Member Monica Mayotte voting no.

          Mayor Singer repeated that the vote was for transmittal of the ordinance to the State for review.

           B.     Resolution No. 40-2022

                  Services User Fee Schedule to set forth fees for the prorated boat launching permit upon the
                  opening of Silver Palm Park; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an
                  effective date

          rates were approved by the Financial Advisory Board. Upon concluding, Mr. Kalvort responded to

          The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed.
