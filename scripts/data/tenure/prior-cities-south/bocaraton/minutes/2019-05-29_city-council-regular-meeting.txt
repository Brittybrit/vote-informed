       9.C.     Resolution No.66-2019
                A resolution of the City of Boca Raton authorizing the Mayor and City Clerk to execute an
                Exclusive Franchise Agreement with Waste Pro of Florida, Inc. for the purpose of providing
                services for the collection of residential waste and recyclable materials; providing for
                severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

       Assistant City Manager Mike Woika gave the presentation, noting that projections had been extended out
       20 years as requested. Additional information was provided in response to questions from the May 28 th
       Workshop. Mr. Woika made himself available for questions and provided a review of the prior day's
       presentation . Municipal Services Director Bob DiChristopher and Mr. Woika responded to Council
       members' questions.

       The public hearing was opened. Cormac Conahan, Michele Damore, James Bonfiglio, Arthur Jacobi, lmran
       Siddiqui, and Steve Griffith commented in favor of the City retaining sanitation services. City employees
       Anthony Herring, Leni Moore, Appolonce Bernadin, Maurice Spates spoke against outsourcing . Chuck
       Ridley, Afifa Khaliq, Marcus Dixon and Rodrigo Hidalgo, on behalf of FPSU and SEIU, encouraged Council
       to keep sanitation services in-house. Carrie Bachner Mullins suggested the City create an advisory board
       to meet the needs of veterans. Mindy Koch inquired about recycling rebates; Bob DiChristopher responded.
       Russel Matthew, Regional Vice President of Waste Pro, spoke on behalf of the company's desire to work
       with the City. David Dee, outside counsel for the City, commented on the proposed contract. Seeing no one
       else come forward, the public comment period was closed.

        Members made final comments in support of keeping residential trash collection an in-house service.

                            Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                            Rodgers, to table Resolution No. 66-2019. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,
                            Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member
                            Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes

 11.    QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A.         Resolution No. 64-2019
                    A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a Planned Mobility Development (PMD) Master
                    Plan and Site Plan to authorize demolition of an approximately 98,000 square foot office building
                    and construction of a 297-unit multifamily residential project generally located at 5400 Broken
                    Sound Boulevard and incorporation of an existing, approximately 59,800 square foot office building
                    at 5300 Broken Sound Boulevard; considering three (3) modifications of development parameters,
                    pursuant to Section 28-1843 and Section 28-1720 (1), Code of Ordinances, to eliminate required
                    planted buffers along sidewalks and to eliminate required street trees within planted buffers: (i)
                    along sidewalks at a maximum distance of 35 feet on center, and (ii) along portions of roadways
                    internal to the property; considering the abandonment of a portion of a platted public utility
                    easement; considering a waiver of plat requirements; providing for repealer; providing an effective
                    date (PMD-18-02)

        The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
        made ex-parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

  Regular Minutes                                          3                                                May 29, 2019
      Tamash Rahman, Chief Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Elements of the existing Master Plan
      and the proposed modifications were presented. The Community Appearance Board recommended
      approval. It was noted that the project meets the mobility objectives of the Comprehensive Plan. The
      applicant's requests to waive platting requirements and abandon a platted utility easement were discussed.
      Recent updates to FPL setback guidelines necessitated modifications to landscape plans LP-01, LP-02,
      LP-03 and LP-09, and Ms. Rahman stated that staff supported the revisions. The Planning and Zoning
      Board recommended approval. City staff recommended approval with the accompanying conditions of
      approval and modified landscape plans. She then made herself available for questions.

      The public hearing was opened. Juan Caycedo, architect of the project, gave a brief presentation
      highlighting architectural features of the proposed project. No one else came forward . There was no cross
      examination or rebuttal. The public hearing was closed.

      John Donaldson, traffic engineer, Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, and Maria Tejera,
      Traffic Engineer, responded to members' questions. Council Member Thomson moved , and Deputy Mayor
      Rodgers seconded, approval of the resolution. The City Attorney provided clarification regarding inclusion
      of replacement landscape sheets LP-01, LP-02, LP-03 and LP-09 as an amendment to the resolution, to
      which both members agreed.

                   Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                   Rodgers, to approve Resolution No. 64-2019 as amended.

      Members made final comments.

                   Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member
                   Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting
                   yes.

 20.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member O'Rourke commented on the tunnel mural project at Spanish River Park and noted that some local
businesses had stopped providing plastic straws. She then highlighted upcoming events in Mizner Park.

Council Member Thomson also commented on the Spanish River Park tunnel mural project.

Mayor Singer complimented local businesses on their transitioning from providing straws. He offered several
suggestions for wording changes to the Strategic Planning goals and priorities. Mayor Singer then asked members
for their input regarding the Boca National Golf Course. Discussion ensued. Mr. Brown advised that the City would
need to conduct a comprehensive evaluation of the District's updated proposal and he reminded members that
there is currently an agreement with the District for their agency to develop the property. Ms. Freiser clarified the
differences in governance and responsibilities between the two bodies. Further discussion ensued. Mayor Singer
expressed the Council's desire to work with the District.

The Strategic Planning priorities were reviewed, and members top priorities tallied . Discussion ensued. Deputy
Mayor Rodgers suggested the results be summarized with three top priorities and five medium priorities. The Deputy
City Manager noted that items not designated as top priorities would still be worked on by City staff; he advised that
a report with the revised set of priorities would be presented at the next meeting.
