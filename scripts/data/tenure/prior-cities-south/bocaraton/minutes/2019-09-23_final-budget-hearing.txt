 3.     REBUDGETING OF FY 2018 - 2019 BUDGET APPROPRIATIONS

Mayor Singer reviewed the purpose of the meeting, noting that rebudgeting would be considered during the public

 4.     PUBLIC HEARING TO FINALIZE THE BUDGET AND ADOPT MILLAGE RATES

         A.     Ordinance No. 5501                (City Clerk reads in full.)

       An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton adopting final millage rates and levying ad valorem taxes for the
       fiscal year beginning October 1, 2019 and ending September 30, 2020; stating the percentage by which
       the millage to be levied exceeds the "rolled-back rate"; directing the City Manager or the Office of
       Management and Budget Director to adjust the adopted millage rates in the event of changes in the
       assessment roll and taxable value; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective
       date

         B.     Ordinance No. 5502                (City Clerk reads title.)

       An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton adopting a final budget and appropriating funds for the fiscal year
       beginning October 1, 2019 and ending September 30, 2020; providing for severability; providing for
       repealer; providing an effective date

       The City Clerk read Ordinance No. 5501 in full and the title of Ordinance No. 5502.

       City Manager Leif Ahnell gave a PowerPoint presentation on the 2019-2020 budget. Topics included, but
       were not limited to, a review of the City's mission, vision, and guiding principles; the policy agenda
       priorities for2019-20; millage and property tax comparison with 2018-19; and assessed property
       valuations over the last 10 years. He then identified the increases or decreases in general operating
       expenses and reviewed the new personnel and programs proposed for the coming year. Various charts
       and graphs were presented illustrating favorable comparisons of Boca Raton to other cities' fire
       assessment fees, property tax rates and water/sewer rates. The increase in the City's sanitation fees was
       noted. Sharon McGuire, Office of Management and Budget Director, then briefly discussed the Capital
       Improvements Program after which she reviewed Exhibits 1 - 3 identifying the rebudgeted items that will
       be carried over to the 2019-20 fiscal year budget. Discussion ensued regarding projects that were not
       approved for funding by the Greater Boca Raton Beach and Park District (BPD). Upon conclusion, Ms.
       McGuire responded to questions. Mr. Ahnell provided additional information.

        Jean-Michel Neault, Compliance Auditor, gave a PowerPoint presentation with a brief overview of the
        proposed changes to the user fee schedule for the coming year (Resolution No. 113-2019). Upon
        conclusion, he made himself available for questions.
  C.      Open public hearing on final budget and final millage rates

Rita Thrasher, representing Boca Raton's Promise, requested funding for mental health programs. She
then responded to questions.

Benjamin Leis, Director of ComicCure, requested funding for the Flamingo Comedy Festival. He then
responded to questions.

  D.      Discussion and explanation of percentage increase in millage over the rolled-back rate
          necessary to fund the budget

  E.      Public comment

There was no further public comment.

  F.      Close public comment

The public comment period was closed.

  G.      City Council shall amend the adopted tentative budget as it sees fit

Referring to Exhibit 4, Deputy Mayor Rodgers asked for an amount to be dedicated to the FAU Research
Park for the next one to two years and thought it should be allocated from the economic development
fund. Council Member Mayotte concurred. Mr. Ahnell provided information. Council members
commented. Andrew Duffel, FAU Research Park, responded to Council members' questions. There was
consensus to include a $100,000 increase.

Council Member O'Rourke discussed the amount she requested for the 'Turtle' Project. There was
consensus to approve the proposed amount. She then asked for funding for the Flamingo Comedy
Festival, which would fund the beginning of this proposed annual event. The City Manager provided
additional information on Economic Development funding. Lengthy discussion ensued. Generally,
Council members expressed concern with the large amount of funding for a for-profit company. It was
suggested that the City become a sponsor or underwrite a specific portion. Mr. Leis provided additional
information and responded to questions. Deputy Mayor Rodgers recommended deferring the request
until after the inaugural Festival in order to make a better determination in 2020. Members agreed.

 Mayor Singer commented on the Non-Profit Funding Requests process. Working from the Mayor's list,
 individual requests were discussed, and changes were agreed to. Discussion ensued on reviewing the
 process going forward. The City Manager recapped the adjustments that were made resulting in an
 increase of $13,000 to the total amount of non-profit funding proposed by the Mayor.

          Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member Andrea
          Levine O'Rourke, to amend the budget to include the FY2019 Non-Profit Organization Funding
          Requests and the additions to Exhibit 4 as stated. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott
          Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member
          Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

 The City Manager suggested voting first on Exhibit 1 as amended.

          Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Monica
          Mayotte, to amend Exhibit 1, Amendments to the FY 2019-20 Proposed Operating Budget, to
          include $185,000 for park restroom renovations eliminated from the BPD budget.

 Discussion ensued regarding the City absorbing costs for projects previously committed to by the BPD
