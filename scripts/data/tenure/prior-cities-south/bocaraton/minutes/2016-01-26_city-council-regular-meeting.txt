12.       REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:


           A.       Ordinance No. 5340

                     An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Section 3. 04, of the City of Boca Raton Charter
                     to establish new annual salaries for the Mayor and members of the City Council; providing that
                     the annual salary of the Mayor shall be 40% of the salary of a member of the Palm Beach County
                     Board of County Commissioners ( 40% of a County Commissioner' s salary is currently
                         38, 355. 20); providing   that the annual salary of the other Council   Members   shall be 30%   of the

                     salary of a member of the Palm Beach County Board of County Commissioners ( 30% of a County
                     Commissioner' s salary is currently $ 28, 766. 40); providing that the annual salaries for the Mayor
                     and members of the City Council shall be increased each year by the increase, if any, in the
                     salary of the members of the Palm Beach County Board of County Commissioners; providing for
                     the submission of the proposed Charter Amendment to the electors of the City at a special
                     election to be held on August 30, 2016 ( the date of the Primary Election); repealing Section 2- 26,
                     Code of Ordinances, which provides for annual salaries; providing for severability; providing for
                     repealer; providing for revision to the City Charter and for codification in the City Code of
                     Ordinances; providing an effective date
                                Ordinance No. 5340


          City Manager Leif Ahnell noted that this ordinance was consistent with the direction provided by Council.

          The public hearing was opened. John Gore offered suggestions that he felt would improve the ordinance.
          Al Zucaro stated his opposition to the ordinance and offered a number of suggestions. James Hendrey
          commented on the proposed salary increase and made several suggestions. Seeing no one else come
          forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

            A.       Discussion of City Council Summer Schedule

            City Manager Ahnell sought direction from Council. Members agreed to cancel the City Council workshop

19.       CITY ATTORNEY         REPORTS:


City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser gave a brief report on Airport Authority appointments regarding unresolved
issues as to who can serve on the Authority and what relationships could rise to the level of a prohibitive conflict.
Ms. Frieser suggested that any applicant be asked to complete a supplemental disclosure of any potential
conflicts    that could affect their service. She sought direction     on how to proceed.        In light of the Commission       on
Ethics inaction with regard to this issue, Deputy Mayor Weinroth suggested that a court be asked to opine on the
matter.     Discussion ensued.       Members   agreed   it was appropriate   that a disclosure   of this information   be provided
to any applicant to the Airport Authority and that an applicant acknowledge any relationship that could potentially
pose a conflict.
