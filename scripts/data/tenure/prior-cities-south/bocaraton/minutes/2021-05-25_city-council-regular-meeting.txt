           The Mayor noted that the public hearings on items 11. A., B., C., D., E., F., and G. would be conducted
           contemporaneously as the items were related, and Ordinance No. 5537 would require four votes for
           passage. A separate vote would be taken on each item if the prior item were approved. The City Attorney
           reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing. Members made their ex­
           parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

           Tamash Rahman, Chief Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began with a review of the
           applicant's request, including, but not limited to, a proposed amendment to the Future Land Use Map, a
           request for rezoning, a conditional use approval and a site plan amendment. Aerial maps were displayed,
           and information about the proposed future land use designation was provided. Analysis of the rezoning
           component was examined, and the traffic study results were presented. Ms. Rahman then explained that
           Staff considered the proposed land use designation to be unreasonable, and the proposed rezoning was
           determined to be incompatible with the City's Comprehensive Plan. The proposed master plan and site
           plan were discussed next, which included suggested improvements and site design components by the
           applicant. Staff did not support the proposed master plan. There were no staff objections to the proposed
           modifications to development parameters, as the relocation of the building closer to the street would be
           expected to promote pedestrian activity. Staff's response to components of the applicant's presentation
           at the December 8, 2020 transmittal hearing was provided. The Planning and Zoning Board
           recommended approval with two revisions, which were agreed to by the applicant and incorporated into
           the site plan. Ms. Rahman concluded by reviewing Staff's recommendation of denial due to the proposed
           change of the land use designation, the inconsistency of the proposed rezoning with the City's
           Comprehensive Plan, and objections to the Master Plan and technical deviation requests. Upon
           concluding, she made herself available for members' questions.

          Bonnie Miskel, on behalf of the applicant, gave a PowerPoint presentation. She began by displaying an
          aerial map of the subject property, noting the existing condition of the subject parcels and the surrounding
          neighborhoods. Ms. Miskel shared a chart and quotes from local news sources to demonstrate the need
          for additional housing for FAU students. A map of student housing developments within the City was
          shown, and a map outlining RH land use designations within the City was presented. John Donaldson,
          Traffic Engineer for the project, provided additional information for the justification of the future land use
          map amendment and rezoning proposals. Ms. Miskel then pointed out components added to the
          proposed site plan by the designers and landscape architects. Drawings of the proposed building
          elevations were shown, as were results of a poll administered by the applicant to City residents which
          showed community support for additional campus-adjacent housing for FAU students. Ms. Miskel
          concluded by outlining commitments to the community, which included a pedestrian bridge to campus,
          24/7 security and shuttle service. Upon concluding, she responded to members' comments.

           The public hearing was opened. Charman Postel, Chris Krebs, Jon Carter, Michael Cairo, Nicholas
           Bamberg, Ryan Rossi, Tykeem McCord, Antonia Frenot, Brian Norton, Briana Moffatt, Kevin Buchanan,
           Felipe Mora, Joseph Justice, Andrea Virgin, Glenn Gromann, Maxwell Simonson, Alan Feinberg, Jr.,
           Michael McGovern, and Brad Busscher expressed their support for the proposed project, citing the need
           for student housing close to the University campus. Sally Celano, property manager of the Windwood
           community, expressed concerns with the proposed rezoning from low to high residential. Kathleen
           MacDonald, Lorena Villegas, Margarita Pazmino, Xianyu Wu, and Renata Pawlowska also shared their
           opposition to proposed rezoning of the property. Cristina Escalante inquired whether the units would be
           available only to student residents. The City Clerk read comments submitted by William Dickey, Marcus
           Wood, and William Giffin, expressing their concerns with the proposed development. Seeing no one else
           indicate their desire to speak, both online and in-person, the public hearing was closed. Tamash Rahman,
           Chief Planner, provided additional information in response to questions posed during the public
           comments and by Council Members. On behalf of the applicant, Bonnie Miskel responded to concerns
           expressed during the public comment period.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member Andy
                         Thomson, to approve Ordinance No. 5537.

           Council Member Thomson addressed concerns expressed by residents during the public hearing, citing
           the provisions being offered by the applicant to alleviate traffic concerns and the need for additional
           student housing. Council Member Mayotte thanked all those that communicated their positions to Council
           and commented on the demand for student housing, noting that the proposed project could alleviate the
           lack of available single-family homes which were currently occupied by students during school semesters.
           Council Member Drucker echoed comments made by Council Members Thomson and Mayotte, noting
           that FAU was no longer a commuter college and that she recognized concerns about security and
           increased traffic. Deputy Mayor O'Rourke introduced the subject of the 20 th Street Corridor and asked for
           member's input. Discussion ensued. Ms. O'Rourke thanked everyone from the public who commented
           and expressed her desire to see additional student housing with lower density than the proposed project.
           Further discussion ensued. Mayor Singer thanked the developer for their efforts to address concerns that
           had been raised and encouraged FAU to continue to build additional on-campus housing. He concurred
           with members that the 20th Street Corridor would need to be made a priority.

                          Motion failed on a 3 to 2 vote: Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica
                          Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes; Mayor Scott Singer and Deputy
                          Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke voting no.
