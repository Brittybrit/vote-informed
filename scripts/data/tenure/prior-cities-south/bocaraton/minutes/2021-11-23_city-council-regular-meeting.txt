12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A       Ordinance No. 5603
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton providing for the vacation and abandonment of a platted
                  utility easement, and approving and accepting a utility easement deed from Bocar Master
                  Association, for upgraded infrastructure for Well 35W, over certain lands generally located at
                  3201 Clint Moore Road; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective
                  date (EA-21-04)

         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave a brief PowerPoint presentation. An aerial
         photograph of the subject area was displayed. Mr. Schaad explained that the City has plans to upgrade
         a raw water well at the site and the boundaries of the existing easement need to be shifted to
         accommodate the improvements. Staff recommends approval. Upon concluding, he made himself
         available for Members' questions.

        The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed.

                           Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member
                           Andy Thomson, to approve Ordinance No. 5603. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                           Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                           Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

18.     CITY MANAGER RECOMMENDATIONS AND REPORTS:

 Acting City Manager George Brown requested approval of an overlap of advertising for a Coastal Construction
 Control Line (CCCL) variance request. The application received from the condominium at 3000 S. Ocean
 Boulevard was a request for a variance as part of a proposed seawall repair. The item is required to be heard
 by the Environmental Advisory Board, the Planning and Zoning Board and the City Council. There was
 consensus among members to allow the expedited advertising.

19.      CITY ATTORNEY REPORTS:

The City Attorney had no report.

20.      MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Deputy Mayor O'Rourke suggested inviting the Director and Chair of the Boca Raton Housing Authority to give
a short presentation at a City Council Workshop. Members agreed. She then commented on Chair Mayotte's
presentation at the prior day's CRA meeting regarding economic development. Ms. O'Rourke offered to repeat
her Goal Setting presentation of a proposed redesign of Palmetto Park Road and the Downtown in order to
create better connectivity and walkability. Members weighed in and discussion ensued. Lastly, she
congratulated Deputy City Manager George Brown on his induction into the Walk of Recognition by the Boca
Raton Historical Society and noted his 44 years of service to the City. Mayor Singer congratulated Mr. Brown
and acknowledged Robert Rollins on his induction into the Historical Society's Walk of Recognition.

Council Member Drucker wished everyone a Happy Thanksgiving and let all those listening know that she
hears their concerns and appreciates their communication on important issues.

Council Member Mayotte commented on the popularity of the course at the Boca Raton Golf & Racquet Club
and wished everyone a Happy Thanksgiving.

Mayor Singer remarked on recent dialogue he had with interested parties regarding the Dixie Manor
community and encouraged additional public conversation to get the facts out to residents. He thanked
congregations from B'nai Israel and Ebenezer Baptist Church for their food distribution in advance of the
holidays and commented on the success of the tree lighting ceremony at Mizner Park. The Mayor highlighted
upcoming events and thanked both Municipal Services and Police Services staff for the extra measures taken
to keep event attendees safe. He concluded by wishing all a Happy Thanksgiving and Happy Hanukkah.

 21.     ADJOURNMENT:
