                                  MINUTES OF THE SPECIAL MEETING
                                            CITY COUNCIL
                                    CITY OF BOCA RATON , FLORIDA
                                      TUESDAY, AUGUST 12, 2025
                                               8:45 AM

The Special Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton , Florida was held in the Auditorium at 6500
          Congress Avenue. The meeting was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at 8:45 AM .

1.     CALL TO ORDER:

2.     PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG :

3.     ROLL CALL:

       Mayor Scott Singer
       Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas
       Council Member Yvette Drucker
       Council Member Andy Thomson
       Council Member Marc Wigder

4.      CITY ATTORNEY APPLICANT INTERVIEWS:

       City Manager George Brown gave a brief summary of the purpose for the meeting and introduced recruiter
       David Deutsch . Mayor Scott Singer summarized the objective for the day.

       An opportunity was provided for the public to speak. Seeing no one come forward , the public comment
       period was closed .

       Discussion ensued regarding the questions that would be asked during the interviews. Members identified
       seven questions to ask each candidate and the interviews began .

       Jorge Camejo offered a brief introduction and was interviewed by Council Members.

The meeting recessed at 10:10 am and resumed at 10:20 am.

       Mark Sohaney offered a brief introduction and was interviewed by Council Members.

The meeting recessed at 11 :15 am and resumed at 11 :23 am.

       Patrick Jordan offered a brief introduction and was interviewed by Council Members.

The meeting recessed for lunch at 12:09 pm and resumed at 1:00 pm.

       Jim Zervis offered a brief introduction and was interviewed by Council Members.

The meeting recessed at 2:00 pm and resumed at 2:14 pm.

       Stan Morris offered a brief introduction and was interviewed by Council Members.

The meeting recessed at 3:03 pm and resumed at 3:16 pm .

       Mayor Singer expressed his appreciation to Mr. Brown for his decades of service and thanked David
       Deutsch for his assistance with the recruiting process. Mayor Singer then thanked all of the applicants,
       praised their accomplishments, recognized the service of those applicants who are City employees,
       and noted the strength of the applicant pool.

                            Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Yvette
                            Drucker, to appoint Mark Sohaney to the position of City Manager.

                            Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson to appoint Jim Zervis to the
                            position of City Manager.

             The Mayor and all Council Members expressed gratitude to the applicants and appreciation for the
             service of the internal applicants. Mayor Singer offered his services as a contract talks facilitator to
             work with the City Attorney on terms of the new City Manager contract, subject to final approval by
             the Council. There was consensus from Members to have the Mayor work with the attorney as
             facilitator, and that, within a year, the new City Manager move to within , or 5 miles from , the City
             limits.

                            Motion to appoint Mark Sohaney to the position of City Manager, with the condition that
                            the City Manager live within 5 miles of the City limits of Boca Raton, passed
                            unanimously on a voice vote.

             Discussion ensued regarding terms of the contract and Mr. Koehler advised that Mr. Brown's
             employment contract was still in effect. Mr. Brown noted that he intended to work until January 4,
             2026 , but that he was willing to work on the transition period desired by Council. He advised that a
             contract for the new City Manager could be ready for consideration at the September 9th City Council
             meeting.

5.       CITY MANAGER REPORTS:

Mr. Brown thanked Mr. Deutsch for his work on the recruitment and selection process and assured Members
that there would be a smooth transition to the next City Manager.

6.       ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 5: 11 pm.
