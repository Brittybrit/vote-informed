             A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a site plan, pursuant to a universal conditional

             from Section 28-1653(1 ), Code of Ordinances, to reduce required dimensions of parking spaces,
             for the Yamato Office Park generally located at 276 and 298 West Yamato Road, subject to

   Mayor Singer noted that the public hearings on Items 11. A., B., C., and D. would be conducted
   contemporaneously as the items were related. A separate vote would taken on each ordinance and
   resolution, and Ordinance No. 5535 would require four votes for passage. The City Attorney reviewed the
   quasi-judicial procedures. Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath
   to those who wished to speak.

   Jim Bell, Long Range Project Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began with a review of the
   request components, which included a proposed future land use change, a request for rezoning, the
   tentative plat and proposed site plan. An aerial view of the subject property was shown. Mr. Bell discussed
   staff concerns over the proposed future land use change and cited issues presented by the adjacent
   orphaned parcel and backout driveway access. Staff did not support the future land use change, as it was
   inconsistent with the Comprehensive Plan. The proposed rezoning was not complementary to the remaining
   parcels. He then outlined details of the site plan. It was noted that the applicant requested one technical
   deviation regarding parking space size, which staff supported. The application complied with land
   development regulations for the proposed office building. Development Services staff does not support the
   application in its current form. Mr. Bell advised Council that should they approve the project, staff would
   then encourage approval of the additional ordinance and two resolutions that would provide conditions.
   Upon concluding, he responded to members' questions.

    The public hearing was opened. Juan Caycedo, on behalf of the applicant, gave a PowerPoint presentation.
    He began with an aerial view of the property and an illustration of the proposed site plan. Mr. Caycedo
    proposed options for the entrance and exit of the adjacent vacant lot. Upon concluding, Mr. Caycedo made
    himself available for members' questions. Rochelle Lecavalier, one of the owners of the subject property,
    explained her outreach to the owner of the adjacent parcel. Alan Klasfeld, representing the owners of the
    adjacent property, commented on past contact with the applicant and expressed his opposition to the
    project. Glenn Gromann shared his support of staff's assessment of the project but conveyed his desire to
    see the property developed in some way. There was no cross examination or rebuttal. Ms. Lecavalier and
    Mr. Caycedo offered final comments. Seeing no one else indicate their desire to speak, the public hearing
    was closed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Mayor Scott Singer to
                  approve Ordinance No. 5535.


    Jim Bell and Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided additional information. Discussion
    ensued. Both property owners indicated their desire to communicate about the project. Mayor Singer asked

    Attorney provided information.

                   An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 23, "Planning and Development," Article
                   IV, Division 4, "Sidewalks and Bikeways," Section 23-162, "Sidewalk Policy," Code of Ordinances,
                   to clarify the requirement to construct new sidewalks and/or expand existing sidewalks as part of a
                   redevelopment; and to exclude the south side of Yamato Road (between Military Trail and Saint
                   Andrews Boulevard only) from the eight foot minimum sidewalk width requirement; amending
                   Sections 23-161 through 23-165 to update terminology and to delete, revise and/or restate certain
                   definitions and terms; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification;
                   providing an effective date (AM-20-04)
      Regular Minutes                                          6                                       September 9, 2020
   Osny Jean, Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Background of similar text amendments brought
   before prior Councils were reviewed. Mr. Jean then provided a summary of the changes to the code
   language in the proposed amendment. Aerial views were displayed of locations in the City where 8'
   sidewalks had been installed. The Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval of the ordinance.
   Upon concluding, he made himself available for members' questions. The City Manager provided
   clarification and additional information. Discussion ensued. Mr. Jean, Mr. Ahnell and Mr. Schaad responded
   to members' questions.

   The public hearing was opened. Neil Schiller, on behalf of B'nai Israel, gave a short PowerPoint
   presentation in support of the ordinance. Glenn Gromann noted that while the sidewalk requirements may
   inversely impact non-profit organizations, creating special dispensations for a limited group of property
   owners may create a liability for the City. Seeing no one else indicate their desire to speak, the public
   hearing was closed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Thomson, Seconded by Mayor Singer, to approve
                  Ordinance No. 5532.

   Council Member Thomson expressed his support for the ordinance. He noted the low amount of pedestrian
   traffic in the area and shared his concern that the requirement places a burden on religious non-profit
   institutions. Council Member Mayotte stated her opposition to the ordinance, as did Deputy Mayor
   O'Rourke, citing their desire to meet walkability goals and enhance pedestrian safety. City Manager Ahnell
   clarified the cost estimate provided by staff for sidewalk extension. Mr. Ahnell advised that, should the City
   undertake the replacement of the sidewalks, ownership of the sidewalk would need to be transferred to the
   City from affected property owners. The City Attorney and City Manager provided additional information.
   Discussion ensued. The City Attorney confirmed that any construction within the City was required to meet
   code requirements. She then read new language into the record to delete the exclusion of the Yamato area
   from the 8' sidewalk requirement. The changes were confirmed by Mr. Schaad.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member
                  Monica Mayotte, to amend the motion on the floor to remove the language excluding the south
                  side of Yamato Road from the sidewalk requirement. Motion carried on a 3 to 1 vote; Mayor
                  Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Monica Mayotte voting
                  yes; Council Member Andy Thomson voting no.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Mayor Scott Singer, to
                  approve Ordinance No. 5532 as amended. Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine
                  O'Rourke, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
