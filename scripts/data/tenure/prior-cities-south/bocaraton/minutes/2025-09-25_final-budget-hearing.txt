                                MINUTES OF THE FINAL BUDGET HEARING
                                             CITY COUNCIL
                                     CITY OF BOCA RATON , FLORIDA
                                    THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 25, 2025
                                                6:00 PM

     The Final Budget Hearing of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton , Florida was held in the Auditorium
           at 6500 Congress Avenue. The meeting was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at 6:00 PM.

1.        PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

2.        ROLL CALL:

          Mayor Scott Singer
          Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas
          Council Member Yvette Drucker
          Council Member Andy Thomson
          Council Member Marc Wigder

3.        REBUDGETING OF FY 2024 - 2025 BUDGET APPROPRIATIONS:

Mayor Singer made opening remarks and discussed the purpose for the meeting .

Senior Budget Analyst Michael Middleman and Deputy City Manager/CFO James Zervis gave the
presentation. Mr. Middleman began with a brief overview of the Mission , Vision, and Values of the City. The
City's Strategic Focus Areas and Priorities were reviewed , and a comparison of millage, property tax, and fire
assessment of the last year and current year were displayed and shown in contrast to those of nearby cities.
Mr. Middleman reviewed the rebudgets proposed for new positions and projects for the upcoming fiscal year,
and he outlined operating costs by department. Mr. Zervis clarified the exhibits provided and noted that
Exhibit 4 was additional funding and revisions requested by Council Members . Upon concluding , Mr.
Middleman , Mr. Zervis, and City Manager George Brown responded to questions and provided additional
information .

4.        PUBLIC HEARING TO FINALIZE THE BUDGET AND ADOPT MILLAGE RATES:

           A.      Ordinance No. 5751
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton adopting final millage rates and levying ad valorem
                  taxes for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2025 and ending September 30 , 2026 ; stating
                  the percentage by which the millage to be levied exceeds the "rolled-back rate"; directing the
                  City Manager or the Office of Management and Budget Director to adjust the adopted millage
                  rates in the event of changes in the assessment roll and taxable value; providing for
                  severability ; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

           B.      Ordinance No. 5752
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton adopting a final budget and appropriating funds for the
                  fiscal year beginning October 1, 2025, and ending September 30, 2026; providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

           The public hearing was opened. Jonathan Pearlman requested information regarding the Tree
           Legacy Program and costs associated with Government Campus Redevelopment , stated that new
           costs associated with the Government Campus Redevelopment should not be included in the
           proposed budget, and asked that the tree survey of Sugar Sand Park be halted . Don Huber expressed
           concern about the need for local governments to reduce expenses . Seeing no one else come forward ,
           the public hearing was closed . The Mayor noted that the City has the lowest property tax rate of any
           other full-service City, and Mr. Zervis advised that cost-cutting measures had resulted in the reduction
           of operating expenses by two million dollars. Mr. Zervis also clarified that there was no increase in
           the millage rate , and if the voters approved a final development agreement for the Government
           Campus Redevelopment, a budget amendment would be brought forth to address fiscal
              impacts related to the development order. City Manager George Brown advised that the tree location
              survey taking place in Sugar Sand Park was necessary to map the condition of the Park and update
              the City's survey of the property.

              Deputy Mayor Nachlas shared a brief PowerPoint regarding boat charters picking up and dropping
              off at Silver Palm Park and expressed concerns about the activity from the Marine Advisory Board.
              Mr. Brown provided additional details about the issue, suggested staff collect more data , and come
              back to Members with options to address the problem . Members weighed in and there was consensus
              for staff to research the issue and present solutions for Council to review . Council Member Wigder
              outlined his suggested revisions to the budget, including both reduction and addition of staff,
              implementation of Al programs, permit waivers for new businesses , updating of the Parks Master
              Plan , and park beautification . Mr. Brown , Mr. Zervis, and Development Services Director Brandon
              Schaad provided additional information. They shared details on the positions that were highlighted
              and advised that a number of the requests could be addressed through existing operations or with
              funds already budgeted. Mayor Singer then addressed the requests from non-profit organizations and
              there was consensus from Members to revise the non-profit awards as noted. Council Members
              thanked the 0MB and Financial Services staff for their hard work.

                                 Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council Member
                                 Yvette Drucker, to adopt Ordinance No. 5751. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                                 Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                                 Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.

                                 Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member
                                 Fran Nachlas, to adopt Ordinance No. 5752 as amended to include Exhibits 1,2,3 as
                                 presented and the revisions to the Non-profits as discussed. Motion carried
                                 unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council Member
                                 Yvette Drucker, Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting
                                 yes.

  5.        RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:

             A.       Resolution No. 142-2025
                      A resolution of the City of Boca Raton adopting the FY 2025/26-2030/31 Capital Improvements
                      Program for the City of Boca Raton; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing
                      an effective date

              City Manager George Brown advised that the full presentation had been made previously by staff.
              Mr. Zervis confirmed that the previous presentation incorporated modifications that were presented
              in Exhibit 3.

              The public hearing was opened. Jon Pearlman commented on CIP funds being allocated to Public
              Works & Engineering and Utilities Services, and made a public records request for the costs for the
              next 2- and 5-year plans. Seeing no one else come forward , the public hearing was closed . The
              Mayor noted that the requested information was available on the City website and confirmed that Mr.
              Zervis had already reached out to Mr. Pearlman with a link to the CIP document that contained a
              project-by-project description .

                                 Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                                 Marc Wigder, to adopt Resolution No. 142-2025 as amended to include Exhibit 4.
                                 Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council
                                 Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc
                                 Wigder voting yes.
