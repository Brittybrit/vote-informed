           A.      Ordinance No. 5350
                   An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Section 3.08 of the Charter of the City of Boca
                   Raton to amend the method for filling vacancies on the City Council, including the office of Mayor:
                   providing that vacancies shall be filled by special election; providing for the submission of the
                   proposed Charter Amendment to the electors of the City at a special election to be held on
                   August 30, 2016 (the date of the Primary Election); amending Section 6-4, Code of Ordinances,
                   to delete a provision rendered obsolete and establish minimum requirements for the qualification
                   period for special elections; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for revision
                   to the City Charter and for codification in the Code of Ordinances; providing an effective date
                            Ordinance No. 5350

           Council Member Singer gave a brief PowerPoint presentation explaining his reasoning for bringing this
           ordinance forward. He noted that this legislation would allow the people to have their say with regard to
           filling a vacancy on the Council. Upon concluding, Mr. Singer responded to questions.




      Regular Minutes                                      4                                               June 14, 2016

     Lenore Wachtel, Betty Grinnan, Andrea O'Rourke, Kevin Meaney, Elizabeth Hoffman, Eddie Weinstein,
     George O'Rourke, JoAnn Landon, James Hendrey, Jack Fox, Beth Di Prato, Al Zucaro, and Helene
     Eichler expressed their support of the ordinance. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public
     hearing was closed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Scott Singer, seconded by Deputy Mayor Michael
                  Mu/laugh, to approve Ordinance No. 5350.

     Deputy Mayor Mullaugh offered brief comments in support of the ordinance. Council Member Weinroth

             Ordinance No. 5352
             An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Section 2-26, Code of Ordinances, to increase
             the annual salaries of the Mayor and members of the City Council as of October 1, 2016;
             providing that the annual salary of the Mayor shall be $38,000.00; providing that the annual salary
             of the other Council Members shall be $28,000.00; providing for the submission of the proposed
             salary increases to the electors of the City at a special election to be held on August 30, 2016
             {the date of the Primary Election); repealing Ordinance No. 5340; providing for severability;
             providing for codification; providing an effective date
                      Ordinance No. 5352

     Council Member Weinroth gave a brief explanation of this ordinance, which he brought forward. He noted
     that he did so in response to many comments he received regarding the automatic salary increases
     provided for in Ordinance 5340 that are not included in this ordinance.

     The public hearing was opened. James Hendrey, Kevin Meaney, Eddie Weinstein, and Helene Eichler
     offered supportive comments regarding increasing Council salaries. Seeing no one else come forward to
     speak, the public hearing was closed.

                      Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                      Michael Mu/laugh, to approve Ordinance No. 5352. Motion carried on a 4 to 1 vote; Mayor
                      Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council
                      Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes; Council Member Singer voting no.
