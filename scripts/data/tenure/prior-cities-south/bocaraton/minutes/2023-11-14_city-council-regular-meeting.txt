         B.        Ordinance No. 5678
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 2, "Administration," Article 11, "City
                  Council," Section 2-27, "Rules of Procedure," Code of Ordinances, to reduce the minimum
                  speaking time allotted to a person wishing to address the Council from five (5) minutes to
                  three (3) minutes; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification:
                  providing an effective date

         Acting City Manager George Brown offered the results of a survey performed by staff, noting that all
         municipalities in Palm Beach County and those contacted in Broward County offer three minutes or
         less of public comment time. Mayor Singer provided comments in favor of the proposed reduction
         in time and advised that time could always be extended at the discretion of the Council.

         The public comment period was opened. Amy Price spoke against the proposed change in public
         comment time. Jonathan Ounjian shared his opposition to the proposed ordinance. Seeing no one
         else come forward, the public comment period was closed.

         Mayor Singer responded to comments made during public comment and offered additional insight.
         Deputy Mayor Mayotte spoke against the reduction of time and advised that she would not be
         supporting this ordinance. Council Member Nachlas explained that she would support the ordinance
         if time could be adjusted or extended as needed. Council Member Yvette Drucker spoke in favor of
         the reduction and advised that she supports the ordinance. Council Member Marc Wigder shared
         that he would support the ordinance, as well as adjustments of the time allotted as demand
         necessitated.

                           Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Fran
                           Nachlas, to approve Ordinance No. 5678. Motion passed 4 to 1; Mayor Scott Singer,
                           Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council Member
                           Marc Wigder voting yes; Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte voting no.

                  and environmental risks: amending Chapter 9, "Miscellaneous Offenses," Article IV, Section
                  9-60, "Supplemental Code Enforcement Procedures," Code of Ordinances, to authorize the

                  1.      Resolution No. 118-2023
                         A resolution of the City of Boca Raton amending the "Schedule of Minimum Civil
                         Penalties for Violations Enforced Pursuant to Section 9-60, Code of Ordinances"
                         (Resolution No. 163-2018, as amended by Resolution No. 68-2022) to provide for a
                         civil penalty for violations of Section 5-4 (nuisance-causing and wildlife feeding
                         prohibited), Section 9-110 (prohibition of the sale or distribution of polystyrene foam
                         products on City property), Section 9-111 (prohibition of balloons and confetti in
                         outdoor areas on City property), Section 10-64(1) (time of day of construction activity),
                         and Section 14-11 (bulk and vegetative waste collection time/placement); and update
                         a cross reference to the Code of Ordinances; providing for severability; providing for
                         repealer; providing an effective date

       Mayor Singer noted that the public hearings on item 11.C. and 11.C.1. would be conducted
       contemporaneously as the items were related, and a separate vote would be taken on each item.
       Should item 11.C. not be adopted, no vote would be taken on item 11 .C.1.

       Jim Bell, Project Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Bell gave a brief background of
       the proposed ordinance and the associated violation fees and made himself available for Members'
       questions. Mr. Wigder asked about the additional fines being proposed aside from the nuisance­
       causing wildlife feeding. Ms. Drucker also inquired about the enforcement of the fines and the number
       of violations that were occurring. Deputy Mayor Mayotte questioned which animals were included in
       the ordinance. Ms. Nachlas agreed that the wildlife fine should not be included with the other items
       in the resolution. Mr. Bell and Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, responded to
       Members' questions and provided additional information.

       The public comment period was opened. Mark Stahlbaum spoke in favor of the ordinance and urged
       Council to approve it. Jonathan Ounjian raised concerns over exactly what would be considered a
       nuisance animal and how the ordinance would be enforced. Seeing no one else come forward, the
       public comment period was closed.

       Extended discussion ensued. City Attorney Diana Grubb Frieser and Mr. Brown provided clarification,
       highlighted sections of the code, and answered Members' questions. There was consensus to
       withdraw Ordinance No. 5679 from consideration, and to table Resolution No. 118-2023 to November
       28, 2023, to be brought back as revised.

                           Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Marc
                           Wigder, to withdraw Ordinance No. 5679. Motion carried unanimously on a voice
                           vote.

                           Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Marc
                           Wigder, to table Resolution No. 118-2023 to November 28, 2023. Motion carried
                           unanimously on a voice vote.
