 12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A        Ordinance No. 5598
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 22, "Marine Structures," Article Ill,
                  "Docks and Other Mooring Facilities," Section 22-63, "Boat Lifts," Code of Ordinances, to
                  provide that certain restrictions on the distance that a boat lift may extend into a waterway shall
                  not be applicable to a boat lift connected or adjacent to a finger pier or dolphin (provided the
                  boat lift does not extend further into the waterway than the finger pier or dolphin); providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-
                  21-06)

Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began with an
analysis of the current code. Mr. Schaad explained that finger piers or dolphin pilings are currently permitted to
extend into a waterway further than a boat lift, and staff could determine no reason that a boatlift should not
extend the same distance. It was also noted that staff received continuous variance requests of this kind, and
that passage of the proposed amendment would not only save staff time and streamline the code, but would
be beneficial to residents, as well. All marine structures would still be required to comply with all other City
requirements. Upon concluding, Mr. Schaad made himself available for Members' questions.

The public hearing was opened. Allan Elfassy and Jay Schneider expressed support for the ordinance. Seeing
no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

Council Member Thomson expressed his condolences to Council Member Mayotte on the death of a family
member. Mr. Thomson then complimented the coaches and athletics staff involved with the COBRA Mini T-ball

Deputy Mayor O'Rourke also extended condolences to Council Member Mayotte and welcomed the new

of Art.

Innovation Campus presentation from the prior day's Workshop and expressed her support. She noted that she
wanted to be sure the cultural aspect was collaborative, not competitive, with the art initiative at Mizner Park.

commented on the City's Arbor Day program that provides a tree to City residents. He concluded by
