12.    REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

       A.     Ordinance No. 5345
              An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton to amend Chapter 28 "Zoning," City's Code of
              Ordinances, relating to building height and height provisions; amending Section 28-391 to delete
              allowance for additional building height in the R-2 Zoning District; amending Sections 28-451, 28-
              481, and 28-511 to set the maximum height for accessory structures at 25 feet (and delete
              allowance for additional height) in the R-3-A, R-3-B, and R-3-C Zoning Districts; amending
              Section 28-780 to delete allowance for additional building height in the B-1 Zoning District;
              amending Sections 28-874, 28-983, 28-1074, and 28-1099 to delete the reference to "stories" and
              retain measurement of height in feet in the C-1, LIRP Employee Service Node, M-1 and M-2
              Zoning Districts; amending Section 28-1126 to delete allowance for additional height for
              accessory structures in the M-3 Zoning District; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
              providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-15-14)
                      Ordinance No. 5345

       Council Member Weinroth addressed the request for postponement and asked for an abbreviated
       presentation and public comment.

       Ingrid Allen, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation beginning with an overview of the history
       of this item. The various height references in the code for the B-1, R-2, R-3-A, R-3-B, R-3-C, C-1, LIRP
       Employee Service Node, M-1, M-2, and M-3 zoning districts were provided, followed by the
       recommendations of staff and the Planning & Zoning Board. Ms. Allen noted that the proposed changes
       are consistent with the Comprehensive Plan and Council's direction. Upon concluding, she responded to
       a number of questions from Council members.

       The public hearing was opened. Evan Aronson, in-house counsel for James and Marta Batmasian,
       reiterated his clients' request to postpone the public hearing on this ordinance until the May 24, 2016 City
       Council meeting. Bonnie Miske! representing a property owner in the northwest district, inquired about
       compliance of her client's building plan as it relates to the proposed ordinance. She will meet with staff to
       discuss the matter.

               Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Council Member Scott
               Singer, to continue the public hearing on Ordinance No. 5345 to May 24, 2016.

       Members made final comments.

               Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council
               Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth
               voting yes.

              Ordinance No. 5346
              An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 4, "Alcoholic Beverages," Code of
              Ordinances, relating to the posting of underage drinking signs; providing for severability; providing
              for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date
                      Ordinance No. 5346

       Mayor Haynie addressed the intent of this ordinance which she brought forward.

       The public hearing was opened. Jonah Fisher thanked the Mayor for her support and advocated adoption
       of the ordinance. He responded to questions from Council members. Alan Feinberg, Jr. inquired as to
       any associated costs and the existence of any law enforcement statistics supporting this as a problem.




             Regular Minutes                              4                               April 26, 2016
        Alexa Lee noted that similar ordinances have been adopted in five municipalities in the County. She also
        addressed the prevalence of underage drinking and responded to questions from Council members.

        Members expressed their thoughts with regard to the ordinance. Council Member Singer suggested
        amendments related to removing jail time as a penalty for non-compliance, the size of the sign to be
        displayed, the telephone numbers for reporting, and provision of 60 days notice to proprietors before the
        ordinance takes effect. The City Attorney read into the record language consistent with the amendments
        outlined by Council Member Singer.

                Motion was made by Council Member Scott Singer, seconded by Deputy Mayor Michael
                Mu/laugh, to approve Ordinance No. 5346 as amended. Motion carried 3 to 2; Mayor Susan
                Haynie, Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Scott Singer voting yes. Council
                Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting no.
