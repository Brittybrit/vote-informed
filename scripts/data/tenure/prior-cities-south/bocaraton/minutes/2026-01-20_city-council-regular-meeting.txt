 11.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A        Ordinance No. 5769
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton authorizing a transaction between the City, the Boca
                  Raton Community Redevelopment Agency (CRA), and Boca Raton City Center, LLC, relating
                  to the redevelopment of the Downtown Campus; providing for a mixed-use, transit-oriented
                  district on City-owned property east of Northwest Second Avenue and the improvement of City­
                  owned property west of Northwest Second Avenue for public parks and civic uses, pursuant to
                  Section 163.380, Florida Statutes, and Chapter 13, Article 111 , "Sale of City Property," Code of
                  Ordinances; provid ing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

                  City Manager Mark Sohaney gave a brief overview of the proposed project, thanked the
                  veterans who provided their input, and commended staff for their work on this project. Deputy
                  City Manager Andy Lukasik highlighted who would be presenting and outlined why and what
                  the Council would be considering later in the meeting. He continued by providing details about
                  the regulatory commitments, site plan requirements, and financing . City Attorney Josh Koehler
                  reviewed the legal agreements, which included the Master Partnership Agreement (MPA),
                  construction phasing, funding , and enforcement rights and remedies. He concluded by
                  referencing the referendum and advised that this agreement would require approval of the
                  residents at the municipal election. Mr. Lukasik provided a quick review of the timeline before
                  introducing Kevin Plenzler, Director at PFM Financial Advisors. Mr. Plenzler shared a summary
                  of the proposed economic and fiscal impacts and expected net revenues to the City. Mr. Zervis
                  provided additional information, and Mr. Plenzler continued with a review of ground rent
                  revenue, capital revenue estimates, and public investment.

                  The public hearing was opened. George Harper, Howard Gainer, John Schrey, Jose Alvarez,
                  Ken Wittenberg , Andy Reese, Larry Colburn, Ed Venezia, Jason Hayward, Ned Kimmelman,
                  Jerry Castillo, Josh Rosenfeld, Ely Nesirie, and Benjamin Schreier, spoke in favor of the project.
                  Joe Majhess, Briana Hagquist, Mike Liebelson, Aphrodite Petinakis, Michelle Grau, Jon
                  Pearlman, Jacob Abraham, Yvonne Zurn Tobe!, Martin Bell, and Betsy Nieporte, spoke against
                  the project.

                  Deputy City Managers Jim Zervis and Andy Lukasik responded to statements made during the
                  public hearing and provided clarification .


The meeting was recessed at 12:31 PM and resumed at 1:17 PM .


                  Judy Morrow, Richard Warner, Elly Gascoyne, Pam Paschke, Lyudmila Pearlman, Alexandra
                  Avello, and Joseph Pujalski expressed their opposition to the project. Elie Bennett, Aliza
                  Bennett, George Garcia, Adam Gold, Jonathan Ounjian , and Vic Rose stated their support of
                  the project. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed .

                  Discussion ensued . Deputy City Manager Jorge Camejo commented on the train station and
                  advised that it was an important feature regardless of who operates it. Mr. Plenzler shared
                  additional comments and information . Council members weighed in and provided their reasons
                  for supporting or opposing the ordinance.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                          Fran Nachlas, to approve Ordinance No. 5769; Motion passed 4 votes to 1; Mayor
                          Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                          Member Marc Wigder voting yes; Council Member Andy Thomson voting no.


The meeting recessed at 2:59 PM and resumed at 3:20 PM .
