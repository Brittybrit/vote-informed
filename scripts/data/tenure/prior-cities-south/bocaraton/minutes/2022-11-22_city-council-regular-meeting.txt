         8.       Resolution No. 134-2022
                  A Resolution of the City of Boca Raton authorizing Brightline Trains Florida LLC to install and
                  maintain a mural on the north wall connecting the main entrances of the Parking Garage and the
                  Train Station on City-owned property generally located at 400 NW 2nd Avenue, 101 NW 4 th Street,
                  and 151 NW 4th Street; Authorizing the City Manager to execute an acknowledgement and waiver
                  by Brightline and the Mural Artist; providing for repealer; providing an effective date.

         Council Member O'Rourke gave a brief overview of the resolution. Brightline Vice President Brian
         Kronberg described the location of the art wall and artist Ben Heller provided his perspective and cited his
         influences of the design of the proposed mural.

         Council Member O'Rourke commented on the cool factor of the mural and thanked the artist for his design.
         Ms. Mayotte thanked Mr. Kronberg and Mr. Heller and expressed her approval of the design 's historical
         significance. Ms. Drucker praised the artist and stated that this would be a great social media location for
         selfies. Mr. Kronberg gave a brief update and advised the station would be open by the end of the year.

                          Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                          Member Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 134-2022. Motion carried unanimously;
                          Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette
                          Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte voting yes.
