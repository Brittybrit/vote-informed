                 2.       Resolution No. 44-2022
                          A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering accepting and approving the plat known
                          as "Banana Patch" (a portion of which is a replat of Tract "A," Tropic Isle 4th Section,
                          together with an unplatted portion consisting of the south one-half of the northwest one­
                          quarter of Section 33, Township 46 South, Range 43 East), for an approximately 1.1 acre
                          property generally located at 7901 Northeast Spanish Trail Court and 7902 Northeast Palm
                          Way, authorizing the Mayor and City Clerk to execute the Plat; providing for repealer;
                          providing an effective date (SUB-21-01) The public hearings on Items B. and B.1. will be
                          conducted contemporaneously as the petitions are related and pursuant to quasi-judicial
                          procedures as required by Item 8.1 (Item B is a legislative matter). A separate vote will be
                          taken on Items B. and 8.1. Should Item B. not be approved, Item 8.1. will not be
                          considered.

          The Mayor noted that the public hearings on items 10.A. and 10.A.2. would be conducted
          contemporaneously as the items were related, and a separate vote would be taken on each item. The
          City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing. Members
          made their ex-parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

          Planner Jacob German gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began with an overview of the applicants'
          request and provided a comparison to the previous approval. An aerial photograph of the location was
          shown, and the site plan was displayed. Mr. German reviewed the requested technical deviation for a 30-
          foot driveway. The Planning and Zoning Board and staff recommended approval. Upon concluding, he
          made himself available for questions.

          The public hearing was opened. Michael Weiner, attorney for the applicant, reviewed the existing
          conditions of the property. He further explained the request for technical deviation and made himself
          available for Members' questions. Seeing no one else come forward the public hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor Andrea
                          Levine O'Rourke, to approve Ordinance No. 5617 Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott
                          Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                          Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

                          Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 44-2022. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                          Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
