      then presented Deputy Mayor Mullaugh with a plaque in recognition of his service. Deputy Mayor

      Mayor Haynie then acknowledged Representative Bill Hager.

6.     OTHER BUSINESS:

        A.    Selection of MPO (Metropolitan Planning Organization) Representatives (2)

               Motion was made by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Scott
               Singer, to appoint Council Member Robert S. Weinroth and Mayor Susan Haynie as Metropolitan
               Planning organization representatives. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

        B.    Selection of Palm Beach County League of Cities Voting Delegate & Alternate

               Motion was made by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Mayor Susan Haynie, to
               appoint Council Member Scott Singer as Palm Beach County League of Cities voting delegate
               and Council Member Robert S. Weinroth as alternate delegate. Motion carried unanimously on a
               voice vote.

        C.    Selection of IPARC (lnterlocal Plan Amendment Review Committee) Representative

               Motion was made by Council Member Scott Singer, seconded by Mayor Susan Haynie , to
               appoint Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke as lnterlocal Plan Amendment Review
               Committee Representative. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.
