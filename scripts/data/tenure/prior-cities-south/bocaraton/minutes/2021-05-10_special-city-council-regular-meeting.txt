                                    MINUTES OF THE SPECIAL MEETING
                                                CITY COUNCIL
                                      CITY OF BOCA RATON, FLORIDA
                                           Monday, May 10, 2021
                                                *3: 30 PM

             The Special Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was called to order
                                          by Mayor Scott Singer at 5:26 PM.

Mayor Singer welcomed all those listening to the Special Council Meeting prior to reading a brief statement
explaining that the meeting would be conducted as a Virtual Meeting using Communications Media Technology.
In addition to virtual participation by members of the public, in-person participation is available in the City Council
Chamber at City Hall. Mayor Singer then listed the members of the City Council appearing remotely. He advised
that City Manager Leif Ahnell and City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser were also attending remotely.

4.     IF THE POTENTIAL DISPOSITION IS FOR LESS THAN FAIR VALUE, CONSIDERATION OF
       PROPOSALS FOR THE POTENTIAL DISPOSITION AND REDEVELOPMENT OF PROPERTY
       OWNED BY THE COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY:

City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser gave a brief overview of the procedures, noting that Council would need to
consider the use of the Mizner Park Amphitheater land which would fall under the City's Code of Ordinances as
sale of City property, and to also give direction to staff to proceed with negotiations in the connection of City
property. Ms. Frieser then responded to question regarding fair market value. She added that Members were only
providing directing to staff at this time.

        A.        If the disposition is for less than fair value, consideration of proposals: Potential
                  disposition and redevelopment of property owned by the Community Redevelopment
                  Agency in accordance with Section 163.380, Florida Statutes, the "Performance Venue
                  Parcel" (City Council authorization is required in the event the disposition is for less than
                  fair value), which redevelopment shall include a performance venue (and may also
                  include the potential "sale" of adjacent land owned by the City of Boca Raton and
                  operated as the Mizner Park Amphitheater, the "Amphitheater Parcel"). Notice of this
                  public hearing has been provided pursuant to the requirements of Section 163.380,
                  Florida Statutes (see below).

                  Consideration of potential "sale" of City property: Potential "sale" of the "Amphitheater
                  Parcel" in connection with the potential disposition and redevelopment of the
                  "Performance Venue Parcel."

                  Questions to proposers: Questions to proposers from members of the Council Members
                  and discussion by the City Council.

                  Public comment.

        Andrea Virgin, President of BRADEC, accompanied by attorney Michael Marshall, thanked everyone for
        their comments and stated that she looked forward to working with the City on this project. Michael
        Marshall, on behalf of Gunster Law Firm, requested clarification for the record that the decision was
        unanimous to proceed with BRADEC's proposal. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser responded that no
        decision was made by City Council but clarified that this was a process to direct staff to proceed with
        negotiations with BRADEC. She stated that this was not a binding decision, but a direction to proceed
        with negotiations and, hopefully, bring back a set of transaction documents to present to the CRA and
        City Council at a noticed hearing for their consideration. Discussion ensued. City Manager Leif Ahnell
        affirmed that he was clear on the direction for staff to begin negotiations with only BRADEC.

                 Discussion/Deliberation/Direction by the City Council: The Council may discuss the
                 proposal selected by the CRA and the potential "sale" of City property. The Council may
                 direct City staff to enter into negotiations regarding the potential "sale" of City property,
                 may request additional information, may take any other action(s) deemed to be in the
                 public interest, or may take no action. If a proposal is selected for negotiations, any
                 proposed transaction(s) will be considered pursuant to all applicable requirements of law
                 and will be subject to final approval by the City at a future hearing.

        Council members agreed that staff was directed to conduct negotiations with only BRADEC. Deputy
        Mayor O'Rourke inquired about the cone of silence, and the City Attorney Frieser responded that the
        cone of silence was in place until an award was made.

5.     ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 5:36 PM.
