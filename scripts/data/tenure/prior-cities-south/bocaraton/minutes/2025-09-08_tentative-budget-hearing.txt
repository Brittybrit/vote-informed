      Council Member Marc Wigder

3.    PRESENTATION ON TENTATIVE BUDGET, PROPOSED MILLAGE RATES, AND CAPITAL
      IMPROVEMENTS PROGRAM:

     Budget Director Sharon McGuire gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began by reviewing the City's
     Vision , Mission and Values, as well as the Strategic Focus Areas and Priorities. Ms. McGuire
     announced the proposed millage rate of 3.6649 mils, which was slightly lower than the FY 2024-25
     rate. A statement of fund balances for the general fund was displayed and additions for the new fiscal
     year were outlined . Ms. McGuire then described the Property Tax Bill by Taxing Authority breakdown.
     Also noted the tax rates and Fire Assessment fees of surrounding cities and how Boca Raton is the
     lowest of the cities compared. Ms. McGuire concluded her presentation with a review of City-wide
     operating costs and made herself available for Members' questions. Mayor Singer asked for clarification
     regarding an increase for certain areas of the budget. Deputy City Manager/CFO Jim Zervis advised
     that this was not a rise in costs but a transfer between funds to more correctly categorize the
     expenditures. Mr. Zervis noted that staff had worked diligently to cut approximately 9 million from the
     overall budget and confirmed that no sacrifice in service occurred due to the reductions.

     Ms. McGuire continued by briefly reviewing the Capital Improvements Program for Fiscal Year 2025/26
     through 2030/31 and how each project aligned with the City's Key Priorities. Upon concluding , she and
     Mr. Zervis responded to Members' questions.
