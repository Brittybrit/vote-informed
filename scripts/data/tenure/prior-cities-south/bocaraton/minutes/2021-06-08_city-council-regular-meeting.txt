          A.      Resolution No. 63-2021
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the Pine Crest School located at 2700
                  Saint Andrews Boulevard: (i) an amended and restated conditional use approval, consolidating
                  previously-issued conditional use approvals, (ii) an amendment to conditional use Resolution
                  No. 67-2008 to approve a Master Plan and authorize the addition of an approximately 5,808
                  square foot two (2)-story Learning Commons Building, which includes a Student Union and flex
                  space classrooms, and certain minor future improvements (tennis pavilion, maintenance
                  building expansion, fine arts building expansion, swimming pool, concessions building,
                  bleachers, scoreboard, and running track); providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                  (CA-07-17R1)

           The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing.
           Council Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who
           wished to speak.

           Heather Hansen, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began by reviewing the
           applicant's request, which included a proposed amended and restated conditional use approval, and
           site plan approval for an additional building. It was noted that the master plan reaffirms the
           improvements approved in 2008. A map and aerial photo of the subject area were shown, and Ms.
           Hanson provided a brief explanation of the both the amended conditions of approval and seven
           additional conditions that had been added to the 2008 resolution. The Planning and Zoning Board

           resolution, subject to the proposed conditions.

 12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A        Ordinance No. 5580
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 9, Code of Ordinances,
                  "Miscellaneous Offenses," by creating Article VII, "Waste Reduction," to prohibit polystyrene
                  foam products, balloons, and confetti in City facilities, on City property, and in City rights of way
                  (as more particularly provided); amending Section 9-60, "Supplemental Code Enforcement
                  Procedures," to provide for enforcement; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                  providing for codification; providing an effective date

        Council Member Mayotte presented the item and began by thanking City staff for their work on the
        ordinance. She highlighted the point that, because the State preempts municipalities from regulating
        many kinds of single-use plastics, the proposed measure would only prohibit use of the specified
        products on City property. Ms. Mayotte explained that the measure would ensure that vendors at special
        events would be prohibited from selling or distributing items packed in polystyrene, and outdoor pavilion
        rental contracts would include a prohibition on balloons and confetti. Ms. Mayotte illustrated the need
        to cut down on waste by sharing photographs she had recently taken of litter in City parks, including
        pictures of discarded balloons and polystyrene plates. She advised she had recently spoken at the May
        7th Beach and Park District meeting, and 5 of 7 commissioners supported the ordinance. City Attorney
        Diana Grub Frieser provided clarification of revisions made to the ordinance since its introduction.

         The public hearing was opened. Karen Leader, Alana Daniel, Rory Fielding, Erica Allen, and Jessica
         Gray expressed their support of the ordinance. Guillermo Asca commented on the need to provide
         education to the public and asked that the ordinance be amended. Glenn Gromann conveyed his
         approval and suggested change to the language.

                           Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                           Andrea Levine O'Rourke, to approve Ordinance No. 5580 as revised.

         Deputy Mayor O'Rourke expressed her support of the ordinance and thanked Council Member Mayotte
         and members of the audience for their work on behalf of the environment. Council Member Drucker
         also shared her approval and her commitment to taking care of our City. Mayor Singer thanked Ms.
         Mayotte for her willingness to address concerns regarding enforcement of the ordinance and indicated
         his support for the item.

                           Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine
                           O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                           Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
