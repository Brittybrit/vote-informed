I.     Welcome

Mayor Scott Singer and Chair Susan Vogelgesang

       Mayor Scott Singer called the meeting to order at 6:00 pm. The participants introduced themselves.

II.    Discussion

       A.      Recreation Needs Assessment Presentation

       Recreation Services Director Michael Kalvort provided opening comments and introduced the
       consultants. Neelay Bhatt, accompanied by Brayton McClure from Pros Consulting, Inc., gave the
       PowerPoint presentation. A review of the strategic plan process was provided, and a number of topics
       were covered, including, but not limited to, a demographics analysis, a recreation trends analysis,
       community input summary, survey methodology, and a priority investment rating. Maps of neighborhood
       parks, ball fields, and tennis courts were also shown. The visioning process was discussed. Mr. Bhatt
       stated that the next step was to conduct a system-wide comprehensive master plant to implement the
       Needs Assessment findings. Discussion ensued on the level of service priorities, how COVID had
       affected activities and the survey projections, and the need for additional marketing of recreation
       offerings. Mr. Kalvort provided additional information regarding the municipal golf course, the addition of
       the Boca Raton Country Club golf course, and how the results of the Needs Assessment will help
       Recreation Services.

       B.      Gumbo Limbo Nature Center

       This item was added to the agenda at the request of the BPD in consideration of recent conversations
       regarding the piping project. Commissioner Craig Ehrnst began by noting the 1984 agreement with the
       City and stated that they are committed to Gumbo Limbo, the volunteers, and the City, and recognized
       their obligations. He expressed concern with the Friends of Gumbo Limbo (Friends) and their lack of
       participation in helping to fund the project. He felt the District or the City should have some type of
       agreement with the Friends which would include financial transparency and a contribution to projects at
       Gumbo Limbo. Discussion ensued. There was consensus among the members of both parties that there
       should be an agreement to formalize the relationship. City Manager Ahnell advised he would direct staff
       to work with the Friends to formalize an agreement and report back in the next few months to both
       parties.
Ill.     Public Comment

         Bruce Diller Verstandig commented on the opening of Ocean Strand, FAU, Gumbo Limbo and Ocean
         Breeze. Greg Galanis commented on the condition of the golf course at the Boca Country Club and
         suggested developing Boca Teeca as an executive golf course and practice area. Earl Starkoff remarked
         on golf operations in Boca, the need for additional pickleball courts and athletic fields, and SABR. Steve
         Rose stated that there should be more pickleball courts in Boca. Raul Travieso, President of the Boca
         Raton Pickleball Club, also spoke in support of building a premier indoor/outdoor pickleball complex at
         either Ocean Breeze or Boca Raton Country Club locations. Cil Ciliberti commented on emails he sent to
         Council members regarding Ocean Breeze and pickleball courts.

         Deputy Mayor O'Rourke commented on whether a pickleball facility could be constructed at the Boca
         Country Club or Ocean Breeze. Mr. Ahnell provided additional information. She then inquired whether the
         District could lease property to an outside entity to run a pickleball facility. Mr. Goren, District Council,
         responded that they would research the option. Discussion ensued on topics including, but not limited to,
         the District's ability to transfer property, constructing a pickleball complex, some type of golf or walking
         trails at Ocean Breeze, and the District's RFI for recreational needs at Ocean Breeze. Mr. Ahnell
         suggesting getting someone onboard to look at both the Boca Country Club and Ocean Breeze sites for a
         pickleball complex and to bring back options by the end of summer. Council members and District
         commissioners provided their final comments and there was consensus to move forward.

IV.      Adjournment

         The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 7: 10 pm.
