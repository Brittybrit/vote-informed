14.    PUBLIC REQUESTS

Meghan Shea, on behalf of Junior League of Boca Raton, expressed support for the proposed train station and
commented on relocation of the Community Garden. Susan Reilly, on behalf of the Library Board, provided a
summary of recommendations regarding safety concerns. Krista Maki shared her support for the proposed station
and relayed concerns regarding construction impacts. Don Kolodz, on behalf of Discover the Palm Beaches, Wendy
Poe, lmran Siddiqui, and Ryan Reiter spoke in support of the proposed train station. Susan Sozin commented in
support of a covered walkway and expressed concerns regarding safety. Christopher Marcello stated his opposition
to the proposed station and additional traffic. Manju Pendakur and Michael Kohner relayed concerns with the
proposed VTUSA lease. Seeing no one else come forward, the public request period was closed.

Mayor Singer provided a review of the VTUSA presentation from the previous day. Brian Kronberg and Jose
Gonzalez, on behalf of VTUSA, responded to a number of members' questions. The City Attorney provided
information regarding provisions of the proposed lease. Deputy City Manager George Brown and City Manager Leif
Ahnell responded to questions about city vehicle parking. Discussion ensued. Topics discussed included, but were
not limited to, parking garage heights, public space within the proposed station, security and the opening of a
crossing at Jeffrey Street. The Mayor provided additional clarification on the proposed lease and thanked Mr.
Gonzalez and Mr. Kronberg for their time.
