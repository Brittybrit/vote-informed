                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering , for the approximately 10.0-acre
                  property generally located at 791 Park of Commerce Boulevard , an amendment to an
                  approved site plan (Planning and Zoning Board Resolution No. 99-03) to authorize a
                  Commercial Industrial Multifamily Development (CIMO) including: (a) conversion of the
                  existing approximately 130,510 square foot office building to a mix of uses, including a
                  fast-casual restaurant with 11 ,447 square feet of indoor area and 2,570 square foot of
                  outdoor dining , a 72 ,428 square foot amateur recreation facility , and 46,635 square
                  feet of office space; and (b) construction of 243 multifamily residential units, including
                  10% affordable and 5% workforce units, with 226 units in an eight (8)-story apartment
                  building with an eight (8)-level integrated parking structure and 17 units in five (5) , three
                  (3)-story townhome-style buildings; together with a technical deviation from Section
                  23-190(b) , Code of Ordinances, to increase the maximum length of two (2) parking
                  rows from 250 feet to 296 feet; and considering the abandonment of a portion of a 12-
                  foot waterline easement; providing for repealer and specifically repealing Planning and
                  Zoning Board Resolution No. 93-12; providing an effective date (SPA-98-34R1)

          The Mayor noted that the public hearings would be conducted contemporaneously as the
          items were interrelated. He noted that a separate vote would be taken on each item and should
          item 11.A.1 . not be approved , no vote would be taken on the remaining item. The City Attorney
          read the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing. Members made
          their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

          Senior Planner Heather Hansen gave the PowerPoint presentation . She reviewed the site
          plans which included an integrated parking structure, residential tower, townhomes,
          restaurant, and an indoor sports facility . She advised that the Planning and Zoning Board and
          staff recommend approval. Upon concluding , she made herself available for questions.
          Discussion ensued. Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided additional
          answers to Members' questions and advised that staff was researching the CIMO
          requirements for public transportation .

          The public hearing was opened . Attorney for the applicant Ele Zachariades gave a brief
          PowerPoint presentation which reviewed the proposed buildings and site plans . Ms.
          Zachariades advised that her client was not asking for any variances and only one technical
          deviation for the parking . She concluded by showing renderings of the proposed project and
          then made herself available for questions. Engineer for the applicant John Donaldson
          provided additional information. Jonathan Ounjian shared his support for the project but had
          concerns with the units per acre and the transit program. Seeing no one else come forward ,
          the public hearing was closed .         •

16.   QUASI-JUDICIAL PUBLIC HEARINGS/VARIANCES & APPEALS:

       A       Appeal - Telecom Medical Office

                1.      Resolution No. 34-2025
                        A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering an appeal of the
                        Planning and Zoning Board Resolution No. 2025-001 , which approved a site plan
                        amendment for an approximately 3.0-acre property generally located at 1001 East
                        Telecom Drive, to authorize construction of a 10,860 square foot free-standing
                        emergency room and related site improvements, including three (3) technical
                        deviations: (i) from Section 23-190(d)2 , Code of Ordinances, to reduce the required
                        driveway reservoir distance for the westernmost driveway on East Telecom Drive from
                        25 feet to 13.74 feet, (ii) from Section 28-1655 , Code of Ordinances, to reduce the
                        required number of vehicle parking spaces from 120 spaces to 82 spaces, and (iii) from
                        Section 28-1651(1)(d) , Code of Ordinances, to increase the maximum number of
                        reserved parking spaces from five (5) spaces to nine (9) spaces; providing for repealer;
                        providing an effective date (SPA-03-13R1)



      Regular Meeting Minutes                                7                                           April 8, 2025
                 The Mayor noted that an extensive quasi-judicial public hearing had been held on March 18,
                 2025 , during which both parties presented evidence , testimony , and argument. He advised
                 that the hearing had been formally closed but that, due to the passage of time, both parties
                 had been invited to submit a recap of their March 18th presentations , and they would be
                 afforded 10 minutes each to address the City Council. The City Attorney confirmed that the
                 quasi-judicial procedures would still govern the limited public hearing. Council Members made
                 ex parte disclosures that occurred since the March 18, 2025, public hearing and it was
                 confirmed that the individuals speaking at this continued public hearing had been duly sworn
                 at the beginning of the March 18th hearing.

                The public hearing was reopened . Development Services Director Brandon Schaad gave a
                brief recap and addressed comments made from the previous meeting. He re iterated that a
                free-standing emergency room was a permitted use in the area and that the requested
                technical deviation for the driveway reservoir wou ld not interfere with multi-modal
                improvements. Mr. Schaad also advised that the applicant's accompanying design
                requirements were the deciding factor in the approval of the reduced parking requirements.
                Bonnie Miskel, attorney for the appellant, provided a recap and advised that her client's main
                objections were to the parking reduction , the driveway reservoir reduction , and the free­
                standing emergency medical room use. Ms. Miskel stated that the operational plan for the
                driveway was not available for the Planning and Zoning Board 's review and that the parking
                provided would be insufficient to meet future demand. The Mayor noted that Ms. Miskel had
                been afforded 59 additional seconds and that this would be provided to the applicant, as well.
                Ms. Miskel then responded to Members' questions. Michael Marshall, attorney for the
                applicant, provided a review of his applicant's case. He reminded Council Members that the
                application had been approved by the Planning and Zoning Board and by City staff, and that
                the burden was on the appellant to support their case. Mr. Marshall stated that the medical
                office use was permitted in the subject location, that information provided by the appellant was
                hypothetical , and that he did not believe the appellant had provided evidence to support their
                objection to the parking reduction. Upon concluding , he made himself available for questions.
                Mayor Singer noted that Mr. Marshall had received an additional 13 seconds and asked Ms.
                Miskel if she would like the same amount of time, which she declined. The public hearing was
                closed.

                The Mayor then reviewed the voting options available to Council.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council Member
                        Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 34-2025 and grant the appeal.

                Members made final comments. There was consensus regarding the need for additional
                medical resources , but Members shared concerns about the technical requirements and
                proposed parking .

                        Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council
                        Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc
                        Wigder voting yes.
