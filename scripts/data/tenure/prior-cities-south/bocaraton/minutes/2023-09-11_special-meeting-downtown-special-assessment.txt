                                      MINUTES OF THE SPECIAL MEETING
                                               CITY COUNCIL
                                        MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 11, 2023
                                                  *1:30 PM


*The Special Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton , Florida was held in the Auditorium at 6500
   Congress Avenue. The meeting was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at approximately 10:05 PM.

1.    PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG :

2.    ROLL CALL:

           Mayor Scott Singer
           Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte
           Council Member Yvette Drucker
           Council Member Fran Nachlas
           Council Member Marc Wigder

The Mayor announced the purpose of the special meeting.

3.    PUBLIC HEARING:

      A.     Resolution No. 90-2023
             A resolution of the City of Boca Raton equalizing the assessments; levying the same as
             assessments against benefited properties within the Downtown Special Assessment District, City
             of Boca Raton, Florida; directing the City Clerk to record assessments in the Improvement Lien
             Book; establishing priority of lien and payment of principal and interest; providing for severability;
             providing for repealer; providing an effective date

       Stephen Timberlake, Special Projects Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He provided a brief
       history of the Downtown Special Assessment, the requirement to fund the debt service and
       administrative costs for the special assessment revenue bonds. Mr. Timberlake noted that the purpose
       of the meeting was to hear and consider any objections to the special assessment. The method of
       calculating the assessment rate was explained and various charts and graphs were presented. No
       objection letters were received. Upon concluding, Mr. Timberlake made himself available for questions.
       Deputy Mayor Mayotte advised that the special assessment bonds would be paid off in 2030.

       The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward, was closed.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Fran Nachlas, seconded by Council Member
                         Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 90-2023. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                         Scott Singer, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Fran Nachlas, Council
                         Member Marc Wigder, Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte voting yes.

 4.   ADJOURNMENT:

 The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 10:09 PM.
