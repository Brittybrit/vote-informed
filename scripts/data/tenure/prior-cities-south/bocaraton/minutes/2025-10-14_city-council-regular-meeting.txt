 10.     RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:


          A.      Government Campus Redevelopment Partnership Opportunity
                  1)      Reports from Staff and CBRE

                  2)      Resolution No . 155-2025
                          A resolution of the City of Boca Raton , authorizing the City Manager to execute the
                          First Amendment to the Interim Agreement with Boca Raton City Center, LLC
                          (Terra/Frisbie) for the Downtown Campus project, extending the agreement term from
                          October 31 , 2025 to May 1, 2026 to allow for a referendum on the project at the March
                          10, 2026 election; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an
                          effective date

                   Deputy City Manager Andy Lukasik advised that this proposed first amendment to the
                   agreement extends the contract based on the referendum on the March 10, 2026 election. He
                   advised that staff recommended approval and made himself available for questions .
                   Discussion ensued.

                  3)      Public Comment

                 The public comment period was opened . Jonathan Pearlman , Joseph Majhess , Pam Paschke,
                 Briana Hagquist, Jonathan Ounjian , Richard Warner, Shannon Otto, and Buffy Tucker all spoke
                 in opposition to the Government Campus project. Seeing no one else come forward the public
                 comment period was closed .

                          Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council Member
                          Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 155-2025.

          A.      Ordinance No. 5753
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning ," Article XV , Division
                 15, "Affordable and Workforce Housing in Commercial and Industrial Zoning Districts," Section
                 28-1644, "Land Development Regulations for Commercial Industrial Multifamily Development
                 (CIM O) pursuant to the Statute ," Code of Ordinances, to provide that the requirement that
                 CIMD's include non-residential uses (including office , restaurant and/or retail sales or services)
