                         MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING
                                      CITY COUNCIL
                              CITY OF BOCA RATON , FLORIDA
                                 TUESDAY, MARCH 31, 2026
                                         10:30 AM

      The Organizational Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton , Florida was held
                           in the Auditorium at 6500 Congress Avenue.

1.   CALL TO ORDER:

     The meeting was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at 10:30 am.

2.   PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

3.   ROLL CALL:

     Mayor Scott Singer
     Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas
     Council Member Yvette Drucker
     Council Member Andy Thomson
     Council Member Marc Wigder

4.   CEREMONIAL PROCEEDINGS:

     A.      Recognition of and comments by outgoing Mayor and Council Member~

             Marc Wigder reflected on his time in office and thanked his colleagues and City staff for their
             commitment and hard work. He extended his best wishes to incoming Mayor Andy Thomson
             and the newly elected Council Members.

             Fran Drucker expressed her gratitude to residents , fellow Council Members, City staff, and her
            family for their support. She concluded by affirming her commitment to staying actively involved
            in the community.

             Scott Singer conveyed his appreciation to past and current Council Members and City staff for
            their hard work and to his family for their steadfast support. He reaffirmed his dedication to the
            City and its future success and thanked residents for the opportunity to seNe.

     B.      Administration of Oath of Office for newly elected Mayor and Council Members

             Pastor David Cassidy administered the oath of office to Mayor Andy Thomson .

             Mr. Antonio Grau administered the oath t'o Council Member Michelle Grau.

             Ms. Lyudmilla Pearlman administered the oath to Council Member Jon Pearlman .

             Mr. Patrick Sipple administered the oath to Council Member Stacy Sipple.

     C.      Comments by newly elected Mayor and Council Members

             Mayor Thomson thanked those who supported him and gave credit to his faith , family , and
             friends . He acknowledged his former Council colleagues , expressed his anticipation of
             working with the new members, and said that after the Memorial Park dedication , a priority
             would be restoring unity to the City and its residents.

             Deputy Mayor Grau expressed her appreciation to her family, friends , Save Boca supporters ,
             and all who participated in the election . She outlined goals to put residents first and protect
               their quality of life, and emphasized the importance of transparency, integrity, and fiscal
               responsibility.

               Council Member Pearlman extended his gratitude to his wife, family , and Save Boca
               supporters. He thanked voters for the historic turnout and pledged to serve all residents.

               Council Member Sipple thanked her family , supporters, and Save Boca. She emphasized
               responsible development, the protection of parks, beaches , and public spaces, and
               highlighted the importance of grace, respect, and a sense of community .

       D.     Comments by Council Member Yvette Drucker

               Council Member Drucker welcomed Congressman Jared Moskowitz and Senator Tina
               Polsky, thanked the former City Council Members for their collaboration , and welcomed the
               new Members . She highlighted initiatives undertaken by the prior Council , highlighted
               upcoming challenges, and her dedication to service.

The meeting recessed at 11 :20 am and reconvened at 11 :42 am.

5.    ORGANIZATIONAL PROCEEDINGS:


       A.     Selection of Deputy Mayor

                     Motion was made by Council Member Stacy Sipple, seconded by Council Member Jon
                     Pearlman, to appoint Council Member Michelle Grau as Deputy Mayor.

              Council Member Yvette Drucker outlined the role of the Deputy Mayor and the responsibilities
              for each of the appointments to be filled . Mayor Thomson thanked Council Member Drucker
              for her role as Chair of the Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO).

                     Motion to appoint Michelle Grau as Deputy Mayor carried unanimously on a voice vote.

       B.     Selection of CRA Chair and Vice Chair

                     Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker to appoint herself as CRA Chair
                     and Council Member Jon Pearlman as CRA Vice Chair.

                     Motion was made by Council Member Jon Pearlman, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                     Michelle Grau, to appoint Mayor Andy Thomson as Chair of the CRA. Council Member
                     Yvette Drucker withdrew her motion.

                     Motion to appoint Mayor Andy Thomson as CRA Chair carried unanimously on a voice
                     vote .

                     Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Michelle Grau, seconded by Mayor Andy
                     Thomson, to appoint Council Member Yvette Drucker as Vice Chair of the CRA. Motion
                     carried unanimously on a voice vote .

       C.     Selection of Affordable Housing Advisory Committee Representative (Ordinance No. 5691)

                     Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                     Michelle Grau, to appoint Council Member Jon Pearlman to the Affordable Housing
                     Advisory Committee. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.
