                                MINUTES OF THE SPECIAL MEETING
                                          CITY COUNCIL
                                  CITY OF BOCA RATON, FLORIDA
                                  MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 12, 2022
                                             1:30 PM*

 *The Special Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was held in the Auditorium at 6500
     Congress Avenue. The meeting was called to order by Mayor Scott Singer at approximately 2:03 PM.

 1.    PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG

 2.    ROLL CALL

         Mayor Scott Singer
         Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke
         Council Member Yvette Drucker
         Council Member Monica Mayotte
         Council Member Andy Thomson

The Mayor announced the purpose of the special meeting.

 3.     PUBLIC HEARING

        A.     Resolution No. 91-2022
               A resolution of the City of Boca Raton equalizing the assessments; levying the same as
               assessments against benefited properties within the Downtown Special Assessment District,
               City of Boca Raton, Florida; directing the City Clerk to record assessments in the Improvement
               Lien Book; establishing priority of lien and payment of principal and interest; providing for
               severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

       Stephen Timberlake, Special Projects Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He provided a brief
       history of the Downtown Special Assessment, the requirement to fund the debt service and
       administrative costs for the special assessment revenue bonds. Mr. Timberlake noted that the purpose
       of the meeting was to hear and consider any objections to the special assessment. The method of
       calculating the assessment rate was explained and various charts and graphs were presented. No
       objection letters were received. Upon concluding, Mr. Timberlake made himself available for questions.

       The public hearing was opened. Tiery Boykin, business owner, commented on expanding the
       parameters of who might pay for the assessments since everyone enjoys the benefits of the Downtown.
       Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed. Mr. Timberlake responded to Mr.
       Boykin's comment.

                       Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member
                       Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 91-2022. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                       Scott Singer, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte,
                       Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

 4.     ADJOURNMENT

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 2:10 PM.
