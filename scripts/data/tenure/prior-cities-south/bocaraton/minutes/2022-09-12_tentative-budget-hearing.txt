3.      PRESENTATION ON TENTATIVE BUDGET, PROPOSED MILLAGE RATES, AND CAPITAL
        IMPROVEMENTS PROGRAM:

       Budget Director Sharon McGuire gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began by giving an overview of
       the proposed millage rate of 3.6784 mils. She further explained the General Fund Operating costs and new
       additions to the Fiscal Year 2022-2023 budget, including new positions and programs. She also described
       the Property Tax Bill by Taxing Authority breakdown and provided an example scenario for an average
       home. Also discussed were the tax rates and Fire Assessment fees of surrounding cities and how we are
       the lowest of the cities compared. Ms. McGuire concluded the Millage portion of her presentation and
       responded to a question about the Beautification Fund. She continued by discussing the Capital
       Improvements Program. Upon concluding she made herself available for questions.
