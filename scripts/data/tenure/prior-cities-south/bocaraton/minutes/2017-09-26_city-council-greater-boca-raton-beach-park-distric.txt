I.      Welcome and Introductions - Mayor Susan Haynie and Chairman Bob Rollins

        Mayor Susan Haynie called the meeting to order at 3:03 pm. The participants introduced themselves.

II.     Discussion Topics

        A       Ocean Breeze Golf Course

        Commissioner Robert Rollins, Chairman of the Greater Boca Raton Beach & Park District (District)
        provided brief comments referencing the report provided to the City and invited questions from Council.
        Mayor Haynie asked that Mr. Koski provide a high level overview of the documents provided to the City.
        before turning the meeting over to Executive Director Koski, which followed. Topics included, but were not
        limited to, the appraisal, the use and operation of the hotel property, improvements to the surrounding
        infrastructure, the cost of course development, and financing for acquisition. Mr. Koski then responded
        to Council members' questions. Mr. Koski noted that the intent is to rename the course Boca National
        Golf Club. Richard Singer and Ed Getherall, with the National Golf Foundation that did a feasibility
        study on behalf of the Beach and Park District, provided further information on income and
        development cost projections for the course. Mr. Koski then reviewed the appraisal and provided
        additional information on the potential uses for the 29 acres. District members then offered comments.

Ill.    Public Comment

        The floor was opened for public comment. Larry Kobren, Harold Chaffee, Declan Murphy, Madeline
        Cuevas, Peggy Haupt, Dottie Provenzano, Robert DuKate, expressed their support of acquiring the
        Ocean Breeze course and the purchase price. Karl Dickey, Doug Coller, Angelo Sands, Cil Ciliberti,
        Glenn Gromann, Lenore Wachtel commented. Roger Kleinschmidt spoke in support of the Boca
        Municipal Golf Course. Judith Teller Kaye and Jay Slapp expressed concern with the cost of Ocean
        Breeze. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public comment period was closed.

        Chairman Rollins indicated that the District was looking for support from the Council to move forward.
        Members made final comments, voiced their concerns and stated their positions. The consensus of
        Council members was to support the District moving forward with the purchase of Ocean Breeze. Mayor
        Haynie then read into record the supportive comments of Jacob Jennings. Chairman Rollins
        acknowledged the support of the Council and stated that the District would move forward with the golf
        course purchase.

IV.     Adjournment

        The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 4:38 pm.
