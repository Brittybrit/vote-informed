    Regular Minutes                                          2                                            May 12, 2020
                      A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a conditional use approval pursuant to Section
                      28-1098(d), Code of Ordinances, to authorize the expansion of office use in an existing building
                      with a General Industrial (M-2) zoning district, for the property located at 4010 Oak Circle; providing
                      for repealer; providing an effective date (CA-19-01)

           The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. The City
           Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak. Council Members made their ex parte
           disclosures.

           Drew Capra, GIS Planner, gave a brief PowerPoint presentation. A description of the project location and
           history of the property development was provided. Mr. Capra reviewed the proposed change of use to
           incorporate office space and outlined the alterations to the parking areas. The traffic analysis was
           presented. The Planning and Zoning Board and Development Services staff recommend approval. Upon
           concluding, Mr. Capra responded to members' questions.

           The public hearing was opened. Charles Putman, on behalf of the applicant, reviewed the site plan and
           the modifications proposed to accommodate office use. Landscaping alterations within the project were
           discussed. Upon concluding, Mr. Putman made himself available for questions. There was no cross
           examination. Seeing no one else indicate that they wished to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                              Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andy
                              Thomson, to approve Resolution No. 33-2020. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott
                              Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member
                              Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

         MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Mayotte noted that the City's recommendation to use facial masks was not a requirement, but that
individual business owners may choose to require customers to wear them. Ms. Mayotte also commented that
members of the Boca Raton Chamber of Commerce had offered to assist City staff with the proposed small business
assistance program.

Deputy Mayor Rodgers stated he had also been contacted by the Chamber of Commerce offering to assist with the
proposed small business program and advised any final decisions would be made by City staff. He responded to
communications he had received from residents regarding the use of facial masks, beach openings and community
pools.

Council Member Andrea O'Rourke remarked on public comments submitted to Council and City staff for the virtual
Council meetings. She then opened a discussion regarding criteria for the City's small business assistance program.
City Manager Ahnell provided additional information. Members weighed in. Ms. O'Rourke commented on social
distancing and asked residents to be respectful of others.

Mayor Scott Singer noted that it was National Police Week and thanked law enforcement and first responders for
their service. He also reminded residents to be mindful of social distancing.
