10.     RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:

         A.      Government Campus Redevelopment Partnership Opportunity

                 1.       Reports from Staff

                  Deputy City Manager and Chief Financial Officer Jim Zervis gave a presentation on the
                  economic and fiscal impact analysis. Members from PFM Mallory Richards and Kevin Plenzler
                  provided an executive summary of the development overview, projected economic impacts,
                  and fiscal impacts. P3 review findings were highlighted , as well as the Public Development
                  Construction Impact. Methodology used to assess the data was described . The Pro Forma
                  analysis was reviewed and financing considerations were discussed, with Mr. Zervis providing
                  additional information . After sharing the Preliminary Series Bond Summaries, discussion
                  ensued with Mr. Zervis and PFM representatives answering Members' questions and
                  providing additional details.

                 2.       Public Comment

                 Mike Massare Ila, Susan Imrie, and Vic Rose spoke in favor of the Government Campus Project.

                 Lorraine Blank, Richard Warner, Martha Parker, Sue White, Michael Kohner, Joe Mahjess,
                 Richard Grubel , Mike Liebelson, Pam Paschke, Ivonne Zurn Tobel, Steve Zurn Tobel , Jonathan
                 Pearlman, Michelle Grau, Jacob Abraham, Mike Manko, Judy Morrow, Betsy Nieporte, Joseph
                 Graubart, Pedro Voyer, Meredith Madsen, Martin Bell, Mario Bennese, Eric Blanchette, and
                 Joseph Bujalski all spoke against the Government Campus Project.

 The meeting was recessed at 8:01 PM and resumed at 8:20 PM .

                 Discussion ensued, with Mr. Zervis and PFM providing answers to questions raised during
                 public comment and offering additional details and clarified facts.

          B.      Resolution No. 180-2025
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton, Florida, providing for submission to the electors of a
                  referendum question in connection with the proposed downtown campus public-private
                  partnership (P3) project, to be considered at the March 10, 2026, Uniform Municipal Election;
                  providing for severability; providing for repealer; and providing an effective date

                          Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Yvette
                          Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 18-2025 and send the ballot language to the
                          Supervisor of Elections to be included on the March 10, 2026 ballot following the bond
                          referendum question.

                  Council Member Thomson then proposed a new version of the ballot language that he
                  believed to be more neutral. He shared a PowerPoint presentation with Members and
                  explained his reasoning for requesting the amended language.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member
                          Marc Wigder, to amend the ballot language included in Resolution No. 180-2025.

                  Council Member Wigder then gave a PowerPoint presentation that addressed the resiliency
                  of the City, reviewed the timeline of the Government Campus Project, and endorsed a
                  balanced path forward. Members weighed in and suggested alternate versions of the
                  language. Lengthy discussion ensued .
