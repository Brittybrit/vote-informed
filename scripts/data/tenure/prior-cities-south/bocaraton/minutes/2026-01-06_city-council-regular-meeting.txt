         A.      Ordinance No. 5762
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28 , "Zoning," Article XV, Division
                 14, "Live Local Act of 2023 Affordable Housing," Sections 28-1637, 28-1638, 28-1639 and 28-
                 1640, Code of Ordinances, to align with Chapters 2024-188 and 2025-172, Laws of Florida
                 with respect to: maximum floor area ratio; reductions to minimum parking requirements;
                 limitations on minimum square footage of non-residential uses, maximum building height
                 adjacent to certain single-family residential areas; post-expiration treatment of developments
                 under the Live Local Act; affordability requirements and enforcement; exemptions for certain
                 parcels near airport runways; requiring affordable units to be rental and not owner-occupied ;
                 eliminating an appeal provision ; relocating standards for the determination of maximum
                 density and adding standards for maximum height and maximum floor area ratio (FAR) ;
                 providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification ; providing an
                 effective date (AM-24-06)

          Development Services Director Brandon Schaad gave the PowerPoint presentation . He provided a
          history of the Live Local Act passed in 2023 , highlighted recent changes enacted by the State
          legislature, and the outlined revisions the City was making to comply with those changes.

          The public hearing was opened and , seeing no one come forward , closed .

                          Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, seconded by Council Member
                          Yvette Drucker, to approve Ordinance No. 5762. Motion carried 4 votes to O; Mayor
                          Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                          Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

         F.      Ordinance No. 5766
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton , Florida, creating Chapter 13, "Public Lands," Article
                 Ill, "Sale of City property, " Section 13-75, "Protection of City-Owned Lands With Public
                 Purpose Safeguards, " Code of Ordinances, to establish requirements for the sale, lease, or
                 disposition of City-Owned Lands greater than one-half (0 .5) acre; providing that such actions
                 shall require two (2) public hearings and written findings that the action serves a public
                 purpose and benefits City residents; providing exceptions for easements, routine municipal
                 purposes, and certain leases or licenses; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                 providing an effective date

          Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided an overview of the ordinance and
          explained that it was intended to create a higher standard for the conveyance of public land greater
          than 0.5 an acre.

          The public hearing was opened . Jacob Abraham, Jon Pearlman, and Joe Mahjess spoke against the
          proposed ordinance. Seeing no one else come forward , the public hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Mayor Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Yvette
                          Drucker, to approve Ordinance No. 5766.

          Council Member Thomson shared that he appreciated the efforts to enhance protection of public
          lands but did not believe the ordinance went far enough . Members weighed in and brief discussion
          ensued .

                          Motion passed 3 votes to 1; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council
                          Member Yvette Drucker voting yes; Council Member Andy Thomson voting no.
