B.   Ordinance No. 5345
            An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton to amend Chapter 28 "Zoning," City's Code of
            Ordinances, relating to building height and height provisions; amending Section 28-391 to delete
            allowance for additional building height in the R-2 Zoning District; amending Sections 28-451, 28-
            481, and 28-511 to set the maximum height for accessory structures at 25 feet (and delete
            allowance for additional height) in the R-3-A, R-3-B, and R-3-C Zoning Districts; amending
            Section 28-780 to delete allowance for additional building height in the B-1 Zoning District;
            amending Sections 28-87 4, 28-983, 28-1074, and 28-1099 to delete the reference to "stories" and
            retain measurement of height in feet in the C-1, LIRP Employee Service Node, M-1 and M-2
            Zoning Districts; amending Section 28-1126 to delete allowance for additional height for
            accessory structures in the M-3 Zoning District; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
            providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-15-14)
                     Ordinance No. 5345

     Ingrid Allen, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation, noting that this public hearing was
     continued from the April 23, 2016 City Council meeting. She provided brief background information and
     then detailed the proposed changes to the various height provisions in Chapter 28. The changes to the
     ordinance recommended by the Planning & Zoning Board were reviewed. Ms. Allen then responded to
     questions about the specific recommendation to treat certain B-1 areas differently.

     The public hearing was opened. Robert Eisen, on behalf of Investments Limited and a number of other
     property owners, gave a PowerPoint presentation beginning with brief comments on B-1 zoning districts.
     He was joined by Doug Mummaw who advocated retaining flexibility in the zoning code. Mr. Eisen then
     spoke in opposition to the ordinance, suggested that a planning task force be created to review the bridge
     to ocean area of Palmetto Park Road and advocated tabling the ordinance. Kevin Meaney, Riviera Civic
     Association, voiced support of a task force to look at Palmetto Park Road east of the lntracoastal
     Waterway and advocated capping building height at 30 feet in that area. Mark Simmons voiced support of
     the ordinance as well as the task force. Glenn Gromann voiced his concerns regarding the ordinance,
     addressed flexibility, and advocated treating the east area of Palmetto Park Road differently. Nancy
     Hendrey agreed with comments of previous speaker regarding a different treatment of the east area of
     Palmetto Park Road.

     Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed. George Brown, Acting City
     Manager, responded to a question regarding creating a new zoning district in the bridge to ocean area of
     Palmetto Park Road. Council members offered their thoughts. In general, members agreed to go ahead
     with a task force, as proposed by several previous speakers, to look at a new zoning district for this area.
     Members commented. The City Attorney noted that a new zoning district could not be created through an
     amendment to this ordinance.

             Motion was made by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Scott
             Singer, to approve Ordinance No. 5345 as amended, with respect to the 8-1 district, to remove
             the strikethrough in Section 5, lines 16 through 19 and change "shall" to "may" on line 18.

     The City Attorney provided clarification and further information on Mr. Rodgers' proposed amendments
     with regard to theB-1 zoning district. Discussion ensued regarding the proposed amendments, tabling the
     ordinance, zoning in progress, and protecting residential areas abutting B-1 zoning districts from
     additional height.

             Motion was withdrawn by Council Member Rodgers.

     The City Attorney spoke to the time frame for zoning in progress versus declaring a moratorium to protect
     the status quo while a task force is underway.

             Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth to table Ordinance No. 5345. Motion
             died for lack of a second.

     Discussion of possible amendments to the ordinance continued.

        Motion was made by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Scott
        Singer, to approve Ordinance No. 5345 as amended to remove the strikethrough in Section 5,

A brief discussion ensued

        Motion was withdrawn by Council Member Rodgers.

        Motion failed on a 2 to 3 vote; Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Robert S.

The City Attorney commented on the task force. Acting City Manager Brown noted that the ordinance
could be adopted without section 5, leaving the B-1 zoning district unchanged. He provided information
on other sections of the ordinance that address height in other zoning districts, all of which staff felt
should be addressed. Mr. Brown noted that staff recommended adopting the ordinance as is.

        Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, seconded by Council Member Jeremy
        Rodgers, to approve Ordinance No. 5345 amended to delete Section 5.

Several suggestions for an amendment to the motion followed.

        Motion second by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers was withdrawn. Motion died for lack of a
        second.

        Motion was made by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Scott
        Singer, to approve Ordinance No. 5345 as amended to change Section 5.

The Mayor announced a short break to allow the City Attorney to craft the amendments.

The meeting was recessed at approximately 7:30 pm.

The meeting reconvened at approximately 7:45 pm.

The City Attorney detailed the proposed amendments, generally consistent with the following: in the title,
delete the phrase "amending Section 28-780 to delete allowance for additional building height in the B-1
Zoning District" and insert "replacing the word "shall" with "may" in Section 28-780; on page 3, line 25,
add after Code, "with the exceptions of amendments proposed for the B-1 Zoning District which were
modified"; and on page 5, lines 16 through 19, the deleted text will be reinstated and on line 18, the word
"shall" will be struck and the word "my" will be inserted.

        Motion was made by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Scott
        Singer, to approve Ordinance No. 5345 as amended. Motion failed on a 2 to 3 vote; Council
        Member Scott Singer, Council Member Jeremy voting yes; Rodgers Deputy Mayor Michael
        Mu/laugh, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, Mayor Susan Haynie voting no.

        Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Council Member Jeremy
        Rodgers, to table Ordinance No. 5345. Motion carried on a 4 to 1 vote; Deputy Mayor Michael
        Mu/laugh, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member
        Robert S. Weinroth voting yes; Mayor Susan Haynie voting no.

Council Member Singer addressed the proposed 1% sales tax and his desire to discuss the matter. He presented

downtown.
