 3.     PRESENTATION ON TENTATIVE BUDGET, PROPOSED MILLAGE RATES, ANO CAPITAL
        IMPROVEMENTS PROGRAM:

City Manager Leif Ahnell gave the PowerPoint presentations on the proposed budget and millage rate as well as
the proposed Capital Improvements Program. The City's guiding principles - Financially Sound City, World-Class
Municipal Services, Strong Partnership with Community and Vibrant and Sustainable City - were reviewed, noting
the policy priorities associated with these principles. Changes to the general fund operating costs for the 2019-
2020 fiscal year were highlighted, as were new personnel and programs. Various charts and tables were
presented illustrating year-over-year millage rates and assessed property valuations; allocation of property taxes;
and comparison of Boca Raton to other cities' fire assessment fees, property tax rates, and water/sewer rates.
The presentation was then turned over to Sharon McGuire, Office of Management and Budget Director, who
reviewed the 2019-2020 portion of the six-year Capital Improvements Program.                Highlights of various
infrastructure, vehicles and equipment, technology updates and renovations requested by City departments were
provided. Upon concluding, Ms. McGuire responded to questions, as did the City Manager.

 4.     PUBLIC HEARING ON TENTATIVE BUDGET ANO PROPOSED MILLAGE RATES:

         A.      Resolution No.101-2019 (City Clerk reads in full.)

                A resolution of the City of Boca Raton adopting proposed millage rates necessary to fund a
                tentative budget for the 2019-2020 fiscal year: providing for severability: providing for repealer;
                providing an effective date

         B.      Resolution No. 102-2019 (City Clerk reads title.)

                A resolution of the City of Boca Raton adopting a tentative budget for the 2019-2020 fiscal year;
                providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

        The City Clerk read Resolution No. 101-2019 in full and the title of Resolution No. 102-2019.

         C.      Discussion, public comments and explanation of "percentage increase in the proposed
                 millage rate over the rolled-back rate necessary to fund the budget, if any."

        Council members had no questions regarding the proposed millage rate or the tentative budget.

          D.     Open public hearing on tentative budget and proposed millage rates.
                        Presentations by non-profit organizations regarding funding requests.

        Mayor Singer opened the public hearing and invited representatives of the non-profit organizations
        present to make presentations in support of their funding requests. Representatives of 47 non-profit
        groups addressed the Council. Andrew Duffel, FAU Research Park, requested additional funding for the
        Park's international business soft landing program. He then responded to questions. Mr. Johnson
        commented on spending wisely.
          E.      City Council "shall amend the tentative budget as it sees fit."

        Deputy Mayor Rodgers suggested pulling out the request from the Mizner Park Cultural Arts Association
        (MPCAA) for staff to get more details and possibly treat it as a one-time line item. Council Member
        Mayotte also commented on the MPCAA. No other amendments to the tentative budget were suggested.

          F.       Re-computation of proposed millage rates.

        There was no re-computation of the millage rate.

          G.       Public announcement of percent, if any, by which recomputed proposed millage rates
                   exceed "rolled-back rate", characterized as "percentage decrease in property taxes
                   tentatively adopted."

        No announcement was necessary.

          H.       Adopt Resolution No. 101-2019 incorporating recomputed proposed millage rates.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Monica
                  Mayotte, to adopt Resolution No. 101-2019. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,
                  Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy
                  Thomson voting yes.

          I.       Adopt Resolution No. 102-2019 incorporating amended tentative budget.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andy
                  Thomson, to adopt Resolution No. 102-2019. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,
                  Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy
                  Thomson voting yes.

          J.       Close public hearing.

         The public hearing was closed.

 5.      RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS

There was no further business.

 6.      ADJOURNMENT

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 8:33 PM.




Susan S. Saxton, City Clerk
