 3.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARING:


         A.     Resolution No. 006-2026
                A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida, regarding a referendum
                petition affidavit submitted pursuant to City Charter Section 6.02; finding that the relief sought
                therein is duplicative of and already provided by a binding Citywide referendum scheduled for
                March 10, 2026; determining that the affidavit does not trigger the Charter's referendum
                process; directing the City Clerk not to process or accept signature pages submitted pursuant
                to said affidavit; providing for severability; providing an effective date

                City Manager Mark Sohaney briefly reviewed the resolution and asked the City Attorney to
                provide additional information . City Attorney Joshua Koehler reaffirmed that all related
                agreements, ordinances, and resolution were contingent on the March 10 municipal election ,
                noting that the referendum was already scheduled for the ballot and that both the City's legal
                team and outside counsel agreed Ordinance 5769 , passed January 20, 2026, was conditional
                on the election outcome.

                The public hearing was opened . Joe Majhess and Jonathan Ounjian expressed their support
                for the ordinance. Jon Pearlman, Richard Warner, and Lyudmilla Pearlman spoke against the
                ordinance. Seeing no one else come forward , the public hearing was closed.

                 Discussion ensued . The Mayor provided additional information in response to statements
                 made and Members expressed their support of the resolution .

                        Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, seconded by Council Member
                        Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 006-2026. Motion carried unanimously;
                        Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Fran Nachlas, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                        Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting yes.
