 12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A       Ordinance No. 5573
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 2, Article Ill, "Boards, Committees,
                  Commissions and Special Magistrates," Division 4, "Community Appearance Board," Section 2-
                  132, "Appeals," Code of Ordinances, to provide that the time period for filing an appeal to the City
                  Council of a decision of the Community Appearance Board shall begin upon the rendition of the
                  decision, and to affirm that the City Council shall consider the appeal upon the expiration of the
                  appeal period and the effectuation of public notice; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                  providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-21-03)

         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave a brief PowerPoint presentation. He explained that
         the proposed amendment would change the appeal window to begin upon execution of the resolution or
         order of decision. He noted that it was consistent with other appeal provisions in the Code and that staff
         recommended approval. Upon concluding, he made himself available for members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Seeing no one online or in-person indicate their desire to speak, the public
         hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member Andy
                          Thomson, to approve Ordinance No. 5573. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott
                          Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                          Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

 20.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Deputy Mayor O'Rourke continued the discussion of permitting virtual attendance by the public at City Council
meetings. Mr. Ahnell provided additional information. Members weighed in. Ms. O'Rourke then noted a virtual
presentation regarding a tree canopy assessment workshop. She concluded by noting the groundbreaking for the
relocated Community Garden and the upcoming Strategic Planning Workshop.

Council Member Drucker inquired when City boards would resume in-person meetings. Mr. Ahnell advised that the
Community Appearance Board and the Planning and Zoning Board would resume in-person meetings in June, and
that staff was currently analyzing space requirements for the other boards. Ms. Drucker then highlighted the return
of City-sponsored outdoor camps and commented on the recycling tips Council Member Mayotte had provided at
a previous meeting.

Council Member Mayotte inquired when Gumbo Limbo Nature Center would re-open. Mr. Ahnell stated that while
some portions of Gumbo Limbo were already opened, the interior portions were mostly operated by volunteers and
were not fully staffed at this time. He noted that the recruitment and processing of volunteers were under way.

Mayor Singer commented on the groundbreaking for the newly relocated and expanded Community Garden. He
then recognized National Law Enforcement Officer's Week and the City's virtual tribute for Police Officer Memorial
Day. Mr. Singer reviewed the schedule for the Strategic Planning Workshops and highlighted the public comment
times. In conclusion, he noted the broad availability of Covid-19 vaccines and encouraged all to stay safe.

 21.     ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 6:52 PM.
