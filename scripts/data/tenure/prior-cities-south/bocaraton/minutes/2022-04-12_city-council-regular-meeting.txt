 10.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

          A        Resolution No. 32-2022
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the property generally located at 6401,
                  6405, 6409, 6413 and 6421 Congress Avenue: (i) an amendment to an approved Planned
                  Mobility Development (PMD) Master Plan (Resolution No. 137-2020) to increase the square
                  footage of the 277-unit multifamily residential project and parking structure, increase the height
                  of the parking structure, reduce the number of parking spaces provided, make related site
                  modifications (and other minor site modifications), and eliminate a requirement to construct a
                  deceleration lane; (ii) site plan approval to authorize development of the 277-unit multifamily
                  residential project; and (iii) the abandonment of portions of a twelve ( 12)-foot water and sewer
                  easement and a twelve (12)-foot sewer easement; providing for repealer; providing an effective
                  date (PMD-18-01R1)

Regular Minutes                                            3                                                April 12, 2022
        The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing.
        Members made their ex-parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to
        speak.

        Tamash Rahman, Chief Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began with a description of
        the property and a review of the applicant's request. Components of the application included an
        amendment to the PMD master plan, a site plan approval, abandonment of a water and sewer easement
        and abandonment of a sewer easement. Elements of the previously approved Master Plan were noted.
        Ms. Rahman then reviewed requested modifications that included, but were not limited to, a parking
        deck on the sixth floor of the parking structure, removal of a previously approved deceleration lane on
        Congress Avenue, and construction of a 6-foot sidewalk along Congress Avenue. Drawings of the
        existing site, the proposed master and site plan were reviewed. The Community Appearance Board
        recommended approval. The previously approved modification of development parameters was
        reviewed. The traffic trip generation chart and proposed easement abandonments were discussed. Ms.
        Rahman advised that the project meets the intent of the PMD, and both the Planning and Zoning Board
        and staff recommend approval of the project with conditions. Upon concluding, Ms. Rahman responded
        to questions. Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, provided additional information.

        The public hearing was opened. Ele Zachariades, on behalf of the applicant, thanked staff for their
        presentation and made herself available for questions. Ryan Abrams, counsel for Millennium Two, LLC.,
        requested 45 minutes between himself and additional witnesses to present their opposition to the project.
        Mayor Singer advised that additional time could be offered at the discretion of the Council. City Attorney
        Diana Grub Frieser provided additional information regarding party status. Arturo Perez, traffic engineer
        speaking on behalf of Millennium Two, LLC, provided a handout and expressed concerns regarding the
        traffic study submitted by the applicant. Mr. Abrams asked for, and was granted, ten minutes to make
        his PowerPoint presentation and Ms. Frieser briefly described two handouts that he had provided to
        Council. Topics discussed by Mr. Abrams included the applicant's traffic engineering analysis, the
        removal of the deceleration lane, and parking and traffic impacts. Jonathan Ounjian, owner of the
        adjacent property, was sworn in and gave a PowerPoint presentation that expressed his opposition to
        the proposed resolution. Mr. Ounjian stated he believed the PMD matrix had been applied inconsistently
        and displayed slides showing comparisons of building entrances that are open to the street vs. those
        that are not, centroid parking, and how complementary uses are determined. Mr. Abrams then made
        closing comments. Chris DaSilva was sworn in and stated that he could assist the interested parties for
        this application. In response to a question by Mayor Singer, Brandon Schaad approximated the amount
        of time he and his staff had devoted to responding to the concerns put forth by Mr. Ounjian and his
        representatives. Mr. Schaad then responded to a number of points raised by the previous speakers and
        addressed questions by Council Members.

         During cross-examination, Mr. Abrams asked questions of Mr. Schaad, acting City Traffic Engineer
         Naresh Machavarapu, and Mr. Perez. Mr. Perez then cross-examined Mr. Machavarapu and Mr.
         Schaad. In conclusion, Mr. Ounjian cross-examined Mr. Schaad. In response to a request from the
         audience, the City Attorney noted that there had been opportunity for public comment earlier in the public
         hearing and the proceeding had moved on to cross-examination. On behalf of the applicant, Ele
         Zachariades summarized the delays that had occurred in the processing of the application in order to
         address Mr. Ounjian's concerns. Ms. Zachariades addressed the project's amendments to the Master
         Plan, the PMD Matrix and the removal of the deceleration lane. Upon concluding, she made herself
         available for Members' questions. The public hearing was closed.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                         Monica Mayotte, to grant Resolution No. 32-2022 with the amendments requested by
                         the applicant.

         Council Members made final comments.

                         Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine
                         O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                         Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
