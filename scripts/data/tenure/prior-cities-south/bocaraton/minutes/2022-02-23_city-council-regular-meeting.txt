 11.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A      Ordinance No. 5604
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering an amendment to the City's Comprehensive
                 Plan to create a property rights element; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                 providing an effective date (SC-21-06)

          Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave a brief presentation. He explained that the
          2021 State Legislature required all governments in Florida to adopt a property rights element to their
          Comprehensive Plan. The proposed ordinance was approved by Council for transmittal to the State in
          December. Having received no substantive comments from the Department of Economic Opportunity,
          Development Services staff recommended adoption. Upon concluding, Mr. Schaad made himself
          available for members' questions.

 19.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

 Council Member Mayotte noted the competition among Florida municipalities called the Florida Race to Zero
 and explained that the project's goal was to encourage commitment to net zero emissions for climate neutrality.
 Ms. Mayotte expressed her desire for the City to participate and offered to enlist speakers to come make a
 presentation. Discussion ensued, and there was consensus for members to hear a presentation from staff at
 a future meeting.

  Council Member Drucker commented on her participation at the State Summit of the Transportation Planning
  Agency (TPA) and noted that City Traffic Engineer Maria Tejera and the City had been nominated for awards.
  She announced her intention to include discussion on Vision Zero at this year's Strategic Planning and remarked
