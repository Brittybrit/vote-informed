3.     PRESENTATION ON TENTATIVE BUDGET, PROPOSED MILLAGE RATES, AND CAPITAL
       IMPROVEMENTS PROGRAM:

City Manager Leif Ahnell gave PowerPoint presentations on the proposed budget and millage rate. He began by
reviewing the City's guiding principles - a Financially Sound City, World Class Municipal Services, Strong
Partnership with Community, and Vibrant and Sustainable City - and noted policy priorities associated with these
principles. Tables and charts were presented illustrating millage rates and assessed property valuations, and
allocation of operating expenses. New items proposed for FY 2021-22 included, but were not limited to, projects,
personnel, and programs. Mr. Ahnell reviewed the City flyer that explains residents' property tax bill. He
highlighted comparisons of Boca Raton to nearby cities' fire assessment fees, property tax rates, and water and
sewer rates. He reported a small increase in water, sewer, and sanitation rates. The presentation was then turned
over to Sharon McGuire, Office of Management and Budget Director, who reviewed the 2021-22 portion of the
six-year Capital Improvements Program. Upon concluding, Ms. McGuire responded to questions, as did the City
Manager.

4.     PUBLIC HEARING ON TENTATIVE BUDGET AND PROPOSED MILLAGE RATES:

        A.     Resolution No.118-2021
               A resolution of the City of Boca Raton adopting proposed millage rates necessary to fund a
               tentative budget for the 2021-2022 fiscal year; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
               providing an effective date

        B.     Resolution No. 119-2021
               A resolution of the City of Boca Raton adopting a tentative budget for the 2021-2022 fiscal year;
               providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

        The City Clerk read Resolution No. 118-2021 in full and the title of Resolution No. 119-2021".°

        C.     Discussion, public comments, and explanation of "percentage increase in the proposed
               millage rate over the rolled-back rate necessary to fund the budget, if any."

        Council members had no questions regarding the proposed millage rate or the tentative budget.

        D.     Open public hearing on tentative budget and proposed millage rates.

       Mayor Singer opened the public hearing, noting that submissions had been received by representatives of
       the non-profit organizations. No one indicated their desire to speak.
         E.      City Council "shall amend the tentative budget as it sees fit."

         The City Manager noted no amendments were made to the tentative budget.

         F.      Re-computation of proposed millage rates.

         There was no re-computation of the millage rate.

         G.      Public announcement of percent, if any, by which recomputed proposed millage rates
                 exceed "rolled-back rate", characterized as "percentage decrease in property taxes
                 tentatively adopted."

         No announcement was necessary.

         H.      Adopt Resolution No. 118-2021 incorporating recomputed proposed millage rates.

                            Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member

         I.       Adopt Resolution No. 119-2021 incorporating amended tentative budget.

5.      RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS

                 Resolution No. 120-2021
         A resolution of the City of Boca Raton imposing Fire Services Assessments against Assessed Property
         (residential, commercial, and industrial) located within the City for the fiscal year beginning October 1,
         2021; approving the fiscal year 2021-2022 rate of assessment; approving the fiscal year 2021-2022
         Assessment Roll (based on the rate of assessment); providing for severability; providing for repealer;
         providing an effective date

         Sharon McGuire gave a brief review on the fire assessment fee, noting that there was no proposed
         increase. Mr. Ahnell provided additional information.

         The public hearing was opened. Seeing no one indicate their desire to speak, the public hearing was
         closed.

                             Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                             Andrea Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 120-2021. Motion carried
                             unanimously on a voice vote; Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                             Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
