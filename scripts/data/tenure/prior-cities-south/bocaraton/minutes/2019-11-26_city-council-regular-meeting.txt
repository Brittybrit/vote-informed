 16.       RESOLUTIONS AND OTHER BUSINESS:

             A       Resolution No. 148-2019
                     A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering the application of 600 SW 3 LLC, for a settlement
                     and reduction of one (1) Code Enforcement lien imposed against property located at 600 SW 3rd
                     Street; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date

           Doug Sheedy, Code Compliance Manager, gave a brief overview and provided background information on
           the code violations, noting that staff does not support the lien reduction. Upon concluding, he made himself
           available for questions.

           The public hearing was opened. Paul Carnrick, applicant, described circumstances leading to the code
           violations on the property. He explained that expired permits had since been closed out and the property
           had recently been sold. Upon concluding, Mr. Carnrick responded to members' questions, as did staff. The
           public hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member Andrea
                          Levine O'Rourke, to reduce the fine by half, to $3,307.50.


       Regular Minutes                                      4                                   November 26, 2019
          Discussion ensued.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                         Member Monica Mayotte, to amend the motion on the floor to reduce the fine to $5,000.
                         Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

           The City Attorney clarified that the lien would be reduced by $1,615 to a total amount of $5,000 and that
           payment in full was to be made within 30 days.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member
                         Monica Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 148-2019 reducing the lien to $5,000. Motion
                         carried on a 4 to 1 vote; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member
                         Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes; Council Member
                         Monica Mayotte voting no.

 20.       MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member O'Rourke asked for additional information regarding potential environmental impacts of the
proposed train station. Members weighed in. Council Member Mayotte suggested Virgin Train USA representatives
be asked to address additional environmental concerns of citizens.

Council Member Mayotte noted that she is working on a resolution regarding single use plastic bags and foam
containers. She thanked Police Chief Alexander for his service and congratulated Interim Chief Michele Miuccio.
Ms. Mayotte reminded residents to be vigilant about their safety during the holidays.

Deputy Mayor Rodgers congratulated Police Chief Alexander on his new position with the Palm Beach County
School Board and commented on the bike path discussion from Monday's Workshop.

Mayor Singer thanked his fellow elected officials and City staff for their service and wished everyone a Happy
Thanksgiving.
