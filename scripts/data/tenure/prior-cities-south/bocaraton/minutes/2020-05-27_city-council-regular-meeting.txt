 11:.    QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

          A.       Ordinance No. 5523
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering a universal conditional (UC) rezoning, pursuant
                  to Article VI, Chapter 23, Code of Ordinances, of property consisting of approximately 4.984 acres,
                  generally located at 1489 and 1499 West Palmetto Park Road, from Motel-Business (R-B-1) to
                  Professional, Office and Institutional (POI), subject to conditions; providing for the appropriate
                  revision of the Zoning District Map; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (UC-19-
                  02/ZC)

          B.       Resolution No. 63-2020
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering an amendment to an approved site plan
                  (Planning and Zoning Resolution No. 83-33R), pursuant to a universal conditional (UC) rezoning
                  request, to authorize the construction of an additional 16,296 square feet of office space, on an
                  approximately 4.984 acre property generally located at 1489 and 1499 West Palmetto Park Road;
                  providing for repealer; providing an effective date (UC-19-02/SPA)

The Mayor advised the public hearings on items 11. A. and B. would be conducted contemporaneously as the items
are related, and a separate vote would be taken on each item. The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial
procedures that would govern the virtual public hearing. Council Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City
Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

Osny Jean, Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began with a review of the petitioner's request and a
description of the property. Elements of the proposed rezoning and site plan amendment were discussed. Drawings
of the pedestrian connection requested by the Planning and Zoning Board were displayed. Mr. Jean presented
results of the traffic study, followed by an explanation of the parking agreement condition with a neighboring
business. The Planning and Zoning Board and Development Services staff recommend approval. Upon concluding,
Mr. Jean made himself available for members' questions.

The public hearing was opened. Matthew Scott gave a presentation on behalf of the applicant. Photographs of the
existing property were compared with renderings of the proposed alterations. Upon concluding, he made himself
available for members' questions. Glenn Gromann expressed his support for the proposed project. There was no
cross examination or rebuttal. Mr. Scott made final comments. Seeing no one else indicate that they wished to
speak, the public hearing was closed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                  Rodgers, to approve Ordinance No. 5523 as revised.

For clarity, the City Attorney provided language regarding the motion.

 12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

          A.       Ordinance No. 5517
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton relating to the Sign Code of the City; repealing Chapter 24,
                  "Signs," Code of Ordinances, in its entirety; adopting a new Chapter 24, "Signs," Code of
                  Ordinances; repealing Section 302.1.3, "Signs," of the "Supplemental Criteria and Procedural Rules
                  of the Community Appearance Board," as enacted by Ordinance No. 2110, and as subsequently
                  amended; repealing the "Sign Design Criteria: A Guideline to Acceptable Sign Design" in its
                  entirety, as enacted by Ordinance No. 3796, and as subsequently amended; amending Chapter
                  28, Zoning, Article XI, "Business and Commercial Districts," Division 11, "City CG Commercial
                  General," and Division 12, "City CHO Commercial High Office," Sections 28-933 and 28-939, to
                  provide for the regulation of noncommercial temporary signs in such zoning districts; providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-18-
                  02)

          B.       Ordinance No. 5518
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton creating Chapter 28, "Zoning," Article XV, Division 1,
                  Section 28-1313, "Flags and Flag Poles," Code of Ordinances, to provide regulations for flags and
                  flag poles; amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Article I, Section 28-2, "Definitions," Code of
                  Ordinances, to add a definition of "Flag"; amending Chapter 26, "Subdivision Regulations," Article
                  111, Division 1, Section 26-102, "Median Strip Beautification and Use," Section 26-103, "Placement,
                  Location of Subdivision Entrance Signs," and Section 26-104, "Optional City Responsibility for
                  Maintenance of Subdivision Entrance Signs," Code of Ordinances, to update the terminology
                  relating to subdivision entrance features and delete provisions relating to the placement and
                  location of subdivision entrance signs; creating Chapter 28, "Zoning," Article XII, Division 7, "M-2
                  General Industrial District," Section 28-1111, "Signage; Student Housing," Code of Ordinances,
                  relating to signage for uses accessory to a student housing facility; providing for severability;
                  providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-19-03)

          C.      Resolution No. 68-2020
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton amending the Boca Raton Municipal Facilities and Services
                  User Fee Schedule to set forth fees for new application processes included in the adoption of a
                  new Chapter 24, "Signs," Code of Ordinances; providing for severability; providing for repealer;
                  providing an effective date

        The Mayor advised the public hearings on items 12. A., B., and C. would be conducted contemporaneously
        as the items are related, and a separate vote would be taken on each item.

         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began with an
         overview of the amendments, citing changes in case law and issues with the existing sign regulations that
         necessitated the need for updates to the sign code. The results of a 2017 Community Appearance Board
         roundtable were discussed. Topics included, but were not limited to, streamlining of the approval process,
         greater design flexibility and the use of logos for brand consistency. Other significant issues included
         banners, temporary real estate signs, and temporary residential election campaign signs. Proposed
         changes regarding flags and flag poles were also discussed. Mr. Schaad highlighted a recommended
         revision to the proposed flag ordinance regarding allowable flagpole height in relation to building height.
         The Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval of the Sign Code ordinance by a vote of 3 ~o 1
         and approved the companion ordinance by a vote of 4 to 0. Staff recommends approval. Upon concluding,
         Mr. Schaad responded to members' questions. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser provided clarification.
