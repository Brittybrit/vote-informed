          E.      Ordinance No. 5561
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending the Police and Firefighters' Retirement System;
                  amending Section 12-134, "Administration" and Section 12-151, "Retirement Dates and Benefits;
                  Optional Provisions for Firefighters" of Article IV, "Pension Plan for Police and Firefighters' of
                  Chapter 12, "Pensions and Retirement," Code of Ordinances, effectuating the pension related
                  provisions of the Collective Bargaining Agreement between the City and the IAFF (Firefighters
                  Union); providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an
                  effective date

          Michael Woika, Deputy City Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Woika noted that the most
          noticeable change to the plan was the extension of the deferred retirement option plan (DROP) period. It
          was noted that changes to the pension plan were associated with firefighters only and not police officers,
          as the only negotiations completed so far had been with the firefighters' union. Mr. Woika reviewed the
          modifications made to the ordinance. Comments submitted by Bob Sugarman, attorney for the Police and
          Firefighters' Pension Board, were read into the record. Mr. Woika then shared responses to the comments
          and addressed questions about administration, delay, postponement, "undropping" and revisions. The
          actuarial impact statement reflected a contribution increase of $167,707. Staff recommends approval of
          the ordinance. Upon concluding, he made himself available for members' questions. The City Attorney
          offered additional clarification of revisions to the ordinance that were not readily apparent due to the
          lightness of the shading. In response to Mr. Sugarman's comments regarding DROP participation, Ms.
          Frieser and Mr. Woika provided additional clarification concerning section 5.

          The public hearing was opened. David Eddinger, President of Firefighters of Boca Raton Local 1560,
          expressed his support of the ordinance. Seeing no one else indicate their desire to speak, the public
          hearing was closed.

                                  Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council
                                  Member Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5561.

          Mayor Singer suggested additional language be added to provide clarification to the end of Section 5,
          page 6, line 3, " ... at which point the member's employment shall terminate." Ms. Frieser noted that while
          the proposed wording did reflect what the section provides, the existing language had already been
          bargained and negotiated with the union. Ms. Frieser also noted that the City's pension lawyer had
          suggested the language be kept as is. The Mayor inquired the wording could be added to the meeting
          minutes, to which Ms. Frieser agreed, and she reiterated that the language of the plan clearly provides
          that the end of the DROP period and the end of member's employment is coterminous. There was
          consensus among members to have the language added to the minutes. Mayor Singer noted that his vote
          to approve the ordinance would be predicated on its inclusion. Deputy Mayor O'Rourke pointed out that
          the communication from Pension Board trustee John Girard was not received until just prior to the meeting.

                                  Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine
                                  O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte,
                                  Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
