                  Palmetto Park Road; providing for repealer, providing an effective date (CA-17-03)
                           Resolution No. 55-2018
                           Drawings

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
         made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to all those who wished to speak.

         Jim Bell, Acting Deputy Director of Development Services, gave the PowerPoint presentation beginning
         with a review of the petitioner's request. The location and zoning of the subject property were identified.
         Details of the proposed project were provided. The site plan was reviewed, noting that the addition of the
         car wash required the conditional use approval. Staffs analysis of the conditional use showed that the
         request met the requirements of the Code. Technical deviations related to driveways and egress were
         also approved by staff. The Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval of the conditional use
         and approved the site plan. Staff recommended approval of the conditional use with the conditions
         included in the resolution.

         The public hearing was opened. Jamie Crowley, Gunster Law Firm on behalf of the petitioner, gave a
         PowerPoint presentation in support of the application. He addressed various aspects of the proposal and
         provided details of the project, noting that they had been working with staff to address the site constraints.
         Concerns expressed in an email from the neighboring property owner were addressed. Mr. Crowley then
         turned the presentation over to Danny Alonso, architect for the project. Mr. Alonso reviewed the site plan
         and provided details of the design of the facility. Information on the operation of the car wash was
         provided. He then responded to Council members' questions. Seeing no one else came forward to speak,
         the public hearing was closed.

                       Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                       Member Monica Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 55-2018.

         The City Attorney referenced page 6 of the Planning and Zoning Board resolution, Exhibit 1 to Resolution
         55-2018, which lists the conditions of approval. She suggested that condition 3 in Section 7 would more
         appropriately be designated as "h", with a, band c renamed 1, 2 and 3. Conditions 4 and 5 would then be
         renumbered 3 and 4. Both Ms. O'Rourke and Ms. Mayotte agreed to incorporate the changes into their
         motion.

                       Motion on Resolution No. 55-2018 as amended carried on a 3 to O vote; Deputy Mayor Scott
                       Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke voting
                       yes.

         A.       Ordinance No. 5444
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering amendments to the transportation element of
                  the City's Comprehensive Plan; amending an objective and policy to establish an alternative
                  transportation concurrency standard (ATCS) for the Boca Royale Planned Unit Development
                  (PUD} to satisfy traffic concurrency along Northwest 2nd Avenue/East Country Club Boulevard
                  north of Yamato Road, subject to certain mitigation measures and conditions, and authorizing
                  execution of a mitigation agreement; deleting references to the Ocean Breeze Development, an
                  expired project; deleting obsolete statutory references; reaffirming that an ATCS may only be
                  authorized by the City Council through an amendment to the Comprehensive Plan; considering a
                  universal conditional amendment to the Future Land Use Map of the Comprehensive Plan by
                  redesignating certain property consisting of approximately 55.12 acres, generally located at 7601
                   East Country Club Boulevard, from Recreation and Open Space (PR) to Residential Low (RL),
                  subject to conditions; providing for revisions to the Future Land Use Map; providing for
                  severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-17-09)
                          Ordinance No. 5444
                          Drawings

         Deputy Mayor Singer noted that this was a transmittal hearing and if the ordinance was not transmitted,
         Ordinance 5445, the rezoning, would not be considered for introduction.

         Jim Bell, Acting Deputy Director of Development Services, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He noted
         that Council would only be considering transmitting the Comprehensive Plan Amendments to the State
         Department of Economic Opportunity. The applicant's overall request that included a rezoning, master
         plan approval, tentative plat approval and site plan approval, was reviewed and the project was
         described, including location, zoning and number of units proposed. Details were provided. Mr. Bell then
         provided staffs analysis of the projects compatibility with the Comp Plan. Topics included the future land
         use change; concurrency of water/sewer, drainage, public schools, and transportation. The traffic analysis
         was reviewed. While the project was noted to be consistent with some policies and objectives in the
         Comp Plan, it was inconsistent with the elements related to recreation and open space as well as
Regular Minutes                                        4                                                  April 24, 2018
                                                         nd
         transportation, in particular the failure of NW 2 Avenue despite the applicant's proposal of a payment in
         mitigation agreement and establishment of an ATCS. These inconsistencies were the reasons to
         recommend denial of the Comp Plan Amendments. The specific language of the proposed amendments
         was presented. Mr. Bell then discussed the Comp Plan objective related to coastal high hazard areas
         and flood zones as related to the construction of homes on the property. The Planning and Zoning Board
         voted to deny recommendation. Staff did not recommend approval of nor transmittal of the Comp Plan
         Amendment.

         Deputy Mayor Singer noted that he had expressed concerns with the project. He then opened the public
         hearing. Michael Marshall, Gunster Law Firm on behalf of the petitioner, began by requesting a
         continuance. He then reviewed the benefits of the project. Mr. Marshall addressed the review criteria for
         consistency with the Comp Plan, the transportation element, existing deficient roadways, the coastal high
         hazard zone and related topics. The developer's offer to provide bike paths and open space access was
         noted. Mr. Marshall countered a number of statements made in the staff presentation. Staff provided
         information. The City Attorney also provided information regarding the City's flood maps and updating of
         same. A brief discussion of open space followed.

         Rob Berthiaume offered his opinion that the project was a bad fit for the area. He thanked the Council
         and staff for their openness and willingness to talk with residents. He then supported recreational space,
         noting that residents are not opposed to any development of the property. Bob Boggs, vice president of
         the Hidden Lake Property Owners Association, agreed with the previous speaker and then addressed
         points made with regard to traffic and schools. Deputy Mayor Singer provided information on schools.
         Rosie Service provided a map handout and stated her opposition to building more residences and giving
         up recreation space. Angelo Sands thanked the residents in his community who spoke up about this
         proposed development. Ray Smolenski asked that the way in which traffic affects emergency services be
         taken into account. Jack McWalter commented on the school capacity letters and school overcrowding.
         Harold Chaffee advocated keeping the property as open space. Seeing no one else come forward to
         speak, the public hearing was closed.

         The City Attorney provided information on the procedure for considering an amendment to the
         Comprehensive Plan and the transmittal of same.

                  An Ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Article V, "Qualifications and
                  Elections," Section 5.03, "Qualifications," of the Charter of the City of Boca Raton to
                  modify the requirements to qualify as a candidate for City Council, including the office of
                  Mayor, by: (i) extending the length of the residency requirement, establishing certain
                  requirements for residency, and requiring proof of such residency (to be prescribed by
                  Ordinance), and (ii) eliminating the $25 qualifying fee and instead requiring the verified
                  signatures of a specified number of registered voters of the City, and providing for
                  procedures for collection, submission, and verification of such signatures (to be
                  prescribed by Ordinance); providing for a special election regarding the proposed
                  Charter amendments to be held on August 28, 2018 (the date of the primary election);
                  amending Chapter 6, "Elections," of the Code of Ordinances to create (subject to
                  approval of one, or both, of the above Charter amendments) a new Section 6-16, "Proof
                  of Residency," prescribing procedures for establishing residency, and a new Section 6-
                  17, "Filing of Petition," prescribing procedures for the collection, submission, and
                  verification of signatures; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for
                  revisions to the City Charter and codification in the City Code, respectively; providing an
                  effective date
                             Ordinance No. 5453

         City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser briefly explained the ordinance brought forward at the direction of the
         Council, noting that the Charter amendments would be placed on the August 28, 2018 primary election
         ballot. Deputy Mayor Singer offered Council Member Mayotte, as the newly-elected member, the
         opportunity to weigh in on the ordinance. Discussion followed on the ordinance and a continuance of the
         public hearing to a date certain. The City Attorney addressed the possibility of a continuance and the
         deadline for submitting ballot question language.

         The City Attorney provided information.
