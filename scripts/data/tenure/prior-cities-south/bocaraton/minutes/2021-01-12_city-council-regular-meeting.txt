 11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

           A.      Resolution No. 1-2021
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the Spanish River Presbyterian Church
                  located at 2400 West Yamato Road: (i) an amended and restated conditional use approval
                  consolidating conditional use Resolution Nos. 1-82, 108-85, 36-94 and 203-94, and (ii) an
                  amendment to the approved conditional use approval (Resolution No. 1-82, as amended) to
                  authorize demolition of the existing outdoor pavilion cafeteria, and construction of an approximately
                  24,410 square foot cafetorium, classroom and office building, and a 1,144 square foot covered
                  walkway; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (CA-20-03)

         The City Attorney read the quasi-judicial procedures which would govern the public hearing. Members made
         their ex parte disclosures. The Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

         Heather Hansen, senior planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began by describing the existing
         property and reviewed components of the applicant's request. A site plan of the property and drawings of
         the proposed building were displayed, and an abandonment of a utility easement was discussed. The
         Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval. Ms. Hansen then reviewed a request by the applicant
         to revise condition of approval #9 for the amended site plan, concerning a management plan for an existing
         one acre preserve. Proposed revisions to the site plan resolution were shown. Development Services staff
         recommended approval of both the conditional use and the proposed amendment to site plan condition #9.

         The public hearing was opened. Lindsay Libes and David Ofstein, on behalf of the applicant, thanked
         Development Services staff for their work and made themselves available for members' questions. There
         was no cross examination or rebuttal. Seeing no one else indicate their desire to speak, the public hearing
         was closed.

                                  Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                                  Andrea Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 1-2021 as amended. Motion
                                  carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke,
                                  Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                                  Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

           B.      Ordinance No. 5555 (First of two public hearings. No vote will be taken.)
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28 "Zoning," Code of Ordinances;
                  amending the lists of permitted and conditional uses in all zoning districts regarding governmental
                  uses and facilities; amending Chapter 28, Article XIII, Division 5, "PL Public Land District," Section
                  28-1238, "Development Parameters," and Article XV, Section 28-1305, "City and beach and parks
                  district parks facilities; City water, sewer, reclaimed water, and stormwater facilities; City buildings,
                  facilities and uses; and City police and fire stations," Code of Ordinances, to: (i) specify whether
                  the Planning and Zoning Board, the Parks and Recreation Board, or the City Council has the
                  authority to review and approve site plan applications for certain City and other Governmental uses;
                  (ii) provide for setback and landscape buffer requirements (for certain City and other governmental
                  uses in nonresidential zoning districts) to be determined as part of a site plan review and approval
                  (except where abutting a residential zoning district); and (iii) delete obsolete language and
                  redundant requirements with respect to such City and other Governmental uses; amending Article
                  XIII, Division 2, "LB Civic Center and Limited Business District," Section 28-1173, "Uses Permitted,"
                  and creating Section 28-1173.1, "Conditional Uses," to require conditional use approval for certain
                  uses that already require City Council approval; reformatting and restating various provisions for
                  clarity; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an
                  effective date (AM-19-01)

         The Mayor noted that this was the first of two public hearings and no vote would be taken.

         Jim Bell, Long Range Project Manager, made the PowerPoint presentation. He began with a summary of
         the ordinance, noting that it was primarily a streamlining measure. He stated that the amendment would
         correct inconsistencies in provisions for a number of sections in the current code, and he briefly reviewed
         the affected sections. Both the Planning and Zoning Board and Development Services staff recommended
         approval. Upon concluding, Mr. Bell made himself available for members' questions. Council Member
         Mayotte asked Mr. Bell about the concerns of the Planning and Zoning Board, specifically in relation to the
         O5C school. Mr. Bell provided additional information.

         The public comment period was opened, and seeing no one indicate their desire to speak, closed. Mayor
         Singer advised the second public hearing on this item would be on January 26, 2021.

          C.       Ordinance No. 5556
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 23, "Planning and Development," Article
                  IV, Division 4, "Sidewalks and Bikeways," Section 23-162, "Sidewalk Policy," Code of Ordinances,
                  to exclude the south side of Yamato Road (between Military Trail and Saint Andrews Boulevard
                  only) from the eight foot minimum sidewalk width requirement; providing for severability; providing
                  for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-20-10)

         Jean Osny, Senior Planner, made the PowerPoint presentation. He began by reviewing the proposed
         amendment and existing sidewalk policy. Mr. Jean provided an analysis of the amendment, including a
         "special corridor" sidewalk standard due to the existing uses in the area. The Planning and Zoning Board
         recommended approval of the ordinance by a vote of 5 to 1.

         The public hearing was opened. Neil Schiller, on behalf of B'Nai Israel, commented in support of the
         ordinance, citing the financial hardship that the requirement would cause to the affected non-profit
         properties. David Ofstein and Ronald Tobias, on behalf of Spanish River Church, also expressed their
         support of the ordinance, citing low pedestrian traffic on that specific stretch of road and the economic
         impact to the religious organizations. Seeing no one else indicate their desire to speak, the public comment
         period was closed.

         Council Member Thomson expressed his support of the ordinance, noting the low amount of pedestrian
         traffic of the subject area. Deputy Mayor O'Rourke and Council Member Mayotte shared their opposition to
         the item, citing their commitment to walkability and safety. Mayor Singer commented in support of the
         ordinance. Council Member Drucker questioned if voting in favor of the ordinance would be setting a
         precedent for future sidewalk exemption requests. Discussion ensued. Brandon Schaad, Development
         Services Director, provided additional information.

                                   Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Mayor Scott
                                   Singer, to approve Ordinance No. 5556. Motion failed on a 2 to 3 vote; Mayor Scott
                                   Singer and Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes; Deputy Mayor Andrea
                                   Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, and Council Member Monica
                                   Mayotte voting no.
