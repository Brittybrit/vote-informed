         C.       Ordinance No. 5661
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Article X, "Multifamily
                 Residential Districts," Code of Ordinances, to: (i) specify that the maximum density set forth in the
                 R-3, R-3-A, R-3-B, R-3-C, R-4, R-5 and R-5-A multifamily zoning districts is applicable to single­
                 family residences and duplexes in such districts (where permitted), and (ii) affirm the minimum plot
                 width, minimum plot depth, and minimum plot area for single-family residences in the R-4 district;
                 providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective
                 date (AM-22-09)

          Mayor Singer advised that this was the first of two public hearings, and no vote would be taken.

          Tori Boone, Zoning Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began with a brief overview and
          described the proposed text amendment. She advised that both staff and the Planning and Zoning Board
          recommended approval. Upon concluding, she made herself available for questions. Discussion ensued
          and Ms. Boone answered Members' questions.

          The public comment period was opened. Ele Zachariades spoke on behalf of her client and gave an
          overview of the proposed changes. Upon concluding, she made herself available for questions. Seeing
          no one else come forward, the public comment period was closed .

         D.       Ordinance No. 5662
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Article I, Section 28-8,
                 "Notice Requirements for Certain Public Hearings," Code of Ordinances to provide public hearing
                 notice requirements for: (i) Individual Development Approval (IDA) applications in Downtown Boca
                 Raton pursuant to Ordinance No. 4035, as amended (ii) amendments to Ordinance No. 4035, (iii)
                 Developments of Regional Impact (ORI) or amendments thereto; and (iv) any development
                 approval (or amendment thereto) within a ORI; amending Section 28-8 for clarity and improved
                 organization; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing
                 an effective date (AM-23-01)

          Senior Planner Peter Begovich gave the PowerPoint presentation . He gave an overview of the proposed
          changes, amendments, and advised that staff and the Planning & Zoning Board recommended approval.
          Upon concluding, he made himself available for questions.

          The public comment period was opened and, seeing no one come forward, closed.

19.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Deputy Mayor Monica Mayotte gave an overview of polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) and the detriment to the
environment, citizens, employees, and our water supply . She advised that there is a settlement pending and she
is working with the City Attorneys to get onboard for the settlement. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser affirmed that
a recommendation will be brought forward for the Council to consider entering a settlement. Discussion ensued.

Council Member Wigder thanked his colleagues and the public for their participation during the CRA and Workshop
meetings from the previous day.

Council Member Yvette Drucker advised she had conducted additional research to gain more information related
to SB 102 (Live Local Act). She then reported that work was being done on the interlocal government agreement
with the Transportation Planning Agency (TPA) and reported on other transit related issues. She also highlighted
Hispanic Heritage Month and thanked the Mayor for a forthcoming proclamation. Discussion ensued.

Mayor Scott Singer highlighted City events including the naming of Lois Martin Way outside of Ebeneezer Baptist
Church and wished a Happy New Year to everyone celebrating the Jewish Holidays.
