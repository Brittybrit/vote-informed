12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

                  Ordinance No. 5455
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton repealing Chapter 19, Code of Ordinances, in its entirety;
                  adopting an updated Chapter 19, Code of Ordinances, entitled "Building Regulations"; adopting
                  the Florida Building Code 6th Edition (2017), and local amendments to the administrative
                  provisions of the Florida Building Code relating to its administration, interpretation, application
                  and enforcement; providing for definitions and applicability; establishing the Code Compliance
                  Division as the Department of Building Safety; providing for the powers and duties of the building
                  official; providing for regulations relating to permit and plan submittal and review, permit issuance,
                  fees, and inspections; providing for regulations relating to occupancy, completion and utility
                  service connections; providing for regulations relating to violations and appeals; providing for the
                  Builders' Board of Adjustment and Appeals; providing for regulations relating to unsafe structures
                  and equipment; adopting local technical amendments relating to flood-resistant design
                  requirements; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing
                  an effective date (AM-17-14)
                            Ordinance No. 5455
                            Chapter 19 - Showing Revisions

         John Cosmo, Building Official, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Cosmo began with historical
         information and then reviewed the content of the building code. Information on the permit process was
         provided. Topics included, but were not limited to, permit application submission, plan review and
         building inspections, and improved Insurance Service Office rating based on the City's enforcement of the
         building code. The significant updates in Chapter 19 were highlighted. Mr. Cosmo explained the revision
         to the table detailing the elevation for buildings. He then made himself available for questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Glenn Gromann complimented the City's building department and
         commented on insurance rates and residential building height. Kathy Cottrell asked that historical
         buildings be included. Brandon Schaad addressed the comment about measurement of the height of a
         home. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

         Mayor Singer reviewed the revision to the table on page 59 provided by Mr. Cosmo.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member
                  Monica Mayotte, to approve Ordinance No. 5455 as amended. Motion carried unanimously on a
                  voice vote; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica
                  Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke voting yes.

        PUBLIC REQUESTS:

Lee Roggenberg inquired about the number of tickets sold to concerts at the Amphitheater and whether the City
subsidized the concerts. He then commented on a lacrosse championship held at Boca Raton High School. He
asked that the City be involved in bringing the event back to Boca Raton next year.

JoAnn Landon expressed concern with recent school shootings and the need for more money from the
Legislature to harden .schools and protect students. The Mayor related the actions that the City has taken to
protect our schools. Council Member O'Rourke provided information on an upcoming event.

David Sergi commented on the use of styrofoam and suggested having a debate on banning it.

Ross Donahue thanked the Council for their outreach and accessibility on various issues.

Douglas Mathieson commented on the importance of butterflies and asked that the City encourage the planting of
landscaping that attract butterflies.
