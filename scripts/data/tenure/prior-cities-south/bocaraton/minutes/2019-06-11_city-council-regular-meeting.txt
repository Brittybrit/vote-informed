         9.G.     Resolution No. 75-2019
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton authorizing the Mayor and City Clerk to execute an interlocal
                  agreement with Palm Beach County for the purpose of developing, constructing, and managing a
                  public safety communications tower on City-owned property that will support public safety
                  communications systems for the City, the County and other authorized public safety users;
                  authorizing the expenditure of funds in an amount not to exceed 3 million dollars ($3,000,000) for
                  the construction of the tower; authorizing the City Manager or designee to execute additional
                  documents necessary to facilitate the development, construction, and management of the
                  communications tower and to make nonmaterial changes to the interlocal agreement as may be
                  acceptable to the City Manager; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an
                  effective date

         Acting City Manager George Brown gave a brief overview of the agreement details. The ownership and
         responsibility of the tower were discussed. The Palm Beach County Zoning Board recommended approval
         to the County Commission. Mr. Brown responded to members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Ele Zachariades presented a brief PowerPoint outlining her client's
         opposition to the tower. Alan Feinberg, Dan Cohen, Jeffrey Greenhaut, George Corso, Constance Weaver,
         Shannon Otto, Sue Trombino, and Sothro Sok spoke against construction of the tower. Seeing no one else
         come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

         Mr. Brown responded to questions. Discussion ensued.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy
                        Rodgers, to postpone Resolution No. 75-2019 to the meeting of July 23, 2019. Motion failed
                        2 votes to 3; Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes;
                        Mayor Scott Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine
                        O'Rourke voting no.

         Further discussion ensued. Mr. Brown and City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser provided additional
         information.

                        Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member Andrea
                        Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 75-2019. Motion carried 3 votes to 2; Mayor Scott
                        Singer, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke voting
                        yes; Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers Council; Member Andy Thomson voting no.

 11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

                  Resolution No. 70-2019
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering an amendment to an approved conditional use
                  and master plan (Resolution No. 1-93, as amended by Resolution Nos. 14-2001 and 160-2010), to
                  extend the master plan buildout date and change the orientation and configuration of certain
                  buildings, for Lynn University located at 3601 North Military Trail; providing for repealer; providing
                  an effective date (CA-10-04R1)

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that governed the public hearing. Members made
         their ex-parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

         Jim Bell, Development Services Long Range Project Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Bell
         reviewed the application and provided analysis of the Conditional Use amendment. The Planning and
         Zoning Board recommended approval. Conditions proposed for deletion and modification were discussed.
         Staff recommended approval.

         The public hearing was opened. Heather Allen, on behalf of the applicant, gave a brief PowerPoint
         presentation and made herself available for questions. There was no cross examination or rebuttal. Seeing
         no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

         Members made final comments.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Council Member Andrea
                  Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 70-2019. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott
                  Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea
                  Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

 12.     REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

                   Ordinance No. 5487 (Second of two public hearings.)
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering amendments to Chapter 28 "Zoning," Code of
                  Ordinances, with regard to assisted living facilities (ALFs), nursing homes and hospitals, to provide
                  for the consistent use of terminology, delete obsolete terms, and affirm that such uses are regulated
                  by beds per acre (BPA) and have a maximum of 75 beds per acre; deleting the obsolete term
                  "orphanage" as a use; amending Chapter 28, Article I, Section 28-3, "Intent, Purposes, Methods,"
                  Code of Ordinances, to affirm, generally, that Chapter 28 is interpreted and applied in accordance
                  with Federal and State law; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for
                  codification; providing an effective date (AM-17-12)

         Tamash Rahman, Chief Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Ms. Rahman briefly reviewed the
         proposed amendment and noted that the changes were consistent with the Comprehensive Plan. The
         Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval. Staff recommended approval, as well. Ms. Rahman
         then made herself available for questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Cristina Escalante commented. Ms. Rahman, the Acting City Manager,
         and the City Attorney provided additional information. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing
         was closed.
