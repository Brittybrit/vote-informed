Regular Minutes                                          3                                         October 27, 2020
          D.       Resolution No. 119-2020
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a site plan, pursuant to a universal conditional
                  (UC) request, to authorize construction of an approximately 6,399 square foot, 25 foot tall
                  professional office building with covered and surface parking, together with a technical deviation
                  from Section 28-1653(1 ), Code of Ordinances, to reduce required dimensions of parking spaces,
                  for the Yamato Office Park generally located at 276 and 298 West Yamato Road, subject to
                  conditions; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (UC-14-02/SPA)

        The Mayor noted that the public hearings on items 11. A., B., C., and D. would be conducted
        contemporaneously as the items were related, and a separate vote would be taken on each item. Ordinance
        No. 5535 would require four votes for passage. The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures
        which would govern the public hearing. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.
        Members made their ex-parte disclosures.

        Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Schaad began
        by providing a brief review of the application components. He explained that staff recommended denial
        based on the compatibility of the proposed future land use and zoning given the parcel configuration and
        surrounding land use. It was noted that if Council approved the land use amendment, however, the
        remaining elements of the application met code standards for approval. If the application was approved,
        Mr. Schaad remarked that approval would not restrict or limit use of the adjacent property in any way under
        its current land use and zoning designations. Upon concluding, he made himself available for members'
        questions.

        The public hearing was opened. Rochelle Lecavalier, on behalf of the applicant, gave a PowerPoint
        presentation. The cross-access easement with the adjoining property owner was highlighted. Drawings of
        the exterior elevations were presented, and considerations to the residential neighborhood were
        emphasized. Ms. Lecavalier discussed her efforts to come to an agreement with the adjacent property
        owner and asked Council to support the project application. Upon concluding, she made herself available
        for members' questions. Mitchell Kirschner, on behalf of the adjoining landowner, expressed agreement
        with staffs recommendation of denial and suggested alternative development that was consistent and
        compatible with the existing residential use land designation. Robert Sweetapple, also speaking on behalf
        of the adjoining landowner, urged Council to follow staffs recommendation and deny the application, and
        stated that the rezoning of the properties should be considered under one consolidated application. Juan
        Caycedo, architect of the proposed project, provided additional information and asked for Council's
        approval. There was no cross-examination or rebuttal. Seeing no one else indicate their desire to speak,
        the public hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member Andy
                          Thomson, to approve Ordinance No. 5535.

        Council Member Mayotte asked the City Attorney to clarify potential issues regarding the proposed land
        use change. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser provided additional information regarding the current
        application and clarification of the term 'spot zoning'. Mr. Schaad confirmed that the adjoining property
        owner would have the same rights offered to them as the current applicant and provided clarification of
        application processing timelines. Members made final comments and expressed their support for the
        project.

                          Motion carried 4 to O; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council
                          Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member Andy
                          Thomson, to approve Ordinance No. 5536. Motion carried 4 to O; Mayor Scott Singer,
                          Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council
                          Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

          A.      Resolution No. 165-2020
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a temporary appointment to fill Council Member
                  Rodgers' seat (temporarily unoccupied due to military leave of absence); providing for repealer;
                  providing an effective date

         City Manager Leif Ahnell briefly reviewed the options available to fill the temporarily vacant council seat.
         Upon concluding, he made himself available for members' questions. Deputy Mayor O'Rourke commented
         on similar situations that the current and prior City Councils had encountered in the past.

         The public comment period was opened. Alan Feinberg expressed his disappointment that candidates
         who are running in March would not be disqualified from filling the temporarily unoccupied seat. Seeing
         no one else indicated their desire to speak, the public comment period was closed.
