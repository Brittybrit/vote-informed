                    Resolution No. 35-2018
                    A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the appeal of a decision of
                    the Builders' Board of Adjustment and Appeals (Resolution BBAA-17-92600001) approving
                    variances from the following sections of the City's Code of Ordinances: Section 22-62(2), to allow
                    a finger pier width of eight (8) feet in lieu of the maximum allowable width of six (6) feet; Section
                    22-62(6) to allow a finger pier to encroach 27.4 feet beyond the maximum encroachment of 45.6
                    feet (fifteen percent (15%) of the width of the waterway but not exceeding sixty (60) feet) into a
                    waterway; Section 22-62(7) to allow a finger pier side setback of forty (40) feet in lieu of the
                    required side setback equal to the distance the finger pier extends out into the waterway (73 feet);
                    and Section 22-63(1) to allow a boat lift to encroach fifty-three (53) feet into a waterway in lieu of
                    the maximum encroachment of twenty (20) feet, for the property located at 600 Northeast Fifth
                    Avenue; subject to conditions; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (BBAA-17-
                    92600001)
                              Resolution No. 35-2018
                              Appellant Submission

           The City Attorney confirmed that quasi-judicial procedures remained in effect. Members made their ex
           parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

           Keith Carney, Senior Zoning Officer, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The property that is the subject of
           the matter was identified and background information was provided. Details of the variances requested
           were provided, as were details of the prior and proposed dock and boat lift. The Marine Advisory Board
           recommended approval with conditions regarding the boat lift and a pumpout facility. The Builders' Board
           of Adjustment and Appeals (BBAA) granted the variances with conditions. Staff recommended approval
           of the variances. Upon concluding, Mr. Carney responded to questions.

           The public hearing was opened. Howard DuBoser, on behalf of the appellant Paul Rosenberg, gave a
           PowerPoint presentation. He outlined his argument in support of granting the appeal and denying the
           variances. Mr. DuBosar noted that the main objection was that the dock will extend 73 feet into the
           lntracoastal, emphasizing that the literal interpretation of the City's Code should prohibit granting the
           variance. He stated that the proposed dock length would impact the Rosenberg's view. Mr. Rosenberg
           then made a statement regarding the Fowler's decision to build a new dock and expressing his concerns.
           Bonnie Miskel, on behalf of the Fowlers, gave a PowerPoint presentation, reviewing the variance criteria.
           Charlie Isiminger, Isiminger & Stubbs Engineering, continued the presentation, reviewing the special and
           unique situations that exist in support of the variance. Ms. Miskel addressed the way in which the
           Fowlers would be deprived of rights commonly enjoyed by owners of properties in the same area. Drew
           Campbell, Environmental & Marine Consulting Services, then addressed the design and location of the
           dock and lift. Matt Butler, Isiminger & Stubbs Engineering, refuted the right to a view and navigation
           impacts as discussed by Mr. DuBosar. Ms. Miske! then concluded the presentation. There was no cross
           examination. During rebuttal, Michael Moskowitz, on behalf of the Fowlers, provided handouts and
           responded to points made by Mr. DuBosar including how the City interprets its Code and the violation of
           the Rosenberg's riparian rights. He concluded that the variances should be granted and the appeal
           denied. Mr. DuBosar rebutted various points, including the age and condition of the old dock, new
           testimony, interpretation of the City's Code, obstruction of views, and other topics. The City Attorney
           provided her analysis of the City Code as it applies to this matter. Brandon Schaad, Development
           Services Director, also addressed the limitation of the length of finger piers. Mr. Carney, Ms. Miske! and
           Mr. DuBosar responded to Council members' questions. The public hearing was closed. The City
           Attorney clarified that, on appeals, the Council can rely on the record and new testimony when making a
           decision.

                          Motion was made by Mayor Susan Haynie, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, to
                          approve Resolution No. 35-2018 affirming the decision of the BBAA and granting the
                          variances.

           Members made final comments and expressed their opinions.
