I.      Welcome and Introductions - Mayor Scott Singer and Chairman Bob Rollins
        Mayor Scott Singer called the meeting to order at 5:33 pm. The participants introduced themselves.

II.     Discussion Topics

        A.      Recreation Needs Assessment

                Commissioner Robert Rollins, Chairman of the Greater Boca Raton Beach & Park District
                (District) reviewed the District's proposal to do a recreation needs assessment and hoped the City
                would share in the cost of the report estimated at $100,000. Michael Kalvort, Recreation
                Services Director, provided additional information on having a community-wide needs
                assessment survey, noting that the last time an assessment was performed was in 1987.
                Discussion followed; members of both bodies offered comments. Mr. Kalvort reviewed the
                timeline for the process, subject to budget approval. The possibility of piggy-backing on another
                contract to shorten the RFP process will be researched.

        B.      Golf Course Update

                Executive Director Art Koski provided background information on the interview process to select a
                golf course architect. The District hopes to sign a consulting agreement with Price/Fazio at their
                meeting later tonight. Upon receipt of the agreement, the consultants will begin preparation of a
                master plan and preliminary designs for the golf course. They hope to begin bidding of the work
                and commence construction in October of 2018, with anticipated completion of the golf course
                only in March of 2019. Other amenities would be completed in phases. There will be a minimum
                of three public hearings to discuss the architect's plans prior to implementation of the final design.
                Discussion followed; members of both bodies offered comments.

        C.      Gumbo Limbo Master Plan

                Chairman Rollins noted there was an estimated $200,000 design fee for the master plan. There
                will be a presentation and discussion on the scope of the plan at the District's August 6, 2018
                meeting. City Manager Leif Ahnell added that a summary of the results of the Waterfront Master
                Plan study conducted by the City's outside consultant was provided to the District. Chair Rollins
                said their budget was recently amended to complete the renovation of the boardwalk and tower.
                Lengthy discussion ensued on additional monies needed to complete construction for both
                Gumbo Limbo and the golf course, and whether the City may be willing to help with the funding or
                if the District would secure another loan.

                Discussion returned to the golf course. Topics included, but were not limited to, additional funds
                needed to complete the construction of the course; the use of the hotel parcel; possible revenue
                generated by a clubhouse; a partnership with the City to share in the additional costs by
                    contributing proceeds from the City's sale of the western golf course; constrained roadways and
                    traffic concerns; the possible abandonment of Jeffrey Street east of Second Avenue versus the
                    connection of Clint Moore Road and Jeffrey Street; and the redevelopment of the Boca Teeca
                    area. The City Manager noted that the City could begin to plan for further financing for the District.
                    It was agreed to have further discussions on financing when plans are completed and more
                    tangible construction costs for both Gumbo Limbo and the golf course are received.

          D.        Non-resident Fees

                    Chair Rollins began the discussion regarding the definition of non-resident, noting the current
                    non-resident fee of $65 per child per sport and the contribution of non-residents to the sports
                    culture. Other commissioners weighed in. In response to a question, the City Manager stated that
                    the City only recovers a small percentage of the total cost of recreation programs from the fees.
                    Discussion ensued on the definition of non-resident and the equitability of fees. District staff has
                    been directed to meet with City staff to work on standardizing rental fees across the different
                    recreational facilities. There was further discussion of residents versus non-residents on sports
                    teams, a fee cap or a reduced rate for families with multiple children or children playing multiple
                    sports, and some consideration for events for employees of local businesses. Michael Kalvort
                    provided additional information and confirmed that the rate for non-residents was approximately
                    25% above the resident fee and that residents felt that the fee schedule was very convoluted.
                    After continued discussion, it was agreed to have staff from both bodies review all the recreation
                    fees, both City and District, and bring back a recommendation that would standardize them ..

Ill.      Public Comment

          The public comment period was opened. Michelle Peel, on behalf of Friends of Gumbo Limbo, spoke in
          support of the Gumbo Limbo Master Plan. Robert DuKate thanked everyone for their support of the golf
          course and offered support of the investment of funds at Gumbo Limbo, wider sidewalks and having a
          recreational facility as opposed to a hotel at the golf course. Susan Caruso spoke in support of Gumbo
          Limbo. Julian Louvet commented on the vegetation clearing along the L-48 canal and hoped for
          beautification projects along that corridor. Mayor Singer responded that the Lake Worth Drainage District
          has suspended further clearing on the canals within the City. Linda Sturdy supported the City and the
          District working together to benefit Gumbo Limbo. Earl Starkoff provided his thoughts on the non-resident
          fees. Darlene Ward supported allocating funds to update and improve Gumbo Limbo. Doug Coller spoke
          against having a hotel at the golf course and extending Jeffrey Street. Lenore Wachtel felt monies from
          the sale of the western golf course should go towards funding the golf course and enhancing Gumbo
          Limbo. Harold Chaffee asked the City to provide a portion of the funds to the District for construction of
          the golf course. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public comment period was closed.

          Members discussed a future joint meeting and asked staff to check for a date in September. Topics also
                                                                              nd
          included the beautification of the entrance to Boca Teeca along NW 2 Avenue, the design fee for phase
          2 of the Spanish River/DeHoernle project to commence in 2021, an agreement between the City and the
          Friends of Gumbo Limbo to allow the opportunity for naming rights in some form and a master local
          agreement.

IV.       Adjournment

          Mayor Singer thanked everyone for their input. The meeting was adjourned without objection at
                    ly 8:11 pm.
