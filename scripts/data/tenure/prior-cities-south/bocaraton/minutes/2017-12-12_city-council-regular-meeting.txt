                Resolution No. 165-2017
                A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a Planned Mobility Development (PMD) Master
                Plan to allow development of a 284-unit multifamily residential project generally located at 5201
                Congress Avenue and 901 West Yamato Road: considering four (4) modifications of development
                 parameters to eliminate required planted buffer areas in certain locations, eliminate a required
                buffer of a parking structure in a specific location, reduce a required building setback from
                Congress Avenue, and reduce a required sidewalk width: providing for repealer; providing an
                effective date (PMD-16-01)
                         Resolution No. 165-2017

        The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
        made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

        Elizabeth Eassa, Development Services Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation beginning with
        background information on planned mobility developments, and then provided the zoning, land use, size
        and current status of the subject site. Ms. Eassa then summarized the proposed project and master plan
        approval. Site access, parking and trip generation were detailed. It was noted that the Community
        Appearance Board reviewed the project but did not formally recommend approvals of elevations or
        landscaping as these are subject to change. Charts were provided illustrating the project's compliance
        with the PMD development requirements. Ms. Eassa then reviewed the four proposed modifications of
        the development parameters for planter and parking structure buffers, setbacks and sidewalk width,
        noting staffs approval of the modifications. It was also noted that the project is consistent with the PM
        land use. The Planning and Zoning Board unanimously recommended approval of the PMD Master Plan.
        Staff recommended approval as well. Upon concluding, Ms. Eassa and Brandon Schaad, Development
        Services Director, responded to Council members' questions.

        The public hearing was opened. Heather Allen, representing the petitioner, gave a PowerPoint
        presentation. The property location and information were provided. Details of the proposed master plan
        were reviewed, noting its enhancement of the adjacent office buildings. Ms. Allen then addressed the
        requested PMD development parameter modifications. Upon concluding, Ms. Allen responded to
        questions. She also provided a handout with details of the analysis done to determine trip generation. In
        response to a question, the City Attorney noted that the master plan approval runs with the property.
        Discussion of shuttle service ensued. Glenn Gromann, speaking as a board member of the Park at

17.    QUASI-JUDICIAL PUBLIC HEARINGSNARIANCES & APPEALS:

        A.       Resolution No. 164-2017
                 A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering the appeal of a decision of
                 the Zoning Board of Adjustment (Resolution BA-17-02} denying Lot 69 Ibis Point Circle, LLC's
                 request for a variance from Section 28-336, Code of Ordinances, with respect to the rear setback
                  line (which setback, pursuant to a PUD authorized by Resolution No. 116-81, was previously
                  reduced to 20 feet}, to allow a rear setback of ten (1 O} feet, in lieu of the required rear setback of
                 twenty (20} feet, in order to construct a new single-family home on an approximately 0.63 acre
                 single-family residential zoned property (R-1-NR-1-B/PUD) located at 4081 Ibis Point Circle;
                  providing for repealer; providing an effective date (BA-17-02}
                          Resolution No. 164-2017

        Mayor Haynie noted that the quasi-judicial procedures reviewed earlier remained in effect. Members
        made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

        Elizabeth Eassa, Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Background information on the variance
        request and the Zoning Board of Adjustment (ZBOA) decision was provided. The code section related to
        rear and side setbacks was reviewed. The zoning, location and shape of the lot were identified.
        Information on the plot lines and definitions of same in relation to the proposed orientation of the house
        on the lot was discussed. Staff determined that the appellant's request for this variance was not
        unreasonable and recommended approval of the appeal of the decision of the ZBOA, thereby granting
        the variance. Ms. Eassa then responded to a number of Council members' questions.

        The public hearing was opened. Mitch Kirschner, attorney with GrayRobinson on behalf of the petitioner,
        gave a PowerPoint presentation. He reviewed the side and rear setbacks of the lot. A comparison of the
        setback in question for the proposed and former homes on this lot was provided. Mr. Kirschner reviewed
        letters of support from neighboring homeowners. It was noted that the rear setback is generally assumed
        to be on the water. Staff's recommendation to approve the appeal and grant the variance was reviewed,
        Mr. Kirschner noting his agreement. He then addressed the objection letter filed by the Elsa Schack,
        owner of the neighboring lot. Upon concluding, he made himself available for questions.

        Elsa Schack read her letter of objection into the record. She then reiterated that the petitioner should
        have to abide by the 20 foot rear setback requirement. Glenn Gromann contended that the variance
        should be granted. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed. There
        was no cross examination. The petitioner waived rebuttal.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member
                          Andrea Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 164-2017 reversing the decision of
                          the Zoning Board of Adjustment and granting the variance.

             Members made final comments.

                          Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers,
                          Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Scott Singer, Council
                          Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes.

                                                     WHO MUST FILE FORM 88
This form is for use by any person serving at the county, city, or other local level of government on an appointed or elected board, council,
commission, authority, or committee. It applies to members of advisory and non-advisory bodies who are presented with a voting conflict of
interest under Section 112.3143, Florida Statutes.

Your responsibilities under the law when faced with voting on a measure in which you have a conflict of interest will vary greatly depending
on whether you hold an elective or appointive position. For this reason, please pay close attention to the instructions on this form before
completing and filing the form.




            INSTRUCTIONS FOR COMPLIANCE WITH SECTION 112.3143, FLORIDA STATUTES
A person holding elective or appointive county, municipal, or other local public office MUST ABSTAIN from voting on a measure which
would inure to his or her special private gain or loss. Each elected or appointed local officer also MUST ABSTAIN from knowingly voting on
a measure which would inure to the special gain or loss of a principal (other than a government agency) by whom he or she is retained
(including the parent, subsidiary, or sibling organization of a principal by which he or she is retained); to the special private gain or loss of a
relative; or to the special private gain or loss of a business associate. Commissioners of community redevelopment agencies (CRAs) under
Sec. 163.356 or 163.357, F.S., and officers of independent special tax districts elected on a one-acre, one-vote basis are not prohibited
from voting in that capacity.
For purposes of this law, a "relative" includes only the officer's father, mother, son, daughter, husband, wife, brother, sister, father-in-law,
mother-in-law, son-in-law, and daughter-in-law. A "business associate" means any person or entity engaged in or carrying on a business
enterprise with the officer as a partner, joint venturer, coowner of property, or corporate shareholder (where the shares of the corporation
are not listed on any national or regional stock exchange).                                                  ·


ELECTED OFFICERS:
In addition to abstaining from voting in the situations described above, you must disclose the conflict:
       PRIOR TO THE VOTE BEING TAKEN by publicly stating to the assembly the nature of your interest in the measure on which you are
       abstaining from voting; and
       WITHIN 15 DAYS AFTER THE VOTE OCCURS by completing and filing this form with the person responsible for recording the
       minutes of the meeting, who should incorporate the form in the minutes.


APPOINTED OFFICERS:
Although you must abstain from voting in the situations described above, you are not prohibited by Section 112.3143 from otherwise
participating in these matters. However, you must disclose the nature of the conflict before making any attempt to influence the decision,
whether orally or in writing and whether made by you or at your direction.
