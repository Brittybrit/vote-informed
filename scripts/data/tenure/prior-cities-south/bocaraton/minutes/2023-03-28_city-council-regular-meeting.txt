 11.    REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

         A.       Ordinance No. 5644
                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Article XVI, "Off-Street
                 Parking and Loading," Code of Ordinances, to expand the uses required to provide electric
                 vehicle parking spaces (from multifamily residential, commercial and industrial uses, to
                 multifamily residential and all non-residential uses) and increase the number of electric vehicle
                 parking spaces required for such uses (from 2% to 5% of the total required off-street parking
                 spaces), decrease the development threshold at which the requirements for electric vehicle
                 parking spaces are imposed (from 50 total parking spaces to 20 total parking spaces) , provide
                 definitions for "EV-capable," "EV-ready" and "EV-installed" parking spaces, and add
                 requirements for such spaces; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for
                 codification; providing an effective date (AM-22-07)

         Sustainability Manager Lindsey Nieratka gave the presentation. Ms. Nieratka began with a summary
         of the current regulations and proposed changes to expand the requirements of electric vehicle
         parking. She showed slides describing the background and purpose of the ordinance and noted that
         Boca Raton has a higher-than-average ownership rate of electric vehicles. Upon concluding, Ms.
         Nieratka responded to Members' questions.

         The public hearing was opened. Jonathan Ounjian asked if this proposed change would affect
         alterations of properties and not just new development. Seeing no one else come forward, the public
         hearing was closed.

         Members discussed the question raised by Mr. Ounjian and Mayor Singer proposed amended
         language. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser and City Manager Leif Ahnell offered insight and additional
         information. Ms. Frieser suggested tabling the topic until a later date in order to provide time for staff
         to conduct additional research.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Mayor Scott
                         Singer, to continue Ordinance No. 5644 to the meeting ofApril 25, 2023. Motion carried
                         unanimously on a voice vote.
