          A       Resolution No. 56-2026
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton amending the name of two streets within the
                  approximately 110-acre property known as The Boca Raton Resort, generally located north
                  of East Camino Real, west of the intracoastal waterway and east of South Federal Highway
                  and SE Mizner Boulevard ; providing for conditions; providing for repealer; providing an
                  effective date (SC-26-01)

           Development Services Deputy Director Erin Sita gave the PowerPoint presentation . She outlined the
           proposed street name change and how it closely aligns with the property's renovations. Upon
           conclud ing , she responded to Members' questions.

           The public comment period was opened . Christina Bilenki , on behalf of the applicant, provided further
           insight and advised that the property is renaming the street in honor of the trees that are on property.
           Pam Paschke asked if the previous speaker was fully compliant with the lobbyist ordinance. Seeing
           no one else come forwa rd, the public comment period was closed .

                          Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Michelle Grau, seconded by Mayor Andy
                          Thomson , to approve Resolution No. 56-2026.

           City Attorney Joshua Koehler confirmed that any lobbyist speaking on behalf of their client must
           identify whom they represent.

                          Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Andy Thomson, Deputy Mayor Michelle Grau,
                          Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Jon Pearlman, Council Member
                          Stacy Sipple voting yes.

          B.      Resolution No. 60-2026
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton providing for submission to the electors of proposed
                  Charter amendments relating to initiative, referendum, and Charter-amendment petition
                  procedures; revising election-timing and ballot-placement procedures; revising petition
                  signature-threshold calculations; creating a status quo preservation mechanism applicable to
                  certified initiative and Charter-amendment petitions pending electoral determination; providing
                  for a special election to be held at the next available election , as determined and made
                  available by the Palm Beach County Supervisor of Elections, but no later than the March 2027
                  Uniform Municipal Election ; providing for severability ; providing for repea ler; providing an
                  effective date

           City Attorney Joshua Koehler gave the PowerPoint presentation and outlined the proposed changes
           to the Charter and Code.

           The public comment period was opened . Pam Paschke thanked Council for the changes. Seeing no
           one else come forward , the public comment period was closed .

          A.       Ordinance No. 5786
                   An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton authorizing , pursuant to Chapter 13, Article Ill , Code
                   of Ordinances, an amendment to an existing lease of approximately 5.12 acres of City-owned
                   land generally located at 1531 West Palmetto Park Road , to extend the expiration date of the
                   lease from June 12, 2034 to June 12, 2044 , to allow for the continued operation of a hospice


Regular Meeting Minutes                                   5                                              June 9, 2026
                   facility, and to update other lease terms, including a required capital improvement investment
                   by the Lessee; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                   (SC-26-01)

           Development Services Deputy Director Erin Sita gave the PowerPoint presentation. She provided a
           history of the property, reviewed the background of upgrades that had been made to the property,
           and outlined the existing and proposed lease extension. Upon concluding, she made herself available
           for questions.

           The public comment period was opened . Stephen Mathison, on behalf of Trustbridge, gave a
           presentation. Council Member Pearlman questioned the term of the lease, why the clause to cancel
           the lease had been removed, and what the yearly payment to the City would be. Pam Paschke asked
           for confirmation if Trustbridge was only being required to invest one million over 20 years. John
           Anderson asked who built the building . Brianna Hagquist asked why we were leasing this land for $1
           per year when there are potential tax cuts coming . Judy Morrow advised that she did not think the
           hospice was rated as high as it should be and that she is not happy with the reviews of the facility.
           Seeing no one else come forward, the public comment period was closed. Stephen
           Mathison responded to comments made .

                          Motion was made by Council Member Stacy Sipple, seconded by Council Member
                          Yvette Drucker, to approve Ordinance No. 5786.

           Discussion ensued and Members discussed the current state of the facility and the service that it
           provides to the community. Members suggested postponing the consideration of the item to a future
           date in order to gather more information .

                          Motion was made by Mayor Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor Michelle Grau
                          to postpone Ordinance No. 5786 to a future date. Motion carried unanimously on a
                          voice vote .
