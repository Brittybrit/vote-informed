        C.       Resolution No. 20-2023
                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton establishing a Parking Meter Zone consisting of
                 approximately two hundred forty (240) parking spaces within the parking lots generally located east
                 of Boca Raton Boulevard, around the Downtown Library and north of 3rd Street; authorizing the
                 City Manager to implement the Parking Meter Zone; providing for severability; providing for
                 repealer; providing an effective date

        City Manager Leif Ahnell gave a brief overview of the proposed parking meter zone at the Downtown
        Library. Council Member Yvette Drucker stated that the parking will be a free service for the library. Ms.
        O'Rourke asked how the metered zone will affect the library patrons. Discussion ensued. George Brown
        Deputy City Manager clarified comments made and responded to Members' questions.

        The public comment period was opened. Cyndi Bloom spoke on behalf of the Friends of the Boca Raton
        Library (Friends). Ms. Bloom remarked on the amount of funds that the Friends had raised for the library,
        stated that she is against the proposed parking meter zone and offered suggestions for an alternative
        solution. Patricia Moran, Board Member of the Friends, expressed her concerns about the implementation
        of a parking meter zone. Seeing no one else come forward , the public comment period was closed.

        Discussion ensued and Members providing additional ideas and Mr. Ahnell offered more information. Ms.
        Drucker clarified that the metered zone for the library would be a free service for the patrons. Ms. Mayotte
        stated that there are many free events at the library and was concerned about the availability of parking
        spaces. Ms. Nachlas suggested to log/ sign in with a library card to use the free parking . There was
        consensus to add signage and postpone the proposed meters to a later date.

       B.        Resolution No. 18-2023
                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the approximately 15.24 acre property
                 generally located at 3320 Airport Road known as Premier Park: (i) an amended and restated site
                 plan approval consolidating Master Plan Resolution No. 137-2013 (as amended by Resolution No.
                 99-2015), and Site Plan Resolution No. 139-2013 (as amended by Resolution No. 101-2015), and
                 (ii) a site plan amendment to authorize, for Phase 2, the construction of a two (2)-story, 50,942
                 square foot retail (furniture store) and business/professional office building; providing for
                 severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SPA-13-06R2)

       The Mayor noted that the hearing on items on 10.A. and 10.B. would be conducted contemporaneously as
       the items were related , and a separate vote would be taken on each item. The City Attorney reviewed the
       quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members disclosed their ex-parte
       discussions. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

       Development Services Planner Heather Hanson gave the presentation. She began by giving an overview
       of the Premier Park Master Plan. She described the proposed amendments and site plan changes. The
       Planning and Zoning Board and Development Services recommended approval as proposed. Upon
       concluding , Ms. Hansen made herself available for questions.

       The public hearing was opened. David Milledge spoke on behalf of the applicant and gave a brief
       presentation. Mr. Milledge shared aerial photos of the subject location, provided details about the existing
       property, and shared an overview of the proposed changes. Upon concluding , he made himself available
       for questions. Jonathan Ounjian made comments concerning fair zoning for all areas of the City. Seeing no
       one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

                         Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member
                         Fran Nachlas, to approve Resolution No. 17-2023.

       Deputy Mayor O'Rourke spoke in favor of the project. Mayor Singer provided final comments.

                         Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke,

14.    INTRODUCTION OF ORDINANCES:

       A         Ordinance No. 5644

                 An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Article XVI, "Off-Street
                 Parking and Loading," Code of Ordinances, to expand the uses required to provide electric vehicle
                 parking spaces (from multifamily residential, commercial and industrial uses, to multifamily
                 residential and all non-residential uses) and increase the number of electric vehicle parking spaces
                 required for such uses (from 2% to 5% of the total required off-street parking spaces), decrease
                 the development threshold at which the requirements for electric vehicle parking spaces are
                 imposed (from 50 total parking spaces to 20 total parking spaces), provide definitions for "EV­
                 capable," "EV-ready" and "EV-installed" parking spaces, and add requirements for such spaces;
                 providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective
                 date (AM-22-07)

Council Member Monica Mayotte introduced the ordinance.
