10.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

          A.       Resolution No. 123-2022
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for the approximately 0.77 acre property
                  generally located at 170 Northwest 20th Street, a conditional use pursuant to Section 28-1098,
                  to authorize a new health and fitness club (gym) (superseding the conditional use for a retail
                  fabric store granted by Resolution No. 220-87) in an existing building in the General Industrial
                  (M-2) zoning district; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (CA-22-01)

          The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing.
          Members disclosed their ex-parte discussions. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who
          wished to speak.

          Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the presentation. He began by displaying an
          aerial photo of the subject location and provided a description of the site's existing condition. The
          applicant's proposed site plan and use as an open floor gym was shown. The Planning and Zoning
          Board recommended approval, and staff recommended approval of the conditional use application.
          Upon concluding, Mr. Schaad responded to questions regarding conditional use approvals in the
          district surrounding 20th Street.

          The public hearing was opened. Ele Zachariades, on behalf of the applicant, remarked on the
          permitted uses of the particular retail location and questioned why her client had been required to
          apply for a conditional use approval. Upon concluding, she made herself available for questions.
          Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

                           Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                           Member Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 123-2022.

           Deputy Mayor O'Rourke requested clarification from staff regarding Ms. Zachariades comments.
           Mayor Singer asked if Mr. Schaad would suggest any further streamlining to the existing Code
           regarding uses in industrial districts. Mr. Schaad responded to the questions posed and provided
           additional information.

                           Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine
                           O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte voting
                           yes.

          B.       Resolution No. 124-2022
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering, for an approximately 1.13 acre property
                  generally located at 6653 Jog Road, accepting and approving the plat known as "Genesis" (a
                  replat of Parcel No. One of "Plaza Real Plat No. 1"), which replat will result in the elimination of
                  a plat restriction limiting the use of the property (Parcel 1) to a bank only; authorizing the Mayor
                  and City Clerk to execute the plat; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SUB-22-
                  01)

          The City Attorney confirmed that the quasi-judicial procedures remained in effect. Members disclosed
          their ex-parte discussions. The City Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak.

          Jim Bell, Long Range Project Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began by displaying
          an aerial photo of the subject property and reviewed the components of the applicant's request. He
          noted that the request involved a replat request of one of two parcels, as well as a site plan
          amendment. The notice for the public hearing was reviewed, including a mailer and newspaper
          publication. The Planning and Zoning Board (P&Z) recommended approval. Mr. Bell commented on
          a discussion by the P&Z regarding potential future uses of one of the parcels and confirmed that
          future uses would have to meet zoning requirements and be reviewed by the Board. Staff finds that
          the proposed changes are consistent with the Comprehensive Plan and recommend approval. Upon
          concluding, he responded to Members' questions.

          The public hearing was opened. Ele Zachariades, on behalf of the applicant, commented on the
          history of the parcel and provided additional information. She then she made herself available for
          Members' questions. Seeing no one else come forward, the public hearing was closed.

                           Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member
                           Yvette Drucker, to approve Resolution No. 124-2022. Motion carried unanimously;
                           Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette
                           Drucker, Council Member Monica Mayotte voting yes.
