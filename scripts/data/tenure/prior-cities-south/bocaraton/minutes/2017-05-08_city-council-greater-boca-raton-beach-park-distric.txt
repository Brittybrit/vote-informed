I.      Welcome and Introductions - Mayor Susan Haynie and Chairman Bob Rollins

        Mayor Susan Haynie called the meeting to order at 4:30 pm. The participants introduced themselves.

II.     Discussion Topics

        A.      Ocean Breeze Golf Course

        Commissioner Robert Rollins, Chairman of the Greater Boca Raton Beach & Park District (District)
        provided brief comments before turning the meeting over to Executive Director Koski. Executive Director
        Koski showed a slide of an aerial view of the golf course, reviewed the history of the property and how the
        District became involved in the acquisition of the course. Mr. Koski proceeded to show a short video
        illustrating the current condition of the property and the District vision of a world class golf resort including
        a pro shop, lounge, hotel and golf academy in partnership with Greg Norman. He explained how the
        District was ready to move forward with an lnterlocal Agreement with the City Council for the benefit of the
        community. A financial pro forma comparing national and local golf courses was reviewed along with
        estimated revenues and expenditures for Ocean Breeze. Discussion followed; members of both bodies
        offered comments. Mr. Koski addressed the District's financial ability to go forward with the purchase, the
        purchase price, and the community benefits of acquiring Ocean Breeze.

Ill.    Public Comment

        The floor was opened for public comment. Betty Grinnan, Judith Teller Kaye, John Mendozza, Ed
        Grecsek and Richard Siemens expressed concerns with the purchase price of Ocean Breeze while
        supporting acquisition of the property. Lupe Eyde, Annabelle Silverman, Barbara Fulkerson, Regina
        Ecklund, Harold Chaffee, Kate Handley, Steven Gross, Mary LaFuente, Dottie Provenzano, Michael
        Heymann, Charles Ventre, Jerry Becker, James Rubino, Lenore Wachtel, Eric Leder, Anthony Ciliberti,
        Joseph Good, Doug Coller, Dale Oliver, Connie Sutherland and Robert DuKate expressed their support
        of acquiring the Ocean Breeze course and the purchase price. Karl Dickey, representing the Libertarian
        Party, did not support purchasing the Ocean Breeze course. Seeing no one else come forward to speak,
        the public comment period was closed.

        Executive Director Koski responded to concerns expressed by previous speakers. Mayor Haynie asked
        whether Lennar would be willing to pull out the hotel parcel from the sale. Executive Director Koski
        replied they would not. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser clarified that, should the District proceed with
        the purchase, the City Council's role would be as a lender and an interlocal agreement would provide for
        repayment. City Council and District members offered final comments. After further discussion, Council
        members directed the City Attorney to move forward with drafting an interlocal agreement.
IV.       Adjournment

          Both Chairman Rollins and Mayor Haynie thanked everyone for their input. The meeting was adjourned
          without objection at approximately 6:45 pm.
