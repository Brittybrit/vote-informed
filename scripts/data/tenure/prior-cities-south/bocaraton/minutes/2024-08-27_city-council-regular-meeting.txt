11.       REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

           A.      Ordinance No. 5698
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28, "Zoning," Code of Ordinances,
                  by: (i) amending Article XVI , "Off-Street Parking and Loading," Section 28-1655, "Required Off­
                  Street Parking," to reduce the minimum parking requirement for medical and dental offices and
                  clinics from one space per 175 square feet to one space per 200 square feet for the first 4,000
                  square feet and one space per 300 square feet thereafter (matching the existing parking rate
                  for General Office), and establish a minimum parking requirement for "Outpatient Surgery
                  Centers" of one space per 175 square feet; and (ii) amending Article I, Section 28-2,
                  "Definitions," to define the term "Outpatient Surgery Center"; providing for severability;
                  providing for repealer; providing for codification; providing an effective date (AM-24-05)

           Zoning Manager Tori Boone gave the PowerPoint presentation. She began with a brief overview of
           the Medical Parking rates and the proposed amendments and advised that staff recommended
           approval. Upon concluding, she made herself available for questions and discussion ensued. Council
           Member Wigder shared a brief PowerPoint and suggested additional language that would further
           define facilities affected by the proposed Code amendment. Development Services Director Brandon
           Schaad and Ms. Boone provided additional details and information.

           The public comment period was opened. Jonathan Ounjian voiced his concern over the amount of
           allowable parking based on certain definitions of uses. Seeing no one else come forward, the public
           comment period was closed.

           Members made final comments regarding the amended language to Sections 28-2 and 28-1655 of the
           Code.
                       Motion was made by Council Member Marc Wigder, seconded by Council Member
                       Andy Thomson, to approve Ordinance No. 5698 as amended. Motion carried
                       unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Yvette Drucker, Council Member
                       Fran Nachlas, Council Member Andy Thomson, Council Member Marc Wigder voting
                       yes.

19.   MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Nachlas advised that there was a bike survey available on the City's Facebook page and
encouraged residents to participate. She then noted that a cone of silence does not prevent the public from
forwarding their comments and concerns to Council Members and encouraged all to do so. Ms. Nachlas
revisited the discussion concerning code enforcement issues and suggested that staff review the authority of
the Special Magistrates in regard to code cases. Development Services Director Brandon Schaad provided
in-depth background about the Dan burg Code Case and answered Members' questions. Extended discussion
ensued and Members weighed in.

Council Member Thomson opined further on the ability of the Special Magistrate to make decisions on behalf
of the City.

Council Member Wigder also shared his thoughts on the Special Magistrate discussion. He then expressed
concerns regarding pedestrian and guardrails and, upon concluding, wished Ms. Nachlas a happy birthday.

Deputy Mayor Drucker welcomed new members of the City staff including Angel Buchannon , the Council's
Administrative Assistant, and Nathan George, the Transportation, Mobility, and Connectivity Director. She
concluded by wishing Council Member Nachlas a happy birthday.

Mayor Singer wished Ms. Nachlas happy birthday and a belated happy birthday to Mr. Thomson. He spoke
about the Council Members' roles to be the voice for constituent concerns and provide policy direction to the
Manager so staff could implement their vision. The Mayor then addressed a number of recent issues on which
residents had received misinformation and advised all that he and the Council Members made certain to relay
residents' concerns to the City Manager. Mr. Brown shared that the City has great employees and work well
as a team.
