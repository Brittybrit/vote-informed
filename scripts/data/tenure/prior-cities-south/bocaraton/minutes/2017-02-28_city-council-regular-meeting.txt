                Ordinance No. 5369
                An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28 "Zoning," Code of Ordinances, to
                incorporate new provisions relating to self-storage facilities (interior storage unit access);
                amending Section 28-307(1) relating to building intensity; amending the C-1 District regulations,
                Sections 28-872 and 28-873; amending the W-1 District regulations, Sections 28-101 i and 28-
                 1019; amending the IG/SI District regulations, Sections 28-1042 and 28-1043; amending the M-1
                District regulations, Sections 28-1072 and 28-1073; amending the M-2 District regulations,
                Sections 28-1097 and 28-1098; amending the M-3 District regulations, Sections 28-1123 and 28-
                1124; amending Article XV, Supplementary District Regulations, Division 1, by creating a new
                Section 28-1310 entitled "Self-Storage Facility (interior storage unit access)" and establishing
                development standards for such use; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing
                for codification; providing an effective date (AM-15-03)
                          Ordinance No. 5369



  Regular Minutes                                   4                                          February 28, 2017
      Ingrid Allen, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. She provided detailed background
      information, reviewing the revisions made to the ordinance at the February 14 City Council meeting. Ms.
      Allen also noted the changes proposed by Council Member Singer. Upon concluding, she made herself
      available for questions.

      The public hearing was opened. Charles Siemon, on behalf of the petitioner, made brief comments in
      support of the ordinance, noting the benefits of rehabilitating existing warehouse buildings. Michael
      Weiner, on behalf of Madison Holdings and Chris Baczewski, made his case for not approving the
      ordinance, advocating taking a slower approach and not rush into changes to six zoning districts. He
      provided an email from a storage owner who addressed what he feels he could now build should this
      pass. Upon concluding, he responded to questions. Tom Head, petitioner, commented on the length of
      time that this ordinance has been under consideration and made himself available for questions. Chris
      Baczewski addressed comments made at a previous meeting and contended that the proposed changes
      are not known in the industry locally. He advocated holding a workshop on this topic and not adopting the
      ordinance. Ele Zachariades offered support of language proposed by Council Member Singer. Seeing no
      one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

      At Council Member Singer's request, the City Attorney reviewed multiple changes to the ordinance that he
      proposed to address a number of the concerns expressed. A copy of the revised ordinance was
      distributed. Members discussed the proposals. George Brown, Deputy City Manager, responded to a
      question about the genesis of this ordinance. Discussion continued; members expressed concerns.
      Council Member Weinroth moved to adopt the original ordinance without any modifications. After further
      discussion, he modified his motion.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Michael
                  Mu/laugh, to adopt Ordinance No. 5369 as revised on November 8, 2016.

      Members provided further comments. Discussion of a postponement followed. Charles Siemon, on
      behalf of the applicant, suggested that Council adopt the ordinance.       Members discussed the
      ramifications of voting on the motion and a motion to reconsider. Mr. Siemon proposed a number of
      revisions that might satisfy Council's concerns. Further changes were proposed by Council Member
      Singer.

                  Council Member Weinroth withdrew his motion.

      Members continued to discuss a number of changes while also expressing their concerns.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Scott
                  Singer, to continue Ordinance No. 5369 to March 28, 2017.

      Members made final comments. It was suggested that staff hold a workshop with stakeholders on this
      subject.

                  Motion carried on a 4 to 1 vote; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council
                  Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott Singer voting yes; Council Member Robert S.
                  Weinroth voting no.

              Ordinance No. 5377
              An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton to amend Chapter 28 "Zoning," Code of Ordinances, to
              incorporate new provisions relating to specialty storage and storage (interior access); amending
              the Definitions, Section 28-2, to define "Collectibles", "Specialty Storage", and "Storage (Interior
              Access)"; amending the LIRP District, Section 28-977, to allow specialty storage and storage
              (interior access) as permitted uses; amending the LIRP District, Section 28-979(j), to remove
              storage uses as a prohibited use; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for
              codification; providing an effective date (AM-16-05)
                       Ordinance No. 5377

      Elizabeth Eassa, Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Background information was provided and
      the proposed revisions were reviewed. Staff's analysis was presented, and it was noted that the petition
      is consistent with the City's Comprehensive Plan.       The Planning & Zoning Board unanimously


Regular Minutes                                      5                                          February 28, 2017
      recommended approval. Ms. Eassa then reviewed several changes that staff proposed for clarification
      and she noted that staff recommends approval.

      The public hearing was opened. Andrea Keiser, on behalf of the petitioner, began by offering an
      explanation of the distinctions between this ordinance and the last one Council considered. Ms. Keiser
      gave a PowerPoint presentation illustrating the type of self-storage being proposed in the LIRP district.
      Upon concluding, she responded to questions from Council members. Michael Weiner advocated that
      consideration of this ordinance be postponed in order for Council to look at the local self-storage industry
      as whole. Chris Baczewski commented on aspects of the industry and advocated holding a workshop on
      this and the previous ordinance. Danielle Vennett commented on the amount of self-storage square
      footage proposed to be allowed by the ordinance, among other topics. She then responded to questions
      regarding the former IBM facility which is the site that prompted this request. Seeing no one else come
      forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

      Council Member Weinroth asked about having on-site administration during hours that the facility is open.
      The City Attorney addressed the proposed revisions proposed by staff. Ms. Eassa addressed how both
      specialty storage and interior access self-storage came to be included in the ordinance. Andrea Keiser
      and Danielle Vennett responded to that question and other questions from Council members. Discussion
      ensued. Members expressed concerns. The City Attorney proposed language to address the definition
      of specialty storage to include business records, as well as including a requirement for an on-site
      attendant during hours when the facility is open to the public. Ms. Frieser noted that all reference to
      "storage (interior access)" would be deleted in the title and recitals. The definition of same would be
      deleted in its entirety. After further discussion, it was decided to revise the cap downward to 500,000
      square feet. Ms. Vennett voiced her disappointment with some of the changes proposed. Further
      discussion ensued.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Robert S.
                  Weinroth, to approve Ordinance No. 5377 as amended. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                  Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council
                  Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes.
