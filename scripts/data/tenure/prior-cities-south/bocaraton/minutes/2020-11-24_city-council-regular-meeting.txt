 11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

          A.      Resolution No. 180-2020
                  A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering an amendment to an approved conditional use
                  and master plan (Resolution No. 1-93, as amended by Resolution Nos. 14-2001, 160-2010 and 70-
                  2019), to amend conditions of approval by eliminating a requirement to extend a left turn lane on
                  North Military Trail, and changing the timing of a required extension of a southbound right turn lane
                  on North Military Trail, for Lynn University located at 3601 North Military Trail; providing for repealer;
                  providing an effective date (CA-10-04R2)

        The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. The City
        Clerk administered the oath to those who wished to speak. Members made their ex parte disclosures.

        John Lindgren, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Mr. Lindgren highlighted the subject
        location and provided a brief overview of the application. The Planning and Zoning Board recommended
        approval. It was noted that the proposed amendment is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan. Staff
        recommends approval. Upon concluding, Mr. Lindgren made himself available for members questions.

        The public hearing was opened. Lauren Odom, on behalf of the applicant, asked members for their approval
        and made herself available for questions. There was no cross examination or rebuttal. Seeing no one else
        indicate their desire to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Andy Thomson, seconded by Deputy Mayor Andrea
                          Levine O'Rourke, to approve Resolution No. 180-2020. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                          Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker,
                          Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.

          B.       Ordinance No. 5551
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton considering amendments to the infrastructure element of
                  the City's Comprehensive Plan to update the City's 10-Year Water Supply Facilities Work Plan,
                  incorporate alternative water supply projects identified in the South Florida Water Management
                  District's Regional Water Supply Plan, and revise potable water level of service standards;
                  providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (SC-20-03)

        Mayor Singer noted that this was a transmittal hearing.

        Heather Hansen, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Ms. Hansen began by describing the
        transmittal process and requirements of the State guidelines. Updated elements of the 2016 Water Supply
        Facilities Work Plan were reviewed, including but not limited to, population projections, potable water
        distribution and storage figures, and City water/reclaimed water commodity rates. Ms. Hansen noted that
        the amendments were consistent with Comprehensive Plan. Both the Planning and Zoning Board and
        Development Services staff recommended approval.

        The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one indicate their desire to speak, closed.

                          Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member Andy
                          Thomson, to transmit Ordinance No. 5551. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott
                          Singer, Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member Yvette Drucker, Council
                          Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
