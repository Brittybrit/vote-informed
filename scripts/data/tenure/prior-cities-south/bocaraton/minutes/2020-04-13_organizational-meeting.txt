                           MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING
                                        CITY COUNCIL
                                CITY OF BOCA RATON, FLORIDA
                                     Monday,April13,2020
                                           1: 30 PM

      The Organizational Meeting of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton, Florida was called to order
                                      by Mayor Scott Singer at 1:30 PM.

1.     CALL TO ORDER:

Mayor Singer welcomed all those listening to the Organizational Meeting prior to reading a brief statement
explaining that the meeting would be conducted as a Virtual Meeting using Communications Media Technology.
He identified the members of the City Council appearing remotely as well as City Manager Leif Ahnell and City
Attorney Diana Grub Frieser.

2.     PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG:

3.     ROLL CALL:

        Mayor Scott Singer
        Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers
        Council Member Monica Mayotte
        Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke

4.    CEREMONIAL PROCEEDINGS:

       A.     Comments by newly elected Council Members

       Mayor Singer noted that he, along with newly elected Council Members Andrea Levine O'Rourke and
       Andy Thomson, were previously sworn into office remotely. He then congratulated both Ms. O'Rourke
       and Mr. Thomson and invited them to give comment. Ms. O'Rourke began by thanking City staff for
       making it possible for everyone to meet and said she was honored to serve the City for another term. Mr.
       Thomson commented that it was an honor to serve again. Mayor Singer also stated it was an honor and
       privilege to serve the residents and work with everyone during these challenging times.

5.    ORGANIZATIONAL PROCEEDINGS:

       A.     Selection of Deputy Mayor

       Council Member O'Rourke suggested that no change be made.

               Motion was made by Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council Member
               Andy Thomson, to reappoint Jeremy Rodgers as Deputy Mayor. Motion carried unanimously on
               a voice vote.

       B.     Selection of CRA Chairman and Vice Chairman

               Motion was made by Council Member Monica Mayotte, seconded by Council Member Andy
               Thomson, to reappoint Andrea O'Rourke as CRA Chairman. Motion carried unanimously on a
               voice vote.

               Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andy
               Thomson, to reappoint Monica Mayotte as CRA Vice Chairman. Motion carried unanimously on a
               voice vote.
6.      OTHER BUSINESS:

         A.      Selection of TPA (Transportation Planning Agency) Representatives (2)

         B.      Selection of Palm Beach County League of Cities Voting Delegate & Alternate

         C.      Selection of !PARC (lnterlocal Plan Amendment Review Committee) Representative

Mayor Singer noted that they could appoint these positions individually or continue with the existing appointments.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Mayotte, to reappoint
                  the current representatives to their positions. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

Mayor Singer congratulated everyone on their reappointments. He then commented on the possibility of an
annual rotation of roles. Council members commented and agreed to have further discussion in the future.

7.     ADJOURNMENT:

The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 1:42 PM.
