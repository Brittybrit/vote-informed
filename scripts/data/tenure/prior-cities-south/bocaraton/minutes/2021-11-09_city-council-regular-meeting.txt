 7.        BOARD APPOINTMENTS:

 The Mayor provided an opportunity for Public Comment regarding Board Appointments. Reginald Cox, John
 Martin, Cassie Kissel Moorman, and Morris Carstarphen expressed their support for applicants that they
 believed represented the community served by the Boca Raton Housing Authority (BRHA). Angela McDonald,
 BRHA board member, suggested expanding the number of appointees on the board. Charles Ridlay, chair of
 Unify of Palm Beach County, commented that a greater percentage of those residents served by the BRHA
 should be represented on the board.
          A       Boca Raton Housing Authority

        Lanette Wright, George Love, and Carol Wolfe interviewed.

        Deputy Mayor O'Rourke commented on the potential to expand the membership of the board. Council
        Member Mayotte and Council Member Drucker agreed. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser advised she
        would research the statutes and report back at a future meeting. Council Members thanked those who
        spoke for coming to the meeting and sharing their concerns.

                         Council Member Andy Thomson nominated Charles Graves, Council Member Monica
                         Mayotte nominated Lanette Wright, and Council Member Yvette Drucker nominated
                         George Love. In the voting, Lanette Wright received three votes and Charles Graves
                         received two votes. Therefore, Ms. Wright was appointed to the Boca Raton Housing
                         Authority.

          B.      Financial Advisory Board

                         Motion was made by Council Member Yvette Drucker, seconded by Council Member
                         Monica Mayotte, to appoint Jeffrey Pinder to the Financial Advisory Board. Motion
                         carried unanimously on a voice vote.

          C.      General Employees' Pension Board

                         Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Andrea Levine O'Rourke, seconded by Council
                         Member Monica Mayotte, to appoint Sidney Seligman, and Mayor Scott Singer
                         nominated Tamara McKee. In the voting, Sidney Seligman received three votes and
                         Tamara McKee received two votes. Therefore, Mr. Seligman was appointed to the
                         General Employees' Pension Board.

 20.     MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Deputy Mayor O'Rourke began by thanking the Scouts for their attendance. She then commented on a letter
received by Council from the Boca Country Club Master Association and noted the work done by City staff to
upgrade the property. She concluded by thanking all who attended the meeting.

Council Member Drucker thanked the Cub Scouts and all who spoke during the meeting and encouraged
residents to reach out to Council Members on issues of concern. She also reported on her participation at
the recent Florida League of Cities conference.

 Council Member Mayotte thanked all of the applicants for the Boca Raton Housing Authority position. She then
 encouraged residents to be aware of the potential for car break-ins, reminded everyone to lock their vehicles
 and to not leave valuables inside.

Mayor Singer reported on the decrease in crime within the City. He then asked for comments on his proposed
Board survey questions. Council Members provided comment and discussion ensued. There was consensus
to add a question regarding board reporting to City Council. Mayor Singer commented on the ribbon cutting at
the new playground at Hughes Park, the TimberTech Championship, Veteran's Day ceremonies and upcoming
holiday events. He concluded by noting the Celebration of Life for former Deputy Mayor and State
Representative Bill Hager.
