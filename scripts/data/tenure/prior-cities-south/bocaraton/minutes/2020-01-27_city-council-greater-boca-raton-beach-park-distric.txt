I.     Welcome - Mayor Scott Singer and Chair Susan Vogelgesang

       Mayor Scott Singer called the meeting to order at 6:00 pm. Council Members and Commissioners
       introduced themselves.

II.    Discussion Topic

       A       Ocean Breeze Golf Course

       Mayor Singer briefly recapped events since the last Joint Meeting. Chair Susan Vogelgesang of the Greater
       Boca Raton Beach & Park District (District) asked Council for approval of the Price-Fazio golf course design,
       as the District had unanimously voted to take over the building of the course. District Executive Director
       Briann Harms and City Manager Leif Ahnell provided additional information regarding recent
       communication between the two entities. Members and Commissioners debated a number of topics,
       including, but not limited to, renegotiation of the interlocal agreement (ILA), potential budget impacts and
       construction timeframe of the proposed golf course. Discussion ensued.

       B.      Other Topics

       There was no business to discuss.

Ill.   Public Comment

       The floor was opened for public comment. Glenn Gromann, Greg Galanis, Jim Ciliberti, Tom Lambert, Rick
       Heard, Fred Romanin, Jim Utterback, John Carter, Alan Feinberg, Pat Simpson, Regina Ecklund, Harold
       Chaffee, Christopher Carr, Roy Jones, Barry Tetrault, Robert Dukate, and Angelo Sands commented on
       their desire to have a golf course built and for both entities to work together. Seeing no one else come
       forward to speak, the public comment period was closed.

       Further discussion ensued. The City Manager and City Attorney commented on the City's amended ILA
       and advised they would review information recently received by the District. Council Members and
       Commissioners made final comments. There was consensus to have staff schedule the next joint meeting.

IV.    Adjournment

       The meeting was adjourned without objection at approximately 8:44 pm.
