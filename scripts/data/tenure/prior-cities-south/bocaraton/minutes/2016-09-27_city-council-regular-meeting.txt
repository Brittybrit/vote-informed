11.    QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

                 Resolution No. 105-2016
                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a planned mobility development (PMD) master
                 plan and site plan to allow for a single phase development of a 90-unit multifamily residential
                 development with 6 parking structures in 6 buildings for APOC Townhomes PMD generally
                 located at 950 Northwest Broken Sound Parkway; considering modifications of development
                 parameters related to: (I) required sidewalks; (II) planted buffer zones for the sidewalks; (Ill)
                 allowance for utilities to be located in the sidewalk along Northwest Broken Sound Parkway; (IV)
                 buffering/screening or wrapping of parking structures; and (V) pedestrian walkways separated
                 from the drive aisles near all entrances and exits to and from parking structures; considering a
                 waiver of plat requirements; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an
                 effective date (PMD-15-01/SPA-15-11)
                          Resolution No. 105-2016 Part I
                          Resolution No. 105-2016 Part II
                          Plans

        The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearings.
        Members noted their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to all those who wished
        to speak.

        Jim Bell, Acting Deputy Director of Development Services, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He began
        with a review of the petition including details of the master plan/site plan, zoning, land use, location, and
        project components. Details of the master plan/site plan, including types of units, parking, amenities, and
        building elevations were provided. The Community Appearance Board approved the project, making
        several recommendations. Mr. Bell reviewed the traffic impacts and the project's compliance with PMD
        requirements, noting that the minimum number of points was reached. The requested project
        modifications were then detailed, as were the improvements being made to the master plan/site plan. The
        Planning & Zoning Board unanimously recommended approval. Staff also recommended approval.
        Upon concluding, Mr. Bell responded to questions from the Council members.

        The public hearing was opened. Bonnie Miskel, on behalf of the petitioner, gave a brief PowerPoint
        presentation reviewing the project and advocating passage of the resolution. Ms. Miskel addressed some
        questions posed regarding the garages in this townhome project under the PMD regulations. The
        presentation was then turned over to project architect Juan Caycedo who described the project design.
        Both Mr. Caycedo and Ms. Miske! then addressed questions about the open parking structures and the
        FAR cap in the PMD requirements. It was noted that a text amendment to fix these limitations on parking
        structures in PMDs was intended to be proposed. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public
        hearing was closed

                      Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor
                      Michael Mu/laugh, to approve Resolution No. 105-2016.

        Members made final comments. The City Manager noted that condition (o) on page 15 of the resolution
        prohibits any modifications, such as doors or walls, to the open parking structures. George Brown, Deputy
        City Manager, suggested a solution that, should the Code be amended as discussed, would allow the
        addition of doors and walls with the approval of the Community Appearance Board (CAB). The petitioner
        confirmed that the footprint will not change; only walls and doors would be added if a Code text
        amendment is adopted. Mr. Brown confirmed that the text amendment that would have limited
        application. The City Attorney then read into the record language to be added to condition (o) on page
        15, addressing the enclosure of six parking structures with walls and doors should the Code be amended
        as discussed and noting that only CAB review would be required. Council Member Weinroth and Deputy
        Mayor Mullaugh, motion maker and seconder, accepted the amendment. The petitioner had no objection.

18.       CITY MANAGER RECOMMENDATIONS AND REPORTS:

           A.           Municipal Golf Course West - Potential Sale of Property

           George Brown, Deputy City Manager, gave a brief presentation, reviewing the process for the potential
           sale of the western municipal golf course. Staff is requesting formal direction from Council, in the form of
           a motion, to proceed with the process that would request submittal of best offers by October 21 which
           City Council would then consider at their November 22 meeting. Mr. Brown then responded to a number
           of questions. The City Attorney and the City Manager provided further information.

           The floor was opened for public comment. Ellyn Bogdanoff, representing GL Homes, and Debbie Leising
           both commented on the process as proposed. Don Huber commented on his group's proposal for the
           Ocean Breeze golf course. Doug Coller advocated having a public golf course in the City. Seeing no one
           else come forward to speak, the public comment period was closed.

           Further discussion ensued. Members agreed to modify the process to entertain only highest, best and
           last offers submitted on October 21; to stipulate that the City will continue to operate the course until
           closing; and that the offers must be delivered to the City Manager's Office on October 21 between 4:30
           and 5 pm. The City Attorney addressed some of the proposed changes to the process.

                            Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, seconded by Mayor Susan Haynie, to
                            move forward with the process as presented and modified. The motion carried 4 to O on a
                            voice vote.

The City Manager had no further reports.
