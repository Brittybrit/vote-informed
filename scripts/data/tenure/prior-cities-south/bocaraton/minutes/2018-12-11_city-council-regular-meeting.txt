14.     PUBLIC REQUESTS:

Aleksandra Gonzalez, speaking on behalf of attorney Michael Weiner for the Spanish Oaks Condominium
Association, commented on traffic safety concerns associated with the Boca Raton Regional Hospital site plan.

Lissette Gonzalez, representing iPic Entertainment in Mizner Park, expressed interest in extending the theater's
parking agreement with the City.

Ellyn Okrent, CEO of Florence Fuller Child Development Center and the Children's Museum, provided an update
on the work being done at the Museum. She asked for support for future funding of renovations to Singing Pines.
Ms. Okrent then responded to questions from members.

Juan Gavilan, owner of Kentucky Fried Chicken on Federal Highway, commented on issues regarding a wall
between his restaurant and the adjoining property.
