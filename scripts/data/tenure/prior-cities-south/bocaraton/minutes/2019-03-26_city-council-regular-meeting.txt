jb       REGULAR PUBLIC HEARINGS:

                  Ordinance No. 5481
                  An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton amending Chapter 28 "Zoning," Article XV,
                  "Supplementary District Regulations," Division 1. Section 28-1302, "Car Agencies," Code
                  of Ordinances, to delete the requirement that a car agency be enclosed by a six foot high
                  masonry wall; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing for codification;
                  providing an effective date (AM-18-07)

          Elizabeth Eassa, Senior Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation. Background information on
          the code was reviewed and analysis of the amendment was provided. The Planning and Zoning
          Board recommended approval. Staff recommended approval as well. Ms. Eassa responded to
          Council members' questions, as did Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, and Diana
          Grub Frieser, City Attorney.

          The public hearing was opened. Doug Mummaw spoke on behalf of the applicant and suggested
          postponement due to new information. Mr. Mummaw addressed the original zoning regulation
          from 1965 and how needs have changed, and explained the rationale behind the request to
          eliminate the wall requirement. He advised that boutique dealers operating within the City would
          be impacted by the Code change. He then responded to questions. Mr. Schaad and Ms. Frieser
          provided additional information. Jim Wood inquired about enforcement of this Code change. Mr.
