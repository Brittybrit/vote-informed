11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS:

                 Resolution No. 148-2016
                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering an amendment to conditional use Resolution
                 No. 3-95 to allow for a child care facility for up to 60 children in an existing building located at St.
                 David Armenian Church, located at 2300 West Yamato Road: providing for severability; providing
                 for repealer: providing an effective date (CA-94-11 R1)
                          Resolution No. 148-2016
                          Drawings

         The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would govern the public hearing. Members
         noted their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to all those who wished to speak.

         Tam ash Rahman, Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation beginning with a review of the request. The
         site location and zoning were provided. The site plan was also presented and its details were reviewed. It
         was noted that the Planning & Zoning Board recommended approved the amendment. The petitioner has
         asked that two conditions of the approval related to accessible routes to the building and a sidewalk along
         Yamato road be removed. Upon conclusion, Ms. Rahman responded to questions from Council members.

         The public hearing was opened. Douglas Mummaw, on behalf of the petitioner, gave a brief PowerPoint
         presentation. He noted that this is one of several churches on Yamato Road that have similar programs.
         He then reviewed the site plan, noting the location of the building and the routes for accessibility and
         parking. Robert Eisen then addressed the conditions that the petitioner is requesting be eliminated from
         the site plan approval, making the case for the request related to accessible route for drop off and noting
         that the condition for an 8 foot sidewalk is acceptable to the petitioner. Both gentlemen then responded to
         questions. Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Michael
                  Mu/laugh, to approve Resolution No. 148-2016 striking condition 8.c.

         Council Member Singer addressed the width of the Yamato Road sidewalk and how to address the
         zoning code that stipulates the 8 foot width. Discussion ensued. The City Attorney provided language to
         be added as a notation to condition 8.e. generally consistent with "in the event that the City Code is
         amended to reduce the required width of the sidewalk to less than 8 feet, then the 8 feet requirement will
         be reduced, but nothing herein suggests an entitlement to a reduction of the sidewalk width."      Both

17.     QUASI-JUDICIAL PUBLIC HEARINGSNARIANCES & APPEALS:

                 Resolution No. 151-2016
                 A resolution of the City of Boca Raton considering a variance from the provisions of Section 24-
                 96{b){1 ), Code of Ordinances, to allow one free-standing sign with eight {8) tenant names on two
                  sign faces {where only four (4) tenant names are allowed on two sign faces): and a second free­
                 standing sign with six (6) tenant names on each sign face {where only four {4) tenant names are
                 allowed on each sign face), for a commercial property located at 1944 NE Fifth Avenue; providing
                 for severability: providing for repealer: providing an effective date {SCV-15-02)
                           Resolution No. 151-2016

         The City Attorney confirmed that quasi-judicial procedures reviewed earlier would continue to govern this
         public hearing. Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the oath to all
         those who wished to speak.

         John Harbilas, Zoning Officer, gave the PowerPoint presentation. The location of the property as well as
         the location of the two monuments signs on the site were identified. A history of the petitioner's requests
         was provided. Both monument signs were described. It was noted that the signs met code with the
         exception of the number of tenant names on the signs. Staff determined that the criteria needed for
         granting a variance have not been met and therefore recommend denial. The Community Appearance
         Board (CAB) actions and recommendations were reviewed. Mr. Harbilas concluded, again noting that
         staff recommends denial. He then responded to a number of questions from Council members, including
         regarding the allowable number of signs at the property and the Council's continuation of the variance to
         allow applicant to work on an acceptable solution.

         The public hearing was opened. Douglas Mummaw, on behalf of the petitioner, gave a PowerPoint
         presentation. He began by addressing the number of signs allowed on the property. He then addressed
         the variance being requested, noting that the brands of the businesses in the plaza need to be
         recognized. Topics included, but were not limited to, the color and number of individual panels and the
         hardship justifications, Mayor Haynie noted her intent to have a roundtable with CAB to discuss many of
         the issues discussed tonight. She also noted that tonight's request is for the number and colors of the
         sign panels only. The City Manager noted that a rewrite of the sign code was underway. Mark Jacobsen,
         CAB chair, provided insight into the decisions of the Board with regard to this request, noting that, as this
         property is quite long, the Board would allow more signs but not more than four panels per sign. He then
         responded to a number of questions. Mr. Mummaw responded to further questions about suggestions
         for changing the proposed signs. No one else came forward to speak. Mr. Mummaw made closing
         remarks. The public hearing was then closed.

         Mayor Haynie reiterated the need for a roundtable with CAB to discuss signage policy as it will affect the
         aesthetics of the community. Council members offered their comments and opinions with regard to a
         roundtable to discuss policy and the variances under consideration. In response to a questions, the City
         Attorney commented on the recent Supreme Court decision on signage, changes to policy with regard to
         signs and Council's options for this variance request. In response to Council Member Singer, Mr.
         Mummaw requested that Council rule on these variances.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Michael
                  Mu/laugh, to approve Resolution No. 151-2016 to grant the variances for two six-panel signs.
                  Motion failed on a 2 to 3 vote; Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Robert S.
                  Weinroth voting yes.; Mayor Susan Haynie, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member
                  Scott Singer voting no.

         The City Attorney reviewed the rules of the Council with regard to a motion to reconsider in order to
         continue the item. Ms. Frieser noted that the rules have an unusual carve out exception not allowing
         reconsideration with regard to ordinances and resolutions. However, the Council is not barred from
         making a motion to allow for a lesser variance. Discussion ensued

                  Motion was made by Council Member Scott Singer, seconded by Council Member Jeremy
                  Rodgers, to approve Resolution No. 151-2016 to grant the variances for two five-panel signs.

         After discussion of possible action that could be taken at this meeting, the current motion was set aside.
