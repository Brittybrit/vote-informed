3.       PUBLIC HEARING

       A.         Resolution No. 106-2019

              A resolution of the City of Boca Raton equalizing the assessments; levying the same as
              assessments against benefited properties within the Downtown Special Assessment District, City
              of Boca Raton, Florida; directing the City Clerk to record assessments in the Improvement Lien
              Book; establishing priority of lien and payment of principal and interest; providing for severability;
              providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                          Resolution No. 106-2019

      Stephen Timberlake, Special Projects Manager, gave a PowerPoint presentation. He provided a brief
      history of the special assessment and noted that the purpose of the meeting was to hear and consider any
      and all objections to the special assessment, which is required to fund the debt service and administrative
      costs for the special assessment revenue bonds. The method of calculating the assessment rate was
      explained and various charts and graphs were presented. Upon concluding, Mr. Timberlake made himself
      available for questions.

      The public hearing was opened and, seeing no one come forward to speak, closed. The Director provided
      additional information.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Monica
                  Mayotte, to approve Resolution No. 106-2019. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor Scott Singer,
                  Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member
                  Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andy Thomson voting yes.
