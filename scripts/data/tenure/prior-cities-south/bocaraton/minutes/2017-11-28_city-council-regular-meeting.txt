       Elizabeth Eassa, Planner, gave the PowerPoint presentation on the five petitions for Yamato Villas. The
       location and zoning of the property were provided. The site plan was reviewed; details were provided .
       Topics included, but were not limited to, site access, driveway length, parking, garages, various
       conditions of the development, elevations, and the tentative plat. The requested modifications to
       development parameters were listed . The traffic analysis was reviewed. The requested technical
       deviation for off-street vehicular circulation was explained. The proposed future land use change and
       rezoning were reviewed. The project's consistency with the Comprehensive Plan was reviewed . The
       Planning and Zoning Board recommended approval of all components of the application. Ms. Eassa
       pointed out the revision to the site plan resolution that was the result of an updated School Capacity
       Available Determination (SCAD) letter including a contribution to the School District. Staff recommended
       approval of the petition, subject to the conditions in the ordinances and resolution. Upon concluding, Ms.
       Eassa responded to Council members' questions, as did Brandon Schaad, Development Services
       Director. Yajaira Moleiro, City Traffic Engineer, also responded to questions about trip generation.

       The public hearing was opened. Heather Allen, on behalf of the applicant, gave a PowerPoint
       presentation in support of the petition . She reviewed the application requests and then provided a history
       of the property and the type of development appropriate for the location. Details were provided on the
       land use change and rezoning. Ms. Allen then introduced the project architect, Juan Caycedo, who
       reviewed the design of the project, in particular the townhouse pods. Ms. Allen then detailed the
       requested technical deviation to allow on-street circulation and a one-way drive aisle. Other topics
       included open space and landscape buffers. In conclusion, Ms. Allen advocated approval and noted that
       the petitioner agreed to the contribution to the School District. Both Ms. Allen and Mr. Caycedo then
       responded to Council members' questions, as did staff.

       Mike Rentler provided copies of a petition signed by a number of neighbors and expressed opposition to
       the project. Citing a number of issues, Carol Rice also expressed opposition to the project. JoAnne
       Weise expressed her concerns and opposition to the project. Charlie Helton commented on the project,
       noting his concern should this property being developed as a commercial site and his support of keeping
       this a residential project.

       During rebuttal, JoAnn Weise addressed previous comments regarding commercial development. Ms.
       Allen then provided brief comments and then responded to further questions. Seeing no one else come
       forward to speak, the public hearing was closed .

       At the request of Council members, Deputy City Manager George Brown provided information on
       previous commercial proposals for the property and the impact of commercial development.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Mayor Susan Haynie, to
                  approve Ordinance No. 5428.

       Members commented, offering their thoughts and opinions. At Council Member Singer's request, the City
       Attorney read draft language to be added to a condition in the site plan resolution that would require the
       School District's SCAD voluntary contribution be spent on schools in Boca Raton. Lengthy discussion
       ensued on a number of issues, in particular changing the land use. The City Attorney reminded all that
       the site plan is an essential component of the Comp Plan amendment; therefore the Comp Plan
       amendment should not be approved if the site plan is not acceptable. Discussion continued. At the
       request of the Mayor, Juan Caycedo addressed the design of the townhouses and the number of parking
       spaces. Ms. Allen also responded to questions. Discussion continued, in particular with regard to school
       overcrowding and parking . It was suggested to continue the matter and also suggested that a School
       Board representative be invited to attend a future meeting to discuss the Board's plans for schools in
       Boca. In general, members agreed to invite a School Board representative to a future meeting and that a
       continuation of the matter was best. Deputy Rodgers withdrew his motion.

                  Motion was made by Mayor Susan Haynie, seconded by Council Member Andrea
                  Levine O'Rourke, to continue Ordinance No. 5428 to January 23, 2018.

       In response to a question, the City Attorney provided information on concurrency with regard to school
       capacity and roadway infrastructure and the Council's ability to deny applications. Discussion followed.
