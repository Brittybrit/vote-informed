11.     QUASI-JUDICIAL AND RELATED PUBLIC HEARINGS

         Mayor Haynie announced that the public hearings on items A., B., and C. would be conducted
         contemporaneously and each petition would be voted on separately. She noted that Ordinance No. 5394
         required four votes for adoption. The City Attorney reviewed the quasi-judicial procedures that would
         govern the public hearing. Members made their ex parte disclosures. The City Clerk administered the
         oath to all those who wished to speak.

         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave a PowerPoint presentation on the ordinances and
         resolution, beginning with a brief review of the application that included a Future Land Use Map
         amendment, a rezoning and a site plan approval. The location, current land use and current zoning of the
         property were provided. Consistency, compatibility and concurrency of the Future Land Use amendment
         and rezoning with the Comprehensive Plan were discussed; concerns and inconsistency were pointed
         out. Mr. Schaad also noted a higher demand for emergency services and the costs related to same. The

         the project and had a favorable view. The Planning and Zoning Board recommended denial of the
         application. Staff also recommends denial. Upon conclusion, Mr. Schaad responded to questions from
         Council members.

         The public hearing was opened. Michael Marshall, on behalf of petitioner Goray-Rosemurgy Senior
         Living, gave a PowerPoint presentation. He noted the mix of uses adjacent to the property and
         contended that the project meets or exceeds the requirements of the proposed zoning. Elevations and
         details of the complex were provided. Mr. Marshall argued that the use is not incompatible with adjacent
         zoning uses and that it is compatible and consistent with the City's Comprehensive Plan. The benefits of
         the project were listed. He then introduced Dan Sacco, Boca Raton Regional Hospital, who expressed
         support for this project that the Hospital finds essential. Jim Rosemurgy, petitioner, made several points in
         support of the project, touting the partnership with the Hospital's Marcus Neuroscience Institute and
         Florida Atlantic University College of Nursing. Gerry Goray, petitioner, addressed the emergency services
         issue and offered support of the project. Council members asked a number of questions of the petitioners.
         Mr. Schaad the read into the record a number of conditions of approval amending Section 2 of the site
         plan resolution, to which the petitioners agreed. The City Attorney added a condition related to the urgent
         care center. Mr. Marshall thanked Council and offered final comments. Seeing no one else come

         A.       Resolution No. 95-2017
                  A resolution of the City Council of the City of Boca Raton considering an appeal by Outfront
                  Media LLC of an administrative decision (on behalf of the Development Services Department)
                  relating to Section 7.14 of the Unified Land Development Code (ULDC) resulting in a denial of
                  Outfront Media's request to alter an existing billboard (off-premise sign) from a mechanically
                  changeable sign face to an electronic, digital sign face (Permit No. 16-5912) located at 2471
                  Butts Road; providing for repealer; providing an effective date (BP16-00005912)

         The City Attorney reviewed the procedures that would be followed as this item is an administrative appeal
         under the County's ULDC.

         Brandon Schaad, Development Services Director, gave the PowerPoint presentation. He identified the
         location, land use and zoning of the property. The billboard was described as was the proposal for the
         change to an electronic face. Mr. Schaad noted that the County billboard settlement agreement has
         expired and, therefore, is no longer applicable. He continued to review his analysis of the situation,
         providing reasons why the sign is non-conforming and not allowable. Details were provided, including
         extensive references to the provisions of the ULDC. Mr. Schaad then reviewed the options before
         Council. Upon concluding he made himself available for questions.

         The public hearing was opened. William McCormick, GrayRobinson on behalf of the appellant, provided
         a notebook to Council. He then began his presentation, referring to various sections in the book, refuting
         the assertions made in the staff presentation and making an extensive case for allowing the electronic
         billboard. Ty Harris, GrayRobinson, offered his perspective on the issue. The public hearing was then
         closed.

         The City Attorney began her comments by stating that Council should not rule in fear of a lawsuit. She
         then addressed a number of the points made by Mr. McCormick, providing counter points and explaining
         a number of the assertions made.

At this time, Mayor Haynie noted that a motion was needed to extend the meeting beyond 11 pm.

Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, to
extend the meeting beyond 11 pm. Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote.

         The City Attorney referenced the extensive staff report and then pointed out the letter from the County
         Zoning Director which stated that electronic billboards are not now and never have been allowed in Palm
         Beach County. Mr. McCormick requested that the hearing be reopened. The Mayor stated that the
         public hearing was closed and his objection to that was noted for the record. Ms. Frieser reviewed further
         aspects of this administrative appeal.

                  Motion was made by Council Member Singer, seconded by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth,
                  to approve Resolution No. 95-2017 to deny the appeal. Motion carried unanimously; Mayor
                  Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke,
                  Council Member Scott Singer, Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes.
