                .L        Resolution No. 60-2016
                           A resolution of the City of Boca Raton providing for a special election regarding an
                           ordinance proposed by initiative to regulate the use of city-owned lntracoastal lands;
                           providing for submission of the ballot question to the electors of the City at a special
                           election on November 8 1 2016 (the date of the General Election); providing for
                           severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                                    Resolution No. 60-2016


       Jim Bell, Planning and Zoning Manager, gave the PowerPoint presentation. A brief overview of the
       background of this initiative petition ordinance. The numerous easements that exist on cit-y land adjacent
       to the lntracoastal were listed. The differences in code section 28-1307 and the proposed section were
       identified. It was noted that the PZB review resulted in a 3-3 vote; so there was no recommendation.
       Upon concluding, Mr. Bell responded to questions from Council members. The City Attorney addressed
       Resolution 60-2016 noting that it provides for a vote should the ordinance not be adopted.

       The public hearing was opened. Jim Wood gave a PowerPoint presentation identifying and discussing a
       number of public/private partnerships for parks, recreation and art. He advocated passage of the
       ordinance. James Hendrey and Jo-Ann Landon advocated passage of the ordinance. Winnie and Joe
       Pedalino commented on the City's parks and beaches and advocated passage of the ordinance. Andrea
       O'Rourke advocated having waterfront public spaces and passage of the ordinance. Jose Barerra
       commented on the site plan for the restaurant proposed for the Wildflower property and use of the
       property for public recreation. He advocated passage of the ordinance. Nancy Liu commented on
       congestion caused by recent development and advocated having a park on the Wildflower property. Ryan
       Dick commented on development in the city and advocated saving green space for the public and
       passage of the ordinance. Nancy Hendrey advocated passage of the ordinance and having green space
       and public parks. Joe Graubart, Kevin Meaney, George O'Rourke, Robbie Denman advocated passage of
       the ordinance. Linda Marinas advocated having a restaurant on the Wildflower property. Al Zucaro
       commented on Mr. Bell's presentation and the Planning & Zoning Board review of the ordinance,
       advocating its passage. Phillip Kittell expressed his view that the ordinance should be voted on by all the
       people. Margaret Fitzsimmons commented on protecting waterfront property and establishing guidelines
       for same. She advocated adoption of the ordinance. Glenn Gromann addressed aspects of the
       proposed ordinance and the Planning & Zoning Board review. Anthony Majhess addressed previous
       comments and his recollections of the Wildflower property acquisition during his tenure on City Council.
       Seeing no one else come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

                    Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Michael
                    Mu/laugh, to approve Resolution No. 60-2016.

       Discussion ensued. The City Attorney addressed questions regarding Ordinance 5356.

                    Motion was made by Council Member Scott Singer to lay Resolution No. 60-2016 on the table
                    and vote on Ordinance No. 5356. Motion died for lack of a second.

       Members commented further.

                    Motion to approve Resolution No. 60-2016 carried unanimously; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy
                    Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers, Council Member Scott Singer,
                    Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes.

                    Motion was made by Council Member Robert S. Weinroth, seconded by Deputy Mayor Michael
                    Mu/laugh, to indefinitely postpone consideration of Ordinance No. 5356. Motion carried on a 4 to
                    1 vote; Mayor Susan Haynie, Deputy Mayor Michael Mu/laugh, Council Member Jeremy Rodgers,
                    Council Member Robert S. Weinroth voting yes; Council Member Scott Singer voting no.

                       Ordinance No. 5357

      C.      Ordinance No. 5358
              An ordinance of the City of Boca Raton providing for the involuntary annexation of property
              generally located north of Clint Moore Road and west of Military Trail and comprising all currently
              unincorporated property in the following subdivisions: Azura (Oxley Northern PUD). Newport Bay
              Club. Tides at Newport Bay, Copper Lake, Hampton Club. Fieldbrook, Boniello's Acres. and
              Boniello's Acres Phase 2. as more particularly described herein, to the corporate limits of the City
              of Boca Raton, Florida, as initiated by the City of Boca Raton; providing for a referendum on the
              question of annexation for the registered electors within the property proposed for annexation;
              providing for a revision of the City's boundaries pursuant to the City Charter to include the
              property annexed; providing for severability; providing for repealer; providing an effective date
                      Ordinance No. 5358

     Mayor Haynie noted that this was the first of two public hearings on Ordinance 5357 and Ordinance 5358
     and no votes would be taken. George Brown, Deputy City Manager, gave a PowerPoint presentation on
     both of the proposed annexation areas based on the analysis completed by PMG Associates. The
     revenues and expenses associated with the annexations were reviewed as was the impact to the
     homeowners in each area. The analysis estimated the net fiscal impact at just over $764,000. The
     recommendation of the report was to move forward. The annexation actions calendar was reviewed.
     Upon concluding, Mr. Brown responded to questions from Council members.

     The public hearing was opened. Michael Vislocky, a resident of Boniello Acres, gave a PowerPoint
     presentation making the case that the City should not annex his neighborhood. He provided a handout
     for the Council in support of his position. Arlene Vislocky also expressed a lack of support for annexation.
     Jonathon Silver commented on what the impacts, other than financial, would be to the residents of
     Boniello Acres. He advocated having all city residents vote on this annexation. Daria Wells voiced her
     lack of support for annexation. Paula Winker asked a number of questions regarding annexation. Susan
     Silver advocated against annexation. George O'Rourke advocated having all City residents vote on
     annexations. George Gersh and Al Zucaro expressed opposition to this annexation. Seeing no one else
     come forward to speak, the public hearing was closed.

     Council members weighed in and posed a number of questions to which George Brown responded. In
     general, direction was given to explore the opportunity for all City residents to vote on annexation as well
     as the opportunity for Boniello Acres to vote separately on annexation of their neighborhood.
