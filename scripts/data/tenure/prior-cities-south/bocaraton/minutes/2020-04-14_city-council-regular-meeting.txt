         MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS:

Council Member Thomson began a discussion regarding reopening the boat ramp for use by commercial fishermen
only. Council Member O'Rourke expressed concerns regarding the safety of staff and potential overcrowding by
large numbers of the public. City Manager Leif Ahnell advised additional staff would be needed to review commercial
fishing licenses and provide admittance to the ramp. Discussion ensued. City Attorney Diana Grub Frieser provided
additional information and clarification. There was general consensus to direct staff to research the topic and report
back at the next Council meeting. Lastly, Mr. Thomson noted that local company SA Company would be having a
free facemask giveaway at their Boca Raton location.

Council Member O'Rourke asked if staff had considered the idea of pro-rating or extending the expiration dates of
parks and facilities permits. The City Manager remarked on the many factors that would need to be taken into
consideration. Ms. O'Rourke then commented on the work of Hospitality Helping Hands (H3) and asked how the
City could contribute.

Deputy Mayor Rodgers suggested the Financial Advisory Board research the possibility of pro-rating existing
permits. He also thanked H3 for their work in the community and suggested staff research ways to offer support.
Members weighed in on the options for assisting H3; there was general agreement to direct staff to research a
financial contribution. The City Attorney noted the Council could consider providing for public comment prior to
taking a vote.

                  Motion was made by Deputy Jeremy Rodgers, seconded by Council Member Andy Thomson, to
                  direct staff to provide a one-time grant of $10,000 to Hospitality Helping Hands for use within the
                  City of Boca Raton.

        Mayor Singer then provided an opportunity for public input. Anthony Barber, owner of Troy's BBQ, shared
        his approval for the City's donation and noted that free meals are also being distributed from his restaurant
        in conjunction with H3. Alan Feinberg Jr., Glenn Gromann, Dee Lawrence and Leo Denlea expressed their
        support for the City's proposed contribution. Seeing no one else indicate their desire to speak, the public
        comment period was closed.

                  Motion carried unanimously on a voice vote; Mayor Scott Singer, Deputy Mayor Jeremy Rodgers,
                  Council Member Monica Mayotte, Council Member Andrea Levine O'Rourke, Council Member
                  Andy Thomson voting yes.
