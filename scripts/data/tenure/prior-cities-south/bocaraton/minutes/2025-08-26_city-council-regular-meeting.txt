           A.      Government Campus Redevelopment Partnership Opportunity
                   1.    Reports from Staff and CBRE

                   Deputy City Manager Andrew Lukasik and Principal of Frisbie Group , Rob Frisbie, provided
                   an update on the Government Campus Redevelopment project. Items highlighted included
                   the construction of a new state~of-the-art Community Center, City Hall with Council Chambers ,
                   and recreational amenities . The project also focuses on enhancing transportation and
                   infrastructure, with efforts to slow traffic speeds through increased landscaping and traffic
                   calming devices. The project was proposed to be developed in phases, with the first phase
                   including the civic buildings , mixed-use residential , and retail spaces, as well as children's
                   playgrounds. The second phase would incorporate office spaces, a hotel , residential units,
                   and restaurants . Off-site developments and improvements included a new tennis center and
                   courts at Meadows Park, preserving the Singing Pines historical building with additional
                   parking , and creating a community garden at the North Park location . Other parks would also
                   see improvements, including new softball fields , restrooms , a nature trail , batting cages , and
                   a concession stand . A skate park was proposed to be built at North Park in conjunction with
                   the Beach and Park District. Mr. Frisbie noted that the project was expected to generate over
                   $3 billion in economic impact. City Manager George Brown provided a brief history of Memorial
                   Park and the surrounding property.

           the areas. She continued with the proposed amendments and subarea use changes. Ms. Cheese­
           Nelson advised that the Planning and Zoning Board and staff recommend approval. Upon concluding ,

           Brandon Schaad responded to Members' questions .
