Private Sector Impact:

                   The fiscal impact on the private sector is indeterminate. SB 422 will provide patients with
                   greater access to abuse-deterrent opioid analgesic drug products, which is expected to
                   reduce opioid drug misuse, abuse, and diversion. The increased use of abuse deterrent
                   drugs is expected to reduce emergency room and drug treatment costs associated with the
                   misuse or abuse of opioids without such abuse deterrent formulations.

                   The OIR notes that the bill does not require health insurance plans to have equivalent cost
                   sharing to the policyholder. As a result, the policyholders may incur additional cost
                   sharing if they switch to the abuse-deterrent opioids.13
