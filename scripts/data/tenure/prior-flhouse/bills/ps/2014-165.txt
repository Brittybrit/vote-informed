C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The possible addition of NCI-designated cancer centers to the state may generate positive fiscal
       outcomes for the private sector including, but not limited to, an increase of high paying jobs and other
       economic benefits. However, the specific economic impact is unknown.
