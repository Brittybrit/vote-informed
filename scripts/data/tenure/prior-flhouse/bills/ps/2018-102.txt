C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

           The direct economic impact is indeterminate. The bill provides insignificant cost savings for various
           license types and could potentially result in minor increases for insurers when agents who are not
           appointed by the insurers make sales under the exchange of business laws.
