Private Sector Impact:

             Tobacco permit holders would be required to replace their point of sale calendars that
             indicate the date that a person is of legal age to purchase tobacco products to new
             calendars that reference nicotine dispensing devices, unless the permit holders are using
             readers, scanners, or other electronic or automated systems. They would also be required
             to replace signage that gives notice that the sale of tobacco products to persons under the
             age of 18 is prohibited with signage that also references nicotine dispensing devices. The
             cost for this requirement is indeterminate.
