         Trustees, Ms. Camilla Schmitz, notified DEO that the District had not taken any action for two or more
         years37 and requested that DEO declare the District inactive.38 On January 21, 2012, DEO published
         the “Notice of Proposed Declaration of Inactive Status of the Northwest Florida Community Hospital
         District” in the Washington County News. Pursuant to statute, the notice required any objections to the
         District being placed on inactive status to be filed with DEO within 21 days of the initial publication of
         the notice; no objections were received. On February 13, 2012, DEO declared the District inactive.
         DEO notified the Speaker of the House, the President of the Senate, pursuant to statute that the district
         had been declared inactive.39

         EFFECT OF THE BILL

         The bill dissolves the Northwest Florida Community Hospital District by repealing ch. 88-532, Laws of
         Florida. Any assets and liabilities of the district are transferred to the Board of County Commissioners
         for Washington County.
