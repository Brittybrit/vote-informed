Private Sector Impact:

                 The bill authorizes the FWC to contract with private entities or individuals to capture or
                 destroy nonnative animals found on lands or waters of the state. This will have a positive
                 indeterminate fiscal impact on the entities or individuals who are awarded such contracts.

                 The bill requires pet dealers to implant a passive integrated transponder (PIT) tag before
                 animals that threaten the state’s wildlife habitat are sold, resold, or offered for sale. This
                 will have a negative indeterminate fiscal impact on pet dealers that offer such animals.
