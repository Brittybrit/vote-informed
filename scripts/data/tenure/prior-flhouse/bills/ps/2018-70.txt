C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       According to the FCC, the use of an invalid, unallocated, or unassigned number provides a strong
       indication that the calling party is spoofing the caller ID to potentially defraud and harm a telephone
       service customer. By authorizing telephone service providers to block such calls, the bill may reduce
       economic harm to victims of fraudulent schemes effectuated through spoofed telephone calls.
