C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
       The bill may have an indeterminate positive impact on private parties who bring actions challenging the
       enactment or enforcement by a local government of an ordinance on a matter preempted by state law,
       in that the prevailing private parties will be authorized to recover their attorney fees for such actions.
