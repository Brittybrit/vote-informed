Private Sector Impact:

                    The bill may have a positive fiscal impact on nurse registries and HHAs that operate
                    multiple licenses within the same AHCA district. If such HHAs or registries are able to
                    replace one or more licensed sites with unlicensed satellite or related offices, they will no
                    longer be required to hold multiple licenses and pay multiple license fees. Additionally,
                    HHAs and nurse registries located in AHCA districts with multiple counties may see a
                    positive fiscal impact if an additional office allows them to reduce travel and other
                    expenses related to having a single office serving multiple counties.
