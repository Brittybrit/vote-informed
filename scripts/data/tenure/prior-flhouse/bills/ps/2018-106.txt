C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Currently, a licensed APRN must maintain professional liability coverage and have a protocol with a
       supervising physician, and a CNS is not required to meet these requirements. A CNS who elects to
       become licensed as an APRN may incur additional costs to meet these APRN licensure requirements.
