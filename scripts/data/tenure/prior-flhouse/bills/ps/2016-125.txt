C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

         The bill deletes the requirements for PAs to submit sworn statements or affidavits. To the extent that a
         PA incurred costs associated with obtaining such statements, the bill may reduce costs associated with
         applying for licensure or renewing licensure.
