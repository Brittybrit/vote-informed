       Effect of Bill

       The bill amends ch. 70-718, Laws of Florida, by revising the space requirements for the issuance of
       SRX licenses to certain restaurants in Hillsborough County. Specifically, the bill reduces the overall
       required floor capacity from 4,000 square feet to no less than 2,500 square feet for a restaurant.
       Restaurants must still have a seating capacity of no less than 100 seats.

       Additionally, the bill includes the sale of nonalcoholic beverages as part of the 51 percent or more of
       gross income required in order for a restaurant to receive the SRX license. Lastly, the bill specifies that
       the DABT is authorized to issue rules, supervise SRX licenses issued, and revoke or suspend the SRX
       licenses for violations of the Beverage Law.
