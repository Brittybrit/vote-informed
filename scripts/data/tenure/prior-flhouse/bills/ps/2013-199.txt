C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill is expected to have most of its impact in the hospitality industry, as bars and restaurants will be
       able to reduce cost and waste associated with spoiled wine.

       Moreover, there may be a benefit to the private sector in being able to purchase larger size containers
       of wine, especially if a bulk rate is available.
