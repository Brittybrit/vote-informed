B. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
       The bill is expected to have an indeterminate but positive impact on the private sector. It will minimize
       the value of assessments and value and duration of surcharges to recover assessments that fund the
       claims and expenses of insolvent title insurers.

   C. FISCAL COMMENTS:
       1. The bill authorizes the receiver to expend excess surcharges remitted under the statute and bill for
          purposes specified by the bill. This includes distributing funds to title insurers that are not claimants
          to receivership estates. These expenditures are outside of and are not the asset of any insolvent
          estate. It is unclear if this activity is subject court order, or solely within the discretion of the receiver
          to expend.
