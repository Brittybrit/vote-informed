Private Sector Impact:

             Under CS/SB 642, the Florida ABLE program will assist individuals with disabilities in
             saving money in tax-advantaged accounts without losing their eligibility for state and
             federal benefits, and thereby providing a pathway for economic independence. The bill
             would allow an indeterminate number of individuals to save additional assets or resources
             in these accounts and use the funds to pay for qualified disability expenses.

             The Florida Prepaid College Board estimates that 4,000 individuals in Florida may open
             ABLE accounts out of an estimated target population of 400,000.
