Private Sector Impact:

                  The bill reduces the maximum amount that may be charged for a permit for a hardwired,
                  low-voltage alarm system by $15 (from $55 to $40), and prohibits any other charges for
                  installation or replacement of such systems. The bill provides that no permits are required
                  for burglar alarm systems or smoke detectors that are not hardwired (wireless alarms and
                  detectors). This will reduce or eliminate permitting costs associated with these systems
                  and detectors.
