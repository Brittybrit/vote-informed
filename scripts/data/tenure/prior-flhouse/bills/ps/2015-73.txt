Private Sector Impact:

                    The continuation of this exemption protects an applicant’s sensitive, personal financial
                    information held by the OFR. The disclosure of such information could make those
                    persons vulnerable to identity theft and other crimes. In addition, the release of such
                    exempted information contained in a credit report or a credit score could adversely affect
                    the name or reputation of an applicant.
