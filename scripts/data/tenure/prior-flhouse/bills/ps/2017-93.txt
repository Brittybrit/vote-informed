Private Sector Impact:

                   CS/CS/CS/SB 398 may cause condominium, cooperative, and homeowners’ associations
                   to incur costs to prepare and deliver an estoppel certificate. The bill will also make the
                   costs that purchasers pay for estoppel certificates more predictable.
