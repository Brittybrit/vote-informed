C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The imposition of new contractual obligations for PBMs to periodically update MAC pricing may impact
       the operations of PBMs and will require PBMs to alter the terms of contracts with plan sponsors and
       contracted pharmacies. Additionally, independent pharmacists may realize costs savings associated
       with more competitive prices for pharmaceuticals on the PBM’s MAC list.
