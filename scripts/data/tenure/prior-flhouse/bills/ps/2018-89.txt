C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill removes any regulatory uncertainty as to the status of a direct primary care agreement as
       insurance. Primary care providers may choose to invest in establishing direct primary care practices
       throughout the state to provide primary care services, which would increase access to such services,
       without concern of facing regulatory action by OIR.
