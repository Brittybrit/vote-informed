C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

          Applicants pay $112 when applying at the Division regional offices for new licenses and $60 for a
          renewal license; applicants applying at tax collectors’ offices would pay $134 for a new license and $72
          for a renewal license, a difference of $22 and $12, respectively.

          Local private companies provide the firearms safety and training courses required for initial licensure. If
          the convenience of applying at tax collectors’ offices increases the number of applications, the private
          companies may experience an increase in revenue as a result of more individuals taking the classes.
