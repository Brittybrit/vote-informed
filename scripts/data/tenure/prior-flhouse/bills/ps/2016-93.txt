C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Although some Florida family law attorneys currently practice collaborative law, the bill could
       theoretically expand the use of collaborative law as an alternative to traditional litigation in family court.
       To the extent that collaborative law reduces costs of litigation, parties in such actions may benefit
       financially from electing to proceed in a collaborative manner.
