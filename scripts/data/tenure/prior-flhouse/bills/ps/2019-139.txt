       C. FISCAL COMMENTS:
           The rule adopts a list of qualifying injuries to third parties that support a claim for first responder PTSD.
           Since the statute creates a new path to indemnity benefits, there are no prior claims to analyze the
           impact the rule will have on expenditures. Rather than reducing expenditures, the rule prevents them
           from developing in the first place for claims associated with third party injuries that are excluded from
           the list provided by the rule.26
