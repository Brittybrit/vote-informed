Private Sector Impact:

                Under the bill, if the lessee maintains insurance, the insurer of the lessee of special
                mobile equipment will bear the risk of losses or injuries or damages caused by the
                negligent operation of special mobile equipment. To the extent that damages exceed the
                lessee’s insurance coverage, those losses will likely be borne by the lessee or other
                person who negligently operates the equipment. Lessors who require a lessee to provide
                proof of insurance will not be responsible for the negligence of others.
