C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
       To the extent that the bill encourages more private insurers to provide coverage for flood loss,
       consumers may ultimately benefit from increased competition. The availability of placing flood
       insurance coverage in the surplus lines market may increase access to coverage for property owners,
       but could negatively affect the development of the Florida flood insurance market by creating an
       uneven competition.
