Private Sector Impact:

                 The bill requires that a defendant attend and complete a parenting course as a condition
                 of probation, if a crime of domestic violence was committed upon or in the presences of a
                 child. This will likely have a negative fiscal impact on defendants who are required to
                 pay for this course as a condition of probation.
