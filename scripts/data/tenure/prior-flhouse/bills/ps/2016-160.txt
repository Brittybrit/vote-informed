Private Sector Impact:

                  By authorizing the OFR to enforce the MLA and federal regulations implementing the
                  MLA, service members and their dependents will be provided greater consumer
                  protections in connection with consumer credit transactions in Florida.

                  Additionally, while a business must already meet the requirements of the bill, a business
                  could be subject to penalties under state law for violations.
