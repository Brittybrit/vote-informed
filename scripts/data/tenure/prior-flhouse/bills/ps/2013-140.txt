C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill potentially reduces the cost to insurers of filing and obtaining approval of products and
       advertising materials, since the Commission provides a clearinghouse that allows insurers to make one
       filing rather than separate filings in each state in which they do business.
