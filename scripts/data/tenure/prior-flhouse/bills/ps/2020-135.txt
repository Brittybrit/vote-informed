The bill clarifies the definition of “hemp extract” to provide that it does not include synthetic CBD or seeds or seed-derived ingredients generally recognized as safe by the United States Food and Drug Administration and must contain more than trace amounts of cannabinoids. As such, the required labeling provisions will not apply to certain food products currently sold and consumed that do not contain more than trace amounts of cannabinoids, such as hemp seeds.
