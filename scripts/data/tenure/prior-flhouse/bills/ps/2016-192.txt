Private Sector Impact:

                 The impact of CS/SB 1106 is indeterminate. The bill provides a moratorium on the OFR
                 enforcement of licensure requirements on Florida-based onshore entities engaging in
                 marketing, and other ITCRO activities described under s. 663.0625, F.S., on behalf of
                 ITEs until June 30, 2017. The moratorium would apply to the Florida-based entity and
                 the ITE.27 An estimated 12 to 15 organizations or entities may qualify for the
                 moratorium. 28
