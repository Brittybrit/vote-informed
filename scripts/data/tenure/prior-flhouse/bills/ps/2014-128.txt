C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The deregulation of concessionaires lowers the costs of holding events in the state of Florida for the
       private sector. The deregulation of concessionaires brings Florida in line with the majority of other
       states that do not license concessionaires.

       Additionally, permitting five percent of seats in a house be issued as complimentary tickets without
       being included in gross receipts or paying post-event taxes on the tickets lowers the costs of holding
       events in the state of Florida. This provision is similar to other states which hold professional boxing,
       kickboxing, and mixed martial arts, including Nevada, which holds the largest number of events of any
       state in the nation.
