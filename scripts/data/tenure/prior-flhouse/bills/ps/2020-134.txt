Health care consumers will have access to patient safety culture survey results from hospitals and ASCs. Consumers may use the information to make informed decisions about where they receive health care services. Hospitals and ASCs with poor survey results may realize a reduction in patient volume, while hospitals and ASCs with positive survey results may realize an increase in patient volume.
