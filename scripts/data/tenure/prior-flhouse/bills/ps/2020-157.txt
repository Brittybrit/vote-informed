As permitted by the bill, the placement of a delay on a disbursement or transaction may decrease losses to investors who are financially preyed upon because such a delay may prevent the money from ever getting into the hands of a bad actor. Once the bad actor receives the money, it is difficult, or in some cases impossible, to ever recover the money. Given the inability to quantify avoidance of future losses to investors, the impact on the private sector is indeterminate.
