C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

        The bill may have an indeterminate positive fiscal impact on the private sector because property
        owners will no longer have to request releases from rights of entry from local governments, water
        management districts, or other agencies of the state or file an action in the circuit court to remove the
        right of entry to obtain marketable title to the property.
