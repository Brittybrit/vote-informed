C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Noninstructional contractors will be charged a fee for the identification badge, as set by DOE. Currently
       school districts that issue identification badges set their own fee. There is no way to know at this time
       whether DOE’s set fee will be higher or lower than that currently charged by the districts. However,
       noninstructional contractors who work in multiple school districts would only be required to obtain and
       pay for one identification badge, rather than obtaining a badge and paying a fee in each individual
       district. This may result in reduced costs for noninstructional contractors.
