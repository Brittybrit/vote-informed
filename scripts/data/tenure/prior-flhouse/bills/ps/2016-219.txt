Private Sector Impact:

                 Under CS/SB 966, many beneficiaries of life or endowment insurance policies and
                 annuities contracts who are unaware of such policies will benefit by claiming benefits
                 after being contacted by a life insurer. If the life insurer remits the funds held or owing
                 under the policy or contract to the DFS, Bureau of Unclaimed Property, beneficiaries will
                 benefit by having a central location with which to search for possible life insurance
                 proceeds.

                 Life insurers will incur indeterminate costs related to identifying policies and contracts
                 subject to the provisions of the bill, conducting searches of the DMF to identify deceased
                 policyholders, and attempting to locate beneficiaries.
