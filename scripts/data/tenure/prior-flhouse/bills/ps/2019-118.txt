C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill requires the SDC and state agencies to each adopt a cloud-first policy in its technology
       sourcing strategy and show preference in procurement for cloud computing solutions that either
       minimize or do not require the use of the SDC whenever possible or feasible, which should reduce SDC
       costs in subsequent years.
