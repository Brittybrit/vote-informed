C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill will have a negative fiscal impact on nursing homes that need to acquire an alternative power
       source to meet the requirements of the rule. Over the first five years the rule is in effect, nursing homes
       that need to meet the requirements of the rule will experience an adverse economic impact of
       $121,380,545.
