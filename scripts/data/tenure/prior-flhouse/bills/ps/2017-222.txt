         September 2011 to March 2016, the library was located in the downtown branch of the Alachua County
         Library District.

         The Library has a five member Board of Trustees.3 The Board consists of the Chief Judge of the Eighth
         Judicial Circuit, the clerk of the circuit court for Alachua County, a member designated by the Alachua
         County Board of County Commissioners, and two attorneys practicing in the county appointed by the
         president of the bar association of the Eighth Judicial Circuit.4

         The Library must be located in the headquarters of the Alachua County Library District.5 The Library is
         financed by appropriations from the Alachua County Board of County Commissioners.6 The
         appropriated funds are placed in the Alachua County Law Library Fund and may only be used for
         operating the law library, including providing for furnishings, equipment, and necessary employees. 7 All
         purchases made by the Library and all donations to the Library must be held as a charitable public trust
         and are exempt from taxation.8

         Effect of the Bill

         The bill allows the chief judge of the Eighth Judicial Circuit and the clerk of the circuit court for Alachua
         County to name a designee to serve in their stead on the Board of Trustees for the Library. The bill also
         authorizes the Board of Trustees to designate a location for the library and removes surplus language.
