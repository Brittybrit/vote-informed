         Effect of the Bill

        The bill creates an exception to general law by prescribing that the process for obtaining the required
        local approval for operation of a cardroom in Seminole County is the referendum process set forth in
        article V, s. 5.1 of the Seminole County Home Rule Charter. The charter process requires first the
        approval of the operation of a cardroom by the Seminole County electors voting in a referendum. If so
        approved, the Seminole County Commission may then determine whether to approve the operation of a
        cardroom. For purposes of the act only, the term “casino gambling” in the Seminole County Home Rule
        Charter is deemed to include all activities authorized by or conducted under s. 849.086, F.S.21
