                                      I. SUBSTANTIVE INFORMATION

    A. EFFECT OF CHANGES:

        Background

        The Immokalee Water and Sewer District, a special taxing district, is an independent special district in
        Collier County that was created in 1978.1 A seven-member board of commissioners, appointed by the
        Governor for four-year terms, governs the Water and Sewer District.2 Currently, commissioners are
        prohibited from receiving compensation for their services, but are entitled to travel and per diem
        reimbursement pursuant to s. 112.061, F.S.3

        Effect of the bill

        The bill changes the current act to provide:

               Each board member may be paid up to $250 per month per member as a salary or honorarium.
               At least a majority plus one vote of the board determines the amount of the salary or
                honorarium.
               A special notice of any meeting at which the board will consider a salary change for a member:
                o Must be published at least once at least 14 days before the meeting.
                o Must be published in a newspaper of general circulation in the county in which the district is
                    located.
               Board members cannot be paid for unexcused absences from regularly scheduled board
                meetings.
               Board must adopt policies by resolution defining the excused and unexcused absences.

          II. FISCAL ANALYSIS, ECONOMIC IMPACT STATEMENT, & NOTICE/REFERENDUM

    A. FISCAL IMPACT ON STATE GOVERNMENT:

        1. Revenues:

            None.

        2. Expenditures:

            None.

    B. FISCAL IMPACT ON LOCAL GOVERNMENTS:

        1. Revenues:

            None.

        2. Expenditures:

            According to the Economic Impact Statement (EIS), the bill will not have any associated costs or
            revenues. However, approximately $21,000, assuming each of the seven Immokalee Water and
            Sewer District board members is paid $250 per month, is the maximum that could be funded for
            compensation from district general revenues.
