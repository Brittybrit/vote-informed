        Effect of Bill

        The bill expands the boundaries of City of Holmes Beach to incorporate submerged lands in
        unincorporated Manatee County. The submerged lands to be annexed are adjacent to the Grassy Point
        Preserve.

        The bill directs the City of Holmes Beach to apply the Manatee County Comprehensive Plan, Manatee
        County Land Development Code, Manatee County Code of Ordinances, and the Florida Building Code
        to the construction, installation, and placement by the county of infrastructure and utilities, navigational
        markers, and other signage in the annexed area, providing for regulatory uniformity in Anna Maria
        Sound.

        The Economic Impact Statement submitted for the bill states it will have no fiscal impact on state or
        local government.9 The bill will enable the city to construct an observation pier for wildlife viewing,
        increasing recreational activities for visitors to the preserve.10 The bill will also provide the city with the
        power to police activities in the coastal waters near the park, such as the unauthorized use of
        motorized vessels.11 The city already owns an enforcement boat for this purpose.12
