Private Sector Impact:

                 The bill expands an existing public records exemption to make confidential and exempt
                 photographs or video or audio recordings that depict or record the killing of a victim of
                 mass violence.

                 The private sector will continue to be subject to the cost associated with an agency
                 making redactions in response to a public records request.
