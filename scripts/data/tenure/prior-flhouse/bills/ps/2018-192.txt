                          Damages
                          The accident caused about $211,000 in medical bills (though
                          some of that amount was negotiated down), $5,500 in property
                          damage, and $4,500 in lost wages. Moreover, Claimant has
                          undergone multiple procedures and had an additional five-day
                          hospital stay due to an infection that formed after his surgery.
                          The accident also caused Claimant to be defibrillated at the
                          emergency room. In light of Claimant's economic and
                          noneconomic damages, including the residual negative effects
                          of his injury, which Claimant still lives with, I find that the
                          amount agreed upon by Claimant and Respondent and sought
                          in the claim bill is reasonable.
