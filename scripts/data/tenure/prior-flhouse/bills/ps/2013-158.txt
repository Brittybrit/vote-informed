C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       More owners of mobile or manufactured homes will be eligible for insurance in Citizens under the bill’s
       reduction of the minimum insured value on these homes from $6,000 to $3,000.

       Mobile or manufactured home owners will be eligible for increased insurance coverage from Citizens as
       the bill requires Citizens to insure certain attached structures to the mobile or manufactured home.
