C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Clarification on the order of permit applications for public swimming pools may result in cost savings
       due to issues and problems being identified prior to construction.

       Owners of one-family and two-family dwellings who are installing or replacing battery-powered smoke
       alarms will have an added cost when replacing them with battery-powered smoke alarms that are
       powered by 10-year non-removable, non-replaceable batteries, as required by the bill.

       Having access to location and pricing information for alternative fuel and electric vehicle charging
       stations may facilitate the use of vehicles utilizing these types of energy.
