Private Sector Impact:

              The consumer protections of s. 627.4554, F.S., will generally apply to all consumers
              purchasing annuities. To the extent that the protections provide greater transparency and
              protection against fraud and misrepresentation, consumers who purchase annuities should
              realize cost savings.
