C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The building permit program should reduce the time spent by local building departments during the site-
       specific building permit application process, which should result in faster permit review times for all
       builders. This should streamline development and could result in increased private economic activity.
