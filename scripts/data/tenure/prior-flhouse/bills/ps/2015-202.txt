         The charter authorizes the District to raise funds as provided in general law but expressly prohibits the
         District Board from imposing or collecting ad valorem taxes. The District will have the same authority as
         ECWCD to collect installment and maintenance assessments. The District will also have the authority
         to impose impact fees for new construction. The fiscal year of the District will be from October 1 through
         September 30.

         The bill repeals the special acts comprising the charter of ECWCD, dissolves that water control district,
         and transfers all rights, title, and possession of property, and all obligations and liabilities of the water
         control district, to the Lehigh Acres Municipal Services Improvement District. The bill also prohibits any
         municipal annexation within the territory of the District but allows for future municipal incorporation of
         the entire area occupied by the District.

         The bill provides all terms and conditions required for the creation and chartering of an independent
         special district.43 However, there was no resolution or other official statement from the Board of
         Commissioners of Lee County stating either that the Board had no objection to the creation of the
         District or that the creation of the District was consistent with the county’s comprehensive plan.44
