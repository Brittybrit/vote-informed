C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

         According to DFS, the value of the surplus property assigned to surplus trustees since 2006 was
         $105,575,140. The maximum amount that the surplus trustee industry could have charged for their
         services is $12,699,017, which represents 12 percent of the total value of the disbursed property.47 The
         minimum amount is $2,111,503, representing the 2 percent search fee. There will be an indeterminate
         impact on surplus trustees.
