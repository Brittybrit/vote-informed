The fiscal impact of this bill to the private sector is indeterminate. While changing the method by which the FWCIGA calculates assessments necessarily changes the base used to determine the assessment, the ultimate changes may be revenue-neutral, as the amount the FWCIGA needs to assess would remain unchanged.
