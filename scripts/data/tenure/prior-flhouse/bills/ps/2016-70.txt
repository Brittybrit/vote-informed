Private Sector Impact:

                 Under the bill, visitors to the state and patients who have an order for physical therapy
                 from a practitioner licensed in another state will be able to receive the ordered physical
                 therapy in this state without incurring additional costs for a medical examination from or
                 assessment by a practitioner of record. Increasing the period of time in which a physical
                 therapist may provide treatment for a patient without a practitioner of record assessing
                 the patient may save patients the cost of examination by a practitioner of record.
