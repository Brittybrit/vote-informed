Private Sector Impact:

               An employee being paid by this method may be able to avoid or reduce check-cashing
               fees or other fees incurred for accessing wages, if the employee does not have access or
               has limited access to traditional banking services.

               An employer may save costs associated with the issuance of a paper check.
