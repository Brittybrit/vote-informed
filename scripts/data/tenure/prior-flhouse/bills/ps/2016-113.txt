Private Sector Impact:

                     The bill may have a positive fiscal impact for obstetrical physicians who receive this
                     notice to allow them adequate time to ensure that they obtain privileges at another
                     hospital. The bill may have a negative fiscal impact on hospitals that fail to comply due
                     to potential administrative fines.
