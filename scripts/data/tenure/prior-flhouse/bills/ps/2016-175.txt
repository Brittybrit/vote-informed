Private Sector Impact:

                  An individual with autism, an autism spectrum disorder or a related developmental
                  disability will be responsible for the expenses of the requested professional, but the
                  victim of an offense may receive reimbursement of these expenses from a defendant who
                  is convicted of the offense. The cost of a professional required by CS/CS/SB 936 is
                  unknown.
