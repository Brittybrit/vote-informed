Private Sector Impact:

             CS/CS/SB 646 is intended to prevent the disclosure of specified personal financial and
             health information of a consumer that is held by the Florida Prepaid College Board,
             Florida ABLE Inc., Florida ABLE program, or an agent or service provider of these
             entities relating to an ABLE account or a participation agreement or any information held
             that could identify a consumer. The bill provides a limited exception for the release of
             such confidential and exempt information to governmental entities in furtherance of their
             duties.
