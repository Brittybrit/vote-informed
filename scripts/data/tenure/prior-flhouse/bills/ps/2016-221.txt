C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

           The bill may have a positive fiscal impact on the private sector since “independent grocery stores and
           supermarkets” and “community facilities,” as part of the program, will be eligible to receive financial
           assistance in the form of grants and loans to improve or set up stores to provide access to healthy food
           and fresh produce.
