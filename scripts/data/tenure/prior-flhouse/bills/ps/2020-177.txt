By preventing the use of balance billing practices by air ambulance service providers, the bill will likely have a negative, indeterminate fiscal impact on those providers. Oppositely, the bill may have a positive fiscal impact on insurers, HMOs, and insureds by limiting payments to air ambulance service providers to “reasonable reimbursement” for services.
