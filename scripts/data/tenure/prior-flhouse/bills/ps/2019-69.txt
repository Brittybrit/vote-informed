C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       In the event of certain crashes, railroad companies may benefit due to reduced delays. To the extent
       that the bill expedites the ability for railroad train passengers to disembark the train and motorists to
       avoid railroad crossings being closed for an extended period, there may be a reduction in lost time and
       pay.
