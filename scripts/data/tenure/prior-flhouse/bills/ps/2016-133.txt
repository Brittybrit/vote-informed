C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
       The bill has positive and negative impacts on the private sector. Positive impacts include: increased
       insurer efficiencies that may result in premium savings being passed on to consumers; increased
       flexibility paying premiums and reallocation of unearned premiums; and reduction of premium
       prepayments in certain circumstances. Negative impacts include: authorization of a new insufficient
       funds fee for failed payments, which is in addition to fees levied by a financial institution and a new
       limitation when an Auto JUA policy may be cancelled by the insured.
