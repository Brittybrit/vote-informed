C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

         For a person experiencing a nonviable birth, a voluntary fee of at least $3 and not more than $5, must
         be paid for the certificate of nonviable birth.

         Healthcare providers and healthcare facilities who are required to registered nonviable births may incur
         costs related to additional administrative burdens.
