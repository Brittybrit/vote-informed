C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill’s protection of trade secrets within information relating to an investigation or examination may
       benefit collection agencies, since disclosure of such information could result in a competitive
       disadvantage in the marketplace. In addition, the bill’s protection of specified personal financial and
       health information of consumers may reduce the risk of identity theft and protect matters of personal
       health which are traditionally private and confidential concerns between patients and health care
       providers.
