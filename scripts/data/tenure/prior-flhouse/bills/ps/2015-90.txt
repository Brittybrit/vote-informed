C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
       Members of a board of directors of a mobile home owners' association who choose to complete the
       required educational training in lieu of certifying that they have read the association's organizing
       documents, rules, and regulations may incur costs for such training. The cost for similar educational
       requirements in the Condominium Act and Cooperative Act range in price from no fee to $200.19
