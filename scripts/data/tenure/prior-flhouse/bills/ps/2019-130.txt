Private Sector Impact:

                 As a condition of registration under ss. 458.308 and 459.003, F.S., a physician who
                 performs office surgical procedures in an office setting, and the office itself if it is a
                 separate legal entity from the physician, must now maintain the same levels of financial
                 responsibility required in ss. 458.320 and 459.0085, F.S. This may produce an additional
                 cost to the physician and the office if they are separate legal entities.
