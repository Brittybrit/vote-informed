Private Sector Impact:

                 Requiring evidenced-based trauma-informed services may necessitate CBCs amending
                 contracts with subcontractors providing direct services to children to include this
                 requirement, if their contracts do not currently do so. The bill does not provide a
                 definition of “trauma-informed.”
