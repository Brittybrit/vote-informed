C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Under SB 2514-A, expanding the state’s trail network has the potential of producing economic benefits
       as well as serving a variety of statewide trip purposes including work, school, shopping, social,
       recreational, and personal fitness purposes.
