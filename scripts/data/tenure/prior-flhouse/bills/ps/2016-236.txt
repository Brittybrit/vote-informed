C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
      Students at state universities and colleges will benefit from having a sufficient amount of time and
      information to seek out the lowest available prices for textbooks and instructional materials. In addition,
      students may realize significant cost savings if institution boards of trustees adopt policies that allow
      innovative pricing techniques and payment options for textbooks and instructional materials.
