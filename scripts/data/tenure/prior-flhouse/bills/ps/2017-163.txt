C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill creates tiered noncriminal boating infractions for operation of a vessel with an expired
       registration and for anchoring or mooring where prohibited. As such, a violator would be charged with a
       noncriminal infraction, cited, and ordered to appear in county court. A person who fails to appear or
       otherwise properly respond to the citation would, in addition to the civil penalty, be charged with failing
       to respond to the citation and upon conviction would be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree,
       punishable by a term of imprisonment not exceeding 60 days or a fine which may not exceed $500.
