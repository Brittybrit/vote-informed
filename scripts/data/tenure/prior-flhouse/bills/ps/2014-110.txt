C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The repeal of the moratorium that prohibits AHCA from issuing CONs for new community nursing home
       beds may have a positive fiscal impact on the construction and nursing home industries in areas where
       there is a current need for new beds. Additional nursing home beds may result in increased
       competition for residents within the nursing home industry. Increased competition may result in more
       choices, lower costs, and higher quality facilities for residents.
