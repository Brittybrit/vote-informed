Individuals who voluntarily registered as chiropractic assistants will no longer have to pay fees associated with such registration due to the bill’s repeal of the registration program. Individuals who wish to obtain a single prosthetist-orthotist registration may save money because they will no longer have to obtain separate prosthetic and orthotic registrations.
