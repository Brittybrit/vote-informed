Private Sector Impact:

             The bill contemplates the FHCC contracting with private providers of health care services
             and products to deliver health care benefits to an additional population of currently
             uninsured individuals who may or may not be seeking health care services now.
             Physicians, hospitals, and other health care providers may be impacted by additional
             individuals seeking health care coverage and there may be a higher demand for such
             services once implemented.

             Additionally, those safety net and other providers who serve this same population and are
             not receiving compensation or are receiving reduced compensation for those services
             may have an additional avenue for revenue.
