Private Sector Impact:

             The impact to the private sector is indeterminate.

             Many payday lenders assert that the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection (CFPB)
             rule imposes additional costs and administrative burdens that will result in reducing the
             availability of deferred presentment transactions. Some consumer advocates assert that
             the CFPB rule provides necessary safeguards to prevent consumers from being caught in
             debt traps.
