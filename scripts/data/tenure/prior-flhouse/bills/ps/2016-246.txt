         EFFECT OF THE BILL

         The bill dissolves the Gilchrist County Development Authority by repealing ch. 97-373, 81-382, 59-
         1308, Laws of Florida. Any assets and liabilities of the district are transferred to the Board of County
         Commissioners for Gilchrist County.
