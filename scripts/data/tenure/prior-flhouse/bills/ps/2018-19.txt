Private Sector Impact:

                 Under ch. 2016-41, L.O.F., the Florida Council on Arts and Culture is permitted to raise
                 funds from private sources to fund the costs associated with the replacement of the statue.
                 The estimated costs associated with the replacement of the statue is $388,000.23 Such
                 funds will be needed if the Joint Committee of the Library of Congress approves the
                 state’s request to replace the statue made by this bill.
