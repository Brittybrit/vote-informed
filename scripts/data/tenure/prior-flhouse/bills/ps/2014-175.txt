Private Sector Impact:

                Proposed payments by concrete masonry manufacturers to Florida Concrete Masonry
                Council, Inc., are self-imposed voluntary assessments on concrete masonry units
                produced and sold in the state. Additionally, the council may accept grants, donations,
                contributions, or gifts.

                The fiscal impact cannot be determined because of the voluntary nature of the anticipated
                revenue.

                Government Sector Impact:

                The council may provide governmental bodies with requested information relating to
                subjects of concern to the industry. The council may act jointly or in cooperation with the
                state or federal government and their agencies in the development or administration of
                programs that the council considers to be consistent with this act.
