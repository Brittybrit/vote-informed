C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

        This bill has an indeterminate23 financial impact on motor vehicle owners and insurance companies.
        Generally, “other than collision”24 automobile insurance, also known as “comprehensive coverage”
        covers intentional damage to a motor vehicle by a third party. If insured, the motor vehicle owner is only
        responsible for the cost of repair up to the amount of the policy deductible.25 Any remaining cost is paid
        by the insurance company pursuant to the terms of the policy. If uninsured, a motor vehicle owner must
        pay the entire cost to repair intentional damage by a third party.

        Under current law, a motor vehicle owner, and an insurance company as a subrogee26 to all of the
        rights to recovery of an insured motor vehicle owner, may recover their respective costs from the party
        which caused the damage in a civil action. If a person who intentionally damages a motor vehicle
        meets the requirements for civil immunity under the bill, the motor vehicle owner and the insurance
        company may be prevented from recovering such costs.
