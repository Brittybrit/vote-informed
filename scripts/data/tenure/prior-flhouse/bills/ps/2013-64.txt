C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Property owners who have a homestead exemption will be able to rent their dwellings for up to 30 days
       a year and retain the homestead status of their property and any applicable Save our Homes
       assessment limitation. As a result, an indeterminate number of additional short-term rental opportunities
       may become available to homestead owners who decide to rent their properties up to 30 days.
