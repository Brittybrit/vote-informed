Private Sector Impact:

                 Plaintiffs who either reside out-of-state at the time of filing a petition in civil court or
                 after filing a petition will not have to post a bond for costs. Additionally, plaintiffs who
                 reside out-of-state will not be subject to dismissal of the case for failure to post a bond for
                 costs.
