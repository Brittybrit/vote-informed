C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill may result in increased savings for drivers utilizing vehicles powered by natural gas fuel, an
       increase in conversions of vehicle fleets from being powered by traditional fuels to natural gas fuel, and
       an increase in natural gas refueling infrastructure across the state.
