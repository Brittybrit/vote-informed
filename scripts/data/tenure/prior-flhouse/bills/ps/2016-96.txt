C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

           The bill creates a noncriminal boating infraction for anchoring in an anchoring limitation area. As such,
           a violator will be charged with a noncriminal infraction, cited, and ordered to appear in county court. The
           noncriminal infraction includes tiered civil penalties. A person who fails to appear or otherwise properly
           respond to the citation will, in addition to the civil penalty, be charged with failing to respond to the
           citation and upon conviction will be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable by a term
           of imprisonment not exceeding 60 days or a fine which must not exceed $500.
