Private Sector Impact:

             Consortiums of local governments that have or may form third-party administrators
             (TPA) for the purpose of administering self-insurance for the local governments may
             benefit from reduced administrative burdens under CS/CS/SB 1170, by no longer being
             required to obtain TPA licensure from the OIR.
