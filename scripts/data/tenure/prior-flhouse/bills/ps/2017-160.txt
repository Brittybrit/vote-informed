C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
       The bill permits HHAs to establish additional locations in smaller areas, such as cities and counties. As
       a result, there may be business growth, additional job opportunities for home health service providers,
       and greater access to home health services.
