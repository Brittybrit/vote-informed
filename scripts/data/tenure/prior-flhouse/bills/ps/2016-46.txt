Private Sector Impact:

                    This bill may help fiduciaries identify assets and bank accounts belonging to those who
                    have died or become incapacitated. The custodians of digital assets, such as email service
                    providers, however, might initially incur costs in reviewing requests for access to digital
                    assets and then making those assets available. Custodians, however, are authorized to
                    assess a reasonable administrative charge for the costs they incur in disclosing digital
                    assets such that this bill should have no impact on them.
