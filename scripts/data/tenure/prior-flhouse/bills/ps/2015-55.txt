Private Sector Impact:

                The bill will allow personnel from Florida traveling under the Emergency Management
                Assistance Compact (EMAC) to be reimbursed for per diem expenses that exceed
                Florida’s per diem rates. The per diem rates for travel under the EMAC are decided in the
                request agreement and vary based on the location of the EMAC mission and the cost of
                living at that location.
