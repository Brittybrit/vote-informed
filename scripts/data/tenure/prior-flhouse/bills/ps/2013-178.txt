Private Sector Impact:

             The bill will require termination or non-renewal of most, if not all, of the contracts
             between CBC lead agencies and providers for IL services for children from age 13
             through age 17.
BILL: CS/SB 1036                                                                                          Page 20

                 The bill also has the potential to reduce the workload of caseworkers and IL case
                 managers which could either result in those staff having more time to spend on other
                 duties or a reduction in the number of those positions.

                 The bill may result in an increased cost of staff training for providers that operate group
                 homes, particularly those using a shift care model.
