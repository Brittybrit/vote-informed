C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
       Specified active duty servicemembers who do not reside in Florida and would otherwise be unable to
       establish Florida residency would be able to pay in-state tuition rates at state universities, Florida
       College System institutions, career centers, and charter technical career centers. Savings are outlined
       below:
