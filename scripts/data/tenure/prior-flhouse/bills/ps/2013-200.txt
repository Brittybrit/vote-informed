C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Private employers will not be required by political subdivisions to provide employment benefits not
       required by state or federal law, except in limited situations, and will have the expectancy of uniformity
       throughout the state with regard to mandated non-wage compensation.
