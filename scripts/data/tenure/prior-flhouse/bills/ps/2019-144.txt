Private Sector Impact:

                  Military members and their families may benefit from the bill in the areas of private
                  property rentals and education.

                  First, servicemembers will not have to come up with as much cash up front when signing
                  a lease. A servicemember also will not be penalized for breaking a lease if he or she
                  becomes eligible to live in and opts to move into privatized military housing.

                  Second, active duty members of the Armed Services and their spouses and dependents
                  will receive the benefit of in-state tuition if the active duty member receives a relocation
                  order after accepting admission but before enrolling at a public postsecondary institution.

                  If the user fee on the sales of a Blue Angel license plate passes in separate legislation,
                  organizations that receive funds collected from these sales will also benefit from this bill.
