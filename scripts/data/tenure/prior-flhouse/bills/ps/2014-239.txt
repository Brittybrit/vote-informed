   C. ECONOMIC IMPACT STATEMENT FILED?                       Yes [X]   No []

       A revenue increase for ENFD of $1.03 million is projected due to additional ad valorem tax receipts
       from the newly-annexed area. Costs of providing services to the annexed area are projected to be less
       than present services due to greater efficiencies. Reduced tax collections by Collier County are
       projected to be offset by the elimination of costs for continued services. The residents of the annexed
       area will have a reduction in this ad valorem tax rate from 2.0 to 1.5 mills.
