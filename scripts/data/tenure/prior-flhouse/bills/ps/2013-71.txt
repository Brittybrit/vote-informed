Private Sector Impact:

             The regulated community anticipates that the DEP NNC rules, while still requiring
             significant investment, will be less expensive to implement than the EPA’s NNC. The
             difference in the cost of compliance is attributable to the implementation of the DEP’s
             NNC rules that account for site-specific criteria and allow for biological variations for
             Florida’s waterbodies that the EPA’s rules do not.
