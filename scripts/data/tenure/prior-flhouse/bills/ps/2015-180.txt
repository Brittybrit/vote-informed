B. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
       The bill will have a positive impact on insurance agents and agencies by increasing the pool of agents
       that qualify for agent in charge designations and facilitating the licensure of qualified applicants. It will
       have a negative impact on them by requiring record retention for five years following the expiration of a
       policy.
