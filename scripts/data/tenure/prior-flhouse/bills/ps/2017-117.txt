Private Sector Impact:

              This bill provides what appears to be a simple and cost-effective means of determining
              parenting time plans to separated or never-married parents who generally have a low
              income. If the parents can agree on the standard parenting time plan or another parenting
              time plan, they will not need to proceed in circuit court and incur the related costs to
              acquire a parenting time order.
