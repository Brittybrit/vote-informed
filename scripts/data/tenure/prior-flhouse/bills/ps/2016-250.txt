        Effect of the Bill

        CS/CS/HB 785 revises the charter of the St. Lucie County Fire District23 to change the District’s
        borrowing limit. Under the bill, the District will be able to borrow an amount not to exceed 10% of its
        operating budget each year. The funds borrowed must be spent in accordance with a supermajority
        vote of the District’s board and the funds cannot be spent on normal operating expenses. The District’s
        operating budget for 2015 is approximately $63 million24 and their total budget is $87,104,738.25
