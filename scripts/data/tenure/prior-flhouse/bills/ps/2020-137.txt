The bill may encourage increased enforcement of ch. 556, F.S. Increased enforcement will result in additional fines imposed on persons who violate certain provisions of ch. 556, F.S. As a result, increased enforcement may deter behaviors that cause damages to property, utility service outages, and serious bodily injury.
