C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

           Dispensing organizations may incur costs associated with meeting the bill’s new quality, safety, and
           security standards unless they already meet such standards. Dispensing organizations will also incur
           costs associated with contracting with testing laboratories. The contract cost is indeterminate and may
           vary within each dispensing organization.
