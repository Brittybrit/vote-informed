C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
      The bill will have a positive fiscal impact on individuals who distribute, sell, or offer to sell pesticides by
      eliminating the supplemental fee for each registered brand of pesticide that contains an active
      ingredient for which the EPA has established a food tolerance limit in 40 C.F.R. part 180.
