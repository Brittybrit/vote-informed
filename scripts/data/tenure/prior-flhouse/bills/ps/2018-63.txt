   Flood insurance covers rising water that sits or flows on the ground and damages property by inundation and flow. Windstorm insurance covers
water falling or driven by wind that damages property by infiltration of the structure from above or laterally while carried by the wind. In short, flood
insurance covers damage related to rising water and windstorm insurance covers damage related to airborne water.
