To the extent that an employer does not currently use an employment verification system, the bill could increase costs to employers. Though the federal government does not charge a fee for the use of E-Verify, using E-Verify or another employment verification system could increase the labor involved in onboarding an employee, especially if the initial response for that employee is not “Employment Authorized.”
