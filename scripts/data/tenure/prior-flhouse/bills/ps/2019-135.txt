Private Sector Impact:

             Hospitals that maintain ASRCs, PSCs, CSCs, or TSCs, are required under CS/CS/SB
             1460 to be certified by a nationally recognized certifying organization and may incur a
             cost for their application for certification. They may need to purchase new software and
             incur labor costs to collect, maintain, and send the required data to the DOH. Such costs,
             if any, are indeterminate.
