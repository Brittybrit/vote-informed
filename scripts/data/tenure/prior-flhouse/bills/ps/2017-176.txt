   C. FISCAL COMMENTS:
       The bill may create an insignificant negative fiscal impact on the Department because staff responsible
       for complying with public record requests could require training to implement the bill’s exemption. In
       addition, the Department could incur costs associated with redacting the exempt information prior to
       releasing the record. Such costs should be absorbed as they are part of the day-to-day operation of the
       agency.
