C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Homeowners who have been harmed by Division II contractors and receive restitution from the Florida
       Homeowners’ Construction Recovery Fund will benefit from the bill.

       Apartment owners with communities of 100 or more apartments who have employees make minor
       repairs to existing electric water heaters or existing electric HVAC systems may experience savings if
       they meet the requirements of and utilize the contractor licensing requirements exemption.
