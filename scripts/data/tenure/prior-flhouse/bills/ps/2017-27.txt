C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill will likely have a positive economic impact on families operating a licensed family foster home
       by providing free family annual passes and a waiver of base camping fees and families who adopt a
       special needs child by providing a one-time family annual entrance pass.
