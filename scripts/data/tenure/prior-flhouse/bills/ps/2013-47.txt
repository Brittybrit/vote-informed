Private Sector Impact:

                  Section 1 of the bill will prevent managing entities from having to revert unexpended
                  state funds from one fiscal year to the next, which may simplify their finances and cash
                  flow. Section 2 of the bill will alter the allocation of community-based care funds among
                  the DCF’s community-based care lead agencies to an unknown degree.
