C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

        Users of MDX’s facilities may see a reduction in the frequency and amount of toll increases. Eligible
        SunPass users of MDX’s toll facilities would benefit from a reduction in toll charges of at least 5
        percent, but not more than 10 percent. The amount of this reduction is dependent on the number of
        eligible trips on MDX toll facilities, and is therefore unknown.

        Users of transportation facilities in Miami-Dade County may benefit from municipal or county
        transportation- or transit-related expenses funded by a portion of MDX’s surplus revenues. MDX may
        benefit from the same funded expenses. The amount, however, is indeterminate, as the funds available
        for such expenses are indeterminate.
