D. FISCAL COMMENTS:

       The bill requires local enforcement agencies to issue each permit label to a contractor for a fee of up to
       $55 with a limited exemption. It is anticipated the bill would lower fees currently imposed by certain
       local jurisdictions and may encourage other local jurisdictions to raise fees resulting in an indeterminate
       fiscal impact.
