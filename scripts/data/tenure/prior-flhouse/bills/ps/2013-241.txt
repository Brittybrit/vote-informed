C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       This bill does not appear to have a direct economic impact on the private sector. The bill may have an
       indirect positive economic impact on the private sector. This bill may lower transaction costs and limit
       unknown liabilities of transfer agents and purchasers of real property.
