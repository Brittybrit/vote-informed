        Effect of the Proposed Changes

        The bill repeals chapters 25231 (1949), 59-588, 63-848, 65-1214, and 81-357, Laws of Fla., removing
        the authority for Charlotte County Commissioners to pay for county commissioner travel expenses
        outside of the statutory scheme set forth in s. 112.061, F.S. The bill also repeals chapter 63-1202,
        Laws of Fla., removing the authority of the board to budget and expend money for entertainment, travel
        expenses, and lodging of visiting dignitaries and public officials.
