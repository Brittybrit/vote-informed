Private Sector Impact:

                 The bill revises distributions of revenue from the excise tax on documents to multiple
                 trust funds and general revenue. The extent of the impact on the private sector will
                 depend on how programs funded from affected trust funds or general revenue are
                 impacted, which cannot be determined at this time.
