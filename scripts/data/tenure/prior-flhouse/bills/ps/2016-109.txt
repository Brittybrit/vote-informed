C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:
            Adult Medicaid recipients may see increased dental care costs. Without the requirement to provide a
            dental benefit under the MMA program, it may no longer be cost effective for plans to maintain a full
            dental network, which may affect the plans’ ability and willingness to continue to offer expanded dental
            benefits to adults.71
