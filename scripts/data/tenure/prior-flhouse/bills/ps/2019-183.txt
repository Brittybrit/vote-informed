        Effect of the Bill

        The bill repeals the Hillsborough County Civil Service Act.

        The bill requires all agencies and authorities previously covered by the Act to provide a fair, neutral,
        and impartial system for administering employee discipline, including suspensions, involuntary
        demotions, and dismissal of employees, as well as the appeals of any such decisions. The bill requires
        the system established by the county to be uniform for all agencies and to provide tenured employees
        with protections that are substantially similar to those provided by the Act.
