Private Sector Impact:

                 A craft distillery may experience an increase in revenue generated by the ability to sell
                 two factory-sealed containers of each branded product or up to four individual containers,
                 whichever is greater.

                 A craft distillery opting to participate in the Florida’s Highway Guide Sign Program will
                 incur costs associated with the placement of signs.
