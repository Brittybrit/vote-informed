C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

            Boat owners who are required to register their boats will not receive an increase in the registration fees.
            Resident and nonresident purchasers of recreational hunting and fishing licenses will not receive an
            increase in their license fees.
