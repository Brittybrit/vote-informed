C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

        The changes to ss. 50.0211 and 50.061, F.S., may have a minimal fiscal impact upon the newspaper
        industry. The industry will be required to provide a searchable database of legal notices at no cost to
        the public and at no additional cost to those parties who seek the legal publications. The Florida Press
        Association estimates that the cost associated with making changes to their website to conform to this
        bill is $3,600, but may increase. The Florida Press Association did not have an estimate for the
        financial impact this bill will have on newspapers, but did state that only a few newspapers would be
        affected.15
