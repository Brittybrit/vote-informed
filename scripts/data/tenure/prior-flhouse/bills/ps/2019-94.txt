C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Unknown. It is unclear if the additional provisions required in order to serve a notice of nonpayment will
       have an effect on the private sector. The new provisions may make it more difficult for a subcontractor
       to file a notice of non-payment. Other provisions may make it easier for a prevailing contractor to collect
       attorney fees in a claim against the surety insurer.
