C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

        Businesses throughout Florida will now be afforded broader protection against all types of fraud and
        business identity theft. Additionally, individuals will be able to more easily identify when identity theft
        has occurred and restore their identity and credit after a case of identity theft.
