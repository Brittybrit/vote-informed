Private Sector Impact:

                The sex offender registry screening requirements of the bill are expected to have a
                nominal effect on the authorities. The state and national registries are public websites that
                can be accessed by persons with minimal computer skills, and searches can be conducted
                relatively quickly. Those authorities electing to perform searches via a commercial
                consumer reporting agency may incur moderate expenses for the screening. The
                authorities may pass these screening costs on to volunteer applicants and incur no costs
                from this screening requirement.

                The Level 1 screenings cost $40.50 each. This cost will likely be passed on to the athletic
                coach applicant and may have a negative impact on recruitment and retention of these
                volunteers.
