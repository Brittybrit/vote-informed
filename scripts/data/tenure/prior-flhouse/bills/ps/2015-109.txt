C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Hospitals may realize a minimal increase in administrative costs associated with providing the
       documentation of observation services in the discharge papers. Hospitals may also realize an increase
       in fines for failing to provide notification of observation services included in the discharge papers.
