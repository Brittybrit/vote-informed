C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Public places may proactively come into compliance with Title III of the ADA in order to prevent having
       to pay attorney’s fees if they are sued in the future for violating Title III of the ADA.

       Since certificates of conformity are only valid for three years, owners of public places may have to pay
       for inspections every three years in order to prove they are compliant with Title III of the ADA.
