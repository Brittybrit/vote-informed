Private Sector Impact:

                 Credit reporting agencies may incur additional costs to establish security freezes for
                 minors and other individuals covered under the bill. However, the credit reporting
                 agencies are permitted to charge up to a $10 fee in most cases to create a security freeze.
                 Receipt of the fee will help minimize costs to the credit reporting agencies.

                 With the increasing incidence of child identity theft, the creation of security freezes for
                 consumer reports and records of minors under age 16 and other persons represented by a
                 guardian or advocate may provide additional safeguards against identity theft.35
