Private Sector Impact:

                 The bill will assure that QPDs will continue to be able to use FHLBank letters of credit as
                 eligible collateral.

                 This change in letters of credit as eligible collateral would eliminate any potential
                 disruption to banks and savings associations that use FHLBank letters of credit as
                 collateral should the long-term sovereign credit rating of the United States drop below
                 triple-A.
