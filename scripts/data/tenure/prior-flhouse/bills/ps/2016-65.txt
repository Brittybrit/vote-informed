C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

        The AHCA will distribute $180,000,000 to hospitals and other qualifying institutions eligible for the
        Statewide Medicaid Residency Program and the Graduate Medical Education Startup Bonus Program.
        Additionally, AHCA will provide reimbursement to private schools and charter schools for providing
        Medicaid school-based services identical to those offered under the Medicaid certified school match
        program and under the same eligibility criteria as children eligible for services under that program.
        Finally, children who are lawfully present in the state will be eligible for health insurance coverage;
        therefore, health care providers may see an increase in patients as a result of increasing the access to
        medical care.
