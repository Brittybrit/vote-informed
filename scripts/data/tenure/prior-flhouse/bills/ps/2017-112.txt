C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       DPOs should realize administrative efficiencies from the elimination of several filing requirements and
       other regulations. DPOs may be more likely to charge more than $30 per month or $360 per year, since
       OIR approval is no longer required.
