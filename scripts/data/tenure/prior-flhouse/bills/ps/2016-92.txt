C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

         The impact to the private sector is indeterminate. Provisions of the bill exempt certain ALF facilities
         licensed prior to July 1, 2016, from meeting requirements of the new uniform firesafety code. However,
         if an ALF were to undergo building rehabilitation as described in the new code established by the State
         Fire Marshal, there could be additional expenditures for the ALF to become compliant with the new
         uniform firesafety code.

         The bill also prohibits a utility from charging a fee in excess of the actual expense of installing and
         maintaining automatic fire sprinkler systems in a licensed ALF. To that extent, ALFs may realize a
         reduction in expenditures associated with the installation and maintenance of automatic fire sprinkler
         systems.
