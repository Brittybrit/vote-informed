Private Sector Impact:

                Developers of condominium will be subject to the cost of recoding the certificate of a
                surveyor and mapper, or an instrument that transfers title to a unit in the condominium
                which is not accompanied by a recorded assignment of developer rights in favor of the
                grantee of such unit (known as the first unit owner deed).
