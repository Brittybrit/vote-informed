         EFFECT OF THE BILL

         The bill dissolves the Eagle Bay Sub-Drainage District, an independent special district, by repealing
         chs. 12010, (1927), 19556 (1939), and 21916 (1943), Laws of Florida. Any assets and liabilities of the
         district are transferred to the Okeechobee County Board of County Commissioners.
