Private Sector Impact:

                The bill may reduce the type of development that can occur near a military base if non-
                conservation lands are purchased by the state. In addition, the buffering of military bases
                to ensure continued mission readiness may prevent future base closures in Florida. As
                stated in the analysis, military operations provide significant, positive economic impacts
                for the state.
