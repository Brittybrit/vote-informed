C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       By repealing s. 570.97, F.S., a one-time lump sum is provided to Florida Animal Friend, Inc., a nonprofit
       organization.

       By amending s. 534.083, F.S., persons engaged in the hauling of livestock will save time and money
       through the elimination of the livestock haulers permit.

       By amending s. 590.02, F.S., persons seeking an authorization for land-clearing may be charged a fee
       by the local government.

       By repealing s. 590.50, F.S., persons selling cypress products will no longer be required to obtain a
       permit from the department.
