C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Prohibiting access to the phone numbers and addresses of crash victims in motor vehicle crash reports
       may help protect crash victims and their families from illegal PIP solicitations. This may have a negative
       fiscal impact to the entities soliciting crash victims or engaged in a business that profits from the sale of
       crash victim information.
