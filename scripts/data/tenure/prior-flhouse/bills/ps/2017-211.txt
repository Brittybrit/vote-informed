       The bill provides that notwithstanding general law to the contrary, in the event a properly permitted sign
       on an interstate highway within Broward County is subsequently obstructed by the construction of a
       ramp, braided bridge, or other permanent visual obstruction within the interstate right of way, then the
       allowable height of the permitted sign will be measured from the top of the visual obstruction. In no
       event, will the height of the sign exceed 100 feet above the crown of the main traveled way of the road
       to which the sign is permitted regardless of the visual obstruction.

       The bill authorizes DOT to promulgate rules or forms necessary to implement the provisions in the bill.
