Private Sector Impact:

                  Some owners of agricultural land will experience lower ad valorem tax assessments.

                  The bill requires the City of Apalachicola to take over administrative responsibilities of
                  the Apalachicola Bay oyster harvesting license fees. This will allow the city to control the
                  allocation of funds for oyster shell restoration activities.
