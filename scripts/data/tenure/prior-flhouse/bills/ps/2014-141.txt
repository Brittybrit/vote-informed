C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

         An authorized entity which opts to stock EAIs must ensure at least one individual holds a certificate
         from an education training program which is evidence the individual can recognize the symptoms of a
         severe allergic reaction and administer an EAI. Each certificate costs $25.
