C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

        Associations operating fewer than 50 units may have increased costs if they are required to do financial
        statements other than cash receipts and expenditures as these associations may choose to hire
        accountants or CAMs to prepare the financial statements. Condominiums or cooperatives that
        consistently vote to waive financial reporting may have decreased costs by being able to waive
        perpetually instead of having to prepare a financial report once every four years.
