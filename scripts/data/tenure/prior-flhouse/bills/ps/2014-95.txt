Private Sector Impact:

                 Individuals who counterfeit tickets or sell or transfer used multiuse tickets or associated
                 cards or wristbands will be subject to increased criminal penalties.

                 However, individuals will no longer be subject to criminal penalties for the resale or
                 transfer of multiday or multievent tickets to entertainment venues other than theme parks.
