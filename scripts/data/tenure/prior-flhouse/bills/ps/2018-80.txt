Private Sector Impact:

                  Veterans requesting the “Veteran” designation on an identification card or a driver
                  license will no longer pay a $1 or $2 fee for the designation when a driver license or
                  identification card is being issued, renewed, or replaced. Additionally, a veteran will be
                  exempt from paying the $6.25 service fee for services rendered pursuant to ch. 322, F.S.,
                  by a tax collector.
