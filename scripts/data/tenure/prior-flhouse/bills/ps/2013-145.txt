C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       The bill prohibits manufacturer and importers of beer from providing coupons that are redeemable by
       the vendor to consumers. It could be anticipated that the consumer may see less opportunity to
       purchase malt beverages at a reduced price.
