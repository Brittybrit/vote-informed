Private Sector Impact:

                   The bill would exempt the sale of securities in connection with the transfer of ownership
                   of a privately held eligible company and the registration of M&A brokers with the OFR if
                   certain conditions are met. This would reduce the regulatory burden and the costs of such
                   transactions incurred by the buyers and sellers of such businesses.
