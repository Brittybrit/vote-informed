Private Sector Impact:

             CS/CS/SB 420 may have an indeterminate negative fiscal impact on individuals who
             allow their livestock, excluding cattle, to stray or violate animal control and cruelty
             ordinances. The provisions in the bill will allow county animal control centers and
             municipalities to collect livestock impoundment fees and enforce ordinances.
