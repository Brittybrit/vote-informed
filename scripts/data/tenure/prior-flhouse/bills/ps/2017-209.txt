        Effect of the Bill

        The bill revises the legal description of the District to annex a 42 acre parcel known as Watercrest. The
        revised legal description also includes the right of way for Bishop’s Pit Road and a parcel of land called
        the Gap Parcel.30

        The Economic Impact Statement estimates the District’s revenues will increase by $1,643,919 in fiscal
        year 2016-2017 and $236,307 in fiscal year 2017-2018, in the form of utility connection fees.
