C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

           The bill may result in a cost savings, in the form of refunds, for first-time-in-college students who
           generate excess credit hours but who graduate with a baccalaureate degree within 4 years of initial
           enrollment.
