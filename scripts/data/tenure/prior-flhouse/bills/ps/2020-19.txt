A veteran who is an FRS member seeking a proof of disability would not have to seek out and submit the certification of disability from two licensed physicians in-state if he or she is receiving treatment from a federal Veterans Health Administration (VHA) facility outside the state. Alternatively, the member may receive certification from physicians treating the member at the VHA facility. Therefore, this bill would potentially save the veteran time and money, and the veteran may receive a disability retirement benefit sooner.
