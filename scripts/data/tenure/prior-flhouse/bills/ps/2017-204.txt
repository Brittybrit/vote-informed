       The bill states that the Board members are to be elected at-large to seats labeled A, B, C, D, and E.
       Members elected to seats A, B, and C must reside within the city limits of Key West. Members elected
       to seats D and E must reside in Monroe County outside of the city limits of Key West, but inside the
       service area of the utility. Beginning with the 2018 general election, members will be elected to seats A,
       B, and D for 4 year terms. Beginning with the 2020 general election, members will be elected to seats
       C and E for 4 year terms. Members elected in 2016 as Groups II and III serve in seats C and E until the
       2020 general election. The bill specifies that the organizational session for the Board occurs after the
       candidates are qualified and sworn into office by the city clerk.

       The bill broadens the scope of authorized advertising venues by removing specific references to
       newspapers, radio, and television. The bill exempts purchases of commodities or contractual services
       from the competitive procurement process when the terms of the contract are satisfactory to the Board
       in their original form or when the terms are in a negotiated form more favorable to the Board. The bill
       authorizes the Board to adopt a policy governing reimbursements for Board members and employees
       expense while traveling on official business.

       The bill makes conforming changes to references to the City of Key West to reflect that Board members
       would be elected in Monroe County outside the City of Key West.

       The Economic Impact Statement submitted with the bill projects it will have no fiscal impact.
