C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

       Florida businesses that pay Florida corporate income tax will not be able to take full advantage, in the
       near-term, of the bonus depreciation and expensing provisions of the American Taxpayer Relief Act of
       2012 for Florida income tax purposes. Instead, the bill allows taxpayers to take advantage of the
       federal depreciation and expensing provisions over a 7- year period rather than in one year.
