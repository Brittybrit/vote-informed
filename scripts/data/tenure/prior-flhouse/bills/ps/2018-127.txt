Private Sector Impact:

                Adults and juveniles who wish to participate in a prearrest diversion program may have
                to pay a fee, depending on the guidelines established by each individual judicial circuit.
                The participant may also have to pay restitution as part of the prearrest diversion
                program, depending on what is indicated by the assessment of the adult or juvenile.
