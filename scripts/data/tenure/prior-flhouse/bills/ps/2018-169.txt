                                     I. SUBSTANTIVE INFORMATION

   A. EFFECT OF CHANGES:

       Present Situation

       The Division of Alcoholic Beverages and Tobacco (DABT) of the Department of Business and
       Profession Regulation (DBPR) is responsible for the enforcement of Florida’s Beverage Laws.

       Florida law limits the number of alcoholic beverage licenses that may be issued to one license for every
       7,500 residents in a county, known as the “quota”. Special Restaurant Beverage (SRX) licenses may
       be issued in excess of the quota limitations in s. 561.20(1), F.S., and are regulated under Rule 61A-
       3.0141, F.A.C. To qualify for the SRX license, a restaurant must have a service area of at least 2,500
       square feet, be equipped to serve at least 150 persons full meals at one time, and derive at least 51
       percent of its revenue from the sale of food and nonalcoholic beverages.

       The specific requirements regarding the issuance of SRX licenses in St. Lucie County are found in ch.
       67-1990, Laws of Florida. In St. Lucie County, SRX licenses may be issued to any bona fide restaurant
       with service for 200 or more patrons at tables and occupying more than 4,000 square feet of floor
       space. Licensees are prohibited from selling alcoholic beverages in packages for consumption off of the
       premises and from operating as a package store.

       Effect of the Bill

       The bill repeals ch. 67-1990, Laws of Florida, relating to the issuance of SRX licenses for bona fide
       restaurants in St. Lucie County. The issuance of subsequent SRX licenses in the county will be as
       provided under general law.
