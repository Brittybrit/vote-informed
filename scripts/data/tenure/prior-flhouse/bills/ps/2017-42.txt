C. DIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT ON PRIVATE SECTOR:

        Operators of natural gas-fueled commercial vehicles will see a reduction in overweight fines associated
        with the increase in weight allowed for those vehicles.

        There may be some reduced costs to the private sector with the increased dollar threshold for rapid
        response contracts since these contracts do not require securing a surety bond.
