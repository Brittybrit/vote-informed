Private Sector Impact:

              Private companies that have approved certifications on the Industry Certification Funding
              List or the Postsecondary Industry Certification Funding List would develop the Florida
              Cyber Security Recognition, the Florida Digital Arts Recognition, and the Florida Digital
              Tools Certificate.

              Students will be provided educational options that are more closely aligned with
              opportunities for employment, thus making it more likely that they will be employed in a
              high-skill, high wage job.
