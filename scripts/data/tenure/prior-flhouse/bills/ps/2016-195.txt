Private Sector Impact:

                Under CS/SB 1176, if the State Programmatic General Permit (SPGP) program is
                expanded to include dredge and fill activities impacting ten acres or less of wetlands or
                other surface waters, additional costs incurred by permit applicants may be reduced as a
                result of the streamlined permitting process.
