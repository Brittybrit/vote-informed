    1                                         A bill to be entitled

    4                Laboratory; providing an appropriation; providing an

    8
    9                Section 1.       Florida International University - Decision

12         Appropriations Project Request 1658, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum

16         the Executive Office of the Governor to fund the Florida
17         International University - Decision Laboratory as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 1658.
