           HB 3011                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                City of Hialeah Educational Academy; providing an
    4                appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       City of Hialeah Educational Academy is an
    9      Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 219, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $1,800,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
14         the Department of Education to fund the City of Hialeah
15         Educational Academy as described in Appropriations Project
16         Request 219. Notwithstanding any law to the contrary, there
17         shall be no recurring funding provided for this Appropriations
18         Project.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
