           HB 2733                                                                                               2019




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Florida SouthWestern State College, Bldg. E & F,
    4                Collier Campus; providing an appropriation; providing
    5                an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Florida SouthWestern State College, Bldg. E &
10         F, Collier Campus is an Appropriations Project as defined in The
11         Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is described
12         in Appropriations Project Request 139, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $9,856,145 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Education to fund the Florida SouthWestern
17         State College, Bldg. E & F, Collier Campus as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 139.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
