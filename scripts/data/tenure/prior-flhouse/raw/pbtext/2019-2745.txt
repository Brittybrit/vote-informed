    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Lehigh Acres Municipal Services Improvement District
    4                Caloosahatchee River & Estuary Storage & Treatment;
    5                providing an appropriation; providing an effective

    7
    8      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    9
10                   Section 1.       Lehigh Acres Municipal Services Improvement
11         District Caloosahatchee River & Estuary Storage & Treatment is
12         an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
13         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
14         Project Request 133, herein incorporated by reference.
15                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
16         of $87,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
17         Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Lehigh Acres
18         Municipal Services Improvement District Caloosahatchee River &
19         Estuary Storage & Treatment as described in Appropriations
20         Project Request 133.
21                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
