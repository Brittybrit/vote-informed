    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Amigos Together for Kids - Strengthening Families and
    4                Communities; providing an appropriation; providing an
    5                effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Amigos Together for Kids - Strengthening
10         Families and Communities is an Appropriations Project as defined
11         in The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is
12         described in Appropriations Project Request 240, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $350,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Children and Families to fund the Amigos Together
17         for Kids - Strengthening Families and Communities as described
18         in Appropriations Project Request 240.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
