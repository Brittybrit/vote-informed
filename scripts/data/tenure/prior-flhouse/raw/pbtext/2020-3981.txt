           HB 3981                                                                                               2020




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Miami Lakes Multipurpose Senior Community Center
    4                (Interior Buildout); providing an appropriation;
    5                providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Miami Lakes Multipurpose Senior Community
10         Center (Interior Buildout) is an Appropriations Project as
11         defined in The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and
12         is described in Appropriations Project Request 938, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $500,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Elder Affairs to fund the Miami Lakes Multipurpose
17         Senior Community Center (Interior Buildout) as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 938.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
