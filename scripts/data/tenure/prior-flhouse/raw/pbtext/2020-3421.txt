    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled

    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8

10         Florida Stroke Registry is an Appropriations Project as defined

12         described in Appropriations Project Request 823, herein
13         incorporated by reference.

15         of $1,200,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Health to fund the University of Miami Miller
17         School of Medicine Florida Stroke Registry as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 823.
