    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Coral Springs - Stormwater Improvement; providing an
    4                appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Coral Springs - Stormwater Improvement is an
    9      Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 1154, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2018-2019 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $130,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
14         Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Coral Springs
15         - Stormwater Improvement as described in Appropriations Project
16         Request 1154.
17                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2018.
