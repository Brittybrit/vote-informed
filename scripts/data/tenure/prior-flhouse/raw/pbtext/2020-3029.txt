    3                Association for Development of the Exceptional

    9      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
10
11                   Section 1.       Association for Development of the Exceptional
12         Culinary Program, Senior Program, and Services to Adults with
13         Autism and Developmental Disabilities is an Appropriations
14         Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida House of
15         Representatives and is described in Appropriations Project
16         Request 788, herein incorporated by reference.
17                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
18         of $500,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
19         Agency for Persons with Disabilities to fund the Association for
20         Development of the Exceptional Culinary Program, Senior Program,
21         and Services to Adults with Autism and Developmental
22         Disabilities as described in Appropriations Project Request 788.
23                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
