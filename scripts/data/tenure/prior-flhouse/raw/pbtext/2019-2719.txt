           HB 2719                                                                                               2019




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                University of Florida Institute of Food and
    4                Agricultural Sciences - Upgrade/Repair Immokalee
    5                Student Research Fields; providing an appropriation;
    6                providing an effective date.
    7
    8      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    9
10                   Section 1.       University of Florida Institute of Food and
11         Agricultural Sciences - Upgrade/Repair Immokalee Student
12         Research Fields is an Appropriations Project as defined in The
13         Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is described
14         in Appropriations Project Request 228, herein incorporated by
15         reference.
16                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
17         of $1,257,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
18         the Department of Education to fund the University of Florida
19         Institute of Food and Agricultural Sciences - Upgrade/Repair
20         Immokalee Student Research Fields as described in Appropriations
21         Project Request 228.
22                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
