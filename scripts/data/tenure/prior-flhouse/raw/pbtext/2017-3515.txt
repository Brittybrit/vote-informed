           HB 3515                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                LaBelle Zone J Septic Tank Conversion to Central
    4                Water; providing an appropriation; providing an
    5                effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       LaBelle Zone J Septic Tank Conversion to
10         Central Water is an Appropriations Project as defined in The
11         Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is described
12         in Appropriations Project Request 69, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $908,500 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Environmental Protection to fund the LaBelle Zone
17         J Septic Tank Conversion to Central Water as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 69. Notwithstanding any law to
19         the contrary, there shall be no recurring funding provided for
20         this Appropriations Project.
21                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
