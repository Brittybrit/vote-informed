    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Miami Lakes - NW 59th Avenue Extension and
    4                Redevelopment; providing an appropriation; providing

    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Miami Lakes - NW 59th Avenue Extension and
10         Redevelopment is an Appropriations Project as defined in The
11         Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is de scribed
12         in Appropriations Project Request 1752, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2022-2023 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $2,000,000 from the State Transportation (Primary) Trust Fund
16         is appropriated to the Department of Transportation to fund the
17         Miami Lakes - NW 59th Avenue Extension and Redevelopment as
18         described in Appropriations Project Request 1752.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2022.
