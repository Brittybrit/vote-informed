           HB 4099                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Four Corners Stormwater/Water Quality Project;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Four Corners Stormwater/Water Quality Project
10         is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The
11         Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 42, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $4,200,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Four
17         Corners Stormwater/Water Quality Project as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 42. Notwithstanding any law to
19         the contrary, there shall be no recurring funding provided for
20         this Appropriations Project.
21                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
