           HB 4377                                                                                               2020




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Collier Community Abstinence Program (CCAP); providing
    4                an appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Collier Community Abstinence Program (CCAP) is
    9      an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 1347, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $200,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
14         Department of Education to fund the Collier Community Abstinence
15         Program (CCAP) as described in Appropriations Project Request
16         1347.
17                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
