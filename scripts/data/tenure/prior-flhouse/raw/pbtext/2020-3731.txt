    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Opa-Locka Executive Airport Infrastructure
    4                Improvements; providing an appropriation; providing an

    8
    9                Section 1.       Opa-Locka Executive Airport Infrastructure

12         in Appropriations Project Request 1244, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $1,360,975 from the State Transportation (Primary) Trust Fund
16         is appropriated to the Department of Transportation to fund the
17         Opa-Locka Executive Airport Infrastructure Improvements as
18         described in Appropriations Project Request 1244.
