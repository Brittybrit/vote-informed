    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                StartUp FIU; providing an appropriation; providing an
    4                effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       StartUp FIU is an Appropriations Project as
    9      defined in The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and
10         is described in Appropriations Project Request 954, herein
11         incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $4,953,640 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
14         the Department of Economic Opportunity to fund the StartUp FIU
15         as described in Appropriations Project Request 954.
16         Notwithstanding any law to the contrary, there shall be no
17         recurring funding provided for this Appropriations Project.
18                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
