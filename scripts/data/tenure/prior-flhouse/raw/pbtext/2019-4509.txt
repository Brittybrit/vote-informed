    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Li'l Abner Foundation Program; providing an
    4                appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Li'l Abner Foundation Program is an
    9      Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 1486, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $184,985 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
14         Department of Education to fund the Li'l Abner Foundation
15         Program as described in Appropriations Project Request 1486.
16                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
