           HB 3975                                                                                               2020




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Miami Lakes East ADA Pedestrian Mobility
    4                Infrastructure Project; providing an appropriation;
    5                providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Miami Lakes East ADA Pedestrian Mobility
10         Infrastructure Project is an Appropriations Project as defined
11         in The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is
12         described in Appropriations Project Request 1387, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $853,000 from the State Transportation (Primary) Trust Fund
16         is appropriated to the Department of Transportation to fund the
17         Miami Lakes East ADA Pedestrian Mobility Infrastructure Project
18         as described in Appropriations Project Request 1387.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
