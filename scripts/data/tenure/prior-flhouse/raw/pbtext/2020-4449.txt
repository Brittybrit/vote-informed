    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Foster Parent Recruitment and Stability Project;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Foster Parent Recruitment and Stability Project
10         is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The
11         Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 1177, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $800,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Children and Families to fund the Foster Parent
17         Recruitment and Stability Project as described in Appropriations
18         Project Request 1177.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
