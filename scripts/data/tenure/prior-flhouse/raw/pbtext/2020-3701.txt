           HB 3701                                                                                               2020




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Little Havana Activities & Nutrition Centers of Dade
    4                County - Adult Day Care; providing an appropriation;
    5                providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Little Havana Activities & Nutrition Centers of
10         Dade County - Adult Day Care is an Appropriations Project as
11         defined in The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and
12         is described in Appropriations Project Request 1000, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $412,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Elder Affairs to fund the Little Havana Activities
17         & Nutrition Centers of Dade County - Adult Day Care as described
18         in Appropriations Project Request 1000.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
