    1                                         A bill to be entitled

    3                Dolphin Research Center Removal of Organic Material in

    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Dolphin Research Center Removal of Organic
10         Material in Dolphin Lagoons is an Appropriations Project as
11         defined in The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and
12         is described in Appropriations Project Request 1669, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $627,041 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Dolphin
17         Research Center Removal of Organic Material in Dolphin Lagoons
18         as described in Appropriations Project Request 1669.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
