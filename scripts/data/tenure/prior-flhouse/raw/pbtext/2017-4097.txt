           HB 4097                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                FIU - Washington Center for Internships; providing an
    4                appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       FIU - Washington Center for Internships is an
    9      Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 441, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $850,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
14         Board of Governors to fund the FIU - Washington Center for
15         Internships as described in Appropriations Project Request 441.
16         Notwithstanding any law to the contrary, there shall be no
17         recurring funding provided for this Appropriations Project.
18                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
