    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Miami-Dade County Mosquito Control Administrative and
    4                Operations Building; providing an appropriation;
    5                providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Miami-Dade County Mosquito Control
10         Administrative and Operations Building is an Appropriations
11         Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida House of
12         Representatives and is described in Appropriations Project
13         Request 1183, herein incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $5,000,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services to fund the
17         Miami-Dade County Mosquito Control Administrative and Operations
18         Building as described in Appropriations Project Request 1183.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
