    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                NW 89th Ave Roadway and Drainage Improvements;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       NW 89th Ave Roadway and Drainage Improvements
10         is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The
11         Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 1007, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $400,000 from the State Transportation (Primary) Trust Fund
16         is appropriated to the Department of Transportation to fund the
17         NW 89th Ave Roadway and Drainage Improvements as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 1007.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
