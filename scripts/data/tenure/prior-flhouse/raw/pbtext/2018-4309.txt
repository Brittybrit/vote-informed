           HB 4309                                                                                               2018




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Deerfield Beach African-American Memorial Park;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Deerfield Beach African-American Memorial Park
10         is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The
11         Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 486, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2018-2019 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $400,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Economic Opportunity to fund the Deerfield Beach
17         African-American Memorial Park as described in Appropriations
18         Project Request 486.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2018.
