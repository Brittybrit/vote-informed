    1                                         A bill to be entitled

    8

12         Appropriations Project Request 223, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum

16         the Department of Education to fund the Literacy Matrix
17         Professional Development System as described in Appropriations
18         Project Request 223.
