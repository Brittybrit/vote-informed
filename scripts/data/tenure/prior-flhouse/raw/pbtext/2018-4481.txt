           HB 4481                                                                                               2018




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                The Arc Nature Coast Life Skills Center & Regional
    4                Hurricane Shelter; providing an appropriation;
    5                providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       The Arc Nature Coast Life Skills Center &
10         Regional Hurricane Shelter is an Appropriations Project as
11         defined in The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and
12         is described in Appropriations Project Request 284, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2018-2019 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $1,750,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Agency for Persons with Disabilities to fund The Arc Nature
17         Coast Life Skills Center & Regional Hurricane Shelter as
18         described in Appropriations Project Request 284.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2018.
