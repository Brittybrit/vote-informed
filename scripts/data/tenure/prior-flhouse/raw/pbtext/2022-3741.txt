           HB 3741                                                                                               2022




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Miami East Flagami Flood Mitigation, Stormwater and
    4                Drainage Improvements; providing an appropriation;
    5                providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Miami East Flagami Flood Mitigation, Stormwater
10         and Drainage Improvements is an Appropriations Project as
11         defined in The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and
12         is described in Appropriations Project Request 1379, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2022-2023 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $3,000,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Miami
17         East Flagami Flood Mitigation, Stormwater and Drainage
18         Improvements as described in Appropriations Project Request
19         1379.
20                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2022.
