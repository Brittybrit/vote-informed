           HB 4565                                                                                               2019




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Schools and First Responder Collaboration via
    4                Mutualink System; providing an appropriation;
    5                providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Schools and First Responder Collaboration via
10         Mutualink System is an Appropriations Project as defined in The
11         Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is described
12         in Appropriations Project Request 1248, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $300,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Law Enforcement to fund the Schools and First
17         Responder Collaboration via Mutualink System as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 1248.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
