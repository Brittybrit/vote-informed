           HB 2173                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Identity Fraud Institute at Hodges University;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Identity Fraud Institute at Hodges University
10         is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The
11         Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 35, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $175,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Education to fund the Identity Fraud Institute at
17         Hodges University as described in Appropriations Project Request
18         35. Notwithstanding any law to the contrary, there shall be no
19         recurring funding provided for this Appropriations Project.
20                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
