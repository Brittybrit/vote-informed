    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Child Care WAGE$ Florida; providing an appropriation;
    4                providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Child Care WAGE$ Florida is an Appropriations
    9      Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida House of
10         Representatives and is described in Appropriations Project
11         Request 1656, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $525,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
14         Office of Early Learning to fund the Child Care WAGE$ Florida as
15         described in Appropriations Project Request 1656.
16                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
