    1                                         A bill to be entitled

    3                Ability Tree Florida R.E.S.T. and Recreation Center;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective

    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Ability Tree Florida R.E.S.T. and Recreation
10         Center is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of
11         The Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 249, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2021-2022 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $220,200 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Agency for Persons with Disabilities to fund the Ability Tree
17         Florida R.E.S.T. and Recreation Center as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 249.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2021.
