    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Miami East Auburndale Drainage & Flooding Mitigation;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Miami East Auburndale Drainage & Flooding
10         Mitigation is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules
11         of The Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 1671, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2022-2023 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $1,500,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Miami
17         East Auburndale Drainage & Flooding Mitigation as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 1671.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2022.
