           HB 4489                                                                                               2019




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Biscayne-Everglades Greenway Design and Construction;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Biscayne-Everglades Greenway Design and
10         Construction is an Appropriations Project as defined in The
11         Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is described
12         in Appropriations Project Request 1499, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $5,000,000 from the State Transportation (Primary) Trust Fund
16         is appropriated to the Department of Transportation to fund the
17         Biscayne-Everglades Greenway Design and Construction as
18         described in Appropriations Project Request 1499.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
