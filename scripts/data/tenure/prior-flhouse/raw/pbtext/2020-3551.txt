           HB 3551                                                                                               2020




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Miami Tidal Valves and Flood Improvements; providing
    4                an appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Miami Tidal Valves and Flood Improvements is an
    9      Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 1269, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $1,500,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
14         the Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Miami
15         Tidal Valves and Flood Improvements as described in
16         Appropriations Project Request 1269.
17                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
