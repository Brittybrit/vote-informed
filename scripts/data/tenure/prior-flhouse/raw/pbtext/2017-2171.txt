           HB 2171                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Hendry County Wastewater Infrastructure on US27-SR80
    4                Phase II; providing an appropriation; providing an
    5                effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Hendry County Wastewater Infrastructure on
10         US27-SR80 Phase II is an Appropriations Project as defined in
11         The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is
12         described in Appropriations Project Request 65, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $5,400,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Hendry
17         County Wastewater Infrastructure on US27-SR80 Phase II as
18         described in Appropriations Project Request 65. Notwithstanding
19         any law to the contrary, there shall be no recurring funding
20         provided for this Appropriations Project.
21                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
