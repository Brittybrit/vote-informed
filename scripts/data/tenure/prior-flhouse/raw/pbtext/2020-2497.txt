    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Florida International University - Washington Center
    4                University Scholarships; providing an appropriation;
    5                providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Florida International University - Washington
10         Center University Scholarships is an Appropriations Project as
11         defined in The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and
12         is described in Appropriations Project Request 377, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $350,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Education to fund the Florida International
17         University - Washington Center University Scholarships as
18         described in Appropriations Project Request 377.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
