           HB 4305                                                                                               2020




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Li'l Abner Foundation Sports and Educational Programs;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Li'l Abner Foundation Sports and Educational
10         Programs is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of
11         The Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 1517, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $185,050 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Health to fund the Li'l Abner Foundation Sports
17         and Educational Programs as described in Appropriations Project
18         Request 1517.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
