    1                                         A bill to be entitled

    8

12         Appropriations Project Request 297, herein incorporated by
13         reference.

15         of $500,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Education to fund the St. Thomas University Trade
17         and Logistics Program as described in Appropriations Project
18         Request 297.
