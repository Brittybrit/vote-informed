           HB 3749                                                                                               2019




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Hialeah Athletic Fields Lighting Upgrade; providing an
    4                appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Hialeah Athletic Fields Lighting Upgrade is an
    9      Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 1254, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $4,000,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
14         the Department of Economic Opportunity to fund the Hialeah
15         Athletic Fields Lighting Upgrade as described in Appropriations
16         Project Request 1254.
17                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
