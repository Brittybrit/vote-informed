           HB 4465                                                                                               2019




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Canal C-6 Bulkhead Study; providing an appropriation;
    4                providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Canal C-6 Bulkhead Study is an Appropriations
    9      Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida House of
10         Representatives and is described in Appropriations Project
11         Request 1622, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $200,000 from the State Transportation (Primary) Trust Fund
14         is appropriated to the Department of Transportation to fund the
15         Canal C-6 Bulkhead Study as described in Appropriations Project
16         Request 1622.
17                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
