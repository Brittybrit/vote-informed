    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                City of Hialeah Gardens - Therapy Pool for the
    4                Physically Challenged; providing an appropriation;
    5                providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       City of Hialeah Gardens - Therapy Pool for the
10         Physically Challenged is an Appropriations Project as defined in
11         The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is
12         described in Appropriations Project Request 1459, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $800,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Elder Affairs to fund the City of Hialeah Gardens
17         - Therapy Pool for the Physically Challenged as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 1459.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
