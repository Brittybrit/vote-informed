    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Continuum of Care for Enhanced Offender
    4                Rehabilitation; providing an appropriation; providing
    5                an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Continuum of Care for Enhanced Offender
10         Rehabilitation is an Appropriations Project as defined in The
11         Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is described
12         in Appropriations Project Request 960, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $2,961,680 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Corrections to fund the Continuum of Care for
17         Enhanced Offender Rehabilitation as described in Appropriations
18         Project Request 960.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
