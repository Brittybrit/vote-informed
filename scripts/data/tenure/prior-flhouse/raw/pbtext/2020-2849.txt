           HB 2849                                                                                               2020




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Comprehensive Health Plan; providing an appropriation;
    4                providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Comprehensive Health Plan is an Appropriations
    9      Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida House of
10         Representatives and is described in Appropriations Project
11         Request 565, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $893,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
14         Department of Education to fund the Comprehensive Health Plan as
15         described in Appropriations Project Request 565.
16                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
