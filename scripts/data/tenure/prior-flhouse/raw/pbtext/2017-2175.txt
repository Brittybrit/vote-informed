           HB 2175                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Florida SouthWestern State College – Physical Plant
    4                West Chiller Replacement - Lee; providing an
    5                appropriation; providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Florida SouthWestern State College – Physical
10         Plant West Chiller Replacement - Lee is an Appropriations
11         Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida House of
12         Representatives and is described in Appropriations Project
13         Request 26, herein incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $1,000,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Education to fund the Florida SouthWestern
17         State College – Physical Plant West Chiller Replacement - Lee as
18         described in Appropriations Project Request 26. Notwithstanding
19         any law to the contrary, there shall be no recurring funding
20         provided for this Appropriations Project.
21                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
