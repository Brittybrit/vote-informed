           HB 3169                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                City of Sunrise Natural Gas Line Expansion; providing
    4                an appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       City of Sunrise Natural Gas Line Expansion is
    9      an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 651, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $1,000,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
14         the Department of Environmental Protection to fund the City of
15         Sunrise Natural Gas Line Expansion as described in
16         Appropriations Project Request 651. Notwithstanding any law to
17         the contrary, there shall be no recurring funding provided for
18         this Appropriations Project.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
