    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Bay of Pigs - Brigade 2506 Museum and Library;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Bay of Pigs - Brigade 2506 Museum and Library
10         is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The
11         Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 791, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2022-2023 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $2,600,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of State to fund the Bay of Pigs - Brigade 2506
17         Museum and Library as described in Appropriations Project
18         Request 791.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2022.
