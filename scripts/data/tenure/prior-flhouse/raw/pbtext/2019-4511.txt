           HB 4511                                                                                               2019




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Tutoring Services; providing an appropriation;
    4                providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Tutoring Services is an Appropriations Project
    9      as defined in The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives
10         and is described in Appropriations Project Request 1440, herein
11         incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $200,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
14         Department of Education to fund the Tutoring Services as
15         described in Appropriations Project Request 1440.
16                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
