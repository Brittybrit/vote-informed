           HB 4091                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Sparsity Funding for Hernando County School District;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Sparsity Funding for Hernando County School
10         District is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of
11         The Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 932, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $2,207,566 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Education to fund the Sparsity Funding for
17         Hernando County School District as described in Appropriations
18         Project Request 932. Notwithstanding any law to the contrary,
19         there shall be no recurring funding provided for this
20         Appropriations Project.
21                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
