           HB 4999                                                                                               2020




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Statewide Sea Level Tracking Program; providing an
    4                appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Statewide Sea Level Tracking Program is an
    9      Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 1598, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $5,955,636 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
14         the Department of Financial Services to fund the Statewide Sea
15         Level Tracking Program as described in Appropriations Project
16         Request 1598.
17                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
