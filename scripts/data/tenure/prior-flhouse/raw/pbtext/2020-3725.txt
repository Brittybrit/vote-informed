           HB 3725                                                                                               2020




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Bay of Pigs - Brigade 2506 Museum; providing an
    4                appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Bay of Pigs - Brigade 2506 Museum is an
    9      Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 1242, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $3,000,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
14         the Department of State to fund the Bay of Pigs - Brigade 2506
15         Museum as described in Appropriations Project Request 1242.
16                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
