           HB 4297                                                                                               2018




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                HANDY Scholars Program; providing an appropriation;
    4                providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       HANDY Scholars Program is an Appropriations
    9      Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida House of
10         Representatives and is described in Appropriations Project
11         Request 1341, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2018-2019 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $125,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
14         Department of Economic Opportunity to fund the HANDY Scholars
15         Program as described in Appropriations Project Request 1341.
16                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2018.
