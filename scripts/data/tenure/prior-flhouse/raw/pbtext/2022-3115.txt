    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Hialeah Southeast Stormwater Infrastructure Project ;

    8
    9                Section 1.       Hialeah Southeast Stormwater Infrastructure

11         The Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 761, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2022-2023 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $3,200,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Hialeah
17         Southeast Stormwater Infrastructure Project as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 761.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2022.
