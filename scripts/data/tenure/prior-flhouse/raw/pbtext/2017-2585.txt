           HB 2585                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Dementia Respite Support Program-Jewish Family and
    4                Community Services of Southwest Florida; providing an
    5                appropriation; providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Dementia Respite Support Program-Jewish Family
10         and Community Services of Southwest Florida is an Appropriations
11         Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida House of
12         Representatives and is described in Appropriations Project
13         Request 31, herein incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $50,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Elder Affairs to fund the Dementia Respite Support
17         Program-Jewish Family and Community Services of Southwest
18         Florida as described in Appropriations Project Request 31.
19         Notwithstanding any law to the contrary, there shall be no
20         recurring funding provided for this Appropriations Project.
21                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
