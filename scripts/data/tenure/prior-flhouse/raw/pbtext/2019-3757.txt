    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Dante Fascell Park – Tennis Court Lighting; providing
    4                an appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Dante Fascell Park – Tennis Court Lighting is
    9      an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 1618, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $250,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
14         Department of Economic Opportunity to fund the Dante Fascell
15         Park – Tennis Court Lighting as described in Appropriations
16         Project Request 1618.
17                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
