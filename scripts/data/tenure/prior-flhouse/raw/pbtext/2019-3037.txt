           HB 3037                                                                                               2019




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Coral Gables Public Safety Building and Regional EOC;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Coral Gables Public Safety Building and
10         Regional EOC is an Appropriations Project as defined in The
11         Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is described
12         in Appropriations Project Request 1246, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $1,500,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Financial Services to fund the Coral Gables
17         Public Safety Building and Regional EOC as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 1246.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
