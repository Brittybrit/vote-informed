           HB 3773                                                                                               2019




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Coral Gables Waterway Quality Assessment and Waterway
    4                Dredging Evaluation; providing an appropriation;
    5                providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Coral Gables Waterway Quality Assessment and
10         Waterway Dredging Evaluation is an Appropriations Project as
11         defined in The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and
12         is described in Appropriations Project Request 1300, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $200,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Coral Gables
17         Waterway Quality Assessment and Waterway Dredging Evaluation as
18         described in Appropriations Project Request 1300.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
