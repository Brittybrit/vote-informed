           HB 2807                                                                                               2022




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Ludlam Trail Corridor High Speed Fiber Internet
    4                Infrastructure; providing an appropriation; providing
    5                an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Ludlam Trail Corridor High Speed Fiber Internet
10         Infrastructure is an Appropriations Project as defined in The
11         Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is described
12         in Appropriations Project Request 370, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2022-2023 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $2,000,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Economic Opportunity to fund the Ludlam Trail
17         Corridor High Speed Fiber Internet Infrastructure as described
18         in Appropriations Project Request 370.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2022.
