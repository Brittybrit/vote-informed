    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled

    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Hendry County Wastewater Infrastructure on US
10         27/SR 80 is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of
11         The Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 1388, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2018-2019 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $3,810,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Hendry
17         County Wastewater Infrastructure on US 27/SR 80 as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 1388.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2018.
