    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Colombian American Chamber of Commerce of Greater
    4                Miami; providing an appropriation; providing an
    5                effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Colombian American Chamber of Commerce of
10         Greater Miami is an Appropriations Project as defined in The
11         Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is described
12         in Appropriations Project Request 1056, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $400,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Economic Opportunity to fund the Colombian
17         American Chamber of Commerce of Greater Miami as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 1056.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
