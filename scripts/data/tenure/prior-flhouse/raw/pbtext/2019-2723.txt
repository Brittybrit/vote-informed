           HB 2723                                                                                               2019




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Informed Families of Florida, Healthy Communities;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Informed Families of Florida, Healthy
10         Communities is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules
11         of The Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 138, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $750,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Children and Families to fund the Informed
17         Families of Florida, Healthy Communities as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 138.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
