           HB 3013                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Easter Seals of South Florida-Hialeah Adult Day Care
    4                Center; providing an appropriation; providing an
    5                effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Easter Seals of South Florida-Hialeah Adult Day
10         Care Center is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules
11         of The Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 178, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $196,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Elder Affairs to fund the Easter Seals of South
17         Florida-Hialeah Adult Day Care Center as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 178. Notwithstanding any law to
19         the contrary, there shall be no recurring funding provided for
20         this Appropriations Project.
21                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
