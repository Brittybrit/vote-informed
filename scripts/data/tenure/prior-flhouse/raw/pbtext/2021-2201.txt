           HB 2201                                                                                               2021




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                UF Health Alzheimer's and Dementia Research; providing
    4                an appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       UF Health Alzheimer's and Dementia Research is
    9      an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 76, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2021-2022 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $3,000,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
14         the Board of Governors to fund the UF Health Alzheimer's and
15         Dementia Research as described in Appropriations Project Request
16         76.
17                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2021.
