           HB 3423                                                                                               2020




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Florida Heiken Children's Vision Program LLC, a
    4                division of Miami Lighthouse for the Blind; providing
    5                an appropriation; providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Florida Heiken Children's Vision Program LLC, a
10         division of Miami Lighthouse for the Blind is an Appropriations
11         Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida House of
12         Representatives and is described in Appropriations Project
13         Request 821, herein incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $250,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Health to fund the Florida Heiken Children's
17         Vision Program LLC, a division of Miami Lighthouse for the Blind
18         as described in Appropriations Project Request 821.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
