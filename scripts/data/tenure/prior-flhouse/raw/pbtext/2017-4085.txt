           HB 4085                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Hernando County Hunters Lake Clean Up & Restoration
    4                Project; providing an appropriation; providing an
    5                effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Hernando County Hunters Lake Clean Up &
10         Restoration Project is an Appropriations Project as defined in
11         The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is
12         described in Appropriations Project Request 779, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $275,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission to fund the Hernando
17         County Hunters Lake Clean Up & Restoration Project as described
18         in Appropriations Project Request 779. Notwithstanding any law
19         to the contrary, there shall be no recurring funding provided
20         for this Appropriations Project.
21                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
