           HB 4089                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                The Arc Nature Coast Life Skills Center; providing an
    4                appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       The Arc Nature Coast Life Skills Center is an
    9      Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 795, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $425,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
14         Agency for Persons with Disabilities to fund the The Arc Nature
15         Coast Life Skills Center as described in Appropriations Project
16         Request 795. Notwithstanding any law to the contrary, there
17         shall be no recurring funding provided for this Appropriations
18         Project.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
