    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                The Guidance Care Center - Monroe County Baker Act
    4                Receiving Facility Upgrades; providing an
    5                appropriation; providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       The Guidance Care Center - Monroe County Baker
10         Act Receiving Facility Upgrades is an Appropriations Project as
11         defined in The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and
12         is described in Appropriations Project Request 822, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $500,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Children and Families to fund The Guidance Care
17         Center - Monroe County Baker Act Receiving Facility Upgrades as
18         described in Appropriations Project Request 822.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
