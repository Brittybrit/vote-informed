           HB 2603                                                                                               2020




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                The Victory for Youth Share Your Heart Program;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       The Victory for Youth Share Your Heart Program
10         is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The
11         Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 323, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $865,280 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Children and Families to fund The Victory for
17         Youth Share Your Heart Program as described in Appropriations
18         Project Request 323.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
