           HB 2805                                                                                               2022




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Goodwill Industries of South Florida; providing an
    4                appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Goodwill Industries of South Florida is an
    9      Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 542, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2022-2023 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $400,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
14         Department of Education to fund the Goodwill Industries of South
15         Florida as described in Appropriations Project Request 542.
16                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2022.
