           HB 3315                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Golden Gate City Outfall System Replacement Program,
    4                Phase I (Northwest Quadrant); providing an
    5                appropriation; providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Golden Gate City Outfall System Replacement
10         Program, Phase I (Northwest Quadrant) is an Appropriations
11         Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida House of
12         Representatives and is described in Appropriations Project
13         Request 57, herein incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $950,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Golden Gate
17         City Outfall System Replacement Program, Phase I (Northwest
18         Quadrant) as described in Appropriations Project Request 57.
19         Notwithstanding any law to the contrary, there shall be no
20         recurring funding provided for this Appropriations Project.
21                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
