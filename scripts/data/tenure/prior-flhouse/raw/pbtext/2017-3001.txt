           HB 3001                                                                                               2017




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Miami Springs Erosion Control and Stabilization of
    4                Drainage Canal Phase II; providing an appropriation;
    5                providing an effective date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Miami Springs Erosion Control and Stabilization
10         of Drainage Canal Phase II is an Appropriations Project as
11         defined in The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and
12         is described in Appropriations Project Request 747, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2017-2018 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $850,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Miami Springs
17         Erosion Control and Stabilization of Drainage Canal Phase II as
18         described in Appropriations Project Request 747. Notwithstanding
19         any law to the contrary, there shall be no recurring funding
20         provided for this Appropriations Project.
21                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2017.
