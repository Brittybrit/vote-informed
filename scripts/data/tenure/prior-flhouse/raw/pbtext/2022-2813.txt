    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled

    8
    9                Section 1.       Miami Springs South Drive Road and Stormwater

12         in Appropriations Project Request 324, herein incorporated by
13         reference.

15         of $2,000,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
16         the Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Miami
17         Springs South Drive Road and Stormwater Improvements as
18         described in Appropriations Project Request 324.
