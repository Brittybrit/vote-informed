    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Pace Center for Girls - Hernando Building; providing
    4                an appropriation; providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Pace Center for Girls - Hernando Building is an
    9      Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
10         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
11         Project Request 259, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2021-2022 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $3,500,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to
14         the Department of Juvenile Justice to fund the Pace Center for
15         Girls - Hernando Building as described in Appropriations Project
16         Request 259.
17                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2021.
