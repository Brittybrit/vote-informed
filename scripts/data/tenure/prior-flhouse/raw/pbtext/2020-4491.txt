    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Cuban Studies Institute - Professional and Economic

    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Cuban Studies Institute - Professional and
10         Economic Counseling is an Appropriations Project as defined in
11         The Rules of The Florida House of Representatives and is
12         described in Appropriations Project Request 1499, herein
13         incorporated by reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $400,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Economic Opportunity to fund the Cuban Studies
17         Institute - Professional and Economic Counseling as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 1499.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
