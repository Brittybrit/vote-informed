           HB 4521                                                                                               2019




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Victory For Youth/Share Your Heart 2019-2020;
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Victory For Youth/Share Your Heart 2019-2020 is
10         an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida
11         House of Representatives and is described in Appropriations
12         Project Request 1608, herein incorporated by reference.
13                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2019-2020 the nonrecurring sum
14         of $865,280 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
15         Department of Children and Families to fund the Victory For
16         Youth/Share Your Heart 2019-2020 as described in Appropriations
17         Project Request 1608.
18                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2019.
