    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Doral Stormwater Improvements NW 89 Pl (25-20 St.);
    4                providing an appropriation; providing an effective
    5                date.
    6
    7      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    8
    9                Section 1.       Doral Stormwater Improvements NW 89 Pl (25-20
10         St.) is an Appropriations Project as defined in The Rules of The
11         Florida House of Representatives and is described in
12         Appropriations Project Request 826, herein incorporated by
13         reference.
14                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2020-2021 the nonrecurring sum
15         of $250,000 from the General Revenue Fund is appropriated to the
16         Department of Environmental Protection to fund the Doral
17         Stormwater Improvements NW 89 Pl (25-20 St.) as described in
18         Appropriations Project Request 826.
19                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2020.
