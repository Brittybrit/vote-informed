           HB 2263                                                                                               2021




    1                                         A bill to be entitled
    2                An act relating to the Appropriations Project titled
    3                Ludlam Trail Corridor; providing an appropriation;
    4                providing an effective date.
    5
    6      Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
    7
    8                Section 1.       Ludlam Trail Corridor is an Appropriations
    9      Project as defined in The Rules of The Florida House of
10         Representatives and is described in Appropriations Project
11         Request 120, herein incorporated by reference.
12                   Section 2.       For fiscal year 2021-2022 the nonrecurring sum
13         of $5,000,000 from the State Transportation (Primary) Trust Fund
14         is appropriated to the Department of Transportation to fund the
15         Ludlam Trail Corridor as described in Appropriations Project
16         Request 120.
17                   Section 3.       This act shall take effect July 1, 2021.
