# County Commission discretionary allocations

Every commissioner gives county money to groups of their choosing from their district's discretionary funds (CBO
Discretionary Reserve, District Designated Project Program, office budget, Marlins and arena naming-rights funds). The
Board approves them on the consent agenda as "D-n ALLOCATIONS" resolutions (the allocations are in memos the
commissioner signs, attached to the agenda packet) or as Clerk's reports read into the minutes.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `minutes-items.json` | Every District 1 item sponsored by Oliver G. Gilbert III (Nov 17, 2020 on) and District 5 item sponsored by Vicki L. Lopez (Nov 18, 2025 on) whose title names allocations, found by reading the Clerk's minutes of all 188 Commission meetings from 2020 to Oct 20, 2026 (`commminute.asp?cmbmeetdate=`). Matter number, date, sponsor, vote and the minutes text. |
| `gilbert-selected.json` | Hand-checked lines for three groups: FYFL United, A3 Foundation and FANM, read from each item's agenda packet (`legistarfiles/Matters/Y20xx/<matter>.pdf`, memos OCR'd where scanned) or the minutes, plus the record facts about the groups. Built into cards by `scripts/county-allocations.mjs`. |
