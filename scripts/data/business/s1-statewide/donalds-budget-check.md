## Answer

No. The official records for the 2017-2020 sessions show no state budget line item, appropriation, member project or bill that named or specifically funded Naples Classical Academy / Phoenix Education Network, Optima Classical Academy / Classical Education Network / Optima Education Network, Jacksonville Classical Academy (or East), Treasure Coast Classical Academy, the Optima Foundation, Optima Management Services, OptimaEd, Onesto, Dewalt Impact Group, or any Hillsdale-affiliated classical charter program. Donalds also filed no House appropriations project for any of them; his roughly 70 project bills from 2017 to 2020 are all Collier, Hendry and Lee infrastructure, colleges, social-service groups and statewide education vendors (Teach For America, Lauren's Kids, Matific, Leader in Me). He voted yes on all four final GAAs (5/8/2017, 3/11/2018, 5/4/2019, 3/19/2020), but none of them contains such a line. Timing matters too: OptimaEd, LLC was not formed until 9/16/2020, Optima Management Services until 2022, and most of the listed schools opened after he left the Legislature. The only related record is CS/HB 807 (2019, Civics Education). Donalds co-sponsored it and voted yes (111-1). It lists Hillsdale College among organizations the Education Commissioner may consult when approving civics instructional materials. It names the college, not a charter school, and appropriates nothing. Not reviewed: the governor's veto list documents themselves (moot, since vetoes come only from the enrolled items searched) and his votes on general charter-school policy bills that he co-sponsored (CS/CS/HB 1197 in 2019, CS/HB 953 in 2020). Those bills name none of the entities.
