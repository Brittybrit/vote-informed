## 2. The five schools and what they paid OptimaEd and Optima Management Services

Two kinds of source. **Audit**: the school's annual audited financial statements, filed with the Florida Auditor General ([charter e-file list](https://flauditor.gov/pages/chschools_efiles.html)). **990**: the school's IRS Form 990, Part VII Section B (five highest-paid independent contractors), read from the IRS e-file render on ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer.

Caveat on 990 figures: Part VII-B instructions call for compensation for the *calendar year* ending within the fiscal year. Some schools seem to report fiscal-year amounts instead, so a 990 figure and the matching audit figure can differ. Both are shown where both exist.

## 3. "More than $30 million over four years" from five schools to the two companies

This is not confirmed as an exact figure, but it is consistent with the records within rounding. Payments to OptimaEd and Optima Management Services only, FY2022-23 through FY2025-26:

| School | Sum | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Naples Classical | $9,136,961 | 990s FY23-25 (includes payroll run through Optima Management Services) |
| Optima Classical | about $18.39 million | Audits: $1,085,266 + ~$2.1M + ~$4.4M + ~$10.8M |
| Jacksonville Classical | $1,952,512 | 990 FY23 |
| Jacksonville Classical East | $692,263 | 990 FY23 |
| Treasure Coast Classical | $222,946 | 990 FY23 |
| **Total** | **about $30.39 million** | |

Using the Optima Classical 990 figures for FY23-25 ($6,759,489) instead of the audits, the total is about $29.56 million. The narrowest reading takes the lower of the audit and 990 figure each year for Optima Classical, and uses the Naples audit fees ($3.74 million for FY23-25) instead of its 990 contractor totals. That gives about $23.9 million. So: **at least about $23.9 million** in fees on the narrowest reading, and about $30.4 million when payroll run through Optima Management Services is counted. Both totals are partial: Naples FY2025-26 fees are not yet audited. The $30 million figure depends on counting the Naples payroll payments and on the rounded Optima Classical audit amounts.

Not found in the records checked: the Herald's statement that the online school paid "$10 million last year." The FY2025-26 audit says "approximately $10.8 million" in service fees. The FY2024-25 audit says about $4.4 million.
