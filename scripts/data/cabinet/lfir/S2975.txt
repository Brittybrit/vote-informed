 With these funds, we will continue to provide transportation and educational services to the students of Citrus County by 
repairing buildings at Crystal River Primary School, Crystal River Middle School, Marine Science Station, and the Crystal 
River Bus Compound. 
With the repair of the Marine Science Station, it will allow students, community organizations, and citizens to access 
educational day programs offered by the Citrus County School District. 
The target population includes preschool students, grade school students, high school students, at-risk youth, and 
community organizations. The number of students being served by this project is over 11,000. 
The following are ways the outcome of the program will be measured: 
Number of students and community members attending the programs offered at the Marine Science Station. 
Number of students being transported out of buses from the Crystal River Bus Compound. 
If there is an area in which the Citrus County School District is deficient, it is suggested that a corrective action plan be 
implemented and monitored to ensure that the deficient area(s) is/are corrected in a timely manner. If the Citrus County 
School District is not meeting the deliverables or the expected performance outcomes outlined in the contract, the State 
has the option to cancel that contract for these services.
