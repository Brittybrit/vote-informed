 Total State Funds Requested (must equal total from question #6)   850,000 
 Funds will directly support services to citizens by providing high school students with an opportunity to enhance learning 
and academic achievement through innovative educational methods. Faculty assigned to the charter school will have 
professional opportunities to teach both high school and college-level courses, fostering academic growth and success 
among motivated students. 
The charter school will combine high school courses with college-level instruction using innovative methods. Instructors 
will have flexibility to tailor teaching to ensure student success. The benefit will be measured by student performance in 
both high school and college courses, reflecting the effectiveness of the approach. Graduates will earn a high school 
diploma, college credits, or an Associate Degree, enhancing employability in technical and professional fields. They will 
be prepared to enter the workforce or pursue advanced degrees, increasing long-term economic opportunities and career 
potential. 
Funding will provide parents and students with alternative educational options. Students will have access to both 
required high school courses for graduation and college-level courses toward earning an associate degree. Additionally, 
teachers will benefit from enhanced professional development opportunities to support their role in delivering high-quality 
education at both levels. 
The target population to be served by this project include the following: university/college students, high school students, 
economically disadvantaged person, at-risk youth, and teachers seeking professional development opportunities to teach 
at both levels, enhancing their skills. We anticipate the individuals to be served between 201-400. 
The charter school will foster a culture of optimism, perseverance, and innovation among students, faculty, and staff. It 
will emphasize positive learning experiences and constructive feedback to encourage growth. Students will be 
empowered to take on leadership roles and have a voice in shaping their education, enriching their cultural experience by 
promoting engagement and responsibility. The level of benefit will be measured through full-time student enrollment and 
academic success, reflecting the positive culture of the charter school. Encouraging learning experiences and giving 
students a voice will drive success. Ongoing assessments, including observations, surveys, and feedback, will track 
progress and identify areas for improvement, ensuring continuous growth. 
A consideration such as withholding a portion of allocated funds until specific deliverables or performance measures are 
met are suggested. Other penalties could include requiring a corrective action plan with measurable milestones, reducing 
future funding allocations, suspending program operations until compliance is achieved, or requiring the return of any 
unused appropriated funds.
