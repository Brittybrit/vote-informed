The building which houses The Academy of Environmental Science was originally built as a residential condominium. The 
Surfside Condominium collapse in 2021 triggered legislative changes for this type of building. In 2022 the building which 
houses the school was inspected by an engineer and a number of structural upgrades were recommended to continue 
occupancy. The funds requested will be used to aid in completing these upgrades and bring the building to a more modern 
code for student safety.

 The facility will be safe to operate at our 120 student capacity for the foreseeable future. The joists being used as 
replacements are resistant to the corrosion which caused this issue over the last 30 years. 
Construction has already begun under the direction of a Licensed Florida Engineer. These funds will be used to 
complete the construction required to be completed to remain open in the 2026-2027 school year. 
The Academy of Environmental Science is the only "A" rated high school in Citrus County, and one of only 2 in the entire 
district. This facility promotes and encourages students to achieve by empowering their natural curiosity in a way that can 
only be achieved by being in the field, rather than just a classroom. The growing number of alumni are deeply rooted in 
our community, and State. Many have become local business owners, scientists, teachers and much more. 
The facility services roughly 120 students per year directly. Indirectly, the public is able to enjoy the property through the 
means of a nature boardwalk that is open to the public. Since 1999, the school has serviced over 2,600 high functioning 
students. 
Data provided by The Citrus County School Board shows that AES has been a top performing school since it opened in 
1999. Even while the rest of the district declined, AES continues to be the top performing school in the district as proven 
by state and local tests. The desired outcome is to continue operating in our current capacity until the school is eventually 
expanded. 
The funds shall be returned.
