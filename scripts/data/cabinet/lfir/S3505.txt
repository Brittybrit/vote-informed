 1. Project Title   Living Creek Village and Traditional-style Council Roundhouse - Santa 
Rosa 
 2. Senate Sponsor   Blaise Ingoglia 
 3. Date of Request   3/19/2025 
This project is construction of a Living Creek Village with Traditional-style Council Roundhouse for education and 
preservation of Native American history and culture. No original Creek Villages of council roundhouses remain due to the 
cultural genocide of the 1800s that aimed to eradicate Native American culture. Other than our small Creek Village display, 
there is a notable absence of a significant replica Creek Village or roundhouse in this region for Historic Tourism. The 
nearest council roundhouse reproduction to be found is in Tallahassee, Florida, with the next closest located approximately 
1,000 miles away in Oklahoma. Visitors will experience emersion in the Creek history and culture offering a path of re- 
connection to the heritage of those having Native American ancestry.

 The Living Creek Village and roundhouse will be made available to both public and private schools as a field trip 
destination. It will provide an emersion experience for the public at large and to students for augmenting the current 
Native American history and culture curriculum. 
A historical and cultural emersion experience for students of both public and private schools is the primary objective with 
the public at large being the secondary objective for a cultural emersion experience. 
The primary target population is both public and private school students. Secondarily is the public at large. Based on the 
number of public and private school students and the number of visitors of our annual two-day Native American Living 
History event it is anticipated there would be more than 12,000 visitors being served annually in perpetuity. 
The benefit would be to enhance historic and cultural knowledge and understanding and further our mission of education 
and preserving the history and culture of Native Americans. Quantitatively the outcome can be measured by the number 
of visitors served each year.
