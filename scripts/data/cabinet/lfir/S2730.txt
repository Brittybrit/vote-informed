 1. Project Title   Crystal Memorial Gardens Cemetery (Crystal River, FL) Revitalization

 2. Senate Sponsor   Blaise Ingoglia 
 3. Date of Request   3/4/2025 
The purpose of the project Crystal Memorial Gardens Cemetery (Crystal River, FL) Revitalization Project (Phase I) funds is 
to help maintain and revitalize the historic Crystal Memorial Gardens Cemetery: 
1) Hire a professional engineer to assess the flooding issues and develop a flood mitigation plan. Implement the 
recommended services to prevent the crypts and bodies from rising 
from the ground, possible relocation of crypts and bodies (if needed), and grading the cemetery land, 
2) Improve the cemetery road, 
3) The remaining trees needs professional servicing (cutting & canopy service), and 
4) Perform Ground Penetrating Radar and Document Headstones & Gravesites.
