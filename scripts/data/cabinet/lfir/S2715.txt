 The funds will be used to develop a new RV campground on state-owned land, expanding recreational access, 
promoting outdoor activity, and enhancing conservation efforts. This project will provide a desirable, managed access to

engagement, all while ensuring the land remains well-maintained and preserved for future generations.

 The funds will be used to develop and construct essential infrastructure for the park, including RV campsites, access 
roads, utility connections, and restrooms. Site preparation will involve grading, installing water and sewer lines, and 
setting up electrical hookups to accommodate visitors. Once completed, the park will offer camping, outdoor recreation, 
and nature exploration opportunities, providing a well-maintained and accessible space for residents and tourists to enjoy 
Florida’s natural resources. 
Recreational access for tourists and locals that want to better access the park. 
The many, many tourists that come to the nature coast. 
This project will expand public access to outdoor recreation, promoting tourism, local economic growth, and 
environmental appreciation while ensuring responsible land management for the state. The new park will provide safe 
and well-equipped camping facilities, attracting visitors and generating revenue for the local community. The success of 
the project will be measured by tracking visitor numbers, campsite reservations, and local economic impact, as well as 
monitoring environmental conditions to ensure sustainable land use and conservation efforts. 
The City of Inverness will build tasks into the scope of work and only complete payouts after benchmarks are reached 
and work is completed, contract will have an appropriate retainage amount to withhold until all work is complete and final 
payment is made.
