 1. Project Title   Expansion/improvement of existing vessel launch facilities 
 2. Senate Sponsor   Blaise Ingoglia 
 3. Date of Request   3/6/2025 
The Crystal River City Council, in partnership with the Citrus County BOCC, is in need of funding for adding capacity at the 
existing Fort Island Trail Park boat ramp. As part of the Riverwalk construction, the boat ramp located at NW 3rd Ave will 
soon be closed. The City previously requested that the State allow for the construction of a new boat ramp and was denied. 
An alternative to a new boat ramp is to improve the capacity at the Fort Island Trail Park ramp and/or parking area. A 
proposal was presented to the Citrus County BOCC and the City and County have entered into an interlocal agreement 
outlining the details of the partnership to include identify funding sources ($3.5 million estimated cost). The city owned boat 
ramp located at Pete’s Pier has deteriorated from age and multiple flooding events and is in need of renovation/repairs 
($1.5 million estimated). Due to the relocation of the NW 3rd Avenue boat ramp, this will be the only public ramp remaining 
within city limits.
