 1. Project Title   Wildwood Regional Water Reclamation Facility - BNR and Capacity 
Expansion Improvements 
 2. Senate Sponsor   Blaise Ingoglia 
 3. Date of Request   3/4/2025 
The recommended improvements contained within the Conceptual Design Report completed by CPH Inc, are necessary at 
the city’s water reclamation facility in order for the city to appropriately treat projected wastewater flows in accordance with 
applicable laws and regulations. The project has two components: improving the existing infrastructure to address aging 
equipment and increasing the wastewater treatment capacity to 5.5 million gallons per day. This BNR facility will meet 
Advanced Wastewater Treatment (AWT) standards set by the Department of Environmental Protection and will help protect 
our environment by reducing nutrient loading to water bodies and wetlands. In addition, these improvements will support 
residential/commercial growth and economic vitality within the City and Sumter County.

 This is a multi-faceted, regional project to replace aging infrastructure (~50 years old) and increase treatment facility 
capacity by the construction of a new AWT treatment facility to keep pace with the rapid population growth within the 
City's service area (City of Wildwood, The Villages, and Coleman Prison (federal). Reclaimed water will be used 
throughout the regional area. These funds will allow the City to: (1) meet current and future regional wastewater 
demands; (2) reduce nutrient loadings to the regional environment and springs (BMAP area); (3) enhance, preserve and 
improve the water quality of the groundwaters, surface waters, Rainbow/Silver springs (BMAP area), and wetlands; (4) 
protect the public from environmental harm; (5) increase economic activity; and (6) create job opportunities. 
The requested funds are for construction of a new regional AWT treatment facility to replace the failing/aging facility 
infrastructure (~50 years old), and to increase treatment capacity to keep pace with the rapid population growth. The City 
provides a regional solution to wastewater treatment and effluent disposal by treating wastewater generated within the 
City's service area (City of Wildwood, The Villages, and Coleman Prison (federal). The reclaimed water produced will be 
used throughout City's regional reclaimed water service area (City of Wildwood and The Villages/North Sumter Utilities). 
The project will also: (1) reduce nutrient loadings to the local area and the Rainbow/Silver Springs BMAP area; (2) 
prevent sanitary sewer and sludge overflows; (3) reduce groundwater demand (for drinking water) and increase 
groundwater quality; and (4) enhance and improve wildlife habitat. 
The improvements will provide the needed wastewater treatment capacity and infrastructure required within the City's 
regional service area due to the rapid population growth being experienced as well as the following: protect critical water 
resources in the regional area (surface waters, wetlands, groundwater, Rainbow/Silver springs (BMAP area); protect 
regional residences and property; protect the general public from harm; and enhance, preserve and improve the 
environment, wildlife quality and resources used by the regional residents and tourists by reducing sanitary sewer and 
sludge management system overflows. This project is critical to the citizens and businesses (both current and future) 
within the regional area (City of Wildwood, The Villages, Sumter County. 
The current City of Wildwood population is ~31,000. The population has doubled since the 2020 census and is estimated 
to increase to 131,000 by 2050. The rapid population increase requires substantial utility system improvements. The 
regional AWT treatment facility is the first major utility project and involves replacement of the existing antiquated 
treatment facility with a new facility capable of meeting Class I Reliability criteria, AWT standards, and significantly 
reducing effluent nutrient concentrations. The capacity will be expanded to account for rapid growth and increased 
wastewater flows. Financial assistance from the State is requested due to the rapid increase in population growth; the 
need to protect the protect the general public and thereby all of the regional natural water bodies from environmental 
harm. 
These funds will allow Wildwood to provide the following benefits/outcome for the regional citizens/tourists: (1) enhance, 
preserve and improve the environment and wildlife quality within the regional area; (2) protect the general public from 
environmental harm and the degradation of regional water resources; (3) protection of public and private property (yards, 
streets, structures, etc.); (4) increase tourism; (5) increase and improve economic activity within the regional area 
(Wildwood, the Villages, Sumter County); (5) improve groundwater quality; (6) improve surface water quality; and (7) 
improve drinking water quality. Outcomes will be measured by the decrease nutrient concentrations, and the reduced 
number of sanitary sewer and sludge management system overflows. 
Fines and clean-up costs associated with sanitary sewer and sludge management system overflows.
