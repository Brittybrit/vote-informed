 1. Project Title   Pasco County Sea Pines Flood Abatement 
 2. Senate Sponsor   Blaise Ingoglia 
 3. Date of Request   3/4/2025 
This area suffered extreme flooding during Hurricane Helene. Construction of stormwater management facilities to abate 
flooding and improve water quality will provide improved stormwater collection systems, conveyances, treatment systems, 
and storage. The project will remove 32 homes from flooding during the 100-year storm event and 1,828 feet of roadway 
from flooding during the 10-year storm event. Funding request will fund design, permitting and construction of stormwater 
infrastructure facilities to abate flooding and improve water quality. Total project cost $22,309,741. Local match 
$13,736,906, SWFWMD grant $2,749,309. State funds requested $5,823,526.
