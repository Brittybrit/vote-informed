This request is for swine, rabbit, and poultry pens as well as a swine, rabbit, and poultry barn to support the youth livestock 
shows and sales for the youth in Hernando County. The Florida Agriculture & Wildlife Expo took on the Youth Livestock 
Show and Sales after support from the Hernando County Board of County Commissions, Hernando County 4-H and 
Hernando County FFA programs. After years of neglect and unsafe conditions at the Hernando County Fair, the 4-H and 
FFA programs requested to show at the EXPO. We have been working on building infrastructure but need additional help.
