 1. Project Title   Hernando County Sheriff's Office Driving Pad and Gun Range 
 2. Senate Sponsor   Blaise Ingoglia 
 3. Date of Request   3/4/2025 
The Hernando County Sheriff's Office is requesting $3.8 million in funding to construct a modern driving pad and gun 
range, marking our agency’s first request of this kind. These state-of-the-art facilities will be integrated into the new 
Hernando County training complex, providing essential resources to ensure our officers are fully equipped to serve the 
community effectively. This initiative will directly enhance our partnership with the Law Enforcement Academies, offering a 
training environment for current and future officers. In addition, these facilities will be made available to state partners, 
including the Florida Department of Law Enforcement, the Florida Highway Patrol, and the Fish and Wildlife Conservation 
Commission, who have also faced difficulties finding suitable training locations following the potential closure of the Sand 
Hill Scout Reservation which previously accommodated these agencies.

 The driving pad and gun range will support growth and development for law enforcement within our agency as well as 
offer the best training resources available to students through our partnership with the Law Enforcement Academies. This 
investment will enhance the effectiveness and safety of law enforcement in our community which leads to strengthened 
public safety. 
The driving pad will provide a controlled environment for high-speed pursuit training, defensive driving techniques, and 
emergency vehicle operation. The gun range will offer a modern, secure location for firearms training, including 
proficiency testing, tactical exercises, and scenario-based drills. This will support the growth and development for law 
enforcement within our community. 
The driving pad will support community programs like the Teen Driving Challenge, which helps students become safer 
drivers by training them in vehicles they typically operate. The gun range will provide a secure facility for the Hernando 
County Sheriff’s Office S.A.F.E. Program (Situational Awareness Firearms Education), a public firearm safety initiative 
available to our community. Additionally, both the driving pad and gun range will enhance training opportunities for 
students attending the law enforcement academies, further supporting the development of skilled and prepared officers. 
The target population includes Hernando County law enforcement officers and the 218,000 residents of Hernando 
County. It will also benefit the Local Law Enforcement Academies, in addition to our state partners such as the Florida 
Department of Law Enforcement, the Florida Highway Patrol, the State Attorney's Office of the Fifth Judicial Circuit, and 
the Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission. 
The expected benefit of this project is to improve the effectiveness, safety, and professionalism of law enforcement 
officers, leading to a safer community in Hernando County. The outcome will be measured through specific performance 
metrics, such as the accuracy and speed of officers' responses in driving and shooting exercises while under pressure. 
These metrics will include reaction times, decision-making accuracy, and adherence to safety protocols during simulated 
high-stress scenarios. Performance improvements will be tracked through pre and post training assessments, focusing on 
skills and decision making under pressure. 
We are committed to meeting all deliverables and performance measures outlined in the contract, and if we're unable to 
do so, we believe the standard contract penalties will be appropriate.
