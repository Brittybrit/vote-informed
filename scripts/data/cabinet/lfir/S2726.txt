 The boat ramp is proposed to be placed on the Barge Canal which has been noted to be an area less subject to 
manatee use. By bringing boaters to this ramp will help reduce boaters in more manatee sensitive areas. Boating and 
boat facilities can help improve mental health by boosting moods with access to natural resources. Construction of the 
project will allow boaters to utilize the barge canal. The project may increase development potential and retail commerce 
as the project is along US19. Boat ramps are critical to eco-tourism. When constructed this ramp will provide boaters with 
direct access to the Gulf. 
Construction of a boat ramp facility will include a 400 foot wide ramp, five gangway's with floating docks (with the ability 
to add 6 more gangways in the future), restroom, 6 picnic pavilions, landscaping, lighting, 63 car parking spaces, 245 
boat parking spaces and associated storm water facilities. 
We anticipate the target population to be greater than 800 pleasure and fishing boaters. 
Construction of the project will allow boaters to utilize the barge canal which can be measured by collecting ramp use 
volume. 
If there is an area in which Citrus County is deficient, it is suggested that a corrective action plan be implemented and 
monitored to ensure that the deficient area(s) is/are corrected in a timely fashion. If Citrus County is not meeting the 
deliverables or the expected performance outlined in the contract, the State has the option to cancel unencumbered 
funds associated with that contract for these services.
