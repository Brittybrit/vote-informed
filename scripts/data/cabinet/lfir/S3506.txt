 This water asset emergency response vessel is equipped to combat fires on waterfront structures and ships, at the Port 
of Pensacola, delivering large volumes of water for firefighting, providing search and rescue operations, aiding in the 
evacuation of people in distress, and offering emergency medical support, all while navigating through hazardous 
environments like rough seas or polluted waters. 
The Pensacola fireboat will enhance public safety by providing rapid water-based emergency response, including 
firefighting, search and rescue, and medical assistance. Positioned strategically along the coastline, it will ensure quicker 
access to the Port of Pensacola, protect valuable maritime assets, and support disaster response efforts, benefiting 
citizens with improved safety and quicker response. 
All citizen of the City of Pensacola, Escambia County and Santa Rosa County that will be in Pensacola Bay. 
Reduction in response time-The city records response times. There should be a decrease in response times for fire boat

Increased boater safety-The number of boat related accident will decrease.

for their needs in the area.
