 This project is designed to enhance public safety training for local and regional law enforcement and correctional 
agencies. 
This project provides required firearms proficiency training for law enforcement and corrections personnel. Additionally, it 
provides a training center for hosting advanced and specialized training for local and regional law enforcement and 
correctional agencies. 
The target population served by this project includes elderly persons, university/college students, and students at 
Withlacoochee Technical College and pursuing a career in law enforcement or corrections. The target population would 
include students in the programs as well as all community citizens being served by graduates employed as law 
enforcement or correctional officers. 
The increase graduation and certification of law enforcement and corrections personnel. This project is essential to 
maintain our current accreditation of our regional training facility for law enforcement and correction educational 
programs. The outcome will be measured by successful completion/graduation rate of law enforcement and corrections 
cadets. 
If there is an area in which the Citrus County School System is deficient, it is suggested that a corrective action plan be 
implemented and monitored to ensure that the deficient area(s) is/are corrected in a timely manner. If the Citrus County 
School System is not meeting the deliverables or the expected performance outcomes outlined in the contract, the State 
has the option to cancel that contract for these services.
