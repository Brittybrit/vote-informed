 1. Project Title   Citrus County Halls River Multi-Use Path Construction Phase 
 2. Senate Sponsor   Blaise Ingoglia 
 3. Date of Request   3/4/2025 
Due to environmental and ROW constraints, the Halls River Road Multi-use Path (MUP) project consists of a 6 to 12 foot 
wide asphalt MUP separate from Halls River Road beginning at South Riverview Circle and ending at US 19 in Citrus 
County, Florida. This 3.2 miles of roadway is currently a two lane section with flush shoulders on each side within 100-120 
feet of right-of-way. The path would connect multiple residential communities to restaurants, various businesses, and 
shopping plaza’s along the route as well as connect to the newly constructed MUP along US19 which will run nearly the 
entire length of Citrus County once completed by the FDOT. 
The County has already contributed $1M towards phase 1 of the project and will contribute another $1M towards phase 2 
of the project. The County would request that phase 2 of the project continue to be managed and implemented by the 
FDOT.
