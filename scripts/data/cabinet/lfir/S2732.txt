 1. Project Title   Citrus County Schools - Crystal River High Health Academy Expansion 
 2. Senate Sponsor   Blaise Ingoglia 
 3. Date of Request   3/4/2025 
The proposed expansion for Crystal River High School’s Health Academy will provide high school students with the 
opportunity to gain practical knowledge and hands-on experience in the healthcare field, preparing them for careers in 
health-related professions. The program will offer specialized coursework, internships, mentorship opportunities, and 
certifications. By equipping students with the skills and experience necessary to pursue careers in this critical and growing 
sector, the Health Academy will not only benefit students but also contribute to addressing the healthcare workforce 
shortage in our community. The requested funds will be used to perform renovations on the current Health Academy 
facilities and to purchase curriculum, supplies, and equipment to meet current industry standards.

 The goal is to empower Citrus County students with the knowledge and skills needed for high-skilled, high-demand 
health related careers by expanding current health academy pathways at Crystal River High School. The students will 
learn technical health focused skills, related hands on training, and master the knowledge needed to be successful and 
earn industry certification in health related fields. Students will be ready for direct employment or advanced training at any 
post secondary school within the Florida College System. 
The Crystal River High School Health Academy will provide courses with challenging standards, hands on training, 
relevant technical knowledge, and skills needed to earn industry certification for direct employment in a health related 
field or advanced training at a post secondary school to build the students' careers in various health pathways. 
The direct services will be four years of high school courses to prepare our students to be employable directly out of high 
school or to continue to advanced training at a postsecondary facility. This will fill the skilled medical trade gap in our 
area, promote economic growth, and establish a high standard of living for the residents of Citrus County. 
The targeted population will be students from across the county who are interested in developing skills needed for 
medically associated careers. We currently have 225 students enrolled annually in the Health Academy at Crystal River 
High School. 
The expected outcome is to increase industry certifications earned, graduation rates, and essential jobs filled for 
community growth. This project will also decrease the high school dropout rate increasing the number of students 
entering the workforce with high-skilled, high-wage jobs. These data points will be gathered and compared to the 
previous year's data. 
Citrus County Schools is committed to addressing Florida's Health Trade shortage through innovative training. In the 
event that performance measures are not met, we will ask for a 60 day performance improvement plan.
