 1. Project Title   Crystal River Waste Water Treatment Plant Upgrades and Expansion 
 2. Senate Sponsor   Blaise Ingoglia 
 3. Date of Request   3/6/2025 
The City has evaluated necessary upgrades to the Crystal River Wastewater Treatment Plant to meet upcoming higher 
effluent treatment standards and expand capacity for growth. A comprehensive Facility Plan was developed to identify the 
process improvements and capacity modifications needed to meet the 3.0 mg/L TN effluent limit, conduct a site evaluation 
and condition assessment to identify deficiencies, collaborate with Citrus County and FGUA to update the City's utility 
service area, project wastewater flows over the next 20 years to assess future capacity needs, and identify three 
improvement alternatives to meet regulatory requirements. The City has secured $12 million in grants and funding toward 
the estimated $24 million total cost, leaving a $12 million funding gap.
