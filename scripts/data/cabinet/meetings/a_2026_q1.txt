FLORIDA LAND AND WATER ADJUDICATORY COMMISSION
AGENDA
February 24, 2026 
1. Request authorization to repeal Rule Chapters 42A-1, 42L-1, and 42V-1, FAC. The
referenced rule chapters and associated rules address three community development
districts that were originally established by the Commission some years ago. The three
rule chapters have since been rendered obsolete as a result of certain local government
or district action. Each rule chapter is identified below with background information
on the local government or district action precipitating the recommendation to repeal.
A. Rule Chapter 42A-1, Indian Trace Community Development District (CDD) 
The Indian Trace CDD was established by the Commission and became effective on
July 15, 1981. There are four rules in Rule Chapter 42A-l, associated with the CDD. The
four rules consist of:
42A-l.001 Creation
42A-l.002 Boundary
42A-l.003 Supervisors
42A-l.004 Effective Date
During a recent rule review exercise pursuant to section 120.05435, Florida Statutes, it was
determined that the rules associated with the Indian Trace CDD were rendered obsolete as a
result of action of the City of Weston through adoption of City Ordinance No. 18 on 9/2/97.
The Ordinance transitioned the CDD to a Chapter 189, Florida Statutes, district. No
notification of this action was ever made to the Commission. Having recently become aware
of the local government action, Commission staff requests authorization to repeal Rule
Chapter 42A-1 and associated rules as they are no longer necessary.
