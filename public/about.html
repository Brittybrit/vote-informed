Ballot305.org

About

Ballot305 is a free, independent, nonpartisan voter guide for Miami-Dade County, built and run by Brit Lawrence (@realbritty), a Miami Beach resident.

Who runs it

Ballot305 is not affiliated with any candidate, campaign, political party, political committee, or government office, including the Miami-Dade Elections Department. It is not an official ballot and does not record votes.

Who pays for it

Ballot305 is paid for by its creator. It takes no money from candidates, campaigns, parties, or political committees. The running costs are web hosting and the AI service used for research.

Where the information comes from

Donor and spending figures come from public filings with the Federal Election Commission, the Florida Division of Elections, Miami-Dade County, and city clerks. Endorsements, ratings, and background come from AI-assisted research of public web sources, linked on each page. How it works explains every rule, including what the tool can get wrong.

Donor names come from public records. They are shown to inform voters and may not be used to solicit contributions or for any commercial purpose. Federal law restricts that use of FEC contributor information.

Corrections

Every candidate and measure page has a Report a problem with this page link at the bottom. Reports go straight to the site owner. We review reports within 24 hours and take down anything false while we correct it.

Candidates, campaigns, and anyone named on the site can also write to corrections@ballot305.org.

Privacy

Ballot305 has no accounts and no ads. Your ballot plan is saved only in the browser on your own device. On a shared computer, use Forget everything on this device under your saved ballot plans when you're done. The site does not collect your name or address. If you send a problem report, it keeps what you wrote, and contact details only if you give them, for 90 days.

Open source

All of the code is public on GitHub under the MIT license.
