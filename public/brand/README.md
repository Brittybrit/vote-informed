# Ballot305 brand kit

## Mark
- `mark.svg`: 305 Bubble. Use for anything 48px and up (home screen, social avatar, press).
- `mark-small.svg`: plain bubble. Use under 48px (browser tab). The 305 is unreadable that small.
- `mark-square.svg`: no rounded corners, for platforms that round or crop for you (iOS, Instagram).

## Wordmark
- `wordmark.svg`: BALL●T305, navy on light backgrounds.
- `wordmark-reverse.svg`: paper on navy.
- `wordmark-org.svg`: with .ORG, when the URL needs to be in the logo.
- The O is always a filled oval. Never set it as a letter O.

## Colors
- Ink (navy) `#131F3C`
- Paper `#FAFAF7`
- Ink soft `#3D4763`
- Lean tags only, never as brand colors: left `#1D4ED8`, right `#B91C1C`, nonpartisan `#374151`

## Type
- Libre Franklin Black (900) for headlines, all caps.
- IBM Plex Mono for labels, data, and fine print.
- Both free on Google Fonts.

## Voice
- Tagline: Follow the money, honey.
- Descriptor: For an informed, engaged Miami-Dade.
- Always say free and nonpartisan. Never imply it is official or affiliated with the elections department.

PNG exports are in `png/`.
