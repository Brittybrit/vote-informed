Error reports

Private page for the site owner. Needs the ADMIN_TOKEN secret.

What the buttons do

Re-research drops the cached profile so the next visitor gets fresh research. Use it when the profile is stale or the fix is likely to come out right on a second pass.

Take down removes the profile and shows visitors "being corrected" until you put it back. Use it for anything false about a real person.

Put back lifts a take-down; the next visitor gets fresh research.

Done removes the report from this list. Reports also expire after 90 days.
